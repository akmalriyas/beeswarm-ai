The **Spider** is a level 4 [mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") that can only be found in the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"). 

It takes 30 minutes for the spider to respawn after being defeated (25 minutes 30 seconds with a [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee")). Defeating it gives 10 [Battle Points](/wiki/Battle_Points "Battle Points") and ![Honey](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c6/Honey.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230410071605)1,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") spread across as 5 ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") tokens. Various other rewards are also possible with their probability increasing with [Loot Luck](/wiki/Loot_Luck "Loot Luck").

## Strategies[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSpider%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * One way to keep idle while defeating the spider is to stay on the first block in the [Bamboo Obby](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses#Bamboo_Field_Obby "Obstacle Courses") \- the spider will try to lunge towards the player, but it will be stopped by the block. The player can also stand near the edge of the [Vicious Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Vicious_Bee_Egg_Claim "Vicious Bee Egg Claim").
  * The [Frozen Field Defenders Glitch](/wiki/Glitches#Frozen_Field_Defenders "Glitches") can be used to kill the spider easily, just like any other normal mob.
  * Another method is to charge at the spider, then jump and glide over the spider, though this will require the [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute") or [Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSpider%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This, the [Mantis](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis") and the [Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf") are the only mobs that can attack players outside of their designated fields.
  * There is a ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") token located within its residence (which is the Spider Cave).
  * The Spider is tied with the Werewolf as the regular mobs with the least amount of spawn locations, as they both only have one.
  * Unlike real-life spiders, who have eight legs and six/eight eyes, the spider only has six legs and four eyes. It also appears to have a head and thorax, despite spiders and other arachnids having a cephalothorax. This is joked about in the Forward Facing Spider [sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker")'s description, which reads: _"Not enough legs to technically be a spider."_
  * Two creatures similar to the spider in appearance called [Cave Monsters](/wiki/Cave_Monster "Cave Monster") are found inside the [Werewolf's Cave](/wiki/Werewolf%27s_Cave "Werewolf's Cave"). However, they have vastly different stats.
  * Spiders have a faint red glow under their eyes, but it’s mostly visible at night.
