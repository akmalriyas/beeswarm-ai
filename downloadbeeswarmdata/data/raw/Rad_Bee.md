**Rad Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Rare bee](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"). 

Rad Bee's favorite treat is ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"). 

Rad Bee likes the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field") and the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). It dislikes the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRad_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 13 [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 80 [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 3 seconds.
  * +25% convert speed, +3 [gather amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.1 [Red Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Red_Pollen "System Page").
