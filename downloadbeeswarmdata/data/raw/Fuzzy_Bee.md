**Fuzzy Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Mythic bee](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic"). 

Fuzzy Bee's favorite treat is ![Pineapple](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"). 

Fuzzy Bee likes the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field") and [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). It dislikes the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFuzzy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 100 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 6 seconds.
  * Makes 40 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 6 seconds.
  * +150% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), -15% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), -50% gather and convert speed, -40 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +90 [Gather Amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats"), +2 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.1 [Bomb Power](/wiki/System_Page#Bomb_Pollen "System Page").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFuzzy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Fuzz Bombs]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Fuzz_Bombs "Ability Tokens")** Spawns 2 (+1 every 5 bee lvls) fuzz bombs that wander the [Field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") for 8 seconds. Catching them collects pollen and [Pollinates](/wiki/Pollination "Pollination") nearby [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The amount of pollen collected scales with buzz bomb multipliers and bee Level.
  * **[[Buzz Bomb+]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")** Collects 7 pollen from 29 surrounding flowers (+10% pollen per Level). This combined with other bombs will increase power.
  * **[[🌟Gifted Ability: Pollen Haze]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Pollen_Haze "Ability Tokens")** Summons a yellow haze over the field for 30s that pollinates up to 30 random flowers every second and improves flower growth rate. Bomb Tokens within the haze are automatically collected and pollinate flowers. If there has been a Pollen Haze over the field within the last 3 minutes, this summons fuzz bombs instead.
  * **[[Passive: Fuzzy Coat]](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Fuzzy_Coat "Passive Abilities")** This bee may pollinate up to 5 nearby flowers (9 if gifted) every time it gathers. Pollinated flowers are temporarily upgraded to grant and store more pollen and grow faster. Lower tier flowers have a higher chance of being pollinated.

Fuzzy Bee has a base pollen collection of **100![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **6 seconds**. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFuzzy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This bee, [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"), and [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") are the first Mythic bees to be added since the release of the original Mythic bees. 
    * It's also the only Mythic bee to be introduced on its own. 
      * Additionally, Fuzzy Bee is the only Mythic bee to be added outside of [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas").
  * This bee is currently the only bee that can pollinate flowers without the help of a [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip").
  * This bee may like the Dandelion Field because [dandelions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taraxacum "wikipedia:Taraxacum") in real life are also soft and fuzzy.
  * This bee, [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee"), [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee"), and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") are the only bees with one skin color.
  * This bee, [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee"), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee") and [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") all have four different [abilities](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), the most for any [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in the game.
  * The [Fuzzy Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Specific_Bee_Eggs "Egg") was the fifth specific bee egg that could have been bought for [Robux](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop"), after [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"), [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee"), Windy Bee, and [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee") and before Precise Bee and Buoyant Bee. 
    * Fuzzy Bee was the first non-Event bee that could have been purchased with Robux.
  * Fuzz bombs can be popped by using petal Shurikens from the [Petal Wand](/wiki/Petal_Wand "Petal Wand") and [Petal Storm](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Petal_Storm "Passive Abilities"), Windy Bee's [tornado](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Tornado "Ability Tokens"), and Puppy Bee's [fetch ball](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Fetch "Ability Tokens"). 
    * Any player can pop fuzz bombs, even if they were summoned by another player's Fuzzy Bee.
  * Fuzzy Bee is the only [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") bee that was introduced in 2020 (not counting [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee"), as it wasn't usable until 2022).
  * Fuzzy Bee's Fuzz Bombs can pop bubbles when the fuzz bombs touch them.
  * There was a visual glitch that causes Fuzzy Bee to not have it's fur on it despite the fact it has it when you obtain it through Eggs or Royal Jelly (You will only see Fuzzy Bee's Body and face)
