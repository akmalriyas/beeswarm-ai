**Commander Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Epic bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"). 

Commander Bee's favorite treat is ![Sunflower Seed](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"). 

Commander Bee likes the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field") and the [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"). It dislikes the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommander_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 15 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 80 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +50% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), +5 [Gather Amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats"), +5% [Critical Chance](/wiki/Critical_Hits "Critical Hits"), +3 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +3% Critical Chance.



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommander_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Focus]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Focus "Ability Tokens")** Grants +3% Critical Chance for 20 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times. [Critical hits](/wiki/Critical_Hits "Critical Hits") increase pollen and damage by 100%.
  * **[[Buzz Bomb]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")** Collects 7 pollen from 13 surrounding [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") (+10% pollen per [Level](/wiki/Bond "Bond")). Combo with other bombs to increase power.

Commander Bee has a base pollen collection of **15![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommander_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Commander Bee's skin is the same as [Looker Bee's](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee").
  * This bee, [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee") and [Frosty Bee](/wiki/Frosty_Bee "Frosty Bee") are the only 3 [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in the game that wear hats by default. 
    * Of the three, this one is the only colorless bee to have one.
    * [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") and [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"), however, can have an accessory by using the ![Festive Wreath](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Wreath](/wiki/Festive_Wreath "Festive Wreath") and ![Reindeer Antlers](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Reindeer Antlers](/wiki/Reindeer_Antlers "Reindeer Antlers") respectively.
  * This bee is one of the 3 bees that have the Focus ability by default, the other 2 are Looker Bee, and [Music Bee](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee").
  * Commander Bee is the only epic bee added in an [update](/wiki/Updates "Updates").
  * A ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Commander Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Commander_Bee_Egg "Egg") can be obtained by capturing [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick") 200 times.
  * As the name and model suggest, this bee is likely inspired by the eponymous video game protagonist known as Commander Keen. 
    * This point is further supported by the expired [code](/wiki/Codes "Codes") "Keen4", which yielded a ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Commander Bee Jelly](/wiki/Egg#Commander_Bee_Jelly "Egg").
