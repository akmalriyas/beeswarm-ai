# Treat

Treat

[![Treat](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a8/Treat.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041321)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a8/Treat.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041321)

_"Increases your bond with a bee by 10."_

A **Treat** is a type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that can be fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to raise its bond by 10. They are the easiest **treat** to obtain but are not the favorite treat of any bee. They can also be used to craft the [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask "Honey Mask") where 9,999 **treats** are needed. 

[![A treat token.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/09/Treat_token.png/revision/latest?cb=20181222124739) [![A treat token.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/09/Treat_token.png/revision/latest?cb=20181222124739)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/09/Treat_token.png/revision/latest?cb=20181222124739) [](/wiki/File:Treat_token.png)

A treat token.

## Ways to Obtain[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTreat%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Treat Dispenser](/wiki/Treat_Dispenser "Treat Dispenser") near the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop")/[Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch") (Like the [Honey Dispenser](/wiki/Honey_Dispenser "Honey Dispenser"), this requires being a member of the [BSS Club](https://www.roblox.com/groups/3982592/Bee-Swarm-Simulator-Club#!/about)).
  * Buying from the Treat Shop next to under Mother Bear's awning for ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)100 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") per treat and capping out at ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)10,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") per treat.
  * As a reward from [memory matches](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match").
  * As a stack reward for adding certain [stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") to the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack"): 
    * The Fork And Knife rewards 500 treats.



### Drops[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTreat%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Farming in the [Dandelion](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"), [Mushroom](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Clover](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), [Spider](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), [Cactus](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), [Pumpkin](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"), [Coconut](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field"), or [Stump](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field") Fields. 
    * Drops from [leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves") in these fields may also yield treats.
  * A relatively high chance of dropping from [rhino beetles](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"), [ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug"), [spiders](/wiki/Spider "Spider"), [mantises](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis"), [scorpions](/wiki/Scorpion "Scorpion"), [werewolves](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf"), [aphids](/wiki/Aphid "Aphid"), [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug"), [Stick Nymphs](/wiki/Stick_Nymph "Stick Nymph"), [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle"), and [stump snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail").
  * A guaranteed drop from [Mondo Chicks](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick").
  * 2nd and 3rd capture of [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick") rewards 2 and 5 treats respectively.
  * "Playing" [Fetch](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Fetch "Ability Tokens") with [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee").
  * As a drop from a [Festive Gift](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens") or [Festive Mark](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Mark "Ability Tokens").
  * As a gift from a [cub buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy").
  * As a reward for completely growing any kind of [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") except for Gummy and Debug sprouts.
  * Harvesting [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles").
  * As a drop from [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom").
  * As a drop from Planters, which include: 
    * ![Plastic Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Plastic Planters](/wiki/Plastic_Planter "Plastic Planter")
    * ![Candy Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Candy Planters](/wiki/Candy_Planter "Candy Planter")
    * ![Red Clay Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Red Clay Planters](/wiki/Red_Clay_Planter "Red Clay Planter")
    * ![Tacky Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tacky Planters](/wiki/Tacky_Planter "Tacky Planter")
  * Activating certain [targets](/wiki/Target "Target") grants a small amount of treats.
  * Using Beesmas Feast during Beesmas Will give treats and other items.



### Quests[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTreat%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Black Bear gives treats on the following quests: 
    * Get Red-y gives 100 treats.
    * One Stop On The Tip Top gives 200 treats.
    * Blue Mushrooms 2 gives 300 treats.
    * Pretty Pumpkins gives 400 treats.
    * Black Bear, Why? gives 500 treats.
    * Bee A Star gives 1,000 treats.
    * The [Mythic Egg](/wiki/Egg#Mythic_Egg "Egg") questline gives treats on all quests, at a sum of 195,500 treats.
    * Black Bear's repeatable quests also reward treats.
  * [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") gives treats on the following quests: 
    * Mark Mechanics gives 3,000 treats.
    * Epistemological Endeavor gives 10,000 treats.
  * All of Mother Bear's quests give treats, 4,020 treats in total.
  * All of Polar Bear's quests give 10 treats.
  * All of Onett's quests give treats, 25,000 treats in total.
  * Every 5th completion of Honey Bee's quest may give treats.
