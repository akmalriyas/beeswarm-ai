The **Party Cogturret** is one of the mobs exclusive to the [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party"). It behaves similarly to a normal [Cogturret](/wiki/Cogturret "Cogturret") but spawns on a random field instead of following the player on the field they are standing on. They first appear on Cake Rank 5 and continue to spawn in the following ranks.

## Strategies[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FParty_Cogturret%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Party Cogturrets aim for where the player is, not where the player will be. Because of this, moving to the side after they fire a cog will often result in Party Cogturrets missing their attack.
  * Party Cogturrets will always shoot straight at where they are facing so you can avoid their attacks simply by not being in front of them.
  * Stand near the Party Cogturrets, this will allow your bees to lock on to the Party Cogturrets and quickly deal damage.
  * [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") provide excellent crowd control, especially since Party Cogturrets often come in pairs or packs. [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") can also stun many Party Cogturrets at once and allow them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mind_Hack "Ability Tokens").
