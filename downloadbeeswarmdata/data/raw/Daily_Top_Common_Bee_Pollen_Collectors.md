# Daily Top Common Bee Pollen Collectors

The **Daily Top Common Bee Pollen Collectors** is one of the 64 [leaderboards](/wiki/Leaderboards "Leaderboards") in the game, and is only viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu in the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page"). This leaderboard shows how much [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") players have collected with only [Common Bees](/wiki/Bees/Common "Bees/Common") on that day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM CST. 

## Prizes[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDaily_Top_Common_Bee_Pollen_Collectors%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

If the player has reached the top 5 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with the **Round Basic Bee[Sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker")**. 

If the player has reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with ![Ticket](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Ticket.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404015820)50 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
