# Spit

[![A patch of flowers that has sparkles.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3d/Sparkles.png/revision/latest?cb=20200531145649) [![A patch of flowers that has sparkles.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3d/Sparkles.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200531145649)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3d/Sparkles.png/revision/latest?cb=20200531145649) [](/wiki/File:Sparkles.png)

A patch of flowers that has sparkles.

**Sparkles** are an effect applied to certain patches of [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") that yields a token containing ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), random [treats](/wiki/Treats "Treats") corresponding to the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") it is in, ![Moon Charm](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Moon Charms](/wiki/Moon_Charm "Moon Charm"), or rarely ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), ![Neonberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Neonberry](/wiki/Neonberry "Neonberry"), ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), or ![Glitter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") when harvested. When enough [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") is collected from a sparkling flower, it will yield a token for any player to collect. If it isn't collected within 10 seconds, the token will disappear. 

The largest plants in the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field"), [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), and [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch") will occasionally generate a face and blow bubbles 3 to 5 times on their respective fields, creating 13 patches of pollen that sparkle. The [Blue HQ's](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ") Bubble Wand blows bubbles on the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"), [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), and [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field") in a similar fashion. Each aforementioned location can generate sparkles around every 45 minutes, individually.[1] Individual [fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies") that spawn during the [night](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle "Day/Night Cycle") also leave behind sparkles on flowers they were on as they are chased off.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSparkles%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * In the [2018-07-11 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-07-11 "Updates"), the change to the size of the Clover Field was not taken into account, so the [Bubble Wand](/wiki/Bubble_Wand "Bubble Wand") model on the Blue HQ could sometimes produce sparkles in the area where there are no flowers.
  * Prior to the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), sparkles covered 4 flowers instead of 13.
  * In the [2018-09-10 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-09-10 "Updates"), the chances of sparkles being created were doubled.
  * The Bubble Wand model on the Blue HQ can leave sparkles right under the largest mushroom in the Mushroom Field, making it difficult to collect the rewards.
  * Sparkles used to be called "spit" by the community, which came from the various faces looking like they were spitting on the field. [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") later on named this effect "sparkles" like it is today. [![Sparkletificated](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fa/Sparkletificated.png/revision/latest?cb=20241214175420) [![Sparkletificated](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fa/Sparkletificated.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20241214175420)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fa/Sparkletificated.png/revision/latest?cb=20241214175420) [](/wiki/File:Sparkletificated.png)
    * According to Onett, the verb form for sparkles is ‘sparkletificate’.
  * When disappearing, the eyes on a face close before fading away.
  * Spirit Bear mentions the sparkles in her dialogue before you receive her 8th quest, remarking how the Sparkles are "gross" in terms of how they were created by the faces.
