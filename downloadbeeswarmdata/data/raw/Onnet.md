**Onett** is a quest giver located past the [Bear Gate](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate"). He gives out a series of five challenging [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests"). The rewards for the last quest include a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"). His quests require the player to complete different tasks, requesting the player to collect 3,673,750,000 [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"), 136,000,000 [goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo"), 153,000 [tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), and to defeat 940 [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") throughout all of his Star Journey quests.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOnett%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * He is the first and only robloxian-species [quest giver](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers"). 
    * He and the [Mysterious Figure](/wiki/Mysterious_Figure "Mysterious Figure") are the only [NPCs](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC") of the robloxian-species.
    * He is the only quest giver whose appearance changes during every event.
  * There is a [Statue of Onett](/wiki/Easter_Eggs#Onett_Statue "Easter Eggs") on top of the 6th [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") (from Left to Right) with the [Honey Dipper](/wiki/Honey_Dipper "Honey Dipper") and the [Port-O-Hive](/wiki/Port-O-Hive "Port-O-Hive") as a backpack, while the Onett NPC has a [Porcelain Dipper](/wiki/Porcelain_Dipper "Porcelain Dipper"), [Porcelain Port-O-Hive](/wiki/Porcelain_Port-O-Hive "Porcelain Port-O-Hive"), [Mondo Belt Bag](/wiki/Mondo_Belt_Bag "Mondo Belt Bag"), [Riley Guard](/wiki/Riley_Guard "Riley Guard"), [Bucko Guard](/wiki/Bucko_Guard "Bucko Guard"), [Beekeeper's Mask](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Mask "Beekeeper's Mask"), and [Beekeeper's Boots](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Boots "Beekeeper's Boots"). 
    * The pollen in the backpack of said statue is a [Code](/wiki/Codes "Codes").
  * Star Journey 1 was originally planned for players to collect 500 [haste tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens"), not 5,000. But because of a scripting error, the requirement was set to 5,000 haste tokens. 
    * The requirement was later reduced to 2,500 haste tokens.
  * The total amount of [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") required to complete his quests is 3,673,750,000 pollen and the total amount of [Goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo") required to complete the quests is 136,000,000 goo.
  * The amount of pollen in Onett's backpack (32318) represents the date when [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator") was first released (on March 23, 2018).
  * Ever since [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") left, Onett's and [Science Bear's](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") quests were the only ones that included collecting goo. However, the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates") introduced [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee") and [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee"), requiring goo for their "Clean-up," "Goo," and "Medley" quests. During all three of the [Beesmas Events](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas"), [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") also required goo in certain quests, and during the [Egg Hunt 2019](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2019 "Egg Hunt 2019") Event, [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear") also required collecting goo for his "Egg Hunt: Polar Bear" quest. [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") also has goo requirements for her quests.
  * He and [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear") were the first quest givers to give quests that required defeating [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), with Science Bear being the last.
  * On Onett's backpack, it says that its capacity is the equivalent to 500k. However, considering the [items](/wiki/Items "Items") he wears, it should be about at least 900k of total capacity.
  * Onett gives 8 quests (including limited quests), the least of all non-event quest givers in the game. 
    * If counting event quests, he is surpassed by [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man"), who gives 4 of them and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug"), who gives only 3.
  * Onett is currently the fourth to last quest giver to be accessed in the game, the third to last being Bubble Bee Man, the second to last being Spirit Bear, and the last being [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear").
  * During the Beesmas events 2018-2021, Onett wore a wreath around his head.
  * During Beesmas 2022, Onett wore a robot mask and a cog on his belt.
  * During Beesmas, once the player finished all of their quests, the player could give him a ![Present](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Present](/wiki/Present "Present"). However, there was a temporary glitch where it wasn't possible to give him one.
  * Onett, Tunnel Bear, and Gummy Bear were "Naughty" in 2018, as stated by Bee Bear. 
    * Onett was also the only NPC who could have been given a present to who was on Bee Bear's "naughty" list.
  * If the player had finished all of his quests during the Beesmas 2019 event, Onett stated that he's been "twisting the lid the wrong way for months."
  * He is one of the two quest givers who did not give an Egg Hunt quest, the other being [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)").
  * During the [Egg Hunt 2020](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2020 "Egg Hunt 2020") Event, Onett wore the [Swarming Egg of the Hive](/wiki/Swarming_Egg_of_the_Hive "Swarming Egg of the Hive"), which given to players when they completed the egg hunt with [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear").
  * When you give Onett a present in [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") 2020, the present you gave him includes a collection of Korean skin care products. The detail that states the products are specifically Korean refers to how Korean skin care products are generally very high-quality.
  * Onett the quest giver and the statue are the only NPCs with a bag.
  * During Beesmas 2020, there was a temporary glitch which allowed players to claim the Honeyday Event buff and other stuff from Onett by talking to him (or rejoining the game) multiple times. This glitch was soon fixed after a shutdown.
  * When interacting with Onett while still having the Star Journey 1 quest, the text at the end of the dialogue is a riddle for one of the codes that would give a ![7-Pronged Cog](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[7-Pronged Cog](/wiki/7-Pronged_Cog "7-Pronged Cog"), used to craft the [Strange Goggles](/wiki/Strange_Goggles "Strange Goggles") in the [Ready Player Two](/wiki/Ready_Player_Two "Ready Player Two") event.
  * Onett and [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear") are the only NPCs whose interaction have been a requirement to complete a [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests"). 
    * You need to talk to Onett for [Mother Bear's](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear") "⌛Waiting With Sun Bear (5/6): And Mother Bear. Again" quest.
