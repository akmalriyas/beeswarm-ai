**Rage Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Epic bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"). 

Rage Bee's favorite treat is ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"). 

Rage Bee likes the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field") and [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). It dislikes the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRage_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 80 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +4 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats"), +10% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +10% [Bee Attack](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Attack "System Page").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRage_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Rage]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Rage "Ability Tokens")** Grants your [Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") +1 attack for 45s. Stacks up to 4 times.
  * **[[Token Link]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Token_Link "Ability Tokens")** Collects all other [Ability Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") (except for other token links), granting 25 honey (+10 per Level) per token collected.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRage_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Rage Bee is the first [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to produce an ability token that has their face on it, the second being [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"), and the third being [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"). 
    * This bee is also the only non-[Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") to do so.
  * Rage Bee and [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee") are the only bees that can produce rage tokens.
  * Rage Bee has three different skin colors, similar to [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"). When gifted, it only has two different skin colors, while gifted Baby Bee maintains this trait.
  * A [Special Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Specific_Bee_Jelly "Royal Jelly") that changes a bee into a Rage Bee could be obtained by giving [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear") a [Present](/wiki/Present "Present") during the [Beesmas 2018 event](/wiki/Updates#2018-12-19 "Updates") (no longer obtainable).
  * The [Rage Aphid](/wiki/Aphid#Rage_Aphid "Aphid") and gifted Spicy Bee has a similar color scheme to Rage Bee's gifted form.
  * This is one of the only bees that can have an extra ability from [Beequips](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") (specifically [Melody](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Melody "Ability Tokens") from the [Toy Horn](/wiki/Toy_Horn "Toy Horn") Beequip).
  * The ability could previously stack up to only 3 times.
  * The blue counterpart for this bee is [Frosty Bee](/wiki/Frosty_Bee "Frosty Bee").
