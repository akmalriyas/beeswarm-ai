_Not to be confused with[Party Mechsquitos](/wiki/Party_Mechsquito "Party Mechsquito"), the smaller counterparts of Party Mega Mechsquitos or the [Mega Mechsquito](/wiki/Mega_Mechsquito "Mega Mechsquito"), the normal version of these._

The **Party Mega Mechsquito** is one of the mobs exclusive to the [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party"). It functions similarly to a regular [Mega Mechsquito](/wiki/Mega_Mechsquito "Mega Mechsquito"), but they roam around a field rather than spawning at the vicinity of the player. They first spawn on Rank 15 and continue to keep spawning in the following ranks. The Party Mega Mechsquitos, similarly to [Mega Mechsquitos,](/wiki/Mega_Mechsquito "Mega Mechsquito") fly away whenever a player is near them. This is the explanation to why the player always keeps distance from them.

## Strategies[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FParty_Mega_Mechsquito%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Since they are essentially the same as their normal counterparts, Mega Mechsquitos, the same methods used for them can be followed. 

  * Party Mega Mechsquitos aim for where the player is, not where the player will be. Because of this, moving around in circles will often result in Party Mega Mechsquitos missing their attack.
  * Stand near the Party Mega Mechsquito, this will allow your bees to lock on to the Party Mega Mechsquito and quickly deal damage.
  * [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") provide excellent crowd control, especially since Party Mega Mechsquitos often come in pairs or packs. [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") can also stun many Party Mega Mechsquitos at once and allow them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mind_Hack "Ability Tokens").
  * Unlike their normal counterparts, they stay in fields which can be used for the players advantage. Stand right outside the field they are in and your bees will attack it while it doesn't aim for you. Note that this can be a bit risky since they can hover around the other parts of the field and don't come close enough for your bees to target them.
