# Battle Points

[![The Global Top Battlers leaderboard.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fd/GlobalTopBattlersOctober5th2019.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20191006020145)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fd/GlobalTopBattlersOctober5th2019.png/revision/latest?cb=20191006020145) [](/wiki/File:GlobalTopBattlersOctober5th2019.png)

The Global Top Battlers leaderboard.

**Battle Points** are points used for [Global Top Battlers Leaderboard](/wiki/Global_Top_Battlers_Leaderboard "Global Top Battlers Leaderboard") rankings and the [Battle Badge](/wiki/Badges#Battle_Badge "Badges"). They are obtained by defeating certain [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs"). 

The player can see how many battle points they have by stepping on the pad in front of the Global Top Battlers Leaderboard or by looking in your [badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges") and scrolling down to the Battle Badge. 

## Battle Points per Mob[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_Points%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * [Ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug"), [rhino beetles](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"), and [cave monsters](/wiki/Cave_Monster "Cave Monster"): 1 point.
  * [Mantises](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis") and [scorpions](/wiki/Scorpion "Scorpion"): 5 points.
  * [Spiders](/wiki/Spider "Spider"): 10 points.
  * [Aphids](/wiki/Aphid "Aphid"): 10 points if normal, 20 if rage, armored, or diamond.
  * [Werewolves](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf"): 25 points.
  * [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle"): 150 points.
  * [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"): 200 points.
  * [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab"): 400 points.
  * [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail"): 500 points.



**Mobs not mentioned give zero battle points.**
