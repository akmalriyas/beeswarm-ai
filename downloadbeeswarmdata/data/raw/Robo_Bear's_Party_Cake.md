# Robo Bear's Party Cake

[![The Robo Party Cake from Beesmas 2022.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/05/Cake.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20230401193148) [![The Robo Party Cake from Beesmas 2022.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/05/Cake.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230401193148)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/05/Cake.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20230401193148) [](/wiki/File:Cake.PNG)

The Robo Party Cake from Beesmas 2022.

The **Robo Party Cake** is a [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas")-exclusive machine that was added in the second part of Beesmas 2022 and has returned in every Beesmas events since. It is accessible after completing [Robo Bear's Party Cake quest](/wiki/Robo_Bear#Beesmas_Quest_-_Robo_Bear's_Party_Cake "Robo Bear") and is able to initiate a special challenge every 3 hours. 

## Appearance[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRobo_Party_Cake%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Before completing Robo Bear's quest, the party cake is only 3 metal trusses, stacked to make a pyramid, with an unlit olive green candle on the top. The candle's number changes every year to show how many years the game's been up. 

After the quest is finished, the "number" candle is lit with neon lights and a blue outline. The cake layers are olive green with different colored frames, the bottom layer blue, the middle yellow, and the top purple. Candles and sparklers are placed on the corners, and neon sprinkles are placed around the front of each layer. 

## Robo Party[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRobo_Party_Cake%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The **Robo Party** is a server-wide event where everyone has to defeat all the Party Robots scattered around the [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"). The party is started immediately after a player interacts with the cake, and has a time limit of 3 minutes. If all Party Robots are defeated, all participants will be rewarded with ![RoboPartyBlessing](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Robo Party Blessing](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Other "Buffs & Debuffs"), and the player who initiated the Robo Party will be rewarded with items, as well as being able to summon a Robo Party with a higher rank. If not all of the party mobs were killed, the player's rank gets lowered. Robo Parties can also be summoned by [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)"). 

If all the Party Robots are defeated, the player who used the Party Cake will get an amount of Robo Party Blessing equal to the rank that was finished (Completing Rank 5 would reward the player with 5 stacks of Robo Party Blessing). 

### Mobs[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRobo_Party_Cake%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The Robo Party summons party variants of [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge") robots. Unlike the Robo Bear Challenge, every enemy will spawn in a field and stay there for the duration of the party. The higher the rank, the higher the level, health and quantity of the robots summoned. There are four types of robots that are spawned during the event: [Party Mechsquitoes](/wiki/Party_Mechsquito "Party Mechsquito"), [Party Cogmowers](/wiki/Party_Cogmower "Party Cogmower"), [Party Cogturrets](/wiki/Party_Cogturret "Party Cogturret"), and [Party Mega Mechsquitoes](/wiki/Party_Mega_Mechsquito "Party Mega Mechsquito"). Party Cogturrets will spawn on Rank 5, while Party Mega Mechsquitoes will spawn on Rank 15.

First-time rewards are the rewards that are given after a player successfully completes a Robo Party rank for the first time. The rewards are always the same for every player. Each rank completed for the first time rewards the player with an amount of ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") equal to the level of the rank completed, ![Stinger](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Stingers](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger"), and at least one [Balloon](/wiki/Balloon "Balloon"). Starting at rank 19, at least one [Drive](/wiki/Drives "Drives") is also guaranteed.

## Strategies for Robo Parties[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRobo_Party_Cake%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using ![Stinger](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Stingers](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger") will give x1.5 attack to your bees for 30 seconds. This can be very useful when killing high-level mobs like Party Cogturrets or Party Mega Mechsquitoes.
  * If the amount of mobs are too overwhelming, use [Star Saw](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Saw "Passive Abilities") (if you have it). Star Saw circles around you for 30 seconds after activation, and while it's difficult to hit Party Mechsquitoes and Party Mega Mechsquitoes (owing to their flying hitboxes), Party Cogmowers and Party Cogturrets can easily be hit with it, allowing you to quickly take down these threats if the Bees are preoccupied with Party Mechsquitoes and Party Mega Mechsquitoes.
  * [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") provide excellent crowd control, especially since most mobs often come in pairs or packs (other than Party Mega Mechsquitos). Digital Bee can also stun many mobs at once and allow them to take extra damage via Mind Hack.
  * Party Cogturrets will always shoot straight at where they are facing so you can avoid their attacks simply by not being in front of them.
  * Party Cogmowers don't aim for the player, allowing players to easily sidestep them.
  * Stand near the Party Mobs, this will allow your bees to lock on to the Party Mobs and quickly deal damage.
  * Party Mechsquitos and Party Mega Mechsquitos aim for where the player is, not where the player will be. Because of this, moving around in circles will often result in them missing their attack.
  * Always keep an eye on your health. While doing Robo Parties, it is easy to be too focused on killing the Party Robots, and not keep track of how much damage you've taken. 
    * If you so happen to die, you can look around for fields with Party Robots when you respawn until your bees wake up.
