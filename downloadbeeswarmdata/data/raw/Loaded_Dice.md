# Loaded dice

Loaded Dice

[![Loaded Dice](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0d/Loaded_Dice.png/revision/latest?cb=20230130020656)

_"Boosts 3 random fields with 300% pollen for 15 minutes. A bit biased towards the field you're in..."_

COOLDOWN

3 Seconds

CAP

25

**Loaded Dice** is an [inventory item](/wiki/Items "Items") added in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"). It boosts three random [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") with 300% [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") for 15 minutes, and the field the player is in has a biased chance, meaning it has a higher chance to boost the field the player is standing in. If the player uses it when there is another dice boost active, it will replace the last dice boosts and duration. It has a cooldown of 3 seconds. 

## Bias Mechanism[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLoaded_Dice%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

To activate the bias, the player must be standing on a field, and when the first loaded dice is used, there will be a 25% chance for the first field picked to be the field you’re standing in, as more loaded dice is used, the chance will increase by 25% with a maximum chance of 100%. If the player leaves the field, the chance will reset.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLoaded_Dice%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * If you some how roll another dice while a dice is already being rolled, this notification would pop up:



You are already rolling a dice

  * This is the only dice to currently have a cap (25). 
    * ![Field Dice](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Field Dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice") used to have a cap of 25 until the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates").
  * This and the ![Smooth Dice](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Smooth Dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice") are the only [items](/wiki/Items "Items") which can produce more than 1 [field boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost") at a time, and produce more than x1 stack of field boost at a time.
  * The fields shown on the dice are the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field"), the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field"), the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"), the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest") and the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"). 
    * There also is an X icon on the front dice. It is presumed it's the "biased" slot for the field that the player is standing on.
  * In real life, loaded dice are rigged dice designed to make one side show up more often than the rest, which correlates to the dice's in-game bias of the field your standing in.
