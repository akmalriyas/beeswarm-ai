**Tadpole Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Mythic bee](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic"). 

Tadpole Bee's favorite treat is ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"). 

Tadpole Bee likes the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest") and [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"). It dislikes the [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTadpole_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 6 seconds.
  * Makes 120 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +40 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), -20% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), -50% Gather Speed, and [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy").
  * 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +25% [Bubble](/wiki/Bubble "Bubble") Duration



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTadpole_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Blue Boost]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Blue_Boost "Ability Tokens")** Grants x1.2 pollen from Blue [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") for 15 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.
  * **[[Summon Frog]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Summon_Frog "Ability Tokens")** Summons a [frog](/wiki/Frog "Frog") that lasts for 20s (+2s per Level). Frogs can make [bubbles](/wiki/Bubble "Bubble") after every few hops, or after collecting a token with their tongue. They also attack nearby enemies with attack equal to 5x this bee's attack. 
    * If Gifted, this has a 10% (+2% per Level) chance to summon a Gifted Frog instead, which has increased tongue range, Token gathering and attack speed, 50% more attack, has a chance to summon [golden bubbles](/wiki/Bubble#Golden_Bubbles "Bubble"), and can hit [balloons](/wiki/Balloon "Balloon") with its tongue to convert pollen to Honey Tokens.
  * **[[🌟Gifted Ability: Baby Love]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Baby_Love "Ability Tokens") **Grants x2 pollen and +50% [Loot Luck](/wiki/Loot_Luck "Loot Luck") for 30 seconds. Loot Luck increases your chance of obtaining prizes from defeating enemies.
  * **[[Passive:](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gathering_Bubbles "Passive Abilities") [Gathering Bubbles+]](/wiki/Bubble#Gathering_Bubbles "Bubble")** 65% chance (85% if Gifted) to spawn a Bubble when gathering. If any player touches it, it pops, collecting 4 Red/6 White/8 Blue pollen (+15% per Gifted blue bee type, +5% per bee level) from 29 nearby flowers and causing them to replenish.

Tadpole Bee has a base pollen collection of **10![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **6 seconds**. That's equivalent to 1.66667 ![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. (**20![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **6 seconds** with x2 Bee Pollen gamepass)

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTadpole_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The Tadpole Bee, [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"), [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"), [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"), and [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee") are the only [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to represent realistic animals. 
    * The Tadpole Bee is the only Mythic bee that represents a realistic animal.
  * Tadpole Bee and [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee") are the only bees with the Baby Love ability. 
    * Tadpole Bee and [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee") are also the only bees that appear smaller than the normal bee size.
  * Tadpole Bee and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") are the only bees with 2 gifted bonuses, excluding other Event Bees who enhance their ability.
  * Tadpole Bee, [Vector Bee](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee"), [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee"), [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee"), [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee") and [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") are the only bees with gifted exclusive abilities.
  * This bee and [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee") are the only bees who can summon other entities. 
    * This is the only bee that can summon a [Mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs").
  * Tadpole Bee and [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee") are the only bees with the Gathering Bubbles passive ability.
  * Tadpole Bee, [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee"), [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee"), [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"), and [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") all have four different [Abilities](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), the most for any bee.
  * Tadpole Bee used to be the only bee in the game that had a decimal base attack (0.5).
  * Tadpole Bee is the only Mythic bee that has its Gifted-exclusive ability token shared with another bee.
  * Along with [Vector Bee](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee") and [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee"), Tadpole Bee was one of the first [Mythic Bees](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic") to be introduced to the game.
  * A [Tadpole Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Specific_Bee_Eggs "Egg") could be obtained by giving [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") a [Present](/wiki/Present "Present") during the Beesmas 2020 event. 
    * Tadpole Bee is the second mythic bee to have an egg of its own, the first being [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee").
  * Tadpole Bee is one of the 8 other bees with a unique trail.
  * Tadpole Bee is the blue counterpart of [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee").
  * Tadpole Bee and [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee") are the only Mythics, when gifted, to not have any glowing parts.
