**Tabby Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") that hatches out of a ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tabby Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Tabby_Bee_Egg "Egg"), which is currently available in the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") for ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)500 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"). 

Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, and the only way to make it gifted is by feeding it a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

Tabby Bee likes the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field") and [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"). It dislikes the [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTabby_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 160 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 3 seconds.
  * +40% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), +15% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +25% Convert Speed, +3 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats"), up to +1680 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), up to +100 [Gather Amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +50% [Critical Power](/wiki/System_Page#Critical_Power "System Page").



Unlike other bees, this bee has a unique perk: its stats increase permanently with the collection of Tabby Love [tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"). At the cap of 1,000 Tabby Love, this bee's maximum stats are: 

  * Collects 110 Pollen in 4 seconds.
  * Makes ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1,760 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 3 seconds.
  * Collects 88 Pollen from 3 lines of 4 flowers with Scratch (1,056 pollen).



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTabby_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Scratch]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Scratch "Ability Tokens")** Collects 7 (+1 per lvl) Pollen from 3 lines of 4 [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). If Gifted, Scratch always does Critical hits.
  * **[[Tabby Love]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Tabby_Love "Ability Tokens")** Permanently grants Tabby Bee +1% Gather Amount, Convert Amount, and pollen from Scratch. Stacks up to 1,000 times. 
    * If at max stacks, Tabby Love grants Tabby Blessing, giving +1% Critical Chance and +25% Critical Power for 20 seconds (+2s per level). If Gifted, it grants Tabby Blessing+, which adds +1% Super-Crit Chance as well.

Tabby Bee has a base pollen collection of **10![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTabby_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * When Tabby Bee was added, the maximum Tabby Love stack was mistakenly set to 500. This mistake was fixed in a later update.
  * Tabby Bee cost ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)250 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") when it was originally released.
  * There used to be an [Easter Egg](/wiki/Easter_Eggs#Tabby_Bee_Block_\(Removed\) "Easter Eggs") behind the hives, which was a block with a distorted photo of Tabby Bee on it. It was removed around 2 June 2018. This was possibly a hint for one of the [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), more specifically, "Meow", which gave five tickets before it expired.
  * Tabby Bee is one of three bees to have an official plush on the [Official Bee Swarm Simulator Website](https://shop.beeswarm.com/), the others being [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee") and [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee").
  * Because of a timer glitch that stopped players from buying it early, Tabby Bee returned on sale between June 2 and June 6, 2018.
  * Tabby Bee was the third Event bee to be added to the game, after [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee") and [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee") respectively.
  * This bee, Bear Bee, [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"), [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"), [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee"), [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"), [and Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") are the only Event bees which can be a first edition bee when hatching them during its first release in-game.
  * Tabby Bee, Bear Bee, Gummy Bee, Festive Bee, Photon Bee, [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), and [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") are the only bees to have a gifted bonus that affects their signature ability.
  * The player’s tabby love stack will not be removed if the player transforms Tabby Bee in any way.
  * Before the May 23, 2024 update, if the player already had max Tabby Love (x1000) and collected another one, it would neither increase the existing buff nor have any effect.
  * Tabby Bee is a reference to [Onett's](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") real-life cat named Sam. This is hinted in one of [Onett's](/wiki/Onett "Onett") dialogues in his star journey [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests"), and later confirmed on Discord.
  * Tabby Bee, along with Basic Bee and [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"), are Onett's favorite bees.
  * Tabby Bee is mentioned in [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear")'s dialogue during his Ultimate Ant Annihilation 1 quest. As Tabby Bee's description says, he states that Tabby Bee was raised by cats.
  * Tabby Bee is one of the few bees based off from animals other than just bees, the others being [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), Puppy Bee, and Bear Bee.
  * Tabby Bee, despite having a 50% energy increase compared to Basic bee listed in its stats, has a base energy of 28, which is only comparable to a 40% energy increase.
  * Tabby Bee can have the highest conversion rate in the game at maximum Tabby Love.
  * This was one of the few bees that could have been summoned from [Onett's Lid Art](/wiki/Onett%27s_Lid_Art "Onett's Lid Art"). 
    * If a Tabby Bee from Onett's Lid Art produced a tabby love token, the buff would not be removed after the Tabby Bee despawns.
