**Riley Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Epic bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"). 

Riley Bee's favorite [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") is ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"). 

Riley Bee likes the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field"), and [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). It dislikes the [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field") and the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRiley_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Based on its description, this [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") is the rival and counterpart of [Bucko Bee](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee"). 
    * Both of them are the leaders of their respective colors.
  * Riley Bee, [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"), and [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee") all have the same skin colors.
  * A giant model of a Riley Bee is on the front of the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ").
  * [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee") is a quest giver represents the gifted version of it in the game.
  * The [Riley Guard](/wiki/Riley_Guard "Riley Guard") is based on this bee.
  * Riley Bee and [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") are the only red bees to like three fields. 
    * Riley Bee is also the only red bee to dislike two fields.
