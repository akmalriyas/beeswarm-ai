A **Paperclip** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 7 [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of two. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [rare](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare") or [epic](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic") [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees").
