## Onett Statue[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEaster_Eggs%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The statue is one of the easiest easter eggs to find and can be found on the top of the rightmost [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") (6th hive). Behind him, there is a [Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"). On his backpack you can see the number 38217, which is actually a code that will give you 5 tickets. You can get to him using a few methods. 

  1. Using the [Yellow Cannon](/wiki/Yellow_Cannon "Yellow Cannon"), [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon"), or [Blue Cannon](/wiki/Blue_Cannon "Blue Cannon") (only glider) in combination with the [parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute")/[glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider") to glide to the top of the hive.
  2. On very rare occasions the statue will fall to the ground, and actually could be found inside the honeycombs within the rightmost hive. Rarely, he can be found behind the 5-Bee Gate.
  3. You can jump on the Puppy Bee's tail on the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") or the Gifted Photon Bee on the ticket tent and jump on top of the hive if you have enough jump power or speed.
  4. You can jump in the Gummy Bee in [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Gummy_Bee_Egg_Claim "Gummy Bee Egg Claim"), and use the Glider to get on the hives.
  5. Jump and use glider from the coconut field to get on top of the hives.



Unlike the Onett NPC found in the [Bear Gate](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate"), he cannot be interacted with.

Shadow Bear

_Main article:[Shadow Bear](/wiki/Shadow_Bear "Shadow Bear")_

[Shadow Bear](/wiki/Shadow_Bear "Shadow Bear") can be found under the platform [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett") (NPC) is located on. He looks the same as [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), but he will not kill upon contact and is much bigger. In front of him is a ![Diamond Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Diamond Egg](/wiki/Egg#Diamond_Egg "Egg") token you can collect, however, if the player touches the ground for long enough, the player will die. If you have enough speed, you can run across the floor without dying. This makes getting the Diamond Egg a challenge. 

If you have enough ping, you can hold jump as the floor only kills you after a set amount of time, which is increased by ping.

The Mysterious Figure

The Mysterious Figure, also known as Eggwick from the Egg Hunt 2019 event, was in the [Blue Maze](/wiki/Mazes#Blue_Maze "Mazes") to the right of the [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") on the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent"). After the Egg Hunt 2019 event ended, the statue was removed. Along with the maze, there was ![Plastic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Plastic Egg](/wiki/Plastic_Egg "Plastic Egg") (now a ticket token) and ![Jelly Beans](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Jelly Beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans"). 

He is the second statue in the game, the first being the Onett statue. The figure has been replaced by a ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token. 

## +5 Pollen Text[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEaster_Eggs%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

[![The +5 pollen text.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5d/3BA71544-29F3-42DA-9B39-ADAA9A4AF820.png/revision/latest?cb=20210426171934) [![The +5 pollen text.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5d/3BA71544-29F3-42DA-9B39-ADAA9A4AF820.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210426171934)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5d/3BA71544-29F3-42DA-9B39-ADAA9A4AF820.png/revision/latest?cb=20210426171934) [](/wiki/File:3BA71544-29F3-42DA-9B39-ADAA9A4AF820.png)

The +5 pollen text.

This Easter Egg is located outside the map next to the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"). Behind it, there is a text decal written "+5". Since it does not float near the ground, as the area around it does not have collision enabled, it may be necessary to use the glider tool to get close. 

## Red HQ Room[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEaster_Eggs%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

This room is hidden behind a wall in the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"). When it was first added, the room had an unusable Red Extract dispenser. The room had a slight change in the 2021-12-26 update, and another change in the 2022-12-26 update. It cannot be accessed without the use of glitches.
