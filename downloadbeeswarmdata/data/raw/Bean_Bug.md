The **Bean Bug** is a passive [mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") that can randomly appear in any [field](/wiki/Field "Field") (except the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field")), be spawned through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") by donating 50-100 [eggs](/wiki/Eggs "Eggs") or be manually spawned by [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)"). They often spawn in one field in groups of 3 or more, and later split up. 

When they spawn, they spit 1-3 ![Jelly Beans](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Jelly Beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans") tokens in volleys over the field which any player can pick up, or can also spit out a Standing Bean Bug [Sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") very rarely. Then they hop to another location on the field and spit more. The tokens only last for a few seconds, much less time than the tokens spawned from regular Jelly Beans. After 7-15 volleys of Jelly Beans, the Bean Bugs hop to other fields to spit Jelly Beans. This process repeats until they despawn after a while. Rarely, it may spawn a ![Hivesticker standing bean bug](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Standing Bean Bug Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker"). 

Bean Bugs can aid the player in collecting [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen")/[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), as Jelly Bean tokens can boost the player’s pollen collection, [Critical Chance](/wiki/System_Page#Critical_Chance "System Page"), [Instant Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Instant_Conversion "System Page"), and other stats. 

The beans were used for the limited time [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") questline to receive 250 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") and for the limited 2019 [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") questlines. They are also used for [Spirit Bear's](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") questline, and also used for Bee Bear's questline. Sun Bear and Bee Bear's Bean Bug questlines could only be received as a part of an event. 

Players are unable to damage it, nor can it damage players, hence why it is a passive mob.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBean_Bug%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Bean Bugs, [frogs](/wiki/Frog "Frog"), [fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies"), [chicks](/wiki/Chicks#Chick "Chicks"), [Spotted Chicks](/wiki/Chicks#Spotted_Chick "Chicks"), and [Hostage Chicks](/wiki/Chicks#Hostage_Chick "Chicks") are the only passive mobs in the game, as they cannot deal damage to players.
  * They were first introduced in the [2019-09-28 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates") when Sun Bear accidentally released the Bean Bugs.
  * Sometimes, they can get stuck in various areas, including the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"), [Basic Bee Gate](/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate "Basic Bee Gate"), the [Vicious Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Vicious_Bee_Egg_Claim "Vicious Bee Egg Claim") and [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") that have solid object(s) while jumping. If they get stuck, they can’t change course and will remain useless until de-spawning. Onett joked about this being "natural selection".
  * These and fireflies are the only passive mobs that are able to produce [tokens.](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens")
  * This, [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug"), [Snowbears](/wiki/Snowbear "Snowbear") and [Mechsquitos](/wiki/Mechsquito "Mechsquito") are the only mobs being able to spawn a [debuff](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#Debuffs "Buffs & Debuffs"). Stick Bug can spawn the Splinter Trap, Snowbear can Freeze with its Snowballs, Mechsquitos can apply Mechsquito Toxin by firing at the player, and Bean Bugs can spawn spoiled Jelly Beans.
  * As confirmed by Bee Bear, Bean Bugs have small brains and cannot understand the concept of naughty or nice.
  * At the end of Beesmas 2020, Onett spawned them as "The Bean Bug Brigade". 
    * They then returned at the end of Beesmas 2021 as they "Awoke from Hibernation".
  * Bean Bugs are the first mob that use jumping to move around, with frogs being the second. 
    * While, technically, chicks jump when they go inside their egg, this is the only time they do so.
