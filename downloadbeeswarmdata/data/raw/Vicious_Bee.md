**Vicious Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event"). It can be claimed for ![Stinger](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)250 [Stingers](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger") at the [Vicious Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Vicious_Bee_Egg_Claim "Vicious Bee Egg Claim"). 

Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, and the only way to make it gifted is by feeding it a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

Vicious Bee likes the [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field") and the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). It dislikes the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVicious_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Vicious Bee's base attack was previously 7 before it was increased to 8 in the 2021-12-26 update.
  * Gifted Vicious Bee's impale ability gets golden-colored spikes, instead of the normal colored (gray) ones. The Impale ability does more base damage when gifted due to the +50% gifted attack bonus. The attack bonus is applied to the bee itself, but it indirectly increases the amount of damage Impale does—though this is not a significant buff due to the majority of Impale's attack scaling off of mob health.
  * Vicious Bee is the only [Bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") who can summon an [Ability Token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") that directly attacks enemies. 
    * This bee and [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") are the only bees who have abilities that directly attack the enemies, namely Impale and [Sniper](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Sniper "Passive Abilities") respectively. ([Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee")'s Mind Hack ability doesn't count since it doesn't directly attack enemies).
  * Vicious Bee is one of three bees to have an official plushie on the [Official Bee Swarm Simulator Website](https://shop.beeswarm.com/), the others being Basic Bee and [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee").
  * Vicious Bee is the only blue bee that likes the Rose Field. It may like the Rose Field along with the Cactus Field due to the fact that roses and cacti have thorns on them. The [Rogue Version](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") can also spawn in both of these fields.
  * Vicious Bee and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") are the only bees with a hostile variant.
  * It would cost the player ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)2,500 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") if they were to buy all 250 stingers from the [Stinger Shop](/wiki/Stinger_Shop "Stinger Shop") to get a Vicious Bee, which makes it the most expensive event bee to get by spending tickets.
  * Vicious Bee, [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee"), and Cobalt Bee are the only blue bees that don’t like [Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry").
  * Vicious Bee, [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"), [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"), Windy Bee, and [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") are the only Event bees that cannot be purchased in the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent"). These four bees, along with [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") and Puppy Bee, are also the only Event bees that could have been obtained without spending tickets. Gummy Bee was in the Ticket Tent at one point, however.
  * Vicious Bee, Cobalt Bee, Crimson Bee, and [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee") are the only event bees that can't have a 1st Edition Flag. 
    * [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") has stated that the reason a [First Edition](/wiki/First_Edition_Bee "First Edition Bee") Vicious Bee does not exist is because there is no room for a 1st Flag on its back with the spikes.[1]
  * Vicious Bee, the [Stick Bug Amulet](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Amulet "Stick Bug Amulet"), and the Spider Field [Market Boost](/wiki/Boost_Market "Boost Market") are the only sources of the -% Mob Respawn Time stats.
  * According to [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear")'s dialogue during his [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests") "Black Bear, Why?", Black Bear dislikes this bee.
