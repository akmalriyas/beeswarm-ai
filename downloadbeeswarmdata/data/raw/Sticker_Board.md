# Sticker Board

The **Public Sticker Board** is located in the [Hive Hub](/wiki/Hive_Hub "Hive Hub"), and allows players to add or take [stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") from it. There is a 22-hour cooldown for taking a sticker from the board and a 10-minute cooldown for adding a sticker to the board. The [Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine "Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine") might occasionally require players to donate a sticker to the Public Sticker Board as well. It serves as a way of obtaining stickers or removing spare stickers.
