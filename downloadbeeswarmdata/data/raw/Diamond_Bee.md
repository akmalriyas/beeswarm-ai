**Diamond Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Legendary bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"). 

Diamond Bee's favorite treat are ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"). 

Diamond Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), and [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"). It dislikes the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDiamond_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 1000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +920 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.2 [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDiamond_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Blue Boost]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens")** Grants x1.2 pollen from Blue flowers for 15 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.
  * **[[Honey Gift+]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Honey_Gift "Ability Tokens")** Sometimes spawns a Honey Token. The amount of honey is equal to 250 * bee level * bee level. (250 times the bee's level squared)
  * **[[Passive: Shimmering Honey]](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Shimmering_Honey "Passive Abilities")** When converting at the hive, this bee grants 25% bonus honey (+2.5% per level). This bonus is doubled if Gifted. This is noticeable by the light blue conversion link when converting.

Diamond Bee has a base pollen collection of **10![Blue Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. That's equivalent to 2.5 ![Blue Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDiamond_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The light colors around Diamond Bee make it nearly translucent.
  * Diamond Bee has the highest base convert rate out of all bees, only beaten by [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee") when its [Tabby Love](/wiki/Tabby_Love "Tabby Love") stack is at 626 or higher.
  * Diamond Bee and [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee") are the only [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in the game which can generate Honey Gift or Honey Gift+ tokens.
  * This, [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee"), [Fire Bee](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee"), [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee"), [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee"), and [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") are the only bees to have masks based on them, specifically the [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask "Diamond Mask") for Diamond Bee.
  * Diamond Bee is the blue counterpart of [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee").
  * Diamond Bee and Gummy Bee are the only bees to have a sparkle effect.
  * Diamond Bee has a unique conversion link color (cyan/light blue). It is the only bee to have a conversion link that is not yellow.
  * Diamond Bee is one of only two bees to have the same name as an [egg](/wiki/Egg "Egg") they can hatch from; the other being [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"), and those eggs being the ![Diamond Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Diamond Egg](/wiki/Egg#Diamond_Egg "Egg") and ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Basic Egg](/wiki/Egg#Basic_Egg "Egg") respectively.
  * Diamond bee may like the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch") because pineapples have historically been symbols of wealth.
