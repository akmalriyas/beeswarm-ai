# Glitter

Glitter

[![Glitter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/63/Glitter.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404021017)

_"Boosts the field you're standing in, granting +100% pollen for 15 minutes."_

COOLDOWN

15 minutes 5 seconds

  
**Glitter** is a craftable [inventory item](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory") that was added in the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"). It grants a +100% [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") boost in the [field](/wiki/Field "Field") the player is standing in for 15 minutes upon activation. If used in a field with a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter"), the planter gains a 25% growth rate boost until it is harvested. **Glitter** has a 15 minute and 5 second cooldown between uses. This is to prevent the user from stacking the item's buff for one field. 

It can be used in all fields except the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field"). Attempting to do so will result in the following message:  You can't boost a challenge field

The following audio plays when glitter is used to boost a field: 

<https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Glitteruse.ogg>

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitter%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is one of four consumable items to grant a [field boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"), the others being [Field Dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [Smooth Dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), and [Loaded Dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice")
    * It also is the one of the four consumables that shows glitter particles on the field when the said field is boosted.
    * This is the only consumable to grant a Field Boost of the player's choice.
  * Spirit Bear mentions in one of her quest dialogues, Spirit's Starter, that glitter was once so rare that 1 pouch of it costed 5 million honey.
  * Glitter’s raw ingredients cost: 
    * 375 [Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry")
    * 375 [Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry")
    * 500 [Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple")
    * 25 [Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") or 25M [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey")
    * 1 [Magic Bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean") or 20 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket")
