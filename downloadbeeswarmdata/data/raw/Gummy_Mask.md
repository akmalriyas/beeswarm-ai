# Gummy Mask

## Gummy Mask

[ ![Gummy Mask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/50/Gummy_Mask.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20210919012419) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/50/Gummy_Mask.png/revision/latest?cb=20210919012419 "Gummy Mask")

### Description

"The official mask of a Gummy Soldier."

### Cost

5,000,000,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), 250 [Glue](/wiki/Glue "Glue"), 100 [Enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"), 100 [Oil](/wiki/Oil "Oil"), 100 [Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") and 1 [Satisfying Vial](/wiki/Nectar_Vial#Satisfying_Vial "Nectar Vial").

### Bonus Stats

+75% Goo  


+10% Goo Conversion  
x1.25 White Pollen  
+25% Pollen  
x1.5 Capacity  
x1.5 White Field Capacity  
+30% Defense  
+20% Bee Ability Rate  
+Passive: Coin Scatter (Needs Honey Mask)  
+Passive: Gummy Morph

> "_The official mask of a Gummy Soldier._ " 

The **Gummy Mask** is a [mask](/wiki/Category:Hats "Category:Hats") available for purchase in [Gummy Bear's Lair](/wiki/Gummy_Bear%27s_Lair "Gummy Bear's Lair"). It can be reached by touching the [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") model on the [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Gummy_Bee_Egg_Claim "Gummy Bee Egg Claim") and using [gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") if the player has obtained the [Goo Hotshot](/wiki/Badges#Goo_Badge "Badges") badge.

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGummy_Mask%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * +75% [Goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo")
  * +10% [Goo Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Goo_Conversion "System Page")
  * x1.25 [White Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#White_Pollen "System Page")
  * +25% [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen")
  * x1.5 [Capacity](/wiki/Capacity "Capacity")
  * x1.5 [White Field Capacity](/wiki/Field_Capacity "Field Capacity")
  * +30% [Defense](/wiki/System_Page#Defense "System Page")
  * +20% [Bee Ability Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Ability_Rate "System Page")
  * [+Passive: Coin Scatter](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Coin_Scatter "Passive Abilities") (Requires [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask "Honey Mask") in order to use): 
    * The passive is activated every 20 [Mark Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mark "Ability Tokens"). When it does, 300% of the player's total [Hive Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Total "System Page") will be converted and scattered into honey tokens, which cannot be collected through a [Token Link](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Token_Link "Ability Tokens") (visible only to the player, Honey Tokens last one minute). The passive has a cooldown of two minutes before being able to be used again.
  * [+Passive: Gummy Morph](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Morph "Passive Abilities"): 
    * The Gummy Morph will trigger every 30 [gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") used OR 10 [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") ability tokens collected (+1 stack per gumdrops used, +3 stacks every Gummy Bee ability). When it triggers, it will turn you into [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear"), your Gummy Bee will glow and leave a trail. In addition, the field will be fully covered in goo. As Gummy Bear, you will gain +75% goo, instantly converting all of it, and have increased jump power and player speed. As Gummy Bee glows, it will gather 1000% more pollen, gain +300 attack, and move 200% faster for 10 seconds. This passive does not have any cooldown time.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGummy_Mask%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * If the ingredients to craft this item were to be crafted, a total of 5,000,000,000 Honey, 75,000 [Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"), 75,000 [Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"), 92,500 [Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"), 5,000 [Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"), 8,000 [Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), 100 [Magic Beans](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean") and 1 [Satisfying Vial](/wiki/Satisfying_Vial "Satisfying Vial") of nectar are needed.
  * Upon activating the Gummy Morph, [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") will have the highest Base Attack Power of 303 damage in total.
  * This is the only mask that represents an [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") (Gummy Bee), as the other masks are based on [Epic](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic") and [Legendary Bees](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary").
  * The Gummy Mask was nerfed in the [2019-04-05 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates") because it was too powerful. 
    * It was later buffed a little in the [2019-04-17 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-17 "Updates"), as people were complaining that it was extremely bad with the nerf.
  * Before the [2019-09-28 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), if the [Unlimited Gumdrops](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Areas "Buffs & Debuffs") buff is active, the passive ability would not be activated from gumdrops. This is because gumdrops in the inventory weren't technically consumed upon use with the Unlimited Gumdrops buff. 
    * After the update, it can now be activated with Unlimited Gumdrops.
  * The [music](/wiki/Music "Music") that plays when the Gummy Bear Morph is active is a slight rendition of [Gummy Bear's](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") theme that plays in the Gummy Bear's Lair.
  * This and the [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask "Honey Mask") are the only masks in the game that requires a badge to access and purchase. 
    * Coincidentally, the Honey Mask is also required to unlock the coin scatter ability on this mask.
  * The Gummy Mask is the only mask in the game that requires a specific badge to access it. 
    * This is the only way to get a Bear Morph ability without Robux and codes before the [2024-05-23 Update](/wiki/Updates#2024-05-23 "Updates").
  * Either this mask or the [Gummy Boots](/wiki/Gummy_Boots "Gummy Boots") were needed in order to receive Gummy Bear's [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") quest.
  * The Gummy Morph icon is the same icon as the one for the Goo badge.
  * This and the [Strange Goggles](/wiki/Strange_Goggles "Strange Goggles") are the only items that are required to interact with NPCs. However, during Beesmas, the Honey Mask was also needed to give [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") a present and to receive its Beesmas quest.
  * This, the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") and the [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask "Diamond Mask") are the only masks with decorative accessories (Gummy Mask has a teal gumdrop).
  * This is the 3rd most expensive item requiring ![Glue](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Glue](/wiki/Glue "Glue"). In front of the Gummy Mask is [Gummy Boots](/wiki/Gummy_Boots "Gummy Boots") (2nd) and [Gummyballer](/wiki/Gummyballer "Gummyballer") (1st).
  * The morph lasts a bit longer than the field being covered in goo during Gummy Morph.
