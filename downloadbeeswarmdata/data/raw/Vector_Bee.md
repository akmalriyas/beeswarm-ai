**Vector Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Mythic bee](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic"). 

Vector Bee's favorite treat is ![Sunflower Seed](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"). 

Vector Bee likes the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field") and the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field"). It dislikes the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVector_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 18 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 144 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 2.72 seconds.
  * +4 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats"), +8 [Gather Amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats"), +16% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +32% Convert Speed, +64 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +128% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +15% Mark Duration.



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVector_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Pollen Mark+]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mark "Ability Tokens")** Marks a large random area on the [Field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") for 8 seconds (+0.25s per Level) that increases all pollen by 50% while that player stands in it. Stacks up to 3 times.
  * **[[Triangulate]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Triangulate "Ability Tokens")** Draws a triangle between the player, the bee, and the token. After 3s, it collects [Ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") and 10 pollen (+2 per Level) from all [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") inside. All Marks within the triangle increase the pollen by 50%. 
    * If the triangle contains a Pollen Mark, it gains x2 White pollen.
    * If the triangle contains a Honey Mark or Festive Mark, it gains 50% instant conversion.
    * If the triangle contains a Precise Mark, it always deals critical hits.
  * **[[🌟Gifted Ability: Mark Surge]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mark_Surge "Ability Tokens")** Causes all of the player's active Marks to collect 7 Pollen (+10% per Level) from all flowers in their radius. Extends the duration of the Marks by 1s (+0.1s per Level). Each mark can perform up to 5 surges. 
    * When a Honey Mark or Festive Mark surges, the Pollen is Instantly Converted.
    * When a Precise Mark surges, it always does Critical Hits.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVector_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Vector Bee's stats follow a [Geometric Progression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_Progression "wikipedia:Geometric Progression") pattern, where each number is double the amount of the previous number.
  * Vector Bee is the only Mythic bee to not share any of its abilities with other bees. It is also the only Mythic Bee to not have a [Passive Ability](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities").
  * Vector Bee is the only [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to have a different skin pattern on each of its sides.
  * Vector Bee's design is heavily inspired by mathematics. 
    * Its relation to trigonometry is implied in its description and the triangular motifs on its head.
    * Vectors reference Euclidean vectors, objects with magnitude and direction. A triangle can be composed of three vectors.
    * Triangulation is a method to determine the location of a point through forming triangles from known points.
  * Vector Bee, [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee"), [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") and [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee") emit light when they are gifted.
  * Vector Bee shares similar color schemes with [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee").
  * Triangulate is [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)")'s favorite ability, due to the fact that he thinks it's satisfying.
  * Mark Surge token is considered by the game as "mark" token despite what the ability does doesn't create mark like what all other mark tokens do. This means the collection of Mark Surge token counts toward some quests, [Coin Scatter passive ability](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Coin_Scatter "Passive Abilities"), and anything that requires the collection of "mark" tokens. 
    * Triangulate, however, is not considered as "mark" ability token.
    * In a similar fashion, Fuzz Bombs ([Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee")'s ability token), Bomb Sync ([Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee") and [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee")'s ability token) are not considered "bomb" ability tokens, and Honey Mark isn't considered as "honey" token.
