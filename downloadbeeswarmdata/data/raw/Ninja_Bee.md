**Ninja Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Legendary bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"). 

Ninja Bee's favorite treat is ![Sunflower Seed](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"). 

Ninja Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field") and [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"). It dislikes the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNinja_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 2 seconds.
  * Makes 80 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 2 seconds.
  * +50% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), Gather Speed, and Convert Speed, +3 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats"), +10% [Critical Chance](/wiki/Critical_Hits "Critical Hits")
  * 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +5% [Bee Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats").

  * **[[Haste]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens")** Grants +10% [Player Movespeed](/wiki/System_Page#Movespeed "System Page") for 20 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.
  * **[[Blue Bomb+]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding Blue [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") (+10% pollen per Level). Combo with other bombs to increase power.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNinja_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * In the early versions of the game, Ninja Bee had an ability called "[Haste+](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Areas "Buffs & Debuffs")," which doubled the player's Movespeed for 20 seconds. This ability has now been stripped from the bee and is currently only obtainable through the [Star Hall's](/wiki/Star_Hall "Star Hall") [Royal Jelly Dispenser](/wiki/Royal_Jelly_Dispenser "Royal Jelly Dispenser") and [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). 
    * Ninja Bee, [Hasty Bee](/wiki/Hasty_Bee "Hasty Bee"), [Shocked Bee](/wiki/Shocked_Bee "Shocked Bee"), and [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee") are the only bees that grant haste without any certain [Beequips](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip").
  * Ninja Bee is the only colored bee to grant haste, with the exception of those with the [Toy Drum](/wiki/Toy_Drum "Toy Drum") Beequip.
  * This bee, [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee"), [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee"), and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") are currently the only bees that have a solitary colored skin.
  * Ninja Bee is the fastest bee in the game, tied with Photon Bee. 
    * In Ninja Bee's description, it is supposedly the "swiftest bee". However, Photon Bee is just as fast.
  * Before the [2018-11-25 Update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"), Ninja Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus was +20% Player Movespeed instead of +5% Bee Movespeed.
  * Ninja Bee, [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee"), and [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") are the only blue bees that do not like ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"). 
    * It is the only non-[Event](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") blue bee to not like blueberries.
    * This and [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") are also the only non-[Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") bee to like [Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed").
  * This bee, Photon Bee, Cobalt Bee, [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee"), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), and Windy Bee are the only bees that leave a trail.
  * Ninja Bee is the blue counterpart of Shy Bee.
  * This bee is the placeholder image for any bee in a hive slot that hasn't loaded yet.
  * Ninja Bee's favorite treat was originally ![Pineapple](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple").
  * Ninja Bee is stated by [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear") to be his choice of leader for the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ").
