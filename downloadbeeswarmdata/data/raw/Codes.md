The codes menu.

Promo **Codes** are a feature added in the May 18, 2018 update. It is located at the top of the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page"). Redeeming them gives prizes such as [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops"), [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), [crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") materials, [wealth clock](/wiki/Wealth_Clock "Wealth Clock"), [magic beans](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), boosts from [ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), or [field boosts](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). 

[Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") posts codes (or hints for codes) in the game itself, on the game's [Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/1537690962), on the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](https://www.roblox.com/communities/3982592) page, on [his Twitter account](https://twitter.com/OnettDev), and on the game's [official Discord server](https://discord.gg/bee). He also sometimes gives out codes to YouTubers, and there have been reports of codes posted on his [Roblox profile page](https://www.roblox.com/users/1912490) and on Roblox advertisements. 

## Notes

  * Do _**_NOT_**_ join the Discord server just to ask for codes! Instead, use the search function to look for posts by Onett (onett1, Onett#0001, 428649652871495691).
  * Codes can only be used **once**.
  * Some older codes may tell you once you redeem them that you have obtained a >x3 boost, though they will still only give you x3.
  * They are _not_ case-sensitive: "Bopmaster", "BopMaster", "BOPMASTER", and "bopmaster" will all work equally. 
    * Codes allow spaces in between: "Bees Buzz 123" and "BeesBuzz123" will work equally.
  * Expired codes do not always say "expired"; sometimes, they say "invalid."
  * When you try to redeem a valid code that you have already used, you will be told: "You have already redeemed [code]."
