**Sun Bear** is a [traveling bear](/wiki/Category:Traveling_Bears "Category:Traveling Bears") who appeared during special events. He is one of the four temporary [quest givers](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers"), the others being [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear"), [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear"), and [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man"). 

He made five appearances. His first visit was from April 27, 2018, to May 13, 2018. He gave 12 quests with rewards including the [Belt Bag](/wiki/Belt_Bag "Belt Bag") and a final reward of a [Mondo Belt Bag](/wiki/Mondo_Belt_Bag "Mondo Belt Bag"). His second visit was from September 10, 2018, to October 8, 2018. He gave 14 quests with rewards including the [Basic Boots](/wiki/Basic_Boots "Basic Boots"), the [Hiking Boots](/wiki/Hiking_Boots "Hiking Boots"), and the [Beekeeper's Boots](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Boots "Beekeeper's Boots"). His third visit was from September 28, 2019, to October 28, 2019. He gave 10 quests themed around [Bean Bugs](/wiki/Bean_Bugs "Bean Bugs"), with a final reward of 250 [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"). His fourth visit was from April 6, 2020 to May 21, 2020, for the [Roblox Egg Hunt 2020](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2020 "Egg Hunt 2020"). His fifth visit was in August 1, 2024 for the [Roblox "The Games"](/wiki/The_Games "The Games") event, later the bear stayed in the game for sometime and at October 29, 2024, he received a new quest line. He left from his fifth visit on April 30, 2025, the same day 2024 Winter Beesmas ended.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSun_Bear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * He was the only traveling bear to not introduce his own [event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event").
  * In the quest '**Focus Feature'** , Sun Bear said to defeat a [Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf") instead of 3 [Spiders](/wiki/Spider "Spider").
  * The [Ability Badge](/wiki/Badges#Ability_Badge "Badges") is represented by him in the game's description.
  * After his spring visit, the belt bags can be bought in [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop") ([Belt Bag](/wiki/Belt_Bag "Belt Bag")) and the [Mountain Top Shop](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Shop "Mountain Top Shop") ([Mondo Belt Bag](/wiki/Mondo_Belt_Bag "Mondo Belt Bag")).
  * During his spring visit, there was a [Ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") behind Sun Bear’s RV. When Sun Bear went away, the ticket stayed there for a little time, but now it's gone.
  * Sun Bear uses the [Knight Animation Package](https://web.roblox.com/catalog/734338331/Knight-Animation-Package).
  * Sun Bear says that only [Rage](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee"), [Exhausted](/wiki/Exhausted_Bee "Exhausted Bee"), and [Music](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee") Bees spawn [Token Link](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Token_Link "Ability Tokens"), but [Shocked Bee](/wiki/Shocked_Bee "Shocked Bee") does as well. This was fixed a few days later.
  * In his second visit ([2018-09-10 Update](/wiki/Updates#2018-09-10 "Updates")) and his third visit ([2019-09-28 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates")), his RV had a [Flag of Phoenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Phoenix) bumper sticker on the back.
  * During his first visit, Sun Bear was shown to be wearing the [Mondo Belt Bag](/wiki/Mondo_Belt_Bag "Mondo Belt Bag"), which he gave at the very end of his quests. During his second visit, he was shown to be wearing both the Mondo Belt Bag and [Beekeeper's Boots](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Boots "Beekeeper's Boots"), which he gave at the end of his quests.
  * Sun Bear gives ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)48 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") throughout all his quests with the ticket token that was behind the RV.
  * During the quest '**Sun Bear's Scavenger Hunt'** , he says 3 more quests are needed to be completed to get the [Beekeeper's Boots](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Boots "Beekeeper's Boots"), when it is actually 4 quests. However, [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") could have done this on purpose, because Sun Bear was completely surprised by [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") in his dialogue, and gives you a quest around this.
  * He was the first [traveling bear](/wiki/Category:Traveling_Bears "Category:Traveling Bears") added to the game, with [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") being the second and [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") being the third.
  * The item prices of his rewards after his visit are: [Basic Boots](/wiki/Basic_Boots "Basic Boots") are 4,400, [Hiking Boots](/wiki/Hiking_Boots "Hiking Boots") are 2,200,000, Beekeeper's Boots are 15,000,000, [Belt Bag](/wiki/Belt_Bag "Belt Bag") is 440,000, and [Mondo Belt Bag](/wiki/Mondo_Belt_Bag "Mondo Belt Bag") is 12,400,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey").
  * He was the only [traveling bear](/wiki/Category:Traveling_Bears "Category:Traveling Bears") who never gave an egg on his last quest.
  * He and [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") were the only [NPCs](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC") to give an [Accessory](/wiki/Category:Accessories "Category:Accessories").
  * It was hinted that Sun Bear is from Arizona, this is from the [Code](/wiki/Codes "Codes") "**Arizona** ", and the City of Arizona flag that was on his RV the second and the third visit.
  * During the quests in his third visit, it is revealed that the reason he had [Bean Bugs](/wiki/Bean_Bugs "Bean Bugs") was that he was taking them to Orlando for an unknown reason.
  * In the quest '**Total Focus'** , Sun Bear said to collect 200 [Focus Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Focus "Ability Tokens") before you received the quest, but he didn't say to collect 200 Focus Tokens after you received the quest.
  * For the '**Total Focus'** quest, after you finish the dialogue with Sun Bear, a [Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match") game will start automatically for free.
  * He stated only [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee") doesn't generate tokens, but [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee") doesn't as well, even though both of them can generate [Inspire tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inspire "Ability Tokens") when they are [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee").
  * To complete every quest on his third visit, you must collect 3835 tokens from [Bean Bugs](/wiki/Bean_Bugs "Bean Bugs").
  * It was possible for his trapped Bean Bug to jump out of its cage. 
    * It was also possible for the player to go in and out of the [Bean Bug's](/wiki/Bean_Bugs "Bean Bugs") cage.
    * This could possibly be the reason that they got out in the first place
  * Onett has stated that Sun Bear isn't welcome on the mountain anymore for accidentally releasing [Bean Bugs](/wiki/Bean_Bugs "Bean Bugs") and the [chicks](/wiki/Chicks "Chicks"). It seems as if Sun Bear was unbanned in 2024 with his visit during [The Games](/wiki/The_Games "The Games").
  * In “Boot Lootin” Sun Bear mentions that he has been to a desert before.
  * In “Light Up The Night” Sun Bear mentions that he prefers [Nighttime](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle "Day/Night Cycle") to Daytime, as he is able to relax.
  * Sun Bear likes [mantises](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis"). Referring to them as ‘cool’ in his quest “Show Me The Honey”.
  * Sun Bear mentions in his quest “Vicious Bee Begone” that he has had an encounter with a [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") while going to visit [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear").
  * In his quest “Total Focus” Sun Bear mentions that he transports ‘exotic things’ around in his RV as a job.
