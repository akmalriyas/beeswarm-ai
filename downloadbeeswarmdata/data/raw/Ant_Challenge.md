The **Ant Challenge** is a [challenge](/wiki/Category:Challenges "Category:Challenges") found past the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate"), with the objective being to defeat as many ants as possible. 

## Accessing and Participating[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAnt_Challenge%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The player needs 20 [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in order to pass the Ant Gate and play the Ant Challenge. An [ant pass](/wiki/Ant_Pass "Ant Pass") is also required each time to participate in the challenge. The player can obtain a free ![Ant Pass](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/da/Ant_Pass.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404033127)[Ant Pass](/wiki/Ant_Pass "Ant Pass") every two hours by using the [Free Ant Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Free_Ant_Pass_Dispenser "Free Ant Pass Dispenser") behind the Ant Gate. If players wish to get more ant passes, they can purchase another one for ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)10 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") each at the [Ant Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Ant_Pass_Dispenser "Ant Pass Dispenser"). [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear") also gives ant passes in his [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") sometimes. 

## Gameplay[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAnt_Challenge%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Upon entering, the remaining [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in a player's bag is instantly converted. The player has 5 minutes to defeat as many ants as they can. The timer on the right side of the anthill shows the time remaining for the challenge. The ants come in waves, starting with level 1 and 2 ants, and they increase in level with each wave. Each wave launches an assortment of ants that contain [Regular Ants](/wiki/Regular_Ant "Regular Ant"), [Fire Ants](/wiki/Fire_Ant "Fire Ant"), [Army Ants](/wiki/Army_Ant "Army Ant"), and/or [Flying Ants](/wiki/Flying_Ant "Flying Ant"). Every 5th wave always contains a [Giant Ant](/wiki/Giant_Ant "Giant Ant"), with the exception of every 40th wave, which contains 2 giant ants. Each ant defeated grants 1 point. 

To progress, fill the meter on the anthill by gathering pollen. The amount of pollen needed to progress to the next wave starts at 4,000 and increases by at least 2,000 more than the previous requirement for each wave.

A lawnmower during the Ant Challenge.

Upon reaching wave 8, the white doors on the sides will start flashing red. This indicates that a lawnmower is about to come out and pass through the lane the door directs to. Move away from the targeted lane to avoid getting damaged. Getting hit by a lawnmower deals 50 base damage. 

Lawnmowers will move faster and appear more frequently as the challenge progresses, with multiple lawnmowers running simultaneously possible near the end of the challenge. 

The challenge ends upon the player's death, leaving the challenge, or when the 5 minutes are up, whichever happens first. While having the [Haste+](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Areas "Buffs & Debuffs") buff, if the player walks up the ant hill, the player may leave the challenge. This is a common glitch.

## Tips[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAnt_Challenge%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Try to collect as many [tokens](/wiki/Token "Token") ([Haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens"), [Baby Love](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Baby_Love "Ability Tokens"), [Bear Morph](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bear_Morph "Ability Tokens"), etc.) as possible before participating in the challenge.
  * [Rage](/wiki/Rage "Rage") tokens can be used to take out ants faster, as they increase [Bee Attack](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Attack "System Page"). 
    * Certain [mutations](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation") on [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that increase attack will also help in defeating ants faster.
  * Higher [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") bees will be able to make the later waves easier to get through. Bees with higher attack and/or speed will also prove to be more effective than those with lower attack and/or speed.
  * A high-level [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") is vital, as the spikes from its ability, [Impale](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Impale "Ability Tokens"), can deal significant damage to multiple ants. In addition, wait to activate the ability when many ants are in the arena to maximize it effectively.
  * A [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Stubborn Bee](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee") can help in this case as it increases the lifespan of ability tokens, giving the player more time to spawn ants before activating abilities.
  * [Windy Bee’s](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") [Tornado](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Tornado "Ability Tokens") can be even more effective than Impale in the Ant Challenge. Not only does it collect pollen, but it also deals massive area damage (which cannot miss) and collects tokens. However, haste stacks and powerful [sprinklers](/wiki/Sprinklers "Sprinklers") are required to maximize their potential.
  * ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") tokens can be used to fill the progress meter on the anthill, so the player may consider using [Honey Bees](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee") or [Diamond Bees](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee") to spawn [Honey Gift](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Honey_Gift "Ability Tokens") tokens, or using a [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") to collect honey tokens from [Festive Gifts](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens").
  * Having [amulets](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet") that boost bee attack damage, a [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee"), and a Gifted [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee") makes it easier to defeat ants.
  * The [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask "Fire Mask") and [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") will boost attack and add [Flame-generating passives](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gathering_Flames "Passive Abilities"). Well placed Flames will grant Flame Heat, collect pollen, and do damage to nearby ants.
  * Using ![Stinger](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Stingers](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger") will boost attack for a short time, which helps clear ants faster and deal with higher-level ants. The player can also use ![Red Extract](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Red Extracts](/wiki/Red_Extract "Red Extract")Red and ![Blue Extract](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blue Extracts](/wiki/Blue_Extract "Blue Extract"), ![Oil](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Oils](/wiki/Oil "Oil"), ![Glue](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Glues](/wiki/Glue "Glue"), ![Tropical Drink](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tropical Drinks](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink"), and ![Gumdrops](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops"). This will help with honey production and boost bee capabilities but isn't necessary to get a good score.
  * ![Coconut](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Coconuts](/wiki/Coconut "Coconut") can prove to be an unpredictable but powerful tool to take out clusters of ants. ![Coconut](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Coconuts](/wiki/Coconut "Coconut") can deal incredible amounts of damage, are capable of damaging high-level ants, and collect decent amounts of pollen. 
    * If the player has [Coconut Clogs](/wiki/Coconut_Clogs "Coconut Clogs"), catching coconuts will also grant [Coconut Haste](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Coconut_Haste "Passive Abilities"), which temporarily makes the player and their bees faster. This can be useful to escape dangerous areas.
  * To maximize the score, defeat as many ants as possible in the first three minutes, as later waves get more difficult and the lawnmowers appear more frequently and move faster.
  * Tools are the best way to fill up the honey meter, this because your bees will be attacking and won't be able to collect pollen.
  * If there is too many ants and the bees can't deal with them, the player shouldn't use their Tool because it will fill up the honey meter (if in the field) and summon more ants. 
    *   * Since the player has 100% [Instant Conversion](/wiki/Instant_Conversion "Instant Conversion") in the Ant Challenge, don't try to go after tokens from [Coin Scatter](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Coin_Scatter "Passive Abilities") or coconuts, since they won't have much value, as they have nothing to convert from, making them virtually useless.
  * Sprinklers can be useful for regenerating flowers and making pollen collection easier, especially when ants and lawnmowers cut the player off from certain areas of the field.
  * During any beesmas, try to play the ant challenge with the Honeyday Buff, this will increase how fast the meter fills
  * Using the [Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider") can help as the player can glide over ants and fire trails, escaping from certain situations.
  * Zoom out all the way to see where all the ants are and where lawnmowers may be coming from.
  * When new ants spawn, it is a good idea to go as far away from the ant hill as possible, to avoid being hit by an incoming ant.
  * [Star Saw](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Saw "Passive Abilities"), a [Supreme Star Amulet](/wiki/Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") passive, is very effective at defeating ants and collecting pollen.
  * Standing near the side of the field where the lawnmowers come out can make the lawnmowers easier to avoid.
  * The player use the small gap between the field and the wall to avoid running over fire trails, though lawnmowers and ants can still damage the player.
  * If a [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm") is summoned during the challenge, it is a good idea to go after the honey tokens in earlier waves or when there aren't too many ants since the honey tokens collected can contribute significantly to the honey meter when a large number of tokens are collected.
  * While the [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister "Coconut Canister")’s ability, Emergency Coconut Shield, is active, getting hit by the lawnmowers will heal the player instead of damaging them.
  * Wear the Gummy Mask and intentionally activate [Gummy Morph](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Morph "Passive Abilities") during harder stages of the challenge, as this grants +300 attack to Gummy Bee.
  * Intentionally activate the Emergency Coconut Shield 2–4 minutes before starting the challenge to avoid wasting it in early levels.
  * Though the ants spawned during the challenge are random, it is possible to predict what ants might spawn. During the first 15 rounds, flying ants are generally more common than army ants. After 15 rounds, army ants become much more common. Giants ants spawn every 5 waves, and always come with either fire ants or regular ants. This information can be helpful when dealing with quests that require defeating specific types of ants.
  * Having ![Invigorating Nectar](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Invigorating Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") can help buff bee attack.

  * If the player leaves their sprinkler(s) in the field and the challenge ends without the player dying, the sprinklers remain in the field.
  * If a player glitches inside and then someone starts it, then the player who glitched in will be kicked out.
  * There is a rare [glitch](/wiki/Glitches#Ants_Stuck_in_Wall "Glitches") where an Ant or a Fire Ant can get stuck on the wall where the lawnmowers come from.
  * There was a glitch prior to February 2022, where sometimes, when the player starts the challenge, they won't actually be teleported inside, forfeiting the challenge instantly.
  * Giant Ants will give the player's bees 3 bond, being the least efficient way to level up bees.
  * It is only possible to go through 100 waves of the challenge. This is because it takes about three seconds to progress to the next wave and there is a total time limit of 5 minutes (300 seconds). 300 seconds divided by 3 seconds per wave would be 100 waves.
  * If the player manages to somehow glitch out of the Ant Challenge, the player will be teleported back to the center of the field. However, if you do this repeatedly, eventually the challenge will end and a message will pop up saying "Ant Challenge ended because you left."
