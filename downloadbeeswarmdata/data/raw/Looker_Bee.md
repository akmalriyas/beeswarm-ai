**Looker Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Rare bee](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"). 

Looker Bee's favorite treat is ![Sunflower Seed](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"). 

Looker Bee likes the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field") and the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"). It dislikes the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field") and the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLooker_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Looker Bee was the "oldest designed character",[1] not necessarily in the form of a bee. It had the signature eyes, was green, and had two little legs.[2]
  * Looker Bee was the first [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to have a [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Royal_Jelly_Variant "Royal Jelly") only turning a bee into it. The jelly was obtainable by using the [code](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), 'ThoseEyes' (expired).
  * The [Looker Guard](/wiki/Looker_Guard "Looker Guard"), which can be bought in the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop"), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Looker Bee.
  * Looker Bee used to have the same gifted hive bonus as [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee") before the nerf on 2021-12-26 update
  * Looker Bee was one of the first bees that were added to the game in an [update](/wiki/Updates "Updates"), along with [Commander Bee](/wiki/Commander_Bee "Commander Bee"), [Music Bee](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee"), and [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee").
  * Looker Bee and [Stubborn Bee](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee") are the only Rare bees that were added after the game's launch.
  * Looker Bee's skin is the same as Commander Bee's skin.
  * Looker Bee is the only bee that dislikes Sunflower Field.
  * The reason Looker Bee likes the Mountain Top Field might be because high places have very good views from above. And the reason Looker Bee likes Clover Field could be based on the effort to look for a four-leafed clover in a field of clovers.
  * The reason Looker Bee dislikes the Sunflower Field may be because of the blinding sunlight from the sun that would be present in a field of sunflowers.
