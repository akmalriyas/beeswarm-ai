# Sunflower Seeds

Sunflower Seed

[![Sunflower Seed](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/66/Sunflower_Seed.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041336)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/66/Sunflower_Seed.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041336)

_"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."_

[![A Sunflower Seed token.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/98/Sunflower_seed.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20210923024803) [![A Sunflower Seed token.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/98/Sunflower_seed.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/130?cb=20210923024803)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/98/Sunflower_seed.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20210923024803) [](/wiki/File:Sunflower_seed.PNG)

A Sunflower Seed token.

A **Sunflower Seed** is a type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that can be fed to a [Bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to [Basic](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"), [Bomber](/wiki/Bomber_Bee "Bomber Bee"), [Looker](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee"), [Demo](/wiki/Demo_Bee "Demo Bee"), [Commander](/wiki/Commander_Bee "Commander Bee"), [Carpenter](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee"), [Ninja](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee"), [Vector](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee"), or [Precise](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") Bees, the amount of [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") they gain is doubled, raising their bond by 50. These bees also have a small chance of becoming [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") when fed Sunflower Seeds, though this only happens with a 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance for [Rare bees](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"), 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance for [Epic bees](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"), 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance for [Common](/wiki/Bees/Common "Bees/Common") and [Legendary bees](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"), and 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance for [Mythic bees](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic") per Sunflower Seed. Feeding a [radioactive](/wiki/Radioactive "Radioactive") bee a sunflower seed has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) to [mutate](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation") a bee. Sunflower Seeds can also be used to craft ![Oil](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Oils](/wiki/Oil "Oil"), ![Paper Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Paper Planters](/wiki/Paper_Planter "Paper Planter"), [Belt Pocket](/wiki/Belt_Pocket "Belt Pocket"), [Mondo Belt Bag](/wiki/Mondo_Belt_Bag "Mondo Belt Bag"), [Bomber Guard](/wiki/Bomber_Guard "Bomber Guard"), [Looker Guard](/wiki/Looker_Guard "Looker Guard") and [Basic Boots](/wiki/Basic_Boots "Basic Boots").
