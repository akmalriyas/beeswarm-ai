**Quests** are a major [mechanic](/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") in [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"). It allows the player to do tasks in order to receive various rewards that allow them to progress further into the game. Quests are not necessary for progression, but are highly recommended. There are a few exceptions, such as [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear")'s [Translator](/wiki/Science_Bear#Translator_Quest_List_\(15\) "Science Bear") questline, and [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear")'s questline, which are required to complete to progress. 

Quests can be obtained and turned in through conversing with various [quest givers](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers"). As of now, 8 active quest [bears](/wiki/Bears "Bears"), 3 [quest bees](/wiki/Category:Quest_Bees "Category:Quest Bees"), [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett"), and 1 [machine](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine "Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine") can give out quests. [Traveling bears](/wiki/Category:Traveling_Bears "Category:Traveling Bears") and other various creatures also give quests but were only available for a limited time in the game. 

Quests have many different types of requirements, such as collecting various amounts of [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") from [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"), [ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), [goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo"), [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") tokens, defeating [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs"), crafting [items](/wiki/Items "Items"), obtaining [badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges"), finding [Stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker"), and discovering [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") types.

Brown Bear is an infinite quest giver. His quests scale up in difficulty the more you complete, with the reward also getting bigger the more difficult it gets. Each quest always rewards a [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), and increasing amounts of honey, depending on the current difficulty of the current quest. Certain thresholds may reward other items as well.

[Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee") is one of the three permanent quest bees. Its quests consist largely of pollen collection from blue fields, defeating blue themed mobs, and collecting/feeding blueberries. Like the other quest bees, it only gives repeatable quests that scale in difficulty. It is located in the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ").

[Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") is one of the three traveling bears to appear in the game. He has appeared four times, with his first visit starting on April 27, 2018, to May 13, 2018, where he gave 12 quests and 2 [belt bags](/wiki/Category:Belts "Category:Belts"). His second visit started on September 10, 2018, to October 8, 2018, where he gave 14 quests and 3 [boots](/wiki/Category:Boots "Category:Boots"). His third visit was from September 28, 2019, to October 28, 2019. During that period, he handed out quests that were mainly themed around [bean bugs](/wiki/Bean_Bug "Bean Bug") with a final reward of 250 tickets. His fourth and last visit started on April 6, 2020, where he participated in the [Egg Hunt 2020](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2020 "Egg Hunt 2020") event and required the player to help three bears with their Egg Hunt quests. Once finished, he would hand out a small variety of rewards and the [Swarming Egg of the Hive](/wiki/Swarming_Egg_of_the_Hive "Swarming Egg of the Hive").

[Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") is one of the three traveling bears who appears every [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas"). During his 2018 visit, which started from December 19, 2018, and ended on January 31, 2019, his quests focused on creating [presents](/wiki/Presents "Presents") and, at the very end, a [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") (or 500 tickets if the player has purchased the [Festive Bee Pack](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop")). During his 2019 visit from December 22, 2019, to February 10, 2020, he gave a [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy") after 10 quests and the Bee Cub skin after another 5 quests. During his 2020 visit from December 25, 2020 to March 1, 2021, He rewarded players with a Festive Bee (500 tickets if Festive Bee was already owned by the player) when his first 15 quests were completed, and rewarded a [Festive Wreath](/wiki/Festive_Wreath "Festive Wreath") beequip for the Festive Bee when all his quests were completed. During his 2021 visit on December 26, 2021, He rewarded players with a special type of his own (Festive Planters), and a [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy") (Tickets if Cub Buddy was already owned by the player) when his first 15 quests were completed. 

For both of his visits, his quests generally have a huge variety of requirements, including collecting tokens, pollen, and goo, defeating mobs, completing quests from Polar Bear, and more. 

During his 2018 Beesmas visit, he stood by the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate"), while in his 2019 Beesmas visit, he stood to the right of the [All-Time Top Honeymakers](/wiki/All-Time_Top_Honeymakers "All-Time Top Honeymakers") and in front of the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field").

[Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") was a traveling bear who arrived on May 26, 2018 and now resides in the Gummy Bear Lair. His quests largely consisted of goo collection from various fields and at the very end, he gave the player a [Gummy Bee Egg](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"). He stood in the gap between the hives and the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop") along with the [Gummy Cannon](/wiki/Gummy_Cannon "Gummy Cannon").
