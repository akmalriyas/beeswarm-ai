# Clouds

## Cloud

[ ![Screenshot 2019-09-28 at 12.06.56 PM](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4e/Screenshot_2019-09-28_at_12.06.56_PM.png/revision/latest?cb=20190928191520) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4e/Screenshot_2019-09-28_at_12.06.56_PM.png/revision/latest?cb=20190928191520 "Screenshot 2019-09-28 at 12.06.56 PM") An entity that regrows flowers and grants short pollen buffs. **Clouds** are small, white rain clouds that can be summoned through various methods (listed below). They move around the [field](/wiki/Field "Field") aimlessly over a 1-5 minute time span, depending on the source of the cloud, raining over 49 surrounding flowers and functioning as secondary [sprinklers](/wiki/Sprinklers "Sprinklers"). Clouds can also help grow a [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") if there is a player in the same field although the [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") given to the sprout depends on the type of sprout and the field. If a player is under a cloud, they will receive the Cloud Boost buff which gives +25% pollen and +10% Unique Instant Conversion (+50% Pollen and +20% Unique Instant Conversion if the player has a [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee")) and lasts for up to 8 seconds. Clouds can also accelerate a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") by up to 20% with 6 clouds (around 8% with 1 cloud), however keeping a cloud in a field for hours is expensive making this method not viable for buffing planters. 

There can only be a maximum of 6 clouds on a field at a time. If the player attempts to use a [cloud vial](/wiki/Cloud_Vial "Cloud Vial") to summon another cloud, a message will pop up saying:  There are too many clouds in this field.

Clouds cannot be summoned in the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field"). Attempting to do so will result in the following message:  You can't make clouds in a challenge field.

## Methods To Summon[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClouds%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using a cloud vial (3 minute duration).
  * Receiving field winds from the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") (5 minute duration).
  * Activating/Defeating [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") (3 minute duration).
  * Summoned by a player's [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") (1 min duration + 10 sec per level of Windy Bee)



## Types[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClouds%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

There are two different types of clouds: The Rain Cloud and the Windy Cloud. 

Rain Cloud: The rain clouds are the ones that are summoned through donations to the Wind Shrine, by using a cloud vial and while fighting Wild Windy Bee. They are beneficial to players and give the cloud buff, grow sprouts, and regenerate flowers. These clouds are also spawned by tamed Windy Bees. 

Windy Cloud: The Windy Cloud is a cloud that is only seen when a Wild Windy Bee has appeared. The cloud floats over a field and contains Wild Windy Bee inside it. Once a player approaches it and jumps up into the cloud, it will become a rain cloud and Wild Windy Bee starts to attack. Like rain clouds, it is still able to give the cloud buff, grow sprouts, and regenerate flowers. 

Collecting a [Cloud](/wiki/Ability_Token#Cloud "Ability Token") token will give the player the message:  🌧️ Windy Bee summoned a Cloud in {Field Name}. 🌧️ If another player's Windy Bee spawns a cloud in your field, a message will appear announcing: 🌧️ {Username}'s Windy Bee summoned a Cloud in this field. 🌧️
