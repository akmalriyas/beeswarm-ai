**Shy Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Legendary bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"). 

Shy Bee's favorite treat is ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"). 

Shy Bee likes the [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field") and the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"). It dislikes the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FShy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 2 seconds.
  * Makes 320 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +100% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), +30% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +50% Gather Speed, +240 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +1 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +5% Bee Ability Pollen.



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FShy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Red Boost]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens")** Grants x1.2 pollen from red [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") for 15s. Stacks up to 10 times.
  * **[[Red Bomb]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")** Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding red flowers (+10% pollen per Level). Combo with other bombs to increase power.
  * **[[Passive: Nectar Lover]](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Nectar_Lover "Passive Abilities")** This bee is twice as likely to sip Nectar from Planters. When it does, it gathers twice as much Nectar and contributes twice as much growth to the Planter.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FShy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This bee, [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee"), [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee"), and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") are the only bees that have only one skin color.
  * This bee used to have a spelling or grammar mistake in its description, along with [Hasty Bee](/wiki/Hasty_Bee "Hasty Bee"), [Shocked Bee](/wiki/Shocked_Bee "Shocked Bee"), and [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"), but it was fixed in an update.
  * This bee is the only bee that has a smoke effect.
  * This bee is the red counterpart of Ninja Bee.
  * A [Special Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Specific_Bee_Jelly "Royal Jelly") that changes a bee into a Shy Bee could have been obtained from the code, 'NewMic' (expired).
  * This bee, [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee") and [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee") are the only legendary bees with a passive ability.
