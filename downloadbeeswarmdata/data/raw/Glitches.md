# Glitches

![Hivesticker 4-pronged vector bee](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) | **"When the Mythic Egg gets the math wrong."** This information in this article can often change and may become inaccurate. **Glitches** , or **bugs** , are situations that can occur in-game that are unintended, and thus likely to be patched in future [updates](/wiki/Updates "Updates"). Some of these glitches can be detrimental to gameplay while others are mostly visual and won't affect players. "Glitches" caused purely or partially by internet are not listed.

### Mobs Unable to Move[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

[![Coconut Crab stuck in the  during 2022-07-20. Image taken from a video on Reddit.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Cococrab.png/revision/latest?cb=20220720073246) [![Coconut Crab stuck in the  during 2022-07-20. Image taken from a video on Reddit.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Cococrab.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/356?cb=20220720073246)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Cococrab.png/revision/latest?cb=20220720073246) [](/wiki/File:Cococrab.png)

Coconut Crab stuck in the [Coconut Cave](/wiki/Coconut_Cave "Coconut Cave") during 2022-07-20. Image taken from a video on [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/BeeSwarmSimulator/comments/w3fqwx/what_the_heck_is_up_with_my_coconut_crab_ive/).

Occasionally, a [mob](/wiki/Mob "Mob") will get trapped in the collision box of certain objects around it. This usually renders them unable to move or escape, which makes killing them easier. The mob can still be attacked and will still drop rewards normally. This glitch seems to happen primarily to [Mantises](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis") when they leap into objects, such as a wall. Sometimes, the [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle") gets stuck in the corner which says "Song Name" near it, making it easy to kill him. This can also happen to [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") in certain places. 

In mid-2022, during the days of July 19–20, 22-31, and August 2–3, all mobs (Excluding the [Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf"), Stick Bug, [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee"), [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee"), and [Aphids](/wiki/Aphid "Aphid")) would be frozen in place upon entering the area they spawn in. Affected bosses include the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab"), [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle"), and [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail"). This makes killing them easier, as the mobs will not be able to voluntarily attack the player. On 2022-07-21, Onett would fix this glitch (temporarily) and added the code "FrozenBugReboot" to suffice for the glitch. The glitch would become fixed and unfixed for multiple weeks until 2022-08-03, when Onett would finally fix the glitch permanently in servers of version 448+.

The mob's head or any other part can move inward. This glitch seems more common with the [Ladybug](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug") and [Spider](/wiki/Spider "Spider").

### Enormous [Mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") HP/Timer Bars[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

This visual glitch occurs when a mob first spawns in. This is because mobs are coded to have a very large default HP/timer number (usually 1,300,400,000) before quickly switching to the normal amount. If the player suffers from bad internet or the server is having issues, it might take a little longer for the number to switch over. 

### [Snowbear](/wiki/Snowbear "Snowbear") Leaving the Spider Field[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Sometimes, if the snowbear gets [Mind Hacked](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), it will wander out of the field, sometimes getting stuck. It can even potentially wander out of the 5 Bee Zone. 

### Confused [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

If the player stands on the entrance to Commando Chick's lair, putting them on the elevated floor and inside the spawn area of Commando Chick at the same time, Commando Chick will not be able to damage the player by touching them. Commando Chick can still damage the player with grenades and the glitch making it go on the ledge while in egg form. 

### Speedy Commando Chick Glitch[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

This glitch occurs randomly when Commando Chick spawns. Commando Chick's feet become stuck in the ground, and it will move around extremely quickly only stopping when hiding in its egg. This bug can occur in conjunction with the Moving Commando in Egg glitch. 

### Grenade Rain[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

This glitch can be performed by constantly exiting and entering the [Commando Chick's hideout](/wiki/Commando_Chick%27s_Hideout "Commando Chick's Hideout") repeatedly, causing the Commando Chick to spawn and despawn while summoning two grenades at a time. The grenades can kill players inside the hideout. This glitch can be annoying for people that are in the hideout that just want to defeat their Commando Chicks. Very rarely, if the player does this enough, it will knock the fleeing Commando Chick into the ground. It will stay there as the new Commando Chick does its normal behavior. After around 5 seconds, it will despawn. 

### Fallen [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Occasionally, [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick") may fall off of the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field") into the [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field") or the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"). Loot dropped from Mondo Chick's defeat will spawn inside the field it was defeated in, and if it fails to reach a field before this happens it spawns no loot at all. This glitch is a result of Mondo entering its egg just close enough to the edge of the Mountain Top that the moment it leaves, if it moves in the correct direction, it falls out of the field. 

### Crashing Mondo[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

This sometimes happens when you let Mondo Chick despawn, when it does its leaving jump animation, it can sometimes crash into the Instant Converter or the Mountain Top Shop, spins back, lays for a few seconds before despawning, 

### Other Players' Mobs Attacking You[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

If a mob has no target (either because the target died or left the attack zone), the mob becomes capable of hitting anyone within its hitbox. This is noticeable when collecting pollen in the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field") when your Coconut Crab has already been defeated. This glitch can be replicated when a player is fighting a Coconut Crab, and you are inside the player’s Coconut Crab’s hitbox. If the player dies or leaves, the Coconut Crab can damage you while traveling back to its spawn location. This can occur with any mob that has a set spawn location. Occasionally, this can also occur with Tunnel Bear. The cause of this glitch is unknown. 

### Invisible Coconut Crab[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

This glitch can occur if the player has bad internet or has set their graphics at 1. The coconut crab will become invisible and unable to attack using its abilities. However, you can still get damaged by the coconut crabs body if you come close to it, since the hitbox is still there even if it visually looks invisible. 

## Player Glitches[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

### Talking to [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") with less than 35 bees[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

If the player has laggy internet or high ping, they can talk to Spirit Bear by gliding from the top of the Windy Bee Gate with less than 35 bees. 

### Shop Trap[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D18&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

This glitch can occur in any shop. When players reset with the shop open, they are unable to close the interface, as there is no button to do so. Players are able to exit the shop by resetting their character and spamming the E key, but this won't work all the time so players may have to reset a few times or rejoin. This mostly happens in [Gummy Bear's Lair](/wiki/Gummy_Bear%27s_Lair "Gummy Bear's Lair"). 

If the player activates the red field booster and quickly open the shop, the button for activating the red field booster will be on the screen instead of the "Close Shop" button. This will result in the player getting stuck inside the shop with no way to close it. The same glitch will occur if the player has a high-speed boost and runs towards the blender at the correct angle then quickly opens the sprinkler shop. 

### Invisible Splinter Trap[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D19&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

If a player respawns right as Stick Bug uses his [Splinter Trap](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge#Splinter_Trap "Stick Bug Challenge"), they will still get the debuff. However, players will not see the pollen requirement to remove it and will continuously deal damage until death unless they fulfill the unknown pollen requirement. 

### Sticker Quests Buggy Progress Bar[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D20&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

### Broken Arm Glitch[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D22&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Typically, because the player is constantly holding a tool, the player is permanently stuck with their arm sticking outwards, away from their body. Sometimes, however, a glitch can cause the players arm to be at rest rather than held up, which makes the tool stick out awkwardly facing the wrong direction. Normally, when the player uses their tool, their arm will be brought down and then back up to collect pollen, but when a player is under the effects of this bug, the arm is brought up, then down, up again, then back down. Visually, this makes it seem as though the tool collected pollen twice, when it is actually just a visual complication. This issue can be fixed by rejoining the game.

When the player pops a [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") too quickly or if a player has high ping, the numbers that show the amount of [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") needed will show the excess [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") that was collected with a negative sign in front of it. The numbers will go below the field, as well as turning red, and if the number of [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") collected is high enough it will turn purple. 

### Phantom Token[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D28&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

This glitch can happen when a player joins the game while a [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm") or [Snowstorm](/wiki/Snowstorm "Snowstorm") is active, after a [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") has been popped, or by other means because the token appeared almost exactly when the player joined. Images of tokens are visible, but not the token itself. Players cannot collect phantom tokens. The phantom tokens remain until the player leaves. This can also happen on PC if you leave the game minimized for too long, causing the lag to prevent visual token despawning. A similar effect can be observed with [flames](/wiki/Flame "Flame").

### Red Hive Slot Glitch[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGlitches%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D31&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

[![Red Hive Slots.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/ba/RobloxScreenShot20191113_201729946.png/revision/latest?cb=20191114013608) [![Red Hive Slots.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/ba/RobloxScreenShot20191113_201729946.png/revision/latest?cb=20191114013608)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/ba/RobloxScreenShot20191113_201729946.png/revision/latest?cb=20191114013608) [](/wiki/File:RobloxScreenShot20191113_201729946.png)

Red Hive Slots.

This glitch makes a [hive slot](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot") look red. To activate it, click and hold a random consumable from the inventory that doesn't give bond ([gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops"), [magic beans](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), etc.) and use it as if it were to be fed to a bee. To fix it, players can either rejoin the game or click another random item ([tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") and [Spirit Petals](/wiki/Spirit_Petal "Spirit Petal") won't work). This also works on empty hive slots but instead of turning the empty hive slot red, it will turn white. Using consumables that can be used outside of fields, like Red and Blue Extracts, can cause multiple bees to have red Hive Slots.
