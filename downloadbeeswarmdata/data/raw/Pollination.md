A large and a star flower, both of which can only be seen via pollination.

**Pollination** is a [mechanic](/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") that causes [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") to grow to higher tiers, increasing their total [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") and multiplier. Flowers that are lower tier than other flowers in the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") have a higher chance of being pollinated. When flowers are pollinated, they will burst flower particles. 

Flowers only stay pollinated for 1 minute, but this can be stacked up to 5 times for 5 minutes. Every minute that an already pollinated flower is not pollinated, it will go down a tier, until it has reverted to its original state. 

Pollination is the only method to achieve large and star flowers, as the highest natural tier is triple. 

## Ways To Pollinate Flowers[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPollination%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * [Fuzzy Bee's](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee") [Fuzz Bombs](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Fuzz_Bombs "Ability Tokens") can pollinate flowers upon popping it.
  * Fuzzy Bee‘s [Fuzzy Coat](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Fuzzy_Coat "Passive Abilities") passive has a chance to pollinate up to 5 surrounding flowers while collecting pollen. This increases to 9 when gifted.
  * Fuzzy Bee's [Pollen Haze](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Pollen_Haze "Ability Tokens") pollinates up to 30 flowers every second. [Bomb](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens") tokens produced in a field with pollen haze are automatically collected and pollinate flowers.
  * [Diamond Mask's](/wiki/Diamond_Mask "Diamond Mask") [Diamond Drain](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Diamond_Drain "Passive Abilities") pollinates 10 blue flowers when activated. The number of flowers pollinated increases by 3 for every blue bee the player has.
  * If [Guiding Star](/wiki/Star_Amulet#Supreme_Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") is active, the star will pollinate 15 (+1 per [gifted bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") type) flowers every 15 seconds.
  * A bee with the ![Poinsettia](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3a/Poinsettia.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230409133457)[Poinsettia](/wiki/Poinsettia "Poinsettia") [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") equipped has an 11% to 20% chance to pollinate flowers while gathering.
  * A bee with the ![Lei](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Lei](/wiki/Lei "Lei") Beequip equipped has a 20% to 50% chance to pollinate flowers while gathering.
  * A bee with the ![Rose Headband](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Rose Headband](/wiki/Rose_Headband "Rose Headband") has a 10% to 20% chance to pollinate flowers while gathering.
