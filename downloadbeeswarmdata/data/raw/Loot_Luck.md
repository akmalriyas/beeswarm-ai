# Loot Luck

**Loot Luck** is a passive attribute that increases the chances of receiving certain rewards from defeated [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs"). The formula to calculate the chance of receiving an [item](/wiki/Items "Items") from a mob when the player defeats it is: 1-((1-base chance) ^luck). A general rule of thumb is that for every 100% Loot Luck the player has, the player gets an extra roll to get each drop. 

It also affects what type of token spawns in [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"). Every time a [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") token spawns in the field, it rolls a "drop table". Every [item](/wiki/Items "Items") has a chance to "pass," with Loot Luck boosting the chance for it to pass. If it does, that item spawns. Otherwise, if it fails, it goes on to the next item. If every item fails, the honey token spawns as it is. 

Loot Luck does not affect [amulet](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet") tiers, stats granted from amulets, drops from [chicks](/wiki/Chicks "Chicks"), or hatching and transforming [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"). 

## Ways to Increase[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLoot_Luck%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Loot Luck can be increased through [belts](/wiki/Category:Belts "Category:Belts"), [buffs](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs "Buffs & Debuffs"), and other ways. 

### [Belts](/wiki/Category:Belts "Category:Belts")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLoot_Luck%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Each belt gives an amount of Loot Luck. 

  * The [Belt Pocket](/wiki/Belt_Pocket "Belt Pocket") gives +25% Loot Luck.
  * The [Belt Bag](/wiki/Belt_Bag "Belt Bag") gives +50% Loot Luck.
  * The [Mondo Belt Bag](/wiki/Mondo_Belt_Bag "Mondo Belt Bag") gives +75% Loot Luck.
  * The [Honeycomb Belt](/wiki/Honeycomb_Belt "Honeycomb Belt") gives +100% Loot Luck.
  * The [Petal Belt](/wiki/Petal_Belt "Petal Belt") gives +101% Loot Luck.
  * The [Coconut Belt](/wiki/Coconut_Belt "Coconut Belt") gives +102% Loot Luck.



### [Gifted Hive Bonuses](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLoot_Luck%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Gifted [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee") gives +25% Loot Luck.



### Buffs[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLoot_Luck%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * [Baby Love](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Baby_Love "Ability Tokens") gives +50% Loot Luck.
  * [Cool Breeze](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Snowglobe_Shake "Ability Tokens") gives from 1% Loot Luck up to 10% Loot Luck at 100% Cool Breeze.



### [Badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLoot_Luck%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The [Clover Badge](/wiki/Badges#Clover_Badges "Badges") can give up to +50% Loot Luck depending on the badge rank. 
    * The Cadet Clover Badge gives +10% Loot Luck.
    * The Hotshot Clover Badge gives +20% Loot Luck.
    * The Ace Clover Badge gives +30% Loot Luck.
    * The Master Clover Badge gives +40% Loot Luck.
    * The Grandmaster Clover Badge gives +50% Loot Luck.



## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLoot_Luck%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Before the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), the Clover Badge did not give any Loot Luck. This was a mistake, which was fixed after said update.
  * The [B.B.M. Mask](/wiki/B.B.M._Mask "B.B.M. Mask") used to grant +50% Loot Luck.
