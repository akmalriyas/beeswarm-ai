The **Rose Field** is one of the four [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") located past the [Honey Bee Gate](/wiki/Honey_Bee_Gate "Honey Bee Gate"). This field can be found near the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"). It is protected by two [scorpions](/wiki/Scorpion "Scorpion"), one level 5, and the other level 6. 

This field can spawn [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [strawberry](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"), [red boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), [red extract](/wiki/Red_Extract "Red Extract"), [bitterberry](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"), and [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean") tokens when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Rose Field are [Rad Bee](/wiki/Rad_Bee "Rad Bee"), [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"), [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee"), [Riley Bee](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee"), [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee"), [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee"), and [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"). Bees that dislike the Rose Field are [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"), [Stubborn Bee](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee"), [Bucko Bee](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee"), [Demo Bee](/wiki/Demo_Bee "Demo Bee"), [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"), and [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee"). 

This field grants Motivating [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is planted here. 

A level 5-7 [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") has a chance of spawning here. 

This is a 20×31 field, containing 620 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are red and white, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

The Rose Field Boost icon.

  * Using the [Red Field Booster](/wiki/Red_Field_Booster "Red Field Booster") may give a Rose [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +100% Rose Field pollen for 15 minutes.
  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") will add 100% Rose Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% - 300% Rose Field pollen for 15 minutes based on the type used and based on its current stack before getting another one.
  * Rose [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Rose Field conversion, and give +5% instant Rose Field conversion and +15% Rose Field pollen for each stack. Rose Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRose_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is one of six fields that [fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies") travel to, with the others being [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), and [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field").
  * Vicious Bee likes this field most likely because roses commonly have thorns on their stems. 
    * It is also the only blue bee to like a field of the opposite color. The only other bee to share this trait is [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") and [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest").
  * This field was one of the fields which has a small section outside of the map.
  * There is a [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token next to this field in a small ledge, which can be accessed by walking a small path along the [Badge Bearer's Guild](/wiki/Badge_Bearer%27s_Guild "Badge Bearer's Guild") wall.
  * This is the only field past the Honey Bee Gate to not be defended by the [Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf").
  * After the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), treat tokens could naturally spawn in this field.
  * This is the only field to be at a lower elevation than the gate leading to it.
  * If a Rogue Vicious Bee spawns in this field, it can attack players inside the Red HQ with its spikes.
  * Stepping in the row of flowers closest to [Robo Bear](/wiki/Robo_Bear "Robo Bear") does not spawn the Scorpions.
