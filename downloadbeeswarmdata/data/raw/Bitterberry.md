# Bitterberry

Bitterberry

[![Bitterberry](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/af/Bitterberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041641)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/af/Bitterberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041641)

_"Increases bond with a bee by 100. Small chance to cause Mutations."_

[![The text that appears in the chat when a bee gains a mutation, fed with bitterberry.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/77/54C3C074-D3F3-4DF1-A9D8-B31A5CB31B69.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230304220307)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/77/54C3C074-D3F3-4DF1-A9D8-B31A5CB31B69.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20230304220307) [](/wiki/File:54C3C074-D3F3-4DF1-A9D8-B31A5CB31B69.jpg)

The text that appears in the chat when a bee gains a mutation, fed with bitterberry.

A **Bitterberry** is a type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that, when fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), raises its [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") by 100 and has a 1/1000 chance to make it [mutated](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation"). The chance is boosted to 1/100 if the bee is [radioactive](/wiki/Radiation "Radiation"). These are not the favorite treat of any bee.
