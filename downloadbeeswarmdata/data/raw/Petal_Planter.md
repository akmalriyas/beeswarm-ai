The **Petal Planter** is a reusable [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") added in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"). Alone, it grows in about 14 hours and stores 100 billion [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). When harvested, it grants bonus Jelly Beans, Field Dice, and Glitter. It can be purchased at the [Petal Shop](/wiki/Petal_Shop "Petal Shop").

The planter grows 50% faster in [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") with white flowers, and grants 50% bonus white pollen on harvest. Colorless bees are 33% more likely to sip [nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") from this planter, and it grants 50% more Comforting and Satisfying nectar. 

Harvesting a fully grown Petal Planter has a 1/4 chance to spawn a [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom"). The spawned puffshroom is between levels 7-8, and has a: 

  * ~89.28% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
  * ~8.93% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
  * ~1.79% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom,
  * ~0.01% chance of being a Mythic Puffshroom.
