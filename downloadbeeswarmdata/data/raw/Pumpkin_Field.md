The **Pumpkin Patch** is a [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") located past the [Honey Bee Gate](/wiki/Honey_Bee_Gate "Honey Bee Gate") and it lies right beside the [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"). It is protected by the [werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf"). 

This field can spawn [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [treat](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), and [glue](/wiki/Glue "Glue") tokens when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Pumpkin Patch are [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee"), [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee"), [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"), and [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee"). Bees that dislike the Pumpkin Patch are [Bomber Bee](/wiki/Bomber_Bee "Bomber Bee"), [Hasty Bee](/wiki/Hasty_Bee "Hasty Bee"), and [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"). 

This field grants Satisfying [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") and an increased chance on dropping waxes when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is planted here. 

A level 1-20 [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") has a chance of spawning here. 

Additionally, the largest pumpkin in the corner of the field seldomly makes a face before generating [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") in the field. 

This is a 17x33 field, containing 561 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are white, red, and blue, in sizes of double and triple.

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPumpkin_Patch%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Field Booster](/wiki/Field_Booster "Field Booster") may give a Pumpkin [Patch Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +200% Pumpkin Patch pollen for 15 minutes.  [![The Pumpkin Patch boost icon.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/69/Pumpkin_Patch_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080225) [![The Pumpkin Patch boost icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/69/Pumpkin_Patch_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080225)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/69/Pumpkin_Patch_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080225)

The Pumpkin Patch boost icon.

  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") in the field will add 100% Pumpkin Patch pollen onto the current boost if it isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [Field Dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [Smooth Dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [Loaded Dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% - 300% Pumpkin Patch pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Pumpkin [Patch Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Pumpkin Patch conversion and give +5% instant Pumpkin Patch conversion and +20% Pumpkin Patch pollen for each stack. Pumpkin Patch Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times normally and can be boosted by up to 15x by using codes.
  * It can be boosted by the [Shell Amulet](/wiki/Shell_Amulet "Shell Amulet"). If it has a Pumpkin Patch Boost, it can boost the field by +5% to +30% depending on the amulet rarity.
  * Giving Polar Bear a [Present](/wiki/Present "Present") gives the player Pumpkin Patch Boost x3.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPumpkin_Patch%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This [field's badge](/wiki/Badges#Pumpkin_Badge "Badges") and the [Sunflower Badge](/wiki/Badges#Sunflower_Badge "Badges") are used 3 times for chances in [Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match"), the most out of any field badges.
  * There is a ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token behind the two pumpkins in the corner.
  * This is one of the 3 fields to have objects come to life. The others are [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch") and [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field").
  * Before the [2018-04-27 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-04-27 "Updates"), the Pumpkin Patch had the same mix of [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") as the Cactus Field.
  * This field used to be the smallest field until the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field") was added. 
    * Due to the expansion of the Stump Field, this field has now become the smallest again (if one weren't to count the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field")).
  * This field is one of three fields to have the word "Patch" in their name instead of "Field," with the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch") and [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch") being the other two.
  * Prior to the 2022-12-26 update, the pumpkins nearest to the [Werewolf's Cave](/wiki/Werewolf%27s_Cave "Werewolf's Cave") had collision. Their collision has since been removed.
