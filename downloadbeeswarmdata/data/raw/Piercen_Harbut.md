_This page is for the developer of the game. For the quest-giving NPC, see[Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett")._

**Piercen Harbut** , better known as **[Onett](https://www.roblox.com/users/1912490/profile)** on Roblox, is the owner and programmer of [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"). Bee Swarm Simulator is his first and main game on Roblox. He owns the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Club "Bee Swarm Simulator Club") group and [Onett's Testing Group](/wiki/Onett%27s_Testing_Group "Onett's Testing Group"). 

Onett runs alternate accounts to handle messages about errors experienced while playing Bee Swarm Simulator. [BeeSwarmBugReport](https://www.roblox.com/users/556387844/profile) handles bug reports and [BeeSwarmSupport](https://www.roblox.com/users/556383852/profile) handles the purchase and saves file reports. Onett also has a secret alternate account named [ltpb2020](https://www.roblox.com/users/1953773238/profile/), which he mainly used to upload decals for the [Ready Player Two](/wiki/Ready_Player_Two "Ready Player Two") event. Another account was found by the name [HiddenBeePotential](https://www.roblox.com/users/5098329817/profile) but seems to only be used for testing as of now. 

[Onett's Twitter account](https://twitter.com/OnettDev/) can be found by searching @OnettDev. 

Onett also has an [Instagram account for the game](https://www.instagram.com/beeswarmsimulator/), a [personal Instagram account](https://www.instagram.com/piercenharbut/), a [Threads account](https://www.threads.com/@piercenharbut), a [YouTube account](https://www.youtube.com/@PiercenHarbut), a [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/OnettBSS) account, a [Reddit account](https://www.reddit.com/u/BeeSwarmDev/s/iutuKGHL2H), and a Discord account (onett1). 

Onett lives in the CST timezone, as confirmed on Discord.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOnett_%28Developer%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Onett had a cat named Sam[1] in real life, which was the inspiration for [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"). It passed away later in 2018.[2]
  * Onett's favorite bees are [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"),[3] [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"),[4] [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"),[4] and [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee").[5]
  * Onett's favorite ability is Triangulate, since he thinks it's satisfying.
  * Onett's username is a reference to a city in the Nintendo game [EarthBound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EarthBound "wikipedia:EarthBound").[6]
  * Onett owns a special [tool](/wiki/Tools "Tools") called the Honey Hammer.
  * Onett's favorite [mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") is the [Rhino Beetle](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle").[7]
  * Onett's favorite in-game music is [bpatrol](/wiki/Music "Music").[8]
  * Occasionally, Onett summons mini events. They have various aliases which replace the username, such as "[Alias] has summoned (a) [mini event]".
  * Onett's birthday is on 1989-12-24, and he is currently 36 years old.
  * Onett was interviewed by Thundermaker300 in November 2018 as part of the Roblox Wiki's Developer Connections project. See [here](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:RBLXDeveloperInterviews/Onett:_Bee_Swarm_Simulator "w:c:roblox:User blog:RBLXDeveloperInterviews/Onett: Bee Swarm Simulator") to read the interview.
