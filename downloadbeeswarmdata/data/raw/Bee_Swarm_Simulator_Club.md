The **[Bee Swarm Simulator Club](https://www.roblox.com/groups/3982592)** is a Roblox group with over 17 million members. Joining it fulfills a prerequisite for the [Sticker Printer](/wiki/Sticker_Printer "Sticker Printer") and grants access to the [Honey Dispenser](/wiki/Honey_Dispenser "Honey Dispenser"), the [Treat Dispenser](/wiki/Treat_Dispenser "Treat Dispenser"), the [Strawberry Dispenser](/wiki/Strawberry_Dispenser "Strawberry Dispenser") and the [Blueberry Dispenser](/wiki/Blueberry_Dispenser "Blueberry Dispenser"). Redeeming certain [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes") also require the player to be in the group (I.E. ClubConverters).

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBee_Swarm_Simulator_Club%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * During [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas") 2020 & 2021, [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") required the usage of the Honey Dispenser in order to complete their Beesmas quest. This meant that users who wanted to complete Beesmas were forced to join the group.
  * Science Bear also forces players to join the club in order to get all the translators due to his quest, “The Power of Information” which requires the use of both the Strawberry Dispenser and the Blueberry Dispenser.
  * The Bee Swarm Simulator Club has the 12th most members of all groups on Roblox. The group with the most members is "BRAZILIAN SPYDER". 
    * The Bee Swarm Simulator Club used to have the most members in a group on all of Roblox, until it got overtaken by Scriptbloxian Studios in 2021.
