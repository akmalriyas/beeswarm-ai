The **Paper Planter** is a single-use [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") added in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"). Alone, it grows in 1 hour and stores 50,000 pollen. It can be bought in the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop") for ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)25,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") and ![Sunflower Seed](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Sunflower Seed](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"). A player can store up to 100 Paper Planters at a time, but only 1 can be active. 

It does not have any growth nor pollen multipliers, and grants x0.75 [nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar"). 

Harvesting a fully grown Paper Planter has a 1/10 chance to spawn a [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom"). The spawned puffshroom is guaranteed to be a level 1 Common Puffshroom.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPaper_Planter%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is the cheapest and fastest-growing planter in the game.
  * It is one of the many planters to not be sold in [Dapper Bear's Shop](/wiki/Dapper_Bear%27s_Shop "Dapper Bear's Shop"), the others being the [Red Clay Planter](/wiki/Red_Clay_Planter "Red Clay Planter"), [Blue Clay Planter](/wiki/Blue_Clay_Planter "Blue Clay Planter"), [Festive Planter](/wiki/Festive_Planter "Festive Planter"), [Ticket Planter](/wiki/Ticket_Planter "Ticket Planter"), [Sticker Planter](/wiki/Sticker_Planter "Sticker Planter"), [Petal Planter](/wiki/Petal_Planter "Petal Planter"), [Hydroponic Planter](/wiki/Hydroponic_Planter "Hydroponic Planter") and [Heat-Treated Planter](/wiki/Heat-Treated_Planter "Heat-Treated Planter").
  * This is the cheapest item in the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop") in terms of honey, only costing 25,000 honey.
  * This is the only item in the Pro Shop that is cheaper than an item in the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop"), being the [Helmet](/wiki/Helmet "Helmet").
  * This, the Ticket Planter and Sticker Planter the are the only one-time use planters that are renewable.
  * This is the only planter in the game that stores more pollen than the honey it costs.
  * It is one of the four planters that are one-time use. The remaining three are the [Sticker Planter](/wiki/Sticker_Planter "Sticker Planter"), [Ticket Planter](/wiki/Ticket_Planter "Ticket Planter") and the [Festive Planter](/wiki/Festive_Planter "Festive Planter"). 
    * It is also the only one-time use planter that can be bought from a store.
  * This is also the only planter that _reduces_ nectar gained, multiplying all nectar by 0.75x. All other planters either boost nectar gain (although some of them only boost certain types) or do not change it at all.
