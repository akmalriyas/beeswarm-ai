**Demon Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Legendary bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"). 

Demon Bee's favorite treat is ![Pineapple](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"). 

Demon Bee likes the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field") and the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"). It dislikes the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDemon_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 35 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 60 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * -25% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +25 [Gather amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats"), -20 convert amount, +10% [Instant Conversion](/wiki/Instant_Conversion "Instant Conversion") (+20% if Gifted), +7 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +20% [Instant Bomb Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Instant_Bomb_Conversion "System Page").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDemon_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Red Bomb]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")** Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding red [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") (+10% pollen per lvl). Combo with other bombs to increase power.
  * **[Red Bomb+]** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding red flowers (+10% pollen per lvl). Combo with other bombs to increase power.
  * **[[Passive:](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gathering_Flames "Passive Abilities") [Gathering Flames]](/wiki/Flame#Gathering_Flames "Flame")** 65% chance (85% if Gifted) to spawn a [Flame](/wiki/Flame "Flame") when gathering. Flames last 4 seconds. Each second they deal 10 damage (+25% of the bee's [base attack](/wiki/Bee_Attack "Bee Attack")) to nearby enemies and collect [5 Red / 3 White / 2 Blue] pollen (+10% per bee level) from 9 flowers. Standing near a flame grants Flame Heat, which increases [Red Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Red_Pollen "System Page") (between x1.2 and x2) and [Bee Attack](/wiki/Bee_Attack "Bee Attack") (between +5% to +25%).

Demon Bee has a base pollen collection of **35![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. That's equivalent to 8.75 ![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDemon_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Demon Bee is one of the slowest [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in the game, but is also one of the most powerful in terms of attack and gather amount.
  * Demon Bee is the only bee in the game to have 2 bomb abilities (Red Bomb and Red Bomb+).
  * Demon Bee, [Riley Bee](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee"), and [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee") have the same skin colors.
  * Both Demon Bee and [Fire Bee](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee") have the same flame effect.
  * The [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask"), which can be purchasable in the [Lava Obby](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses#Lava_Obby "Obstacle Courses"), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Demon Bee.
  * [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") has said on Discord that during early stages, it was supposed to summon Fire Bees. This role has now been taken over by [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee").
  * Before the [2020-04-06 Update](/wiki/Updates#2020-04-06 "Updates"), Demon Bee had the highest base gather amount in the game with the exception of a [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee") with a 500+ stack of [Tabby Love](/wiki/Tabby_Love "Tabby Love"). Since then, [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee") has taken that position.
  * Demon Bee is the red counterpart of [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee").
  * One reason Demon Bee dislikes the Mountain Top Field might be because it is closer to heaven (the sky) than all other Fields.
  * This bee can be summoned by the [Honeyday Candles](/wiki/Honeyday_Candles "Honeyday Candles").
  * This Bee, Fire Bee, [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee"), [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") and [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee") are the only red bees that do not like Strawberries.
  * In the in-game stat menu for Demon Bee, its attack is stated to be 6 more than the normal amount (1). However, it is actually 7 more.
