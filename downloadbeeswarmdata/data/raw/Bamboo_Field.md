The **Bamboo Field** is a [field](/wiki/Field "Field") located past the [Basic Bee Gate](/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate "Basic Bee Gate"), to the right of the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), and 2 fields away from the [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field"). It is protected by two [Rhino Beetles](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"), one being level 2 and the other level 3. 

This is an 18×39 field, containing 702 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are blue and white, in sizes of single, double, and triple. 

This field grants Comforting [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is planted here. 

This field can spawn [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [blueberry](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"), [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [blue boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), and [blue extract](/wiki/Blue_Extract "Blue Extract") tokens when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Bamboo Field are [Cool Bee](/wiki/Cool_Bee "Cool Bee"), [Bucko Bee](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee"), [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"), [Carpenter Bee](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee"), and [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee"). Bees that dislike the Bamboo Field are [Riley Bee](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee"), [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee"), & [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee").

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBamboo_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

[![The Bamboo Field Boost icon.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b0/Bamboo_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080335) [![The Bamboo Field Boost icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b0/Bamboo_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080335)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b0/Bamboo_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080335)

The Bamboo Field Boost icon.

  * Using the [Blue Field Booster](/wiki/Blue_Field_Booster "Blue Field Booster") may give a Bamboo [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +100% Bamboo Field pollen for 15 minutes.
  * Using [Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") will add 100% Bamboo Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [Field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [Smooth Dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [Loaded Dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% to 300% of Bamboo Field pollen for 15 minutes based on what type of dice is used, and also based on the current stack before the boost is added.
  * Bamboo [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Bamboo Field conversion and give +5% instant Bamboo Field conversion and +15% Bamboo Field pollen for each stack. Bamboo Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
  * It can be boosted by the [King Beetle Amulet](/wiki/King_Beetle_Amulet "King Beetle Amulet"). If it has a Bamboo Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +100%.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBamboo_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is one of six fields that [fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies") can travel to, with the others being [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"), Spider Field, and Strawberry Field.
  * The player can find 2 [royal jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") near the Bamboo Field, one in a nook under the ramp to the [Brave Bee Gate](/wiki/Brave_Bee_Gate "Brave Bee Gate"), and the other at the end of an [obstacle course](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses#Bamboo_Obby "Obstacle Courses"), starting in between this field and the Spider Field.
  * The bamboo stems have claw marks on them, presumably from Panda Bear.
  * If a player stands on the corner of the Bamboo Field closest to the Spider Field while a [Snowbear](/wiki/Snowbear "Snowbear") is summoned, the Snowbear can throw snowballs and damage the player.
  * The three bees that dislike the Bamboo Field also have another disliked field (Riley Bee and Precise Bee; Pine Tree Forest, Windy Bee; Strawberry Field)
