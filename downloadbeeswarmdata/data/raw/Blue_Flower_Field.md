The **Blue Flower Field** is one of the five [starting](/wiki/Starter_Zone "Starter Zone") [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") any player can visit upon starting the game. It is the first blue field accessible in the game. It is protected by a level 1 [rhino beetle](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"). 

On the north-east side of the field, a glass-like panel can be seen in the wall, with a blue flower leading into it. Making contact with it will teleport the player inside [King Beetle's Lair](/wiki/King_Beetle%27s_Lair "King Beetle's Lair"). 

You can find the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ") on the other side of the field. 

This field can spawn [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [blueberry](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"), [neonberry](/wiki/Neonberry "Neonberry"), [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [blue boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), [blue extract](/wiki/Blue_Extract "Blue Extract"), and [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean") tokens when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen").

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Blue Flower Field include [Bumble Bee](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee"), [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee"), [Bucko Bee](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee"), [Frosty Bee](/wiki/Frosty_Bee "Frosty Bee"), [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"), [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee"), [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee"), and [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"). Bees that dislike the Blue Flower Field include [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee"), [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"), and [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee"). 

This field grants Refreshing [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is planted here. 

  
This is a 17×43 field made up of 731 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are blue and white, in sizes of single and double.

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlue_Flower_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Blue Field Booster](/wiki/Blue_Field_Booster "Blue Field Booster") may give a Blue Flower [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +300% Blue Flower Field pollen for 15 minutes.
  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") will add 100% Blue Flower Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), and [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% - 300% Blue Flower Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Blue Flower [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Blue Flower Field conversion and gives +5% instant Blue Flower Field conversion and +25% Blue Flower Field pollen for each stack. Blue Flower Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
  * Redeeming certain valid codes: 
    * Luther (Activates Blue Flower Field Code Buff + other stuff).
  * It can be boosted by the [King Beetle Amulet](/wiki/King_Beetle_Amulet "King Beetle Amulet"). If it has a Blue Flower Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +100%.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlue_Flower_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This field is adjacent to the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ") and [King Beetle's Lair](/wiki/King_Beetle%27s_Lair "King Beetle's Lair").
  * Sometimes, the giant [Bubble Wand](/wiki/Bubble_Wand "Bubble Wand") model on the Blue HQ gets a face and will blow bubbles towards this field, along with the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), and [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"), to produce [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles").
  * In the early days of the game, the decorative flowers in this field used to face upwards. This was changed since rhino beetles would get stuck behind the flowers, unable to attack. 
    * However, you can still see the decorative flowers facing upwards in the tutorial.
  * According to [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear"), this field used to be the "Blueberry Field." 
    * According to Onett, the reason why it isn’t Blueberry Field is because the blueberry 3D texture he finds/makes are too close to being a "Blue ball".[1]
