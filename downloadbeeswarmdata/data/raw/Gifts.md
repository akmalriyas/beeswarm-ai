_Not to be confused with[Presents](/wiki/Present "Present") and [Ornament Presents](/wiki/Ornament_Presents "Ornament Presents")._

**Gift Boxes** are presents that appear during each [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") update, which are located around the map. To open them, the player needs to help a certain number of [NPCs](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC") complete their [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas") decorations by completing their [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests"). There are 13 gift boxes this year. 

  * 2025

  * 2024 (Winter)

  * 2024 (Summer)

  * 2022

  * 2021

  * 2020

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGift_Boxes%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Originally, the Mondo Gift Box required 12 NPCs. 
    * When the second update had arrived, it changed to 14 NPCs.
    * In Beesmas 2021, the Mondo Gift Box required 14 NPCs, then due to the occurrence of [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear") being added, it changed to 15 NPCs. Everything that required 6-11 NPCs were made to require 1 more than before, and the Festive Gift Box was added, requiring 13 NPCs. This happened after the Midnight Gift Box was added.
    * In Beesmas 2022, the requirements of every gift box that required above 7 NPCs had their requirement increase by 1. This happened after Chestnut Gift Box was added in part 2 of Beesmas, where Spirit Bear, Gummy Bear, Bubble Bee Man, Robo Bear, and [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") got new Beesmas quests.
  * The Midnight Gift Box and the Chestnut Gift Box were the only gift boxes to be added in the middle of Beesmas. 
    * The Midnight Gift Box was the first to accomplish this.
  * The Mondo Gift Box was the last gift box to get removed from the game in Beesmas 2020, because [B.B.M's](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") quest was extended by 1 week.
  * The Stellar Gift Box (Beesmas 2025, 6th present) is currently the only Gift Box to have a unique shape outside of stripes.
  * The Blinking Gift Box (Beesmas 2025, 8th present) is the only one to have an animation.
  * It was **not** necessary to have 35 bees to access the Pristine, Restful, and Glass Gift Boxes. Instead, the player could go to the roof of the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"), where they could jump onto the ledge with the box. However, taking just a few steps beyond that would bring them back to the spawn point.
  * There were 10 gift boxes in total during Beesmas 2020, 12 gift boxes in 2021 and 2022, and there are 13 gift boxes this year. There has never been a gift box for helping one NPC.. 
    * Up to the 2024-12-25 update, there was never a gift box for helping two NPCs, until the Elite Gift Box was changed from 3 NPCs to 2 NPCs. 
      * Additionally, the Elite Gift Box was originally by the ladder to [Panda Bear's](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear") platform, which makes the Elite Gift Box the most modified gift box.
