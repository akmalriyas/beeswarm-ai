## Strategies[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FParty_Mechsquito%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Since they are essentially the same as their normal counterparts, Mechsquitos, the same methods used for them can be followed. 

  * Party Mechsquitos aim for where the player is, not where the player will be. Because of this, moving around in circles will often result in Party Mechsquitos missing their attack.
  * Stand near the Party Mechsquito, and this will allow your bees to lock on to the Party Mechsquito and quickly deal damage.
  * [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") provide excellent crowd control, especially since Party Mechsquitos often come in pairs or packs. [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") can also stun many Party Mechsquitos at once and allow them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mind_Hack "Ability Tokens").
  * Unlike their normal counterparts, they stay in fields which can be used for the players advantage. Stand right outside the field they are in and your bees will attack it while it doesn't aim for you. Note that this can be a bit risky since they can hover around the other parts of the field and don't come close enough for your bees to target them.
