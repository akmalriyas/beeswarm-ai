**[Onett's Testing Group](https://www.roblox.com/groups/5211428)** is a Roblox group with 66K+ members. Joining it grants access to the **[Test Realm](https://www.roblox.com/games/4079902982)** , a ROBLOX place where [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") tests updates before releasing them to the [main game](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"). 

You can not freely join the group, however. You must send a join request and wait for Onett or any other group admin (in this case, zlib) to accept your request for admission. This group is notorious for being very hard to get into, and people are randomly accepted at unannounced times (usually during periods when the Test Realm is open).
