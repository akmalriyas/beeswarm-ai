Aged Gingerbread Bear

[![Gingerbread Bear](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ae/Gingerbread_Bear.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041818)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ae/Gingerbread_Bear.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041818)

_"This cookie has aged an entire year or more! Gives 500 bond to the fed bee and has a 1.1% chance to turn that bee Gifted."_

> Not to be confused with ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear").

An **Aged Gingerbread Bear** is a type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that was added in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"). When fed to a bee, it raises its [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") by 500 and has a 1.1% chance of turning the bee gifted. Unused ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") would transform into aged gingerbread bears after the next [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") has started. These cannot be used to purchase items from [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog"). 

The formula for the chance of turning a bee gifted is as follows:  ( [ 1 − 0.989 x ] ∗ 100 ) % {\displaystyle ([1 - 0.989^x]* 100)\%} ![{\\displaystyle \(\[1-0.989^{x}\]*100\)\\%}](https://services.fandom.com/mathoid-facade/v1/media/math/render/svg/945bf8493a17abfa53a950165e8e071c385c784b), where x is the number of ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Aged Gingerbread Bears** fed to a bee. 

## Ways to Obtain[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAged_Gingerbread_Bear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The only way to obtain Aged Gingerbread Bears is by saving ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") until the start of the next Beesmas.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAged_Gingerbread_Bear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This, along with ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") and ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treats](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), are the only items that can turn [Event bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") gifted (as they lack favorite treats.)
  * ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Aged Gingerbread Bears** do not apply for quests that ask for the player to feed ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") to their bees, such as [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") "Snow Cub Reformation (2/5)" quest.
  * If you got the free Gingerbread Bear from the Retro Swarm Challenge before Winter Beesmas 2024, even if it wasn’t a year before, when Beesmas started, that Gingerbread Bear immediately turned into an Aged one.
  * Feeding 100 Aged Gingerbread Bears to a bee does not guarantee to turn it gifted. The chance of it becoming gifted is about 66.9%.
