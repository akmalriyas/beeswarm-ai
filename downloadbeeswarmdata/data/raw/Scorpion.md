The **Scorpion** is a [mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") that spawns in the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). 

It takes 20 minutes for a scorpion to respawn after being defeated (17 minutes with [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee")). Defeating it guarantees 5 [Battle Points](/wiki/Battle_Points "Battle Points") and 1000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"). Various other rewards are possible with their probability increasing with [Loot Luck](/wiki/Loot_Luck "Loot Luck"). It deals 25 damage to the player (before accounting [Defense](/wiki/System_Page#Defense "System Page")).
