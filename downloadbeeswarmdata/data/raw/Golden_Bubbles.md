# Golden Bubbles

**Bubbles** are objects that can be produced by certain [tools](/wiki/Tools "Tools"), [abilities](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), or [passive abilities](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities"). Bubbles have a percentage chance to be spawned by certain bees when gathering. Bubbles spawn on the flower that the bee gathered and last for 4 seconds. When popped, they collect 4 red, 6 white, and 8 blue pollen from 29 surrounding flowers (+15% per gifted blue bee type, +5% per bee level) and cause nearby flowers to grow back to full. 

Bubbles can be generated by [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee"), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), [frogs](/wiki/Frog "Frog"), the [Bubble Wand](/wiki/Bubble_Wand "Bubble Wand"), [Bubble Mask](/wiki/Bubble_Mask "Bubble Mask") and [Diamond Mask's](/wiki/Diamond_Mask "Diamond Mask") Bubble Bombs passive, and the [Pop Star](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities") passive. 

Bubbles can be popped by other players, petal shurikens, [coconuts](/wiki/Coconut "Coconut"), tornadoes, [Star Saw](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities"), [Fuzz Bombs](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Fuzz_Bombs "Ability Tokens"), the [Dark Scythe](/wiki/Dark_Scythe "Dark Scythe")'s trail, [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee")'s Fetch and the [Tide Popper](/wiki/Tide_Popper "Tide Popper")'s waves. 

Popping bubbles helps to grow Pop Stars and will increase [Bubble Bloat](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Passives "Buffs & Debuffs"). The bigger the Pop Star grows, the more bubbles it will spawn. 

If a player pops a bubble belonging to another player, the pollen collected by the bubble will be granted to the owner of the bubble. This also applies to Pop Star growth.

## Gathering Bubbles[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBubble%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The **Gathering Bubbles** passive gives a chance for a bee to spawn a bubble while gathering pollen from a field. Gathering Bubbles+ gives a higher chance for a bee to spawn a bubble. 

There is a 25% chance (50% if gifted) for Bubble Bees' Gathering Bubbles passive to spawn a bubble while gathering, and a 65% (85% if gifted) chance for Tadpole Bees' Gathering Bubbles+ passive to spawn a bubble while gathering. 

A [Bead Lizard](/wiki/Bead_Lizard "Bead Lizard") gives from +10% Gathering Bubbles chance to +12% Gathering Bubbles chance for an individual bee. 

## Bubble Pollen[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBubble%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

[![The Bubble Pollen stat in the System Page.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/dc/BubblePollenStat1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230701234719) [![The Bubble Pollen stat in the System Page.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/dc/BubblePollenStat1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230701234719)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/dc/BubblePollenStat1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230701234719) [](/wiki/File:BubblePollenStat1.png)

The Bubble Pollen stat in the System Page.

**Bubble Pollen** is the amount of pollen that bubbles can collect. The player can check how much bubble pollen they have in the system page. 

Bubble Pollen can be increased by a few ways. 

  * The [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask "Diamond Mask") gives a x1.5 Bubble Pollen multiplier.
  * The [Bubble Mask](/wiki/Bubble_Mask "Bubble Mask") gives a 1.25x Bubble Pollen multiplier.
  * [Gifted Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee") gives a x1.25 Bubble Pollen multiplier.
  * [Beequips](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"): 
    * A Bead Lizard can give +1% Bubble Pollen as a hive bonus from waxing.
    * A [Bubble Light](/wiki/Bubble_Light "Bubble Light") gives from +15% Bubble Pollen to +30% Bubble Pollen for an individual bee. Using [waxes](/wiki/Waxes "Waxes") can increase the amount up to +60% Bubble Pollen.
    * A Bubble Light can give +1% Bubble Pollen as a hive bonus. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +11% Bubble Pollen.
    * A Camphor Lip Balm can give 15% Bubble pollen hive bonus. Using waxes can increase the amount up to 25% Bubble pollen.
  * [Cog Amulets](/wiki/Cog_Amulet "Cog Amulet"): 
    * The Bronze Cog Amulet can give from +3% Bubble Pollen up to +5% Bubble Pollen.
    * The Silver Cog Amulet can give from +5% Bubble Pollen up to +6% Bubble Pollen.
    * The Gold Cog Amulet can give from +8% Bubble Pollen up to +9% Bubble Pollen.
    * The Diamond Cog Amulet can give extra Bubble Pollen.
    * The Supreme Cog Amulet can give from +15% Bubble Pollen up to +20% Bubble Pollen.
  * [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"): 
    * Epic Upgrades: 
      * Pop-Up: This upgrade gives x2 Bubble Pollen.
      * RGB: This upgrade gives x1.25 Bubble Pollen.



## Improving Bubbles[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBubble%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Aside from increasing the amount of pollen that bubbles can collect, the player can also improve bubbles a few other ways. 

### Golden Bubbles[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBubble%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

A **Golden Bubble** is a special variant of a bubble that can only occur when a [golden balloon](/wiki/Balloon#Golden_Balloon "Balloon") floats over a normal bubble or it can be summoned by a gifted frog. 

When a golden bubble is popped, it collects +100% more pollen than a normal bubble per field boost stack, contributes 50% more time to Pop Star's bubble bloat than a normal bubble, has 100% [Instant Conversion](/wiki/Instant_Conversion "Instant Conversion") (unless going into a balloon), and has a 10% chance to spawn a honey token. 

The only way to increase the Golden Bubble pollen besides [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost") is with Camphor Lip Balm. 

### Instant Bubble Conversion[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBubble%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

[![The Instant Bubble Conversion stat in the System Page.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ab/InstantBubbleConversionStat1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230702192939) [![The Instant Bubble Conversion stat in the System Page.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ab/InstantBubbleConversionStat1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230702192939)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ab/InstantBubbleConversionStat1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230702192939) [](/wiki/File:InstantBubbleConversionStat1.png)

The Instant Bubble Conversion stat in the System Page.

**Instant Bubble Conversion** is how much pollen collected by the bubbles is instantly converted. There are currently no ways to increase Instant Bubble Conversion. 

### Bubble Duration[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBubble%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Bubble Duration** affects how long bubbles last before they disappear. The base duration is 4 seconds and can be increased to 5 seconds. There is currently only one way to increase Bubble Duration. Note that bubbles spawned from Bubbles Bombs last 8 seconds (10 seconds if one has a Gifted Tadpole bee). 

  * Gifted Tadpole bee grants +25% Bubble Duration



### Other Improvements[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBubble%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Certain upgrades from the Robo Bear Challenge can be used to improve bubbles. 

  * Epic Upgrades: 
    * Invert: This upgrade makes bubbles collect x4 pollen from red flowers.
    * Pop-Up: This upgrade gives x1.5 Bubble Lifespan.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBubble%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The maximum Bubble Pollen a player can have at a time is 925%. This can be achieved by having the Diamond Mask (x2), a Bead Lizard (+1%), three Bubble Lights (+33%), a camphor lip balm (+25%), a Supreme Cog Amulet (+20%), Gifted Bubble Bee (x1.25), activating the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack") with certain [stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") (+6%), Pop-Up upgrade (x2), RGB upgrade (x1.25).
  * Popping a bubble has a 1/1,000,000 chance to spawn a Tadpole sticker.
  * Bubbles are the counterpart of [flames](/wiki/Flame "Flame").
