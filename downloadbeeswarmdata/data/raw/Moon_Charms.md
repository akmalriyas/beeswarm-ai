# Moon Charms

Moon Charm

[![Moon Charm](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b6/Moon_Charm.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041630)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b6/Moon_Charm.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041630)

_"Increases your bond with a bee by 250."_

[![The two types of moon charm tokens: On the left, there is a token that is not glowing, dropped by mobs or mythic meteors. On the right, there is a glowing variant that is dropped during nighttime from fireflies, sparkles, and moon sprouts.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/20/RobloxScreenShot20190224_131610641_%282%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20190224122257)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/20/RobloxScreenShot20190224_131610641_%282%29.png/revision/latest?cb=20190224122257) [](/wiki/File:RobloxScreenShot20190224_131610641_\(2\).png)

The two types of moon charm tokens: On the left, there is a token that is not glowing, dropped by mobs or mythic meteors. On the right, there is a glowing variant that is dropped during nighttime from fireflies, sparkles, and moon sprouts.

A **Moon Charm** , when fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), raises its [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") by 250. Moon Charms are also used in generating [Moon Amulets](/wiki/Moon_Amulet "Moon Amulet") and they are used in crafting of [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") and the [Helmet](/wiki/Helmet "Helmet"). If fed to a [radioactive](/wiki/Radiation "Radiation") bee, it has a 1/2,500 (0.04%) chance to give that bee a [mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation"). 

Moon charm tokens last for 10 seconds before disappearing.
