# Flames

> _Not to be confused with the flame trail produced by[Fire Ants](/wiki/Fire_Ant "Fire Ant")._

**Flames** are objects that can be produced by certain [tools](/wiki/Tools "Tools"), [ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), or [passive abilities](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities"). Flames have a percentage chance to be spawned by certain bees when gathering, lasting for 4 seconds. Each second, the flames collect 5 red, 3 white, or 2 blue [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") from 9 surrounding flowers (+2% per red bee and +4% per gifted red bee if spawned by the player, +10% per bee level if spawned by a bee), deals 10 + (25% of [base Bee Attack](/wiki/Bee_Attack "Bee Attack")) damage per second to any mob (15 if spawned by the player), and grants the player [flame heat](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Passives "Buffs & Debuffs"). 

Flames can be generated by the [Scythe](/wiki/Scythe "Scythe"), [Fire Bee](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee"), [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee"), [Spicy Bee's](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee") [Inferno](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inferno "Ability Tokens"), bees with the ![Bead Lizard](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Bead Lizard](/wiki/Bead_Lizard "Bead Lizard") or ![Electric Candle](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Electric Candle](/wiki/Electric_Candle "Electric Candle") beequips, [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask "Fire Mask")'s Ignite passive, and [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask")'s X-Flame passive. 

Flames are used to help grow Scorching Stars. 

## Flame Heat[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlame%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Flame Heat** grants extra Red Pollen and Bee Attack starting at x1.2 [Red Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Red_Pollen "System Page") and +5% [Bee Attack](/wiki/Bee_Attack "Bee Attack") Multiplier. The more flames around the player, the faster the Flame Heat grows up to a maximum of 20 seconds at x2 Red Pollen and +25% Bee Attack Multiplier. Flame Heat boosts [Spicy Bee's](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee") Movespeed and gather speed and can also be consumed by [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") to convert Pollen into Honey. 

## Gathering Flames[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlame%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The **Gathering Flames** passive gives a chance for a bee to spawn a flame while gathering pollen from a field. Gathering Flames+ gives a higher chance for a bee to spawn a flame. 

There is a 35% chance (50% if gifted) for Fire Bees' Gathering Flames passive to spawn a flame while gathering, and a 65% (85% if gifted) chance for Demon Bees' Gathering Flames+ to spawn a flame while gathering. 

The Gathering Flames passive chance can be increased with the ![Bead Lizard](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Bead Lizard](/wiki/Bead_Lizard "Bead Lizard") or ![Electric Candle](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Electric Candle](/wiki/Electric_Candle "Electric Candle") beequip for individual bees. 

  * A ![Bead Lizard](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Bead Lizard](/wiki/Bead_Lizard "Bead Lizard") gives from +9% Gathering Flames chance to +14% Gathering Flames chance.
  * An ![Electric Candle](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Electric Candle](/wiki/Electric_Candle "Electric Candle") gives from +1% Gathering Flames chance to +5% Gathering Flames chance. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +25% Gathering Flames chance.



## Dark Flames[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlame%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Flames that are hit with [Dark Scythe](/wiki/Dark_Scythe "Dark Scythe") will be converted into **dark flames** , a dark violet version of normal flames which collects pollen from 13 surrounding flowers (instead of 9), deals 50% more damage, and grants dark heat and also normal flame heat. Dark flames also grant 33% more growth to Scorching Stars. 

## Flame Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlame%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

There are multiple aspects of flames that can be improved by stats and modifiers. 

### Flame Pollen[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlame%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Flame Pollen** is the amount of pollen that flames can collect. The player can check how much flame pollen they have in the system page. 

[![The Flame Pollen stat in the System Page.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d4/FlamePollenStat1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230701230202) [![The Flame Pollen stat in the System Page.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d4/FlamePollenStat1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230701230202)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d4/FlamePollenStat1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230701230202) [](/wiki/File:FlamePollenStat1.png)

The Flame Pollen stat in the System Page.

Flame Pollen can be increased in a few ways: 

  * The Demon Mask gives a x1.25 Flame Pollen multiplier.
  * Gifted Fire Bee gives a x1.25 Flame Pollen multiplier.
  * [Beequips](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"): 
    * An ![Electric Candle](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Electric Candle](/wiki/Electric_Candle "Electric Candle") gives from +5% Flame Pollen to +10% Flame Pollen for an individual bee. Using [waxes](/wiki/Waxes "Waxes") can increase the amount up to 50% Flame Pollen.
    * An ![Electric Candle](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Electric Candle](/wiki/Electric_Candle "Electric Candle") can give +1% Flame Pollen as a hive bonus. Using waxes can increase the amount up to 11% Flame Pollen.
  * [Cog Amulets](/wiki/Cog_Amulet "Cog Amulet"): 
    * The Silver Cog Amulet can give up to +5% Flame Pollen.
    * The Gold Cog Amulet can give up to +8% Flame Pollen.
    * The Diamond Cog Amulet can give from +12% Flame Pollen up to +14% Flame Pollen.
    * The Supreme Cog Amulet can give from +15% Flame Pollen up to +20% Flame Pollen.
  * [Stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker"): 
    * The Small Flame Bee Sticker gives +3% Flame Pollen.
    * The Dark Flame Sticker gives +3% Flame Pollen.
    * The Orange Swirled Marble Sticker gives +3% Flame Pollen.
  * [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"): 
    * Epic Upgrades: 
      * Furnace: This upgrade gives x1.5 Flame Pollen.



### Flame Duration[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlame%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Flame Duration** is how long flames will last. Flame Duration can be increased by Spicy Bee's gifted bonus (25% more time) and [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") Spicy Bee's [Flame Fuel](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Flame_Fuel "Ability Tokens") ability (50% more time). In addition, Flame Fuel causes the flames to instantly convert 50,000 pollen plus 10% (+5% per Field Boost) of the hive's total convert amount. 

### Instant Flame Conversion[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlame%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

[![The Instant Flame Conversion stat in the System Page.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a6/InstantFlameConversionStat1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230702192408) [![The Instant Flame Conversion stat in the System Page.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a6/InstantFlameConversionStat1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230702192408)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a6/InstantFlameConversionStat1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230702192408) [](/wiki/File:InstantFlameConversionStat1.png)

The Instant Flame Conversion stat in the System Page.

**Instant Flame Conversion** is how much pollen collected by flames are instantly converted. It can be increased by a few ways. 

  * The Fire Mask gives +50% Instant Flame Conversion.
  * The Demon Mask gives +50% Instant Flame Conversion.
  * A Scorching Star gives +50% Instant Flame Conversion.



### Other Improvements[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlame%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Certain upgrades from the Robo Bear Challenge can be used to improve flames. 

  * Epic Upgrades: 
    * Furnace: This upgrade gives x1.5 Flame Duration and x1.5 Flame Damage.
    * Invert: This upgrade makes flames collect x4 pollen from blue flowers.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlame%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The maximum Flame Pollen a player can have is 380%. This can be achieved by having the Demon Mask (x1.25), Gifted Fire Bee (x1.25), a Supreme Cog Amulet (+20%), three ![Electric Candle](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Electric Candles](/wiki/Electric_Candle "Electric Candle") (+33%), activating the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack") with certain stickers (+9%), and the Furnace upgrade (x1.5). 
    * The exact amount is 379.6875% but it rounds up to 380%.
  * The maximum Instant Flame Conversion a player can have is 100%. This can be achieved by having the Fire Mask (+50%) or the Demon Mask (+50%) while having a Scorching Star (+50%).
  * The texture for flames were changed in the 2021-12-26 update. This was probably to help with the texture for dark flames.
  * Flames generated by another player's bees will appear dark red instead of yellow-orange.
  * Flames are the counterpart of [bubbles](/wiki/Bubble "Bubble").
