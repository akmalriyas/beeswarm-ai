The **Honey Bee Gate** , also called the **15 Bee Gate** , is a [gate](/wiki/Category:Gates "Category:Gates") that requires 15 [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to be able to pass through. It is the third gate that the player should be able to access, ahead of the [Brave Bee Gate](/wiki/Brave_Bee_Gate "Brave Bee Gate") and the [Basic Bee Gate](/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate "Basic Bee Gate"). 

There are 4 different fields behind the Honey Bee Gate, being the [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"), the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"), and the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). 

It is given its name from the large [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee") located on top of it. 

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney_Bee_Gate%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This gate has the highest number of [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") behind it, which is four.
  * There were numerous ways to bypass the gate without 15 bees, but as of the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), it will always teleport the player back to spawn.
  * It has the second-most quest givers, at 4, with the Starter Zone beating it with 5.
  * This gate holds the ramp that leads to the [Lion Bee Gate](/wiki/Lion_Bee_Gate "Lion Bee Gate"), also known as the the 25 Bee Gate.
  * This gate has the most assets in it, with 19 of them.
