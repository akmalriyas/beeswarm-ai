A **Golden Cogmower** is a [mob](/wiki/Mob "Mob") that appears during [Robo Bear's Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"), known for its distinct golden and bronze accents, which separates itself from its much more common counterpart, the [Cogmower](/wiki/Cogmower "Cogmower"). They are the _rarest mob type_ that appears in Robo Bear Challenge, only spawning a total of 3 times: 

  * once on either rounds 3, 4, or 6;
  * once again on either rounds 12, 14, or 16;
  * and once more on either rounds 21, or 22.



They can appear only on 4 following fields: 

  * [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field")
  * [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch")
  * [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch")
  * [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field")



They do not despawn until the round is over. Unlike regular Cogmowers, Golden Cogmowers will not follow the player if they go to another field, they stay in the random field they spawned in. 

Golden Cogmowers rise up from the field before slowly moving around the field, draining flowers they pass over. The higher the level of the Golden Cogmower, the more pollen is drained. Golden Cogmowers bounce off of the edges of the field. They also only take half as much damage from [Critical Hits](/wiki/Critical_Hits "Critical Hits"). 

A Golden Cogmower's level can range from 6-22, depending on what round of Robo Bear Challenge the player is on.

## Strategies[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGolden_Cogmower%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Golden Cogmowers don't aim for the player, allowing players to easily sidestep them.
  * Stand near the Golden Cogmower, this will allow your bees to lock on to the Golden Cogmower and quickly deal damage.
  * [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") provides very strong single-target damage. [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") can also stun the Golden Cogmower in place and allow it to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mind_Hack "Ability Tokens") (this still allows the Golden Cogmower to take half the damage from critical hits).
  * After starting a round, quickly use the [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon") to check all fields for a Golden Cogmower. The cogs it drops can help you afford 1 or 2 extra upgrades during the challenge.
