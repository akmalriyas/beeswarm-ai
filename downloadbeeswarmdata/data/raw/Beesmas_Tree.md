# Beesmas Tree

_Not to be confused with[Beesmas Tree Hat](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree_Hat "Beesmas Tree Hat"), a [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") with a similar name._

[![The Beesmas Tree.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ad/Beesmastree.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20191222234856)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ad/Beesmastree.png/revision/latest?cb=20191222234856) [](/wiki/File:Beesmastree.png)

The Beesmas Tree.

The **Beesmas Tree** is a feature that appeared during Beesmas 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024 and 2025. The Beesmas Tree allows the player to view their collected [ornaments](/wiki/Ornaments "Ornaments"), who they are from, and what boosts they give. The tree is located in front of the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate"), near the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop"), and next to the hives. 

## Beesmas 2025[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBeesmas_Tree%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The Beesmas Tree made another return in Beesmas 2025, functioning the exact same way as during Winter Beesmas 2024. Conducting a gift exchange with an NPC would grant players an ornament that would grant special boosts on the Beesmas Tree. 

The bottom row holds five ornaments. They are given, from left to right, from [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear"), [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear"), [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear"), [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear"), and [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear"). The second row of the tree holds another five ornaments. They are, from left to right, from [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear"), [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear"), [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett"), [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear"), and [Robo Bear](/wiki/Robo_Bear "Robo Bear"). The third row of the tree holds four ornaments. They are, from left to right, from [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee"), [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee"), and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug"). The ornaments on the 4th row of the Beesmas Tree hold two ornaments. They are, from left to right, Honey Bee and Gummy Bear. The ornament on the top of the tree is [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man")'s ornament. 

Hidden at the bottom of the tree, there is ![Whirligig](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5f/Whirligig.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404031705)1 [Whirligig](/wiki/Whirligig "Whirligig") along with ![Bitterberry](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/af/Bitterberry.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404041641)5 [Bitterberries](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"). On top of the trunk of the Beesmas Tree, there is ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") token.

The Beesmas Tree returned in 2020, and it functioned the same. To put ornaments on the tree the player needed to collect and give [presents](/wiki/Present "Present") to each corresponding [NPC](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC"). In exchange, the player would receive their ornament along with a few special boosts.

To put ornaments on the tree, the player had to complete the [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests") of the corresponding [NPC](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC"), then talk to them. The ornament would then automatically go onto the tree.

They were given, in order from left to right, from [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear"), [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear"), [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear"), and [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear"). The second row of the tree held another four ornaments. They were, from left to right, from [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear"), [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear"), [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett"), and [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear").
