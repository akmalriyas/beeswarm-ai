**Carpenter Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Legendary bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"). 

Carpenter Bee's favorite treat is ![Sunflower Seed](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"). 

Carpenter Bee likes the [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"), and [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). It dislikes the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCarpenter_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 3 seconds.
  * Makes 120 [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * -20% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +25% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), +25% gather speed, +40 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +4 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.25 [Tool Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Tool_Pollen "System Page").



## Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCarpenter_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * [**[Honey Mark+]**](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mark "Ability Tokens") Marks a large random area on the [Field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") for 8 seconds (+0.25s per level) that grants 2 Conversion Links and x1.25 [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page") while you stand on it. Stacks up to 3 times.
  * **[[Pollen Mark]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mark "Ability Tokens")** Marks a random area on the field for 7 seconds (+0.2s per level) that increases all pollen up to 50% while you stand in it. Stacks up to 3 times.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCarpenter_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Just like [Music Bee](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee"), this bee originated from Number1Riker's Bee Swarm showcase. 
    * Carpenter Bee and Music Bee are also the only Legendary bees added in an [update](/wiki/Updates "Updates").
  * This bee uses the wood texture and has a hammer mesh in its body.
  * This bee, [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee"), and [Vector Bee](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee") are the only non-[Event bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") that have special body parts (hammer mesh).
  * This is one of two bees added in the [2018-11-25 Update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"), along with [Stubborn Bee](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee").
  * One reason Carpenter Bee likes the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest") might be because pine trees are made of wood. 
    * One reason Carpenter Bee dislikes the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field") might be because on the tops of tall mountains, there are no trees.
  * Carpenter Bee, along with [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee") and [Bumble Bee](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee"), are the only bees named after their real-life counterparts.
  * Carpenter Bee is the only bee that can generate the Honey Mark+ ability token.
  * This bee was one of the bees to have a specific jelly in the [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog"). The jelly was given as part of the Pinecone Bundle.
  * This bee is the only Legendary bee who can't find a [sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") while gathering.
