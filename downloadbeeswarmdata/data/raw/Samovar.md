# Samovar

[![The Samovar when completed.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/77/Completedsamovar.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/238?cb=20230128015918)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/77/Completedsamovar.png/revision/latest?cb=20230128015918) [](/wiki/File:Completedsamovar.png)

The Samovar when completed.

The **Samovar** is a [machine](/wiki/Category:Machines "Category:Machines") that was added in [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") 2021 and has returned in Beesmas 2022, 2024, and 2025. This machine can be unlocked by completing [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear")'s Beesmas quest, and when activated, it rewards a random [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") type and [items](/wiki/Items "Items") near the cup. The Samovar has a cooldown of 6 hours. 

If a player stands on the activation platform with Samovar without completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas 2021/2022 quest, the following message will appear:  This strange thing is looking a little rusty...

When a player uses the Samovar, the following messages appear **(where _< Nth>_ refers to the number of times you've used the Samovar previously)**:   
This is your <Nth> time using the Samovar.  
Each time you use it, the brew gets stronger...  


## Drops[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSamovar%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_It is important to note that despite what is said, the Samovar lists how many times it was used**previously** , not how many uses it is on currently.  
_ For example: If the message says you are on the 24th use, then you are actually on the 25th use. 

Every time the Samovar is used, the amount of Nectar received will increase by 5 minutes, capping at 4 hours and affected by nectar multipliers. ![Honeysuckle](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honeysuckles](/wiki/Honeysuckle "Honeysuckle") increases by 1 each use, stopping at ![Honeysuckle](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)30 [Honeysuckles](/wiki/Honeysuckle "Honeysuckle"). 

The Samovar has a fixed cycle of rewards: ![Oil](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Oil](/wiki/Oil "Oil") -> ![Enzymes](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes") -> ![Gumdrops](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") -> ![Glitter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") -> ![Ticket Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Ticket Planter](/wiki/Ticket_Planter "Ticket Planter"). 

On the 25th use, a ![Turpentine](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Turpentine](/wiki/Turpentine "Turpentine") is rewarded instead.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSamovar%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This and the [Robo Party Cake](/wiki/Robo_Party_Cake "Robo Party Cake") are currently the only Beesmas decorations not added in the Beesmas 2020 event.
  * It would take 36 uses to get the max amount of nectar from this machine, and therefore 216 hours of cooldown, or 9 days.
  * The nectar collected from the Samovar will contribute to quests requiring a specific type of nectar.
