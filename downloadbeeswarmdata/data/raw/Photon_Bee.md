**Photon Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") that hatches out of a ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Photon Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Photon_Bee_Egg "Egg"), which is available in the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") for ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)500 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"). 

Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, the only way to make Photon Bee gifted is either with a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

Photon Bee likes the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch") and the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"). It dislikes the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPhoton_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 20 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 2 seconds.
  * Makes 240 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 2 seconds.
  * +50% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +50% gather and convert speed, +10 [Gather Amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats"), +160 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +20% [Instant Conversion](/wiki/Instant_Conversion "Instant Conversion"), +3 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats"). Doesn't require [Sleep](/wiki/Energy "Energy").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +5% [Instant Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Instant_Conversion "System Page").



## Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPhoton_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Beamstorm]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Beamstorm "Ability Tokens")** Fires 25 (+2 per lvl) yellow (white if gifted) beams from the sky which collect and double ALL pollen from [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") they hit. If Gifted, Beamstorm collects x1.5 more white pollen for a total of x3 white pollen and ALL pollen is instantly converted.
  * **[[Haste]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens")** Grants +10% [Player Movespeed](/wiki/System_Page#Movespeed "System Page") for 20 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPhoton_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Photon Bee's energy is set to 999,999,999,999,999.
  * It was the first Event bee to be added in the Ticket Tent and the second Event bee added to the game, with [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee") being the first. 
    * It was also the first Event bee that is purchased using tickets.
  * This bee and the [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee") are tied as the fastest [Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in the game, with a speed of 21.
  * Photon and Exhausted Bee are the only bees that don't require sleep and have infinite energy. However, they still need to rest at the hive when you die.
  * This bee, [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") and [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") are the only Event bees that have [Ability Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") generated by non-Event bees.
  * This bee, Ninja Bee, [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee"), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"), [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee"), and [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") are the only bees that create a trail behind them (not counting particle trails).
  * This is the first event bee with a trail. 
    * [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") also creates a trail, but it can only be seen while the [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask") ability is active.
  * This bee, Bear Bee, Festive Bee, Gummy Bee, [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee") and [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee") are the only bees to have a [Gifted Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee#List_of_Hive_Bonuses "Gifted Bee") that affects their signature ability. 
    * If Photon Bee is gifted, its Beamstorm is white instead of yellow and instantly converts its collected pollen.
  * The sun model on Photon Bee's rear has 20 spikes.
  * Because Photon Bee has infinite energy, there is a [glitch](/wiki/Glitches "Glitches") if a [Royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") is used on a Photon Bee, the transformed bee will have a ridiculous amount of energy. 
    * This glitch is also shared when transforming an Exhausted Bee, since that has endless energy as well.
  * All versions of the Ticket Tent have had this bee on top, except for the initial release.
  * Although Photon Bee's energy is infinite, it is still possible to get an energy [mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation") on Photon Bee. However, the mutation will have no effect, similar to an attack mutation on a [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee").
  * Photon Bee's Beamstorm ability is able to collect pollen from other fields. If the token was close enough to the other field, the range of the Beamstorm can possibly be able to reach it.
