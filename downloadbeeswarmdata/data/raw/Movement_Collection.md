# Movement Collection

[![A moon amulet with the movement collection buff.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f8/Moon_amulet_movement_collection.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210216003226)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f8/Moon_amulet_movement_collection.png/revision/latest?cb=20210216003226) [](/wiki/File:Moon_amulet_movement_collection.png)

A moon amulet with the movement collection buff.

**Movement Collection** is a type of boost that allows the player to collect [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") when walking on [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The higher the movement collection, the greater amount of pollen the player collects from walking on flowers. This boost also collects [goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo") if the player walks on any goo patches. 

## Ways to Increase[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMovement_Collection%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The player can get this boost from wearing [boots](/wiki/Category:Boots "Category:Boots") or from a [Moon Amulet](/wiki/Moon_Amulet "Moon Amulet"). 

  * The Moon Amulet can give from +1 Movement Collection to +3 Movement Collection.
  * Boots: 
    * The [Basic Boots](/wiki/Basic_Boots "Basic Boots") give +2 Movement Collection.
    * The [Hiking Boots](/wiki/Hiking_Boots "Hiking Boots") give +6 Movement Collection.
    * The [Beekeeper's Boots](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Boots "Beekeeper's Boots") give +10 Movement Collection.
    * The [Coconut Clogs](/wiki/Coconut_Clogs "Coconut Clogs") give +14 Movement Collection.
    * The [Gummy Boots](/wiki/Gummy_Boots "Gummy Boots") give +16 Movement Collection.



## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMovement_Collection%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Movement Collection isn't shown in the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page"), unlike most other boosts, such as [Loot Luck](/wiki/Loot_Luck "Loot Luck") and [Instant Conversion](/wiki/Instant_Conversion "Instant Conversion").
  * If the player has movement collection on their Moon Amulet, they can still collect pollen even without boots equipped.
  * The maximum Movement Collection a player can have is 19. This can be achieved by having a Gummy Boots (+16) and a Moon Amulet (+3).
