**Pink Shades** are a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 10 [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [Basic](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"), [Rad](/wiki/Rad_Bee "Rad Bee"), [Bomber](/wiki/Bomber_Bee "Bomber Bee"), [Demo](/wiki/Demo_Bee "Demo Bee"), [Honey](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee"), and [Shy](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee") [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), but they must be [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee").
