The **Stick Bug Amulet** is an [amulet](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet") that can be obtained from the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge"). In order to obtain these amulets, the player will need to get [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") to a certain level as well as reach a certain score during the Stick Bug Challenge. 

The amulets grant buffs to the owner and always increase [capacity](/wiki/Capacity "Capacity"), [convert rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page") and [Tool Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Tool_Pollen "System Page") as they go up a tier. Higher tier amulets grant more and better buffs. 

The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the player's score, up to 200 million score[1], where its quality is maxed.
