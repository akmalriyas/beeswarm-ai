> _Not to be confused with[Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") (the worker bee counterpart)._

**Digital Bee** (also known as **?NULL** , **0x4C4F4C** , and **> > ☺**) was an NPC located inside the [Computer](/wiki/Computer "Computer"). Digital Bee was involved in the [Ready Player Two](/wiki/Ready_Player_Two "Ready Player Two") event, acting as a quest NPC, giving out three puzzles for the player to complete. 

The player had to buy and equip the [Strange Goggles](/wiki/Strange_Goggles "Strange Goggles") in order to interact with Digital Bee. 

Upon completing puzzle 3, the player would be able to walk into the screen where Digital Bee originally was and claim a ![Star Jelly](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e7/Star_Jelly.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404020706)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly"). The “[How to Program BASIC](/wiki/How_to_Program_BASIC "How to Program BASIC")” book also takes Digital Bee's place until the player leaves the game. 

Its appearance is a computer with a pixelated smiley face (or the symbols it gives). It has an antenna and a body and wings similar to a regular bee.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDigital_Bee_%28NPC%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Digital Bee is the only NPC that doesn't communicate using words/dialogue and uses symbols instead.
  * Some of the sounds it makes are Windows XP sounds but modified slightly. When it first appears, the (slightly modified) Windows XP startup sound will play.
  * Before the [2022-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2022-12-26 "Updates"), Digital Bee and ![Marshmallow Bee](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Marshmallow Bee](/wiki/Marshmallow_Bee "Marshmallow Bee") were the only bees that the player couldn't have in their hive. 
    * Since Digital Bee has now been added in as an obtainable bee for the player's hive, this only applies to ![Marshmallow Bee](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Marshmallow Bee](/wiki/Marshmallow_Bee "Marshmallow Bee") now.
  * This bee is the only NPC bee that gives puzzles rather than quests.
  * Digital Bee is the only NPC able to directly modify fields’ flowers.
