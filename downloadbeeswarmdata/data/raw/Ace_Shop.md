# Ace Shop

[![The Badge Bearer's Guild.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/28/BadgerBearersGuild2024.png/revision/latest?cb=20240122122856)

The **Badge Bearer's Guild** is a [shop](/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops") located near the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). The player will need at least 15 [badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges") of any rank past Junior to be permitted to enter the shop. 

The shop sells a set of tools called [sprinklers](/wiki/Sprinklers "Sprinklers"). There are currently 5 sprinklers that can be bought in the shop. The player can also find the [Blender](/wiki/Blender "Blender") here, where they can [craft](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") certain [items](/wiki/Items "Items") from other materials. There is a [Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token on top of the Blender that requires an [accessory](/wiki/Category:Accessories "Category:Accessories") that boosts [Jump Boost](/wiki/System_Page#Jump_Boost "System Page") or [Bear morph](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bear_Morph "Ability Tokens") activated to reach it. The [Mega Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Mega_Memory_Match "Memory Match") can also be found inside of this building. 

On the opposite side of where the sprinklers are is a gate that requires 1 Ace Badge to pass through. Behind it, the player will discover another shop. This shop sells the [Honeycomb Belt](/wiki/Honeycomb_Belt "Honeycomb Belt"), [Crimson Guard](/wiki/Crimson_Guard "Crimson Guard"), [Cobalt Guard](/wiki/Cobalt_Guard "Cobalt Guard"), and [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask "Honey Mask"), which appear to be hanging inside of a large honeycomb. In front of the honeycomb, there is another Star Jelly token. Behind the honeycomb, there is a secret glass-like passage that cannot be entered. It leads to a void-like area below the map. 

Outside, it is decorated with the Badge icon in the menu on top of the entrance, along with a large [Basic Sprinkler](/wiki/Basic_Sprinkler "Basic Sprinkler") on the roof. The large sprinkler is not solid and can be walked through. The [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower") Summoner also resides on the roof.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBadge_Bearer%2527s_Guild%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This shop is one of the shops that don’t have ‘Shop’ in its name, the other shops being the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent"), the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"), the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ"), [Gummy Bear's Lair](/wiki/Gummy_Bear%27s_Lair "Gummy Bear's Lair"), and the [Coconut Cave](/wiki/Coconut_Cave "Coconut Cave").
  * The Badge Bearer's Guild, Ace Shop, and Gummy Bear's Lair are the only locations that require [badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges") to enter.
  * All the sprinklers on display in the shop except The Supreme Saturator are solid.
  * The star jelly on the Blender used to be on the ground in the middle of the shop, but that has changed since the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates").
  * In the [2018-12-19 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-12-19 "Updates"), a [present](/wiki/Present "Present") token was added inside the shop. It was located on top of the honeycomb of the Ace Shop but has since been removed.
  * Behind the Ace Badge section is a narrow corridor with a transparent barrier that prevents the player from progressing further.
