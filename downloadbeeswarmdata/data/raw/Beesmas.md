# Beesmas

This category houses all pages related to **Beesmas**. Beesmas is an annual event that occurs around late December through late March, and is based on Christmas. Beesmas was first introduced in the [2018-12-19 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-12-19 "Updates") and can be seen in the [2019-12-23](/wiki/Updates#2019-12-23 "Updates"), [2020-12-25](/wiki/Updates#2020-12-25 "Updates"), [2021-12-26](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"), [2022-12-26](/wiki/Updates#2022-12-26 "Updates"), [2024-07-17](/wiki/Updates#2024-07-17 "Updates"), [2024-12-17](/wiki/Updates#2024-12-17 "Updates"), and [2025-12-25](/wiki/Updates#12-25-25 "Updates") updates.
