# Gifted Gold Egg

[![Eggs available inside the .](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/14/New_egg_page.png/revision/latest?cb=20240823021608) [![Eggs available inside the .](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/14/New_egg_page.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240823021608)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/14/New_egg_page.png/revision/latest?cb=20240823021608) [](/wiki/File:New_egg_page.png)

Eggs available inside the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop").

**Eggs** are a type of [inventory item](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory") where the player drags into their [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") to hatch a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), can be donated to the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or are used to roll the [Sticker Printer](/wiki/Sticker_Printer "Sticker Printer"). They are also the items that help players start playing in the [game](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"). Eggs come in many different types, some offering chances at different rarities of bees, and some hatching a specific bee. Currently, a total of 6 (or 11 if counting the [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") variants) different types of eggs exist in the game, not including [Plastic Eggs](/wiki/Plastic_Egg "Plastic Egg"), specific bee eggs, the [Flight of the Bumble Egg](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Bumble_Egg "Flight of the Bumble Egg"), or the [Swarming Egg of the Hive](/wiki/Swarming_Egg_of_the_Hive "Swarming Egg of the Hive"). 

Ever since the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), players do not need a vacant [hive slot](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot") to hatch an egg. Now, players can hatch an egg on top of an existing bee to replace it. The level of the bee remains the same, acting like a [Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"). 

Please also note that due to regulations in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands, Belgium, and Australia, any eggs in the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop") won't be available to purchase for British, Dutch, Belgian, and Australian consumers. This restriction applies to most of the eggs in the shop.

### Basic Egg

[![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fa/Basic_Egg.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404043532) The Basic Egg is the most common type of egg found in the game. It will almost always hatch into a [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee") (87% chance), but in rare cases, it can hatch into a [Rare Bee](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare") (10% chance), an [Epic Bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic") (2.5% chance), a [Legendary Bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary") (0.5% chance), or into a [Gifted Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") (0.4% chance), with a 0.348% (1 out of 287.35632184) chance of getting a Gifted Basic Bee from the egg. [Mythic bees](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic") and [Event bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") cannot be hatched from this egg. This egg cannot be used on a Basic Bee. 

_Ways to obtain a Basic Egg:_

  * First-time players receive a free Basic Egg in their inventory.
  * Purchasing from the [Basic Egg Shop](/wiki/Basic_Egg_Shop "Basic Egg Shop") located near the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field") for a base price of ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") and capping at ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)10,000,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"). Every time the player purchases a Basic Egg, the price of the next Basic Egg scales up.
  * Adding the Round Basic Bee [Sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") to the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack") rewards 1 Basic Egg.



### Silver Egg

[![Silver Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/42/Silver_Egg.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404043555) The Silver Egg is a type of egg that will most likely hatch into a Rare bee (64.9% chance), but it has a small chance to hatch into an Epic bee (30% chance), a Legendary bee (5% chance), or a Mythic bee (0.1% chance). A Basic Bee or an Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg. 

_Ways to obtain a Silver Egg:_

  * Purchasing from [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") for ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)50 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") each.
  * Purchasing from the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop") for ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)100 Robux.
  * Finishing [Black Bear's](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear") and [Panda Bear's](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear") Silver Egg [questline](/wiki/Quests "Quests").
  * As a reward from [Brown Bear's](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear") 25th quest.
  * Completing the 25th [Sticker-Seeker quest](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine "Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine") rewards you 1 Silver Egg.
  * An extremely low chance of dropping from a [Mantis](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis") (in increments of 1 or 5).
  * A low chance of dropping from the [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle") and [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear").
  * A low chance to drop from [Rare Sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout").
  * An extremely rare chance from [leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves") when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field").
  * As a drop from a ![Festive Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Planter](/wiki/Festive_Planter "Festive Planter").
  * A rare chance to drop from [stockings](/wiki/Stockings "Stockings").
  * A rare reward from the [Winter Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Winter_Memory_Match "Memory Match").

  * Completing the 100th quest of the [Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine "Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine") rewards you 1 Gifted Silver Egg.
  * An exceptionally rare chance of dropping from [werewolves](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf").

_Ways to obtain a Gifted Gold Egg:_

  * Defeating [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear") for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, including a Gifted Gold Egg, but with lower chances of obtaining upon subsequent defeats.
  * As a reward for getting Top 25 in the [Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors](/wiki/Daily_Top_Epic_Bee_Pollen_Collectors "Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors") by the end of a day.
  * As a Playtime Grandmaster [badge](/wiki/Badges "Badges") reward from donating tickets to the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine").

### Gifted Diamond Egg

[![Gifted Diamond Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/52/Gifted_Diamond_Egg.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404043728) A Gifted Diamond Egg shares the same probabilities as a Diamond Egg, but the egg will hatch that bee into its gifted variant. This type of egg cannot be applied to craft the [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask "Diamond Mask"). 

_Ways to obtain a Gifted Diamond Egg:_

  * Defeating [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear") for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, including a gifted diamond egg, but with lower chances of obtaining upon subsequent defeats as mentioned above.
  * As a reward for being in the top 25 in the [Daily Top Honey Gift Recievers](/wiki/Daily_Top_Honey_Gift_Receivers "Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers").
  * As a Quest Grandmaster badge reward from donating ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") to the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine").
  * Completing 100, 250, 500, 750 and 1,000 [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee") or [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee") quests awards one gifted diamond egg each.
  * Completing 425 [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear") quests awards one Gifted Diamond Egg.
  * Completing the [Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine "Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine") 200th quest awards one Gifted Diamond Egg.
  * An extremely rare chance of dropping upon defeating [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail"), or the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab").



#### Outdated Ways

  * Opening the [Mondo Present](/wiki/Ornament_Presents "Ornament Presents") during Beesmas 2019 gave 2 gifted diamond eggs and other items.

### Star Egg

[![Star Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/40/Star_Egg.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404043938) The Star Egg is a type of egg that hatches into any type of [gifted bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") that is not already in the player's hive, aside from gifted Event bees. If the player already has all the different types of gifted bees in their hive besides gifted event bees, the egg will hatch into a random type of gifted bee instead. The egg will never hatch a gifted mythic bee until all other gifted non-event bees are in the hive. 

_Ways to obtain a Star Egg:_

  * Defeating [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear") for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, including a star egg, but with lower chances of obtaining upon subsequent defeats.

  * Completing [Panda Bear's](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear") first ten star treat/ant quests ("Royal Rumble" quest).
  * An exceptionally rare chance to drop upon defeating the [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle").
  * An exceptionally rare chance of dropping from a ![Festive Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Planter](/wiki/Festive_Planter "Festive Planter").

## Specific Event Bee Eggs[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEgg%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

[Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") eggs are a special type of egg that will always hatch into a specific type of Event bee. After an Event bee egg is hatched, the player will receive an exclusive type of [permanent royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Royal_Jelly_Variants "Royal Jelly") for that specific Event bee. The Event bee royal jelly will turn any bee into that kind of Event bee. It cannot be applied if the player currently has that Event bee in their hive though. And instead of hatching into a random bee of a particular rarity, **specific event bee eggs** will always hatch into the egg's respective event bee. All of these eggs share the same appearance of the basic egg. 

  * Bear Bee Egg

  * Cobalt Bee Egg

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEgg%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee"), [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"), [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"), [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee") and [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") Eggs are the only types of [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") eggs that could or can be obtained without spending ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)Robux or ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"). (A Bear Bee Egg can be obtained by redeeming a [Bear Bee Voucher](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker").)
  * Star Eggs and Basic Eggs are the only two eggs that can hatch into a [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee")(Gifted variant if Star Egg)
  * [Plastic Eggs](/wiki/Plastic_Egg "Plastic Egg"), the [Flight of the Bumble Egg](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Bumble_Egg "Flight of the Bumble Egg") (Egg Hunt 2019), and the [Swarming Egg of the Hive](/wiki/Swarming_Egg_of_the_Hive "Swarming Egg of the Hive") (Egg Hunt 2020) are the only types of eggs that do not hatch into a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees").
  * Any kind of specific bee egg and event bee egg will have the same icon as a Basic Egg in the inventory.
  * The Bear Bee, Festive Bee, [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"), Windy Bee, and Digital Bee Eggs are the only type of Event bee eggs that can or could be obtained directly by spending robux. However, as of now, Bear Bee is the only event bee that can be obtained directly by Robux
  * Basic Eggs are the only type of eggs that can be obtained directly with honey.
  * The star that appears on the Star Egg is slightly larger than the stars on the other types of gifted eggs.
  * The Mythic Egg is the only type of egg with the name of the bee rarity that it hatches into. 
    * The Mythic Egg is also the only egg that has a 100% of hatching into a certain bee rarity (except for Event bee eggs and specific bee eggs). However, before mythic bees were added, Diamond Eggs used to have a 100% chance of hatching into a Legendary bee.
  * The Diamond and Basic Eggs are the only non-specific or event eggs that can hatch into a bee of their name (the [Diamond](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee") and Basic Bees, respectively), though you can get other bees from these eggs.
  * The Mythic Egg is also the only egg with a letter on it.
  * The Choose-a-Mythic Egg is the only egg that allows the player to choose which bee hatches.
  * The [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee"), [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"), and the [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") Eggs are the only (non-event) specific bee eggs that could have been bought from the Robux Shop.
  * The Gifted Silver Egg and the Star Egg are the only Gifted eggs that can be obtained by completing the main questlines from [NPCs](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC") (Black Bear's "Getting Stumped", Mother Bear's "Seven To Seven", and Panda Bear's "Royal Rumble").
  * The player cannot hatch a specific bee egg on that specific bee. For example, a Bumble Bee Egg cannot be hatched on a Bumble Bee. 
    * Even though Basic Eggs don't guarantee a Basic Bee, they still can't be used on Basic Bees.
  * The Basic Egg and Choose-A-Mythic Egg are the only eggs without gifted variants of themselves, excluding specific bee eggs. 
    * Similarly, the Star Egg is the only gifted egg without a regular variant.
  * The Bubble Bee Egg is the only non-Event specific bee egg that can be obtained from a quest (B.B.M's Naughty List).
  * The Tadpole Bee Egg is the only egg that can be obtained by giving an NPC present (obtained specifically from Bubble Bee Man).
