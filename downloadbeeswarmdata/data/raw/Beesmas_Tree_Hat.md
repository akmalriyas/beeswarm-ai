_Not to be confused with[Beesmas Tree](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree "Beesmas Tree"), a Beesmas-exclusive location of a similar name._

A **Beesmas Tree Hat** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 10 [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas") [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of three. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") with a [mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation").
