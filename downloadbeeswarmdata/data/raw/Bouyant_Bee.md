**Buoyant Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Mythic bee](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic"). 

Buoyant Bee's favorite treat is ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"). 

Buoyant Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"), [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), and [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field"). It does not dislike any field. 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBuoyant_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 15 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 5 seconds.
  * Makes 150 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 3 seconds.
  * +3 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats"), -25% Gather Speed, +5 [Gather Amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats"), +25% Convert Speed, +70 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +200% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy").
  * 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.2 [Capacity](/wiki/Capacity "Capacity").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBuoyant_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Blue Bomb]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")** Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding Blue [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") (+10% pollen per Level). Combo with other bombs to increase power.
  * **[[Inflate Balloons]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inflate_Balloons "Ability Tokens")** Increases the pollen in [Balloons](/wiki/Balloon "Balloon") on your field by 5% (+0.01% per Level), then spawns a Blue Balloon for 20s (+2s per level) with capacity that scales with bee level and your capacity. 
    * Pollen collected from flowers near (the shaded ones) the balloon is increased by 10% (20% if blue) and added to the balloon instead of your [Container](/wiki/Items#Bags "Items"). Upon filling up or timing out, the balloon floats back to your [Hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"). Converting from a balloon at your hive grants bonus Capacity and [Honey at Hive](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_At_Hive "System Page") for 1 hour.
    * Standing near a balloon grants a small bonus to Pollen, Blue Pollen, and ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") from Tokens which stacks up to 10 times.
    * If Gifted, this has a 5% (+0.5% per level, doubled when the field is boosted) chance to summon a Gold Balloon instead, which has x2 Capacity and an additional x1.25 Pollen instead.
  * **[[🌟Gifted Ability: Surprise Party]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Surprise_Party "Ability Tokens")** Summons a Gold Balloon (+1 every 5 levels) for all players, then causes all Balloons to spawn random ability tokens for a few seconds. Gold Balloons gain bonus pollen (increases with bee level) and transform nearby [Bubbles](/wiki/Bubble "Bubble") into Gold Bubbles, which collect 50% more pollen and have a chance to spawn [Honey Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Honey_Gift "Ability Tokens"). If you have triggered a Surprise Party within the last 8 minutes, performs "Inflate Balloons" instead (always spawns a Gold Balloon). When a Surprise Party is activated, a server wide message will say "🎉Surprise Balloons from [Player's Display Name]."
  * **[[Passive: Balloon Enthusiast]](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Balloon_Enthusiast "Passive Abilities")** When converting from Balloons, this bee has x3 convert amount. Additionally, this bee's base attack scales up to x3 with [Balloon Blessing](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Areas "Buffs & Debuffs").

Buoyant Bee has a base pollen collection of **15![Blue Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **5 seconds**. That's equivalent to 3 ![Blue Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. (**30![Blue Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **5 seconds** with x2 Bee Pollen gamepass)

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBuoyant_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This bee, [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee"), [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"), and [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") are the only bees that can grant boosts to all the players on the server.
  * This bee, [Commander Bee](/wiki/Commander_Bee "Commander Bee") and [Frosty Bee](/wiki/Frosty_Bee "Frosty Bee") are the only 3 [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in the game that wear hats. 
    * As such, the Buoyant Bee is the only hat-wearing bee that is not an Epic.
  * This is the only bee to not dislike any field. 
    * [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") stated on Discord that the reason why Buoyant Bee has no disliked fields is that it's supposed to be a "happy" bee.
  * The Buoyant Bee and the [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee") have the most amount of finite [energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy").
  * The Buoyant Bee, [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee"), and [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee") are the only Mythic bees to be added since the release of the original three [Mythic bees](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic").
  * The Buoyant Bee and the Precise Bee are the only two bees to have their own ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)Robux pack in the same update.
  * The Buoyant Bee and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") are the only bees to be able to produce a White Boost token. Buoyant Bee, however, only produces it through Surprise Parties, meaning it has to be gifted in order to produce white boost tokens.
  * If you have a lot of Balloon Blessing, the bee will have the same attack as [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee") because of the Balloon Enthusiast passive.
  * If the player is not in a field when another player in the same server activates a Surprise Party, the subtitle for it will appear on the player's screen but won't summon any Golden Balloons.
  * Gifted Buoyant Bee's hat glows.
  * This bee’s gifted form is similar to [Bumble Bee's](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee") gifted appearance. 
    * Additionally, both Buoyant Bee and Bumble Bee are the only bees to have gifted abilities that multiply a player’s base capacity.
  * This bee used to be able to summon Blue Bomb+ tokens, but it was nerfed to Blue Bomb in the [2022-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2022-12-26 "Updates").
