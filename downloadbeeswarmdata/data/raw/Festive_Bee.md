**Festive Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event"). It can be purchased in the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") for ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)500 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"). 

Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, and the only way to make it gifted is by feeding it a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

Festive Bee likes the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), and the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). It dislikes the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"). 

## Stats

  * Collects 40 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 150 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 1 second.
  * +20% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +30 [Gather Amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats"), +70 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +75% [Convert Speed](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.25 [Convert Rate at Hive](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate_At_Hive "System Page")



### Abilities

  * **[[Festive Gift]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens")** : Grants random gifts including [Treats](/wiki/Treats "Treats"), honey, and "[Festive Blessing](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Blessing "Ability Tokens")" to all players on the server. Gifts are improved with [Level](/wiki/Level "Level"). A message will appear saying "🎁 Festive Gifts from [Player's Display Name]! 🎁". If Gifted, can grant rarer gifts and "[Beesmas Cheer](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Beesmas_Cheer "Ability Tokens")".
  * **[[Honey Mark]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mark "Ability Tokens")** : Marks a random area on the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") for 7 seconds (+0.2s per level) that grants 2 Conversion Links and x1.25 Convert Rate while you stand in it. Stacks up to 3 times.
  * **[[Red Bomb+]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")** : Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding red [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") (+10% pollen per level). You can combo with other bombs to increase power.

Festive Bee has a base pollen collection of **40![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. That's equivalent to 10 ![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. (**80![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds** with x2 Bee Pollen gamepass)

## Trivia

  * When this bee's egg was moved to the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent"), it replaced [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee")'s spot.
  * This was the third Event bee to have ability tokens that can be summoned by non-Event bees (Honey Mark and Red Bomb+), the first being [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee") ([Haste](/wiki/Ability_Token#Haste "Ability Token")) and the second being [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") (Blue Bomb+).
  * This bee likes the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). This is a reference due to most Christmas trees being pine trees. 
    * This also makes it the only red bee to like this field.
  * This bee, [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"), and [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") are the only bees that can apply buffs to the entire server. (More specifically, Balloons, Festive Gifts, and Field Corruption.)
  * This bee, [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee") and [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee") are the only red bees to produce tokens that don't give primary benefit to only red (Festive Bee yields Honey Mark tokens and both Rage Bee and Spicy Bee yield [Rage](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Rage "Ability Tokens") tokens).
  * This was the second bee available to be bought for Robux, the first being [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"). 
    * However, this was the first bee to be obtainable through both [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") and Robux; and the second bee to be obtained through quests overall, the first being Gummy Bee.
  * When Festive Gift activates, it plays the first few notes from the classic Christmas song "[Deck the Halls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deck_the_Halls "wikipedia:Deck the Halls")". When collecting the Festive Blessing and Beesmas Cheer tokens, the players can hear the next few notes.
  * A [First Edition](/wiki/First_Edition_Bee "First Edition Bee") Festive Bee's flag is offset and is placed behind where it normally is to make room for the bee's bow.
  * Due to a bug, Festive Bees obtained from the Ticket Tent before the [2019-09-28 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates") might have received a First Edition Flag in that update despite not being a true First Edition Bee.
  * It was the only Event bee that could have been obtained in two different ways (excluding Robux packs) at the same time: The Festive Present next to Bee Bear and in the Ticket Tent for 500 tickets during Beesmas 2019.
  * This bee, along with [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"), are the only bees with tinted wings when not gifted.
  * Bee Bear's Festive Bee is female. 
    * Festive Bee was obtainable during the first three Beesmas events with means besides tickets.
    * It could be obtained during [Beesmas 2018](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas") by completing the Bee Bear questline or by purchasing the Festive Bee Pack for ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1,600 Robux in the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop").
    * It could have been obtained through the [Festive Present](/wiki/Ornament_Presents "Ornament Presents"), in the Beesmas 2019 Event. If the player already had Festive Bee, they would instead get ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)500 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
    * In Beesmas 2020, this bee can be obtained after finishing Bee Bear’s first questline. If the player already had a Festive Bee, they were awarded ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)500 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") instead.
  * This bee, [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"), and [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee") are the only [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that have an exclusive [beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") for themselves.
  * Festive Bee is the only non-colorless bee capable of making Mark tokens.
  * After Beesmas 2020, the models of Festive and Puppy Bee, wearing their respective exclusive beequips, were moved outside of the main map, just behind [Commando Chick's Hideout](/wiki/Commando_Chick%27s_Hideout "Commando Chick's Hideout").
  * Festive Bee and [Riley Bee](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee") are the only red bees to like three fields.
  * Festive bee is the only bee to have a [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas")-exclusive [sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker").
