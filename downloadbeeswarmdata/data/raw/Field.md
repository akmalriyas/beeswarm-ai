# Field

**Fields** , also referred to as "patches" or "[forests](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest")" are square/rectangular areas of [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") scattered around the map, where players are able to collect [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"), which can be converted into [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") at their [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"), or by other methods. There are a total of 23 different fields in the game, including those present in the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge"), [Hive Hub](/wiki/Hive_Hub "Hive Hub") and [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge"). Each field consists of flowers, which come in three different colors: blue, red, and white. The flowers also vary each time a player joins a server based on how the field is generated. The more flowers in each square, the more pollen it gives and the faster it regrows; however, [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee") or any bee equipped with a ![Poinsettia](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3a/Poinsettia.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230409133457)[Poinsettia](/wiki/Poinsettia "Poinsettia"), ![Rose Headband](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/33/Rose_Headband.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20240528162100)[Rose Headband](/wiki/Rose_Headband "Rose Headband") or ![Lei](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Lei.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230418235916)[Lei](/wiki/Lei "Lei") can pollinate it and increase the number of flowers on a square. [Guiding Star](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities") pollinates flowers when it hovers over a field. [Diamond Drain](/wiki/Passives#Diamond_Drain "Passives") also pollinates blue flowers. Fields can also spawn ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") and ![Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat") in form of tokens.
