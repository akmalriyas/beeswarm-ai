_Not to be confused with[Festive Nymphs](/wiki/Festive_Nymph "Festive Nymph"), a version of this mob that only appears in Beesmas._

The **Stick Nymph** is a [mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") that is summoned by [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") during the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge"). 

Stick Nymphs will follow the player closest to them and will deal damage if they touch a player. Despite their low stats, Stick Nymphs are almost always in large groups, often overpowering weaker players.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStick_Nymph%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Stick Nymphs can follow the player out of the [Field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") and can either go through some objects, be obstructed by them, or go into certain locations, such as the [King Beetle's Lair](/wiki/King_Beetle_Lair "King Beetle Lair"), which is not always the same as what the player cannot go through. This can allow the player to exploit them and deal damage from afar.
  * Stick Nymphs, just like [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Chicks#Mondo_Chick "Chicks") and the [Festive Nymphs](/wiki/Festive_Nymph "Festive Nymph"), lose health every second, Mondo Chick always loses 333 health per second, but Stick Nymphs and Festive Nymph's health devours faster when their level is higher, and is possible to be defeated naturally unlike Mondo Chick who only has 300 health left when reaching 15 minutes. If their health drains to 0 without the players, they still will drop rewards.
  * Stick Nymphs can jump, but if you get on a platform higher enough where they couldn't reach you and away enough from them, they cannot jump into you.
  * Stick Nymphs and Festive Nymph are the only mobs that can be summoned by another mob. 
    * These are two of 18 mobs that cannot spawn naturally, as in the player cannot simply walk in a field and fight it, the other mobs being the five types of [Ants](/wiki/Ants "Ants"), [Snowbear](/wiki/Snowbear "Snowbear"), Stick Bug, the five [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge") Mobs, and the four [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party") mobs.
  * Stick Nymphs were the first mob to drop tokens upon defeat. Stick Bug doesn't count because Stick Bug is impossible to defeat, as knocking Stick Bug down to 0 health will only raise its level by 1 point and reset its health to full in the process.
  * Whenever Stick Nymphs spawn in the [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field"), there is a glitch where they can go up the ramp.
  * Stick Nymphs may take away some health upon respawning after the player's death if there are large amounts of them on your body.
  * There is no grace period when taking hits from a stick nymph, therefore a cluster of them can be very lethal.
