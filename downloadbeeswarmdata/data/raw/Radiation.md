# Radiation

[![A bee is radioactive when there is an orange and white ☢️ symbol on the right of the bee's slot, along with green particles.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/33/Radiated_Mutated_Hive.gif/revision/latest?cb=20191223151215)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/33/Radiated_Mutated_Hive.gif/revision/latest?cb=20191223151215) [](/wiki/File:Radiated_Mutated_Hive.gif)

A bee is radioactive when there is an orange and white ☢️ symbol on the right of the bee's slot, along with green particles.

[![A radioactive bee.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c7/Radiated_Bee.png/revision/latest?cb=20200110135348)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c7/Radiated_Bee.png/revision/latest?cb=20200110135348) [](/wiki/File:Radiated_Bee.png)

A radioactive bee.

**Radiation** is a [mechanic](/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") that gives a temporary “radioactive” status to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that lasts for 11 minutes. Bees will stay radioactive regardless of any changes to their [hive slots](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot") during these 11 minutes. 

When a bee is radioactive, it has a higher chance of gaining a [mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation") when using ![Bitterberry](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/af/Bitterberry.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404041641)[Bitterberries](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"), ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), ![Moon Charm](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Moon Charms](/wiki/Moon_Charm "Moon Charm"), or ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly"). It also allows the chance of a bee gaining a mutation by feeding it special treats. Certain radioactive bees can find [stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") in some fields. 

Bees glow green when radioactive. The brightness of the bee is based on how much longer the radioactivity will last: the brighter the bee, the longer until the radioactivity ends. A radioactive bee's hive slot will also emit green particles. 

## Ways of Obtaining[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRadiation%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Feeding a bee a ![Neonberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Neonberry](/wiki/Neonberry "Neonberry") will make the bee radioactive.
  * If a bee is mutated, it occasionally becomes radioactive. This has a chance to make surrounding bees radioactive as well.
  * Global radioactivity events are initiated by [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)"). At the end of Beesmas 2020, he put up a game-wide message, saying "☢️ Testing global radioactivity... ☢️", and caused many bees to become radioactive.
  * Occasionally, a bee will randomly become radioactive without meeting any of the above criteria.
