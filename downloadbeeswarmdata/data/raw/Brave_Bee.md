**Brave Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Rare Bee](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"). 

Brave Bee's favorite treat is ![Pineapple](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"). 

Brave Bee likes the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field") and the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"). It dislikes the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBrave_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Brave Bee and [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee") are the only [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in the game that don't produce any [ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") unless they are in their gifted form or have a [beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") that grants an ability token.
  * The [Brave Bee Gate](/wiki/Brave_Bee_Gate "Brave Bee Gate") has a large model of a Brave Bee on it.
  * Brave Bee is the strongest Rare bee in terms of attack, with a base attack power of 6.
  * The [Brave Guard](/wiki/Brave_Guard "Brave Guard"), which is purchased in the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop"), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Brave Bee.
  * Brave Bee's body color scheme is a darker version of the [Vector Bee's](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee") colour scheme.
  * Brave bee is one of the 16 bees to have a sticker based off of them.
  * During [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") "Ability Token Tidings" quest [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") refers to Brave Bee and Basic Bee as "Poor little merely mortals bees". 
    * Bee Bear also says they aren't magic because they can't create ability tokens.
  * Although it is non-functional, Brave Bee is the only bee in the game to have a coded disliked treat, being [blueberries](/wiki/Blueberries "Blueberries").
