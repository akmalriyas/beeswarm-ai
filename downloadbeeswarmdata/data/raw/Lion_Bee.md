The **Lion Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Legendary bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"). 

Lion Bee's favorite treat is [Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"). 

Lion Bee likes the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field") and the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"). It dislikes the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLion_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The Lion Bee has the highest **base** attack stat in the game.
  * Lion Bee is referred to by [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear") as **"he"** , has a mane, and is called a king in its description. This makes Lion Bee the only confirmed male bee in the game.
  * The Lion Bee is tied for the third-highest amount of base [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy") in the game; it is only beaten by infinite stamina bees such as [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee") or [Exhausted Bee](/wiki/Exhausted_Bee "Exhausted Bee"), and is tied with [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee").
  * There is a [gate](/wiki/Category:Gates "Category:Gates") dedicated to it, known as the [Lion Bee Gate](/wiki/Lion_Bee_Gate "Lion Bee Gate"), the entrance to the 25 Bee Zone. It is located near [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear").
  * Lion Bee, [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"), [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"), and [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee") are the only bees that are based on real-life animals that are not Bee Species. 
    * Lion Bee and Tabby Bee are the only bees that are based on felines.
    * Lion Bee is the only Legendary bee that is based on a real-life non-bee species of animal.
  * Lion Bee is also the only bee in the game that likes the Ant Field. 
    * Lion Bee may like the Ant Field as a reference to [antlions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antlion "wikipedia:Antlion") in real life.
    * It might also like the Ant Field because it has the highest base attack stat in the game and the Ant Challenge is about fighting [ants](/wiki/Ants "Ants").
