_

**Ornament Presents** were presents that sat at different locations around the map during the [Beesmas 2019 event](/wiki/Updates#2019-12-23 "Updates"). They have been since discontinued in following Beesmas events, being replaced by the visually identical [Gift Boxes](/wiki/Gift_Boxes "Gift Boxes"). 

<https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Present1.ogg> [](/wiki/File:Present1.ogg)

The sound when you open a gift.

There were 10 different types of ornament presents that the player could have opened. In order to open them up, the player would have needed to obtain a certain number of [ornaments](/wiki/Ornaments "Ornaments") from [NPCs](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC"). When an ornament present was opened, it dropped various [items](/wiki/Items "Items") (in token form) around the present box. Even though the rewards were tokens, the items given could stack past their limits if applicable. Each present could only be opened once.

  * A token that gave ![Gumdrops](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)15 [Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") was located behind the Golden Present.
  * Tokens that gave ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") and ![Micro-Converter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)3 [Micro-Converters](/wiki/Micro-Converter "Micro-Converter") were each next to the Mythic Present.
  * If some tokens weren't collected from an ornament present, the items from the tokens would immediately go to the player's inventory upon despawning.
  * The Golden Present's room has been replaced and reused as [Commando Chick's Hideout](/wiki/Commando_Chick%27s_Hideout "Commando Chick's Hideout").
  * The Gooey Present's room can still be found behind the [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Gummy_Bee_Egg_Claim "Gummy Bee Egg Claim"). The Mythic Present's room, located to the left of the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"), could have also been found until a few months later, when it was removed.
