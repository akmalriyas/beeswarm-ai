**Hasty Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Rare bee](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"). 

Hasty Bee's favorite treat is ![Pineapple](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple")

Hasty Bee likes the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field") and [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"). It dislikes the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch") and [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHasty_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 3 seconds.
  * Makes 80 [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 3 seconds.
  * +40% [movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +25% gather and convert speed.
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +15% [Player Movespeed](/wiki/System_Page#Movespeed "System Page").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHasty_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The [Hasty Guard](/wiki/Hasty_Guard "Hasty Guard"), which can be bought in the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop"), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Hasty Bee.
  * This is the only [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to have a [gifted bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee#List_of_Hive_Bonuses "Gifted Bee") that affects Player Movespeed. 
    * However, [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee") also originally gave the same gifted bonus before it was changed to +5% Bee Movespeed in the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates").
  * This bee, [Shocked Bee](/wiki/Shocked_Bee "Shocked Bee"), [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee"), and [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee") are the only bees that have either a spelling or grammar mistake in their description.
  * A [special jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Specific_Bee_Jelly "Royal Jelly") that changes a bee into a Hasty Bee can be obtained by using the [code](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), 'HasteHelper' (expired).
  * This bee, Shocked Bee, Ninja Bee, and [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee"), are the only bees that grant the Haste token without any certain [Beequips](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip").
  * Hasty Bee most likely dislikes the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field") because it is home to the [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail"), who moves very slowly.
  * Hasty Bee is the fastest Rare bee.
