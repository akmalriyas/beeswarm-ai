The **Egg Hunt Info** was a [quest giver](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers") that was located in front of the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate"). This was the quest location of the [Egg Hunt 2019](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2019 "Egg Hunt 2019") event that occurred in Roblox for [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"). The player could collect ![Marshmallow Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/73/Marshmallow_Bee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230125035057)3 [Marshmallow Bees](/wiki/Marshmallow_Bee "Marshmallow Bee") and turn in it here for various in-game rewards, the [Flight of the Bumble Egg](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Bumble_Egg "Flight of the Bumble Egg"), and a [badge](/wiki/Badges#Unobtainable "Badges"). If obtained before the event ended, the player could equip the Flight of the Bumble Egg in the avatar tab on the Roblox website. 

Each of the [NPCs](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC") in game at the time, except for the [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") and [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett"), gave a quest for a marshmallow bee. 

Note: If you completed the Egg Hunt quests before they were modified, you will be rewarded two [Field Dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), two marshmallow bees, and five [Jelly Beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans").

Egg Hunt 2019  | Welcome Egg Hunters! Look at this one - egg-ceptionally bee-utiful. Meticulously molded out of the finest beeswax. And one just like it can be yours! Here's how: Each bear around the map has an Egg Hunt Quest for you. Every time you complete one, you'll be rewarded with a [Marshmallow Bee]. (It's just a cute springtime treat - not a real bee) Return here with 3 [Marshmallow Bees] to trade them for the Egg Hunt Egg! If you're new here, you should talk to Black Bear and Mother Bear first. They'll keep things simple. You can find them near the Sunflower Field. Let's get to it! Happy Hunting!  
  
_-During-_   
Black Bear, Mother Bear, and Brown Bear have the simplest quests for you. If you're new to the game, be sure to talk to these 3 first. Black Bear can be found to the right of the hives near the Red Cannon. Mother Bear can be found near the Sunflower Field. Brown Bear can be found near the Clover Field. If you a _[sic]_ [Marshmallow Bee], there are other bears on the mountain with more for you.   
_-Completion-_   
1... 2... 3 [Marshmallow Bees]! You've earned our Egg Hunt Egg, good work! It's _[sic]_ proper name is "Flight Of The Bumble Egg". Wear this bad boy on your head to truly embody the concept of bee swarm simulation. Hopefully that didn't take too long - we do things a little slow around here. You're free to buzz off now! But there are many more [Marshmallow Bees] to earn during this Egg Hunt event! Work your way up the mountain and introduce yourself to all the bears. When you're out of bears, well, try talking to whoever else you run into. We've got a lot to see here if you've got the time and a taste for the grind. For now, you ought to collect the other Egg Hunt eggs. You're welcome back anytime!   
Conclusion  | You earned the Egg Hunt Egg, isn't it nice? Now you can bring a swarm of bees into whichever ROBLOX game you choose. Go, spread those bees, collect the rest of the eggs. But remember, there's more to do here during the Egg Hunt. Make sure you talk to all the bears!
