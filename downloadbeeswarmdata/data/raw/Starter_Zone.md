# Starter Zone

The **Starter Zone** is the collection of places that do not need any [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") prerequisites to access. Any player can access the Starter Zone, no matter how far they are into the game. This is also where the players' [hives](/wiki/Hive "Hive") are.
