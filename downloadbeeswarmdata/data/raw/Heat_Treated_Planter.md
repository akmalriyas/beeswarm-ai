The **Heat-Treated Planter** is a reusable [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") added in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"). Alone, it grows in about 12 hours and stores around 25 billion pollen. When harvested, it grants bonus ![Red Extract](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Red Extracts](/wiki/Red_Extract "Red Extract") and ![Enzymes](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"). It is purchasable in the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"). The player also needs to complete 150 [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee") quests for it to be unlocked.

It grows up to 50% faster in fields with red flowers. Red bees are 33.3% more likely to sip [nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") from it, but blue bees are 50% less likely to sip from it. When harvested, the planter grants 50% more red pollen, but also 50% less blue pollen. It also grants 40% more Invigorating and Motivating Nectar. 

Harvesting a fully-grown Heat-Treated Planter has a 2/9 chance to spawn a [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom"). The spawned puffshroom is between levels 6-7, and has a: 

  * ~72.59% chance of being a Common Puffshroom,
  * ~21.78% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
  * ~5.44% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
  * ~0.18% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom,
  * ~0.01% chance of being a Mythic Puffshroom.
