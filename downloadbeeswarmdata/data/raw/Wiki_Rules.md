# Wiki Rules

The rules listed here, as well as the [Editing Guidelines](/wiki/Editing_Guidelines "Editing Guidelines"), the [Discussions Guidelines](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/d/g), and Fandom's [Terms of Use](https://www.fandom.com/terms-of-use), are the general basis for what isn't allowed on the wiki. Punishments will still be conducted on a case-by-case basis, meaning they may not match what is stated here, and repeated or more severe violations will result in a harsher punishment. 

If you got punished for something that is not listed here, it doesn't mean you did nothing wrong. If you aren't sure why you got a punishment or think it is too severe, contact a [staff member](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Wiki:Staff "Bee Swarm Simulator Wiki:Staff"). DO NOT create a new account to appeal a block, for it will be treated as ban evasion. Such matters should be treated on Community Central.

## **Verbal Reminder**

_It is usually given to users who have made very minor, first-time violations. These are sometimes used instead of reminders in the forms of replies or messages._

  * Avoid using languages aside from English. This is to prevent a language barrier.
  * Do not ask to become a staff member. You have to earn it first.



## **Reminder**

_It is usually given to users who have made first-time violations, more specifically minor violations._

  * Do not minimod, or act like a staff member when you are not one. Report to a real staff member if you find a situation that needs attention.
  * Do not edit user profiles that don't belong to you without permission.
  * Do not spam, as it can cover something up and cause a disruption. Minor spam, particularly ones that are simply unneeded, will result in a reminder.
  * Do not use profanity. Bypasses, including censoring or acronyms, are still not allowed and will result in a reminder, regardless of how much is censored. 
    * The only exception to this rule is "lmao."



## **Warning**

_It is usually given to users who have made more severe violations._

  * Do not spam, as it can cover something up and cause a disruption. Severe spam, particularly ones that flood a page, making it hard to scroll, will result in a warning.
  * Do not use profanity in any way shape or form. This includes bypassing, partial censorship, or acronyms.
  * Do not delete a staff member's reminder or warning. It negates the purpose of reminding someone of their errors, as it hides it from their view unless a staff member restores it. The only exceptions are: 
    * If a staff member states that the warning is null.
    * If it is a two-day necroposting punishment from October 17th, 2021 or before.
  * Do not advertise anything, including wikis or social media, on anywhere except your profile. Posting videos is fine as long as you do not tell people to subscribe or view other videos.
  * Do not abuse wiki features to edit or badge farm. Minor cases, specifically short ones that do not ruin a page, will result in a warning.
  * Do not engage in edit wars, for it will cause disruption on a certain page. Talk to a staff member about a certain edit to check if it is permitted. Short edit wars will result in a warning.
  * Do not start or encourage toxicity, drama, harassment, or bullying. Minor cases will result in a warning. 
    * This includes e-dating. It's weird.



## **Block**

_It is usually given to users who have made the most severe violations, or users that have made enough violations that they should not be allowed to talk or edit on the wiki for some time._

  * Do not engage in edit wars, for it will cause disruption on a certain page. Talk to a staff member about a certain edit to check if it is permitted. Prolonged edit wars, especially after a clear explanation is provided, will result in a ban. **[Block Time: One Week — One Year.]**
  * Do not post gory scenes. Unless the content is way too gory, it will warrant a temporary block. **[Block Time: One Week — One Year.]**
  * Do not start or encourage toxicity, harassment, bullying, or drama. Major cases will result in a ban. **[Block Time: Two Weeks — One Year.]**
    * Posts about leaving the wiki _that play off of previous drama_ may be grounds for a block.
  * Do not abuse wiki features to edit or badge farm. Major cases, especially ones that do more to ruin a page or are continuous, will result in a ban. **[Punishment: One Month — One Year.]**
  * Do not add or show fake content to the wiki as if it was real. **[Block Time: One Month — One Year]**
  * Vandalism on this wiki is prohibited. Minor cases will result in a block. **[Block Time: One Month — One Year.]**
  * For your online safety, you are not allowed to have an account on the wiki until you are 13 years old. **[Block Time: Until the user turns 13.]**
  * Do not present yourself with an inappropriate username, profile picture, or masthead. Usernames that indicate impersonation also fall under this category. You will be blocked until you change the things that you are asked to change. **[Block Time: Until the appropriate changes have been made.]**
  * Do not attempt to evade a block given to you by creating a new account. If you are caught, all of your found alternate accounts will receive a perma-block, and your main account will receive an extension. **[Block Time: Twice your original block duration.]**



## **Indefinite Block**

_Aside from global blocks, this is the worst punishment anyone can get. It is usually given to users whose actions have been so severe that they should no longer be allowed on the wiki._

  * Do not post or link to NSFW, including gory, illegal, explicit, or sexual content.
  * Do not abuse wiki features to edit or badge farm. Ones that severely damage the wiki will result in an indefinite block.
  * Do not start or encourage toxicity, harassment, bullying, or drama. Very severe cases will result in an indefinite block.
  * Do not add or show fake content to the wiki as if it was real. Deliberately repeating the same thing to prove that something fake is genuine will result in an indefinite block.
  * Vandalism will result in an indefinite block if: 
    * It is intentional or severe.
    * It is one of your first edits, in which it will be treated as trolling regardless of the type of vandalism.
  * Trolling on this wiki is prohibited.
  * Impersonating someone, whether they are a user of this wiki or otherwise, is not allowed. An indefinite block does not apply to cases relating to usernames.
  * Do not raid wikis, whether it is on this wiki or another wiki. If you are caught trying to promote a raid, you will be blocked and reported to your related or target wikis.
  * Do not attempt to evade a block given to you by creating a new account. If you are caught and your original ban reasons and durations are malicious enough or you have ban evaded twice, all of your found accounts will be blocked for an indefinite amount of time.
