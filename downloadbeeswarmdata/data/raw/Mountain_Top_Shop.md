# Mountain Top Shop

[![The Mountain Top Shop at an angle.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/38/GliderShop.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/294?cb=20180421050057)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/38/GliderShop.png/revision/latest?cb=20180421050057 "the Mountain Top Shop at an angle.")the Mountain Top Shop at an angle.

The **Mountain Top Shop** , also called the **Top Shop** , is a [shop](/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops") located past the [Lion Bee Gate](/wiki/Lion_Bee_Gate "Lion Bee Gate"). Before the [Mountain Top Field's](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field") name was known, this shop used to be called the **25 Bee Shop**. The shop sells various high-end [items](/wiki/Items "Items") and is run by [Top Bear](/wiki/Top_Bear "Top Bear"). 

There is a ![Royal Jelly](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9f/Royal_Jelly.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404020655)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token on the roof and a ![Ticket](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Ticket.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404015820)[Ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") token in a corner inside the shop. In addition, the respawn timer for the [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick") can be found on the side facing the Mountain Top Field.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMountain_Top_Shop%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * [Mondo Belt Bag](/wiki/Mondo_Belt_Bag "Mondo Belt Bag") and [Beekeeper's Boots](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Boots "Beekeeper's Boots") could've also been obtained by completing [Sun Bear's](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") quests before he left during his first visit.
  * This shop is the only place in the game where the player can purchase additional [hive slots](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot").
  * Top Bear was introduced in [2018-07-11 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-07-11 "Updates"), which means this shop was once not run by any bear before said update.
  * This shop used to have the eviction for sale until the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), when it was removed.
  * When the game was first released, the only item in this shop was the Glider until the [2018-04-10 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-04-10 "Updates") added the [Beekeeper's Mask](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Mask "Beekeeper's Mask") and Hive Slots.
