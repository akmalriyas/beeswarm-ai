# Caustic Wax

Caustic Wax

[![Caustic Wax](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bc/Caustic_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042819)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bc/Caustic_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042819)

_"A glowing hunk of beeswax. Has a 25% chance to GREATLY improve a Beequip - but a 75% chance to DESTROY it!"_

**Caustic Wax** is an [inventory item](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory") and a type of [wax](/wiki/Waxes "Waxes") added in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates") that can be used on a [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") to boost its stats. Upon usage, it has a 25% chance of greatly improving the Beequip's stats; however, it has a 75% chance of permanently destroying the Beequip instead, destroying the Beequip and also wasting the Caustic Wax. Caustic Wax has [Upgrade Value](/wiki/Beequip#Stats,_Waxes,_Chances "Beequip") of 4. 

It also can be used to craft a variety of different items, such as [planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter") and [tools](/wiki/Tools "Tools"). 

When successfully applying Caustic Wax to a bee's Beequip, a message pops up saying:  
The Wax improved the Beequip!

If the wax fails to upgrade the Beequip, a message will pop up saying:  
The Wax destroyed the Beequip...

Prior to the removal of Permanent Beequips, using a Caustic Wax on a ![Festive Wreath](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6b/Festive_Wreath.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230409133741)[Festive Wreath](/wiki/Festive_Wreath "Festive Wreath") or ![Reindeer Antlers](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/69/Reindeer_Antlers.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230409132612)[Reindeer Antlers](/wiki/Reindeer_Antlers "Reindeer Antlers") resulted in the following message:  
Cannot use this wax on a Permanent Beequip
