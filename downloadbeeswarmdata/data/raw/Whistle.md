The **Whistle** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 8 [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee"), [Commander Bee](/wiki/Commander_Bee "Commander Bee"), [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"), [Demo Bee](/wiki/Demo_Bee "Demo Bee"), [Cool Bee](/wiki/Cool_Bee "Cool Bee"), and [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee").
