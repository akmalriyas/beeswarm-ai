The **Mantis** is a [mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") that defends the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch") and the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). 

It takes 20 minutes for a Mantis to respawn after being defeated (17 minutes if the player has a [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee")). 

This Mob's level can range from 4-6 depending on the [field](/wiki/Field "Field") it is located at. 

The Mantis is green with red eyes. Its arms stick out, similar to a praying mantis.

## Strategy[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMantis%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Due to the Mantis' long attack range, the player’s [Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") normally won’t target it until they get closer/when the mantis itself attacks. Try to get at the very edge of its enormous jump range. Then, when it stops to get ready to attack, walk in a very wide circle around it. If done correctly, the mantis will undershoot and land where you were a few seconds before. This is often close enough for your Bees to start attacking, even once it retreats back. Also, keep in mind the [Frozen Field Defenders Glitch](/wiki/Glitches#Frozen_Field_Defenders "Glitches") can be used to defeat it, just like with most other mobs. When a Mantis is stuck in an object, the player can also defeat it more easily.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMantis%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * When a [Bear](/wiki/Category:Bears "Category:Bears") gives a quest that involves more than one mantis, its plural form was spelled incorrectly, such as "4 Mantiss" rather than "4 Mantises" or "4 Mantis"; This was fixed at an unknown time. 
    * The same incorrect plural form applies to the Werewolf.
    * In [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") dialogue for his quests, he asks you to defeat x "Mantises", but in the quest list, it still says "Mantiss".
  * In Bee Bear's quest, ‘ _BEE-liz Honey-dad_ ’, he told the player to defeat 10 Mantises, but if the player talked to him again, he didn't mention this.
  * The mantis in the Pineapple Patch can get stuck in the wall surrounding the field or in the smaller pineapple behind the [Treat Dispenser](/wiki/Treat_Dispenser "Treat Dispenser"), though this is rare. 
    * Before the [2019-09-28 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), the pine trees in the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest") could be collided with and the mantises could get stuck in them.
  * Mantises have the longest attack jump length of any mob, only rivaled by the [Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf"). Because of this, the mantises can jump outside of the field that they're defending into others.
  * The mantis has the same respawn time as the [Scorpion](/wiki/Scorpion "Scorpion").
  * During Beesmas 2020, you could get the [Beesmas Top](/wiki/Beesmas_Top "Beesmas Top") from the Mantis.
  * As stated by Number1Riker, a [Former Moderator](/wiki/BSS_Staff#Former_BSS_Staff "BSS Staff") of the Bee Swarm Simulator Discord server, the Mantis was originally supposed to be a cricket before being changed into the former. The reason for the change is unknown.
