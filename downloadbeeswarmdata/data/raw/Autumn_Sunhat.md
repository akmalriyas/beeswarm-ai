The **Autumn Sunhat** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 16 [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [Hasty](/wiki/Hasty_Bee "Hasty Bee"), [Bumble](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee"), [Looker](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee"), [Exhausted](/wiki/Exhausted_Bee "Exhausted Bee"), [Diamond](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee") and [Puppy](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee") [Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees").
