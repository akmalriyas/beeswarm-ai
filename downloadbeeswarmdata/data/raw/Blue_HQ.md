# Blue HQ

## Blue HQ

[ ![BlueHQBeesmas2021](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b8/BlueHQBeesmas2021.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20211230153809) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b8/BlueHQBeesmas2021.png/revision/latest?cb=20211230153809 "BlueHQBeesmas2021") Front view of the Blue HQ.

### Location

Behind the Blue Flower Field

The **Blue HQ** is a two-story base with many features. The entrance can be found at the far end of the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), located in the [Starter Zone](/wiki/Starter_Zone "Starter Zone"). It has a [Bucko Bee](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee") model above the entrance. To enter, the player must have discovered 4 different blue bee types. The Blue HQ is the blue counterpart to the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"). 

On the roof, there is an enlarged [Bubble Wand](/wiki/Bubble_Wand "Bubble Wand"). This wand occasionally grows a face and blows [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") to nearby fields. This includes the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), Blue Flower Field, and the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"). 

## Interior[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlue_HQ%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

On the first floor, there are four different objects: 

  * A [Blue Teleporter](/wiki/Blue_Teleporter "Blue Teleporter") that will teleport the player near the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") and [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon"),
  * A set of blue [items](/wiki/Items "Items") for sale, (See the 'Store' section below.)
  * The [Blueberry Dispenser](/wiki/Blueberry_Dispenser "Blueberry Dispenser"), which gives a varying amount ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry") depending on the number of * blue bees the player has in their [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") every 4 hours,
  * The [All-Time Top Blue Collectors](/wiki/All-Time_Top_Blue_Collectors "All-Time Top Blue Collectors") [leaderboard](/wiki/Leaderboards "Leaderboards"), which shows the top 100 players who have collected the most blue pollen.



On the second floor, there are more features: 

  * A ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token located near the front window.
  * A [Blue Field Booster](/wiki/Blue_Field_Booster "Blue Field Booster") that gives a 15-minute duration [field boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost") for either the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field") (1 stack, only when [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail") is on cooldown), [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest") (1 stack), [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field") (1 stack), or the Blue Flower Field (3 stacks), and has a 45-minute cooldown.
  * [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee"), who will require a ![Translator](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Translator](/wiki/Translator "Translator") for it to hand out [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests"),
  * The [Top Bucko Bee Helpers](/wiki/Top_Bucko_Bee_Helpers "Top Bucko Bee Helpers") leaderboard, which is located to the left of Gifted Bucko Bee and shows the top 100 players who have completed the most Bucko Bee quests,
  * The [Daily Top Blue Collectors](/wiki/Daily_Top_Blue_Collectors "Daily Top Blue Collectors") leaderboard, which is located to the right of Gifted Bucko Bee and shows the top 100 players who have collected the most blue pollen in a day.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlue_HQ%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The Tide Popper is currently the most expensive item able to be bought in the Blue HQ.
  * If the player goes to the corner of the ramp inside the Blue HQ closest to the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), they will be able to hear the Crawlers music.
  * Unlike the [Riley Bee](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee") model on the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"), the wings of the Bucko Bee model don't have collision.
  * On 2022-01-01, there was a glitch where if the player attempted to view the Tide Popper, the game would freeze and they won't be able to exit the shop, except for rejoining the game.
  * The Blue HQ is also known as the "Blue Clubhouse." If the player does not have 4 blue bee types discovered, the top of their screen will say, "Discover 4 Blue Bee types to enter the **Blue Clubhouse**."
