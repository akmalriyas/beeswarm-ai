# Honeystorm

[![The Honeystorm summoner.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d3/HoneystormPad.png/revision/latest?cb=20260131044020) [![The Honeystorm summoner.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d3/HoneystormPad.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20260131044020)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d3/HoneystormPad.png/revision/latest?cb=20260131044020) [](/wiki/File:HoneystormPad.png)

The Honeystorm summoner.

A **Honeystorm** is a server-wide event that can be summoned by using the pad near [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear") by anyone who has discovered at least four [Legendary Bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary") types. When summoned, a server-wide announcement will state:  
[Username] has summoned a Honeystorm!  
Upon being summoned, a golden-yellow fog will appear along with a multitude of [honey tokens](/wiki/Honey "Honey") and some [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") tokens that will begin to randomly appear across all [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"), even the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge"). The storm lasts for 20 seconds and has a cooldown of 4 hours. It can also be randomly triggered, in which the announcement says:   
A Honeystorm has been summoned!  
When a honeystorm is summoned, the honey tokens make raindrop-like popping noises when they appear. Like with the [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower"), there's an audio of a distorted voice clip from [IcedTeaLatte](/wiki/BSS_Staff "BSS Staff"), a staff member for the game, saying "Honeystorm" to indicate that a honeystorm has been summoned.[1]

Fields with bigger [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") give higher amounts of honey per token, with their totals being increased by the [Honey from Tokens](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_From_Tokens "System Page") stat. These honey tokens also despawn faster than normal honey tokens.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoneystorm%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The honeystorm fog is normally tinted yellow, but during Beesmas in 2018 and 2019, the fog is white. 
    * As of Beesmas 2020, this is no longer the case, since [Snowstorms](/wiki/Snowstorm "Snowstorm") use the white fog instead.
  * This and the [Special Sprout Summoner](/wiki/Special_Sprout_Summoner "Special Sprout Summoner") are the only [machines](/wiki/Category:Machines "Category:Machines") that require the player to discover Legendary bee types to be activated, with this requiring 4 and the Special Sprout Summoner requiring 8.
  * A honeystorm's yellow tint could generate enough light to make the [cave monsters](/wiki/Cave_Monster "Cave Monster") in the Werewolf's cave visible.
  * There is a [glitch](/wiki/Glitches "Glitches") where if a switch between day and [night](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle "Day/Night Cycle") occurs while a honeystorm is active, the fog will disappear. This is purely visual and the honeystorm will still function as normal. This glitch is shared with Mythic Meteor Showers and Snowstorms.
  * There was a short-lived issue around 2018-05-26 that caused the Honeystorm Summoner to be removed. If a player encountered a server without a Honeystorm Summoner, they could simply leave the game and come back. This was, of course, fixed later.
  * After the [2018-09-10 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-09-10 "Updates"), the Honeystorm Summoner was moved and the [Wealth Clock](/wiki/Wealth_Clock "Wealth Clock") now sits in its former spot. Now it is located between the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field") and [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ").
  * Upon receiving the quest "Witness Grandeur", [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") summons a honeystorm.
  * According to [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear"), [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") summons the honeystorms.
  * If you summon honeystorms at night, the fog will stay even after the storm until daytime.
  * Honeystorms can stack with other honeystorms. This does not affect the intensity of the honeystorms, but instead increases the duration.
