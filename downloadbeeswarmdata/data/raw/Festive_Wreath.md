## Trivia

  * The ![Festive Wreath](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Festive Wreath** and the ![Reindeer Antlers](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Reindeer Antlers](/wiki/Reindeer_Antlers "Reindeer Antlers") share many similarities: 
    * The two are the only Beequips to have models visible on the bee that they are equipped to.
    * Before the [2024-01-12 update](/wiki/Updates#2024-01-12 "Updates")'s removal of permanent Beequips, the two were the only permanent Beequips in the game. 
      * This was the second permanent Beequip added to the game, preceded by the Reindeer Antlers.
      * They were the only Beequips that cannot be discarded.
      * ![Caustic Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Caustic Waxes](/wiki/Caustic_Wax "Caustic Wax") were unable to be used on these two Beequips. However, this is no longer the case.
  * A model of a Festive Bee with the Festive Wreath can be found outside the map, next to a model of a [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee") with Reindeer Antlers.
  * This is the one of three Beequips to give a unique ability. 
    * The other two are ![Snowglobe](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowglobe](/wiki/Snowglobe "Snowglobe") and ![Reindeer Antlers](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Reindeer Antlers](/wiki/Reindeer_Antlers "Reindeer Antlers").
  * The model of the Festive Wreath on Festive Bee is the [Deluxe Christmas Wreath](https://www.roblox.com/catalog/191097654/Deluxe-Christmas-Wreath) Roblox accessory.
