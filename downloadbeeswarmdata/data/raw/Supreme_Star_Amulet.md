# Supreme Star Amulet

![Digital Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/27/Digital_Bee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20230415203844) | **This piece of content contains information obtained through datamining.** Due to the nature of the information, details may be inaccurate or outdated.

Date of datamined file: December 19th, 2024  
---|---  
  
The **Star Amulet** is an [amulet](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet") that can be purchased in five different tiers from machines in the [Star Hall](/wiki/Star_Hall "Star Hall"). 

The amulets grant buffs to the owner and always increase [capacity](/wiki/Capacity "Capacity") as they go up a tier. Higher tier amulets grant more and better buffs. Diamond and Supreme Star Amulets also have additional [Star Passive](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Amulet_Passives "Passive Abilities") abilities. 

Each Star Amulet requires the user to have a certain number of [gifted bee](/wiki/Gifted_bee "Gifted bee") types discovered. After the user generates a Star Amulet, the user must wait 1 second to generate another one. 

The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the number of discovered gifted bee types up to a certain number, where its quality is maxed: 

  * For non-Supreme Star Amulets, the player needs 45 types.
  * For Supreme Star Amulet, the player needs 50 types. 
    * Note that this is currently not attainable, as there are only a total of 46 bee types in the game.



When the player generates a Star Amulet, the following audio plays, with the sound pitch decreasing when going up in tiers: 

<https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Amulet_Generation_Star.ogg>

The Supreme Star Passives are the main feature of Supreme Star Amulets. There are 6 passives that the player can get, with a 1/6 chance of getting a specific one. There is also a 1/50 chance to get another passive, resulting in two passive abilities. 

If the player has 500B honey or more when generating an amulet, they can choose to spend that amount to guarantee a double passive. 

The tables below give the percentage of a certain stat on the amulet having strength within a specific range, at a few quality milestones. To see the percentage of a certain stat having a specific strength value, see [the designated subpage](/wiki/Star_Amulet/Probability#Supreme_Star_Amulet "Star Amulet/Probability"). Note that as of currently, the player can only have at most 46 discovered gifted types; the rest are only added for completion's sake.

## Trivia

  * The Supreme Star Amulet Generator takes the place of the original spot taken by Diamond Star Amulet Generator, only a bit more moved back.
  * The Diamond Star Amulet and Supreme Star Amulet are the only two Amulets in the game that can have or always have passive abilities on them.
  * The Diamond Star Amulet can only give 2 types of [passive abilities](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities"), being Guiding Star and Star Shower.


  * The Supreme Star Amulet is the only Star Amulet added after the original release of Star Amulets. It was introduced in the [2020-06-06 update](/wiki/Updates#2020-06-06 "Updates"), while the others were released in the [2018-07-11 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-07-11 "Updates"). 
    * The Star Amulets used to require the gifted bee types in the player's [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"). However, this was changed to only requiring discovering gifted bee types when the Supreme Star Amulet was added in the game.


  * The Supreme Star Amulet is the only Star Amulet that can have double passive abilities.


  * The Diamond Star Amulet is the only Star Amulet that has received a buff after its release. 
    * In the [2020-06-06 update](/wiki/Updates#2020-06-06 "Updates"), Diamond Star Amulets got a 1/100 chance of getting a passive when generated.


  * The summoned Gummy Star/Pop Star/Scorching Star rotates counterclockwise around the player.
  * The background audio of Supreme Star Amulet passive abilities plays 3 times for Gummy and Scorching star, 1.5 times for Pop star and 7 times for Star Saw.


  * An [Ant Pass](/wiki/Ant_Pass "Ant Pass") token is located behind the Supreme Star Amulet Generator. 
    * It used to be behind the Diamond Star Amulet Generator before the Supreme Star Amulet Generator took its place.


  * The Star Shower passive functions similarly to [Science Bear's](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") [Beesmas Lights](/wiki/Beesmas_Lights "Beesmas Lights").
  * The audio for obtaining a Star Amulet was the same audio for completing the [Ready Player Two](/wiki/Ready_Player_Two "Ready Player Two") ROBLOX event (if the player has ALL the event badges).
  * When the Star Shower Passive is activated, the big star that indicates the area that the stars will fall has a slightly stretched top.
  * If a player dies while Scorching Star, Pop Star, or Gummy Star is active, the star will continue to hover wherever the player dies. It will be without the star’s model and will not follow the player, but it will still play its tune. It will also continue giving the buffs that the passive grants. 
    * In some cases, if the player has activated Pop Star and reaches the time limit, sometimes it will activate another Pop Star buff. However, it won't count the number of popped bubbles.
  * If the player has between 490 billion and 500 billion honey, it is still possible to buy the offer of granting a guaranteed double passive on a Supreme Star Amulet, costing only 490 billion.
  * The Diamond Star Amulet has a 1/1,000,000 chance of giving a Star Cub Skin.
