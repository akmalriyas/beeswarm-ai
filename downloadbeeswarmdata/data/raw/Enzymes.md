# Enzymes

Enzymes

[![Enzymes](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/62/Enzymes.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404021131)

_"Grants +10% Instant Conversion and x1.25 Convert Rate for 10 minutes."_

COOLDOWN

1 second

**Enzymes** are an [inventory item](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory") that was added in the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"). When used, it grants +10% [Instant Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Instant_Conversion "System Page") and x1.25 [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page") for 10 minutes when activated. Using another ![Enzymes](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Enzymes** will not stack them, but will reset the timer. ![Enzymes](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Enzymes** can also be used for crafting other [accessories](/wiki/Category:Accessories "Category:Accessories") or items. Using a ![Super Smoothie](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Super Smoothie](/wiki/Super_Smoothie "Super Smoothie") will override the buff.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEnzymes%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * ![Enzymes](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Enzymes** used to look like pills. However, Roblox removed them because they appeared to have violated the Roblox Terms Of Service. They were later re-created, published under the name "Pineapple Candies" and with the appearance they have currently.
  * If multiple ![Enzymes](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Enzymes** are used in a [Blender's](/wiki/Blender "Blender") crafting recipe, the chat log displaying the lost materials would display "Enzymess" adding an extra 's' instead of the proper plural. This small bug applies to ![Gumdrops](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") and various other items.
