# Seedling

_Were you looking for[Magic Bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean")?_

[![The sprout model.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/35/Sprouttransparent.png/revision/latest?cb=20200622002845) [![The sprout model.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/35/Sprouttransparent.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200622002845)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/35/Sprouttransparent.png/revision/latest?cb=20200622002845) [](/wiki/File:Sprouttransparent.png)

The sprout model.

A **sprout** is a plant that spawns in the center of a [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields"). Sprouts emit a bright yellow beacon that reaches the sky, and have a counter at the base of it representing a [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") amount. When players collect pollen, or when a [cloud](/wiki/Clouds "Clouds") is on the field (requires at least one player being in the field for the cloud give growth), the number counts down and it gradually grows taller. Once the pollen counter reaches 0, it sends out shockwave and scatters various tokens throughout the field. If no interaction with a sprout happens for more than 5 minutes, it will despawn. 

## Spawning[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSprout%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Sprouts can spawn randomly around the map and can also be planted using ![Magic Bean](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Magic Beans](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"). It can also be summoned by using the [Special Sprout Summoner](/wiki/Special_Sprout_Summoner "Special Sprout Summoner") near the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"), by anyone who has discovered all eight [Legendary Bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary") types, once every sixteen hours. 

When a sprout spawns, it will broadcast a server-wide message:   
🌱A (Rarity) Sprout has appeared...🌱

If a player has planted a sprout, the message would read:  
🌱 {Username} has planted a (Rarity) Sprout...🌱

If Sticker Sprouts are about to spawn in the [Hive Hub](/wiki/Hive_Hub "Hive Hub"), the message will read:  
🌱 Sticker Sprouts are about to spawn in the Hive Hub...🌱

When the Sticker Sprout has spawned in the Hive Hub, the message will read:   
🌱 A Sticker Sprout has spawned in the Hive Hub...🌱

Sprouts cannot spawn or be planted in the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field"). 

## Variants[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSprout%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

There are ten different variants of sprouts: 

  * Sprout (Green) (Common)
  * Rare Sprout (Silver) (Rare)
  * Epic Sprout (Gold) (Very Rare)
  * Legendary Sprout (Teal) (Extremely Rare)
  * Supreme Sprout (Luminescent green) (Unfathomably Rare)
  * Moon Sprout (Luminescent blue during both night and day, only available during nighttime (common))
  * Gummy Sprout (Translucent pink) (Very Rare)
  * Sticker Sprout (Rainbow, colors shifting) (Spawns only every 3 hours in the [Hub Field](/wiki/Hub_Field "Hub Field"), or a extremely rare chance when planting a magic bean in the Hub Field)
  * Festive Sprout (White and Red stripes) (Planted by ![Festive Bean](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Beans](/wiki/Festive_Bean "Festive Bean") or [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)"))
  * Debug Sprout (Black) (Can only be planted by [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett"))



The amount of pollen needed for the sprout for it to be harvested depends on the rarity of the sprout and the field it's in.

### Location Specific Drops[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSprout%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Depending on where the sprout is planted, the drops spawned can vary in amounts or may drop certain items. 

  * A sprout in a [red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") field yields 4 times more ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry") than in any other field. No ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry") will spawn. 
    * A sprout in the [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field") will yield twice as many ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry") than sprouts in other red fields.
  * A sprout in a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") field yields 6 times more ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry") than in any other field. No ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry") will spawn.
  * A sprout in the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field") yields 7 times more ![Sunflower Seed](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed") than in any other field.
  * A sprout in the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch") yields 7 times more ![Pineapple](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple") than in any other field.
  * A sprout in the [35 Bee Zone](/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate "Windy Bee Gate") yields 20% more rewards than sprouts in other areas. It also requires significantly more pollen to pop.
  * A sprout in the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field") yields ![Coconut](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Coconuts](/wiki/Coconut "Coconut") and ![Tropical Drink](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tropical Drinks](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink") in addition to other treats.
  * Other fields may produce more regular ![Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat") than any other treats depending on the field.

## Tips[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSprout%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Various items, abilities, and equipment can boost player movespeed, helping to collect tokens more efficiently before they despawn. 
    * Using ![Oil](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Oils](/wiki/Oil "Oil") grants 1.2x player movespeed.
    * Equipping the [Hasty Guard](/wiki/Hasty_Guard "Hasty Guard") grants 1.1x player movespeed.
    * Having [haste](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens "Buffs & Debuffs") can increase the player's [movespeed](/wiki/System_Page#Movespeed "System Page") up to 2x. 
      * [Haste producing bees](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens") are the primary source of this buff.
      * The Honey, Treat, Strawberry, and Blueberry [Dispensers](/wiki/Dispensers "Dispensers") give 5 stacks of Haste.
      * The [Royal Jelly Dispenser](/wiki/Royal_Jelly_Dispenser "Royal Jelly Dispenser") gives 10 stacks of Haste.
    * If the player and has either the [Coconut Clogs](/wiki/Coconut_Clogs "Coconut Clogs") or the [Gummy Boots](/wiki/Gummy_Boots "Gummy Boots") equipped, they can have a coconut fall on them and grant the player a short burst of [Coconut Haste Surge](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Coconut_Haste "Passive Abilities").
    * [Bear Bee's](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee") ![Bear Morph](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Bear Morph](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bear_Morph "Ability Tokens") [ability](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") can significantly increase player movespeed as well.
    * The Free Royal Jelly Dispenser gives a stack of Haste+.
  * [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee") and the ![Box-O-Frogs](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Boxes-O-Frogs](/wiki/Box-O-Frogs "Box-O-Frogs") can summon [frogs](/wiki/Frog "Frog") that collect sprout tokens.
  * The [Star Saw](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Saw "Passive Abilities") can collect massive amounts of sprout tokens.
  * [Windy Bee's](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") ![Tornado Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tornado](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Tornado "Ability Tokens") can also collect massive amounts of sprout tokens.
  * ![Cub Buddy](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Cub Buddies](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins "Cub Buddy") can also collect sprout tokens.
  * For Sticker Sprouts in particular, try to stick to one spot, and collect whatever loot spawns close to you. Loot that spawns too far from you will almost certainly be picked up by another player before you reach it, so stick to whatever's in reach.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSprout%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * If a ![Festive Bean](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Bean](/wiki/Festive_Bean "Festive Bean") is planted by a player, the ![Festive Blessing Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Blessing](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Blessing "Ability Tokens") and ![Beesmas Cheer Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Beesmas Cheer](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Beesmas_Cheer "Ability Tokens") buffs are only obtainable by the player that planted it. 
    * If the sprout is planted by Onett, anyone can collect the tokens.
  * Gummy Sprouts are the only sprouts that are translucent.
  * Most sprout notifications are displayed in gold, but a few have unique displays: 
    * Gummy Sprouts have a light purple notification.
    * Supreme, Moon, and Debug Sprouts have gray notifications.
    * Festive Sprouts have a red notification.
    * Sticker Sprouts have a rainbow notification.
  * Many of the treat drop statistics are mentioned in Spirit Bear's dialogue.
  * Sprouts were originally called "Seedlings" when they were released and then the name got changed in the [2019-12-23 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-12-23 "Updates"). Its original message was "🌱A Seedling has sprouted...🌱"
  * The Supreme Sprout previously required half the [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") that a Legendary Sprout would need. It now requires 50% more pollen than a Legendary Sprout.
  * Since the [2019-23-12 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-12-23 "Updates"), Onett has been able to plant sprouts server-wide throughout the game. They can be planted under unique names.
  * Supreme Sprouts were formerly named "Mythical Sprouts" before the 2019-12-23 update.
  * If the player turned in [Stick Bug's Egg Hunt 2019 Quest](/wiki/Stick_Bug#Egg_Hunt_2019_Quest "Stick Bug"), Stick Bug spawned an Epic, Legendary, and a Supreme Sprout.
  * The "[Special Sprout Summoner](/wiki/Special_Sprout_Summoner "Special Sprout Summoner")" was formerly called "Sprout Summoner", and it would plant any type of sprout randomly except for Debug, Festive and Sticker Sprouts.
  * The Debug Sprout is the only sprout that does not drop honey tokens.
  * If a sprout spawns before a player joins the server, the sprout and the sprout's pollen counter will be invisible to that player, however it can still be popped by the player.
  * On mobile, there is a glitch where the sprouts in the server are invisible and can't be popped by the player.
  * Sticker Sprouts are the only sprouts that grow on their own (without clouds). It loses about 1M pollen per second, even if no one is collecting in the Hub Field.
  * If the player who spawned the sprout leaves before the sprout has popped, the sprout will not spawn anything.
  * The Debug Sprout is the only type of sprout that cannot be planted by a player (besides Onett, who can plant them at will).
  * Sprouts are mentioned by [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") to only exist on the Mountain. As he mentions “I've been all around the world, and I've never seen ANYTHING like those before.”
  * If the player is lagging, they may see the pollen number on the sprout become negative for a brief moment when they pop it.
  * Supreme Sprouts and Moon Sprouts are the only sprouts that glow.
