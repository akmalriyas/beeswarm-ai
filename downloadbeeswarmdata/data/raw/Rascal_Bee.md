**Rascal Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Rare bee](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"). 

Rascal Bee's favorite treat are ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"). 

Rascal Bee likes the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field") and the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). It dislikes the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRascal_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 80 [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +15% [movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +2 [attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.25 [Red Bomb Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Red_Bomb_Pollen "System Page").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRascal_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Its blue counterpart is [Bumble Bee](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee").
  * Rascal Bee, [Riley Bee](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee"), and [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee") have the same skin colors.
  * Rascal Bee is one of [Onett's](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") favorite [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") types because he likes its facial expression.
  * In Onett's inventory, it is revealed that Rascal Bee was originally going to have a different face.
  * In Rascal Bee's description, "misch**i** evous" is written as "mischev**i** ous", a common misspelling of the word.
  * Defeated [ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug") will sometimes drop [Rascal Bee eggs](/wiki/Egg#Specific_Bee_Eggs "Egg").
  * Rascal Bee has the second highest attack of all Rare bees, only behind [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee").
  * A [special jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Specific_Bee_Jelly "Royal Jelly") that changes a bee into a Rascal Bee could have been obtained from the [code](/wiki/Codes "Codes") 'MischiefMaker' (expired).
