# Mob

There are 34 different kinds of **enemies/creatures/monsters** (colloquially called “**mobs** ”) in [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"). 

The most abundant mob in terms of spawn points is the [rhino beetle](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"), as there are five spawn points for them in the game, with [Ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug") being the second, with four spawn points. The mob with the longest spawn time is the [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail"), respawning in 96 hours. 

The 8 entities that can attack other mobs are [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), [flames](/wiki/Flame "Flame"), [frogs](/wiki/Frog "Frog"), [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee"), [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee"), [abilities](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), [passives](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities"), [coconuts](/wiki/Coconut "Coconut"), and the coal summoned from the [Naughty List](/wiki/Naughty_List "Naughty List") during [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas"). One method for defeating mobs that reliably works for any mob that defends a [field](/wiki/Field "Field") (everything other than the [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle"), [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), [cave monsters](/wiki/Cave_Monster "Cave Monster"), [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug"), [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee"), [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee"), and the [Commando Chick](/wiki/Chicks#Commando_Chick "Chicks")), with the exception of the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab") and [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Chicks#Mondo_Chick "Chicks"), is to jump continuously. It is one of the [glitches](/wiki/Glitches "Glitches") where when jumping, mobs will freeze and not attack which leads to the player’s bees thinking the mob isn't a threat and not attack it. Players would have to make sure their bees "know" to attack a mob before starting to jump - if the mob has not started attacking them - by waiting for their bees to begin attacking the mob before jumping. 

Before the [2019-02-01 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-02-01 "Updates"), rewards from defeating any mob were sent to the player's inventory right after the mob was defeated. After the update, the mobs were altered to drop a ring of tokens around it instead ([battle points](/wiki/Battle_Points "Battle Points") do not spawn as tokens). Other players cannot collect tokens dropped by the mob if the mob was not spawned by them. The tokens can be collected by a [token link](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Token_Link "Ability Tokens"), unless the rewards are from public mobs, meaning other players can collect the drops regardless of whether they attacked it or not. 

Mobs have levels that are shown next to their name. If a player’s bees are at a lower level than the mob they are attacking, they have a higher chance of missing their attacks depending on how far the level of the bee is from the level of the mob being attacked. This means that the lower the level of the bee, the more likely a mob that has a higher level than the bee attacking will deflect or dodge the bee's attacks. 

Some mobs are considered passive. This means that they will not attack the player, nor the player will attack them. These include [bean bugs](/wiki/Bean_Bugs "Bean Bugs"), [frogs](/wiki/Frog "Frog"), [fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies"), with the exception of [Chicks](/wiki/Chicks#Chick "Chicks"), [Spotted Chicks](/wiki/Chicks#Spotted_Chick "Chicks"), and [Hostage Chicks](/wiki/Chicks#Hostage_Chick "Chicks"). 

While some mobs are considered [bosses](/wiki/Category:Bosses "Category:Bosses"), others do not qualify for this designation. These mobs are called [mini bosses](/wiki/Category:Mini_Bosses "Category:Mini Bosses"), distinct from regular mobs but not qualifying as a boss. Wild Windy Bee, Rogue Vicious Bee, [Snowbear](/wiki/Snowbear "Snowbear"), Commando Chick, and Stump Snail are all in this category. 

Daylight Saving Time will affect the respawn time of the Stump Snail, King Beetle, and Tunnel Bear. 

[Loot Luck](/wiki/Loot_Luck "Loot Luck") has the ability to directly affect the quantity and quality of the rewards that mobs drop upon defeat but will not affect honey dropped by mobs (that is instead affected by [Honey from Tokens](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_From_Tokens "System Page").) 

When the Mondo Chick and Commando Chick use their egg block ability, it will block almost all bees' attacks, even if the bee is the same level or a higher level than it. 

Stump Snail has the highest health out of all mobs and does not intentionally attack the player. Instead, it moves in a circle around the edge of the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"). However, it will deal damage if the player stands in the front of the snail or in its head. Standing inside the shell will not deal any damage to the player. 

From 2022-07-20 to 2022-07-29, there was a Roblox bug that caused mobs to not function properly. 

## Regular Mobs[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMobs%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Regular mobs stay in certain fields and will attack the player if they enter that field. Once defeated, they will respawn after a period of time. 

Monster Respawn Time can lower the time it takes for these mobs to respawn. For example, the Stump Snail normally takes 96 hours or 4 days to respawn, however, if the player owns a [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"), then that time is reduced by 15% to 81 hours and 36 minutes or 3.4 days.
