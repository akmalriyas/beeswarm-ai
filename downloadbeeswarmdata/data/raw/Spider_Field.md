The **Spider Field** is a [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") located past the [Basic Bee Gate](/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate "Basic Bee Gate") and lies between the [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field") and [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"). The field is protected by the [spider](/wiki/Spider "Spider"). 

This field can spawn [bitterberry](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"), [treat](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [enzyme](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"), and [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean") tokens when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Spider Field are [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee"), [Commander Bee](/wiki/Commander_Bee "Commander Bee"), [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee"), [Shocked Bee](/wiki/Shocked_Bee "Shocked Bee"), [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee"), [Vector Bee](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee"), and [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"). Bees that dislike the Spider Field are [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"), [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee"), and [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"). 

This field grants Motivating [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is placed here. 

A level 2-4 [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") has the chance of spawning here. 

It is a 26x28 field, containing 728 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are all white, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSpider_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Field Booster](/wiki/Field_Booster "Field Booster") may give a Spider [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +200% Spider Field pollen for 15 minutes.  [![The Spider Field Boost icon.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Spider_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080146) [![The Spider Field Boost icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Spider_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080146)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Spider_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080146)

The Spider Field Boost icon.

  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") will add 100% Spider Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% Spider Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Spider [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Spider Field conversion and give +5% instant Spider Field conversion and +20% Spider Field pollen for each stack. Spider Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
  * It can be boosted by the [Shell Amulet](/wiki/Shell_Amulet "Shell Amulet"). If it has a Spider Field Boost, it can boost the field by +5% to +30% depending on the amulet rarity.
  * Giving [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear") a ![Present](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Present](/wiki/Present "Present") will give the field a x3 boost for Beesmas 2025.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSpider_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The Spider Field is the only field where all of the flowers are white. This also makes it the only field where all of the flowers are the same color, as there are no fields that are entire blue or red.
  * There is a ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") hidden in the middle of the spider cave.
  * This is one of six fields where fireflies can land, with the others being Bamboo Field, [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"), and Strawberry Field.
  * There is a [glitch](/wiki/Glitches "Glitches") that occurs at the Spider Field where amulets and cub buddies are spotted without an owner. This happens when a player left the server at the Spider Field.
  * This field is one of [Black Bear's](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear") favorite fields, as mentioned in his dialogue.
  * This field, the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"), the [Hub Field](/wiki/Hub_Field "Hub Field"), and the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field") are the only fields that are not named after a plant or a fruit.
  * The Spider Cave, the Stump Field's Cave, and the [Coconut Cave](/wiki/Coconut_Cave "Coconut Cave") have the exact same cave decoration.
  * This field, along with Clover Field, Cactus Field, Rose Field, Mountain Top Field, and Pepper Patch are the only fields in which a Rogue Vicious Bee can spawn.
  * This is one of the only two fields named after the mob(s) that reside within the field. The other being the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field"), with the multiple types of [ants](/wiki/Ants "Ants").
  * This field has a row of flowers inside the mountain wall that's normally not visible, but you can still collect pollen in that area, kind of like how the flowers in the [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field") that go through the ramp aren't visible normally, but you can still collect those flowers.
