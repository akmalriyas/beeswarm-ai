The **Ant Amulet** is an [amulet](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet") that is always obtained from participating in the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge"). 

The amulet gives the player buffs and will always boost [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page"). There are five tiers of Ant Amulets, each awarded for reaching a certain point milestone in the Ant Challenge. 

The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the player’s score until a score of 400,[1] where its quality is maxed.
