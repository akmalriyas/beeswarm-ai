# Diamond Mask

## Diamond Mask

[ ![Diamond Mask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/75/Diamond_Mask.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403022722) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/75/Diamond_Mask.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403022722 "Diamond Mask")

### Description

"Proudly show your extreme wealth to the world. Shine so brightly that others will complain."

### Cost

5,000,000,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), 250 [Blue Extracts](/wiki/Blue_Extract "Blue Extract"), 150 [Oil](/wiki/Oil "Oil"), 100 [Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter"), 5 [Diamond Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Diamond_Egg "Egg"), 1 [Comforting Vial](/wiki/Comforting_Vial "Comforting Vial").

### Bonus Stats

x1.5 Blue Pollen  


+15% Instant Blue Conversion  
+25% Pollen  
x3 Capacity  
x1.5 Bubble Pollen  
+35% Defense  
+20% Bee Ability Rate  
+Passive: Bubble Bombs (Needs Bubble Mask)  
+Passive: Diamond Drain

> "_Proudly show your extreme wealth to the world. Shine so brightly that others will complain._ " 

The **Diamond Mask** is a [mask](/wiki/Category:Hats "Category:Hats") and the [blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") counterpart of the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask"). It can be reached by finding the doorway near the end of the [Cloud Obby](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses#Cloud_Obby "Obstacle Courses"), then completing an additional obstacle course made up of moon platforms. The player is able to skip the Cloud Obby by using a [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute") or [Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider") with enough height to steer themselves to the entrance to the [Diamond Room](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses#Diamond_Room "Obstacle Courses"). If the player owns the Diamond Mask, the moon platforms in the Diamond Room are permanently solid and are shaded a deep blue.

    * Every 25th [Bomb Ability Token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens") spawns 25 [Bubbles](/wiki/Bubble "Bubble") that last for 8 seconds. if a player touches them, they pop, collecting 4R/6W/8B pollen, increased by 15% per [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") blue [Bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") type, from 29 nearby [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") and causes them to grow. (Cooldown: 2 minutes)
  * [+Passive: Diamond Drain](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Diamond_Drain "Passive Abilities"): 
    * Every 30th blue [Ability Token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") converts 5 million Pollen plus a percentage of the [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Total "System Page") of all of your bees combined, increased by 10% per blue bee and +50% per gifted blue bee type in your [Hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"). It also [Pollinates](/wiki/Pollination "Pollination") 10 (+3 per blue bee) blue flowers within the field. (Cooldown: 45 seconds)

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDiamond_Mask%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Prior to the [2019-09-28 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), the player was unable to jump on the moon platforms during the day, similar to the moon platforms in the [Bear Gate](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate") and behind [Dapper Bear's Shop](/wiki/Dapper_Bear%27s_Shop "Dapper Bear's Shop"), even if they already own the Diamond Mask.
  * If the ingredients to craft this item were to be crafted, a total of 5,000,000,000 honey, 4,000 [Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), 12,500 [Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"), 7,500 [Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"), 100 [Magic Beans](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), 2,500 Moon Charms, and ![Diamond Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5 [Diamond Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Diamond_Egg "Egg") are needed, or 2000 [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") if one decides to purchase all the diamond eggs from the [ticket tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent").
  * This and the [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask "Honey Mask") are the only [items](/wiki/Items "Items") that require [eggs](/wiki/Egg "Egg") to craft.
  * This mask and the Demon Mask are the only masks in the game that resemble [Legendary bees](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"). 
    * The Diamond Mask is based on [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee") and uses the same face decal as it.
  * When receiving the quest 'Breath of Blue', [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") says that Diamond Drain is activated on every 25th [Blue Boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") token collected, though it was later changed to every 30th blue ability token collected. 
    * Spirit Bear also states that the amount of pollen converted by the ability decreases with each use, though this feature was eventually removed.
  * The Diamond Mask Shop, the [Hub Field Shop](/wiki/Hub_Field_Shop "Hub Field Shop"), and the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") Shop are the only shops in the game that have a single item.
  * This, the Demon Mask, and the Gummy Mask are the only masks with decorative accessories (the Diamond Mask has a tiara).
  * Unlike the Demon Mask and Gummy Mask, the sound effect of its passive is not based on music in the game.
  * This mask, the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask"), and the [Mondo B.B.M. Mask](/wiki/Mondo_B.B.M._Mask "Mondo B.B.M. Mask") are the only masks that require an obby to equip or reach the shop.
