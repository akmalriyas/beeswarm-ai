**Fire Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Epic bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"). 

Fire Bee's favorite treat is ![Pineapple](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"). 

Fire Bee likes the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), and [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field"). It dislikes the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFire_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 80 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * -20% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +25% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), +3 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.25 [Flame Pollen](/wiki/Flame#Flame_Pollen "Flame").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFire_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Red Bomb+]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding red [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") (+10% pollen per Level). Combo with other bombs to increase power.
  * **[[Passive:](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gathering_Flames "Passive Abilities") [Gathering Flames]](/wiki/Flame#Gathering_Flames "Flame")** 35% chance (50% if Gifted) to spawn a [Flame](/wiki/Flame "Flame") when gathering. Flames last 4 seconds. Each second they deal 10 damage (+25% of the bee's [base attack](/wiki/Bee_Attack "Bee Attack")) to nearby enemies and collect [5 Red / 3 White / 2 Blue] pollen (+10% per bee level) from 9 flowers. Standing near a flame grants Flame Heat, which increases [Red Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Red_Pollen "System Page") (between x1.2 and x2) and [Bee Attack](/wiki/Bee_Attack "Bee Attack") (between +5% to +25%).

Fire Bee has a base pollen collection of **12![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. That's equivalent to 3 ![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. (**24![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds** with x2 Bee Pollen gamepass)

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFire_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask "Fire Mask"), which can be purchased in the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Fire Bee.
  * Fire Bee can be summoned by [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee") with its [Inferno](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inferno "Ability Tokens") ability, making it the only bee summonable by [ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"). 
    * This bee can also be summoned by using the [Honeyday Candles](/wiki/Honeyday_Candles "Honeyday Candles").
  * Fire Bee is the red counterpart of [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee").
  * This bee was one of the bees to have a specific jelly in the [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog"). The jelly was given as part of the Cozy Bundle.
