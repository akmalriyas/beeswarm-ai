# Masks

This category houses all pages about **hats** (or in most cases, **masks**), which is a sub-category of [accessories](/wiki/Category:Accessories "Category:Accessories"). This type of accessory is worn on the player's head. Each hat/mask can be [crafted](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") with [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") and other various materials. Hats give out a variety of boosts that are unique to them. 

Some hats have [passive abilities](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities"). For example, the [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask "Honey Mask") has [Coin Scatter](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Coin_Scatter "Passive Abilities"). Some hats can have 2 passive abilities if one is unlocked from another hat. For example, the player can have the Ignite passive on the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") if they own the [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask "Fire Mask").
