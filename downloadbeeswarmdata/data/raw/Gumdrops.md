# Gumdrops

Gumdrops

[![Gumdrops](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6f/Gumdrops.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404021459)

_"Use while standing in a field to cover flowers in goo. Goo grants bonus honey."_

COOLDOWN

2 seconds

**Gumdrops** are an [inventory item](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory") that was introduced in the [Gummy Invasion Event](/wiki/Updates#2018-05-26 "Updates"). When used, gumdrop blobs fall from the air and splatter [goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo") if they hit a [flower](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). Goo grants an additional 40% bonus [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), with bigger goo puddles granting even more bonus honey. **Gumdrops** can also be used to craft other gear, such as the [Gummyballer](/wiki/Gummyballer "Gummyballer"). 

[![Gumdrops falling in a field.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/49/GumdropsLandField.png/revision/latest?cb=20181223220426) [![Gumdrops falling in a field.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/49/GumdropsLandField.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20181223220426)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/49/GumdropsLandField.png/revision/latest?cb=20181223220426) [](/wiki/File:GumdropsLandField.png)

Gumdrops falling in a field.

Placing **gumdrops** in a hotbar slot triggers an ON/OFF switch to appear beneath it. Toggling the switch allows the player to use gumdrops automatically every two seconds. The switch can be turned off by manually using gumdrops in any way.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGumdrops%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Gumdrops are the only item that changes the overlay on the [leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves").
  * Gumdrops are the cheapest items in the game that cost tickets, costing only 1/3 tickets, and they are also the cheapest to speed up in the Blender, along with [moon charms](/wiki/Moon_Charm "Moon Charm"), requiring only 1 ticket for every 10 gumdrops.
  * If the player crafts glue in the Blender, it will say " - [Amount of glues crafting * 50] _Gumdropss_ " instead of "_Gumdrops_ ". This also happens when donating gumdrops to the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") and getting Gummy Bee from the Gummy Bee Egg Claim.
  * Unlimited Gumdrops is a buff allows the player to use gumdrops without taking them away from the player's inventory for 3 minutes (only obtainable from [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes") and the [Glue Dispenser](/wiki/Glue_Dispenser "Glue Dispenser")). This does not increase the number of gumdrops the player has.
  * To enter [Gummy Bear's Lair](/wiki/Gummy_Bear%27s_Lair "Gummy Bear's Lair"), the player will need to use a gumdrop on top of the Gummy Bee at the Gummy Bee Egg Claim near the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate") after earning the Goo Hotshot badge.
  * Gumdrops are currently the only way to get Gummy Bee after the [2019-04-05 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates").
  * After the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), the hotkey for gumdrops (G), along with the hotkey of ![Stinger](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Stingers](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger") and ![Micro-Converter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Micro-Converters](/wiki/Micro-Converter "Micro-Converter"), were removed.
  * Before the 2019-04-05 update, if the player attempted to use gumdrops before the cooldown was over, a message in the bottom right corner would say: [Must wait X seconds to use gumdrops again]. If the player doesn’t have any gumdrops, a message will appear saying: [You don't have any Gumdrops].
