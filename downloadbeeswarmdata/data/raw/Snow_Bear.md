**Snowbear** is a [mini-boss](/wiki/Category:Mini_Bosses "Category:Mini Bosses") that can be summoned every 1 hour and 30 minutes at the [Snowbear Summoner](/wiki/Snowbear_Summoner "Snowbear Summoner") after completing [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear")'s Beesmas quest. When summoned, it spawns in the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), and players have 60 seconds (1 minute) to defeat it. If 60 seconds has passed without enough damage done to it, it will despawn (melt). All players' bees can attack another player's Snowbear, but the player that summoned it would receive more rewards. 

## Attacks[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnowbear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Snowbear has two different attacks: 

  * Contact: If a player makes contact with Snowbear, it deals a base damage of 36. Snowbear does not track players; instead it moves randomly through the field hitting players along the way.
  * Snowball Hurl: Snowbear hurls a snowball at a player who is in, or around, the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"). This snowball deals 60 base damage and applies the ["Frozen" Debuff](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#Debuffs "Buffs & Debuffs") to the player who gets hit by the snowball for approximately 2.5 seconds, applying ×0 [player movespeed](/wiki/System_Page#Movespeed "System Page") and [Jump Power](/wiki/System_Page#Jump_Power "System Page"), as well as ×0.2 [Bee Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats").

## Strategies[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnowbear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Every time the player defeats the Snowbear, its health increases (e.g. 20,000 health at level 5), thus making it stronger and harder to defeat in a short amount of time. High-level bees that can do a good amount of damage are recommended. 

Its reload time for using a snowball is short, so if it catches the player in ice, it may be able to keep them frozen by using the snowball attack on them repeatedly. The snowball will still do damage while the player is frozen, and the player will die if they get hit multiple times. If they have a [glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider") or [parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute"), they can use it to dodge the snowballs. If they get frozen, immediately after thawing, they can jump away and use the glider to prevent being frozen a second time. It is also a good idea to try and stay behind the Snowbear, as it takes time for it to turn around. 

It is also recommended to have another player have their bees attack a high-leveled Snowbear. 

## Tips[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnowbear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Use ![Oil](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Oils](/wiki/Oil "Oil") to increase the [Bee Movespeed](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Movespeed "System Page"), and ![Tropical Drink](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tropical Drinks](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink") to increase [Critical Chance](/wiki/Critical_Hits "Critical Hits"). ![Super Smoothie](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Super Smoothies](/wiki/Super_Smoothie "Super Smoothie") are also an option, as they increase both stats. However, they are expensive, so it is not recommended to use them unless you are far into the game and/or the Snowbear is very high-level.
  * Certain items like [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") and [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask "Fire Mask")'s passive abilities, [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee")'s tornado ability, [frogs](/wiki/Frog "Frog") and [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee")'s [Inferno](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inferno "Ability Tokens") ability ignore enemy levels meaning they will always deal damage regardless of the Snowbear's level.
  * Have multiple bees with high attack such as [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee") or [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee").
  * Use ![Stinger](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Stingers](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger") to multiply the bee's attack by ×1.5 for 30 seconds. 
    * If you have the [Star Saw](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Saw "Passive Abilities") passive, you can use it to deal more damage.
  * Certain bees that generate battle tokens may be a necessity. 
    * It is recommended to have multiple [Rage Bees](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee") or Spicy Bees, in order to get more [Rage Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Rage "Ability Tokens").
    * Vicious Bee is important, as it can deal massive damage with [Impale](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Impale "Ability Tokens").
    * Digital Bee is useful, as it can stun and make it take more damage with its [Mind Hack](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") ability.
    * Bees that can generate [Focus](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Focus "Ability Tokens") tokens, such as [Looker Bees](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee"), [Commander Bees](/wiki/Commander_Bee "Commander Bee"), [Music Bees](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee"), and [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee") (with [Reindeer Antlers](/wiki/Reindeer_Antlers "Reindeer Antlers")) can help greatly.
    * Music Bee can generate [Melody](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Melody "Ability Tokens"), which increases critical power. Melody can also be generated by bees that have the ![Toy Horn](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Toy Horn](/wiki/Toy_Horn "Toy Horn") or the ![Kazoo](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Kazoo](/wiki/Kazoo "Kazoo") equipped.
    * [Precise Bees](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") generate [Target Practice](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Target_Practice "Ability Tokens") tokens, which grants [Precision](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens "Buffs & Debuffs"), increasing [Super-Crit Chance](/wiki/System_Page#Super-Crit_Chance "System Page").
  * High-leveled bees are useful for landing hits on high-leveled Snowbears. 
    * Precise Bees can be very useful against high-leveled Snowbears, as their attacks never miss if the Snowbear is one level higher (two if Gifted).
  * The Snowball attack will land sooner than the actual snowball, so to prevent being frozen by seemingly nothing, try to keep moving while remaining in range of the Snowbear. 
    * In addition, if a snowball comes your way, do not run away in the direction of the snowball, as you will likely be frozen. Instead, run towards the left or the right of it.
  * [Gifted Bees](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") have 50% more attack than normal bees, making them useful for fighting Snowbears.
  * Having other players in the server to help the player is also a viable strategy.
  * Use Gifted Bees that increase bee attack, critical chance, critical power, super-crit chance and bee movespeed, such as Gifted Looker Bee, Gifted [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee"), Gifted Rage Bee, Gifted Commander Bee, Gifted [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee"), Gifted [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee") and Gifted [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee").
  * Use ![Jelly Beans](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Jelly Beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans") to get the green Jelly Bean token, which boosts Critical Chance.
  * Equip accessories that can boost the attack of the player's bees, primarily guards and masks, such as Fire Mask and Demon Mask.
  * Use [Onett's Lid Art](/wiki/Onett%27s_Lid_Art "Onett's Lid Art"), [Honeyday Candles](/wiki/Honeyday_Candles "Honeyday Candles"), [Gummy Beacon](/wiki/Gummy_Beacon "Gummy Beacon"), and the [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)")'s [Honey Wreath](/wiki/Honey_Wreath "Honey Wreath") as three of the Beesmas decorations summon 3 extra bees and the Honey Wreath will summon 1 extra bee for a certain amount of time.
  * With the [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister "Coconut Canister"), a player can intentionally activate the [Emergency Coconut Shield](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Emergency_Coconut_Shield "Passive Abilities") to gain a ×1.25 bee attack boost for 10 seconds while also being immune to damage. Do note that having the Emergency Coconut Shield active does not make a player immune to freezing.
  * Try to stack battle buffs such as focus and precision to their maximum before spawning the Snowbear.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnowbear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Snowbear uses the Cartoony Animation Pack.
  * The icon for the Frozen debuff is the asset for [Ice Cube](https://battlefordreamisland.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Cube "w:c:battlefordreamisland:Ice Cube") from the animated show [Battle for Dream Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_for_Dream_Island).
  * Snowbear and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") are the only hostile mobs that can be directly summoned by players. They are also the only mobs whose cooldowns are not affected by [Gifted Vicious Bee's](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") Gifted Hive Bonus.
  * Snowbear is the fourth mob to be removed from the game, after the [Chick](/wiki/Chicks "Chicks"), Spotted Chick, and Hostage Chick. 
    * It's also the only mob to return after being removed.
  * Snowbear's leveling up mechanic work similarly to Stick Bug and [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee"). 
    * It's also the only mob that has a changing size, which also increases by leveling. This can make it the largest mob in the game.
  * Snowbear is the first and only mob able to freeze a player.
  * Snowbear and [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear") are the only hostile mobs that are bears.
  * Similar to Tunnel Bear and [Shadow Bear](/wiki/Shadow_Bear "Shadow Bear"), Snowbear has no face and uses one texture.
  * This is the fourth mob to not drop [Honey Tokens,](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Honey_Gift "Ability Tokens") with the first three being the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab"), [ants](/wiki/Ants "Ants") and the [cave monsters](/wiki/Cave_Monster "Cave Monster").
  * This, the [Naughty List](/wiki/Naughty_List "Naughty List") (plus the quest related with it), [Festive Nymphs](/wiki/Festive_Nymph "Festive Nymph"), and ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") were the only Beesmas 2020 content that was extended.
  * Although a majority of the game refers to this mob as **Snowbear** , the message the player gets when they fail to defeat Snowbear says "**Snow Bear** ".
  * This, [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear") and [Shadow Bear](/wiki/Shadow_Bear "Shadow Bear") are the only faceless bears.
  * There was a glitch which causes the Snowbear to go idle, and it does not move around or attack the player. The cause of this glitch is unknown.
  * Snowbear has its own [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy") skin, the ![Snow Cub](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snow Cub](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins "Cub Buddy"), which was the final reward for [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear")'s 2021 [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") quest. 
    * However, unlike its counterpart, the Snow Cub skin has a face and a carrot for a nose, and uses the Ninja Animation Pack.
  * There is a glitch which causes Snowbear to spawn inside the Mushroom Field. The cause of this glitch is unknown.
  * Digital bee´s mind hack ability can make it teleport to Mushroom Field, Bamboo Field or Strawberry Field.
  * Although spawning in a field with no stuff, Snowbear has collision.
  * Snowbear's top hat is a retextured version of the [Psychedelic Top Hat](https://www.roblox.com/catalog/80293983/Psychedelic-Top-Hat).
