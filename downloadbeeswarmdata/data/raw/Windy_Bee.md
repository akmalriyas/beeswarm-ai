_This page is for the tamed version of Windy Bee. For the hostile version, see[Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee")._

**Windy Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") that hatches from a ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Windy Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Windy_Bee_Egg "Egg"), which can be obtained from donating ![Cloud Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Cloud Vials](/wiki/Cloud_Vial "Cloud Vial") after having previously donated a ![Spirit Petal](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Spirit Petal](/wiki/Spirit_Petal "Spirit Petal") to the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine"). 

Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite Treat, and the only ways to make it gifted are by feeding it a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

Windy Bee likes the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field") and the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field"). It dislikes the [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field") and the [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field").

  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee#List_of_Hive_Bonuses "Gifted Bee"): +15% [Instant White Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Instant_White_Conversion "System Page"), x2 Boosts From [Clouds](/wiki/Clouds "Clouds").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWindy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[White Boost]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens")** Grants x1.2 pollen from White [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") for 15s. Stacks up to 10 times.
  * **[[Rain Cloud]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Rain_Cloud "Ability Tokens")** Summons a Cloud in a different field that lasts for 60s (+10s per level). Clouds make flowers grow and grant +25% Pollen and 10% Unique Instant Conversion to nearby players (50% Pollen and 20% Unique IC if the player has a gifted Windy Bee). If another player's Windy Bee spawns a cloud in the field the player is on, a message will appear announcing "🌧️ [Username]'s Windy Bee summoned a Cloud in this field. 🌧️"
  * **[[Tornado]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Tornado "Ability Tokens")** Consumes your "[Haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens")" stacks to summon a Tornado which damages enemies and collects [Tokens](/wiki/Tokens "Tokens") and Pollen as it roams the field. Lifespan, speed, and damage are increased by the number of Haste stacks spent and bee level.

Windy Bee has a base pollen collection of **10![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **3 seconds**. That's equivalent to 3.33333 ![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWindy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Windy Bee's tornado path doesn't actually travel across the field randomly. It instead loosely follows a grid. This is slightly more noticeable when more haste tokens are spent and the tornado is faster.
  * It was formerly available to purchase for ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)800 Robux until the deadline, October 27 of 2019. 
    * This was the 3rd bee available to be bought for Robux; the second being [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") and the first being [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee").
  * There is a gate dedicated to it, known as the [Windy Bee Gate](/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate "Windy Bee Gate") or the 35 bee gate.
  * This, and [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") are the only bees to have a hostile variant.
  * This bee, [Music Bee](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee"), [Vector Bee](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee"), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee"), Fuzzy Bee, Buoyant Bee, Festive Bee and [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") are the only bees with three different ability tokens, the most of any bee.
  * This bee, [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee"), [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee"), and Fuzzy Bee are the only bees that have only one skin color.
  * This bee is the only bee to have the same skin color when it turns [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee").
  * This bee and Puppy Bee are the only event bees that don't have an 'enhanced' ability when gifted.
  * This bee is the only bee to have 2 gifted bonuses, excluding other Event bees who enhance their ability.
  * Currently, this bee, Bear Bee, [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"), Digital Bee and Vicious Bee are the only event bees that can't be bought in the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent").
  * This is the only Event Bee to dislike 2 fields.
  * This, along with Buoyant Bee, are the only bees to be able to produce a White Boost token. However Buoyant Bee only produces it through Surprise Parties.
  * The Tornado ability, [frogs](/wiki/Frog "Frog"), [Puppy Bee's](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee") [Fetch](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Fetch "Ability Tokens") ability, the [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy") and [Star Saw](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Saw "Passive Abilities") are the only entities that can pick up [items](/wiki/Items "Items") outside of personal tokens for the player.
  * This bee and Festive Bee are the only non-gifted bees that have tinted wings.
  * This bee was hinted at during the [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear") quest "Black Bear, Why?" when he says this: "Vicious Bee isn't the only bee that causes trouble! One time, I saw a strange bee destroy a whole field with powerful gusts of wind! I wonder if there are any other mysterious bees roaming free out there?"
  * At one point, a model of a Windy Bee could be found outside the map, alongside with a model of a Vicious Bee.
  * Supposedly, Windy Bee likes the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field") because dandelion seeds 'go wherever the wind takes them'.
