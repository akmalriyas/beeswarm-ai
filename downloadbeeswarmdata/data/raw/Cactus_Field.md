The **Cactus Field** is a [field](/wiki/Field "Field") located past the [Honey Bee Gate](/wiki/Honey_Bee_Gate "Honey Bee Gate") and lies right beside the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"). It is protected by the [Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf"). 

This field can spawn [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [treat](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), [strawberry](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"), [blueberry](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"), [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), [bitterberry](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"), and [stinger](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger") tokens when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Cactus Field are [Bomber Bee](/wiki/Bomber_Bee "Bomber Bee"), [Hasty Bee](/wiki/Hasty_Bee "Hasty Bee"), [Commander Bee](/wiki/Commander_Bee "Commander Bee"), [Demo Bee](/wiki/Demo_Bee "Demo Bee"), and [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"). Bees that dislike the Cactus Field are [Exhausted Bee](/wiki/Exhausted_Bee "Exhausted Bee"), [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"), [Music Bee](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee"), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), and [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"). 

This field grants Invigorating [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is planted here. 

A Level 4-7 [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") has a chance of spawning in this field. This is a 33×18 field, containing 594 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are white, red, and blue, in sizes of double and triple.

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCactus_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Field Booster](/wiki/Field_Booster "Field Booster") may give a Cactus [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +100% Cactus Field pollen for 15 minutes.
  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") will add 100% Cactus Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out. [![The Cactus Field Boost icon.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f9/Cactus_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080214) [![The Cactus Field Boost icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f9/Cactus_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080214)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f9/Cactus_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080214)

The Cactus Field Boost icon.

Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), and [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% - 300% Cactus Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't maxed out and is based on the type of dice used.
  * Cactus [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Cactus Field conversion and give s+5% instant Cactus Field conversion and +15% Cactus Field pollen for each stack. Cactus Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
  * The [Icicles](/wiki/Icicles "Icicles") [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") can grant up to +10% Cactus Field pollen.
  * It can be boosted by the [Shell Amulet](/wiki/Shell_Amulet "Shell Amulet"). If it has a Cactus Field Boost, it can boost the field by +5% to +27% depending on the amulet rarity.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCactus_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is the only field where three types of [treats](/wiki/Treats "Treats") can be found naturally in the same place.
  * This is the fourth-smallest field in the game, the smaller ones being the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"), [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field"), and the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field").
  * It is possible to hide behind the cactus closest to the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest") so the werewolf cannot attack the player.
  * This is one of six fields where [fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies") travel to, with the others being [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"), [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"), [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), and Strawberry Field.
  * This is one of three fields that is protected by the werewolf, the others being the Pumpkin Patch and the Pine Tree Forest.
