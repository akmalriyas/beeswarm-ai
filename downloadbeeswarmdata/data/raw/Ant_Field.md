The **Ant Field** is a [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") located past the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate") and near the [Top Ant Exterminators](/wiki/Top_Ant_Exterminators "Top Ant Exterminators") leaderboard. It can only be accessed by starting the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge") which will summon waves of [ants](/wiki/Ants "Ants"). 

This field can produce [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") and [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") tokens while collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). 

The only [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that likes the Ant Field is [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"). There are no bees that dislike the Ant Field. 

[Planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter") cannot be planted in this field, although [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") has stated that the Ant Field contains [Invigorating Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar"). 

This is a 12×32 field, containing 384 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are white, red, and blue, and the only natural size in single.

  * Using an [Ant Pass](/wiki/Ant_Pass "Ant Pass") to start the Ant Challenge will give 100% Instant Ant Field Conversion.
  * There is no possible to way to boost the pollen for the Ant Field.
  * The flowers can still be [pollinated](/wiki/Pollination "Pollination").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAnt_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * You can collect pollen from the Ant Field outside the challenge by using the [Sticker-Seeker](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker "Sticker-Seeker") or spawning [flames](/wiki/Flame "Flame") with the [Scythe](/wiki/Scythe "Scythe").
  * The Ant Field is the only [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") that does not have its own [badge](/wiki/Badges "Badges").
  * [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee") is the only bee to like the Ant Field; all other bees are neutral to this field.
  * The Ant Field is the only field to have exclusively single [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). 
    * The flowers on this field contain 50 pollen per flower, and instead of going up by 15 pollen per tier, go up 10 per [pollination](/wiki/Pollination "Pollination") tier up to 90, although pollen multiplier remains the same as regular flowers.
  * The Ant Field is the only field that can't be boosted with [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter"), the only field where a [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean") cannot be planted, the only field where [clouds](/wiki/Clouds "Clouds") cannot be summoned, the only field that had no Market Boost from the [Boost Market](/wiki/Boost_Market "Boost Market"), the only field that can't get [winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") from the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine"), and the only field where [planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter") cannot be planted.
  * The Ant Field is the smallest field in the game with 384 flowers, smaller than the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch") with 561 flowers and the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field") with 563 flowers.
  * The Ant Field is one of the 5 fields that are exclusively used for a challenge. The other others include the [Mixed Brick Field](/wiki/Mixed_Brick_Field "Mixed Brick Field"), [White Brick Field](/wiki/White_Brick_Field "White Brick Field"), [Blue Brick Field](/wiki/Blue_Brick_Field "Blue Brick Field") and [Red Brick Field](/wiki/Red_Brick_Field "Red Brick Field").
  * Players are able to glitch into the Ant Field without triggering the actual challenge. This can only be achieved by having a slow internet connection or [bear morph](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bear_Morph "Ability Tokens") and/or [haste+](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Areas "Buffs & Debuffs").
  * Despite being impossible to boost, the Ant Field has an unused [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost") icon.
