# Shops

**Shops** contain various [items](/wiki/Items "Items") that can be purchased with [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") or other currencies and, for some (usually found in mid to late-game shops), [crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") materials as well. They may have items such as [bags](/wiki/Category:Bags "Category:Bags"), [guards](/wiki/Category:Guards "Category:Guards"), [tools](/wiki/Category:Tools "Category:Tools"), eggs, and other miscellaneous items that can give more [capacity](/wiki/System_Page#Capacity_Multiplier "System Page") and other [buffs](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs "Buffs & Debuffs"). Some shops sell only one type of item and may have a price that increases every time the item is purchased, such as tickets.
