The **Tacky Planter** is a reusable planter added in 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in about 8 hours and stores around 15 million pollen. When harvested, it grants bonus ![Field Dice](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Field Dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice") and ![Sunflower Seed](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"). It can be purchased in [Dapper Bear's Shop](/wiki/Dapper_Bear%27s_Shop "Dapper Bear's Shop").

It grows 25% faster and grants 100% more pollen on harvest in the [Starter Zone](/wiki/Starter_Zone "Starter Zone") ([Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field"), [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"), [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field")), and grants 25% more Satisfying and Comforting Nectar. 

Harvesting a fully grown Tacky Planter has a 1/5 chance to spawn a [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom"). The spawned puffshroom is level 5, and has a: 

  * ~73.15% chance of being a Common Puffshroom,
  * ~21.95% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
  * ~4.75% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
  * ~0.15% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom.

When claimed, the planter gives up to 22 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, 2 of the tokens are guaranteed to be ![Field Dice](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Field Dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice") and ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"). The others are picked randomly from the following, non-exhaustive list (note that the list does not include rewards based on the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") the planter is in): 

  * Bonus

  * Other

  * Special
