**Onett's Lid Art** , or **Yard Art** is a Beesmas decoration which is unlocked after completing [Onett's](/wiki/Onett "Onett") Yard Art [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests"). 

The art is located past the [Bear Gate](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate") next to Onett. It can be used every 8 hours. When activated, the player receives 3 [temporary bees](/wiki/Bees#Summoned_Bees "Bees"), an assortment of items, and a [Guiding Star](/wiki/Guiding_Star "Guiding Star") is activated. 

If a player attempts to use the Lid Art before completing Onett's quest, it displays the message: This art is missing something... It's not very impactful.

## Appearance[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOnett%2527s_Lid_Art%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The Lid Art consists of (left to right) [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") on [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle"), [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear"), [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear"), [Bumble Bee](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee") and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug"), and a [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee") fence gate. 

The names confirmed by Onett so far are: Queen Spirit Bear (Spirit Bear), King Stick Bug (Stick Bug), and King Bumble Bee (Bumble Bee).

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOnett%2527s_Lid_Art%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Onett's Lid Art is the only Beesmas decoration to activate a passive ability, that being the Guiding Star.
  * Originally, the message that would appear if the player tried to use Onett's Lid Art before they completed [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett")'s quest was This art isn't as impactful without Baby Bee in the middle...
  * This, the [Gummy Beacon](/wiki/Gummy_Beacon "Gummy Beacon"), and the [Naughty List](/wiki/Naughty_List "Naughty List") are tied for the longest cooldown of Beesmas decorations of 8 hours.
  * This and the Gummy Beacon were the only ways to have multiple [Event bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") of the same kind, although they are temporary.
  * This art is a reference to the birth of Jesus, which is celebrated by people of Christian faith during Christmas.
  * This is one of the 4 machines that grant the player temporary bees, the others being the [Honeyday Candles](/wiki/Honeyday_Candles "Honeyday Candles"), the [Gummy Beacon](/wiki/Gummy_Beacon "Gummy Beacon") and the [Honey Wreath](/wiki/Honey_Wreath "Honey Wreath") (after completing [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)")'s quest).
  * Sometimes when players look at it from a certain view, some parts of it don't show up.
  * Having a Cobalt Bee from the Lid Art with Crimson Bee in your hive also activates both a red and blue pulse.
  * [Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") summoned by Onett's Lid Art are able to attack the mobs the player's actual bees are attacking. However, they cannot convert at the [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive").
  * Bees summoned by the Lid Art in 2020 may also have been gifted. It is unknown whether they granted a hive bonus or not.
  * This is the only way to summon a Guiding Star without a [Supreme](/wiki/Star_Amulet#Supreme_Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") or [Diamond](/wiki/Star_Amulet#Diamond_Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") Star Amulet with the respective passive.
