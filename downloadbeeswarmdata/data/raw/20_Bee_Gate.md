The **Ant Gate** , also called the **20 Bee Gate** , is a [gate](/wiki/Category:Gates "Category:Gates") that requires 20 [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to pass through. It is found near the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop"), behind the traveling/event bear (e.g. [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear")) area. The gate gets its name from the large [Army Ant](/wiki/Army_Ant "Army Ant") on it.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAnt_Gate%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This gate and the [Bear Gate](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate") are the only gates that don't have a bee to represent them.
  * This gate and the [Windy Bee Gate](/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate "Windy Bee Gate") are the only gates in the game that don't have a ![Royal Jelly](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9f/Royal_Jelly.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404020655)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token in the area behind it. 
    * However, the Windy Bee Gate has a ![Star Jelly](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e7/Star_Jelly.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404020706)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token, making this the only gate without any royal jelly tokens at all.
  * This gate and the Windy Bee Gate are also the only gates that is designed based on a component of the zone (e. g. The Ant Gate has the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge") in it).
  * The [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Gummy_Bee_Egg_Claim "Gummy Bee Egg Claim") is near this gate, to the side of the Ant Gate closest to the [hives](/wiki/Hive "Hive").
  * There is a ![Ticket](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Ticket.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404015820)[Ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") token on top of the gate.
  * The [Army Ant](/wiki/Army_Ant "Army Ant") on top of the gate has glowing eyes, but in the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge"), Army Ants (and all the other [ants](/wiki/Ants "Ants")) do not have glowing eyes.
  * As of the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), you will get teleported back to spawn if you manage to bypass the gate.
  * There is a secret path behind the Global Top Ant Exterminators Leaderboard, which leads to an ![Ant Pass](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Ant Pass](/wiki/Ant_Pass "Ant Pass") and the Gummy Bee Egg Claim.
  * This gate indirectly has a temporary quest giver, being [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") and his quest "Gummy Bear's Goo Years Beacon".
  * This and the [Basic Bee Gate](/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate "Basic Bee Gate") are the only gates that lead to an area where there is nothing able to be purchased with ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey").
  * This is the only gate to have a [mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") representing it.
