# Ticket

Ticket

[![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Ticket.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404015820)

_"Used to activate and purchase special things!"_

**Tickets** are a form of [currency](/wiki/Category:Currency "Category:Currency") added in the [2018-04-27 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-4-27 "Updates"). They can be used to purchase certain [items](/wiki/Items "Items").

    * ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5 **Tickets** for earning Cadet badges.
    * ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)10 **Tickets** for earning Hotshot badges.
    * ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)15 **Tickets** for earning Ace badges.
    * ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)25 **Tickets** for earning Master badges.
    * ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)40 **Tickets** for earning Grandmaster badges.

(![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)2,335 **Tickets** if not counting the currently impossible Sticker Stack Grandmaster Badge.)
  * The player can buy ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets** from the [Ticket Shop](/wiki/Ticket_Shop "Ticket Shop") with ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), located outside the [Mountain Top Shop](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Shop "Mountain Top Shop"). The price increases with every ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Ticket** bought.
  * Some [bears](/wiki/Category:Bears "Category:Bears") give ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets** as rewards for completing certain [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests").
  * Redeeming certain valid [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"): 
    * 38217 (Gives ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5 **Tickets**).
    * Bopmaster (Gives ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5 **Tickets**).
    * Crawlers (Gives ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5 **Tickets**).
    * Roof (Gives ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5 **Tickets**).
    * Connoisseur (Gives ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5 **Tickets**).
    * Wax (Gives ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5 **Tickets** \+ other items).
  * Buying them from the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop") for ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)Robux: 
    * 1 Ticket for 10 Robux ( ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)10 Robux per ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Ticket**).
    * 100 Tickets for 400 Robux ( ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)4 Robux per ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Ticket**).

    * 1,800 Tickets for 4,500 Robux ( ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)2.5 Robux per ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Ticket**).
  * The [Wealth Clock](/wiki/Wealth_Clock "Wealth Clock") gives ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets** every hour after interacting with it, and increases incrementally every hour, stopping at the fifth (1 ticket first time, 2 second time, 3 third time, 4 fourth time, and 5 fifth time and onwards).
  * They can spawn as a potential match from Normal, Mega, Night and Extreme [Memory Matches](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match").
  * Clicking on any Hidden [Sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") grants ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 **Ticket** each (excluding seeker-stickers).
  * Adding certain [Stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") to the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack") grants ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets**.
  * As a Reward for being in the top 100 of any daily leaderboard, ranging from 25, 50 to 100 ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets** depending on the leaderboard.
  * Redeeming a ![Ticket Voucher](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Ticket Voucher](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") grants ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)100 **Tickets**.
  * ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1,000 **Tickets** will be given if you already have a [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") after purchasing the [Robo Bear's Shop's](/wiki/Robo_Bear%27s_Shop "Robo Bear's Shop") [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") deal.



### Drops[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTicket%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * As a drop from [planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter"): 
    * The number of ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets** it can potentially drop depends on the type of planter.
    * ![Ticket Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Ticket Planters](/wiki/Ticket_Planter "Ticket Planter") always drop ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)10+ **Tickets** when harvested at full growth.
  * As a common drop from [Mythic Meteors](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower").
  * As a drop from [Stockings](/wiki/Stockings "Stockings").
  * There is a chance of getting a ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Ticket** as a reward for defeating [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs"). The probability depends on the type of mob as well as the player’s Loot Luck.
  * [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail"), and [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle") are guaranteed to grant ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets** upon defeat.
  * [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") will yield a limited amount of ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets** per day.
  * The [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick") will always drop ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 **Ticket** upon being captured. 
    * Capturing it for the 25th time rewards ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)100 **Tickets**.


  * Defeating [Snowbear](/wiki/Snowbear "Snowbear") will drop an amount of ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets** equal its level upon defeat.
  * Completing a [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party_Cake#Robo_Party "Robo Party Cake") will reward an amount of ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets** equal to the party's rank.
  * Through a ![Cub Buddy](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins "Cub Buddy") gift.
  * [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom") drop ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets** (the rarer the Puffshroom, the more it gives).
  * [Honeystorms](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm") are guaranteed to spawn ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Ticket** tokens.
  * As a [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge") Reward.
  * As a rare drop from [Festive Gifts](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens").
  * As a [Sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") reward, besides Moon and Debug variants (the rarer the sprout, the more ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets** it will yield; rare sprouts and above guarantee ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets**).
  * [Sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") may yield ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Tickets**.
  * Very rarely, [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") may generate a ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Ticket** token. The base probability is around 1/1000 (0.1%), however it does increase with [Loot Luck](/wiki/Loot_Luck "Loot Luck") and [Ticket Chance](/wiki/System_Page#Ticket_Chance "System Page").
