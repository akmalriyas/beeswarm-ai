# System Page

_This page is for stats that apply to the player, collecting[pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"), and the hive. For stats that apply to [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), see [Stats](/wiki/Stats "Stats")._

A small part of the System Page.

The **System Page** is shown by clicking the cog in the menu bar. It is in between the [Badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges") page and the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop") page. Here, the player can redeem [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), send [Trade Requests](/wiki/Trading "Trading"), view the [Global Leaderboard](/wiki/Leaderboards "Leaderboards"), use the [Cub Console](/wiki/Cub_Console "Cub Console"), view the players' stats, turn on and off in-game [music](/wiki/Music "Music"), toggle Pollen Text, Abbreviate Pollen, allow Trade Requests, display and hide other players' [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), toggle Reducing Strobing VFX, and toggle Auto-Jelly. Turning off all in-game music only silences the music, not the sound effects (such as the sounds of [Ability Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") and obtaining [Legendary](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"), [Mythic](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic"), [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"), and [Event](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event")). You can mute all sound by turning off the in-game sound on the Roblox menu. 

## Character Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The tab also lists many of the player's active stat values, though not all of them. The following values are listed and explained: 

### Convert Amount[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts the base conversion of your bees before multipliers by a certain amount.  
Example: A [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee") with 20 Convert Amount will convert ![Honey](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c6/Honey.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230410071605)100 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds instead of ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)80 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.  
It can be boosted with [amulets](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet"), the [B.B.M. Mask](/wiki/B.B.M._Mask "B.B.M. Mask"), the [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask "Honey Mask") and certain [belts](/wiki/Category:Belts "Category:Belts"). This stat was formerly named 'Conversion Amount' until it was changed in the [Beesmas 2019 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-12-23 "Updates"). 

### Convert Rate[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts your bees conversion by the following percentage.  
Example: A Basic Bee with 200% Convert Rate will convert ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)160 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds instead of ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)80 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.  
It can be boosted with amulets, ![Enzymes](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"), ![Marshmallow Bee](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Marshmallow Bees](/wiki/Marshmallow_Bee "Marshmallow Bee"), ![Super Smoothie](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Super Smoothies](/wiki/Super_Smoothie "Super Smoothie"), [Science Enhancement](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_NPCs "Buffs & Debuffs"), the Conversion Buff, the [Honeyday Event buff](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Other "Buffs & Debuffs"), the [Honey Badge](/wiki/Badges#Honey_Badge "Badges"), [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee"), the Yellow [Jellybean](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans"), Jellybean Sharing Bonus, certain [Stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") in the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack"), some [beequips](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") and most [Bags](/wiki/Bags "Bags"). This stat was formerly named 'Conversion Rate' until it was changed in the Beesmas 2019 update. 

### Convert Rate At Hive[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts your bees’ conversion by the following percentage at the [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"). This stat can stack with Convert Rate.  
Example: A level 1 Basic Bee with 200% Convert Rate and 200% Hive Convert Rate will convert ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)320 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds while converting at the hive, but it will only convert ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)160 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") outside of the hive.  
It can be increased with the [Pumpkin Badge](/wiki/Badges#Pumpkin_Badge "Badges"), Bubble Bloat and certain items. This buff was used to be "Hive Convert Rate" until the 2021-12-26 update. 

### Bonus Convert Amount[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Increases the base conversion of the player's bees. It can be increased by certain amulets and items. 

### [Loot Luck](/wiki/Loot_Luck "Loot Luck")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Loot Luck is a passive attribute that increases the chances of receiving rewards from defeated [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs").   
It can be increased with [Baby Love](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Baby_Love "Ability Tokens"), the [Clover Badge](/wiki/Badges#Clover_Badge "Badges"), [Baby Bee’s](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee") Gifted Hive Bonus and certain items.  
This stat was formerly named 'Luck' until it was changed in the [2019-04-05 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates"). 

### [Critical Chance](/wiki/Critical_Hits#Critical_Chance "Critical Hits")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The chance of your bees hitting stronger or collecting more pollen. More accurately - by how big your Critical Power is.  
Example: A Basic Bee with 50% Critical Chance will hit with increased damage half of the time.  
It can be boosted with amulets, ![Tropical Drink](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tropical Drinks](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink"), [Focus](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Focus "Ability Tokens"), the [Green Jellybean](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans"), Gifted [Commander Bee](/wiki/Commander_Bee "Commander Bee") and certain items. Currently, the highest critical chance a player can have at a time is 88%. 

### [Critical Power](/wiki/Critical_Hits#Critical_Power "Critical Hits")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

This stat multiplies how strong a Critical Hit will be. It applies to fighting and pollen collecting.  
Example: Basic Bee with 300% Critical Power (on critical hit) will hit with the power of 3 Basic Bees (3 damage).  
It can be boosted with the Ant Amulet, [Melody](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Melody "Ability Tokens"), the [Battle Badge](/wiki/Badges#Battle_Badge "Badges"), Gifted [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"), Gifted [Looker Bee](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee") and with certain items. The calculated max Critical Power is 695%. 

### [Super-Crit Chance](/wiki/Critical_Hits#Super-Crit_Chance "Critical Hits")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The possibility for a critical hit to Super-Crit. Super-Crits deals 33% more damage and collect twice as much pollen as a regular critical hit, and are instantly converted. When a hit is a Super-Crit, there will be a purple color indicating the damage and the **!** sign next to it. It can be boosted by having a Gifted Precise Bee, Tabby Blessing, Precision, a precise mark, the Bruteforce upgrade in the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"), purchasing Bloxiade in the [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge"), or using a ![Super Smoothie](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Super Smoothie](/wiki/Super_Smoothie "Super Smoothie"). The max possible Super-Crit Chance you can have at a time is 53% (assuming you are in the Robo Bear Challenge). 

### [Super-Crit Power](/wiki/Critical_Hits#Super-Crit_Power "Critical Hits")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The amount pollen is multiplied by when performing Super-Crits (after applying Critical Power). Super-crit attacks are only increased by 33% as much as Pollen Super-crits. It can only be boosted by the [Whistle](/wiki/Whistle "Whistle") and Kazoo Beequips, a [Cog Amulet](/wiki/Cog_Amulet "Cog Amulet") or temporarily boosted by the [Dark Scythe](/wiki/Dark_Scythe "Dark Scythe"). 

### Bee Ability Rate[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Increases the speed at which the player's bees create [Ability Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens").  
Example: If it takes a bee about 20 seconds to produce a token, with 20% Bee Ability Rate, the bee is likely to take ~16 seconds to produce the token.  
Bee ability rate can be boosted by Star Amulets, Motivating Nectar, certain masks, items, bee mutations, and Beequips. 

### [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Increases pollen from all sources (both from [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") and [tools](/wiki/Category:Tools "Category:Tools")).  
Example: With 100% pollen, the player can collect 1,000 pollen, and with 110% pollen, it will able to collect 1,100 pollen from the same amount of time and [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers").  
It can be increased with amulets, [Bear Morph](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bear_Morph "Ability Tokens"), [Inspire](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inspire "Ability Tokens"), Baby Love, [Pollen Marks](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mark "Ability Tokens"), the [Honeyday Event buff](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Other "Buffs & Debuffs"), from [Clouds](/wiki/Clouds "Clouds"), Gifted Basic Bee, Gifted Bear Bee, the [Periwinkle Jellybean](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans") or with certain items. 

### White Pollen[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts your white pollen collection. It can be increased with amulets, ![Tropical Drink](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tropical Drinks](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink"), ![Marshmallow Bee](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Marshmallow Bees](/wiki/Marshmallow_Bee "Marshmallow Bee"), [White Boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), the [Spider Badge](/wiki/Badges#Spider_Badge "Badges"), the [Coconut Badge](/wiki/Badges#Coconut_Badge "Badges"), Gifted [Shocked Bee](/wiki/Shocked_Bee "Shocked Bee"), the [White Jellybean](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans"), Wind Boosts, or with certain items. 

### Red Pollen[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts your red pollen collection. It can be increased with amulets, ![Red Extract](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Red Extracts](/wiki/Red_Extract "Red Extract"), [Red Boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), [Flame Heat](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Passives "Buffs & Debuffs"), the [Strawberry Badge](/wiki/Badges#Strawberry_Badge "Badges"), Gifted Rad Bee or with certain items. 

### Blue Pollen[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts your blue pollen collection. It can be increased with Amulets, ![Blue Extract](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blue Extracts](/wiki/Blue_Extract "Blue Extract"), [Blue Boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), with the [Bamboo Badge](/wiki/Badges#Bamboo_Badge "Badges"), Gifted Cool Bee or with certain items. 

### Bomb Pollen[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts your pollen collection from all bombs.  
Example: If the player collects 5,000 pollen with a Buzz Bomb token, with 200% Bomb Pollen they will collect 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.  
It can be increased with Amulets, [Bomb Combos](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens "Buffs & Debuffs"), Gifted Bomber Bee, Festive Mark, Black Jelly Bean or with certain items. 

### Buzz Bomb Pollen[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts your pollen collection from Buzz Bombs and [Fuzz Bombs](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Fuzz_Bombs "Ability Tokens"). It can be increased with amulets, Gifted Demo Bee, or with certain items.

Boosts your pollen collection from Blue Bombs. It can be increased with Amulets, Gifted Frosty Bee, or with certain items. 

### [Bubble Pollen](/wiki/Bubble#Bubble_Pollen "Bubble")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D20&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts your pollen collection from [bubbles](/wiki/Bubble "Bubble").  
Example: If the player collects 5,000 pollen with a bubble, with 200% Pollen from Bubbles they will collect 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.  
It can be increased with Gifted [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee"), [Cog Amulet](/wiki/Cog_Amulet "Cog Amulet"), [Bubble Mask](/wiki/Bubble_Mask "Bubble Mask") or the [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask "Diamond Mask"). 

### [Flame Pollen](/wiki/Flame#Flame_Pollen "Flame")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D21&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts your pollen collection from [flames](/wiki/Flame "Flame") created by bees, [ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), and [passive abilities](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities").  
Example: If the flames collect 1,000 over time, with 200% Pollen from Flames they will collect 2,000 pollen from the same flowers.  
It can be increased with Gifted [Fire Bee](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee"), [Cog Amulet](/wiki/Cog_Amulet "Cog Amulet"), or the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask"). 

### Bee Gather Pollen[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D22&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts pollen collection for all bees.  
Example: With 200% Bee Gather Pollen, Basic Bee will collect 20 pollen in 4 seconds.  
It can be increased with amulets, ![Glue](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Glues](/wiki/Glue "Glue"), Gifted [Music Bee](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee"), the [x2 Pollen from Bees Gamepass](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop") or with certain items. 

### Bee Ability Pollen[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D23&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts pollen collection from all bees' abilities and passives.  
It can be increased with Gifted [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee") Hive Bonus, certain [Beequips](/wiki/Beequips "Beequips"), or [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack"). 

### Tool Pollen[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D24&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Boosts your pollen collection from your tool.  
Example: With 100% (base) Tool Pollen, the player will collect 1,000 pollen with their tool at once. With 125% Tool Pollen player will collect 1,250 pollen with their tool at once at the same place.  
It can be increased with amulets, ![Glue](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Glues](/wiki/Glue "Glue"), Gifted [Carpenter Bee](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee"), with the [Stump Badge](/wiki/Badges#Stump_Badge "Badges") or with certain items. 

### [Instant Conversion](/wiki/Instant_Conversion "Instant Conversion")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D25&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Instantly converts your pollen when you are collecting pollen on a field.  
Example: a Basic Bee with 50% Instant Conversion that collects 10 pollen will make half of it instantly convert into ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"). You will have ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") and 5 pollen, so you can collect more pollen.  
It can be boosted with amulets, ![Enzymes](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"), the [Dandelion Badge](/wiki/Badges#Dandelion_Badge "Badges"), the [Cactus Badge](/wiki/Badges#Cactus_Badge "Badges"), Gifted [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee"), [Festive Blessing](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Blessing "Ability Tokens") buff, or with certain items. 

### Instant White Conversion[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D26&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Instantly converts white pollen you collect, in addition to Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the [Pineapple Badge](/wiki/Badges#Pineapple_Badge "Badges"), Gifted [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"), or with the [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister "Coconut Canister").

### Defense[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSystem_Page%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D37&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Reduces the damage that you take.  
Example: If a mob deals 50 damage, with 50% Defense it will deal 25 damage instead.  
It can be increased with the [Shell Amulet](/wiki/Shell_Amulet "Shell Amulet"), [hats](/wiki/Category:Hats "Category:Hats"), or with [guards](/wiki/Category:Guards "Category:Guards"). Having 100% defense means that you take no damage and can be achieved by activating the [emergency coconut shield](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Emergency_Coconut_Shield "Passive Abilities") passive. The maximum defense without the emergency coconut shield is 60%. This is achieved by having the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") or [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask "Diamond Mask") (+35%), both the [Crimson](/wiki/Crimson_Guard "Crimson Guard") and [Cobalt Guards](/wiki/Cobalt_Guard "Cobalt Guard") (+10% each), and +5% on a Shell Amulet. Defense was originally Damage Reduction.

[![The Auto-Jelly settings.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7c/3CDE71CC-E05C-4EAA-A98E-BD3E71C85BCB.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200105082826) [![The Auto-Jelly settings.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7c/3CDE71CC-E05C-4EAA-A98E-BD3E71C85BCB.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200105082826)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7c/3CDE71CC-E05C-4EAA-A98E-BD3E71C85BCB.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200105082826) [](/wiki/File:3CDE71CC-E05C-4EAA-A98E-BD3E71C85BCB.jpeg)

[![The message when an auto-jelly setting is on.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/11/AutoJellyReminder.png/revision/latest?cb=20200104054011) [![The message when an auto-jelly setting is on.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/11/AutoJellyReminder.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200104054011)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/11/AutoJellyReminder.png/revision/latest?cb=20200104054011) [](/wiki/File:AutoJellyReminder.png)

The message when an auto-jelly setting is on.

The **Auto-Jelly Settings** can be found at the bottom of the System Page. There are options for ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") or ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") to auto roll until the requirements are fulfilled. It can be used to help players obtain a bee they desire quickly. There are options for the requirement the jelly will roll into: 

  * Until Legendary: Jellies will auto roll until you get a [Legendary](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary") or [Mythic](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic") Bee.
  * Until Mythic: Jellies will auto roll until you get a Mythic Bee.
  * Require Gifted: Jellies will auto roll until you get a [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") or Mythic Bee.
  * Require Mutation: Jellies will auto roll until you get a [Mutated](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation") or Mythic Bee.



Multiple options can be turned on at a time, whilst also making the auto jelly stop on the combined requirement.  
Example: When the Legendary and Gifted options are turned on, the jellies will auto roll until you get a Gifted Legendary Bee. When the Legendary, Gifted, and Mutation options are turned on, the jellies will auto roll until you get a Gifted Legendary Bee with a Mutation. 

If the Until Legendary option or both the Until Legendary and Until Mythic options are turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Legendary Bee or Mythic Bee. If only the Require Gifted option is turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Bee or a Mythic Bee. If only the Require Mutation option is turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Bee or a Mutated Bee. If both the Require Gifted and Require Mutation options are turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Mutated Bee or Mythic Bee. 

If the Mutation option is turned on, but the bee you selected isn't radioactive, a pop-up after using a ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") will say that you can't auto-roll ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") for mutation. Unless the bee is radioactive, you will have to use the ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") one at a time. 

When using a ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") on a bee with an auto-jelly option enabled, a reminder appears after the message that the auto-jelly option is on.

The extra warning pop-up when selecting extremely rare options.

If you turn on the following options, an extra pop-up will warn the player that the options they selected are very rare: 

  * Mythic and Gifted.
  * Mythic and Mutation.
  * Mythic, Gifted, and Mutation.
  * Legendary, Gifted, and Mutation.

When using a ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") on a bee, another pop-up will appear, reminding the player that an auto-jelly setting is on and will use as many star jellies as it needs in order to fulfill its requirement. This pop-up will appear even if only the Until Gifted option is turned on. If you are using the Require Gifted option with ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), you will still receive the warning, but nothing will happen.
