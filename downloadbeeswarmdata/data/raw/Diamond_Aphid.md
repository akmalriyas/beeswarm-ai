**Aphids** are hostile [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") that have a 0.932% chance (1 in 107) of spawning from collecting [leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves"), with the chances increasing when a Field is experiencing a period of low activity (i.e. players are not visiting said field very often). Their level and health correlate to which [field](/wiki/Field "Field") the aphid spawns in, like [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee"). Sharing a similar trait with [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab"), [Chicks](/wiki/Chicks#Chick "Chicks"), [Hostage Chicks](/wiki/Chicks#Hostage_Chick "Chicks"), [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee"), [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Chicks#Mondo_Chick "Chicks"), and [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom"), players have a limited time (30 seconds) to defeat an aphid. After the time is up, the aphid despawns. The timer is shown above its health. Aphids attack in a similar matter to [Stick Nymphs](/wiki/Stick_Nymph "Stick Nymph") and [Giant Ants](/wiki/Ants "Ants"), following the nearest player around slowly. Aphids are public mobs, meaning that anyone can collect the drops regardless of whether or not they attacked it. 

Defeating an aphid awards 10 [Battle Points](/wiki/Battle_Points "Battle Points") (20 if defeating one of the 3 rare types), [Bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond"), and various other rewards. Some rewards have a cooldown, meaning that they will not be obtainable for a while after receiving them once, regardless of the level or type. However, you can still get them through other players spawning an aphid with said rewards at the expense of applying the same cooldown to them also.

The Diamond aphid possesses more health than the other aphids, which allows it to survive for a longer period of time. It looks similar to a [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee"), with a sky-blue body and white eyes, legs, and antennae.

## Tips[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAphid%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Jumping and/or running around aphids will prevent them from attacking effectively since they slowly follow players.
  * If players cannot defeat an aphid due to higher level and health, leveling up bees and having bees specializing in attack, such as [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"), will help kill the aphid faster.
  * Using various [consumables](/wiki/Category:Consumables "Category:Consumables") and [passives](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities") such as ![Stinger](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Stingers](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger"), ![Coconut](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Coconuts](/wiki/Coconut "Coconut"), ![Tropical Drink](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tropical Drinks](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink"), ![Oil](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Oils](/wiki/Oil "Oil"), or [Star Saw](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Saw "Passive Abilities") can help defeat aphids faster.
