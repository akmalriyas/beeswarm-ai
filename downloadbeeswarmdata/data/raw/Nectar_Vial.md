# Nectar Vial

[![Nectar Vials inside a player's inventory.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0b/AllNectarVials.png/revision/latest?cb=20241230001720) [![Nectar Vials inside a player's inventory.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0b/AllNectarVials.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20241230001720)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0b/AllNectarVials.png/revision/latest?cb=20241230001720) [](/wiki/File:AllNectarVials.png)

Nectar Vials inside a player's inventory.

**Nectar Vials** are a type of inventory [item](/wiki/Items "Items") that can be used to increase a player's current [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") by 4 hours when used. Using any Nectar Vial grants 4 hours of their respective nectar type. You can hold a maximum of 10 Nectar Vials of each type at a time. However, this limit can be bypassed by obtaining them through [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") or [Robux Packs](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop").

## Variants[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNectar_Vial%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

There is a total of six variants of Nectar Vials. Five of which; immediately grant 4 hours of a type of Nectar buff to the player. The other grants 4 hours of every Nectar buff to all players in the server. The type of Nectar that the player is granted depends on the type of Nectar Vial that they choose to use, with the exception of the ![Nectar Shower Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Nectar Shower Vial](/wiki/Nectar_Shower_Vial "Nectar Shower Vial").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNectar_Vial%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * While most of the Nectar Vials have a letter on them which represents the first letter of their name (C for Comforting, I for Invigorating, M for Motivating, S for Satisfying), the ![Refreshing Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Refreshing Vial](/wiki/Refreshing_Vial "Refreshing Vial") doesn't have an R on it to represent the first letter of its name. The ![Nectar Shower Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Nectar Shower Vial](/wiki/Nectar_Shower_Vial "Nectar Shower Vial") has no icon at all, as it just seems to be a mix of all the previous Nectars.
  * There is only a limited amount of Nectar Vials that can be obtained by the player without the use of the [Nectar Condenser](/wiki/Nectar_Condenser "Nectar Condenser"). 
    * For ![Comforting Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Comforting Vials](/wiki/Comforting_Vial "Comforting Vial"), the total amount that can be obtained is 26.
    * For ![Invigorating Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Invigorating Vials](/wiki/Invigorating_Vial "Invigorating Vial"), the total amount that can be obtained is 22.
    * For ![Motivating Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Motivating Vials](/wiki/Motivating_Vial "Motivating Vial"), the total amount that can be obtained is 13.
    * For ![Refreshing Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Refreshing Vials](/wiki/Refreshing_Vial "Refreshing Vial"), the total amount that can be obtained is 14.
    * For ![Satisfying Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Satisfying Vials](/wiki/Satisfying_Vial "Satisfying Vial"), the total amount that can be obtained is 19.
    * For ![Nectar Shower Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Nectar Shower Vials](/wiki/Nectar_Shower_Vial "Nectar Shower Vial"), the total amount that can be obtained is 34.
  * The ![Nectar Shower Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Nectar Shower Vial](/wiki/Nectar_Shower_Vial "Nectar Shower Vial") is the only vial that is not obtainable from the [Nectar Condenser](/wiki/Nectar_Condenser "Nectar Condenser").
  * Using Nectar Vials does count toward collecting nectar in [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests"). 
    * However, they do not contribute to the [Top Nectar Collector Leaderboards](/wiki/Daily_Top_Nectar_Collectors "Daily Top Nectar Collectors").
