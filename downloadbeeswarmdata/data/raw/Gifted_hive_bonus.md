# Gifted hive bonus

[![A bee can be identified as gifted if there is a yellow hexagon around its .](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c7/GiftedHive.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20180716213233)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c7/GiftedHive.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20180716213233) [](/wiki/File:GiftedHive.jpeg)

A bee can be identified as gifted if there is a yellow hexagon around its [hive slot](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot").

[![A bee becoming gifted when fed its favorite treat, star treat, gingerbread bear, or an aged gingerbread bear.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/19/A8E60B10-CD9D-4117-A06B-B8C81BEA15A3.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20200712072921)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/19/A8E60B10-CD9D-4117-A06B-B8C81BEA15A3.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200712072921) [](/wiki/File:A8E60B10-CD9D-4117-A06B-B8C81BEA15A3.jpeg)

A bee becoming gifted when fed its favorite treat, star treat, gingerbread bear, or an aged gingerbread bear.

**Gifted bees** are a special type of [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") with improved [stats](/wiki/Stats "Stats") (x1.5 more collection, convert rate, and attack) and a buff called a "Gifted Hive Bonus." It improves the performance of the player and/or their bees, similar to [badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges"). 

Every time the player uses a ![Royal Jelly](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9f/Royal_Jelly.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404020655)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") or hatches an [egg](/wiki/Egg "Egg"), they have a 0.4% (1 in 250) chance to get a gifted bee. Similarly, feeding a bee its favorite [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") has a chance for it to turn gifted, though this only happens with a 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance for [Rare bees](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"), 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance for [Epic bees](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"), 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance for [Common](/wiki/Bees/Common "Bees/Common") and [Legendary bees](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"), and 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance for [Mythic bees](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic") per favorite treat. Feeding a bee a ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") also has a 1% (1/100) chance of turning it into its gifted version. Feeding a bee an ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear") has a 1.1% (1/90.909091) chance of turning it into its gifted version. 

![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treats](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat") will always turn the chosen bee into its gifted version. This is the only method that can turn [Event bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") like [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee") and [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee") into a gifted bee (excluding gingerbread bears). If the player feeds a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat") to an already gifted bee, the bee will only gain the bond and no extra benefits. ![Star Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Egg](/wiki/Egg#Star_Egg "Egg") will always hatch into a non-event gifted bee that is not already found in the [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") unless the player already has all non-Event bees in their gifted forms, in which case it hatches into a random non-Event gifted bee instead. ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") will always turn the chosen bee into a random non-Event gifted bee, using the same odds as a royal jelly (except for a Gifted [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee")). 

A gifted bee's UI upon obtaining will cycle through colors, with text saying, "⭐ Your [type] Bee Transformed Into A Gifted [type] Bee!⭐" and play the following sound file: 

<https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Giftsong.ogg>

On top of the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop"), there is a chamber called the [Star Hall](/wiki/Star_Hall "Star Hall"). There, the player can generate [Star Amulets](/wiki/Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") and claim royal jellies depending on the number of gifted bees they currently have, as well as giving them Haste+ for 1 minute. 

You can tell what gifted bees you have by looking at the "⭐" icon in the bee menu. There is no star if the gifted variant hasn't been discovered yet, a faded star if it's been discovered but isn't in your hive, and a black star if you have it currently in your hive. This can help track what bees are needed for [Star Amulet](/wiki/Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") progress. 

## Effects[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGifted_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The bee is equipped with a new color scheme, unique to each type of bee, stained wings, and a star on its back and sides. The hive icon doesn't change color.
  * x1.5 [gather amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats").
  * x1.5 [convert rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page").
  * x1.5 [attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * Applies a Hive Bonus specific to that bee (see below list for details).
  * Improved certain [passive abilities](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Bee_Passives "Passive Abilities").
  * New ability token - [**[Inspire]**](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inspire "Ability Tokens"): Doubles pollen gained for 5 seconds, Stacks up to 50 times, giving up to a x51 bonus.
  * Able to generate [⭐ Gifted Ability] Tokens if the bee has one.
  * A glowing gold hexagon around its hive slot (as seen in the picture above).
  * Able to claim an additional royal jelly from the [Royal Jelly Dispenser](/wiki/Free_Royal_Jelly_Dispenser "Free Royal Jelly Dispenser") in the Star Hall per gifted bee in the hive.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGifted_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Since [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee") is now purchasable from the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop"), players who purchased a Bear Bee from the game launch event had their Bear Bee turned into a [First Edition](/wiki/First_Edition_Bee "First Edition Bee") gifted Bear Bee. 
    * Therefore, this makes Bear Bee the only [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") that can be gifted without the use of a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat") or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear").
  * Multiple gifted bees of the same type do _NOT_ stack their hive bonus multiple times, nor do they count towards the number of gifted bee types in the hive. However, they will still have improved stats over their normal version.
  * **Using a royal jelly or a non-gifted egg on a gifted bee will almost certainly make the bee LOSE ITS GIFTED STATUS. The chance of the new bee being gifted is 1/250.**
  * Windy Bee is the only bee to give two different gifted hive bonuses.
  * Tadpole Bee used to give two different gifted hive bonuses before the 2024-05-23 update.
