The **Daily Top Ant Field Collectors** was, at the time, one of the 66 [leaderboards](/wiki/Leaderboards "Leaderboards") in the game. Unlike other leaderboards, it did not appear in the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page"), nor anywhere else in-game. This leaderboard measured how much [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") players had collected in the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field") that day. It reset every day at 12:00 AM CST.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDaily_Top_Ant_Field_Collectors%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This and the Brick Field Leaderboards were the only leaderboards not to be displayed anywhere in the game.
  * This was the fourth leaderboard to be removed, the rest being the [Most Commando Captures](/wiki/Most_Commando_Captures "Most Commando Captures"), [Highest Snowbear Level](/wiki/Highest_Snowbear_Level "Highest Snowbear Level") and [Highest Robo Party Cake Rank](/wiki/Highest_Robo_Party_Cake_Rank "Highest Robo Party Cake Rank") leaderboard.
  * This leaderboard shares a reward with the [Top Ant Exterminators](/wiki/Top_Ant_Exterminators "Top Ant Exterminators") leaderboard. 
    * These are the only leaderboards to have shared rewards.
