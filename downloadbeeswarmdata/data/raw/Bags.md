# Bags

**Bags** are [accessories](/wiki/Category:Accessories "Category:Accessories") that can be bought in the various [shops](/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops") around the map. The main purpose of bags is to store [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") gathered by the player until it is full, to which the player will need to go to their [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") and convert it into [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), use a Coconut, use an [Instant Converter](/wiki/Instant_Converter "Instant Converter"), use a [Micro-Converter](/wiki/Micro-Converter "Micro-Converter"), use a [Honeysuckle](/wiki/Honeysuckle "Honeysuckle") (only converts some) or admire [Black Bear's](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear") [Honey Wreath](/wiki/Honey_Wreath "Honey Wreath") (only during Beesmas). When the player's bag is full, the following message is displayed:  Pollen container full. Go to your hive to make honey. They may use an [Instant Converter](/wiki/Instant_Converter "Instant Converter") to instantly get all their honey (costs 1 ticket) or use a micro-converter. Certain bags are intended to give the player additional boosts other than capacity, such as [Instant Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Instant_Conversion "System Page"), [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page"), [Bee Attack](/wiki/Bee_Attack "Bee Attack") and even [Passives](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities") (currently only from the [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister "Coconut Canister")). There are other accessories (such as [belt bags](/wiki/Category:Belts "Category:Belts")) that increase capacity. The Pouch bag is given automatically to new players. There are currently 12 bags in the game.
