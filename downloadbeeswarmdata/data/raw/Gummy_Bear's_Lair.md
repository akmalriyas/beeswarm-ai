# Gummy Bear's Lair

Gummy Bear's Lair.

The platform to talk to Gummy Bear. This was used for Gummy Bear's Beesmas 2020 quest, or now to teleport out of the lair. Only players with the Gummy Mask can use this.

**Gummy Bear's Lair** is a location that can only be accessed by having a [Goo Hotshot Badge](/wiki/Badges#Goo_Badge "Badges") or above. 

[![The prompt to enter the Gummy Lair.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bf/Eee4a72469aa45acd97d01685d93a3e1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20200831191859)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bf/Eee4a72469aa45acd97d01685d93a3e1.png/revision/latest?cb=20200831191859) [](/wiki/File:Eee4a72469aa45acd97d01685d93a3e1.png)

The prompt to enter the Gummy Lair.

To get into the lair, touch the [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") model on the [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Gummy_Bee_Egg_Claim "Gummy Bee Egg Claim") and use a [gumdrop](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops"). The player can reach the egg claim by going behind the [Ant Leaderboard](/wiki/Top_Ant_Exterminators "Top Ant Exterminators") and following the path (where there's also an [ant pass](/wiki/Ant_Pass "Ant Pass") token). They can also climb the Stinger Dispenser, go across the gate and reach the Egg Claim or use a cannon, such as the [Red](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon") or [Yellow](/wiki/Yellow_Cannon "Yellow Cannon") Cannon in combination with the [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute")/[Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider"). 

If the player doesn't have the Goo Hotshot Badge, then the following message will prompt:  Only Goo Hotshots can hear Gummy Bee...

[![The notification that appears when the player is on top of the Gummy Bee model and does not have the Goo Hotshot Badge.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/69/Capture73762.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20181201013000) [![The notification that appears when the player is on top of the Gummy Bee model and does not have the Goo Hotshot Badge.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/69/Capture73762.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20181201013000)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/69/Capture73762.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20181201013000) [](/wiki/File:Capture73762.jpg)

The notification that appears when the player is on top of the Gummy Bee model and does not have the Goo Hotshot Badge.

Otherwise, the player will be prompted to use gumdrops to enter the Lair with this message:

The Gummy Bee wants Gumdrops

The lair contains [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") and Gummy Bee. It also has the [Glue Dispenser](/wiki/Glue_Dispenser "Glue Dispenser"), which activates the [Unlimited Gumdrops](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities") passive, allowing [gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") to not decrease in count when used. Additionally, it has a shop that sells the [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask"), [Gummy Boots](/wiki/Gummy_Boots "Gummy Boots") and the [Gummyballer](/wiki/Gummyballer "Gummyballer").

Gummy Bear: "What's sweeter than honey?"  
"Glorious gumdrops... they're just the start!"  
"A sweet and sour, ooey-gooey universe..."  
"Can't you see it too?" (slight pause)  
"What's that Gummy Bee?"  
"They're not seeing clearly?"  
"YOU'RE ALWAYS RIGHT!"  
"GOO-dbye, beekeeper... HAH!"

After he finishes his speech, Gummy Bear will instantly kill the player, sending the player back to their [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"). 

If the player owns the Gummy Mask or the Gummy Boots, they can talk to Gummy Bear, but he does not give a quest, unless it is [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas"). It is not required to have either of the two equipped to talk to him.

## Notes[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGummy_Bear%2527s_Lair%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Players should avoid going to Gummy Bear's Lair if they have a lot of [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in their [container](/wiki/Bags "Bags"), as they will eventually die due to Gummy Bear killing them or by the player needing to reset their character. However, the player can avoid losing pollen by rejoining the game, using a [Micro-Converter](/wiki/Micro-Converter "Micro-Converter") to convert all of the pollen beforehand, or using a ![Whirligig](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Whirligig](/wiki/Whirligig "Whirligig"). 
    * During Beesmas 2020 and 2021, the player would be able to exit the lair if they have given Gummy Bear a present.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGummy_Bear%2527s_Lair%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Before Beesmas 2020, the only way to leave the lair without exiting or losing connection to the game is to die. The player can die by either courtesy of [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") or by resetting their character.
  * This is currently the only location not connected to the mountain; instead, it is high up in the sky. The player is able to see the silhouette of the lair if they look closely at the sky.
  * [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") refers to Gummy Bear as **naughty** in 2018, most likely because he kills the player upon finishing his dialogue, and the fact that he tried to "invade" the mountain.
  * Originally, the lair required the Goo Ace Badge or above, which was a mistake on [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)")'s end. It was quickly fixed to allow people with Goo Hotshot as well, so there could be more players that could actually access the lair.
  * This, the Ace Shop, and the [Badge Bearer's Guild](/wiki/Badge_Bearer%27s_Guild "Badge Bearer's Guild") are the only places that require [badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges") to enter them. 
    * Gummy Bear's Lair is the only location that requires a specific badge to enter.
  * Before the [2019-04-05 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates"), the [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") that was used to teleport was on top of the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent"). Now Gummy Bee is replaced with a model of [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") and was instead put at the [Ant Field](/wiki/Fields "Fields").
  * When Gummy Bear's Lair first came out, it said, “Gummy Bee wants 1 [Gumdrop](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops").” Now, it says “The Gummy Bee wants Gumdrops.” 
    * However, if the player is visiting Gummy Bear's Lair for the first time, standing on the Gummy Bee model will say "The Gummy Bee wants 1 Gumdrops."
  * [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") mentions how to get to the lair in her quest dialogue.
  * Some players may experience lag when they are getting close to Gummy Bear.
  * This shop and the [Petal Shop](/wiki/Petal_Shop "Petal Shop") are the only shops with items in a triangular formation.
  * The player can experience minor glitches in the lair. 
    * When the player walks up to Gummy Bear, he sometimes fails to kill the player after he finishes his speech.
    * After Gummy Bear kills the player, their body parts will slip down until they reach the end of the shop. If the player presses E at the right time, they will be stuck in the shop, resulting in the [Shop Trap glitch](/wiki/Glitches#Shop_Trap "Glitches"). Only leaving the game could fix this glitch.
    * On rare occasions, [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") can push the player to Gummy Bear's Lair, making them glitch into it.
