**Bubble Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Epic bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"). 

Bubble Bee's favorite treat is ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"). 

Bubble Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field") and the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). It dislikes the [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBubble_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 160 [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +15% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +80 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +2 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.25 Bubble Pollen.



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBubble_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Blue Bomb]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")** Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding blue [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") (+10% pollen per level). Combo with other Bombs to increase power.
  * **[[Passive:](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gathering_Bubbles "Passive Abilities") [Gathering Bubbles]](/wiki/Bubble#Gathering_Bubbles "Bubble")** 35% chance (50% if Gifted) to spawn a [bubble](/wiki/Bubble "Bubble") when gathering. If any Player touches it, it pops, collecting 4 red/6 white/8 blue pollen (+15% per gifted blue bee type, +5% per bee level) from 29 nearby Flowers and causing them to grow.

Bubble Bee has a base pollen collection of **10![Blue Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBubble_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Bubble Bee is the blue counterpart of [Fire Bee](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee").
  * Bubble Bee emits a bubble effect, a trait also shared with [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee") and its gifted variant.
  * Bubble Bee and [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee") are the only [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that have the Gathering Bubbles passive ability.
  * The [Bubble Mask](/wiki/Bubble_Mask "Bubble Mask"), which can be purchased in the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ"), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Bubble Bee.
  * While gathering in the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), it has an uncommon chance to find a Sitting Green Shirt Bear [Sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker").
  * In spite of this bee's name, Bubble Bee has nothing to do with [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man"). 
    * However, Bubble Bee Man did give out a ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Bubble Bee Jelly](/wiki/Egg#Bubble_Bee_Jelly "Egg") in [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") 2018 and a ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Bubble Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Bubble_Bee_Egg "Egg") in Beesmas 2020 and Beesmas 2021.
