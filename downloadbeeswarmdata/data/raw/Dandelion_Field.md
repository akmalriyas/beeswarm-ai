The **Dandelion Field** is one of the five [starting](/wiki/Starter_Zone "Starter Zone") [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") any player is able to visit upon starting the game. There are no [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") that protect this field. 

This field can produce [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [red boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), blue boost, [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [glue](/wiki/Glue "Glue"), [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter"), [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), and [treat](/wiki/Treat "Treat") tokens when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen").

Bees that like the Dandelion Field are [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"), [Bomber Bee](/wiki/Bomber_Bee "Bomber Bee"), [Demo Bee](/wiki/Demo_Bee "Demo Bee"), [Exhausted Bee](/wiki/Exhausted_Bee "Exhausted Bee"), [Music Bee](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee"), [Stubborn Bee](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee"), [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"), [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee"), and [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee"). Bees that dislike the Dandelion Field are [Commander Bee](/wiki/Commander_Bee "Commander Bee"), [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee"), and [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"). 

This field gives Comforting [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is placed here. 

A level 1-5 [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") can spawn here. 

This is an 18x36 field, containing 648 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are white, red, and blue, in sizes of single and double.

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDandelion_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Field Booster](/wiki/Field_Booster "Field Booster") may give a Dandelion [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +300% Dandelion Field pollen for 15 minutes.
  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") in the field will add 100% Dandelion Field pollen onto the current boost if it isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100%-300% Dandelion Field pollen for 15 minutes based on the dice used if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Dandelion [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of 20% instant Dandelion Field conversion and give +5% instant Dandelion Field conversion and +25% Dandelion Field pollen for each stack. Dandelion Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
  * Redeeming certain valid codes: 
    * Jumpstart (Activates Dandelion Field Code Buff + other stuff).
    * PlushFriday (Gives Dandelion Field Code Buff + other stuff).
  * It can be boosted by the [Shell Amulet](/wiki/Shell_Amulet "Shell Amulet"). If it has a Dandelion Field Boost, it can boost the field by +5% to +30% depending on the amulet rarity.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDandelion_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Before the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), the Dandelion Field was the only field that was mob-proof, meaning no mobs could spawn/attack there.
  * There is a [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token on top of the tallest dandelion which can be obtained by using the [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute")/[Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider") with cannons or from a high place. 
    * This is also the only dandelion in the field that has collision at its top.
  * The [Bubble Wand](/wiki/Bubble_Wand "Bubble Wand") model on the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ") can grow a face and blow [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") onto this field, along with the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), and [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field").
  * The [All-Time Top Honeymakers Leaderboard](/wiki/All-Time_Top_Honeymakers "All-Time Top Honeymakers") is adjacent to the Dandelion Field.
  * There used to be a [glitch](/wiki/Glitches "Glitches") where [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") could fall from the tallest mushroom and land in the Dandelion Field. This has now been patched.
  * During the Ready Player Two event, the field can be seen glitching after using [the Computer](/wiki/The_Computer "The Computer").
  * [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett") threatens to remove every field but the Dandelion Field if the [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") and [bears](/wiki/Bears "Bears") don't stop complaining in his Star Journey 2 quest dialogue. He then follows this up stating he wouldn't really do this though.
