# Stick Cub

[![The default skin.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3c/Cub_Buddy.png/revision/latest?cb=20200710143249) [![The default skin.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3c/Cub_Buddy.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/174?cb=20200710143249)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3c/Cub_Buddy.png/revision/latest?cb=20200710143249) [](/wiki/File:Cub_Buddy.png)

The default skin.

A **Cub Buddy** is a small, baby version of a [bear](/wiki/Bears "Bears") that follows the player around, collects tokens, and creates gifts. Whenever the player's cub buddy gets too far away, it will teleport back to them. 

Cub buddies have settings that can be accessed and viewed through the [Cub Console](/wiki/Cub_Console "Cub Console"), which is located on the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page"). 

When the player's cub buddy produces [items](/wiki/Items "Items"), it dances, emits heart particles, and an in-game message will pop up, saying: 

🐻(Cub Buddy Name) made some gifts!

[![The Cub Console.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fd/Cub_Console_New_%28Sticker_Update%29.png/revision/latest?cb=20240122214745) [![The Cub Console.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fd/Cub_Console_New_%28Sticker_Update%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240122214745)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fd/Cub_Console_New_%28Sticker_Update%29.png/revision/latest?cb=20240122214745) [](/wiki/File:Cub_Console_New_\(Sticker_Update\).png)

The Cub Console.

The Cub Console can be accessed by clicking on the green Cub Console button under the options menu, and allows the player to customize and check their Cub Buddy. The player can equip or unequip their Cub Buddy, modify its name, and view its statistics. 

If the player has a Cub Buddy, they may rename it by clicking on the "Name" window, typing in the desired name, and pressing enter. This name is subject to filtering, and may be rejected by the system. 

Cub Buddies age depending on how long it has been equipped while the player is in-game. If it is at the age of newborn, that means that it has been equipped for less than an hour. Once it has been equipped for more than one hour, the Cub Console will measure its age in hours. Once it has been equipped for more than 24 hours, the Cub Console will measure its age in days and hours. Once it has been equipped for more than 3 days, the Cub Console will no longer measure its age in hours. Instead, the Cub Console will solely measure its age in weeks and days. Once it has been equipped for more than 4 weeks, the Cub Console will measure its age in months and weeks.

## Possible Gift Rewards[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCub_Buddy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Cub buddies will produce gifts in a ring of 5 tokens, which usually contain common items like ![Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), but can also produce rarer items like tickets or ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"). When a cub buddy starts to dance and heart particles appear above its head, that means it's about to produce some gifts. Cub buddies produce gifts every 1–5 minutes. Similar to [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") and [leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves"), the rewards granted by cub buddies typically depend on the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") the player stands in (e.g. getting 3 [sunflower seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed") while standing in the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field")).

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCub_Buddy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * While Bee Bear said that the cub buddies are dolls animated by "Beesmas magic," [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear") said, "He says the [Cub Buddies] are animated by the magic of Beesmas... But in reality, Bee Bear had to build and program them himself.” Also, [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") said, "They're just robots, you know. But they're so lifelike, it's uncanny!"
  * If the player bought the Cub Buddy Launch Pack, and then completed Bee Bear's 10th quest, the player was rewarded with 250 tickets since the player can only have one of each cub buddy skin. 
    * If the process is reversed, the player will instead be rewarded with 250 tickets in addition to the 250 tickets that they would normally get for a total of 500 tickets and the Noob Cub skin for purchasing the pack.
  * When the player gets their first cub buddy, it will automatically be named "Buddy".
  * A cub buddy uses the wave emote when idle, the point emote when collecting a token, the dance emote before it produces gifts, and the cheer emote when it makes the gifts.
  * Most cub buddies use the [Cartoony Animation Package](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/56/Cartoony-Animation-Package). The Brown Cub, Snow Cub, Robo Cub, Peppermint Robo Cub, and Gloomy Cub are the only cubs to use a different one, being the [Knight Animation Package](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/68/Knight-Animation-Package), the [Ninja Animation Package](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/75/Ninja-Animation-Package) and [Robot Animation Package](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/82/Robot-Animation-Pack), and [Elder Animation Package](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/48/Elder-Animation-Package) respectively. 
    * Coincidentally, the Cartoony Animation Package is the one used by [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear"), [Noob Bear](/wiki/Noob_Bear "Noob Bear"), and [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear").
  * There used to be a [glitch](/wiki/Glitches "Glitches") that would make cub buddies "wear" an item if they teleported on top of it, such as the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask"), [Elite Blue Guard](/wiki/Elite_Blue_Guard "Elite Blue Guard"), and [Riley Guard](/wiki/Riley_Guard "Riley Guard"). This could also happen if the owner died/reset on top of the cub buddy. This was because the cub buddy is a Humanoid Model. This [Glitch](/wiki/Glitches "Glitches") was patched in an unknown update.
  * There is a glitch that causes cub buddies to stay in the game after their owners leave. The cub buddy can still collect any public tokens when it is left in the game, but it doesn't give the rewards to their owner.
  * Cub buddies used to be the most expensive item in the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent"), costing 2,000 tickets.
