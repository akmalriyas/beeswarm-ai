A **Camo Bandana** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 8 [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of three. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") with a "[Bomb](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")" ability.
