**Puppy Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event"). It hatches out of a ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Puppy Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Puppy_Bee_Egg "Egg"), which is available in the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") for ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)500 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"). 

Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, and the only way to make it gifted is by feeding it a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

Puppy Bee likes the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field") and the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"). It dislikes the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field").

### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPuppy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Fetch]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Fetch "Ability Tokens")** Creates a ball that collects tokens and increases pollen by 100% (+15% per [Level](/wiki/Bond "Bond")) as you and Puppy Bee kick it to each other. Collection increases per kick. Combo hits to win [Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat").
  * **[[Puppy Love]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Puppy_Love "Ability Tokens")** Increases your bond with up to 10 nearby bees by 60 (+20 per lvl). Grants +10% Bee Movespeed and +50% Bee Gather Amount for 10 seconds (+1s per lvl).

Puppy Bee has a base pollen collection of **25![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. That's equivalent to 6.25 ![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPuppy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * When Puppy Bee came out on the [2018-09-10 Update](/wiki/Updates#2018-09-10 "Updates"), it was on sale in the Ticket Tent for 250 tickets until September 22, 2018.
  * It takes 3 hits of the ball by the owner and 3 hits by the Puppy Bee (3 volleys) to drop a [treat token](/wiki/Treat "Treat"). The more times the ball is hit in a row, the more treats are earned from the treat token (Doubled every time a combo is done, starting at 1, capping at 256). 
    * Once the treat token reaches the capped amount of treats, the ball will permanently stay light green and will not be able to produce any more treat tokens.
    * It takes a total of 54 hits to reach the max amount of treats, and 60 to get 10x Reindeer Guidance.
    * When a treat token of this kind is collected, even if more than 1 treat is earned from the treat token, it will always count as 1 treat token collected in quests.
  * Puppy Bee was the first bee to be able to create treat tokens, the second being [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") with its [festive gift](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens") ability.
  * Puppy Bee was likely made to be [Tabby Bee's](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee") canine counterpart.
  * Sometimes, the ball spawned by the fetch token can get stuck, allowing the player and Puppy Bee to easily pass it back and forth.
  * Puppy Bee was the first [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that has a 3D model on its face (its tongue).
  * Puppy Bee, [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"), and Tabby Bee are currently the only bees that were sold at a discount for a limited amount of time.
  * This bee, Bear Bee, [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"), Tabby Bee, Festive Bee, [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee"), and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") are the only Event bees that can be a [First Edition Bee](/wiki/First_Edition_Bee "First Edition Bee"). 
    * First Edition Puppy Bee could have been obtained by buying it before September 22, 2018.
  * Treat tokens produced by Puppy Bee's fetch can be collected with a [Token Link](/wiki/Ability_Token#Token_Link "Ability Token"), similar to [Honey Gifts](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Honey_Gift "Ability Tokens").
  * Puppy Bee is the third bee to produce a token that has its face on it, the second being Tabby Bee, and the first being [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee").
  * This is the only bee that is able to raise the bond of other bees, through its puppy love token.
  * This bee, [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"), [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee"), [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee"), and Windy Bee are the only Event bees that don't have an 'enhanced' ability if gifted.
  * The puppy love token glows when collected.
  * Puppy Bee's wings clip through its ears.
  * Bee Bear's Puppy Bee is male.
  * This and Festive Bee are the only bees that can produce gingerbread bears.
  * This bee, Festive Bee, and [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee") are the only bees that have an exclusive beequip for themselves.
  * During the Beesmas 2020 Event, players could purchase a special bundle for ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)800 Robux in the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop") that would grant them a ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Puppy Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Puppy_Bee_Egg "Egg"), the special ![Reindeer Antlers](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Reindeer Antlers](/wiki/Reindeer_Antlers "Reindeer Antlers") Beequip specifically made for the Puppy Bee, and ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)50 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") as a bonus. 
    * If the player already had a Puppy Bee when they bought the bundle, they would get 500 tickets instead.
    * This makes Puppy Bee the only bee available to purchase in the Robux Shop twice.
  * This bee, along with [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), Tabby Bee, [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"), and Bear Bee are the only bees based on real animals.
