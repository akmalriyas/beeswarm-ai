The **Marshmallow Bee** is an [inventory item](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory") that was added during the [Egg Hunt 2019](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2019 "Egg Hunt 2019") event. It has a capacity limit of 25, which can only be exceeded by [code](/wiki/Codes "Codes") and [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests") rewards. Collecting 3 Marshmallow Bees and handing them in to the [Egg Hunt Info](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_Info "Egg Hunt Info") gave the player the [Flight of the Bumble Egg](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Bumble_Egg "Flight of the Bumble Egg") and a [badge](/wiki/Badges#Unobtainable "Badges") during the Egg Hunt 2019 Event. 

When used, it grants the following [buffs](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Items "Buffs & Debuffs") for 30 minutes: 

  * +50% [White Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#White_Pollen "System Page").
  * +50% [Capacity](/wiki/System_Page#Capacity_Multiplier "System Page").
  * +250% [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarshmallow_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is the only item to use the word "eat" instead of "use" in the pop-up when double clicked in the inventory.
  * This is one of the four [consumables](/wiki/Category:Consumables "Category:Consumables") that increase capacity, the others being the ![Purple Potion](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Purple Potion](/wiki/Purple_Potion "Purple Potion"), ![Super Smoothie](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Super Smoothie](/wiki/Super_Smoothie "Super Smoothie") and ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake").
  * There are sugar crystals on the Marshmallow Bee, something most real life marshmallows don't have. This may have taken influence from/be a reference to the brand [Peeps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peeps "wikipedia:Peeps"), as they are also animal-shaped marshmallows coated in sugar, and are popular during Easter.
  * Before the [April 6th, 2020 update](/wiki/Updates#2020-04-06 "Updates"), there was no capacity limit on how many Marshmallow Bees could be stored in the player's inventory. This was likely because the player could only obtain a limited amount of Marshmallow Bees prior to this update.
  * Marshmallow bees are one of the few consumable buffs that won't be overridden by a ![Super Smoothie](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Super Smoothie](/wiki/Super_Smoothie "Super Smoothie") buff. The others being ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake"), ![Stinger](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Stingers](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger"), ![Jelly Beans](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Jelly Beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans") and ![Glitter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter").
  * Marshmallow Bee is the only bee that the player can't have in their hive.
