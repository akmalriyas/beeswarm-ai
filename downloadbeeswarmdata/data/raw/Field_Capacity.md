# Field Capacity

[![The Pepper Patch Capacity Buff Icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/88/Field_Capacity_Buff_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20201105221345)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/88/Field_Capacity_Buff_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20201105221345) [](/wiki/File:Field_Capacity_Buff_Icon.png)

The Pepper Patch Capacity Buff Icon.

**Field Capacity** is a [mechanic](/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") that increases the player's [Capacity Multiplier](/wiki/Capacity "Capacity") depending on what kind of [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") they are in. There are 2 types of field capacity: colored field capacity (shown as a stat) and field capacity for a specific field (shown as a buff). 

## Ways to Obtain

  * Donating certain stickers to the sticker stack can grant field capacity.
  * Red Field Capacity can be obtained from [Riley Bee's](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee") gifted hive bonus, the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") and [Scorching Star](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Scorching_Star "Passive Abilities").
  * Blue Field Capacity can be obtained from [Bucko Bee's](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee") gifted hive bonus and [Bubble Bloat](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Passives "Buffs & Debuffs") (Bubble Bloat can only be obtained if the player has [Pop Star](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities") passive on [Supreme Star Amulet](/wiki/Star_Amulet "Star Amulet")).
  * White Field Capacity can be obtained from [Exhausted Bee's](/wiki/Exhausted_Bee "Exhausted Bee") gifted hive bonus, the [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask") and [Gummy Star](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Star "Passive Abilities").
  * Coconut Field Capacity can be obtained from the Coconut Canister (x1.25).
  * Redeeming certain valid [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"): 
    * ThreeBeeVee (gives [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field") Code Buff, granting +100% Mountain Top Field Capacity).
  * Using certain [Beequips](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") that include a Field Capacity boost stat. 
    * The ![Warm Scarf](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/43/Warm_Scarf.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230409130940)[Warm Scarf](/wiki/Warm_Scarf "Warm Scarf") grants +1% to +3% Red and White Field Capacity.
    * The ![Pinecone](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a7/Pinecone.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230409131349)[Pinecone](/wiki/Pinecone "Pinecone") grants +8% to +15% Pine Tree Forest Capacity.
    * The ![Snow Tiara](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snow Tiara](/wiki/Snow_Tiara "Snow Tiara") grants +2% to +5% Blue Field Capacity and +3% to +5% White Field Capacity.
    * The ![Rose Headband](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Rose Headband](/wiki/Rose_Headband "Rose Headband") grants 14% to +40% Rose Field Capacity.

## Colored Field Capacity Formula

The following formula shows how much capacity multiplier a player gets in a field: 

Let x equal the colored capacity multiplier.  
Let y equal the percentage of that [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") type in the field.

## Trivia

  * The [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field") is the only field where the player is guaranteed to get 100% of a colored capacity bonus, specifically white field capacity. 
    * This is achievable with Exhausted Bee's gifted hive bonus, by using the [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask"), activating Gummy Star, the Warm Scarf's hive bonus, or the Snow Tiara's hive bonus.
    * The only opposite version of this is in the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"); it generates no white flowers and therefore doesn't give white field capacity.
    * It is also possible to get 100% of a colored capacity bonus on any other field if they generate entirely with one color of flowers, but the odds of a field generating like that is exceptionally rare.
