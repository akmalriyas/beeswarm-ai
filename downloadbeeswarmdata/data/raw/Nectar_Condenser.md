## Information

### Usage

Convert nectar into nectar vials

### Requirement(s)

35 bees

### Cooldown

5 seconds

### Location

[35 Bee Zone](/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate "Windy Bee Gate")

  
_Not to be confused with the[Nectar Pot](/wiki/Nectar_Pot "Nectar Pot"), located in 30 Bee Zone, used to store one type of nectar at a time._

The **Nectar Condenser** is a machine added in the [January 12, 2024 update](/wiki/Updates#2024-01-12 "Updates") and is located past the [Windy Bee Gate](/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate "Windy Bee Gate") near the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine"). It allows the player to condense 12 hours of any [nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") to receive a [vial](/wiki/Nectar_Vial "Nectar Vial") that holds 4 hours of nectar. This vial can be used at any time and grants 4 hours of that nectar type. It requires at least 35 bees to use and access. The Nectar Condenser cannot be used to make ![Nectar Shower Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Nectar Shower Vials](/wiki/Nectar_Shower_Vial "Nectar Shower Vial"). 

[![The menu of the Nectar Condenser.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/06/Nectar_condenser_menu.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240113105619) [![The menu of the Nectar Condenser.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/06/Nectar_condenser_menu.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240113105619)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/06/Nectar_condenser_menu.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240113105619) [](/wiki/File:Nectar_condenser_menu.jpg)

The menu of the Nectar Condenser.

When condensing nectar, it has a 1/25 (4%) chance to drop a [sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") corresponding to the nectar condensed. The place where it drops depends on the nectar that was condensed: 

  * Refreshing in [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field").
  * Satisfying in [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field").
  * Motivating in [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field").
  * Comforting in [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field").
  * Invigorating in [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch").



If you try to condense a nectar but already have 10 or more of that type of vial, a message will pop up saying: “You already have 10 or more [nectar type] Vials."

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNectar_Condenser%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * By default, the model of the Nectar Condenser is set to be purple and has a Motivating Nectar decal. After its first use, the Nectar Condenser will always show the last nectar type condensed by the player.
  * The hitbox of the Nectar Condenser is different from its shape. You can be a bit off to the side on the Nectar Condenser and won’t fall. This is most likely due to the fact its placeholder model shared one with the [Nectar Pot](/wiki/Nectar_Pot "Nectar Pot") during testing, and Onett never changed the hitbox for it.
  * The player can reach the Nectar Condenser even if they don't have 35 bees, by jumping off the roof of the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ") and gliding down to it, although they won't be able to use it.
