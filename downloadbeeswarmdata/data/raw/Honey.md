# Honey

[![The honey icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c6/Honey.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410071605)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c6/Honey.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410071605)

The honey icon.

**Honey** is the main [currency](/wiki/Category:Currency "Category:Currency") in [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"). 

## Overview[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

### Usage[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Honey** can be used to purchase many [items](/wiki/Items "Items") such as [tools](/wiki/Tools "Tools"), [bags](/wiki/Bags "Bags"), and in-game [accessories](/wiki/Category:Accessories "Category:Accessories") such as [guards](/wiki/Category:Guards "Category:Guards") and [gliding tools](/wiki/Category:Gliding_Tools "Category:Gliding Tools"), and to play [Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match"). Honey can also be used to purchase products such as ![Ticket](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Ticket.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404015820)[Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), ![Basic Egg](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fa/Basic_Egg.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404043532)[Basic Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Basic_Egg "Egg"), ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), ![Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), and [hive slots](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot"). In addition, Honey used along with ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), could have been used to purchase [Market Boosts](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Areas "Buffs & Debuffs") from the [Boost Market](/wiki/Boost_Market "Boost Market"), which is now removed. The price of many [consumables](/wiki/Category:Consumables "Category:Consumables") increases with each purchase, for example, ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Basic Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Basic_Egg "Egg"). 

### Obtaining Honey[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Honey is usually obtained by converting [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") into it. It can be converted at the [Hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") or (partly) instantly converted using an [Instant Converter](/wiki/Instant_Converter "Instant Converter") or with a ![Micro-Converter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Micro-Converter](/wiki/Micro-Converter "Micro-Converter"), catching a falling ![Coconut](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Coconut](/wiki/Coconut "Coconut") or Science Bear's falling [Beesmas Light](/wiki/Beesmas_Lights "Beesmas Lights") (only during Beesmas), activating [Coin Scatter](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Coin_Scatter "Passive Abilities") and/or [Diamond Drain](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Diamond_Drain "Passive Abilities"). Honey can also be obtained by completing [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests"), redeeming [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), defeating [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs"), and collecting [Sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") and [Goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo") from [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"). [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee")'s [Impale](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Impale "Ability Tokens") ability spawns a honey token if it hits a [mob](/wiki/Mob "Mob"). [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee")'s [festive gift](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens") ability always spawns Honey tokens. Some other [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") have the ability to give a [Honey Gift](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Honey_Gift "Ability Tokens") or a [Token Link](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Token_Link "Ability Tokens"). Certain [hats](/wiki/Category:Hats "Category:Hats"), guards, and [amulets](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet") have the ability to [instantly convert](/wiki/Instant_Conversion "Instant Conversion") a percentage of pollen into honey. Popping [bubbles](/wiki/Bubble "Bubble"), spawning a [flame](/wiki/Flame "Flame"), having field conversion such as [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind"), collecting a [Bomb token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens") produced by a bee can result in instant conversion as well. The player can also get honey for participating in the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge"). Honey tokens can appear when the player collects pollen in a [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") and can be obtained from [Honeystorms](/wiki/Honeystorms "Honeystorms"). The [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower") awards Honey tokens after catching a meteor. The [Honey Dispenser](/wiki/Honey_Dispenser "Honey Dispenser") is also an option, however, it is only available to those who are in the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](https://www.roblox.com/groups/3982592/Bee-Swarm-Simulator-Club#!/about). 

## [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page")[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Bees convert pollen into Honey at a set rate. For example, [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee") makes 80 honey in 4 seconds, translating into a conversion rate of 20 honey per second. [Badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges"), accessories, [gamepasses](/wiki/Robux_Shop#Gamepasses "Robux Shop"), ![Enzymes](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"), and [Science Enhancement](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_NPCs "Buffs & Debuffs") can increase the convert rate. The production rate stacks and adds up. There is currently no convert rate cap in the game.
