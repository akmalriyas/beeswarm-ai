The **Cave Monster** is a level 11 [mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") that spawns inside the [Werewolf's Cave](/wiki/Werewolf%27s_Cave "Werewolf's Cave"). They look identical to a [spider](/wiki/Spider "Spider"), except that they lack eyes. Cave Monsters make eerie whispering noises when the player gets close to them. A [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm") and a [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower") (and during the Beesmas events, a [Snow Storm](/wiki/Snow_Storm "Snow Storm")) can make it bright enough to see their bodies. 

Their primary purpose is to guard a ![Gold Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gold Egg](/wiki/Egg#Gold_Egg "Egg") located at the end of the cave. The best way to reach it is to have [Haste+](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens"), Haste x5 or higher, or by using the [glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider"). Be aware that the roof of the cave is not very high, so it is easy to hit your head and slow you down. 

It is possible to defeat the cave monster. However, they move very fast, so some slower [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") will be unable to reach them. The best way to defeat them is by activating the [Emergency Coconut Shield](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Emergency_Coconut_Shield "Passive Abilities") and running quickly toward the gold egg token. Cave Monsters are the only mobs to (albeit rarely) drop a [Honey Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Specific_Bee_Eggs "Egg"). If the player does not have the [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister "Coconut Canister"), another method is to quickly move into and then out of their spawning zone. You can also use the [Star Saw](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Saw "Passive Abilities") passive ability from the [Supreme Star Amulet](/wiki/Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") or Festive Bee's Beesmas Cheer ability. It takes 30 minutes for one to respawn and they don't count as spiders towards [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") from [bears](/wiki/Bear "Bear").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCave_Monster%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") had stated that cave monsters have a rare drop and can only be obtained by defeating them, but he also said that the drop is not very useful. This was later confirmed to be a Honey Bee egg. Onett had also jokingly said that "Saving the [honey bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee") from the cave monsters was the ultimate goal of [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator")."[1]
  * Their noises can be heard throughout the entire map, along with [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick"), [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick"), and a few other things.
  * Cave monsters were the first mobs that are able to instantly kill the player and one of two mobs that instantly kill the player upon touching them (the other being [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear")). 
    * This makes the cave monsters the most lethal non-boss mob in the game.
  * Cave monsters have the second highest base level of any mob in-game, with the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab") having the highest.
  * Cave monsters, [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle"), Tunnel Bear, [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee"), [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug"), [RBC mobs](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"), [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee"), [aphids](/wiki/Aphid "Aphid") and [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick") are the mobs who don't belong to any particular fields.
  * There used to be 4 cave monsters because the gold egg was once the rarest [item](/wiki/Items "Items") in the game.
  * Cave monsters have one of the fewest possible amounts of drops in the game, only behind [ants](/wiki/Ants "Ants"), and [RBC mobs](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"), which have none. 
    * Cave monsters, Coconut Crab, [RBC Mobs](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"), [Snowbear](/wiki/Snowbear "Snowbear"), and ants are the only mobs that don't drop honey upon death.
  * Cave monsters are currently the only mobs with a set respawn time that [quest givers](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers") do not require the player to defeat.
  * Cave monsters are the only defeatable mobs that do not have a health bar. 
    * Between the [2018-11-25](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates") and the [2018-12-25](/wiki/Updates#2018-12-25 "Updates") [updates](/wiki/Updates "Updates"), the Cave Monsters had a health bar that revealed they were level 11 and had 400 health.
  * When donating the Honey Bee Egg to the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine"), there is a decent chance it will spawn [Bean Bugs](/wiki/Bean_Bug "Bean Bug"). This makes killing the Cave Monster somewhat useful as some quests require collecting tokens from Bean Bugs.
