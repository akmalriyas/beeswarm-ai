A **Ticket Planter** is a single use [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") added in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"). Alone, it grows in about 2 hours and stores 50,000 [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). When harvested, it grants bonus Tickets. A player can store up to 100 Ticket Planters at a time, but only 1 can be active. 

It does not have any growth nor pollen multipliers, and grants x2 [nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar"). 

Harvesting a fully grown Ticket Planter is guaranteed to spawn a [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom"). The spawned puffshroom is between levels 1-2, and is guaranteed to be a Common Puffshroom.

## Ways to obtain[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTicket_Planter%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear") gives 3 Ticket Planters if the player speaks to him for the first time.
  * As a reward from certain [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests"): 
    * If the player completes Dapper Bear’s quests and they already got Beequip slots from [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") in previous years, they get a Ticket Planter for each Beequip slot.
    * Bee Bear's "Searching For Snowflakes" quest rewards 1 Ticket Planter and other items.
  * As a rare reward from the [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge").
  * As a rare drop from [Sticker Sprouts](/wiki/Sprout#Variants "Sprout").
  * As a Stack Reward for adding certain stickers to the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack"): 
    * ![Hivesticker dapper from above](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Dapper From Above Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") rewards 1 Ticket Planter.
    * ![Ticket Voucher](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Ticket Voucher](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") rewards 1 Ticket Planter
  * Buying the Tempting Ticket Pack from [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog") for ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)55,555 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") gives 5 Ticket Planters and other items.
  * As a drop from Dapper Bear’s [Samovar](/wiki/Samovar "Samovar") after every 5th use (except for the 25th, which drops a [turpentine](/wiki/Turpentine "Turpentine") instead).



### Outdated Ways[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTicket_Planter%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * By completing [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee")'s 2021 Beesmas Quest.
  * By completing some of [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") 2021 and 2022 quests.
  * By buying certain items/bundles from [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog"): 
    * Buying the Festive Bundle for ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5,000 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") and ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)100 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") during Beesmas 2021 gave 10 Ticket Planters.
    * Buying the Ticket Planter for ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)100 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake").
  * Opening certain [Gift Boxes](/wiki/Gift_Boxes "Gift Boxes"): 
    * Opening the Gilded Gift Box gave 1 Ticket Planter and other items.
    * Opening the Jubilant Gift Box gave 4 Ticket Planters and other items.
    * Opening the Vibrant Gift Box gave 1 Ticket Planter and other items.
    * Opening the Obnoxious Gift Box gave 1 Ticket Planter and other items.
    * Opening the Soothing Gift Box gave 2 Ticket Planters and other items.
  * Using certain expired [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"): 
    * "2Billion" gave 1 ticket planter and other items.
  * Purchasing certain Robux packs: 
    * Purchasing the Cyber Mondo Monday Pack for 800 robux gave 10 Ticket Planters and other items.
    * Purchasing the 15 Ticket Planters offer for 400 robux gave 15 Ticket Planters.
  * As a drop from [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party") mobs.
  * As a reward for completing your Robo Party.
  * As a drop from Dapper Bear's [Samovar](/wiki/Samovar "Samovar") (Dropped after every 5th use, except the 25th).

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTicket_Planter%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is one of the two planters that is guaranteed to spawn a [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom"). The other is the ![Sticker Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sticker Planter](/wiki/Sticker_Planter "Sticker Planter").
  * This, the ![Sticker Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sticker Planter](/wiki/Sticker_Planter "Sticker Planter") and the ![Paper Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Paper Planter](/wiki/Paper_Planter "Paper Planter") share the same base model. 
    * They also share the same outline for their decals.
  * It is one of two planters that mentions an in-game item in its name, the other planter is the ![Sticker Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sticker Planter](/wiki/Sticker_Planter "Sticker Planter").
  * It gives the second-best nectar multiplier in the game, 2x, behind the [Festive Planter](/wiki/Festive_Planter "Festive Planter") with 3x.
  * It is one of 4 single-use planters, the others being the ![Paper Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Paper Planter](/wiki/Paper_Planter "Paper Planter"), the ![Festive Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Planter](/wiki/Festive_Planter "Festive Planter") and the ![Sticker Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sticker Planter](/wiki/Sticker_Planter "Sticker Planter").
  * This, the ![Sticker Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sticker Planter](/wiki/Sticker_Planter "Sticker Planter") and the ![Paper Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Paper Planter](/wiki/Paper_Planter "Paper Planter") are the only one-use planters in the game that can be obtained infinitely.
