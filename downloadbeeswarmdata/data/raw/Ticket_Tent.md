[ ![TicketTent2021](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/44/TicketTent2021.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20260203055053) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/44/TicketTent2021.png/revision/latest?cb=20260203055053 "TicketTent2021")

Behind the [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon")

_This page is for the shop that sells items for tickets. For the shop that sells tickets, see the[Ticket Shop](/wiki/Ticket_Shop "Ticket Shop") page._

The **Ticket Tent** is a [shop](/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops") located behind the [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon") and near the [Hives](/wiki/Hive "Hive"). It sells six types of [Event Bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event"), ![Silver Egg](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/42/Silver_Egg.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404043555)[Silver Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Silver_Egg "Egg"), ![Gold Egg](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/50/Gold_Egg.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404043612)[Gold Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Gold_Egg "Egg"), ![Diamond Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Diamond Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Diamond_Egg "Egg"), ![Mythic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Mythic Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Mythic_Egg "Egg"), and ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treats](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"). They can only be purchased with ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), hence its name. There is also a ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token on top of the Ticket Tent.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTicket_Tent%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The only bee in this shop that could be obtained without tickets (for a limited time) was [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee"). 
    * This used to also apply to [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"), which is now permanently available for 2500 [gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops").
  * The Royal Jelly token on top of the Ticket Tent was mentioned by [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") in his dialogue.
  * Buying a [star treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat") from here is one of the only ways to obtain star treats without Robux.
  * Before the [2019-04-05 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-04-05 "Updates"), if the player had the [Goo Hotshot Badge](/wiki/Badges#Goo_Badge "Badges"), they could go up to the Gummy Bee on top of the tent and use gumdrops to enter [Gummy Bear's Lair](/wiki/Gummy_Bear%27s_Lair "Gummy Bear's Lair"). However, this mechanic was moved to the [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Gummy_Bee_Egg_Claim "Gummy Bee Egg Claim") when it was added.
  * The wings on the gifted Photon Bee's model are not transparent, while the giant Festive Bee and Puppy Bees' wings are. 
    * Additionally, the wings of the Festive Bee model aren't solid, while the wings of the Puppy Bee and Gifted Photon Bee models are.
    * The Photon Bee is also the only model that is in [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") form.
  * When the Ticket Tent was implemented, the most expensive item was the star treat, costing 1,000 tickets. However, after the Beesmas 2019 event ended, the most expensive item was the Cub Buddy, costing 2,000 tickets. After the 2024-05-23 update, Mythic Eggs now cost the most, at 2,500 tickets.
  * Like the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop") or [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop"), [Spotted chicks](/wiki/Chicks#Spotted_Chick "Chicks") could spawn here during the [Egg Hunt 2020](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2020 "Egg Hunt 2020") event.
  * [Cub Buddies](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy") used to be sold in this shop, but after the 2024-05-23 update, this is no longer the case.
  * It is possible to see the back of the Ticket Tent by going out of bounds behind the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field").
