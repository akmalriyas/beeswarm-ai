The **Candy Ring** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 14 [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [Stubborn](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee"), [Bumble](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee"), [Honey](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee"), [Diamond](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee"), [Festive](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") and [Gummy](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees").
