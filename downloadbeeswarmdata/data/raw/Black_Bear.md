**Black Bear** is a [quest giver](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers") located at the very beginning of the game. He is one of the eight permanent [Quest Bears](/wiki/Category:Bears "Category:Bears") that can be accessed in the game, with the others being [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear"), [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear"), [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear"), [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear"), [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear"), [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear"), and [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear"). After the player has completed all of Black Bear's main questlines, the player can obtain repetitive [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") every hour, similar to Brown Bear. 

He is usually the first quest bear to be accessed, along with Mother Bear, Brown Bear, and [Traveling Bears](/wiki/Quest_Givers#Traveling/Other_NPCs "Quest Givers") (only during special events). He provides quests to players to help them progress in the game and to provide extra goals to reach for. This particular bear focuses on [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") collecting. He is located near the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") and the [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon"), but he is also near the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field") and the [Hives](/wiki/Hive "Hive"). Black Bear was a quest giver for the [Egg Hunt 2019](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2019 "Egg Hunt 2019") and [Egg Hunt 2020](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2020 "Egg Hunt 2020") events and was one of the NPCs to give an Ornament quest for Beesmas 2019. He also had a quest during Beesmas (2020 and beyond) for the [Honey Wreath](/wiki/Honey_Wreath "Honey Wreath"). 

Black Bear's quests require collecting pollen for him. In return, he will give the player various rewards, including ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), a ![Silver Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Silver Egg](/wiki/Egg#Silver_Egg "Egg"), a ![Gold Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gold Egg](/wiki/Egg#Gold_Egg "Egg"), a ![Diamond Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Diamond Egg](/wiki/Egg#Diamond_Egg "Egg"), a ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), and eventually gives a ![Mythic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Mythic Egg](/wiki/Egg#Mythic_Egg "Egg"). He also gives many side rewards in the later quests. 

Through all of his quests, he rewards 2,352,532,300 Honey. When doing the quests for the Diamond Egg, he will give![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") every 4 quests for a total of 5 royal jellies, and a Diamond Egg. When doing quests for the Star Jelly, he'll reward ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") and a![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") for every quest completed except the quest "Solo On The Stump,” for which Black Bear gives ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)10 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") instead of the usual 5 Tickets.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlack_Bear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Black Bear proudly calls himself a "Honey Connoisseur" in his dialogue. This is what the "Black Bear's Title?" hint (behind the [Instant Converter](/wiki/Instant_Converter "Instant Converter") where [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear") is) refers to, leading to the [Code](/wiki/Codes "Codes") "Connoisseur".
  * He is the third [bear](/wiki/Category:Bears "Category:Bears") to have a list of endless repeating quests.
  * Black Bear uses the [Cartoony Animation Package](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/56/Cartoony-Animation-Package).
  * He is the only quest giver to have normal quests and repeatable quests.
  * The repeatable quests used to be given one after another, with no cooldown. 
    * As of now, the repeatable quests have a cooldown of one hour.
  * After the [2020-04-06 Update](/wiki/Updates#2020-04-06 "Updates"), where Black Bear got his ![Mythic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Mythic Egg](/wiki/Egg#Mythic_Egg "Egg") quests, if the player already had a repeatable quest, it was unable to be turned in until they had completed all of Black Bear's Mythic Egg quests.
  * Black Bear is one of the twelve [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy") skins.
  * He is self-aware that he is in a [Roblox game](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"), along with [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear"), [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear"), [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear"), [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear"), and [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear"), as he says, “But this is ROBLOX! Anything goes!” He also says "the 'luck' stat in the game," mentions waiting for an update to happen, and mentions that he's a 'virtual bear.'
  * He was the first bear to give a ![Diamond Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Diamond Egg](/wiki/Egg#Diamond_Egg "Egg"), with Panda Bear being the second, Brown Bear being the third, and [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear") being the fourth.
  * He was the second bear to give a Mythic Egg, with Brown Bear being the first and Dapper Bear being the third.
  * He, Mother Bear, and Brown Bear were "nice" in 2018, as stated by [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") during the Beesmas 2018 Event.
  * In "Pretty Pumpkins" during dialogue, originally it was only "Test.". This was a mistake, and the actual dialogue was added sometime later.
  * He, Panda Bear, and Spirit Bear are the only bears that mention the history of the mountain.
  * He mentions a mysterious bee once "destroyed an entire field with powerful gusts of wind" in the "Black Bear, Why?" dialogue. This was later revealed that said bee was [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee").
  * He is acrophobic, or afraid of heights, as said in the quest "High Altitude".
  * He is also arachnophobic, or afraid of spiders, as said in the quest "Cobweb Sweeper".
