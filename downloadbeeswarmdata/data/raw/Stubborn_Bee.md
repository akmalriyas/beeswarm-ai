**Stubborn Bee** is a Colorless [Rare bee](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"). 

Stubborn Bee's favorite treat is ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry") . 

Stubborn Bee likes the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field") and the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"). It dislikes the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStubborn_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 pollen in 6 seconds.
  * Makes 80 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * -15% [movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +25% convert speed, +2 [attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +15% [Ability Token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") Lifespan.



## Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStubborn_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Pollen Mark]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mark "Ability Tokens")** Marks a random area on the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") for 7 seconds (+0.25s per level) that increases all pollen up to 50% while you stand in it. Stacks up to 3 times.

Stubborn Bee has a base pollen collection of **10![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. That's equivalent to 2.5 ![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStubborn_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Stubborn Bee was one of the two bees added in the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"), the other being [Carpenter Bee](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee").
  * Stubborn Bee and [Looker Bee](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee") are the only Rare bees added after the game launch.
  * The gifted hive bonus (+15% ability token lifespan) used to be broken. The player could have a Gifted Stubborn Bee in their hive, but the ability token lifespan would not apply. This was fixed on an unknown update in February 2021.
  * Stubborn Bee is the only Colorless Rare Bee that does not have its own [guard](/wiki/Category:Guards "Category:Guards") in the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop"). This is because it was added in the [2018-11-25](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates") update, after the guards were added.
