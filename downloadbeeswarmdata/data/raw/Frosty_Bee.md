**Frosty Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Epic bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"). 

Frosty Bee's favorite treat is ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"). 

Frosty Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), and the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"). It dislikes the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field") and the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFrosty_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 80 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +25% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), -20% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.25 [Blue Bomb Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Blue_Bomb_Pollen "System Page").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFrosty_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Blue Boost]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens")** Grants x1.2 pollen from blue [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") for 15s. Stacks up to 10 times.
  * **[[Blue Bomb+]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding blue flowers (+10% pollen per Level). Combo with other bombs to increase power.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFrosty_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Frosty Bee, [Commander Bee](/wiki/Commander_Bee "Commander Bee"), and [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee") are the only [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that wear hats.
  * Frosty Bee is likely a reference to Frosty the Snowman.
  * The red counterpart of Frosty Bee is [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee").
  * A Frosty Bee Jelly could have been obtained from one of the bundles/packs in [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%E2%80%99s_Catalog "Bee Bear’s Catalog").
  * This bee's name is the placeholder's name for any bee in a hive slot that hasn't loaded yet.
  * The bee may like [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field") because it is cold on high mountains.
