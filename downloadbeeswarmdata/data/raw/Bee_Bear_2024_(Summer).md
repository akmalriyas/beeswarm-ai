Field Boosts are 15 minute buffs that increase the pollen you collect in a specific field by 100% (or more!) You can obtain Field Boost stacks from using [Field Dice], which chooses a field at random. But [Field Dice] can be hard to find, and pricey to craft... That's where Field Boosters come in! Field Boosters are magic machines that grant Field Boosts to random fields for free, once every 45 minutes. If you want to make honey fast, you should use them as much as possible! You can find one in the 25 Bee Zone, one in the Red HQ, and one in the Blue HQ.
