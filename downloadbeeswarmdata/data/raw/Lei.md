A **Lei** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 14 [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [Basic](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"), [Looker](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee"), [Stubborn](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee"), [Hasty](/wiki/Hasty_Bee "Hasty Bee"), and [Exhausted](/wiki/Exhausted_Bee "Exhausted Bee") [Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees").
