# Star Treat

Star Treat

[![Star Treat](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/Star_Treat.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041522)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/Star_Treat.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041522)

_"Turns a bee into a Gifted bee!"_

[![The text that appears in the chat when a bee turns gifted via a treat.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b0/Giftedvectorbee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/280?cb=20200202204042)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b0/Giftedvectorbee.png/revision/latest?cb=20200202204042) [](/wiki/File:Giftedvectorbee.png)

The text that appears in the chat when a bee turns gifted via a treat.

A **Star Treat** is a special type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that, when fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), raises its [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") by 1,000 and transforms the bee into its [gifted form](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"). It is the only treat in the game which is guaranteed to turn a bee gifted. It is also one of the only ways to get gifted [Event bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event"), including ![Gingerbread Bear](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ae/Gingerbread_Bear.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404041818)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") and ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

## Ways to Obtain[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStar_Treat%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Buying it from [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog") for ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)10,000 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake").
  * Buying it for ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1,000 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") inside the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent").
  * Playing [Night Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Night_Memory_Match "Memory Match")/[Extreme Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Extreme_Memory_Match "Memory Match")/[Winter Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Winter_Memory_Match "Memory Match") (exceptionally rare).
  * Given by the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") if you already have a [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") after buying the [Windy Bee Pack](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop").



### Drops[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStar_Treat%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * An exceptionally rare drop from the [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick"), [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab"), and [fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies").
  * Capturing [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick") for the 100th time guarantees a star treat.
  * An exceptionally low chance of dropping from [Mythic Meteors](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower").
  * An extremely rare drop from ![Festive Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Planters](/wiki/Festive_Planter "Festive Planter").



### Quests[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStar_Treat%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Completing all of [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett")'s quests, [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear")'s quests, and [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear")'s quests.
  * Completing 750 [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear") quests rewards a star treat. Every 500 quests after the 750th Brown Bear quest also rewards a star treat. Additionally, his 1000th quest grants the player 5 Star Treats.



### Outdated Ways[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStar_Treat%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Buying the Gingerbread Cub Bundle in [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog") (1 if the player didn't have a cub buddy, 2 if they already had one.)
  * An extremely low chance of getting it from [Winter Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Winter_Memory_Match "Memory Match").
  * Completing the 15th [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") quest rewards a star treat if the player already owns a cub buddy.
  * An exceptionally rare drop from [Festive Nymphs](/wiki/Festive_Nymph "Festive Nymph").
  * An extremely rare drop from the [Stockings](/wiki/Stockings "Stockings").
  * By opening certain [Gift Boxes](/wiki/Gift_Boxes "Gift Boxes"): 
    * The [Mondo Gift Box](/wiki/Gift_Boxes "Gift Boxes") gave 1 star treat + other items during Beesmas 2020, 2021 and 2022.
  * Buying it from Bee Bear's Catalog for ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)10,000 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStar_Treat%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This and the ![Atomic Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Atomic Treat](/wiki/Atomic_Treat "Atomic Treat") give the most [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") (1,000 bond) out of any treat, followed by a single ![Neonberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Neonberry](/wiki/Neonberry "Neonberry") and ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear") (500 bond), then ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") and ![Moon Charm](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Moon Charms](/wiki/Moon_Charm "Moon Charm") (250 bond).
  * The star treat, along with ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear") are the only treats that cannot be obtained from sprouts.
  * The star treat formerly gave 100 bond instead of 1,000.
  * Feeding a Star Treat to a gifted [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee") has a 1/3 chance to spawn a Bear Bee Offer [sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker").
