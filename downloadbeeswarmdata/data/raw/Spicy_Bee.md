**Spicy Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Mythic bee](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic"). 

Spicy Bee's favorite treat is ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"). 

Spicy Bee likes the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch"). It dislikes the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSpicy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 14 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 300 [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 2 seconds.
  * +220 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +50% Convert Speed, +4 Gather Amount, +4 [Attack](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Attack "System Page").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +25% [Flame Duration](/wiki/Flame#Flame_Duration "Flame").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSpicy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Inferno]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inferno "Ability Tokens")** Summons a ring of 6 [flames](/wiki/Flame "Flame"), then [summons](/wiki/Bees#Summoned_Bees "Bees") 2 [Fire Bees](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee") that last for 15s (+1s per Level) with levels equal to 2 less than this bee's level. If Gifted, each Fire Bee has a 10% (+1% per Level) chance of being Gifted as well. However, the gifted Fire Bees from inferno **DO NOT** grant their gifted hive bonus.
  * **[[Rage]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Rage "Ability Tokens")** Grants your bees +1 attack for 45s. Stacks up to 4 times.
  * [**[🌟Gifted Ability: Flame Fuel]**](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Flame_Fuel "Ability Tokens") For 15 seconds, Spicy Bee tosses oil into any flames that spawn, causing them to last 50% longer and instantly convert 50k pollen and (10 + 5 per Field Boost stack)% of your [Hive's](/wiki/Hive "Hive") [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"). Unless converted to a [dark flame](/wiki/Flame#Dark_Flames "Flame"), Spicy Bee won't throw oil into flames currently on the field.
  * **[[Passive: Steam Engine]](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Steam_Engine "Passive Abilities")** This bee’s [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats") and gather speed drastically increases up to 100% with [Flame Heat](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#Flame_Heat "Buffs & Debuffs").

Spicy Bee has a base pollen collection of **14![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. That's equivalent to 3.5 ![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSpicy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Spicy Bee, [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee"), [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee") and [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") all have four different [Abilities](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), the most for any [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees").
  * This bee and [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee") are the only bees with the Rage Ability.
  * Spicy Bee’s gifted color scheme resembles Rage Bee’s gifted and Rage Aphids' color scheme.
  * This bee and Tadpole Bee are the only Bees who can summon other entities. 
    * This is the only bee that can [summon](/wiki/Bees#Summoned_Bees "Bees") other bees.
  * This bee was originally going to be themed like a train, hence the Steam Engine passive ability, train sounds the tokens make and exhaust pipes on the bee's body.[1]
  * While Steam Engine is active, Spicy Bee will emit a continuous trail of steam.
  * This bee, along with Vector Bee, Buoyant Bee, and Precise bee emits light when gifted.
  * Unlike other gifted bees, the star on top of a gifted Spicy Bee is a different color to the star on its side, with a green star on the top and 2 black ones on the side.
  * This is the only Mythic bee to have completely whole numbered base [stats](/wiki/Stats "Stats").
  * The Fire Bees summoned by its Inferno ability cannot convert pollen in the player's hive.
  * At launch, this bee was improperly balanced because of a typo. It was supposed to have each flame converting 1% of the player’s total conversion, but instead it converted 1% of their capacity. X-Flame converted 29% of the player's capacity ([Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") was required). It was quickly noticed and nerfed to the regular stats it has.
  * When gifted, Spicy Bee's exhaust pipes emit more steam.
  * Spicy Bee is the only bee to like one field.
  * If Spicy Bee is level 1 or 2, the Fire Bees will be level 1 instead of two levels below. This is because there are no levels that go below 1.
