The **Egg Hunt 2020** was a Roblox event that spanned across multiple games. In each game, players had to complete a certain task/quest in order to obtain one of the eggs, which they could then wear on their avatar. 

## Story[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEgg_Hunt_2020%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

[Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") stated that he wanted to work with the bears for the Egg Hunt. He said that he would hide exotic eggs on the map (presumably in the form of a scavenger/egg hunt). The eggs hatched, however, and Sun Bear needed the players to capture the [chicks](/wiki/Chicks "Chicks") and bring them to [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear"), [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear"), and [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear"). 

## How to Obtain the Egg[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEgg_Hunt_2020%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

To get the egg from [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"), the player needed to talk to [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") near the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate") to receive the [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests"). Then, the player needed to help three bears (Black, Brown, and Mother Bears) with their Egg Hunt 2020 quests. Their quests revolved around capturing chicks, along with a few other side objectives. 

Once the player had helped the bears, they had to return to Sun Bear for a few special rewards along with the [Swarming Egg of the Hive](/wiki/Swarming_Egg_of_the_Hive "Swarming Egg of the Hive"), which players can wear on their Roblox avatar as a hat.
