_This page is for the Stick Bug NPC. For attack behaviour and the actual challenge, please see[Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge")_

**Stick Bug** is a [NPC](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC") found on the tallest mushroom in the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), next to the [Top Stick Bug Fighters](/wiki/Top_Stick_Bug_Fighters "Top Stick Bug Fighters") Leaderboard. It is accessible by using the [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon")/[Yellow Cannon](/wiki/Yellow_Cannon "Yellow Cannon")/[Blue Cannon](/wiki/Blue_Cannon "Blue Cannon") with the [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute")/[Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider"), or using the [Beekeeper's Boots](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Boots "Beekeeper's Boots") and jumping from the second mushroom to the one with the ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"). Players will need a ![Translator](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Translator](/wiki/Translator "Translator") to talk to Stick Bug, where they can then ask it to start the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge"). Stick Bug then appears as a [boss mob](/wiki/Category:Bosses "Category:Bosses") and always spawns in the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field") first. Upon defeat, it flees to a random [Field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") from this list: 

  * [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field")
  * [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field")
  * [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field")
  * [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field")
  * [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch")
  * [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field")
  * [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch")
  * [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field")



The player can start the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge") for free every 1 and a half days (1d, 12hr), by initiating it anytime for ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)50 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), or by giving Stick Bug a ![Present](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Present](/wiki/Present "Present") during [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas"). 

When the challenge ends, the player will be awarded an [amulet](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Amulet "Stick Bug Amulet") depending on their score (_More information in the[Stick Bug Amulet](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Amulet "Stick Bug Amulet") page_).

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStick_Bug%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * If the player gave Stick Bug a [Present](/wiki/Present "Present"), started its Egg Hunt quest, started its 2019 [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") Quest, or finished any of its [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") quests (except for 2019), the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge") would automatically start.
  * Stick Bug, [Shadow Bear](/wiki/Shadow_Bear "Shadow Bear"), the [Cub Buddies](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy"), [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") and [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") are the only NPCs that dance.
  * There is a [glitch](/wiki/Glitches#Stick_Bug_Falls_Down "Glitches") where Stick Bug may fall over from its regular location on top of the tallest mushroom. If this occurs, it will either be in the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"), the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), or somewhere in between. 
    * Another glitch is on the [Brave Bee Gate](/wiki/Brave_Bee_Gate "Brave Bee Gate") ramp, where walking to the bottom of the ramp and on the corner of the [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field") will cause Stick Bug to briefly get stuck there when it tries to follow the player.
  * In [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") 2019, Stick Bug gave the most [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") when given a present (![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)33,333,333 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey")), the next being [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear") at only ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey").
  * If Stick Bug moves to the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), it has a chance of falling and getting stuck in the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch") or [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch").
  * Stick Bug and [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") are the only mobs that are impossible to defeat, as depleting all of their HP will only level them up and regain their HP.
  * Stick Bug waves when the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge") ends.
  * Stick Bug is one of the three NPCs that requires a [Translator](/wiki/Translator "Translator") to interact with, the others being [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee") and [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee").
  * Stick Bug's boss fight theme became part of the popular bait-and-switch meme "[Get stickbugged lol](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fC7oUOUEEi4)", which spread far from the BSS Community. 
    * Stick Bug is aware of this meme, indicated by his [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") 2020 dialogue for receiving a [Present](/wiki/Present "Present").
  * In [Spirit Bear's](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") dialogue, she states that Stick Bug scared her as a cub, which indicates that Stick Bug has been around for a long time.
  * Stick Bug's Beesmas 2022 Quest originally gave ![Caustic Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Caustic Wax](/wiki/Caustic_Wax "Caustic Wax"), no ![Swirled Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Swirled Waxes](/wiki/Swirled_Wax "Swirled Wax"), no ![Ticket Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Ticket Planters](/wiki/Ticket_Planter "Ticket Planter"), and ![Invigorating Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Invigorating Vial](/wiki/Invigorating_Vial "Invigorating Vial"). This was soon buffed to ![Caustic Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)3 [Caustic Waxes](/wiki/Caustic_Wax "Caustic Wax"), ![Swirled Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Swirled Wax](/wiki/Swirled_Wax "Swirled Wax"), ![Ticket Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Ticket Planter](/wiki/Ticket_Planter "Ticket Planter"), and ![Invigorating Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)2 [Invigorating Vials](/wiki/Invigorating_Vial "Invigorating Vial").
  * As stated by [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear"), Stick Bug loves [pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple").
  * Stick Bug is the only NPC that needs a ![Translator](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Translator](/wiki/Translator "Translator") that isn't also a permanent quest giver.
  * During [Beesmas 2024](/wiki/Updates#2024-07-17 "Updates"), [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") would start a Festive [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge") at random every few days.
  * During the [Egg Hunt 2019 Event Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-17 "Updates"), Stick Bug would plant an Epic, Legendary, and Supreme [Sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") after finishing its [Egg Hunt](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2019 "Egg Hunt 2019") quest. 
    * This makes Stick Bug the only NPC able to spawn [Sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout").
