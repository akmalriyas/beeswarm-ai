# Stockings

![Digital Bee](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) | **This piece of content contains information obtained through datamining.** Due to the nature of the information, details may be inaccurate or outdated.

The **Stockings** are a [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas")-exclusive machine that could be unlocked after completing [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear")'s Beesmas quest. Introduced during [Beesmas 2020](/wiki/Updates#2020-12-25 "Updates"), they have returned every Beesmas since. 

The Stockings are located at the bottom of the hill where Brown Bear stands, next to the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"). When used, they will spawn one random [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") and two additional random items in front of them. The cooldown for use is 1 hour.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStockings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * If the player attempts to use the Stockings before the player completed Brown Bear's 2021 Beesmas quest, it will display text saying _"This mantle looks so barren without Stockings on the hooks..."_
  * Every time the player uses the Stockings, they can only get 1 of each item, excluding gumdrops and moon charms.
  * Tokens spawned by the Stockings can be collected by a [Token Link](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Token_Link "Ability Tokens").
  * [Loot Luck](/wiki/Loot_Luck "Loot Luck") does not affect the Stocking drops.
