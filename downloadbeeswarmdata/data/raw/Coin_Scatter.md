Passive Ability icons which can be seen above the hotbar, or bottom right next to the hotbar on mobile. These icons are for Haste Pulser, Coconut Haste Surge, Ignite, X-Flame, and Emergency Coconut Shield.

**Passive Abilities,** or simply **passives** are effects found on some [accessories](/wiki/Category:Accessories "Category:Accessories"), [amulets](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet"), or [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"). These abilities are active all the time or only activate when specific actions are done, as an additional effect of those actions. 

Passive abilities granted by an accessory or an amulet are displayed near the hotbar. Some passive abilities require the player to do the same action multiple times, as shown by the number displayed on the passive's icon, indicating how close it is to activating the ability. When the ability is activated, the number will reset to 0. 

Some passive abilities have a cooldown. When in cooldown, the number of seconds left will be displayed on the passive's icon in pale red. No trigger will activate the ability, nor will the trigger counter be increased. These passive abilities also have an initial cooldown when the player equips items that have those passives to prevent bypassing the cooldown. 

## Accessory Passives

### Haste Pulser

[![The icon for Haste Pulser.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7a/HastePulser.png/revision/latest?cb=20240228191600)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7a/HastePulser.png/revision/latest?cb=20240228191600)

The icon for Haste Pulser.

**Haste Pulser** activates every 30th [Haste token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens") collected. 

When activated, it fires a blue pulse that hops to each blue bee, identical to that of [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee"). 

Haste Pulser is granted by equipping the [Cobalt Guard](/wiki/Cobalt_Guard "Cobalt Guard"). 

If the player has no blue bees, then nothing happens.

[![The icon for Focus Pulser.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/71/FocusPulser.png/revision/latest?cb=20240227224126)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/71/FocusPulser.png/revision/latest?cb=20240227224126)

The icon for Focus Pulser.

**Focus Pulser** activates every 30th [Focus token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Focus "Ability Tokens") collected. 

When activated, it fires a red pulse that hops to each red bee, identical to that of [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee"). 

Focus Pulser is granted by equipping the [Crimson Guard](/wiki/Crimson_Guard "Crimson Guard"). 

If the player has no red bees, then nothing happens.

The icon for Bubble Bombs.

**Bubble Bombs** activates every 25th [Bomb token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens") collected, with a 2-minute cooldown. 

When activated, it spawns 25 [bubbles](/wiki/Bubble "Bubble") randomly in the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") the player is in. 

Bubble Bomb is only granted by equipping the [Bubble Mask](/wiki/Bubble_Mask "Bubble Mask"), or the [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask "Diamond Mask") if the player owns the Bubble Mask.

Coin Scatter is granted by equipping the [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask "Honey Mask"), or the [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask") if the player owns the Honey Mask.

### Combo Coconuts

[![The icon for Combo Coconuts.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Combococonuts.png/revision/latest?cb=20240501221749) [![The icon for Combo Coconuts.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Combococonuts.png/revision/latest?cb=20240501221749)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Combococonuts.png/revision/latest?cb=20240501221749)

[![A Combo Coconut.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/08/Combo_Coconut_Use.gif/revision/latest?cb=20250701015453) [![A Combo Coconut.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/08/Combo_Coconut_Use.gif/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250701015453)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/08/Combo_Coconut_Use.gif/revision/latest?cb=20250701015453) [](/wiki/File:Combo_Coconut_Use.gif)

A Combo Coconut.

The 40th [Coconut](/wiki/Coconut "Coconut") dropped becomes a Combo Coconut. When caught by any player, the Combo Coconut is kicked and either falls onto the summoner's field (if on the first drop, the coconut was caught within the blue circle) or onto another player's field. 

Every time it is kicked, it speeds up and gives the kicker stacks of [Coconut Combo](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Passives "Buffs & Debuffs"). Many more stacks are granted if caught by other players in fields with different colors:[1]

  * Gives 1 stack by default.
  * Gives 1 bonus stack if caught in a field different from where it was last caught.
  * Between +1 to +4 bonus stacks depending on how long it has been since the coconut was caught in a field with the same color classification (Red/Blue/White/Mixed) as the current field it's being caught in.
  * +10 bonus stacks if the catcher is another player who has not yet caught this coconut, in a field with a color classification that the coconut has not yet been caught in.



The buff grants between 25% to 75% Unique Instant Conversion, between x1.2 to x2 Pollen, and between +1% to +50% Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, White Pollen, Bee Attack, and Honey From Tokens. 

When the Coconut fails to be caught, it despawns and reapplies the buff to all players who caught it at least once. 

Combo Coconuts cannot be passed to players in the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field"). Combo Coconuts spawned in the Ant Field will always result in a miss. 

There is a limit of 50 Combo Coconut kicks before it ends prematurely. 

The blue circle doesn't work as intended and even if caught within it on the first drop, the coconut will fall onto another player's field. 

Combo Coconuts is granted by equipping the [Coconut Belt](/wiki/Coconut_Belt "Coconut Belt").

### Nectar Lover

**Nectar Lover** is a passive ability exclusive to [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee"). It makes Shy Bee twice as likely to sip from a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") than any other bee. When sipping, it also collects twice as much [nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") and contributes twice as much to the growth of the planter. 

### Shimmering Honey

[![Diamond Bee's Conversion Link color when Shimmering Honey is activated.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b2/90935ED9-7B1D-47E4-94EE-B714907A58E7.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20220305070857) [![Diamond Bee's Conversion Link color when Shimmering Honey is activated.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b2/90935ED9-7B1D-47E4-94EE-B714907A58E7.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/120?cb=20220305070857)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b2/90935ED9-7B1D-47E4-94EE-B714907A58E7.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20220305070857) [](/wiki/File:90935ED9-7B1D-47E4-94EE-B714907A58E7.jpg)

Diamond Bee's Conversion Link color when Shimmering Honey is activated.

**Shimmering Honey** is a passive ability exclusive to [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee"). Whenever this bee converts at the hive, it grants a 25% bonus honey (+2.5% per level). This bonus is doubled if the bee is Gifted. 

### Balloon Enthusiast

**Balloon Enthusiast** is a passive ability exclusive to [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"). When converting from Balloons, this bee gains x3 its normal [convert amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"). Additionally, this bee's base attack scales up to x3 with [Balloon Blessing](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens "Buffs & Debuffs"). 

### Sniper

**Sniper** is a passive ability exclusive to [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee"). In battle, this bee uses slow, long-range, more accurate shots and deals x2 damage. The accuracy is calculated as if the bee were 1 level higher (2 if gifted), and it can never be below 5%. If the shot is a [Critical Hit](/wiki/Critical_Hits "Critical Hits"), the reload time after shots is cut in half. If it's a [Super-Crit](/wiki/Critical_Hits#Super-Crit "Critical Hits"), it's reduced by 75%. 

### Drive Expansion

**Drive Expansion** is a passive ability exclusive to [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee"). Using a [Drive](/wiki/Drives "Drives") while this bee is active in [Robo Bear's Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge") corrupts the field you're standing in by an amount proportional to the number of flowers that match the Drive's color (Glitched Drives corrupt all fields equally). Additionally, it permanently enhances this bee, increasing the Corruption of its abilities and granting it the following stats:   
\- Red Drive: +0.03 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats")   
\- Blue Drive: +2 [Convert Amount](/wiki/Stats#Production_Amount "Stats")   
\- White Drive: +0.25 [Gather Amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats")   
\- Glitched Drive: +0.05% [Ability Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Ability_Rate "System Page")

Every ![Glitched Drive](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)10 [Glitched Drives](/wiki/Drives#Glitched_Drive "Drives") Enhancements grants a Hive Bonus of 0.03% Ability Duplication Chance. 

Digital Bee can be enhanced with up to 500 of each type of Drive. You can view the number of enhancements by clicking on Digital Bee's [hive slot](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot"). 

Upon maxing out with all 2000 Drives, Digital Bee gains +10 [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats").

**Free Gumdrops** is a passive ability that can only be obtained through the [Unlimited Gumdrops](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Areas "Buffs & Debuffs") buff, which can be activated through certain [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes") and the [Glue Dispenser](/wiki/Glue_Dispenser "Glue Dispenser").
