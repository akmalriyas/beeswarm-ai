_"Grants x1.25 Blue Pollen for 10 minutes."_

COOLDOWN

1 second

The **Blue Extract** is an [inventory item](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory") added in the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"). The purpose of it is to use it as material to craft equipment and accessories, or for a 10-minute boost that grants x1.25 [Blue Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Blue_Pollen "System Page"). The [buff](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Items "Buffs & Debuffs") cannot stack, and will only reset its timer if the player used a **Blue Extract** when the effect is already active. Using a ![Purple Potion](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Purple_Potion.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404023738)[Purple Potion](/wiki/Purple_Potion "Purple Potion") or ![Super Smoothie](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5f/Super_Smoothie.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404023801)[Super Smoothie](/wiki/Super_Smoothie "Super Smoothie") will override the buff.

### Drops[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlue_Extract%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * As a rare drop from [rhino beetles](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle") and [mantises](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis").
  * As a common drop from [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle") and [Diamond Aphids](/wiki/Aphid#Diamond_Aphid "Aphid").
  * Farming in the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"), [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"), or the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field") can rarely drop blue extracts.
  * A rare drop from [leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves") in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, or the Stump Field.
  * As a rare drop from [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") (the odds increase depending on the rarity of the sprout and if the sprout is located on the blue fields).
  * As a drop from a [Festive Gift](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens") (if [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") is [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee")) or [Festive Mark](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Mark "Ability Tokens").
  * As a rare gift from a [cub buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy").
  * As a drop from the certain [Planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter"): 
    * The ![Blue Clay Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blue Clay Planter](/wiki/Blue_Clay_Planter "Blue Clay Planter"), and ![Hydroponic Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Hydroponic Planter](/wiki/Hydroponic_Planter "Hydroponic Planter") are guaranteed to drop blue extracts when harvested at 100% growth. 
      * The ![Hydroponic Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Hydroponic Planter](/wiki/Hydroponic_Planter "Hydroponic Planter") drops extra blue extracts in blue fields (the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, and the Stump Field).
      * Planting the ![Hydroponic Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Hydroponic Planter](/wiki/Hydroponic_Planter "Hydroponic Planter") in the Blue Flower Field, then the Bamboo Field, then the Pine Tree Forest, then the Stump Field results in an additional ![Blue Extract](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)100 **Blue Extracts** (36-day cooldown).
    * Planters harvested in blue fields may drop blue extracts.
  * As a drop from a [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") badge shiver.

    * Meticulously Crafted gives 1 blue extract and other items.
