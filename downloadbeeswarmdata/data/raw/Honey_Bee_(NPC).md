**Honey Bee** is a [Quest Giver](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers") and one of the three permanent [Quest Bees](/wiki/Category:Quest_Bees "Category:Quest Bees"), the others being [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee") and [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee"). Honey Bee is located on the rock near the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). Added in the [2019-02-01 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-02-01 "Updates"), its original purpose was to give players something to do while the next update was being worked on, but it has now become a Permanent Quest Giver. 

It doesn't require a ![Translator](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Translator](/wiki/Translator "Translator") to give quests, making it different from the other quest-giving bees.

## Tips[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney_Bee_%28NPC%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Fighting [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") and [Stick Nymphs](/wiki/Stick_Nymph "Stick Nymph") and/or the [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick") are very effective for completing its quests, as they spawn many honey tokens. Defeating normal [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") for their token rewards can also help with Honey Bee's quests.
  * Defeating many [Wild Windy Bees](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") is another way of completing this quest faster.
  * Farming [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") is a viable way, as they spawn many honey tokens.
  * [Coin Scatter](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Coin_Scatter "Passive Abilities") is also an effective way of collecting honey tokens quickly. 
    * Coin Scatter can be easily triggered by having multiple [Stubborn Bees](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee"), [Honey Bees](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee"), [Carpenter Bees](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee") and [Vector Bees](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee").
    * Use Coin Scatter on fields where there aren't many decorations blocking them, such as the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field").
  * Catching [coconuts](/wiki/Coconut "Coconut") and [meteors](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower") produces many honey tokens. 
    * Summoning a [honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm") is also an option.
  * Lower level [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") such as the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field") spawn more honey tokens than higher-level fields such as the Mountain Top Field.
  * Have bees that can spawn [honey gift](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Honey_Gift "Ability Tokens") tokens in your hive in order to complete the quests faster.
  * Using the [Gummy Star](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Star "Passive Abilities") passive would be effective as it spawns many honey tokens.
  * When the quest is finished, claim it **ONLY** when you're about to boost due to its 2x conversion.



## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney_Bee_%28NPC%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is the third bee-based [NPC](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC") in the game. It is also first bee-based NPC that isn't in its gifted form, the second being the [Digital Bee NPC](/wiki/Digital_Bee_\(NPC\) "Digital Bee \(NPC\)").
  * Honey Bee is the only Bee Quest giver that you don't need a translator to talk to.
  * Honey Bee, [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear"), and [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear") are the only quest givers to give a buff as a reward for finishing a quest.
  * Honey Bee, along with [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee"), [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee"), [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett"), [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug"), and [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") are the only non-bear quest givers.
  * In the [Egg Hunt 2019 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-17 "Updates"), there was a ![Plastic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Plastic Egg](/wiki/Plastic_Egg "Plastic Egg") token hidden behind it. 
    * It has been replaced by a ticket token granting 3 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
    * Honey Bee was one of the three quest givers that did not give an Egg Hunt quest, the others being Onett and [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear").
  * Honey Bee is the only NPC whose [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") quest affected another NPC's machine (Black Bear's Honey Wreath).
  * Honey Bee along with Spirit Bear are the only quest givers that can summon a [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm"). 
    * According to [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear"), [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") can also summon honeystorms.[1]
  * Honey Bee is one of 3 NPCs who have moved place, the others being [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") and [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear").
  * Honey Bee's quests are the only repeatable quests which don't contain the NPCs name followed by a colon (:).
  * During Beemas 2021, there was an error with Honey Bee’s ornament that made the Bag of Honey ornament and buffs are not visible to the player. This bug has been fixed and all players who gave Honey Bee a present before 2022-01-06 were refunded.
  * Honey Bee is one of the 2 NPCs (the other being Science Bear) that require you to join the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](https://www.roblox.com/groups/3982592/Bee-Swarm-Simulator-Club#!/about) in order to access the Club Dispensers and complete their quests. Its Beesmas quests require you to use the Honey Dispenser.
  * It used to be located next to the ramp leading to the [Basic Bee Gate](/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate "Basic Bee Gate"), but it was moved behind the biggest tree in the Pine Tree Forest in the [2020-06-06 Update](/wiki/Updates#2020-06-06 "Updates"). 
    * It was possible to start the quest before the [2020-06-06 Update](/wiki/Updates#2020-06-06 "Updates"), and not have 15 bees when it moved, making the quest incompletable until you got 15 bees.
  * Honey Bee is the only Bee NPC who doesn't have a specific HQ since Gifted Bucko and Gifted Riley represent the Blue and Red HQ respectively and there is currently no Colorless HQ.
