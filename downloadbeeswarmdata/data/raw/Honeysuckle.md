# Honeysuckle

Honeysuckle

[![Honeysuckle](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/21/Honeysuckle.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031718)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/21/Honeysuckle.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031718)

_"Automatically makes bees instantly convert some Pollen into Honey when your bag becomes full. (Cooldown 30s)"_

COOLDOWN

30 seconds

The **Honeysuckle** is a consumable inventory item that was introduced in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"). Unlike other consumable items with an auto setting, **Honeysuckles** are automatically used once a player's pollen container is full, and it is also the only item with an auto setting that cannot be put in the hotbar. When a player's pollen container is full, the Honeysuckle causes the player's [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to instantly convert some [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in their [bag](/wiki/Bags "Bags") into [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"). It has a cooldown of 30 seconds before it can activate again, and can be toggled on or off by clicking the item in the inventory. 

Unlike most consumables, this cannot be dragged to the hotbar. There is, however, an On/Off switch in the honeysuckle's inventory section. Turning it ON causes it to work automatically when the bag becomes full, and turning it OFF disables using honeysuckles.

  * As a rare drop from [Ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug").
  * As a drop from [planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter"). 
    * Planters in [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field") and especially [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field") give honeysuckles more often than other fields.
    * The ![Blue Clay Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blue Clay Planters](/wiki/Blue_Clay_Planter "Blue Clay Planter") drops honeysuckles as a bonus drop.
  * As a reward from the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge").
  * As a common reward from [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge").
  * As a reward from the [Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match"), [Mega Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Mega_Memory_Match "Memory Match"), [Night Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Night_Memory_Match "Memory Match") or [Extreme Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Extreme_Memory_Match "Memory Match").
  * As a Stack Reward for adding certain [stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") to the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack"): 
    * The Small Tickseed rewards 10 honeysuckles.
    * The Small White Daisy rewards 10 honeysuckles.
    * The Small Pink Tulip rewards 10 honeysuckles.
    * The Small Dandelion rewards 10 honeysuckles.
  * As a drop from [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party") mobs.
  * As a reward from the Robo Party.
  * Using the [Samovar](/wiki/Samovar "Samovar") grants 3-30 Honeysuckles (Starts from 3, increases by 1 each use and caps at 30).

## How to Use[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoneysuckle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Three things are needed for a honeysuckle to activate: 

  1. There is an available honeysuckle in inventory, i.e. it is turned on and not on cooldown.
  2. The player's bag is full.
  3. More pollen is collected.
