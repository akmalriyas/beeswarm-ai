# Gingerbread Bears

Gingerbread Bear

[![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ae/Gingerbread_Bear.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041818)

_"A cookie shaped like a bear! Gives 250 bond to the fed bee and has a 1% chance to turn that bee Gifted."_

_This article is about the item. For the similarly named Gingerbread Cub skin, see[Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy")_.___  
_ _Not to be confused with an[Aged Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear")._

A **Gingerbread Bear** is a unique type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that was added in the [2020-12-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2020-12-25 "Updates"). It is obtainable by checking [Mother Bear's](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear") [Gingerbread House](/wiki/Gingerbread_House "Gingerbread House"), defeating certain [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") and bosses, opening [Gift Boxes](/wiki/Gift_Boxes "Gift Boxes"), completing [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") quests, giving ![Present](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Presents](/wiki/Present "Present") to certain [NPCs](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers") around the map, completing NPCs Beesmas quests, using [Onett's Lid Art](/wiki/Onett%27s_Lid_Art "Onett's Lid Art"), and buying certain [Robux Packs](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop"). 

## Overview[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGingerbread_Bear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Gingerbread bears have 2 functions. The player could save them up and spend it on [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog") during the Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024 and 2025 events, or feed them to [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"). In addition to granting [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond"), it has a 1% chance to make them [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"). They can also be saved up until the next Beesmas event, where they turn into ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

The formula of turning a bee gifted: 

( 1 − 0.99 x ) × 100 % {\displaystyle (1-0.99^{x})\times 100\%} ![{\\displaystyle \(1-0.99^{x}\)\\times 100\\%}](https://services.fandom.com/mathoid-facade/v1/media/math/render/svg/e2b7cbc734cf8bfd6660baa5fd06e16757621ed2) (𝑥 is the number of the ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Gingerbread Bears** fed to a bee)

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGingerbread_Bear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Gingerbread Bears** , along with ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake"), are the only currencies that can be used in Bee Bear's Catalog. 
    * However, unlike ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Gingerbread Bears** are not removed from the player's inventory when Beesmas ends and are still obtainable and usable.
  * This, along with the ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat") and ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"), are the only items in the game that can turn [Event bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") gifted.
  * This and the ![Festive Bean](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Bean](/wiki/Festive_Bean "Festive Bean") are the only event items that are infinitely renewable outside of an event.
  * Feeding ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)100 **Gingerbread Bears** to a bee does not guarantee to turn it gifted. The chance of it becoming gifted is around 63%.
