A **Single Mitten** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 6 [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas") [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of three. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [rare](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"), [epic](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"), or [legendary](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary") [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees").
