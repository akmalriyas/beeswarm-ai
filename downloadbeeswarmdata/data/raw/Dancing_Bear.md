# Dancing Bear

[![An image of Shadow Bear, along with where the Diamond Egg token is.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/36/RobloxScreenShot20230730_140033303_%282%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20230730060756)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/36/RobloxScreenShot20230730_140033303_%282%29.png/revision/latest?cb=20230730060756) [](/wiki/File:RobloxScreenShot20230730_140033303_\(2\).png)

An image of Shadow Bear, along with where the Diamond Egg token is.

**Shadow Bear** is located inside the [30 Bee Area](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate"), under the platform where [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett") is. It is floating just above the floor, similar to [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear"). There is a ![Diamond Egg](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/70/Diamond_Egg.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404043735)[Diamond Egg](/wiki/Egg#Diamond_Egg "Egg") token in front of it. Unfortunately, the floor around it is made of a shadow substance that kills you instantly, no matter how much [defense](/wiki/System_Page#Defense "System Page") you have. 

To reach the egg token, equip either the [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute") or the [Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider") and make sure your [bag](/wiki/Bags "Bags") is empty so you don't lose any [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). 

  * If you're using the Parachute, go up to where the ramp meets the platform. Jump to the side and immediately deploy the parachute. Make a beeline to the front of Shadow Bear where you can just make out the sparkle of the token.
  * If you're using the Glider, jump off the ledge where the ticket token is located, and then glide straight to the diamond egg token.
  * Whichever way, you will die eventually, but that's okay, as you get to keep the diamond egg in your inventory.



It may be easier to use the Parachute as it is slower than the Glider, so you can focus more on the location of the diamond egg.

  * If the player is in a laggy server, it is possible for them to walk on the floor and grab the diamond egg.
  * Before the 2021 Beesmas Event, you could rapidly jump on the floor and grab the ![Diamond Egg](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/70/Diamond_Egg.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404043735)[Diamond Egg](/wiki/Egg#Diamond_Egg "Egg") without dying. Currently, this method is patched.
  * Shadow Bear is the largest of all the [bears](/wiki/Category:Bears "Category:Bears"). It looks just like [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), except for its greater size and it does not chase you.
  * It is the only bear who has no function: it doesn't give quests, it doesn't run a [shop](/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops"), and it doesn't protect an area. [Noob Bear](/wiki/Noob_Bear "Noob Bear"), [Pro Bear](/wiki/Pro_Bear "Pro Bear"), and [Top Bear](/wiki/Top_Bear "Top Bear") have no function, but they run shops.
  * Shadow Bear, [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear"), all [Cub Buddies](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy"), and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") are the only NPCs who use a dance emote (/e dance, /e dance2, and /e dance3 are types of Roblox dances, Shadow Bear is using /e dance3). Other bears can do /e wave, which is also an emote.
  * Shadow Bear, [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), and [Snowbear](/wiki/Snowbear "Snowbear") are the only faceless bears.
  * Shadow Bear could possibly be the Giant Bear that [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear") alludes to in his "Solo On The Stump" quest.
