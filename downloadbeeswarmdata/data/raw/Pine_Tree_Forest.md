The **Pine Tree Forest** is a [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") located past the [Honey Bee Gate](/wiki/Honey_Bee_Gate "Honey Bee Gate") and near the [Badge Bearer's Guild](/wiki/Badge_Bearer%27s_Guild "Badge Bearer's Guild"). It is protected by two [mantises](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis") and the [Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf"). 

This field can spawn [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [blueberry](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"), [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [blue extract](/wiki/Blue_Extract "Blue Extract"), [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), and [neonberry](/wiki/Neonberry "Neonberry") tokens while gathering [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). [Planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter") in this field grant extra [whirligigs](/wiki/Whirligig "Whirligig") and blue extracts. 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Pine Tree Forest are [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"), [Cool Bee](/wiki/Cool_Bee "Cool Bee"), [Bucko Bee](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee"), [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee"), [Carpenter Bee](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee"), [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee"), [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee"), [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee"), and [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"). Bees that dislike the Pine Tree Forest are [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"), [Rad Bee](/wiki/Rad_Bee "Rad Bee"), [Riley Bee](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee"), [Fire Bee](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee"), [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee"), [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee"), [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") and [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"). 

This field grants Comforting [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a planter is planted here. 

This is a 23x31 field, containing 713 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are white and blue, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

[![The Pine Tree Forest boost icon.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/db/Pine_Tree_Forest_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080237) [![The Pine Tree Forest boost icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/db/Pine_Tree_Forest_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080237)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/db/Pine_Tree_Forest_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080237)

The Pine Tree Forest boost icon.

  * Using the [Blue Field Booster](/wiki/Blue_Field_Booster "Blue Field Booster") may give a Pine Tree [Forest Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +100% Pine Tree Forest pollen for 15 minutes.
  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") will add 100% Pine Tree Forest pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% - +300% Pine Tree Forest pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Pine Tree [Forest Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of 20% instant Pine Tree Forest conversion and give +5% instant Pine Tree Forest conversion and +15% Pine Tree Forest pollen for each stack. Pine Tree Forest Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
  * The [Pinecone](/wiki/Pinecone "Pinecone") [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") can grant up to +15% Pine Tree [Forest Capacity](/wiki/Field_Capacity "Field Capacity") and +10% Pine Tree Forest Pollen.
  * Giving [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") a [present](/wiki/Present "Present") during the Beesmas 2025 event gives you a x3 Pine Tree Forest boost.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPine_Tree_Forest%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Festive Bee likes the Pine Tree Forest likely since most Christmas trees are coniferous trees, like pine trees. 
    * It is also the only red bee to like a field of the opposite color. The only other bee that shares this trait is [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") liking the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field").
  * The Pine Tree Forest is the only field with "Forest" in its name instead of "Field" or "Patch".
  * This field is guarded by the werewolf and 2 mantises. This makes the Pine Tree Forest the field with the most [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") guarding it, at 3. 
    * Standing in the area of the field closest to the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack") will prevent the werewolf from detecting the player.
  * There is a [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token on top of the tallest pine tree in this field. To get it, the player can do the [Cloud Obby](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses#Cloud_Obby "Obstacle Courses") starting near [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear") using the [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute") to glide to the next step. However, it is much easier to use the [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon") and the Parachute/[Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider").
  * As of the [2020-06-06 update](/wiki/Updates#2020-06-06 "Updates"), [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") can be found on the gray platform behind the Pine Tree Forest.
  * The Pine Tree Forest has shrunk since it was released. Before the Badge Bearer's Guild was added, the field stretched all the way to where the Badge Bearer's Guild currently is. When the Badge Bearer's Guild was added, the field was shrunken to end at the door of the guild. It has been shrunken again, now being farther from the guild's door. 
    * One of the pine trees still remains near the [Honey Bee Gate](/wiki/Honey_Bee_Gate "Honey Bee Gate") from the field's original spot.
