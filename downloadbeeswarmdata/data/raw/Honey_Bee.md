**Honey Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Epic Bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"). 

Honey Bee's favorite treat is ![Pineapple](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"). 

Honey Bee likes the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch") and the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"). It dislikes the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 360 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 2 seconds.
  * +280 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +50% convert speed.
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.5 [Honey From Tokens](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_From_Tokens "System Page").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Honey Gift]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Honey_Gift "Ability Tokens")** Sometimes spawns a honey token. The amount of honey is equal to 100x (x being the bee's level).
  * **[[Honey Mark]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mark "Ability Tokens")** Marks a random area on the [Field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") for 7 seconds (+0.2s per Level) that grants 2 Conversion Links and x1.25 [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page") when you stand in it. Stacks up to 3 times.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * There is a [gate](/wiki/Category:Gates "Category:Gates") dedicated to the [bee](/wiki/Bee "Bee"), the [Honey Bee Gate](/wiki/Honey_Bee_Gate "Honey Bee Gate") (or the 15 Bee Zone).
  * The only way to obtain a ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Honey_Bee_Egg "Egg") is to defeat [cave monsters](/wiki/Cave_Monster "Cave Monster"). 
    * This may be the reason why Honey Bee dislikes the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), due to the similarities between [spiders](/wiki/Spider "Spider") and cave monsters.
  * The [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask "Honey Mask"), which can be purchased in the [Ace Badge](/wiki/Badges "Badges") area of the [Badge Bearer's Guild](/wiki/Badge_Bearer%27s_Guild "Badge Bearer's Guild"), resembles Honey Bee's face decal and color scheme.
  * Before the [2018-11-25 Update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"), the [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") Hive Bonus of Honey Bee was +250% [Conversion Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page"). This was later updated to x1.5 Honey From Tokens.
  * Honey Bee is one of the two bees that generate honey gifts, the other being [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee").
  * Honey Bee was given the honey mark ability after the [2019-04-05 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates").
  * Both Honey Bee and [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee") emit the same bubble effect, although the colors of these 2 bees are different.
  * Along with [Bumble Bee](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee") and [Carpenter Bee](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee"), Honey Bee are based on bees found in real life, though they don't show resemblance to the actual bee.
  * During [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") 2020 and 2021, players were able to receive a Honey Bee Jelly if they gave a present to [Honey Bee (NPC)](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)"). 
    * During Beesmas 2021, [Honey Bee Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Specific_Bee_Jelly "Royal Jelly") was also available by purchasing the Honeysuckle Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
