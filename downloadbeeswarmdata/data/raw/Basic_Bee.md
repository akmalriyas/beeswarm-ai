**Basic Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Common bee](/wiki/Bees/Common "Bees/Common"). It is usually the first bee to be discovered and hatched. 

Basic Bee can be only hatched from a ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Basic Egg](/wiki/Egg#Basic_Egg "Egg"), a ![Star Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Egg](/wiki/Egg#Star_Egg "Egg"), or obtained through the currently unobtainable Basic Bee Jelly. It cannot be obtained from normal ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") or ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly"). 

Basic Bee's favorite treat is ![Sunflower Seed](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"). 

Basic Bee likes the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field"), [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), and [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"). It dislikes the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBasic_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Basic Bee is the only type of Common bee.
  * Due to the large number of appearances Basic Bee makes within the game and thumbnails, it is considered the main mascot of Bee Swarm Simulator.
  * Basic Bee is one of three bees to have an official plushie on the [Official Bee Swarm Simulator Website](https://shop.beeswarm.com/), the others being Tabby Bee and Vicious Bee.
  * The statistics for all other [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") are described in terms of their difference from Basic Bee’s statistics. For example, +20 collection means that the bee it's describing has collected 20 more pollen than Basic Bee.
  * Basic Bee and [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee") are the only bees without abilities. 
    * However, if they are in their [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") forms, they still can produce [Inspire tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inspire "Ability Tokens").
    * And specific [Beequips](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") grants abilities like [Focus](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Focus "Ability Tokens") or [Melody](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Melody "Ability Tokens") tokens.
    * Because of this, during [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") "Ability Token Tidings" quest [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") refers to Basic Bee and [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee") as "Poor little merely mortal bees". 
      * Bee Bear also says they aren't magic due to this fact.
  * Basic Bee and [Event Bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") are the only types of bee that cannot be obtained from a normal royal jelly since Basic Bee is a Common Bee.
  * The [code](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), "DontUseThisJelly" (expired) gave a [Basic Bee jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Royal_Jelly_Variants "Royal Jelly") that could be used on a bee to transform any bee into a Basic Bee.
  * Basic Bee and [Demo Bee](/wiki/Demo_Bee "Demo Bee") share the same skin color pattern when they are not in their gifted forms.
  * Basic Bee and [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee") possess the same colors but in a different pattern.
  * Basic Bee's gifted bonus used to give +20% [Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Pollen "System Page"), but it later got buffed after the [2019-04-05 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates") into x1.2 Pollen, so it can now increase with any additional pollen multipliers that the player has.
  * An image of a winking Basic Bee face appears at the end of the White Tunnel. 
    * This may refer to the currently valid code called "Wink".
    * This also appeared in the thumbnail of the [2018-06-02 update.](/wiki/Updates#2018-06-02 "Updates")
  * Basic Bee is the only kind of bee to appear in every different version of the thumbnail for the game.
  * Basic Bee, along with Tabby Bee and [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"), are all of [Onett's](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") favorite bees. 
    * This was revealed through Onett's dialogue.
  * Basic Bee's face appears on the bee menu icon, found near the top left corner of the screen. 
    * Its face is also visible on ![Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), and the [beequip case](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip").
  * The chances of getting a Gifted Basic Bee from sunflower seeds is 1 in 12,000. 
    * The same chances apply to Legendary Bees if you feed them their favorite treats.
  * The chances of hatching a Gifted Basic Bee from a Basic Egg is 0.348% (1 out of 287.35632184).
  * On very rare occasions, Basic Bee's face would disappear on most surfaces that use its texture.
  * Basic Bee, [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), Tabby Bee, [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"), Puppy Bee, and Bear Bee are the only bees to represent realistic animals. 
    * But only the [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), Tabby Bee, [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"), Puppy Bee, and Bear Bee represent realistic animals if Basic Bee is not counted since all the bees in this game are bees.
  * Basic Bee, [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee"), [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee"), [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"), and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") are the only bees in the game featured on a gate.
