**Gummy Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event"). It can be purchased for ![Gumdrops](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)2,500 [Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") from the [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Gummy_Bee_Egg_Claim "Gummy Bee Egg Claim"). 

Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, and the only way to make it gifted is by feeding it a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

Gummy Bee likes the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), and the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"). It dislikes the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGummy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 700 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +150% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), +620 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +2 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +5% [Honey Per Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_Per_Pollen "System Page").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGummy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Gumdrop Barrage]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Gumdrop_Barrage "Ability Tokens")** Launches Gumdrops in a large area, covering the field in goo. [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") covered in goo grant bonus Honey that increases with the size of the Goo puddle.
  * **[[Glob]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Glob "Ability Tokens")** Covers 49 surrounding flowers in goo. If gifted, covers 81 Flowers instead.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGummy_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * There is a model of the Gummy Bee on the [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Gummy_Bee_Egg_Claim "Gummy Bee Egg Claim"). Using [gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") right after touching it and receiving the message; "The Gummy Bee wants gumdrops" will teleport the player to [Gummy Bear's Lair](/wiki/Gummy_Bear%27s_Lair "Gummy Bear's Lair"). The player must have the [Goo Hotshot Badge](/wiki/Badges#Goo_Badge "Badges") to enter the lair, or else there is a message that says "Only Goo Hotshots can hear Gummy Bee...".
  * Gummy Bee is the only [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") that doesn't have extra body parts.
  * Gummy Bee and [Fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies") share the same face, which resembles [Basic Bee's](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee") face.
  * The Gummy Bee is the fourth [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") to be released in an [Update](/wiki/Updates "Updates"). 
    * This bee was also the first bee to ever be a prize for completing the [Quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") of a [Traveling Bear](/wiki/Category:Traveling_Bears "Category:Traveling Bears").
    * It was also the third bee to have been unobtainable for a while.
  * The Gummy Bee, [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"), [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"), [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"), [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee"), [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee"), and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") are the only Event bees which can be a [First Edition Bee](/wiki/First_Edition_Bee "First Edition Bee").
  * This is the only bee that is translucent.
  * The Gummy Bee was once obtainable through completing all 15 quests of [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear"). 
    * Before the [2019-04-05 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates"), the player could buy Gummy Bee in the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") for 500 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
    * [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") took the place of Gummy Bee in the Ticket Tent after the 2019-04-05 Update.
  * The Gummy Bee and [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee") are the only bees that have a sparkle effect that surrounds their bodies.
  * Gummy Bee emits a faint light from its eyes that can be easily seen in dark places.
  * This is the only Event bee to have a [Mask](/wiki/Category:Hats "Category:Hats") based off of it, the [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask"). 
    * The other bees that have a mask based off them are [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee"), [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee"), [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee"), [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee"), and [Fire Bee](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee").
  * Gummy Bee is the third bee in the game to at some point cost a currency besides tickets, the first being Bear Bee (Robux), the second being [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") ([Stingers](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger")), and the fourth being [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") ([Cloud Vials](/wiki/Cloud_Vial "Cloud Vial")).
  * This bee, [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"), [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee"), [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee"), and [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee") are the only bees whose signature abilities are directly buffed by being gifted.
  * If it were to be bought by buying gumdrops from the [Gumdrop Shop](/wiki/Gumdrop_Shop "Gumdrop Shop"), it would cost 834 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), with 2 [gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") left over.
  * When [Gummy Morph](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Morph "Passive Abilities") is active, this bee has the highest base attack, an attack of 303 being about 30 times the base attack of [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee").
  * Gummy Bee can find two stickers when in a specific field: 
    * Gummy Bee has a chance to find a Launching Rocket [Sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") in the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field").
    * Gummy Bee has a very rare chance to find a Yellow Sticky Hand [Sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") in the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field").
  * In the in-game stat menu for Gummy Bee, its convert amount is stated to be 720 more than the normal amount (80). However, it is actually 620 more.
