# Capacity

**Capacity** is a [mechanic](/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") that shows how much [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") the player can store at a time. Capacity can be increased in a variety of ways including [buffs](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs "Buffs & Debuffs"), [items](/wiki/Items "Items"), [stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker"), and [amulets](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet"). Capacity can be increased by static amounts or by multipliers. Static increases can be shown simply through addition symbols (+) while multipliers can be shown through multiplication symbols (x) or percentages (%). The player's Capacity is displayed in the top-middle section, labelled as "Pollen" and also on the player's bag. The player can see how much their capacity is multiplied by looking for the Capacity Multiplier stat in the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page").

    * The Gold Stick Bug Amulet gives from +150,000 Capacity up to +200,000 Capacity.
    * The Diamond Stick Bug Amulet gives from +200,000 Capacity up to +300,000 Capacity.
  * The [Cog Amulet](/wiki/Cog_Amulet "Cog Amulet") can give up to +250,000 Capacity depending on the tier.

  * Using a ![Marshmallow Bee](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Marshmallow Bee](/wiki/Marshmallow_Bee "Marshmallow Bee") gives +50% Capacity. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
  * Using a ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflake](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") or collecting the [Snowglobe Shake](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Snowglobe_Shake "Ability Tokens") ability token from the ![Snowglobe](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowglobe](/wiki/Snowglobe "Snowglobe") Beequip gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.
  * Using a ![Glitched Drive](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Glitched Drive](/wiki/Drives#Glitched_Drive "Drives") during the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge") gives a x1.25 Capacity Multiplier.
  * Making combos with [Reindeer Fetch](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Fetch "Ability Tokens") from [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee") with the ![Reindeer Antlers](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Reindeer Antlers](/wiki/Reindeer_Antlers "Reindeer Antlers") Beequip equipped can give from a x1.01 Capacity Multiplier up to a x1.1 Capacity Multiplier.
  * Defeating the [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick") for the Mondo Chick Blessing can give from a x1.02 Capacity Multiplier up to a x1.2 Capacity Multiplier.
  * Balloon Blessing can give from a x1.03 capacity multiplier up to a x4 Capacity Multiplier.
  * Galentine's Blessing (Beesmas only) gives +50% Capacity.
  * Beesmas Repentance (Beesmas only) gives a x1.05 Capacity Multiplier.
  * Defeating [Festive Nymphs](/wiki/Festive_Nymph "Festive Nymph") during Beesmas will guarantee a Festive Nymph Blessing token drop which can stack up to give from +1,000 Capacity up to +250,000 Capacity. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
  * Defeating Party Robots or completing a [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party") during Beesmas can give Robo Party Blessing which can stack up to give from +1,000 Capacity up to +100,000 Capacity. The buff can also be given from certain codes.
  * The Guiding Star Aura gives x2.5 Capacity to the player who summoned the star and x1.25 Capacity to other players.
  * The Capacity Code buff, which is given by certain codes, gives a x2 Capacity Multiplier.

  * The highest static Capacity the player can have at a time if you were to remove all multipliers is 4,807,000. This is achieved by having: 
    * Mondo B.B.M. Mask (+250K)
    * Coconut Canister (+1M)
    * Crimson Guard (+250K)
    * Cobalt Guard (+250K)
    * Coconut Belt (+350K)
    * Moon Amulet (+250K)
    * Stick Bug Amulet (+300K)
    * Cog Amulet (+250K)
    * Two Berets (+27K)
    * Three Single Mittens (+45K)
    * Three Beesmas Tree Hats (+105K)
    * Festive Wreath (+110K)
    * Festive Nymph Blessing (+250K)
    * Robo Party Blessing (+100K)
    * RAM upgrade maxed (+750K)
    * Grandmaster Ability Badge (+250K).
    * All Sticker Stack Effects (+270K) 
      * If all +(%) multipliers are applied, the player would have a total of 15,704,500 Capacity.
      * If all multipliers were applied, the player would have a total of 771,391,706,905,963 Capacity. 
        * This Is achieved by having Most from above and: 
          * Replace Mondo B.B.M. Mask With Diamond Mask (-250k but x3)
          * Supreme Star Amulet (x2.5)
          * Gifted Hive Bonuses (x1.1 & x1.2)
          * Replace one Beret With [Puppy Bee's Antlers](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip")
  * The lowest possible capacity the player can have is 0.7734357 but can not display it. By equipping the [Pouch](/wiki/Pouch "Pouch") (+200) and having every upgrade that reduces capacity in the Robo Bear Challenge while having no other Capacity increasers or multipliers.
  * The Capacity Buff, Field Code Buff, Field Capacity Buff, and the x2 Pollen Boost are the only code exclusive buffs.
