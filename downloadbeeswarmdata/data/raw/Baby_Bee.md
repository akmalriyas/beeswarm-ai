**Baby Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Legendary bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"). 

Baby Bee’s favorite treat is ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"). 

Baby Bee likes the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"), [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field"), [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), and the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"). It dislikes the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"), [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch") and the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBaby_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Baby Bee has the second-lowest base energy (lowest being [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee")), second-lowest base [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page") (lowest being [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee")), and the lowest base gather amount in the game. Baby Bee also has the second-slowest base gather speed, 1 second faster than Tadpole Bee and Fuzzy Bee.
  * Baby Bee and Tadpole Bee tie for the smallest [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in the game. They are also the only two bee types to have a different size compared to other bees.
  * Baby Bee is the only bee that has 0 base attack. Because of that, it cannot attack any [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs"), even if it has an attack mutation. 
    * Baby Bee used to be able to attack and could do damage if the player had any damage boosts. After the [2018-11-25 Update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"), this is no longer the case.
    * According to [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear"), the reason why they don't attack is because "They're too dumb to notice" the mobs.
    * Even though it says, "0 attack", attack added to baby bee still counts towards total attack, which can buff [Star Saw](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Saw "Passive Abilities").
  * Baby Bee, [Bumble Bee](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee"), [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee"), and [Exhausted Bee](/wiki/Exhausted_Bee "Exhausted Bee") are tied as the slowest bees in the game.
  * Baby Bee has the most field preferences in the game. It also dislikes the most [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"). 
    * Baby Bee prefers the lower-tier fields (Dandelion, Blue Flower, Sunflower, etc.) and dislikes the higher-tier fields (Rose, Cactus, Pine Tree Forest, etc.).
  * Baby Bee is the only bee that isn't red to like the Mushroom Field.
  * Baby Bee and [Stubborn Bee](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee") are the only [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") bees that like strawberries.
  * Baby Bee and [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee") are the only bees with three unique colors on their skins. Baby Bee is also the only gifted bee with three unique colors on its skin.
  * There is a small chance that a Baby Bee egg will drop from [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear").
  * Before 2018-11-25 update, the Gifted Hive Bonus of Baby Bee was +5% Loot Luck, which was then changed to +10% in said update. This was then changed to +20% Loot Luck in the [2019-04-05 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates") and once again to +25% Loot Luck in the Beesmas 2019 update.
  * Onett paid $2 for the laughing baby sound that Baby Love uses. It can be bought [Here](https://audiojungle.net/item/baby-laughing/21354817).
  * This is one of the only 4 bees that has a specific egg obtainable from mob drops, with the others being a Rascal Bee egg from ladybugs, a Bumble Bee egg from rhino beetles, or a Honey Bee egg from cave monsters.
  * Baby Bee, [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"), and Bumble Bee are the only bees shown on [Onett's Lid Art](/wiki/Onett%27s_Lid_Art "Onett's Lid Art").
  * When gifted and in the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"), [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field"), [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), or [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field") it has a very rare chance to find a ![Hivesticker pale heart](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pale Heart Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker").
  * Baby Bee's in-game stat menu does not actually display that its convert speed and gather speed is decreased by 25%.
