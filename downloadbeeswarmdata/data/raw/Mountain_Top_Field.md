The **Mountain Top Field** is a [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") located past the [Lion Bee Gate](/wiki/Lion_Bee_Gate "Lion Bee Gate"). The [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick") protects this field and respawns roughly every hour. 

This field grants Invigorating [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is planted and harvested here. 

This field can spawn [Red Boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), [Blue Boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [Magic Bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), [Ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [Bitterberry](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"), and [Neonberry](/wiki/Neonberry "Neonberry") Tokens while collecting pollen. 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Mountain Top Field are [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"), [Looker Bee](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee"), [Frosty Bee](/wiki/Frosty_Bee "Frosty Bee"), [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee"), [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee"), [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"), [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee"), [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") and [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"). Bees that dislike the Mountain Top Field are [Carpenter Bee](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee") and [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee"). 

This is a 24×28 field, containing 672 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are red and blue, and the natural size is triple.

## Possible Events[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMountain_Top_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * A level 6-8 [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") may spawn here either naturally, when the night starts, or if a player has used a [Night Bell](/wiki/Night_Bell "Night Bell").
  * A level 4-6 [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") may spawn here, if manually summoned by a player from the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine"), it is level 5-6 instead. 
    * There are special conditions where a Wild Windy Bee can spawn here.
    * When claiming Spirit Bear’s “Tickle the Wind” quest, 3 level 4 Wild Windy Bee's will spawn here.
  * Mondo Chick spawns here at the beginning of the hour.
  * During a [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge"). If Stick Bug is in [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"), and [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), he may jump to this field.
  * An [Aphid](/wiki/Aphid "Aphid") may spawn here upon gathering [Leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves").



## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMountain_Top_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Mountain Top [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Mountain Top Field conversion and give +5% instant Mountain Top Field conversion and +10% Mountain Top Field pollen for each stack. Mountain Top Field can stack up to a total of 13 times.
  * Redeeming certain valid codes: 
    * ThreeBeeVee (Gives Mountain Top Field Boost x3, Mountain Top Field Capacity x3, and Mountain Top Field Code + other stuff).

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMountain_Top_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is the only field that doesn't produce [treats](/wiki/Treats "Treats") from farming. However, harvesting [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") in the Mountain Top Field will still drop normal rewards.
  * Leaves in this field are much less frequent, and always either spawn bitterberries, [aphids](/wiki/Aphid "Aphid"), or a [sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker").
  * This was the only field that wasn't targeted by the [Gummy Cannon](/wiki/Gummy_Cannon "Gummy Cannon") during the [Gummy Invasion](/wiki/Category:Gummy_Invasion "Category:Gummy Invasion") event. (The [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"), [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field"), [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch"), and the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field") were not in the game at that time.)
  * The Mountain Top Field used to not have a badge dedicated to it. 
    * This was changed in the 2024-01-12 Update, when the Mountain Top Field received a [badge](/wiki/Badges "Badges"), thus making the Ant Field the only field without a badge, and, later, the Brick Fields as well.
  * This is the first field that consists of only triple flowers, the second being the Stump Field, the third and fourth being the Pepper Patch and the Coconut Field. 
    * This is also the only field without any white flowers.
  * Before the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"), the [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon") required 22 discovered [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") types, but now it requires 25 instead, so the player can't bypass the Lion Bee Gate without 25 bees or 25 bee types discovered. 
    * After the 2019-09-28 update, even by bypassing the Lion Bee Gate with 25 bee types discovered but not 25 bees, the player will be teleported back to spawn.
  * The Mountain Top Field requires the most amount of pollen to unlock its Grandmaster badge, at a grand total of 1 quadrillion pollen.
  * The Mountain Top Field is the only field that cannot be boosted without the help of items or codes.
