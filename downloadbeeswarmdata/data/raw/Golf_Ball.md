# Golf Ball

![Hivesticker eviction](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) | **This piece of content goes bye bye.** The following content has been removed from the game. The contents below may be archival, but feel free to edit below.

**Golf** is a system that was introduced with the [2024-08-01 update](/wiki/Updates#2024-08-01 "Updates") as part of Bee Swarm Simulator's experience in "[The Games](/wiki/The_Games "The Games")" event and removed in the [2025-04-30 update](/wiki/Updates#2025-04-30 "Updates"). 

Golf was used throughout [Sun Bear's](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") 2024 questline, his quests required the player to hit golf balls into holes, and to activate Targets. The golf quests were exclusive to [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") when this mechanic was around. 

Golf only appears in the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"), [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field") and [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field").

## Golf Ball

  * Normal 




[ ![GolfBallMidair](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/51/GolfBallMidair.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20240804002648) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/51/GolfBallMidair.png/revision/latest?cb=20240804002648 "GolfBallMidair")

**Golf balls** have a pollen meter, which can be filled, similar to [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom") and [Blooms](/wiki/Bloom "Bloom"). When filled, the player can putt the ball, by collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") near the ball when it's pollen meter is full. The further away the player stands, the farther and higher the ball will go. A hit golf ball collects [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") from [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") underneath it, however, they do not interact with [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") or [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") but they do have collision with the environment and Pink Fences. Golf balls can also hit targets, giving the player some rewards. Unlike [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom") and [Blooms](/wiki/Bloom "Bloom"), the golf ball only has a single appearance and never levels up the more times you putt it. There is also no variation of golf balls.

There can only be two Golf Balls in a field at any time. A new Golf Ball spawns after they are putted into a hole or sink into mud.

## Hole

[ ![GolfFlagDandelionField](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/03/GolfFlagDandelionField.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20240804005544) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/03/GolfFlagDandelionField.png/revision/latest?cb=20240804005544 "GolfFlagDandelionField")

### Effects

Putting a golf ball into a hole spawns 7 Honey tokens.

### Source

Appears in the Dandelion, Blue Flower and Sunflower Fields.

**Holes** are located in the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"), [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field") and [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field"). Putting the golf ball into a hole rewards a ring of ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)7 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") tokens around the hole, which scales by the amount of points and the level of the golf ball. After putting a golf ball into a Hole, a new golf ball with a different level spawns.

## Mud

[ ![Mud](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/39/Mud.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20240804024406) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/39/Mud.png/revision/latest?cb=20240804024406 "Mud")

### Effects

Makes Golf Balls that stop on it respawn.

### Source

Appears in the Dandelion, Blue Flower and Sunflower Fields.

**Mud** is found on [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") in the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"), [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field") and [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field"). Mud tints all flowers underneath it brown. If a golf ball comes to a complete stop over mud, it sinks and a new golf ball spawns. 

This is the same mud as the pattern [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") turns into during the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge").

## Pink Fence

[ ![PinkFenceBlueFlowerField](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/21/PinkFenceBlueFlowerField.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20240804015232) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/21/PinkFenceBlueFlowerField.png/revision/latest?cb=20240804015232 "PinkFenceBlueFlowerField")

### Effects

Blocks Golf Balls shot at it.

### Source

Appears in the Blue Flower Field and Sunflower Field.

**Pink Fences** are a set of transparent pink fences that can be found in the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field") and [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field"). The fences block Golf Balls shot at it. 

They can only hit moving Golf Balls, and do not affect players, [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs"), or any other mechanics.
