The **Flight of the Bumble Egg** is a cosmetic hat that could have been obtained by completing the quest from [Egg Hunt Info](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_Info "Egg Hunt Info"), from April 17, 2019, through May 13, 2019, which was during the Roblox [Egg Hunt 2019](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2019 "Egg Hunt 2019") event. Since it is a Roblox hat, players can wear it on their avatar for display. 

The quest required ![Marshmallow Bee](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)3 [Marshmallow Bees](/wiki/Marshmallow_Bee "Marshmallow Bee"), which could be obtained by any NPC on the map (at the time) except [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett") and the [Honey Bee NPC](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)"), and through the [code](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), “Marshmallow” (now invalid). When the player had 3 marshmallow bees in their inventory, they could turn it in to the Egg Hunt Info for the Flight of the Bumble Egg. Receiving the Flight of the Bumble Egg also rewards an [Event Badge](/wiki/Badges#Unobtainable "Badges") exclusive for the Egg Hunt.
