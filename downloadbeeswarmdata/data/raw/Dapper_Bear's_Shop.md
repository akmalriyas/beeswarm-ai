# Dapper Bear's Shop

## Dapper Bear's Shop

[ ![DapperBearsShop](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d3/DapperBearsShop.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20220401000549) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d3/DapperBearsShop.png/revision/latest?cb=20220401000549 "DapperBearsShop") The exterior of Dapper Bear's shop.

### Location

Past the Brave Bee Gate, near the Stump Field.

**Dapper Bear's Shop** is a shop located behind the Brave Bee Gate. Located on a hilltop, the player is able to access the shop by jumping between ledges to climb the hill. The shop sells reusable [Planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter") and 6 beequips, with the 3rd, 4th, 5th, and 6th beequips requiring you to complete a certain number of [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear")'s quests in order to unlock them. You also have to pay honey and tickets for each beequip with the amounts scaling from the quality of the beequip. This is also where [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear") is located. 

To enter Dapper Bear's Shop, the player must wear the [Propeller Hat](/wiki/Propeller_Hat "Propeller Hat") and the [Hiking Boots](/wiki/Hiking_Boots "Hiking Boots"), or a better hat and boots. If the player wears anything worse ([Helmet](/wiki/Helmet "Helmet"), [Strange Goggles](/wiki/Strange_Goggles "Strange Goggles"), [B.B.M. Mask](/wiki/B.B.M._Mask "B.B.M. Mask"), [Mondo B.B.M. Mask](/wiki/Mondo_B.B.M._Mask "Mondo B.B.M. Mask"), [Basic Boots](/wiki/Basic_Boots "Basic Boots")) and tries to enter the shop, a red player will prevent the player from entering with the prompt:  You Must Wear a Nice Hat and Cool Boots to enter Dapper Bear's Shop.

The shop has a tan interior with a blue floor and ceiling, as well as two windows in the front. Above the entrance is an image of a Beequip Case. On the left side of the interior, there is the Beequip shop and the [Highest Damage to a Single Puffshroom](/wiki/Highest_Damage_to_a_Single_Puffshroom "Highest Damage to a Single Puffshroom") leaderboard. On the right side of the shop is the Planter shop. The roof of the shop has a ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token, 25 ![Honeysuckle](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honeysuckles](/wiki/Honeysuckle "Honeysuckle"), and 3 ![Smooth Dice](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Smooth Dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"). 

The shop has an [obstacle course](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses#Dapper_Bear's_Shop_Obby "Obstacle Courses") on the right side of the shop that leads up to the roof. It splits up into two paths: a rocky wall and a moon obby. The rocky wall allows access the roof, where a royal jelly and a honeysuckle token are hidden. The moon obby leads to a ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") on a platform behind the shop. It is also possible to bypass the obby by using a [Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider"). 

As of the time of writing, during every [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") since 2021, Dapper Bear's [Samovar](/wiki/Samovar "Samovar") is located next to the shop entrance. In many Beesmas events since, [Gift Boxes](/wiki/Gift_Boxes "Gift Boxes") were also placed on the roof, or near the shop in some capacity.

_Much of the information provided came from the[Dapper Bear's Beequip Shop Data spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1HwelyR0D-nwBUbvuFOLDHZUviyUbpNKBg7RdKQDP3yc/edit?usp=sharing). Information without proof has been stripped._

The Beequip Shop offers [Beequips](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"), sold in 6 slots. Only the first 2 are unlocked for the player at first; the 3rd, 4th, 5th, and 6th slots respectively require 5, 10, 15, and 20 quests finished from [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear"). Higher slots can give better beequips, with better chances for a high potential. The cost of a given beequip scales based on the type of the beequip and its potential. 

Beequips displayed in the shop can show half-star potential values, unlike when inspecting from the Beequip Case. Note that despite what inspecting the beequip after buying it shows, the potential of the bought beequip is unchanged. 

Each slot is refreshed with a new beequip after a certain amount of time. More specifically, starting from 0 AM (UTC): 

  * Slot 1 refreshes every 2 hours.
  * Slot 2 and 3 refrehses every 4 hours.
  * Slot 4 and 5 refreshes every 8 hours.
  * Slot 6 refreshes every 24 hours.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDapper_Bear%2527s_Shop%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This shop and [Robo Bear's Shop](/wiki/Robo_Bear%27s_Shop "Robo Bear's Shop") are the only shops where the Bear NPC owner can talk to you. If you have bought Gummy Boots, Mask or Gummyballer, You can talk to Gummy Bear.
  * This is the only shop where you need to equip a certain hat and boots to access (Propeller Hat and Hiking Boots or something "higher" than them).
  * This shop, [Robo Bear’s Shop](/wiki/Robo_Bear%27s_Shop "Robo Bear's Shop"), [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") and the [Coconut Cave](/wiki/Coconut_Cave "Coconut Cave") are the only shops where the music that is playing isn't "[vendor](/wiki/Music "Music")". 
    * Unlike the other two, Dapper Bear’s Shop has its own music.
  * Using the [Hiking Boots](/wiki/Hiking_Boots "Hiking Boots") can help with completing the obby. The jump power is low enough to not bump into the roof, but high enough to be able to grab the [Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") at the end. A [Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider") is also recommended.
  * There used to be a [Beequip Storage](/wiki/Beequip_Storage "Beequip Storage") case to Dapper Bear's left, but it was moved to the spawn.
