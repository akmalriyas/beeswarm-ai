**Goo** is a purple-to-teal substance that gives the player bonus [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") when spread across [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). It is required for all of [Onett’s](/wiki/Onett "Onett") and [Spirit Bear's](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") and was required for all of [Gummy Bear's](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") quests, as well as for some [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee") and [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee") quests and some of [Science Bear's](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") quests. Using [gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") is the easiest and most common way to manually spread goo until one gets the [Gummy Boots](/wiki/Gummy_Boots "Gummy Boots"). To collect goo, collect pollen as usual (with [tools](/wiki/Tools "Tools"), [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), and/or their [ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens")) from flowers that are covered in goo. 

The following formula shows how much bonus honey a player gets from goo. Let _p_ equal the amount of pollen collected from a puddle of goo, and _f_ the amount of flowers inside the puddle. 

p f 200 {\displaystyle p\sqrt{\frac{f}{200}}} ![{\\displaystyle p{\\sqrt {\\frac {f}{200}}}}](https://services.fandom.com/mathoid-facade/v1/media/math/render/svg/fed231a2357db0c7476f35fade6e8d34a5154f48)

This means that the larger the goo puddle, the more bonus honey the player gets. For example, collecting 500,000 pollen from a goo puddle of 80 flowers would grant roughly 316,228 bonus honey. 

Unlike ability tokens, goo can be freely shared with other players. Collecting goo depletes the underlying flowers, but does not actually "use up" the goo itself, unless you collect goo with the [Gummyballer](/wiki/Gummyballer "Gummyballer"). Goo puddles disappear on their own over a short period of time. 

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGoo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Gummy Bear's [Gummy Cannon](/wiki/Gummy_Cannon "Gummy Cannon") is capable of shooting [gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrop "Gumdrop") at all [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") except the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field"), [Hub Field](/wiki/Hub_Field "Hub Field"), and [Brick Fields](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge"). When the gumdrops land, they turn into goo. Gummy Bear continually cycles through all 13 fields excluding the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field") in a random order. When he targeted a field, the server announces it in the bottom right corner as well as in the chat window. 
    * The Gummy Cannon doesn’t always shoot in the center of fields. Sometimes, the gumdrops landed outside of the field, making them vanish and sometimes going to the surrounding fields.
  * If a gumdrop falls outside of a field and does not land on a flower, it disappears.
  * [Gummy Bee's](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") [Glob](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Glob "Ability Tokens") and [Gumdrop Barrage](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Gumdrop_Barrage "Ability Tokens") abilities both spread goo in fields.
  * Activating [Gummy Mask’s](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask") [Gummy Morph passive ability](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Morph "Passive Abilities") covers the entire field the wearer is in with goo.
  * Whether goo is pink or teal is decided based on the flower it falls on. For example, the top-left flower (Closest to Mushroom field) in Dandelion Field will always have pink goo, whereas the bottom-left flower in Dandelion will always have teal goo. This is only visual and does not affect earned honey.
