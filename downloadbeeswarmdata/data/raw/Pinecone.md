A **Pinecone** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 9 [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas") [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of two. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [Bumble](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee"), [Stubborn](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee"), [Bucko](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee"), [Frosty](/wiki/Frosty_Bee "Frosty Bee"), [Carpenter](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee"), or [Bear](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee") [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees").
