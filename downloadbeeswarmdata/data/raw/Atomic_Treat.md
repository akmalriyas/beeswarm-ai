# Atomic Treat

Atomic Treat

[![Atomic Treat](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Atomic_Treat.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041728)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Atomic_Treat.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041728)

_"Always causes a random Mutation in a bee! Mutations grant bonus stats."_

[![An atomic treat token.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fc/Atomic_Treat_Token.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/120?cb=20201228193545)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fc/Atomic_Treat_Token.png/revision/latest?cb=20201228193545) [](/wiki/File:Atomic_Treat_Token.png)

An atomic treat token.

An **Atomic Treat** , when fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), raises its [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") by 1000 and gives the bee a random [mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation"). The mutations are [Attack](/wiki/Bee_Attack "Bee Attack"), [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), [Gather Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Gather_Total "System Page"), [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), and [Bee Ability Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Ability_Rate "System Page"). These are not the favorite treat of any bee.

  * As a very rare drop from defeating the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab").

  * As a rare drop from [Debug Sprouts](/wiki/Sprout#Debug_Sprout "Sprout").
  * As a very rare drop from a [Mythic Meteor](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower").
