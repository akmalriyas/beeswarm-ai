![Hivesticker eviction](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e3/Hivesticker_eviction.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20240120131955) | **This piece of content goes bye bye.** The following content has been removed from the game. The **Daily Top Mixed Brick Field Collectors** was, at the time, one of the 66 [leaderboards](/wiki/Leaderboards "Leaderboards") in the game. Unlike other leaderboards, it did not appear in the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page"), nor anywhere else in-game. This leaderboard measured how much [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") players had collected in the [Mixed Brick Field](/wiki/Mixed_Brick_Field "Mixed Brick Field") that day. It reset every day at 12:00 AM CST.

If the player had reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they would be awarded with the ![Ticket](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Ticket.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404015820)25 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").

  * This, along with the other Brick Field collector leaderboards, and the [Daily Top Ant Field Collectors](/wiki/Daily_Top_Ant_Field_Collectors "Daily Top Ant Field Collectors") were the only leaderboards not to be displayed anywhere in the game.
  * This was tied to be the fourth leaderboard to be removed, tied with the aforementioned leaderboards, and the rest being the [Most Commando Captures](/wiki/Most_Commando_Captures "Most Commando Captures"), [Highest Snowbear Level](/wiki/Highest_Snowbear_Level "Highest Snowbear Level") and [Highest Robo Party Cake Rank](/wiki/Highest_Robo_Party_Cake_Rank "Highest Robo Party Cake Rank") leaderboard.
