**Icicles** are a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 10 [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas") [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). They have an equip limit of two. They have a blue color requirement. They can only be equipped to bees that know the [Blue Bomb](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens") ability.
