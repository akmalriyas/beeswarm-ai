# Snowstorm

[![An active snow storm.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cf/Snowstorm.png/revision/latest?cb=20201228210218 "An active Snow Storm.")An active Snow Storm.

A **Snow Storm** is a server-wide event that can be summoned by using the [Snow Machine](/wiki/Snow_Machine "Snow Machine") or randomly in the game. It is similar to a [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm"), but spawns ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") instead. A Snow Storm lasts for 20 seconds. 

When a player uses the Snow Machine to summon a Snow Storm, a server-wide announcement states:  ❄️ {Username} has activated the Snow Machine! ❄️

![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") will start to fall rapidly from the sky on every field. There is no audio at the beginning of a Snow Storm, unlike a Honeystorm or [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower"). 

When a Snow Storm is randomly summoned, the message states:  ❄️ A Snow Storm has begun... ❄️

Occasionally, [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") would spawn a Snow Storm, usually under an alias. This is similar to how he globally summons Mythic Meteor Showers, [nighttime](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle#Night "Day/Night Cycle"), special [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout"), and [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs"). 

  * Snowcub summoning a Snow Storm.

  * Texas Bear summoning a Snow Storm.

  * Festive Bee summoning a Snow Storm in an attempt to re-freeze the Bean Bug Brigade.

  * Gifted Gingerbread Bear summoning a Snow Storm during Beesmas 2020.

  * A Gifted Festive Nymph (Lvl 9) (Aspect of Snow Mutation) (Winter Cap Nymph-quip with 5 Caustic Waxes) (HP Saves) summoning a Snow Storm during Beesmas 2021.

  * Gloomy Cub's Cold Heart summoning a Snow Storm.

  * Gloomy Cub's Immobilizing Pessism summoning a Snow Storm.

  * The 1,000,000th Snowflake spent on the Catalog summoning a Snow Storm.

  * The realization that Beesmas is actually over summoning a Snow Storm.

  * Gifted Gatito Bee summoning a Snow Storm.

  * Festive Nymph summoning a Snow Storm.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnow_Storm%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Like Honeystorms and Mythic Meteor Showers, Snow Storms can light up the [Werewolf's Cave](/wiki/Werewolf%27s_Cave "Werewolf's Cave") enough to see the [Cave Monsters](/wiki/Cave_Monster "Cave Monster") and the ![Gold Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gold Egg](/wiki/Egg#Gold_Egg "Egg") at the end of the cave. 
    * Unlike Honeystorms, Snow Storms don't spawn snowflake tokens in the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge").
  * During Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025, giving [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") a ![Present](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Present](/wiki/Present "Present") would trigger a Snow Storm after her dialogue finishes and the [ornament](/wiki/Ornaments "Ornaments") was given. 
    * Finishing Spirit Bear’s Beesmas quest would also summon a Snow Storm.
  * The ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") keep falling for a few seconds after a Snow Storm ends because it takes time for the ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") to reach the ground.
  * If a Snow Storm is summoned during [nighttime](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle#Night "Day/Night Cycle"), the white fog will stay even after the Snow Storm has ended until nighttime ends.
