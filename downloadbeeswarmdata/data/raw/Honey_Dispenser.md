## Honey Dispenser

[ ![Honey Dispenser](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/91/Honey_Dispenser.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20201005134940) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/91/Honey_Dispenser.png/revision/latest?cb=20201005134940 "Honey Dispenser") The Honey Dispenser

## Information

### Usage

Giving ![Honey](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c6/Honey.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230410071605)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in proportion to the number of [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") the player has.

### Requirement(s)

Membership in the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Club "Bee Swarm Simulator Club") group

### Cooldown

1 hour

### Location

In front of the [Lava Obby](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses "Obstacle Courses"), near the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate")

  
The **Honey Dispenser** is a [dispenser](/wiki/Dispenser "Dispenser") that grants ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") and x5 [haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens") every time it's used. It has a 1 hour cooldown and requires the player to be in the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](https://www.roblox.com/groups/3982592/Bee-Swarm-Simulator-Club#!/about) in order to be used by the player. It initially gave 5x haste and ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)500 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), but after the [2018-04-27 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-04-27 "Updates"), the amount was changed to ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)100 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") per [bee](/wiki/Bee "Bee") in the player's [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") and x5 haste. Since then, the amount of ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") was changed again. 

It is one of the four club dispensers in the game. However, it is the first one to be accessed. 

The [Lava Obby](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses#Lava_Obby "Obstacle Courses") can be found behind it and leads to a ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token at the back of the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop") and also to the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") shop. 

If the player tries to use the Honey Dispenser without being in the group, they will get a prompt saying that they must join the Bee Swarm Group (or club). 

## Honey reward amount[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney_Dispenser%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The amount of honey given is based on the number of bees the player has in their hive. More specifically, let _cnt_ be the number of bees in the player's hive: 

  * The amount of honey the player receives is equal to  ⌊ c n t 1.7 + 0.5 ⌋ × 100 {\displaystyle \left\lfloor {cnt}^{1.7}+0.5\right\rfloor \times 100} ![{\\displaystyle \\left\\lfloor {cnt}^{1.7}+0.5\\right\\rfloor \\times 100}](https://services.fandom.com/mathoid-facade/v1/media/math/render/svg/beee22f11c951d46785600ccb7fd727c05515e25), or 500 if the result is less than 500.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHoney_Dispenser%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * During the [Gummy Invasion](/wiki/Category:Gummy_Invasion "Category:Gummy Invasion"), the dispenser granted an additional ![Gumdrops](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") per use.
  * This, the [Strawberry Dispenser](/wiki/Strawberry_Dispenser "Strawberry Dispenser"), the [Blueberry Dispenser](/wiki/Blueberry_Dispenser "Blueberry Dispenser"), the [Royal Jelly Dispenser](/wiki/Royal_Jelly_Dispenser "Royal Jelly Dispenser"), and the [Glue Dispenser](/wiki/Glue_Dispenser "Glue Dispenser") are the only dispensers that are required to be used for a [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests") ("Honey Bee's Honey Wreath?" from [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") in 2020 and 2021 required the player to use the Honey Dispenser, "BBM's Naughty List" from [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") in Beesmas 2022 requires the player to use the [Royal Jelly Dispenser](/wiki/Royal_Jelly_Dispenser "Royal Jelly Dispenser"), and "The Power of Information" from [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") requires the player to use the other three).
  * This is one of four dispensers requiring the player to be part of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club with the others being the [Treat Dispenser](/wiki/Treat_Dispenser "Treat Dispenser"), the [Strawberry Dispenser](/wiki/Strawberry_Dispenser "Strawberry Dispenser"), and the [Blueberry Dispenser](/wiki/Blueberry_Dispenser "Blueberry Dispenser").
  * When using this dispenser, there is no notification in the chat log of the ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") given.
