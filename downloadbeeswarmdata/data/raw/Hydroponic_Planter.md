The **Hydroponic Planter** is a reusable [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") added in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"). Alone, it grows in about 12 hours and stores around 25 billion pollen. When harvested, it grants bonus ![Blue Extract](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blue Extracts](/wiki/Blue_Extract "Blue Extract") and ![Oil](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Oils](/wiki/Oil "Oil"). It is purchasable in the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ"). It requires the player to complete 150 [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee") quests for it to be unlocked.

When planted in a field, it grows up to 50% faster in fields with blue flowers. Blue bees are 33.3% more likely to sip [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") while red bees are 50% less likely to sip. When harvested, this planter grants 50% more blue pollen, but also 50% less red pollen. It also grants 40% more Comforting and Refreshing Nectar. 

Harvesting a fully-grown Hydroponic Planter has a 2/9 chance of spawning a [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom"). The spawned puffshroom is between levels 6-7, and has a: 

  * ~72.59% chance of being a Common Puffshroom,
  * ~21.78% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
  * ~5.44% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
  * ~0.18% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom,
  * ~0.01% chance of being a Mythic Puffshroom.

The others are picked randomly from the following, non-exhaustive list (note that the list does not include rewards based on the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") the planter is in): 

  * Bonus

  * Other

  * Special
