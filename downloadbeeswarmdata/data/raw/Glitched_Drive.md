# Glitched Drive

**Drives** are inventory items that were introduced in the [2022-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2022-12-26 "Updates"). They can be used to repair [Robo Bear](/wiki/Robo_Bear "Robo Bear"), purchase [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee"), upgrade Digital Bee, and grant boosts during a round of the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"). All Drives (except ![Broken Drive](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Broken Drives](/wiki/Broken_Drive "Broken Drive")) can be purchased from Robo Bear's Shop for ![Cog](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)50 [Cogs](/wiki/Cog "Cog") (![Cog](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)100 [Cogs](/wiki/Cog "Cog") for Glitched, and 5 of each other drive), and an increasing amount of ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") with each purchase.

## Broken Drive[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrives%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_Main article:[Broken Drive](/wiki/Broken_Drive "Broken Drive")_

[![Broken Drive](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f3/Broken_Drive.png/revision/latest?cb=20230507111545) [![Broken Drive](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f3/Broken_Drive.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/113?cb=20230507111545)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f3/Broken_Drive.png/revision/latest?cb=20230507111545) [](/wiki/File:Broken_Drive.png)

The [Broken Drive](/wiki/Broken_Drive "Broken Drive") is a unique drive added in the [2023-05-07 update](/wiki/Updates#2023-05-07 "Updates") that can be collected only once, from an area behind the [Robo Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Robo_Pass_Dispenser "Robo Pass Dispenser") and [Free Robo Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Free_Robo_Pass_Dispenser "Free Robo Pass Dispenser"). Once collected, it initiates Science Bear's drive questline. After completing Science Bear's quest, the Broken Drive will be replaced with a ![White Drive](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)White Drive.

  * Found behind the Robo Pass Dispensers in the [30 Bee Zone](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrives%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Excluding the Broken Drive, these are the only items that can permanently enhance a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees").
  * Excluding the Broken Drive, these are the only items that can only be used during a challenge.
  * The existence of a Yellow Drive is hinted through Robo Bear’s 2022 present dialogue. 
    * This may be a reference to the unused yellow flowers found in [Onett's](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") decal inventory.
