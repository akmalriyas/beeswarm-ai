The **Strawberry Field** is a [field](/wiki/Field "Field") located past the [Basic Bee Gate](/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate "Basic Bee Gate"), to the left of the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), and beside the [Vicious Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Vicious_Bee_Egg_Claim "Vicious Bee Egg Claim"). This field is protected by 2 [ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug"); one of which is level 2 and the other level 3. 

This field can spawn [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [Strawberry](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"), [Red Boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), [Red Extract](/wiki/Red_Extract "Red Extract"), [Bitterberry](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"), [Ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") and [Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") tokens while collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Strawberry Field are [Fire Bee](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee"), [Riley Bee](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee"), and [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee"). Bees that dislike the Strawberry Field are [Cool Bee](/wiki/Cool_Bee "Cool Bee"), [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee"), [Bucko Bee](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee"), and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"). 

This field grants Refreshing [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is placed here. 

It is a 22x26 field, containing 572 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are red and white, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStrawberry_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Red Field Booster](/wiki/Red_Field_Booster "Red Field Booster") may give a Strawberry [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +100% Strawberry Field pollen for 15 minutes.  [![The Strawberry Field boost icon.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/65/Strawberry_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080140) [![The Strawberry Field boost icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/65/Strawberry_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080140)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/65/Strawberry_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080140)

The Strawberry Field boost icon.

  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") in the Strawberry Field will add 100% Strawberry Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add 100% - 300% Strawberry Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out and also what type of dice is used.
  * Strawberry [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Strawberry Field conversion, and give +5% instant Strawberry Field conversion and +15% Strawberry Field pollen for each stack. Strawberry Field Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.
  * The field can be boosted by the [King Beetle Amulet](/wiki/King_Beetle_Amulet "King Beetle Amulet"). If it has a Strawberry Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% up to a max of +100% pollen.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStrawberry_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is the third smallest field in the game, outranked by the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field") and [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch").
  * There is a strawberry token behind the biggest strawberry in the top left of the field.
  * This is one of six fields that [fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies") can travel to during [nighttime](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle "Day/Night Cycle"), with the others being [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"), and Spider Field.
  * [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear") states that finding strawberry tokens are much more common in this field than in any other field.
  * According to [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear"), the Strawberry in the field were planted from seeds imported from Sweden. She also states that [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") in this field spawn the most strawberry tokens, 8 times more than normal sprouts.
  * If you run towards the strawberry on the corner fast enough, you may glitch inside of the strawberry. If this happens you will have to reset your character, rejoin the game, or use a ![Whirligig](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Whirligig](/wiki/Whirligig "Whirligig").
