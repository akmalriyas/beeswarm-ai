A **beequip** is a type of [inventory item](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory") that can be worn by [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"). When equipped to a bee, they alter its stats and can also give bonuses to the hive. Beequips can only be given to bees that are at or above the level of the beequip. Some beequips can only be given to certain bees or types of bees - for example, the ![Bubble Light](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Bubble Light](/wiki/Bubble_Light "Bubble Light") requires a bee with an energy [mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation"). 

## Beequip Case[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBeequip%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

[![The Beequip Case.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e9/Beequip_Case.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404022327) [![The Beequip Case.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e9/Beequip_Case.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20230404022327)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e9/Beequip_Case.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404022327) [](/wiki/File:Beequip_Case.png)

The **Beequip Case**.

When a beequip is obtained, they are initially stored in a **Beequip Case**. Beequips in a case can be given to bees by dragging the beequip to a hive slot, or moved between the case, storage, and inbox. 

The Beequip Case is obtained when the player first talks to [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear") at his [shop](/wiki/Dapper_Bear%27s_Shop "Dapper Bear's Shop"). The player starts off with five slots but can obtain additional slots from [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear")'s quests and [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear")'s quests during Beesmas 2020, with the total amount of slots being 15 beequips. 

Opening the Beequip Case allows the player to see all equipped Beequips on everyone's hives, albeit they can only interact with their own hive. Holding or clicking on an equipped Beequip in the case will cause said Beequip that is currently on a hive slot to enlarge and sway, highlighting it's location. Directly reviewing a Beequip from the hive slot will not cause this effect.

If all the slots of the beequip case are filled, or if the player have not gotten a Beequip Case, Beequips are moved to the Beequip Storage, located near the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"), or the [Public Sticker Board](/wiki/Public_Sticker_Board "Public Sticker Board") in the Hive Hub. Beequips in the storage _cannot_ be given to bees. 

By default, the storage has 10 slots. However, the player can purchase 90 additional slots, for a max total of 100, with a total of 123,500 tickets. Additional slots could have been obtained by completing some of Bee Bear's quests during Beesmas 2020. 

If both the storage and case are full, the beequips are stored in the Beequip Inbox. The inbox can store the player's 25 most recent beequips, but they have a 48-hour time limit before being discarded automatically. If the player doesn't want a certain beequip in the inbox to be deleted, they can empty a slot in the case, and then they can keep the beequip. 

Permanent beequips can be stored in the 'Permanents' section of the Beequip Storage without taking up any space. However, those special beequips will still require a case slot in order to be equipped to a bee. Currently, there are no permanent beequips, since the ones that used to be (the [Reindeer Antlers](/wiki/Reindeer_Antlers "Reindeer Antlers") and [Festive Wreath](/wiki/Festive_Wreath "Festive Wreath")) were made normal beequips, with Onett saying that he might get rid of the permanent beequip feature.

## Generation[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBeequip%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_This section gives a short explanation of how a Beequip generates its stats. For a more in-depth explanation, visit[the linked subarticle](/wiki/Beequip/Generation "Beequip/Generation"). (incomplete)_

### Potential[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBeequip%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Beequips have a potential that can range from 0-5 stars. The potential of a beequip **cannot** change. 

When displayed, the potential will round to the nearest star (nearest half-circle in Dapper Bear’s Shop). 

Generally, the higher the potential a beequip has: 

  * the better the probability of the beequip having better base stats,
  * the easier it is to get rarer stats when upgrading the beequip with [waxes](/wiki/Waxes "Waxes"),
  * and the better the probability of a wax upgrade giving more stats.



### Base Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBeequip%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

When a beequip is first generated, or when a ![Swirled Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Swirled Wax](/wiki/Swirled_Wax "Swirled Wax") is applied to it, it is given a new set of base stats. 

Each possible stat on the beequip has a chance of being in this new set of base stats, with either an exact value, or a value between 2 pre-determined limits that is biased to values near a value that may increase with the beequip's potential. 

Usually, the better the beequip's potential is, the more likely the beequip is to have the base stat, and the base stat is more likely to have a higher value. However, due to some quirks with the generation, this may not be the case, and the probability of the beequip having the base stat is randomized. 

Interally, this process is done using a complex function. For more information on how this function works, visit [the linked module page](/wiki/Module:RQValue "Module:RQValue"). For more information on how this function is used, visit [the linked subsection.](/wiki/Beequip/Generation#Base "Beequip/Generation")

_Note that the base stats are not dependent on![Caustic Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[ Caustic Waxes](/wiki/Caustic_Wax "Caustic Wax"), so it is possible to have an otherwise Caustic-only stat on a beequip as its base stats._

### Waxes[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBeequip%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_Main article:[Waxes](/wiki/Waxes "Waxes")_

When a wax is applied successfully, it gives the beequip a number of **wax points**. Each wax point can upgrade exactly 1 of the beequip's stat. Swirled Waxes do not give any wax points, but it rerolls every wax point that has been applied to the beequip. 

The amount of wax points a wax can give is given by the below table: 

Wax  | Wax points   
---|---  
![Soft Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Soft Wax](/wiki/Soft_Wax "Soft Wax") | 1   
![Hard Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Hard Wax](/wiki/Hard_Wax "Hard Wax") | 2   
![Caustic Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Caustic Wax](/wiki/Caustic_Wax "Caustic Wax") / ![Debug Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Debug Wax](/wiki/Debug_Wax "Debug Wax") | 4   
![Swirled Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Swirled Wax](/wiki/Swirled_Wax "Swirled Wax") | 0   
  
Every upgradable stat on a beequip has a weight value. The probability of a wax point upgrading a stat is equal to its weight value divided by the sum of all upgradable stats' weight values. This weight value may increase with the beequip's potential. 

When a wax point upgrades a stat, it will upgrade the stat by either an exact value, or a value between 2 pre-determined ranges, biased to values near a value that increases with the beequip's potential. 

A stat may only be upgraded a certain number of times. When a stat reaches its maximum number of upgrades, it is removed from the pool, improving the probabilities of upgrading all other stats. 

Usually, the better the beequip's potential is, the more likely the wax point is to choose rarer stats, and the wax point is more likely to upgrade the chosen stat by a higher amount. However, due to some quirks with the generation, this may not be the case, and the probability of a wax point picking a stat is randomized. 

Interally, this process is done using a complex function. For more information on how this function works, visit [the linked module page](/wiki/Module:RQValue "Module:RQValue"). For more information on how this function is used, visit [the linked subsection.](/wiki/Beequip/Generation#Upgrade "Beequip/Generation")
