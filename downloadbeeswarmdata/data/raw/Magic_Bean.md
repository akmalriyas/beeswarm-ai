COOLDOWN

> _Were you looking for[Sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") or [Festive Bean](/wiki/Festive_Bean "Festive Bean")?_

A **Magic Bean** is an [inventory item](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory") that was added in the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"). It can be used to summon a random sprout in the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") the player is standing in. When one is planted, a server-wide message will appear saying:  🌱{Username} has planted a (Sprout Rarity) Sprout...🌱

## Ways to Obtain[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMagic_Bean%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Purchasing for ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)20 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") each in the [Magic Bean Shop](/wiki/Magic_Bean_Shop "Magic Bean Shop") on the right side of [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field").
  * Purchasing for 30 Robux for 1 Magic bean or 200 Robux for 10 Magic beans in the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop").
  * As a reward from the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge").
  * As a reward from donating to the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine").
  * As a reward from [Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match") games.

    * Forward Facing Aphid rewards 1 magic bean.

    * Flying Magenta Critter rewards 1 magic bean.

    * Purple Pointed Critter rewards 1 magic bean.
    * Orange Leg Critter rewards 1 magic bean.
    * Sprout rewards 1 magic bean.
    * Critter in a Stocking rewards 1 magic bean.
    * Festive Pea rewards 1 magic bean.
  * Redeeming certain valid [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). 
    * ClubBean (Gives 1 magic bean and other stuff, requires being a member of the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](https://www.roblox.com/My/Groups.aspx?gid=3982592))



### Drops[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMagic_Bean%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Unknown chance of dropping when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields").
  * As an unknown chance from [Leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves").
  * As a drop from [all aphids](/wiki/Aphid "Aphid") and the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab").
  * As a rare drop from a [rhino beetle](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"), [ladybug](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug"), [spider](/wiki/Spider "Spider"), [Stick Nymphs](/wiki/Stick_Nymph "Stick Nymph"), [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug"), [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle"), [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee"), [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail"), and the [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick").
  * As a rare drop from [Festive Gift](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens") and [Festive Mark](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Mark "Ability Tokens").
  * As a rare gift from a [cub buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy").
  * A chance of dropping from any [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
  * As a drop from certain [planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter"), rarity depending on which one and where it was planted.
  * As a drop from [Stockings](/wiki/Stockings "Stockings").
