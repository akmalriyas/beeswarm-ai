## Beesmas Feast

## Information

### Usage

Obtaining [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond"), ![Bitterberry](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/af/Bitterberry.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404041641)[Bitterberries](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"), and various [treats](/wiki/Treats "Treats")

### Requirement(s)

Completion of Polar Bear's Beesmas quest

### Cooldown

90 minutes

### Location

Behind [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear")

The **Beesmas Feast** is a [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas")-exclusive machine which can be unlocked after completing [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear")'s Beesmas [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests"). 

It can be used every 90 minutes. When used, it grants the player [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") for all of their [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") and a variety of [treats](/wiki/Treats "Treats"). It is positioned behind Polar Bear. The amount of bond and rewards are affected by the amount of [Polar Power](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_NPCs "Buffs & Debuffs") the player currently has. 

Upon digging into the feast, two notifications appear:  
🍴 Serving up some Beesmas Dinner 🍴  
🍴 More Polar Power = Larger Servings! 🍴

If a player walks onto the platform without completing his quest, it displays this message, "An empty plate... where’s the Beesmas Feast, Polar Bear?"

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBeesmas_Feast%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * A variety of foods can be seen on the Beesmas feast. These include: 
    * Bitterberries.
    * Treats.
    * A floret of broccoli.
    * A carrot.
    * A stew that seems to contain chives, onions, corn, peas, and meat.


  * Polar Bear's feast design is reminiscent to Big Ol' Turkey Legs, a running gag within the [ThnxCya](https://www.youtube.com/user/ThnxCya) community. 
    * Polar Bear's dialogue after you complete his Beesmas 2020 quest directly referenced this, with the phrase "_A big ol' turkey and treat..._ ".
  * [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") stated on Discord that the Beesmas Feast was made using the Hostage Chicks from the [Egg Hunt 2020](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2020 "Egg Hunt 2020") event.[1]
  * The eating sound is a cut out section of the song "_[Nom Nom Nom Nom Nom Nom Nom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SMWi7CLoZ2Q)_ ", made by Parry Grip.
  * The Beesmas Feast used to drop all the 4 treats but was changed in Beesmas 2022.
  * It was introduced in [Beesmas 2020](/wiki/Updates#12.25.2020 "Updates"), and has returned in every Beesmas since.
