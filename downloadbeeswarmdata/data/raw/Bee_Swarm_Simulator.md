**[Bee Swarm Simulator](https://www.roblox.com/games/1537690962/)** is an online multiplayer game made in **[Roblox](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Roblox "w:c:roblox:Roblox")** by **[Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)")**. The purpose of the game is to hatch [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to make a swarm, collect [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"), and make it into [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"). The game was in development for about three months[1][2] until it was created on March 21, 2018 and was released to the public on March 23, 2018. 

_**Please note that this page does not need much expansion or information as this wiki is dedicated to the game. Please check out the other pages for more info.**_

## Gameplay

The player starts off with the [Tutorial](/wiki/Tutorial "Tutorial"), an optional 36-page slideshow which can be accessed again at any time from the “?” button on the top right corner of the screen. After that, they can claim any of the 6 unclaimed [hives](/wiki/Hive "Hive") by pressing 'E' or clicking on the large blue button on the top of the screen when you are near your hive. New players will automatically receive a ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Basic Egg](/wiki/Egg#Basic_Egg "Egg"), a [Pouch](/wiki/Pouch "Pouch"), and a [Scooper](/wiki/Scooper "Scooper"). The player can use their tool to collect pollen from any [Fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") available. Their bag will store the pollen collected, and their bee(s) can convert the pollen into honey while the player is at their hive. The honey can be used to purchase new [Tools](/wiki/Category:Tools "Category:Tools"), [Accessories](/wiki/Category:Accessories "Category:Accessories"), [Eggs](/wiki/Egg "Egg"), and various other [Items](/wiki/Items "Items") to progress further into the game. 

The player can also receive [Quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") from [NPCs](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC"), which mainly focus on collecting pollen and defeating [Mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs"), but can also have other, more advanced objectives, like collecting [Ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") and [Goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo"). 

## Private Servers

Private servers cost ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)150 Robux per month. Owners of private servers have access to the following commands: 

  * "/kick PlayerName" - Kicks the given player. PlayerName must be their true Roblox username, the one that displays over their hive (not their Display Name).
  * "/ban PlayerName" - Kicks the named player and automatically kicks them if they attempt to rejoin the server. This ban will remain until the server is closed or "/unban" is used.
  * "/unban PlayerName" - Removes the named player from the ban list.



If a private server becomes inactive by not paying for it, banned players will be unbanned when the server becomes active again.

## Game Statistics

Bee Swarm Simulator currently has 4B+ visits, 6.35M+ favorites, 2.8M+ likes, 135K dislikes and a 95% rating. 

Bee Swarm Simulator is the nineteenth game on Roblox to reach 1 billion visits. 

Bee Swarm Simulator is the third most visited simulator on Roblox.
