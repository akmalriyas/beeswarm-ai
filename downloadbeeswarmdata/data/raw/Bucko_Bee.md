**Bucko Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Epic bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"). 

Bucko Bee's favorite [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") is ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"). 

Bucko Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"), and the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). It dislikes the [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field"), and [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBucko_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * As per the description, this [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") is the rival and counterpart of [Riley Bee](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee"). 
    * Both bees are the leaders of their respective colors.
  * Bucko Bee and [Bumble Bee](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee") have the same skin colors.
  * There is a giant model of this bee on top of the entrance of the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ").
  * A [Quest Bee](/wiki/Category:Quest_Bees "Category:Quest Bees") represents the gifted version of it in the top floor of the Blue HQ.
  * Bucko Bee is the third strongest blue bee, only behind Vicious Bee and Cobalt Bee.
  * The [Bucko Guard](/wiki/Bucko_Guard "Bucko Guard") is based on Bucko Bee.
  * Bucko Bee, [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"), and Bumble Bee are the only Blue bees to like at least three fields. 
    * Bucko Bee is also the only Blue bee to dislike two fields.
