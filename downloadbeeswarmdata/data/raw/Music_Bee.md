**Music Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Legendary bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"). 

Music Bee's favorite treat is ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"). 

Music Bee likes the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field") and the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"). It dislikes the [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMusic_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 16 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 240 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +15% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +6 [Gather Amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats"), +160 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page").
  * 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +25% [Pollen From Bee Gathering](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Gather_Pollen "System Page").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMusic_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Melody]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Melody "Ability Tokens")** Grants +100% [Critical Power](/wiki/System_Page#Critical_Power "System Page") for 30 seconds. Critical Power increases the pollen and damage of critical hits.
  * **[[Focus]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Focus "Ability Tokens")** Grants +3% [Critical Chance](/wiki/System_Page#Critical_Chance "System Page") for 20 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times. Critical hits increase pollen and damage by 100%.
  * **[[Token Link]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Token_Link "Ability Tokens")** Collects all other [Ability Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), granting ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)25 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") (+10 per Level) per token collected.

That's equivalent to 4 ![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMusic_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This bee and [Carpenter Bee](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee") are the only Legendary bees added in an [update](/wiki/Updates "Updates").
  * It has a tail that symbolizes a quaver, also known as an eighth note. 
    * In musical notation terms, its face is a slur between an eighth note and a dotted quarter note. 
      * These are also known as a quaver and a dotted crotchet.
  * Music Bee is the only Legendary bee with 3 abilities.
  * This is the only non-[blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") bee to like blueberries.
  * Music Bee and [Shocked Bee](/wiki/Shocked_Bee "Shocked Bee") have the same skin pattern.
  * This is the only bee that generates a melody token without Beequips.
  * A [Special Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Specific_Bee_Jelly "Royal Jelly") that changes a bee into a Music Bee could be obtained by opening the [Funky Present](/wiki/Ornament_Presents "Ornament Presents") during the [Beesmas 2019 event](/wiki/Updates#2019-12-23 "Updates"). However, as the event has ended, the jelly was unobtainable until the Beesmas 2020 event where it could be purchased in the Beesmas Tree Bundle along with ![Festive Bean](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)5 [Festive Beans](/wiki/Festive_Bean "Festive Bean"), and a ![Beesmas Tree Hat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Beesmas Tree Hat](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree_Hat "Beesmas Tree Hat") [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). During Beesmas Summer 2024, this jelly could be obtained from the Percussive Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog. 
    * The Music Bee Egg could be obtained from the Musical Mega-Bundle during Beesmas Winter 2024.
