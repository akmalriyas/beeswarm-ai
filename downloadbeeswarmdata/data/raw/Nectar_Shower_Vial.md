# Nectar Shower Vial

Nectar Shower Vial

[![Nectar Shower Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a2/Nectar_Shower_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20241225154532)

_"Grants 4 hours of all Nectar types to every player in the server!"_

COOLDOWN

5 Seconds

CAP

10

The **Nectar Shower Vial** is a consumable [nectar vial](/wiki/Nectar_Vial "Nectar Vial") [item](/wiki/Items "Items") that was added in the [2024-12-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2024-12-25 "Updates"). Once used, it grants 4 hours of [all nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") [buffs](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs "Buffs & Debuffs") to every player in the server. 

When one is used, a message pops up saying  (Username) has popped open a Nectar Shower Vial! for every player in the server.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNectar_Shower_Vial%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This item and the ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Nectar Tester](/wiki/Nectar_Tester "Nectar Tester") are the only items that grant more than one type of nectar buff.

    * This is the only [Nectar Vial](/wiki/Nectar_Vial "Nectar Vial") that grants other players nectar.
