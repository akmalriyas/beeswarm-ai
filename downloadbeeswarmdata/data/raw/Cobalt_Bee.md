**Cobalt Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event"). It can be obtained by purchasing a ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Cobalt Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Cobalt_Bee_Egg "Egg") in the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") for ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)250 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"). 

Its abilities are enhanced when [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee") is also in the hive. 

Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, and the only way to make it gifted is by feeding it a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

Cobalt Bee likes the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field") and the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). It dislikes the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch").

  * Makes 140 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 3 seconds.

### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCobalt_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Blue Pulse]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Pulse "Ability Tokens")** Fires a pulse that hops between your blue bees, collecting pollen around them (+10% per Level). Power increases with each hop. If you own a Crimson Bee, fires a [Red Pulse](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Pulse "Ability Tokens") as well. 
    * If Gifted, the pulse also causes bees to instantly convert pollen equal to 25% of the bee's Convert Amount (+25% per Field Boost stack).
  * **[[Blue Bomb Sync]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb_Sync "Ability Tokens")** Grants 10% Blue Pollen and Instant Blue Conversion allows [Blue Bombs](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens") to collect from white [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") for 30 sec. If red bomb sync is active, this applies to red flowers as well.

Cobalt Bee has a base pollen collection of **10![Blue Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. That's equivalent to 2.5 ![Blue Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCobalt_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The only difference between Cobalt Bee and Crimson Bee's [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") preferences is that the Cobalt Bee likes the Pine Tree Forest, whereas Crimson Bee prefers the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field").
  * Cobalt Bee and Crimson Bee have the same [stats](/wiki/Stats "Stats").
  * Cobalt Bee and Crimson Bee are the first colored Event bees, followed by [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") and [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee").
  * Cobalt Bee and Crimson Bee are the only [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that can activate another [ability](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") apart from their own.
  * When Crimson and Cobalt Bee are both in the same hive, the player will get the following bonuses from these ability tokens: 
    * Blue pulse additionally fires a red pulse to collect from red bees.
    * If both Red Bomb Sync and Blue Bomb Sync are activated simultaneously, blue bombs can collect from red and white flowers.
  * This is one of the Event bees who have different color faces on the hive rather than the bee itself, the others being Crimson Bee, Festive Bee, and Vicious Bee.
  * This was the first Blue Event bee, with Vicious Bee being the second.
  * Cobalt Bee and Crimson Bee are currently the only event bees to cost 250 tickets. 
    * The [first edition](/wiki/First_Edition_Bee "First Edition Bee") [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee") and [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee") could once be purchased with 250 tickets as well.
  * This bee, Crimson Bee, [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee"), [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee"), [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"), [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee"), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), and [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee") are the only bees that leave a trail.
  * The [Cobalt Guard](/wiki/Cobalt_Guard "Cobalt Guard"), which can be crafted in the [Badge Bearer's Guild](/wiki/Badge_Bearer%27s_Guild "Badge Bearer's Guild"), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Cobalt Bee. 
    * The Cobalt Guard has a [Passive Ability](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Haste_Pulser "Passive Abilities") that looks similar to Cobalt Bee's blue pulse ability.
  * This bee, Crimson Bee, Puppy Bee, and Windy Bee are the only Event bees that don't have an 'enhanced' ability if gifted.
  * Cobalt Bee's original face decal had a mouth that strongly resembled that of the [Cool Bee](/wiki/Cool_Bee "Cool Bee"). The decal was scrapped before the release of the bee.
