The **Windy Bee Gate** , or the **35 Bee Gate** , is a gate that requires 35 [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to unlock. It is located on the right of the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") and [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon"). It gets its name from the large [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") figure on top of it. 

Inside this Gate is the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field"), the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch"), [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear"), the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine"), the Extreme [Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match"), and two [Shops](/wiki/Shops "Shops"): the [Petal Shop](/wiki/Petal_Shop "Petal Shop") and [Coconut Cave](/wiki/Coconut_Cave "Coconut Cave"). The [Petal Shop](/wiki/Petal_Shop "Petal Shop") sells the [Petal Wand](/wiki/Petal_Wand "Petal Wand"), [Petal Belt](/wiki/Petal_Belt "Petal Belt") and the [Petal Planter](/wiki/Petal_Planter "Petal Planter"). while the Coconut Cave sells the [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister "Coconut Canister"), [Coconut Clogs](/wiki/Coconut_Clogs "Coconut Clogs") and [Coconut Belt](/wiki/Coconut_Belt "Coconut Belt").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWindy_Bee_Gate%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This gate is the first and currently the only gate in the game that has an [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") representing it.
  * This gate, along with the [Bear Gate](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate"), are currently the only gates that require purchasing [hive slots](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot") to pass. 
    * The gate also plays the same music as the Bear Gate.
  * This gate gives access to the blocked off area in the [Blue Maze](/wiki/Mazes#Blue_Maze "Mazes") through the [Petal Shop](/wiki/Petal_Shop "Petal Shop").
  * This gate is the only place where sprouts can't appear naturally or be planted with the [Special Sprout Summoner](/wiki/Special_Sprout_Summoner "Special Sprout Summoner").
  * The sprouts planted by a player require more pollen to grow than in other locations for better loot and guaranteed more drops.
