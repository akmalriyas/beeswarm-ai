# Neonberry

Neonberry

[![Neonberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bb/Neonberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041653)

_"Increases bond with a bee by 500 and makes it Radioactive for a while."_

A **Neonberry** is a type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that, when fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), raises its [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") by 500 and makes it [radioactive](/wiki/Radiation "Radiation") for 11 minutes. This is the only treat to have guaranteed chance of making the bee radioactive. These are not the favorite treat of any bee.

### Drops[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNeonberry%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Has a low chance of dropping from [rhino beetles](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"), [ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug"), [mantises](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis"), [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle"), [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail"), [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick"), and [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab").
  * Has a low chance of appearing when harvesting [Sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles").
  * Farming in the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), and the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest") has a rare chance to drop neonberries. 
    * Harvesting [Leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves") in these fields may also yield neonberries.
  * As a rare drop from a [Festive Mark](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Mark "Ability Tokens").
  * As a gift from a [cub buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy").
  * As an uncommon drop from all [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout"). 
    * Debug sprouts are guaranteed to drop neonberries.
  * As a rare drop from [Mythic Meteors](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower").
  * As an uncommon drop from [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom").
  * As a drop from [planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter"). 
    * The [Pesticide Planter](/wiki/Pesticide_Planter "Pesticide Planter") drops extra neonberries in the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"), [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), and the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field").
    * Planting the [Pesticide Planter](/wiki/Pesticide_Planter "Pesticide Planter") in the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"), then the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), then the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field") results in at least 25 neonberries (1 week cooldown).
