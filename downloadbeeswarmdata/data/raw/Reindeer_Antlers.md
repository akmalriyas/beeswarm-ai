## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FReindeer_Antlers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The ![Reindeer Antlers](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Reindeer Antlers** and the ![Festive Wreath](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Wreath](/wiki/Festive_Wreath "Festive Wreath") share many similarities: 
    * The two are the only Beequips to have models visible on the bee that they are equipped to.
    * Before the [2024-01-12 update](/wiki/Updates#2024-01-12 "Updates")'s removal of permanent Beequips, the two were the only permanent Beequips in the game. 
      * This is the first permanent Beequip added to the game, followed by the Festive Wreath.
      * These were the only Beequips to have a set potential, with this Beequip always having max potential and the ![Festive Wreath](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Wreath](/wiki/Festive_Wreath "Festive Wreath") always having a potential of 3 stars (6 points).
      * They were the only Beequips that cannot be discarded.
      * ![Caustic Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Caustic Waxes](/wiki/Caustic_Wax "Caustic Wax") were unable to be used on these two Beequips. However, this is no longer the case.
  * A model of a Puppy Bee with ![Reindeer Antlers](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Reindeer Antlers** can be found outside the map, next to a model of a [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") with the Festive Wreath.
  * This Beequip is likely a reference to Dr. Seuss’ children's book “How the Grinch Stole Christmas”, where the Grinch dresses his dog as a reindeer.
  * This is the one of three Beequips to give a unique ability. 
    * The other two are the ![Snowglobe](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowglobe](/wiki/Snowglobe "Snowglobe") and ![Festive Wreath](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Festive Wreath](/wiki/Festive_Wreath "Festive Wreath").
