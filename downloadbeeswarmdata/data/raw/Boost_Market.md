## Boost Market

[ ![Boostmarket](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/28/Boostmarket.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20200406183332) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/28/Boostmarket.png/revision/latest?cb=20200406183332 "Boostmarket")

### Location

Past the [Bear Gate](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate") on the right.

[![The Boost Market page interface.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Boost_Market.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200407142734)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Boost_Market.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20200407142734) [](/wiki/File:Boost_Market.jpg)

The Boost Market page interface.

The **Boost Market** was a [shop](/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops") that could have been found in the [30 Bee Zone](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate"). Since the [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas") 2021 [update](/wiki/Updates "Updates"), it has been replaced by the [Nectar Pot](/wiki/Nectar_Pot "Nectar Pot"). 

Every 30 minutes, the shop allowed the player to purchase a market boost at a certain price. The price depended on the number of people who have purchased that specific boost, much like in a real [stock market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_Market "wikipedia:Stock Market"). It cost both ![Ticket](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Ticket.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404015820)[Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") and ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") to purchase a boost. These boosts lasted for 30 minutes. Market boosts could also have been obtained from certain [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), like RedMarket, or the ones used in the [Ready Player Two](/wiki/Ready_Player_Two "Ready Player Two") event.
