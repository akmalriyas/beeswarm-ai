The Sticker Quest Giver and the Sticker-Seeker leaderboard.

The **Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine** is a [quest giver](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers") in the [Hive Hub](/wiki/Hive_Hub "Hive Hub"). To interact with it, the player must own and have the [Sticker-Seeker](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker "Sticker-Seeker") equipped. The player must also have a total of 15 bees hatched to interact with the machine. 

Similar to [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear"), [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear"), [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)"), [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee"), and [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee"), it can give infinite quests, with extra rewards at certain milestones, up to the 1000th quest. 

## Ranks[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

A player's rank is the amount of Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's quests they have completed. Ranks are also the way the [Top Sticker-Seekers](/wiki/Top_Sticker-Seekers "Top Sticker-Seekers") leaderboard is measured, and increases every time a player completes a quest. The higher the rank, the better the rewards. For example, a player is more likely to get Sticker Planters when they have achieved further ranks than players with a Rank of 5. Quest requirement amounts also scale to the ranks. 

## Quests[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Obtaining and turning in [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") from this quest giver require the player to have the Sticker-Seeker equipped. 

The only quest it can give is **🔎 Sticker-Seeker: Rank _X_** , where _X_ represents the quest's rank. 

2 requirements will always show up in this quest, along with many other requirements: 

  * finding a number of [Seeker Stickers](/wiki/Sticker#Seeker_Stickers "Sticker") hidden around the map,
  * and collecting pollen with the [Sticker-Seeker](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker "Sticker-Seeker").

### Other requirements[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The remaining requirements are randomly picked from a pool. The number of extra requirements given starts at 1, increases to 2 at quest 100, and finally to 3 at quest 250. The same requirement cannot appear 2 quest ranks in a row. 

The requirements are chosen using the following steps: 

  * All possible requirements are added to a pool, if they: 
    * have not appeared in the last quest,
    * and the player has reached the minimum rank requirement for it to appear.
  * Each requirement is then given a weight, and the game picks out one requirement using said weights. The probability of a quest being picked is equal to its weight divided by the sum of all requirements' weights in the pool.
  * After a requirement is picked, it is removed from the pool, and another one is picked until it has reached its limit of requirements.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * It is the only [Quest Giver](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers") that does not have any dialogue and does not notify to talk to the Machine again after quest completion.
  * The Sticker Quest Giver is the fourth randomized [Quest Giver](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers") to give [eggs](/wiki/Egg "Egg") at certain milestones, the other three being [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee"), [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee"), and [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear").
  * The quests it gives always includes the requirements "Collect {amount} of Pollen using [Sticker-Seeker](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker "Sticker-Seeker")," and "Find 2-7 Seeker-Stickers hidden in {zones}."
  * Sometimes, a quest requirement can get so long you can’t see part of it. 
    * Progressing in the requirement a bit may fix this problem.
  * When having the Sticker-Seeker equipped and standing on the pad with the machine in front of it, normally there is a message saying 



Your Sticker-Seeker quest is in progress… (if you have not yet completed the quest). However, after standing on the pad for a few seconds, it changes to the next egg reward, and at what rank you will obtain it. 

  * The Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine has 8 Stickers on it.
  * The difficulty of the quests it gives is supposed to scale linearly with the player's rank, up to rank 150. However, due to an oversight, the difficulty instead stays the same until rank 150, where it takes a massive leap.
