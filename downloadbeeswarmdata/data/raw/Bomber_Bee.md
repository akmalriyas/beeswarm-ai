**Bomber Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Rare bee](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"). 

Bomber Bee's favorite treat is ![Sunflower Seed](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"). 

Bomber Bee likes the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field") and the [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"). It dislikes the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBomber_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 120 [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +10% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +40 [convert amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +1 [attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.1 [Bomb Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Bomb_Pollen "System Page").

  * **[[Buzz Bomb]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens")** Collects 7 pollen from 13 surrounding [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") (+10% pollen per [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond")). Combo with other bombs to increase power and stacks up to 10 times.
