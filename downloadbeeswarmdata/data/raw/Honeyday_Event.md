# Honeyday Event

**Buffs and Debuffs** are one of the core gameplay [mechanics](/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"). The player can obtain them from [amulets](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet"), [quest givers](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers"), [items](/wiki/Items "Items"), [ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), and different dispensers. Some buffs and debuffs are exclusive to one source, such as the Splinter Trap can only be obtained from the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge"), while others can be obtained in different ways, such as [blue boosts](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens") can be obtained from the [Blueberry Dispenser](/wiki/Blueberry_Dispenser "Blueberry Dispenser"), [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), and [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). 

All status effects can be found in the top margin of the screen, above the main menu. The effects are sorted from left to right, with newer effects appearing on the right. Hovering over the icon for a buff/debuff will give information on the stack amount, effect, and duration (if possible). On certain devices, icons for [accessories](/wiki/Category:Accessories "Category:Accessories") can also be found here instead of next to the hotbar.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBuffs_%2526_Debuffs%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Puffshroom Blessing, Festive Nymph Blessing and Robo Party Blessing are currently broken and stop working after rejoining a server, even if it still displays above. A player has to refresh it after a rejoin to keep it applied. 
    * Onett is aware of this bug and has attempted to fix it, being unsuccessful so far.[1]
  * The icon for the Frozen debuff is the asset of [Ice Cube](https://battlefordreamisland.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Cube "w:c:battlefordreamisland:Ice Cube"), a character from the animated web series [Battle for Dream Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_for_Dream_Island "wikipedia:Battle for Dream Island").
  * The 3YearParty code (now expired) used to reward unlimited gumdrops for an hour, but there was a glitch where if the player left and rejoined the game, the buff would disappear. Onett later removed the buff from the code.
  * The shortest buff is Coconut Haste Surge (2 seconds) while the longest is the Honeyday event, Launch Code Boost, and Galentine's Blessing (48 hours each).
  * Speaking to [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett") during [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") would give the player the Honeyday Event buff, even without claiming the hive (if it hadn't been claimed yet).
  * The Splinter Trap icon is the same as the Haste+ icon, which is ironic since they have the opposite effect.
  * Receiving more stacks of Robo Bear Challenge is less detrimental. For example, in round 2, the debuff is x0.12 capacity, which is better than x0.1 in round 1.
  * Haste+ was originally planned as one of Ninja Bee's abilities, but was soon scrapped.[2]
