**Robo Bear** is an [NPC](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC") released in Beesmas 2022 update. It is located in the 15 Bee Zone, found next to the Red HQ and at the other end of the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). The main feature of Robo Bear is initiating the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"). Before gaining access to Robo Bear, the player will be first given a quest to assemble the missing parts of Robo Bear being Red Drive, White Drive, Blue Drive, and Glitched Drive. The drives can be obtained in the following quests: 

  * [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear"): Repairing the White Drive
  * [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear"): Bartering for the Blue Drive
  * [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear"): Ready For the Red Drive?
  * [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear"): Corrupting the Glitched Drive



When all requirements are met, the player can then access talking to Robo Bear and initiate the Robo Bear Challenge at the cost of 1 Robo Pass, obtainable through the dispensers in the 30 bee zone. 

During Beesmas 2022, players were able to complete the quest by purchasing a white drive in [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog"), completing Science Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest for a red drive, completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest for a blue drive, and completing [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") “Decorating For Drives” quest for a glitched drive.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRobo_Bear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Robo Bear is the second NPC in the game that can initiate a challenge, the first being [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug").
  * Robo Bear uses the [Robot Animation Pack](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/82/Robot-Animation-Pack).
  * Robo Bear is one of the few quest givers who own a shop, the others being [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear"), [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear"), [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear"), and [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear").
  * Robo Bear's original face decal was made on 2019-02-24, eventually releasing on 2022-12-14 for [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas").
  * Robo Bear is the only bear to publicly have its design changed from before release.
  * Robo Bear's Quest, "Repairing Robo Bear" can be instantly completed once redeemed, that is if you already have the [Drives](/wiki/Drives "Drives").
