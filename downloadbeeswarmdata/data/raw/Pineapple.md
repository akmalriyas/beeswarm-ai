# Pineapple

Pineapple

[![Pineapple](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fb/Pineapple.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041445)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fb/Pineapple.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041445)

_"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."_

[![A ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e5/Pineapple_Token.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/168?cb=20240624232915)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e5/Pineapple_Token.png/revision/latest?cb=20240624232915) [](/wiki/File:Pineapple_Token.png)

A ![Pineapple](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fb/Pineapple.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404041445)**Pineapple** token.

A **Pineapple** is a type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that can be fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to a [Hasty](/wiki/Hasty_Bee "Hasty Bee"), [Brave](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee"), [Honey](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee"), [Exhausted](/wiki/Exhausted_Bee "Exhausted Bee"), [Shocked](/wiki/Shocked_Bee "Shocked Bee"), [Fire](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee"), [Demon](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee"), [Lion](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"), or [Fuzzy](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee") Bee, the amount of [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") they gain is doubled. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed ![Pineapple](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)**Pineapples**. [Mythic bees](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic") have a 1/24,000 chance to be gifted; [Legendary Bees](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"), a 1/12,000 chance; [Epic bees](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"), a 1/10,000 chance; and [Rare bees](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"), a 1/8,000 chance. Feeding a [radioactive](/wiki/Radioactive "Radioactive") bee a pineapple has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) to [mutate](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation") a bee.
