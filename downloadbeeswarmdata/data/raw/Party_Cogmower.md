_Not to be confused with[Cogmowers](/wiki/Cogmower "Cogmower"), the normal version of these or [Golden Cogmowers](/wiki/Golden_Cogmower "Golden Cogmower"), the rarer, golden counterpart of Cogmowers._

The **Party Cogmower** is one of the mobs exclusive to Robo Party. It functions similar to a [Golden Cogmower](/wiki/Golden_Cogmower "Golden Cogmower"), only staying at one field until the party ends.

## Strategies[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FParty_Cogmower%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Party Cogmowers don't aim for the player, allowing players to easily sidestep them.
  * Stand near the Party Cogmower, this will allow your bees to lock on to the Party Cogmower and quickly deal damage.
  * [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") provides very strong single-target damage. [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") can also stun the Party Cogmower in place and allow it to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mind_Hack "Ability Tokens").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FParty_Cogmower%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is one of the first mobs to appear during a [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party") if the player is just starting at the first ranks.
  * This is the only Robo Party mob to not have the same health per level as its normal version. 
    * The health per level is exactly half of its normal version's health per level.
  * When Bubble Bee Man's Winter Beesmas 2024 quest was added, it originally referred to Party Cogmowers as "Festive Cogmowers".
