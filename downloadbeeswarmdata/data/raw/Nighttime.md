# Nighttime

The **Day/Night Cycle** is a feature added in the [2018-09-10 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-09-10 "Updates"). Before the update, it was always daytime. 

As of the 12/?/24 update, the Day/Night Cycle is broken and the time it takes before night comes is currently irregular.

The game during daytime.

Daytime previously lasted from around 10 to 30 minutes (for an average of 20 minutes), but recently daytime lasts from 2–10 minutes. All [music](/wiki/Music "Music") plays as normal. This is the default setting for the game.

[![The game during nighttime.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/33/RobloxPlayerBeta_2018-09-24_18-02-11.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20180925001259)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/33/RobloxPlayerBeta_2018-09-24_18-02-11.png/revision/latest?cb=20180925001259) [](/wiki/File:RobloxPlayerBeta_2018-09-24_18-02-11.png)

The game during nighttime.

When the day ends, the sun goes down quickly, and the sky shifts to black, indicating that it is nighttime. During this cycle, all outdoor music is replaced by [mountaincall](/wiki/Music#mountaincall "Music"). Entering any area that plays the [vendor](/wiki/Music#vendor "Music"), [crawlers](/wiki/Music#crawlers "Music"), [gbtune1](/wiki/Music#gbtune1 "Music"), [hibernation1](/wiki/Music#hibernation1 "Music"), [starhall](/wiki/Music#starhall "Music") music or music playing during an [Ant](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge"), [Robo](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge") or [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge") will override the night music. [Fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies") will also appear, stopping in different [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"). The background of the server and walls will turn pitch black. A [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") has a higher chance of spawning every night. A moon sprout also has a 60% chance of spawning every night. 

Walking near a firefly will make it fly into the air and leave a 1x1 patch of [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles"). Once a player has removed all the fireflies, one of the tokens below will appear in the middle of their formation:

  * ![Glitter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") (Very rare).
  * ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") (Extremely rare).
  * ![Night Bell](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Night Bell](/wiki/Night_Bell "Night Bell") (Extremely rare).

Fireflies can only stop in the same field three times. After removing all the fireflies in a field three times, they will fly to a different field. Fireflies will only stop in the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field") and [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"). 

Night lasts 2 minutes 35 seconds, which is the duration of the night theme (mountaincall). Ever since the [2018-11-25](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates") and [2018-12-19](/wiki/Updates#2018-12-19 "Updates") updates, moon-shaped platforms leading to the [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask "Diamond Mask") and the platforms leading to [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") and the [Night Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match") are solid yellow only during nighttime if the player has a [Moon Amulet](/wiki/Moon_Amulet "Moon Amulet") (if the player owns the Diamond Mask, the platforms to the Diamond Mask will be blue and stay permanently solid). When [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") are planted at night, there is a 60% chance for it to be a moon sprout, with the odds of all other sprout types being altered (Supreme Sprouts become more common while the rest are rarer).
