## Information

### Usage

Gives ![Honey](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c6/Honey.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230410071605)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), ![Treat](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a8/Treat.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404041321)[Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), ![Pineapple](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fb/Pineapple.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404041445)[Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple") and ![Haste Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens")

### Requirement(s)

Membership in the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Club "Bee Swarm Simulator Club") group

### Cooldown

1 hour

### Location

Next to the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop")

  
The **Treat Dispenser** is a [dispenser](/wiki/Dispenser "Dispenser") located between the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch") and the entrance of the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop"). Members of the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Club "Bee Swarm Simulator Club") can use it to collect ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), ![Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), ![Pineapple](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Pineapple](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"), and ![Haste Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)x5 [Haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens") every hour. The number of ![Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat") varies, depending on the number of [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") the player has. 

If a player tries to use the Treat Dispenser without being in the group, they will get a prompt saying they must join the Bee Swarm Simulator Club. 

## Honey and Treats reward amounts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTreat_Dispenser%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The amount of honey and treats given is based on the number of bees the player has in their hive. More specifically, let _cnt_ be the number of bees in the player's hive: 

  * The amount of honey the player receives is equal to  ⌊ c n t 1.5 + 0.5 ⌋ × 50 {\displaystyle \left\lfloor {cnt}^{1.5}+0.5\right\rfloor \times 50} ![{\\displaystyle \\left\\lfloor {cnt}^{1.5}+0.5\\right\\rfloor \\times 50}](https://services.fandom.com/mathoid-facade/v1/media/math/render/svg/b0f5bea35df7cc6ccf292317de3dca4a8528b415), or 500 if the result is less than 500.
  * The amount of treats the player receives is equal to  ⌊ c n t 2.5 100 + 0.5 ⌋ {\displaystyle \left\lfloor {\frac {{cnt}^{2.5}}{100}}+0.5\right\rfloor } ![{\\displaystyle \\left\\lfloor {\\frac {{cnt}^{2.5}}{100}}+0.5\\right\\rfloor }](https://services.fandom.com/mathoid-facade/v1/media/math/render/svg/af148b4e31f97b0b5561db681c9067b96314d974), or 10 if the result is less than 10.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTreat_Dispenser%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is one of three dispensers that give ![Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat") in the game with the other two being the [Blueberry Dispenser](/wiki/Blueberry_Dispenser "Blueberry Dispenser") and the [Strawberry Dispenser](/wiki/Strawberry_Dispenser "Strawberry Dispenser"). 
    * This is the only dispenser to give two different treats, being ![Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat") and ![Pineapple](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple").
  * This is one of four dispensers requiring the player to be part of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club with the others being the [Honey Dispenser](/wiki/Honey_Dispenser "Honey Dispenser"), the Blueberry Dispenser, and the Strawberry Dispenser.
