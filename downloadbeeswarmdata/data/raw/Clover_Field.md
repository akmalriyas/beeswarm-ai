The **Clover Field** is one of the five [starting](/wiki/Starter_Zone "Starter Zone") [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") any player is able to visit upon starting the game. It is protected by a [Ladybug](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug") and a [Rhino Beetle](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"). 

## Events[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClover_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * A [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom") may naturally spawn here between the 15th minute (xx:15) and 45th minute (xx:45) of the hour.
  * A level 1-3 [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") may spawn here.
  * An active [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") may move to this field.
  * During a [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge"), if [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") is in [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field") or [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"), he will move to this field.



This field is a 26x29 field, containing 754 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are white, red, and blue, in sizes of single and double.

**_Note:_ flower counts within the table may vary due to rounding. Additionally, in game, all flowers are randomly generated and may not precisely align with the percentages.**

This field can spawn [treat](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), [red boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), blue boost, [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), gold or diamond [egg](/wiki/Eggs "Eggs") and [star jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") tokens when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Clover Field are [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"), [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee"), [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee"), [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee"), [Looker Bee](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee"), [Stubborn Bee](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee"), [Music Bee](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee"), [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"), and [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"). Bees that dislike the Clover Field are [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee") and [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee"). 

[Planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter") planted here produce Invigorating [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar"). 

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClover_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Field Booster](/wiki/Field_Booster "Field Booster") may give a Clover [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +200% Clover Field pollen for 15 minutes.
  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") will add 100% Clover Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes, if it isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% [(field dice)](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice") \- 300% [(loaded dice)](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") Clover Field pollen for 15 minutes if the boost isn't maxed out yet.
  * Clover [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Clover Field conversion and give +5% instant Clover Field conversion and +20% Clover Field pollen for each stack. All Field Winds (Including Clover Field Winds) can stack up to a total of 13 times.
  * Redeeming certain valid codes: 
    * Troggles (Gives Clover Field Code Buff + other stuff).
  * It can be boosted by the [King Beetle Amulet](/wiki/King_Beetle_Amulet "King Beetle Amulet"). If it has a Clover Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +100%.
  * It can be boosted by the [Shell Amulet](/wiki/Shell_Amulet "Shell Amulet"). If it has a Clover Field Boost, it can boost the field by +5% to +30% depending on the amulet rarity.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClover_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The field booster for the Clover Field has been decreased three times. In the [2018-07-11 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-07-11 "Updates"), it was decreased from x5 to x4, in the [2019-04-05 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates") it was decreased to x3, and in the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), it was decreased to x2.
  * Before the 2018-07-11 update, the Clover Field had 39 rows instead of the current 26 so it had 1131 flowers, and it was the largest field in the game. It is no longer the largest field in the game, with only 5 less flowers than the new largest, the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch").
  * The Bubble Wand on top of the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ") will sometimes blow [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") on to this field, along with the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field") and [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field").
  * The [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm") Summoner used to be where the [Wealth Clock](/wiki/Wealth_Clock "Wealth Clock") is currently, but when the Wealth Clock was added, the Honeystorm Pad was moved to right beside the bush in the Clover Field where the Rhino Beetle spawns.
  * The [King Beetle Lair](/wiki/King_Beetle_Lair "King Beetle Lair") is right under this field.
  * This is the first field the player can encounter in the game that is defended by two [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs").
  * When a [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") spawns in the Clover Field, its spike can be seen in King Beetle's Lair, which can still be activated. 
    * If a [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") is in the Clover Field, the stem and the light can be seen in King Beetle's Lair.
  * Clover Field has a higher chance than any other field of dropping royal jelly when a sprout pops. 
    * This field also has a low chance of spawning a star jelly.
  * If the player has the [Spark Staff](/wiki/Spark_Staff "Spark Staff"), is in the King Beetle's Lair, and jumps, they are able to collect pollen from the Clover Field.
  * This is the only field that gives eggs.
  * This is the only starting field with roughly the same amount of Single and Double flowers, while the other 4 fields in the Starter Zone all have around 90% Single flowers, and the rest being Double.
