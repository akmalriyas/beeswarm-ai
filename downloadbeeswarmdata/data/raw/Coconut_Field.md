The **Coconut Field** is a [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") added in the [September 28, 2019 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), located past the [Windy Bee Gate](/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate "Windy Bee Gate") and next to [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear"). It is home to and protected by the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab"). 

The field can spawn [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), [coconuts](/wiki/Coconut "Coconut"), [tropical drinks](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink"), [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") and [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean") tokens when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Coconut Field are [Vector Bee](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee"), [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"), [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee"), and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"). There are no bees that dislike the Coconut Field. 

This field grants Refreshing [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is planted here. 

A level 1-20 [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") has a chance to spawn in this field. 

This is a 30x21 field, containing 630 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are white, red, and blue, and the only natural size is triple.

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCoconut_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") will add 100% Coconut Field [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% - 300% Coconut Field pollen for 15 minutes based on the type used if it isn't already maxed out. [![The Coconut Field boost icon.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8b/Coconut_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230409172301) [![The Coconut Field boost icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8b/Coconut_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230409172301)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8b/Coconut_Field_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230409172301)

The Coconut Field boost icon.

  * Coconut [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% Instant Coconut Field Conversion and give +5% Instant Coconut Field Conversion and +10% Coconut Field pollen for each stack. Coconut Field Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.
  * Using the [Coconut Dispenser](/wiki/Coconut_Dispenser "Coconut Dispenser") will always give Coconut [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost") x1 (if the player has [Coconut Badge](/wiki/Badges#Coconut_Badges "Badges") cadet or higher), along with coconuts depending on the player's badge.
  * Equipping the [Coconut Clogs](/wiki/Coconut_Clogs "Coconut Clogs") grants x1.15 Coconut Field pollen.
  * Equipping the [Coconut Belt](/wiki/Coconut_Belt "Coconut Belt") grants x1.1 Coconut Field pollen.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCoconut_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is the only field where [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") and [leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves") can yield coconuts and tropical drinks.
  * It is also the only field to have a regular [boss](/wiki/Category:Bosses "Category:Bosses") defending it.
  * This is one of four fields to solely have contain triple flowers without pollination, the others being the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"), and the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch"). 
    * Furthermore, this field is the only triple-flower field dominated by white flowers.
  * Under the coconut tree opposite of the cave and closest to [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear"), there is a coconut token that can be collected to give 10 coconuts.
  * This and the Pepper Patch are the only fields where sprouts cannot naturally appear or be planted with the [Special Sprout Summoner](/wiki/Special_Sprout_Summoner "Special Sprout Summoner").
  * Like the [Pepper Badge](/wiki/Badges#Pepper_Badge "Badges"), the [Coconut Badge](/wiki/Badges#Coconut_Badge "Badges") requires more pollen collected than the other field badges. For example, the cadet badge for the Coconut Field requires 50,000,000 (50 million) pollen, compared to the normal requirement of 250,000 (250 thousand) pollen.
  * It is possible to gather pollen from this field without 35 [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") from the ledge facing the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent"). 
    * Players can also collect from the field by going outside the map and finding the back of the Ticket Tent. Jumping on the Ticket Tent with enough jump height will reach the field, allowing bees to farm.
  * The Coconut Dispenser used to boost this field differently based on the player's Coconut Badge, with the Junior badge giving no boost, Cadet giving a +100% pollen boost, Hotshot giving a +200% pollen boost, and anything Ace and above giving a +300% pollen boost. After the [Beesmas 2019 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-12-23 "Updates"), the Coconut Dispenser will always provide a +100% pollen boost, regardless of the player's Coconut Badge.
  * This field, the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field") and the [Hub Field](/wiki/Hub_Field "Hub Field") are the only fields without any bees to dislike them.
