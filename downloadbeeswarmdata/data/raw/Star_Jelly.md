## Royal Jelly[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRoyal_Jelly%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Royal Jelly** is an [inventory item](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory") that can be found as a token around the map, received as certain [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests") rewards, redeemed in valid [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), purchased from various [shops](/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops") or [dispensers](/wiki/Category:Dispenser "Category:Dispenser"), or various other different ways. When used on a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), it turns it into either a [Rare](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"), [Epic](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"), [Legendary](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"), [Mythic](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic"), a [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") variant of any of those bees, or a [mutated](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation") variant of those bees. 

The probabilities of Royal Jelly transformations are following: 

Variant  | Chance   
---|---  
Common  | 0%   
Rare  | ~70%  
Epic  | 27%   
Legendary  | 3%   
Mythic  | 0.004% (1/25,000)   
Event  | 0%   
Gifted  | 0.4% (1/250)   
Mutated (Not radioactive)  | 0.02% (1/5,000)   
Mutated (Radioactive)  | 1% (1/100)   
  
Royal Jelly does **NOT** necessarily turn a bee into a better rarity. For example, if a player used it on a Legendary bee, they have the same 70% chance of getting a Rare bee as if they had used it on a [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"). This rule also applies to gifted and mutated bees. 

## Ways to Obtain[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRoyal_Jelly%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Completing [Brown Bear's](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear") quests rewards one or more Royal Jelly and other items. Other quest givers give Royal Jelly less frequently.
  * Completing [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear")'s Diamond Egg, Star Jelly, and Mythic Egg quests rewards one or more royal jellies.
  * Completing [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear")'s quests often rewards multiple Royal Jellies. The amount generally increases with each further quest taken.
  * The [Royal Jelly Dispenser](/wiki/Royal_Jelly_Dispenser "Royal Jelly Dispenser") near the [Slingshot](/wiki/Slingshot "Slingshot"), [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), and [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field") dispenses them for 6 [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") each while also giving 10x [Haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens").
  * Royal Jelly can be purchased individually or in packs of 10 from the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop"). It costs 45R$ for one Royal Jelly, and 300R$ for 10 royal jellies.
  * Royal Jelly can be found in different hidden and hard-to-reach locations. These jellies can be obtained only once for every player (In other words, they don't respawn).
  * Royal Jelly can be claimed using the dispenser in the [Star Hall](/wiki/Star_Hall "Star Hall") every 22 hours. The number of Royal Jelly the player obtain depends on the number of [Gifted bees](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") in their [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive").
  * Finishing the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge") gives at least 2 Royal Jelly with the quantity increasing as the score increases by 50 points.
  * From the [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") made by the faces that can appear on certain objects or by [fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies").
  * Finishing the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge") or drops from [Stick Nymphs](/wiki/Stick_Nymph "Stick Nymph") during the challenge.
  * Royal Jelly can also naturally spawn in [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"), but the probability is extremely low.
  * Growing a [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") may drop Royal Jelly tokens depending on the type of sprout.
  * The [Royal Jelly Shop](/wiki/Royal_Jelly_Shop "Royal Jelly Shop") near the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"), where players can buy a Royal Jelly for 250,000 [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") initially, with the price increasing by 10,000 honey with each purchase until capping at 1,000,000 honey.
  * As a drop from specific [Planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter") ([Ticket](/wiki/Ticket_Planter "Ticket Planter"), [Plastic](/wiki/Plastic_Planter "Plastic Planter"), [Blue Clay](/wiki/Blue_Clay_Planter "Blue Clay Planter"), [Tacky](/wiki/Tacky_Planter "Tacky Planter"), [Petal](/wiki/Petal_Planter "Petal Planter")).
  * A sprout in Clover Field drops more Royal Jelly than any other field.
  * As a reward from placing certain [Stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") on the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack").
  * Defeating [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") may drop Royal Jelly.
  * As a reward from the [Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match").
  * As a drop from [Festive Gifts](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens").
  * As a drop from [Festive Mark](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Mark "Ability Tokens").
  * As a reward from the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine").
  * As a gift from a [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy").

## Star Jelly[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRoyal_Jelly%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Star Jelly turns a bee into a random gifted bee, with the same rarity chances as normal Royal Jellies. 

  * Certain quests are guaranteed to give Star Jellies, while others are randomized. 
    * There is a chance to get a Star Jelly for doing [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear")'s repeatable quests.

    * Obtained by finishing [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear")'s Beesperanto and Epistemological Endeavor quests in the ![Translator](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Translator](/wiki/Translator "Translator") questline and Corrupting The ![Glitched Drive](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Glitched Drive](/wiki/Drives#Glitched_Drive "Drives") in the Drives questline.
    * Obtained by finishing the 90th and 195th quest from [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear").
    * Obtained by finishing the 5th, 15th, and 21st - 30th quests from [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear").
    * Obtained by starting the 7th [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") quest.
    * Obtained by finishing the 25th quest from [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee") or [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee").
    * Obtained by finishing the 5th quest and 10th quest from [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear").
    * Obtained by finishing the 2nd quest in the Drive questline.
    * Every 10 quests completed from [Honey Bee (NPC)](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") rewards a Star Jelly. ## Specific Bee Jelly Variants[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRoyal_Jelly%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Instead of transforming into a random bee of a particular rarity, **specific bee jellies** will always hatch into the jellies respective bee. All of these jellies share the same appearance of a royal jelly. These are primarily obtained from limited-time codes, Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas, and Bubble Bee Man. 

  * [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee") Jelly was obtained from the code _DontUseThisJelly_ [Expired]; it turns any bee into a Basic Bee.
  * [Bumble Bee](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee") Jelly is obtained from completing [B.B.M Mission](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man#Quest_.28Inside_30_Bee_Gate.29 "Bubble Bee Man") quest from Bubble Bee Man in the [30 Bee Area](/wiki/Bear_Gate#Inside_The_30_Bee_Area "Bear Gate") and from the codes "BeeDay2019" [Expired] and "Cubly" [Expired]; it turns any bee into a Bumble Bee.
  * [Hasty Bee](/wiki/Hasty_Bee "Hasty Bee") Jelly is obtained from receiving Bee Bear's Beesmas Blitz quest and from the code _HasteHelper_ [Expired]; it turns any bee into a Hasty Bee.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRoyal_Jelly%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Event bee jellies have infinite uses; as long as the hive does not already have that bee, the player can use the jelly.
  * Royal Jelly is based off the real-life counterpart. The eponymous substance is used as nutrition for honey bee larvae and queens.
  * [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") gave a hint about the Royal Jelly on top of the Ticket Tent in his dialogue.

[![IMG 20200405 180050](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7b/IMG_20200405_180050.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20200405110855) [![IMG 20200405 180050](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7b/IMG_20200405_180050.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20200405110855)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7b/IMG_20200405_180050.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20200405110855) [](/wiki/File:IMG_20200405_180050.jpg) [![Marks](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/Marks.png/revision/latest?cb=20181211013303) [![Marks](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/Marks.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20181211013303)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/Marks.png/revision/latest?cb=20181211013303) [](/wiki/File:Marks.png)

  * When the player is transforming any bee with any type of royal jelly, the number of exclamation marks after the bee's name is the same as that bee's tier. 
    * Common/Rare = 1 exclamation mark (!), Epic = 2 exclamation marks (!!), Legendary/Event = 3 exclamation marks (!!!) and Mythic = 4 exclamation marks (!!!!).
  * Using a specific jelly on said bee (eg. using a Bubble Bee Jelly on a Bubble Bee) does not use the jelly, and no transformation pop-up appears.
  * Event Bee jellies cannot make the bee gifted even if it was gifted before.
  * The plural for royal jelly is incorrectly spelled as 'Royal Jellys', rather than 'Royal Jellies'. This is a mistake that has happened with other items with a plural form that exchanges a 'y' for 'ies'.
  * Using a normal jelly on a gifted/mutated bee is almost **CERTAIN** to cause the bee to lose its gifted/mutated status. It is a 1/250 chance to get a gifted bee back and 1/5000 chance to get a mutated bee back. However, the bee will most likely have a different mutation.
  * If you attempt to use a royal jelly on a bee inbetween [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge") rounds, it will prompt you with the message: "⚠ Hatching or transforming a bee will end your Robo Bear Challenge. Continue?⚠". Choosing "Yes" will end your Robo Bear Challenge, and "No" will cancel it.
  * The rarest possible bee obtained from Royal Jelly is a specific mythic bee that is gifted and has the mutation bee ability rate with a chance of 1/104T.
  * The description of Royal Jelly does not actually say that you can get a Mythic bee.
  * Star Jelly token behind [Blue Maze](/wiki/Mazes#Blue_Maze "Mazes") used to give 3 Star Jellies, instead of 1.
  * An effective method of obtaining large amounts of Star Jelly is fighting [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") with its Beesmas quests completed, as the player can obtain a few star jellies when the challenge ends and some from Festive Nymphs or [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") himself.


  * Star Jelly’s raw ingredients cost: 
    * 1125 [Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry")
    * 1125 [Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry")
    * 1500 [Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple")
    * 175 Royal Jellies or 175M [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey")
    * 3 [Magic Beans](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean") or 60 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket")
