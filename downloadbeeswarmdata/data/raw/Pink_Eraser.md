The **Pink Eraser** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 12 [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of one and has no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") with a [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page") [mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation").
