The **King Beetle** is a level 7 [boss mob](/wiki/Category:Bosses "Category:Bosses") that resides in his [lair](/wiki/King_Beetle%27s_Lair "King Beetle's Lair"). The entrance to his lair is on the wall between the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field") and the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), above the blue flower closest to the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ") via a transparent wall. He has 2,500 health and deals 40 damage to the player per hit if the player has no [Defense](/wiki/System_Page#Defense "System Page"). 

He takes 1 day (24 hours) to respawn after being defeated (20 hours and 24 minutes with a [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee")). The rewards for defeating him vary, but always include 150 [Battle Points](/wiki/Battle_Points "Battle Points"), as well as [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") and [Bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond"). 

There is a one-in-seven (approximately 14%) chance that he will drop a [King Beetle Amulet](/wiki/King_Beetle_Amulet "King Beetle Amulet") when defeated. Having more [Loot Luck](/wiki/Loot_Luck "Loot Luck") increases the probabilities of other drops but doesn’t improve the probability of a King Beetle Amulet dropping. After the [2019-02-01 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-02-01 "Updates"), King Beetle drops honey tokens and rewards in a token circle around the center of the lair unlike all other mobs who drop the circle around the place of their death (except for [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail"), [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), and [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee")) unless the player receives a King Beetle Amulet, in which it goes directly into the player's inventory.

## Strategies[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKing_Beetle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Defeating a King Beetle is challenging and he might kill players several times before they succeed (It's recommended that players don't fight King Beetle with a full bag). Here are some recommended strategies for defeating him: 

  * Use King Beetle's predictable pattern of behavior to stop and run. He moves toward the player, stops to "aim," and then lunges. If the player stays still long enough for him to aim (about 3 seconds), but then quickly moves away, King Beetle will lunge at where the player used to be, not where the player has moved to. Thus, this method entails running along the edge of the lair, stopping, looking at King Beetle and waiting for him to lock onto the player, and then quickly jumping and running to a different point along the edge of the lair. Rinse and repeat until he is defeated.
  * Circling King Beetle allows the player to defeat King Beetle faster. If the player keeps circling around their King Beetle while keeping a good distance from him, their bees can get very close to King Beetle. Though the player might get hit the first few times, the beetle will most likely be defeated.
  * Run around the lair and occasionally look at his health. It's very helpful if players have Rage Bees, so they can run around and grab the tokens. However, if the player is too far away from him, their bees will come back to them rather than attacking the beetle. Building up maximum [Haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens") can help, although it may run out before King Beetle gets defeated. Using the [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute") or [Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider") can also help since gliding through the air can be faster than running.
  * If the player is certain that their bees will kill him before they die, they can just wait for their bees to defeat him while the player stands in front of King Beetle, doing nothing during the process.
  * If a Rogue Vicious Bee is active in the Clover Field, players can trick it into damaging King Beetle.
  * Due to a bug, if the player gets the King Beetle stuck on the clue 'Song Name' by standing in the top right corner, King Beetle cannot attack them. This may take several attempts to work.
  * If the player gets too far from King Beetle, their bees will follow them instead of attacking it. But if the player gets too close, it's very easy to accidentally touch its aura which will damage the player.
  * If more than one player is trying to fight King Beetle at the same time, his pattern of behavior can become less predictable. It can also be difficult to tell which King Beetle is targeting them. For best results, wait for the other player's King Beetle to be defeated before entering its lair.
  * If the player's bees' levels are lower than 7, their attempts to attack will most likely miss. This will make defeating it take much longer than if they were level 7 or above.
  * When dodging King Beetle, keep in mind he does not follow directly behind the player but can sidestep to get in their way.
  * If you die, the King Beetle's health will reset, unlike [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick") and [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail").



## Tips[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKing_Beetle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Some things can help the player defeat King Beetle, regardless of which strategy the player chooses. 

  * Having more bees and having bees with high attack power such as [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"), [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"), [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee"), [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee"), [Riley Bee](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee"), [Bucko Bee](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee"), [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee"), [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee"), and [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee").
  * Having high-level bees so attacks don't miss (Recommended level 5-7 with at least one Rage Bee and 20 [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees")).
  * Wearing an [Accessory](/wiki/Category:Accessories "Category:Accessories") that can boost [Bee Attack](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Attack "System Page"), [Move speed](/wiki/System_Page#Movespeed "System Page"), [Jump Power](/wiki/System_Page#Jump_Power "System Page"), or Defense, such as [guards](/wiki/Category:Guards "Category:Guards").
  * Collecting [Rage](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Rage "Ability Tokens"), [Focus](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Focus "Ability Tokens") and [Melody](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Melody "Ability Tokens") tokens can be helpful, especially if the player has fewer bees. Collecting [haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens") and [bear morph](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bear_Morph "Ability Tokens") tokens can also help dodge King Beetle's attack.
  * While it is possible to defeat King Beetle using the arrow keys to move, WASD is the suggested method, because it allows the player to keep looking at King Beetle while they are moving.
  * The Parachute or Glider can be used to quickly get out of King Beetle's attack path.
  * Having at least 20-25 bees with all of them at least level 7 is usually enough to defeat King Beetle using the above strategies.
  * Using a [Stinger](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger") can be useful with low level bees.



## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKing_Beetle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * King Beetle used to guarantee an amulet upon death.
  * In the back right corner of the lair, there is something written on the floor. This is a hint for a [code](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). 
    * The clue is "Song Name", which refers to the music that plays in the lair. If the player checks [Onett's inventory](https://www.roblox.com/library/1598349092/crawlers), the user will see that it's called Crawlers.
  * King Beetle was the first boss to be added to the game.
  * King Beetle has the fifth-largest amount of health in the game for any mob, with [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear") being the fourth, [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab") the third, [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Chicks#Mondo_Chick "Chicks") the second, and [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail") the first. On certain occasions, Rogue Vicious Bee, [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee"), [Aphids](/wiki/Aphid "Aphid"), and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") will have more health depending on their level. In this case, King Beetle can be considered the weakest boss in terms of health.
  * King Beetle and Stump Snail are the only mobs that can drop an amulet, the [King Beetle Amulet](/wiki/King_Beetle_Amulet "King Beetle Amulet") and the [Shell Amulet](/wiki/Shell_Amulet "Shell Amulet") respectively.
  * There was a myth that clicking on King Beetle has an effect. However, this was disproven as [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") has confirmed that King Beetle does not even have a [click detector](http://robloxdev.com/api-reference/class/ClickDetector).
  * Before the [2018-07-11 Update](/wiki/Updates#2018-07-11 "Updates"), King Beetle took two days to respawn after being defeated, granted more battle points and despawned after about 5 minutes.
  * King Beetle is a hybrid of a [Rhino Beetle](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle") and a [Ladybug](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug"), as stated by Onett on Discord.[1]
  * Onett stated that the King Beetle emits toxins that makes the player hallucinate, which is why both he and his lair are strangely colored.[2]
  * An active King Beetle's health bar can be seen through the Clover Field and the Blue Flower Field.
  * King Beetle is the only boss that doesn’t have a time limit to kill it. 
    * King beetle is also the only boss to drop its loot in a circle.
