_This page is for stats that apply to bees. For stats that apply to the player, collecting pollen, and the[hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"), see [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page")._  
**Stats** (short for statistics) refer to the aspects of each [bee](/wiki/Bee "Bee") that are the basis for how that bee performs. Certain stats increase with higher [levels](/wiki/Bond "Bond"). 

## Rarity[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStats%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Rarity** refers to how often bees of each category can be expected to be generated from [eggs](/wiki/Eggs "Eggs") or [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"). Bees of greater rarity tend to be of higher stats and possessing their own unique ability, but rarity itself does not have any bearing on performance. An [Event](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") tier of bees are aquired through their unique obtainment method. 

## Color[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStats%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Color** affects a bee's ability to gather [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") from [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"), improving their Gather Amount by 50% when collecting from flowers of a matching color. [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") and [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") are the only colors of bees that have this bonus. [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") bees do not experience any boost to Gather Amount, even from white flowers. 

## Bond[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStats%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_Main article:[Bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond")_

**[Bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond")** is a stat to determine if a bee has enough "experience" to level up. The following methods can affect experience: 

  * Treats
  * Collecting Pollen
  * Bond from battle
  * Puppy Love
  * Digging in to the [Beesmas Feast](/wiki/Beesmas_Feast "Beesmas Feast") during Beesmas.



## Energy[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStats%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_Main article:[Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy")_

**Energy** represents the number of times a bee will gather pollen from a flower and attack [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") before returning to the [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") to rest (30 seconds to fully recharge upon entering the hive) 

  * Spawning [Ability Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") does not cost a bee any energy
  * The energy of all bees in a swarm can be increased with stacks of _[Polar Power](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_NPCs "Buffs & Debuffs")_ from [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear")'s quests, or from an energy [mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation")
  * Energy is increased by 5% for each level above 1
  * Resetting yourself will cause bees to go back to the hive, but they will not recharge their energy
  * An _energy reset_ can be achieved by reseting again when bees are inside of the hive after a prior reset



The formula for energy is: _Base Energy * (1 + .05 * (Level - 1)) * Polar Power + Energy Mutation_

## Speed[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStats%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Speed** , short for **Bee Movespeed** , is how fast a bee moves and performs actions (contrary to its name). Speed indrectly affects all basic processes of a bee such as: 

  * Gathering
  * Attacking
  * Sipping nectar in a planter
  * Converting at hive.



Bees move at approximately 1.5 times their move speed when not close to their owner and 0.5 of their move speed when returning to the hive to rest. The speed of all bees in a swarm can be increased by [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Ninja Bee's](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee") hive bonus, [Beesmas Cheer](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Beesmas_Cheer "Ability Tokens"), [Playtime Badge](/wiki/Badges#Playtime_Badge "Badges"), [oil](/wiki/Oil "Oil"), [super smoothies](/wiki/Super_Smoothie "Super Smoothie"), and activating [Coconut Haste](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Coconut_Haste "Passive Abilities"). 

The formula for Bee Movespeed is: _Base Bee Movespeed * (1 + .03 * (Level - 1)) * (1 + Ninja Bee Hive Bonus + Playtime Badge Bonus)_

## Attack[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStats%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Attack** is how much damage the bee will do when fighting mobs. Attack remains constant at all levels, but can be boosted through various [accessories](/wiki/Category:Accessories "Category:Accessories"), [items](/wiki/Items "Items"), [amulets](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet"), certain [gifted hive bonuses](/wiki/Gifted_Bee#List_of_Hive_Bonuses "Gifted Bee") and ability tokens. Gifted bees have 50% more attack. 

The formula for a bee's Attack is: (Base Attack Damage + Attack Buffs) * Attack multiplier (* 1.5 If the bee is gifted) 

## Gather Amount[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStats%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Gather Amount** describes how much pollen a bee will collect from a flower and how quickly it does so. The amount collected increases by 10% for each level above 1. Gather amount may be increased with; amulets, certain Gifted Bee bonuses, [Gamepasses,](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop") or items. 

The formula for Gather Amount is: _Base Gather Amount * (1 + .10 * (Level - 1)) * Gifted Multiplier_

## Production Amount[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStats%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Production Amount** describes how much pollen a bee will convert into [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") at the hive and how quickly it does so. The amount converted increases by 10% for each level above 1. 

The formula for Production Amount is: _(Base Production Amount + Production Amount) * (1 + .10 * (Level - 1)) * Conversion Rate * Gifted Multiplier_

## Mutations[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStats%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_Main article:[Mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation")_

**Mutations** are a bonus granted through feeding bitterberries to a bee when they are under the **radioactive** effect. Mutations can grant bonus: 

  * Attack
  * Convert Amount
  * Gather Amount
  * Energy
  * Bee Ability Rate
  * Bee Movespeed
  * Critical Chance
  * Instant Conversion



## Gifted[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStats%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_Main article:[Gifted Bees](/wiki/Gifted_Bees "Gifted Bees")_

**Gifted** is a bonus granted through feeding a bee's favourite treat, ![Star Treat](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/Star_Treat.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404041522)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear"), or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). This stat grants: 

  * Multiplier to gather amount, convert amount, and bee attack all together by 1.5x
  * Ability to spawn - [[Inspire](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens")] tokens
  * A gifted hive bonus (only once for each bee)
  * A gifted ability (if the bee has one)
  * A new appearance
