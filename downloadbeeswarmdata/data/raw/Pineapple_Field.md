The **Pineapple Patch** is a [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") located past the [Brave Bee Gate](/wiki/Brave_Bee_Gate "Brave Bee Gate") and to the right of the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop"). It is protected by a level 4 [mantis](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis") and a level 5 [rhino beetle](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"). 

This is a 33x23 field, containing 759 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are white, red, and blue, in sizes of single, double, and triple. 

Flowers  | White | Red | Blue | Total   
---|---|---|---|---  
Single | 137  | 18%  | 8  | 1%  | 8  | 1%  | **152** | **20%**  
Double | 342  | 45%  | 19  | 2.5%  | 19  | 2.5%  | **380** | **50%**  
Triple | 205  | 27%  | 11  | 1.5%  | 11  | 1.5%  | **228** | **30%**  
Total  | **683** | **90%** | **38** | **5%** | **38** | **5%** | **759** | **100%**  
  
**_Note:_ flower counts within the table may vary due to rounding. Additionally, in game, all flowers are randomly generated and may not precisely align with the percentages.**

This field can spawn [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [pineapple](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"), [bitterberry](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"), [neonberry](/wiki/Neonberry "Neonberry"), [enzyme](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"), [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), [glue](/wiki/Glue "Glue") and [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") tokens when gathering [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). 

This field grants Satisfying [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is planted here. 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Pineapple Patch are [Shocked Bee](/wiki/Shocked_Bee "Shocked Bee"), [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee"), [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"), [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"), and [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee"). Bees that dislike the Pineapple Patch are [Vector Bee](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee"), [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee"), and [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee"). 

A level 1-20 [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") has the chance of spawning here. 

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPineapple_Patch%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Field Booster](/wiki/Field_Booster "Field Booster") may give a [Pineapple Patch Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +200% Pineapple Patch pollen for 15 minutes.  [![The Pineapple Patch Boost icon.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4e/Pineapple_Patch_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080202) [![The Pineapple Patch Boost icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4e/Pineapple_Patch_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080202)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4e/Pineapple_Patch_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410080202)

The Pineapple Patch Boost icon.

  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") will add 100% Pineapple Patch pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% - +300% Pineapple Patch pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Pineapple Patch [winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Pineapple Patch conversion and give +5% instant Pineapple Patch conversion and +20% Pineapple Patch pollen for each stack. Pineapple Patch Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
  * The [Moon Amulet](/wiki/Moon_Amulet "Moon Amulet") can give a pollen boost for the Pineapple Patch between +1% and +150%.
  * It can be boosted by the [Shell Amulet](/wiki/Shell_Amulet "Shell Amulet"). If it has a Pineapple Patch Boost, it can boost the field by +7% to +30% depending on the amulet rarity.
  * Donating either the Menacing Mantis or Pineapple Patch Stamp [Sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") to the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack") boosts the Pineapple Patch by 5% and 10% respectively.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPineapple_Patch%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is the the biggest field in the game.
  * The mobs in this field ([Rhino Beetle](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle") and [Mantis](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis")) are associated with blue, despite the field being white.
  * There is a [pineapple](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple") token behind the pineapple closest to the shop. Collecting it grants the player five pineapples.
  * There is a [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") token worth three tickets on top of the giant pineapple. It can be obtain by using the [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon") and the [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute") or [Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider"), or by jumping up from the side of the pineapple.
  * There is a [glue](/wiki/Glue "Glue") token behind the biggest pineapple between the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field").
  * This was the third biggest field in the game before the [2018-07-11 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-07-11 "Updates").
  * This field is one of the three fields to have the word "Patch" in their name instead of "Field," along with the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch") and the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch"). 
    * The [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest") is another field without the word "Field" in it, having "Forest" in its name instead.
  * This is one of six fields that [fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies") can travel to, with the others being [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"), [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), and [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field").
  * This is one of the fields that the [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") can move to during the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge").
  * This field, along with the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field") and the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"), is the only field where an object may come to life and shoot sparkles.
  * The biggest pineapple will occasionally grow a face, where it will blow bubbles at patches of flowers, producing [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") (in diamond formation) upon contact. Collecting pollen on the affected flowers can produce tickets, pineapples, [moon charm](/wiki/Moon_Charm "Moon Charm"), [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), [enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"), or honey tokens.
  * While doing one of [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear")'s quests, Spirit Bear states that the Pineapple Patch was originally supposed to be a "Rhubarb Field". Instead, she and Stick Bug planted pineapples.
  * This is the only field to be guarded by two mobs of different tiers (Rhino Beetle and Mantis), if you don't include the [Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf") guarding the Pine Tree Forest as it merely happens to be located in the Werewolf's attack territory.
