**Pro Bear** is a [bear](/wiki/Category:Bears "Category:Bears") who runs the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop "Pro Shop"). He is the second shop bear encountered by most players. His presence is not required to purchase [items](/wiki/Items "Items") in the shop. 

Pro Bear has a similar appearance to [Noob Bear](/wiki/Noob_Bear "Noob Bear") and [Top Bear](/wiki/Top_Bear "Top Bear"): yellow skin, red shirt, green pants, and a smiley face. The only difference between them is the colors of their clothing.

  * Pro Bear is one of the four bears in the [10 Bee Area](/wiki/Brave_Bee_Gate "Brave Bee Gate"), the others being [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear"), [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear"), and [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear").
  * This bear, Noob Bear, Top Bear, and [Shadow Bear](/wiki/Shadow_Bear "Shadow Bear") are the only bears without a function whatsoever.
  * Behind Pro Bear, there is a [maze](/wiki/Mazes#Pro_Shop_Maze "Mazes") that contains a [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token, [sunflower seed](/wiki/Sunflower_Seeds "Sunflower Seeds") token, and has the ability to host [hidden stickers](/wiki/Sticker#Hidden_Stickers "Sticker").
