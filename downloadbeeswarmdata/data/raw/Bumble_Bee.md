**Bumble Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Rare bee](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"). 

Bumble Bee's favorite treat is ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"). 

Bumble Bee likes the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field") and the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"). It dislikes the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBumble_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Bumble Bee has the same skin color as the [Bucko Bee](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee").
  * Bumble Bee is the blue counterpart of the [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee").
  * Defeated [Rhino Beetles](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle") will sometimes drop ![Basic Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Bumble Bee Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Bumble_Bee_Egg "Egg").
  * A [special jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Royal_Jelly_Variants "Royal Jelly") that changes a bee into a Bumble Bee could have been obtained from [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") and the [code](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), 'BeeDay2019' and 'Cubly' (expired).
  * Bumble Bee, [Carpenter Bee](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee"), and [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee") are named after their real-life counterparts.
  * Bumble Bee, Bucko Bee, and [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee") are the only blue bees to like at least three fields.
  * Bumble Bee is the slowest rare bee.
  * Bumble bees gifted stat used to be +20% blue bomb pollen and has since been changed.
