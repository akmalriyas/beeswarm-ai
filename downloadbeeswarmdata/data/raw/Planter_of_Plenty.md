**The Planter Of Plenty** is a reusable planter added in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"). Alone, it grows in about 16 hours and stores 1 trillion [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). When harvested, it grants many bonus items, including Neonberries, Waxes and Turpentine. It can be purchased at the [Dapper Bear's Shop](/wiki/Dapper_Bear%27s_Shop "Dapper Bear's Shop").

It grows 50% faster in the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch"), [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"), [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field"), and the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), and grants 50% more pollen in the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"). [Gifted Bees](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") are 50% more likely to sip [nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") from this planter, and it grants +50% of all Nectar Types. 

Harvesting a fully grown Planter Of Plenty has a 1/3 chance to spawn a [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom"). The spawned puffshroom is between levels 9-10, and has a: 

  * ~72.94% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
  * ~24.07% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
  * ~2.92% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom,
  * ~0.07% chance of being a Mythic Puffshroom.
