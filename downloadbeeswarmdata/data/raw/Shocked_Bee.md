**Shocked Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Epic bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"). 

Shocked Bee's favorite treat is [Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"). 

Shocked Bee likes the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field") and the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"). It dislikes the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FShocked_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 80 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 2 seconds.
  * +40% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +50% [Convert Speed](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page"), +1 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats"), -50% [Sleep Time](/wiki/Stats#Energy "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.1 [White Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#White_Pollen "System Page").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FShocked_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Haste]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens")** Grants +10% [Player Movespeed](/wiki/System_Page#Movespeed "System Page") for 20 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.
  * **[[Token Link]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Token_Link "Ability Tokens")** Collects all other [Ability Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), granting ![Honey](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)25 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") (+10 per lvl) per token collected.

Shocked Bee has a base pollen collection of **10![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. That's equivalent to 2.5 ![White Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FShocked_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Shocked Bee, [Hasty Bee](/wiki/Hasty_Bee "Hasty Bee"), [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee"), and [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee") are the only [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in the game that has a spelling or grammar mistake in their description.
  * Shocked Bee requires less sleep than other bees, the only bee to have this trait.
  * Shocked Bee and [Music Bee](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee") have the same skin pattern.
  * Shocked Bee is the fastest bee to wake up from sleeping.
  * Shocked Bee used to have the [Buzz Bomb ability](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens"), but it was replaced by Token Link.
  * This is the only gifted bee to not have tinted wings.
