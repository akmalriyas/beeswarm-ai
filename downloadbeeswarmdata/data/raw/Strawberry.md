# Strawberry

Strawberry

[![Strawberry](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6d/Strawberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041355)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6d/Strawberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041355)

_"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."_

A **Strawberry** is a type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that can be fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to a [Rascal](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"), [Rad](/wiki/Rad_Bee "Rad Bee"), [Rage](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee"), [Riley](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee"), [Baby](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"), [Stubborn](/wiki/Stubborn_Bee "Stubborn Bee"), [Shy](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee"), or [Spicy](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee") Bee, the amount of [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") they gain is doubled. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed strawberries, though this only happens with a 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance for [Rare bees](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"), 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance for [Epic bees](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"), 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance for [Common](/wiki/Bees/Common "Bees/Common") and [Legendary bees](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"), and 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance for [Mythic bees](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic") per strawberry. Feeding a [radioactive](/wiki/Radioactive "Radioactive") bee a strawberry has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) to [mutate](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation") a bee. 

## Ways to Obtain[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStrawberry%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * There is a strawberry token hidden behind the largest strawberry in the corner of [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field").
  * From using the [Strawberry Dispenser](/wiki/Strawberry_Dispenser "Strawberry Dispenser") near the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"). The player will get one strawberry per red bee in their hive per use.
  * As a reward from the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge").
  * Redeeming certain valid [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"): 
    * 15MMembers (Gives 15 strawberries + other stuff).
  * As a reward from [memory matches](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match").
  * As a stack reward for adding certain [stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") to the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack"): 
    * ![Hivesticker pink cupcake](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f7/Hivesticker_pink_cupcake.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20240114165656)[Pink Cupcake Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") rewards 50 strawberries.
    * ![Hivesticker pale heart](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pale Heart Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") rewards 50 strawberries.
  * Digging in to the [Beesmas Feast](/wiki/Beesmas_Feast "Beesmas Feast").



### Drops[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStrawberry%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Farming in the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field"), [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), and the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch"). 
    * Harvesting [Leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves") in these fields may also yield strawberries.
  * As a drop from [Ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug"), [Scorpions](/wiki/Scorpion "Scorpion"), and [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle").
  * As a drop from a [Festive Gift](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens") or [Festive Mark](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Mark "Ability Tokens").
  * As a gift from a [cub buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy").
  * As a reward for completely growing any kind of [Sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts. 
    * Planting a sprout in the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"), and [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch") yields 4x more strawberries than other fields. Planting a sprout in the [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field") yields 8x more strawberries than other fields.
  * Harvesting [Sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") (higher chances in red fields).
  * As a drop from [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom") in red fields or fields with high amounts of red flowers ([Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), and [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field")).
  * As a drop from planters (besides ![Tacky Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Tacky Planter](/wiki/Tacky_Planter "Tacky Planter"), ![Blue Clay Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blue Clay Planter](/wiki/Blue_Clay_Planter "Blue Clay Planter") and ![The Planter Of Plenty](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[The Planter Of Plenty](/wiki/The_Planter_Of_Plenty "The Planter Of Plenty")), most likely in red fields. 
    * The ![Red Clay Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Red Clay Planter](/wiki/Red_Clay_Planter "Red Clay Planter") always drops strawberries upon harvest at full growth.
