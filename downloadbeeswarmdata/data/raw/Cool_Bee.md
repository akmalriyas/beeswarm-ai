**Cool Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Rare bee](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"). 

Cool Bee's favorite treat is ![Blueberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"). 

Cool Bee likes the [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field") and the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). It dislikes the [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCool_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 3 seconds.
  * Makes 120 [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +25% gather speed, +40 [convert amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), +1 [attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): x1.1 [Blue Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Blue_Pollen "System Page").

  * **[[Blue Boost]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens")** Grants x1.2 pollen from blue [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") for 15 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.
