![Carpenter Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/75/Carpenter_Bee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20200403235126) | **This piece of content recently got an update.** We are in the process of catching up to the game. **Critical Hits** are moments of increased [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") collection and attack damage, doubled at its base power. Their frequency is increased with higher [Critical Chance](/wiki/System_Page#Critical_Chance "System Page"), and their strength is increased by greater [Critical Power](/wiki/System_Page#Critical_Power "System Page"). A critical hit will have the pollen amount/attack damage shake and change color, similar to the animation below.

## Critical Chance[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCritical_Hits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Critical Chance** is the chance of a hit being critical hit. The player can see how much their Critical Chance is by looking for the Critical Chance stat in the System Page. Critical Chance can be improved by a variety of ways such as [ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), [guards](/wiki/Category:Guards "Category:Guards"), and [inventory items](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory").

## Super-Crit[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCritical_Hits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D23&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

When performing a critical hit, there is a chance to perform a **Super-Crit** (purple version of a critical hit) which doubles the pollen collection while also instantly converting the pollen collected, while for the attack damage is increased by roughly 33% of the player's Super-Crit Power. The frequency for a Super-Crit is increased with higher Super-Crit Chance, and their strength is increased by greater Super-Crit Power. 

### Super-Crit Chance[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCritical_Hits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D24&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Super-Crit Chance** is how often a critical hit will be a Super-Crit. The player can see how much their Super-Crit Chance is by looking for the Super-Crit Chance stat in the System Page. Super-Crit Chance can be improved by several ways.

### Super-Crit Power[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCritical_Hits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D31&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

**Super-Crit Power** is how strong a Super-Crit will be. The player can see how much their Super-Crit Power is by looking for the Super-Crit Power stat in the System Page. Super-Crit Power can be improved by several ways. 

#### Bees[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCritical_Hits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D32&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

##### Beequips[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCritical_Hits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D33&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Individual Bee: 
    * A ![Camo Bandana](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Camo Bandana](/wiki/Camo_Bandana "Camo Bandana") can give up to +14% Super-Crit Power.
  * Hive Bonus: 
    * A ![Whistle](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Whistle](/wiki/Whistle "Whistle") can give from +1% Super-Crit Power to +20% Super-Crit Power from waxing.
    * ![Pink Shades](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Pink Shades](/wiki/Pink_Shades "Pink Shades") from +1% Super-Crit Power to +5% Super-Crit Power. Using waxes can increase the amount up to +9% Super-Crit Power.
    * A ![Demon Talisman](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Demon Talisman](/wiki/Demon_Talisman "Demon Talisman") can give up to 10% Super-Crit Power as a hive bonus from waxing.
