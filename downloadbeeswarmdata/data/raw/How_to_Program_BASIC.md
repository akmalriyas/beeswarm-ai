The **How to Program BASIC** is a Roblox cosmetic hat that was able to be obtained by solving all the puzzles from [The Computer](/wiki/The_Computer "The Computer"). It was available starting from November 23, 2020 through the [Ready Player Two](/wiki/Ready_Player_Two "Ready Player Two") event. As it is a Roblox hat, players can wear it on their avatar. When the player solves the puzzles, the hat will appear in the center inside the computer. However, if they leave the game, it won't appear when they rejoin.
