**Noob Bear** is the [bear](/wiki/Bear "Bear") who runs the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop"). Like other Shop Bears, his presence is not required to purchase from the shop, and players cannot interact with him.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNoob_Bear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * He is the first shop bear to be accessed in the game.
  * As with the other bears, Noob Bear can sometimes fall over or fall apart. 
    * This [glitch](/wiki/Glitches "Glitches") has been mostly patched, so it occurs very rarely as of now.
  * This bear, [Pro Bear](/wiki/Pro_Bear "Pro Bear"), [Top Bear](/wiki/Top_Bear "Top Bear"), and [Shadow Bear](/wiki/Shadow_Bear "Shadow Bear") are the only bears without any significant purpose.
  * Noob Bear uses the [Cartoony Animation Package](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/56/Cartoony-Animation-Package).
  * Noob Bear is represented by one of the twelve [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy") skins, specifically the ![Noob Cub](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Noob Cub](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins "Cub Buddy"). This skin was obtainable by buying the [Cub Buddy Launch Pack](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop").
