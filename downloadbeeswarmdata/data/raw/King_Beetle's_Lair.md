# King Beetle's Lair

**King Beetle's Lair** is the location where [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle") spawns. It can be accessed from the light green door between [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ") and under the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field") and next to the far end of the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"). Inside the lair, the exit can be accessed by climbing a ladder leaning adjacent to the entrance. The interior of the lair is completely empty, except for the ladder for leaving. Both doors are one-way. The player will be pushed through either door if they stand too close, even if they aren’t moving. The entrance to the lair cycles in color, so it stands out from the rest of the environment. 

The King Beetle spawns on the back wall adjacent to the Noob Shop and [Lava Obby](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses#Lava_Obby "Obstacle Courses"). 

Near the back right corner of the lair, there is some text on the floor, "Song Name". It refers to the song that plays inside the lair and is a hint to the [code](/wiki/Codes "Codes"), "Crawlers".

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKing_Beetle%2527s_Lair%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * In early game versions, if players went too close to the side of the lair that is closest to the Noob Shop, they could die due to a lava block clipping into the wall from the lava obby. This was later patched.
  * The bottom of the pole of the [Honeystorm Summoner](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm") sign pokes into the top of the lair.
  * If there is a [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") in the Clover Field, the bottom of its light column and the remaining pollen number can be seen in the lair. 
    * This is similar to when [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee")’s spikes and [clouds](/wiki/Clouds "Clouds") spawn in the Clover Field.
  * There is a [glitch](/wiki/Glitches "Glitches") where the King Beetle can fail to lunge and get stuck when in the 'Song Name' corner.
  * During the [Beesmas 2018 Event](/wiki/Updates#2018-12-25 "Updates"), the red roof of the lair could be seen on the sides of the flower blocks in the Clover Field. 
    * However, the red roof's glow near the Noob Shop wall is still visible even when it was not snowy.
  * Any [tool](/wiki/Tools "Tools") can collect [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") from the Clover Field inside the lair if the player continuously jumps and has enough [Jump Power](/wiki/System_Page#Jump_Power "System Page"). However, the [Spark Staff](/wiki/Spark_Staff "Spark Staff") doesn't require too much Jump Power.
  * If the player has enough Jump Power and jumps high enough, the [rhino beetle](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle") and [ladybug](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug") in the Clover Field can be triggered. The player's bees may also think that they are in the Clover Field and start collecting pollen.
  * The entrance to the lair is lighter than the color of the surrounding ground and slightly changes in. During Beesmas, it is much more noticeable as the walls surrounding the entrance are white.
  * The cycling colors of the entrance matches the cycling colors and pattern of any supreme-tier amulet.
