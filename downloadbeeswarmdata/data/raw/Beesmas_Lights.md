# Beesmas Lights

Beesmas Lights, unlocked after completing [Science Bear's](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") Beesmas quest.

**Beesmas Lights** would appear in fields as the player collects pollen after completing [Science Bear's](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") Beesmas Lights [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests"). 

They fall from the sky in a similar way to the [Star Shower passive](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Shower "Passive Abilities"), taking 2 seconds to fall. Whenever a Beesmas Light is caught, it grants the [Inspire](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inspire "Ability Tokens") buff and converts based on the player's [convert total](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Total "System Page"). If the player doesn't catch it, the light collects pollen instead. Beesmas Lights spawn after collecting 25 Ability Tokens (with a 30 second cooldown), dropping 5 lights into the field. Beesmas Lights spawn in any field, including the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field") and the fields in [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge"). 

If the player approaches the switch for the Beesmas Lights, located beside [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") and has not yet completed his quest, a text box will display reading the following text: "This [_sic_] Beesmas Lights aren't powering on..."

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBeesmas_Lights%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The Beesmas Lights has the same function as the [Star Shower passive](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Shower "Passive Abilities") with the difference of the fact that the star shower is more focused in a small area than Beesmas Lights. 
    * This means that if the passive is disregarded, the Beesmas Lights are the only way to get the inspire buff without [gifted bees](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") or redeeming certain [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes").
  * Catching a falling Beesmas Light has a 1 in 10,000 chance to reward a Red/Blue/Green/Yellow Falling Beesmas Light [sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") (depending on the color of the light).
  * Even though falling Beesmas Lights were introduced during the Beesmas 2020 event, the Beesmas Light decorations along the mountain were present during prior Beesmas events.
  * Over the course of the removal of several Beesmas events, Beesmas Lights would still fall into fields a few days after Beesmas was over. 
    * [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man")'s Beesmas quest dialogue also tends to take longer to be removed.
  * The text displayed when standing under the light switch for the Beesmas Lights displays "This Beesmas Lights" instead of "The Beesmas Lights". This is likely an oversight by [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)").
