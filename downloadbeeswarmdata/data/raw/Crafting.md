# Crafting

## Blender

[ ![Betterblender](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a4/Betterblender.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20260218183516) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a4/Betterblender.png/revision/latest?cb=20260218183516 "Betterblender") The Blender

## Information

### Usage

Crafting consumable items from ingredients

### Requirement(s)

Access to the [Badge Bearer's Guild](/wiki/Badge_Bearer%27s_Guild "Badge Bearer's Guild")

### Cooldown

None

### Location

Inside the [Badge Bearer's Guild](/wiki/Badge_Bearer%27s_Guild "Badge Bearer's Guild")

  
The **Blender** is a [machine](/wiki/Category:Machines "Category:Machines") located in the [Badge Bearer's Guild](/wiki/Badge_Bearer%27s_Guild "Badge Bearer's Guild"). The machine's usage is to create [consumable](/wiki/Category:Consumables "Category:Consumables") [items](/wiki/Items "Items") from pre-existing items. However, the player is only able to craft one type of item at a time. There is a total of 20 different items that the machine can craft.

[![The Blender interface when crafting an item.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7e/Blendermenu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200406191405)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7e/Blendermenu.png/revision/latest?cb=20200406191405) [](/wiki/File:Blendermenu.png)

The Blender interface when crafting an item.

Each item takes 5 minutes to craft and the timer only counts down when the player is in-game, unless they have an ![Offline Voucher](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bc/Offline_Voucher.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20251226052657)[Offline Voucher](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker"), in which it will stay active for up to 24 hours after they log out. The player can end the crafting process at any time by clicking on the "End Crafting" button. This will give the player all finished items, and all unfinished items will be turned back into their ingredients and given back to the player. If the button is green, then everything has been blended. If not (red button), then the Blender has not finished blending. The player can also speed up the blending for a varying amount of ![Ticket](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Ticket.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404015820)[Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"). For every 1 item being crafted, the Blender requires 1 more ticket to speed up. Except for ![Moon Charm](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Moon Charms](/wiki/Moon_Charm "Moon Charm") and ![Gumdrops](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops"), for which each ticket serves for speeding up whole 10 items.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlender%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * There is a ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token on top of the lid of the Blender. It can be obtained by repeatedly jumping on the handle with enough [Jump Power](/wiki/System_Page#Jump_Power "System Page") or by standing on the gate to the Ace Badge section and gliding towards the top. Additionally, you may perform a difficult head-hitter to get it.
  * If you select to craft something but not actually ‘blend’ it, when you leave the blender and open it up again, it would show that you would select the number of red extracts you want to craft, even if the amount displayed is above the amount you would be able to craft with your resources, though you won't be able to select the Confirm option.
  * During the start of the Beesmas 2022, players could make progress for "collect items" quests through blender refunds; however, this was soon fixed.
  * Crafting something that requires 2 or more strawberries will appear as [-n Strawberrys], being incorrectly spelt as the correct word should be strawberries.
  * [![There is a misspelling present when using enzymes for crafting.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1b/Enzymesmispell.png/revision/latest?cb=20251004090507) [![There is a misspelling present when using enzymes for crafting.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1b/Enzymesmispell.png/revision/latest?cb=20251004090507)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1b/Enzymesmispell.png/revision/latest?cb=20251004090507) [](/wiki/File:Enzymesmispell.png)

There is a misspelling present when using enzymes for crafting.

Crafting something that requires enzymes will appear as [-n Enzymess], being incorrectly spelt with an extra s at the end. However, if you craft the enzymes themselves, the correct spelling will appear.
  * The message above the buttons while crafting appears differently if one has the Offline Voucher redeemed. If the player has the Offline Voucher redeemed, it will say: "You've redeemed the offline voucher! The blender will continue running for up to 24 hours while you are offline."
