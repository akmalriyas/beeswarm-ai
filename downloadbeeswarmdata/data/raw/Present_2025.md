# Present/2025

Presents returned once again in Beesmas 2025. They still have their main function of adding [Ornaments](/wiki/Ornaments "Ornaments") to the [Beesmas Tree](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree "Beesmas Tree") when given to NPCs, though players can't give a present to [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") as the bear is not present. 

## Ways to obtain[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPresent%2F2025%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Multiple quests from [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") award a total of ![Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/02/Present.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404045255)10 [Presents](/wiki/Present "Present"), along with other rewards.
  * [Bee Bear’s Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%E2%80%99s_Catalog "Bee Bear’s Catalog") sold ![Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/02/Present.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404045255)1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present") for ![Snowflake](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/27/Snowflake.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230406132508)250 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") and ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)10 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear").
  * [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett") gives ![Present](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present") (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event's period.
  * Donating ![Strawberry](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry") to the [Galentine Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine#Galentine_Shrine "Wind Shrine") has a chance to give ![Present](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present"), along with other rewards.
  * Completing [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party")'s rank 25 for the first time gives ![Present](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present"), along with other rewards.
