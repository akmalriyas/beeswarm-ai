# Gummy Beacon

[![The Gummy Beacon](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4c/Gummy_Beacon.png/revision/latest?cb=20210131193123) [![The Gummy Beacon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4c/Gummy_Beacon.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210131193123)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4c/Gummy_Beacon.png/revision/latest?cb=20210131193123) [](/wiki/File:Gummy_Beacon.png)

The Gummy Beacon after the player has turned in Gummy Bear's Beesmas quest.

The **Gummy Beacon** is a [Beesmas](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas") 2020+ machine that can be unlocked after completing [Gummy Bear's](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") Beesmas [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests"). It is located on top of the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop"), on the corner closest to the [Royal Jelly Dispenser](/wiki/Royal_Jelly_Dispenser "Royal Jelly Dispenser") next to the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), in front of the [Star Hall](/wiki/Star_Hall "Star Hall"). If the player attempts to activate it without completing Gummy Bear's Beesmas quest, it reads: _"The satellite dish isn't working, but it still emits bad vibes..."_ Completing his quest allows the player to activate a Gummy Siege. 

## Gummy Siege

Upon activating, a platform will appear with [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") and [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") in front of the Gummy Beacon, and the [Gummy Morph](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Morph "Passive Abilities") tune will play. Then, it will [summon](/wiki/Bees#Summoned_Bees "Bees") three level 8-20 Gummy Bees for five minutes. Gummy Bear will begin to shoot [goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo") puddles at random fields similar to the [Gummy Invasion](/wiki/Category:Gummy_Invasion "Category:Gummy Invasion") event. The targeted field will also spawn ![Gumdrops](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") tokens. Gummy Bear will pick five random fields (excluding the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field") and [Hub Field](/wiki/Hub_Field "Hub Field")), and shoots goo for one minute per field, making the Gummy Siege 5 minutes long. The cooldown for Gummy Siege is 8 Hours.

Three random leveled Gummy Bees being summoned.

When it starts, two server-wide messages will appear saying the following lines:  Gummy Bear: "Artillery has arrived, Gummy Soldier {Username}." Gummy Bear: "Beesmas ends now. Commence GOO YEAR celebrations!"

As Gummy Bear targets fields, more server-wide messages will appear showing which field is being targeted: 

Gummy Bear: "Sight set on {Field Name}!"

## Trivia

  * This is the second and currently the only way of making Gummy Bear appear in the mountain, the first being using the [Gummy Cannon](/wiki/Gummy_Cannon "Gummy Cannon"). 
    * Although Gummy Bear is shown during the siege, he can still be seen in his lair.
    * This is also the only way that the Gummy Cannon can appear.
  * This is one of the 4 machines that summons temporary bees, the others being the [Honeyday Candles](/wiki/Honeyday_Candles "Honeyday Candles"), [Onett's Lid Art](/wiki/Onett%27s_Lid_Art "Onett's Lid Art"), and Honey Bee's [Honey Wreath](/wiki/Honey_Wreath "Honey Wreath").
  * This is the only way to have more than 1 Gummy Bee, though it is only for a limited amount of time.
  * The Gummy Bees given by the Gummy Beacon can help player activate the [Gummy Morph](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Morph "Passive Abilities") very quickly by spawning tokens that contribute towards the passive.
  * This and Onett's Lid Art are the only ways to have multiple [Event Bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event"). However, they only last for a limited amount of time.
  * This, Onett's Lid Art and the [Naughty List](/wiki/Naughty_List "Naughty List") are Beesmas machines that have the longest cooldowns in the game, at 8 hours.
  * The Gummy Bees from the Gummy Siege are not affected by the [Gummy Morph](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Morph "Passive Abilities") Passive from the [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask"), however their [abilities](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") will still contribute to activating the passive.
  * After the Beesmas 2025 update, The summoned Gummy Bee NPC will appear extremely shaky when it idles. This is due to it's model still using Roblox's old movement engine that broke with an update.
