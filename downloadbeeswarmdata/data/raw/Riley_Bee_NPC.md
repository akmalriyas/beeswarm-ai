**Gifted Riley Bee** is a [quest giver](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers") and one of three permanent [Quest Bees](/wiki/Category:Quest_Bees "Category:Quest Bees"), the others being [![Gifted Bucko Bee NPC](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9e/Gifted_Bucko_Bee_NPC.png/revision/latest?cb=20210907180446) [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee") and [![Honey Bee NPC](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/49/Honey_Bee_NPC.png/revision/latest?cb=20210907181947) [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)"). It resides on the roof of the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"), next to the [Top Riley Bee Helpers](/wiki/Top_Riley_Bee_Helpers "Top Riley Bee Helpers") [leaderboard](/wiki/Leaderboards "Leaderboards"). The player cannot speak or receive/complete its [Quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") without a ![Translator](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Translator](/wiki/Translator "Translator"), which is given by [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") three times. The player must complete 150 quests from Gifted Riley Bee to unlock crafting the ![Heat-Treated Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Heat-Treated Planter](/wiki/Heat-Treated_Planter "Heat-Treated Planter"), and 250 to unlock crafting the [![Dark Scythe](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6b/Dark_Scythe.png/revision/latest?cb=20211226225244) [Dark Scythe](/wiki/Dark_Scythe "Dark Scythe").

The reward will vary depending on the number of quests the player has completed. Rewards will always contain one ![Red Extract](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Red Extract](/wiki/Red_Extract "Red Extract") and [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGifted_Riley_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Since the quests are all chosen at random, including the first, the formula for the first requirements of any quest is, "x multiplied by (number of Gifted Riley Bee quests completed +1)"
  * Gifted Riley Bee is one of the three [NPCs](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC") that are based on [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), the others being Gifted Bucko Bee and Honey Bee (NPC).
  * Gifted Riley Bee is the first and only quest giver so far to be in the Red HQ.
  * There was a [visual glitch](/wiki/Glitches#Wingless_Bees "Glitches") where if you looked through the glass roof over the Red HQ and looked at Gifted Riley Bee, Gifted Riley Bee would have appeared wingless. However, in the [2019-09-28 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), the glass roof was removed.
  * While Gifted Riley Bee says to defeat 50 scorpions in the Honeyday Candles 2020 dialogue, the requirement was replaced with collecting 500 red bomb tokens.
  * Gifted Riley Bee says that the 2021 Honeyday Candles give a ![Swirled Wax](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Swirled Waxes](/wiki/Swirled_Wax "Swirled Wax") for each use, but the candles can actually give all types of waxes and at a varying amount, with Swirled Waxes being a rare drop. This is because before the [2021-26-12 Update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"), Swirled Wax was the only wax in the game and was given after every use.
  * The second half of the dialogue of Gifted Riley Bee's Beesmas 2021 present dialogue is repeated from Beesmas 2020's as Gifted Riley Bee mentions setting up the bPad.
  * The "bPad" is obviously a reference to the iPad, with the "i" being replaced with a "b" given that Riley Bee is a bee.
