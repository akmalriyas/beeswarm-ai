_This article is about the bee called the "Bear Bee." For the[Bear](/wiki/Category:Bears "Category:Bears") NPC of a similar name, see "[Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear")."_

**Bear Bee** is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless") [Event bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event"). You can get this bee by redeeming a ![Bear Bee Voucher](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Bear Bee Voucher](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker"), which can be obtained by purchasing in the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop") for ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)800 Robux, from trading, as an incredibly rare drop from [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear") and very rarely from the [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge"). 

Similar to all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, and can only become [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") if the player bought it during its release, by feeding it a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

Bear Bee likes the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch") and the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). It dislikes the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field").

  * **[[Bear Morph]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bear_Morph "Ability Tokens")** Transforms you into a random bear! Grants ×2 Pollen and boosts [Speed](/wiki/System_Page#Movespeed "System Page") and [Jump Power](/wiki/System_Page#Jump_Power "System Page"). If Gifted, has a 20% chance to transform the player into a rare bear that gives special boosts (either [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear"), which gives x2.5 Pollen and an additional x1.5 [Pollen from Bee Gathering](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Gather_Pollen "System Page") or [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear"), which adds +1 Conversion Link).

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBear_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Bear Morph’s extra jump power and speed can give early access to things such as [royal jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") and [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") that can normally only be accessed later in the game.
  * The First-edition [Bear Bee Pack](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop") was available in the first month of the game's release for 650 Robux. Later, when it went on sale again, it cost 1,000 Robux. It currently costs 800 Robux.
  * This was the first Event bee added to the game and the only Event bee which was in the original release.
  * This was the first bee that is obtainable with Robux, the second being [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee"), the third being [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"), the fourth being [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee"), the fifth being [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"), the sixth and seventh being [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") and [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"), the eighth being [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee").
  * This bee, Festive Bee, [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"), [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"), Windy Bee, and Digital Bee are the only Event bees obtainable without spending tickets. 
    * Gummy Bee was previously purchasable with tickets and the Festive Bee is currently purchasable for 500 tickets.
  * This bee, Gummy Bee, [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"), Puppy Bee, Festive Bee, Windy Bee, and Digital Bee are the only bees that can be a First Edition Bee.
  * Before the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"), Bear Bee's [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee#List_of_Hive_Bonuses "Gifted Bee") was +20% [White Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#White_Pollen "System Page"). It was later changed to +40% White Pollen and then to +5% Pollen. The current [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee#List_of_Hive_Bonuses "Gifted Bee") is +10% Pollen.
  * This bee, Tabby Bee, Puppy Bee, [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), and [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee") are the only bees based on real life animals other than bees.
  * This bee, Festive Bee, Gummy Bee, [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee"), and Tabby Bee are the only bees to have a gifted bonus that affects its signature ability. 
    * This enhanced Bear Morph in the gifted hive bonus, adding Science Bear and Mother Bear morphs, was added in the [2019-04-17 Update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-17 "Updates").
  * This bee is the only bee who can transform the player into a bear, and one of three ways to do so. The other ways are by using the [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask"), which can transform the player into [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear"), and redeeming certain expired [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes").
  * This is the only Event bee that can become gifted without the use of a star treat or gingerbread bear, as all First Edition Bear Bees become automatically gifted when the owner joins the game if they are not gifted yet.
  * There is a glitch in which dying with bear morph active will leave the player's character without a head when it deactivates.
  * There is a [sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") that can be obtained by a Gifted Bear Bee, that being the Bear Bee Offer Sticker, as an extremely rare drop while gathering in the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), or a 1/3 (approx. 33.3%) chance from feeding it a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat").
