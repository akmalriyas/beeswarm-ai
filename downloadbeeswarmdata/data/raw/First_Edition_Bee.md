# First Edition Bee

[![The decal for the First Edition Flag.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f0/1stflag.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404052534) [![The decal for the First Edition Flag.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f0/1stflag.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404052534)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f0/1stflag.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404052534)

The decal for the First Edition Flag.

A **first edition bee** is a designation given to certain [Event bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") bought or earned during their first release. This designation is represented by a white and yellow "1st" flag appearing on top of the bee, and the flag emits a sparkling effect. The flag is purely cosmetic and does not change or affect gameplay at all. 

This designation is currently only available for [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"), [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"), [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"), [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"), [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee"), [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee"), and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"). 

**If the player removes a first edition bee, the bee will retain its first edition status when added back to the[hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive").**

## First Edition Bees[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFirst_Edition_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Bear Bee: Bought during the first month after the game's launch (all First Edition Bear Bees got an additional bonus of being automatically [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee")).
  * Tabby Bee and Puppy Bee: Purchased during the their respective release sales (half price).
  * Gummy Bee: Obtained after completing all of [Gummy Bear's](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") during the [Gummy Invasion Event](/wiki/Category:Gummy_Invasion "Category:Gummy Invasion").
  * Festive Bee: Obtained after completing all of [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") quests during the [Beesmas 2018 Event](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas") or obtaining it from the Festive Bee Pack for ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)1,600 Robux in the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop").
  * Windy Bee: Obtained as a special prize from the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or purchased in the Robux Shop for ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)800 Robux before October 27, 2019.
  * Digital Bee: Obtained from [Robo Bear's Shop](/wiki/Robo_Bear%27s_Shop "Robo Bear's Shop") behind [Robo Bear](/wiki/Robo_Bear "Robo Bear") or by purchasing the Digital Bee Pack for ![Robux](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)2,500 Robux in the Robux Shop during [Beesmas 2022.](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas")

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFirst_Edition_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") has stated that the reason a First Edition [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") does not exist is because there is no room for a 1st Flag on its back with the spikes.
  * The earliest 1st Edition Bee was [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee"), coming in at the game's release.
  * Even though First Edition Bear Bees are automatically gifted, the gifted status can still be removed if a player adds it to the hive again.
