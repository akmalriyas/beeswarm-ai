# OST

**Music** is a component in [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"). It is heard in the background during gameplay. Certain areas of the map have different songs. Music can be turned on or off in the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page"). 

There are 15 songs principally used, all found in [Onett's inventory.](https://www.roblox.com/users/1912490/inventory/#!/audio)
