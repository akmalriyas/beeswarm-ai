The **Wind Shrine** is located beyond the [Windy Bee Gate](/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate "Windy Bee Gate"), next to the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch"). It allows players to donate items to it in exchange for rewards such as [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") and [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"). The Wind Shrine has a cooldown of 1 hour between each donation. Donating [cloud vials](/wiki/Cloud_Vial "Cloud Vial") after donating a [Spirit Petal](/wiki/Spirit_Petal "Spirit Petal") is currently the only way to obtain a [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"). 

The Wind Shrine also indicates if a [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") is active in one of the [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"). If the chimes are continuously moving around and nobody has donated recently, a Wild Windy Bee will be somewhere around the map.

## Usage

Upon interacting with the shrine, it says "Would you like to make an offering to the Wind Shrine?" If the player clicks on the dialogue, a window pop-up will appear, in which it allows the player to donate any amount of almost any item in their inventory to the shrine. Upon doing so, a message follows, with the chimes moving around swiftly. If the player cancels the donation, it instead says "You canceled the donation." 

If the player interacts with the Shrine during cooldown, it says "You may only donate to the Shrine once per hour. Come back in x: y." (x = minutes, y = seconds) 

If the player wants to donate multiple items at once, they can hold down the "+" button. The rate at which the amount of that certain item increases the longer the "+" button is held down. 

### Favor

Favor is a [mechanic](/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") that affects the player's rewards from the Wind Shrine. Favor is increased by donating items. 

**A Spirit Petal is NOT required to be donated to start increasing favor.**

Favor can be roughly estimated by the messages received when the player is rewarded winds or is donating cloud vials for a Windy Bee. 

### Honey

All donations, excluding [spirit petals](/wiki/Spirit_Petal "Spirit Petal"), grant honey, regardless of the other rewards. For example, if the player spawns a Wild Windy Bee, they will get the same amount of honey from the donation as they would if they had gotten winds instead. ### Obtaining a Windy Bee

**To get the Windy Bee from the Shrine, players must first donate a spirit petal to it.** After donating a Spirit petal, any Cloud Vial donated gives the player a chance to obtain a Windy Bee Egg. The player's chance to obtain the Windy Bee Egg depends on how much favor the player has with the shrine. 

If the player receives a Windy Bee Egg, the Wind Shrine will display the following message: "And it calls back to you!". The Windy Bee victory jingle will play, and server-wide message will appear in the chat, reading "🎉 [Player] has tamed a Windy Bee! 🎉", but if it fails, the message will instead read, "But it doesn't respond..."

### Badge Shivers

Badge shivers are rewards that are linked to the player's [Badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges"). The player can shiver a badge when they have the required badges by donating certain items. Shivering a badge does not do anything to the badge itself, only granting certain rewards if you have certain badges. Donating more items at a time gives a higher chance of shivering the related badge. Some badge shivers may require multiple badges or certain tiers of badges. Other badges cannot be shivered, or they are currently unknown. Below is a list of all known badge shivers.

## Field Winds

When you donate certain items to the shrine, you have a chance to summon Field Winds. The winds are random and not guaranteed. The more of the item you donate and the higher your favor, the better chance you have at getting a high stack of winds. 

  * Generally, the color of item you donate correlates with the color of field you get.
  * Purple potion has bias to get a large stack in red or blue fields. Generally, it is recommended to donate a purple potion for boosting.
  * Eggs often give a large stack in most of the fields. The rarer the egg, the better and more winds.
  * Swirled wax tends to give 7-8x white field winds.
  * Gifted Gold Egg often gives 7-9x winds.
  * Gifted Diamond Egg often gives 10-11x winds.

## Donations

The shrine will grant different rewards depending on the item donated. All donations will grant Honey and favor (both of which increase based on the amount donated), but other effects may vary. A list of possible outcomes is written below. 

[![The rewards from donating a Spirit Petal to the Wind Shrine.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a7/Spirit_Petal_Rewards.png/revision/latest?cb=20240131204150) [![The rewards from donating a Spirit Petal to the Wind Shrine.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a7/Spirit_Petal_Rewards.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240131204150)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a7/Spirit_Petal_Rewards.png/revision/latest?cb=20240131204150) [](/wiki/File:Spirit_Petal_Rewards.png)

The rewards from donating a Spirit Petal to the Wind Shrine.

  * Summoning clouds on fields and granting field winds.
  * Summoning a [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee").
  * Summoning [Bean Bugs](/wiki/Bean_Bug "Bean Bug") and granting a [Jelly Bean](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans").
  * Granting badge rewards.
  * Enabling the player to receive a [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") and granting other rewards. 
    * This refers to when you donate the [Spirit Petal](/wiki/Spirit_Petal "Spirit Petal"), which can only be done once, which grants 500 [Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), 1 [Star Jelly](/wiki/Star_Jelly "Star Jelly"), 5 [Enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"), 5 [Oils](/wiki/Oil "Oil"), 5 [Red Extracts](/wiki/Red_Extract "Red Extract"), 5 [Blue Extracts](/wiki/Blue_Extract "Blue Extract"), 5 [Tropical Drinks](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink") and 5 [Magic Beans](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean").
  * Failing to obtain a [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") and granting [Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat").
  * Succeeding to obtain a [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee").
  * Granting [Galentine's Blessing](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Areas "Buffs & Debuffs"), and other rewards during Beesmas.
  * Granting 7 Jelly Beans ([Ready Player Two](/wiki/Ready_Player_Two "Ready Player Two") Event, although still possible as of 2025-11-28).



Certain items are more likely to have certain effects. For example, donating eggs other than Basic Eggs and Honey Bee Eggs will always grant field winds and donating Star Jellies, Magic Beans or Field Dice is likely to summon a Wild Windy Bee. Some items also have biases when granting field winds, such as [Purple Potions](/wiki/Purple_Potion "Purple Potion") being more likely to grant field winds for colored fields. Donating a diamond egg (or blue extracts) has a higher chance of granting winds for blue fields. 

**Keep in mind that the rewards are somewhat random, as offering the same donation twice in the same amount may have a different result. However, more items in the donation can grant better rewards.**
