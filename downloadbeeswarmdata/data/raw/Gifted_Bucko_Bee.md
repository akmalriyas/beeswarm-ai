**Gifted Bucko Bee** is a [quest giver](/wiki/Quest_Givers "Quest Givers") and one of three permanent [Quest Bees](/wiki/Category:Quest_Bees "Category:Quest Bees"), the others being ![Gifted Riley Bee NPC](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee") and ![Honey Bee NPC](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)"). Gifted Bucko Bee is located on the second floor of the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ"), in between the [Top Bucko Bee Helpers](/wiki/Top_Bucko_Bee_Helpers "Top Bucko Bee Helpers") and the [Daily Top Blue Collectors](/wiki/Daily_Top_Blue_Collectors "Daily Top Blue Collectors") [Leaderboards](/wiki/Leaderboards "Leaderboards"). The player cannot start receiving or completing its [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") without a ![Translator](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Translator](/wiki/Translator "Translator"), which is given by [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") three times. Completing 150 quests from Gifted Bucko Bee is required to craft the ![Hydroponic Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Hydroponic Planter](/wiki/Hydroponic_Planter "Hydroponic Planter") and completing 250 quests from Gifted Bucko Bee is required to craft the [Tide Popper](/wiki/Tide_Popper "Tide Popper").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGifted_Bucko_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Since the quests are all chosen at random, including the first, the formula for the first requirements of any quest is "x divided by (number of Gifted Bucko Bee quests completed +1)".
  * Gifted Bucko Bee is one of the three [NPCs](/wiki/Category:NPC "Category:NPC") to be based on [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), the other two being [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee") and [Honey Bee (NPC)](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)"). 
    * This Bee is the only quest bee to reside indoors, though Gifted Riley Bee resided indoors too before an update.
  * In the Bucko Bee's [Snow Machine](/wiki/Snow_Machine "Snow Machine") for Beesmas 2020 dialogue, Gifted Bucko Bee says to defeat ten spiders. However, the [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests") requires the player to defeat five spiders.
  * Gifted Bucko Bee enjoys playing with marbles, as evidenced by their [Present](/wiki/Present "Present") dialogue in which they remark “Can't wait to crack this open and roll those bad boys around.”
