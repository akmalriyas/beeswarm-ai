The **Stump Snail** is a level 6 [mob](/wiki/Mob "Mob") that occupies the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"). With 30,000,000 health, it idly moves along the circumference of the field, posing no direct threat but dealing 30 damage upon contact. 

Like the [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick"), the Stump Snail's health is saved, allowing players to attack it in multiple sessions until it is fully defeated. 

Once defeated, it takes 96 hours (4 days) to respawn. If the player has the [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"), the time is reduced to 81 hours and 36 minutes (3.4 days).

## Tips/Strategies[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStump_Snail%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Having bees oriented toward fighting, such as [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee"), [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee"), [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"), [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"), [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") and [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee") will help defeat the Stump Snail faster.


  * Two additional [Fire Bees](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee") from [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee")'s [Inferno](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inferno "Ability Tokens") and [Frogs](/wiki/Frog "Frog") from [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee")'s [Summon Frog](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Summon_Frog "Ability Tokens") may be summoned to deal further damage.
  * [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") can deal more damage and provides [Critical](/wiki/System_Page#Critical_Chance "System Page") and [Super-Crit chance](/wiki/Critical_Hits#Super-Crit_Chance "Critical Hits") to increase bee damage.
  * The player should wear equipment that boosts the bee attack, notably the [Fire mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask "Fire Mask") or [Demon mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask"). 
    * If the player has the [Gummy mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask"), they can intentionally trigger [Gummy Morph](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Morph "Passive Abilities") to boost [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee")'s attack for 10 seconds.
  * A player with the [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister "Coconut Canister") can intentionally trigger the [Emergency Coconut Shield](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Emergency_Coconut_Shield "Passive Abilities") to receive 10 seconds of ×1.25 Bee Attack.
  * When AFK, a [cub buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy") is recommended as it is capable of collecting tokens the player would otherwise miss.
  * [Stingers](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger") greatly boost attack, [Oil](/wiki/Oil "Oil") increases [Bee Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), and [Tropical Drinks](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink") increase critical chance by 5%.
  * During the [Beesmas 2020](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas"), 2021, 2022, summer 2024 events, and 2025, the summoned bees from [Onett's Lid Art](/wiki/Onett%27s_Lid_Art "Onett's Lid Art"), [Honeyday Candles](/wiki/Honeyday_Candles "Honeyday Candles"), and [Gummy Siege](/wiki/Gummy_Siege "Gummy Siege") could be used to attack the Stump Snail.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStump_Snail%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * It is one of two [mobs](/wiki/Mob "Mob") where the damage dealt to it is saved over multiple sessions, the other being [Commando Chick](/wiki/Chicks#Commando_Chick "Chicks").
  * Stump Snail, [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle"), and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") are the only mobs that can drop [amulets](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet"). 
    * Stump Snail is the only mob that is guaranteed to drop an amulet.
  * This is the only mob that guarantees to drop [glue](/wiki/Glue "Glue") upon defeat.
  * As of the [2019-01-02 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-02-01 "Updates"), the Stump Snail, [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee"), King Beetle (only if it drops a [King Beetle Amulet](/wiki/King_Beetle_Amulet "King Beetle Amulet")), and [ants](/wiki/Ants "Ants") are the only mobs that do not drop tokens when defeat; instead the loot directly goes to the player's inventory. 
    * Even though the Stump Snail does not drop tokens, the total amount of honey rewarded is still increased by [Honey From Tokens](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_From_Tokens "System Page").
  * [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") refers to the Stump Snail as "Ol' Stumpy," which may be a reference to Youtuber [ThnxCya](https://www.youtube.com/user/ThnxCya) as he is also known to refer to the Stump Snail as "Ol' Stumpy."
  * It is possible to damage other players' snails with [flames](/wiki/Flame "Flame").
  * Stump Snail originally had 25,000,000 health prior to the [2019-04-05 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates"). The change was due to most bees' attacks being buffed in said update.
  * Stump Snail has the most health and the longest respawn time among all mobs.
  * Previously, there was a bug where the Stump Snail would spawn at a lower position than usual, causing it to bob up before falling under the field. 
    * On rare occasions, it would fall significantly beneath the map beyond the player's bees' attack range. During such occurrences, bees would be unable to focus on it, flying up and down and rendering its defeat unrealistic.
    * This has since been patched.
  * This is one of the three hostile mobs that require multiple days to respawn, the others being [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear") and [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab").
  * When a player leaves the field, the snail returns to its cave without a specific target. In this state, it can damage any player that comes into contact with it.
  * Only the head of a stump snail can deal damage.
  * If the player is in the middle of a round of [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"), the player's bees will not target Stump Snail. This is likely so the player can collect pollen from the Stump Field for the challenge's quests without having to worry about the snail distracting their bees. However, the snail will still spawn and can damage the player.
