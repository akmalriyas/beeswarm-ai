The Swarming Egg of the Hive.

The **Swarming Egg of the Hive** is a cosmetic hat accessory that could have been obtained from completing the quest from [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") during the Roblox [Egg Hunt 2020](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2020 "Egg Hunt 2020") event. As it is a Roblox hat, players can wear it on their avatar for display. The [Onett statue](/wiki/Easter_Eggs#Statue_of_Onett "Easter Eggs") on top of the [hives](/wiki/Hive "Hive") and the [quest giver Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett") wore the Swarming Egg of the Hive during the event. It could have been obtained from April 7 to April 28, 2020, during the three-week duration of the event. 

Like the [Flight of the Bumble Egg](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Bumble_Egg "Flight of the Bumble Egg") and [How To Program BASIC](/wiki/How_to_Program_BASIC "How to Program BASIC"), the Swarming Egg of the Hive does not give any special boosts and is just a Roblox hat that was only obtainable for a limited time.
