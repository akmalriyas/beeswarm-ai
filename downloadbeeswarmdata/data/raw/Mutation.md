**Mutations** are a [mechanic](/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") that buffs the [stats](/wiki/Stats "Stats") of certain [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in the player’s [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"). Mutated bees have a different colored [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") number in their [hive slot](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot") and on their wings than the standard white, respective to what stat it's mutated with. A mutated bee can become temporarily [radioactive](/wiki/Radiation "Radiation"), and the radiation can spread to neighboring bees in the hive. 

Every time the player feeds a bee a [bitterberry](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"), it has a 0.1% chance to get a random mutation. [Royal jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") and [star jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") has a 0.02% chance of turning a bee into a mutated bee. [Atomic treats](/wiki/Atomic_Treat "Atomic Treat") guarantee a mutation when fed to a bee, regardless of whether it is radioactive or not. 

If the bee is radioactive: 

  * The chance of a mutation when using a bitterberry is increased to 1%,
  * The chance of a mutation when using a royal jelly or star jelly is increased to 1%,
  * Using many special treats ([sunflower seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"), [strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"), [blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"), [pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"), [moon charms](/wiki/Moon_Charm "Moon Charm")) have a 0.004% chance to mutate the bee. This is irregardless of whether the bee likes the treat or not.



Note that using a royal jelly or an egg on a mutated bee will almost certainly make the bee lose its mutation, just like gifted bees losing their gifted status. 

A mutated bee's GUI on discovery shows a pop-up to the left of the bee, which shows the player the mutation the bee has gained, with a text box reading,  ☢️ {Bee type} gained a mutation! (+x% [Mutation Buff]) ☢️

Mutations are separate from every other modifiers in the game, meaning that: 

  * If a mutation is given as a percentage, it is multiplied directly to the bee's base stats after every other modifiers are applied.
  * If a mutation is given as a whole number, it is added to the bee's base stats before multiplicative modifiers are applied.

## Mutation strength[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMutation%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_Some of the information in this section has been modified for better reading. For the raw data, visit[Module:Mutation Stats](/wiki/Module:Mutation_Stats "Module:Mutation Stats") and [the data it uses](/wiki/Module:Mutation_Stats/data "Module:Mutation Stats/data")._

The upper bound of a mutation increases with the level of the mutated bee, allowing it to receive better mutations. More specifically, let _lvl_ be the level of the mutated bee: 

  * l v l P e r c e n t a g e = ( l v l − 1 ) / ( 25 − 1 ) {\displaystyle lvlPercentage=(lvl-1)/(25-1)} ![{\\displaystyle lvlPercentage=\(lvl-1\)/\(25-1\)}](https://services.fandom.com/mathoid-facade/v1/media/math/render/svg/a002e25f2272f554a07e72a980baeba81179da76)
  * The upper bound of a mutation is scaled between the minimum and maximum upper bound of the mutation, based on _lvlPercentage_ :  u p p e r B o u n d = m i n U p p e r B o u n d + ( m a x U p p e r B o u n d − m i n U p p e r B o u n d ) ∗ l v l P e r c e n t a g e {\displaystyle upperBound=minUpperBound+(maxUpperBound-minUpperBound)*lvlPercentage} ![{\\displaystyle upperBound=minUpperBound+\(maxUpperBound-minUpperBound\)*lvlPercentage}](https://services.fandom.com/mathoid-facade/v1/media/math/render/svg/e0794839f49dba8304a54a33d869796d06e95892).
  * The lower bound of a mutation also go through this process. However, the minimum and maximum lower bound of any mutation is the same, so there are no changes.



After that, the _randomBias_ function is invoked with the below parameters, and the result is rounded to the nearest interval to give the final result:  
` randomBias(lowerBound, upperBound, lowerBound, 0.5) `  
This gives a random value between _lowerBound_ and _upperBound_ , with a bias to values near _lowerBound_. This value is then rounded to the nearest interval to get the bee's mutation strength.  
For more information on how this function works, [see the designated module page](/wiki/Module:RandomBias "Module:RandomBias").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMutation%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * It is possible to obtain mutations that are almost entirely useless to certain bees. These include: 
    * An Attack Mutation on [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"), since it is unable to attack. 
      * While Attack mutations do not allow Baby Bee to attack, it will still add to the player's total attack, which can buff [Star Saw](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Saw "Passive Abilities").
    * A Bee Ability Rate mutation on an ungifted [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee") or [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee") without a [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") adding an ability, both of which lack abilities.
    * An Energy mutation on a [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee") or [Exhausted Bee](/wiki/Exhausted_Bee "Exhausted Bee"), as both bees have infinite energy. 
      * However, this still increases the amount of nectar the two bees can sip from a Planter.
  * The ![Beesmas Tree Hat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Beesmas Tree Hat](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree_Hat "Beesmas Tree Hat") Beequip requires a bee to have a mutation to be equipped, making any mutation potentially useful for any bee.
  * The ![Bubble Light](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Bubble Light](/wiki/Bubble_Light "Bubble Light") Beequip requires a bee to have a Energy mutation to be equipped.
  * When mutating a bee, any equipped Beequip is automatically unequipped and must be re-equipped by the player if they wish to continue using it.
  * Depending on the game's language, the [radiation emoji might be changed](/wiki/Glitches#Translation_Glitch "Glitches").
