# Snow Bear Summoner

[![Snowbear Summoner2](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/72/Snowbear_Summoner2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20260217215402)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/72/Snowbear_Summoner2.png/revision/latest?cb=20260217215402) [](/wiki/File:Snowbear_Summoner2.png)

The **Snowbear Summoner** is a Beesmas-exclusive summoner machine. It would be located next to the [Basic Bee Gate](/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate "Basic Bee Gate"), [Highest Snowbear Level](/wiki/Highest_Snowbear_Level "Highest Snowbear Level") leaderboard, and [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"). It can be used after completing [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear")'s Snowbear quest. It has a cooldown of 90 minutes before being able to be used again to avoid instant farming or overcrowding of [Snowbears](/wiki/Snowbear "Snowbear") in the Spider Field. 

If a player attempts to use the summoner without completing Panda Bear's Snowbear quest, the following message will appear: _'Last year's Snowbear is already complete! But it's starting to melt...'_

## Appearance[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnowbear_Summoner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The Snowbear Summoner is a snowman-shaped summoner. Without completing Panda Bear's quest, it is just 3 snowballs stacked on top of each other, 2 snowballs as the ears and 2 sticks as the hands with no decorations. If the player completes Panda Bear's Snowbear quest, it will have a top hat, 3 black buttons for the torso of the snowbear, 2 black buttons for the eyes, and 1 giant black button as the nose. 

## Usage[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnowbear_Summoner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The Snowbear Summoner summons a snowbear onto the Spider Field, which at first use, spawns a level 1 snowbear. The message when a player steps on the pad will be: 'Summon a level <current level> Snowbear.' For example, if a player has defeated a level 4 snowbear, the "<current level>" displayed on the message will be level 5. After each defeat, the level will increase by 1 the next time the player spawns a snowbear.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnowbear_Summoner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is the first machine that can summon a [bear](/wiki/Category:Bears "Category:Bears") manually by the player. 
    * However, this was the second feature mechanic to summon a mob manually. The first one being summoning [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge").
  * It is the first mechanic to summon a [mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") that has a defined level into a specific field, which is the Spider Field.
  * The cooldown of the Snowbear Summoner is not affected by owning a [Gifted Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") because the player had to summon it, unlike regular mobs who automatically respawn.
