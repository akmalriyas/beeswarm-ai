# Coconut Cave

[![The inside of the Coconut Cave.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fe/CocoCaveIntererior.png/revision/latest?cb=20220409185532) [![The inside of the Coconut Cave.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fe/CocoCaveIntererior.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/210?cb=20220409185532)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fe/CocoCaveIntererior.png/revision/latest?cb=20220409185532) [](/wiki/File:CocoCaveIntererior.png)

The inside of the Coconut Cave.

The **Coconut Cave** is a cave located next to the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field"). To access the cave, the player is required to defeat the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab") when it respawns out of the cave, If the player has not defeated the Coconut Crab yet and attempts to enter the cave (without actually standing in the field), they will be teleported back to the spawn place. There is also an invisible barrier on top of the Cave to prevent players from entering the cave when the Coconut Crab is not defeated yet, this is used to avoid lagging players from entering the cave without Coconut Crab being defeated. 

The interior contains the [Fastest Crab Slayers Leaderboard](/wiki/Fastest_Crab_Slayers_Leaderboard "Fastest Crab Slayers Leaderboard"), the [Coconut Dispenser](/wiki/Coconut_Dispenser "Coconut Dispenser"), and a [shop](/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops") where the player can buy the [Coconut Clogs](/wiki/Coconut_Clogs "Coconut Clogs"), the [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister "Coconut Canister") and the [Coconut Belt](/wiki/Coconut_Belt "Coconut Belt").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCoconut_Cave%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * If the player is in the Coconut Cave when the Coconut Crab respawns, they will be teleported the same way if they tried to bypass a [gate](/wiki/Category:Gates "Category:Gates") without meeting the requirements. The teleport area also encompasses the roof of the cave. This can be avoided by going into the hole above the Coconut Dispenser.
  * There is a hole above the Coconut Dispenser that leads to a tunnel containing 4 tokens: one [enzyme](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"), 5 [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), 5 [jelly beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans"), and a [Robo Pass](/wiki/Robo_Pass "Robo Pass").
  * This is the only shop that is guarded by a [mob](/wiki/Mob "Mob").
  * This shop, [Robo Bear’s Shop](/wiki/Robo_Bear%27s_Shop "Robo Bear's Shop"), [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent"), and [Dapper Bear’s Shop](/wiki/Dapper_Bear%E2%80%99s_Shop "Dapper Bear’s Shop") are the only shops that do not play the [vendor](/wiki/Music#Vendor "Music") soundtrack.
  * If the player looks through the cave with the [Porcelain Dipper](/wiki/Porcelain_Dipper "Porcelain Dipper") or through the ![Hydroponic Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Hydroponic Planter](/wiki/Hydroponic_Planter "Hydroponic Planter"), they can see the interior of the cave without having to go in.
