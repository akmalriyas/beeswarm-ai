# Petal Shop

## Petal Shop

[ ![Petal Shop new](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b1/Petal_Shop_new.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20260204015331) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b1/Petal_Shop_new.png/revision/latest?cb=20260204015331 "Petal Shop new") The interior of the Petal Shop.

### Location

In the cave near the Windy Bee Gate, under the Wind Shrine.

The **Petal Shop** is a [shop](/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops") located past the [Windy Bee Gate](/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate "Windy Bee Gate"). Inside, the player can craft the [Petal Wand](/wiki/Petal_Wand "Petal Wand"), the [Petal Belt](/wiki/Petal_Belt "Petal Belt") and the [Petal Planter](/wiki/Petal_Planter "Petal Planter"). The [All-Time Top White Collectors](/wiki/All-Time_Top_White_Collectors "All-Time Top White Collectors") Leaderboard and [Daily Top White Collectors](/wiki/Daily_Top_White_Collectors "Daily Top White Collectors") Leaderboard can also be found here. 

It is called the Petal Shop because of the giant petals draped across the back wall. In addition, each of its items require a [spirit petal](/wiki/Spirit_Petal "Spirit Petal") to [craft](/wiki/Category:Crafted "Category:Crafted") (except for the Petal Planter).

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPetal_Shop%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The wall behind the Petal Shop is semi-transparent and can be walked through. Behind it, there's a corridor which leads to a [star jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token that can be seen in the [Blue Maze](/wiki/Mazes#Blue_Maze "Mazes"), from where the jellybeans token is found. The token awards one star jelly. 
    * There used to be the [Mysterious Figure](/wiki/Mysterious_Figure "Mysterious Figure") in the place of the Star Jelly.
  * Occasionally, rain from [clouds](/wiki/Clouds "Clouds") in the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch") may be visible in the Petal Shop. 
    * The beam of a [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") can also be seen behind the semi-transparent wall.
