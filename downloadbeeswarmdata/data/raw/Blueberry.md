# Blueberry

Blueberry

[![Blueberry](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9e/Blueberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041412)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9e/Blueberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041412)

_"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."_

A **Blueberry** is a type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that can be fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to a [Bumble](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee"), [Cool](/wiki/Cool_Bee "Cool Bee"), [Bubble](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee"), [Frosty](/wiki/Frosty_Bee "Frosty Bee"), [Bucko](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee"), [Diamond](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee"), [Music](/wiki/Music_Bee "Music Bee"), [Tadpole](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), or [Buoyant](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee") Bee, the amount of [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") they gain is doubled. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed blueberries, though this only happens with a 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance for [Rare bees](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"), 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance for [Epic bees](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"), 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance for [Common](/wiki/Bees/Common "Bees/Common") and [Legendary bees](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary"), and 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance for [Mythic bees](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic") per blueberry. Feeding a [radioactive](/wiki/Radioactive "Radioactive") bee a blueberry has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) to [mutate](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation") a bee. 

## Ways to Obtain[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlueberry%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * From using the [Blueberry Dispenser](/wiki/Blueberry_Dispenser "Blueberry Dispenser") inside the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ"). The player will get one blueberry per blue bee in their hive per use.
  * As a reward from the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge").
  * Redeeming certain valid [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"): 
    * 1MLikes (Gives 1 blueberry + other stuff).
    * 15MMembers (Gives 15 blueberries + other stuff).
  * As a reward from [memory matches](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match").



### Drops[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlueberry%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Farming in the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), and the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). 
    * Harvesting [leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves") in these fields may also yield blueberries.
  * As a drop from [Rhino Beetles](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"), [Mantises](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis"), and [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle").
  * As a drop from a [Festive Gift](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens") or [Festive Mark](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Mark "Ability Tokens").
  * As a gift from your [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy").
  * As a reward for completely growing any [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts. 
    * Planting a sprout in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, or Stump Field yields 6x more blueberries than other fields.
  * Harvesting [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") (Higher chances in blue fields).
  * As a drop from [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom").
  * As a drop from planters in blue fields. 
    * Non-blue fields that contain a significant portion of blue flowers can drop Blueberries (Clover Field, Cactus Field, Mountain Top Field).
    * When in blue fields, ![Paper Planter](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/75/Paper_Planter.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230406145534)[Paper Planter](/wiki/Paper_Planter "Paper Planter") drops multiple Blueberry tokens in increments of 1, 3, 5, and 10.
