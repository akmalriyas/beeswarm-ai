The Moon Amulet icon.

The **Moon Amulet** is an [amulet](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet") created by spending 100 [Moon Charms](/wiki/Moon_Charm "Moon Charm") at the [Moon Amulet Generator](/wiki/Moon_Amulet_Generator "Moon Amulet Generator"). 

This amulet grants [buffs](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs "Buffs & Debuffs") to the owner and allows the player to walk on the moon-shaped platforms (located at the back of [Dapper Bear’s Shop](/wiki/Dapper_Bear%E2%80%99s_Shop "Dapper Bear’s Shop"), in the [Diamond Room](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses#Diamond_Room "Obstacle Courses"), and at the [30 Bee Zone Obby](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate")) at [night](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle "Day/Night Cycle"). 

The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the number of Moon Amulets the player has generated, up to 40 amulets, where its quality is maxed. 

When using the generator, a message will appear in the chat, saying:  ![🌙](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) The moon seems to grow brighter...  ![🌙](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)

If the player has maxed out their capacity buff, the message will instead say:  ![🌙](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) The moon seems happy!  ![🌙](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)
