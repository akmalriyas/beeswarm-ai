The **Blueberry Dispenser** is a [Dispenser](/wiki/Dispenser "Dispenser") located on the first floor of the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ"), next to the [Blue Teleporter](/wiki/Blue_Teleporter "Blue Teleporter"). When used, it grants the player [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens") x5, [blue boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens") x10, and 1 [blueberry](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry") for each [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") in the player's [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"). 

It has a cooldown of 4 hours. 

If the player does not have any blue bees in their hive, it will not grant any blueberries. 

The player must be in the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Club "Bee Swarm Simulator Club") in order to use this. 

## Honey and Blueberry reward amounts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlueberry_Dispenser%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

The amount of honey and blueberries given is based on the number of Blue bees the player has in their hive. More specifically, let _cnt_ be the number of Blue bees in the player's hive: 

  * The amount of honey the player receives is equal to  ⌊ c n t 1.5 + 0.5 ⌋ × 200 {\displaystyle \left\lfloor {cnt}^{1.5}+0.5\right\rfloor \times 200} ![{\\displaystyle \\left\\lfloor {cnt}^{1.5}+0.5\\right\\rfloor \\times 200}](https://services.fandom.com/mathoid-facade/v1/media/math/render/svg/d01bdb7260f069f8afb67fd55b9b811bc115a1e2), or 500 if the result is less than 500.
  * The amount of blueberries the player receives is equal to _cnt_.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlueberry_Dispenser%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Before the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"), the Blueberry Dispenser was in the [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee")'s spot. However, it was moved after said update to make room for the NPC.
  * The only time this dispenser appears in a quest is in [Science Bear's](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") _"The Power of Information"_.
  * This dispenser gives the same honey per bee as the [Strawberry Dispenser](/wiki/Strawberry_Dispenser "Strawberry Dispenser").
  * This is one of three dispensers that give [treats](/wiki/Treats "Treats") in the game with the other two being the [Treat Dispenser](/wiki/Treat_Dispenser "Treat Dispenser") and the Strawberry Dispenser.
  * This is one of four dispensers requiring the player to be part of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club with the others being the [Honey Dispenser](/wiki/Honey_Dispenser "Honey Dispenser"), the Treat Dispenser, and the Strawberry Dispenser.
