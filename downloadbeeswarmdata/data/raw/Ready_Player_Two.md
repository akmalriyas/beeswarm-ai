**Ready Player Two** was a Roblox event that began on November 23, 2020 and ended on May 23rd, 2024. The event spans across seven games, including [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"), where players must decipher each game's clues and obtain its badge along with the [How to Program BASIC](/wiki/How_to_Program_BASIC "How to Program BASIC") accessory to wear on their avatar.

Each code awards the player a [7-Pronged Cog](/wiki/7-Pronged_Cog "7-Pronged Cog"). They can be used to craft [Strange Goggles](/wiki/Strange_Goggles "Strange Goggles"). When equipped, the player needs to go to [The Computer](/wiki/The_Computer "The Computer"), where they can now interact with it. Upon use, the player will see [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee_\(NPC\) "Digital Bee \(NPC\)"). Digital Bee acts as an NPC and will begin citing the first puzzle. Upon completing each puzzle, the player is rewarded with various items, and can go back to The Computer to receive the next puzzle. The puzzle guides are listed below. 

When the third and final puzzle is done, interacting with The Computer will award the game's event item, badge, and a [Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly").
