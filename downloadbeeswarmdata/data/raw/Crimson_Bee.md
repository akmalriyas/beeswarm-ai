**Crimson Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Event Bee](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event"). It can be obtained by purchasing a [Crimson Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Event_Bee_Eggs "Egg") in the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") for ![Ticket](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)250 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"). 

Its abilities are enhanced when [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee") is also in the hive. 

Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, and the only ways to make it gifted are by feeding it a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") or ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear"). 

Crimson Bee likes the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field") and the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). It dislikes the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrimson_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 10 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 140 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 3 seconds.
  * +75% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), +30% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), +25% Convert Speed, +60 [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), 10% [Instant Conversion](/wiki/Instant_Conversion "Instant Conversion"), +6 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats"), 5% [Critical Chance](/wiki/Critical_Hits "Critical Hits").
  * 🌟 [Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +15% [Instant Red Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Instant_Red_Conversion "System Page").



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrimson_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Red Pulse]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Pulse "Ability Tokens")** Fires a pulse that hops between your red bees, collecting pollen around them (+10% per level). Power increases with each hop. If you own Cobalt Bee, fires a [Blue Pulse](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Pulse "Ability Tokens") as well. 
    * If Gifted, the pulse also causes bees to instantly convert pollen equal to 25% of the bee's Convert Amount (+25% per Field Boost stack).
  * **[[Red Bomb Sync]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb_Sync "Ability Tokens")** Grants 10% Red Pollen and Instant Red Conversion and allows [Red Bombs](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb "Ability Tokens") to collect from white [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") for 30 sec. If [Blue Bomb Sync](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb_Sync "Ability Tokens") is active, applies to blue flowers as well.

Crimson Bee has a base pollen collection of **10![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. That's equivalent to 2.5 ![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrimson_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The only difference between Crimson Bee and Cobalt Bee's [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") preferences is that the Crimson Bee likes the Rose Field, whereas Cobalt Bee prefers the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest").
  * This is one of the two [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") which have [Abilities](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens") that can trigger another bee's ability, the other being its counterpart, Cobalt Bee.
  * This is one of the four Event bees who have different color faces on the hive rather than the bee itself, the others being Cobalt Bee, [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee"), and [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"). 
    * This is the first bee that is both a red bee and an Event bee, with Festive Bee being the second.
  * When Crimson and Cobalt Bee are both in the same hive, the player gets the following bonuses from these ability tokens: 
    * Red pulse additionally fires a Blue Pulse to collect from blue bees.
    * If both red bomb sync and blue bomb sync are activated, red bombs can collect from blue and white flowers.
  * This bee, along with Cobalt Bee, are currently the only Event bees to cost 250 tickets. 
    * The [first edition](/wiki/First_Edition_Bee "First Edition Bee") [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee") and [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee") could once be purchased with 250 tickets as well.

[![Apples](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/99/Apples.png/revision/latest?cb=20200504054553) [![Apples](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/99/Apples.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200504054553)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/99/Apples.png/revision/latest?cb=20200504054553) [](/wiki/File:Apples.png)

  * The description of the Red Bomb Sync ability used to be spelled incorrectly as "apples" instead of "applies". 
    * [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") was aware of this and instead changed the "apples" to "🍎 s".
    * However, this has since been corrected.
  * This bee, Cobalt Bee, [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee"), [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee") and [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee "Shy Bee") are the only bees that create a trail.
  * The [Crimson Guard](/wiki/Crimson_Guard "Crimson Guard"), which can be purchased in the [Badge Bearer's Guild](/wiki/Badge_Bearer%27s_Guild "Badge Bearer's Guild"), has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Crimson Bee. 
    * The Crimson Guard has a [passive ability](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities") similar to Crimson Bee's red pulse ability.
  * This bee, [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee"), [Puppy Bee](/wiki/Puppy_Bee "Puppy Bee"), and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") are the only event bees that don't have an 'enhanced' ability if gifted.
