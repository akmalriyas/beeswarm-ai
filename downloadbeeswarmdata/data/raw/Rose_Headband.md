The **Rose Headband** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 13 [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [Basic](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"), [Rascal](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"), [Riley](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee"), [Diamond](/wiki/Diamond_Bee "Diamond Bee"), [Windy](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") and [Vicious](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees").
