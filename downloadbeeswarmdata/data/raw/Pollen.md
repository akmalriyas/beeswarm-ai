# Pollen

_Are you looking for[Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") or [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey")?_

**Pollen** is one of the main features in [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"). Pollen is collected in order to make [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"). It can be obtained by using [tools](/wiki/Tools "Tools") (via tapping or clicking) on [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"), from [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") collecting pollen and/or activating their [abilities](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), from some [passive abilities](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities"), from [meteors](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower"), and from [coconuts](/wiki/Coconut "Coconut"). Flowers can have different sizes and different colors. Some [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") require pollen to be collected from a specific flower color or field. There are many sources of pollen multipliers, including [amulets](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet"), [bags](/wiki/Bags "Bags"), [masks](/wiki/Masks "Masks"), [badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges"), [hive bonuses](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"), abilities, [field boosts](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"), [field winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind"), and certain passive abilities. 

Pollen is mainly used to convert it into [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") at the player's [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"), through [Instant Converters](/wiki/Instant_Converter "Instant Converter"), by using a [Micro-Converter](/wiki/Micro-Converter "Micro-Converter"), [Honeysuckle](/wiki/Honeysuckle "Honeysuckle"), etc. This is one of the most common ways to get honey. Pollen is also used as a counter to track how much a player has farmed from a [field](/wiki/Field "Field") in [badges](/wiki/Badges "Badges").

In order to get more pollen per collection, collect the biggest flowers on the field. The bigger the flower, the larger the pollen multiplier. There are 5 different flower sizes: single, double, triple, large, and star.

## Pollination[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPollen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_Main article:[Pollination](/wiki/Pollination "Pollination")_

When a flower is pollinated, it upgrades any kind of flower, with star flowers being the exception. Flowers can be pollinated by [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee"), The [Diamond Drain](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Diamond_Drain "Passive Abilities") Passive, bees with the [Poinsettia](/wiki/Poinsettia "Poinsettia") or [Lei](/wiki/Lei "Lei") beequip, and [Guiding Star](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Guiding_Star "Passive Abilities").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPollen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Single, double, and triple flowers are the only types of flowers that can naturally spawn in fields. Large and star flowers can only be spawned through pollination.
  * Before the [2020-04-06 update](/wiki/Updates#2020-04-06 "Updates"), the only stages of flowers were single, double, and large, with large flowers being the third level. This was changed to the current system, where the third tier was replaced with triple flowers, moving large to stage four.
  * In real life, nectar is used by bees to be turned into honey and not pollen.
