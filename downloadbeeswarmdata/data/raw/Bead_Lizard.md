A **Bead Lizard** is a [level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") 9 [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip"). It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [common](/wiki/Bees/Common "Bees/Common") or [rare](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare") [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees").
