# Swirled Wax

Swirled Wax

[![Swirled Wax](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/91/Swirled_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042825)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/91/Swirled_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042825)

_"A spiraled hunk of beeswax. Used to redetermine the stats of a Bee's Beequip."_

**Swirled Wax** is an [inventory item](/wiki/Category:Inventory "Category:Inventory") and a type of [wax](/wiki/Waxes "Waxes") that can be used on a [beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") to reroll its stats. Beequips with higher potential have a greater chance of having better stats when rerolled. Swirled wax has [Upgrade Value](/wiki/Beequip#Stats,_Waxes,_Chances "Beequip") of 0. 

When used on a beequip, it will change its stats. If the Beequip has waxes used on it, it will alter the enhancements as well. 

When a swirled wax is applied to a beequip, a message pops up saying: 

The Wax altered the Beequip's stats

Sometimes, the stats won't show any changes when you use a swirled wax. Most of the time, the bug can be fixed by rejoining the game.
