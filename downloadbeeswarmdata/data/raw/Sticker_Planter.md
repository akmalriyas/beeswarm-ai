The **Sticker Planter** is a single-use [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") added in the [2024-01-12 update](/wiki/Updates#2024-01-12 "Updates"). Alone, it grows in about 3 hours of playtime and stores 200,000 [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). A player can store up to 25 Sticker Planters at a time, but only 1 can be active. 

It does not have any growth nor pollen multipliers, and grants x2 [nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar"). 

Harvesting a fully grown Sticker Planter is guaranteed to spawn a [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom"). The spawned puffshroom is between levels 5-7, and has a: 

  * ~89.28% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
  * ~17.86% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
  * ~0.89% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom,
  * ~0.01% chance of being a Mythic Puffshroom.

## Ways To Obtain[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSticker_Planter%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * As a drop from [Sticker Sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout").
  * By purchasing it in the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop") for 100 Robux (requires having 20 bees to purchase, and has a limit of 10 per player.)
  *   * Completing [Sticker-Seeker](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine "Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine") quests has between a 2% and 5% chance of awarding a Sticker Planter based on if you haven't completed any Sticker-Seeker quests or if you have completed 500 or more respectively. 
    * Completing Quests 11, 33, 55, 66, 77, 99, 111, 133, 144, 155, 166, 199, 244, 255, 266, 277, or 411 each guarantee a Sticker Planter.
    * Completing Quest 222 and 422 guarantees 2 Sticker Planters.
    * Completing Quest 333 guarantees 3 Sticker Planters.
    * Completing Quest 444 guarantees 4 Sticker Planters.
    * Completing Quest 555 guarantees 5 Sticker Planters.
  * Completing [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear")'s "Stocking Up On Stickers" quest rewards a Sticker Planter and other items.
  * Purchasing the Saint Puff's Pack from [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog") for ![Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)50 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") gives a Sticker Planter and other items.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSticker_Planter%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The Sticker Planter continuously alternates between different colors, which can be tuned down in the [Settings](/wiki/Settings "Settings").
  * Out of the four single-use planters, the Sticker Planter is the only one of them to have a cap of 25.
  * This, the ![Ticket Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Ticket Planter](/wiki/Ticket_Planter "Ticket Planter") and the![Paper Planter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Paper Planter](/wiki/Paper_Planter "Paper Planter") share the same base models. 
    * They also share the same outline for their decals.
  * It is tied with the Ticket Planter for the second-best Nectar multiplier in the game, behind the [Festive Planter](/wiki/Festive_Planter "Festive Planter") at x3.
  * It is one of four single-use planters in the game, the others being the Festive Planter, the Ticket Planter and the Paper Planter.
  * This and the Ticket Planter are the only planters that will always spawn a [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom") at full growth. 
    * Additionally, this is the only planter to always spawn at least a Rare Puffshroom.
