## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBees%2FCommon%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The Common rarity and the [Event](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") rarity are the only rarities of bees that can't be obtained by using a normal ![Royal Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") or ![Star Jelly](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly").
  * Excluding [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") forms, this and [Rare bees](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare") are the only rarity of bees that has a bee that can't spawn [ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"). The rare bee that can't spawn ability tokens is [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee").
  * The [Hive Slot](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot") color for a **Common Bee** is brown, which may represent bronze.
  * The Common rarity is the rarity with the least amount of bee types, having only 1 bee type.
  * This rarity has only three ways of getting a bee of this rarity which are [Basic Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Basic_Egg "Egg"), [Star Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Star_Egg "Egg"), and the [Basic Bee Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Specific_Bee_Jelly_Variants "Royal Jelly").
