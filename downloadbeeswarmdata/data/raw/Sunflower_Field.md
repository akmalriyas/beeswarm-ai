The **Sunflower Field** is one of the five [starting](/wiki/Starter_Zone "Starter Zone") [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") that any player can visit upon starting the game. There are no [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") that protect this [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields"). 

This field can spawn [sunflower seed](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"), [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [red boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), [blue boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), [oil](/wiki/Oil "Oil"), and [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean") tokens when gathering [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen").

This [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") grants Satisfying [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is planted here. 

[Bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that like the Sunflower Field are [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"), [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"), [Hasty Bee](/wiki/Hasty_Bee "Hasty Bee"), and [Exhausted Bee](/wiki/Exhausted_Bee "Exhausted Bee"). The only bee that dislikes the Sunflower Field is [Looker Bee](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee"). 

This is a 20×33 field, containing 660 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") are white, red, and blue, in sizes of single and double.

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSunflower_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Field Booster](/wiki/Field_Booster "Field Booster") may give a Sunflower [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +300% Sunflower Field pollen for 15 minutes.
  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") will add 100% Sunflower Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes, if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% Sunflower Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Sunflower [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of 20% instant Sunflower Field conversion and give +5% instant Sunflower Field conversion and +25% Sunflower Field pollen for each stack. Sunflower Field Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.
  * It can be boosted by the [Shell Amulet](/wiki/Shell_Amulet "Shell Amulet"). If it has a Sunflower Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +27% depending on the amulet rarity.
  * Giving a Present to Black Bear grants 3x Sunflower Field Boost + other stuff.
  * Having an active [Autumn Sunhat](/wiki/Autumn_Sunhat "Autumn Sunhat") [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") with a Sunflower Field Pollen Hive Bonus (+18% to +24% base, up to +44% with Waxing)
  * Having an active [Lei](/wiki/Lei "Lei") [Beequip](/wiki/Beequip "Beequip") with a Sunflower Field Pollen Hive Bonus (+3% to +5% base, not guaranteed, up to +25% with Waxing)

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSunflower_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This is the only [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") in the game where the player can find [sunflower seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed") that spawn naturally, without the need of [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout").
  * This is the first field [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear") assigns a [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests") to.
  * The [Sunflower badges](/wiki/Badges#Sunflower_Badge "Badges") and the [Pumpkin badges](/wiki/Badges#Pumpkin_Badge "Badges") are used 3 times for chances in [Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match"), the most for any field badges.
  * This was the second-biggest field in the game, the biggest one being the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"). After the [2018-07-11 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-07-11 "Updates"), this isn't the case anymore. 
    * Similar to why the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field") was shrunk, this field was decreased in size to make room for [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear") and the [Treat Shop](/wiki/Treat_Shop "Treat Shop"), which can be found at the end of the field.
  * This field along with the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch") and the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch") are the only fields where an object may come to life and shoot [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles").
  * The fence bordering the Sunflower Field and the [hives](/wiki/Hive "Hive") was temporarily removed after Beesmas Winter 2024. It was soon added back in [the following update](/wiki/Updates#2025-06-30 "Updates").
