The **Pepper Patch** is a [field](/wiki/Field "Field") that was added in the [September 28, 2019 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-9-28 "Updates"), located past the [Windy Bee Gate](/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate "Windy Bee Gate"), to the right of the [Extreme Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Extreme_Memory_Match "Memory Match") and to the left of the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine"). There are no [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") that protect this field. 

This is a 21x27 field, containing 567 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are red and white, and the only natural size is triple.

Triple | 85  | 15%  | 482  | 85%  | 0  | 0%  | **567** | **100%**  
Total  | **85** | **15%** | **482** | **85%** | **0** | **0%** | **567** | **100%**  
  
**_Note:_ flower counts within the table may vary due to rounding. Additionally, in game, all flowers are randomly generated and may not precisely align with the percentages.**

This field can spawn [strawberry](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"), [bitterberry](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"), [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), [red extract](/wiki/Red_Extract "Red Extract"), [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") and [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean") tokens when gathering [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"). The only [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") that likes the Pepper Patch is [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee"). Bees that dislike the Pepper Patch are [Looker Bee](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee"), [Frosty Bee](/wiki/Frosty_Bee "Frosty Bee"), [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee") and [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee"). 

This field grants Invigorating [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is planted here. 

A level 9-12 [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") has a chance to spawn here. 

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPepper_Patch%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Red Field Booster](/wiki/Red_Field_Booster "Red Field Booster") may give a Pepper [Patch Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost") (only if the player has 35+ bees). When it does, it will give +100% Pepper Patch pollen for 15 minutes.
  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") will add 100% Pepper Patch pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.

[![The Pepper Patch boost icon.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/42/Pepper_Patch_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230409172456) [![The Pepper Patch boost icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/42/Pepper_Patch_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230409172456)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/42/Pepper_Patch_Icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230409172456)

The Pepper Patch boost icon.

  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% - +300% Pepper Patch pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Pepper Patch [Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Pepper Patch conversion and give +5% instant Pepper Patch conversion and +10% Pepper Patch pollen for each stack. Pepper Patch Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.



### The methods below are no longer possible.[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPepper_Patch%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Redeeming certain expired codes: 
    * Sriracha (Gave Pepper Patch Boost x2 and Pepper Patch Capacity x2 + other stuff).
    * 10mMembers (Gives Pepper Patch Code Buff + other stuff).
    * DemiDecade (Gives Pepper Patch Boost x4 + Pepper Patch Winds x15 + other stuff).
    * Gumaden10T (Gave Pepper Patch Boost x1 and Pepper Patch Winds x3 + other stuff).
    * RedMarket (Gave Pepper Patch Boost x1, Pepper Patch Market Boost, and Pepper Patch Capacity x1).
    * FrozenBugReboot (Gave Pepper Patch Boost x4 and Pepper Patch Code Buff + other stuff).
  * Purchasing a Pepper Patch Market Boost from the [Boost Market](/wiki/Boost_Market "Boost Market") or certain codes gave x1.25 Pepper Patch Pollen along with 1.5x [Pepper Patch Capacity](/wiki/Field_Capacity "Field Capacity"), x1.25 [Flame Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Pollen_From_Flames "System Page"), and x1.25 [Convert Rate At Hive](/wiki/System_Page#Hive_Convert_Rate "System Page").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPepper_Patch%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * This, the Coconut Field, the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"), the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field") and the [Hub Field](/wiki/Hub_Field "Hub Field") are currently the only fields released in updates.
  * This, the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), Stump Field, and Coconut Field are the only fields to solely have natural triple flowers.
  * The Pepper Patch is the only field past a bee gate with nothing guarding it.
  * This and the Coconut Field are the only fields where [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") cannot appear naturally or be planted with the [Special Sprout Summoner](/wiki/Special_Sprout_Summoner "Special Sprout Summoner").
  * The [Pepper Badge](/wiki/Badges#Pepper_Badge "Badges"), [Coconut Badge](/wiki/Badges#Coconut_Badge "Badges"), [Mountain Top Badge](/wiki/Badges#Mountain_Top_Badges "Badges") and the [Hive Hub Badge](/wiki/Badges#Hive_Hub_Badges "Badges") require more pollen collected than the other field badges.
  * The Pepper Patch used to cover the entire platform it is placed on. Now, the field has shrunk a little and the area was amplified. As a result, it does not cover the edges of the platform anymore. 
    * The peppers used as decoration were also reorganized.
  * This is the only red field that gives [Invigorating Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar").
  * There used to be a ![Marshmallow Bee](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Marshmallow Bee](/wiki/Marshmallow_Bee "Marshmallow Bee") token next to this field.
