# Gummy Cannon

The **Gummy Cannon** is [Gummy Bear's](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") personal cannon that he used to fire [gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") onto [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"). It was brought to the mountain in the [2018-05-26](/wiki/Updates#2018-05-26 "Updates") update and was removed alongside Gummy Bear at a later date. The Gummy Cannon's color scheme is identical to the color of [goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo"), gumdrops, [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee"), and Gummy Bear, with a teal base and a pink barrel. It was found next to Gummy Bear and Gummy Bee. 

When Gummy Bear launched the cannon, all players in the server would get a notification that said:  Sights Set on the [Field Name]!

Gumdrops would be scattered all over the selected field until the field changed. 

Starting in Beesmas 2020 and returning each Beesmas after, it is summoned by using the [Gummy Beacon](/wiki/Gummy_Beacon "Gummy Beacon") to summon a [Gummy Siege](/wiki/Gummy_Siege "Gummy Siege") after completing Gummy Bear's [Beesmas Quest](/wiki/Gummy_Bear#2021_Beesmas_Quest_-_Gummy_Beacon "Gummy Bear"). Each time gumdrops are scattered on a field, a gumdrops token appear in its place.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGummy_Cannon%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * The Gummy Cannon is the only cannon that wasn't used for player [transportation](/wiki/Category:Transport "Category:Transport"). It is only used for decoration. 
    * It is also the first and only cannon to launch [items](/wiki/Items "Items").
  * When the Gummy Cannon launches gumdrops, the animation of the gumdrops falling onto the selected field is the same one as the player using gumdrops in the field.
  * The Gummy Cannon is not completely accurate; the gumdrops it launched could miss the fields it aims at, and possibly even splatter goo into nearby fields.
  * This is the only cannon that was removed in-game and returned.
  * The cannon shoots 5-7 gumdrops before aiming at other fields.
  * There used to be a gumdrop token behind the cannon. 
    * After the [event](/wiki/Category:Gummy_Invasion "Category:Gummy Invasion") ended, the gumdrop token was covered with a green path, which made it more difficult to get, although it is possible to get it if the player moves along the edge.
