The **Egg Hunt 2019** was a Roblox event that spanned across 42 games, including [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"), all connected to one central hub. In each game, players had to complete a certain task/quest in order to obtain an egg, which they could then wear on their avatar. In doing so, they could unlock more areas in the Hub World to gain powers, which they could use to defeat the final boss. More information can be found [here](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2019:_Scrambled_in_Time "w:c:roblox:Egg Hunt 2019: Scrambled in Time"). 

To get the egg from Bee Swarm Simulator, players needed to interact with the [Egg Hunt Info](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_Info "Egg Hunt Info") near the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate") to receive the quest. Then, three [marshmallow bees](/wiki/Marshmallow_Bee "Marshmallow Bee") are needed to be obtained. When the player had obtained three Marshmallow Bees, they would have to return to Egg Hunt Info for a few special rewards along with the [Flight of the Bumble Egg](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Bumble_Egg "Flight of the Bumble Egg"), which players can wear on their Roblox avatar as a hat.
