# Instant Conversion

**Instant Conversion** is a [mechanic](/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") that instantly converts some of the player's collected [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") into [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"). The amount of converted pollen depends on how much instant conversion (%) the player has, with support from the respective type of instant conversion (white, red, and blue instant conversion, etc.). This effectively makes the player's [bag](/wiki/Bags "Bags") bigger, in the sense that the player can collect more pollen than the bag would normally hold. The player's [Honey Per Pollen](/wiki/Honey_Per_Pollen "Honey Per Pollen") stat boosts the amount of honey received from instant conversion. There are also color variants for each [flower](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") color ([blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue"), [red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red"), [white](/wiki/Category:Colorless "Category:Colorless")) that only instantly convert pollen of their respective colors. 

## Instant Conversion Variants[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInstant_Conversion%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Instant Conversion has **8 variants** : 

  * Flame Conversion
  * Field Conversion (e.g. [field winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind"))
  * Colored Conversion
  * Goo Conversion
  * Bomb Conversion
  * Unique Instant Conversion
  * Instant Tool Conversion
  * Bee Gather Conversion



### Goo Conversion[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInstant_Conversion%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_Goo Conversion_ converts a **flat amount of pollen** equal to a percentage of the [Goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo") collected from a flower. 

### Unique Instant Conversion[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInstant_Conversion%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

_Unique Instant Conversion_ is applied **independently** of all other Instant Conversion types. 

For example, if you have **two different sources of 50% Unique Instant Conversion** , each source is applied separately. This results in an effective **75% Instant Conversion** , not 100%. 

## Conversion Order[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInstant_Conversion%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Instant Conversion is applied in the following order:[1]

  1. Colored Conversion
  2. Field Conversion
  3. Regular Instant Conversion
  4. Goo Conversion



It is unknown where Unique Instant Conversion and Ability-specific Instant Conversion is placed in this order. 

For example, if a player has **50%** of **four** different conversion variants and collects **10,000** red pollen, **50%** of the pollen is converted, then **50%** of the remaining amount, and so on for each variant.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInstant_Conversion%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

    * The [Gummy Morph](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Morph "Passive Abilities") also grants 200% Goo Conversion while covering the whole field in goo.
    * The [Ant Challenge Buff](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens "Buffs & Debuffs") grants 100% instant [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field") Conversion.
    * Pollen collected from the [Hub Field](/wiki/Hub_Field "Hub Field") is instantly converted.
  * [Super-Crits](/wiki/Critical_Hits#Super-Crit "Critical Hits") and [Golden Bubbles](/wiki/Bubble#Golden_Bubbles "Bubble") instantly convert all pollen they collect.
  * Tadpole Bee used to give 25% Instant Bubble Conversion before the 2024-05-23 update.
