The **Mushroom Field** is one of the five [starting](/wiki/Starter_Zone "Starter Zone") [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields") any player is able to visit upon starting the game. It is the first [red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") field accessible to players in the game. The field is protected by a level 1 [ladybug](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug"). 

This field can spawn [treat](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), [strawberry](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry"), [bitterberry](/wiki/Bitterberry "Bitterberry"), [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), [red boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), [royal jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"), [magic bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean"), and [red extract](/wiki/Red_Extract "Red Extract") tokens when collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen").

Bees that like the Mushroom Field include [Rad Bee](/wiki/Rad_Bee "Rad Bee"), [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"), [Fire Bee](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee"), [Riley Bee](/wiki/Riley_Bee "Riley Bee"), [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"), [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee"), and [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee"). Bees that dislike the Mushroom Field include [Bumble Bee](/wiki/Bumble_Bee "Bumble Bee"), [Frosty Bee](/wiki/Frosty_Bee "Frosty Bee"), [Shocked Bee](/wiki/Shocked_Bee "Shocked Bee"), and [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee"). 

This field gives Motivating [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") when a [planter](/wiki/Planter "Planter") is planted here. 

This is a 32x23 field made up of 736 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The flowers are red and white, in sizes of single and double.

## Obtaining Boosts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMushroom_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Using the [Red Field Booster](/wiki/Red_Field_Booster "Red Field Booster") may give a Mushroom [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"). When it does, it will give +300% Mushroom Field pollen for 15 minutes.
  * Using [glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") in the field will add 100% Mushroom Field pollen onto the current boost if the boost isn't already maxed out.
  * Using [field dice](/wiki/Field_Dice "Field Dice"), [smooth dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice "Smooth Dice"), or [loaded dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice "Loaded Dice") may add +100% - +300% Mushroom Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out and based on the type used.
  * Mushroom [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind "Field Wind") can be obtained through the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") or [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"). They have a base of +20% instant Mushroom Field conversion and give +5% instant Mushroom Field conversion and +25% Mushroom Field pollen for each stack. Mushroom Field Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.
  * It can be boosted by the [King Beetle Amulet](/wiki/King_Beetle_Amulet "King Beetle Amulet"). If it has a Mushroom Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +100%.

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMushroom_Field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * There is a small [parkour course](/wiki/Obstacle_Courses#Mushroom_Field_Obby "Obstacle Courses") in the Mushroom Field that can be done to obtain a royal jelly.
  * The [Bubble Wand](/wiki/Bubble_Wand "Bubble Wand") model on top of the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ") can occasionally grow a face and blow [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") onto this field, along with the [Blue Flower](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"), [Clover](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), and [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field").
  * [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") is found on the tallest mushroom, along with the [Top Stick Bug Fighters](/wiki/Top_Stick_Bug_Fighters "Top Stick Bug Fighters") leaderboard. This is where the player can start the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge") if they have given Stick Bug a [translator](/wiki/Translator "Translator").
  * After the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates") was released, Stick Bug would sometimes fall off the tallest mushroom and land in or near the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field"). This was a visual [glitch](/wiki/Glitches#Onett/Stick_Bug/Mondo_Chick_Falling "Glitches") and didn't affect gameplay. This glitch was patched a few days after the update.
  * This is one of the two fields in the Starter Zone with a Ladybug in it, the other being the Clover Field.
  * The [Yellow Walking Wiggly Person sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") cannot spawn in this field.
