**[The Games](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Games "w:c:Roblox:The Games")** was a Roblox event that spanned across multiple games. In each participating game, players could earn 8 [badges](/wiki/Badges#Unobtainable "Badges") to contribute to overarching prizes in the main event hub. 

In Bee Swarm Simulator, 5 of the 8 badges were awarded for collecting Shines, and 3 of the badges were awarded Silver for completing quests.

_Main article:[Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear")_

Sun Bear arrived at the mountain and brought ⛳ [golfing](/wiki/Golf "Golf") to the mountain. He had 3 quests required for the event, each one rewarding a Silver, which contributes to prizes in The Games event hub. 

The 5 Shines were located in the following locations: 

  * On top of Sun Bear's RV
  * Inside the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent").
  * Inside the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop").
  * Next to the flower in the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field").
  * To the right of the [Beesmas Tree](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree "Beesmas Tree").
