**[The Classic](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Classic "w:c:roblox:The Classic")** was a Roblox event that spanned across multiple games. In each game, players had to earn 15 badges to contribute to overarching prizes in the main event hub. 10 of the 15 badges were awarded for collecting Tix (usually hidden around the map like in BSS) and 5 of the badges awarded Tokens for completing quests or achieving certain milestones.

_Main article:[Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge")_

The Retro Swarm Challenge is a round-based minigame where the main objective is to defend the hives from [Slimes](/wiki/Slime "Slime") and [Zombies](/wiki/Zombie "Zombie"). In order to be successful, the player must save up Bricks to buy bees and weapons. The challenge subplace hosts 4 exclusive fields (Red, White, Blue and Mixed Brick Fields).
