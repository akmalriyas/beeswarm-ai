# Leaderboards

_This page is for the in-game leaderboards. For this wiki's Achievement Leaderboard, see the[Achievement](/wiki/Special:Leaderboard "Special:Leaderboard") page._

**Leaderboards** are a feature within the game. Each leaderboard displays the ranks of the top 100 players, showing ten ranks at a time. Leaderboards are usually not in real-time but update very often. Upon standing on the white pad in front of a leaderboard, it will show a message stating the player's score for the corresponding leaderboard. 

Out of the 64 total leaderboards, 18 are able to be viewed at various locations around the map, and the rest are available to view in the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page"). There are 6 leaderboards that have been removed. 

On daily and monthly leaderboards, players are able to gain rewards by placing high enough in one. The rewards can be [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), consumable [items](/wiki/Items "Items") or [stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker").

## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeaderboards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * All leaderboards that reset on a daily basis reset at 12:00am GMT-5.
  * [The Highest Snowbear Level leaderboard](/wiki/Highest_Snowbear_Level "Highest Snowbear Level") was added on Christmas Day 2020 with the Beesmas update. This was removed 1 week after Beesmas 2020 ended.
  * The [Top Brown Bear Helpers](/wiki/Top_Brown_Bear_Helpers "Top Brown Bear Helpers") leaderboard is the only leaderboard that does not show the player's current amount of the stated objective. 
    * The player can still view their score either in the leaderboard menu in the System Page or by speaking to [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear") when claiming or turning in a quest.
    * The [Most Commando Captures Leaderboard](/wiki/Most_Commando_Captures_Leaderboard "Most Commando Captures Leaderboard") also shared this property but has since been removed from the game entirely.
  * On 2022-03-17, [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") abbreviated the amount of honey for the Daily Top Honeymakers and the All-Time Top Honeymakers leaderboard due to players having massive amounts of honey.
