# Machines

This category houses all topics about the **machines** in the game. The main purpose for machines is to give the player various [consumables](/wiki/Category:Consumables "Category:Consumables"), boosts, transport the player quickly to different parts of the map, summon events, or generate different types of [amulets](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet"). This also houses the sub-category of [dispensers](/wiki/Category:Dispenser "Category:Dispenser").
