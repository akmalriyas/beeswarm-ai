A **Mega Mechsquito** is a [mob](/wiki/Mob "Mob") that appears during [Robo Bear's Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"). 

This is the fourth and last unique mob type a player will encounter in Robo Bear's Challenge, first appearing at Round 15. 

Mega Mechsquitos behave similarly to [Mechsquitos](/wiki/Mechsquito "Mechsquito"). Flies away from the player when they are not attacking and staying in position while attacking the player from a distance. Unlike the Mechsquitos, they try to get away from the player farther than Mechsquitos and consecutively shoot at the player with 6 small exploding projectiles in a short amount of time, inflicting [Mechsquito Toxin](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#Debuffs "Buffs & Debuffs") debuff upon hitting the player. 

A Mega Mechsquito's level can range from 13-22 depending on what round of Robo Bear's Challenge the player is on. 

In Round 15 of the Robo Bear Challenge, the player has to defeat 10 Mega Mechsquitos and collect 2,000,000,000 pollen of a certain color to complete it.

## Strategies[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMega_Mechsquito%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Mega Mechsquitos aim for where the player is, not where the player will be. Because of this, moving around in circles will often result in Mega Mechsquitos missing their attack.
  * Stand near the Mega Mechsquito, this will allow your bees to lock on to the Mechsquito and quickly deal damage.
  * [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") provide excellent crowd control, especially since Mega Mechsquitos often come in pairs or packs. [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") can also stun many Mega Mechsquitos at once and allow them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mind_Hack "Ability Tokens").
