**Precise Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Mythic bee](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic"). 

Precise Bee's favorite [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") is ![Sunflower Seed](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed"). 

Precise Bee likes the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field") and [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"). It dislikes the [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field") and [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest"). 

## Stats[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrecise_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Collects 20 [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
  * Makes 80 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds.
  * +7 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack "Stats"), -20% Movespeed, +10 [Gather Amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats"), +100% [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), +5% [Critical Chance](/wiki/Critical_Hits "Critical Hits"), +3% Super-Crit Chance.
  * 🌟[Gifted Hive Bonus](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"): +3% Super-Crit Chance.



### Abilities[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrecise_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * **[[Target Practice]](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Target_Practice "Ability Tokens")** Causes the bee to fly into the air and project 3 targets on the field for a few seconds. Running on a target activates it. 
    * When all 3 targets are activated, this collects all ability tokens and grants a stack of [Precision](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens "Buffs & Debuffs"), which grants +2% Super-Crit Chance for 60s and stacks up to 10 times. Super-Crits can only occur for Critical hits and gain x2 power and 100% [Instant Conversion](/wiki/Instant_Conversion "Instant Conversion").
    * After a few seconds, any target activated will generate a [Focus](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Focus "Ability Tokens") token and be shot, collecting pollen equal to double this bee's attack (+10% per [Level](/wiki/Bond "Bond")) from 29 [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). With Flame Heat, the shots gain up to x4 Pollen and up to 50% instant conversion. Any unactivated target spawns a [Red Boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens") token instead.
    * If the player is shot while standing on a target, this collects all ability tokens and consumes your [Flame Heat](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Flame?so=search#Flame_Heat) to instantly convert Pollen in your bag, with half turning into Honey Tokens. The amount converted is equal to 50% of your Convert Total + 10x this bee's convert amount and scales up to x10 with the Flame Heat consumed and up to x3 with your current field's boost stacks.
    * If Gifted: When only the farthest target is activated, it will summon a Precise Mark which grants +7% [Critical Chance](/wiki/System_Page#Critical_Chance "System Page") and Super-Crit Chance and stacks up to 3 times.
    * If the player collects a Target Practice token that is outside the field, they will immediately be granted either the Precision or the Focus buff. The chances of getting Precision buff are 20%, and 80% for Focus buff.
  * **[[Passive: Sniper]](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Sniper "Passive Abilities")** When fighting mobs, this bee uses slow, long ranged shots that are more accurate and deal x2 damage. The accuracy is calculated as if the bee was 1 level higher (2 if gifted), and it can never be below 5%. If the shot is a [Critical Hit](/wiki/Critical_Hits "Critical Hits"), the reload time after shots is reduced by 50%. If it's a Super-Crit, it's reduced by 75%.

Precise Bee has a base pollen collection of **20![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)** in **4 seconds**. That's equivalent to 5 ![Red Boost Token](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) per second. ## Trivia[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fbee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrecise_Bee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

  * Excluding Event bees, this bee, [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee") and [Fire Bee](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee") are the only red bees currently in the game whose favorite treat is not strawberries. 
    * This is the only red Mythic Bee who doesn’t like strawberries.
  * The Precise Bee is the only Mythic Bee that does not have a [gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") ability.
  * Precise Bee's attack is based on a long-ranged weapon with a sharp scope, such as a sniper.
  * This is the only bee that can attack [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") from outside the standard bee range, and likewise the only bee that shoots projectiles towards hostile mobs to deal damage.
  * This bee's attack changes appearance when landing a Critical Hit or Super-Crit. It fires a bigger projectile when landing a Critical Hit, and fires a purple projectile when landing a Super-Crit. This makes Precise Bee the only bee to have a standard attack that changes depending on whether a Critical hit (Or Super-Crit) are rolled.
  * This bee, [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"), and [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee "Fuzzy Bee") are the only mythic bees to be added since the release of the original three Mythic bees, which are [Vector Bee](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee"), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee") and [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee"). 
    * Precise Bee and Buoyant Bee were both added in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates").
  * This bee, Vector Bee, Spicy Bee and Buoyant Bee all have glowing parts when gifted. 
    * This bee is the only bee that has a part of their body that glows even if it is not gifted.
  * This, along with Tabby Bee, are the only bees that have an ability that grants Super-Critical chance.
  * This bee is the only bee to always hit a level 26 mob (27 if gifted) when it is level 25. Its passive guarantees that it never misses against mobs one level higher (two if gifted.)
  * Unlike Fuzz Bombs and Bubbles, players cannot activate each other's targets. 
    * The Precise Bee target practice of other players is seen as gray to prevent confusion on which one is the player's.
    * If the "Hide Other Bees" option were activated, other player's targets will be invisible instead.
  * This bee, [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee "Cobalt Bee"), [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee "Crimson Bee"), [Photon Bee](/wiki/Photon_Bee "Photon Bee"), [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee"), and [Ninja Bee](/wiki/Ninja_Bee "Ninja Bee") are the only bees that have a trail.
  * There used to be a [glitch](/wiki/Glitches "Glitches") with Gifted Precise Bee's Precise Mark, that even after the farthest target is activated, it was supposed to summon the mark, but it often wouldn't spawn. 
    * Another glitch can occur where when a target is hit, it does not function which commonly occur due to server lag.
    * After giving [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee") a ![Present](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Presents](/wiki/Present "Present") during the Beesmas 2022 Event, [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee") explains how people would die for a Precise Mark, possibly referencing this specific bug.
  * Precise Bee's attack can still be seen by other Players even if they have their Hide Other Bees option on.
  * Factoring in the passive and the attack, this bee has the highest attack damage, only surpassed by [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") when the Gummy Bear Morph ability is active.
  * Although spawning Precise Marks is considered a mark ability, it is not affected by Mark Ability Pollen multipliers.
  * Target Practice does not receive the 10% pollen buff per level.
