# Zombie

The **Zombie** is a mob exclusive to the Retro Swarm Challenge. It resembles a Slime but is generally easier to manage in combat.

## Behavior

Zombies move in a straight line toward the hives. Unlike Slimes, Zombies prioritize chasing the first nearby player to enter their aggression zone and will continue pursuing that player until they are defeated or the player moves out of range.

## Combat Tips and Strategy

Zombies are relatively straightforward to handle. Because they move in a straight line but can be distracted by nearby players, their threat level is significantly reduced if you manage the encounter correctly.

**General Strategies:**
*   **Crowd Control:** Since Zombies often spawn in hordes, using crowd-controlling bees like the [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") and [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") is highly recommended. Abilities such as Mind Hack and Impale are especially useful during later rounds when Zombie health increases drastically.
*   **Damage Warning:** Zombies deal damage simply by making contact. Recklessness can lead to quick death from a horde, so maintain caution. Unlike Slimes, however, Zombies do not increase in size with level, which makes them less dangerous upon impact.

**Equipment and Progression:**
*   **Weapon Upgrades:** Upgrade from the normal sword to the Firebrand as soon as possible for a massive damage boost, allowing you to easily kill low-to-mid-level Zombies before they can inflict significant damage. The Illumina offers even greater help, though it may become less relevant by rounds 20 or 21.
*   **Late Game:** Swords become increasingly ineffective around round 20/21 as Zombie health rises and spawn numbers increase. At this point, purchasing bees that match the Zombies' level becomes advisable, although this is often too costly for early to mid-game players.

**Utility Tools:**
*   **Trowel:** The Trowel can temporarily block Zombies from approaching the hives. This tool is most useful when dealing with Slimes as well, allowing you to prioritize steering Slimes toward the player (a maneuver not possible with Zombies).
*   **Passive Abilities:** [Star Saw](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Amulet_Passives "Passive Abilities") can effectively target and manage hordes of Zombies.
