# Lei

![Lei](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Lei.png/revision/latest?cb=20230418235916)

**Level:** 14
**Color:** Any
**Limit:** 1
**Description:** "Threaded petals to drape around your décolletage."
**Type:** Beesmas

A **Lei** is a Level 14 Beequip. It has an equip limit of one and no color requirement. It can be equipped to Basic, Looker, Stubborn, Hasty, and Exhausted Bees.
