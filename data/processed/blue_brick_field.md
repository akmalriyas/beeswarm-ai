# Blue Brick Field

[![Blue Brick Field](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/03/Blue_Brick_Field.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/275?cb=20250724191205)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/03/Blue_Brick_Field.png/revision/latest?cb=20250724191205)

## Field Statistics
*   **Color:** Blue
*   **Bee Prerequisite:** 10 bees
*   **Dimensions:** 19 × 19 flowers
*   **Total Flower Count:** 361
*   **Bee Preferences:** None (Likes: None, Dislikes: None)

The **Blue Brick Field** is one of the four fields located in the Retro Swarm Challenge. It features a 19x19 grid containing 361 flowers, which are white and blue, appearing in single, double, or triple sizes. This field can produce brick tokens while collecting pollen.

### Flower Distribution
| Size | White Flowers | Red Flowers | Blue Flowers | Total Count | Percentage (Total) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Single | 14 | 3.75% | 0 | 90 | 25% |
| Double | 27 | 7.5% | 0 | 181 | 50% |
| Triple | 14 | 3.75% | 0 | 90 | 25% |
| **Total** | **54** | **15%** | **0** | **361** | **100%** |

*Note: Flower counts within the table may vary due to rounding. In-game, all flowers are randomly generated and may not precisely align with these percentages.*

## Mechanics
### Obtaining Boosts
Using [Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") in the Blue Brick Field grants a 100% Blue Brick Field pollen boost for 15 minutes.

### Trivia
*   The Blue Brick Field, along with three other fields in the Retro Swarm Challenge, is one of the only fields without an associated field stamp.
*   All these challenge fields borrow the Sunflower Field's boost icon.
