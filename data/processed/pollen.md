# Pollen

Pollen is a core resource in Bee Swarm Simulator, primarily collected to be converted into Honey. It can be obtained through several methods:
*   **Collection:** Using tools (tapping or clicking) on Flowers, or being gathered by Bees while they collect pollen and/or activate their Abilities.
*   **Passive Sources:** Certain Passive Abilities provide pollen.
*   **Events/Items:** Meteors (Mythic Meteor Shower) and Coconuts are sources of pollen.

Flowers come in various sizes and colors. Some Quests require players to gather pollen from specific flower colors or fields.

### Pollen Multipliers
Pollen collection can be significantly multiplied using various game mechanics, including:
*   Amulets
*   Bags
*   Masks
*   Badges
*   Hive Bonuses (Gifted Bee)
*   Field Boosts and Field Winds
*   Specific Passive Abilities

### Usage of Pollen
Pollen is mainly used to convert it into Honey at the player's Hive, using Instant Converters, Micro-Converters, or Honeysuckle. This is one of the most common ways to acquire honey. Additionally, pollen serves as a counter to track how much a player has farmed from a Field when participating in Badges.

Collection efficiency can be further increased by utilizing specialized tools (like the Scythe, which doubles red pollen, or the Bubble Wand, which doubles blue pollen). Furthermore, using Ability Tokens such as Bear Morph, Inspire, Beesmas Cheer, and Baby Love; acquiring bonuses from Bear Bee and Basic Bee Gifted status; leveraging game mechanics, expanding the hive, and utilizing various items and passive abilities all contribute to better pollen collection.

## Pollination
(See [Pollination] for more details.)

When a flower is pollinated, it upgrades any type of flower, with Star flowers being the sole exception. Flowers can be pollinated by:
*   Fuzzy Bee
*   The Diamond Drain Passive Ability
*   Bees equipped with Poinsettia or Lei Beequip
*   Guiding Star Passive Ability

## Trivia
*   Single, Double, and Triple flowers are the only types of flowers that can naturally spawn in fields. Large and Star flowers must be spawned through pollination.
*   Prior to the 2020-04-06 update, flower stages were limited to Single, Double, and Large (where Large was the third tier). This system evolved into the current structure, replacing the third tier with Triple flowers and moving Large to stage four.
*   In real life, bees use nectar—not pollen—to produce honey.
