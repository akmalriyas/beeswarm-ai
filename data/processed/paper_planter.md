# Paper Planter

**Disclaimer:** This content contains information obtained through datamining and testing assumptions. While core stats (like token count and guaranteed items) have been verified, the specific probabilities of loot generation are based on observed data and may be subject to change or incomplete understanding of game mechanics.

---

The **Paper Planter** is a single-use planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. It is a biodegradable pot that grows quickly and can be harvested for pollen, items, and Nectar boosts.

[![Paper Planter](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/75/Paper_Planter.png/revision/latest?cb=20230406145534)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/75/Paper_Planter.png/revision/latest?cb=20230406145534)

> "A biodegradable pot that grows in about 1hr. Harvest to collect pollen, items, and Nectar boosts!"

### Loot Generation
When fully grown and harvested:
1.  The planter has a $\frac{1}{10}$ chance to spawn a Puffshroom (guaranteed to be a Level 1 Common Puffshroom).
2.  It yields up to 10 tokens worth of random items, drawn from a non-exhaustive list of potential drops.

## Trivia

*   The Paper Planter is the cheapest and fastest-growing planter in the game.
*   It is one of four single-use planters: Sticker Planter, Ticket Planter, Festive Planter, and Paper Planter. It is also the only one-time use planter that can be purchased from a store (the Pro Shop).
*   This planter is unique among all planters because it *reduces* nectar gained, multiplying all nectar by 0.75x. All other planters either boost nectar gain or have no effect on it.
*   It is the only planter in the game that stores more pollen than the honey cost required to purchase it.
*   Unlike many specialized planters (like Red Clay or Blue Clay), it is not sold by Dapper Bear's Shop.
