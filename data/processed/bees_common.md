## Trivia and Mechanics

*   The Common rarity and the Event rarity are the only bee rarities that cannot be obtained by using a normal Royal Jelly or Star Jelly.
*   Excluding gifted forms, Common bees and Rare bees are the only types of bees that may have a variant unable to spawn Ability Tokens (the specific rare bee mentioned is the Brave Bee).
*   The Hive Slot color assigned to a Common Bee is brown, which typically represents bronze.
*   Common rarity has the fewest available bee types in the game, currently consisting of only one type (Basic Bee).
*   There are three ways to obtain a common-rarity bee: through Basic Eggs, Star Eggs, and via the Basic Bee Jelly variant of Royal Jelly.
