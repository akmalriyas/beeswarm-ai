# System Page

This page details stats that apply to the player, collecting pollen, and the hive. For stats that apply specifically to bees, please see the [Stats] page.

The **System Page** is accessed via the cog icon in the menu bar. Here, players can redeem [Codes], send [Trade Requests], view the [Global Leaderboard], use the [Cub Console], view player statistics, and manage various game settings such as music toggles, Pollen Text display, Abbreviated Pollen display, trade request allowance, visibility of other players' bees, Reducing Strobing VFX, and Auto-Jelly.

Turning off in-game music only silences the background tracks; sound effects (such as those from [Ability Tokens], obtaining [Legendary] or [Mythic] Bees, [Gifted Bee], and [Event] Bees) will remain audible. To mute all sounds, use the in-game sound option on Roblox.

## Character Stats

The System Page lists many of the player's active stat values. The following stats are detailed below:

### Convert Amount
Boosts the base conversion rate of your bees before multipliers are applied.
*Example:* A [Basic Bee] with 20 Convert Amount will convert 100 [Honey] in 4 seconds, compared to 80 [Honey] without the boost.
This stat can be boosted by [Amulets], the [B.B.M. Mask], the [Honey Mask], and certain [Belts]. It was formerly named 'Conversion Amount' until the Beesmas 2019 update.

### Convert Rate
Boosts your bees' conversion rate by a specific percentage.
*Example:* A Basic Bee with 200% Convert Rate will convert 160 [Honey] in 4 seconds, compared to 80 [Honey] without the boost.
This stat can be boosted by amulets, [Enzymes], [Marshmallow Bees], [Super Smoothies], [Science Enhancement] buffs, the Conversion Buff, the [Honeyday Event buff], the [Honey Badge], [Gifted Bee] [Diamond Bee], Yellow [Jelly Beans], Jellybean Sharing Bonus, certain [Stickers] in the [Sticker Stack], some [Beequips], and most [Bags]. It was formerly named 'Conversion Rate' until the Beesmas 2019 update.

### Convert Rate At Hive
Boosts your bees’ conversion rate by a percentage specifically when converting at the [Hive]. This stat stacks with Convert Rate.
*Example:* A level 1 Basic Bee with both 200% Convert Rate and 200% Hive Convert Rate will convert 320 [Honey] in 4 seconds while at the hive, but only 160 [Honey] outside of it.
It can be increased by the [Pumpkin Badge], Bubble Bloat, and certain items. This buff was previously called "Hive Convert Rate" until the 2021-12-26 update.

### Bonus Convert Amount
Increases the base conversion amount of your player's bees. It can be increased by specific amulets and items.

### Loot Luck
Loot Luck is a passive attribute that increases the chances of receiving rewards from defeated [Mobs].
It can be increased with [Baby Love] (Ability Token), the [Clover Badge], [Baby Bee’s] Gifted Hive Bonus, and certain items. It was formerly named 'Luck' until the 2019-04-05 update.

### Critical Chance
The chance of your bees hitting stronger or collecting more pollen. More accurately, it determines how large your Critical Power is.
*Example:* A Basic Bee with 50% Critical Chance will hit with increased damage half of the time.
It can be boosted by amulets, [Tropical Drinks], [Focus] (Ability Token), the [Green Jellybean], Gifted [Commander Bee], and certain items. Currently, the highest critical chance a player can achieve is 88%.

### Critical Power
This stat multiplies how strong a Critical Hit will be. It applies to both fighting and pollen collecting.
*Example:* A Basic Bee with 300% Critical Power (on a critical hit) will deal the power of 3 Basic Bees (3 damage).
It can be boosted by the Ant Amulet, [Melody] (Ability Token), the [Battle Badge], Gifted [Tabby Bee], Gifted [Looker Bee], and certain items. The calculated maximum Critical Power is 695%.

### Super-Crit Chance
The possibility for a critical hit to become a Super-Crit. Super-Crits deal 33% more damage and collect twice as much pollen as a regular critical hit, and are instantly converted. When a hit is a Super-Crit, it will be indicated by a purple color and the **!** sign next to the damage amount.
It can be boosted by having a Gifted Precise Bee, Tabby Blessing, Precision, a precise mark, the Bruteforce upgrade in the [Robo Bear Challenge], purchasing Bloxiade in the [Retro Swarm Challenge], or using a [Super Smoothie]. The maximum possible Super-Crit Chance is 53% (assuming participation in the Robo Bear Challenge).

### Super-Crit Power
This stat determines how much pollen is multiplied when performing Super-Crits (after applying Critical Power). Super-crit attacks are increased by 33% as much as Pollen Super-crits. It can only be boosted by the [Whistle] and Kazoo Beequips, a [Cog Amulet], or temporarily boosted by the [Dark Scythe].

### Bee Ability Rate
Increases the speed at which your bees create [Ability Tokens].
*Example:* If it normally takes a bee 20 seconds to produce a token, having 20% Bee Ability Rate will likely reduce that time to approximately 16 seconds.
Bee ability rate can be boosted by Star Amulets, Motivating Nectar, certain masks, items, bee mutations, and Beequips.

### Pollen
Increases pollen collected from all sources (both from [Bees] and [Tools]).
*Example:* With 100% Pollen, the player collects 1,000 pollen; with 110% Pollen, they collect 1,100 pollen from the same amount of time and [Flowers].
It can be increased by amulets, [Bear Morph] (Ability Token), [Inspire] (Ability Token), Baby Love, [Pollen Marks] (Ability Token), the [Honeyday Event buff], clouds, Gifted Basic Bee, Gifted Bear Bee, the [Periwinkle Jellybean], or certain items.

### White Pollen
Boosts your white pollen collection. It can be increased by amulets, [Tropical Drinks], [Marshmallow Bees], [White Boost] (Ability Token), the [Spider Badge], the [Coconut Badge], Gifted [Shocked Bee], the [White Jellybean], Wind Boosts, or certain items.

### Red Pollen
Boosts your red pollen collection. It can be increased by amulets, [Red Extracts], [Red Boost] (Ability Token), [Flame Heat] buffs, the [Strawberry Badge], Gifted Rad Bee, or certain items.

### Blue Pollen
Boosts your blue pollen collection. It can be increased with Amulets, [Blue Extracts], [Blue Boost] (Ability Token), the [Bamboo Badge], Gifted Cool Bee, or certain items.

### Bomb Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection from all bombs.
*Example:* If a player collects 5,000 pollen using a Buzz Bomb token, 200% Bomb Pollen will result in 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.
It can be increased with Amulets, [Bomb Combos] buffs, Gifted Bomber Bee, Festive Mark, Black Jelly Bean, or certain items.

### Buzz Bomb Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection specifically from Buzz Bombs and [Fuzz Bombs]. It can be increased with amulets, Gifted Demo Bee, or certain items.

### Red Bomb Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection from Red Bombs. It can be increased with Amulets, Gifted Rascal Bee, Festive Mark, or certain items.

### Blue Bomb Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection from Blue Bombs. It can be increased with Amulets, Gifted Frosty Bee, or certain items.

### Bubble Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection from [Bubbles].
*Example:* If a player collects 5,000 pollen with a bubble, 200% Bubble Pollen will result in 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.
It can be increased with Gifted [Bubble Bee], [Cog Amulet], [Bubble Mask], or the [Diamond Mask].

### Flame Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection from [Flames] created by bees, [Ability Tokens], and [Passive Abilities].
*Example:* If flames collect 1,000 over time, 200% Flame Pollen will result in 2,000 pollen from the same flowers.
It can be increased with Gifted [Fire Bee], [Cog Amulet], or the [Demon Mask].

### Bee Gather Pollen
Boosts pollen collection for all bees.
*Example:* With 200% Bee Gather Pollen, a Basic Bee will collect 20 pollen in 4 seconds.
It can be increased with amulets, [Glues], Gifted [Music Bee], the [x2 Pollen from Bees Gamepass], or certain items.

### Bee Ability Pollen
Boosts pollen collection derived from all bees' abilities and passives.
It can be increased with Gifted [Shy Bee] Hive Bonus, certain [Beequips], or the [Sticker Stack].

### Tool Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection when using your tool.
*Example:* With 100% (base) Tool Pollen, the player collects 1,000 pollen at once with their tool. With 125% Tool Pollen, they collect 1,250 pollen at once in the same location.
It can be increased with amulets, [Glues], Gifted [Carpenter Bee], or the [Stump Badge].

### Instant Conversion
Instantly converts your collected pollen while you are gathering on a field.
*Example:* A Basic Bee with 50% Instant Conversion that collects 10 pollen will instantly convert half of it into [Honey]. You will have 5 [Honey] and 5 remaining pollen, allowing for further collection.
It can be boosted by amulets, [Enzymes], the [Dandelion Badge], the [Cactus Badge], Gifted [Photon Bee], the [Festive Blessing] buff, or certain items.

### Instant White Conversion
Instantly converts white pollen you collect, in addition to standard Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the [Pineapple Badge], Gifted [Windy Bee], or the [Coconut Canister].

### Instant Red Conversion
Instantly converts red pollen you collect, in addition to standard Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the [Mushroom Badge], Gifted [Crimson Bee], or certain items.

### Instant Blue Conversion
Instantly converts blue pollen you collect, in addition to standard Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the [Blue Flower Badge], Gifted [Cobalt Bee], [Diamond Mask], or certain items.

### Instant Bomb Conversion
Instantly converts pollen collected by your bomb. It can be temporarily increased by standing in a festive mark, or permanently increased by owning a Gifted [Demon Bee].

### Instant Flame Conversion
Instantly converts pollen collected by your summoned flames. This can be boosted by equipping the Demon Mask, or you can temporarily gain 50% Instant Flame Conversion by triggering the [Scorching Star Passive].

### Instant Bubble Conversion
Instantly converts pollen collected by your popped bubbles. Currently, there are no known ways to obtain this boost.

### Honey Per Pollen
Allows you to yield more [Honey] from the same amount of pollen, affecting conversion, [Instant Converters], and Instant Conversion.
*Example:* With 200% Honey Per Pollen, a player will make 2,000 [Honey] from 1,000 Pollen.
It can be increased with the [Moon Amulet], the [Wealth Clock], the [Goo Badge], Gifted [Gummy Bee], or Nectar.

### Honey At Hive
Increases the amount of [Honey] you convert when converting at the hive. This stat can be boosted by Satisfying Nectar and Balloon Blessing. The Shimmering [Honey] from a [Diamond Bee] directly multiplies this stat.

### Movespeed
The character's movement speed, measured in studs. It can be increased with amulets, [Oil], [Haste or Haste+] (Ability Token), [Bear Morph] (Ability Token), [Coconut Haste] (Passive Ability), Gifted [Hasty Bee], or various [Boots].

### Movement Collection
Allows the player to collect [Pollen] while walking on [Flowers]. The boost is gained by wearing specific [Boots] or possessing a [Moon Amulet]. Higher Movement Collection results in greater pollen collection from walking on flowers. This boost also collects [Goo] if you walk over goo patches.

### Jump Power
The character's jump height, measured in studs. It can be increased with [Bear Morph] (Ability Token), all boots (except Basic Boots), or the [Propeller Hat]. The minimum jump power is 55 and the maximum is 97.

### Defense
Reduces the damage you take.
*Example:* If a mob deals 50 damage, having 50% Defense reduces it to 25 damage.
It can be increased with the [Shell Amulet], various [Hats], or [Guards]. 100% defense means taking no damage and is achievable by activating the [Emergency Coconut Shield] passive. The maximum defense without this shield is 60%, which can be achieved through the [Demon Mask] or [Diamond Mask] (+35%), both [Crimson] and [Cobalt Guards] (+10% each), and a Shell Amulet (+5%). Defense was originally known as Damage Reduction.

### Bond From Treats
Gives additional [Bond] for your bees when you feed them [Treats].
*Example:* If you feed 100 normal treats (granting 1,000 total bond), 125% Bond From Treats will grant 1,250 bond instead.
It can be increased with the [Moon Amulet], Gifted [Puppy Bee], or as a Hive Bonus from the [Reindeer Antlers] beequip.

### Bond From Battle
Gives additional bond for your bees when you are defeating a [Mob].
*Example:* Normally, [Scorpions] grant 15 bond per bee upon defeat. With 150% Bond From Battle, your bees will receive 22.5 Bond instead.
It can be increased with amulets.

### Honey From Tokens
Boosts the amount of [Honey] received from all tokens that yield honey (such as Honey Gift, Honey Gift+, mob loot, Coin Scatter, and Honeystorm).
*Example:* A level 2 [Diamond Bee] gives 2,000 [Honey] with its Honey Gift+ token. With 200% Honey From Tokens, it will give 4,000 [Honey].
It can be increased with amulets, Gifted [Honey Bee], or certain items.

### Bee Attack
Makes all your bees, regardless of color, deal more damage per hit. The bonus is additive: each point of Bee Attack adds one bee attack (damage) to the base attack value.
*Example:* With +4 Bee Attack, a Basic Bee will attack with the power of 5 Basic Bees (Base Attack + Bee Attack multiplier).
It can be increased with amulets, [Rage] (Ability Token), Gifted [Brave Bee], or certain item buffs and beequips.

### Red Attack
Same as Bee Attack, but applies only to Red bees. It can be boosted with amulets or certain items.

### Blue Attack
Same as Bee Attack, but applies only to Blue bees. It can be boosted with amulets or certain items.

### Colorless Attack
Same as Bee Attack, but applies only to Colorless bees. It can be boosted with amulets or certain items.

### Attack Multiplier
Multiplies the attack power of all bees by a percentage.
It can be increased with Amulets, [Stingers], Flame Heat, [Emergency Coconut Shield] passive, Gifted Rage Bee, or the [Demon Mask].

### Goo
Multiplies all collected goo. It can be increased with the Shell Amulet, [Gummy Morph] (Passive Ability), [Gummy Mask], [Gummy Boots], [Gummy Star] (Passive Ability), or [Gummyballer].

### Goo Conversion
Instantly converts pollen equal to the amount of goo gathered. With a high enough Goo Conversion percentage and Goo multiplier, this can effectively serve as 100% Instant Conversion. It can be increased by amulets, Gummy Mask, Gummy Morph, or Gummy Star.

### Capacity Multiplier
Provides additional pollen storage capacity based on the percentage value.
*Example:* If your backpack holds 200,000 pollen and you have a 200% Capacity Multiplier, it can hold up to 400,000 instead.
It can be increased with amulets, [Sunflower Badge], [Quest Badge], [Bumble Bee]'s Gifted Hive Bonus, [Buoyant Bee]'s Gifted Hive Bonus, and certain items. (Note: If the multiplier is a decimal percentage, it rounds the displayed number but does not affect capacity.)

### White Field Capacity
Provides additional pollen storage based on the percentage of white flowers in the current field.
*Example:* With 15,000,000 base Capacity and 180% White Field Capacity (in a Spider Field), your total capacity is 27,000,000.
It can be increased with items like the [Gummy Mask], Exhausted Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, [Polar Bear]'s Ornament, and other inventory items. (Note: If the multiplier is a decimal percentage, it rounds the displayed number but does not affect capacity.)

### Red Field Capacity
Provides additional pollen storage based on the percentage of red flowers in the current field.
*Example:* With 15,000,000 base Capacity and 150% Red Field Capacity (on a fully red field), your total capacity is 22,500,000.
It can be increased with items like [Demon Mask], Riley Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, [Gifted Riley Bee (NPC)] Ornament, the Rose Badge, and other inventory items. (Note: If the multiplier is a decimal percentage, it rounds the displayed number but does not affect capacity.)

### Blue Field Capacity
Provides additional pollen storage based on the percentage of blue flowers in the current field.
*Example:* With 15,000,000 base Capacity and 200% Blue Field Capacity (on a fully blue field), your total capacity is 30,000,000.
It can be increased with buffs like Bucko Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, [Gifted Bucko Bee (NPC)] Ornament, the Pine Tree Badge, and other inventory items. (Note: If the multiplier is a decimal percentage, it rounds the displayed number but does not affect capacity.)

### Convert Total
The combined total conversion amount of all your bees after applying conversion modifiers.

### Gather Total
The combined total gather amount of all your bees before pollen modifiers are applied.

### Ticket Chance
A stat boost that increases the chances of obtaining [Tickets]. It can be obtained through the [Moon Amulet], Festive Wreath, stacking [Ticket Vouchers] (Sticker), and the now unobtainable Ticket Chance gamepass.

### Attack Total
The combined total attack power of all your bees after applying attack modifiers.

## Auto-Jelly Settings

The **Auto-Jelly Settings** are located at the bottom of the System Page. These settings allow players to use [Royal Jellies] or [Star Jellies] to automatically roll for specific bee requirements until they are met, helping players obtain desired bees quickly.

Players can set multiple options simultaneously; the auto jelly will stop when the combined requirement is fulfilled.

**Auto-Jelly Requirements:**
*   **Until Legendary:** Rolls until a [Legendary] or [Mythic] Bee is obtained.
*   **Until Mythic:** Rolls until a Mythic Bee is obtained.
*   **Require Gifted:** Rolls until a [Gifted] or Mythic Bee is obtained.
*   **Require Mutation:** Rolls until a [Mutated] or Mythic Bee is obtained.

**Combined Requirements Examples:**
*   If "Legendary" and "Gifted" are active, the jelly rolls until a Gifted Legendary Bee is acquired.
*   If "Legendary," "Gifted," and "Mutation" are active, it rolls until a Gifted Legendary Bee with a Mutation is acquired.

**Specific Rules:**
1.  If "Until Legendary" or both "Until Legendary" and "Until Mythic" are active, the jelly will roll until either a Legendary or Mythic Bee is obtained.
2.  If only "Require Gifted" is on, it rolls until a Gifted Bee or a Mythic Bee is obtained.
3.  If only "Require Mutation" is on, it rolls until a Gifted Bee or a Mutated Bee is obtained.
4.  If both "Require Gifted" and "Require Mutation" are on, it rolls until a Gifted Mutated Bee or Mythic Bee is obtained.

**Usage Notes:**
*   If the selected bee is not radioactive, using [Royal Jellies] with mutation enabled will trigger a pop-up warning that auto-rolling for mutation is unavailable. In this case, jellies must be used one at a time.
*   When using a Royal Jelly on a bee with an auto-jelly option active, a reminder message appears confirming the setting is on.
*   **Rare Option Warnings:** An extra pop-up warns the player if they select extremely rare combinations: Mythic and Gifted; Mythic and Mutation; Mythic, Gifted, and Mutation; or Legendary, Gifted, and Mutation.
*   When using a [Star Jelly] on a bee with auto-jelly enabled, another reminder pop-up appears, noting that the jelly will use as many star jellies as needed to fulfill the requirement (even if only "Until Gifted" is active). If you are using the Require Gifted option with Star Jellies, this warning still appears, but no action is taken.
