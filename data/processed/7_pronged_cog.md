The **7-Pronged Cog** is a special inventory item introduced during the [Ready Player Two](/wiki/Ready_Player_Two "Ready Player Two") event on November 23, 2020. It served as one of two required ingredients to craft the [Strange Goggles](/wiki/Strange_Goggles "Strange Goggles").

To obtain these Cogs during the event, players had to redeem seven specific codes that were discovered through various riddles scattered across the map. Since the 7-Pronged Cog is unobtainable but not completely removed from the game, any copies redeemed during the event remain in players' inventories.
