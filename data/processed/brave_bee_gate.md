# Brave Bee Gate

![BraveBeeGate](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/96/BraveBeeGate.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20180421062845) The Brave Bee Gate.

The Brave Bee Gate, also known as the 10 Bee Gate, is a specialized gate that requires players to possess 10 Bees in order to pass through it. It derives its name from the large Brave Bee featured on top of the structure.

### Mobs
The following mobs can be found in or around the Brave Bee Gate area:
*   Stump Snail
*   Tunnel Bear
*   Mantis
*   Rhino Beetle

### Quest Givers
Players can interact with these characters at the gate location:
*   Science Bear
*   Dapper Bear

### Shops
The following shops are accessible from or near the Brave Bee Gate:
*   Pro Shop
*   Dapper Bear's Shop
*   Magic Bean Shop

### Fields
This gate is located within or adjacent to these fields:
*   Pineapple Patch
*   Stump Field

### Extras & Mechanics
The area also contains several interactive elements and features:
*   Yellow Cannon (Transportation)
*   Memory Match (Machine/Game)
*   Treat Dispenser (Machine)
*   Instant Converter (Machine)
    *   White Tunnel (Related Feature)
*   Pro Shop Maze (Mazes)
*   Beesmas Lights Switch (Exclusive to Beesmas events)

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   **Blue HQ Shortcut:** If the gate is unlocked, players can reach the top of the Blue HQ by jumping off a ledge near Science Bear. This provides a shortcut to Brown Bear, the Honeystorm Summoner, the Wealth Clock, and Commando Chick's Hideout. It may also grant access to Gifted Bucko Bee's Snow Machine during Beesmas.
*   **Slingshot Bypass (Historical):** Previously, the Slingshot could bypass the gate even without 10 bees. This exploit was patched in the 2019-09-28 update; players who do not meet the entry requirements are now teleported back to spawn.
*   **Rogue Vicious Bee Exclusion:** The Brave Bee Gate area does not contain any fields where Rogue Vicious Bees can spawn.
