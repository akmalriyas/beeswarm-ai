# Rad Bee

**Rad Bee** is a Rare, Red bee known for its stylish appearance and preference for red flowers. Its motto is: "A stylish bee with a taste for red flowers. Everyone wants to be this bee."

## Characteristics
*   **Rarity:** Rare
*   **Color:** Red
*   **Energy:** 20
*   **Speed:** 14
*   **Attack:** 1

Rad Bee's favorite treat is Strawberries, and it enjoys visiting the Mushroom Field and Rose Field. It dislikes the Pine Tree Forest.
