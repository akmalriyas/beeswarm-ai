# Spider Field

The Spider Field is a field located past the Basic Bee Gate, situated between the Strawberry Field and Bamboo Field. This unique area is protected by spiders.

## Field Statistics

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Field Color** | White |
| **Bee Prerequisites** | 5 bees |
| **Nectar Type** | Motivating Nectar |
| **Dimensions** | 28 × 26 flowers |
| **Total Flower Count** | 728 |

### Flower Distribution

The field contains only white flowers, available in single, double, and triple sizes.

## Mechanics and Drops

When collecting pollen in the Spider Field, players can spawn various tokens, including bitterberry, treat, honey, ticket, enzyme, and magic bean.

This field grants Motivating Nectar when a Planter is placed here. A level 2-4 Rogue Vicious Bee has a chance of spawning in this area.

## Obtaining Boosts

Several methods can be used to enhance the Spider Field's pollen yield:

*   **Field Booster:** Using a Field Booster may grant a Spider Field Boost, providing +200% Spider Field pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Applying Glitter adds 100% Spider Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes (if not already maxed).
*   **Dice:** Using Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice may add +100% Spider Field pollen for 15 minutes (if not already maxed).
*   **Field Winds:** Spider Field Winds can be obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes. They provide a base of +20% instant Spider Field conversion and grant +5% instant conversion plus +20% pollen per stack, stacking up to 13 times.
*   **Shell Amulet:** If equipped with a Spider Field Boost, the Shell Amulet can boost the field by +5% to +30%, depending on its rarity.
*   **Dapper Bear Gift:** Giving Dapper Bear a Present grants the field a x3 boost for Beesmas 2025.

### Historical/Obsolete Methods
The following methods for obtaining Spider Field Boosts are no longer possible:

*   Giving presents to Onett or Dapper Bear during earlier events (Beesmas 2021).
*   Purchasing a Spider Field Market Boost from the Boost Market (provided x1.5 Field Capacity, x1.25 Pollen, -5% Monster Respawn Time, and x1.25 Convert Rate At Hive).
*   Redeeming specific expired codes (e.g., Tabby, BANNED, 1MLikes, PlushFriday, WeekExtension, GummyBoost, ThnxCyaBoost, AceUnhatched, etc.).

## Trivia

*   The Spider Field is unique as it is the only field where all flowers are white, making it the only field composed entirely of a single color (unlike fields that are entirely blue or red).
*   A Ticket token can be found hidden in the middle of the spider cave.
*   This field is one of six locations where fireflies can land; the others are Bamboo Field, Cactus Field, Pineapple Patch, Rose Field, and Strawberry Field.
*   There was a glitch at this field where amulets and cub buddies could be spotted without an owner if a player left the server while present.
*   Black Bear has mentioned that this field is one of his favorite fields.
*   The Spider Field, Stump Field, and Mountain Top Field are among the only fields not named after a plant or fruit.
*   The Spider Cave, the Stump Field's Cave, and the Coconut Cave share the exact same cave decoration.
*   Along with Clover Field, Cactus Field, Rose Field, Mountain Top Field, and Pepper Patch, this field is one of the few where a Rogue Vicious Bee can spawn.
*   This field is one of only two fields named after the mob(s) residing within it; the other being Ant Field (which features multiple types of ants).
*   There is an area inside the mountain wall that contains a row of normally invisible flowers, allowing players to collect pollen there, similar to certain hidden areas in Bamboo Field.
