# Royal Jelly Dispenser

![Royal Jelly Dispenser](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1c/Royal_Jelly_Dispenser.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20180507121316) The Royal Jelly Dispenser is a dispenser found near the Clover Field and the Noob Shop.

## Information

### Usage
The Royal Jelly Dispenser exchanges items for rewards:
*   **Output:** 1 Royal Jelly
*   **Cost:** 6 Tickets and 10 Haste Ability Tokens

### Mechanics
*   **Cooldown:** 3 seconds
*   **Location:** Near the Clover Field and the Noob Shop.

> *Note: Do not confuse this dispenser with the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser (which costs nothing) or the Royal Jelly Shop.*

## Trivia
*   In terms of honey value, the Royal Jelly Dispenser is considered overpriced compared to the Royal Jelly Shop. While 1 Royal Jelly costs 1 million honey at the shop, purchasing 6 Tickets from the Ticket Shop would cost more than a million honey.
*   The Royal Jelly Ticket Dispenser is one of three ticket-requiring dispensers (along with the Ant Pass Dispenser and Robo Pass Dispenser). All three have free counterparts, though these free versions feature significantly longer cooldowns. The free counterpart for the Royal Jelly Dispenser specifically requires Gifted Bees to be utilized.
*   The Royal Jelly Dispenser, along with the Honey Dispenser, Strawberry Dispenser, Blueberry Dispenser, Glue Dispenser, Samovar, and others, is one of the limited set of dispensers required for various in-game quests.
