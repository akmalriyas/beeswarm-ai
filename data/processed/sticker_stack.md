# Sticker Stack

[![The Sticker Stack while activated](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2c/The_sticker_stack_while_activated..jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240113023527)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2c/The_sticker_stack_while_activated..jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240113023527)

The **Sticker Stack** is a machine located in the [Honey Bee Gate](/wiki/Honey_Bee_Gate "Honey Bee Gate") area, adjacent to the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest") and the [Honey Bee NPC](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)").

It allows players to donate a sticker, hive skin, cub skin, or voucher. In return, the player receives a temporary boost and an item based on the donated sticker. The base duration of the buff is 15 minutes, increasing by 10 seconds for every sticker stacked (maximum duration: 1 hour). Each type of sticker can only be used once in the stack.

## Usage

To use the Sticker Stack, players must step into the podium in front of it and open the menu. There are two ways to activate the machine:
1. Pay 25 tickets.
2. Use a unique sticker that has not yet been stacked.

Both methods grant the sticker stack buff. If an unused sticker is used for activation, it grants a new buff specific to that sticker, and permanently adds 10 seconds to the duration of future activations. The sticker then cannot be used again. Players also receive rewards based on the sticker stacked.

## Stack Combos

Stack Combos are achieved by adding a specific set of stickers to the Sticker Stack. Currently, two combos can be completed:

**Combo 1:** Capricorn Star Sign + Aquarius Star Sign + Pisces Star Sign + Aries Star Sign + Taurus Star Sign + Gemini Star Sign + Cancer Star Sign + Leo Star Sign + Virgo Star Sign + Libra Star Sign + Scorpio Star Sign + Sagittarius Star Sign
*   Reward: [Star Cub](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins "Cub Buddy")

**Combo 2:** White Button Mushroom + Fly Agaric Mushroom + Porcini Mushroom + Oiler Mushroom + Morel Mushroom + Chanterelle Mushroom + Shiitake Mushroom + Black Truffle Mushroom
*   Reward: [Prismatic Mushroom Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker")

## Trivia

*   The Sticker Stack was introduced in the January 12, 2024 update, alongside Stickers and the Hive Hub.
*   The Sticker Stack buff ends a few seconds before the machine becomes available for use again.
*   If a reward from the sticker stack has a capacity limit (e.g., Micro-Converter), that limit is ignored, and the player still receives the reward even if they already possess the maximum amount.
*   While the machine can be used for 5 sticker stack badges, achieving its Grandmaster variant is currently impossible as it requires stacking 500 different stickers, and only 290 unique stickers exist in the game.
*   Certain stickers require extra confirmation before being stacked, including all vouchers, cub skins, and hive skins.
