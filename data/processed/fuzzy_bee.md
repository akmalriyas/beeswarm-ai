# Fuzzy Bee

**Fuzzy Bee** is a Colorless Mythic bee, known for its unkempt appearance and fur which aids in pollination.

[![Fuzzy Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c3/Fuzzy_Bee.png/revision/latest?cb=20230130022457)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c3/Fuzzy_Bee.png/revision/latest?cb=20230130022457) [![FuzzyBee Gifted](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5d/FuzzyBee_Gifted.png/revision/latest?cb=20230130024636)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5d/FuzzyBee_Gifted.png/revision/latest?cb=20230130024636)

**Rarity:** Mythic
**Color:** Colorless
**Base Stats:**
*   Energy: 50
*   Speed: 11.9
*   Attack: 3

Fuzzy Bee's favorite treat is Pineapples. It likes the Dandelion Field and Pine Tree Forest, but dislikes the Pepper Patch.

## Abilities

*   **Fuzz Bombs:** Spawns 2 fuzz bombs (increases by 1 every 5 bee levels) that wander the field for 8 seconds. Catching these collects pollen and pollinates nearby flowers. The collected pollen scales with bomb multipliers and bee level.
*   **Buzz Bomb+:** Collects 7 pollen from 29 surrounding flowers (+10% pollen per Level). This ability enhances power when combined with other bombs.
*   **🌟Gifted Ability: Pollen Haze:** Summons a yellow haze over the field for 30 seconds, pollinating up to 30 random flowers every second and improving flower growth rate. Bomb Tokens within the haze are automatically collected and pollinate flowers. If Pollen Haze has been active in the last 3 minutes, this ability summons fuzz bombs instead.
*   **Passive: Fuzzy Coat:** This bee can pollinate up to 5 nearby flowers (9 if gifted) every time it gathers. Pollinated flowers are temporarily upgraded to grant and store more pollen and grow faster. Lower tier flowers have a higher chance of being affected.

## Trivia

*   Along with Buoyant Bee and Precise Bee, Fuzzy Bee is one of the first Mythic bees introduced since the original set. It is also unique in being the only Mythic bee introduced independently, outside of Beesmas events.
*   Fuzzy Bee is currently the only bee capable of pollinating flowers without requiring a Beequip.
*   The bee may like the Dandelion Field because dandelions are naturally soft and fuzzy.
*   Fuzzy Bee shares the single-skin color trait with Shy Bee, Ninja Bee, and Windy Bee.
*   Spicy Bee, Tadpole Bee, Buoyant Bee, and Digital Bee are among the bees with four different abilities; Fuzzy Bee has three primary abilities (plus passive).
*   The Fuzzy Bee Egg was the fifth specific bee egg available for purchase using Robux, following Bear Bee, Festive Bee, Windy Bee, and Puppy Bee. It was also the first non-Event bee purchasable with Robux.
*   Fuzz bombs can be popped by various player abilities, including Petal Shurikens from the Petal Wand/Petal Storm, Windy Bee's tornado, and Puppy Bee's fetch ball. Any player can pop these bombs, regardless of who summoned them.
*   Fuzzy Bee is the only Hive bee introduced in 2020 (excluding Digital Bee, which was usable later).
*   Fuzz Bombs have the unique property of popping bubbles upon contact.
*   There has been a visual glitch where Fuzzy Bee appears without its fur despite possessing it when obtained through Eggs or Royal Jelly.
