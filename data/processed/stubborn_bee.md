# Stubborn Bee

**Stubborn Bee** is a Colorless Rare bee, known for its hard-headed nature and tendency to direct others.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Rare |
| **Color** | Colorless |
| **Energy** | 20 |
| **Speed** | 11.9 |
| **Attack** | 3 |

Stubborn Bee's favorite treat is Strawberries, and it enjoys visiting the Dandelion Field and Pineapple Patch. It dislikes the Rose Field.

## Stats & Mechanics

### Collection Rates
*   **Pollen:** Collects 10 pollen in 6 seconds (Base).
    *   With White Boost Token: Base collection of **10** pollen in 4 seconds, equivalent to 2.5 per second.
    *   With x2 Bee Pollen Gamepass: **20** pollen in 4 seconds.
*   **Honey:** Makes 80 Honey in 3 seconds (Base).
    *   Equivalent production rate: 26.67 honey per second.
    *   With x2 Honeymaking Speed Gamepass: **80** Honey in 1.5 seconds.

### Stat Modifiers
Stubborn Bee possesses the following inherent modifiers:
*   -15% Movespeed
*   +25% Convert Speed
*   +2 Attack

### Gifted Hive Bonus
When housed in a Gifted Hive, Stubborn Bee receives a bonus of +15% Ability Token Lifespan.

## Abilities

**Pollen Mark:** Marks a random area on the field for 7 seconds (+0.25s per level). While standing within this marked area, all pollen collection is increased by up to 50%. This ability can stack up to 3 times.

### Trivia
*   Stubborn Bee was one of two bees added in the 2018-11-25 update, alongside Carpenter Bee.
*   Along with Looker Bee, Stubborn Bee is one of the only Rare bees introduced after the game's initial launch.
*   The Gifted Hive Bonus (+15% ability token lifespan) was previously bugged; players could have a Gifted Stubborn Bee without receiving the bonus until it was fixed in February 2021.
*   Stubborn Bee is unique among Colorless Rare Bees as it does not have its own dedicated guard listing in the Pro Shop, due to being added before the guards category was implemented in the 2018-11-25 update.
