# Beekeeper's Mask

![BeekeepersMask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5e/Beekeeper%27s_Mask.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20200403235417)

The **Beekeeper's Mask** is a specialized mask accessory, often described as "A veiled hat only worn by real-deal beekeepers." It can be purchased from the Mountain Top Shop.

## Stats and Effects
This item provides significant bonuses to gathering efficiency and defense:

*   +20% Pollen
*   +20% Bee Gather Pollen (Pollen collected by bees)
*   +10% Bee Ability Rate
*   +25% Defense

## Acquisition Details

### Purchase Cost
The mask can be purchased using the following resources:
*   20,000,000 Honey
*   5 Enzymes
*   3 Glue
*   1 Glitter

### Crafting Ingredients
The required materials for crafting are:
*   20,000,000 Honey
*   5 Enzymes
*   3 Glue
*   1 Glitter

## Trivia and History
*   This mask belongs to a group of accessories (along with the Propeller Hat, Helmet, and Strange Goggles) whose designs are not based on bees.
*   Prior to the 2018-11-25 update, it was one of the most expensive hat accessories in the game, costing 25 million honey and requiring no crafting materials.
*   The item underwent a rebalance during the 2018-09-10 Update. Before this change, its stats included:
    *   +25% Pollen from Bees
    *   +25% Instant Conversion
    *   +25% Defense
    *   +10% Bee Ability Rate
    *   +100% Loot Luck
    *   +20 Jump Power
    *   +6 Player Movespeed
