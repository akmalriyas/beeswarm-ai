# White Brick Field

[![White Brick Field](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f7/White_Brick_Field.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/275?cb=20250724191201)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f7/White_Brick_Field.png/revision/latest?cb=20250724191201)

## Field Statistics
*   **Color:** White
*   **Bee Prerequisite:** 10 bees
*   **Dimensions:** 19 × 19 flowers
*   **Total Flower Count:** 361
*   **Bee Preferences:** None (Likes/Dislikes)

The **White Brick Field** is one of the four fields located in the Retro Swarm Challenge. This 19x19 field contains 361 flowers, which are a mix of white, blue, and red, available in single, double, and triple sizes. The primary output of this field is brick tokens while collecting pollen.

Since planters cannot be used within the Retro Swarm Challenge, there is no confirmed nectar type for this field.

## Obtaining Boosts
Using [Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") in the White Brick Field grants a 100% White Brick Field pollen boost for 15 minutes.

## Trivia
*   This field, along with three other fields in the Retro Swarm Challenge, is one of the only fields without an associated field stamp.
*   All these specific challenge fields borrow the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field")'s boost icon.
