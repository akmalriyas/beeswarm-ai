# Nectar Tester

The **Nectar Tester** is a special item that functions similarly to a [Nectar Vial](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Nectar_Vial), but with significantly greater utility. While standard nectar vials provide a single type of nectar for 4 hours, the Nectar Tester grants every available type of nectar simultaneously for a full 24 hours.

**Cooldown:** 1 hour

## Acquisition Status

Currently, there is no way to obtain the Nectar Tester in the main game.

*   **Past Availability:** The item was originally intended only for the Test Realm but was later added to the main game during the Summer Beesmas event of 2024. It was available as a reward for completing "B.B.M's Naughty List" quest given by [Bubble Bee Man](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man).

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   **Origin:** The item originated in the Test Realm as an efficient way to acquire multiple types of nectar without spending excessive time.
*   **Value:** It provides a total amount of nectar equivalent to 30 standard Nectar Vials, granting a cumulative total of 120 hours of nectar benefits.
*   **Exclusivity:** The Nectar Tester is one of only two inventory items (along with [Debug Wax](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Debug_Wax)) that can be obtained exclusively through Bubble Bee Man's quests.
*   **Iconography:** This item shares the same icon as the [Basic Egg](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Egg#Basic_Egg), likely because items without a specific decal often default to this generic appearance.
