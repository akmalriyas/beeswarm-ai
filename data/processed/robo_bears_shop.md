# Robo Bear's Shop

![RoboBear'sShop](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/99/RoboBear%27sShop.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20260204020853)

## Overview
Robo Bear's Shop is a shop run by Robo Bear, located right of the Red HQ and next to the Rose Field. It sells all types of Drives and Digital Bees. The shop was added alongside the rest of Robo Bear's area in the 2022-12-26 update.

*Note: "Robo Bear's Shop" is not the official name; it is likely named after its owner.*

## Pricing Mechanics
The price for colored drives follows a specific formula based on the purchase order ($n$).

$$P(n) = 250,000 n^2$$

Where $n$ represents the sequential drive of that type being purchased (e.g., the 1st is 250,000, the 2nd is 1,000,000, and so on). The price for Glitched Drives begins at $n + 1$.

## Trivia
*   This shop, along with the Coconut Cave and Dapper Bear's Shop, is one of only three shops that does not use the "Vendor" soundtrack upon entry.
*   If the Digital Bee was purchased using a Robux Pack, the player receives 1000 tickets upon purchase (bypassing the standard ticket requirement).
