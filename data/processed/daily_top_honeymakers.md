# Daily Top Honeymakers Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Honeymakers** is one of the 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. This leaderboard tracks and displays how much honey players have produced each day.

## Mechanics

*   **Display:** The board shows 10 positions at a time, but the scroll bar allows viewing up to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.
*   **Interaction:** When a player steps on the designated white pad in front of the board, a message appears stating, "You've made (x) Honey today," where 'x' is the total honey produced since the last daily reset.

## Location

The leaderboard is situated near the **All-Time Top Honeymakers** leaderboard, next to the **Basic Egg Shop**, and in front of the **Sunflower Field**.

## Prizes

Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 receive either the Right Gold Swirl Fleuron Sticker or the Left Gold Swirl Fleuron Sticker.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 are awarded 100 Tickets.
