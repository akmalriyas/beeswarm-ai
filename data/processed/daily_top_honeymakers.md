# Daily Top Honeymakers Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Honeymakers** is one of the 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. This leaderboard tracks and displays the total amount of honey players have produced each day.

## Mechanics and Reset
*   **Display:** The board shows 10 positions at a time, but the scroll bar allows viewing up to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** It resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.
*   **Interaction:** When a player steps on the designated white pad in front of the leaderboard, a message appears stating how much honey they have made since the last daily reset (e.g., "You've made X Honey today").

## Location
The Daily Top Honeymakers Leaderboard is located near the All-Time Top Honeymakers leaderboard, next to the Basic Egg Shop, and in front of the Sunflower Field.

## Prizes
Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 receive either the Right Gold Swirl Fleuron Sticker or the Left Gold Swirl Fleuron Sticker.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 are awarded 100 Tickets.
