# Dandelion Field

The Dandelion Field is one of the five starting fields available to players when beginning the game. It contains no protective mobs.

## Field Statistics and Characteristics

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Field Color** | White |
| **Nectar Type** | Comforting Nectar |
| **Dimensions** | 36 × 18 flowers |
| **Total Flower Count** | 648 |
| **Bee Prerequisites** | None |

### Flower Distribution (Total: 648)

The field contains white, red, and blue flowers in single, double, and triple sizes.

*Note: Flower counts within the table may vary due to rounding. In game, all flowers are randomly generated and may not precisely align with these percentages.*

## Bee Preferences

### Likes
Bees that prefer the Dandelion Field include: Baby Bee, Bomber Bee, Demo Bee, Exhausted Bee, Music Bee, Stubborn Bee, Windy Bee, Fuzzy Bee, and Digital Bee.

### Dislikes
Bees that dislike the Dandelion Field include: Brave Bee, Commander Bee, and Vicious Bee.

## Resources and Drops

When collecting pollen in this field, players can obtain Honey, red boost tokens, blue boost tokens, Ticket, Glue, Glitter, Magic Bean, Treat, and Nectar.

### Item Drop Chances
*   **Treats:** 20% Chance
*   **Glues:** 0.01% Chance
*   **Magic Beans:** 0.0059% Chance
*   **Glitter:** 0.005% Chance

When a Planter is placed in this field, it produces Comforting Nectar. A level 1-5 Wild Windy Bee can also spawn here.

## Boost Mechanics

The Dandelion Field can be enhanced using various methods:

*   **Field Booster:** Using a Field Booster may grant a Dandelion Field Boost, providing +300% Dandelion Field pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Applying Glitter adds 100% Dandelion Field pollen to the current boost, provided it is not already maximized.
*   **Dice:** Using Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice can add between +100% and +300% Dandelion Field pollen for 15 minutes, depending on the dice used, if the current boost is not maxed out.
*   **Field Winds:** Dandelion Field Winds are obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes. They offer a base of 20% instant Dandelion Field conversion and provide +5% instant Dandelion Field conversion plus +25% Dandelion Field pollen per stack, stacking up to a total of 13 times.
*   **Codes:** Specific valid codes can grant boosts (e.g., `Jumpstart` or `PlushFriday`).
*   **Shell Amulet:** If the Shell Amulet possesses a Dandelion Field Boost, it can further boost the field by +5% to +30%, depending on its rarity.

### Historical/Obsolete Boosting Methods
The following methods for obtaining boosts are no longer possible:

*   **NPC Gifts:** Giving presents to NPCs like Mother Bear or Onett previously granted specific Dandelion Field Boosts and items.
*   **Expired Codes:** Various expired codes (e.g., `AccentMaster`, `Wink`, `TornadoGlitch`) provided Dandelion Field Boosts and Winds.
*   **Market Purchases:** Purchasing a Dandelion Field Market Boost from the Boost Market previously granted 1.25x Dandelion Field Pollen, x1.5 Field Capacity, +10% Bee Ability Rate, and x1.25 Convert Rate At Hive.

## Gallery & Trivia

### Visuals
*   The field features a royal jelly token on top of the tallest dandelion. This is also the only dandelion in the field with collision at its top, obtainable using Parachute/Glider with cannons or from a high place.
*   It can be affected by sparkles blown onto it from the Bubble Wand model on Blue HQ, alongside Mushroom Field, Clover Field, and Blue Flower Field.

### Trivia
*   Prior to the 2019-09-28 update, Dandelion Field was the only field that was mob-proof.
*   The All-Time Top Honeymakers Leaderboard is located adjacent to this field.
*   A previous glitch allowed Stick Bugs to fall from the tallest mushroom and land in the Dandelion Field; this has since been patched.
*   During the Ready Player Two event, the field was observed glitching after using The Computer.
*   Onett once threatened to remove all fields except the Dandelion Field if bees and bears did not stop complaining during his Star Journey 2 quest dialogue (though he later stated he wouldn't actually do it).
