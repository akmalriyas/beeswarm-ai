# Daily Top Stick Bug Fighters Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Stick Bug Fighters** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in the game, viewable through the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page. This leaderboard tracks and displays the highest scores players achieve during the daily **Stick Bug Challenge**. It shows 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view all 100 ranked spots. The leaderboard resets every day at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

**Top 5 Finishers:**
Players who rank in the top 5 when the leaderboard resets are awarded one **Stick Cub** (a Cub Buddy skin).

**Top 100 Finishers:**
If a player reaches the top 100 by the end of the day, they will be awarded 100 **Tickets**.

## Trivia

*   During the 2024-10-13 update, the requirement for obtaining the Stick Cub skin was temporarily changed to award it to users with the top 50 highest scores. This requirement reverted back to the top 5 scores following the 2024-10-29 update.
