# Daily Top Blue Pollen Collectors

The **Daily Top Blue Pollen Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator, tracking how much blue pollen players have collected on a given day.

## Mechanics and Reset
*   This leaderboard displays the top 10 positions at any time, but users can scroll to view all 100 ranked players.
*   The data resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.
*   When a player steps on the white pad in front of the board, a message appears displaying their current daily collection status: "You've collected 'X' Blue Pollen today (Resets in 'Y')".

## Location and Prizes
**Location:**
The leaderboard is located inside the Blue HQ, specifically on the second floor, to the right of the Gifted Bucko Bee. It is situated next to the All-Time Top Blue Collectors Leaderboard.

**Prizes:**
Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:
*   **Top 25:** Awarded either the Left Shining Diamond Fleuron Sticker or the Right Shining Diamond Fleuron Sticker.
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 25 Tickets.
