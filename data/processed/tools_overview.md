# Tools

Tools are items used to gather pollen. Some tools possess special abilities or collect better from specific colored flowers (white, red, and blue). All tools can be enhanced through the Tool Pollen stat. The newest tool introduced is the Sticker-Seeker, bringing the current total of available tools to twenty.

## Acquisition and Cost
Every player begins with a basic Scooper. Better tools must be purchased from various shops:

*   **Noob Shop:** Sells the cheapest tools, ranging from 0 to 14 thousand honey.
*   **Pro Shop:** Offers tools priced between 40 thousand and 1.5 million honey.
*   **Mountain Top Shop:** Features the fourth most expensive tools, with prices spanning 20 million to 150 million honey.
*   **Petal Shop:** Stocks the third most expensive tool at 1.5 billion honey.
*   **Red HQ & Blue HQ:** Each has two tools available, ranging from 3.5 million to 2.5 trillion honey.
*   **Gummy Bear's Lair:** Hosts the Gummyballer, currently the most expensive tool in the game. It costs 10 trillion honey and various crafting materials.

The Gummyballer is unique among tools as it can directly collect goo from a field.

## Usage and Boosts
Players can automate pollen collection by holding down the mouse button on a computer or holding an open area of the screen on mobile devices.

Various items provide temporary boosts:
*   **Glue:** Provides a 10-minute boost granting x1.25 Tool Pollen and bees.
*   **Purple Potion:** Grants a 15-minute duration with the following multipliers:
    *   x1.25 Capacity
    *   x1.25 Convert Rate At Hive
    *   x1.5 Red Pollen
    *   x1.5 Blue Pollen
    *   x1.3 Bee Gather Pollen
    *   x1.3 Tool Pollen

**Technical Note (PC):** A glitch exists where holding down the click button, then pressing Ctrl + Esc or Alt + Tab automatically activates the tool. However, clicking the screen will deactivate it. This can also be achieved by moving the mouse outside of the window boundaries in windowed mode, though this technique is limited to the launcher version of the application.
