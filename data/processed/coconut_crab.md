# Coconut Crab

The **Coconut Crab** is a level 12 boss mob that defends the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field). It has 250,000 health and moves along the back wall of the field.

## Mechanics and Gameplay

The Coconut Crab is a challenging encounter, similar to the [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear). Like other timed mobs such as [Chicks](/wiki/Chicks), [Aphids](/wiki/Aphid), and [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom), players have a limited time (two hours) to defeat it. After this period, its health resets. The remaining time is displayed above the mob's health bar.

Unlike the [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail) or the [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick), the Coconut Crab's health does not save if you leave the field; it must be defeated while inside the designated area. If a player leaves, its health resets, and it returns to the [Coconut Cave](/wiki/Coconut_Cave).

### Attacks

The Coconut Crab alternates between two primary attack types:

1.  **Claw Attack:** The crab attempts to align itself with the player before rapidly jabbing with its claws. Each hit deals 40 damage (before Defense is applied). It attacks 7 times, pauses to reposition near the player, and repeats this cycle twice more, totaling 21 claw strikes.
2.  **Coconut Attack:** The crab moves to the middle of the back wall and jumps up 8 times. Each jump causes one large coconut to fall into the field, dealing 150 damage (before Defense is applied). The first falling coconut always targets the player, while subsequent coconuts fall randomly across the field.

### Combat Tips

*   **Avoid Contact:** Do not make contact with the Coconut Crab's hitbox, as this deals significant damage and can be lethal if your defense is low.
*   **Dodge Claw Attack:** The claw attack can be avoided by walking or gliding past the crab as it moves closer. If you continue moving while it attempts to strike, you will pass through the attack. Using a [Glider](/wiki/Glider) allows players to dodge most hits.
*   **Positioning During Coconut Attack:** Do not remain in the middle of the field once the coconut attack is finished (unless you have high movement speed), as the crab will immediately initiate its claw attack, resulting in massive damage.
*   **Field Boundaries:** Avoid stepping outside the field while fighting, as this resets the Crab's health. Touching the walls can occasionally reset its health. Excessive vertical jumping may also cause the player to exit the range and trigger a health reset.
*   **Exploiting Bugs:** It is possible to defeat the Coconut Crab from the edge of the field due to a known bug. By timing a jump correctly after the second coconut fall, players can avoid the falling coconuts while standing on the edge.
*   **High-Level Bees:** Utilize bees specialized in combat, such as [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee), [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee), [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee), [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee), and [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee), to defeat the Crab quickly.
*   **Token Collection:** Bees with the [Token Link](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Token_Link) ability are recommended for collecting tokens in high-risk areas. Activating all three Targets or being hit by Precise Bee's [Target Practice](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Target_Practice) will collect all available tokens.
*   **Buff Stacking:** Before the fight, gather Focus tokens on the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch). Once you have a $\times 10$ stack of focus, jump into the Coconut Field. Collecting a [Melody](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Melody) token (if possible without losing focus) further enhances critical power.
*   **Optimal Stance:** Stand behind the coconut tree farthest from the Coconut Cave and closest to Spirit Bear. This position often causes the Crab to become stuck, giving your bees a better chance of victory.
*   **Leveling Bees:** Ensure your bees are leveled up to Level 12 or higher. Bees with equal or higher levels will never miss their attacks on this mob.

## Drops and Loot

The loot dropped by the Coconut Crab is scattered around the field, similar to drops from a [Sprout](/wiki/Sprout) or [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Chicks#Mondo_Chick). Only the player who defeated their own Coconut Crab can see and pick up these tokens. A [Token Link](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Token_Link) ability, along with abilities like Tornado (Windy Bee), Triangulate (Vector Bee), or Target Practice (Precise Bee), can also be used to collect them.

The quality, quantity, and rarity of the items dropped depend on how quickly it was defeated, similar to the Mondo Chick. However, even slower kills can yield rare drops.

## Advanced Tactics and Gear

*   **Coconut Canister:** Equipping the [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister) allows players to intentionally activate the [Emergency Coconut Shield](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Emergency_Coconut_Shield), granting $\times 1.25$ Bee Attack and 100% Defense for 10 seconds, while dropping 5 falling coconuts onto the field.
*   **Stinger Usage:** Using a ![Stinger](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/Stinger.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404041522) grants a $\times 1.5$ Bee Attack buff for 30 seconds. Using three stingers activates Star Saw, which deals damage and collects tokens that might otherwise be dangerous to reach.
*   **Oil:** Using [Oil](/wiki/Oil) provides $\times 1.2$ to both Player Movespeed and Bee Movespeed for 10 minutes, aiding in dodging attacks and increasing attack speed.
*   **Tropical Drink:** This item grants +5% Critical Chance for 10 minutes.
*   **Masks and Boots:** The [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask) or [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask) are recommended for their passive bonuses, boosting overall damage. For physical boosts, consider equipping [Beekeeper's Boots](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Boots), [Coconut Clogs](/wiki/Coconut_Clogs), or [Gummy Boots](/wiki/Gummy_Boots) for +1, +2, and +3 Bee Attack respectively.
*   **Gummy Mask:** If only the [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask) is equipped, players can trigger [Gummy Morph] by using [Gumdrops], temporarily increasing their [Gummy Bee]'s attack. This mask also boosts bee ability rate.
*   **Jelly Beans:** [Green jelly beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans) provide +3% critical chance for 1 minute; collecting three grants a total of +5%.
*   **Nectars and Buffs:** Invigorating Nectar provides $1.01 - 1.1$ Bee Attack, while Motivating Nectar offers $+1\%$ to $+5\%$ Bee Ability Rate. The [Beesmas Cheer](/wiki/Ability_Tokens) buff grants $\times 2$ Bee Movespeed.
*   **Cub Buddy:** Owning a ![Cub Buddy](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/Cub_Buddy.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404041522) can be beneficial as the Cub Buddy can collect tokens even while inside or in front of the Crab's attack range.

## Trivia

*   The Coconut Crab has the highest base level of any mob in the game, surpassing [Cave Monsters] (Level 11).
*   It holds the third-highest base health among all mobs, behind the [Mondo Chick] (300,000 HP) and the [Stump Snail] (30 million HP).
*   The Coconut Crab is unique as a boss mob that spawns in a cave (excluding the Stump Snail, which is considered a mini-boss).
*   It is one of the few mobs capable of instantly killing the player upon being hit by its falling coconut or touching it.
*   This creature is based on the real-life animal *Birgus latro*, commonly known as the Coconut Crab.
*   The Coconut Crab is among the fastest mobs, second only to Stick Bug in later stages of the [Stick Bug Challenge]. It is also one of the largest mobs in the game.
*   Like the Stump Snail, it prevents easy grinding in its guarded fields until defeated. Furthermore, it is the only mob protecting a shop; defeating it grants access to the [Coconut Cave] and allows for easier farming in the Coconut Field.
*   According to [Spirit Bear], the Crab is an invasive species accidentally brought into the game when she imported coconuts. It grew large due to her neglect.
*   Once defeated, standing on top of the Coconut Cave will not cause a teleport back to spawn; this feature was implemented to prevent players from cheating by defeating the Crab on that platform.
*   If you jump out of the field but quickly re-enter it, the crab’s HP bar resets, but its timer does not.
*   If [Digital Bee] stuns the Coconut Crab during a coconut drop phase, it will immediately revert to the claw attack phase once the stun wears off.
