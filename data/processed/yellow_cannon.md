# Yellow Cannon

The **Yellow Cannon** is a transportational machine available once the player has discovered 12 different bee types. It is situated on a hill, accessible from the Brave Bee Gate, located next to Science Bear and the Red Teleporter exit. The cannon operates with a 5-second cooldown period and targets the spawn platform.

## Usage and Shortcuts
When combined with the Parachute or Glider, the Yellow Cannon allows players to perform several shortcuts across the map. This combination also enables access to Royal Jelly tokens that were previously inaccessible, such as those found in the Dandelion Field (on top of hives), near the Noob Shop entrance of Star Hall, and near Brown Bear.

## Trivia
*   The Yellow Cannon is one of four cannons in the game; the others are the Blue Cannon, Red Cannon, and Gummy Cannon.
*   It is one of three transportational cannons (along with the Blue Cannon and Red Cannon).
*   The cannon has a 5-second cooldown. While this period is usually unnoticeable during regular gameplay due to the distance traveled, the cooldown can be observed by using the Glider or Parachute glitch to stop all momentum immediately after exiting the cannon. This shared cooldown applies to all transportational cannons.
