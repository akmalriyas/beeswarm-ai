# Music Bee

**Music Bee** is a Colorless, Legendary bee known for its beautiful buzz, which can motivate others. Its favorite treats are Blueberries, and it enjoys the Clover Field and Dandelion Field while disliking the Cactus Field.

***

## Statistics & Traits

| Trait | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Legendary |
| **Color** | Colorless |
| **Energy** | 20 |
| **Speed** | 16.1 |
| **Attack** | 1 |

### Gathering & Production Rates

*   **Pollen Collection:** Collects 16 Pollen in 4 seconds (equivalent to 4 White Boost Tokens per second).
    *   *With x2 Bee Pollen gamepass:* Collects 32 Pollen in 4 seconds.
*   **Honey Production:** Makes 240 Honey in 4 seconds (equivalent to 60 Honey per second).
    *   *With x2 Honeymaking Speed:* Produces 240 Honey in 2 seconds.

### Bonuses and Enhancements

*   **Base Gathering:** 16 Pollen / 4 seconds.
*   **Gifted Hive Bonus:** +25% Pollen From Bee Gathering.
*   **Stats Boosts:** +15% Movespeed, +6 Gather Amount, +160 Convert Amount.

***

## Abilities

Music Bee possesses three unique abilities:

*   **Melody:** Grants +100% Critical Power for 30 seconds. (Critical Power increases the pollen and damage of critical hits.)
*   **Focus:** Grants +3% Critical Chance for 20 seconds. This ability stacks up to 10 times. (Critical hits increase pollen and damage by 100%.)
*   **Token Link:** Collects all other Ability Tokens, granting 25 Honey (+10 per Level) per token collected.

## Trivia and Lore

*   Music Bee, along with Carpenter Bee, is one of the few Legendary bees added in a single update.
*   The bee's tail symbolizes a quaver (eighth note), and its face is designed as a slur between an eighth note and a dotted quarter note.
*   Music Bee is unique among Legendary bees for having three distinct abilities.
*   It is the only non-Blue bee that likes Blueberries.
*   Music Bee shares the same skin pattern with Shocked Bee.
*   This is the only bee that generates a Melody token without requiring Beequips.
*   A Special Jelly capable of transforming a bee into Music Bee was previously obtainable during the Beesmas 2019 event via Funky Presents. It later became available in the Beesmas Tree Bundle (Beesmas 2020) and, more recently, through the Percussive Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog (Beesmas Summer 2024). The Music Bee Egg was also obtainable from the Musical Mega-Bundle during Beesmas Winter 2024.
