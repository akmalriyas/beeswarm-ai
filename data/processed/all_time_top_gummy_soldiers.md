## Overview

The All-Time Top Gummy Soldiers leaderboard is one of the game's 64 leaderboards, accessible via the Global Leaderboards menu in the System Page. This board tracks the total amount of Goo collected by players over time. The display shows 10 positions at a time, and users can scroll to view up to the 100th place. Only legitimate players are maintained on these leaderboards; hackers and exploiters will be removed.
