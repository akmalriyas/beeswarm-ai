# Daily Top Red Pollen Collectors

The **Daily Top Red Pollen Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator, tracking how much red pollen players have collected on a given day. The leaderboard displays the top 10 positions at any time, but players can scroll to view up to 100th place.

This ranking resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Mechanics and Location
To check your current standing, players must step on the designated white pad in front of the leaderboard. A message will then appear stating: "You've collected 'X' Red Pollen today (Resets in 'Y')."

The leaderboard is located inside the Red HQ, situated between the ladder and the Red Field Booster.

## Prizes
Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 receive either the **Left Mythic Gem Fleuron Sticker** or the **Right Mythic Gem Fleuron Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 are awarded a [Ticket].
