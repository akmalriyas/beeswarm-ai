# Propeller Hat

![Propeller](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/96/Propeller_Hat.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403022321)

## Overview
The **Propeller Hat** is a [Hat Accessory](Category:Hats) that can be purchased at the Pro Shop. It is described as "A playful hat that keeps the sun out of your eyes."

> "_A playful hat that keeps the sun out of your eyes._"

## Stats and Cost
The Propeller Hat provides several beneficial bonuses to the wearer, but it is a costly item.

**Cost:**
*   2,500,000 Honey
*   25 Gumdrops
*   100 Pineapples
*   5 Moon Charms

**Bonus Stats:**
*   +15% Pollen
*   +5% Bee Ability Rate
*   +10% Defense
*   +10 Jump Power

## Trivia and History
*   This is the only available Hat in the Pro Shop.
*   It was historically the most expensive item available in the Pro Shop.
*   The Propeller Hat, along with Hiking Boots, are required to access Dapper Bear's Shop.
*   Among all masks, this hat (along with Beekeeper's Mask, Helmet, and Strange Goggles) is one of the few that does not originate from bees or NPCs.
*   When paired with Hiking Boots, it allows players to access the Rose Field from the Sunflower Field, enabling access to various Royal Jelly tokens across the map.
*   **Historical Changes:**
    *   After the 2018-09-10 Update, its stats were nerfed: +15 Jump Power was reduced to +10, and +4 Movespeed was removed.
    *   Before the 2018-11-25 Update, it cost 3,000,000 honey and required no crafting materials.
    *   Following the 2018-09-10 update, this became the only hat capable of granting Jump Power.
