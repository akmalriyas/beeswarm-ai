# Coconut Canister

[![CoconutCanister](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9b/Coconut_Canister.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20191006004936)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9b/Coconut_Canister.png/revision/latest?cb=20191006004936 "CoconutCanister")

**Description:**
A back-mounted coconut that protects you during emergencies.

The **Coconut Canister** is a bag item, purchasable in the Coconut Cave. It holds the most base capacity and has the highest price of all containers.

### Stats & Capacity

| Metric | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Base Cost | 25,000,000,000 Honey, 150 Tropical Drinks, 250 Coconuts, 150 Red Extracts, 150 Blue Extracts, and 2 Refreshing Vials. |
| Capacity | 1,000,000 Pollen. |

### Bonus Stats

*   +400% Convert Rate
*   x1.5 Convert Rate At Hive
*   +10% Instant Conversion
*   +10% Instant White Conversion
*   +100% White Pollen
*   **Passive: Inspire Coconuts**
*   **Passive: Emergency Coconut Shield**

---

### Passive Abilities Details

#### 🛡️ Emergency Coconut Shield
This passive activates when the player takes damage. The canister summons 5 falling coconuts and grants the "Coconut Shield" effect, which provides +100% Defense and x1.25 Bee Attack for 10 seconds. It has a 5-minute cooldown between activations.

#### 🌱 Inspire Coconuts
This passive activates after obtaining 5 stacks of Inspire Ability Tokens. When triggered, it summons 5 falling coconuts in the field.

### Trivia & Mechanics

*   The Coconut Canister, along with the Porcelain Port-O-Hive, Red Port-O-Hive, and Blue Port-O-Hive, is one of only three containers that requires materials other than honey for crafting.
*   If crafted using a Blender, the cost increases significantly to 25 Billion Honey, 1750 Coconuts, 15000 Pineapples, 15000 Sunflower Seeds, 7500 Strawberries, 7500 Blueberries, and 9000 Royal Jellies. This process requires 87.5 hours of Playtime or 1050 Tickets to speed up crafting.
*   When the Coconut Canister was initially introduced, it required only 150 Coconuts for crafting instead of the current 250, due to the maximum capacity limits at that time.
*   This is one of the few items in the game (along with Gummy Boots) that grants two passive abilities without requiring additional purchases.
*   The red and blue "buttons" on the canister glow alternately, mimicking ambulance lights. This visual effect syncs across all players who have the item equipped and relates to the Emergency Coconut Shield's siren sound upon activation.
*   **Activation Condition:** The player must take direct damage from a mob (e.g., Fire Ant). Taking damage from environmental hazards like fire trails, lawn mowers, or dying in an Obstacle Course will not activate the shield.
*   The Emergency Coconut Shield does not protect against the 'Twigs' ability from Stick Bug.
*   There is a rare bug where activating the Emergency Coconut Shield when the player should have died results in a very small amount of remaining health.
*   Although the item displays a x1.25 Capacity buff, this stat is intended to be a x1.25 Coconut Field Capacity buff and does not currently function as a capacity multiplier.
