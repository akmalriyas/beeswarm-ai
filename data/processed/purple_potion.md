# Purple Potion

[![Purple Potion](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Purple_Potion.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404023738)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Purple_Potion.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404023738)

> "Grants x1.25 Capacity and the improved boosts of Red and Blue Extracts and Glue for 15 minutes."

**Cooldown:** 1 second

A **Purple Potion** is a craftable inventory item introduced in the 2020-04-06 update. When consumed, it grants the following temporary buffs for 15 minutes:

*   x1.25 Capacity Multiplier
*   x1.25 Convert Rate At Hive
*   x1.5 Red Pollen boost
*   x1.5 Blue Pollen boost
*   x1.3 Bee Gather Pollen boost
*   x1.3 Tool Pollen boost

### Buff Mechanics and Overrides

A Purple Potion buff cannot coexist simultaneously with the buffs provided by **Red Extract**, **Blue Extract**, or **Glue**. If any of these competing buffs are active when a purple potion is used, they will be overridden. However, using a **Super Smoothie** will override the Purple Potion's buff because the Purple Potion is an ingredient in its crafting recipe.

## Acquisition Methods

### Crafting
The item can be crafted using a Blender with the following ingredients:
*   3 Red Extracts
*   3 Blue Extracts
*   3 Glues
*   3 Neonberries

### Quest and Event Rewards
Purple Potions can be obtained through various in-game activities, including:
*   Completing Black Bear's "Mountain Mix" quest.
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 6th, 7th, and 9th quests.
*   Receiving them as a reward from the Robo Bear Challenge.

### Sticker Stack Bonus
One Purple Potion is rewarded as a bonus for adding specific stickers to the Sticker Stack:
*   Abstract Color Painting Sticker
*   Purple Fleuron Sticker
*   Royal Bear Sticker
*   Royal Symbol Sticker

## Drops

**Mobs:**
The item can drop as a Rare item from certain mobs, including:
*   Rage Aphid
*   King Beetle
*   Tunnel Bear

**Planters:**
It can also be dropped by various planters. Specifically, planting a Red Clay Planter in a red field and then moving it to a blue field (or vice versa) has a high chance of dropping a Purple Potion. Other planters that may drop the item include:
*   Paper Planter
*   Plastic Planter
*   Candy Planter
*   Blue Clay Planter
*   Pesticide Planter
*   Hydroponic Planter
*   Petal Planter
*   Festive Planter

**Rare Drops:**
*   Very Rare drop from Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Exceptionally Rare drop from Wild Windy Bees.

## Outdated Acquisition Methods (Historical Data)

The following methods for obtaining Purple Potions are no longer active or have been superseded:
*   Donating strawberries to the Galentine Shrine during Beesmas (5 potions).
*   Giving Science Bear a present.
*   Purchasing specific bundles in Bee Bear's Catalog (e.g., Violet Bundle, Extract Bundle).
*   Receiving rare drops from Stockings.
*   Rewards for completing various Beesmas 2022 quests with Bee Bear and Honey Bee.
*   Opening certain Gift Boxes (Gummy, Mythic, Purple) during the event period.

## Trivia

*   Purple Potion is one of four consumables that can boost capacity, alongside Marshmallow Bee, Super Smoothie, and Snowflake.
*   Prior to its official addition, a glitch allowed it to be obtained early from King Beetle and Tunnel Bear; at this time, it appeared as a basic egg in the inventory but could be donated to the Wind Shrine.
*   Donating one Purple Potion to the Wind Shrine can grant winds ranging from 5x to 9x for 4-6 red/blue fields.
