## Overview
The Festive Planter is a single-use planter exclusive to Beesmas events, introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. It does not possess inherent growth or pollen multipliers but provides a substantial x3 multiplier to all nectar types.

### Harvesting Mechanics
When harvested:
1.  The planter yields up to 80 tokens worth of items.
2.  If the planter was fully grown, there is a **1/10 chance** to spawn a Puffshroom (guaranteed Common Puffshroom, level 1-2).
3.  If the planter was fully grown, one token is guaranteed to be a Ticket.

### Possible Drops List
The remaining tokens are picked randomly from this non-exhaustive list:
*   Tickets
*   Field Dice, Smooth Dice, Loaded Dice
*   Soft Waxes, Hard Waxes, Caustic Waxes, Swirled Waxes
*   Whirligigs, Jelly Beans, Gumdrops
*   Royal Jellies, Star Jellies
*   Honeysuckles, Enzymes, Oils, Red Extracts, Blue Extracts, Glitter, Glue
*   Micro-Converters, Pineapples, Strawberries, Blueberries, Sunflower Seeds, Bitterberries, Neonberries
*   Treats, Star Treats, Atomic Treats
*   Moon Charms, Night Bells, Purple Potions, Tropical Drinks
*   Flying Bee Bear Sticker (Rare), Super Smoothie (Rare)
*   Mythic Egg (Very Rare), Festive Bean (Exceptionally Rare), Star Egg (Exceptionally Rare)

## Ways to Obtain

### Current Methods
*   **Robux Shop:** Purchasing the "Feelin' Festive Pack" for 800 Robux grants 5 Festive Planters.
*   **Bee Bear's Catalog:**
    *   Purchasing the "Festive Fun Pack" (1,000 Snowflakes + 5 Gingerbread Bears) grants 1 Festive Planter.
    *   Purchasing the "Purple-Turple Mega Pack" (25,000 Snowflakes + 250 Gingerbread Bears) grants 10 Festive Planters.
*   **Quests/Rewards:**
    *   Completing Bee Bear's "An Extra Merry Mission."
    *   Completing Spirit Bear's Galentine Shrine quest.
    *   Completing Onett's Lid Art quest.
    *   As a reward for completing Rank 40 and 50 of your Robo Party.

## Trivia
*   The Festive Planter is the only planter capable of dropping Magic Beans.
*   It possesses the highest nectar multiplier available in the game (x3).
*   It belongs to a group of four non-reusable planters, alongside the Ticket Planter, Sticker Planter, and Paper Planter. Of these four, the Festive Planter is unique in being non-renewable.
