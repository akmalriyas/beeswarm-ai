The Highest Robo Bear Challenge Scores is one of the game's leaderboards. This leaderboard tracks and displays the top 100 players who have achieved the highest scores in the Robo Bear Challenge. It shows ten ranks at a time, allowing players to scroll down to view all entries up to the 100th place.

**Mechanics:**
When a player steps on the designated white pad associated with this leaderboard, a message will appear displaying their personal high score: "Your highest score in Robo Bear's Challenge: {Player's High Score}".

## Location

The leaderboard is located behind the Robo Bear character, to the left of his shop.

## Trivia

*   This leaderboard, along with the Highest Damage to a Single Puffshroom leaderboard, is one of only two leaderboards that features a background frame color other than brown.
*   It is also the only leaderboard that does not have a wooden frame.
