The **Top Brown Bear Helpers** is one of the game's leaderboards. This leaderboard tracks the top players who have completed the most quests for the [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear"). While it displays only 10 positions at a time, players can scroll down to view all 100 ranked participants.

### Mechanics and Location
The Brown Bear Helper leaderboard is unique among leaderboards because it does not feature an in-game progress pad for the player to track their current standing; instead, the Brown Bear provides quest completion feedback directly upon task fulfillment.

**Location:** The Brown Bear can be found to the right of the main Brown Bear area.

### Achievements and Milestones
*   **Brown Cub Skin:** On June 7, 2020, Lipsisas was the first user to reach 300 quests and obtain the [Brown Cub](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins "Cub Buddy") skin.
*   **1,000 Quests Milestone:** Sometime in mid-April 2023, 7Elevenss became the first user to complete 1,000 Brown Bear quests.

### Trivia and Game Notes
*   This is the only leaderboard that counts the total amount of quests completed specifically for a [bear](/wiki/Category:Bears "Category:Bears").
*   Historically, there were three leaderboards lacking progress pads. The [Most Commando Captures](/wiki/Most_Commando_Captures "Most Commando Captures") leaderboard was removed from the game, and the [Highest Damage to a Single Puffshroom](/wiki/Highest_Damage_to_a_Single_Puffshroom "Highest Damage to a Single Puffshroom") leaderboard received a progress pad later on.
*   The current top player, 7Elevenss, also holds the top rank on the [Top Riley Bee Helpers](/wiki/Top_Riley_Bee_Helpers "Top Riley Bee Helpers") leaderboard.
