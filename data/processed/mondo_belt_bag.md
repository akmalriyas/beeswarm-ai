# Mondo Belt Bag

![Mondo Belt Bag](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ad/Mondo_Belt_Bag.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403020419)

## Overview
The Mondo Belt Bag is a belt accessory found in the Mountain Top Shop. It is described as "A highly embellished Belt Bag imported from a far away land."

### Stats and Bonuses
*   **Capacity:** +100,000
*   **Convert Amount:** +50
*   **Loot Luck:** +75%
*   **Buzz Bomb Pollen:** +10%
*   **Bee Attack:** +1 (Colorless Bee Attack)

### Crafting Cost
To craft the Mondo Belt Bag, you need:
*   12,400,000 Honey
*   1 Soft Wax
*   150 Pineapples
*   150 Sunflower Seeds
*   10 Stingers

## Trivia
*   This is the second belt accessory awarded by Sun Bear during his first visit; the first was the standard Belt Bag.
*   In the 2018-09-10 update, Sun Bear returned for a second visit wearing the Mondo Belt Bag and Beekeeper's Boots. The version worn by Sun Bear is notably larger than the player's item.
*   Prior to the 2018-11-25 update, the Mondo Belt Bag did not require Stingers, Pineapples, or Sunflower Seeds for purchase.
*   This belt accessory ranks as the fourth strongest in the game, following the Honeycomb Belt, Petal Belt, and Coconut Belt.
*   It is one of the few items that transitioned from being a quest reward to becoming purchasable in a shop. Other items with this quality include the standard Belt Bag and the original three pairs of boots.
