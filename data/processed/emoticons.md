# Emoticons

Emoticons are a mechanic used to display a bee's current mood. A bee's mood can affect several aspects of gameplay, including how much bond it gains when collecting pollen, its movement speed, and whether it can find certain stickers while gathering. It does not impact other core statistics, such as the pollen collection rate or honey conversion speed.

## Bee Moods

**Happy:**
A bee expresses happiness if it is fed a treat or if it is working in (or most recently was working) a field that it likes. When happy, bees gain extra bond from gathering in fields they like and move slightly faster than usual. The Precise Bee also expresses happiness when successfully completing its Target Practice ability.

**Sad:**
A bee expresses unhappiness if it is working in a field it dislikes or if the last field it worked in was one it disliked. Bees gain less bond from gathering in fields they dislike, but their movement speed does not decrease.
