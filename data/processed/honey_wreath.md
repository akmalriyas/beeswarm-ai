# Honey Wreath

[![The Honey Wreath's appearance after completing Black Bear's Beesmas quest.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0e/Honey_Wreath.png/revision/latest?cb=20201228102524)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0e/Honey_Wreath.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/122?cb=20201228102524)

The **Honey Wreath** is a Beesmas machine unlocked after completing [Black Bear]'s Honey Wreath quest.

## Mechanics and Usage
When activated, the Honey Wreath converts all of the player's [pollen] into [honey]. The amount of honey obtained is calculated by multiplying the pollen count by the player's [Honey Per Pollen] stat, scattering eight honey tokens around the wreath for collection. This total yield is further multiplied by the [Honey From Tokens] system page modifier.

The machine has a cooldown of 30 minutes and is located between the [Windy Bee Gate] and the [Ticket Tent].

**Usage Notes:**
*   If used before being decorated, the wreath displays the message: "This Wreath looks frankly uninspired..."
*   If the player uses the Honey Wreath with less than 801 pollen in their bag, each token rewards 100 honey (for a total of 800), which is then modified by [Honey Per Pollen] and [Honey From Tokens].

## Upgrades
The Honey Wreath can be upgraded by completing Honey Bee's Beesmas quest, "Honey Bee’s Honey Wreath?". This upgrade provides two benefits:
1.  It grants 50% more honey upon use.
2.  It summons a level 20 [Honey Bee] that lasts for 30 minutes.

## Appearance Evolution
The appearance of the wreath changes based on player progression:
*   **Initial State:** Before completing Black Bear's quest, it appears as an ordinary wreath.
*   **Black Bear Completion:** After completing "Black Bear's Honey Wreath" quest, the wreath is decorated with honey icons and features the [Flight of the Bumble Egg].
*   **Honey Bee Upgrade:** Upon completing Honey Bee's upgrade quest, the wreath changes to feature a Gifted [Honey Bee] face in the center.

## Trivia
*   The wreath model originates from the Christmas Wreath hat on Roblox's catalog.
*   The egg hung on the wreath is the [Flight of the Bumble Egg], which was a limited item obtainable during the [Egg Hunt 2019] event.
*   Similar to [coconuts], the honey received from this wreath is increased by the [Honey From Tokens] modifier.
*   This is one of four Beesmas machines that grants temporary bees (only when upgraded via Honey Bee's quest). The others are the [Honeyday Candles], the [Gummy Beacon], and [Onett's Lid Art].
*   The summoned Honey Bee can attack mobs alongside the player's regular bees. For example, if the player's bees attack a [King Beetle], the summoned bee will also participate in the attack.
    *   If the player converts pollen at their hive while the upgraded Honey Bee is present, the bee remains idle near the player and does not convert pollen unless an [Ability Tokens] Mark is active.
*   Using the Honey Wreath is a requirement for certain quests given by both [Bee Bear] and [Honey Bee (NPC)].
