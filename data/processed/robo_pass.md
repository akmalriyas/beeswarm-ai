# Robo Pass

A **Robo Pass** is an inventory item introduced in the 2022-12-26 update. It is required for players to participate in the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge).

## Item Details

*   **Usage:** Used by talking to Robo Bear and initiating Round 1 of the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   **Maximum Cap:** Players can hold a maximum of 10 Robo Passes, unless they are purchased through Robux packs or received via specific quests/rewards that bypass this limit.
*   **Cooldown:** None.

## Acquisition Methods (Current)

Robo Passes can be obtained through several methods:

### Dispensers
*   **Free Dispenser:** Located past the Bear Gate, this dispenser provides one Robo Pass every 22 hours.
*   **Ticketed Dispenser:** Located to the right of the Free Dispenser, this dispenser grants a Robo Pass for 50 Tickets and has no cooldown.

### Drops & Rewards
Robo Passes can be acquired as rare drops or rewards from various sources:
*   **Mob Drops:** Rhino Beetles, Ladybugs, Spiders, Scorpions, Coconut Crab, Tunnel Bear, King Beetle, and Armored Aphids.
*   **Location Finds:** A token is hidden behind a rock in the Coconut Cave, and another is located on the green cog near Star Hall.
*   **Challenges & Resources:** As a possible reward from the Ant Challenge or as a drop from Puffshrooms.
*   **Sticker Rewards:** Adding specific stickers to the Sticker Stack grants Robo Passes:
    *   Robot Head Sticker (1 Pass)
    *   Party Robo Bear Sticker (1 Pass)

## Acquisition Methods (Historical/Outdated)

The following methods were available in previous updates but are no longer active or relevant:

*   **Beesmas 2022 Drops:** Tokens were previously hidden behind the Robo Pass Dispensers, inside The Computer, and within the Beesmas Tree.
*   **Level Rewards:** Defeating Level 4, 11, and 18 Snowbears provided passes.
*   **Event Bundles & Quests:** Passes were distributed as rewards from specific bundles (e.g., Digital Bee pack, Mondo Robo Bundle) or certain Beesmas 2022 quests (Onett's, Riley Bee's, Bucko Bee's, Stick Bug's).
*   **Gift Boxes:** Certain Gift Boxes (Charcoal, Obnoxious, Tropical) provided one pass. Additionally, giving Presents to specific characters during the Beesmas 2022 Event granted passes.
*   **Catalog Purchases:** The Reindeer Games Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog offered a large quantity of Robo Passes.

## Trivia

*   The implementation of the Robo Pass was historically complex, taking over three years after it was initially leaked on April 4, 2019.
*   Unlike Ant Passes, Robo Passes cannot be donated to the Wind Shrine and will not appear in the donation selection menu.
