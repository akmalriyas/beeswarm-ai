# Field Capacity

**Field Capacity** is a mechanic that increases the player's Capacity Multiplier depending on the type of field they are in. There are two types of field capacity: colored field capacity (displayed as a stat) and specific field capacity (displayed as a buff).

## Ways to Obtain

Players can gain Field Capacity through various methods:

*   **Sticker Donation:** Donating certain stickers to the sticker stack can grant field capacity.
*   **Red Field Capacity:** Can be obtained from Riley Bee's gifted hive bonus, the Demon Mask, and Scorching Star (Passive Ability).
*   **Blue Field Capacity:** Can be obtained from Bucko Bee's gifted hive bonus and Bubble Bloat (a buff that requires having the Pop Star passive on the Supreme Star Amulet).
*   **White Field Capacity:** Can be obtained from Exhausted Bee's gifted hive bonus, the Gummy Mask, and Gummy Star (Passive Ability).
*   **Coconut Field Capacity:** Obtained via the Coconut Canister (x1.25 multiplier).
*   **Codes:** Redeeming certain valid codes:
    *   `ThreeBeeVee`: Grants Mountain Top Field Code Buff (+100% Mountain Top Field Capacity).
*   **Beequips:** Using Beequips that include a Field Capacity boost stat:
    *   **Warm Scarf:** Grants +1% to +3% Red and White Field Capacity.
    *   **Pinecone:** Grants +8% to +15% Pine Tree Forest Capacity.
    *   **Snow Tiara:** Grants +2% to +5% Blue Field Capacity and +3% to +5% White Field Capacity.
    *   **Rose Headband:** Grants 14% to +40% Rose Field Capacity.

    *   All Ready Player Two codes provided various field Code Buffs.

## Colored Field Capacity Formula

The following formula determines the capacity multiplier a player receives in a field:

Let $x$ equal the colored capacity multiplier (the base stat).
Let $y$ equal the percentage of that pollen type present in the field.

Capacity Multiplier in the Field $= \left( x - 1 \right) \times \frac{y}{100} + 1$

## Trivia

*   The Spider Field is the only field where a player is guaranteed to receive 100% of a colored capacity bonus, specifically white field capacity. This can be achieved using Exhausted Bee's gifted hive bonus, the Gummy Mask, activating Gummy Star, or utilizing the Warm Scarf's or Snow Tiara's hive bonuses.
*   The Mountain Top Field is the opposite; it generates no white flowers and therefore does not grant white field capacity.
*   It is possible to achieve 100% of a colored capacity bonus on any other field if that field exclusively generates one color of flowers, but this occurrence is exceptionally rare.
