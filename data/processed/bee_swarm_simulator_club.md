# Bee Swarm Simulator Club

The **[Bee Swarm Simulator Club](https://www.roblox.com/groups/3982592)** is a massive Roblox group associated with *Bee Swarm Simulator*. It currently boasts over 18 million members (as of February 2026).

## Group Details
*   **Group Owner:** Onett
*   **Roles:** Members, Silenced, Deer people, Community Contributor, Moderator, Admin, Group Manager, Omelette
*   **Members:** 18M+
*   **Games Owned:** None (Bee Swarm Simulator is not owned by this group)

## Mechanics and Significance
Joining the Bee Swarm Simulator Club serves as a crucial prerequisite for accessing several in-game features. Membership grants players access to:
*   The [Sticker Printer](/wiki/Sticker_Printer)
*   The [Honey Dispenser](/wiki/Honey_Dispenser)
*   The [Treat Dispenser](/wiki/Treat_Dispenser)
*   The [Strawberry Dispenser](/wiki/Strawberry_Dispenser)
*   The [Blueberry Dispenser](/wiki/Blueberry_Dispenser)

Furthermore, redeeming certain in-game [codes](/wiki/Codes) requires the player to be a member of this group (e.g., ClubConverters).

## Trivia and History
*   **Beesmas Requirement:** During the Beesmas events of 2020 and 2021, players needed to utilize the Honey Dispenser to complete their quests. This mechanic effectively forced users who wished to participate in Beesmas to join the club.
*   **Science Bear Quest:** The quest "The Power of Information" from Science Bear also requires members to join the club to obtain all necessary translators, necessitating the use of both the Strawberry and Blueberry Dispensers.
*   **Membership Ranking:** At one point, the Bee Swarm Simulator Club held the title of having the most members among all Roblox groups. It was later overtaken by Scriptbloxian Studios in 2021. The club currently ranks among the top groups on Roblox.
