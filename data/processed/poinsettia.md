# Poinsettia

Poinsettia is a Level 6 Beesmas Beequip with an equip limit of two. It has no color requirement and can only be equipped to bees that know the Boost ability.

"A gorgeous red flower so vibrant it is reserved for a special time of the year."
🎄Beesmas

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Ways
1. Purchasing a Poinsettia Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
2. As a rare reward from the Ant Challenge during the Beesmas event (higher scores increase drop chance).

### Outdated Ways
1. Reward from Spirit Bear's Galentine's Shrine quest in Beesmas 2021.
2. Purchasing the Beequip Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog for 75 Snowflakes and 15 Gingerbread Bears.
3. Purchasing the Poinsettia Pack in Bee Bear's Catalog for 500 Snowflakes and 15 Gingerbread Bears.

## Trivia
* After Beesmas 2022, this Beequip remained obtainable through the Ant Challenge. This makes it the second Beesmas Beequip to still be available after its event period (the first being Icicles).
* Poinsettia, along with Lei and Rose Headband, is one of the few Beequips that provides a Gather Pollination Chance buff, making it an alternative method for bees to pollinate flowers besides using Fuzzy Bee abilities.
* During Beesmas 2025, when a player gifted Riley Bee, the game mentioned the present was a Poinsettia with a 20% Red Pollen Hive Bonus.
