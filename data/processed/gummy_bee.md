# Gummy Bee

**Gummy Bee** is a Colorless Event bee, known for its sweet, squishy appearance and ability to cover flowers in goo to grant bonus honey.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Event |
| **Color** | Colorless |
| **Energy** | 50 |
| **Speed** | 14 |
| **Attack** | 3 |

### Acquisition and Gifted Status

Gummy Bee can be purchased for 2,500 Gumdrops from the Gummy Bee Egg Claim.

Like all Event bees, it does not have a favorite treat. To make Gummy Bee gifted, you must feed it one of the following:
*   Star Treat
*   Gingerbread Bear
*   Aged Gingerbread Bear

### Habitat Preferences

Gummy Bee likes the Pineapple Patch, Mountain Top Field, and Stump Field. It dislikes the Pumpkin Patch.

## Stats & Mechanics

**Pollen Collection:**
Gummy Bee collects 10 Pollen in 4 seconds (equivalent to 2.5 per second). With a White Boost Token, this increases to 20 Pollen in 4 seconds (5 per second). With an x2 Bee Pollen gamepass, it can collect 20 Pollen in 4 seconds (5 per second).

**Honey Production:**
Gummy Bee makes 700 Honey in 4 seconds (equivalent to 175 per second). With an x2 Honeymaking Speed buff, this increases to 700 Honey in 2 seconds.

**Stat Bonuses:**
*   +150% Energy
*   +620 Convert Amount
*   +2 Attack
*   🌟 **Gifted Hive Bonus:** +5% Honey Per Pollen

### Abilities

Gummy Bee possesses two unique abilities:

*   **Gumdrop Barrage:** Launches Gumdrops in a large area, covering the field in goo. Flowers covered in this goo grant bonus Honey, with the bonus increasing based on the size of the Goo puddle.
*   **Glob:** Covers 49 surrounding flowers in goo. If Gummy Bee is gifted, it covers 81 flowers instead.

## Gallery and Visuals

*   **Hive Slots:** The bee has distinct hive slot designs for both its standard form and gifted status.
*   **Forms:** Gummy Bee can enter a "Gummy Morph" state, which is also available for the gifted version.
*   **Appearance:** It is noted as being translucent and emits a faint light from its eyes.

## Trivia

*   **Unique Traits:** Gummy Bee is the only Event bee that does not have extra body parts. It is also the only translucent bee in the game.
*   **Goo Mechanics:** The egg claim allows players to use Gumdrops after touching it, teleporting them to Gummy Bear's Lair (requires the Goo Hotshot Badge).
*   **First Edition Status:** Along with Bear Bee, Tabby Bee, Puppy Bee, Festive Bee, Digital Bee, and Windy Bee, Gummy Bee is one of the few Event bees that can be a First Edition Bee.
*   **Historical Availability:** It was once obtainable by completing all 15 quests for Gummy Bear. Before the 2019-04-05 Update, it could be bought in the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets. Festive Bee replaced it afterward.
*   **Attack Buffs:** When Gummy Morph is active, its base attack reaches 303, which is approximately 30 times the base attack of Lion Bee.
*   **Stickers:** Gummy Bee has a chance to find specific stickers in certain fields: Launching Rocket Sticker in Clover Field, and a rare Yellow Sticky Hand Sticker in Sunflower Field.
*   **Masks:** It is the only Event bee to have a mask based on its design (the Gummy Mask).
*   **Iconography:** The Gummy Bee and Diamond Bee are the only bees that possess a surrounding sparkle effect.
