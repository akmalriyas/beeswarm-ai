## Overview and Mechanics

The All-Time Top Robo Bear Challenge Scores is one of the game's leaderboards. This leaderboard tracks the top players who have achieved the highest scores in the **Robo Bear Challenge**. It displays ten ranks at a time, allowing players to scroll down to view all 100 ranked participants.

When a player steps on the designated white pad in front of the leaderboard, a message appears displaying their personal high score: "Your highest score in Robo Bear's Challenge: {Player's High Score}".

## Location

The leaderboard is situated behind the **Robo Bear** character, to the left of his shop.

## Trivia

*   This leaderboard, along with the Highest Damage to a Single Puffshroom leaderboard, is one of only two leaderboards that features a background frame color other than brown.
*   It is also the only leaderboard that does not have a wooden frame.
