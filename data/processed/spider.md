# Spider

The **Spider** is a level 4 mob found exclusively in the Spider Field. It is not to be confused with the Cave Monster, which has a similar appearance but different stats.

## Stats and Mechanics

| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Health | 100 | |

| Battle Points (BP) | 10 | Reward upon defeat. |
| Bond | 40 | |
| Honey | 1,000 | Spread across 5 tokens. |

**Respawn Time:**
*   Standard: 30 minutes
*   With Gifted Bee (Vicious Bee): 25 minutes 30 seconds

## Location Details

The Spider is found in the **Spider Field**, which borders the Strawberry Field, Bamboo Fields, and the Basic Bee Gate.

| Field | Quantity | Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Spider Field (and surrounding area) | 1 | 4 |

## Strategies for Engagement

1.  **Idle Combat:** To maintain a safe distance while defeating the Spider, remain on the first block of the Bamboo Obby. The spider will attempt to lunge but will be stopped by the obstacle. Alternatively, stand near the edge of the Vicious Bee Egg Claim area.
2.  **Glitching:** Utilize the Frozen Field Defenders Glitch method to defeat the Spider easily, similar to any other standard mob.
3.  **Aerial Attack:** Charge at the spider, then jump and glide over it. This technique requires either a Parachute or a Glider item.

## Trivia

*   The Spider, Mantis, and Werewolf are the only mobs capable of attacking players outside of their designated fields.
*   A Ticket token can be found within its residence (the Spider Cave).
*   Along with the Werewolf, the Spider has one of the fewest spawn locations among regular mobs.
*   Unlike real-life spiders, which typically have eight legs and six or eight eyes, the in-game spider only possesses six legs and four eyes. This discrepancy is humorously noted in the description for the Forward Facing Spider sticker: *"Not enough legs to technically be a spider."*
*   Two creatures similar in appearance to the Spider, known as Cave Monsters, are found inside the Werewolf's Cave; however, their statistics are vastly different from the Spider.
*   Spiders possess a faint red glow under their eyes, which is most noticeable at night.
