# Stockings

The **Stockings** are a machine exclusive to Beesmas, which can be unlocked after completing the "Brown Bear" Beesmas quest. They were introduced during the Beesmas 2020 update and have returned every subsequent year.

## Location and Mechanics

The Stockings are located at the bottom of the hill where Brown Bear stands, next to the Clover Field.

When used, the machine spawns one random Beequip and two additional random items in front of it. The cooldown for using the Stockings is 1 hour.

## Drop Rates and Quality

The quality of the Beequip dropped by the Stockings is determined by a formula based on the number of bees ($\text{cnt}$) the player has when claiming the item.

**Quality Calculation:**
1. Calculate $\text{middle} = \lfloor \frac{\text{cnt}}{50} \rfloor$, clamped between 0 and 1.
2. The quality range is defined by: $0.1 \times \text{middle}^{1.5}$ to $0.5 + 0.4 \times \text{middle}^{1.5}$.

## Trivia

*   If a player attempts to use the Stockings before completing Brown Bear's 2021 Beesmas quest, a message will display: *"This mantle looks so barren without Stockings on the hooks..."*
*   Each time the Stockings are used, players can only receive one of each item type, with the exception of Gumdrops and Moon Charms.
*   Tokens spawned by the Stockings can be collected using an Ability Token Link.
*   The Loot Luck ability does not affect the drops received from the Stockings.
