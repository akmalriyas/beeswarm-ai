# Robo Party Cake

[![The Robo Party Cake from Beesmas 2022.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/05/Cake.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20230401193148)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/05/Cake.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20230401193148)

The **Robo Party Cake** is a Beesmas-exclusive machine introduced in the second part of Beesmas 2022, and it has returned to every subsequent Beesmas event. It becomes accessible after completing the [Robo Bear's Party Cake quest](Robo_Bear#Beesmas_Quest_-_Robo_Bear's_Party_Cake) and can initiate a special challenge every three hours.

## Appearance

Before Robo Bear's quest is completed, the cake appears as only three metal trusses stacked in a pyramid shape, topped with an unlit olive green candle. This candle's number changes annually to reflect the game's age.

After the quest is finished, the "number" candle lights up with neon lights and a blue outline. The cake layers are olive green, featuring different colored frames: the bottom layer is blue, the middle is yellow, and the top is purple. Candles and sparklers adorn the corners, while neon sprinkles decorate the front of each layer.

## Robo Party Event Mechanics

The **Robo Party** is a server-wide event requiring all participants to defeat the scattered Party Robots found in the [Fields](Fields). The party begins immediately after a player interacts with the cake and has a strict time limit of three minutes.

*   **Success:** If all Party Robots are defeated, all participants receive the [Robo Party Blessing](Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Other), and the initiating player is rewarded with items and the ability to summon a higher-ranked Robo Party.
*   **Failure:** If not all party mobs are killed within the time limit, the initiating player's rank is lowered.

Robo Parties can also be summoned manually by [Onett](Onett_(Developer)).

If successful, the player who initiated the Robo Party receives an amount of Robo Party Blessing equal to the final achieved rank (e.g., completing Rank 5 rewards the player with 5 stacks of Robo Party Blessing).

### Mobs
The Robo Party summons party variants of [Robo Bear Challenge](Robo_Bear_Challenge) robots. Unlike standard challenges, every enemy spawns in a field and remains there for the duration of the party. The rank determines the level, health, and quantity of the summoned robots. There are four types of robots:

*   [Party Mechsquitoes](Party_Mechsquito)
*   [Party Cogmowers](Party_Cogmower)
*   [Party Cogturrets](Party_Cogturret)
*   [Party Mega Mechsquitoes](Party_Mega_Mechsquito)

Note that Party Cogturrets spawn starting at Rank 5, and Party Mega Mechsquitoes spawn starting at Rank 15.

### Strategies for Robo Parties
To maximize success in a Robo Party:

*   **Stingers:** Using [Stingers](Stinger) grants bees x1.5 attack damage for 30 seconds, which is highly effective against high-level mobs like Party Cogturrets or Party Mega Mechsquitoes.
*   **Star Saw:** If the player possesses [Star Saw](Passive_Abilities#Star_Saw), it circles them for 30 seconds after activation. While difficult to hit flying enemies (Mechsquitoes/Mega Mechsquitoes), it allows easy damage against Party Cogmowers and Party Cogturrets while bees are distracted by the flyers.
*   **Crowd Control:** [Vicious Bee](Vicious_Bee) and [Windy Bee](Windy_Bee) provide excellent crowd control, especially since mobs often appear in pairs or packs (excluding Mega Mechsquitoes). Digital Bees can stun multiple mobs for extra damage via Mind Hack.
*   **Targeting:** Party Cogturrets shoot straight at their facing direction; avoid standing directly in front of them. Party Cogmowers do not aim specifically at the player, allowing players to easily sidestep them.
*   **Positioning:** Stand near the party mobs so your bees can lock on and deal damage quickly.
*   **Evasion:** Party Mechsquitoes and Mega Mechsquitoes target the player's current location, not where they are moving. Moving in circles often causes these flying enemies to miss their attacks.
*   **Safety:** Always monitor your health. It is easy to become overly focused on combat during a Robo Party. If you die, look for fields containing Party Robots upon respawning until your bees recover.
