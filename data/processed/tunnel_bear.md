# Tunnel Bear

Tunnel Bear is a hostile Level 9 boss mob found in the White Tunnel. It is one of five bosses in the game, alongside King Beetle, Coconut Crab, Stick Bug, and Mondo Chick.

**Respawns:**
*   Standard: Every 48 hours (2 days).
*   Gifted Hive Bonus: Every 40 hours and 48 minutes (1.7 days) if a Gifted Vicious Bee is present in the player's hive.

## Location and Entry

Tunnel Bear spawns in the White Tunnel, which is accessible via the Instant Converter near Science Bear.

**Access Methods:**
1.  Jump through the chimney on top of the Instant Converter near the Pineapple Patch.
2.  Enter the right end of the Instant Converter and drop down into the chute.
3.  Climb up to Yellow Cannon/Science Bear and jump down from there.

Upon entering, players land in a small entryway with green and white walls. Players must pass through a semi-transparent white wall to find the tunnel proper. Tunnel Bear will quickly drop down near the entrance after this point. The walls gradually narrow deeper into the tunnel.

**Exiting:**
To exit the White Tunnel, stand on the Teleport Pad (a flat gray circle in the middle of the tunnel). This teleports the player to the Instant Converter near the Sunflower Field.

If Tunnel Bear does not appear upon entry, a countdown timer will float in mid-air, indicating the time remaining until it respawns.

## Battle Strategy

Tunnel Bear moves slowly and predictably along its path toward the player's location. Players have 2 minutes and 30 seconds to defeat it before it despawns.

**General Tactics:**
*   **Distance Management:** Maintain a safe distance from Tunnel Bear. While staying too far away stops your bees from attacking, getting too close risks instant death due to its large hitbox (which is wider than its body).
*   **Engagement Strategy:** A recommended strategy is to walk in front of the bear and stay just close enough for your bees to attack while leading it down the tunnel.
*   **Early Tunnel Fight:** For the first defeat, keeping Tunnel Bear near the beginning of the White Tunnel and staying close to one of the side walls can slow it slightly (as it has no wall collisions). This strategy is necessary if you are aiming to secure a Gifted Egg before the tunnel narrows too much.
*   **High-Level Bees:** If your bees are mostly Level 9, they should be able to defeat Tunnel Bear quickly without needing complex maneuvering.

**Advanced Tips & Considerations:**
*   **Token Collection:** When collecting tokens, especially near low health, avoid risky actions. Do not attempt to grab rage tokens directly next to the boss; maintain distance until it is defeated. Cub Buddies can help collect tokens behind the bear.
*   **Pre-Activation:** Players can pre-activate Ability Tokens like Rage and Focus using enemies in the Pineapple Patch or Stump Field.
*   **Bee Optimization:** Use bees with high Attack stats (e.g., Cobalt Bee, Vicious Bee, Lion Bee) or those that boost fighting capabilities (Looker Bee, Gifted Brave Bee).
*   **Energy Management:** Bees like Photon Bee and Exhausted Bee have endless energy. If your bees are low on energy, fight other mobs until they sleep to recharge.
*   **Glitch Exploitation:** A highly effective method is to exploit a glitch: when Tunnel Bear drops down into the tunnel, walk purposefully to the left side of the tunnel. Its arm will clip into the wall, causing it to become stuck and significantly easier to defeat.

## Drops

Tunnel Bear's tokens drop in a straight line upon defeat. The likelihood of receiving multiple types of drops increases with Loot Luck.

**Guaranteed (First Defeat):**
*   200 Battle Points
*   1,000 Bond
*   5,000,000 Honey
*   A random Gifted Egg (Silver, Gold, or Diamond) OR a Star Egg.

**Subsequent Defeats:**
*   200 Battle Points
*   1,000 Bond
*   5,000,000 Honey
*   Possible drops include: Royal Jellies (5), Tickets (10), Moon Charms (20), Gingerbread Bears (Beesmas only).

**Potential Loot Drops:**
The boss can also drop various items based on chance and player luck, including:
*   Royal Jelly (Increments of 5, 10, 20, or 50)
*   Tickets (Increments of 10, 15, 20, 25, 50, or 100)
*   Gumdrops (Increments of 25-250)
*   Treats (Increments of 15-50, 100, 500, 5000, or 50,000)
*   Sunflower Seeds / Pineapples (Increments of 50-500)
*   Micro-Converters (Increments of 1, 5, or 10)
*   Enzymes / Oils / Magic Beans (Increments of 1, 3, 5, or 10)
*   Moon Charms (Increments of 5, 20, 40, or 100)
*   Robo Passes / Ant Pass / Glitter (Common)
*   Star Jelly (Rare)
*   Loaded Dice (Rare)
*   Diamond Egg / Gifted Silver/Gold Eggs / Basic Bee Egg (Very Rare)
*   Gifted Diamond Egg / Black Balloon / Night Bell / Purple Potion / Basic Black Hive Skin / Super Smoothie / Star Egg (Extremely Rare or Nearly Impossible)

**Trivia:**
*   Tunnel Bear uses the Cartoony Animation Pack.
*   When Tunnel Bear despawns (due to player death, timer expiration, or leaving the area), it performs a wave animation (/e wave). It also waves when stunned by Digital Bee's Mind Hack.
*   Like King Beetle and Cave Monsters, Tunnel Bear has a hitbox slightly wider than its body, meaning players can die upon contact without physically touching the bear.
*   The despawn timer was introduced in the 2019-04-05 update, making it much harder to defeat by simply circulating the tunnel with low attack rates.
*   Tunnel Bear is one of only two mobs (the other being Mythic Meteor Shower) that drops loot in a straight line.
*   It was added as the second boss and the first hostile bear among three (Gummy Bear and Snowbear).
*   Tunnel Bear looks identical to Shadow Bear, but unlike Shadow Bear, it is hostile and kills players upon contact.
