# Masks

This category contains all pages detailing various hats and masks, which fall under the larger category of [Accessories](/wiki/Category:Accessories). These items are worn on the player's head.

Masks and hats can be crafted using materials such as Honey and other resources. They provide a variety of unique boosts to the player.

Some masks possess passive abilities. For example, the [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask) grants the Coin Scatter ability. It is possible for certain masks to have two passive abilities if one is unlocked through ownership of another mask (e.g., possessing the Ignite passive on the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask) by owning the [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask)).
