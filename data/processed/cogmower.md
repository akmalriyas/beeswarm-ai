# Cogmower

*Note: Do not confuse the standard Cogmower with its rarer, golden counterpart, the [Golden Cogmower](/wiki/Golden_Cogmower "Golden Cogmower"), or the Beesmas Edition variant, the [Party Cogmower](/wiki/Party_Cogmower "Party Cogmower").*

## Overview

A Cogmower is a mob that exclusively appears during the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"). It is the second mob encountered in this challenge, appearing as early as Round 5.

### Stats

| Attribute | Details |

| **Damage** | 50 (Increases with level) |
| **Health** | Increases with level and round. |
| **Level Range** | 5–22 |

## Mechanics and Behavior

Cogmowers rise from the field before moving slowly around aimlessly in a straight line, destroying layers and removing goo from flowers upon passing near them.

*   **Movement:** When the player switches fields, the Cogmower sinks into its former location and ascends to follow the player in their current or recently visited field.
*   **Collision:** They bounce in different directions when hitting the field borders or another Cogmower.
*   **Damage Resistance:** Cogmowers only take half the damage from [Critical Hits](/wiki/Critical_Hits "Critical Hits").
*   **Pollen Drain:** The higher a Cogmower's level, the more pollen it drains from flowers.

### Round Specific Spawning

**General Behavior (Non-Target Rounds):** When not in specific target rounds, Cogmowers move and follow the player across fields.

**Round 10 (Main Target Round):**
In Round 10, Cogmowers are a primary target mob. Players must defeat 10 of them. They do not randomly spawn but remain fixed in their initial field and will not follow the player if they move to another area.
*   **Set 1:** 4 Cogmowers (Spawns in Pineapple Patch, Bamboo Field, or Clover Field).
*   **Set 2:** 3 Cogmowers (Spawns in Dandelion Field, Sunflower Field, or Strawberry Field).
*   **Set 3:** 3 Cogmowers (Spawns in Cactus Field, Pumpkin Patch, or Pine Tree Forest).

**Round 20 (Main Target Round):**
In Round 20, players must defeat 20 Cogmowers. Four sets of 5 Cogmowers spawn. They appear in fields similar to Round 10, with the addition of the [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field").

## Strategies and Counterplay

*   **Targeting:** Cogmowers do not actively aim for the player, allowing players to easily sidestep them.
*   **Hitbox Advantage:** Their hitbox is positioned directly in front of the mob rather than over it, meaning players can stand inside a Cogmower while ensuring their bees target it effectively.
*   **Engagement:** Positioning near a Cogmower allows your bees to lock on and deal damage quickly.
*   **Crowd Control:** [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee") and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") are excellent for crowd control, especially since they often appear in packs. [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee") can stun multiple Cogmowers simultaneously, enabling them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mind_Hack "Ability Tokens").
*   **Area Damage:** The `Star Saw` Passive from the [Supreme Star Amulet](/wiki/Star_Amulet#Supreme_Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") is useful as it circles the player for 30 seconds and can damage multiple Cogmowers at once.
*   **Field Regeneration:** Using Frogs, Pollen Haze, or multiple [Supreme Saturators](/wiki/The_Supreme_Saturator "The Supreme Saturator") can provide significant field regeneration to counteract the flowers drained by the mowers.

## Trivia

*   The name Cogmower is a portmanteau of "Cog" and "Lawnmower."
*   In addition to the [Golden Cogmower](/wiki/Golden_Cogmower "Golden Cogmower"), they also have a Beesmas variant called the [Party Cogmower](/wiki/Party_Cogmower "Party Cogmower").
*   Cogmowers, Golden Cogmowers, Party Cogmowers, [Cogturrets](/wiki/Cogturret "Cogturret"), Party Cogturrets, and [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") are the only mobs capable of collecting layers of flowers.
*   If a Cogmower is stunned by Digital Bee's Mind Hack but subsequently hit by an unaffected attacker, it will still bounce.
