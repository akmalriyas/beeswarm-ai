# Red Teleporter

The **Red Teleporter** is a portal located inside the Red HQ. When activated, it instantly teleports the player to a specific location directly behind Science Bear.

This transportation is strictly one-way; therefore, the destination teleporter cannot be used to travel back into the Red HQ.

### Activation
To use the Red Teleporter:
*   **PC/Laptop:** Walk onto the white teleport pad and press the "E" key.
*   **Mobile:** Walk onto the pad and tap the button that appears at the top of the screen.

## Trivia & Mechanics

*   **Visuals:** Similar to its blue counterpart, the Red Teleporter previously featured an animation where rings revolved in circles. This animation could sometimes become buggy, slowing down until it stopped completely.
*   **Hidden Ticket:** A **Ticket** token is located near the ceiling of the Red HQ. Players can collect this by jumping onto the Red Teleporter pad and then performing a jump or parachute maneuver toward the token. Enhanced Jump Power (such as that gained from Bear Morph ability tokens or boots) is required to reach it.
*   **Map Boundaries:** The seven circles on the Red Portal near Science Bear are solid barriers. However, with enhanced Jump Power, it is possible to jump outside of the map's boundaries by jumping directly onto the center part of the portal.
