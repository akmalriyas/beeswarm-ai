# Daily Top Aphid Exterminators Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Aphid Exterminators** is one of 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator, viewable through the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page. This leaderboard tracks the total number of aphids players have killed within a single day.

## Mechanics and Reset
*   **Tracking:** The board displays the top 10 positions at any time, but users can scroll to view up to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Rewards
Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 5 Finishers:** Receive the **Wavy Cyan Hive Skin**.
*   **Top 100 Finishers:** Receive 50 **Tickets**.
