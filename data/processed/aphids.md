# Aphids

Aphids are hostile mobs that spawn from collecting leaves across almost every field in the game (excluding the Ant Field, Hub Field, and Stump Field). They possess a low base chance of spawning (0.932%, or 1 in 107), but this chance increases when a field experiences low player activity.

## General Mechanics

Aphids are temporary encounters; players have a limited time of **30 seconds** to defeat them before they despawn. This timer is displayed above the aphid's health bar.

*   **Behavior:** Aphids slowly follow the nearest player, attacking similarly to Stick Nymphs and Giant Ants.
*   **Drops:** They are public mobs, meaning any player can collect drops regardless of whether or not they attacked the aphid.
*   **Rewards:** Defeating an aphid awards 10 Battle Points (20 for rare types), Bond, and various other rewards. Some rewards have a cooldown period after collection, though these can still be obtained from other players who spawn them.

## Aphid Types

Aphids appear in four variants: Normal, Rage, Armored, and Diamond. Different types have specific spawn conditions, health profiles, damage output, and drop tables.

### 🟢 Normal Aphid
This is the most common aphid type, possessing the least amount of health and dealing the least damage. It has a green body with red eyes and neon yellow-green legs and antennae.

**Possible Drops:**
*   10 Battle Points
*   Honey
*   Micro-Converters
*   Magic Beans
*   Gumdrops
*   Treats
*   Soft Waxes (Guaranteed)
*   Field Dice (Rare)
*   Tickets (Rare)
*   Forward Facing Aphid Sticker (Rare)
*   Gingerbread Bears (Beesmas Only)

### 🔴 Rage Aphid
One of the three rare types, the Rage Aphid has higher health and deals the most damage among all aphids. It is colored similarly to a Gifted Bee or Spicy Bee, featuring red bodies and green eyes, legs, and antennae. This type has a higher chance of spawning in fields with red flowers.

**Possible Drops:**
*   20 Battle Points
*   Honey
*   Micro-Converters
*   Magic Beans
*   Gumdrops
*   Strawberries
*   Pineapples
*   Bitterberries
*   Stingers
*   Red Extract (1-5)
*   Enzymes
*   Ant Passes
*   Hard Waxes
*   Caustic Waxes
*   Purple Potion (Rare)
*   Gingerbread Bears (Beesmas Only)
*   Red Wailing Cry Sticker (Extremely Rare)

### 🛡️ Armored Aphid
This rare type has higher defense compared to other aphids, allowing it to survive for a longer duration. It is more likely to spawn in mixed fields and fields with white flowers. The aphid features a metallic gray body with red eyes and black legs and antennae.

**Possible Drops:**
*   20 Battle Points

*   Snowflakes (Beesmas exclusive)
*   Gingerbread Bears (Beesmas exclusive)
*   Small Shield Sticker (Extremely Rare)

## Tips for Combat

*   **Movement:** Jumping and/or running around aphids can prevent them from attacking effectively, as they follow players slowly.
*   **Combat Aid:** If an aphid's level and health are too high, leveling up bees or using specialized attack bees (like Vicious Bee) will increase the chance of a quick kill.
*   **Boosts:** Using consumables and passive abilities such as Stingers, Coconuts, Tropical Drinks, Oils, or Star Saw can help defeat aphids faster.

## Trivia

*   Aphids can also spawn during the Retro Swarm Challenge.
*   Collecting leaves while dealing with Mythic Meteor Showers or Wild Windy Bees can also trigger aphid spawns.
