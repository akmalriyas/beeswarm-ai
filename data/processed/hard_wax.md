# Hard Wax

[![Hard Wax](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d2/Hard_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042803)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d2/Hard_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042803)

> "A firm hunk of beeswax. Has a 60% chance to improve a bee's Beequip."

**Hard Wax** is an inventory item and a type of wax, introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. It can be used on a **Beequip** to attempt to improve its stats. Upon usage, Hard Wax has a 60% chance of successfully improving the Beequip's stats. If it fails (40% chance), no improvement occurs, and the wax is consumed. Hard Wax has an Upgrade Value of 2 for Beequips.

Hard Waxes can also be used in crafting to create tools and items, such as Planters.

### Usage Messages
*   **Success:** "The Wax has Improved the Beequip!"
*   **Failure:** "The Wax didn't seem to help..."

## Ways to Obtain

*   **Crafting:** Can be crafted using a Blender with the following ingredients:
    *   3 Soft Waxes
    *   3 Enzymes
    *   33 Bitterberries
    *   33 Royal Jellies
*   **Challenges:** Obtained as a common reward from the Robo Bear Challenge and the Retro Swarm Challenge.
*   **Sticker Stack Rewards:** Received as a stack reward for adding specific stickers to the Sticker Stack:
    *   Precise Eye Sticker (1 Hard Wax)
    *   Red Palm Hand (1 Hard Wax)
    *   Eviction (1 Hard Wax)
    *   Small Shield Sticker (1 Hard Wax)
    *   Prehistoric Hand (1 Hard Wax)
    *   Prehistoric Boar (1 Hard Wax)
    *   Ionic Column Top (1 Hard Wax)
    *   Ionic Column Middle (1 Hard Wax)
    *   Ionic Column Base (1 Hard Wax)
    *   Shiitake Mushroom (1 Hard Wax)
    *   Black Truffle Mushroom (1 Hard Wax)

### Drops

*   As a drop from Rare+ Puffshrooms.
*   Three hard waxes are dropped by a Level 5 Snowbear.
*   As a reward from Gifted Riley Bee's Honeyday Candles (16.3% chance).
*   As a rare drop from Planters:
    *   Planters located in the Pumpkin Patch increase the frequency of wax drops, including Hard Wax.
    *   Red Clay Planters and Planters Of Plenty drop bonus waxes, which include Hard Wax.

### Quests

Hard Wax is awarded by Dapper Bear during several quests:
*   All About Appearances (#4): 3 Hard Waxes + other items.
*   Creative Comforts (#6): 3 Hard Waxes + other items.
*   Wandering Ambition (#8): 1 Hard Wax + other items.
*   Unintended Intimidation (#9): 3 Hard Waxes + other items.
*   Solitarily Steadfast (#14): 10 Hard Waxes + other items.

## Trivia

*   **Raw Ingredient Cost:** Hard Wax requires the following ingredients:
    *   15 Honeysuckles
    *   150 Sunflower Seeds
    *   300 Pineapples
    *   33 Bitterberries
    *   153 Royal Jellies OR 153,000,000 Honey
*   **Improvement Probability:** The probability of Hard Wax improving a Beequip five times in a row is 7.776%, or approximately once every 12.86 Beequips.
*   Hard Wax is unique as the only wax that can result in no change to a Beequip upon usage.
