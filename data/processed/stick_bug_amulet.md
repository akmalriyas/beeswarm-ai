The **Stick Bug Amulet** is an [amulet](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet") obtained by completing the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge"). To acquire these amulets, a player must reach a specific score and achieve a certain level with the [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") during the challenge.

These amulets provide buffs to the owner, consistently increasing [Capacity](/wiki/Capacity "Capacity"), [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page"), and [Tool Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Tool_Pollen "System Page") as they advance in tier. Higher-tier amulets grant more substantial buffs.

The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the player's score, capping at 200 million score[1].

## Trivia

*   This is the only amulet that can grant Monster Respawn Time as a stat.
    *   The ability to modify mob respawn time comes from the [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee")'s gifted bonus, along with the [Icicles](/wiki/Icicles "Icicles") Beequip and other sources of negative Mob Respawn Time stats.
