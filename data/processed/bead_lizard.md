# Bead Lizard

The **Bead Lizard** is a Level 9 Beequip, characterized by threaded plastic beads arranged to resemble a reptile. It has an equip limit of one and no color requirement. It can only be equipped to Common or Rare Bees.

> "Threaded plastic beads arranged to look like a reptile. Would make a good keychain."
> — Beesmas

The Bead Lizard can be obtained in the following ways:

*   As a very rare drop from the Petal Planter.
