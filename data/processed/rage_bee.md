# Rage Bee

**Rage Bee** is an Epic, Red bee known for its intense demeanor. It harnesses its rage to become more powerful.

[![Rage Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7b/Rage_Bee.png/revision/latest?cb=20200404000023)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7b/Rage_Bee.png/revision/latest?cb=20200404000023) [![RageBee Gifted](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/88/RageBee_Gifted.png/revision/latest?cb=20200404000903)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/88/RageBee_Gifted.png/revision/latest?cb=20200404000903)

> "A very angry bee who has been wronged its whole life. It harnesses its rage to become more powerful."

**Characteristics:**
*   **Rarity:** Epic
*   **Color:** Red
*   **Energy:** 20
*   **Speed:** 15.4
*   **Attack:** 5

**Preferences:**
*   **Favorite Treat:** Strawberries
*   **Likes:** Spider Field, Rose Field
*   **Dislikes:** Blue Flower Field

## Stats and Bonuses

Rage Bee has a base pollen collection of **10** in **4 seconds**, which equates to 2.5 per second. With the x2 Bee Pollen gamepass, this increases to **20** in **4 seconds**.

*   Collects 10 Pollen in 4 seconds (Base).
*   Makes 80 Honey in 4 seconds (Base).
*   Provides +4 Attack and +10% Movespeed.
*   🌟 **Gifted Hive Bonus:** Grants an additional +10% Bee Attack.

### Abilities

Rage Bee possesses two unique ability tokens:

*   **Rage Token:** Grants all bees +1 attack for 45 seconds. This effect can stack up to 4 times.
*   **Token Link:** Collects all other Ability Tokens (excluding other token links), granting 25 honey (+10 per Level) per token collected.

**Trivia:**
*   Rage Bee is the first bee to produce an ability token featuring its face, followed by Tabby Bee and Puppy Bee. It is also the only non-Event bee to possess this trait.
*   Along with Spicy Bee, Rage Bee is one of only two bees capable of producing rage tokens.
*   Rage Bee has three distinct skin colors, similar to Baby Bee. When gifted, it maintains only two color variations, a trait shared by Gifted Baby Bee.
*   A Special Jelly that transforms a bee into a Rage Bee was once obtainable by giving Panda Bear a Present during the Beesmas 2018 event (it is no longer available).
*   The Rage Aphid and gifted Spicy Bee share a similar color scheme to Rage Bee's gifted form.
*   Rage Bee is one of the few bees that can gain an extra ability from Beequips, specifically Melody from the Toy Horn Beequip. This stacking limit previously was only 3 times.
*   The blue counterpart to this bee is Frosty Bee.
