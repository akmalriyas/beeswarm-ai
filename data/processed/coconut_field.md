## Field Statistics
*   **Field Color:** White
*   **Bee Prerequisite:** 35 bees
*   **Nectar Type:** Refreshing Nectar
*   **Dimensions:** 30 × 21 flowers
*   **Total Flower Count:** 630
*   **Disliked Bees:** None

### Bee Preferences (Likes)
The following bees prefer the Coconut Field:
*   Vector Bee
*   Buoyant Bee
*   Digital Bee
*   Windy Bee

## Description and Mechanics

The **Coconut Field** is a field introduced in the September 28, 2019 update. It is located past the Windy Bee Gate and adjacent to Spirit Bear. The field is protected by the Coconut Crab.

When collecting pollen here, players can spawn various tokens, including honey, treats, coconuts, tropical drinks, tickets, and magic beans.

This field grants Refreshing Nectar when a planter is placed within it. A level 1-20 Wild Windy Bee has a chance to spawn in this area.

## Boosts and Enhancements

### Pollen Boosting Items
*   **Glitter:** Adds 100% Coconut Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes (if not already maxed out).
*   **Field Dice / Smooth Dice / Loaded Dice:** May add +100% to +300% Coconut Field pollen for 15 minutes, depending on the die type used (if not already maxed out).

### Permanent and Consumable Boosts
*   **Coconut Field Winds:** Obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes. They start with a base of +20% Instant Coconut Field Conversion, providing an additional +5% Instant Coconut Field Conversion and +10% Coconut Field pollen per stack. These winds can stack up to 15 times.
*   **Coconut Dispenser:** Always grants Coconut Field Boost x1 (if the player has Cadet badge or higher), along with coconuts based on the player's badge level.
*   **Equipment Bonuses:**
    *   Equipping **Coconut Clogs** grants x1.15 Coconut Field pollen.
    *   Equipping **Coconut Belt** grants x1.1 Coconut Field pollen.

### Obsolete Boost Methods
The following methods are no longer functional:
*   Giving a Gummy Bear a present provided Coconut Field Boost x3 and other effects.
*   Redeeming certain expired codes (Gumaden10T, DarzethDoodads, FrozenBugReboot).
*   Purchasing a Coconut Field Market Boost from the Boost Market previously granted x1.5 Field Capacity, x1.25 Coconut Field Pollen, x2.5 Pollen from Coconuts, and x1.1 White Pollen.
*   Using the Mocito100T code (Gave Coconut Field Boost x2 and Field Capacity x2).

## Trivia

*   This is the only field where sprouts and leaves can yield coconuts and tropical drinks.
*   It is also the only field to have a regular boss defending it.
*   Coconut Field is one of four fields that solely contain triple flowers without pollination (the others are Mountain Top Field, Stump Field, and Pepper Patch). Furthermore, it is the only triple-flower field dominated by white flowers.
*   A coconut token can be collected under the coconut tree opposite the cave and closest to Spirit Bear, yielding 10 coconuts.
*   This field and the Pepper Patch are the only fields where sprouts cannot naturally appear or be planted using the Special Sprout Summoner.
*   The Coconut Badge requires significantly more pollen than other field badges; for instance, the Cadet badge requires 50 million pollen compared to the normal requirement of 250 thousand.
*   Pollen can be gathered from this field without meeting the 35 bee prerequisite by accessing the ledge facing the Ticket Tent or by going outside the map and finding the back of the Ticket Tent.
*   Historically, the Coconut Dispenser provided different pollen boosts based on the player's Coconut Badge (Junior: none; Cadet: +100%; Hotshot: +200%; Ace+: +300%). After the Beesmas 2019 update, it always provides a +100% pollen boost.
*   Along with Ant Field and Hub Field, this is one of only three fields without any bees that dislike them.
