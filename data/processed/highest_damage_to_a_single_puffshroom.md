## Game Mechanics and Location

This leaderboard is found within Dapper Bear’s Shop. It is located on the left side of Dapper Bear in his shop area.

## Achievements and Milestones
*   **1 Billion Damage:** In late December 2021, one billion damage was dealt to a single Puffshroom for the first time.
*   **10 Billion Damage:** Between February and March 2022, ten billion damage was dealt to a single Puffshroom for the first time.
*   **100 Billion Damage:** HavingFun2228 was the first player to deal 100 billion damage to a single puffshroom.
*   **1 Trillion Damage:** SupereamXD was the first player to deal 1 trillion damage to a single puffshroom.

## Trivia
This leaderboard, along with the Highest Robo Bear Challenge Scores leaderboard, is one of the only two leaderboards that features a background frame color other than brown.
