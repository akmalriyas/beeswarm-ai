# Invigorating Vial

[![Invigorating Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4e/Invigorating_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042317)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4e/Invigorating_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042317)

> "Grants 4 hours of Invigorating Nectar. (Boosts: Red Pollen, Convert Rate, Bee Attack.)"

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 1 Second
*   **Cap:** 10

The **Invigorating Vial** is a consumable nectar vial item introduced in the December 26, 2022 update. When used, it instantly grants the player 4 hours of the Invigorating Nectar buff. Additionally, this vial can be used as an ingredient to craft various accessories and tools.

## Acquisition Methods (Current)

*   Completing Dapper Bear's 9th and 12th quests rewards one Invigorating Vial.
*   Using the Nectar Condenser on 12 hours of Invigorating Nectar yields one Invigorating Vial.
*   Adding the Invigorating Nectar Icon to the Sticker Stack rewards one Invigorating Vial.
*   Giving a Present to Gifted Riley Bee rewards one Invigorating Vial (along with other items).
*   Defeating a Level 10 Snowbear drops one Invigorating Vial.

## Acquisition Methods (Outdated)

The following methods for obtaining the Invigorating Vial are no longer active:

*   Purchasing the Charming Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   Purchasing the Hefty Honeyday Bundle in Bee Bear’s Catalog (granted 5 Vials).
*   Giving Gifted Riley Bee a Present during Beesmas 2022.
*   Opening the Obnoxious Gift Box.
*   Opening the Cherry Gift Box (from 2022 events).
*   Completing Stick Bug's Beesmas 2022 quest (granted 2 Vials).
*   Defeating a Level 10 during Beesmas 2022, Summer 2024, or Winter 2024.
*   Defeating a Level 24 during Beesmas 2022, Summer 2024, or Winter 2024.
*   Purchasing the Nighttime Nectar Pack in the Robux Shop (for 400 Robux).

## Crafting Uses

The Invigorating Vial is used to craft several items:

**Demon Mask:**
*   1 Invigorating Vial
*   5B Honey
*   500 Stingers
*   250 Red Extracts
*   150 Enzymes
*   100 Glues

**Dark Scythe:**
*   3 Invigorating Vials
*   2.5T Honey
*   1,500 Red Extracts
*   150 Stingers
*   100 Hard Waxes
*   50 Caustic Waxes
*   50 Super Smoothies
