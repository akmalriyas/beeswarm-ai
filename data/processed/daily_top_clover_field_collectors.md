# Daily Top Clover Field Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Clover Field Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator. This leaderboard tracks how much pollen players have collected specifically within the Clover Field on a given day.

### Mechanics and Viewing
*   **Visibility:** The leaderboard can only be accessed via the Global Leaderboards menu.
*   **Display:** It shows 10 player positions at a time, but users can scroll down to view up to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

### Prizes
Players are rewarded based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25 Finishers:** Awarded a Clover Field Stamp Sticker.
*   **Top 100 Finishers:** Awarded 25 Tickets.
