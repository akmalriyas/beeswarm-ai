# Winter Spotlight

[![WinterSpotlightLogo](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/27/WinterSpotlightLogo.webp/revision/latest?cb=20241221212106)](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Winter_Spotlight)

| Detail | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | December 17, 2024, to January 2, 2025 |
| **Special Quest Givers** | Bee Bear |
| **Special Additions** | Beesmas 2024 (Winter) |

The Winter Spotlight is a Roblox event that spanned across multiple games within the Bee Swarm Simulator universe. During this event, players participated in various activities to earn two types of collectible badges: "Standard Tokens" and "Elite Tokens." These tokens contributed toward overarching prizes available in the main event hub.

## Mechanics

*   **Standard Tokens:** Players earned Standard Tokens by completing the first three quests given by Bee Bear.
*   **Elite Tokens:** Elite Tokens were awarded upon opening specific items, such as the Elite Gift Box.
