# Riley Bee

Riley Bee is an Epic Red worker bee, known as the leader of the Red bees and a long-time rival of Bucko Bee. Its fiery nature sets it apart from other bees. For information on the quest-giving version, see [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee).

## General Information
*   **Rarity:** Epic
*   **Color:** Red
*   **Base Energy:** 25
*   **Base Speed:** 15.4
*   **Base Attack:** 5
*   **Favorite Treat:** Strawberries
*   **Likes:** Mushroom Field, Strawberry Field, Rose Field
*   **Dislikes:** Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest

## Stats and Mechanics
Riley Bee is a highly effective worker bee with strong gathering capabilities.

### Gathering Rates
*   **Pollen Collection:** 10 Pollen in 2 seconds (Base). This equates to 5 Pollen per second.
    *   With the [Red Boost] ability, Riley Bee grants x1.2 pollen from red Flowers for 15 seconds, stacking up to 10 times.
    *   Pollen collection can be significantly boosted by using Red or Blue Boost Tokens (see table below).
*   **Honey Production:** 140 Honey in 4 seconds (Base). This equates to 35 Honey per second.

### Stat Bonuses
Riley Bee provides the following bonuses:
*   +10% Movespeed
*   +25% Energy
*   +4 Attack
*   +50% Gather Speed
*   +60 Convert Amount

**Gifted Hive Bonus:** +20% Red Field Capacity.

## Abilities
**Red Boost:** Grants x1.2 pollen from red Flowers for 15 seconds. This ability can stack up to 10 times.

## Trivia
*   Riley Bee is the rival and counterpart to Bucko Bee, as both are leaders of their respective colors.
*   Riley Bee shares skin colors with Rascal Bee and Demon Bee.
*   A giant model of Riley Bee is featured on the front of the Red HQ.
*   The Gifted Riley Bee serves as a quest giver in the game.
*   The Riley Guard unit is based on this bee.
*   Riley Bee and Festive Bee are the only red bees to like three fields, while Riley Bee is also the only red bee to dislike two fields.
