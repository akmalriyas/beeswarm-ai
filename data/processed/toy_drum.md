A **Toy Drum** is a Level 7 Beesmas Beequip. It has an equip limit of two and requires no specific color. It can only be equipped to Brave, Demo, Bucko, or Riley bees.

### Outdated/Previous Ways
*   Reward from the Tan Gift Box.
*   Item from the Merry Mythic Pack in the Robux Shop.
*   Purchasing the Beequip pack in Bee Bear's Catalog (gave 1 Toy Drum + Other items).
*   Completing Gummy Bear's Beesmas quest (gave 1 Toy Drum + Other items).
*   Reward from the Obnoxious Gift Box.
*   Purchasing the Percusive Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog (gave 1 Toy Drum + Other items).
*   Purchasing the Musical Mega-Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   Possible drop from the Aureolin Gift Box.

## Trivia
*   This is one of three instrumental Beequips. The other two are the Toy Horn and Kazoo.
*   This is one of eight beequips that can grant a negative stat. The others are the Kazoo, Whistle, Beesmas Top, Toy Horn, Demon Talisman, Pink Eraser, Lump Of Coal, and Pink Eraser.
