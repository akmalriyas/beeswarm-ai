# Aphid

Aphids are hostile mobs that spawn from collecting leaves, possessing a base chance of 0.932% (1 in 107). This spawning chance increases when a field experiences low activity (i.e., players are not visiting the field frequently). They are public mobs, meaning any player can collect drops regardless of whether or not they attacked them.

## General Mechanics

*   **Location:** Leaves; found in every field except the Ant Field, Hub Field, and Stump Field.
*   **Respawn:** N/A; small chance to spawn from leaves.
*   **Combat Timer:** Players have a limited time of 30 seconds to defeat an aphid. After this timer expires, the aphid despawns. This timer is displayed above the mob's health bar.
*   **Movement:** Aphids attack by slowly following the nearest player, similar to Stick Nymphs and Giant Ants.
*   **Leveling:** An aphid's level and health correlate directly to the field it spawns in (e.g., Rogue Vicious Bee).

## Types

Aphids appear in four variants: Normal, Rage, Armored, and Diamond. Different types have specific spawn rates and unique rewards.

### 🟢 Normal Aphid
This is the most common aphid variant. It has the lowest health and deals the least damage. Its appearance features a green body with red eyes and neon yellow-green legs and antennae.

**Possible Drops:**
*   10 Battle Points
*   Honey
*   Micro-Converters
*   Magic Beans
*   Gumdrops
*   Treats
*   Soft Waxes (Guaranteed)
*   Field Dice (Rare)
*   Tickets (Rare)
*   Forward Facing Aphid Sticker (Rare)
*   Gingerbread Bears (Beesmas Only)

### 🔴 Rage Aphid
One of the three rare aphid types, the Rage Aphid has higher health and deals the most damage. It has a higher chance to spawn in fields containing red flowers. Its coloring resembles a Gifted Bee or Spicy Bee: red body with green eyes, legs, and antennae.

*   Purple Potion (Rare)
*   Gingerbread Bears (Beesmas Only)
*   Red Wailing Cry Sticker (Extremely Rare)

### 🛡️ Armored Aphid
Another rare type, the Armored Aphid has higher defense compared to other aphids, allowing it to survive longer. It is more likely to spawn in mixed fields and fields with white flowers. Its appearance features a metallic gray body with red eyes and black legs and antennae.

## Tips for Combat

*   **Movement:** Jumping and/or running around aphids will prevent them from attacking effectively, as they slowly follow players.
*   **Preparation:** If you struggle to defeat an aphid due to its high level or health, leveling up your bees and specializing in attack (such as Vicious Bees) can increase kill speed.
*   **Enhancements:** Using consumables and passive abilities—like Stingers, Coconuts, Tropical Drinks, Oils, or the Star Saw passive ability—can significantly help defeat aphids faster.

## Trivia

*   Aphids can spawn during the Retro Swarm Challenge.
*   Collecting leaves while participating in a Mythic Meteor Shower or with Wild Windy Bees can also trigger aphid spawns.
