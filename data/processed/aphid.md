# Aphids

Aphids are hostile mobs that can spawn from collecting leaves, with a base chance of 0.932% (1 in 107). This spawning chance increases when a field experiences low player activity. They are public mobs, meaning any player can collect the drops regardless of whether or not they attacked them.

## General Mechanics and Stats

Aphids' level and health correlate to the specific field they spawn in, similar to other specialized mobs like Rogue Vicious Bee.

**Combat:**
*   Players have a limited time (30 seconds) to defeat an aphid; after this timer expires, the aphid despawns. This timer is displayed above its health bar.
*   Aphids attack by slowly following the nearest player.
*   They award 10 Battle Points upon defeat, with rare types yielding 20 Battle Points.

**Stats Table:**

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Leaves; found in every field (excluding Ant Field, Hub Field, and Stump Field). |

| **Health** | Varies based on the aphid's level and type. |
| **Damage** | Varies based on the aphid's type. |
| **Battle Points** | 10 (Normal); 20 (Rage, Armored, Diamond). |
| **Honey Drop** | Correlates with the aphid's level. |

## Types

Aphids appear in four variants: Normal, Rage, Armored, and Diamond. Different types may spawn at varying encounter rates, and their rewards differ significantly. Honey amounts depend on the aphid's level and the player's honey per token boost.

### Normal Aphid
This is the most common type of aphid. It possesses the least health and deals the least damage among all aphids. Its appearance includes a green body with red eyes and neon yellow-green legs and antennae.

**Possible Drops:**
*   10 Battle Points
*   Honey
*   Micro-Converters
*   Magic Beans
*   Gumdrops
*   Treats
*   Soft Waxes (Guaranteed)
*   Field Dice (Rare)
*   Tickets (Rare)
*   Forward Facing Aphid Sticker (Rare)
*   Gingerbread Bears (Beesmas Only)

### Rage Aphid
The Rage Aphid is one of the three rare types. It has a higher chance to spawn in fields containing red flowers. This type possesses higher health and deals the most damage compared to other aphids. They are colored similarly to Gifted Bee or Rage Bee, featuring red bodies with green eyes, legs, and antennae.

**Possible Drops:**
*   20 Battle Points
*   Honey
*   Micro-Converters
*   Magic Beans
*   Gumdrops
*   Strawberries
*   Pineapples
*   Bitterberries
*   Stingers
*   Red Extract (1-5)
*   Enzymes
*   Ant Passes
*   Hard Waxes
*   Caustic Waxes
*   Purple Potion (Rare)
*   Gingerbread Bears (Beesmas Only)
*   Red Wailing Cry Sticker (Extremely Rare)

### Armored Aphid
The Armored Aphid is one of the three rare types. It has a higher chance of spawning in mixed fields and fields with white flowers. This aphid features increased defense, allowing it to survive for a longer duration. Its appearance includes a metallic gray body with red eyes and black legs and antennae.

*   Gingerbread Bears (Beesmas exclusive)

The Diamond Aphid is one of the three rare types. It has a higher chance to spawn in fields with blue flowers. This aphid possesses more health than other variants, allowing it to survive for an extended period. It resembles a Diamond Bee, featuring a sky-blue body with white eyes, legs, and antennae.

## Tips for Combat

*   **Movement:** Jumping and/or running around aphids can prevent them from attacking effectively, as they slowly follow players.
*   **Strategy:** If a player cannot defeat an aphid due to high level or health, leveling up bees and utilizing attack-specialized bees (such as Vicious Bee) will increase the chance of defeating it quickly.
*   **Enhancements:** Using various consumables and passive abilities—like Stingers, Coconuts, Tropical Drinks, Oils, or Star Saw—can help defeat aphids faster.

## Trivia

*   Aphids can spawn in the Retro Swarm Challenge.
*   Collecting leaves via Mythic Meteor Shower events or Wild Windy Bee actions can also trigger aphid spawns.
