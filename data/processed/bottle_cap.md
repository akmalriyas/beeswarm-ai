**Level:** 4
**Color:** Any
**Limit:** 3
"The discarded top from a bottle of pop."
🎄Beesmas

### Overview
The **Bottle Cap** is a Level 4 Beequip. It has an equip limit of two and requires no specific color. It can only be equipped to Common or Rare Bees. The item was released in the April 1, 2022 update.
