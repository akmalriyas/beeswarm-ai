# Nectar Vial

Nectar Vials are inventory items used to instantly increase a player's current Nectar buff by 4 hours when consumed. Each vial grants 4 hours of its respective nectar type. Players can hold a maximum of 10 vials of each type, though this limit can be bypassed through quests or Robux Packs.

## Acquisition Methods

Nectar Vials can be obtained in several ways:

*   **Conversion:** Using the Nectar Condenser to convert 12 hours of nectar into one Nectar Vial (which stores 4 hours).
*   **Sticker Rewards:** As a reward for adding specific stickers to the Sticker Stack:
    *   Adding the Satisfying, Refreshing, Motivating, Invigorating, and Comforting Nectar Icons rewards the player with their respective vial.
    *   Adding the Offline Voucher sticker rewards 1 Nectar Shower Vial.
*   **Mob Drops:** Defeating a Level 10 Snowbear drops 1 Invigorating Vial.
*   **Robux Packs:**
    *   The Feelin' Festive Pack (800 Robux) grants 5 Nectar Shower Vials and other items.
*   **Bee Bear's Catalog Bundles:**
    *   Frozen Forest Pack (1,200 Snowflakes + 40 Gingerbread Bears) grants 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
    *   Season's Sharing Bundle (600 Snowflakes + 60 Gingerbread Bears) grants 1 Nectar Shower Vial and other items.
*   **NPC Gifts:**
    *   Giving Gifted Bucko Bee a Present rewards 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
    *   Giving Gifted Riley Bee a Present rewards 1 Invigorating Vial and other items.
*   **Gift Boxes (2025):**
    *   Opening the Ruby Gift Box rewards 1 Invigorating Vial and other items.
    *   Opening the Restful Gift Box rewards 1 Nectar Shower Vial and other items.
*   **Quest Rewards:**
    *   Completing several Dapper Bear quests reward the player with at least one of every nectar vial type. Specific examples include:
        *   Satisfying Vials: Means of Mutation (5), The Trick To Tickets (11), More Than Myth (15).
        *   Comforting Vials: Creative Comforts (6), The Long Haul (10), Ongoing Maintenance (13), Barter For The Blue Drive.
        *   Refreshing Vials: Keeping It Chromatic (7), Solitarily Steadfast (14), Dapper Bear's Samovar.
        *   Motivating Vials: Wandering Ambition (8).
        *   Invigorating Vials: Unintended Intimidation (9), Invigorating View (12).
        *   Nectar Shower Vials: Sticker Style (16).

### Outdated Acquisition Methods

The following methods for obtaining Nectar Vials are no longer active:

*   **Beesmas 2022 Quests:** Rewards included various vials from quests given by Onett, Gifted Riley Bee, Dapper Bear's, Stick Bug, Gifted Bucko Bee, Polar Bear, Bubble Bee Man, and Bee Bear.
*   **Seasonal Gifts/Drops:** Giving presents to NPCs (Dapper Bear, Gifted Bucko Bee, Gifted Riley Bee) during specific seasonal events, or defeating Snowbears during Beesmas 2022, Summer 2024, or Winter 2024.
*   **Gift Boxes:** Rewards from Silky, Cherry, Gooey, Obnoxious, Tropical, Soothing, and Mythic Gift Boxes in previous seasons.
*   **Bee Bear's Catalog Bundles (Outdated):** Specific bundles like Hefty Honeyday, Tropical, Violet, Angelic, Charming, and Refreshing provided vials.
*   **Robux Shop Packs (Outdated):** Nighttime Nectar Pack and Royal Winter Wonder Haul pack offered various vial rewards.

## Variants and Effects

There are six types of Nectar Vials. Five types grant a 4-hour buff to the player who uses them, while the Nectar Shower Vial grants a universal server buff.

### Comforting Vial
Instantly gives the player 4 hours of the Comforting Nectar buff.

### Invigorating Vial
Instantly gives the player 4 hours of the Invigorating Nectar buff.

### Motivating Vial
Instantly gives the player 4 hours of the Motivating Nectar buff.

### Refreshing Vial
Instantly gives the player 4 hours of the Refreshing Nectar buff.

### Satisfying Vial
Instantly gives the player 4 hours of the Satisfying Nectar buff.

### Nectar Shower Vial
Instantly grants all players in the server 4 hours of every Nectar buff.

**Trivia:**
*   Most Nectar Vials feature a letter corresponding to their name (C for Comforting, I for Invigorating, M for Motivating, S for Satisfying). The Refreshing Vial does not have an 'R' icon. The Nectar Shower Vial has no specific icon as it represents all nectar types.
*   There is a limited total amount of each vial type that can be obtained without using the Nectar Condenser:
    *   Comforting Vials: 26
    *   Invigorating Vials: 22
    *   Motivating Vials: 13
    *   Refreshing Vials: 14
    *   Satisfying Vials: 19
    *   Nectar Shower Vials: 34
*   The Nectar Shower Vial is the only vial type not obtainable from the Nectar Condenser.
*   Using Nectar Vials counts toward collecting nectar in quests, but they do not contribute to the Daily Top Nectar Collectors leaderboards.
