# Tide Popper

**Overview**

The Tide Popper is a specialized tool that can be crafted in the Blue HQ. To unlock it, players must have completed at least 250 Gifted Bucko Bee quests.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Shop Location** | Blue HQ |
| **Quest Requirement** | Complete 250 Gifted Bucko Bee quests |
| **Range** | 22 Flowers (19 in image) |
| **Collecting Pattern** | Spike |
| **Base Collection Time** | 1 second |

**Pollen Collection Stats**

The Tide Popper's base collection rates vary by flower type and size. A special multiplier applies to Blue Pollen.

| White | 6 | 12 | 18 | 24 | 30 |
| Red | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |

*Special Multiplier: x1.25 Blue Pollen*

**Crafting Ingredients**

The Tide Popper requires the following materials to craft:

*   2.5T Honey
*   1,500 Blue Extracts
*   150 Stingers
*   150 Tropical Drinks
*   75 Swirled Waxes
*   50 Super Smoothies
*   3 Comforting Vials

**Mechanics and Abilities**

The Tide Popper functions by releasing waves of water with each swing. Every third swing releases a wave that collects pollen, pops bubbles, and converts pollen when a bee is hit by the wave.

**Tide Power:**
When a bubble is popped (by any means), it grants a stack of "Tide Power." Popping a golden bubble grants 2 stacks. Tide Power increases the tool's swinging speed and strengthens the waves.

*   **Large Wave Activation:** When Tide Power reaches 495 or more stacks, each swing releases a large wave.
*   **Tidal Surge:** Upon reaching 500 stacks of Tide Power, the buff is replaced by "Tidal Surge." This surge lasts for 10 seconds and grants x2.5 to x1.5 tool swinging speed (starting at x2.5 and gradually decreasing). Every swing during a Tidal Surge releases a large tidal wave. Popping a bubble while under Tidal Surge extends its duration.

**Tide Blessing:**
Activating a Tidal Surge grants 1 minute of "Tide Blessing." When a tidal wave hits one of the player's balloons, it causes the balloon to convert up to 8% of your Convert Total into Honey Tokens (worth x2.5). If the balloon is golden, it also grants an additional 10 seconds of Tide Blessing.

*   **Tide Blessing Stacks:** This buff stacks up to 4 hours and provides bonuses including:
    *   x1.2 Blue Pollen
    *   Honey From Tokens
    *   Convert Rate at Hive
    *   Pollen from Bees
    *   Tool Pollen

**Trivia & Notes**

*   The Tide Popper was originally named the "Aqua Popper."
*   It has counterparts: the Dark Scythe (Red HQ) and Gummyballer (Gummy Bear’s Lair).
*   A large wave can convert pollen without needing Tidal Surge.
*   There are historical notes regarding glitches related to this item, including an exploit that allowed acquisition before meeting quest requirements, which was later patched.
*   The Tide Popper, along with the Dark Scythe and Gummyballer, is one of the most expensive items in the game (2.5T Honey).
