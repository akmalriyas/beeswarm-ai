# Mondo Present

**Archived Content Notice:** This content refers to a piece of game material that has been removed from the current version of Bee Swarm Simulator. While this information may be archival, it is provided for historical reference.

***

Mondo Presents were special ornamental presents featured during the Beesmas 2019 event. They have since been discontinued in subsequent Beesmas events and replaced by visually identical Gift Boxes. These are not to be confused with standard [Presents] or generic [Gift Boxes].

The sound associated with opening these gifts is available here: <https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Present1.ogg>

There were 12 different types of ornament presents players could open. To unlock them, players needed to obtain a specific number of [Ornaments] from various [NPCs]. Upon opening an ornament present, it would drop various [Items] (in token form) around the box. Although the rewards were initially tokens, these items could stack past their normal limits if applicable. Each present could only be opened once.

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   A token that yielded 15 [Gumdrops] was located behind the Golden Present.
*   Tokens yielding 1 [Royal Jelly] and 3 [Micro-Converters] were each positioned next to the Mythic Present.
*   If tokens from an ornament present were not collected, the associated items would immediately transfer to the player's inventory upon the token despawning.
*   The room housing the Golden Present has since been replaced and repurposed as [Commando Chick's Hideout](/wiki/Commando_Chick%27s_Hideout "Commando Chick's Hideout").
*   The Gooey Present's location remains accessible behind the [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Gummy_Bee_Egg_Claim "Gummy Bee Egg Claim"). The Mythic Present's original room, located to the left of the Stump Field, was also removed from the map after a few months.
