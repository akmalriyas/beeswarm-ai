# Frequently Asked Questions

## Game Mechanics FAQs

### How do I cut the vines near the Wealth Clock?

To clear the vines by the Wealth Clock, you must equip either the Clippers, Scissors, or Scythe. Walk up to the pad in front of the vine and press "E" to cut them. Once cleared, you can enter the area behind the vines using any tool you prefer.

### How do I obtain a Spirit Petal?

Spirit Bear awards one spirit petal for every 10 completed Spirit Bear quests. Since there are 30 total quests, players can earn a maximum of three spirit petals.

### How can I get a Windy Bee?

There are two methods:
1.  **Historical Purchase:** Before Beesmas 2019, the Windy Bee could be purchased from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux.
2.  **Quest/Donation Method:** Spirit petals obtained from Spirit Bear's quests can be donated to the Wind Shrine. Additionally, donating Cloud Vials (and other items) increases your chance of obtaining a Windy Bee.

*Note on Chance:* You do not need to donate a spirit petal to begin increasing your chances; you only need one to qualify for the possibility. The probability increases with every item donation to the Wind Shrine, but acquiring the bee is generally a long-term endeavor (see Favor).

### How do I obtain a Translator?

Finish all of Science Bear's new quests.

### Who should I give my Translators to?

It is commonly recommended to distribute your translators as follows:

**1. First Translator:**
*   Give it to the bee corresponding to your specialization (Red or Blue). If you are better at red pollen collection, give it to Gifted Riley Bee; if blue, give it to Gifted Bucko Bee. This allows you to start completing easier quests and earning rewards in your strongest color.
*   If your skills are balanced between red and blue, give the first translator to Gifted Riley Bee. This is because its quests may reward Stingers, an item highly useful for acquiring Vicious Bee and Demon Mask.

**2. Second Translator:**
*   Give it to the other gifted bee you did not choose in step one (e.g., if you chose Riley Bee first, give it to Bucko Bee). This allows you to begin progressing on quests in your secondary color.

**3. Last Translator:**
*   The final translator should go to Stick Bug. While Stick Bug is less immediately rewarding than the gifted bees, giving it a translator does not significantly impact gameplay because the Stick Bug Challenge periodically starts due to Onett, and other players can summon it regardless of who holds the translator.

### How do I start the Stick Bug Challenge?

First, obtain a Translator. Then, go talk to the Stick Bug located on top of the mushroom. (You will need either a Parachute or Glider; if you lack these items, you are likely not at a high enough level to attempt the challenge.)

### Which Event Bee should I prioritize obtaining first?

The general consensus is that Tabby Bee should be obtained first. Starting early allows you to begin collecting Tabby Love sooner, helping your Tabby Bee reach maximum level faster. Photon Bee is usually considered the next priority. After these initial steps, prioritization depends on your current swarm composition, colors, and active quests, making a universal guideline difficult.

### Should I use the Scythe or the Bubble Wand?

This choice is subjective and depends on several factors:
*   **Pollen Collection:** Based purely on pollen collection efficiency, the Bubble Wand has a slight advantage. However, this isn't the whole story.
*   **Personal Preference:** Consider your color balance (more red vs. more blue bees) and whether you prefer collecting in lines or circles. If you are focused on large quantities of red pollen, for example, one tool might be better suited. Many players use both tools—one for red fields and the other for blue/neutral fields.
*   **Scaling:** As your bee count increases, the difference between the two tools becomes less significant, as most pollen will come from stacked bombs or critical hits.
*   **Goo Collection (Special Note):** Most players find that the Scythe is superior for collecting goo because its collection radius is often smaller than many goo puddles, allowing it to pick up more residue.

### How do I locate Royal Jelly and Tickets?

Please refer to the dedicated pages on Royal Jelly and Tickets for specific location information.

### What are the current Codes?

There is no "today's code." Codes are issued by Onett when he deems fit, which is typically a monthly or yearly occurrence, not a daily one. If you believe there is a new code, check the main Codes page. If you are not a registered user, ensure you click both refresh links in the white box at the top of the page to view the most current information.

### I saw a new code but it isn't on the page! How do I add it?

To prevent misinformation, we restrict the display of codes. If you cannot see a code or notice an error, first refresh the page (in case of a delay). If the issue persists, please contact any active wiki staff member, and they will address the problem as quickly as possible.

### How long until an Event Bee disappears?

There are currently no plans to remove any Event Bees. The term "Event Bee" signifies that the bee cannot be obtained through ordinary eggs or royal jellies; it does not mean the bee is temporary.

### How do I access the Test Realm?

You must be part of Onett's Testing Group to join the Test Realm when it is active. Membership approval is granted solely by Onett, usually coinciding with new update testing periods.

## Royal Jelly Mechanics

#### Using a royal jelly on a bee results in a worse rarity. Why?
The probabilities for royal jelly do not change based on the target bee's current rarity or type. Even if you apply it to an Event Bee, it is highly likely to transform into a Rare bee.

#### Does using a royal jelly reset a bee's level?
Royal jelly only transforms the *type* of the bee, not its level; therefore, the resulting bee will retain its original level (e.g., Level 7). However, Gifted status is considered part of the type, so if you use a royal jelly on a gifted bee, the gift status **will** reset.

#### Will using a royal jelly on Tabby Bee reset my Tabby Love stack?
No, your accumulated Tabby Love will be preserved and saved when applying royal jelly to a Tabby Bee.

#### I accidentally deleted an Event Bee. How can I recover it?
Event Bees come with a special royal jelly that allows any bee to transform into the specific event bee in question. Since you can only have one of each type of event bee at a time, this special jelly is specifically designed for recovering from accidental deletion.

#### I accidentally deleted a gifted bee or mutation. Can I recover it?
No, there is currently no method to undo the deletion of a gifted bee or a mutation.

### Why does my Event Bee have a "1st" flag?
This indicates that your bee is a First Edition Bee. Examples include Bear Bees acquired during the game's initial month, Gummy Bees obtained from Gummy Bear's quests, and Tabby Bees purchased when they were first available in the Ticket Tent.

### A player is stuck in my hive (Hive #6).
If you are in Hive #6 (the Onett statue hive), a character may sometimes fall into the honeycomb structure. You can simply ignore this; your bees will function normally, and the hive remains operational despite his presence.

### Do hidden royal jellies or tickets respawn?
No, these items are one-time only finds.

### Help! I am missing limbs/head, or I turned into a strange half-human/half-bear monster!
This typically occurs if you died or reset while in Bear Morph mode, or if you leave the game while transformed. To fix this, wait until your character is no longer fully bear-shaped (or only partially transformed), then exit and rejoin the game.

### Where should I report bugs?
First, check the Known Glitches page to ensure your issue is not already documented. Next, perform a standard troubleshooting step: leave the game completely and relaunch it. (**Important:** Do not reset your character; close the entire game window and reopen it.) If the bug persists and significantly impacts gameplay, please submit a report using the official bug report form.
