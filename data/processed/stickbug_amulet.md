# Stickbug Amulet

The **Stick Bug Amulet** is an amulet obtained from the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge). To acquire these amulets, players must reach a specific score and achieve a certain level with the [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug).

Amulets grant buffs to the owner. As they increase in tier, they always boost the player's [Capacity](/wiki/Capacity), [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate), and [Tool Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Tool_Pollen). Higher tiers provide more significant and superior bonuses.

The amulet's quality increases linearly with the player's score, capping out at 200 million score.

## Requirements

Amulet acquisition requires meeting specific score and Stick Bug level thresholds:

| Minimum Score | Required Stick Bug Level | Amulet Tier |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1 Million | Level 5 | Bronze Stick Amulet |
| 8 Million | Level 8 | Silver Stick Amulet |
| 20 Million | Level 11 | Gold Stick Amulet |
| 50 Million | Level 13 | Diamond Stick Amulet |

## Possible Buffs

The amulets provide a variety of stat buffs. For detailed information on how amulets generate stats, see [Amulet#Generation](/wiki/Amulet#Generation).

### Bronze Stick Bug Amulet
This amulet provides the following potential buffs:

*   **Guaranteed:**
    *   [Capacity](/wiki/System_Page#Capacity_Multiplier): +25,000 to +100,000 (Intervals of 25,000)
    *   [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate): x1.05 to x1.1 (Intervals of 0.01)
*   **Potentially:**
    *   [Pollen From Tools](/wiki/System_Page#Tool_Pollen): +1% to +4% (Intervals of 1%; 0.01%)
    *   Select at most 1: [Red Bee Attack](/wiki/System_Page#Red_Attack) or [Blue Bee Attack](/wiki/System_Page#Blue_Attack): +1 (1%; 1%)
    *   Select 1: [White Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#White_Pollen), [Red Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Red_Pollen), [Blue Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Blue_Pollen): +10% to +15% (Intervals of 1%; 25%)
    *   [Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Pollen): +1% to +3% (Intervals of 1%; 25%)
    *   Select 1: [Honey From Tokens](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_From_Tokens): +20% to +40% (Intervals of 1%; 16.6667%)
    *   [Bee Attack](/wiki/System_Page#Attack_Multiplier): +3% to +5% (Intervals of 1%; 16.6667%)
    *   Select 1: [Blue Bomb Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Blue_Bomb_Pollen), [Red Bomb Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Red_Bomb_Pollen), [Buzz Bomb Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Buzz_Bomb_Pollen): +10% to +15% (Intervals of 1%; 16.6667%)
    *   [Bomb Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Bomb_Pollen): +5% to +8% (Intervals of 1%; 16.6667%)

*(Note: Detailed probability tables for stat ranges at various quality milestones are omitted here for brevity but remain available on the designated subpage.)*

## Trivia

*   This is the only amulet that can grant Monster Respawn Time as a stat. This effect is also available through the [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee) gifted bonus, and via the [Icicles](/wiki/Icicles) Beequip.
*   While this was previously the only amulet with a Diamond tier, the [Star Amulet](/wiki/Star_Amulet) gained a Supreme variant in the 2020-06-06 update.
