The Hub Field is a field introduced in the January 12, 2024 update. It is located within the Hive Hub, accessible through the trading portal beyond the Ant Gate.

This field contains 697 flowers, which come in white, blue, and red, across single, double, and triple sizes.

## Mechanics and Spawns

**Pollen Conversion:** All pollen collected in this field instantly converts to honey.
**Sprout Generation:** When planting a Magic Bean on the Hub Field, there is a rare chance of it spawning a Sticker Sprout. Additionally, a Sticker Sprout is guaranteed to spawn every 3 hours, provided no sprout currently occupies the field.

**Possible Token Spawns:** This field can spawn tokens including Blueberry, Honey, Pineapple, Strawberry, Sunflower Seed, Treats, and Magic Bean.

**Nectar Generation:** Only through specific glitches does this field grant Comforting Nectar when a planter is placed here.

## Obtaining Boosts

*   Using Glitter adds 100% Hub Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes, provided the boost is not already maxed out.

### Outdated Methods
The following codes previously offered Hub Field boosts:
*   `BeequipTradeReboot`: Gave x4 Hub Field Boost and x15 Hub Field Winds + other items.
*   `6irthday`: Gave x6 Hub Field Boost + other items.

## Trivia

*   **Pollen Collection:** Along with the Ant Field and Stump Field, the Hub Field is one of only three fields where players can collect pollen without standing directly on the flowers.
*   **Field Shape/Movement:** The Hub Field (along with the Ant Field and Stump Field) is a non-quadrilateral field, and it is one of the only fields where Wild Windy Bee cannot spawn or fly to.
*   **Development History:** This, the Coconut Field, the Pepper Patch, the Ant Field, and the Stump Field are among the fields added after the game's initial release.
*   **Boost Restrictions:** The Hub Field, Mountain Top Field, and Coconut Field are among the only fields that cannot be given a field boost by Field Boosters.
*   **Aesthetics:** This field is one of three fields without decorations (the others being the Ant Field and Mountain Top Field). Its name derives from its location within the Hive Hub.
*   **Boost Icon:** The Hub Field and the Brick Fields are unique in that their field boost icons are reused from their associated sticker stamp, rather than having a truly unique icon.
*   **Consumable Restrictions:** Many consumable items are disabled while on this field. Players cannot use sprinklers, stingers, cloud vials, balloons, snowflakes, planters, whirligigs, night bells, or any kind of dice. Sprinklers are completely disabled here. However, Glitter, Jelly Beans, and Box-O-Frogs can still be used.
*   **Instant Conversion:** The Hub Field is the only field that instantly converts collected pollen into honey (the Ant Field requires an Ant Challenge Buff to achieve this).
*   **Testing Ground:** The Hub Field was used during testing for Combo Coconut abilities, determining that a single ability could land a maximum of 50 times before ending.
*   **Sprout Notification:** Five minutes before a natural Sticker Sprout spawns, the message "🌱 A Sticker Sprout is about to spawn in Public Server Hive Hubs… 🌱" will be displayed.
*   **Bloom Colors:** The Hub Field spawns the most colors of Bloom petals across all fields. It cannot spawn yellow or merigold.
