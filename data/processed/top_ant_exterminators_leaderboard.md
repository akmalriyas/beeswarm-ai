The Global Top Ant Exterminators Leaderboard is one of the game's leaderboards, tracking players' highest scores in the Ant Challenge. It displays the top 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view scores all the way to the 100th place.

**Mechanics:**
*   When a player steps on the white pad in front of the leaderboard structure, a message displaying "Your Highscore: X" will appear (where X is the player's current high score).
*   The physical structure conceals a secret passage that provides access to an Ant Pass and the Gummy Bee Egg Claim.

## Location

This leaderboard is located in front of the Ant Challenge room, near the Ant Pass Dispenser and the Free Ant Pass Dispensers.

## Achievements

Key milestones in achieving high scores include:
*   **2018-08-01:** SDMittens was the first user to reach 200 Ant Exterminations.
*   **2018-12-26:** SDMittens was the first user to reach 300 Ant Exterminations.
*   **2019-12-14:** Lipsisas was the first user to reach 400 Ant Exterminations.
*   **2020-01-10:** Zlib was the first user to reach 500 Ant Exterminations.

## Trivia

*   All scores on this leaderboard were reset during the 2018-11-25 update.
*   The lowest recorded score on the leaderboard is 530 points, achieved by players ranked from 67th to 100th place.
*   On 2024-01-12, many players' high scores were lost due to an unknown data wipe event.
