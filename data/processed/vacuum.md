# Vacuum

**Vacuum** is a tool available for purchase in the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop").

## Stats
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | 14,000 Honey |
| **Range** | 13 Flowers |
| **Collecting Pattern** | Surround |
| **Collecting Time** | 0.8 seconds |
| **Base Collection Rate** | 2 pollen per flower (Total Base: 32.5 pollen/sec) |

### Pollen Collection Details
The Vacuum collects pollen from surrounding flowers based on the type and size of the patch:

| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Red | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Blue | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |

*Special Multiplier(s): None*

## Description and Mechanics
The Vacuum is a complex tool found in the Noob Shop. It functions by collecting pollen from up to 13 surrounding flower patches every 0.8 seconds, resulting in a base collection rate of 32.5 pollen per second.

### Appearance
The vacuum cleaner resembles a standard household appliance: a brownish bag with a grey handle connecting to a suction unit colored grey and black, featuring two yellow squares at the front that resemble lights.

### Sticker
The Vacuum is associated with a specific [Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Stickers "Sticker").

## Trivia
*   This tool is the third most expensive item available in the Noob Shop, following the Canister and the Helmet.
*   It shares a similar collection range to the Honey Dipper and the Porcelain Dipper; however, both of those tools have a larger collection range than the Vacuum. The Porcelain Dipper also features a unique mechanic: it summons a pillar of light every 10th swing that collects pollen.
*   The design of this tool is based on the household appliance known as the vacuum cleaner.
