# Frog

Frogs are passive mobs introduced in the 2019-12-23 Update. They can be summoned by players and come in two types: Normal Frogs and Gifted Frogs.

## Mechanics and Behavior

Frogs are passive entities that do not inflict harm on players or other frogs, nor can they be harmed by hostile entities.

### Summoning
*   **Normal Frogs:** Can be summoned by collecting a Summon Frog ability token generated by a Tadpole Bee, or by using Box-O-Frogs.
*   **Gifted Frogs:** Can be summoned by Box-O-Frogs, or by a Gifted Tadpole Bee when collecting its Summon Frog ability token (10% chance, increasing by 2% per level).

### Despawn Timer
Frogs despawn after a set period:
*   3 minutes if summoned via Box-O-Frogs.
*   20 seconds (+2s per level) if summoned via Tadpole Bee.

### Abilities
When summoned, frogs perform the following actions:
1.  **Hopping:** They hop around the field until they despawn, creating Bubbles on their 2nd, 3rd, and 4th hop.
2.  **Token Collection:** Frogs use their tongues to collect Ability Tokens, Honey Tokens, and other tokens (such as rewards from Mobs) for the player, which also creates Bubbles.
3.  **Combat:** When summoned via Box-O-Frogs, frogs can attack Hostile Mobs. This attack ignores accuracy checks and deals either 7 damage or x5 the damage of the summoning Tadpole Bee's attack power.

### Gifted Frog Enhancements
Gifted Frogs possess several advantages over Normal Frogs:
*   Longer tongue range and faster token collection/attack speed.
*   50% more attack power.
*   A chance to summon Gold Bubbles while hopping.
*   They can hit balloons with their tongue, converting pollen into honey tokens.

## Audio
The following sounds are associated with frogs:
*   **Summoning:** Plays when a frog is summoned by the Summon Frog ability.
*   **Token Collection:** Plays when a frog collects a token.
*   **Activity:** Plays continuous "ribbit" sounds while the frog is active in the field.

## Trivia
*   Frogs are unique as they are the only mob that can be summoned using an ability token, and they are the only passive mob hostile to harmful mobs.
*   They are one of only three entities (along with Cub Buddies and Windy Bee) capable of collecting tokens from flowers, leaves, and sprouts for the player.
*   Frogs, Rogue Vicious Bees, and Wild Windy Bees are among the few mobs that can deal damage to other hostile mobs; however, frogs are unique in deliberately attacking other mobs.
*   Collecting bubbles from frogs summoned by other players grants pollen and contributes to both players' quest requirements.
*   The frog and Rogue Vicious Bee are the only mobs capable of being spawned using an item (Box-O-Frogs for Frogs, Night Bell for Rogue Vicious Bees).
*   Frogs can sometimes fall outside their designated field at high elevations, causing them to hop uselessly until they despawn.
*   The frog and Rogue Vicious Bee are the only mobs that have a gifted variant.
*   Frogs will only spawn facing towards the 30-Bee Zone or away from the Hive.
*   Frogs are the only passive mobs capable of being summoned by a Bee.
