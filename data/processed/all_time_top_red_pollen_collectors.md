The All-Time Top Red Pollen Collectors leaderboard tracks the total amount of red pollen collected by players over time. It is one of 64 leaderboards available in the game and displays 10 positions at a time; players can scroll down to view up to 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on these leaderboards (hackers or exploiters will be removed).

**In-Game Mechanics:**
When a player steps on the white pad located near this leaderboard, a message will display showing their total accumulated red pollen amount ("You've collected 'x' Red Pollen").

## Location
The leaderboard is situated inside the Red HQ, positioned between the ladder and the Dark Scythe.
