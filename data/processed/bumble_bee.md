# Bumble Bee

**Bumble Bee** is a [Blue](/wiki/Category:Blue "Category:Blue") [Rare bee](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare"). It is described as, *"A mellow fellow who moves a little slow, but works harder and longer than others."*

## General Information
*   **Rarity:** Rare
*   **Color:** Blue
*   **Energy:** 40
*   **Speed:** 10.5
*   **Attack:** 1

Bumble Bee's favorite treat is [Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry "Blueberry"). It likes the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field") and the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"), but dislikes the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field").

## Stats
Bumble Bee has a base pollen collection of **18** in **4 seconds**, which is equivalent to 4.5 per second. With the x2 Bee Pollen gamepass, this increases to 36 in 4 seconds (9 per second).

*   **Pollen Collection:** Collects 18 [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in 4 seconds.
*   **Honey Production:** Makes 80 [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") in 4 seconds, equivalent to 20 per second.
*   **Stat Bonuses:** +100% [energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), +8 [gather amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount "Stats"), -25% [movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats").
*   **Gifted Hive Bonus:** Gifted Bumble Bees provide a x1.1 multiplier to Capacity.

## Abilities
Bumble Bee possesses the **Blue Bomb** ability, which collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding blue [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). The pollen collected increases by 10% per level. Combining multiple Blue Bombs can increase overall power.

## Trivia
*   Bumble Bee shares the same skin color as the [Bucko Bee](/wiki/Bucko_Bee "Bucko Bee").
*   It is the blue counterpart of the [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee").
*   Defeated [Rhino Beetles](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle") may drop [Bumble Bee Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Bumble_Bee_Egg "Egg").
*   A special jelly that transforms a bee into a Bumble Bee could have been obtained from [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man"), using the codes 'BeeDay2019' and 'Cubly' (expired).
*   Bumble Bee, [Carpenter Bee](/wiki/Carpenter_Bee "Carpenter Bee"), and [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee "Honey Bee") are named after their real-life counterparts.
*   Bumble Bee, Bucko Bee, and [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee") are the only blue bees that like at least three fields.
*   It is noted as the slowest rare bee.
*   The Gifted stat bonus for Bumble Bees was previously +20% Blue Bomb pollen but has since been updated.
