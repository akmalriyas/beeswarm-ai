## Field Statistics
*   **Field Color:** Blue
*   **Bee Prerequisites:** 10 bees
*   **Nectar Type:** Motivating Nectar
*   **Flower Count:** 563 Flowers

### Bee Preferences
**Likes:** Shocked Bee, Diamond Bee, Lion Bee, Gummy Bee, Photon Bee
**Dislikes:** Vector Bee, Cobalt Bee, Crimson Bee

## Description and Mechanics

The Stump Field is a field type that was introduced in the November 25, 2018 update. It is located past the Brave Bee Gate, adjacent to the Pineapple Patch.

When collecting pollen in this field, players can spawn various tokens, including honey, red boost, blue boost, treats, gumdrops, glue, magic beans, tickets, and blue extract.

**Bee Compatibility:**
*   Bees that like the Stump Field include Bumble Bee, Exhausted Bee, Tadpole Bee, and Gummy Bee.
*   Bees that dislike the Stump Field include Hasty Bee, Baby Bee, and Spicy Bee.

This field grants Motivating Nectar when a planter is placed within it. It contains 563 flowers, which are white, red, and blue. The only natural flower size available in this field is triple.

## Possible Events
*   **Stump Snail:** A Stump Snail protects the field by circling the circumference of the tree stump but does not attack players directly. As long as the Stump Snail is alive, bees will prioritize damaging it over collecting pollen or spawning tokens that collect pollen (such as bombs and boosts).
*   **Stick Bug Challenge:** If a Stick Bug is present in Mountain Top Field, Bamboo Field, or Pineapple Patch during an active Stick Bug Challenge, he may summon a defense totem.

## Obtaining Boosts
The Stump Field can be enhanced using various items:

*   **Blue Field Booster:** Using this booster may grant a Stump Field boost if the Stump Snail is on respawn cooldown. This provides +100% Stump Field pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Applying glitter adds 100% Stump Field pollen to the current boost for 15 minutes, provided the boost is not already maximized.
*   **Dice:** Using field dice, smooth dice, or loaded dice can add +100% to +300% Stump Field pollen for 15 minutes, depending on the type of dice used, assuming the boost is not maxed out.
*   **Field Winds:** Stump Field Winds, obtainable via the Wind Shrine or codes, provide a base of +20% instant Stump Field conversion and grant +5% instant Stump Field conversion and +10% Stump Field pollen per stack for 30 minutes. These winds can stack up to 15 times.
*   **Shell Amulet:** This amulet boosts the field by +10% to +65%, depending on its rarity. All Shell Amulets guarantee this buff.
*   **Guiding Star/Onett's Lid Art:** Activating the Guiding Star passive ability can summon a guiding star to this field if it is one of the player's five least collected fields. Additionally, using Onett's Lid Art can summon a guiding star here.

### Deprecated Boost Methods
The following methods for obtaining Stump Field boosts are no longer possible:
*   Giving a Present to Onett provided x1 Stump Field Boost and other items/boosts.
*   Purchasing a Stump Field Market Boost from the Boost Market granted x1.5 Field Capacity, x1.25 Stump Field Pollen, x1.1 Goo, and x1.25 Convert Rate At Hive for 30 minutes.
*   Redeeming certain expired codes (MoreTime, 2MFavorites, Reboooot, Willgoold, GravyGoodies, RobziRampage, FrozenBugReboot) provided various Stump Field Boosts and other rewards.

## Trivia
*   Before the September 28, 2019 update, the Stump Field was a more balanced colored field. It was later updated to function as a blue-oriented counterpart to the Pepper Patch.
*   This is the first field players encounter that naturally only has triple flowers; the second is Mountain Top Field, followed by Coconut Field and Pepper Patch.
*   It is the only field where gumdrops can be obtained from leaves.
*   Along with the Hub Field, this is one of the two non-quadrilateral fields in the game (it was previously the only one before the 2024-01-12 Update).
*   This is the only blue field where blueberries cannot spawn naturally. It is also the only blue field that contains red flowers.
*   Along with the Ant Field and Hub Field, this is one of three fields where Wild Windy Bees cannot spawn or fly to. However, Onett can summon them here; if defeated, they remain in place until their timer expires due to a lack of programmed flight patterns for Stump Field Windy Bees.
*   This field, Coconut Field, Pepper Patch, Ant Field, and Hub Field were all added after the game's initial release.
*   Stump Field, Coconut Field, and Spider Field share the same cave appearance. Unlike Werewolf's Cave or Coconut Cave, the Stump Snail's Cave offers no special rewards, making it similar to the Spider Field's cave.
*   There is a glue token located between the large pineapple and the base of the stump.
*   According to Black Bear lore, there was once a gigantic tree blocking sunlight; a huge bear cut it down, allowing a field to grow on the resulting stump—the origin of the Stump Field's name. Spirit Bear also climbed this tree before it was cut down and met Windy Bee during the quest "The Sky Over The Stump."
*   This is one of three fields without decorations (along with Ant Field, Mountain Top Field, and Hub Field), deriving its name from the stump it sits upon.
*   During Beesmas 2019 and 2020, an obstacle course was located to the left of the field; completing it led to a Purple Gift Box. This has since been replaced by Dapper Bear's Shop.
*   This is the only field that can be completely covered by The Supreme Saturator sprinkler.
*   The Stump Field is currently the third smallest field in the game, following Pumpkin Patch and Ant Field.
*   Because the field has a square hitbox, planters can be placed on its outer rim. Harvesting a planter using this glitch causes loot tokens to spawn on the closest flowers in the field.
*   This is the only field where the flowers are arranged in a circle, unlike other fields which are rectangular or square.
