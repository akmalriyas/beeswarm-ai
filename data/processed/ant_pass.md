# Ant Pass

[![Ant Pass](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/da/Ant_Pass.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404033127)

> "Grants you 1 admission to the Ant Challenge."

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** None
*   **Cap:** 10

An **Ant Pass** is an inventory item introduced in the 2018-07-11 update. It must be used to participate in the [Ant Challenge]. Players are limited to a maximum of 10 Ant Passes in their inventory at any time (unless they possessed more than 10 before the 2019-04-05 update, purchased them from Robux packs, or received them via quests).

To use an Ant Pass, players must step on the pad in front of the Ant Challenge and press "E" (or click the prompt).

## Ways to Obtain

*   **Free Dispenser:** A Free Ant Pass Dispenser is located past the Ant Gate and can be used every two hours.
*   **Paid Dispenser:** An Ant Pass Dispenser, located next to the free dispenser, grants an Ant Pass for 10 [Tickets]. This dispenser has no cooldown.
    *   *Note: If a player already possesses 10 or more Ant Passes, they cannot claim passes from either dispenser.*
*   **Quests:** Completing Panda Bear's Ant Questline rewards an Ant Pass after every quest completion (excluding Ultimate Ant Annihilation 5).
*   **Mob Drops:** They can be rewarded by defeating certain mobs, including the Ladybug, Rage Aphid, King Beetle, and Tunnel Bear.
*   **Milestone Rewards:** Defeating the Commando Chick may reward Ant Passes if the player reaches specific milestones.
*   **Robux Shop:** Ant Passes are sometimes available in the Robux Shop, often bundled with other items.
*   **Hidden Tokens:** There are two hidden Ant Pass tokens in the game:
    1.  Behind the Ant Challenge (located behind the Top Ant Exterminators Leaderboard, through a tunnel to the exit).
    2.  At the very back of Star Hall, behind the Supreme Star Amulet Generator.
*   **Snowbear:** Summoning and defeating a level 4 Snowbear rewards 3 Ant Passes.
*   **Bee Bear's Catalog:** Purchasing the Poinsettia Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog (for 250 Snowflakes and 25 Gingerbread Bears) grants 5 Ant Passes, along with other items.

### Outdated Methods

The following methods for obtaining Ant Passes are no longer active:
*   Redeeming certain expired codes (e.g., Oodlesofnoodles, ExtraPass, SecretProfilecode).
*   Completing Bubble Bee Man's B.B.M. Mission quest.

## Trivia

*   If a player finds an Ant Pass token, the game text reads: "You found **a** Ant Pass," instead of "You found **an** Ant Pass."
*   The plural form of the item is incorrectly spelled as "Ant Passs" in some contexts.
