# Blue Cannon

The **Blue Cannon** is a transportation device located on a rock platform situated between the Pine Tree Forest and the Rose Field, neighboring the Royal Jelly Shop.

### Mechanics and Usage

*   **Availability:** The cannon becomes available for use once the player has discovered 16 different types of bees.
*   **Cooldown:** It has a 5-second cooldown period. This cooldown is shared by all transportational cannons (Yellow Cannon, Red Cannon, etc.). While this duration is usually unnoticeable during normal gameplay due to the distance traveled, it can be observed using the Glider or Parachute glitch to stop momentum immediately after exiting the cannon.
*   **Default Trajectory:** If used without specific buffs or items—such as a Parachute or Glider—the Blue Cannon typically launches the player toward the nearest row of flowers in the Clover Field, closest to the Brown Bear. Occasionally, it may undershoot the target, landing near the Slingshot or between the Mushroom Field and the Dandelion Field.
*   **Advanced Use:** When used in conjunction with either a Parachute or Glider, the Blue Cannon can be utilized to collect the Royal Jelly token located on top of the tallest dandelion in the Dandelion Field. Furthermore, players who possess either the Parachute or Glider can use this cannon to reach the Star Hall.

## Trivia

*   The Blue Cannon is one of four cannons available in the game; the others are the Yellow Cannon, Red Cannon, and Gummy Cannon.
*   It is also one of three transportational cannons in the game, alongside the Yellow Cannon and Red Cannon.
