# All-Time Top Bucko Bee Helpers

The **All-Time Top Bucko Bee Helpers** is one of the game's leaderboards, tracking the top players who have completed the most quests for Gifted Bucko Bee. The leaderboard displays up to 100 entries and can show 10 positions at a time. If a player stands on the designated white circle in the location, they can view their personal quest completion count.
