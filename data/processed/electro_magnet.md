The **Electro-Magnet** is a tool added in the 2018-06-02 update. It can be purchased from the Pro Shop.

## Crafting Ingredients

To craft the Electro-Magnet, you need:
*   300,000 Honey

## Trivia

*   This tool is an upgraded version of the Magnet. Both tools collect pollen in a square pattern, but they differ visually and mechanically. The Electro-Magnet is yellow and white with visible electricity on its tips; the Magnet is red and gray without electricity. Furthermore, the Electro-Magnet collects faster and features four tips, while the Magnet only has two.
*   The Electro-Magnet belongs to a group of tools (including Clippers, Golden Rake, Spark Staff, Petal Wand, Dark Scythe, Tide Popper, and Gummyballer) that were all added in a single update.
*   The associated sticker for the Electro-Magnet is missing its handle design element.
