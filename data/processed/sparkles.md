# Sparkles

Sparkles are a visual effect applied to certain patches of flowers, yielding a token when harvested. These tokens can contain various rewards, including Honey, random Treats corresponding to the field they are in, Moon Charms, or rarely, Tickets, Royal Jelly, Neonberry, Star Jelly, or Glitter.

When enough pollen is collected from a sparkling flower, it generates a collectible token for any player. This token must be picked up within 10 seconds before it disappears.

### Generation Mechanics

Sparkles are generated through several in-game mechanisms:

*   **Large Plants:** The largest plants in the Sunflower Field, Pineapple Patch, and Pumpkin Patch occasionally generate a face and blow bubbles (3 to 5 times) on their respective fields. This process creates 13 patches of sparkling pollen.
*   **Blue HQ Bubble Wand:** The Blue HQ's Bubble Wand generates bubbles on the Dandelion Field, Mushroom Field, Blue Flower Field, and Clover Field in a similar fashion. Each of these locations can generate sparkles around every 45 minutes individually.
*   **Fireflies:** Individual fireflies that spawn during the night cycle leave behind sparkles on any flowers they were resting on as they are chased away.

## Faces

The game features various plant faces associated with this effect:

*   Plant Face (Blinking)
*   Plant Face (Inactive)
*   Plant Face (Active)

## Trivia and History

*   **Community Name:** Sparkles were initially referred to by the community as "spit," due to the appearance of the various plant faces seemingly spitting on the field. Onett later renamed this effect "sparkles." The verb form for this action is 'sparkletificate'.
*   **Visuals:** When a face fades away, its eyes close before it disappears.
*   **Updates:**
    *   Prior to the 2019-09-28 update, sparkles covered only 4 flowers instead of the current 13.
    *   In the 2018-09-10 update, the chances of sparkles being created were doubled.
    *   The Blue HQ Bubble Wand model can sometimes leave sparkles directly under large mushrooms in the Mushroom Field, making rewards difficult to collect.
    *   An early bug (pre-2018-07-11) involved the Clover Field size change not being accounted for, causing the Bubble Wand on the Blue HQ to occasionally produce sparkles where no flowers existed.
*   **Lore:** Spirit Bear mentions the existence of these sparkles in her dialogue before the player receives her eighth quest, remarking negatively on how they were created by the faces.
