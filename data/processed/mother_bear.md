# Mother Bear

**Mother Bear** is a permanent quest giver and one of the eight permanent bear characters in Bee Swarm Simulator.

## Overview

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Species** | Quest Bear |
| **Location** | Far end of the Sunflower Field, under the tent next to the Treat Shop. |
| **Bee Prerequisites** | None |

### Appearance Colors
*   **Fur:** `#a5954d` / `#897e4b` (Shade)
*   **Snout:** `#a8974f` / `#a89f70`
*   **Torso:** `#b2b184`
*   **Arms:** `#968643`
*   **Legs:** `#a6944d`

Mother Bear's quests focus on leveling up the player's bees and feeding them treats. She is located below the red-and-white-striped awning at the far end of the Sunflower Field, near the Strawberry Field and Mushroom Field.

She introduces new treats during her questline and provides a **Star Egg** near the end of the line, along with a **Star Treat**.

## Dialogue Insights

Mother Bear provides unique in-game information during her quests:

**Treat Tutorial:** Explains that bees have emotions and bond with the player. Higher level bees gather more pollen, produce more honey, move faster, and defend better. Bond increases by 10 when feeding a Treat.
**Bonding With Bees:** Different bee types prefer different fields. Pink numbers indicate increased bond. Happier bees are faster.
**Search For A Sunflower Seed:** Rare treats can be found in the fields or dropped by defeated bugs. Basic, Bomber, Looker, Demo, Commander, and Ninja Bee all love Sunflower Seeds (gaining double bond).
**The Gist of Jellies:** Explains that **Royal Jelly** transforms a bee while retaining its level and bond, but changing its stats. Brown Bear can reward Royal Jellies.
**Search for Strawberries:** Strawberries raise bond by 25. Rascal, Rad, Rage, Riley, Baby, and Shy Bee all love them (gaining double bond) and have a chance to become Gifted when fed them.
**Binging on Blueberries:** Blueberries are favored by Bumble, Cool, Bubble, Frosty, Bucko, Diamond, and Music Bee (gaining double bond). They can be found in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, and Pine Tree Forest, or dropped by Rhino Beetles.
**Royal Jelly Jamboree:** Introduces Gifted Bees—rare bees with star patterns that collect 50% more pollen and make honey 50% faster. Hive Bonuses enhance swarm power (e.g., a Gifted Cool Bee increases blue pollen by 10%).
**Search for Sunflower Seeds:** Higher level bees are necessary to defend against higher-level monsters; lower level attacks may "MISS."
**Picking out Pineapples:** Pineapple is popular, favored by Brave, Hasty, Shocked, Exhausted, Honey, Fire, Lion, and Demon Bee. The Treat Dispenser near the Pro Shop also drops Pineapples.
**Seven to Seven:** Rewards a **Star Egg**, which always hatches into a Gifted Bee. First Gifted Bees of each type grant a Hive Bonus (e.g., a Gifted Basic Bee increases all pollen by 20%).
**Mother Bear's Midterm (Second):** Introduces the **Star Treat**, which instantly turns any bee Gifted, allowing the player to choose their desired Gifted type. Mentions that three Star Treats are obtainable for free: hers, Panda Bear's, and one from Onett.
**Eight to Eight:** Special treats can be found in fields or dropped by monsters, but challenging activities (like the Ant Challenge) are reliable sources.
**Sights on the Stars:** Collecting different types of Gifted Bees allows players to generate **Star Amulets**. These trinkets multiply backpack space: Bronze (5 Types), Silver (10 Types), Gold (20 Types), and Diamond (30 Types).
**The Family Final:** The ultimate goal is a hive of every Gifted Bee at Level 10. Completing this quest grants the final Star Treat, which turns any bee Gifted, and provides an additional Royal Jelly daily in the Star Hall.

## Trivia & Lore

*   Mother Bear is the first female bear added to the game; she and Spirit Bear are the only female bears.
*   She, along with Brown Bear and Onett, are quest givers who provide Tickets in non-event quests.
*   She, Panda Bear, Brown Bear, and Onett are among the few quest givers who give Star Treats.
*   Mother Bear is unique among permanent bears for not requiring players to collect pollen or defeat mobs (excluding event quests).
*   Like Dapper Bear, Mother Bear's face decal emits light, visible in dark places.
*   She mentions that Onett is the developer of Bee Swarm Simulator during her "Midterm" quest.
