# Cave Monster

## Overview

The Cave Monster is a level 11 mob that spawns exclusively inside Werewolf's Cave. It looks visually identical to a regular Spider, except for the absence of eyes. When players approach them, they emit eerie whispering noises.

| Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Health** | 400 |
| **Damage** | 10,000 |
| **Battle Points** | 1 |
| **Bond Honey** | N/A |
| **Honey Level** | 11 |

*Note: Do not confuse the Cave Monster with a regular Spider.*

## Location and Mechanics

### Location
Werewolf's Cave

### Respawn Timer
30 minutes

Cave Monsters primarily guard a Gold Egg located at the end of Werewolf's Cave. Due to the cave's low ceiling, players must be cautious of hitting their heads, which can cause slowing effects.

**Combat Strategy:**
These monsters move very quickly, making them difficult for slower bees to reach. The most effective ways to defeat them include:
1. Activating the **Emergency Coconut Shield** passive ability and running toward the gold egg token.
2. Utilizing the **Star Saw** passive ability (from the Supreme Star Amulet or Festive Bee's Beesmas Cheer).
3. Quickly moving into and then out of their spawning zone if the player does not possess a Coconut Canister.

Players can also reach the Gold Egg using Haste+ (Haste x5 or higher) or by employing a Glider.

## Drops

Cave Monsters are one of the few mobs that rarely drop items. They are the only mobs to occasionally drop a **Honey Bee Egg**.

*   **Honey Bee Egg:** Somewhat rare drop.

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Lethality:** Cave Monsters are among the most lethal non-boss mobs in the game, as they can instantly kill the player upon touch (along with Tunnel Bear).
*   **Rarity & Drops:** Originally, there were four Cave Monsters because the Gold Egg was once an extremely rare item. They have one of the fewest possible amounts of drops, only rivaled by Ants and certain Robo Bear Challenge mobs which drop nothing.
*   **Unique Properties:**
    *   They are currently the only defeatable mobs with a set respawn time that does not require the player to kill them for quest purposes.
    *   They are one of the few defeatable mobs that do not display a health bar (though they did have a visible 400 HP, Level 11 health bar during updates in late 2018).
    *   Cave Monsters do not count as spiders toward quests given by Bears.
*   **Bean Bug Connection:** Donating the Honey Bee Egg to the Wind Shrine has a decent chance of spawning Bean Bugs, making the act of killing Cave Monsters indirectly useful for collecting tokens required for certain quests.
*   **Developer Notes:** Onett, a developer, once joked that "Saving the honey bee from the cave monsters was the ultimate goal of Bee Swarm Simulator."
