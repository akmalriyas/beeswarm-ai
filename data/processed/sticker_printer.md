# Sticker Printer

![StickerPrinter](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8f/StickerPrinter.png/revision/latest?cb=20240123233115 "StickerPrinter") The Sticker Printer

## Information
The **Sticker Printer** is a machine located to the right of the Instant Converter, next to the Mountain Top Field. It requires 25 bees to operate and has a cooldown of 1 hour.

It costs eggs (excluding Bee Specific Eggs) to print stickers.

## Mechanics
The Sticker Printer prints one sticker upon use and has a mandatory 1-hour cooldown before it can be used again.

The type and quality of the resulting sticker are determined by the egg donated. Before using an egg, players can check its potential chances for Hive Skin, Cub Skin, or specific stickers by clicking on it.

**Usage Requirements:**
To use the Sticker Printer, a player's Roblox account must be at least 7 days old and they must be a member of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club.
