# Brave Bee

**Brave Bee** is a Colorless and Rare bee known for its loyalty ("This loyal bee will do anything to protect its owner.").

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Rare |
| **Color** | Colorless |
| **Energy** | 30 |
| **Speed** | 16.8 |
| **Attack** | 6 |

Brave Bee's favorite treat is Pineapples. It likes the Clover Field and Spider Field, but dislikes the Dandelion Field.

## Stats & Production

### Collection Rates (Base)
*   Collects 10 Pollen in 4 seconds.
*   Makes 200 Honey in 4 seconds.

### Stat Bonuses
Brave Bee provides the following bonuses:
*   +20% Movespeed
*   +50% Energy
*   +120 Convert Amount
*   +5 Attack

**Gifted Hive Bonus:** +1 Bee Attack

### Abilities
None.

## Trivia

*   Brave Bee and Basic Bee are unique among bees in that they do not produce Ability Tokens unless they are gifted or equipped with a specific Beequip.
*   The Brave Bee Gate features a large model of the bee.
*   It is noted as the strongest Rare bee regarding attack power, starting at 6.
*   The Brave Guard, an item purchased in the Pro Shop, shares design elements and stats dedicated to Brave Bee.
*   Brave Bee's body color scheme is a darker variation of the Vector Bee's color scheme.
*   One of sixteen bees that have a corresponding sticker.
*   During Bee Bear's "Ability Token Tidings" quest, Bee Bear refers to Brave Bee and Basic Bee as "Poor little merely mortals bees," noting their inability to create ability tokens.
*   Although non-functional in terms of preference, Brave Bee is the only bee with a coded disliked treat: blueberries.
