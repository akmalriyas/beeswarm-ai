# Jar

![Jar](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/77/Jar.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190402214910)

### Description
A durable plastic jar. It holds over twice as much capacity as the Pouch!

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cost** | 650 Honey |
| **Capacity** | 750 Pollen |

> "A durable Plastic Jar. Holds over twice as much as the Pouch!"

The **Jar** is a Bag that can be purchased in the Noob Shop.

### Crafting Ingredients
*   650 Honey

### Stats
*   +750 Capacity.

### Trivia
*   This is the cheapest Bag available for purchase, both in the Noob Shop and overall in the game.
*   It is the third cheapest item when considering its cost in Honey, only surpassed by the Pouch, Scooper, and Strange Goggles.
*   The Jar and the Pouch are the only Bags whose base capacity exceeds their honey cost.
*   Although its description states it holds over twice as much as the Pouch, it actually holds more than three times as much.
*   A larger version of the Jar exists in the Pro Shop called the Mega-Jug, which has a base Capacity of 25,000 Pollen for a price of 50,000 Honey.
