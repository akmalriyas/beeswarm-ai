# Oil

![Oil](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/06/Oil.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404021100)

> "Grants x1.2 Bee and Player Movespeed for 10 minutes"

**Cooldown:** 1 second

Oil is an inventory item introduced in the 2018-11-25 update. When activated, it grants a buff of x1.2 to both Bee and Player Movespeed for 10 minutes. Using another Oil resets the timer but does not stack. It can also be used as a component in crafting other accessories or items. This buff can be overridden by using a Super Smoothie.

## Acquisition Methods

Oil can be obtained through several methods:

### Crafting
*   **Blender:** 50 Sunflower Seeds + 10 Royal Jellies

### Drops and Rewards
*   Matching a pair of Oil in Memory Match.
*   As a reward for donating to the Wind Shrine.
*   As a common drop from Robo Bear Challenge.
*   As a common drop from Stick Bug Challenge (Stick Bugs and Defense Totems yield oils).
*   **Sticker Stack Rewards:** As a stack reward for adding specific stickers, Oil is rewarded by:
    *   Flying Photon Bee
    *   Drooping Stubborn Bee
    *   Fuzz Bomb
    *   Yellow Coffee Mug
    *   White Flag
    *   Standing Beekeeper
    *   Honey Dipper

### Mob Drops
Oil drops from various mobs, including:
*   Tunnel Bear
*   Coconut Crab
*   Stump Snail
*   Stick Bug
*   Mantis (Rare)
*   Werewolf (Rare)
*   Armored Aphid (Rare)
*   King Beetle (Rare)
*   Wild Windy Bee (Rare)

### Farming and Environment
*   Harvesting leaves in the Sunflower Field may rarely yield oil.
*   A rare drop from sprouts (odds increase based on sprout rarity and location in the Sunflower Field).
*   **Planters:**
    *   Fully grown planters in the Sunflower Field almost always guarantee an Oil when harvested (Cooldown Unknown).
    *   Hydroponic Planters often drop a large amount of oil. Growing one three times consecutively in the Sunflower Field guarantees 100 oil.

### Gifts and Events
*   As a drop from a Festive Gift (if gifted by a Festive Bee) or a Festive Mark.
*   A rare gift from a Cub Buddy.
*   From Dapper Bear's Samovar (appears on the first use and every fifth use).
*   Reward from Robo Party Cake.
*   Drop from Stockings.

### Quests
Oil can be acquired through specific quests:
*   **Black Bear:** Repeatable quests have a chance of yielding oil upon completion.
*   **Brown Bear:** Rewards 1 Oil on the 35th, 105th, 160th, 185th, 255th, and 285th quests.
*   **Science Bear:** Awards oil for completing "Subsidized Agriculture" and "Meditating On Phenomenon."
*   **Polar Bear:** Occasionally gives oil after quest completion.
