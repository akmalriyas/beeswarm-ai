# Nectar Shower Vial

[![Nectar Shower Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a2/Nectar_Shower_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20241225154532)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a2/Nectar_Shower_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20241225154532)

> "Grants 4 hours of all Nectar types to every player in the server!"

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 5 Seconds
*   **Cap:** 10

The **Nectar Shower Vial** is a consumable nectar vial item introduced in the 2024-12-25 update. Upon use, it grants all players in the server a buff of all Nectars for 4 hours. When activated, a message appears in the chat stating: "(Username) has popped open a Nectar Shower Vial!"

## Ways to Obtain

*   **Sticker Stack Reward:** It can be obtained as a stack reward when adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack, specifically via the ![Offline Voucher](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index).
*   **Bee Bear's Catalog (Season's Sharing Bundle):** Purchasing this bundle for 600 Snowflakes and 60 Gingerbread Bears grants one Nectar Shower Vial along with other items.
*   **Robux Purchase (Feelin' Festive Pack):** The Feelin' Festive Pack costs 800 Robux and grants five Nectar Shower Vials, among other contents.
*   **Spirit Bear Quest:** Completing Spirit Bear's Galentine Shrine quest during Beesmas 2025 rewards one Nectar Shower Vial and additional items.
*   **Dapper Bear Quest:** Completing Dapper Bear's Sticker Style quest rewards one Nectar Shower Vial and additional items.

### Outdated Acquisition Methods

The following methods for obtaining the vial are now outdated:

*   **Royal Winter Wonder Haul (Expired April 30, 2025):** Costing 4,500 Robux, this haul granted ten Nectar Shower Vials and other items.
*   **Bee Bear Quest:** Completing Bee Bear's "Cheering Up Gloomy Cub (4/5): Material Things?" quest rewarded three Nectar Shower Vials.
*   **Bubble Bee Man Quest:** Completing Bubble Bee Man's "BBM's Naughty List" during Winter Beesmas 2024 rewarded seven Nectar Shower Vials.
*   **Cyber Monday Mythic Pack:** Purchasing this pack for 1,000 Robux granted five Nectar Shower Vials and other items.

## Trivia

*   This item, along with the Nectar Tester, is one of only two items that grants more than one type of nectar buff.
*   The Nectar Shower Vial and Snowflake are the only consumable items that **directly** grant buffs to all players in the server. Furthermore, it is the only Nectar Vial capable of granting nectar benefits to other players.
