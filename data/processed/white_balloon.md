# White Balloon

[![White Balloon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/da/White_Balloon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031425)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/da/White_Balloon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031425)

> "Floats over the field for 5m. Stores up to 10x your capacity and works best with White flowers."

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 7 minutes
*   **Capacity Cap (Inventory):** 25
*   **Field Limit:** 1 per player

The **White Balloon** is a consumable item introduced in the December 26, 2021 update. When used, it creates a white balloon over the player's current field. This balloon acts as a storage unit for pollen collected underneath it.

**Mechanics:**
*   It holds up to 10 times the player's standard capacity.
*   Pollen collected below the balloon receives a 10% increase in yield.
*   White pollen specifically increases by 20%.
*   The balloon floats over the field for 5 minutes before automatically floating to the hive balloon.

***

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Ways to Obtain
*   As an exceptionally rare drop from a Spider.
*   As a rare reward after completing a Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Completing certain Dapper Bear quests:
    *   Wandering Ambition rewards 1 White Balloon and other items.
    *   The Long Haul rewards 1 White Balloon and other items.
    *   Solitarily Steadfast rewards 3 White Balloons and other items.

### Outdated Ways (Historical)
*   Completing Bee Bear's Beesmas Bouquet and Snow Cub Reformation (4/5).
*   Purchasing the Buoyant Pack in the Robux Shop, which granted 3 white balloons and other items.
*   Purchasing the Balloon Bundle in Bee Bear’s Catalog during Beesmas 2021, granting 3 White Balloons.
*   Opening certain Gift Boxes during the Beesmas 2021 event:
    *   The Gilded Gift Box gave 1 White Balloon and other items.
*   Opening certain Gift Boxes during the Beesmas 2022 event:
    *   The Lapis Gift Box gave 2 White Balloons and other items.
    *   The Tropical Gift Box gave 3 White Balloons and other items.
*   Redeeming certain expired codes (e.g., FourYearFiesta, 6irthday).
*   Giving a present to Science Bear in Beesmas 2022 granted 1 White Balloon and other items.
*   Purchasing the Balloon Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog for 333 Snowflakes, granting 2 White Balloons and other items.
*   As a reward from certain Robo Party ranks.
