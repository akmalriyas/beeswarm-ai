# Gummy Beacon

The Gummy Beacon is a Beesmas machine introduced in the 2020+ updates. It can be unlocked after completing the "Gummy Bear's" Beesmas quest.

## Location and Activation

The beacon is located on top of the Noob Shop, situated on the corner closest to the Royal Jelly Dispenser, next to the Clover Field, and in front of the Star Hall.

*   **Pre-Quest:** If a player attempts to activate the Gummy Beacon without completing "Gummy Bear's" Beesmas quest, it displays the message: *"The satellite dish isn't working, but it still emits bad vibes..."*
*   **Post-Quest:** Completing the required quest allows the player to activate a Gummy Siege.

## Gummy Siege Mechanics

Upon activation, a platform appears featuring Gummy Bear and Gummy Bee in front of the beacon, accompanied by the Gummy Morph tune. The siege then begins:

1.  **Summoning:** Three random leveled Gummy Bees (level 8–20) are summoned for a duration of five minutes.
2.  **Goo Attacks:** Gummy Bear begins shooting goo puddles at random fields, similar to the Gummy Invasion event. The targeted field also spawns Gumdrops tokens.
3.  **Targeting Cycle:** Gummy Bear selects five random fields (excluding the Ant Field and Hub Field). For each selected field, he shoots goo for one minute, resulting in a total siege duration of five minutes.

The cooldown period for the Gummy Siege is 8 hours.

### Server Messages During Siege

When the Gummy Siege starts, two server-wide messages appear:
*   Gummy Bear: "Artillery has arrived, Gummy Soldier {Username}."
*   Gummy Bear: "Beesmas ends now. Commence GOO YEAR celebrations!"

As Gummy Bear targets fields, additional server-wide messages display the target location:
*   Gummy Bear: "Sight set on {Field Name}!"

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   **Gummy Bear Appearances:** This is the second, and currently only, way to make Gummy Bear appear in the mountain; the first method involves using the Gummy Cannon. Although visible during the siege, he can still be seen in his lair.
*   **Machine Type:** The Gummy Beacon is one of four machines that summon temporary bees (the others being Honeyday Candles, Onett's Lid Art, and Honey Bee's Honey Wreath).
*   **Bee Count:** It is the only way to have more than one Gummy Bee at a time, though this effect is limited in duration.
*   **Passive Contribution:** The Gumdrops tokens generated by the Gummy Bees help players activate the Gummy Morph passive ability quickly.
*   **Event Bees:** Along with Onett's Lid Art, the Gummy Beacon is one of only two ways to generate multiple Event Bees, though these are also temporary.
*   **Cooldown Status:** The Gummy Beacon, Onett's Lid Art, and the Naughty List are among the Beesmas machines that possess the longest cooldown (8 hours).
*   **Gummy Morph Interaction:** While the Gummy Bees summoned by the siege are not affected by the Gummy Mask passive ability, their abilities still contribute to activating the passive.
*   **Visual Glitch:** Following the Beesmas 2025 update, the summoned Gummy Bee NPC exhibits an extremely shaky idle animation due to its model using Roblox's legacy movement engine.
