## Mechanics and Location

To check a player's personal blue pollen count in the game, players can step on the white pad located near the leaderboard. A message will then appear stating: "You've collected 'x' Blue Pollen," where 'x' is the amount of pollen the player has gathered.

**Location:**
The leaderboard is found on the first floor of the **Blue HQ**, situated next to the ramp leading to the second floor.
