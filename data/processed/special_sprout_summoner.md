# Special Sprout Summoner

[![The Special Sprout Summoner.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d5/SpecialSproutSummoner.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20181221090551)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d5/SpecialSproutSummoner.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20181221090551) [](/wiki/File:SpecialSproutSummoner.jpeg)

The **Special Sprout Summoner** is a machine located near the Red HQ and Sunflower Field. It allows players who have discovered all 8 Legendary bee types to summon one of several sprout rarities—rare, epic, legendary, supreme, gummy, or moon—in a random field (excluding Coconut Field or Pepper Patch).

The machine has a cooldown of 16 hours. It was introduced along with sprouts in the 2018-09-10 update and is decorated with a rare sprout that emits particles.

When successfully used, the game announces:
🌱 {Username} has planted a(n) [Rarity] Sprout...🌱

## Trivia

*   The Special Sprout Summoner and the Honeystorm summoner are the only machines that require discovering Legendary bee types to be used. The Special Sprout Summoner requires 8 Legendary bees, while the Honeystorm summoner requires 4.
    *   Similarly, the Mythic Meteor Shower summoner requires discovering 3 Mythic bee types to be usable.
*   Prior to the 2018-11-25 update, this machine was originally a normal sprout summoner called the Sprout Summoner. It had a standard appearance and only required the discovery of 7 Legendary bees (as there were only 7 at that time). Its cooldown was shorter (8 hours), and it had a chance to summon normal sprouts.
    *   Before this update, Magic Beans did not exist, making this summoner the sole method for players to manually summon a sprout.
*   Following the 2018-11-25 update, the Special Sprout Summoner can no longer summon a normal sprout.
