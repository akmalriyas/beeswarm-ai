# Moon Amulet

[![The Moon Amulet icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f9/Moon_Amulet.png/revision/latest?cb=20240227002833)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f9/Moon_Amulet.png/revision/latest?cb=20240227002833)

The **Moon Amulet** is an amulet created by spending 100 Moon Charms at the Moon Amulet Generator.

### Functionality
This amulet grants buffs to the owner and allows the player to walk on moon-shaped platforms found in specific locations: Dapper Bear’s Shop, the Diamond Room (Obstacle Courses), and the 30 Bee Zone Obby—but only during the night cycle.

When generating an amulet using the generator, a chat message appears:
*   **Standard:** "The moon seems to grow brighter..."
*   **Max Capacity Buffed:** "The moon seems happy!"

Generating a Moon Amulet also triggers the audio file: <https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:MoonAmuletGet.ogg>

### Quality and Scaling
The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the total number of Moon Amulets generated, up to 40 amulets (maximum quality).

**Capacity Buff Formula:**
The capacity granted by the amulet is calculated as follows, rounded to the nearest 25,000:
$$capacity = 25000 + 225000 \times \min\left(1, {\frac {quality}{0.24875}}\right)$$

*Note: The capacity increases by 25,000 with each new amulet, except for the 7th amulet.*

### Possible Buffs
Moon Amulets can grant a variety of stats. Below is a list of possible buffs and their base ranges/probabilities:

| Stat | Range | Probability Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Capacity** (Guaranteed) | +25,000 - +250,000 | Intervals of 25,000 (100%) |
| **Ticket Chance** (Potentially) | +1% - +5% | Intervals of 1% (0.01%) |
| **Movement Collection** (Potentially) | +1 - +3 | Intervals of 1 (0.04%) |
| **Bond From Treats** (Select 1) | +3% - +10% | Intervals of 1% (50%) |
| **White Pollen** (Select 1) | +20% - +50% | Intervals of 1% (50%) |
| **Pollen** | +1% - +5% | Intervals of 1% (14.2857%) |
| **Honey Per Pollen** | +2% - +8% | Intervals of 1% (14.2857%) |
| **Instant Conversion** | +1% - +5% | Intervals of 1% (14.2857%) |
| **Bond From Battle** | +5% - +12% | Intervals of 1% (14.2857%) |
| **Pollen From Tools** | +10% - +20% | Intervals of 1% (14.2857%) |
| **Bee Gather Pollen** | +10% - +25% | Intervals of 1% (14.2857%) |
| **Pineapple Patch Pollen** | +50% - +150% | Intervals of 1% (14.2857%) |
