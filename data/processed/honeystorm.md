# Honeystorms

A **Honeystorm** is a server-wide event that can be summoned by any player who has discovered at least four [Legendary Bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary "Bees/Legendary") types, using the pad located near the [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear").

When successfully summoned, a server-wide announcement is made:
`[Username] has summoned a Honeystorm!`

Upon activation, a golden-yellow fog appears across the map. This event causes numerous [honey tokens](/wiki/Honey "Honey") and [ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") tokens to randomly appear in all [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"), including the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge").

**Mechanics:**
*   **Duration & Cooldown:** The storm lasts for 20 seconds and has a cooldown of 4 hours.
*   **Random Trigger:** Honeystorms can also be triggered randomly, with an announcement stating: `A Honeystorm has been summoned!`
*   **Audio Cue:** When a honeystorm is summoned, the honey tokens emit raindrop-like popping noises. Similar to the [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower"), there is also an audio clip of a distorted voice from BSS Staff member [IcedTeaLatte](/wiki/BSS_Staff "BSS Staff") saying "Honeystorm."
*   **Honey Yield:** Fields with larger [flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers") yield higher amounts of honey per token. The total amount collected is increased by the player's [Honey from Tokens](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_From_Tokens "System Page") stat.
*   **Token Despawn:** Honeystorm tokens despawn faster than standard honey tokens.

## Trivia

*   The honeystorm fog is normally yellow, but during Beesmas in 2018 and 2019, it was white. As of Beesmas 2020, this color change has been replaced by [Snowstorms](/wiki/Snowstorm "Snowstorm") using a white fog.
*   The Honeystorm Summoner and the [Special Sprout Summoner](/wiki/Special_Sprout_Summoner "Special Sprout Summoner") are the only machines that require players to discover Legendary bee types for activation (Honeystorm requires 4, Special Sprout Summoner requires 8).
*   A honeystorm's yellow tint can generate enough light to make [Cave Monsters](/wiki/Cave_Monster "Cave Monster") in the Werewolf's cave visible.
*   There is a known [glitch](/wiki/Glitches "Glitches"): if the game switches between day and [night](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle "Day/Night Cycle") while a honeystorm is active, the fog will visually disappear. The storm itself continues to function normally. This glitch is shared with Mythic Meteor Showers and Snowstorms.
*   Around 2018-05-26, there was a temporary issue where the Honeystorm Summoner could be removed from a server. Players encountering this could simply leave and rejoin the game; the issue was later fixed.
*   Following the [2018-09-10 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-09-10 "Updates"), the Honeystorm Summoner was relocated, and the [Wealth Clock](/wiki/Wealth_Clock "Wealth Clock") now occupies its former spot. The summoner is currently located between the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field") and [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ").
*   The honeystorm can be summoned by [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") when receiving the quest "Witness Grandeur."
*   According to [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear"), [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") is also capable of summoning honeystorms.
*   If a honeystorm is summoned at night, the fog will persist even after the storm ends until daytime arrives.
*   Honeystorms can stack with other active honeystorms. This stacking increases the total duration of the event but does not increase its intensity.
