# Tacky Planter

The Tacky Planter is a reusable planter introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. It can be purchased from Dapper Bear's Shop and serves as a source of bonus items, nectar, and potential puffshrooms.

***
*Note: This content incorporates datamined information regarding specific probabilities and mechanics, which may supersede general in-game descriptions.*
***

## Stats & Acquisition

| Attribute | Value | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reusable** | Yes | Permanent planter. |
| **Base Grow Time** | ~8 hours | Standard growth time. |
| **Pollen Capacity** | 15,000,000 Pollen | Maximum pollen storage capacity. |
| **Nectar Bonus** | +25% Satisfying & Comforting | Base nectar quality increase. |

### Cost to Purchase
*   50,000,000 Honey
*   10 Magic Beans
*   1 Purple Potion
*   5 Hard Waxes
*   20 Soft Waxes

## Field Bonuses (Starter Zone)

When planted in the Starter Zone (including Sunflower, Dandelion, Mushroom, Blue Flower, and Clover Fields), the Tacky Planter receives significant bonuses:

*   **Grow Time:** Reduced by 25%.
*   **Pollen Multiplier:** +100% Pollen.
*   **Nectar Bonus:** Grants an additional +25% Satisfying Nectar and +25% Comforting Nectar.

## Harvesting Mechanics & Drops

### Puffshroom Spawn Chance
Harvesting a fully grown Tacky Planter has a 1/5 chance to spawn a Level 5 Puffshroom. The rarity probabilities for the spawned puffshroom are:

*   **Common:** ~73.15%
*   **Rare:** ~21.95%
*   **Epic:** ~4.75%
*   **Legendary:** ~0.15%

### Token Drops
When harvested, the planter yields up to 22 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, two specific tokens are guaranteed: one Field Dice and one Ticket. The remaining tokens are randomly drawn from a non-exhaustive list of rewards (which does not include field-specific bonuses):

*   Bonus
*   Other
*   Special

## Special Rewards & Trivia

**Advanced Drops:**
The Tacky Planter can yield several unique items depending on the specific conditions under which it is harvested:

*   **Swirled Wax:** Harvested from a Pumpkin Patch. (1 month cooldown)
*   **Caustic Wax:** Harvested from a Bamboo Field. (1 month cooldown)
*   **Loaded Dice:** Harvested from a Mountain Top Field. (1 month cooldown)
*   **Purple Potion:** Requires harvesting the planter sequentially from both Mushroom Field and Blue Flower Field. (Unknown cooldown)
*   **Dapper From Above Sticker:** Awarded once per player upon successfully completing a specific five-field chain: Clover $\rightarrow$ Coconut $\rightarrow$ Stump $\rightarrow$ Pepper $\rightarrow$ Mountain Top, with all planters being 100% harvested.
