# Daily Top Coconut Field Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Coconut Field Collectors** is one of 64 available leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. It can only be viewed through the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page.

This leaderboard tracks the amount of pollen players have collected specifically within the Coconut Field on a given day. The board displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes and Rewards

Players are rewarded based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach a rank in the top 25 receive the **Coconut Field Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach a rank in the top 100 receive 25 **Tickets**.
