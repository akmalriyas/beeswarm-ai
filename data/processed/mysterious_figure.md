# Mysterious Figure (Eggwick / Evilwick)

The **Mysterious Figure** (also known as **Eggwick** or **Evilwick**) was a short, faceless figure characterized by light gray skin and a gray hood. Initially, he could be found in the Blue Maze, positioned behind the glass wall on the path to the right. He was non-interactive and could not be collided with. Note that there were two small hallways adjacent to his location; players could bypass the surrounding wall using a game glitch to enter these passages.

Since the 2019-09-28 update, the figure has been replaced by a Star Jelly token. This item can only be obtained by entering a passage in the Petal Shop, which is located within the Windy Bee Gate.

## Trivia
*   He, along with Onett, Stick Bug, Gifted Riley Bee, Gifted Bucko Bee, Honey Bee (NPC), and Digital Bee (NPC), are the only NPCs that do not belong to the Bear category.
    *   Furthermore, he and Onett are the only Robloxian-species NPCs in the game.
*   This figure represents the Hooded Figure from the Roblox Egg Hunt 2019 event, an appearance also seen in other participating games and the hub world.
*   He would occasionally wave at the player.
*   The Mysterious Figure was unique as the only NPC that players normally could not reach due to a glass wall barrier. This made him the third such NPC, following Onett and Bubble Bee Man.
