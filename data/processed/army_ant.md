# Army Ant

An **Army Ant** is one of five mobs belonging to the family of Ants. These ants are exclusively found within the [Ant Challenge], which is located past the [Ant Gate].

## Location

Army Ants spawn in the [Ant Field] while the [Ant Challenge] is active.

## Combat Mechanics and Health Scaling

This mob is one of the first enemies players encounter during the Ant Challenge, capable of appearing even on the initial waves, though they typically begin appearing around the second or third wave.

Army Ants are characterized by their army helmets and aggressive pursuit of the player. They possess a moderate amount of health compared to other ants and have defensive capabilities that reduce incoming damage. Their level increases depending on which wave of the Ant Challenge the player is currently facing, ranging from Level 1 up to Level 16.

## Strategies

*   **Movement:** Army Ants are relatively slow, allowing players time to react and dodge their pounce attacks.
*   **Evasion:** By jumping past an Army Ant, your bees can lock onto the target while you maneuver around it, avoiding being pounced upon.
*   **Crowd Control:** When they spawn in waves, powerful crowd control abilities are highly effective. [Vicious Bee] and [Windy Bee] provide excellent area denial to counter large groups of Army Ants. [Digital Bee] can also stun multiple ants simultaneously, allowing them to take increased damage via [Mind Hack].

*   The [Ant Gate] is a dedicated gate associated with this mob type.
*   This mob is featured in depictions found on the [Ant Pass].
