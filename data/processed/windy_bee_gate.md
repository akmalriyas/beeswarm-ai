# Windy Bee Gate

![GreenWindyGate](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b3/GreenWindyGate.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20200513044757) The Windy Bee Gate is a major gate in the game, recognizable by the large Windy Bee figure atop it.

## Overview and Mechanics
The Windy Bee Gate, also referred to as the 35 Bee Gate, requires players to have 35 Bees to unlock. It is situated on the right side of both the Ticket Tent and the Red Cannon.

### Contents
Inside the gate, players gain access to several unique areas:
*   **Fields:** Coconut Field, Pepper Patch
*   **Quest Givers/Mobs:** Spirit Bear (Quest Giver), Coconut Crab (Mob)
*   **Shops:** Petal Shop, Coconut Cave
*   **Extras/Features:** Wind Shrine, Nectar Condenser, Extreme Memory Match, Coconut Dispenser, Blue Maze, Fastest Crab Slayers Leaderboard, Daily Top White Collectors, All-Time Top White Collectors

### Shops Inventory
The gate houses two distinct shops:
*   **Petal Shop:** Sells the Petal Wand, Petal Belt, and Petal Planter.
*   **Coconut Cave:** Sells the Coconut Canister, Coconut Clogs, and Coconut Belt.

## Gameplay Notes
*   This gate is the first, and currently the only, gate in the game to feature an Event Bee representing it.
*   Along with the Bear Gate, this is one of the few gates that requires players to purchase a Hive Slot to pass through. Both gates share the same background music.
*   The Petal Shop provides access to the blocked-off area within the Blue Maze.
*   This location is unique because sprouts cannot appear naturally or be planted using the Special Sprout Summoner here. However, sprouts planted by players in this gate require more pollen to grow than elsewhere, resulting in better loot and guaranteed drops.

## Music
The soundtrack "Overflowin4" plays when passing through this gate.
