# Windy Bee Gate

[ ![GreenWindyGate](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b3/GreenWindyGate.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20200513044757) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b3/GreenWindyGate.png/revision/latest?cb=20200513044757 "Green Windy Gate")

The **Windy Bee Gate** (also known as the 35 Bee Gate) is a gate that requires 35 bees to unlock. It is named after the large Windy Bee figure displayed on top of it.

## Location and Contents
This gate is situated to the right of the Ticket Tent and Red Cannon. Upon entering, players gain access to several key areas:

*   **Fields:** Coconut Field and Pepper Patch.
*   **Mobs:** Coconut Crab.
*   **Quest Givers:** Spirit Bear.
*   **Shops:** Petal Shop and Coconut Cave.
*   **Extras/Features:** Wind Shrine, Nectar Condenser, Coconut Dispenser, Blue Maze access, and various leaderboards (Fastest Crab Slayers, Daily Top White Collectors, All-Time Top White Collectors).

### Shops Inventory
The gate provides access to two unique shops:

*   **Petal Shop:** Sells the Petal Wand, Petal Belt, and Petal Planter.
*   **Coconut Cave:** Sells the Coconut Canister, Coconut Clogs, and Coconut Belt.

## Music
The soundtrack "Overflowin4" plays when passing through this gate.

## Trivia & Mechanics
*   This is the first, and currently only, gate in the game that features an Event Bee representing it.
*   Along with the Bear Gate, this is one of the few gates that requires purchasing a Hive Slot to pass. Both gates share the same background music.
*   The Petal Shop provides access to the blocked-off area within the Blue Maze.
*   This gate is unique because sprouts cannot appear naturally or be planted using the Special Sprout Summoner here. However, sprouts planted by players in this location require more pollen to grow than elsewhere, resulting in better loot and guaranteed drops.
