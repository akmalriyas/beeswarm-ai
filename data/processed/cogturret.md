# Cogturret

A **Cogturret** is a mob that appears during the Robo Bear Challenge. It is the third type of mob encountered in this challenge, first appearing at round 11.

![Cogturret](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2e/Cogturret.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20231203125525)

## Location and Mechanics

**Location:** Any field while the Robo Bear Challenge is active.
**Classification:** Mob (Robo Bear Challenge)

### Behavior Cycle
Cogturrets always position themselves on the field's edge, following a fixed behavior cycle similar to Cogmowers:

1.  **Hiding Phase:** The turret hides under the field and moves along the edges, either randomly or toward the player. During this state, it cannot be targeted.
2.  **Ascension/Targeting Phase:** It ascends and becomes targetable again.
3.  **Attack Phase:** After a short delay, the Cogturret blasts a giant red cog projectile in a straight line. This attack destroys flowers, removing all layers of the flower and any goo covering it.
4.  **Repeat:** The turret hides again and repeats the cycle.

## Strategies and Counterplay

*   **Evasion:** Cogturrets aim either at the player or randomly. Since they have a delay when ascending to attack, moving to the side immediately after they fire a cog often results in them missing their shot.
*   **Positioning:** The turrets always shoot straight at where they are facing. To avoid damage, ensure you are not directly in front of them during the attack phase.
*   **Damage Focus:** Standing near the Cogturret allows your bees to lock on and quickly deal damage.
*   **Crowd Control:** Vicious Bee and Windy Bee provide excellent crowd control, especially since Cogturrets often appear in pairs or packs. Digital Bee can also stun multiple turrets at once, allowing them to take extra damage via Mind Hack.
