# Blueberries

[![Blueberry](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9e/Blueberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041412)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9e/Blueberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041412)

*"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."*

A **Blueberry** is a type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that can be fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") to raise its bond by 25.

### Bee Interactions and Effects
When fed to specific bee types, the bond gain is doubled:
*   Bumble Bee
*   Cool Bee
*   Bubble Bee
*   Frosty Bee
*   Bucko Bee
*   Diamond Bee
*   Music Bee
*   Tadpole Bee
*   Buoyant Bee

**Gift Chance:** Feeding blueberries offers a small chance for bees to become gifted, with probabilities based on rarity:
*   Rare Bees: 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) per blueberry
*   Epic Bees: 0.01% (1 in 10,000) per blueberry
*   Common/Legendary Bees: 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) per blueberry
*   Mythic Bees: 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) per blueberry

**Mutation Risk:** Feeding a [radioactive](/wiki/Radioactive "Radioactive") bee a blueberry carries a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) chance to cause mutation.

## Ways to Obtain
Blueberries can be acquired through various methods:

*   **Dispenser:** Using the [Blueberry Dispenser](/wiki/Blueberry_Dispenser "Blueberry Dispenser") inside the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ"). Players receive one blueberry per blue bee in their hive per use.
*   **Challenges & Rewards:** As a reward from the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge") or by redeeming specific valid [codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes"):
    *   `1MLikes` (Gives 1 blueberry + other items)
    *   `15MMembers` (Gives 15 blueberries + other items)
*   **Memory Matches:** As a reward from the [Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match") minigame.

### Drops and Farming
Blueberries can also be found as drops or through farming:

*   **Farming:** Harvesting [leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves") in the following fields may yield blueberries:
    *   [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field")
    *   [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field")
    *   [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field")
    *   [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest")
    *(Note: Planting a sprout in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, or Stump Field yields 6x more blueberries than other fields.)*
*   **Beetle Drops:** As a drop from [Rhino Beetles](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"), [Mantises](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis"), and [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle").
*   **Gift Tokens:** As a drop from a [Festive Gift](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift "Ability Tokens") or [Festive Mark](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Mark "Ability Tokens").
*   **Cub Buddy:** As a gift from your [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy").
*   **Sprout Growth:** As a reward for completely growing any [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") except Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
*   **Sparkles:** Harvesting [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles"), with higher chances in blue fields.
*   **Puffshrooms:** As a drop from [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom").
*   **Planters:** As a drop from planters in blue fields. Non-blue fields containing significant blue flowers (Clover Field, Cactus Field, Mountain Top Field) can also yield them. Paper Planters in blue fields specifically drop multiple blueberry tokens in increments of 1, 3, 5, and 10.

## Quests
*   **Gifted Bucko Bee:** May reward the player with blueberries as a special reward.
*   **Spirit Bear Quest:** The "Out-Questing Questions" quest rewards 1 blueberry from Spirit Bear.

## Outdated Information
The following methods for obtaining Blueberries are no longer valid:

*   Redeeming expired codes: `10mMembers` (Gave 10 blueberries + other items), `5mMembers`, and `FourYearFiesta`.
*   As a drop from Hostage Chick.
*   Digging in to the [Beesmas Feast](/wiki/Beesmas_Feast "Beesmas Feast").
