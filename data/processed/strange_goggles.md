# Strange Goggles

![Strange Goggles](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Strange_Goggles.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20201123141044 "Strange Goggles")

**Status:** Unobtainable / Inaccessible (Though the item itself still exists in the game.)

### Description
"This futuristic looking headset doesn't seem to do very much..."

### Stats
*   **Capacity:** +77
*   **Defense:** +7%

### Acquisition and History
The Strange Goggles is a hat accessory introduced during the 2020-11-23 Update. It was originally available for purchase in the Noob Shop, but it was later removed from the shop in the 2024-07-17 Update due to being bugged.

This item was created for the Ready Player Two event in Bee Swarm Simulator and is distinct from other accessories because it required redeeming seven specific codes (Dysentery, Luther, Millie, CarmenSanDiego, Jumpstart, Troggles, and WordFactory) to obtain all of its components. Upon equipping this accessory, players were able to interact with the Digital Bee NPC in The Computer area.

Currently, while the Strange Goggles can still be worn, it cannot be re-equipped.

### Crafting Costs
To craft the Strange Goggles, players require:
*   77 Honey
*   1 Micro-Converter
*   7 7-Pronged Cogs

### Trivia
*   It is one of the cheapest items in the game regarding honey cost, alongside the Pouch and Scooper (which are received for free).
*   The Strange Goggles is unique as it does not appear in the standard item/gear inventory space.
*   It was one of the first accessories required to enter specific areas, following the Propeller Hat and Hiking Boots.
*   When wearing these goggles while interacting with the Digital Bee's quests, the objective field occasionally "glitches," displaying brightly colored textures on Field decorations. This effect is purely cosmetic and does not impact gameplay.
*   This accessory is the third hat to become unobtainable; the first two were the B.B.M. Mask and the Mondo B.B.M. Mask. Unlike those previous items, this hat was removed from circulation entirely, meaning it cannot be re-equipped.
