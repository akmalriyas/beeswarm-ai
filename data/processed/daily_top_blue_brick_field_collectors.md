# Daily Top Blue Brick Field Collectors

This leaderboard is now archived, as it has been removed from the game. It was one of 66 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator at the time.

The **Daily Top Blue Brick Field Collectors** measured how much pollen players had collected within the Blue Brick Field challenge during a single day. Unlike most other leaderboards, this specific leaderboard did not appear in the System Page or anywhere else in-game. It reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

If a player reached the top 100 ranking by the end of the day, they were awarded 25 Tickets.

## Trivia

*   This leaderboard, along with other Brick Field collector leaderboards and the Daily Top Ant Field Collectors, was one of the few leaderboards that did not display anywhere in the game interface.
*   It was tied for the fourth leaderboard to be removed from the game, alongside Most Commando Captures, Highest Snowbear Level, and Highest Robo Party Cake Rank.
