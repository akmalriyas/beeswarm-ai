# Blue Flower Field

The Blue Flower Field is one of the five starting fields available to players in Bee Swarm Simulator. It is the first blue field accessible and is protected by a Level 1 Rhino Beetle.

## Field Features and Mechanics

The Blue Flower Field is adjacent to the Blue HQ and King Beetle's Lair. On the north-east side of the field, a glass panel allows players to teleport into King Beetle's Lair. The field can spawn various valuable tokens when collecting pollen: Honey, Blueberry, Neonberry, Ticket, Blue Boost (Ability Tokens), Blue Extract, and Magic Bean.

### Item Drop Rates
When collecting pollen in this field, players have the following chances of receiving specific items:

*   Blueberries: 12.5% Chance
*   Blue Extracts: 0.0133% Chance
*   Neonberries: 0.0111% Chance
*   Magic Beans: 0.002% Chance

### Boost Mechanics
Players can enhance pollen collection using various methods:

*   **Field Boosters:** Using the Blue Field Booster grants +300% Blue Flower Field pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Adds 100% Blue Flower Field pollen to the current boost (up to maximum).
*   **Dice:** Field Dice, Smooth Dice, and Loaded Dice can add between +100% and +300% Blue Flower Field pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Field Winds:** Obtained from the Wind Shrine or codes, these provide a base of +20% instant conversion and grant +5% instant conversion plus +25% Blue Flower Field pollen per stack (up to 13 stacks).
*   **Amulets:** The King Beetle Amulet can boost the field by +1% to +100% if it possesses a Blue Flower Field Boost.

***Note on Past Methods:*** *Methods such as receiving boosts from specific NPCs (e.g., Gifted Bucko Bee, Onett) or redeeming certain expired codes are no longer possible.*

## Trivia
*   The field is located next to the Blue HQ and King Beetle's Lair.
*   In early game development, decorative flowers in this field faced upwards, but this was changed so that Rhino Beetles could properly attack them. The upward-facing orientation can still be seen in the tutorial.
*   According to Spirit Bear, this field used to be called the "Blueberry Field." Onett suggested it wasn't renamed because his blueberry 3D textures were too close to a "Blue ball."
