# Shops

Shops are vendor locations within Bee Swarm Simulator where players can purchase various [items](/wiki/Items). These items can be acquired using multiple currencies, including [Honey](/wiki/Honey), [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket), and specialized [crafting](/wiki/Crafting) materials (often found in mid-to-late game shops).

Shops typically stock utility items such as [bags](/wiki/Category:Bags), [guards](/wiki/Category:Guards), [tools](/wiki/Category:Tools), eggs, and other miscellaneous goods. These purchases often provide significant benefits, including increased [capacity](/wiki/System_Page#Capacity_Multiplier) or various [buffs](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs).

Some specialized shops focus on a single type of item, such as ticket vendors, whose prices may increase with each successive purchase.
