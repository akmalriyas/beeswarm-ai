# Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers

The **Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator. It can only be viewed via the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page.

This leaderboard tracks how many [Honey Gift Tokens](Ability_Tokens#Honey_Gift) a player has collected on a given day. The display shows 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll to view up to the 100th place. This ranking resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes
The leaderboard offers two tiers of rewards based on daily performance:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 by the end of the day are awarded a [Gifted Diamond Egg](Egg).
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 by the end of the day are awarded 50 [Tickets](Ticket).
