# Daily Top Mythic Bee Pollen Collectors

The **Daily Top Mythic Bee Pollen Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards available in the game. It can only be viewed through the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page.

This leaderboard tracks how much [Pollen] players have collected specifically using only [Mythic Bees] on a given day. The board displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view results up to the 100th place. The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

**Top 5 Finishers:**
Players who reach the top 5 by the end of the day are awarded the **Mythic M Sticker**.

**Top 100 Finishers:**
Players who reach the top 100 by the end of the day are awarded 25 [Tickets].
