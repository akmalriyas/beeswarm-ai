# Diamond Bee

**Diamond Bee** is a Legendary, Blue bee known for its extreme wealth and pompous attitude.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Legendary |
| **Color** | Blue |
| **Energy** | 20 |
| **Speed** | 14 |
| **Attack** | 1 |

Diamond Bee's favorite treats are Blueberries, and it enjoys visiting the Blue Flower Field and Pineapple Patch. It dislikes the Rose Field.

## Stats & Conversion

*   Collects 10 Pollen in 4 seconds (2.5 per second).
*   Makes 1000 Honey in 4 seconds (250 per second).
*   Base Convert Amount: +920.
*   **Gifted Hive Bonus:** x1.2 Convert Rate.

## Abilities & Passives

Diamond Bee possesses several unique abilities and passive traits:

*   **Blue Boost:** Grants a x1.2 pollen bonus from Blue flowers for 15 seconds. This ability can stack up to 10 times.
*   **Honey Gift+:** Occasionally spawns a Honey Token. The amount of honey produced is calculated as $250 \times (\text{Bee Level})^2$.
*   **Passive: Shimmering Honey:** When converting at the hive, this bee grants a 25% bonus to honey production (+2.5% per level). This bonus is doubled if the bee is Gifted.

## Trivia

*   Diamond Bee's light colors make it appear nearly translucent.
*   It possesses one of the highest base convert rates in the game, surpassed only by Tabby Bee when its Tabby Love stack reaches 626 or higher.
*   Along with Honey Bee, Diamond Bee is one of only two bees capable of generating Honey Gift or Honey Gift+ tokens.
*   Diamond Bee shares a unique conversion link color (cyan/light blue); it is the only bee whose conversion link is not yellow.
*   It is one of only two bees to share its name with an egg it can hatch from: Diamond Egg, and Basic Bee with Basic Egg.
*   The pineapple patch may be favored by Diamond Bee due to pineapples historically symbolizing wealth.
