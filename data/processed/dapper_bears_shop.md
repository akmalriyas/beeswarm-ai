# Dapper Bear's Shop

[ ![DapperBearsShop](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d3/DapperBearsShop.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20220401000549) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d3/DapperBearsShop.png/revision/latest?cb=20220401000549 "Dapper Bears' Shop") The exterior of Dapper Bear's shop.

## Overview
Dapper Bear's Shop is located past the Brave Bee Gate, near the Stump Field, on a hilltop. Players must access the shop by climbing the hill and jumping between ledges.

The shop sells reusable Planters and 6 types of Beequips. The third through sixth beequips require players to complete specific quests for Dapper Bear to unlock them. All purchases require payment in Honey and Tickets, with costs scaling based on the quality of the item. This location is also where Dapper Bear NPC resides.

**Access Requirements:**
To enter the shop, the player must wear either a Propeller Hat and Hiking Boots, or better equivalent gear. If a player attempts to enter while wearing inferior items (such as Helmet, Strange Goggles, B.B.M. Mask, or Mondo B.B.M. Mask), they will be blocked by a red player with the prompt: "You Must Wear a Nice Hat and Cool Boots to enter Dapper Bear's Shop."

**Shop Interior:**
The shop features a tan interior with blue flooring and ceiling, and two front windows. Above the entrance is an image of a Beequip Case. The left side houses the Beequip shop and the Highest Damage to a Single Puffshroom leaderboard. The right side contains the Planter shop.

The roof decorations include one Royal Jelly token, 25 Honeysuckles, and 3 Smooth Dice.

**Obstacle Course:**
An obstacle course is located on the right side of the shop, leading up to the roof. This course splits into two paths: a rocky wall and a moon obby.
*   The rocky wall provides access to the roof, where a Royal Jelly and Honeysuckle token are hidden.
*   The moon obby leads to a Star Jelly on a platform behind the shop.
Players can bypass this entire obstacle course using a Glider.

**Events:**
During Beesmas events since 2021, Dapper Bear's Samovar has been located next to the shop entrance. Additionally, Gift Boxes have frequently appeared on or near the roof during various Beesmas events.

## Beequip Shop

The Beequip Shop offers various Beequips across 6 slots. Only the first two slots are available initially; unlocking slots 3, 4, 5, and 6 requires completing 5, 10, 15, and 20 quests for Dapper Bear, respectively. Higher slots offer better potential items. The cost of a beequip scales based on its type and potential.

**Note:** Beequips displayed in the shop may show half-star potential values, but the actual potential upon purchase remains unchanged from what is shown in the Beequip Case. Each slot refreshes with a new item at specific intervals (starting 0 AM UTC):
*   Slot 1: Every 2 hours
*   Slots 2 & 3: Every 4 hours
*   Slots 4 & 5: Every 8 hours
*   Slot 6: Every 24 hours

## Trivia
*   Dapper Bear's Shop and Robo Bear's Shop are the only shops where the Bear NPC owner can converse with the player. If a player has purchased Gummy Boots, Mask, or Gummyballer, they can also talk to Gummy Bear.
*   This is one of only two locations that requires specific gear (Propeller Hat and Hiking Boots, or better) for entry.
*   Dapper Bear's Shop features its own unique music, unlike Robo Bear’s Shop, Ticket Tent, and Coconut Cave.
*   Using Hiking Boots can assist in completing the shop's obby. The jump power is sufficient to reach the Star Jelly at the end without bumping into the roof. A Glider is also recommended for traversing the course.
*   A Beequip Storage case used to be located to Dapper Bear's left but has since been moved to the spawn area.
