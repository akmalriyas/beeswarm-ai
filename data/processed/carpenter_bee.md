# Carpenter Bee

**Carpenter Bee** is a Colorless Legendary bee, known for its knack for construction and unique wood-based body structure.

## Abilities

### Honey Mark+
Marks a large random area on the field for 8 seconds (+0.25s per level). While standing in this area, you gain:
*   2 Conversion Links
*   x1.25 Convert Rate
This ability stacks up to 3 times.

### Pollen Mark
Marks a random area on the field for 7 seconds (+0.2s per level). While standing in this area, all pollen gathered is increased by up to 50%. This ability stacks up to 3 times.

## Trivia

*   Carpenter Bee, along with Music Bee, originated from Number1Riker's Bee Swarm showcase and are among the only Legendary bees added in a single update.
*   This bee features wood textures and includes a hammer mesh in its body model. It is one of three non-Event bees (along with Spicy Bee and Vector Bee) to have special body parts.
*   Carpenter Bee was part of the 2018-11-25 Update, alongside Stubborn Bee.
*   Its preference for Pine Tree Forest may be due to pine trees being made of wood, while its dislike for Mountain Top Field might stem from the lack of trees in high altitudes.
*   Carpenter Bee, Honey Bee, and Bumble Bee are among the few bees named after their real-life counterparts.
*   It is unique as the only bee capable of generating the **Honey Mark+** ability token.
*   This bee was featured in the Bee Bear's Catalog, specifically given as part of the Pinecone Bundle.
*   Carpenter Bee is one of the few Legendary bees that cannot find a sticker while gathering.
