# Demo Bee

**Demo Bee** is an Epic, Colorless bee known as an elite Bomber Bee and expert in explosives.

> "An elite Bomber Bee who has worked its way up the ranks. It is an expert in explosives."

**Rarity:** Epic
**Color:** Colorless
**Energy:** 20
**Speed:** 16.8
**Attack:** 3

## Traits and Preferences

Demo Bee's favorite treat is [Sunflower Seeds].
*   **Likes:** Dandelion Field, Cactus Field
*   **Dislikes:** Rose Field

## Trivia

*   Demo Bee and Basic Bee share the same skin colors.
*   As noted in its description, Demo Bees are elite Bomber Bees. They share very similar abilities and descriptions with Bomber Bee, though they like the same Fields but dislike different ones.
*   In-game stats for Demo Bee incorrectly state the convert amount is 170 more than normal (80). It is actually 250 more. This discrepancy is speculated to be a developer oversight by Onett after buffing its convert amount from 250 to 330.
