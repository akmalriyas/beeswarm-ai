# Present / Beesmas 2022 Event

This content details the mechanics and rewards associated with the Presents given during the Beesmas 2022 event. While this specific event has concluded, the information remains archival.

Presents returned for Beesmas 2022. Their primary function was to add [Ornaments](/wiki/Ornaments) to the [Beesmas Tree](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree) when given to NPCs. During this event, Presents could also be given to the new NPC, [Robo Bear](/wiki/Robo_Bear).

## Ways to Obtain
*   **Bee Bear Quests:** Multiple quests from Bee Bear awarded a total of 12 [Presents](/wiki/Present), along with other rewards.
*   **Bee Bear’s Catalog:** The catalog sold 1 [Present](/wiki/Present) for 250 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake) and 10 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear).
*   **Onett Hive Claim:** Onett gave 1 [Present](/wiki/Present) (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event period.
*   **Galentine Shrine Donation:** Donating [Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry) to the [Wind Shrine#Galentine_Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine#Galentine_Shrine) had a chance to give 1 [Present](/wiki/Present), along with other rewards.
*   **Robo Party Rank:** Completing [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party)'s rank 25 for the first time gave 1 [Present](/wiki/Present), along with other rewards.
