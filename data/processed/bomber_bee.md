# Bomber Bee

**Bomber Bee** is a Colorless and Rare bee.

> "This crafty bee makes bombs which collect pollen from all nearby flowers."

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Rare |
| **Color** | Colorless |
| **Energy** | 20 |
| **Speed** | 15.4 |
| **Attack** | 2 |

Bomber Bee's favorite treat is Sunflower Seeds. It likes the Dandelion Field and the Cactus Field, but dislikes the Pumpkin Patch.

## Stats & Mechanics

### Production Rates
*   **Pollen Collection:** Collects 10 Pollen in 4 seconds (Base). This equates to 2.5 Pollen per second. With the x2 Bee Pollen gamepass, this increases to 20 Pollen in 4 seconds.
*   **Honey Production:** Makes 120 Honey in 4 seconds (Base). This equates to 30 Honey per second. With the x2 Honeymaking Speed gamepass, this increases to 120 Honey in 2 seconds.

### Stat Bonuses
Bomber Bee provides the following bonuses:
*   +10% Movespeed
*   +40 Convert Amount
*   +1 Attack
*   **Gifted Hive Bonus:** x1.1 Bomb Pollen yield.

### Abilities: Buzz Bomb
The Bomber Bee possesses the **Buzz Bomb** ability. This ability collects 7 pollen from 13 surrounding flowers, with an additional +10% pollen per Bond level. The power of this bomb increases when combined with other bombs and can stack up to 10 times.
