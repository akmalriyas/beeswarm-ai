# Bee Swarm Simulator Egg Types

Eggs are inventory items in *Bee Swarm Simulator*. Players hatch them by dragging them into their hive, resulting in a new bee. Eggs can also be donated to the Wind Shrine or used at the Sticker Printer. They serve as an entry point for players starting the game.

There are many types of eggs, some offering chances at different bee rarities, while others guarantee a specific outcome (specific or event bees). Currently, there are 6 core egg types (Basic, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Mythic, Star), plus various gifted variants and specialized non-event/event bee eggs.

**Hatching Mechanics:**
Since the 2019-09-28 update, players no longer require a vacant hive slot to hatch an egg; they can replace an existing bee with the newly hatched one. The level of the replaced bee remains unchanged, acting like Royal Jelly.

***Note on Regional Restrictions:*** *Due to regulations in the United Kingdom, Netherlands, Belgium, and Australia, most eggs available in the Robux Shop are restricted from purchase by consumers in these regions.*

## General Egg Types

### Basic Egg
The Basic Egg is the most common type of egg found in the game. It has a high chance (87%) to hatch into a Basic Bee. In rare cases, it may hatch into:
*   Rare Bee (10% chance)
*   Epic Bee (2.5% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (0.5% chance)
*   Gifted Bee (0.4% chance)

There is a 0.348% chance of hatching a Gifted Basic Bee specifically from this egg. Mythic and Event bees cannot be hatched from the Basic Egg, and it cannot be used on a Basic Bee.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Free upon starting the game for first-time players.
*   Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop near the Sunflower Field (Price scales up with each purchase).
*   Receiving 1 egg by adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack.

### Silver Egg
The Silver Egg most likely hatches into a Rare bee (64.9% chance), but it also has chances to hatch into:
*   Epic Bee (30% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (5% chance)
*   Mythic Bee (0.1% chance)

A Basic or Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 50 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 100 Robux.
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Silver Egg questlines.
*   Receiving a reward from Brown Bear's 25th quest.
*   Completing the 25th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Mantis (1 or 5 increments), King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Rare Sprouts, Leaves (when collecting pollen in Clover Field).
*   Other sources: Festive Planter, Stockings, Winter Memory Match reward.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Expired code "PreUpdate" rewards.
*   Rewards from Beesmas 2020/2021 events or Bee Bear's Catalog purchases during those periods.

### Gifted Silver Egg
This egg shares the same probabilities as a standard Silver Egg, but it guarantees that the hatched bee will be its gifted variant.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time (guarantees any type of gifted egg, though subsequent defeats have lower chances).
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors poll.
*   Completing Black Bear's "Getting Stumped" quest.
*   Receiving 1 egg upon completing the 100th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Werewolf (exceptionally rare), Stump Snail (rare), Scorpion (exceptionally rare), Sticker Sprout.

### Gold Egg
The Gold Egg most likely hatches into an Epic bee (79% chance). It also has chances to hatch into a Legendary bee (20% chance) or a Mythic bee (1% chance). Basic, Rare, and Event bees cannot be hatched from this egg.

*Note: 5 Gold Eggs are required to craft the Honey Mask.*

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 100 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 200 Robux each.
*   Rewards: Robo Bear Challenge, Extreme Memory Match.
*   In token form in Werewolf's Cave (guarded by two cave monsters).
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Gold Egg questlines.
*   Receiving rewards from Brown Bear's 50th, 340th, and 390th quests.
*   Completing the 50th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick 10 times.
*   Drops: King Beetle (low chance), Scorpion (extremely low chance), Epic sprouts (rare), Leaves (when collecting pollen in Clover Field, extremely rare).
*   Other sources: Ticket Planter, Candy Planter, Red Clay Planter, Festive Planter, Pesticide Planter, Petal Planter. Stockings (rare drop), Winter Memory Match reward.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Opening the Golden Present or specific Black Friday/seasonal Robux bundles.

### Gifted Gold Egg
This egg shares probabilities with a standard Gold Egg but guarantees the gifted variant. It cannot be used to craft the Honey Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time (guarantees any type of gifted egg).
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors poll.
*   Playtime Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 50 Gifted Bucko Bee or 50 Gifted Riley Bee quests.
*   Completing Brown Bear's 275th quest.
*   Receiving 1 egg upon completing the 150th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Stump Snail (very rare), Coconut Crab (very rare), Mantis (exceptionally rare).

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Opening the Mondo Present during Beesmas 2019.

### Diamond Egg
The Diamond Egg most likely hatches into a Legendary bee (95% chance), with a rare chance to hatch into a Mythic bee (5% chance). Basic, Rare, Epic, and Event bees cannot be hatched from this egg.

*Note: 5 Diamond Eggs are required to craft the Diamond Mask.*

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Token location: Past Bear Gate, under the platform where Onett stands (requires Glider or Parachute near Shadow Bear).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 400 Robux each.
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 400 Tickets each.
*   Rewards: Robo Bear Challenge, Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Black Bear's Diamond Egg questline.
*   Completing Panda Bear's "Colossal Combat" quest (fifth star treat/ant quest).
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 10th quest ("The Long Haul").
*   Receiving rewards from Brown Bear's 75th and 525th quests.
*   Completing the 75th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Stump Snail, Diamond Aphids, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Coconut Crab (rare chance). Spiders, Mantises, Werewolves (very rare chance). Legendary sprouts (rare), Supreme sprouts (guaranteed drop). Leaves (when collecting pollen in Clover Field, extremely rare).
*   Other sources: Mythic Meteor Shower reward, Festive Planter, Winter Memory Match reward.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Purchasing certain seasonal Robux offers.

### Gifted Diamond Egg
This egg shares probabilities with a standard Diamond Egg but guarantees the gifted variant. It cannot be used to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time (guarantees any type of gifted egg).
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers poll.
*   Quest Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 100, 250, 500, 750, or 1,000 Gifted Bucko Bee/Gifted Riley Bee quests.
*   Completing Brown Bear's 425th quest.
*   Receiving 1 egg upon completing the 200th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Tunnel Bear (extremely rare), Stump Snail, Coconut Crab (exceptionally rare).

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Opening the Mondo Present during Beesmas 2019.

### Mythic Egg
The Mythic Egg always hatches into a Mythic bee. The player cannot obtain any other type of bee from this egg besides a Mythic bee, though a Gifted Mythic bee is possible (very rare).

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Memory Matchers poll.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 1,200 Robux each (limited to 3 eggs per player).
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 2,500 Tickets each.
*   Rewards: Robo Bear Challenge (very rare), Extreme Memory Match (extremely rare).
*   Completing Black Bear's 75th quest ("Myth in The Making").
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 15th quest ("More Than Myth").
*   Receiving rewards from Brown Bear's 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
*   Receiving 1 egg upon completing the 300th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick for the 50th time (guarantees a Mythic Egg).
*   Drops: Stump Snail, Mondo Chick, Coconut Crab (extremely rare chance). Meteor Shower (extremely rare chance). Festive Planter (very rare drop).
*   Other sources: Bee Bear's Catalog purchase. Gummy Bear’s Goo Year’s Beacon quest reward during Beesmas 2025.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Purchasing seasonal Robux bundles or opening specific Gift Boxes/Presents.

### Gifted Mythic Egg
This egg shares probabilities with a standard Mythic Egg but guarantees the gifted variant.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Rare Bee Pollen Collectors poll.
*   Completing 500 Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quests.
*   Nearly impossible drop from the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Bee Bear's Catalog purchase (10,000 Snowflakes and 150 Gingerbread Bears).

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Purchasing specific seasonal bundles or opening certain Gift Boxes/Presents.

### Choose-A-Mythic Egg
This unique egg allows the player to select any Mythic Bee they desire upon hatching.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing the Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul from the Robux Shop for 4,500 Robux.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Expired seasonal Robux purchases.

### Star Egg
The Star Egg hatches into any type of gifted bee that is not already in the player's hive (excluding gifted Event bees). If all available non-event gifted bees are owned, it hatches into a random gifted bee. It will only hatch a Gifted Mythic Bee once all other eligible gifted bees are collected.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time (guarantees any type of gifted egg).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 eggs per player).
*   Drops: Mythic Meteor Shower (exceptionally rare), King Beetle (exceptionally rare), Festive Planter (exceptionally rare drop), Party Mechsquito (exceptionally rare drop).

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Purchasing specific seasonal Robux offers.

## Specific Event Bee Eggs

Event bee eggs always hatch into their respective named event bee and grant an exclusive permanent royal jelly of that type, provided the player does not already own that specific event bee. All share the appearance of a basic egg.

### Bear Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Bear Bee (100%).
**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux. *(Previously cost 1,000 Robux or was discounted).*
*   Obtaining a Bear Bee Voucher from players who bought a Basic Egg.

### Cobalt Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Cobalt Bee (100%).
**Ways to Obtain:** Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.

### Crimson Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Crimson Bee (100%).
**Ways to Obtain:** Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.

### Digital Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Digital Bee (100%).
**Ways to Obtain:** From Robo Bear's Shop after acquiring a Diamond Cog Amulet, 7,777,777 Honey, and 5 of all Drives. *(Previously available in the Digital Bee Pack for 2,500 Robux).*

### Festive Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Festive Bee (100%).
**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.
*   Completing the 15th quest of Bee Bear ("Festive Fight Finale") during Beesmas 2020 (if player doesn't own Festive Bee).
*   Opening the Festive Present during Beesmas 2019.

### Gummy Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Gummy Bee (100%).
**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Claiming at the Gummy Bee Egg Claim for 2,500 Gumdrops.
*   *(Previously available from completing all of Gummy Bear's quests during the Gummy Invasion event or purchasing in the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets).*

### Photon Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Photon Bee (100%).
**Ways to Obtain:** Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.

### Puppy Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Puppy Bee (100%).
**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets. *(Previously cost 250 Tickets or was available in Beesmas 2020 for 800 Robux).*

### Tabby Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Tabby Bee (100%).
**Ways to Obtain:** Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.

### Vicious Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Vicious Bee (100%).
**Ways to Obtain:** Claiming at the Vicious Bee Egg Claim for 250 Stingers.

### Windy Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Windy Bee (100%).
**Ways to Obtain:** Donating a Spirit Petal to the Wind Shrine, followed by donating Cloud Vials. *(Previously available in the Windy Bee Pack for 800 Robux).*
