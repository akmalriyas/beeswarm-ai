# Mob Guide

There are 34 different types of enemies or creatures (colloquially called "mobs") in Bee Swarm Simulator.

The most abundant mob by spawn points is the Rhino Beetle, which has five spawn points. Ladybugs are the second most abundant with four spawn points. The Stump Snail has the longest respawn time, taking 96 hours to reappear.

## Combat Mechanics and Abilities

**Attacking Entities:**
Eight entities can attack other mobs: Bees, Flames, Frogs, Rogue Vicious Bee, Wild Windy Bee, Ability Tokens, Passive Abilities, Coconuts, and coal summoned from the Naughty List during Beesmas.

**The Jump Glitch:**
A reliable method for defeating most field-defending mobs (excluding King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Cave Monster, Stick Bug, Rogue Vicious Bee, Wild Windy Bee, Commando Chick, Coconut Crab, and Mondo Chick) is continuous jumping. This glitch causes mobs to freeze and stop attacking, leading the player's bees to perceive them as non-threatening. To use this effectively, players must ensure their bees have already initiated an attack on the mob before starting the jump sequence.

**Loot System Changes:**
Prior to the 2019-02-01 update, rewards from defeating any mob were sent directly to the player's inventory. After this update, mobs drop a ring of tokens around them (note: Battle Points do not spawn as tokens). Other players cannot collect tokens dropped by a mob unless that mob was spawned by them. Tokens can be collected via a token link, unless the rewards are from public mobs, in which case any player may collect the drops regardless of who attacked it.

**Leveling and Combat:**
Mobs have levels displayed next to their names. If a player's bees are at a lower level than the mob they are attacking, there is an increased chance that attacks will miss. This means that the lower the bee's level compared to the mob's level, the more likely the higher-level mob will deflect or dodge the attack.

**Passive Mobs:**
Some mobs are passive; they neither attack the player nor can be attacked by the player. These include Bean Bugs, Frogs, and Fireflies (with exceptions for Chicks, Spotted Chicks, and Hostage Chicks).

**Bosses and Mini-Bosses:**
Mobs are categorized as either standard mobs, mini bosses, or bosses based on their special properties or unique rewards.

*   **Mini Bosses:** These exhibit special qualities but do not qualify as a full boss. Examples include Wild Windy Bee, Rogue Vicious Bee, Snowbear, Commando Chick, and Stump Snail.
*   **Bosses:** These mobs possess distinct properties or drop unique rewards that differentiate them from standard field mobs.

**Special Mob Mechanics:**
*   **Daylight Saving Time (DST):** DST affects the respawn time of the Stump Snail, King Beetle, and Tunnel Bear.
*   **Loot Luck:** This ability directly influences the quantity and quality of mob drops upon defeat but does not affect honey dropped by mobs (which is governed by Honey from Tokens).
*   **Egg Block Ability:** When Mondo Chick and Commando Chick use their egg block ability, it blocks almost all bees' attacks, even if the bee is at an equal or higher level.
*   **Stump Snail Behavior:** Stump Snail has the highest health among all mobs and does not intentionally attack the player. It moves in a circle around the edge of the Stump Field. However, damage will be dealt if the player stands directly in front of the snail or inside its head; standing within the shell is safe.

## Regular Mobs

Regular mobs remain in specific fields and will attack the player upon entering that field. Once defeated, they respawn after a set period.

**Monster Respawn Time Reduction:**
The Monster Respawn Time can be reduced by owning a Gifted Vicious Bee. For example, Stump Snail normally takes 96 hours (4 days) to respawn; with a Gifted Vicious Bee, this time is reduced by 15% to 81 hours and 36 minutes (3.4 days).

| Mob | Level Range | Fields Defending | Respawn Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ladybug | 1-3 | Mushroom Field, Clover Field, Strawberry Field | 5 minutes |
| Rhino Beetle | 1-3, 5 | Blue Flower Field, Clover Field, Bamboo Field, Pineapple Patch | 5 minutes |
| Spider | 4 | Spider Field | 30 minutes |
| Mantis | 4-6 | Pineapple Patch, Pine Tree Forest | 20 minutes |
| Scorpion | 5-6 | Rose Field | 20 minutes |
| Werewolf | 7 | Cactus Field, Pumpkin Patch, Pine Tree Forest | 1 hour |
