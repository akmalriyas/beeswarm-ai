# Chick Types Overview (Removed Content)

Chicks are mobs introduced as part of the Egg Hunt 2020 event. They possess a special ability to hide inside their eggshells, which increases their defense and the chance for a bee's attacks to be blocked.

There were five different types of chicks: Normal Chicks, Hostage Chicks, Spotted Chicks, Commando Chicks, and Mondo Chicks.
*   **Damage:** Only Commando Chicks and Mondo Chicks could deal damage to players; all other chick types (Normal, Hostage, Spotted) were passive.
*   **Removal Status:** Normal, Hostage, and Spotted Chicks were removed from the game at the end of Egg Hunt 2020.

---

## Normal Chick

The Normal Chick was a passive mob that could spawn in the Sunflower Field and would appear randomly when players collected pollen. These chicks could only spawn if the player had the Black Bear's Sunflower Corral Quest active, and they spawned with a level range of 1-15.

## Hostage Chick

Hostage Chicks were passive mobs that had a chance of spawning after defeating a hostile mob or a Boss. (Defeating Ants or other chicks would not spawn a hostage chick.) They spawned with a level range of 1-15 and always despawned 30 seconds after spawning. Mobs that are more difficult to defeat, such as Tunnel Bear, were more likely to cause a hostage chick to spawn. Hostage chicks from tougher mobs had higher health, levels, and better rewards.

## Spotted Chick

Spotted Chicks were passive mobs that could appear in the Noob Shop, Pro Shop, Ticket Tent, and Blue Maze. They spawned with a level range of 3-9. Capturing a spotted chick triggered a spawn event in another location:
*   Captured in Noob Shop $\rightarrow$ Spawns in Ticket Tent.
*   Captured in Pro Shop $\rightarrow$ Spawns in Blue Maze.

## Commando Chick (Mini-Boss)

The Commando Chick is a mini-boss that spawns in its designated hideout. Its hideout is located to the left of Brown Bear and the Wealth Clock. To clear the vines blocking access, players must equip Clippers, Scissors, Scythe, or Dark Scythe. Once cut, the vines never regrow.

**Mechanics:**
*   **Respawn Time:** 30:00 (25:30 with Gifted Bee Vicious Bee).
*   **Attacks:** When hiding in its egg, it throws grenades (1 if >2/3 health, 2 if <2/3 health, 3 if <1/3 health, 4 if <1/100 health). The egg cracks after a few seconds, causing the chick to stop moving briefly.
*   **Damage:** Deals 20 damage when colliding with the player and 50 damage if the player is near an exploding grenade.
*   **Progression:** Starts at 150 Health and Level 3. Its health and level increase with each successive capture, reaching Level 6 by the fifth capture.

## Mondo Chick (Boss)

The Mondo Chick is a level 8 boss located in the Mountain Top Field. It respawns every hour at the full hour mark and its rewards are shareable among players.

**Mechanics:**
*   **Spawn State:** Always spawns at Level 8 with 300,000 health.
*   **Time Limit:** The chick automatically takes 333 damage per second. If not defeated within 15 minutes of spawning, it despawns (similar to Commando Chick).
*   **Egg State:** It sometimes hides in its egg, blocking attacks. This state can be prematurely cracked by dealing 7500 damage to the egg.
*   **Defeat Location:** If it leaves the Mountain Top field before defeat, rewards spawn in that new field. If defeated outside of any field, drops form in a ring.

**Trivia:**
*   According to Sun Bear, the chicks are invasive to the Mountain, and their eggs are exotic.
*   Mother Bear noted that Spotted Chicks dislike the sun.
*   Black Bear stated that bees do not hurt chicks when attacking; instead, they work together to calm them down and capture them as they are "frightened."
*   Although other players' Commando Chicks cannot damage the player directly, their grenades can. A player's bees can also damage other players' Commando Chicks.
*   The Commando Chick is considered a mini-boss despite every player having their own instance of it.
*   The Mondo Chick's noise can be heard throughout the entire map (similar to Cave Monsters).
*   Mondo Chick is the only mob with a fixed spawn point in the Mountain Top Field.
*   Spotted Chick is the only mob that spawned in shops.
*   During the soft launch of Egg Hunt 2020, Mondo Chick had 250,000 health before being changed to 300,000.
*   Rogue Vicious Bee's spikes can deal damage to mobs, allowing it to assist in killing the Mondo Chick.
*   If a player already has the Mondo Chick Blessing buff and defeats another one, the new blessing overrides the old buff, resetting its timer and potentially upgrading or degrading the buff based on defeat time.
*   Mondo Chick, Stick Nymphs, and Festive Nymphs are the only mobs that take damage without being directly attacked by something else.
*   If a bee lands a critical hit on a chick hiding in its egg, it ignores the damage reduction and deals normal critical damage.
*   As of Beesmas 2020 update, Mondo Chick no longer drops Marshmallow Bee.
*   There was a glitch where Commando Chick would remain stuck in its egg; exiting and re-entering the hideout usually fixed this.
*   Commando Chick is the only chick with red eyes; all other chicks have black eyes.
