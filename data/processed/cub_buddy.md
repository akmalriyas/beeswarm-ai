# Cub Buddy

[![The default skin.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3c/Cub_Buddy.png/revision/latest?cb=20200710143249)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3c/Cub_Buddy.png/revision/latest?cb=20200710143249)

The default skin for the Cub Buddy.

A **Cub Buddy** is a small, baby version of a [bear](/wiki/Bears "Bears") that follows the player around. It collects tokens and creates gifts. If the cub buddy gets too far away from the player, it will automatically teleport back to them.

Cub Buddies have customizable settings accessible through the [Cub Console](/wiki/Cub_Console "Cub Console"), which is located on the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page").

When a player's cub buddy produces items, it dances, emits heart particles, and an in-game message pops up:
> 🐻(Cub Buddy Name) made some gifts!

## Cub Console

[![The Cub Console.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fd/Cub_Console_New_(Sticker_Update).png/revision/latest?cb=20240122214745)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fd/Cub_Console_New_(Sticker_Update).png/revision/latest?cb=20240122214745)

The Cub Console allows the player to customize and check their Cub Buddy by clicking on the green Cub Console button under the options menu. Players can equip or unequip their cub buddy, modify its name, and view its statistics.

If a player owns a cub buddy, they may rename it by clicking on the "Name" window, typing in the desired name, and pressing enter. Note that this name is subject to filtering and may be rejected by the system.

### Cub Buddy Aging
Cub Buddies age based on how long they have been equipped while the player is in-game:
*   **Newborn:** Less than one hour equipped.
*   **1 Hour+:** Age measured in hours.
*   **24 Hours+ (1 Day+):** Age measured in days and hours.
*   **3 Days+:** Age measured solely in weeks and days.
*   **4 Weeks+:** Age measured in months and weeks.

## Ways to Obtain

Cub Buddies can be obtained through several methods:

*   **Redeeming a [Cub Buddy Voucher](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker"):**
    *   Purchasing in the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop") for 600 Robux.
    *   As an unfathomably rare reward from [Robo Bear's Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge").
    *   Trading with other players.
*   **Beesmas 2025:**
    *   Completing [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") 15th quest, "Festive Bee's Trial." (This grants either a Cub Buddy or a Cub Buddy Voucher if the player does not already own one.)

### Cub Skins

Skins can be acquired through various achievements and purchases:

*   **Brown Cub Skin:** Obtainable by completing 300 [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear") quests.
*   **Robo Cub Skin:** Obtainable by beating Round 25 in [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"), collecting 250,000 [Cogs](/wiki/Cog "Cog"), or placing Top 10 on the [Daily Top Robo Bear Challenge Scores](/wiki/Daily_Top_Robo_Bear_Challenge_Scores "Daily Top Robo Bear Challenge Scores").
*   **Stick Cub Skin:** Obtainable by placing Top 50 on the Daily Stick Bug score leaderboard or as a drop from [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") (Unbelievably Rare). The odds for challenge rewards vary widely, from around 1 in 25,000 (at 10 million score) to around 1 in 670 (at over 1,000,000,000 score).
*   **Star Cub Skin:** Obtainable by sticking all 12 Star Signs to the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack"), rolling it from the [Sticker Printer](/wiki/Sticker_Printer "Sticker Printer"), or purchasing a [Diamond Star Amulet](/wiki/Star_Amulet#Diamond_Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") (1 in 1,000,000 chance).

### Outdated Acquisition Methods
The following methods are no longer current:

*   **Ticket Tent:** Purchasing for 2,000 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") granted a cub buddy and the Black Cub skin.
*   **Beesmas 2019:**
    *   Completing Bee Bear's 10th quest, "A Beesmas Miracle" (gave a cub buddy and the Black Cub skin).
    *   Completing Bee Bear's 15th quest, "Bee Cub Workshop 5" (gave the Bee Cub skin).
    *   Purchasing the Cub Buddy Launch Pack for 800R$ (gave a cub buddy, the Black Cub skin, and the Noob Cub skin).
*   **Beesmas 2020:** Purchasing in [Bee Bear’s Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%E2%80%99s_Catalog "Bee Bear’s Catalog") for 250 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") (gave a cub buddy, the Black Cub Skin, Gingerbread Cub skin, and a star treat).
*   **Beesmas 2021:**
    *   Completing Bee Bear's 15th quest, "Cub Buddy Beckoning" (gave the Black Cub skin or a [Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat") if owned).
    *   Completing Bee Bear's 20th quest, "Snow Cub Reformation 5/5" (gave the Snow Cub skin).
*   **Beesmas 2022:**
    *   Completing Bee Bear's 15th quest, "Cogs For The Cub Buddy" (gave a cub buddy and the Black Cub skin, or a [star treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") if owned).
    *   Completing Bee Bear's 20th quest, "Peppermint Preparation (5/5)" (gave the Peppermint Robo Cub).
*   **Beesmas 2024 (Summer):**
    *   Completing Bee Bear's 15th quest, "Here comes Cub Buddy" (gave a cub buddy voucher, redeemable for a Black Cub skin and the actual Cub Buddy).
    *   Completing Bee Bear's 20th quest, "Doodle Cub Creation (5/5)" (gave the Doodle Cub skin).
*   **Beesmas 2024 (Winter):**
    *   Completing Bee Bear's "Crafting a Cub Buddy" quest. (Gave either a Cub Buddy or a Cub Buddy Voucher.)
    *   Completing Bee Bear's "Cheering Up Gloomy Cub (5/5): Anything???" quest. (Gave the Gloomy Cub skin.)

## Possible Gift Rewards

Cub buddies produce gifts in a ring of 5 tokens, typically containing common items like [Treats](/wiki/Treat "Treat"), but they can also yield rarer items such as tickets or [Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly"). When a cub buddy begins to dance and heart particles appear above its head, it is about to produce gifts. Cub buddies generate gifts every 1–5 minutes.

Similar to sprouts and leaves, the rewards granted by cub buddies depend on the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields") the player is standing in (e.g., receiving Sunflower Seeds while in a Sunflower Field).

## Trivia

*   Bee Bear claimed that cub buddies are dolls animated by "Beesmas magic," but [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear") stated that Bee Bear had to build and program them himself. [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") added, "They're just robots, you know. But they're so lifelike, it's uncanny!"
*   If a player purchased the Cub Buddy Launch Pack and subsequently completed Bee Bear's 10th quest, they were rewarded with 250 tickets (since players can only own one of each cub buddy skin). Conversely, if the process is reversed, the player receives 500 tickets total (the normal reward plus an additional 250) and the Noob Cub skin.
*   When a player acquires their first cub buddy, it is automatically named "Buddy."
*   A cub buddy uses specific emotes: the wave emote when idle, the point emote when collecting a token, the dance emote before producing gifts, and the cheer emote when making the gifts.
*   Most cub buddies use the [Cartoony Animation Package]. However, the Brown Cub, Snow Cub, Robo Cub, Peppermint Robo Cub, and Gloomy Cub use different packages: Knight, Ninja, Robot, and Elder Animation Packages, respectively. Coincidentally, these are also used by [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear"), [Noob Bear](/wiki/Noob_Bear "Noob Bear"), and [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear").
*   There was a glitch where cub buddies would "wear" an item if they teleported onto it (such as the Demon Mask, Elite Blue Guard, or Riley Guard). This occurred because the cub buddy is a Humanoid Model and was patched in an unknown update.
*   A bug exists that allows cub buddies to remain in the game even after their owners leave. While they can still collect public tokens, these rewards are not given to the owner.
*   Cub Buddies used to cost 2,000 tickets in the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent").
*   The maximum name length for a cub buddy is 16 characters, and names are filtered by Roblox.
*   Although a cub buddy can collect any token (player or public), it cannot claim its own gifts unless it picks up a Token Link.
*   Most cub skins are based on their original NPC/mob counterparts. Exceptions include the Gingerbread Cub skin (a more elaborate version of the [Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear")) and the Peppermint Robo Cub (a reskin of the normal Robo Cub). The Doodle Cub fits the sticker update theme.
*   Bee Bear has stated that a Snow Cub’s favorite treat is [Basic Eggs](/wiki/Egg "Egg").
*   Cub buddies are affected by the player's [Haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens") stacks.
*   The Robo Cub skin was one of three skins (along with Stick Cub and Star Cub) to have two ways of acquisition, and it is the first skin to have three ways of obtainment.
*   The Peppermint Robo Cub next to Bee Bear is unique as the only NPC that performs an emote originating from the Roblox catalog—the Robot emote.
*   Rarely, a special gift will be made, consisting of 1 [Tropical Drink](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink"), 1 [Enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"), 1 [Red Extract](/wiki/Red_Extract "Red Extract"), 1 [Blue Extract](/wiki/Blue_Extract "Blue Extract"), and 1 [Oil](/wiki/Oil "Oil").
