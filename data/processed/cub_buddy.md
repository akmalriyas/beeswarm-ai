# Cub Buddy

A **Cub Buddy** is a small, baby version of a [bear](Bears) that follows the player around, collects tokens, and creates gifts. If the cub buddy gets too far away from the player, it will automatically teleport back.

Cub buddies have settings accessible through the [Cub Console](Cub_Console), which is located on the [System Page](System_Page). When a cub buddy produces items, it dances, emits heart particles, and displays an in-game message:
> 🐻(Cub Buddy Name) made some gifts!

## Cub Console

The Cub Console allows players to customize and check their Cub Buddy. Players can equip or unequip the buddy, modify its name, and view its statistics.

If a player owns a cub buddy, they may rename it by clicking on the "Name" window and pressing enter. Note that names are subject to system filtering and may be rejected.

Cub Buddies age based on how long they have been equipped while the player is in-game:
*   **Newborn:** Equipped for less than one hour.
*   **1 Hour+:** Age measured in hours.
*   **24 Hours+ (1 Day+):** Age measured in days and hours.
*   **3 Days+:** Age measured solely in weeks and days.
*   **4 Weeks+:** Age measured in months and weeks.

## Ways to Obtain

Cub Buddies can be acquired through several methods:

*   **Vouchers/Tickets:** Redeeming a [Cub Buddy Voucher](Sticker) by purchasing it in the [Robux Shop](Robux_Shop) for 600 Robux, receiving it as an unfathomably rare reward from [Robo Bear's Challenge](Robo_Bear_Challenge), or trading with other players.
*   **Beesmas Events:**
    *   **Beesmas 2025:** Completing [Bee Bear's](Bee_Bear) 15th quest, "Festive Bee's Trial" (rewards either a Cub Buddy or a Voucher).

### Cub Skins

Cub Buddies can wear various skins obtained through different achievements:

*   **Brown Cub Skin:** Obtainable by completing 300 [Brown Bear](Brown_Bear) quests.
*   **Robo Cub Skin:** Obtainable by beating Round 25 in [Robo Bear Challenge](Robo_Bear_Challenge), collecting 250,000 [Cogs](Cog), or placing Top 10 on the Daily Top Robo Bear Challenge Scores.
*   **Stick Cub Skin:** Obtainable by placing Top 50 on the Daily Stick Bug score leaderboard or as a drop from [Stick Bug](Stick_Bug) (Unbelievably Rare). The odds for this drop vary significantly based on the score achieved, ranging from approximately 1 in 25,000 to 1 in 670.
*   **Star Cub Skin:** Obtainable by sticking all 12 Star Signs to the [Sticker Stack](Sticker_Stack), rolling it from the [Sticker Printer](Sticker_Printer), or purchasing a [Diamond Star Amulet](Star_Amulet) (1 in 1,000,000 chance).

## Possible Gift Rewards

Cub buddies produce gifts in a ring of five tokens. These rings typically contain common items like [Treats](Treat), but can also yield rarer goods such as tickets or [Royal Jellies](Royal_Jelly). A cub buddy begins to dance and heart particles appear above its head before producing gifts, which occurs every 1–5 minutes.

The rewards granted by cub buddies are dependent on the [Field](Fields) the player is standing in (e.g., receiving sunflower seeds while in a Sunflower Field).

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   **Cub Buddy Animation:** A cub buddy uses the wave emote when idle, the point emote when collecting a token, the dance emote before producing gifts, and the cheer emote when making them.
*   **Animation Packages:** Most cubs use the [Cartoony Animation Package]. However, specific skins (Brown Cub, Snow Cub, Robo Cub, Peppermint Robo Cub, Gloomy Cub) use different packages: Knight, Ninja, Robot, and Elder, respectively.
*   **Initial Naming:** When a player acquires their first cub buddy, it is automatically named "Buddy."
*   **Token Collection:** While a cub buddy can collect any token (player or public), it cannot collect its own gifts unless the owner picks up a Token Link.
*   **Glitch History:** Historically, there was a glitch where cub buddies would "wear" an item if they teleported over it, as they are Humanoid Models. This bug has been patched. Another glitch allowed cubs to remain in the game after their owners left, though they could only collect public tokens without rewarding the owner.
*   **Skin Design:** Most skins are based on their original NPC/mob counterpart. Exceptions include the Gingerbread Cub (an elaborate version of the [Gingerbread Bear](Gingerbread_Bear)) and the Peppermint Robo Cub (a reskin of the normal Robo cub).
*   **Ability Interaction:** Cub buddies are affected by the player's [Haste](Ability_Tokens#Haste) stacks.
*   **Rare Gift Combination:** Rarely, a special gift is produced consisting of 1 [Tropical Drink], 1 [Enzymes], 1 [Red Extract], 1 [Blue Extract], and 1 [Oil].
