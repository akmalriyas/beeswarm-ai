# Cobalt Guard

![Cobaltguard](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3c/Cobalt_Guard.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20181128090512)

## Overview
The Cobalt Guard is a high-tier blue guard that can be crafted in the Badge Bearer's Guild. It is described as: "A guard bestowed with the heroic power of Cobalt Bee - Defender of the Blue Bees." When equipped on the right shoulder, it leaves a trail similar to Cobalt Bee's trail.

## Crafting
The Cobalt Guard requires the following materials:
*   200,000,000 Honey
*   100 Blue Extracts
*   100 Stingers
*   50 Enzymes
*   25 Glitter

## Stats and Abilities
Equipping the Cobalt Guard provides the following bonuses:
*   +250,000 Capacity
*   +50% Blue Pollen
*   +20% Blue Bomb Pollen
*   +3 Blue Bee Attack
*   +10% Defense
*   +100% Critical Power
*   **Passive Ability: Haste Pulser**
    *   Effect: Every 30th Haste token releases a blue pulse that hops to each blue bee, collecting pollen around them. The power of the pulse increases with each hop.
    *   Note: Unlike ability tokens, this effect cannot trigger a red pulse, even if the player owns a Crimson Bee or the Crimson Guard.

## Trivia and Lore
*   The Cobalt Guard is one of only two guards (the other being the Crimson Guard) that possess a passive ability.
*   It is based on an Event bee, similar to the Crimson Guard.
*   Its red counterpart is the Crimson Guard.
*   This guard is unique among blue guards as it cannot be purchased in the Blue HQ.
*   Both the Cobalt Guard and the Crimson Guard are the only guards in the game that leave a trail behind when the player moves. The blue trail's minimum length value is higher than the Crimson Guard's, making the blue trail less smooth (which may prevent it from showing when using a Petal Wand).
