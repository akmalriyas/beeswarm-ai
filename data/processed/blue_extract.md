# Blue Extract

[![Blue Extract](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/df/Blue_Extract.png/revision/latest?cb=20230129205708)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/df/Blue_Extract.png/revision/latest?cb=20230129205708)

> "Grants x1.25 Blue Pollen for 10 minutes."

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 1 second

The **Blue Extract** is an inventory item introduced in the 2018-11-25 update. It serves two primary purposes: as a material for crafting equipment and accessories, or to activate a temporary buff that grants x1.25 Blue Pollen for 10 minutes.

### Mechanics
*   The associated buff cannot stack. The timer will only reset if the player uses a **Blue Extract** while the effect is already active.
*   Using a [Purple Potion](/wiki/Purple_Potion) or a [Super Smoothie](/wiki/Super_Smoothie) will override the Blue Extract buff entirely.

## Ways to Obtain

### Crafting & Trading
*   **Blender:** Crafted using 50 [Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry) and 10 [Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly).
*   **Memory Match:** Matching a pair of blue extracts in [Mega Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Mega_Memory_Match) yields 1 Blue Extract. Matching a pair in [Extreme Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Extreme_Memory_Match) yields 3 Blue Extracts.
*   **Bee Bear's Catalog:** Can be purchased via bundles:
    *   The Super Snowman Bundle grants 25 blue extracts + other items.
    *   The Snow Queen Bundle grants 25 blue extracts + other items.

### Drops & Rewards
*   **Mobs:** Rare drop from [Rhino Beetles](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle) and [Mantises](/wiki/Mantis). Common drop from [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle) and [Diamond Aphids](/wiki/Aphid#Diamond_Aphid).
*   **Fields:** Can rarely drop when farming in the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field), [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field), [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest), or the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field).
*   **Leaves & Sprouts:** A rare drop from leaves in blue fields, and a rare drop from [Sprouts](/wiki/Sprout) (odds increase based on sprout rarity and field location).
*   **Gifts & Tokens:** Drops from certain [Festive Gifts](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Gift) or [Festive Marks](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Festive_Mark), and as a rare gift from a [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy).
*   **Planters:** Guaranteed drop when harvesting at 100% growth from the [Blue Clay Planter](/wiki/Blue_Clay_Planter) and [Hydroponic Planter](/wiki/Hydroponic_Planter).
    *   The Hydroponic Planter drops extra blue extracts in blue fields.
    *   Placing a Hydroponic Planter sequentially across the four blue fields (Blue Flower Field $\rightarrow$ Bamboo Field $\rightarrow$ Pine Tree Forest $\rightarrow$ Stump Field) results in an additional 100 Blue Extracts (36-day cooldown).
*   **Shrines:** Drops from a [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine) badge shiver.

## Quests
*   **Gifted Bucko Bee:** All quests always reward at least one blue extract upon completion.
*   **Black Bear:** Rewards Blue Extracts on the following quests:
    *   Walk Through The Woods (1 blue extract + other items).
    *   Bamboo Boogie 2: Bamboo Boogaloo (1 blue extract + other items).
    *   You Blue It (1 blue extract + other items).
    *   Repeatable quests occasionally grant blue extracts.
*   **Science Bear:** Rewards Blue Extracts on the quest Meticulously Crafted (1 blue extract + other items).
