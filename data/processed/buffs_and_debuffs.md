# Buffs & Debuffs

Buffs and Debuffs are core gameplay mechanics in Bee Swarm Simulator. Players can acquire these status effects from various sources, including amulets, quest givers, items, ability tokens, codes, and different dispensers. Some buffs are exclusive to a single source (e.g., the Splinter Trap from the Stick Bug Challenge), while others can be obtained through multiple methods (e.g., blue boosts from Blueberry Dispensers, bees, or flowers).

All status effects are displayed in the top margin of the screen, above the main menu. Effects are sorted from left to right, with newer effects appearing on the right. Hovering over an icon provides details regarding stack amount, effect, and duration (if applicable). On certain devices, accessory icons may appear here instead of next to the hotbar.

## Trivia

*   Puffshroom Blessing, Festive Nymph Blessing, and Robo Party Blessing are currently buggy; they stop working after rejoining a server even if displayed above the player. A refresh is required to maintain the buff.
*   The icon for the Frozen debuff uses an asset from Battle for Dream Island.
*   The 3YearParty code (now expired) used to reward unlimited gumdrops, but it had a glitch where the buff disappeared upon leaving and rejoining the game. Onett later removed this buff from the code.
*   The shortest buff is Coconut Haste Surge (2 seconds), while the longest are Honeyday Event, Launch Code Boost, and Galentine's Blessing (48 hours each).
*   Speaking to Onett during Beesmas grants the player the Honeyday Event buff, even if the hive hasn't been claimed yet.
*   The Splinter Trap icon is identical to the Haste+ icon, which is ironic given their opposing effects.
*   Receiving more stacks of Robo Bear Challenge is less detrimental (e.g., Round 2 debuff is x0.12 capacity, better than Round 1's x0.1).
*   Haste+ was originally planned as one of Ninja Bee's abilities but was later scrapped.
