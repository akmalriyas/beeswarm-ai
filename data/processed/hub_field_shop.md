# Hub Field Shop

The **Hub Field Shop** is a shop located within the Hive Hub, situated next to the Hub Field. It functions as a quest giver in the game.

## Inventory and Mechanics

This shop sells one item: the Sticker Seeker.

| Item | Cost | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Sticker-Seeker](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker) | 7,000,000 Honey, 1 Glue, 1 Oil, 5 Soft Waxes, 5 Neonberries, 10 Micro-Converters | Collects 10 pollen from 13 patches in 1 second. Has a chance to find stickers in the field and unlocks the Sticker Seeker quests! |

## Quests

Quests originating from this shop require the use of the Sticker Seeker. These quests are multi-part, involving several objectives:
*   Collecting pollen using the Sticker Seeker.
*   Donating collected stickers to the [Public Sticker Board](/wiki/Public_Sticker_Board).
*   Collecting Rainbow Sticker Tokens.
*   Gathering stickers from sprouts or puffshrooms.
*   Finding hidden Seeker-Stickers at random bee zones, shops, or HQs.
