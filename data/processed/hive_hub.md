# Hive Hub

The **Hive Hub** is a unique location introduced in the January 12, 2024 update. It should not be confused with the [Hub Field](/wiki/Hub_Field).

Located inside the Hive Hub Portal (which is situated beyond the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate)), the Hive Hub serves as a central trading and crafting area for players.

## Features
The location offers several key features:
*   **Hives:** 36 player-claimable hives are available.
*   **Sticker Board:** A [Public Sticker Board](/wiki/Public_Sticker_Board) is present for displaying stickers.
*   **Sticker Seeker:** Players can craft the [Sticker-Seeker](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker), a specialized tool used for collecting stickers, which unlocks the [Sticker-Seeker's Quests](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine).
*   **Hub Field:** This dedicated field instantly converts all collected pollen into honey. Additionally, flowers regrow very frequently here, eliminating the need for Sprinklers.
*   **Beequip Storage:** A chest is available for storing Beequip items.
*   **Sticker Sprouts:** [Sprout](/wiki/Sprout) instances spawn in the Hub every three hours.

The Hive Hub's primary purpose is to facilitate player trading and provide access to sticker collection mechanics. Notably, Bees do not spawn within the Hive Hub area to help reduce server lag.

### Summer Beesmas 2024 Event
During the Summer Beesmas 2024 event, an obstacle course (obby) was added. Upon completing the obby, players land on a hexagonal platform containing two tokens: a [Smooth Dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice) and a [Red Balloon](/wiki/Red_Balloon).

## Tips
*   **Sharing:** Sharing [Jelly Beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans) is possible in the Hive Hub, making it convenient for quests or utilizing sharing buffs.
*   **Performance Warning:** The Hive Hub can be resource-intensive. Users are advised to have a capable device and set their graphics to level 1 to prevent crashes and major frame drops.

## Restricted Items
The following items cannot be used or exist within the Hive Hub:
*   [Sprinklers](/wiki/Sprinklers)
*   [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake)
*   [Stingers](/wiki/Stinger) (Note: This prevents [Star Saw's Passive Ability](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Saw) from activating here.)
*   [Cloud Vials](/wiki/Cloud_Vial)
*   Various Dice types: [Field Dice](/wiki/Field_Dice), [Smooth Dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice), and [Loaded Dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice).
*   [Whirligigs](/wiki/Whirligig)
*   [Night Bells](/wiki/Night_Bell)
*   All types of [Balloons](/wiki/Balloon).
*   All types of [Planters](/wiki/Planter).

## Trivia and Mechanics
*   **No Bee Spawning:** The Hive Hub, along with the [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge) lobby, are the only locations where player Bees do not spawn.
*   **Beesmas Tokens:** During Beesmas events, the hexagonal platform features specific tokens:
    *   Summer Beesmas 2024 featured the Celestial [Gift Box](/wiki/Gift_Boxes#2024_\(Summer\)).
    *   Winter Beesmas 2024 featured the Speckled [Gift Box](/wiki/Gift_Boxes#2024_\(Winter\)).
    *   Both Gift Boxes and the [Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear) token have since been removed after their respective events.
*   **Planter Bug:** A previous issue where placing a Planter prompted "Cannot use (type) Planter in Challenge" was fixed in an unknown update.
*   **Visibility:** The main structures of the Hive Hub—including the [Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine), [Public Sticker Board](/wiki/Public_Sticker_Board), and Gift Boxes—can be viewed from behind the Bee Swarm Mountain, beyond the TP barrier at the edge of the base plate on the main map.
*   **Entry Bypass:** It is possible to enter the Hive Hub without meeting the 20-bee requirement. This can be done by climbing the wall to the left of the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate), following the wall, jumping down behind the [Stinger Shop](/wiki/Stinger_Shop), and then navigating over the fence. Alternatively, players can join anyone in the Hive Hub directly from their profile without meeting requirements.
*   **Sprout Notification:** Shortly before a natural Sticker Sprout spawns, the message "🌱 A Sticker Sprout is about to spawn in (Public Server) Hive Hubs… 🌱" will be displayed.
*   All primary machines and features of the Hive Hub are located behind the entrance portal.
