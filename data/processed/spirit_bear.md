# Spirit Bear

Spirit Bear is a permanent quest giver and one of eight unique bears in Bee Swarm Simulator. She holds a Petal Wand and wears a Petal Belt.

## Overview
| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Species** | Quest Bear |
| **Location** | Between the Coconut Field and the Pepper Patch, in front of the Extreme Memory Match. |
| **Bee Prerequisites** | 35 Bees |
| **Color Scheme (Fur)** | #6f6f6d / #5d5e5b |
| **Color Scheme (Details)** | Snout: #393a35, Torso: #939586, Arms: #656663, Legs: #555653 |

Spirit Bear offers a total of 30 non-repeatable quests. Her later quests require massive amounts of resources, including billions of Pollen, Grandmaster Badges, and large quantities of Ability Tokens.

### Quest Rewards & Lore
*   **Primary Reward:** Spirit Petals (granted every ten quests).
*   **Spirit Petal Usage:** Each petal can be used to craft the Petal Wand or Petal Belt, or donated to the Wind Shrine for a chance at obtaining a Windy Bee Egg.
*   **Lore Highlights:** She is deeply invested in themes of nature's magic, beauty, and the cyclical nature of life (like seasons/memories). She frequently references her deceased sister and has an eccentric, rambling personality.

## Dialogue & Lore Insights
Spirit Bear's dialogue provides extensive lore regarding the mountain and game mechanics:

*   **Magic and Nature:** She believes deeply in the "magic of nature," contrasting it with purely scientific views. She discusses how plants need to be understood through feeling, not just observation.
*   **Game Mechanics:**
    *   **Glitter:** Boosts pollen collection in a field by 100% and can extend Field Boosts (like those from Field Dice).
    *   **Spirit Petals:** Used for crafting the Petal Wand or Petal Belt, or donating to the Wind Shrine.
    *   **Sprouts:** Different fields yield different rewards when planting Sprouts (e.g., Sunflower/Pineapple Patch yields 7x more seeds/pineapples; Red Fields yield 4x more strawberries).
    *   **Gifted Bees:** They receive a 50% bonus to conversion, gathering, and attack, along with defined stat bonuses that do not stack.
    *   **Grandmaster Badges:** She emphasizes that the combined effect of many badges (billions/trillions of honey) is more important than any single badge's small boost.
*   **Personal Lore:** She frequently references her deceased sister and has a complex relationship with other bears, such as Brown Bear (whom she knew since he was a cub).

## Trivia & Game Facts

*   **Appearance/Identity:** Spirit Bear is based on the Kermode Bear subspecies. She is one of only two female permanent quest givers (the other being Mother Bear).
*   **Access Methods:** While typically requiring 35 bees, it is possible to access her via glitching by gliding onto the Windy Bee statue at the 35 Bee Gate or climbing the large rock near the Coconut Field.
*   **Unique Mechanics:** She is the only permanent quest giver who requires players to play Memory Match and use the Wealth Clock.
*   **Dialogue Length:** Her dialogue is exceptionally long, with over 21,000 words across all her quests.
*   **Code:** The code "BeesBuzz123" was mentioned in her dialogue as a password for her personal website.

## Exclusive Beesmas Quests (Archival/Seasonal)

These seasonal quests are tied to the Galentine Shrine and Snow Storm mechanics:

**Spirit Bear's Galentine Shrine (Winter)**
*   **Requirements:** Collect 4B White Pollen, Catch 1,100 White Bloom Petals, Donate 1 Purple Potion and 1 Star Jelly. Match 80 Pairs in Memory Match Games. Find 20 Hidden Stickers on Surfaces around the map.
*   **Rewards:** Summons a Snow Storm, access to Winter Memory Match, turns Wind Shrine into Galentine Shrine.

**Spirit Bear's Galentine Shrine (Summer)**
*   **Requirements:** Collect 220M White Pollen, 80M Rose Field Pollen, 4.4M Goo from White Flowers. Use the Nectar Condenser. Match 40 pairs in Memory Match Games. Donate 10 Hard Waxes and 1 Star Jelly to the Wind Shrine.
*   **Rewards:** Summons a Snow Storm, access to Winter Memory Match, turns Wind Shrine into Galentine Shrine.
