# All-Time Fastest Crab Slayers Leaderboard

This leaderboard tracks the fastest times players have defeated the Coconut Crab in Bee Swarm Simulator. It is one of the game's 64 available leaderboards and displays the least time taken to defeat the boss. The board shows the top 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to 100 entries.

## Mechanics and Location

If a player has never defeated the Coconut Crab, the game interface will prompt them with the message: "You haven't defeated the Coconut Crab."

The challenge is located inside the Coconut Cave, which can only be accessed after defeating the Coconut Crab. This cave is situated past the Windy Bee Gate.

## Trivia

*   This is the only leaderboard dedicated solely to ranking by time (the time before defeat). It joins the Highest Snowbear Level and Top Stick Bug Fighters leaderboards as one of only three boss-ranking leaderboards.
*   It is the sole leaderboard designed for tracking the shortest amount of time required to accomplish a specific goal.
*   The leaderboard has been updated and repolished multiple times.
*   Historically, there was an oversight where Coconut Crab's health was accidentally set to only 20 HP by Onett. During this period, many players achieved unusually fast times. The leaderboard was subsequently reset in June 2024.
