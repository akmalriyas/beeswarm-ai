# Jelly Beans

> **Disclaimer:** This content contains information obtained through datamining. Due to the nature of this data, details may be inaccurate or outdated. The buffs for all jelly bean types are accessible in-game, but specific details regarding Spoiled Jelly Beans cannot always be verified due to their rarity. (Data mined: December 19th, 2024)

[![Jelly Beans](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/ed/Jelly_Beans.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031446)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/ed/Jelly_Beans.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031446)

_"Scatters various buff-granting beans on nearby flowers. Works best when shared."_

**Cooldown:** 30 seconds
**Cap:** 100

Jelly Beans are an inventory item introduced in the Egg Hunt 2019 update. When used, they spread jelly beans of varying colors in the direction the player is facing. Each color represents a different buff. The beans turn into collectable tokens when they land on flowers, and each individual bean spawns between 11 and 13 tokens.

Each buff lasts for one minute.

### Mechanics
*   **Sharing Bonus:** If multiple players are nearby, picking up the tokens grants both the owner (who used the jelly beans) and the collector the token's buffs, along with the Jelly Bean Sharing Bonus to the owner. Therefore, sharing is highly recommended.
*   **Usage Restrictions:** The player can only hold a maximum of 100 jelly beans at a time. To obtain more than the cap, the item must be acquired via quests, purchased from Robux packs, or found in certain ornaments (which are now unobtainable).
*   **Activation Note:** Jelly Beans can be activated anywhere; however, it is recommended to use them in a field, as no tokens will spawn if they do not land on valid terrain.

### Jelly Bean Sharing Bonus
This bonus is granted when a player uses jelly beans and another player collects the resulting tokens. It provides:
*   +100% Convert Rate
*   +10% White Pollen
*   +10% Red Pollen
*   +10% Blue Pollen

The duration of this boost depends on how many tokens another player picks up, eventually capping out at 30 minutes.

## Acquisition Methods

### Standard Drops & Rewards
Jelly Beans can be obtained through various in-game activities:

**Challenges:**
*   Retro Swarm Challenge (Reward)
*   Ant Challenge (Reward)
*   Robo Bear Challenge (Common Reward)
*   Memory Match (Reward)

**Sticker Stack Rewards:**
Adding the following stickers to a Sticker Stack grants 5 Jelly Beans each:
*   Standing Bean Bug Sticker
*   Baseball Swing Sticker
*   Colorful Buttons Sticker

**Planter Drops:**
Jelly Beans can drop as a bonus from certain planters:
*   Candy Planter
*   Petal Planter
*   The Planter Of Plenty

**Donations & Gifts:**
*   Donating items to the Wind Shrine.
*   Rarely through a Cub Buddy gift (in increments of 1 or 5).

### Tokens and Locations
Jelly bean tokens can be found in specific locations:
*   **Blue Maze:** Taking the path to the right next to the Ticket Tent leads to a token.
*   **Coconut Cave:** Passing through the hole on top of the Coconut Dispenser yields tokens.

## Historical & Trivia Notes

*   **Token Sound:** When a jelly bean token is collected, one of five specific audios plays (Jb1.ogg through Jb5.ogg).
*   **Bean Bugs:** Bean Bugs are passive mobs that spawn in random fields and scatter jelly bean tokens around their current field. They are the second way to obtain tokens.
*   **Naming Convention:** The Merigold Jelly Bean is often misspelled throughout the game; the correct spelling is 'Marigold'.
*   **Past Changes:** Prior to the 2019-09-28 update, Jelly Beans did not have a maximum capacity and could be obtained in unlimited amounts.
*   **Wind Shrine Donation:** Donating any number of Jelly Beans for the first time to the Wind Shrine returns 7 Jelly Beans.
