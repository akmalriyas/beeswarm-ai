# Porcelain Dipper

[![Porcelain Dipper](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e1/Porcelain_Dipper.png/revision/latest?cb=20240625063750)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e1/Porcelain_Dipper.png/revision/latest?cb=20240625063750)

The **Porcelain Dipper** is a tool added in the 2018-06-02 update, available for purchase at the Mountain Top Shop. It emits bubbles and sparkles while collecting pollen.

## Overview & Stats
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | 150,000,000 Honey |
| **Shop** | Mountain Top Shop |
| **Range** | 49 Flowers |
| **Collecting Pattern** | Surround |
| **Collecting Time** | 0.7 seconds |
| **Base Collection** | 3 pollen per swing |

### Pollen Collection Rates (Per Swing)
The tool collects different amounts of pollen based on flower type:

| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 4.5 | 9 | 13.5 | 18 | 22.5 |
| Red | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |
| Blue | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |

*Special Multiplier: +50% White Pollen Multiplier.*

The average base pollen collection rate is 245 pollen per second (315 from white flowers and 210 from red/blue flowers, assuming all flowers are single-size). It has a maximum base pollen collection rate of 315 pollen per second, achievable only in the Spider Field.

## Mechanics
Every tenth swing activates an ability: a large copy of the tool rises up, summoning a pillar of light. This pillar collects significant pollen from 29 flowers (located in a 5x5 area around the player and four other flowers positioned ahead, to the side, and behind the player). The ability also plays a specific audio file.

## Trivia
*   **Price History:** The price was increased from 100 million Honey to 150 million Honey after the 2019-04-05 update due to the addition of its special ability.
*   **Predecessor:** It is an upgraded version of the Honey Dipper, which can be bought in the Pro Shop.
*   **Tool History:** The Porcelain Dipper was the second tool to feature a unique ability, following the Golden Rake.
*   **Visual Utility:** While some decals are not visible through the glass (like bee faces or flame effects), the dipper can be used to view certain objects in specific areas, such as the Coconut Canister, Coconut Clogs, and Coconut Belt within the Coconut Cave, if positioned correctly.
*   **Performance Comparison:** The Porcelain Dipper's average collection rate is 41% better than both the Scythe and the Bubble Wand. However, the Scythe excels at red flowers (+48%) and the Bubble Wand excels at blue flowers (+48%). Since most fields are mixed, neither of those specialized tools achieves its theoretical maximum rate.
*   **Honeystorm Effect:** The Porcelain Dipper glows white during a honeystorm.
*   **Ability Limitations:** The ability can activate outside of fields. However, the light pillar will not collect pollen if the player is positioned above the field (e.g., jumping or gliding over it), even if the pillar visually touches the flowers. A specific interaction in the King Beetle's Lair allows players to collect pollen from the Clover Field by timing a jump before the beam effect.
