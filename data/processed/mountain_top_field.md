# Mountain Top Field

The **Mountain Top Field** is a field located past the [Lion Bee Gate](/wiki/Lion_Bee_Gate). It is protected by the [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick) and respawns approximately every hour.

## Field Stats

| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Field Color** | Red/Blue |
| **Required Bees** | 25 |
| **Nectar Type** | [Invigorating Nectar](/wiki/Invigorating_Nectar) |
| **Dimensions** | 24 × 28 flowers |
| **Total Flowers** | 672 |

### Flower Distribution
The field consists entirely of triple flowers, with no white flowers.

| Type | White | Red | Blue | Total |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Single | 0 | 0% | 0 | 0% | 0 | 0% | **0** | **0%** |
| Double | 0 | 0% | 0 | 0% | 0 | 0% | **0** | **0%** |
| Triple | 0 | 50% (336) | 50% (336) | **672** | **100%** |

***Note:** Flower counts may vary slightly due to in-game rounding, and flowers are randomly generated.*

## Mechanics and Events

When a [planter](/wiki/Planter) is planted and harvested here, it grants Invigorating Nectar. While collecting pollen, this field can spawn various tokens: [Red Boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost), [Blue Boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost), [Honey](/wiki/Honey), [Magic Bean](/wiki/Magic_Bean), [Ticket](/wiki/Ticket), [Bitterberry](/wiki/Bitterberry), and [Neonberry](/wiki/Neonberry).

### Possible Events
*   **Rogue Vicious Bee:** A level 6-8 [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee) may spawn naturally, at night, or if a player uses a [Night Bell](/wiki/Night_Bell).
*   **Wild Windy Bee:** A level 4-6 [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee) may spawn here. If summoned manually from the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine), it spawns at level 5-6. Three level 4 Wild Windy Bees will also spawn when claiming Spirit Bear’s “Tickle the Wind” quest.
*   **Mondo Chick:** The Mondo Chick spawns here at the beginning of every hour.
*   **Stick Bug Challenge:** During a [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge), if Stick Bug is present in the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch), [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch), and [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field), he may jump to this field.
*   **Aphid:** An [Aphid](/wiki/Aphid) may spawn here upon gathering [Leaves](/wiki/Leaves).

## Obtaining Boosts

Boosts can be applied using various methods:

*   **Items:** Using [Glitter](/wiki/Glitter) adds 100% Mountain Top Field pollen to the current boost for 15 minutes (if not maxed out). Similarly, [Field Dice](/wiki/Field_Dice), [Smooth Dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice), or [Loaded Dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice) may add +100% Mountain Top Field pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Field Winds:** [Mountain Top Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind) can be obtained via the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine) or codes. They provide a base of +20% instant conversion and grant +5% instant conversion and +10% pollen per stack, stacking up to 13 times total.
*   **Codes:** Redeeming certain valid codes grants boosts (e.g., `ThreeBeeVee` provides Mountain Top Field Boost x3, Capacity x3, and Code).

### Outdated Methods (Historical Data)
The field has historically received boosts through various means:

*   **Present Gifts:** Giving a [Present](/wiki/Present) to Onett provided boosts in 2020 (x1 boost) and later in 2021 and 2024 (x3 boost).
*   **Market Boosts:** Purchasing a Mountain Top Field [Market Boost](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Areas) from the [Boost Market](/wiki/Boost_Market) provided x1.5 Capacity, x1.25 Pollen, +25% Tool Pollen, and x1.25 Convert Rate At Hive.
*   **Expired Codes:** Many codes granted boosts (e.g., `Keen4`, `3YearParty`, `Reboots` gave Mountain Top Field Boost x3 or x4; `TallTallMountain` gave x2).
