## Field Description & Mechanics

The Mountain Top Field is a field located past the Lion Bee Gate. The Mondo Chick protects this field and respawns roughly every hour.

When a planter is planted and harvested here, it grants Invigorating Nectar. While collecting pollen, this field can spawn various tokens, including Red Boost, Blue Boost, Honey, Magic Bean, Ticket, Bitterberry, and Neonberry Tokens.

## Possible Events

*   A level 6-8 Rogue Vicious Bee may spawn naturally, at night, or if a player uses the Night Bell.
*   A level 4-6 Wild Windy Bee may spawn here; if summoned manually from the Wind Shrine, it is level 5-6. Special conditions allow for its spawning here, including when claiming Spirit Bear’s “Tickle the Wind” quest (which spawns three level 4 Wild Windy Bees).
*   Mondo Chick spawns at the beginning of every hour.
*   During a Stick Bug Challenge, if the Stick Bug is in the Pineapple Patch, Pumpkin Patch, and Cactus Field, he may jump to this field.
*   An Aphid may spawn here upon gathering Leaves.

## Obtaining Boosts

Boosts for Mountain Top Field can be acquired through several methods:

*   **Items:** Using Glitter adds 100% Mountain Top Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes (if not maxed). Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice may also add +100% Mountain Top Field pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Field Winds:** Mountain Top Field Winds can be obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes. They provide a base of +20% instant conversion and grant +5% instant conversion and +10% Mountain Top Field pollen per stack, stacking up to 13 times total.
*   **Codes:** Redeeming certain valid codes grants boosts (e.g., `ThreeBeeVee` provides Mountain Top Field Boost x3, Capacity x3, and a Code).

### Outdated/Historical Methods
Historically, the field could be boosted via:
*   Giving Presents to Onett (2020 gave x1 boost; 2021/2024 gave x3 boost).
*   Purchasing Market Boosts from the Boost Market or certain codes.

## Trivia

*   This is the only field that does not produce Treats from farming, although harvesting sprouts still drops normal rewards.
*   Leaves in this field are less frequent and always spawn Bitterberries, Aphids, or a Sticker.
*   It was uniquely immune to the Gummy Cannon during the Gummy Invasion event.
*   The Mountain Top Field received a badge in the 2024-01-12 Update, making it one of the few fields with dedicated badges.
*   It is the first field consisting entirely of triple flowers (the Stump Field is the second). It also lacks any white flowers.
*   Before the 2018-11-25 update, the Red Cannon required 22 discovered bee types; this requirement was raised to 25. After the 2019-09-28 update, bypassing the Lion Bee Gate with only 25 discovered bee types (but not 25 actual bees) results in teleportation back to spawn.
*   The field requires a massive 1 quadrillion pollen to unlock its Grandmaster badge.
*   It is the only field that cannot be boosted without external items or codes.
