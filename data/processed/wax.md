# Wax

Waxes are inventory items introduced in the 2021-12-26 Update. They function as modifiers, allowing players to change the stats and abilities of Beequips.

There are currently five different types of Waxes. Players can apply a maximum of five Waxes to any single Beequip. If more modifications are desired, a Turpentine must first be used on the Beequip to remove existing waxes, reverting its stats to their original state.

Beyond modifying gear, Waxes are also utilized in crafting processes for items such as Planters, Tools, and even other types of Waxes within the Blender.

## Wax Types

The following five wax types are currently available:

*   [Soft Wax](/wiki/Soft_Wax)
*   [Hard Wax](/wiki/Hard_Wax)
*   [Caustic Wax](/wiki/Caustic_Wax)
*   [Debug Wax](/wiki/Debug_Wax)

## Use in Quests

Waxes are required for various quests across several Bear characters:

### Dapper Bear
*   **A Good Infection (#3):** Requires collecting 10 Soft Waxes.
*   **All About Appearances (#4):** Requires using 5 Soft Waxes and 1 Hard Wax.
*   **The Long Haul (#10):** Requires using 10 Hard Waxes.
*   **Ongoing Maintanence (#13):** Requires using 50 Soft Waxes.

### Science Bear
*   **Repairing The White Drive:** Requires obtaining 12 Soft Waxes.
*   **Corrupting The Glitched Drive:** Requires obtaining 12 Hard Waxes.

### Bee Bear
*   **Finishing The Festive Planter (#10):** Requires collecting 10 Soft Waxes.
*   **Upgrading The Honeydays (#13):** Requires using 5 Soft Waxes.
*   **Cogs For The Cub Buddy (#15):** Requires using 5 Hard Waxes.
*   **Peppermint Preparation (2/5) (#17):** Requires collecting 30 Soft Waxes.
*   **Peppermint Preparation (3/5) (#18):** Requires using 1 Swirled Wax.
*   **Peppermint Preparation (4/5) (#19):** Requires collecting 30 Hard Waxes and 5 Caustic Waxes.

### Bubble Bee Man
*   **B.B.M's Naughty List (Beesmas 2021):** Requires collecting 555 Soft Wax tokens and using 9 Caustic Waxes.
*   **B.B.M's Naughty List (Beesmas 2022):** Requires destroying 31 Beequips modified with Caustic Waxes.
