# Present/2018 Event

This content was introduced during the "Beesmas 2018" event, which took place on December 25th, 2018. Presents were required to complete certain quests given by Bee Bear.

Unlike subsequent years, players could not receive Ornaments from giving NPCs presents at this time, as ornaments had not yet been added to the game.

## Obtaining Presents

Presents could be acquired through several methods:

*   **Bee Bear Quests:** Multiple quests provided by Bee Bear awarded a total of 6 Presents, along with other rewards.
*   **Onett Hive Claim:** Onett rewarded 1 Present upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event period (along with other items).
*   **Code Redemption:** Using the code "ByeBeeBear" granted 1 Present.
*   **Map Scatters:** Presents were scattered throughout the map in various locations:
    *   Behind presents that Bee Bear had placed during the event.
    *   On top of the honeycomb in the Ace Shop within the Badge Bearer's Guild.
    *   Inside the Bear Gate, located on the other side of Bubble Bee Man (near the Moon Parkour obstacle course).
