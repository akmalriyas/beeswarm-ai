# Battle Points

Battle Points are a currency used to rank on the Global Top Battlers Leaderboard and acquire the Battle Badge. These points are earned by defeating specific mobs in the game.

Players can track their current Battle Point total either by stepping on the designated pad near the Global Top Battlers Leaderboard or by viewing their Badges and checking the Battle Badge entry.
