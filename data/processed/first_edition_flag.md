# First Edition Flag

[![The decal for the First Edition Flag.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f0/1stflag.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404052534)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f0/1stflag.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404052534)

The First Edition Flag is a cosmetic decal that designates certain bees as "first edition." This designation is represented by a white and yellow "1st" flag appearing on top of the bee, which emits a sparkling effect. The flag has no impact on gameplay or statistics whatsoever.

This status is currently available for: Bear Bee, Tabby Bee, Gummy Bee, Puppy Bee, Festive Bee, Digital Bee, and Windy Bee.

**Note:** If a player removes a first edition bee, the bee will retain its first edition status when added back to the hive.

## First Edition Bees

*   **Bear Bee:** Bought during the first month after the game's launch. All First Edition Bear Bees automatically received an additional bonus of being gifted.
*   **Tabby Bee and Puppy Bee:** Purchased during their respective release sales at half price.
*   **Gummy Bee:** Obtained after completing all quests associated with Gummy Bear during the Gummy Invasion Event.
*   **Festive Bee:** Can be obtained by completing all quests for Bee Bear during the Beesmas 2018 Event, or by purchasing the Festive Bee Pack for 1,600 Robux in the Robux Shop.
*   **Windy Bee:** Obtained as a special prize from the Wind Shrine, or purchased in the Robux Shop for 800 Robux before October 27, 2019.
*   **Digital Bee:** Can be obtained from Robo Bear's Shop behind Robo Bear, or by purchasing the Digital Bee Pack for 2,500 Robux during Beesmas 2022.

## Trivia

*   Onett (Developer) stated that a First Edition Vicious Bee does not exist because there is no physical room for the "1st" flag decal on its spiked back.
*   The earliest first edition bee was the Bear Bee, which debuted at the game's initial release.
*   Although First Edition Bear Bees are automatically gifted upon acquisition, their gifted status can still be removed if a player manually removes and re-adds them to the hive.
