# Pro Bear

**Pro Bear** is a shop bear found in the Pro Shop. He is typically the second shop bear encountered by players. His presence is not required to purchase items from the shop.

## Description

Pro Bear shares a similar appearance to Noob Bear and Top Bear, featuring yellow skin, red clothing, green pants, and a smiley face. The primary difference between these bears is the specific color scheme of their attire.

## Trivia & Mechanics

*   Pro Bear is one of four specialized bears located in the Brave Bee Gate area; the others are Science Bear, Dapper Bear, and Tunnel Bear.
*   Pro Bear, Noob Bear, Top Bear, and Shadow Bear are considered non-functional decorative bears (they do not offer unique services).
*   Behind Pro Bear's location is a maze that contains valuable tokens: Royal Jelly and Sunflower Seed. This area also hosts hidden stickers.
