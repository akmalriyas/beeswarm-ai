# Paper Angel

**Paper Angel** is a Level 8 Beesmas Beequip with an equip limit of two. It has no color requirement and can be equipped to Bomber, Rascal, Shy, or Photon Bees.

*   **Description:** "Designs for this folded paper doll came to you in a dream."
*   **Type:** Beesmas
*   **Limit:** 2
*   **Color:** Any

## Ways to Obtain

Paper Angel can be obtained through several methods:

### Current Methods
*   As a rare drop from Festive Nymph.
*   A rare chance drop from Onett's Lid Art.
*   As a reward for participating in the Stick Bug Challenge.
*   From the Crafty Gift Box (2025).

### Outdated/Removed Methods
*   From the Pristine Gift Box.
*   From the Paper Gift Box.
*   Purchasing the Angelic Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   Obtainable after completing Summer 2024's "Onett's Yard Art on The Lid" quest.
*   Purchasing the Starry Night Special pack for 1,700 Robux.
