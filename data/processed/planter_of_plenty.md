# Planter of Plenty

The Planter Of Plenty is a reusable, permanent planter introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. It can be purchased at Dapper Bear's Shop. When harvested, it grants various bonus items and has a chance to spawn a Puffshroom.

***
**Note on Data Accuracy:** This content includes information obtained through datamining and supported assumptions based on extensive testing. Some stats have been corrected from the in-game description, including specific probabilities for loot generation.
***

## Stats & Mechanics

| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reusable** | Yes | Permanent planter. |
| **Growth Time (Alone)** | 16 hours | Standard growth time. |
| **Pollen Capacity** | 1 Trillion Pollen | Maximum pollen storage. |
| **Pollen Multiplier** | +33% | Applied only when placed in the Mountain Top Field. |
| **Nectar Multipliers** | +50% each | Applies to Refreshing, Comforting, Satisfying, and Motivating nectar types. |

### Growth Bonuses (Field Modifiers)
The Planter of Plenty grows 50% faster in the following fields:
*   Pepper Patch
*   Stump Field
*   Coconut Field
*   Mountain Top Field

It also grants +50% more pollen when placed in the Mountain Top Field. Gifted Bees are 50% more likely to sip nectar from this planter, and it provides a +50% bonus across all Nectar Types.

## Crafting & Acquisition

### Crafting Ingredients
The Planter of Plenty can be crafted using the following materials:

*   100T Honey
*   500 Magic Beans
*   100 Super Smoothies
*   100 Swirled Waxes
*   100 Caustic Waxes
*   25 Turpentine

### Purchase Price
The planter can also be purchased at Dapper Bear's Shop.

## Harvesting & Drops

Harvesting a fully grown Planter of Plenty has a 1/3 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 9 and 10, with the following rarity probabilities:

*   **Rare:** ~72.94%
*   **Epic:** ~24.07%
*   **Legendary:** ~2.92%
*   **Mythic:** ~0.07%

### Claimed Drops
When claimed, the planter yields up to 60 tokens worth of items. If it was fully grown when harvested, two specific tokens are guaranteed:

*   5 Neonberries
*   10 Tickets

The remaining tokens are picked randomly from a non-exhaustive list of rewards (which does not include field-specific bonuses).

**Potential Drop Items:**

| Item | Chance/Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Soft Waxes | ~50% Chance |
| Caustic Waxes | ~33% Chance |
| Turpentine | Rare (~10% chance) |
| Neonberries, Tickets, etc. | Randomly selected from various bonus/other items. |

## Trivia

*   **Cost Comparison:** The Planter of Plenty is one of the most expensive items in the game regarding honey and requires a large number of ingredients to craft.
*   **Pollen Discrepancy:** While the in-game description suggests it grants +50% pollen from its boosted fields, datamined data indicates it only provides a +33% pollen boost specifically when placed in the Mountain Top Field.
*   **UI Detail:** The harvesting prompt displays "Harvest the The Planter of Plenty," including two instances of "the" due to the planter's name structure.
