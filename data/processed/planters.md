# Planters

[![All of the planter models](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4b/ActuallyAllPlanters.gif/revision/latest?cb=20251120002136)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4b/ActuallyAllPlanters.gif/revision/latest?cb=20251120002136)

**Planters** are inventory items that can be deployed in [Fields](Fields). Only one of each type of planter can be planted at any time, and only one planter can be placed in each field. A player is limited to having up to 3 planters active simultaneously. Harvesting a planter grants [Nectar](Nectar), random loot based on the planter and the field, and has a chance to summon a [Puffshroom](Puffshroom). Planters remain where they are placed until claimed by the player.

## Mechanics

To use a planter, drag it from your inventory or select it from your hotbar while standing on an unused field. A deployed planter will naturally grow over time only when you are playing the game. If you possess an Offline Voucher, it will continue growing for up to 24 hours after you log out.

### Growth and Buffs
The growth of a planter can be boosted by several factors:
*   **Glitter:** Using [Glitter](Glitter) on the field increases growth speed by 25% until harvest.
*   **Active Clouds:** Active clouds in the field provide an 8% faster growth bonus each, up to a maximum of 20%. This bonus lasts until the clouds despawn.
*   **Field Synergy:** Planters can grow faster in specific fields (e.g., [Red Clay Planter](Red_Clay_Planter) grows 25% faster in red fields).
*   **Bee Interaction:** When bees sip on the planter by standing in the field, they convert pollen from your bag, grant nectar, and increase growth. The amount of growth added depends on the bee's energy, while the amount of nectar granted depends on the bee's collection amount and the specific planter deployed.

### Harvesting
After reaching 95% growth, the planter rolls to determine if it will "smoke." If it smokes, a Puffshroom will spawn upon harvest. The chance for smoking varies by planter type; the [Ticket Planter](Ticket_Planter) and [Sticker Planter](Sticker_Planter) are guaranteed to smoke.

Upon harvesting, the planter drops three types of resources: nectar, pollen, and items.
*   **Nectar:** Guaranteed upon harvest. The type of nectar is determined by the field it was deployed in. Some planters grant more nectar than others.
*   **Items/Loot:** Dropped based on a certain percentage of growth. The specific items are affected by both the planter type and the field.
*   **Pollen:** Based on the planter's total growth. Any excess pollen gained beyond the player's capacity is instantly converted.

If the planter is smoking while at full growth, it will release a Puffshroom upon harvest, with its level influenced by the planter type used.

### Field Degradation
When harvesting a planter, the field "degrades." When a field degrades, the natural growth rate of any new planter planted there slows down. The amount of degradation depends on the current percentage of growth achieved on the harvested planter corresponding to that planter's natural growth time. Additionally, harvesting always degrades the field by 1 hour.

Every field can be degraded by a maximum of 48 hours. Increased degradation results in slower natural growth and longer sustained degradation. When planted in a degraded field, the planter's color turns brown instead of green, becoming darker as degradation increases. The planter's color may eventually return to green, but the field remains permanently degraded.

## Planter Types

There are currently 14 planters in-game, categorized by their usage:

**One-time Use Planters:**
These planters cannot be reused after harvest and require the player to acquire more copies for subsequent use.
*   [Paper Planter](Paper_Planter)
*   [Ticket Planter](Ticket_Planter)
*   [Festive Planter](Festive_Planter)
*   [Sticker Planter](Sticker_Planter)

**Permanent Planters:**
These planters can be indefinitely reused after acquisition and return to the player's inventory.
*   [Plastic Planter](Plastic_Planter)
*   [Candy Planter](Candy_Planter)
*   [Red Clay Planter](Red_Clay_Planter)
*   [Blue Clay Planter](Blue_Clay_Planter)
*   [Tacky Planter](Tacky_Planter)
*   [Pesticide Planter](Pesticide_Planter)
*   [Heat-Treated Planter](Heat-Treated_Planter)
*   [Hydroponic Planter](Hydroponic_Planter)
*   [Petal Planter](Petal_Planter)
*   [The Planter Of Plenty](The_Planter_Of_Plenty)

## Trivia
*   All planters (excluding the [Heat-Treated Planter](Heat-Treated_Planter), [Hydroponic Planter](Hydroponic_Planter), and [Sticker Planter](Sticker_Planter)) were added in the 2021-12-26 update.
*   There are currently 10 reusable planters, adding 10 more inventory items that can be reused indefinitely after purchase.
*   Onett hired Not Nert for modeling all of the planter models.
*   Similar to other placeable items, any planters placed down before a player joins will not appear in their game session.
*   The [Festive Planter](Festive_Planter) is the only planter that can drop [Gingerbread Bears](Gingerbread_Bear) and [Festive Beans](Festive_Bean).
*   Pollen gained from planters counts toward quests requiring pollen.
*   [Sticker Planter](Sticker_Planter) was added in the 2024-01-12 update.
*   Bees do not sip from planters during battle.
*   Despite many being labeled "1/1" in inventory, most listed planters are reusable after purchase.
*   The time taken for a bee to sip nectar from a planter is twice the bee's gathering speed.
