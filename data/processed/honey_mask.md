# Honey Mask

![Honey Mask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f0/Honey_Mask.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/267?cb=20190403022334)

The **Honey Mask** is a hat accessory that can be purchased at the Ace Shop in the Badge Bearer's Guild. It is also available for crafting.

> "This handsome mask is guaranteed to bring satisfaction into your life."

## Acquisition and Cost

### Purchase Price
*   100,000,000 Honey
*   9,999 Treats
*   50 Oil
*   25 Enzymes
*   5 Gold Eggs

### Crafting Ingredients
The mask can be crafted using the following components:
*   100,000,000 Honey
*   9,999 Treats
*   50 Oil
*   25 Enzymes
*   5 Gold Eggs

*(Note: If all required crafting ingredients were to be produced from raw materials, the process would take approximately 375 minutes (6 hours and 15 minutes).)*

## Stats and Abilities

The Honey Mask provides a variety of bonuses when equipped:

| Stat | Bonus |
| :--- | :--- |
| Pollen Collection | +25% |
| Bee Gather Pollen | +25% |
| Capacity | x1.25 |
| Honey From Tokens | +50% |
| Convert Amount | +80 |
| Defense | +30% |
| Bee Ability Rate | +15% |

### Passive: Coin Scatter
This passive ability activates every 20 Mark Tokens. When activated, 300% of the player's total Convert Total is converted and scattered into Honey tokens (visible only to the player). These Honey tokens last for one minute. (Cooldown: 2 Minutes)

## Trivia

*   The Honey Mask, along with the Bubble Mask, Fire Mask, Bucko Guard, and Riley Guard, represents epic bees.
*   It is one of only two items (along with the Diamond Mask) that requires eggs to be crafted.
*   After the 2019-04-05 update, the Instant Conversion buff was removed from this mask and all other masks.
*   The Coin Scatter passive ability was added after the 2019-09-28 update. If a player owns the Honey Mask, its passive is inherited by the Gummy Mask.
*   The Honey Mask and the Gummy Mask are the only masks in the game that require a specific Badge to access and purchase.
*   Token links cannot collect honey tokens generated by Coin Scatter; however, Vector Bee's Triangulate can.
