# Daily Top Robo Bear Challenge Scores

The **Daily Top Robo Bear Challenge Scores** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator, viewable through the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page. This leaderboard tracks the highest scores players achieve in the [Robo Bear Challenge] on a given day. It displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll to see up to the 100th place. The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

**Top 5 Finishers:**
Players who reach the top 5 by the end of the day are awarded a [Robo Cub] (a Cub Buddy skin).

**Top 100 Finishers:**
Players who reach the top 100 by the end of the day are awarded 100 [Tickets].
