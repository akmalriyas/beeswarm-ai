# Flower

Flowers are common flora found in all fields and serve as a primary source of pollen, which can subsequently be converted into honey. Players can interact with flowers using various methods to harvest pollen.

## Mechanics and Properties

**Flower Sizes and Pollen Yield:**
There are five distinct flower sizes: single, double, triple, large, and star. Each size dictates the base pollen multiplier and the total available pollen before regrowth is required.

| Size | Pollen Multiplier (per harvest) | Base Total Pollen Amount |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Single | 1 | 30 |
| Double | 2 | 45 |
| Triple | 3 | 60 |
| Large | 4 | 75 |
| Star | 5 | 90 |

*Note: The base total pollen amount is subject to change in specific fields, such as the Ant Field (which holds 50 pollen plus 10 per tier, up to 90).*

**Enhancements:**
The pollen yield and growth rate of flowers can be improved through various game mechanics, including better tools, badges, ability tokens, and field boosters. Additionally, larger flowers naturally grow at a faster rate than smaller ones.

**Regrowth and Growth Rate:**
Flowers can be regenerated using Sprinklers, Clouds, or Bubbles (Passive Abilities). The Pollen Haze ability token further improves the overall flower growth rate.

**Flower Colors:**
There are three primary colors: white, red, and blue. These colors correspond to both flowers and bees. When a red or blue bee harvests a flower of the matching color, they receive a 50% bonus pollen yield. Flower color is also critical for various quests and bee abilities.

**Special Interactions:**
Occasionally, a flower patch may emit green leaves. Collecting pollen from such patches can result in receiving an item, earning a sticker, or spawning an aphid.

## Trivia

*   Yellow flower decals exist in Onett's inventory but have never appeared in the game, suggesting they were scrapped during development.
*   White flowers are the most common, appearing in nearly all fields except for Mountain Top Field. Spider Field is unique as it contains only white flowers.
*   Only five fields contain exactly one flower size type: Ant Field (single), and Stump Field, Mountain Top Field, Coconut Field, and Pepper Patch (triple).
*   Star flowers emit a glow during the nighttime cycle.
*   Prior to the 2020-04-06 update, flower designs were different, and sizes were limited to small, double, and large.
*   If viewed from a nearly flat angle, flowers may appear to change into their corresponding colors.
*   Pollination is only achievable using specific bee equip: Fuzzy Bee's abilities, the Passive Abilities (Fuzzy Coat, Diamond Drain, Guiding Star), or bees wearing Poinsettia/Lei gear.
*   Flavor profiles are associated with color: Blue flowers taste minty, red flowers taste spicy, and white flowers taste neutral.
