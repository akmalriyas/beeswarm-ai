## Overview and Mechanics

The All-Time Top White Pollen Collectors leaderboard is one of the game's 64 leaderboards, and it features an in-game model among the 20 available. This board tracks the total amount of white pollen players have collected over time. The displayed view shows 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on these leaderboards for extended periods (hackers and exploiters will be removed).

**In-Game Interaction:**
If a player steps on the white pad in front of the collector, a message will display: "You've collected 'x' White Pollen," where 'x' is the amount of white pollen the player has gathered.

## Location

The white pollen collector is located inside the 35 Bee zone, situated on the left wall of the Petal Shop, near the entrance.
