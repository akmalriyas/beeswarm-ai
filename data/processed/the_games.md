# The Games Event

**The Games** was a major Roblox event that spanned across multiple games within Bee Swarm Simulator. It ran from August 1, 2024, to August 11, 2024.

## Overview and Mechanics

The event featured several unique additions, including the introduction of golfing minigames in the Sunflower Field, Dandelion Field, and Blue Flower Field. The special quest giver for this event was the Sun Bear.

Players participated by earning badges to contribute toward overarching prizes in a main event hub. Across all participating games, players could earn 8 total badges:
*   **5 Badges:** Awarded for collecting Shines.
*   **3 Badges:** Awarded Silver currency for completing quests given by the Sun Bear.

### Shine Locations

The five collectible Shines were located in the following areas:
*   On top of the Sun Bear's RV.
*   Inside the Ticket Tent.
*   Inside the Noob Shop.
*   Next to the flower in the Blue Flower Field.
*   To the right of the Beesmas Tree.
