# Tabby Bee

**Tabby Bee** is a Colorless Event bee that hatches from the Tabby Bee Egg, which can be obtained in the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.

*   **Description:** "This affectionate bee was raised by cats. It becomes a better worker as it warms up to you."
*   **Rarity:** Event
*   **Color:** Colorless

## Appearance & Gifted Status

Tabby Bee comes in two forms: Original and Gifted.

Like all other Event bees, Tabby Bee has no favorite treat. To make it a Gifted bee, it must be fed a Star Treat, Gingerbread Bear, or Aged Gingerbread Bear.

### Habitat Preferences
*   **Likes:** Spider Field, Clover Field
*   **Dislikes:** Cactus Field

## Stats and Mechanics

Tabby Bee possesses unique mechanics that allow its stats to increase permanently through the collection of Tabby Love tokens.

### Max Stats (At 1,000 Tabby Love)
When the bee reaches the maximum stack of 1,000 Tabby Love tokens:
*   **Pollen Collection Rate:** 110 Pollen in 4 seconds.
*   **Honey Production Rate:** 1,760 Honey in 3 seconds.
*   **Scratch Yield (Max):** Collects 88 Pollen from 3 lines of 4 flowers with Scratch (totaling 1,056 pollen).

### Gifted Bee Bonus
A Gifted Tabby Bee receives a +50% Critical Power bonus. If the bee is Gifted and has max Tabby Love stacks, it gains **Tabby Blessing+**, which adds +1% Super-Crit Chance to the standard Tabby Blessing (+1% Critical Chance and +25% Critical Power for 20 seconds).

## Abilities

**Scratch:**
Collects 7 Pollen (plus +1 per level) from three lines of four flowers. If Gifted, Scratch always deals Critical hits.

**Tabby Love:**
Permanently grants Tabby Bee +1% Gather Amount, Convert Amount, and pollen yield from Scratch. This stacks up to 1,000 times.

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Developer Reference:** Tabby Bee is a reference to Onett's real-life cat named Sam. This was hinted in his dialogue and later confirmed on Discord.
*   **Favorite Bees:** Along with Basic Bee and Rascal Bee, Tabby Bee is one of Onett's favorite bees. It is also mentioned in Panda Bear's dialogue during his Ultimate Ant Annihilation 1 quest.
*   **First Edition Status:** Tabby Bee was the third Event bee added to the game (after Photon Bee and Bear Bee) and is eligible to be a First Edition Bee when hatched during its initial release.
*   **Unique Gifted Mechanic:** Tabby Bee, along with Tadpole Bee, Gummy Bee, Festive Bee, Photon Bee, Vicious Bee, and Bear Bee, are among the few bees whose gifted bonus affects their signature ability.
*   **Historical Glitches:** When first added, the maximum Tabby Love stack was mistakenly set to 500 before being corrected. Due to a timer glitch that prevented early purchases, Tabby Bee returned on sale between June 2 and June 6, 2018.
*   **Animal Theme:** Tabby Bee is one of the few bees based on non-bee animals in the game (alongside Lion Bee, Tadpole Bee, Puppy Bee, and Bear Bee).
