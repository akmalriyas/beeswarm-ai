# Enzymes

Enzymes is an inventory item that grants powerful temporary buffs when used.

> Grants +10% Instant Conversion and x1.25 Convert Rate for 10 minutes.

**Cooldown:** 1 second

## Description and Mechanics

Enzymes were introduced in the November 25, 2018 update. When activated, it provides a buff lasting 10 minutes:
*   +10% Instant Conversion
*   x1.25 Convert Rate

Using another Enzyme will reset the timer but does not stack the buffs. Enzymes can also be used for crafting other accessories or items. Note that using a Super Smoothie will override the enzyme buff.

## Acquisition Methods

### Crafting
Enzymes can be crafted at the Blender using:
*   50 Pineapples
*   10 Royal Jellies

### Drops and Rewards
Enzymes can be obtained through various means:

**Mobs & Items:**
*   As a drop from Armored Aphid, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, and Coconut Crab.
*   As a rare drop from Scorpion and Spider.
*   From Stick Bug, Stick Nymph, or Defense Totems (in the Stick Bug Challenge).
*   From Planters: Regular planters in Pineapple Patch yield Enzymes. Heat-Treated Planters offer bonus drops; planting them in Pineapple Patch and Pepper Patch can yield 100 Enzymes (unknown cooldown), and three consecutive plantings guarantee 100 Enzymes (unknown cooldown).
*   As a drop from Stockings.

**Activities & Events:**
*   Potential match reward from Memory Matches.
*   Redeeming certain valid Codes.
*   Stack Rewards for adding specific Stickers to the Sticker Stack, including Honey Bee Bear, Panicked Science Bear, Forward Facing Spider, Standing Caterpillar, Yellow Hi, Orange Step Array, Orange Green Tri Deco, all yielding 1 Enzyme.

**Quests:**
Enzymes are frequently awarded by various bears:
*   **Black Bear:** Gives 1 Enzyme in The 15 Bee Zone and occasionally following repeatable quests.
*   **Brown Bear:** Awards 1 Enzyme on the 45th, 115th, 130th, 165th, and 260th quests; awards 3 Enzymes on the 230th quest.
*   **Science Bear:** Rewards include:
    *   Breaking Down Badges (1 Enzyme)
    *   Meditating On Phenomenon (1 Enzyme)
    *   The Power Of Information (3 Enzymes)
    *   Epistemological Endeavor (4 Enzymes)
*   **Polar Bear:** Has a chance to drop Enzymes after completing a quest.
*   **Spirit Bear:** Awards 5 Enzymes in Rhubarb That Could Have Been, and 25 Enzymes in Dancing With Stick Bug.

**Token Locations:**
Enzymes tokens can be found:
*   In the bottom left corner of Gummy Bear's Lair.
*   Next to Night Memory Match inside the Bear Gate.
*   In the tunnel accessed through the hole above the Coconut Dispenser.

## Outdated Acquisition Methods (Historical)

The following methods have been retired or changed:
*   Giving a Present to certain NPCs, such as Science Bear, Onett, and Stick Bug.
*   Completing Bubble Bee Man's B.B.M. Mission.
*   Science Bear's Epistemological Endeavor quest previously gave 5 Enzymes instead of 4.
*   Science Bear's ornament quest completion reward.
*   Drops from Dapper Bear's Samovar (appeared after every 2nd and 5th use).
*   Bee Bear quests: Shiverin' In The Snow (3 Enzymes), Merry Mechquitos (5 Enzymes), Bee Cub Workshop 3 (1 Enzyme).
*   Purchasing certain expired packs in the Robux Shop (e.g., Colossal Crafts Pack gave 50 Enzymes, Winter Wonder Pack gave 60 Enzymes, Merry Mutations Pack gave 100 Enzymes).
*   Redeeming specific expired codes (e.g., GummySausage provided 1 Enzyme).
*   Purchasing bundles from Bee Bear's Catalog (Pinecone Bundle: 30 Enzymes; Brass Bundle: 15 Enzymes).
*   Opening certain gift boxes (Soft Gift Box: 2 Enzymes, Brass Gift Box: 20 Enzymes, Beige Gift Box: 3 Enzymes, Earthen Gift Box: 10 Enzymes).

## Trivia

*   Enzymes originally resembled pills. However, due to perceived violations of the Roblox Terms Of Service, they were removed and later re-created under the name "Pineapple Candies" with their current appearance.
*   If multiple Enzymes are used in a Blender crafting recipe, the chat log displaying lost materials may incorrectly display "Enzymess," adding an extra 's' instead of the proper plural. This bug also applies to Gumdrops and other items.
