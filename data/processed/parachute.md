# Parachute

The **Parachute** is a gliding tool that can be purchased at the Pro Shop.

## Description and Mechanics

The Parachute allows the player to glide through the air. Its in-game description reads: "A parachute you can use to glide down the mountain and reach new places! Press jump while in the air to open."

**Usage:**
*   To activate gliding, the player must jump, followed by another jump while mid-air.
*   If the player becomes airborne via an external method (e.g., a cannon), they must double jump to eject the Parachute.
*   To disengage the glide, the player jumps once more.

The gliding feature is hardcoded into the player character and will continue even during lag spikes (though the parachute may not appear immediately).

**Gameplay Utility:**
While slower than walking, the Parachute enables players to reach map locations that would otherwise be inaccessible through standard movement. It is vital for collecting resources such as Royal Jelly and Ticket tokens, and for utilizing various shortcuts across the map. Furthermore, it can be used defensively to dodge mobs, as most enemies cannot "detect" the player while they are in mid-air. Combining the Parachute with cannons allows for efficient map navigation.

**Limitations:**
The Parachute's gliding speed is not affected by Haste ability tokens, Haste+ buffs, Bear Morph, or other general speed boosts.

## Trivia

*   It has an upgraded form called the Glider, which can be purchased in the Mountain Top Shop.
*   If a player times a jump precisely as they hit the ground while using the Parachute, they will receive a slight forward launch boost.
*   Enabling shift-lock allows players to slightly strafe sideways while gliding with this tool.
*   The Parachute can be used in conjunction with the Glider for movement during game modes like Memory Match.
