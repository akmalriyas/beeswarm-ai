The **Swarming Egg of the Hive** is a cosmetic hat accessory that was obtainable during the Roblox *Egg Hunt 2020* event. It could have been acquired by completing a quest from Sun Bear. As a Roblox avatar item, players wore it for display purposes. During the event (April 7 to April 28, 2020), the Onett statue and the quest giver Onett were seen wearing this accessory.

Like other limited-time cosmetic items such as the Flight of the Bumble Egg or How To Program BASIC, the Swarming Egg of the Hive does not provide any special in-game boosts; it is purely a collectible hat.
