# Computer

The **Computer** was a green screen structure located between the Windy Bee Gate and the Red HQ. It served as the core element of the Ready Player Two event. The object features an ellipsis (...) written on its surface and can only be interacted with while wearing Strange Goggles. Due to changes in the Noob Shop, players must now have the item equipped before interacting with it.

## Interior Mechanics
When a player interacts with the Computer while wearing Strange Goggles, the Digital Bee NPC appears. The Digital Bee gives the player a series of puzzles that must be completed to earn the How to Program BASIC cosmetic hat.

Upon successfully completing all puzzles given by Digital Bee, players can enter the computer's interior. Inside, they receive a Star Jelly and a Robo Pass (a reward only available during Beesmas 2022). The How to Program BASIC cosmetic floats in Digital Bee's original spot upon completion. While inside the Computer, the music track "overflowin4" plays, which is also heard in the 30 Bee Zone and 35 Bee Zone.
