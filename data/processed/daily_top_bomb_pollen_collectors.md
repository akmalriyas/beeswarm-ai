# Daily Top Bomb Pollen Collectors (Archived)

This leaderboard was a feature of the game, once listed among 66 global leaderboards and viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page.

The **Daily Top Bomb Pollen Collectors** tracked how much Pollen players collected specifically using only Bomb Abilities during that day. The leaderboard displayed the top 10 positions at a time, allowing players to scroll through results up to the 100th place. It reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes
For achieving a rank within the Top 100 when the leaderboard reset, players were awarded the **Bomber Bee Bear Sticker**.
