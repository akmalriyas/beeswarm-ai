# Cog Amulet

![Digital Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/27/Digital_Bee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20230415203844) | **Disclaimer:** This content is derived from datamining and may contain inaccurate or outdated information.

**Assumptions Made:**
*   The amulet's quality increases linearly with the number of total cogs collected during the challenge, up to a maximum of 1000 cogs. The exact formula for calculating this quality increase is unknown.
*   All tiers of the amulet maintain consistent quality scaling, even if certain high-quality tiers are unobtainable.

The **Cog Amulet** is an accessory obtained by completing specific rounds within the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge). The higher the round completed in the challenge, the higher the tier of the Cog Amulet acquired.

These amulets provide buffs to the owner and consistently increase both **Capacity** and **Attack** as they advance through tiers. Higher-tier amulets grant more potent bonuses.

## Trivia

*   The Cog Amulet is the only amulet that unlocks items in a [shop](/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops").
*   It is also the only amulet to boost **Nectar**, Super-Crit Power, Event Bee Ability Pollen, Mark Ability Pollen, Flame Pollen, and Bubble Pollen.
