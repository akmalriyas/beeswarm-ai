# Baby Bee

**Baby Bee** is a Colorless [Legendary bee](/wiki/Bees/Legendary). It is described as bringing joy and luck, though it is not skilled at typical bee tasks yet.

## Stats and Mechanics

Baby Bee's base pollen collection is **10** in **5 seconds**, which equates to 2 per second.

It makes **80 Honey** in **5 seconds**, or 16 per second.

**Stat Modifiers:**
*   -25% Movespeed, Energy, gather speed, and convert speed.
*   Cannot attack (Base Attack is 0).
*   🌟 **Gifted Hive Bonus:** +25% Loot Luck.

### Abilities

**Baby Love:** Grants x2 Pollen collection and +50% Loot Luck for 30 seconds. Loot Luck increases the chance of obtaining prizes from defeating enemies.

### Trivia
*   **Base Stats Comparison:** Baby Bee has the second-lowest base energy (after Tadpole Bee), the second-lowest base Convert Rate (after Fuzzy Bee), and one of the lowest gather amounts in the game. It also has the second-slowest base gather speed, being 1 second faster than both Tadpole Bee and Fuzzy Bee.
*   **Size:** Baby Bee and Tadpole Bee are tied for the smallest bees in the game; they are the only two bee types with a size different from standard bees.
*   **Attack Limitation:** Baby Bee has 0 base attack, meaning it cannot damage mobs even if equipped with an attack mutation. This was changed after the 2018-11-25 Update. However, any added attack still contributes to total attack and can buff passive abilities like Star Saw.
*   **Speed Ranking:** Baby Bee, Bumble Bee, Demon Bee, and Exhausted Bee are tied for the slowest bees in the game.
*   **Field Preferences:** It has the most field preferences (likes) and dislikes in the game. It prefers lower-tier fields (Dandelion, Blue Flower, Sunflower) and dislikes higher-tier fields (Rose, Cactus, Pine Tree Forest).
*   **Mushroom Field Preference:** Baby Bee is one of only two bees that likes the Mushroom Field without being red.
*   **Colorless Bees:** Along with Stubborn Bee, it is one of the only Colorless bees that likes strawberries.
*   **Skin Colors:** Baby Bee and Rage Bee are the only bees with three unique colors on their skins; Baby Bee is also the only gifted bee with this trait.
*   **Egg Drops:** There is a small chance that a Baby Bee egg will drop from Tunnel Bear.
*   **Gifted Hive Bonus History:** The Gifted Hive Bonus has changed over time: +5% Loot Luck (pre-2018-11-25), then +10% (2018-11-25 Update), +20% (2019-04-05 Update), and finally settled at +25%.
*   **Onett's Lid Art:** Onett paid $2 for the laughing baby sound used in Baby Love. Baby Bee, Basic Bee, and Bumble Bee are among the only bees featured on Onett's Lid Art.
*   **Sticker Chance:** When gifted and placed in Dandelion Field, Sunflower Field, Mushroom Field, or Blue Flower Field, it has a rare chance to find a Pale Heart Sticker.
*   **Stat Display Bug:** Baby Bee's in-game stat menu does not accurately display that its convert speed and gather speed are decreased by 25%.
