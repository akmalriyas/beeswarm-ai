## Trivia and Mechanics

*   The Common rarity and the [Event](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") rarity are the only rarities of bees that cannot be obtained using a normal [Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") or [Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly").
*   Excluding gifted forms, Common and [Rare bees](/wiki/Bees/Rare "Bees/Rare") are the only rarities that may contain a bee unable to spawn [ability tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"). The specific rare bee lacking this ability is the [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee").
*   The designated color for a **Common Bee** in the [Hive Slot](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot") system is brown (often representing bronze).
*   Common rarity has the fewest bee types, consisting of only one type: the Basic Bee.
*   There are three ways to obtain a Common rarity bee: through [Basic Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Basic_Egg "Basic Egg"), [Star Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Star_Egg "Egg"), and via the [Basic Bee Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Specific_Bee_Jelly_Variants "Royal Jelly").
