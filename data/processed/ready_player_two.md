**Ready Player Two** was a Roblox event that ran from November 23, 2020, until May 23, 2024. The event spanned across seven different games within the simulator. Players were required to decipher clues in each game and obtain its corresponding badge, alongside acquiring the [How to Program BASIC](/wiki/How_to_Program_BASIC "How to Program BASIC") accessory for their avatar.

## Progression

Each code awards the player a [7-Pronged Cog](/wiki/7-Pronged_Cog "7-Pronged Cog"). These cogs can be used to craft [Strange Goggles](/wiki/Strange_Goggles "Strange Goggles").

When equipped, the Strange Goggles allow players to interact with [The Computer](/wiki/The_Computer "The Computer"). Upon interaction, the player encounters [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee_\(NPC\) "Digital Bee \(NPC\)") which serves as an NPC and begins citing the first puzzle. After completing each subsequent puzzle, the player is rewarded with various items and can return to The Computer to receive the next challenge.

Upon successfully completing the third and final puzzle, interacting with The Computer awards the game's event item, badge, and a [Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly").
