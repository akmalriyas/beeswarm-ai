# Honeycomb Belt

![Image-2](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/41/Image-2.png/revision/latest?cb=20190317064222 "Honeycomb Belt")

## Description
A luxurious faux honeycomb that can be worn as a belt to greatly enhance your pollen capacity.

The Honeycomb Belt is an accessory located in the Badge Bearer's Guild.

## Stats and Effects
| Stat | Bonus |
| :--- | :--- |
| Capacity | +150,000 |
| Capacity | +25% |
| Convert Amount | +75 |
| Loot Luck | +100% |
| Buzz Bomb Pollen | +20% |
| Honey From Tokens | +50% |
| Colorless Bee Attack | +1 |

## Cost and Crafting
**Cost:**

*   2,500 Gumdrops
*   1,250 Royal Jelly
*   2,500 Pineapples
*   1,250 Sunflower Seeds

## Trivia
*   It is the 3rd most expensive belt accessory in the game, behind the Petal Belt and the Coconut Belt.
*   This item was one of four belts released after Sun Bear left; the others are the Petal Belt, the Coconut Belt, and the Belt Pocket.
*   Prior to the 2019-04-05 update, the Honeycomb Belt's description was simply "Honey."
