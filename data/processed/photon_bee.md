# Photon Bee

**Photon Bee** is a Colorless Event bee that hatches from the [Photon Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Photon_Bee_Egg), which can be obtained in the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.

*"An entity made of pure light temporarily taking on the form of a bee."*

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Event |
| **Color** | Colorless |
| **Energy** | Unlimited |
| **Speed** | 21 |
| **Attack** | 4 |

### Appearance & Gifted Status
Photon Bee comes in two forms: Original and Gifted.

*   **Gifted Requirement:** Like all Event bees, Photon Bee does not have a favorite treat. It can be gifted using a Star Treat, Gingerbread Bear, or Aged Gingerbread Bear.

### Habitat Preferences
Photon Bee likes the Pineapple Patch and the Pumpkin Patch, but dislikes the Clover Field.

## Statistics & Mechanics

### Collection Rates
*   **Pollen Collection:** 20 Pollen in 2 seconds (Equivalent to 10 per second).
    *   With x2 Bee Pollen gamepass: 40 Pollen in 2 seconds.
*   **Honey Production:** 240 Honey in 2 seconds (Equivalent to 120 per second).
    *   With x2 Honeymaking Speed: 240 Honey in 1 second.

### Base Stats & Bonuses
*   **Base Pollen Collection:** 20 (White Boost Token) in 2 seconds.
*   **Stat Modifiers:** +50% Movespeed, +50% gather and convert speed, +10 Gather Amount, +160 Convert Amount, +20% Instant Conversion, +3 Attack.
*   **Energy Requirement:** Does not require Sleep.

### Gifted Hive Bonus
When gifted, Photon Bee receives a bonus:
*   +5% Instant Conversion.
*   The Beamstorm ability changes from yellow to white and instantly converts all collected pollen.

## Abilities

Photon Bee possesses two unique abilities:

1.  **Beamstorm:** Fires 25 (+2 per level) beams from the sky. These beams collect and double ALL pollen from Flowers they hit.
    *   **Gifted Effect:** Beamstorm collects x1.5 more white pollen, resulting in a total of x3 white pollen, and instantly converts all collected pollen.
2.  **Haste:** Grants +10% Player Movespeed for 20 seconds. This ability stacks up to 10 times.

## Trivia & Lore

*   Photon Bee's energy is set to an extremely high value (999,999,999,999,999).
*   It was the first Event bee added to the Ticket Tent and the second overall Event bee in the game (after Bear Bee), making it the first Event bee purchasable with tickets.
*   Photon Bee shares a speed of 21 with Ninja Bee, tying them for the fastest bees in the game.
*   Along with Exhausted Bee, Photon Bee is one of the only bees that does not require sleep and has infinite energy. However, they still need to rest at the hive when the player dies.
*   This bee is the first Event bee to feature a trail behind it (though Gummy Bee also creates a visible trail under specific conditions).
*   Photon Bee, Festive Bee, and Vicious Bee are among the few Event bees that can generate Ability Tokens from non-Event bees.
*   If gifted, Photon Bee's Beamstorm changes color to white instead of yellow and instantly converts collected pollen.
*   Due to its infinite energy, using Royal Jelly on a Photon Bee results in a game glitch where the transformed bee gains an absurd amount of energy (a glitch also shared with Exhausted Bees).
*   Photon Bee's Beamstorm ability has range and can potentially collect pollen from adjacent fields if the token is close enough.
