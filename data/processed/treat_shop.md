# Treat Shop

[![The Treat Shop in-game.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cc/Treat-removebg-preview.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/225?cb=20210509205858)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cc/Treat-removebg-preview.png/revision/latest?cb=20210509205858) [](/wiki/File:Treat-removebg-preview.png)

The **Treat Shop** is a shop located to the left of Mother Bear. It sells treats, which have a base cost of 100 honey.

### Pricing Mechanics
The price of treats increases by 100 honey for every treat purchased after the first one. This price scaling caps at a maximum of 10,000 honey per treated item.

Players can purchase treats in various increments: 1, 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000, 1,000,000, 10,000,000, 100,000,000, 1,000,000,000, and 10,000,000,000.
