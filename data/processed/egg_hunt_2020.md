## Overview
*   **Duration:** April 7, 2020, to April 28, 2020
*   **Special Quest Givers:** [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear")
*   **Special Additions:** [Chicks](/wiki/Chicks "Chicks")

The **Egg Hunt 2020** was a Roblox event that spanned across multiple games. In each game, players had to complete specific tasks or quests to obtain one of the eggs, which could then be worn on their avatar.

## Story
[Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") initiated the Egg Hunt by stating he wanted to collaborate with the bears for the event. He planned to hide exotic eggs across the map (in the form of a scavenger hunt). When the eggs hatched, Sun Bear required players to capture [chicks](/wiki/Chicks "Chicks") and deliver them to three specific bear characters: [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear"), [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear"), and [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear").

## How to Obtain the Egg
To acquire the egg in [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"), players first needed to speak with [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") near the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate") to receive the initial quest.

Players then had to assist the three bears (Black, Brown, and Mother Bears) with their respective Egg Hunt 2020 quests. These quests primarily involved capturing chicks, alongside several other side objectives.

Upon completing all tasks for the bears, players returned to Sun Bear to claim special rewards, including the [Swarming Egg of the Hive](/wiki/Swarming_Egg_of_the_Hive "Swarming Egg of the Hive"), which could be worn on their Roblox avatar as a hat.
