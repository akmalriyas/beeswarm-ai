# Elite Barrel

![Elite Barrel](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/62/Elite_Barrel.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403021616)

## Overview
The Elite Barrel is a high-tier storage bag available for purchase in the Pro Shop. It provides significant boosts to pollen capacity and conversion rates, making it essential for advanced harvesting strategies.

### Stats & Cost
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cost** | 650,000 Honey |
| **Capacity Bonus** | +125,000 Pollen |
| **Convert Rate Bonus** | +70% |

### Crafting Materials
*   650,000 Honey

## Description and Mechanics
The Elite Barrel is described as "the world's most advanced barrel." While its description suggests it increases pollen from certain fields, trivia confirms that the barrel itself does not increase Pollen collected from a Field. Its primary function is to enhance storage capacity and improve conversion efficiency.

## Trivia
*   Despite its promotional description, the Elite Barrel does not provide an increased yield of Pollen when collecting from Fields.
*   It is the second most expensive bag available in the Pro Shop, surpassed only by the Port-O-Hive.
*   Among all items in the Pro Shop, it ranks as the fifth most expensive item, behind the Port-O-Hive, Honey Dipper, Hiking Boots, and Propeller Hat.
