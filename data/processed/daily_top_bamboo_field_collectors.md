# Daily Top Bamboo Field Collectors

The **Daily Top Bamboo Field Collectors** is one of the game's leaderboards, tracked within the Global Leaderboards menu via the System Page. This leaderboard tracks the total amount of pollen collected by players specifically in the Bamboo Field on a given day.

It displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes and Rewards

Players are awarded prizes based on their rank by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 positions receive a **Bamboo Field Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 positions receive 25 **Tickets**.
