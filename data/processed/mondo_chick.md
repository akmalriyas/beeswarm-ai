# Mondo Chick

The **Mondo Chick** is a level 8 boss chick located in the Mountain Top Field. It respawns every hour at the full hour (e.g., 1:00, 2:00), and its timer can be found on the side of the Mountain Top Shop. The respawn time is unaffected by the Monster Respawn Time stat. Its rewards can be shared between players.

## Statistics & Mechanics

| Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Health | 300,000 |
| Damage | 75 |
| Battle Points | 0 |
| Bond Level | 8 |

**Behavior:**
The Mondo Chick always spawns at level 8 with 300,000 health. It takes 333 damage automatically each second. If not defeated within 15 minutes of spawning, it will despawn like the Commando Chick.

*   **Egg Phase:** The Mondo Chick will hide in its egg after several seconds, stopping movement and blocking some attacks. This egg can be cracked by dealing 7,500 damage or by waiting 23 seconds via automatic damage.
*   **Defeat Timer:** If the chick is not defeated within 15 minutes of spawning, it despawns.
*   **Escape:** When its time runs out, it jumps towards the Lion Bee Gate entrance.

## Rewards and Capture Times

Players who defeat the Mondo Chick receive rewards, including a **Mondo Chick Blessing**. The amount of blessing received depends on how quickly the chick is defeated:

| Time to Defeat | Amount of Mondo Chick Blessing |
| :--- | :--- |
| < 5 seconds | x10 |
| 6 – 10 seconds | x9 |
| 11 – 15 seconds | x8 |
| 16 – 20 seconds | x7 |
| 21 – 30 seconds | x6 |
| 31 – 59 seconds | x5 |
| 1:00 - 2:30 | x4 |
| 2:31 - 5:00 | x3 |
| 5:01 - 10:00 | x2 |
| 10:01 - 15:00 | x1 |

**Messages:**
*   **Spawn:** A server-wide message displays: "🐣 Mondo Chick has spawned!"
*   **Defeated:** Players who damaged it receive three messages displaying: "Defeated Mondo Chick!", "🐣 Captured Mondo Chick in [Time] (Fastest Time: [Fastest Time])/[(New Record)]", and "Reward: Mondo Chick Blessing x[Amount]". *(Note: A glitch may sometimes display the message without the Fastest Time information.)*
*   **Escape:** When time runs out, a notification displays: "🐣 The Mondo Chick got away!"

## Tips for Combat

To defeat the Mondo Chick efficiently, consider these strategies:

1.  **Bee Specialization:** Use bees specialized in attack, such as Vicious Bee, Lion Bee, Rage Bee, Spicy Bee, and Precise Bee.
2.  **Ability Tokens:** Utilize bees that produce ability tokens (Focus, Rage, Melody) to boost Critical Chance, Critical Power, and Bee Attack. Stacking Focus before the spawn is recommended. If you own a Precise Bee, stacking Precision can increase Super-Crit Chance.
3.  **Equipment:** Equip the Demon Mask or Fire Mask for improved attack damage.
4.  **Coconut Canister:** Activating the Emergency Coconut Shield grants 10 seconds of ×1.25 Bee Attack and drops five falling coconuts onto the field.
5.  **Nectar & Leveling:** Use Nectars (like Invigorating Nectar) for increased Bee Attack. Ensure bees are leveled to level 8 or higher; lower-level bees may miss attacks against this boss.
6.  **Distance Management:** Maintain distance from the Mondo Chick, as it can randomly change direction and deal significant damage to players.
7.  **Consumables & Passives:** Items like Stingers, Coconuts, Oils, or passive abilities such as Star Saw can aid in faster defeat.
8.  **Rogue Vicious Bees (Advanced):** Rogue Vicious Bee spikes can significantly damage the Mondo Chick. This is most effective if a Vicious Bee spawns during a nighttime sequence on the Mountain Top Field shortly before the boss appears, though this scenario is unlikely. Using a Night Bell guarantees a spawn but requires luck regarding placement and rarity.

## Trivia

*   The Mondo Chick's noises can be heard throughout the entire map, similar to Cave Monsters.
*   It is the only mob that has a fixed spawn point in the Mountain Top Field.
*   During the soft launch of the Egg Hunt 2020 update, its health was 250,000 before being changed to 300,000.
*   If you already have the Mondo Chick Blessing buff and defeat another one, the new blessing will override the old buff, potentially degrading or upgrading it based on the capture time.
*   Mondo Chick, Stick Nymphs, and Festive Nymphs are mobs that take damage without being attacked by anything else.
*   Mondo Chick, Festive Nymphs, and Robo Party are the three mobs capable of dropping a "blessing" buff; Mondo Chick is the only one to guarantee it upon defeat. Unlike the others, Mondo Chick grants the blessing directly as an active buff, not in token form.
*   If the Mondo Chick falls out of the Mountain Top Field into another field, its rewards will spawn there. However, if it dies outside of any field, its drops will form in a ring.
*   As of the Beesmas 2020 update, it no longer drops Marshmallow Bees.
