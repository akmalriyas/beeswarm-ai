# Mondo Chick

The **Mondo Chick** is a level 8 boss and chick located in the Mountain Top Field. It respawns every hour at the full hour (e.g., 1:00, 2:00), and its timer can be monitored on the Mountain Top Shop. The respawn time is unaffected by the Monster Respawn Time stat. Its rewards are shareable among players.

## Stats and Mechanics

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Health | 300,000 |
| Damage | 75 |
| Battle Points | 0 |
| Bond Level | 8 |

**Behavior:**
The Mondo Chick always spawns at level 8 with 300,000 health. It automatically takes 333 damage per second, meaning it will reach 300 health after approximately 15 minutes of spawning. If the chick is not defeated within this 15-minute window, it despawns, similar to the Commando Chick.

**Combat:**
Due to its large range and high damage output, maintaining distance from the Mondo Chick is highly recommended. The creature has a unique mechanic where it will hide in its egg after several seconds, ceasing movement and blocking some attacks. This egg can be cracked by dealing 7,500 damage or by waiting 23 seconds via automatic damage.

**Interactions:**
*   If a Rogue Vicious Bee spawns in the Mountain Top Field, it can attack the Mondo Chick, causing its health to drop rapidly.
*   When spawned, a server-wide message displays: "🐣 Mondo Chick has spawned!"

## Capture and Rewards

Players who damage the Mondo Chick are rewarded based on how quickly they defeat it. The rewards include **Mondo Chick Blessing** (which applies directly to the player's active buffs) and various drops.

### Defeat Notifications
*   **Successful Capture:** "Defeated Mondo Chick!" followed by "🐣 Captured Mondo Chick in [Time] (Fastest Time: [Fastest Time])/[(New Record)] 🐣 Reward: Mondo Chick Blessing x[Amount]"
*   **Glitch Notification:** Sometimes the notification may display without the fastest time record: "🐣 Captured Mondo Chick in [Time] (Fastest Time:) 🐣"
*   **Timeout:** If its timer runs out, it jumps toward the Lion Bee Gate entrance. A message notifies players who damaged it: "🐣 The Mondo Chick got away!"

## Tips for Combat

To defeat the Mondo Chick efficiently and maximize rewards:

1.  **Bee Specialization:** Use bees specialized in attack, such as Vicious Bee, Lion Bee, Rage Bee, Spicy Bee, or Precise Bee.
2.  **Ability Tokens:** Utilize bees that produce ability tokens (Focus, Rage, Melody) to boost Critical Chance, Critical Power, and Bee Attack. Build a Focus stack before the spawn. If you own a Precise Bee, stacking Precision can increase Super-Crit Chance.
3.  **Equipment:** The Demon Mask or Fire Mask are recommended for improving player attack damage.
4.  **Consumables/Passives:** Nectars (like Invigorating Nectar) grant increased Bee Attack. Passive abilities such as Stingers, Coconuts, Oils, and Star Saw can aid in the defeat.
5.  **Coconut Canister:** Equipping a Coconut Canister allows players to activate the Emergency Coconut Shield for 10 seconds of $\times 1.25$ Bee Attack while dropping five coconuts onto the field.
6.  **Leveling:** Level up bees to level 8 or higher. Bees at the same level or higher as the Mondo Chick are guaranteed not to miss their attacks.
7.  **Distance:** Maintain distance from the chick, as it can randomly change direction and deal significant damage to players.
8.  **Rogue Vicious Bee Strategy (Advanced):** Rogue Vicious Bees can significantly speed up the kill due to their spike damage against other mobs. This is most effective if a Vicious Bee spawns during a nighttime sequence on the Mountain Top Field shortly before the Mondo Chick appears, though this scenario is unlikely. Using a Night Bell guarantees a spawn but requires luck regarding placement and rarity of the bell itself.

## Trivia

*   The Mondo Chick's chirping noises can be heard throughout the entire map, similar to Cave Monsters.
*   It is the only mob with a fixed spawn point in the Mountain Top Field.
*   During the soft launch of Egg Hunt 2020, its health was 250,000 before being changed to 300,000.
*   If you already possess the Mondo Chick Blessing buff and defeat another one, the new blessing will override the old one, resetting its timer and potentially upgrading or degrading the buff based on the capture time.
*   Mondo Chick, Stick Nymphs, and Festive Nymphs are unique mobs that take damage without being actively attacked by a bee.
*   Mondo Chick, Festive Nymphs, and Robo Party are the only three mobs capable of dropping a "blessing" buff; Mondo Chick is the only one to guarantee it upon defeat, and it applies directly as an active buff rather than a token.
*   If the Mondo Chick falls out of the Mountain Top Field into another area, its rewards will spawn in that new field. If it dies outside of any defined field, its drops will form in a ring.
*   As of the Beesmas 2020 update, it no longer drops Marshmallow Bees.
