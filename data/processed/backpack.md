# Backpack

![Backpack](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b1/Backpack.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190402215341)

## Description
A heavy-duty backpack. This bag can be purchased in the Noob Shop.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cost** | 5,500 Honey |
| **Capacity** | 3,500 Pollen |

> "A heavy-duty Backpack."

## Stats
*   Increases capacity by +3,500.

## Crafting Ingredients
To craft a backpack, you require:
*   5,500 Honey

## Trivia
*   The Backpack and the Canister are the only bags in the Noob Shop that cost more than their base Capacity.
*   It is the second most expensive bag in the Noob Shop, behind the Canister.
*   This backpack, along with the Pouch, is one of the few visually realistic bags and the only bag featuring a texture in the game.
