# Comforting Vial

[![Comforting Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/39/Comforting_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042309)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/39/Comforting_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042309)

> Grants 4 hours of Comforting Nectar. (Boosts: Blue Pollen, Convert Rate, Convert Rate at hive.)

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 1 Second
*   **Cap:** 10

The **Comforting Vial** is a consumable nectar vial item that was introduced in the 2022-12-26 update. When used, it instantly grants the player 4 hours of the Comforting Nectar buff. Additionally, this item can be used to craft various accessories and tools.

## Acquisition Methods (Current)

*   **Dapper Bear Quests:** Completing Dapper Bear's 6th, 10th, and 13th quests rewards one Comforting Vial.
*   **Blue Drive Quest:** Completing Dapper Bear's Blue Drive quest rewards one Comforting Vial.
*   **Nectar Condenser:** Using the Nectar Condenser to condense 12 hours of Comforting Nectar yields a vial.
*   **Sticker Stack:** Adding the Comforting Nectar Icon to the Sticker Stack rewards one Comforting Vial.
*   **Bee Bear's Catalog:** Purchasing the Frozen Forest Pack (Snowflake x1, Gingerbread Bear x60) grants one Comforting Vial and other items.
*   **Gifted Bucko Bee:** Giving a Present to Gifted Bucko Bee rewards one Comforting Vial and other items.

## Acquisition Methods (Outdated/Historical)

The following methods for obtaining the Comforting Vial are no longer active:

*   Giving a Present to Gifted Bucko Bee during Beesmas 2022.
*   Buying the Hefty Honeyday Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   Purchasing the Nighttime Nectar Pack in the Robux Shop.
*   Opening certain gift boxes, such as the Soothing Gift Box (rewarding two vials).
*   Completing specific Beesmas 2022 quests:
    *   Bucko Bee's Snow Machine (Gifted Bucko Bee)
    *   Peppermint Preparation 2 (Bee Bear)
    *   Bubble Bee Man's Naughty List (rewarding eight vials)
