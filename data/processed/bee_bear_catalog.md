## Eggs & Jellies

### Eggs

| Egg Type | Price | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Gold Egg | 40 Snowflakes, 4 Gingerbread Bears | Always hatches into an Epic, Legendary, or Mythic bee. |
| Diamond Egg | 20 Gingerbread Bears | Always hatches into a Legendary or Mythic bee. |
| Mythic Egg | 500 Snowflakes, 100 Gingerbread Bears | Always hatches into a random Mythic bee. |

### Jellies (Bee Transformation)

**Royal Jelly**
*   **Price:** 10 Snowflakes
*   **Effect:** Transforms a bee into a special bee.

**Star Jelly**
*   **Price:** 500 Snowflakes
*   **Effect:** Transforms a bee into a random Gifted Bee.
