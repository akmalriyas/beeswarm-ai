# Stick Bug Challenge

The **Stick Bug Challenge** is a boss fight feature that lasts for 10 minutes. It can be initiated in several ways:

*   A player talks to and starts the challenge with [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug).
*   When a player begins [Spirit Bear]'s "Dancing With Stick Bug" quest.
*   Globally, if [Onett] chooses to activate it.
*   During [Beesmas] events when completing Stick Bug's Beesmas quest or giving him a present.

If a player starts the challenge independently, they must have previously given a [translator](/wiki/Translator) to Stick Bug. The initial cost is 50 [tickets](/wiki/Ticket), but it can be started for free once every 36 hours (1.5 days). If the free option is available, the ticket payment option will be disabled.

The challenge takes place across the entire server and is open to all players. Additionally, giving a [present](/wiki/Present) to Stick Bug or starting the Egg Hunt quest will initiate the challenge (provided a translator has been given to Stick Bug).

When the challenge begins, a chat message appears:
⚠️ {Username} has started the Stick Bug Challenge! ⚠️

Stick Bug then spawns in the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field). If Onett summons him, the message reads:
⚠️ Onett has started the Stick Bug Challenge! ⚠️

During the challenge, Stick Bug moves between various fields and engages players using a set of attacks. Once players deal sufficient damage to Stick Bug, he moves to a new field, leaving behind a circle of rewards. As Stick Bug transitions between fields, he gains health, increases in speed, acquires stronger attacks (e.g., more [Stick Nymphs](/wiki/Stick_Nymph) spawn), and levels up. A persistent brown window on the side of the screen displays the challenge duration, Stick Bug's current level and field, and the player's accumulated points.

Stick Bug can only appear in the following fields:
[Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field), [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field), [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field), [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field), [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field), [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch), or [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field). He always begins in the Sunflower Field. When he moves to a new field, a chat message appears:
⚠️ Stick Bug's headed to the {Field Name}! ⚠️

## Attacks

Stick Bug utilizes numerous attack patterns throughout the challenge.

### Normal Behavior and Sequence

Before initiating an attack, Stick Bug regularly runs toward one of the players in the current field. Being hit by him deals damage to the player. His movement speed, the number of stick nymphs he summons, and his attack execution speed all increase with his level. He follows a fixed sequence of attacks:
1. Splinter Trap
2. Spinners
3. Flailing Sticks
4. Summon Stick Nymphs
5. Stick Totem (Defense Totem)
6. Flailing Sticks
7. Hiding
8. Stick Flail

Stick Bug uses specific variations of these attacks depending on his level, but the overall pattern remains consistent.

### Flailing Sticks

Stick Bug performs a dance emote, and a large red warning circle appears around him with a radius of approximately 6 [flowers](/wiki/Flowers). After a short delay, he flails sticks, dealing damage (damage scales with his level). Each flail deals damage only once; players cannot be damaged by the same flail again. Following this attack, he summons several [Stick Nymphs](/wiki/Stick_Nymph) around himself. The number of nymphs deployed depends on the specific emote used:
*   The dance emote
*   The dance2 emote
*   The point emote

### Stick Nymphs

Stick Bug uses the dance3 emote, and a large red warning circle (radius of about 6 flowers) appears. After a few seconds, he waves, summoning a horde of stick nymphs inside the red circle. The movement speed, quantity, and level of these nymphs are based on Stick Bug's current level and increase as he levels up.

If the player has completed Stick Bug's Beesmas quest after 2020, there is a chance he will summon a special variant called [Festive Nymphs](/wiki/Festive_Nymph). These have the same attributes as normal stick nymphs but drop better loot.

### Spinners

Three or four (depending on Stick Bug's level) red warning circles appear near the corners of the field, each with a radius of about 3 flowers. After some time, he deploys rotating one-bladed flails within these circles. The rotation speed gradually increases as Stick Bug levels up. At higher levels, he initiates this attack multiple times, stacking more than a single blade in one spot simultaneously. These deployed flails disappear after a period.

### Hiding

After completing his attack sequence, Stick Bug stands still and hides in the field. He becomes two-dimensional (2D) and can only be seen by shadows within the flowers. In this phase, Stick Bug cannot be targeted. To force him out of his 2D form, players must collect pollen from the affected flowers on his 2D body. The required amount increases with his level. Once the requirement reaches zero, he returns to a three-dimensional (3D) state and stands still before restarting his attack sequence. The Splinter Trap activates after this ability is executed.

### Splinter Trap

This trap activates upon Stick Bug leveling up, when he hides, or during Level 12+. Stick Bug pauses briefly and uses the point emote. Players who contributed to attacking him during the current level become targeted by a splinter trap on their head/s. The trap reduces the player's movement speed and jump power by 25% and deals 3 damage per second.

When trapped, players receive a chat message:
You've been trapped in twigs! Collect pollen to remove them. (The splinter trap icon is identical to the [Haste+](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste) icon.)

### Defense Totem

Starting at Level 6, Stick Bug builds a defense totem based on the field he is currently standing in. While active, Stick Bug gains 25% defense, reducing incoming damage by that amount. He can stack up to three totems (75% total defense), but will not use this attack if three consecutive totems are already active. To destroy a totem, players must collect pollen in the field where it was summoned. Destroying a totem yields rewards.

Stick Bug builds totems in the following fields:
*   [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field)
*   [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field)
*   [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field)
*   [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field)
*   [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field)
*   [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest)
*   [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field)

A server-wide message is sent when he uses this attack:
⚠️ Stick Bug has built a Defense Totem in the {Field Name}! ⚠️

## Pathing

After Stick Bug's health is depleted, loot drops, and he moves to another field. The next field depends on his current location (possible outcomes are listed below):

| Current Field | Possible Next Fields |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sunflower Field | Clover Field, Strawberry Field |
| Clover Field | Sunflower Field, Bamboo Field, Strawberry Field |
| Bamboo Field | Cactus Field, Pumpkin Patch |
| Strawberry Field | Sunflower Field, Bamboo Field |
| Cactus Field | Strawberry Field, Pumpkin Patch |
| Pineapple Patch | Bamboo Field |
| Mountain Top Field | Sunflower Field |
| Pumpkin Patch | Strawberry Field |

## Tips and Strategy

*   **Preparation:** Ensure there are no distracting mobs (like [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Chicks#Mondo_Chick)) before starting.
*   **Bee Leveling:** Bees with a level lower than Stick Bug are more likely to miss attacks.
*   **Teamwork:** Bring strong players, as the number of participants speeds up Stick Bug's leveling rate.
*   **Attack Pattern Awareness:** Remember that after landing in a field, Stick Bug typically uses Flailing Sticks before attacking the player. Level 12+ Stick Bug has a different pattern, starting with two Spinner attacks before targeting the player.
*   **Path Optimization:** Utilize optimal paths to minimize travel time between fields.
*   **Bamboo Glitch:** The Bamboo Glitch (going to stairs in Bamboo) can stun Stick Bug.
*   **Equipment Utility:** Certain gear is highly beneficial:
    *   Many gears boost bee attack stats.
    *   The [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask) and [Demon Masks](/wiki/Demon_Mask) provide helpful stat boosts (attack, defense) and spawn flames via passives.
    *   The [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask)'s Gummy Morph passive significantly enhances [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee)'s stats, including attack.
    *   The [Dark Scythe](/wiki/Dark_Scythe) generates Dark Flames, which boost Super-Crit Power and deal extra damage.
    *   The [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister) offers two useful passives: Inspire Coconuts and Emergency Coconut Shield. The shield grants 100% defense and x1.25 bee attack for a few seconds; it should be intentionally activated later in the challenge to maximize its impact.
    *   The [Glider](/wiki/Glider) and boots aid navigation or escaping difficult situations. [Coconut Clogs](/wiki/Coconut_Clogs) or [Gummy Boots](/wiki/Gummy_Boots)'s Coconut Haste Surge can be useful in a pinch.
*   **Recommended Bees:** High-attack bees include [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee), [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee), [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee), [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee), and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee). Vicious, Windy, and Spicy Bees are effective against both Stick Bug and Stick Nymphs as they deal damage to all mobs in range.
    *   The [Impale](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Impale) ability can be used when Stick Nymphs spawn for maximum efficiency.
    *   While [Tornado](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Tornado)'s pathing is uncontrolled, it can still be used to lure Stick Nymphs into it.
    *   Gifted bees (x1.5 attack) and those with helpful hive bonuses ([Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee), [Looker Bee](/wiki/Looker_Bee), [Commander Bee](/wiki/Commander_Bee), [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee)) increase damage output.
    *   Buffs from bees: Precise Bee's Target Practice boosts Super-Crit Chance; Focus grants Critical Chance; Music Bee's Melody boosts Critical Power; Rage and Spicy Bees grant Rage (attack boost). Haste-producing bees are useful for map traversal and dodging. Flame Heat provides +5% to +25% Bee Attack. Digital Bee's [Mind Hack](/wiki/Ability_Tokens) can stun Nymphs and Stick Bug.
*   **Item Usage:**
    *   [Stingers](/wiki/Stinger) boost bee attack by 1.5x for 30 seconds, activating Star Saw (requires Supreme Star Amulet).
    *   [Oils](/wiki/Oil) increase movement speed, and [Tropical Drinks](/wiki/Tropical_Drink) increase critical hit chance (both last 10 minutes). [Super Smoothies](/wiki/Super_Smoothie) provide buffs and an additional +1% Super-Crit Chance.
    *   [Coconuts](/wiki/Coconut) deal significant damage when landing on Stick Bug or Nymphs; the Coconut Canister passives also summon coconuts.
    *   Save [Invigorating Nectar](/wiki/Nectar) for an additional x1.01 to x1.10 bee attack, depending on remaining duration.
*   **Totem Management:** When a totem is built in a nearby field, send the teammate best suited to destroy it quickly (e.g., the player with the highest blue pollen collection for a Blue Flower Field totem). If the totem is in the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field), prioritize someone who recently defeated a Stump Snail over someone currently near one. Totems can be safely dealt with when Stick Bug hides, as he cannot build more totems then and Nymphs are likely to despawn upon player return to the field.
*   **Tactical Positioning:** One teammate can lure Stick Nymphs away from other players to prevent distraction. Placing [sprinklers](/wiki/Sprinklers) in the current field helps with flower regeneration, mitigating risks associated with Splinter Traps or time loss during hiding phases. [Clouds](/wiki/Clouds) also aid flower regeneration.
*   **Safety & Performance:** Stand on higher areas (like the ramp at the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch)) to avoid most attacks and prevent Stick Nymphs from reaching you. Reducing graphic settings can help mitigate potential lag issues. Vector Bee and Precise Bee are excellent for pollen collection during hiding phases.
*   **Sticker Stack:** The [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack) is useful if it provides buffs to Bee Movespeed, Critical Power, Super Crit Power, or Bee Attack.

## Audio and Gallery

The challenge music is "Stickbug." Upon depleting Stick Bug's health, one of several random audio clips will play. Visual assets include images of Stick Bug in battle, his 2D form, Onett initiating the challenge, a defense totem, and specific Beesmas event screenshots.

## Trivia

*   One leg on Stick Bug's Defense Totem is slightly lighter than the others.
*   There are glitches that allow players to deal extra damage without risk: running into walls near the Bamboo Field ramp causes him to get stuck, or hitting the boundary of [Clover Field] causes him to fall over briefly.
*   If the challenge ends during the [night](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle), normal music will play instead of the night theme.
*   When starting the "Dancing With Stick Bug" quest, the chat message is ⚠ {Username} has started the Stick Bug Challenge! ⚠, even if the player has completed his Beesmas Quest (in which case Festive Nymphs will not spawn).
*   The initial message before the challenge began was: "Stick Bug will spawn in the Sunflower Field. Work together to defeat him and level him up. The higher the level, the better the rewards."
*   If players join a server mid-challenge, Stick Bug's stat file shows Level 1, but this resets to normal upon his subsequent defeat.
*   Giving Stick Bug a present starts the challenge. Completing his Beesmas quests (2020/2021/2024 Summer and Winter/2025) also triggers it.
*   There is a [glitch](/wiki/Glitches#Invisible_Splinter_Trap) where if a player dies while trapped, the splinter trap effect persists until they die again, though the pollen counter remains visible for removal.
*   Stick Bug can inconsistently walk out of his field to attack before retracting and resuming his pattern. When jumping between fields, he maintains his hitbox and can still damage players.
*   This is the only permanent challenge in the game that affects all server members.
