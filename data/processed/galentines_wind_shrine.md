# Galentine Wind Shrine

The **Galentine Wind Shrine** is a special version of the standard Wind Shrine, available after completing specific Spirit Bear Beesmas quests (2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025). It is decorated for Galentine’s Day, featuring five hearts, a large red bow on top, a smaller white bow between two hearts, and pink-painted pillars.

The shrine is located beyond the Windy Bee Gate, next to the Pepper Patch. Like the standard Wind Shrine, it allows players to donate items in exchange for rewards such as Field Winds and Honey. It has a cooldown of 1 hour between donations.

## Mechanics Overview

### Donation Process
When interacting with the shrine, the player is prompted to make an offering. A pop-up window allows donation of almost any item from the inventory. Upon successful donation, chimes move swiftly around the structure. If the player cancels, a message confirms the cancellation.

*   **Cooldown:** If interacted with during cooldown, the shrine displays: "You may only donate to the Shrine once per hour. Come back in x: y."
*   **Bulk Donation:** Players can hold down the "+" button to increase donation amounts at an accelerating rate.

### Favor Mechanic
Favor is a core mechanic that affects rewards from the Wind Shrine. It increases with every donation (5,000 + the favor value of all items donated). **A Spirit Petal is not required to start increasing favor.**

## Wind Shrine Rewards (Standard)

### Field Winds and Wild Windy Bees
Donating certain items grants a chance to summon Field Winds. The probability of high-stack winds increases with the amount donated and player favor.

*   **Color Correlation:** Generally, the color of the item correlates with the color of the field summoned.
*   **Purple Potion:** Recommended for boosting, as it biases toward large stacks in red or blue fields.
*   **Eggs:** Rarer eggs tend to grant better/more winds across most fields.
*   **Swirled Wax:** Tends to give 7–8x white field winds.
*   **Gifted Gold Egg:** Often gives 7–9x winds.
*   **Gifted Diamond Egg:** Often gives 10–11x winds.

The Wind Shrine also indicates if a Wild Windy Bee is active in any fields. If the chimes are continuously moving and no donation has been made recently, a Wild Windy Bee will be somewhere on the map.

### Obtaining a Windy Bee
To obtain a Windy Bee from the shrine, players must first donate a Spirit Petal. After this initial donation, donating a Cloud Vial provides a chance to receive a Windy Bee Egg. The success rate is dependent on the player's favor level.

*   **Success Message:** "And it calls back to you!". The victory jingle plays, and a server-wide chat message reads: "🎉 [Player] has tamed a Windy Bee! 🎉"
*   **Failure Message:** "But it doesn't respond..."

*(Note: Players who purchase the Windy Bee Egg from the Robux Shop receive a Star Treat instead.)*

## Galentine Shrine (Event Specific)

The Galentine Shrine is a special version of the Wind Shrine available during specific Beesmas events. Donating Strawberries offers a chance to receive rewards and the **Galentine's Blessing buff**, which lasts for two days.

**Probability Calculation:**
The odds of receiving the blessing start at 1% with one Strawberry and scale linearly up to 50,000, where it is guaranteed.

$P(x) = \left(\frac{0.99}{49999}(x-1)+0.01\right) \cdot 100\%$
*(Where $x$ is the number of Strawberries donated.)*

## Donation Outcomes (General)

All donations grant Honey and Favor. The specific rewards depend on the item donated:

*   **Spirit Petal:** Grants 500 Treats, 1 Star Jelly, 5 Enzymes, 5 Oils, 5 Red Extracts, 5 Blue Extracts, 5 Tropical Drinks, and 5 Magic Beans (only once).
*   **Field Wind Buffs:** Summoning clouds on fields and granting field winds. Certain items bias the wind color (e.g., Diamond Eggs/Blue Extracts favor blue fields).
*   **Wild Windy Bee Spawn:** A sudden breeze sweeps the item into the sky, followed by a message indicating "Wild Windy Bee is in one of the fields."
*   **Bean Bug Spawn:** The items vanish and pop right before your eyes.

## Trivia and Notes

*   The Wind Shrine is based on a percussion instrument: the wind chimes.
*   According to Spirit Bear, the Wind Shrine is one of the oldest standing monuments in the game.
*   **Donation Restrictions:** Items such as Super Smoothies, Translators, Sprinkler Builders, specific Bee Eggs/Jellies (excluding Honey Bee Egg), Spirit Petals (after first donation), Gingerbread Bears, Snowflakes, Balloons, Planters, Robo Passes, Beequip Cases, and 7-Pronged Cogs cannot be donated.
*   **Error Handling:** If a player attempts to donate with less than 35 bees, they receive an error message. Attempting to donate zero items results in a specific error pop-up.
*   If a player disconnects before rewards fade out after a donation, the next donation will grant the last set of rewards and reset the cooldown (unless at least one reward was collected).
