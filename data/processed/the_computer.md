The Computer was a green screen located between the Windy Bee Gate and the Red HQ. It served as the core location for the Ready Player Two event. The structure features an ellipsis (...) written on it and can only be interacted with while wearing Strange Goggles. Due to changes in the Noob Shop, players must now already possess the Strange Goggles item to interact with The Computer.

## Interior Mechanics

When a player interacts with The Computer while equipped with Strange Goggles, Digital Bee (NPC) appears. Digital Bee provides the player with several puzzles that must be completed sequentially to earn the How to Program BASIC cosmetic hat.

Upon successfully completing all of Digital Bee's assigned puzzles, the player gains access to the computer's interior. Inside, the player receives a Star Jelly and a Robo Pass (a reward only available during Beesmas 2022). The How to Program BASIC cosmetic floats in Digital Bee's original spot upon completion. While inside the Computer, the music track "overflowin4" plays, which is also heard in the 30 Bee Zone and 35 Bee Zone.
