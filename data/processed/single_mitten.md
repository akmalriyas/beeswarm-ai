# Single Mitten

![Single Mitten](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/ee/Single_Mitten.png/revision/latest?cb=20230409130751)

**General Information**
*   **Level:** 6
*   **Color:** Any
*   **Limit:** 3
*   **Description:** "This winter-themed mitten is missing its partner."
*   **Category:** Beesmas (Winter Themed)

A Single Mitten is a Level 6 Beequip. It has an equip limit of three and requires no specific color. It can only be equipped to Rare, Epic, or Legendary bees.
