# Aged Gingerbread Bear

[![Gingerbread Bear](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ae/Gingerbread_Bear.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041818)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ae/Gingerbread_Bear.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041818)

*"This cookie has aged an entire year or more! Gives 500 bond to the fed bee and has a 1.1% chance to turn that bee Gifted."*

> Not to be confused with [Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear").

An **Aged Gingerbread Bear** is a type of [Treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") introduced in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates").

### Mechanics and Stats
When fed to a bee, an Aged Gingerbread Bear:
*   Increases the bee's [Bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") by 500.
*   Has a 1.1% chance of turning the bee Gifted.

Unused [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") will transform into Aged Gingerbread Bears after the next [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas") begins. These aged treats cannot be used to purchase items from [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog").

The formula for calculating the chance of turning a bee Gifted is:
$$([1 - 0.989^x] \times 100)\%$$
Where $x$ is the total number of Aged Gingerbread Bears fed to that specific bee.

### Acquisition
The only way to obtain Aged Gingerbread Bears is by saving regular [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") until the start of a new Beesmas event.

### Trivia
*   Along with [Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") and [Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat"), Aged Gingerbread Bears are one of the few items capable of turning [Event bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event") Gifted, as they lack favorite treats.
*   Aged Gingerbread Bears do not count toward quests that require feeding regular [Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") to bees (e.g., Bee Bear's "Snow Cub Reformation (2/5)" quest).
*   If a free Gingerbread Bear was obtained during the Retro Swarm Challenge before Winter Beesmas 2024, it immediately aged upon the start of Beesmas, regardless of whether a full year had passed.
*   Feeding 100 Aged Gingerbread Bears to a single bee does not guarantee a Gifted status; the probability is approximately 66.9%.
