# Bubble Bee

**Bubble Bee** is an Epic Blue bee. It originated in the ocean as a larva and has a fondness for blue flowers, which remind it of home.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Epic |
| **Color** | Blue |
| **Energy** | 20 |
| **Speed** | 16.1 |
| **Attack** | 3 |

***

## Statistics and Traits

Bubble Bee's favorite treat is Blueberries. It enjoys the Blue Flower Field and Pine Tree Forest, but dislikes the Strawberry Field.

### Base Collection Rates
*   **Pollen:** Collects 10 Pollen in 4 seconds (2.5 per second).
*   **Honey:** Makes 160 Honey in 4 seconds (40 per second).

### Stat Bonuses
*   +15% Movespeed
*   +80 Convert Amount
*   +2 Attack
*   🌟 **Gifted Hive Bonus:** x1.25 Bubble Pollen collection.

## Abilities

**Blue Bomb:** Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding blue Flowers (+10% pollen per level). Combining this ability with other Bombs increases its power.

**Passive: Gathering Bubbles:** There is a 35% chance (50% if Gifted) to spawn a bubble when gathering. If any Player touches the bubble, it pops, collecting 4 red / 6 white / 8 blue pollen from 29 nearby Flowers and causing them to grow.

### Trivia
*   Bubble Bee is the blue counterpart of Fire Bee.
*   It emits a bubble effect, a trait also shared with Honey Bee and its gifted variant.
*   Along with Tadpole Bee, Bubble Bee is one of only two bees that possess the Gathering Bubbles passive ability.
*   The Bubble Mask, available in Blue HQ, features a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Bubble Bee.
*   While gathering in the Clover Field, there is an uncommon chance to find a Sitting Green Shirt Bear Sticker.
*   Despite its name, Bubble Bee has no connection to Bubble Bee Man. However, Bubble Bee Man did distribute Bubble Bee Jelly during Beesmas 2018 and Bubble Bee Egg during Beesmas 2020 and Beesmas 2021.
