# Passive Abilities Overview

**Passive abilities**, or simply **passives**, are effects found on certain accessories, amulets, or bees. These abilities can be active all the time or only activate when specific actions are performed as an additional effect of those actions.

Passives granted by an accessory or amulet are displayed near the hotbar. Some passive abilities require the player to perform the same action multiple times; the number displayed on the icon indicates how close it is to activating the ability, and this counter resets upon activation.

Some passives have a cooldown. When in cooldown, the remaining time is displayed on the passive's icon in pale red. No trigger will activate the ability, nor will the trigger counter be increased during cooldown. These abilities also possess an initial cooldown when items are first equipped to prevent immediate bypassing of the cooldown timer.

## Accessory Passives

### Haste Pulser
*Granted by: Cobalt Guard*

**Haste Pulser** activates every 30th [Haste token] collected. When activated, it fires a blue pulse that hops to each blue bee, identical to the effect of a [Cobalt Bee]. If no blue bees are present, nothing happens.

### Focus Pulser
*Granted by: Crimson Guard*

**Focus Pulser** activates every 30th [Focus token] collected. When activated, it fires a red pulse that hops to each red bee, identical to the effect of a [Crimson Bee]. If no red bees are present, nothing happens.

### Bubble Bombs
*Granted by: Bubble Mask or Diamond Mask (if owning Bubble Mask)*

**Bubble Bombs** activates every 25th [Bomb token] collected, with a 2-minute cooldown. When activated, it spawns 25 [bubbles] randomly in the player's current field.

### Diamond Drain
*Granted by: Diamond Mask*

**Diamond Drain** activates every 30th [Blue ability token] collected, with a 45-second cooldown. When activated, it summons a giant diamond that instantly converts 5 million pollen plus the player's total conversion amount. The conversion amount increases by 10% for every Blue bee owned and by 50% for every Gifted Blue bee type owned. It also pollinates 10 (+3 per Blue bee) blue flowers within the field.

### Ignite
*Granted by: Fire Mask or Demon Mask (if owning Fire Mask)*

**Ignite** activates every 15th [Red ability token] collected. When activated, it creates 5 [flames] in a plus shape at the location where the 15th red token was collected.

### X-Flame
*Granted by: Demon Mask*

**X-Flame** activates every 25th Battle [ability token] collected (tokens generated in combat), with a 20-second cooldown. When activated, it creates 29 [flames] in an X shape around the player.

### Coin Scatter
*Granted by: Honey Mask or Gummy Mask (if owning Honey Mask)*

**Coin Scatter** activates every 20th [Mark token] collected, with a 2-minute cooldown. When activated, it converts pollen equal to 300% of the player's total conversion amount and scatters this value into 24 honey tokens around the field the player was last in. These honey tokens last for 1 minute. Each token provides a minimum of 1000 honey, increasing with the converted pollen amount and the player's [Honey From Tokens] stat. Note that these tokens cannot be collected by Token Link but can be collected by Triangulate.

### Gummy Morph
*Granted by: Gummy Mask*

**Gummy Morph** activates every 30 [Gumdrops] used or 10 [Gummy Bee ability tokens] collected (where one Gummy Bee token counts as 3 gumdrops). When activated, the player transforms into a [Gummy Bear], the Gummy Bee glows, and the field is covered in [goo].

*   **As Gummy Bear:** The player gains +75% Goo, +100% Instant Goo Conversion, +24 Movespeed, and +60 Jump Power.
*   **As Glowing Gummy Bee:** For 10 seconds, the bee gains +1000% Gather Amount, +300 Attack, and +200% Movespeed. It also leaves a trail while glowing.

### Coconut Haste
*Granted by: Coconut Clogs or Gummy Boots*

**Coconut Haste** activates whenever the player is hit by a falling [Coconut]. When activated, the player gains one stack of [Haste] and 2 seconds of [Coconut Surge], granting x1.5 Bee Movespeed and +10 Player Movespeed.

### Goo Trail
*Granted by: Gummy Boots*

**Goo Trail** passively covers 5 surrounding flowers with [goo] wherever the player walks on a field. This passive is always active while equipped, and flowers directly behind the player receive more goo than those surrounding them.

### Emergency Coconut Shield
*Granted by: Coconut Canister*

**Emergency Coconut Shield** activates when the player takes damage directly from a [mob], with a 5-minute cooldown. When activated, it summons 5 [Coconuts] in the field the player was last in, grants 100% Defense, and increases Bee Attack by x1.25 for 10 seconds. This shield does not protect against instant kill sources like kill bricks, [Tunnel Bear], or [Cave Monsters].

### Inspire Coconuts
*Granted by: Coconut Canister*

**Inspire Coconuts** activates every 5th [Inspire token] collected, Star Shower's shooting stars, or [Beesmas Lights] collected. When activated, it summons 5 falling [Coconuts] in the field the player was last in.

### Petal Storm
*Granted by: Petal Belt or Coconut Belt (if owning Petal Belt)*

**Petal Storm** activates every 30th [boost token] collected, with a 30-second cooldown. When activated, it fires 30 Petal Shurikens around the player in a spiral pattern. If a shuriken passes through a bee, that bee instantly converts 10,000 pollen, increased by 7% of its conversion amount.

### Combo Coconuts
*Granted by: Coconut Belt*

**Combo Coconuts** activates every 40th [Coconut] the player drops onto a field (whether through items or abilities). The 40th coconut becomes a Combo Coconut. When caught by any player, it is kicked and falls onto either the summoner's field (if caught on the first drop within the blue circle) or another player's field.

Every time it is kicked, it speeds up and grants the kicker stacks of [Coconut Combo]. Bonus stacks are granted based on catching conditions:
*   Default kick gives 1 stack.
*   Catching in a different colored field than where it was last caught grants 1 bonus stack.
*   Between +1 to +4 bonus stacks depending on how long it has been since the coconut was caught in a field with the same color classification (Red/Blue/White/Mixed) as the current field.
*   +10 bonus stacks if the catcher is another player who has not yet caught this specific coconut, and they are in a field with a color classification the coconut has not yet been caught in.

The buff grants between 25% to 75% Unique Instant Conversion, between x1.2 to x2 Pollen, and between +1% to +50% Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, White Pollen, Bee Attack, and Honey From Tokens. If the coconut fails to be caught, it despawns and reapplies the buff to all players who have caught it at least once.

*Note: Combo Coconuts cannot be passed into the [Ant Field]. There is a limit of 50 Combo Coconut kicks before premature termination.*

## Star Amulet Passives

### Guiding Star
*Granted by: Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)*

**Guiding Star** activates every 250th [Boost token] collected, with a 5-minute cooldown. It permanently grants 1.25x capacity. When activated, it summons a Guiding Star over one of the five fields the summoner has collected the least from for 10 minutes. The probability of selection is based on collection amount: the $n$-th least collected field has a $\frac{6-n}{15}$ chance of being chosen.

The star grants x2.5 Pollen, Convert Rate, and Capacity to the summoner, and x1.25 Pollen, Convert Rate, and Capacity to other players in that field. While active, it pollinates 15 (+1 per Gifted Bee Type) flowers every 15 seconds. It disappears if the summoning player leaves the server.

### Star Shower
*Granted by: Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)*

**Star Shower** activates every 40th [Boost] or [Mark] token collected, with a 30-second cooldown. It permanently grants 1.25x capacity. When activated, 10 shooting stars fall onto the field where the player is standing. These stars collect and instantly convert 30 pollen (+50% per Gifted Bee Type) from 5 flowers. Catching a shooting star grants one stack of [Inspire] and instantly converts 1,000,000 pollen, plus an amount equal to 200% of the player's total conversion amount.

### Pop Star
*Granted by: Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)*

**Pop Star** activates every 30th [Boost token] collected, with a 1-minute cooldown. It permanently grants x1.25 Blue Field Capacity. When activated, it summons a Pop Star that provides 10% Instant Blue Conversion and x2 Blue Pollen for 45 seconds.

The Pop Star automatically applies 2 minutes of [Bubble Bloat]. While active, every bubble popped increases the bonus Blue Pollen by 1% (up to x4.5 from x2) and grants 4 seconds of Bubble Bloat (6 seconds if gold, 1 second otherwise). Bubble Bloat boosts Blue Field Capacity and Convert Rate at Hive by up to x6 for up to 2.5 hours. When the Pop Star ends, it spawns several bubbles over the field proportional to the number of bubbles popped.

### Gummy Star
*Granted by: Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)*

**Gummy Star** activates either at a 2% chance when using Gumdrops or on the 75th use after the cooldown ends, with a 1-minute cooldown. It permanently grants x1.25 White Field Capacity. When activated, it summons a Gummy Star that provides 10% Instant White Pollen and Goo Conversion for 45 seconds. Collecting goo during this time causes the star to grow, granting bonus White Pollen and Goo (up to x2).

After 45 seconds, the Gummy Star pops, scattering honey tokens and [Gumdrops] on the field. The total value of these honey tokens is a percentage of the goo collected during the star's duration, equal to 1.5% per gifted colorless bee type on the player's hive. The number of gumdrop tokens increases with the amount of goo collected.

### Scorching Star
*Granted by: Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)*

**Scorching Star** activates every 30th [Boost token] collected, with a 1-minute cooldown. It permanently grants x1.25 Red Field Capacity. When activated, it summons a Scorching Star for 45 seconds, granting 50% Instant Flame Conversion, +5 Conversion Links, and x2 Red Pollen and Convert Rate. Standing near flames causes the star to grow, increasing bonus Red Pollen and Convert Rate (up to x5 from x2) and the number of Conversion Links (up to 30 from 5). When Scorching Star ends, it converts all pollen in the player's bag into Honey.

### Star Saw
*Granted by: Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)*

**Star Saw** activates after using 3 [Stingers]. The third stinger used is refunded, and a Star Saw is summoned for 30 seconds. The saw circles the player, damaging hostile mobs (15% of Total Attack, with accuracy based on average bee level), collecting tokens, and converting 3 pollen (+0.5 per every 100 Total Attack) from 5 flowers ten times per second. It instantly converts all additional gathered pollen.

## Bee Passives

### Gathering Bubbles
*Exclusive to: Bubble Bee, Tadpole Bee, Frogs*

**Bubbles** have a percentage chance of being spawned by certain bees when gathering.
*   Bubble Bees: 35% chance (50% if gifted).
*   Tadpole Bees: 65% chance (85% if gifted).
When a bubble is popped, the audio file `Bubblepop.ogg` plays.

### Gathering Flames
*Exclusive to: Fire Bee, Demon Bee, Spicy Bee's Inferno, Electric Candle Beequip*

**Flames** have a percentage chance of being spawned by certain bees when gathering.
*   Fire Bees: 35% chance (50% if gifted).
*   Demon Bees: 55% chance (75% if gifted).

### Steam Engine
*Exclusive to: Spicy Bee*

**Steam Engine** boosts any Spicy Bee's speed by up to 100% while active, granting [Flame Heat]. While active, the Spicy Bee increases the number of steam particles from its exhaust pipes proportionally with the amount of flame heat.

### Fuzzy Coat
*Exclusive to: Fuzzy Bee*

**Fuzzy Coat** has a chance to pollinate up to 5 surrounding flowers (9 if gifted) when gathering. Lower-tier flowers have a higher probability of being pollinated than higher-tier flowers.

### Nectar Lover
*Exclusive to: Shy Bee*

**Nectar Lover** makes the Shy Bee twice as likely to sip from a [planter] compared to any other bee. When sipping, it collects twice as much nectar and contributes twice as much to the planter's growth.

### Shimmering Honey
*Exclusive to: Diamond Bee*

**Shimmering Honey** grants a 25% bonus honey whenever this bee converts at the hive (+2.5% per level). This bonus is doubled if the bee is Gifted.

### Balloon Enthusiast
*Exclusive to: Buoyant Bee*

When converting from Balloons, this bee gains x3 its normal convert amount. Additionally, its base attack scales up to x3 with [Balloon Blessing].

### Sniper
*Exclusive to: Precise Bee*

In battle, this bee uses slow, long-range, and more accurate shots dealing x2 damage. Accuracy is calculated as if the bee were 1 level higher (2 if gifted), and it cannot fall below 5%. If the shot is a [Critical Hit], reload time is halved; if it's a [Super-Crit], it is reduced by 75%.

### Drive Expansion
*Exclusive to: Digital Bee*

While active in [Robo Bear Challenge], using a [Drive] corrupts the field proportional to the number of flowers matching the Drive's color (Glitched Drives corrupt all fields equally). This passive permanently enhances the bee, increasing Corruption of its abilities and granting specific stats based on the drive type:
*   Red Drive: +0.03 Attack
*   Blue Drive: +2 Convert Amount
*   White Drive: +0.25 Gather Amount
*   Glitched Drive: +0.05% Ability Rate

Every 10 [Glitched Drives] Enhancements grants a Hive Bonus of 0.03% Ability Duplication Chance. Digital Bee can be enhanced with up to 500 of each drive type. Upon maxing out all 2000 drives, Digital Bee gains +10 Movespeed.

## Other Passives

### Unlimited Gumdrops
*Obtained through: Unlimited Gumdrops buff (via codes/Glue Dispenser)*

**Free Gumdrops** allows the player to use their [Gumdrops] without losing them from inventory. Having this buff active does not increase the amount of gumdrops in the player's inventory.
