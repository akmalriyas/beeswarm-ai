# Puffshroom

[![A common Puffshroom.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Puffshroom.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20211226205002)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Puffshroom.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20211226205002)

A common Puffshroom is a passive, spore-generating entity in the game.

**Mechanics and Spawning**
Puffshrooms spawn naturally on the 15th and 45th minute of every hour in random fields, excluding Hive Hub and Challenge Fields (Ant and Retro Swarm Challenges). They can also appear in player planters, with their base level dependent on the planter type.

To defeat a Puffshroom, players must collect pollen around its vicinity until the pollen meter is full. Upon defeat, the Puffshroom drops loot and may release 0 to 4 spores, which generate new Puffshrooms in nearby fields. The first (or main) Puffshroom will last for 5 minutes before naturally despawning; a timer indicating the remaining time is displayed above it.

**Leveling and Rarities**
Puffshrooms have unique leveling mechanics tied to their generation history. A Puffshroom gains a chance to level up if the field it occupies has not been visited by its ancestor. Conversely, if an ancestor Puffshroom has touched the field, the Puffshroom may have a chance of decreasing in level instead. This generational effect lasts for up to 8 generations and cannot be effective once the Puffshroom reaches or exceeds 8 generations before being reset.

As Puffshrooms reach higher levels, their chances of spawning rarer variants increase:

*   **Common:** Level 1+
*   **Rare:** Level 5+
*   **Epic:** Level 8+
*   **Legendary:** Level 10+
*   **Mythic:** Level 12+

## Loot and Rewards

Puffshrooms have daily loot limits, similar to Wild Windy Bee. The drops are specific to each player and cannot be stolen from others.

**Loot Determination:**
The quality of the loot depends on:
1.  The Puffshroom's rarity and level.
2.  The amount of damage dealt by the player during its defeat.

If a player has not contributed enough damage (i.e., did not fill the pollen meter), the Puffshroom will only drop Honey tokens and Treats. More contribution results in more plentiful and rarer rewards.

**Guaranteed Drops:**
*   **Honey:** Increased based on rarity and system bonuses like Honey From Tokens.
*   **Treats:** Increments scale with the Puffshroom's level.

## Puffshroom Blessing

Defeating any Rare+ Puffshroom grants **Puffshroom Blessing** (a buff) to all contributing players. The number of stacks granted depends on the Puffshroom's level and rarity:

*   **Rare:** x0.5 Level
*   **Epic:** x1 Level
*   **Legendary:** x1.5 Level
*   **Mythic:** x2 Level

This stack count is doubled (x2) if the Puffshroom is defeated in a field that has not been visited by its ancestor.

Puffshroom Blessing grants +1% to +10% of the following buffs for 3 hours, with a maximum of 100 stacks:
*   Instant Conversion
*   Pollen
*   Capacity
*   Honey from Tokens

## Strategies and Tips

*   **Spore Tracking:** The clouds of spores indicate where subsequent Puffshrooms will spawn.
*   **Teamwork:** A large team increases the speed at which required pollen can be collected.
*   **Buff Optimization:** Activating as many buffs as possible optimizes collection efficiency.
*   **Recommended Materials (Non-Expensive):** Oil, Tropical Drink, Blue Extract, and Red Extract are recommended for movement/efficiency.
*   **Field Boosters:** Use Red Field Booster, Blue Field Booster, Mountain Top Field Booster, and Coconut Dispenser to enhance spawns.
*   **Efficiency Tools:** Glitter or Field Dice can increase efficiency in relevant fields.
*   **Coconut Haste:** When equipped with Coconut Clogs or Gummy Boots, using Coconuts grants Coconut Haste and an additional stack of Haste, aiding spore tracking.
*   **Pollen Collection Power:** Windy Bee's Tornado Ability is effective for collecting large amounts of pollen from groups.
*   **Field Management:** Setting up Sprinklers near Puffshrooms helps surrounding flowers regrow faster.
*   **Leveling Strategy:** Targeting fields that the Puffshroom's previous eight generations have not visited increases its chance to level up.
*   **Mob Control:** Defeating tough mobs (like Mondo Chick or Coconut Crab) before engaging Puffshrooms prevents bee distraction.
*   **Mass Spawning ("15 Zone Strat"):** A highly effective method for generating large numbers of Puffshrooms is bouncing them between three specific fields (e.g., Cactus, Pumpkin, Pine Tree), with additional players allowing the inclusion of more fields like Rose or Strawberry.

## Trivia and Lore

*   The name "Puffshroom" may be a reference to the plant "Puff-shroom" from the *Plants vs. Zombies* series.
*   Puffshrooms are based on the real-life mushroom, *Calvatia gigantea*, which is known for its rapid spore dispersal.
*   Unlike most mobs, Puffshrooms have no way to be "missed," despite having a level system.
*   They emit faint brown smoke particles intended to represent spores.
*   Bee Bear mentions that Puffshrooms are an invasive species, suggesting they may have been accidentally introduced by Dapper Bear (similar to other accidental introductions in the game).
*   Puffshroom is unique as it is not based on an animal and requires pollen to be defeated (Sprouts do not count as a mob).
*   Harvesting certain planters can bypass standard level restrictions; for instance, a level 5 Legendary Puffshroom can spawn from a Blue Clay Planter despite its minimum requirement being Level 10.
*   Defeating higher-tier Puffshrooms counts toward quests that require defeating specific rarities (e.g., killing a Mythic counts as completing the quest requirement for Common, Rare, Epic, or Legendary).
*   All rarity levels have unique spawn sounds; for example, an Epic Puffshroom's sound is lower pitched than a Rare one.
*   Puffshrooms are immune to damage during a Robo Bear Challenge.
*   The developer, Onett, has demonstrated the ability to globally spawn Puffshrooms of any rarity and level, used both for trolling and assisting players with quest progression.
