# Present 2024 (Winter)

Presents returned for the Beesmas 2024 Winter event. While their primary function remained adding [Ornaments](/wiki/Ornaments "Ornaments") to the [Beesmas Tree](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree "Beesmas Tree") when given to NPCs, they could also be gifted to the [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear").

## Acquisition Methods

Presents can be obtained through various means during the event:

*   **Quests:** Multiple quests from Bee Bear awarded a total of 10 [Presents](/wiki/Present "Present"), along with other rewards.
*   **Bee Bear’s Catalog:** The catalog sold 1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present") for 250 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") and 10 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear").
*   **Onett Hive Claim:** [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett") gave 1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present") (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event period.
*   **Galentine Shrine Donation:** Donating [Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry "Strawberry") to the [Galentine Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine#Galentine_Shrine "Wind Shrine") had a chance to give 1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present"), along with other rewards.
*   **Robo Party Rank:** Completing [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party")'s rank 25 for the first time gave 1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present"), along with other rewards.
*   **Event Codes:**
    *   Using the code "LatePresents" granted 2 [Presents](/wiki/Present "Present").
    *   Using the code "LatePresent" granted 1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present").
