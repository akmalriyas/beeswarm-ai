# Daily Top Rose Field Collectors

The **Daily Top Rose Field Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. This leaderboard tracks how much pollen players have collected specifically within the Rose Field on a given day.

## Mechanics and Reset
*   It displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place.
*   The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.
*   Access is restricted to the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page.

## Prizes
Prizes are awarded when the leaderboard resets based on a player's rank:

*   **Top 25:** Players who rank in the top 25 receive a **Rose Field Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who rank in the top 100 receive 25 **Tickets**.
