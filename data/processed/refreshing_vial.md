# Refreshing Vial

[![Refreshing vial icon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d6/Refreshingvial.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042344)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d6/Refreshingvial.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042344)

The **Refreshing Vial** is a consumable nectar vial item, introduced in the 2022-12-26 update. It grants the player 4 hours of the Refreshing Nectar buff instantly upon use. This item can also be used as an ingredient to craft other accessories.

### Stats
*   **Cooldown:** 1 Second
*   **Cap:** 10
*   **Effect:** Grants 4 hours of Refreshing Nectar (Boosts: Red Pollen, Blue Convert Rate, Bee Ability Pollen).

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Ways to Obtain
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 7th and 14th quests rewards 1 **Refreshing Vial**.
*   Using the Nectar Condenser on 12 hours of Refreshing Nectar yields 1 **Refreshing Vial**.
*   Adding the Refreshing Nectar Icon to the Sticker Stack rewards 1 **Refreshing Vial**.
*   Completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas quest rewards 1 **Refreshing Vial** and other items.

### Outdated Ways (No longer available)
*   Purchasing certain bundles from Bee Bear's Catalog:
    *   Tropical Bundle (Summer 2024): Granted 1 **Refreshing Vial**.
    *   Refreshing Bundle (Beesmas 2022): Granted 1 **Refreshing Vial**.
    *   Hefty Honeyday Bundle (Beesmas 2022): Granted 5 **Refreshing Vials**.
*   Purchasing the Nighttime Nectar Pack in the Robux Shop: Granted 1 **Refreshing Vial**.
*   Opening the Tropical Gift Box: Granted 1 **Refreshing Vial**.
*   Completing Onett's Beesmas 2022 Quest: Rewarded 1 **Refreshing Vial**.

## Crafting Uses

The Refreshing Vial can be used in various crafting recipes. The total required amount for all single-purchase items is 6 **Refreshing Vials**.

| Required Vials | Other Ingredients | Result Item |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | 10B Honey, 150 Coconuts, 50 Tropical Drinks, 100 Glues, 100 Oils | Coconut Clogs |
| 2 | 25B Honey, 150 Red Extracts, 150 Blue Extracts | Coconut Canister |
| 3 | 7.5T Honey, 500 Coconuts, 1,500 Tropical Drinks, 200 Purple Potions, 200 Hard Waxes, 3 Turpentines | Coconut Belt |
