# Riley Guard

![Riley Guard](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f8/Riley_Guard.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403020236)

## Overview
The Riley Guard is a piece of armor forged by the leader of Red Bees! It is a guard purchasable in the Red HQ and is equipped on the left shoulder.

### Stats
| Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Capacity | +150,000 |
| Red Pollen | +35% |
| Defense | +10% |
| Critical Chance | +8% |
| Red Bee Attack | +2 |
| Red Bomb Pollen | +10% |

### Cost and Crafting Ingredients
**Purchase Cost:**
*   Honey: 30,000,000
*   Red Extracts: 10
*   Strawberries: 100
*   Glue: 5
*   Stingers: 25

***

## Trivia and Lore
*   The Riley Guard is one of three guards available in the Red HQ; the others are the Elite Red Guard and the standard Red Guard.
*   This guard represents the Riley Bee.
*   It is the highest-tiered guard that can be obtained from the Red HQ.
*   Its blue counterpart is the Bucko Guard.
*   **Historical Note:** Before the 2018-11-25 update, the Riley Guard cost 60,000,000 honey and required no crafting materials.
*   If a player were to craft the necessary ingredients from base resources, it would require 30,000,000 honey, 1,350 strawberries, 750 pineapples, 750 blueberries, and 150 royal jellies.
