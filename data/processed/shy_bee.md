# Shy Bee

**Shy Bee** is a Red Legendary bee known for its introverted nature. Its description states, "This talented bee doesn't like to socialize; it just wants to work and be left alone."

### Characteristics
*   **Rarity:** Legendary
*   **Color:** Red
*   **Energy:** 40
*   **Speed:** 18.2
*   **Attack:** 2

Shy Bee's favorite treat is Strawberries, and it enjoys visiting the Strawberry Field and Pumpkin Patch. It dislikes the Pine Tree Forest.

### Stats & Production Rates

| Stat | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pollen Collection** | 10 Pollen in 2 seconds (Base) | Equivalent to 5 pollen per second. |
| **Honey Production** | 320 Honey in 4 seconds (Base) | Equivalent to 80 honey per second. |

**Stat Bonuses:**
*   +100% Energy
*   +30% Movespeed
*   +50% Gather Speed
*   +240 Convert Amount
*   +1 Attack
*   🌟 **Gifted Hive Bonus:** +5% Bee Ability Pollen.

### Abilities

*   **Red Boost (Ability Token):** Grants x1.2 pollen from red Flowers for 15 seconds. This ability stacks up to 10 times.
*   **Red Bomb (Ability Token):** Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding red flowers (+10% pollen per Level). Combining this with other bombs increases its power.
*   **Passive: Nectar Lover:** Shy Bee is twice as likely to sip Nectar from Planters. When it does, it gathers twice as much Nectar and contributes twice as much growth to the Planter.

**Trivia:**
*   This bee, along with Ninja Bee, Fuzzy Bee, and Windy Bee, is one of the few bees that has only a single skin color.
*   It was previously subject to spelling or grammar mistakes in its description, which were later corrected alongside Hasty Bee, Shocked Bee, and Rascal Bee.
*   Shy Bee is unique among all bees for possessing a smoke effect.
*   This bee serves as the red counterpart to Ninja Bee.
*   A Special Jelly that could transform a bee into a Shy Bee was obtainable via the expired code 'NewMic'.
*   Along with Diamond Bee and Demon Bee, Shy Bee is one of the few legendary bees equipped with a passive ability.
