# Mythic Meteor Shower

A **Mythic Meteor Shower** is a server-wide event introduced in the 2020-06-06 update. It was originally located on the Pro Shop but was relocated to the Badge Bearer's Guild during the 2024-01-12 update.

## Summoning Mechanics
To summon a Mythic Meteor Shower, a player must have discovered at least three different types of Mythic Bees. The event has a cooldown period of 22 hours.

**Player Summon:**
When summoned by a player, the distorted voice of IcedTeaLatte announces "Mythic Meteors," and a server-wide announcement is displayed: `[Username] has summoned a Mythic Meteor Shower! ☄️`

**Onett's Global Summons:**
Occasionally, Onett (the Developer) will summon a global meteor shower. In most cases, he uses a custom alias, resulting in the announcement: `☄️ A Mythic Meteor Shower has been summoned! ☄️`. These events sometimes occur before Beesmas ends.

## Event Details
When activated, a light purple fog appears, and meteors begin raining down on three random fields. These affected fields display white boxes falling from the sky (similar to the Pollen Haze box), indicating where meteors are landing.

*   **Duration:** The event lasts for 3 minutes.
*   **Field Swaps:** During the duration, the three chosen fields will swap locations a total of three times, occurring approximately once every minute.
*   **Prizes and Scaling:** Rewards scale with the size of the meteor drop zone; smaller meteors offer a higher chance of more valuable rewards.

### Pollen Collection
The mechanics for pollen collection vary based on who summoned the event:

1.  **Player-Summoned Shower:** Meteors collect and instantly convert pollen for the summoner. The amount collected is determined by the number of Mythic and Gifted Mythic Bees in the player's hive.
2.  **Caught Meteor (Player Summon):** If a player catches a meteor from a player-summoned shower, it collects twice as much pollen from the field and delivers it to the original summoner.

### In-Game Prompts
*   If a player is on cooldown, the summoning pad will prompt: "Summon a Mythic Meteor Shower ([time remaining])."
*   If an event is currently active and the player steps on the pad (regardless of their personal cooldown status), it will prompt: "A Meteor Shower is in progress."

## Drops
When a player stands on the drop zone of a meteor (indicated by a purple circle), reward tokens are distributed in a line across the field, often leading to another meteor's drop zone.

*   **Token Quantity:** The number of tokens per meteor is determined by the number of Mythic bees in the summoner's hive.
*   **Rarity Scaling:** The rarity of reward tokens increases as the size of the meteor drop zone decreases (rarer items are more common on smaller meteors).
*   **Collection:** Tokens can be collected using token links and other collection methods. Drops are not affected by a player's Loot Luck.

## Trivia and Mechanics
*   **Weather Interaction:** Similar to a Honeystorm or Snowstorm, the purple fog will disappear if the time cycle changes from day to night or vice versa. If a player summons a Honeystorm during a meteor shower, the yellow fog replaces the purple fog until the Honeystorm ends.
*   **Consumable Boosts:** Player-summoned meteors are boosted by field consumables like Glitter and Field Dice, allowing the summoner to collect significantly more Honey.
*   **Diminishing Returns:** Like Wild Windy Bee, the quality of rewards is subject to diminishing marginal utility; participating in multiple Mythic Meteor Showers within a single day results in gradually worse rewards.
*   **Requirements:** This event is one of three summoners that require players to discover specific Mythic bee types (along with the Special Sprout Summoner and Honeystorm). It is the only machine requiring this discovery mechanic.
*   **Audio:** When player-summoned, both the Mythic Meteor Shower and the Honeystorm play IcedTeaLatte's distorted voice announcing the event name.
