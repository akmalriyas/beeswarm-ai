# Shadow Bear

[![An image of Shadow Bear, along with where the Diamond Egg token is.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/36/RobloxScreenShot20230730_140033303_%282%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20230730060756)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/36/RobloxScreenShot20230730_140033303_%282%29.png/revision/latest?cb=20230730060756) [](/wiki/File:RobloxScreenShot20230730_140033303_\(2\).png)

**Shadow Bear** is located inside the [30 Bee Area](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate"), specifically under the platform where [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett") is. It floats just above the floor, similar to [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear"). A ![Diamond Egg](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/70/Diamond_Egg.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404043735)[Diamond Egg](/wiki/Egg#Diamond_Egg "Egg") token is positioned directly in front of it.

**Mechanics and Acquisition**
The floor surrounding Shadow Bear is made of a shadow substance that causes instant death, regardless of the player's [defense](/wiki/System_Page#Defense "System Page"). To reach the Diamond Egg token, players must equip either the [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute") or the [Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider"), and ensure their [bag](/wiki/Bags "Bags") is empty to prevent losing pollen.

*   **Using the Parachute:** Go up to where the ramp meets the platform. Jump sideways and immediately deploy the parachute. Head directly toward Shadow Bear until you can see the sparkle of the token.
*   **Using the Glider:** Jump off the ledge where the ticket token is located, then glide straight towards the Diamond Egg token.

While both methods eventually result in death due to the floor hazard, the player successfully acquires the diamond egg and keeps it in their inventory. The Parachute method may be easier as its slower speed allows for better focus on the target location compared to the Glider.

## Trivia

*   **Lag Exploits:** In laggy servers, players have reported being able to walk on the floor and grab the Diamond Egg token without dying.
*   **Patched Method:** Prior to the 2021 Beesmas Event, a method existed where players could rapidly jump on the floor and collect the Diamond Egg without dying; this exploit has since been patched.
*   **Size and Appearance:** Shadow Bear is the largest of all [bears](/wiki/Category:Bears "Category:Bears"). It resembles [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear") but is significantly larger, and it does not pursue players.
*   **Functionality:** Unlike other bears (such as [Noob Bear](/wiki/Noob_Bear "Noob Bear"), [Pro Bear](/wiki/Pro_Bear "Pro Bear"), and [Top Bear](/wiki/Top_Bear "Top Bear") which run shops), Shadow Bear has no active function; it does not offer quests, nor does it operate a shop or protect an area.
*   **Emotes:** Shadow Bear, along with [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear"), all [Cub Buddies](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy"), and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug"), are the only NPCs that use a dance emote (specifically /e dance3). Other bears can perform the /e wave emote.
*   **Faceless Bears:** Shadow Bear, [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), and [Snowbear](/wiki/Snowbear "Snowbear") are the only faceless bears in the game.
*   **Lore Connection:** It is speculated that Shadow Bear could be the Giant Bear alluded to by [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear") during his "Solo On The Stump" quest.
