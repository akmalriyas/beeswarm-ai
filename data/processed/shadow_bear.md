# Shadow Bear

[![An image of Shadow Bear, along with where the Diamond Egg token is.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/36/RobloxScreenShot20230730_140033303_%282%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20230730060756)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/36/RobloxScreenShot20230730_140033303_%282%29.png/revision/latest?cb=20230730060756) [](/wiki/File:RobloxScreenShot20230730_140033303_\(2\).png)

Shadow Bear is located inside the [Bear Gate](/wiki/Bear_Gate), specifically under the platform where [Onett](/wiki/Onett) is situated in the 30 Bee Area. It floats just above the floor, similar to [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear). A [Diamond Egg](/wiki/Egg#Diamond_Egg) token is positioned directly in front of it.

**Danger Warning:** The floor surrounding Shadow Bear is composed of a shadow substance that results in instant death, regardless of the player's defense level.

### Obtaining the Diamond Egg Token

To reach the egg token safely (or semi-safely), players must equip either the [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute) or the [Glider](/wiki/Glider). It is crucial to ensure your [bag](/wiki/Bags) is empty before attempting this, as carrying pollen risks losing it upon death.

**Method 1: Using the Parachute**
Go up to where the ramp meets the platform. Jump sideways and immediately deploy the parachute. Navigate directly toward Shadow Bear until you can see the sparkle of the token.

**Method 2: Using the Glider**
Jump off the ledge where the ticket token is located, and then glide straight towards the diamond egg token.

Regardless of the method used, death is inevitable in this area; however, the Diamond Egg token will be retained in your inventory upon dying. The Parachute method may be easier as its slower speed allows for better focus on locating the token.

## Trivia

*   **Lag Exploits:** In laggy servers, it is possible to walk onto the floor and grab the diamond egg without immediate death.
*   **Patched Method:** Before the 2021 Beesmas Event, players could rapidly jump on the floor and collect the Diamond Egg token without dying. This method has since been patched.
*   **Size and Appearance:** Shadow Bear is the largest bear in the game. It resembles [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear), but it is significantly larger and does not pursue players.
*   **Functionality:** Shadow Bear is unique among bears as it lacks typical functions: it provides no quests, runs no shop, and does not protect an area. (Note: While [Noob Bear](/wiki/Noob_Bear), [Pro Bear](/wiki/Pro_Bear), and [Top Bear](/wiki/Top_Bear) also lack specific protective functions, they do operate shops.)
*   **Emotes:** Shadow Bear, along with [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear), all [Cub Buddies](/wiki/Cub_Buddy), and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug), are the only NPCs that utilize a dance emote (specifically /e dance3). Other bears can perform the /e wave emote.
*   **Faceless Bears:** Shadow Bear, [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear), and [Snowbear](/wiki/Snowbear) are the only faceless bears in the game.
*   **Lore Connection:** There is a possibility that Shadow Bear could be the Giant Bear alluded to by [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear) during his "Solo On The Stump" quest.
