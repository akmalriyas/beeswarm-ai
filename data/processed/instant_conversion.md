# Instant Conversion

**Instant Conversion** is a core mechanic that instantly converts some of the player's collected pollen into honey. The amount converted depends on the player's total Instant Conversion percentage, supported by various specific conversion types (white, red, blue, etc.). This mechanism effectively increases the player's storage capacity, allowing them to collect more pollen than their bag would normally hold.

The **Honey Per Pollen** stat boosts the amount of honey received from instant conversion. Furthermore, there are color variants for each flower type (blue, red, white) that only instantly convert pollen matching their respective colors.

## Instant Conversion Variants

There are eight distinct types of Instant Conversion:
*   Flame Conversion
*   Field Conversion (e.g., Field Wind)
*   Colored Conversion
*   Goo Conversion
*   Bomb Conversion
*   Unique Instant Conversion
*   Instant Tool Conversion
*   Bee Gather Conversion

### Goo Conversion
Goo Conversion converts a flat amount of pollen equal to a percentage of the **Goo** collected from a flower.

### Unique Instant Conversion
Unique Instant Conversion is applied independently of all other Instant Conversion types. For example, if a player possesses two different sources of 50% Unique Instant Conversion, each source is applied separately, resulting in an effective 75% conversion (not 100%).

## Conversion Order

Instant Conversion applies in the following sequence:
1.  Colored Conversion
2.  Field Conversion
3.  Regular Instant Conversion
4.  Goo Conversion

The placement of Unique Instant Conversion and Ability-specific Instant Conversion within this order is unknown. For instance, if a player has 50% from four different conversion variants and collects 10,000 red pollen, 50% is converted, then 50% of the remaining amount, and so on for each variant.

## Increasing Instant Conversion

Instant Conversion can be increased by various items and buffs:
*   **Enzymes:** +10%
*   **Tropical Drinks:** +10%
*   **Super Smoothies:** +20% (Overwrites effects from Enzymes and Tropical Drinks)
*   **Cactus Badge:** +5%
*   **Gifted Bee / Photon Bee:** +5%
*   **Port-O-Hive:** 5%
*   **Coconut Canister / Porcelain Port-O-Hive:** +10%
*   **Star Amulet:** Up to 12%
*   **Moon Amulet:** Up to 5%
*   **Festive Blessing Buff:** 100%

The maximum Instant Conversion is 157%, not accounting for codes.

### Specific Conversion Boosts:
*   **Instant White Conversion:** Increased by amulets, the Pineapple Badge, Gifted Windy Bee, or Coconut Canister.
*   **Instant Red Conversion:** Increased by the Mushroom Badge (up to 15%), Gifted Crimson Bee (15%), or Demon Mask (20%).
*   **Instant Blue Conversion:** Increased by the Blue Flower Badge (up to 15%), Gifted Cobalt Bee (15%), or Diamond Mask (15%).
*   **Instant Field Conversion (Field Winds):** Granted by donating to the Wind Shrine or through certain codes.
*   **Goo Conversion:** Increased by Shell Amulet, Gummy Mask, Gummy Morph, or Gummy Star.
*   **Instant Flame Conversion:** Granted by Fire Mask, Demon Mask, and Scorching Star.
*   **Instant Bomb Conversion:** Granted by Festive Mark and Gifted Demon Bee.
*   **Unique Instant Conversion:** Granted by Coconut Belt, Clouds, Puffshroom, Beequip, Refreshing Nectar, and Bloom.
*   **Instant Bee Gather Conversion:** Granted by the Pink Jelly Bean buff.
*   **Instant Tool Conversion:** Granted by the Teal Jelly Bean buff.

## Effects

When instant conversion is active and pollen text is enabled, players will see yellow numbers alongside blue/red/white pollen counts while working in fields. These yellow numbers indicate the amount of pollen that instantly became honey. Collected honey amounts also display in yellow, visible in the bottom right corner of the screen.

The formula for the effective increase in capacity provided by instant conversion is: $(x / (100 - x))$, where $x$ is the player's total instant conversion percentage.

## Trivia

*   The Festive Blessing buff from a Festive Bee's Festive Gift token or Festive Sprouts grants 100% Instant Conversion temporarily. If the player has a full container with 100% Instant Conversion, collecting pollen will not transfer into honey.
*   Gummy Morph also grants 200% Goo Conversion while covering the entire field in goo.
*   The Ant Challenge Buff grants 100% instant Ant Field Conversion.
*   Pollen collected from the Hub Field is instantly converted.
*   Super-Crits and Golden Bubbles instantly convert all pollen they collect.
*   Tadpole Bee previously offered 25% Instant Bubble Conversion before the May 23, 2024 update.
