# Honeysuckle

[![Honeysuckle](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/21/Honeysuckle.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031718)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/21/Honeysuckle.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031718)

> "Automatically makes bees instantly convert some Pollen into Honey when your bag becomes full. (Cooldown 30s)"

**Cooldown:** 30 seconds

The **Honeysuckle** is a consumable inventory item introduced in the [2021-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2021-12-26 "Updates"). It functions as an automatic conversion tool: when a player's pollen container is full, and the Honeysuckle is active, it causes the bees to instantly convert some of the collected [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in their bag into [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey").

**Mechanics:**
*   Honeysuckles are automatically used once a player's pollen container reaches capacity.
*   It is the only consumable item with an auto setting that cannot be placed in the hotbar.
*   The item has a 30-second cooldown before it can activate again.
*   Players can toggle its automatic function on or off via the inventory interface.

## Ways to Obtain

Honeysuckles can be acquired through various methods:

**Drops & Rewards:**
*   As a rare drop from [Ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug").
*   From [planters](/wiki/Planter "Planter"): Planters in the Blue Flower Field, Rose Field, and especially Sunflower Field yield Honeysuckles more frequently. The [Blue Clay Planter](/wiki/Blue_Clay_Planter "Blue Clay Planter") drops them as a bonus item.
*   As a reward from the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge").
*   As a common reward from the [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge").
*   From various Memory Match challenges: [Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match"), [Mega Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Mega_Memory_Match "Memory Match"), [Night Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Night_Memory_Match "Memory Match"), or [Extreme Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Extreme_Memory_Match "Memory Match").
*   As a drop from [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party") mobs.

**Stack Rewards:**
Honeysuckles can be obtained as stack rewards for adding specific stickers to the Sticker Stack:
*   Small Tickseed (10 Honeysuckles)
*   Small White Daisy (10 Honeysuckles)
*   Small Pink Tulip (10 Honeysuckles)
*   Small Dandelion (10 Honeysuckles)

**Purchasing & Locations:**
*   Using the [Samovar](/wiki/Samovar "Samovar") grants 3 to 30 Honeysuckles. The starting amount is 3, and it increases by one with each use, capping at 30.
*   A Honeysuckle token can be found on the roof of [Dapper Bear's Shop](/wiki/Dapper_Bear%27s_Shop "Dapper Bear's Shop"), granting 25 Honeysuckles.

## How to Use

For a Honeysuckle to successfully activate and convert pollen, three conditions must be met:
1.  The item must be active in the inventory (turned ON) and not currently on cooldown.
2.  The player's bag must be full of pollen.
3.  More pollen must be collected by the bees.
