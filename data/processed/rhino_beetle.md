# Rhino Beetle

The **Rhino Beetle** is a mob found in several starter fields, serving as the blue variant of the Ladybug. It is one of the first mobs players encounter and defends four different zones.

## Stats and Mechanics

| Attribute | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 5 |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Health | 10 | 16 | 22 | 34 |
| Damage | - | - | - | 18 |

**Rewards:**
*   **Battle Points (BP):** 1
*   **Bond:** 5
*   **Honey:** Approximately 75 (spread across 4 tokens)
*   **Level Range:** 1 to 5, depending on the field it spawns in.

**Respawn Timer:**
The standard respawn time is 5 minutes. This can be reduced to 4 minutes and 15 seconds with a Gifted Bee's hive bonus.

## Locations

## Combat Strategies

Rhino Beetles generally follow standard mob behavior: they pause to aim before lunging. If the player stops while the beetle is aiming, it will attack the spot where the player was previously located, not their current position. Players can utilize environmental cover in certain fields, such as the bamboo stalks in Bamboo Field. The Frozen Field Defenders Glitch is noted as an effective tactic against this mob.

## Trivia

*   The respawn timer starts at 5 minutes, but there is usually a brief delay (5-10 seconds) before it becomes active again after the timer expires.
*   Rhino Beetles are one of the most common mobs and guard four fields.
*   A mob similar to the Rhino Beetle is the King Beetle. According to developer Onett, the King Beetle is a hybrid created from a Ladybug and a Rhino Beetle.
*   It possesses the lowest starting health among all mobs (10 Health at Level 1).
*   This mob is noted as one of Onett's favorite mobs.
