# Gifted Bees

A bee is identified as "Gifted" when a yellow hexagon appears around its hive slot.

## Mechanics and Acquisition

**Gifted bees** are a special variant of standard bees that possess improved statistics (x1.5 multiplier to collection, convert rate, and attack) and receive a unique "Gifted Hive Bonus." This enhancement improves the player's performance, similar to game badges.

### Chance-Based Transformation
*   **Royal Jelly/Eggs:** Every time a player uses Royal Jelly or hatches an Egg, there is a 0.4% (1 in 250) chance of obtaining a gifted bee.
*   **Treats:** Feeding a bee its favorite treat offers various chances to become gifted:
    *   Rare Bees: 0.0125% (1 in 8,000)
    *   Epic Bees: 0.01% (1 in 10,000)
    *   Common/Legendary Bees: 0.0083% (1 in 12,000)
    *   Mythic Bees: 0.00417% (1 in 24,000)

### Guaranteed Transformation Methods
Certain items guarantee the transformation into a gifted bee:
*   **Star Treats:** Always transform the chosen bee into its gifted version. This is the only method that can gift Event bees (like Bear Bee and Tabby Bee), excluding gingerbread bears. If fed to an already gifted bee, it only grants bond experience, no extra benefits.
*   **Star Eggs:** Will always hatch into a non-event gifted bee, unless the player already owns all non-Event gifted variants in their hive; in that case, it hatches into a random non-Event gifted bee.
*   **Star Jelly:** Always transforms the chosen bee into a random non-Event gifted bee, using the same odds as Royal Jelly (with the exception of Basic Bees).

### Gingerbread Bear Transformations
*   **Gingerbread Bear:** Has a 1% (1/100) chance of turning a bee into its gifted version.
*   **Aged Gingerbread Bear:** Has a 1.1% (1/90.909091) chance of turning a bee into its gifted version.

### Tracking Gifted Bees
Gifted bees are identifiable by the "⭐" icon in the bee menu:
*   **Black Star:** The player currently owns this gifted variant in their hive.
*   **Faded Star:** The variant has been discovered but is not yet in the hive.
*   **No Star:** The variant has not yet been discovered.

## Effects of a Gifted Bee

A gifted bee features a unique color scheme, stained wings, and a star on its back and sides. It also gains several mechanical benefits:

*   **Base Stat Boosts:** +x1.5 to Gather Amount, Convert Rate, and Attack.
*   **Appearance:** A glowing gold hexagon appears around its hive slot.
*   **New Ability Token:** Gains the **[Inspire]** token, which doubles pollen gained for 5 seconds and stacks up to 50 times (up to a x51 bonus). Gifted bees can also generate ⭐ Gifted Ability Tokens if they possess one.
*   **Royal Jelly Dispenser Bonus:** Each gifted bee allows the player to claim an additional royal jelly from the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser in the Star Hall.

## Trivia

*   Since Bear Bee is now purchasable from the Robux Shop, players who purchased a Bear Bee during the game launch event had their bee automatically converted into a First Edition gifted Bear Bee. This makes Bear Bee the only Event bee that can be gifted without using a Star Treat or Gingerbread Bears.
*   Multiple gifted bees of the same type do not stack their hive bonus multiple times, nor do they count towards the number of unique gifted bee types in the hive. However, they still retain all improved base stats over their normal version.
*   Using Royal Jelly or a non-gifted Egg on an existing gifted bee will almost certainly cause the bee to lose its gifted status. The chance for the new resulting bee to be gifted is 1/250.
*   Windy Bee is unique in that it provides two different gifted hive bonuses.
*   Tadpole Bee previously provided two different gifted hive bonuses before the May 23, 2024 update.
