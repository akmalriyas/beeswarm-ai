# Free Robo Pass Dispenser

The **Free Robo Pass Dispenser** is a dispenser located in the 30 Bee Zone, positioned directly opposite the Nectar Pot. It allows players to obtain a free Robo Pass for participation in the Robo Bear Challenge.

## Mechanics and Usage

*   **Item Granted:** 1 Robo Pass
*   **Requirement(s):** Access to the 30 Bee Zone (Bear Gate).
*   **Cooldown:** 22 hours.
*   **Location:** Near the entrance of the 30 Bee Zone (Bear Gate).
*   **Inventory Limit:** Players cannot use this dispenser if they already possess 10 or more Robo Passes.

> *Note: This should not be confused with the standard Robo Pass Dispenser, which requires tickets.*

## Trivia and Game Lore

*   This dispenser shares a 22-hour cooldown with the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser and the Glue Dispenser, making it tied for the longest cooldown among all dispensers in the game.
*   It is one of only two free dispensers (alongside the Free Ant Pass Dispenser) that does not grant any buffs when used.
*   Several dispensers do not grant Haste upon use; this dispenser falls into that category alongside the Robo Pass Dispenser, Ant Pass Dispenser, Free Ant Pass Dispenser, Coconut Dispenser, and Glue Dispenser.
*   Unlike many other machines, this dispenser (along with the Robo Pass Dispenser, Ant Pass Dispenser, and Free Ant Pass Dispenser) does not feature visible faucets; instead, it uses a small compartment resembling a vending machine or ATM.
*   Previously, there was a free Robo Pass token located behind the dispenser, but this has since been replaced by the Broken Drive.
