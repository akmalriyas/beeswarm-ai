# Buoyant Bee

**Buoyant Bee** is a Blue Mythic bee, known for its cheerful demeanor and ability to float above the competition.

*"Just like a balloon, nothing can keep this bee down. It's always ready to party."*

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Mythic |
| **Color** | Blue |
| **Energy** | 60 |
| **Speed** | 14 |
| **Attack** | 4 |

Buoyant Bee's favorite treat is Blueberries. It thrives in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Mountain Top Field, and Coconut Field, showing no dislike for any field type.

## Statistics & Production

### Base Stats
*   Collects 15 Pollen in 5 seconds (Equivalent to 3 Pollen per second).
*   Makes 150 Honey in 3 seconds (Equivalent to 50 Honey per second).

## Abilities

**Blue Bomb:** Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding Blue Flowers (+10% pollen per Level). Combos with other bombs to increase power.

**Inflate Balloons:**
*   Increases the pollen in existing Balloons on your field by 5% (+0.01% per Level).
*   Spawns a Blue Balloon for 20 seconds (+2s per level) with capacity scaling based on bee level and player capacity.
*   Pollen collected from flowers near (shaded ones) the balloon is increased by 10% (20% if blue) and added to the balloon instead of your container.
*   Upon filling or timing out, the balloon returns to the Hive. Converting a balloon at the hive grants bonus Capacity and Honey at Hive for 1 hour.
*   Standing near a balloon provides a small stackable bonus (up to 10 times) to Pollen, Blue Pollen, and Honey from Tokens.
*   **Gifted Bonus:** Has a 5% chance (+0.5% per level, doubled when the field is boosted) to summon a Gold Balloon instead, which has x2 Capacity and an additional x1.25 Pollen.

**🌟 Gifted Ability: Surprise Party:**
*   Summons a Gold Balloon (+1 every 5 levels) for all players.
*   Causes all Balloons to spawn random ability tokens for a few seconds.
*   Gold Balloons gain bonus pollen (increases with bee level) and transform nearby Bubbles into Gold Bubbles, which collect 50% more pollen and have a chance to spawn Honey Tokens.
*   If triggered within the last 8 minutes, it performs "Inflate Balloons" instead (always spawning a Gold Balloon).
*   A server-wide message announces: "🎉Surprise Balloons from [Player's Display Name]."

**Passive: Balloon Enthusiast:**
*   When converting from Balloons, this bee has x3 convert amount.
*   The bee's base attack scales up to x3 when affected by Balloon Blessing.

## Trivia & Lore

*   Buoyant Bee, Digital Bee, Windy Bee, and Festive Bee are the only bees capable of granting boosts to all players on the server.
*   Commander Bee and Frosty Bee are two other hat-wearing bees; Buoyant Bee is the only non-Epic hat-wearing bee.
*   This bee is unique in that it does not dislike any field, a trait attributed by Onett (Developer) to its "happy" nature.
*   Buoyant Bee and Lion Bee possess the highest amount of finite Energy.
*   Along with Precise Bee and Fuzzy Bee, Buoyant Bee was one of the Mythic bees added after the original three Mythics.
*   Buoyant Bee and Precise Bee were introduced in the same update as their respective Robux packs.
*   Buoyant Bee and Windy Bee are the only bees that can produce a White Boost token; however, Buoyant Bee requires being gifted to generate them via Surprise Parties.
*   Due to the Balloon Enthusiast passive, if the bee has significant Balloon Blessing, its attack power matches that of Lion Bee.
*   If a player is not in a field when another server member activates a Surprise Party, the subtitle appears but no Golden Balloons are summoned.
*   The gifted form resembles Bumble Bee's appearance. Both Buoyant Bee and Bumble Bee are unique for having gifted abilities that multiply a player’s base capacity.
*   This bee was previously able to summon Blue Bomb+ tokens but was nerfed to Blue Bomb in the 2022-12-26 update.
