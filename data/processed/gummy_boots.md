# Gummy Boots

![Gummy Boots](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fb/Gummy_Boots.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20181128213854)

## Description
"Squishy boots that leave a trail of Goo wherever you go."

The Gummy Boots are a pair of boots added in the 2018-11-25 update. They can be purchased in the Gummy Bear's Lair.

## Stats and Effects
Gummy Boots provide the following bonuses:

*   **+25% Goo**
*   **+20% Pollen**
*   **+3 Bee Attack**
*   **+50% Honey From Tokens**
*   **+9 Player Movespeed**
*   **+22 Jump Power**
*   **+16 Movement Collection**

### Passive Abilities
The Gummy Boots possess two passive abilities:

*   **Goo Trail:** Wherever the player walks, this ability covers 5 surrounding flowers in Goo.
*   **Coconut Haste:** Activates whenever the player catches a falling coconut. The player gains a stack of haste and activates a 2-second Coconut Haste Surge boost, granting x1.5 Bee Movespeed and +10 Player Movespeed. (The player does not need Coconut Clogs for this passive.)

## Trivia and History
*   These boots were the first item to feature a passive ability, as Coconut Clogs were not yet in the game when they were released.
*   They are one of only two items (along with the Coconut Canister) that grant passives regardless of whether the player owns Coconut Clogs.
*   Initially, the Goo Trail passive covered only one flower directly underneath the player; this was later buffed to cover the nearest 5 flowers.
*   The initial crafting price for these boots included 999 Glue, 300 Glitter, 300 Red Extracts, and 300 Blue Extracts. This price was subsequently nerfed on the 2021-12-26 update to the current rate (500 Glue, 250 Glitter, 250 Red Extracts, 250 Blue Extracts).
*   The Gummy Boots were necessary for players who wished to give a present to Gummy Bear during his Beesmas quest.
*   Before the 2021-12-26 update, they were the most expensive item in terms of honey, excluding hive slots.
