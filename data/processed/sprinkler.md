# Sprinklers

Sprinklers are items placed in fields by players to enhance flower regeneration. They function as permanent tools that can be accessed via the eggs/items tab; players activate them by placing the item on the bottom middle of their screen or using corresponding hotkeys.

**Usage and Mechanics:**
*   **Placement:** Sprinklers must be placed while standing in flowers within a field. They cannot be placed in the Hub Field.
*   **Tool vs. Item:** Players purchase a permanent *sprinkler builder* tool, which allows them to place physical sprinklers into fields.
*   **Capacity:** Each player has a limited number of active sprinklers they can maintain. If a player attempts to place more than their limit, the oldest sprinkler will be removed.
*   **Persistence:** A placed sprinkler remains in the field until the player who placed it removes it, or until that player dies or leaves the game.
*   **Proximity Restriction:** Players cannot place their own sprinklers within 8 flowers of each other. However, they can place them adjacent to another player's sprinkler. Attempting to place one too close results in a notification: "Cannot place sprinklers this close to eachother."
*   **Glitches:** The proximity restriction can be overridden if the player utilizes the Sprinkler Glitch by placing a sprinkler mid-air.

**Cooldown:** 1 second (for placement/activation)

## Tiers and Acquisition

Sprinklers are available for purchase in the Badge Bearer's Guild, accessible after obtaining 15 badges of any kind. The tier of the sprinkler determines its material color, effectiveness, and capabilities.

| Sprinkler Name | Unlock Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Sprinkler** | Unlocked upon entry to the Badge Bearer's Guild (Cadet Tier) |
| **Silver Soakers** | 10 Hotshot Badges (Tier 2) |
| **Golden Gushers** | 10 Ace Badges (Tier 3) |
| **Diamond Drenchers** | 10 Master Badges (Tier 4) |
| **The Supreme Saturator** | 10 Grandmaster Badges (Tier 5) |

*Note: The Supreme Saturator is an exception to the standard design, featuring a giant golden sprinkler sticking through a neon blue bear head.*
