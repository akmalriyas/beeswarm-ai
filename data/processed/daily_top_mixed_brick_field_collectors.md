# Daily Top Mixed Brick Field Collectors

The **Daily Top Mixed Brick Field Collectors** was, at one point in the game's history, one of 66 available leaderboards. Unlike most other leaderboards, this specific leaderboard did not appear on the System Page or anywhere else within the game interface. It tracked the total amount of [Pollen](wiki/Pollen) collected by players specifically within the [Mixed Brick Field](wiki/Mixed_Brick_Field) during a single day. The data for this leaderboard reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes
If a player managed to reach the top 100 positions on this board by the end of the day, they were awarded 25 [Tickets](wiki/Ticket).

## Trivia
*   This leaderboard, along with other Brick Field collector leaderboards and the [Daily Top Ant Field Collectors](wiki/Daily_Top_Ant_Field_Collectors), was one of the few leaderboards that did not display any information within the game itself.
*   It is noted as being tied for the fourth leaderboard to be removed from the game, alongside other defunct boards such as [Most Commando Captures](wiki/Most_Commando_Captures), [Highest Snowbear Level](wiki/Highest_Snowbear_Level), and [Highest Robo Party Cake Rank](wiki/Highest_Robo_Party_Cake_Rank).
