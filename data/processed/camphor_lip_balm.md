## Acquisition Methods

Camphor Lip Balm can be obtained in the following ways:

*   Purchased from Dapper Bear's Beequip Shop.
*   Awarded as a reward from the Ant Challenge.
*   Rarely dropped by Blue Clay Planter, Red Clay Planter, Heat-Treated Planter, and The Planter Of Plenty.

## Trivia

The design of the Camphor Lip Balm is most likely based on Carmex, a brand of lip balm whose active ingredients include camphor.
