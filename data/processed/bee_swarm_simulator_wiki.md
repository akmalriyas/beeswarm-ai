# Bee Swarm Simulator Wiki Overview

Welcome to the collaborative community wiki for **Bee Swarm Simulator**, a hive-themed ROBLOX game created by [Onett](https://www.roblox.com/games/1537690962/Bee-Swarm-Simulator).

This wiki is open for community editing. As of the last update, we have recorded **446,808** edits across **701 articles** and **27,010 images**.

## News & Updates Log

**April 23, 2026 (Minor Update): Beesmas Extended**
Beesmas has been extended until May 31st. [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear) has received five final quests, rewarding the player with the exclusive [Petal Cub](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins).

**December 27, 2025 (Minor Update): x2 Event**
The Honeyday Event is active: Join the game to gain x2 Pollen and x2 Convert Rate for 48 hours. Additionally, [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear) quests have been released!

**December 25, 2025: Beesmas 2025**
*   **New Mechanic:** [[Bloom](/wiki/Bloom)]. Find Blooms in fields or grow them using the [Bloom Shaker](/wiki/Bloom_Shaker). Catching petals grants various buffs.
*   **Vouchers:** Two new vouchers have been added: The Offline Voucher and Ticket Voucher.
*   **Traveling Bear:** [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear) is available to help prepare for Beesmas by delivering Presents.
*   **Decorations:** Complete honeyday decorations via [[Category:Beesmas_Decorations](/wiki/Category:Beesmas_Decorations)] to unlock special map machines.
*   **Beesmas Tree:** Exchange gifts with the Mountain's inhabitants to obtain boost-granting [Ornaments](/wiki/Ornaments).
*   **Catalog:** [[Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog)] allows players to purchase special limited-time items using Gingerbread Bears and Snowflakes.
*   **Stickers:** Limited time festive stickers are available during Beesmas!

**December 25, 2024 (Minor Update): Christmas Day**
The Honeyday Event is active: Join the game to gain x2 Pollen and x2 Convert Rate for 48 hours. A new Robux Pack, "Royal Winter Wonder Haul," was introduced, featuring the [Nectar Shower Vial](/wiki/Nectar_Shower_Vial) and the [Icy Crowned Hive Skin Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index).

**December 17, 2024: Second Beesmas!**
*   **Winter Spotlight Partnership:** Partnered with the [[Winter Spotlight](/wiki/Winter_Spotlight)] Roblox Event. Players can complete tasks in Bee Swarm to earn currency for the main event (ends January 2nd, 2025). In-game rewards remain obtainable after the Roblox event concludes.
*   **Traveling Bear:** Complete the first three quests from [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear) to receive a Standard Token for Winter Spotlight. Continue his quest line to obtain Presents and a [Festive Planter](/wiki/Festive_Planter).
*   **Elite Tokens:** Open the [Gift Boxes](https://www.roblox.com/games/1537690962) to receive an Elite Token for Winter Spotlight.
*   **Decorations & Catalog:** Similar to Beesmas 2025, decorations and Bee Bear's Catalog are available during this event.

**August 1st, 2024: The Games Roblox Event**
*   **Traveling Bear (Sun Bear):** [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear) returned with limited-time quests for the Roblox event. Completing three quests grants points toward progression in the Roblox event (event ends August 11th).
*   **New Mechanic: Golf:** Introducing a new mechanic where players can hit golf balls into holes and activate targets to receive rewards.

**Other Rules:**
*   Advertising external social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Twitter) or other wikis is prohibited and will result in punishment.
*   Vandalism results in a warning and/or block.
*   Adding fake content or making false claims (including codes) is forbidden.
*   Editing another user's profile without permission requires a warning/reminder.
*   Evading a block leads to longer punishments.
*   Creating or encouraging drama will result in a block. Any discussion post suggesting departure may also be grounds for a block, depending on the reasoning.
*   Cursing is not tolerated; bypasses are disallowed regardless of censoring methods used.

**Warning:** If you receive a warning for breaking any rule, do not delete it, as this will lead to heavier consequences. If you believe your block was unjust or too severe, contact a [staff member](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Wiki:Staff).
