# Royal Jelly Shop

The **Royal Jelly Shop** is a shop located on a rocky hill, situated between the Rose Field and the Pine Tree Forest. It is positioned to the right of and just below the Blue Cannon. Players can purchase Royal Jelly here in exchange for Honey.

*Note: Do not confuse this shop with the Royal Jelly Dispenser.*

## Purchasing Mechanics

The price of Royal Jelly increases as the player purchases more units, up to a maximum cost.

**Initial Cost:** The first unit purchased costs 250,000 Honey.
**Incremental Cost:** Each subsequent unit adds 10,000 Honey to the base price.
**Maximum Cost:** The price eventually caps at 1,000,000 Honey per unit.

The formula for the price of one Royal Jelly (P) based on the amount already purchased (n) is:

$$ P(n) = \begin{cases}250{,}000+10{,}000n & 0 \leq n < 75\\1{,}000{,}000 & n \geq 75\end{cases} $$

Royal Jelly can be purchased in the following increments:
1, 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000, 1,000,000, 10,000,000, or 100,000,000.
