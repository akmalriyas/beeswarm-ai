## Overview

The All-Time Top Nectar Collectors leaderboard is one of the game's extensive leaderboards. It tracks how much nectar players have collected over time. This board is only accessible through the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page.

The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, and players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. To ensure fairness, only legitimate players are maintained on the leaderboards; hackers or exploiters will be removed.
