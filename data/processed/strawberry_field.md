## Description and Ecology
The Strawberry Field is a field located past the Basic Bee Gate, to the left of the Spider Field, and beside the Vicious Bee Egg Claim. This field is protected by two ladybugs (one level 2, one level 3).

When a planter is placed here, it grants Refreshing Nectar. While collecting pollen, this field can spawn Honey, Strawberry, Red Boost, Red Extract, Bitterberry, Ticket, and Royal Jelly tokens.

### Bee Preferences
*   **Likes:** Fire Bee, Riley Bee, Shy Bee
*   **Dislikes:** Cool Bee, Bubble Bee, Bucko Bee, Windy Bee

## Mechanics and Boosting
The field can be boosted using various items and abilities:

*   **Red Field Booster:** Using this booster may grant a Strawberry Field Boost, providing +100% Strawberry Field pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Adding glitter to the field will increase current boost by 100% Strawberry Field pollen for 15 minutes (if not already maxed).
*   **Dice:** Using Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice may add 100%–300% Strawberry Field pollen for 15 minutes (if not already maxed), depending on the dice type used.
*   **Field Winds:** Strawberry Field Winds can be obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes. They provide a base of +20% instant Strawberry Field conversion, and each stack adds +5% instant conversion and +15% pollen, stacking up to 15 times total.
*   **King Beetle Amulet:** If equipped with a Strawberry Field Boost, this amulet can boost the field by +1% up to a maximum of +100% pollen.

### Obsolete Boosting Methods
The following methods for obtaining boosts are no longer available:
*   Giving presents to certain NPCs (e.g., Gifted Riley Bee provided x3 boosts; Onett provided x1 boost).
*   Purchasing Market Boosts from the Boost Market (provided x1.5 Field Capacity, x1.25 Pollen, and x1.25 Convert Rate).
*   Redeeming specific expired codes (e.g., JollyJelly gave x3 boosts and x3 Winds; WeekExtension gave x3 boosts and x10 Winds).

## Trivia
*   This is the third smallest field in the game, following Stump Field and Pumpkin Patch.
*   There is a strawberry token located behind the biggest strawberry in the top left corner of the field.
*   The Strawberry Field is one of six fields that fireflies can travel to during nighttime (the others are Bamboo Field, Cactus Field, Pineapple Patch, Rose Field, Spider Field).
*   Mother Bear states that finding strawberry tokens is significantly more common in this field than in any other.
*   Spirit Bear mentions that the strawberries were imported from Sweden and that sprouts in this field spawn 8 times more strawberry tokens than normal sprouts.
*   A glitch exists where running towards the corner strawberry fast enough can cause a player to get stuck inside it, requiring a character reset or Whirligig use to resolve.
