# Treats

**Treats** are consumable items that grant **Bond** to bees in Bee Swarm Simulator. There are currently twelve different types of treats available.

## Feeding Mechanics

To feed a treat, players must go to their **Hive** and open the item menu. The process involves dragging the desired treat onto the cell of the target bee. Players can then:
*   Specify a number of treats to feed.
*   Use all selected treats on that bee.
*   Feed the exact amount needed to level up the bee (if possible).

If a player feeds a bee its favorite treat, or either **Gingerbread Bear** variant, an "Until Gifted" option becomes available. This feature automatically rolls until the bee achieves the **Gifted Bee** status, similar to using auto-jelly settings. Note that players cannot feed other players' bees.

## Trivia and Lore Notes

*   **Bond Value:** A singular **Star Treat** or **Atomic Treat** grants the most bond (1,000 bond). A single Neonberry or Aged Gingerbread Bear grants 500 bond. Gingerbread Bears and Moon Charms grant 250 bond.
*   **Gifted Chance:** According to developer Onett, the chance of obtaining a Gifted bee from feeding favorite treats varies by rarity:
    *   Rare bees: 1 in 8,000
    *   Epic bees: 1 in 10,000
    *   Legendary and Common bees: 1 in 12,000
    *   Mythic bees: 1 in 24,000
*   **Usage:** Treats, pineapples, strawberries, and blueberries are the only treats that can be obtained from dispensers.
*   **Mechanics Detail:** Onett has stated that bees do not actually eat the treats; instead, they take them as gifts and hide them in fields for others to find.
*   **Historical Note (Bond):** The Star Treat formerly granted 100 bond before being updated to 1,000 bond.
*   **Historical Note (Favorites):** Previously, the Pineapple was the only treat favored by nine bees; currently, Strawberry is the only treat favored by eight bees, as other treats now have nine bee favorites.
*   **Obtainment:** The Sunflower Seed is the only main treat that cannot be accessed through shops or dispensers.
*   **Glitch/Input:** If a player attempts to feed a bee using an input that is not a number, only one treat will be fed.
