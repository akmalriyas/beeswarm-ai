# Obstacle Courses

Obstacle Courses, often referred to as "obbies," are short platforming challenges found on the main map. Each course typically features a reward or a shop at its conclusion. These courses vary in difficulty. They provide players with activities and offer rewards such as [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket), [Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_jelly), or [Star Jelly](/wiki/Star_Jelly). Some obstacle courses also lead to specific [Hats](/wiki/Category:Hats) that can be purchased.

## Mushroom Field Obby

[![The design of the Mushroom Field obstacle course.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d0/Obby1.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20181213175850)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d0/Obby1.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20181213175850)

The Mushroom Field Obby is located in the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field). It is the first obstacle course available to players. While no special items are required, having jump-enhancing gear makes the course easier. The path begins with an invisible wall between the first and second mushroom steps. On the largest mushroom, there are three circular pads (two red and one white), followed by three air circles marked with red mushrooms. At the end of the course, a final mushroom holds a Royal Jelly token.

Players can bypass this obstacle course using a [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute) or [Glider](/wiki/Glider), or any method that achieves sufficient height, such as the [Yellow Cannon](/wiki/Yellow_Cannon).

## Lava Obby

[![The design of the Lava Obby.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/dd/Noobshopobbynoplayer.png/revision/latest?cb=20210224031045)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/dd/Noobshopobbynoplayer.png/revision/latest?cb=20210224031045)

The Lava Obby is hidden behind the [Honey Dispenser](/wiki/Honey_Dispenser) or accessible through a wall hole in the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop). Upon entry, players navigate green blocks surrounded by lava; touching the lava results in instant death. Recommended gear includes the [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute), [Glider](/wiki/Glider), [Propeller Hat](/wiki/Propeller_Hat), or Gummy Boots/Coconut Clogs. Alternatively, rapid jumping upon hitting the lava can keep a player alive until they reach a platform.

The course starts with four blocks in a straight line, leading to a split path.
*   **Forward Path:** Continues toward the shop for the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask). This requires decent jump power to clear a large ledge.
*   **Left Turn Path:** Involves six more blocks with a left turn. The player ascends until reaching a small hole, where they find Royal Jelly inside. Passing through this hole deposits the player on an awning within the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop), allowing them to exit the obby without restarting.

**Bypass Methods:**
1.  Jump and glide from the shelf above the hat and belt to the top of the awning. This requires highly enhanced jumping ability (at minimum, [Beekeeper's Boots](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Boots) plus [Bear Morph](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bear_Morph)). The Glider may be necessary instead of the Parachute.
2.  Stand on top of the [Noob Bear](/wiki/Noob_Bear)'s cash register and hop onto the awning to collect Royal Jelly. Both the Propeller Hat and Beekeeper's Boots provide sufficient jump power for this maneuver.

*Note: During the Egg Hunt 2019, players could obtain a Plastic Egg token by immediately turning right upon entry; this has since been replaced by a ticket token yielding three tickets.*

## Commando Chick's Hideout Obby

[![The location of the Commando Chick's Hideout.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2d/CommandoChickHideout.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200406200050)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2d/CommandoChickHideout.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200406200050)

This obstacle course is situated between the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ) and the [Wealth Clock](/wiki/Wealth_Clock), replacing the former location of the Golden Present Obby. The entrance is a hole in the wall, blocked by vines. Players must use [Clippers](/wiki/Clippers), [Scissors](/wiki/Scissors), or the [Scythe](/wiki/Scythe) to cut the vines; this action permanently removes them.

The course consists of evenly spaced blocks that gradually increase in height, leading the player across to the arena. The [Commando Chick's Hideout](/wiki/Commando_Chick%27s_Hideout) is located past this obstacle course.

## Bamboo Field Obby

[![The design of the Bamboo Field obstacle course.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/13/156149170171928899.png/revision/latest?cb=20190628195042)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/13/156149170171928899.png/revision/latest?cb=20190628195042)

This obstacle course is located between the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field) and the [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field). Players must have at least 5 bees to access it. No special jump-enhancing gear is required to complete the course.

The path begins with nine rocks (alternating brown and gray): three leading from Spider Field to Bamboo Field, three going in the opposite direction, and three returning to the original direction. Following the rocks are three bamboo sticks, with Royal Jelly located on the final stick. The [Moon Amulet Generator](/wiki/Moon_Amulet_Generator) is also accessible via this course.

Players can bypass this obstacle course by parachuting down from the ramp into the 25 Bee Zone or using methods like the [Slingshot](/wiki/Slingshot).

## Dapper Bear's Shop Obby

[![Full view of Dapper Bear's Shop Obby.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/97/Dapper_Bear%27s_Shop_Obby_Image.png/revision/latest?cb=20211229051731)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/97/Dapper_Bear%27s_Shop_Obby_Image.png/revision/latest?cb=20211229051731)

The Dapper Shop Obby is accessed by going to the right wall of [Dapper Bear's Shop](/wiki/Dapper_Bear%27s_Shop) and jumping onto the three protruding planter displays. Two paths are available:

1.  **Right Ledge Path:** Jump onto a gray ledge on the right, then onto the shop roof. Rewards include [Honeysuckle](/wiki/Honeysuckle), Royal Jelly, [Smooth Dice](/wiki/Smooth_Dice) token, and the [Mythic Gift Box](/wiki/Gift_Boxes) (now unavailable).
2.  **Orange Ledge Path:** Jump onto an orange ledge partially covered by a roof, then jump onto two moon platforms to reach a Star Jelly token.

*Note: If the moon platforms are disabled, this section can be skipped using [Hiking Boots](/wiki/Hiking_Boots), [Glider](/wiki/Glider), and a stack of Haste+.* The jump power is sufficient to grab the Star Jelly without hitting the roof. Using [Basic Boots](/wiki/Basic_Boots) is not recommended as they lack the necessary jumping boost, and a Glider is advised for navigating the moon platforms if they are active.

## Cloud Obby

[![The design of the Cloud obstacle course, behind Polar Bear.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/94/Obby4v2.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20200713210535)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/94/Obby4v2.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20200713210535)

This obby is located past the [Honey Bee Gate](/wiki/Honey_Bee_Gate), starting near [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear), and concluding on the tallest tree in [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest). The Parachute or Glider is required to pass.

Players should climb the cliffs to the highest point to the left of Polar Bear. Using a parachute or glider, they jump to the next two cliffs. Turning left, players glide along the tops of four clouds until reaching the entrance to the Diamond Room on the last cloud. A pine tree adjacent to this final cloud holds Royal Jelly above it; players can walk off the cloud into the token to collect it.

**Bypass Methods:**
*   Using the Parachute/Glider and the [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon).
*   Parachuting/gliding down from the 25 Bee Zone for a variable skip.

## Diamond Room

The Diamond Room is located at the end of the Cloud Obby. It leads to a [Glitter](/wiki/Glitter) token, the [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask), and a sky-blue room featuring two moons used for parkour.

**Moon Mechanics:**
*   The moons only become solid if the player possesses a [Moon Amulet] during nighttime.
*   If the player owns the Diamond Mask, the moons are always solid and appear blue.
*   With sufficient speed and the [Glider](/wiki/Glider), players can skip the moon parkour entirely.

## 30 Bee Zone Obby

The 30 Bee Zone obstacle course is located to the right of [Onett](/wiki/Onett). This is the final obstacle course players can reach.

**Mechanics:**
*   During the day, the moons leading up to the Bear Gate are slightly invisible and non-solid, making them inaccessible.
*   At night, if the player has a Moon Amulet, they can parkour up to the Bear Gate. The moon platforms lead to [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man).

**Rewards:**
The course offers a Royal Jelly token in front of Bubble Bee Man and an [Enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes) token on the other side, next to the [Night Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match). (Previously, a Present was located where the Enzymes are now.)

*Note: Following the 2019-12-23 update, Onett added more moons, making this obstacle course easier.*

## Hive Hub Obby

Added during the [2024-07-17](/wiki/Updates#2024-07-17) Beesmas update, this obstacle course is located at the entrance of the [Hive Hub](/wiki/Hive_Hub). The platforms feature large gaps, requiring players to use the [Glider](/wiki/Glider) for every jump. These platforms lead to a [Loaded Dice](/wiki/Loaded_Dice) token, a [Red Balloon](/wiki/Red_Balloon) token, and a [Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear) token (for both 2024/25 Beesmas events).

## Mythic Present Obby (Archived Content)

**This content has been removed from the game.**

The Mythic Present obstacle course was previously located to the left of the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field) and was only accessible during Beesmas 2019 and 2020. Access required passing through the [Brave Bee Gate](/wiki/Brave_Bee_Gate) and heading left into a wall hole. The course consisted of jumping across many purple balls to reach an end platform where the Mythic Present was found.

**Rewards:**
*   Royal Jelly token (left of present): 1 Royal Jelly.
*   Micro-Converter token (right of present): 3 Micro-Converters.

*Note: During Beesmas 2019, exiting the map area where this obby was located and sliding against the wall resulted in player death because the floor remained lethal.*
