# Hiking Boots

![Hiking Boots](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Hiking_Boots.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403022412)

## Description
A durable pair of boots helpful for traversing the mountain.

The **Hiking Boots** are a type of boot that was added in the 2018-09-10 update and can be purchased from the Pro Shop.

## Stats & Cost
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Player Movespeed | +5 |
| Jump Power | +10 |
| Movement Collection | +6 |
| Bee Attack | +1 |

**Cost:** 2,200,000 Honey, 50 Strawberries, and 50 Blueberries.

## Trivia
*   These are the second most expensive item in the Pro Shop (in terms of honey), only behind the Propeller Hat.
*   Along with the Propeller Hat, these boots are required to access Dapper Bear's Shop.
*   They could have been obtained from Sun Bear after completing his tenth quest before he left on 2018-10-08.
*   Before the 2018-11-25 update, this item, Basic Boots, and Beekeeper's Boots did not require any crafting materials.
*   Unlike Gummy Boots and Coconut Clogs, these boots (along with Basic Boots and Beekeeper's Boots) were obtainable by quests.
