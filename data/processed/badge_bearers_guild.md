# Badge Bearer's Guild

[![The Badge Bearer's Guild.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/28/BadgerBearersGuild2024.png/revision/latest?cb=20240122122856)

The **Badge Bearer's Guild** is a shop located near the Pine Tree Forest. Players must possess at least 15 badges of any rank past Junior to gain entry.

### Features and Layout
The main area sells various tools, including five types of sprinklers. Additionally, a Blender is available for crafting certain items from other materials. A Star Jelly token rests on top of the Blender, which requires an accessory that boosts Jump Boost or Bear morph ability to reach it. The Mega Memory Match game can also be found inside this building.

On the opposite side of the sprinkler area, there is a gate requiring 1 Ace Badge to pass through. Behind this gate lies a second shop. This secondary shop sells the Honeycomb Belt, Crimson Guard, Cobalt Guard, and Honey Mask, which are displayed hanging within a large honeycomb structure. Another Star Jelly token is located in front of the honeycomb. A secret glass-like passage behind the honeycomb leads into a void-like area below the map.

Externally, the guild is marked with the Badge icon on top of its entrance and features a large Basic Sprinkler on the roof (which is non-solid and walkable). The Mythic Meteor Shower Summoner also resides on the roof.

## Music
When inside the shop, the audio track "Vendor" plays. This music is also heard in several other shops and locations, including the Noob Shop, Pro Shop, Mountain Top Shop, Blue HQ, Red HQ, Petal Shop, Hive Hub, and the Blue Maze.

## Trivia
*   The Badge Bearer's Guild is one of a few shops that does not have "Shop" in its name (others include Ticket Tent, Red HQ, Blue HQ, Gummy Bear's Lair, and Coconut Cave).
*   Along with the Ace Shop and Gummy Bear's Lair, this location requires players to possess badges for entry.
*   All sprinklers displayed in the shop are solid, except for The Supreme Saturator.
*   The Star Jelly token on the Blender was originally located on the ground in the middle of the shop but moved since the 2018-11-25 update.
*   In the 2018-12-19 update, a Present token was briefly added inside the shop (on top of the Ace Shop's honeycomb) before being removed.
*   Behind the Ace Badge section is a narrow corridor blocked by a transparent barrier, preventing further progression.
