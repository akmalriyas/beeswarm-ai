# Clippers

**Clippers** are a tool in Bee Swarm Simulator.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | 2,200 Honey |
| **Shop** | Noob Shop |

## Pollen Collection Stats

The Clippers collect pollen from flowers based on the size of the flower and the collection pattern (Single).

| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 9 | 18 | 27 | 36 | 45 |
| Red | 9 | 18 | 27 | 36 | 45 |
| Blue | 9 | 18 | 27 | 36 | 45 |

**Collection Details:**
*   **Range:** 1 Flower tile
*   **Collecting Pattern:** Single
*   **Collecting Time:** 0.6 seconds
*   **Base Collection:** 9 pollen per flower (per single collection)
*   **Pollen Rate:** 13.85 pollen per second

## Description and History

The Clippers are a tool that was introduced in the 2018-06-02 update. They collect 9 pollen from one flower tile directly in front of the player every 0.6 seconds, resulting in a base collection rate of 13.85 pollen per second.

**Trivia:**
*   The Clippers are considered the smaller and weaker version of the Scissors, distinguished by their yellow handle color.
*   Along with the Scissors and Scythe, the Clippers are one of three items capable of cutting vines in front of Commando Chick's Hideout.
*   These tools (Clippers and Scissors) are unique in targeting only a single flower at a time.

## Crafting Ingredients

To craft the Clippers, you require:
