## Dialogue & Lore

### General Narrative Summary
The Beesmas event is centered around Bee Bear, a magical mascot who needs the help of beekeepers to spread "Honeyday Cheer" and complete various presents for all creatures on the mountain. The ultimate goal is to craft a special **Festive Bee** (or receive 500 Tickets if one already exists). Bubble Bee Man, an older, wiser mythological figure, is also central to the event's magic and belief system.

### Quest Dialogue Snippets
*   **Beesmas Begins:** "Ho ho ho! Merry Beesmas!! Happy Honeydays!!!... I need you and your bees to be my little elves! Help me complete 3 quests, and I'll be able to make a Present. Bring a Present to a bear, and they'll give you something in return!"
*   **BEElieve:** "I'm just one of the magical entities devoted to spreading Beesmas cheer! There is another... much older and much wiser. He goes my many names: Father Beesmas... Santa Buzz... But his proper name is Bubble Bee Man. If you bee-lieve in him, he can bring to you Beesmas cheer..."
*   **White Beesmas:** "Where I'm from, it snows all year long! It's a truly magical place... For this next quest, I'll need you to collect 10,000 White pollen! To make it fun, pretend you're shoveling snow!"
*   **A Time For Giving:** "This is the season for giving! And nobody deserves gifts more than our bees. Before you can do any more work, we should fill your bees up with tasty treats! Feed your bees Sunflower Seeds, Strawberries and Blueberries, 10 of each..."
*   **The Night Before Beesmas:** "We've just one more gift to make for others. And after that, we'll make something special for you!... Collect 4,000,000 pollen from the Mountain Top Field... Collect 2,000,000 White pollen..."
*   **Festive Bee Workshop 3:** "Here we are... Finish up this Festive Bee for you, my dear friend! ... Your very own 1st Edition Festive Bee*!!!!!! (*Or 500 tickets, if you've already purchased one, you can't have 2, you understand) It's a Beesmas Miracle!"
*   **Conclusion:** "It's me, the spirit of Bee Bear!! That's right, I'm not actually here - I'm far far away. Delivering gifts to well behaved beekeepers and cubs all over the world!!!!"
