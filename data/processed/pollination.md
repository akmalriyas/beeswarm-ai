**Pollination** is a mechanic that causes flowers to grow to higher tiers, increasing their total pollen and multiplier. Flowers that are lower tier than other flowers in the field have a higher chance of being pollinated. When flowers are pollinated, they will burst flower particles.

Flowers remain pollinated for 1 minute, but this effect can be stacked up to 5 times, resulting in a total duration of 5 minutes. If an already pollinated flower is not pollinated within the one-minute window, it will regress down a tier until it reverts to its original state.

Pollination is the only method available to achieve large and star flowers, as the highest natural tier achievable without this mechanic is triple.

## Methods of Pollinating Flowers

*   **Fuzzy Bee's Fuzz Bombs:** Fuzzy Bees can pollinate flowers upon popping their Fuzz Bombs.
*   **Fuzzy Coat Passive:** The Fuzzy Bee's *Fuzzy Coat* passive has a chance to pollinate up to 5 surrounding flowers while collecting pollen. This number increases to 9 when the bee is gifted.
*   **Pollen Haze:** Fuzzy Bee’s *Pollen Haze* ability pollinates up to 30 flowers every second. Bomb tokens produced in a field with Pollen Haze are automatically collected and used to pollinate flowers.
*   **Diamond Mask's Diamond Drain:** When activated, the Diamond Mask's *Diamond Drain* passive pollinates 10 blue flowers. The number of flowers pollinated increases by 3 for every blue bee the player owns.
*   **Guiding Star Amulet:** If the Guiding Star amulet is active, it will pollinate 15 (+1 per gifted bee type) flowers every 15 seconds.
*   **Beequip Effects:**
    *   A bee equipped with a Poinsettia has an 11% to 20% chance to pollinate flowers while gathering.
    *   A bee equipped with a Lei has a 20% to 50% chance to pollinate flowers while gathering.
    *   A bee wearing the Rose Headband has a 10% to 20% chance to pollinate flowers while gathering.

## Trivia

*   Pollination is the only way to directly change the tier of flowers in a field.
*   Fuzzy Bee is the only bee in the game capable of pollinating flowers without requiring a specific Beequip.
