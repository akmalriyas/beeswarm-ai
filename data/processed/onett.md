# Onett

**Onett** is a quest-giver located past the Bear Gate.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Species | Robloxian |
| Location | Behind the [Bear Gate](/wiki/Bear_Gate) |
| Bee Prerequisites | 30 bees |

*This NPC provides a series of challenging quests, culminating in a Star Treat reward. His total quest requirements include collecting 3,673,750,000 Pollen, 136,000,000 Goo, 153,000 Ability Tokens, and defeating 940 Mobs across all his Star Journey quests.*

## Quests

Onett currently offers multiple quest lines: Star Journey, Ornament Quest, Beesmas Quest, and seasonal events.

## Trivia

*   Onett is the first and only Robloxian-species Quest Giver. He and the Mysterious Figure are the only NPCs of the Robloxian species.
*   He is the only quest giver whose appearance changes during every event (e.g., wreath, robot mask, fruitcake).
*   A statue of Onett exists on top of the 6th hive (from left to right), equipped with a Honey Dipper and Port-O-Hive backpack. The pollen in this statue's backpack is a Code.
*   Star Journey 1 was originally planned for players to collect 500 Haste Tokens, but due to a scripting error, the requirement was set to 5,000 before being reduced to 2,500.
*   The total amount of Pollen required across all his quests is 3,673,750,000, and the total Goo required is 136,000,000.
*   The pollen count in Onett's backpack (32318) represents the date Bee Swarm Simulator was first released (March 23, 2018).
*   Onett and Science Bear were among the few quest givers who included Goo requirements for their quests, a mechanic that became more common after Gummy Bear's departure.
*   He and Panda Bear were the first quest givers to give quests requiring defeating Tunnel Bear, with Science Bear being the last.
*   Onett gives 8 quests (including limited quests), which is one of the fewest among non-event quest givers in the game.
*   During Beesmas events from 2018–2021, Onett wore a wreath around his head. In Beesmas 2022, he wore a robot mask and a cog on his belt.
*   Onett is one of only two NPCs whose interaction has been a requirement to complete a quest (the other being Polar Bear).
