# Onett (Quest Giver)

[![OnettNPC](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d0/OnettNPC.png/revision/latest?cb=20240413003313)

Onett is a quest giver located past the [Bear Gate](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate"). He gives out a series of five challenging [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests"), known as the Star Journey. The rewards for completing the final quest include a ![Star Treat](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat").

The Star Journey quests require the player to complete extensive tasks across all five stages, collectively demanding 3,673,750,000 [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen"), 136,000,000 [goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo"), 153,000 [tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), and the defeat of 940 [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs").

## Trivia

*   Onett is the first and only [Robloxian](/wiki/Robloxian "Robloxian") species quest giver.
    *   He and the [Mysterious Figure](/wiki/Mysterious_Figure "Mysterious Figure") are the only NPCs of the Robloxian species.
    *   He is the only quest giver whose appearance changes during every event.
*   A [Statue of Onett](/wiki/Easter_Eggs#Onett_Statue "Easter Eggs") stands on top of the 6th [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") (from Left to Right). The statue features a [Honey Dipper](/wiki/Honey_Dipper "Honey Dipper") and the [Port-O-Hive](/wiki/Port-O-Hive "Port-O-Hive") as a backpack. Onett himself carries a [Porcelain Dipper](/wiki/Porcelain_Dipper "Porcelain Dipper"), [Porcelain Port-O-Hive](/wiki/Porcelain_Port-O-Hive "Porcelain Port-O-Hive"), [Mondo Belt Bag](/wiki/Mondo_Belt_Bag "Mondo Belt Bag"), [Riley Guard](/wiki/Riley_Guard "Riley Guard"), [Bucko Guard](/wiki/Bucko_Guard "Bucko Guard"), [Beekeeper's Mask](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Mask "Beekeeper's Mask"), and [Beekeeper's Boots](/wiki/Beekeeper%27s_Boots "Beekeeper's Boots").
    *   The pollen in the statue's backpack is a [Code](/wiki/Codes "Codes").
*   Star Journey 1 was originally planned to require 500 [Haste Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens"), but due to a scripting error, it was set to 5,000. This requirement was later reduced to 2,500 Haste Tokens.
*   The total amount of [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") required for all his quests is 3,673,750,000, and the total amount of [Goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo") is 136,000,000.
*   The pollen count in Onett's backpack (32318) represents the date when [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator") was first released (March 23, 2018).
*   Since [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") left, Onett's and [Science Bear's](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") quests were the only ones that included collecting goo. However, the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates") introduced [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee "Gifted Bucko Bee") and [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee "Gifted Riley Bee"), which required goo for their quests. Furthermore, all three of the [Beesmas Events](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas") required goo in certain quests from [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear"), and during the [Egg Hunt 2019](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2019 "Egg Hunt 2019") Event, [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear") also required collecting goo.
*   He and [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear") were the first quest givers to require defeating a [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), with Science Bear being the last.
*   Onett's backpack capacity is listed as 500k, but considering his equipped items, it should have a total capacity of at least 900k.
*   Onett gives 8 quests (including limited quests), making him one of the fewest non-event quest givers in the game.
*   He is currently the fourth to last quest giver accessed in the game, preceded by Bubble Bee Man, Spirit Bear, and Dapper Bear.
*   During Beesmas events from 2018–2021, Onett wore a wreath around his head.
*   In Beesmas 2022, he wore a robot mask and a cog on his belt.
*   During Beesmas, players could give him a [Present](/wiki/Present "Present") upon quest completion, though there was a temporary glitch preventing this.
*   Onett, Tunnel Bear, and Gummy Bear were designated as "Naughty" in 2018 by Bee Bear. Onett was the only NPC who could receive a present from Bee Bear's "naughty" list.
*   If a player completed all of his quests during Beesmas 2019, Onett mentioned that he had been "twisting the lid the wrong way for months."
*   He is one of two quest givers who did not offer an Egg Hunt quest; the other being [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)")
*   During the [Egg Hunt 2020](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2020 "Egg Hunt 2020") Event, Onett wore the [Swarming Egg of the Hive](/wiki/Swarming_Egg_of_the_Hive "Swarming Egg of the Hive"), which players received upon completing the egg hunt with [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear").
*   When giving him a present during Beesmas 2020, the gift included Korean skin care products.
*   Onett and the statue are the only NPCs who possess a bag.
*   During Beesmas 2020, a temporary glitch allowed players to claim the Honeyday Event buff by repeatedly interacting with Onett. This was later fixed after a shutdown.
*   When interacting with Onett while still having Star Journey 1 active, the dialogue ends with a riddle for one of the codes used in the [Ready Player Two](/wiki/Ready_Player_Two "Ready Player Two") event.
*   Onett and [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear "Polar Bear") are the only NPCs whose interaction has been a requirement to complete a quest (e.g., talking to Onett for Mother Bear's "⌛Waiting With Sun Bear" quest).
