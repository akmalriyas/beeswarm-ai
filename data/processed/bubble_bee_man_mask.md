# Bubble Bee Man Mask

![B.B.M. Mask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fd/BBMThumb.png/revision/latest?cb=20251210193027 "B.B.M. Mask")

The **B.B.M. Mask**, also known as the Bubble Bee Man Mask, is a mask accessory in *Bee Swarm Simulator*.

## Stats
*   **Capacity:** +75,000
*   **Convert Amount:** +25
*   **Honey From Tokens:** +50%
*   **Blue Bee Attack:** +1

## Acquisition and Mechanics
The B.B.M. Mask was originally obtainable by completing the "Helping B.B.M." quest, which was part of Beesmas 2018 events. Upon completion, the mask automatically replaced any other hat the player was wearing.

Currently, players can reequip this mask by talking to Bubble Bee Man in the 30 Bee Zone (provided they do not already own the Mondo B.B.M. Mask).

## Trivia
*   The B.B.M. Mask and the Mondo B.B.M. Mask were among the first masks that could be equipped by interacting with an NPC.
*   In Roblox, the B.B.M. Mask is classified as a hat accessory.
*   These two items (the BBM Mask and Mondo B.B.M. Mask) are unique in that they cannot be purchased and do not have item descriptions.
*   Following the 2019-04-05 Update, this mask became unobtainable because the NPC who provided it, Bubble Bee Man, was removed from its former location behind the Global Top Battlers leaderboard. These two masks are the only unobtainable accessories in the game.
*   The stats for the B.B.M. Mask have been modified three times throughout the game's history.
*   Wearing the B.B.M. Mask does not grant access to Dapper Bear's Shop.
