# Gummy Bear's Lair

[![Gummy Bear's Lair.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/10/GummyLairBeesmas2021.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20211227001120)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/10/GummyLairBeesmas2021.png/revision/latest?cb=20211227001120) [](/wiki/File:GummyLairBeesmas2021.png)

## Overview
**Gummy Bear's Lair** is a unique location in the game that can only be accessed by players who possess the [Goo Hotshot Badge](/wiki/Badges#Goo_Badge "Badges") or higher. It contains Gummy Bear and Gummy Bee, along with several interactive elements.

[![The platform to talk to Gummy Bear. This was used for Gummy Bear's Beesmas 2020 quest, or now to teleport out of the lair. Only players with the Gummy Mask can use this.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/79/QuestPlatform.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210222050044)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/79/QuestPlatform.png/revision/latest?cb=20210222050044) [](/wiki/File:QuestPlatform.png)

This platform is used to initiate dialogue with Gummy Bear or to teleport out of the lair, but only players equipped with the [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask") can use it for teleportation.

## Accessing the Lair
To enter the lair, a player must touch the [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee") model located at the [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Gummy_Bee_Egg_Claim "Gummy Bee Egg Claim") and use a [gumdrop](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops").

**Reaching the Gummy Bee Egg Claim:**
Players can reach this location in several ways:
*   By going behind the [Ant Leaderboard](/wiki/Top_Ant_Exterminators "Top Ant Exterminators") and following the path (which also contains an [ant pass](/wiki/Ant_Pass "Ant Pass") token).
*   By climbing the Stinger Dispenser, crossing the gate, and reaching the Egg Claim.
*   Using a cannon, such as the [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon") or [Yellow Cannon](/wiki/Yellow_Cannon "Yellow Cannon"), in combination with a [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute") or [Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider").

**Access Restriction:**
If a player does not possess the Goo Hotshot Badge, they will receive a notification stating: "Only Goo Hotshots can hear Gummy Bee..."

[![The notification that appears when the player is on top of the Gummy Bee model and does not have the Goo Hotshot Badge.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/69/Capture73762.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20181201013000) [![The notification that appears when the player is on top of the Gummy Bee model and does not have the Goo Hotshot Badge.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/69/Capture73762.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20181201013000)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/69/Capture73762.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20181201013000) [](/wiki/File:Capture73762.jpg)

If access is granted, the player will be prompted to use gumdrops with the message: "The Gummy Bee wants Gumdrops."

## Lair Contents
The lair houses [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear "Gummy Bear") and Gummy Bee. It also features a [Glue Dispenser](/wiki/Glue_Dispenser "Glue Dispenser"), which activates the [Unlimited Gumdrops](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities") passive, allowing gumdrops to be used without decreasing their count.

## Dialogue Mechanics
### Gummy Bear's Initial Interaction
When a player approaches Gummy Bear without owning the [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask") or [Gummy Boots](/wiki/Gummy_Boots "Gummy Boots"), he will deliver dialogue via chat and notifications.

[![The in-game dialogue of Gummy Bear](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6a/Gummy_Bear_Dialogue.png/revision/latest?cb=20250106021232) [![The in-game dialogue of Gummy Bear](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6a/Gummy_Bear_Dialogue.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20250106021232)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6a/Gummy_Bear_Dialogue.png/revision/latest?cb=20250106021232) [](/wiki/File:Gummy_Bear_Dialogue.png)

### Gummy Beacon Dialogue
If the [Gummy Beacon](/wiki/Gummy_Beacon "Gummy Beacon") is activated, Gummy Bear delivers a series of lines:
*   "What's sweeter than honey?"
*   "Glorious gumdrops... they're just the start!"
*   "A sweet and sour, ooey-gooey universe..."
*   "Can't you see it too?" (slight pause)
*   "What's that Gummy Bee?"
*   "They're not seeing clearly?"
*   "YOU'RE ALWAYS RIGHT!"
*   "GOO-dbye, beekeeper... HAH!"

After completing this speech, Gummy Bear instantly kills the player, sending them back to their [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive").

### Player Dialogue Options
If a player owns the Gummy Mask or Gummy Boots, they can talk to Gummy Bear. He will not offer a quest unless it is related to [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas "Beesmas"). It is not necessary to have either item equipped to initiate dialogue.

**Dialogue Flow:**
A beekeeper approaches Gummy Bee and is given two options: "Talk to Gummy Bear" or "Return to hive."

*   **Talk to Gummy Bear:** "Our Gummy Army marches forward at a steady, viscous pace. An inevitable ooze that envelopes all things, uniting us all! This glorious gummy world awaits you and your Gummy Bee as well. A place where nothing is bitter, and EVERYTHING is sweet!"
*   **Return to hive:** "Back to work, soldier!" (The player is teleported back to their hive.)

If the player has purchased the Gummy Mask after Beesmas 2021, only the dialogue option for talking to Gummy Bear will be available.

## Notes and Mechanics
*   **Pollen Risk:** Players are advised to avoid visiting Gummy Bear's Lair if they have a large amount of [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") in their bags. Due to Gummy Bear's killing mechanic or the need to reset the character, players risk losing this pollen. To mitigate this, players can rejoin the game, use a [Micro-Converter](/wiki/Micro-Converter "Micro-Converter") beforehand, or utilize a [Whirligig](/wiki/Whirligig "Whirligig").
*   **Exit Mechanic (Past):** During Beesmas 2020 and 2021, players could exit the lair by giving Gummy Bear a present.
*   **Token Locations:**
    *   1 [Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") behind the Gummy Boots case.
    *   1 [Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") in front of Gummy Bear.
    *   1 [Enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes") in the corner of the lair.

## Trivia
*   Before Beesmas 2020, the only way to leave the lair without exiting or losing connection was by dying (either via Gummy Bear's action or character reset).
*   Unlike most locations, this area is not connected to the mountain; it is situated high in the sky and its silhouette can be seen from a distance.
*   [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") referred to Gummy Bear as "naughty" in 2018, likely due to his habit of killing players upon dialogue completion or his attempt to "invade" the mountain area.
*   Originally, the lair required the Goo Ace Badge or higher, but this was corrected by [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") to allow access for those with the Goo Hotshot Badge.
*   Gummy Bear's Lair is one of only three locations (alongside the Ace Shop and the [Badge Bearer's Guild](/wiki/Badge_Bearer%27s_Guild "Badge Bearer's Guild")) that requires a specific badge for entry.
*   Prior to the 2019-04-05 update, the teleporting Gummy Bee was located on top of the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent"). It has since been replaced by a model of [Festive Bee](/wiki/Festive_Bee "Festive Bee") and moved to the [Ant Field](/wiki/Fields "Fields").
*   The initial prompt message changed from "Gummy Bee wants 1 Gumdrop" to "The Gummy Bee wants Gumdrops." However, first-time visitors will still see the singular version: "The Gummy Bee wants 1 Gumdrops."
*   [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") mentions how to reach the lair in her quest dialogue.
*   **Glitches:** Players may experience minor glitches here, including failing kills after Gummy Bear's speech or getting stuck in the shop if they press 'E' at the right moment after being killed (resulting in the [Shop Trap glitch](/wiki/Glitches#Shop_Trap "Glitches")). Rare instances of [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee") can push players into the lair, causing them to glitch.
*   This shop and the [Petal Shop](/wiki/Petal_Shop "Petal Shop") are the only shops featuring items arranged in a triangular formation.
