# Pineapple Patch Field Guide

The Pineapple Patch is a large field located past the Brave Bee Gate and to the right of the Pro Shop. It is guarded by a level 4 Mantis and a level 5 Rhino Beetle.

## Field Statistics
| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Field Color** | White |
| **Bee Prerequisite** | 10 bees |
| **Nectar Type** | Satisfying Nectar |
| **Dimensions** | 33 x 23 flowers |
| **Total Flower Count** | 759 |

## Pollen and Tokens
When gathering pollen in this field, players can obtain the following tokens: Honey, Pineapple, Bitterberry, Neonberry, Enzyme, Ticket, Magic Bean, Glue, and Glitter. This field grants Satisfying Nectar when a Planter is planted here.

### Flower Distribution
The flowers are white, red, and blue, appearing in single, double, or triple sizes. (Note: In-game generation is random and may not perfectly match these counts.)

| Size | White Count | Red Count | Blue Count | Total Flowers |

| **Double** | 342 | 19 | 19 | 380 |
| **Triple** | 205 | 11 | 11 | 228 |
| **Total** | **683** | **38** | **38** | **759** |

## Bee Preferences
The Pineapple Patch attracts specific bee types:

*   **Likes:** Shocked Bee, Diamond Bee, Lion Bee, Gummy Bee, Photon Bee.
*   **Dislikes:** Vector Bee, Cobalt Bee, Crimson Bee.

A Wild Windy Bee (Level 1-20) has a chance of spawning in this field.

## Field Boosts and Mechanics
Various methods can be used to enhance the Pineapple Patch's pollen yield:

### Primary Boost Methods
*   **Field Booster:** Using a Field Booster may grant a Pineapple Patch Boost, providing +200% Pineapple Patch pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Using Glitter adds 100% Pineapple Patch pollen to the current boost for 15 minutes (if not already maximized).
*   **Dice:** Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice may add between +100% and +300% Pineapple Patch pollen for 15 minutes (if not already maximized).

### Passive & Amulet Boosts
*   **Pineapple Patch Winds:** Obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes. They provide a base of +20% instant Pineapple Patch conversion, plus +5% instant conversion and +20% pollen per stack. These winds can stack up to 13 times.
*   **Moon Amulet:** Can grant a pollen boost between +1% and +150%. This effect can be boosted by the Shell Amulet, which adds +7% to +30% depending on its rarity if it already has a Pineapple Patch Boost active.
*   **Stickers:** Donating the Menacing Mantis or Pineapple Patch Stamp Sticker to the Sticker Stack boosts the field by 5% and 10%, respectively.

### Historical/Deprecated Methods (No Longer Possible)
The following methods for obtaining Pineapple Patch boosts are no longer available:

*   **Beesmas Gifts:** Receiving specific gifts from NPCs like Science Bear or Onett during Beesmas events provided various levels of Pineapple Patch Boosts.
*   **Market Purchases:** Purchasing a Pineapple Patch Market Boost from the Boost Market granted x1.5 Pineapple Patch Capacity, x1.25 Pineapple Patch Pollen, x1.25 White Pollen, and x1.25 Convert Rate At Hive.
*   **Expired Codes:** Certain codes (e.g., Tabby, ClubBean, WalmartToys, Shutdown) previously granted various levels of Pineapple Patch Boosts or related buffs/winds.

## Trivia and Lore
*   This is the largest field in the game.
*   The mobs associated with this field (Rhino Beetle and Mantis) are blue, despite the field having a white color scheme.
*   A specific pineapple token near the Pro Shop grants five pineapples upon collection.
*   A ticket token worth three tickets can be found on top of the giant pineapple, accessible via the Red Cannon, Parachute/Glider, or by jumping from the side.
*   A Glue token is located behind the largest pineapple between the Stump Field.
*   Before a 2018-07-11 update, this was the third biggest field in the game.
*   It is one of only three fields named with "Patch" (alongside Pumpkin Patch and Pepper Patch). The Pine Tree Forest uses "Forest."
*   This field is one of six locations where Fireflies can travel: Bamboo Field, Cactus Field, Rose Field, Spider Field, Strawberry Field, and Pineapple Patch.
*   It is a destination for the Stick Bug during the Stick Bug Challenge.
*   Along with Sunflower Field and Pumpkin Patch, this is one of only three fields where an object may come to life and shoot sparkles.
*   The biggest pineapple occasionally displays a face that blows bubbles at flowers, producing sparkles (in diamond formation). Collecting pollen from these affected flowers can yield tickets, pineapples, Moon Charm, Royal Jelly, Enzymes, or Honey tokens.
*   Spirit Bear lore states the Pineapple Patch was originally intended to be a "Rhubarb Field" before being converted by her and Stick Bug into a pineapple patch.
*   It is unique in that it is guarded by two mobs of different tiers (Rhino Beetle and Mantis).
