# Coconut Clogs

![CoconutClogsShop](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b2/CoconutClogsShop.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/267?cb=20191006004608)

The **Coconut Clogs** are a pair of boots that can be purchased in the Coconut Cave. They are described as clunky kicks designed to help players "Kick around Coconuts with this pair of clunky kicks for a surge of speed."

## Acquisition and Cost

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :--- | :--- |
| Honey | 10,000,000,000 |
| Coconuts | 150 |
| Tropical Drinks | 50 |
| Glue | 100 |
| Oil | 100 |
| Refreshing Vial | 1 |

## Stats and Bonuses

The Coconut Clogs provide the following bonuses:

*   **Pollen:** +15%
*   **Coconut Field Pollen:** x1.15
*   **Honey From Tokens:** +25%
*   **Bee Attack:** +2
*   **Player Movespeed:** +7
*   **Jump Power:** +22
*   **Movement Collection:** +14

### Passive Ability: Coconut Haste

The passive ability activates whenever the player catches a falling coconut. Upon activation, the player gains one stack of **Haste** and triggers a two-second Coconut Haste Surge boost. This surge grants x1.5 Bee Movespeed and an additional +10 Player Movespeed.

## Trivia

*   This is the second boot accessory that could not have been received from Sun Bear, primarily because it did not exist at the time of its introduction. It followed the Gummy Boots.
*   It is unique among boots as it is the only pair whose name does not end with "Boots."
*   In terms of honey cost (excluding hive slots), this item ranks as the ninth most expensive in the game.
*   The Coconut Clogs are the second boot accessory to feature a passive ability; the Gummy Boots were the first.
*   This, along with the Coconut Canister and the Coconut Belt, is one of only three items that requires defeating a mob (specifically the Coconut Crab) for access.
*   Along with the Coconut Belt, this item boosts pollen collected from a field.
