# Movement Collection

[![A moon amulet with the movement collection buff.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f8/Moon_amulet_movement_collection.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210216003226)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f8/Moon_amulet_movement_collection.png/revision/latest?cb=20210216003226) [](/wiki/File:Moon_amulet_movement_collection.png)

**Movement Collection** is a boost that allows the player to collect pollen when walking on flowers. The higher the Movement Collection stat, the greater amount of pollen collected from traversing flowers. This boost also collects goo if the player walks on any goo patches.

## Ways to Increase

The player can gain this boost by wearing specific boots or by equipping a Moon Amulet.

*   **Moon Amulet:** Can provide between +1 and +3 Movement Collection.
*   **Boots:**
    *   Basic Boots: +2 Movement Collection.
    *   Hiking Boots: +6 Movement Collection.
    *   Beekeeper's Boots: +10 Movement Collection.
    *   Coconut Clogs: +14 Movement Collection.
    *   Gummy Boots: +16 Movement Collection.

## Trivia

*   Movement Collection is not displayed on the System Page, unlike other boosts such as Loot Luck or Instant Conversion.
*   If a player has Movement Collection from their Moon Amulet, they can still collect pollen even without boots equipped.
*   The maximum Movement Collection a player can achieve is 19. This is reached by combining Gummy Boots (+16) and a Moon Amulet (+3).
