# Mazes

Mazes are secret areas within the game that contain valuable items. To obtain these rewards, players must navigate through the maze paths to find them. Currently, there are two distinct mazes available in the game.

## Blue Maze

The **Blue Maze** is situated next to the Ticket Tent (or behind the Beige Gift Box during Beesmas 2021). It was introduced in the Egg Hunt 2019 Update. Since it is located within the Starter Zone, no specific bee requisites are needed to enter.

Inside the maze, players can obtain several items:
*   **Tickets:** Five tickets can be collected by taking a left turn first, followed by a right turn.
*   **Jelly Bean Token:** This token is found by following the path on the right side of the maze.
*   **Star Jelly Token (Royal Jelly):** While initially visible on the right, this token was blocked by a glass wall. It can be obtained by passing through a passage located behind the Petal Shop.

Historically, during the 2019 Egg Hunt, the tickets were housed in a plastic egg. Spotted Chicks could also spawn within this maze before they were removed from the area.

## Pro Shop Maze

The **Pro Shop Maze** is located behind the Pro Bear in the Pro Shop. Access to this maze requires a minimum of 10 bees to pass through the Brave Bee Gate, which is adjacent to the Pro Shop.

Players can find two types of tokens here:
*   **Royal Jelly:** To obtain this token, proceed forward to the end of the hall and turn right twice. Then, follow the first available opening to locate the royal jelly token.
*   **Sunflower Seed Token:** This token awards 10 sunflower seeds. The path is similar to finding the Royal Jelly; however, instead of turning on the last right, continue to the end of the hall and follow through the final corridor.

If a player approaches closely enough, they can grab both tokens simultaneously by passing through the walls, simplifying collection.

## Trivia

Before the Egg Hunt 2019 event concluded on May 13, 2019, the Blue Maze featured different items: a plastic egg token replaced the ticket token, and the Mysterious Figure was present in place of the star jelly token. These elements were subsequently removed in a minor update, although the rooms they occupied remain part of the maze structure.
