# Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors

The **Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in the game. It can only be viewed through the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page.

This leaderboard tracks how much [Pollen] players have collected specifically using only [Legendary Bees] on a given day. The display shows 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view all the way to the 100th place. The ranking resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

Players are awarded prizes based on their placement by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Awarded a [Gifted Silver Egg](Egg#Gifted_Silver_Egg).
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 25 [Tickets](Ticket).
