# Beesmas

Beesmas is an annual holiday event in Bee Swarm Simulator, themed around Christmas. The event typically runs from late December through late March.

The event was first introduced in the 2018-12-19 update and has since appeared in multiple updates, including those released on:
*   2019-12-23
*   2020-12-25
*   2021-12-26
*   2022-12-26
*   2024-07-17
*   2024-12-17
*   2025-12-25

Items associated with the Beesmas event often persist in the game even after the event concludes, such as Festive Beans and Gingerbread Bears.
