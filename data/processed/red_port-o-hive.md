# Red Port-O-Hive

![Red Port-O-Hive](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/ed/Red_Port-O-Hive.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190405220757)

## Description
A Port-O-Hive dipped in shiny red paint.

**Cost:** 12,500,000 Honey, 2 Red Extracts, 2 Soft Wax
**Capacity:** 400,000 Pollen

### Bonus Stats
*   +150% Convert Rate
*   +10% Red Bee Convert Rate
*   +10% Instant Red Conversion
*   +10% Red Pollen

> "A Port-O-Hive dipped in shiny red paint."

The **Red Port-O-Hive** is a bag item added in the 2019-04-05 Update. It can be purchased exclusively at the Red HQ.

## Crafting Ingredients
*   12,500,000 Honey
*   2 Red Extracts
*   2 Soft Wax

## Stats
*   **Capacity:** +400,000
*   **Convert Rate:** +150%
*   **Red Bee Convert Rate:** +10%
*   **Instant Red Conversion:** +10%
*   **Red Pollen:** +10%

## Trivia
*   This is one of the four Port-O-Hive variants in the game. The others are the regular Port-O-Hive, the Blue Port-O-Hive, and the Porcelain Port-O-Hive.
    *   The Red and Blue Port-O-Hives were among the last Port-O-Hive variants added to the game.
*   Prior to the 2024-05-23 Update, this item had no material requirements for crafting.
*   This is the only bag purchasable in the Red HQ.
