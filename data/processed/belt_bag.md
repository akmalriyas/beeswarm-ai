# Belt Bag

![Belt Bag](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c0/Belt_Bag.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403022426)

## Overview
The Belt Bag is a belt accessory found in the Pro Shop. It is described as "A convenient pouch designed for easy access."

### Acquisition and Cost
**Purchase Price:** 440,000 Honey, 50 Pineapples, 50 Sunflower Seeds, and 3 Stingers.

## Stats
The Belt Bag provides the following bonuses:
*   +25,000 Capacity
*   +20 Convert Amount
*   +50% Loot Luck

## Trivia
*   This item was originally given by Sun Bear on his first visit after completing the "Snag a Belt Bag" quest. After Sun Bear left, it was placed for sale at the Pro Shop.
*   Prior to the 2018-11-25 update, this item did not require pineapples, sunflower seeds, or stingers for purchase because those items had not yet been introduced to the game.
*   The Belt Bag is one of the few items that transitioned from being a quest reward into a purchasable shop item. Other items with this quality include the Mondo Belt Bag, Basic Boots, Hiking Boots, and Beekeeper's Boots.
