# Daily Top Pumpkin Patch Collectors

The **Daily Top Pumpkin Patch Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator, viewable only via the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page. This leaderboard tracks how much pollen players have collected within the Pumpkin Patch during a single day.

It displays 10 positions at a time; however, players can scroll down to view all rankings up to the 100th place. The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

Players who achieve specific ranks by the end of the day are awarded prizes:

*   **Top 25:** Players ranked in the top 25 receive a **Pumpkin Patch Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players ranked in the top 100 receive 25 **Tickets**.
