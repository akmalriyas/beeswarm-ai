# Golden Rake

The **Golden Rake** is a tool added in the 2019-04-05 update, available for purchase at the Mountain Top Shop. It is considered a superior version of the standard Rake.

## Statistics and Mechanics

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | 20,000,000 Honey |
| **Shop** | Mountain Top Shop |
| **Range** | 26 Flowers |
| **Collecting Time** | 0.75 seconds |
| **Base Collection** | 7 pollen (from 4 lines of 4 patches) |

### Pollen Collection Details
The Golden Rake collects 7 pollen from 4 lines of 4 patches in 0.75 seconds.

*   **Supercharge Mechanic:** The rake gets supercharged every fifth scoop, collecting 7 pollen from 4 lines of 12 patches during this boosted state. It also emits sparkles when active.
*   **Collection Rates:**
    *   Average Base Pollen Collection Rate: 149.333 pollen per second.
    *   Total Pollen Collection Rate (including supercharge): 238.933 pollen per second.

### Flower Multipliers
The base collection of 7 pollen can be multiplied based on the flower type:

| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 7 | 14 | 21 | 28 | 35 |
| Red | 7 | 14 | 21 | 28 | 35 |
| Blue | 7 | 14 | 21 | 28 | 35 |

*Special Multiplier(s): None*

## Trivia

*   **Sideways Collection:** If the supercharged Golden Rake is activated precisely when the player turns their body, it will collect pollen sideways.
*   **Field Activation:** Like other tools with abilities, it still activates even when the player is not standing in a designated field.
*   **Upgrade Path:** This tool is an upgrade to the standard Rake, which can be purchased at the Noob Shop for 800 Honey.
