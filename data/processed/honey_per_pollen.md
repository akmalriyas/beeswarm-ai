# Honey Per Pollen

**Honey Per Pollen** is a mechanic that increases the amount of honey earned when converting pollen. For example, if a player has 10 pollen in their container with a 110% Honey Per Pollen multiplier, they would receive 11 honey upon conversion. To calculate the expected honey yield, multiply the bag space (B) by the Honey Per Pollen percentage.

Similar to other boosts, such as Instant Conversion, players can always check their current Honey Per Pollen percentage on the System Page. It starts at 100% and increases as the player acquires more related boosts.

Honey Per Pollen, along with boosts like Instant Conversion, Goo, and extra capacity, is one of the most effective ways to maximize honey production efficiency. These boosts work synergistically and are crucial for optimizing processes like grinding or converting pollen using a hive balloon.

It is important to distinguish between Honey Per Pollen and Instant Conversion. While both aid in honey production, Instant Conversion converts collected pollen into honey instantly. Conversely, Honey Per Pollen affects the *quantity* of honey produced for every unit of pollen converted.

## Other Honey per Pollen Variants

Since there are multiple methods for converting pollen into honey, several variants of Honey per Pollen exist that apply to specific conversion types, such as converting at the hive or using Instant Conversion. Like the base stat, these variants start at 100%.

Each variant stacks multiplicatively with the main Honey Per Pollen boost and other applicable variants. For example, if a player has 110% Honey per Pollen (base) and 150% Honey at Hive, their effective conversion rate when using the hive will be 165%.

### Honey from Instant Conversion

**Honey from Instant Conversion** is a specific Honey per Pollen stat that applies when pollen is converted using any method classified as instant conversion (including the Instant Converter). This stat does not appear on the System Page, and its sources are limited.
