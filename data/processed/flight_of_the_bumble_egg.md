# Flight of the Bumble Egg

[![Flight of the Bumble Egg](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c5/Flight_of_the_Bumble_Egg.png/revision/latest?cb=20260104044530)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c5/Flight_of_the_Bumble_Egg.png/revision/latest?cb=20260104044530)

The **Flight of the Bumble Egg** is a cosmetic hat that was obtainable during the Roblox [Egg Hunt 2019](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_2019 "Egg Hunt 2019") event. It was available from April 17, 2019, through May 13, 2019. As a Roblox hat, players can wear it on their avatar for display.

### Acquisition
To obtain the Flight of the Bumble Egg, players needed to complete a quest via [Egg Hunt Info](/wiki/Egg_Hunt_Info "Egg Hunt Info"). The quest required turning in three [Marshmallow Bees](/wiki/Marshmallow_Bee "Marshmallow Bee"). These bees could be acquired from any NPC on the map (at that time), excluding the NPCs located in [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett") and the [Honey Bee NPC](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)")—or by using the now-invalid code, “Marshmallow.”

Upon successfully turning in three Marshmallow Bees to the Egg Hunt Info NPC, players would receive the Flight of the Bumble Egg. This item also awarded an exclusive [Event Badge](/wiki/Badges#Unobtainable "Badges") specific to the Egg Hunt event.

## Trivia
*   Like the [Swarming Egg of the Hive](/wiki/Swarming_Egg_of_the_Hive "Swarming Egg of the Hive") and [How to Program BASIC](/wiki/How_to_Program_BASIC "How to Program BASIC"), the Flight of the Bumble Egg does not provide any special boosts.
*   This item is used in crafting or association with [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear "Black Bear")’s [Honey Wreath](/wiki/Honey_Wreath "Honey Wreath").
*   The name is a reference to the song "[Flight of the Bumble Bee](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZO5KTJTwhE&t=2s)," composed by Rimsky-Korsakov, a Russian composer.
