# Whirligig

[![Whirligig](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5f/Whirligig.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031705)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5f/Whirligig.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031705)

*"Teleports you and your bees back to your hive and briefly increases Convert Rate."*

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 3 minutes
*   **Cap:** 100

Whirligigs are inventory items introduced in the Beesmas 2021 update. Using one teleports the player and their bees back to the hive while temporarily increasing the Convert Rate.

Players have a limit of 100 Whirligigs. Obtaining more than this cap will not add them to the inventory, unless they are acquired through quests or codes. Attempting to use a Whirligig without having claimed a hive will result in the message: "You must claim a hive to use a Whirligig."

## Ways to Obtain

*   A common drop from the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   A reward from the Retro Swarm Challenge.
*   As a rare drop from mobs such as Rhino Beetles and Mantises.
*   As a reward for completing your own Robo Party.
*   As a drop from Robo Party mobs.
*   Purchasing the Frozen Forest Pack or the Winter Winds Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   As a drop from certain planters:
    *   Planters harvested in Pine Tree Forest have a higher chance to drop Whirligigs than other fields.
    *   The Petal Planter yields the most Whirligigs, though the Blue Clay Planter has the highest drop chance.
*   As a Stack Reward for adding specific stickers to the Sticker Stack:
    *   The Launching Rocket rewards 10 Whirligigs.
    *   The Simple Mountain rewards 10 Whirligigs.

### Outdated Ways (No longer available)

*   Rewards from certain quests, including:
    *   Bee Bear's Bringing On Beesmas (#1): x5 Whirligigs.
    *   Bee Bear's Very Merry Berries (#7): x2 Whirligigs.
    *   Bee Bear's By Golly, Get Jolly (#9): x10 Whirligigs.
    *   Bee Bear's Merry Mushroom Mania (#12): x10 Whirligigs.
    *   Bee Bear's Cub Buddy Beckoning (#15): x5 Whirligigs.
*   Completing Bee Bear's 8th, 10th, and 15th quests (rewards were 10, 10, and 25 respectively).
*   Rewards from the Whirling Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   Purchasing the Starter Sampler Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   Opening certain Gift Boxes across the map:
    *   The Beige Gift Box provided 10 Whirligigs and other items.
*   Hitting the target on the right wall in the Blue Flower Field next to the King Beetle's Lair rewarded 3 Whirligigs.

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   **Ant Challenge Exit:** Using a Whirligig during an Ant Challenge teleports the player to the center of the Ant Field, effectively ending the challenge.
*   **White Tunnel Exit:** It is one of several ways to exit the White Tunnel (other methods include defeating or being killed by Tunnel Bear, leaving/rejoining the game, and using the gray plate).
*   **Lore Inspiration:** The item is based on maple tree seeds, which are nicknamed "whirligigs." When falling, these seeds spin, allowing them to travel further from the tree—a mechanic that may have inspired the Whirligig's teleportation function.
*   **Historical Cooldown:** Before the 2022-12-26 update, Whirligigs had a cooldown of 5 minutes.
*   **Frozen Debuff Interaction:** If the player has the Frozen debuff (e.g., from Snowbear), the Whirligig will only teleport the player's bees, not the player themselves. This may also occur if the player is experiencing lag.
