# Hasty Bee

**Hasty Bee** is a Colorless and Rare bee. It is known for its speed, often described as "A quick bee who always zips around." Its movement can sometimes even accelerate the player.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Rare |
| **Color** | Colorless |
| **Energy** | 20 |
| **Speed** | 19.6 |
| **Attack** | 1 |

### Preferences
*   **Favorite Treat:** Pineapples
*   **Likes:** Sunflower Field, Cactus Field
*   **Dislikes:** Pumpkin Patch, Stump Field

### Stats and Abilities

Hasty Bee excels in gathering due to its high movement and collection speeds.

| Statistic | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pollen Collection Rate** | 10 Pollen / 3 seconds (3.33/s) | Base rate. Can reach 20 Pollen in 3 seconds with a White Boost Token. |
| **Honey Production Rate** | 80 Honey / 3 seconds (26.67/s) | Base rate. Can reach 80 Honey in 1.5 seconds with the x2 Honeymaking Speed gamepass. |
| **Movement Bonus** | +40% Movespeed | General movement boost. |
| **Gather/Convert Bonus** | +25% Gather and Convert Speed | Efficiency boost for resource gathering. |

#### Gifted Hive Bonus
*   **Gifted Bee:** Grants +15% Player Movespeed.

#### Abilities
*   **Haste:** Grants +10% Player Movespeed for 20 seconds. This ability stacks up to 10 times (e.g., x5 Haste grants +50% Movespeed).

**Trivia:**
*   The Hasty Guard, available in the Pro Shop, has special stats and design dedicated to Hasty Bee.
*   Hasty Bee is unique as the only bee whose Gifted Hive Bonus directly affects Player Movespeed. (Note: Ninja Bee previously shared this bonus before an update).
*   Along with Shocked Bee, Shy Bee, and Rascal Bee, Hasty Bee shares a description that contains either a spelling or grammar mistake ("zips arounds").
*   A special jelly capable of transforming a bee into a Hasty Bee was obtainable using the expired code 'HasteHelper'.
*   Hasty Bee, Shocked Bee, Ninja Bee, and Photon Bee are among the few bees that grant the Haste token without requiring specific Beequips.
*   It is speculated that Hasty Bee dislikes the Stump Field because it is home to the slow-moving Stump Snail.
*   Hasty Bee holds the title of the fastest Rare bee in the game.
