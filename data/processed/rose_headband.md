# Rose Headband

The **Rose Headband** is a Level 13 Beequip with an equip limit of one. It has no color requirement and can be equipped to Basic, Rascal, Riley, Diamond, Windy, and Vicious Bees.

*Flavor Text:* "Compliments a bee's rosy complexion and flower-esque fleeting youth."
*Seasonal:* Beesmas
