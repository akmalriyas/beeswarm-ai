# Party Mechsquito

The **Party Mechsquito** is a mob exclusive to the Robo Party event. It functions similarly to a regular Mechsquito, but unlike its normal counterpart, it roams around fields instead of following the player directly.

[ ![Partymosquito](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/60/Partymosquito.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20230402142236) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/60/Partymosquito.png/revision/latest?cb=20230402142236 "Party Mechsquito")

## Location
When Robo Party is active, Party Mechsquitos can be found in any field except the Coconut Field, Spider Field, and Ant Field.

## Strategies

Since Party Mechsquitos share characteristics with normal Mechsquitos, standard combat methods apply. However, their field-based movement offers unique tactical opportunities:

*   **Movement:** Unlike some mobs, Party Mechsquitos aim for the player's current location, not where they are moving toward. Moving in circles can often cause them to miss attacks.
*   **Engagement Range:** Standing close to a Party Mechsquito allows your bees to lock on and deal damage quickly.
*   **Crowd Control:** Using [Vicious Bee] and [Windy Bee] is highly effective, especially since they frequently appear in packs. [Digital Bee] can stun multiple targets simultaneously, allowing for increased damage via [Mind Hack].
*   **Field Advantage:** Because they remain within fields, players can position themselves just outside the field boundary. This allows your bees to attack the mob while it does not aim directly at you. Be cautious, as they may hover around the edges of the field and be difficult to target effectively from a distance.

## Trivia

*   Party Mechsquito is one of the earliest mobs introduced during a Robo Party for players starting at lower ranks.
