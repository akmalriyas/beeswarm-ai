# Slingshot

[![The Slingshot.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1f/Slingshot2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240826082740)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1f/Slingshot2.png/revision/latest?cb=20240826082740) [](/wiki/File:Slingshot2.png)

The **Slingshot** is a transportation device available to the player once they have discovered 8 different bee types. It is located next to the Clover Field, typically transporting the player past the Brave Bee Gate and directly in front of the Pro Shop. However, it may undershoot, causing the player to land in front of Panda Bear.

## Trivia

[![A player being launched towards Panda Bear.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/29/Slingpand.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/270?cb=20210225150514)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/29/Slingpand.png/revision/latest?cb=20210225150514) [](/wiki/File:Slingpand.png)

A player being launched towards Panda Bear.

*   If the player has 8 different bee types but does not yet have 10 bees in their Hive, they will always be teleported back to the spawn pad if they attempt to pass the Brave Bee Gate. This mechanic was previously used as a method to bypass the gate.
*   The variability in destination is attributed by Onett (Developer) to "noob scripting." To increase the chance of landing at the Pro Shop (the intended destination), players should jump onto the pad; conversely, walking onto the pad increases the chance of launching toward Panda Bear ("it's a feature, not a bug").
*   This is the only non-cannon transport device that launches the player.
*   A smaller version of the Slingshot is used as a weapon in the Retro Swarm Challenge.
