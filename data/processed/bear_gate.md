# Bear Gate

The **Bear Gate**, also known as the **30 Bee Gate**, is a gate added in the 2018-7-11 update that requires 30 bees to pass through. It is categorized as one of the largest gates in the game by size and requires the second-largest number of bees, only behind the Windy Bee Gate.

**Location:**
The Bear Gate is located past the Mountain Top Field, situated between the Ticket Shop and the Mountain Top Instant Converter.

**Features & Mechanics:**
Underneath the platform within the gate, players can find a Diamond Egg token and Shadow Bear. To reach this egg token, players must equip either the Parachute or the Glider. It is crucial to have an empty container before attempting this maneuver, as touching the floor kills the player.

### Trivia and History
*   **Size:** The Bear Gate is the largest gate in the game by size.
*   **Unique Attributes:** It is the only gate that does not have a field behind it, nor does it feature the requirement pad, egg sign, or blue arch surrounding it.
*   **Launch Status:** Along with the Windy Bee Gate and Ant Gate, this was one of the three gates that did not exist at launch. These three gates (along with the Brave Bee Gate) do not lead to any other gate in the game.
*   **Requirements:** This gate and the Windy Bee Gate are unique as they require players to own a Hive Slot to pass through them.
*   **Design Notes:** Along with the Ant Gate, it is one of only two gates that does not have a bee model on top of it.
*   **Interior:** The area beyond the gate is indoors, meaning an invisible barrier prevents access to the sky, preventing direct travel via the Red Cannon.
*   **Historical Changes (Patches & Updates):**
    *   The player used to be able to enter the Bear Gate's crown, but this has since been patched.
    *   A previous glitch allowed players to use the Red Cannon upon respawn to enter the gate without dying; this exploit has also been patched.
    *   The Boost Market previously resided inside the Bear Gate but was replaced by the Nectar Pot during the 2021-26-12 update.
    *   Previously, the Red Cannon's angle allowed players to land on top of the gate; this ability has since been removed.
