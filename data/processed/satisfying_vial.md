# Satisfying Vial

[![Satisfying Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/84/Satisfying_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042358)

A consumable nectar vial that grants 4 hours of Satisfying Nectar, boosting White Pollen yield, Red Convert Rate, and Honey at Hive production.

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 1 Second
*   **Cap:** 10

The **Satisfying Vial** is a consumable item added in the December 26, 2022 update. When used, it instantly grants the player the Satisfying Nectar buff for four hours. It can also be utilized as an ingredient to craft other accessories and tools.

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Ways
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 5th, 11th, and 15th quests each rewards one **Satisfying Vial**.
*   Condensing 12 hours of Satisfying Nectar using the Nectar Condenser yields a **Satisfying Vial**.
*   Adding the Satisfying Nectar Icon to the Sticker Stack rewards one **Satisfying Vial**.

### Outdated Ways (No longer available)
*   Purchasing the Hefty Honeyday Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog granted 5 **Satisfying Vials**.
*   Purchasing the Nighttime Nectar Pack in the Robux Shop granted 1 **Satisfying Vial**.
*   Completing Polar Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest rewarded 1 **Satisfying Vial**.
*   Opening the Gooey Gift Box rewarded 1 **Satisfying Vial**.
*   Opening the Silky Gift Box rewarded 1 **Satisfying Vial**.
*   Purchasing the Angelic Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog granted 1 **Satisfying Vial**.
*   Completing Bee Bear's Peppermint Preparation (5/5) quest rewarded 5 **Satisfying Vials**.
