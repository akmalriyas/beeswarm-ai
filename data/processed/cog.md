# Cog

Cogs are inventory items specifically used within the Robo Bear Challenge. They should not be confused with the [7-Pronged Cog](/wiki/7-Pronged_Cog).

> Used for purchases during Robo Bear's Challenge. Removed when the challenge ends.

## Mechanics and Usage

**Cogs** were introduced in the 2022-12-26 update. They serve as a temporary currency used exclusively within the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge). Players use Cogs to purchase upgrades, acquire [Drives](/wiki/Drives), and reroll bees or upgrades during the challenge.

Once the Robo Bear Challenge concludes, all collected Cogs are removed from the player's inventory.

Additionally, players can accumulate a total of 250,000 Cogs across all challenges to unlock the [Robo Cub](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins "Cub Buddy") skin for their Cub Buddy.

## How to Obtain Cogs

Cogs can be acquired through two primary methods:

1.  **Completing Robo Bear Challenge Quests:**
    *   Starting a new challenge grants 10 Cogs immediately.
    *   The difficulty of the quest determines the total number of Cogs awarded.
    *   Bonus Cogs may be received for completing a quest within the first two minutes.
    *   Certain upgrades purchased during the challenge can also provide bonus Cogs.

2.  **Defeating Golden Cogmowers:**
    *   Defeating a [Golden Cogmower](/wiki/Golden_Cogmower) guarantees at least 14 Cogs, with the potential to scale up to 18 Cogs per kill.

### Outdated Acquisition Methods

In the past, Cogs were also awarded as a reward for completing [BBM's Naughty List](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") during Beesmas 2024 events.

## Trivia

*   The Cog was one of the first items added to the game that functioned both as an exclusive challenge reward and a temporary form of currency. This mechanic has since been expanded, allowing Cogs to be acquired outside of the Robo Bear Challenge (e.g., from [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man")).
*   The item's design bears a slight resemblance to the cog found on top of the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop").
