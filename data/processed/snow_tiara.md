# Snow Tiara

The **Snow Tiara** is a Level 12 Beesmas Beequip, perfect for occasions where you build snowbears with your sister. It has an equip limit of one and no color requirement.

It can only be equipped to Cool, Stubborn, Shocked, Frosty, Diamond, and Windy bees. The bee must be Gifted for the item to be equipped.

## Acquisition Methods
The Snow Tiara can be obtained through several methods:
*   Purchasing the Snow Queen Bundle from Bee Bear’s Catalog.
*   An extremely rare chance drop from Spiders.
*   Defeating a level 20 Snowbear.

**Outdated/Removed Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing the Merry Mondo, Snowqueen, or Royal Winter Bundles from Bee Bear’s Catalog.
*   Opening Soothing Gift Boxes.
*   As a drop from the Tacky Planter.
*   Purchasing the Royal Blue Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
