# Egg

Eggs are a type of inventory item that players drag into their hive to hatch a bee. They can also be donated to the Wind Shrine or used to roll on the Sticker Printer. Eggs are fundamental items for starting gameplay in Bee Swarm Simulator.

Eggs come in many different types, some offering chances at various bee rarities, while others guarantee a specific bee type. Currently, there are 6 main egg tiers (Basic, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Mythic, Star), plus numerous specific and gifted variants.

**Key Mechanics:**
*   Since the 2019-09-28 update, players no longer require a vacant hive slot to hatch an egg; they can hatch it on top of an existing bee. The level of the resulting bee remains the same as the host bee, functioning similarly to Royal Jelly.
*   Eggs are available in the Robux Shop. (Note: Due to regulations in the UK, Netherlands, Belgium, and Australia, certain eggs in the shop may not be available for purchase by consumers in those regions.)

## General Egg Types

### Basic Egg
The most common egg type found in the game. It will almost always hatch into a Basic Bee (87% chance). However, it can rarely hatch into:
*   Rare Bee (10% chance)
*   Epic Bee (2.5% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (0.5% chance)
*   Gifted Bee (0.4% chance), including a Gifted Basic Bee (0.348% chance).

**Restrictions:** Mythic Bees and Event Bees cannot be hatched from this egg. This egg also cannot be used on a Basic Bee.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Free upon starting the game.
*   Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop near the Sunflower Field (Base price: 1,000 Honey; Caps at 10,000,000 Honey). The purchase price scales up with each subsequent buy.
*   Receiving 1 egg for adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack.

### Silver Egg
This egg most likely hatches into a Rare bee (64.9% chance), but also has chances to hatch into:
*   Epic Bee (30% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (5% chance)
*   Mythic Bee (0.1% chance)

**Restrictions:** Basic Bees or Event bees cannot be hatched from this egg.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 50 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 100 Robux.
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Silver Egg questlines.
*   Receiving a reward from Brown Bear's 25th quest.
*   Completing the 25th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine rewards you 1 Silver Egg.
*   Drops: Mantis (low/extremely low chance), King Beetle and Tunnel Bear (low chance), Rare Sprouts (low chance), Leaves when collecting Pollen in Clover Field (extremely rare chance).
*   From a Festive Planter drop.
*   Rarely from Stockings or the Winter Memory Match reward.

### Gifted Silver Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a standard Silver Egg, but guarantees that the resulting bee will be its gifted variant.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time (guarantees any type of gifted egg, though subsequent defeats have lower chances).
*   Reward for placing in the Top 25 of the Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Completing Black Bear's "Getting Stumped" quest.
*   Receiving 1 Gifted Silver Egg upon completing the 100th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Werewolf (exceptionally rare chance), Stump Snail (rare chance), Scorpion (exceptionally rare chance), Sticker Sprout (drop).

### Gold Egg
This egg is most likely to hatch into an Epic bee (79% chance), but also has chances for:
*   Legendary Bee (20% chance)
*   Mythic Bee (1% chance)

**Restrictions:** Basic Bees, Rare bees, or Event bees cannot hatch from this egg.
*(Note: 5 Gold Eggs are required to craft the Honey Mask.)*

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 100 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 200 Robux each.
*   Rewards: Robo Bear Challenge (rare), Extreme Memory Match (rare).
*   In token form in Werewolf's Cave (guarded by two Cave Monsters).
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Gold Egg questlines.
*   Receiving rewards from Brown Bear's 50th, 340th, and 390th quests.
*   Completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 50th quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick 10 times.
*   Drops: King Beetle (low chance), Scorpion (extremely low chance), Epic Sprouts (rare chance), Leaves when collecting Pollen in Clover Field (extremely rare chance).
*   From Ticket Planter, Candy Planter, Red Clay Planter, Festive Planter, Pesticide Planter, and Petal Planter.
*   Rarely from Stockings or the Winter Memory Match reward.

### Gifted Gold Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a standard Gold Egg, but guarantees that the resulting bee will be its gifted variant. This type of egg cannot be used to craft the Honey Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time (guarantees any type of gifted egg).
*   Reward for placing in the Top 25 of the Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Playtime Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 50 Gifted Bucko Bee quests or 50 Gifted Riley Bee quests (rewards 1 egg).
*   Completing Brown Bear's 275th quest (rewards 1 egg).
*   Receiving 1 Gifted Gold Egg upon completing the 150th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Stump Snail and Coconut Crab (very rare chance), Mantis (exceptionally rare chance).

### Diamond Egg
This egg is most likely to hatch into a Legendary bee (95% chance), but has a rare chance to hatch into a Mythic bee (5% chance).

**Restrictions:** Basic Bees, Rare bees, Epic bees, or Event bees cannot hatch from this egg.
*(Note: 5 Diamond Eggs are required to craft the Diamond Mask.)*

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Token location: Past Bear Gate, under the platform where Onett stands (accessible via Glider or Parachute near Shadow Bear).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 400 Robux each.
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 400 Tickets each.
*   Rewards: Robo Bear Challenge (rare), Extreme Memory Match (rare).
*   Completing Black Bear's Diamond Egg questline.
*   Completing Panda Bear's "Colossal Combat" quest.
*   Completing Dapper Bear's tenth quest, "The Long Haul".
*   Receiving rewards from Brown Bear's 75th and 525th quests.
*   Completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 75th quest.
*   Drops: Stump Snail, Diamond Aphids, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Coconut Crab (rare chance).
*   Spiders, Mantises, Werewolves (very rare chance).
*   Legendary Sprouts (rare chance).
*   Supreme Sprouts (guaranteed drop).
*   Leaves when collecting Pollen in Clover Field (extremely rare chance).
*   Very rare reward from catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   From Festive Planter.
*   Rare reward from the Winter Memory Match.

### Gifted Diamond Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a standard Diamond Egg, but guarantees that the resulting bee will be its gifted variant. This type of egg cannot be used to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time (guarantees any type of gifted egg).
*   Reward for placing in the Top 25 of the Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers.
*   Quest Grandmaster badge reward from donating Tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 100, 250, 500, 750, and 1,000 Gifted Bucko Bee or Gifted Riley Bee quests (each awards one egg).
*   Completing Brown Bear's 425th quest.
*   Receiving 1 Gifted Diamond Egg upon completing the 200th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, or Coconut Crab (extremely rare chance).

### Mythic Egg
This egg will always hatch into a Mythic bee. The player cannot obtain any other type of bee from this egg besides a Mythic bee. (A Gifted Mythic bee is possible but very rare.)

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Reward for placing in the Top 25 of the Daily Top Memory Matchers.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 1,200 Robux each (limited to 3 eggs per player).
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 2,500 Tickets each.
*   Rewards: Robo Bear Challenge (very rare), Extreme Memory Match (extremely rare chance).
*   Completing Black Bear's 75th quest ("Myth in The Making").
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 15th quest ("More Than Myth").
*   Receiving rewards from Brown Bear's 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
*   Receiving 1 Mythic Egg upon completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 300th quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick for the 50th time (guarantees a Mythic Egg).
*   Drops: Stump Snail, Mondo Chick, and Coconut Crab (extremely rare chance). Mondo Chick has a higher drop chance when defeated faster.
*   Extremely rare chance from catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   From Festive Planter.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 500 Snowflakes and 100 Gingerbread Bears.
*   Completing Gummy Bear’s Goo Year’s Beacon quest during Beesmas 2025.

### Gifted Mythic Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a standard Mythic Egg, but guarantees that the resulting bee will be its gifted variant.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Reward for placing in the Top 25 of the Daily Top Rare Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Completing 500 Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quests.
*   Nearly impossible drop from the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 10,000 Snowflakes and 150 Gingerbread Bears.

### Choose-A-Mythic Egg
This unique egg allows the player to select any Mythic Bee they desire upon hatching.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing the Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul from the Robux Shop for 4,500 Robux.

### Star Egg
The Star Egg hatches into any type of gifted bee that is not already in the player's hive (excluding gifted Event bees). If the player possesses all non-event gifted bees, it will hatch a random gifted bee instead. It will never hatch a Gifted Mythic Bee until all other non-event gifted bees are collected.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time (guarantees any type of gifted egg).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 eggs per player).
*   Drops: Mythic Meteor Shower (exceptionally rare chance), King Beetle (exceptionally rare chance), Festive Planter (exceptionally rare chance), Party Mechsquito (exceptionally rare drop).

*   Can also be obtained by redeeming a Bear Bee Voucher received from players who bought the Basic Egg.
