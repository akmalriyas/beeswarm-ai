# Daily Top Pineapple Patch Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Pineapple Patch Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator, viewable through the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page. This leaderboard tracks the amount of pollen players have collected specifically within the Pineapple Patch during a single day.

## Mechanics and Reset
*   **Scope:** The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll to view up to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes
Players are rewarded based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25 Finishers:** Receive a **Pineapple Patch Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100 Finishers:** Receive 25 **Tickets**.
