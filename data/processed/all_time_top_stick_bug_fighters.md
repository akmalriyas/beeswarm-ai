# All-Time Top Stick Bug Fighters Leaderboard

The Global Top Stick Bug Fighters leaderboard is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator. This board tracks the top 100 scores achieved by players fighting Stick Bugs within the Stick Bug Challenge.

Players can view their personal high score for the Stick Bug Challenge by standing on the white pad located in front of the leaderboard, which displays "Your highscore: [ScoreVariable]".

### History and Mechanics

The Top Stick Bug Fighters leaderboard has undergone several resets due to game glitches:

*   It was initially reset on September 27, 2019, after bugs related to the fight were fixed.
*   A subsequent reset occurred on December 26, 2022.
*   The most recent reset took place on January 12, 2024. This last reset was triggered by exploits involving using attack buffs from Robo Bear's Challenge to drastically increase damage output.

**Note:** Following the January 12, 2024, reset, the score displayed when stepping on the podium reflects the player's score *before* the reset, but this pre-reset score is no longer recorded in the leaderboard itself.

### Location

The leaderboard is situated right behind Stick Bug, positioned atop the largest mushroom in the Mushroom Field.

**Access Methods:**
1.  **Cannon/Glider Method:** Use a Red, Blue, or Yellow Cannon paired with either a Parachute or Glider.
2.  **Aerial Descent:** Glide or parachute from a high vantage point, such as near the Lion Bee Gate.
3.  **Obby Route:** Complete the Mushroom Field Obby and jump/glide to reach the tallest mushroom.

### Achievements

*   **100 Million Milestone:** zlib was the first player to achieve a score of 100 million or more.
*   **1 Billion Milestone:** Rara0w0x3 was the first player to achieve a score of 1 billion or more.

### Trivia

*   This leaderboard, along with the Highest Snowbear Level and Fastest Crab Slayers leaderboards, is one of only three in the game that ranks "bosses."
