# Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors

The **Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in the game. It can only be viewed via the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page.

This leaderboard tracks how much Pollen players have collected specifically using only Epic Bees within a single day. The display shows 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view all entries up to the 100th place. The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 by the end of the day are awarded a Gifted Gold Egg.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 by the end of the day are awarded 25 Tickets.
