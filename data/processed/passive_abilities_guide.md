# Passive Abilities Guide

**Passive abilities**, or simply **passives**, are effects found on certain accessories, amulets, or bees. These abilities can be active at all times or only activate when specific actions are performed as an additional effect of those actions.

Passives granted by an accessory or amulet are displayed near the hotbar. Some passive abilities require the player to perform the same action multiple times; the number displayed on the icon indicates how close it is to activation, and resets to 0 upon use.

Some passives have a cooldown. When in cooldown, the remaining time is shown on the passive's icon in pale red. No trigger will activate the ability, nor will the trigger counter be increased during this period. Items with these passives also possess an initial cooldown upon equipping them to prevent immediate bypassing.

## Accessory Passives

### Haste Pulser
*   **Source:** Cobalt Guard accessory.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th **Haste token** collected.
*   **Effect:** Fires a blue pulse that hops to each blue bee, identical to the effect of a Cobalt Bee.
*   **Condition:** Does nothing if the player has no blue bees.

### Focus Pulser
*   **Source:** Crimson Guard accessory.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th **Focus token** collected.
*   **Effect:** Fires a red pulse that hops to each red bee, identical to the effect of a Crimson Bee.
*   **Condition:** Does nothing if the player has no red bees.

### Bubble Bombs
*   **Source:** Bubble Mask or Diamond Mask (if Bubble Mask is owned).
*   **Activation:** Activates every 25th **Bomb token** collected, with a 2-minute cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Spawns 25 bubbles randomly in the player's current field.

### Diamond Drain
*   **Source:** Diamond Mask accessory.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th **Blue ability token** collected, with a 45-second cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Summons a giant diamond that instantly converts 5 million pollen plus the player's total conversion amount.
    *   Conversion bonus: +10% for every Blue bee owned.
    *   Conversion bonus: +50% for every Gifted Blue bee type owned.
    *   Also pollinates 10 (+3 per Blue bee) blue flowers within the field.

### Ignite
*   **Source:** Fire Mask or Demon Mask (if Fire Mask is owned).
*   **Activation:** Activates every 15th **Red ability token** collected.
*   **Effect:** Creates 5 flames in a plus shape at the location where the 15th red token was collected.

### X-Flame
*   **Source:** Demon Mask accessory.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 25th Battle ability token (tokens generated in combat), with a 20-second cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Creates 29 flames in an X shape around the player.

### Coin Scatter
*   **Source:** Honey Mask or Gummy Mask (if Honey Mask is owned).
*   **Activation:** Activates every 20th **Mark token** collected, with a 2-minute cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Converts pollen equal to 300% of the player's total conversion amount and scatters it into 24 honey tokens around the field the player was last in.
    *   Honey Token Value: Minimum of 1000 honey, increasing with converted pollen and the player's Honey From Tokens stat.
    *   Collection Note: These tokens cannot be collected by Token Link but can be collected by Triangulate.

### Gummy Morph
*   **Source:** Gummy Mask accessory.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30 Gumdrops used OR 10 **Gummy Bee ability tokens** collected (Gummy Bee tokens count as 3 gumdrops).
*   **Effect (Player):** The player transforms into a Gummy Bear, the Gummy Bee glows, and the field is covered in goo. As Gummy Bear, the player gains:
    *   +75% Goo
    *   +100% Instant Goo Conversion
    *   +24 Movespeed
    *   +60 Jump Power
*   **Effect (Gummy Bee):** While glowing, the Gummy Bee gains:
    *   +1000% Gather Amount
    *   +300 Attack
    *   +200% Movespeed for 10 seconds.
    *   The Gummy Bee also leaves a trail while glowing.

### Coconut Haste
*   **Source:** Coconut Clogs or Gummy Boots footwear.
*   **Activation:** Activates whenever the player is hit by a falling Coconut.
*   **Effect:** The player gains one stack of Haste and 2 seconds of Coconut Surge, granting x1.5 Bee Movespeed and +10 Player Movespeed.

### Goo Trail
*   **Source:** Gummy Boots footwear.
*   **Activation:** Always active while equipped.
*   **Effect:** Passively covers 5 surrounding flowers with goo wherever the player walks on a field. Flowers directly behind the player receive more goo than those surrounding them.

### Emergency Coconut Shield
*   **Source:** Coconut Canister accessory.
*   **Activation:** Activates when the player takes damage directly from a mob, with a 5-minute cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Summons 5 Coconuts in the field the player was last in, grants 100% Defense, and increases Bee Attack by x1.25 for 10 seconds.
    *   *Note:* This shield does not protect against instant kill sources like Tunnel Bear or Cave Monsters, though it can be used preemptively.

### Inspire Coconuts
*   **Source:** Coconut Canister accessory.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 5th **Inspire token**, Star Shower shooting stars, or Beesmas Light collected.
*   **Effect:** Summons 5 falling Coconuts in the field the player was last in.

### Petal Storm
*   **Source:** Petal Belt or Coconut Belt (if Petal Belt is owned).
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th **Boost token** collected, with a 30-second cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Fires 30 Petal Shurikens around the player in a spiral pattern. When a shuriken passes through a bee, the bee instantly converts 10,000 pollen, increased by 7% of its conversion amount.

### Combo Coconuts
*   **Source:** Coconut Belt accessory.
*   **Activation:** Activates when the 40th Coconut dropped onto a field (via items or abilities) becomes a Combo Coconut.
*   **Effect:** When caught by any player, the Combo Coconut is kicked and falls onto either the summoner's field (if caught on the first drop within the blue circle) or another player's field.
    *   Kicking grants stacks of **Coconut Combo**. Bonus stacks are granted based on color changes:
        *   Default kick: 1 stack.
        *   Different field catch: +1 bonus stack.
        *   Same color classification (Red/Blue/White/Mixed): +1 to +4 bonus stacks, depending on time since last catch in that color.
        *   New player/new color field: +10 bonus stacks.
    *   **Buff:** Grants between 25% to 75% Unique Instant Conversion, between x1.2 to x2 Pollen, and between +1% to +50% Red, Blue, White Pollen, Bee Attack, and Honey From Tokens.
    *   **Failure:** If not caught, it despawns, reapplying the buff to all players who previously caught it.
    *   **Limits:** Cannot be passed in Ant Fields; limited to 50 kicks before premature end.

## Star Amulet Passives

### Guiding Star
*   **Source:** Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive). Can also be summoned during the Beesmas event using Onett's Lid Art.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 250th **Boost token** collected, with a 5-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants 1.25x capacity.
*   **Effect (Active):** Summons a Guiding Star over one of the five fields the summoner has collected the least from for 10 minutes. The chance of selection is based on relative collection amount: the $n$-th least collected field has a $\frac{6-n}{15}$ chance of being chosen.
    *   **Summoner Buff:** x2.5 Pollen, Convert Rate, and Capacity.
    *   **Other Players in Field:** x1.25 Pollen, Convert Rate, and Capacity.
    *   **Pollination:** While active, the star pollinates 15 (+1 per Gifted Bee Type) flowers every 15 seconds.
    *   *Note:* Disappears if the summoner leaves the server.

### Star Shower
*   **Source:** Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive). Similar to Beesmas Lights, which can be activated during the Beesmas event via Science Bear's quest.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 40th **Boost** or **Mark token** collected, with a 30-second cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants 1.25x capacity.
*   **Effect (Active):** 10 shooting stars fall onto the player's current field.
    *   **Flower Conversion:** Each star collects and instantly converts 30 pollen (+50% per Gifted Bee Type) from 5 flowers.
    *   **Catching a Star:** Grants one stack of **Inspire** and instantly converts 1,000,000 pollen, added by 200% of the player's total conversion amount.

### Pop Star
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive).
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th **Boost token** collected, with a 1-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants x1.25 Blue Field Capacity.
*   **Effect (Active):** Summons a Pop Star for 45 seconds, granting:
    *   10% Instant Blue Conversion.
    *   x2 Blue Pollen.
    *   Automatically applies 2 minutes of **Bubble Bloat**.
    *   **Bubble Interaction:** While active, every bubble popped increases the bonus Blue Pollen by 1% (up to x4.5 from x2) and grants Bubble Bloat: 4 seconds (6 seconds if gold), or 1 second (if not player's).
    *   **Bubble Bloat Effect:** Increases Blue Field Capacity and Convert Rate at Hive by up to x6 for up to 2.5 hours.
    *   **End State:** When it ends, the Pop Star spawns several bubbles over the field that increase with the number of Bubbles popped.

### Gummy Star
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive).
*   **Activation:** Activated at a 2% chance when using Gumdrops, or on the 75th use after the cooldown ends, with a 1-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants x1.25 White Field Capacity.
*   **Effect (Active):** Summons a Gummy Star for 45 seconds, granting:
    *   10% Instant White Pollen and Goo Conversion.
    *   Collecting goo during this time makes the star grow, granting bonus White Pollen and Goo (up to x2).
    *   **End State:** The Gummy Star pops, scattering honey tokens and Gumdrops tokens on the field.
        *   Honey Token Value: Total value is a percentage of the goo collected, equal to 1.5% per gifted colorless bee type on the player's hive.
        *   Gumdrop Tokens: Increase with the amount of goo collected.

### Scorching Star
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive).
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th **Boost token** collected, with a 1-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants x1.25 Red Field Capacity.
*   **Effect (Active):** Summons a Scorching Star for 45 seconds, granting:
    *   50% Instant Flame Conversion.
    *   +5 Conversion Links.
    *   x2 Red Pollen and Convert Rate.
    *   **Growth:** Standing near flames causes the star to grow, increasing bonus Red Pollen/Convert Rate (up to x5 from x2) and Conversion Links (up to 30 from 5).
    *   **End State:** When Scorching Star ends, it converts all of the player's pollen in their bag into Honey.

### Star Saw
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive).
*   **Activation:** Activates after using 3 Stingers. The third Stinger used is refunded, and the Star Saw summons for 30 seconds.
*   **Effect (Active):** The saw circles around the player, performing several functions:
    *   Damages hostile mobs (15% of Total Attack). Accuracy equals the average bee level of the hive (excluding summons).
    *   Collects tokens.
    *   Pollination/Conversion: Collects and instantly converts 3 pollen (+0.5 for every 100 Total Attack) from 5 flowers, 10 times per second. The saw instantly converts additional gathered pollen.

## Bee Passives

### Gathering Bubbles
*   **Source:** Bubble Bee, Tadpole Bee, or Frogs.
*   **Effect:** These bees have a percentage chance to spawn bubbles when gathering.
    *   Bubble Bees: 35% chance (50% if gifted).
    *   Tadpole Bees: 65% chance (85% if gifted).

### Gathering Flames
*   **Source:** Fire Bee, Demon Bee, Spicy Bee's Inferno, or bees with the Electric Candle Beequip.
*   **Effect:** These bees have a percentage chance to spawn flames when gathering.
    *   Fire Bees: 35% chance (50% if gifted).
    *   Demon Bees: 55% chance (75% if gifted).

### Steam Engine
*   **Source:** Spicy Bee exclusive passive.
*   **Effect:** Boosts any Spicy Bee's speed by up to 100% while active with Flame Heat. While active, the Spicy Bee increases steam particle count proportionally to the amount of flame heat.

### Fuzzy Coat
*   **Source:** Fuzzy Bee exclusive passive.
*   **Effect:** Has a chance to pollinate up to 5 surrounding flowers (9 if gifted) when gathering. Lower-tier flowers have a higher probability of being pollinated than higher-tier flowers.

### Nectar Lover
*   **Source:** Shy Bee exclusive passive.
*   **Effect:** Makes the Shy Bee twice as likely to sip from a planter compared to any other bee. When sipping, it collects and contributes twice as much nectar to the planter's growth.

### Shimmering Honey
*   **Source:** Diamond Bee exclusive passive.
*   **Effect:** Whenever this bee converts at the hive, it grants a 25% bonus honey (+2.5% per level). This bonus is doubled if the bee is Gifted.

### Balloon Enthusiast
*   **Source:** Buoyant Bee exclusive passive.
*   **Effect (Balloons):** When converting from Balloons, this bee gains x3 its normal convert amount. Additionally, its base attack scales up to x3 with Balloon Blessing.

### Sniper
*   **Source:** Precise Bee exclusive passive.
*   **Effect (Battle):** Uses slow, long-range, more accurate shots and deals x2 damage. Accuracy is calculated as if the bee were 1 level higher (2 if gifted), never falling below 5%. Critical Hit reload time is halved; Super-Crit reload time is reduced by 75%.

### Drive Expansion
*   **Source:** Digital Bee exclusive passive in Robo Bear's Challenge.
*   **Effect:** Using a Drive while active corrupts the field proportional to matching flower colors (Glitched Drives corrupt all fields equally). This permanently enhances the bee, granting stats:
    *   Red Drive: +0.03 Attack
    *   Blue Drive: +2 Convert Amount
    *   White Drive: +0.25 Gather Amount
    *   Glitched Drive: +0.05% Ability Rate
*   **Enhancement Bonus:** Every 10 Glitched Drives Enhancements grants a Hive Bonus of 0.03% Ability Duplication Chance.
*   **Maxing Out:** Digital Bee can be enhanced with up to 500 of each Drive type (2000 total). Upon maxing out, the bee gains +10 Movespeed.

## Other Passives

### Unlimited Gumdrops
*   **Source:** Obtained through the **Unlimited Gumdrops** buff (activated via certain codes and the Glue Dispenser).
*   **Effect:** Allows the player to use their Gumdrops without losing them from inventory. This does not increase the total amount of gumdrops in the inventory.

## Trivia

*   There is a visual glitch where, if the player has the Fire, Honey, Bubble, Demon, Gummy, or Diamond Mask equipped, looking at it through the coconut shield will cause the front of the mask to disappear due to glass textures overriding particles and transparent parts.
*   Bubble Bombs causes each bubble popped to increase in pitch; this effect is unique to that passive.
