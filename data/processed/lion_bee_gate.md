# Lion Bee Gate

[ ![LionBeeGate](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c3/LionBeeGate.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20180421063328) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c3/LionBeeGate.png/revision/latest?cb=20180421063328 "Lion Bee Gate") The Lion Bee Gate is a major gate in the game, also known as the Mountain Top Gate or the 25 Bee Gate.

This gate requires **25 Bees** to pass through and is located at the end of the slope beside the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch "Pumpkin Patch"). It derives its name from the large statue of a [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee") situated on top of it.

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   **Bypassing the Gate:** As of the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), if a player attempts to bypass the gate (either by jumping around it or using the [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon") without having 25 bees), they will be teleported back to spawn.
*   **Gate Similarities:** The Lion Bee Gate shares several unique characteristics with the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate"):
    *   They are among the few gates that do not have a dedicated quest giver behind them (excluding specific locations like the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine in the Hive Hub Portal).
    *   They are notable for containing only one field. This is unique compared to other gates, though exceptions exist (such as the Hub Field inside the Hive Hub Portal).
*   **Historical Context:** Previously, this gate served as the final barrier accessed in the game until the addition of the Bear Gate during the [2018-7-11 update](/wiki/Updates "Updates").
