# Lion Bee Gate (25 Bee Gate)

![LionBeeGate](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c3/LionBeeGate.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20180421063328)

The Lion Bee Gate, also known as the Mountain Top Gate or the 25 Bee Gate, is a gate that requires 25 bees to pass through. It is located at the end of the slope beside the Pumpkin Patch. The gate gets its name from the large statue of a Lion Bee located on top of it.

## Contents
*   **Mobs:** Mondo Chick
*   **Shops:** Mountain Top Shop, Ticket Shop
*   **Fields:** Mountain Top Field
*   **Extras/Items:** Bear Gate, Instant Converter, Field Booster

---

## Description
The area surrounding the Lion Bee Gate contains several key locations and items: the Mountain Top Field, Mondo Chick, Sticker Printer, Field Booster, Instant Converter, Mountain Top Shop, and Ticket Shop.

## Trivia & Mechanics
*   **Bypassing:** As of the 2019-09-28 update, if a player attempts to bypass the gate (by jumping around it or using the Red Cannon) without having 25 bees, they will be teleported back to spawn.
*   **Gate Characteristics:** The Lion Bee Gate shares several similarities with the Ant Gate:
    *   They are among the few gates that do not have a quest giver located behind them (with exceptions like the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine past the Ant Gate).
    *   They are unique in only containing one field.
*   **Historical Note:** This gate was previously considered the final area accessed in the game until the Bear Gate was added during the 2018-7-11 update.
