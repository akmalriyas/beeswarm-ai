# Robot Bear

**Robo Bear** is a Robotic NPC introduced in the Beesmas 2022 update. It serves as a challenging quest giver and host of the Robo Bear Challenge.

## Robo Bear Challenge: Main Quest (Repairing Robo Bear)

Before accessing the challenge, players must complete a quest to assemble Robo Bear by obtaining four specific Drives. These drives are acquired through various quests from other NPCs:

*   **White Drive:** Obtained via Science Bear's quest.
*   **Blue Drive:** Bartered for from Dapper Bear.
*   **Red Drive:** Obtained from Panda Bear.
*   **Glitched Drive:** Corrupted via Science Bear's quest.

Once all requirements are met, the player can initiate the Robo Bear Challenge by spending 1 Robo Pass (obtainable from dispensers in the 30 Bee Zone).

### Challenge Mechanics and Dialogue Summary

**Initial State:**
Robo Bear is initially non-operational. The dialogue indicates that the unit lacks necessary Drives for normal function, forcing it into hibernation mode until the drives are installed.

**Challenge Introduction (Post-Repair):**
Robo Bear introduces the challenge, stating its purpose: to push beekeepers to their limits by generating unique combinations of quests and controlling robotic monsters. The player must spend 1 Robo Pass to begin.

**Core Rules:**
*   The Challenge consists of multiple rounds, each with a 5-minute time limit.
*   Players select bees from a random selection taken from their hive. Any unselected bees are **DEACTIVATED** for the duration of the round, meaning they cannot assist and do not apply Gifted Hive bonuses.
*   Players spend [Cogs] to purchase Upgrades that modify swarm stats throughout the challenge. Unaffordable upgrades can be locked for future rounds.
*   The player must complete the quest within 5 minutes while mechanical minions attack them. Successful completion rewards [Cogs].
*   [Cogs] are used for Upgrades, rerolls, and Drives, but they are **REMOVED** at the end of the Challenge.
*   Rounds increase in difficulty until elimination or successful completion of Round 25 (Victorious).
*   Honey produced during rounds is not saved. The challenge ends immediately if the player leaves the game during a round.

**Challenge Progression & Bonuses:**
| Round | Key Information / Hint | Rewards/Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Start** | Initial bees are heavily restricted (only 3 active). Tool Pollen and Capacity are greatly reduced but increase each round. | - |
| **General** | Players can use [Cogs] between rounds to re-roll bee/upgrade options or purchase Drives. | - |
| **Round 5+** | Completion of certain milestones grants a Cog Amulet upon challenge completion. | Bronze (5), Silver (10), Gold (15), Diamond (20), Supreme (25) |
| **Round 8** | [Drives] can be activated during the Challenge, granting temporary Pollen/Capacity multipliers and +1 attack for one round. | - |
| **Round 10** | Players must defeat roaming Cogmowers to earn a Silver Cog Amulet upon completion. | - |
| **Round 12** | Stacking Nectars before the challenge is statistically proven to increase performance; neglecting them can cause early termination. | - |
| **Round 15** | Completion of this round leads toward earning a Gold Cog Amulet. | - |
| **Round 17** | Golden Cogmowers are guaranteed to spawn in three different rounds, offering bonus [Cogs] upon defeat. | - |
| **Round 20** | Special event: Infiltration by the Digital Bee requires completion of this round for a Diamond Cog Amulet. | - |
| **Round 25** | The final round. Success grants the Supreme Cog Amulet. All remaining [Cogs] will be removed upon completion. | Supreme Cog Amulet |

***Note on Deactivation:*** *At the start of the challenge, all but three of the player's bees are deactivated. Deactivated bees do not assist and their Gifted Hive/Beequip bonuses are removed.*

### Trivia
*   Robo Bear is the second NPC in the game to initiate a challenge; the first was Stick Bug.
*   It utilizes the Robot Animation Pack from Roblox.
*   Robo Bear is one of the few quest givers who owns a shop, alongside Mother Bear, Dapper Bear, Gummy Bear, and Bee Bear.
*   The original face decal for Robo Bear was created on 2019-02-24, eventually releasing in Beesmas 2022 (2022-12-14).
*   Robo Bear is the only bear whose design has been publicly changed from its initial release model.
*   The "Repairing Robo Bear" quest can be instantly completed once redeemed if the player already possesses all four required Drives.
