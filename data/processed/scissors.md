# Scissors

**Scissors** is a tool available for purchase at the Pro Shop.

| Attribute | Value |

**Pollen Yield by Flower Tier:**
| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 30 | 60 | 90 | 120 | 150 |
| Red | 30 | 60 | 90 | 120 | 150 |
| Blue | 30 | 60 | 90 | 120 | 150 |

*Special Multiplier(s): None*

The Scissors are a tool that collects all pollen from the patch in front of you within 0.5 seconds. It has a maximum base collection rate of 60 pollen per second from single flowers, 90 pollen per second from double flowers, 120 pollen per second from triple flowers, and 150 pollen per second from large flowers. The yield varies significantly based on how much pollen the flower holds (depending on its tier), though nearby flowers may not always be fully saturated, preventing the tool from reaching its complete potential.

## Crafting Ingredients
The Scissors require 850,000 Honey to craft.

## Trivia
*   The Scissors are an enhanced version of the Clippers. The main difference in appearance is that the Clippers have smaller size and yellow handles, while the Scissors feature red and blue handles.
*   This tool, along with the Clippers, Scythe, and Dark Scythe, is one of four items capable of cutting vines leading to Commando Chick's Hideout.
*   The Scissors and the Spark Staff are the only tools that collect all pollen from the flowers they hit.
*   The Scissors and the Clippers are the only tools designed to collect pollen from a single flower.
*   This tool can become significantly more powerful than other tools in its tier when collecting from higher-tier flowers, such as those pollinated by other players' Fuzzy Bees.
