# Rose Field

The Rose Field is one of the four fields located past the Honey Bee Gate, situated near the Red HQ. It is protected by two scorpions—one level 5 and one level 6.

## Field Statistics

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Field Color** | Red |
| **Bee Prerequisites** | 15 bees |
| **Nectar Type** | Motivating Nectar |
| **Dimensions** | 31 × 20 flowers |
| **Total Flower Count** | 620 |

## Mechanics and Spawns

**Pollen Tokens:** When collecting pollen, this field can spawn Honey, Strawberry, Red Boost (Ability Tokens), Red Extract, Bitterberry, and Magic Bean tokens.

**Nectar Generation:** Planting a Planter in the Rose Field grants Motivating Nectar.

**Mobs:** A level 5-7 Rogue Vicious Bee has a chance of spawning here. If one spawns, it can attack players inside the Red HQ with its spikes.

## Obtaining Boosts

Several methods can enhance pollen collection in the Rose Field:

*   **Red Field Booster:** Using this item may grant a Rose Field Boost, providing +100% Rose Field pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Adds 100% Rose Field pollen onto an existing boost for 15 minutes (if not already maxed out).
*   **Dice:** Using Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice can add +100% to +300% Rose Field pollen for 15 minutes, depending on the dice type and current stack.
*   **Field Winds:** Obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes, these provide a base of +20% instant Rose Field conversion. Each stack adds +5% instant Rose Field conversion and +15% Rose Field pollen, stacking up to 13 times total.

***Note: The following methods for obtaining boosts are no longer possible:***
*   Giving presents to Onett or Gifted Riley Bees during Beesmas 2021.
*   Redeeming certain expired codes (e.g., LateNightGumdrops, Discord100k, Valentine).
*   Purchasing a Rose Field Market Boost from the Boost Market, which grants 1.25x Rose Field Pollen, x1.5 Rose Field Capacity, +10% Bee Attack, and +50 Convert Amount.

## Trivia

*   The Rose Field is one of six fields that Fireflies travel to; the others are Bamboo Field, Cactus Field, Pineapple Patch, Spider Field, and Strawberry Field.
*   Vicious Bee likes this field likely because roses commonly have thorns on their stems. It is also the only blue bee to like a field of the opposite color (the other sharing this trait is Festive Bee at Pine Tree Forest).
*   This field was previously known for having a small section that clipped outside of the map.
*   A Royal Jelly token can be found next to this field in a small ledge, accessible by walking along the Badge Bearer's Guild wall.
*   It is the only field past the Honey Bee Gate not defended by the Werewolf.
*   After the 2019-09-28 update, treat tokens could naturally spawn here.
*   This is the only field to be at a lower elevation than the gate leading to it.
*   Stepping in the row of flowers closest to Robo Bear does not spawn the Scorpions.
