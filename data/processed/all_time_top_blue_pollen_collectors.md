The All-Time Top Blue Pollen Collectors is one of the game's leaderboards, tracking how much blue pollen players have collected over time. The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, and users can scroll down to view up to 100th place. Only legitimate players are maintained on these leaderboards (hackers or exploiters will be removed).

**In-Game Mechanic:**
When a player steps on the white pad in front of this leaderboard, a message appears displaying their total blue pollen collected (e.g., "You've collected 'x' Blue Pollen").

## Location
This leaderboard is located on the first floor of the Blue HQ, situated next to the ramp leading to the second floor.
