# Snowstorm

[![An active snow storm.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cf/Snowstorm.png/revision/latest?cb=20201228210218 "An active Snow Storm.")
A **Snowstorm** is a server-wide event that can be triggered either randomly or by using the [Snow Machine](/wiki/Snow_Machine). It functions similarly to a Honeystorm, but instead of honey particles, it spawns ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake). A Snowstorm lasts for 20 seconds.

### Summoning Mechanics
When a player uses the Snow Machine to summon a Snowstorm, a server-wide announcement is displayed: `❄️ {Username} has activated the Snow Machine! ❄️`

Whether summoned via the machine or randomly, ![Snowflake](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake) begin falling rapidly from the sky across every field. Unlike a Honeystorm or [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower), there is no initial audio cue when a Snowstorm begins.

If a Snowstorm is summoned randomly, the message displayed is: `❄️ A Snow Storm has begun... ❄️`

### Special Summoners and Events
Occasionally, [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") will spawn a Snowstorm, typically under an alias. This mirrors how he globally summons Mythic Meteor Showers, nighttime events, special sprouts, and mobs.

Snowstorms have also been summoned by various unique in-game occurrences:
*   Snowcub summoning a Snowstorm.
*   Texas Bear summoning a Snowstorm.
*   Festive Bee attempting to re-freeze the Bean Bug Brigade.
*   Gifted Gingerbread Bear during Beesmas 2020.
*   A Gifted Festive Nymph (Lvl 9) (Aspect of Snow Mutation) (Winter Cap Nymph-quip with 5 Caustic Waxes) (HP Saves) during Beesmas 2021.
*   Gloomy Cub's Cold Heart summoning a Snowstorm.
*   Gloomy Cub's Immobilizing Pessism summoning a Snowstorm.
*   The 1,000,000th Snowflake spent on the Catalog.
*   The realization that Beesmas is actually over.
*   Gifted Gatito Bee summoning a Snowstorm.
*   Festive Nymph summoning a Snowstorm.

## Trivia
*   Like Honeystorms and Mythic Meteor Showers, Snowstorms can illuminate [Werewolf's Cave](/wiki/Werewolf%27s_Cave) sufficiently to reveal the [Cave Monsters](/wiki/Cave_Monster) and the ![Gold Egg](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Gold Egg](/wiki/Egg#Gold_Egg) at the cave's end. However, unlike Honeystorms, Snowstorms do not spawn snowflake tokens in the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge).
*   During Beesmas events (2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025), giving a [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear) a ![Present](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Present](/wiki/Present) after her dialogue finished and the [ornament](/wiki/Ornaments) was given would trigger a Snowstorm. Finishing Spirit Bear’s Beesmas quest also summons a Snowstorm.
*   The ![Snowflakes](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) continue to fall for several seconds after a Snowstorm ends, as it takes time for the particles to reach the ground.
*   If a Snowstorm is summoned during nighttime, the resulting white fog will persist even after the event concludes until the night cycle ends.
