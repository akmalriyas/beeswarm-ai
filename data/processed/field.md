# Field

Fields, also referred to as "patches" or specific environments like Pine Tree Forests, are square or rectangular areas scattered across the map where players collect pollen. This collected pollen can then be converted into honey at a hive or through other methods.

There are 23 distinct fields in the game, including those found within the Ant Challenge, Hive Hub, and Retro Swarm Challenge. Each field contains flowers, which come in three primary colors: blue, red, and white. The specific flower composition of any given field is randomized upon server generation.

### Field Mechanics
The density of flowers directly impacts their yield; fields with more flowers per square provide greater pollen and regenerate faster. Several mechanisms can increase the number of flowers on a square:

*   **Bee Equipment:** Fuzzy Bees or bees equipped with specific items, such as Poinsettia, Rose Headband, or Lei, can pollinate the field to increase flower density.
*   **Passive Abilities:** The Guiding Star passive ability will automatically pollinate flowers when hovering over a field.
*   **Diamond Drain:** This passive also specifically pollinates blue flowers.

Fields may also spawn tokens of Honey and Treats.
