# Bubble Wand

The **Bubble Wand** is a tool available for purchase in the Blue HQ. It specializes in pollen collection and has unique secondary mechanics related to bubbles.

## Stats & Mechanics

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | 3,500,000 Honey |
| **Shop** | Blue HQ |
| **Range** | 16 Flowers |
| **Collecting Pattern** | Ring |
| **Collecting Time** | 0.8 seconds |
| **Base Collection** | 6 pollen per flower patch |

### Pollen Collection Rates

The Bubble Wand has an average base pollen collection rate of 160 pollen per second, with a special multiplier for blue flowers:

*   **White Flowers:** 6 pollen (per single swing)
*   **Red Flowers:** 6 pollen (per single swing)
*   **Blue Flowers:** 12 pollen (per single swing), multiplied by $\times2$ (Total 24 pollen per single swing).

Every 10th swing, the Bubble Wand spawns a bubble somewhere on the field. It also emits bubble particles while in use.

## Trivia

*   A replica of the Bubble Wand can be found resting on the roof of the Blue HQ.
*   Its counterpart is the Scythe (the red version). The Bubble Wand collects slightly more pollen per second than the Scythe, although this difference is minimal. However, its collection pattern can sometimes make it harder to collect specific items like goo, sparkles, or leaves.
*   It is the second cheapest item available in the Blue HQ, only behind the Blue Guard.
