# Elite Blue Guard

![Elite Blue Guard](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8b/Elite_Blue_Guard.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403020315 "Elite Blue Guard")

The **Elite Blue Guard** is a guard purchasable in the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ). It is equipped on the right shoulder and is reserved for dedicated Blue Beekeepers.

> "_A Blue Guard reserved for the most dedicated Blue Beekeepers._ "

### Cost
To craft the Elite Blue Guard, you need:
*   5,000,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey)
*   3 [Blue Extracts](/wiki/Blue_Extract)
*   50 [Blueberries](/wiki/Blueberry)
*   5 [Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly)
*   15 [Moon Charms](/wiki/Moon_Charm)

### Bonus Stats
The Elite Blue Guard provides the following stat bonuses:
*   +75,000 Capacity
*   +25% Blue Pollen
*   +7% Defense
*   +50% Critical Power
*   +1 Blue Bee Attack

*   Prior to the second part of the [2020-12-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2020-12-25), players could glitch their Cub Buddy into this guard.
*   This guard has a counterpart, the [Elite Red Guard](/wiki/Elite_Red_Guard), which is red.
