# Blue Field Booster

![BlueFieldBooster2022](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b4/BlueFieldBooster2022.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20220326012850) The Blue Field Booster is a machine that provides a field boost to a random blue field for 15 minutes.

## Mechanics and Usage

The Blue Field Booster grants a temporary pollen yield increase to one randomly selected blue field.

*   **Duration:** 15 minutes
*   **Cooldown:** 45 minutes
*   **Location:** Second floor of the Blue HQ, next to Gifted Bucko Bee.
*   **Prerequisite:** The player must have discovered at least five different types of blue bees.

When activated, the following audio plays: [Fieldbooster.ogg](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Fieldbooster.ogg)

## Boost Effects

The booster applies the following pollen multipliers to eligible fields:

*   **Blue Flower Field:** +300% Pollen
*   **Bamboo Field:** +100% Pollen
*   **Pine Tree Forest:** +100% Pollen
*   **Stump Field:** +100% Pollen (Only active if the Stump Snail is on respawn cooldown.)

## Trivia

*   The Blue Field Booster is one of three field boosters in the game, alongside the Field Booster and the Red Field Booster. They share a similar design, differing only by color.
*   In an update, the Bamboo Field Boost was nerfed from 200% to 100% pollen.
