# Market Boost (Obsolete)

The **Boost Market** was a shop found in the 30 Bee Zone, located past the Bear Gate on the right side of the map. It has been replaced by the Nectar Pot since the Beesmas 2021 update.

## Mechanics and Functionality

The Boost Market allowed players to purchase temporary market boosts every 30 minutes. The price of these boosts was dynamic, fluctuating based on how many other players had purchased that specific boost, mimicking a real-world stock market.

To acquire a boost, the player needed to spend both **Tickets** and **Honey**. These purchased boosts provided various multiplicative or additive bonuses for a duration of 30 minutes. Boosts could also be obtained via specific codes (such as RedMarket) or during events like Ready Player Two.

When a market boost was successfully purchased, the following audio played:
<https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Boostmarketsound.ogg>

## Trivia
*   The Boost Market was removed because Onett referred to it as a "Failed Experiment." Furthermore, the prices became excessively high due to exploitation by players.
*   Along with the Boost Market, Evictions and the Most Commando Captures Leaderboard were among the few pieces of content removed from the game that were not exclusive to specific events.
