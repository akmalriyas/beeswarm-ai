# Science Bear

**Science Bear** is a permanent quest giver and one of eight unique bears in Bee Swarm Simulator. He is known for his scientific nature, often framing quests as experiments into the mechanics of the game world.

## Science Enhancement Buff

Science Bear's quests reward the player with **Science Enhancement**, a buff that increases the bees' honey production rate. The bonus is calculated based on the level of enhancement achieved ($\_n\_$).

**Production Rate Formula:** $25(\_n\_ + 1)\%$
**Honey per Second (Basic Bee) Formula:** $5(\_n\_ + 5)\%$

### Enhancement Levels 1–16
| Level | Production Rate | Honey/sec (Basic Bee) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1 | 25% | 25 |
| 4 | 100% | 40 |
| 8 | 200% | 60 |
| 12 | 300% | 80 |
| 16 | 400% | 100 |

### Enhancement Levels 17–31
| Level | Production Rate | Honey/sec (Basic Bee) |

## Dialogue Insights
Science Bear's dialogue often provides deep dives into scientific concepts and game mechanics:

*   **Bee Discovery:** He explains that new bees can be hatched from eggs (Basic, Silver, Gold, Diamond) or transformed using Royal Jelly.
*   **Pollination/Botany:** He discusses the symbiotic relationship between bees and flowers, noting how different fields yield specific pollen types.
*   **Technology & Efficiency:** He details how technology (like Field Boosters and specialized tools) increases efficiency multipliers in honey production.
*   **Language & Communication:** In "Beesperanto," he explores the concept of universal language barriers and the potential for direct communication with bees, leading to his quest for a second Translator.
*   **Game Mechanics:** He references internal game limitations (e.g., server capacity limits on hive size) and explains how mechanics like pheromones are simulated via "Mark" abilities.

## Trivia & Lore

*   **Design Inspiration:** Science Bear is based on the Spectacled Bear, and he wears goggles/spectacles.
*   **Unique Role:** He is one of only three quest givers to activate a buff after every quest (along with Polar Bear and Honey Bee NPC).
*   **Quest Uniqueness:** He is the only bear who requires players to collect pollen using specific tools (Spark Staff, Porcelain Dipper) and the only bear to give a Translator.
*   **Mathematical Rewards:** Many of his honey rewards are based on mathematical constants ($\pi$, $e$, powers of 2).
*   **Self-Awareness:** Like several other bears, he acknowledges that he is in a simulated environment (referencing "the device you're playing this game on").
*   **Quest Progression:** He originally had 11 quests, which increased to 31 over various updates.
