# Red Guard

![Red Guard](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4c/Red_Guard.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403020205 "Red Guard")

## Description
A durable pad worn on the left shoulder of Red Beekeepers. It is one of three available guards in the Red HQ, alongside the Elite Red Guard and Riley Guard.

> "_A durable pad worn on the left shoulder of Red Beekeepers._ " 

## Stats
The Red Guard provides the following bonuses:
*   **+25,000 Capacity**
*   **+15% Red Pollen**
*   **+5% Defense**
*   **+1 Red Bee Attack**

## Cost and Crafting Ingredients
To craft a Red Guard, players require:
*   750,000 Honey
*   50 Strawberries
*   1 Royal Jelly
*   1 Stinger

## Trivia
*   Before the 2018-11-25 update, the guard cost 1 million honey and did not require crafting materials.
*   Its counterpart is the Blue Guard.
*   It is the cheapest guard available in the Red HQ, and also the cheapest item sold there.
