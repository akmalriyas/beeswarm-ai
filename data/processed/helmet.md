# Helmet

![Helmet](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/37/Helmet.png/revision/latest?cb=20190402215018 "Helmet")

## Description
A hard hat that grants bonus pollen and helps prevent head injuries. It is a hat accessory available for purchase at the Noob Shop.

## Stats
*   **Pollen Bonus:** +10%
*   **Defense Bonus:** +25%

## Acquisition Details
The Helmet can be purchased with the following ingredients:
*   30,000 Honey
*   5 Pineapples
*   1 Moon Charm

## Trivia
*   It closely resembles an American football helmet, but it is completely yellow and lacks a faceguard.
*   Along with the Propeller Hat, Beekeeper's Mask, and Strange Goggles, this item is one of the few masks not based on a bee.
*   This is the most expensive item in the Noob Shop.
*   It is the cheapest obtainable mask in the game when measured by honey cost.
