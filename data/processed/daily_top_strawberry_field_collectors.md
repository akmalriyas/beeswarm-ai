# Daily Top Strawberry Field Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Strawberry Field Collectors** is one of the game's leaderboards, tracking how much pollen players have collected specifically in the Strawberry Field on a given day.

## Mechanics and Viewing
*   This leaderboard is accessible via the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page.
*   It displays 10 positions at a time, but users can scroll down to view up to the 100th place.
*   The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes
Players are awarded rewards based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 receive a **Strawberry Field Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 receive 25 **Tickets**.
