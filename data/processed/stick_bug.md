# Stick Bug NPC Guide

Stick Bug is a unique Non-Player Character (NPC) found on top of the tallest mushroom in the Mushroom Field, located next to the Top Stick Bug Fighters Leaderboard. It serves as the starting point for various challenges and quests within Bee Swarm Simulator.

## Interaction Mechanics

Players must use a Translator to speak with Stick Bug. The interaction initiates various challenges or dialogue trees depending on the context (e.g., Beesmas event).

### Dialogue Flow

**Initial Talk (Without Translator):**
Tsk tsk tsk tsk. T tuh tsk TSKK VRZz tsk tsk! *(Stick Bug speaks in bug noises.)*
*Options: Leave, Give a Translator.*

**Initial Talk (With Translator - First Time):**
"Hey there flesh-bug. I am Stick Bug. I move, I jump, I step to my own beat! I was here before the bees and honey. Jump into my world... You think you and your bees can take on me? Think you can match my stide [sic]? You'll be over in your head. But I'd like to see you try. Come back if you're brave."

**Initial Talk (With Translator - Subsequent):**
"Hey there flesh-bug. Feeling brave? How about you and your friends join me in my dance? Once it starts, I'll hop into the fields. Deal as much damage to me as you can in 10 minutes to earn rewards. The higher the score, the better they will be. Ready to get down?"

**Challenge Initiation:**
*   **Free Entry:** Start the challenge for free.
*   **Ticket Entry:** Pay 50 Tickets to start the challenge.
*   **Present (Beesmas Exclusive):** Giving Stick Bug a Present during Beesmas initiates the relevant quest/challenge.

Upon completing a challenge, players are awarded an [Stick Bug Amulet] based on their score.

## Quest Lines: Festive Nymphs Hats

Stick Bug is central to several seasonal quests involving helping the Stick Nymphs get festive hats, which enables the spawning of powerful Festive Nymphs during challenges.

### 1. Beesmas Quest - Stick Bug's Nymph Hats (Original/Early Version)
*   **Requirement:** Obtain a Walking Stick Nymph Sticker and complete various collection/defeat tasks.
*   **Requirements List:**
    *   Collect 9,000,000,000 Pollen from the Pineapple Patch.
    *   Catch 1,000 Petals in the Sunflower Field.
    *   Collect 4,000,000,000 Pollen with Rare Bees.
    *   Collect 6,000 Tokens from Stick Bug.
    *   Collect 5,000 Tokens from Stick Bug Defense Totems.
    *   Discard 20 Beequips.
    *   Use 20 Red Extracts.
    *   Defeat 100 Mechsquitoes.
    *   Defeat 10 Werewolves.
    *   Defeat 4 Aphids.
    *   Defeat 4 Coconut Crabs.

**Completion Rewards:**
*   Starts the Stick Bug Challenge.
*   Allows Festive Nymphs to spawn during the challenge.
*   Festive Nymphs grant improved loot and a stack of "Festive Nymph Blessing" upon defeat (Bonus Capacity, Instant Bee Gather Conversion, Honey Per Pollen, and Bee Attack; stacks up to 250 times for 8 hours).

## Trivia & Lore

*   Stick Bug is one of only three NPCs (along with Gifted Riley Bee and Gifted Bucko Bee) that requires a Translator to interact with.
*   Stick Bug, along with Shadow Bear, Cub Buddies, Bee Bear, and Spirit Bear, are the only NPCs in the game known for dancing.
*   If a player gives Stick Bug a Present, starts its Egg Hunt quest, or completes any of its Beesmas quests (excluding 2019), the Stick Bug Challenge will automatically start.
*   Stick Bug's boss fight theme became part of the popular bait-and-switch meme "[Get stickbugged lol]". He is aware of this meme, as indicated by his 2020 dialogue for receiving a Present.
*   In Spirit Bear's dialogue, she mentions that Stick Bug scared her as a cub, indicating the NPC has been around for a long time in the game's lore.
*   Stick Bug loves pineapples, as stated by Spirit Bear.
*   **Glitches:** There are known glitches where Stick Bug may fall from its regular location on top of the tallest mushroom and appear in the Dandelion Field, Mushroom Field, or between them. Another glitch occurs near the Brave Bee Gate ramp/Bamboo Field corner, causing him to briefly get stuck when following a player.
*   **Mob Behavior:** Stick Bug and Wild Windy Bee are unique mobs that cannot be defeated; depleting their HP only levels them up and restores their health.
