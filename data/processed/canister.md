# Canister

![Canister](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5f/Canister.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190402214838)

## Overview
The Canister is a high-tech container that can be purchased in the Noob Shop. It significantly improves conversion speed and storage capacity for your hive.

**Description:** "A high-tech container that improves conversion speed."

### Stats & Costs
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Purchase Cost** | 22,000 Honey |
| **Capacity Bonus** | +10,000 |
| **Convert Rate Bonus** | +30% |

### Crafting Ingredients
*   22,000 Honey

## Trivia
*   The Canister is the biggest and most expensive Bag available in the Noob Shop.
*   It is the only bag in the Noob Shop that provides a buff other than increased Capacity, and it is the cheapest of those specialized bags.
*   In terms of honey cost at the Noob Shop, it is the second most expensive item, trailing only the Helmet (30,000 Honey).
