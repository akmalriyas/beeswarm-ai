# Looker Bee

**Looker Bee** is a Colorless and Rare bee. It is known for its observational nature, often gaining valuable insights into the hive environment.

**Rarity:** Rare
**Color:** Colorless
**Base Stats:**

*   Speed: 14

## Preferences and Habitat

Looker Bee's favorite treat is Sunflower Seeds.

| Preference | Location/Item | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Likes | Clover Field, Mountain Top Field | Positive |
| Dislikes | Sunflower Field, Pepper Patch | Negative |

## Statistics and Abilities

### Base Stats
*   **Pollen Collection:** 13 pollen in 4 seconds (Equivalent to 3.25 per second).
*   **Honey Production:** 160 honey in 4 seconds (Equivalent to 40 per second).
*   **Bonuses:** +80 Convert Amount, +3 Gather Amount, +5% Critical Chance.

### Gifted Hive Bonus
When housed in a Gifted Hive:
*   +25% Critical Power.

### Abilities
*   **Focus (Ability Token):** Grants +3% Critical Chance for 20 seconds. This ability stacks up to 10 times. *Note: Critical Hits increase pollen and damage by 100%.*

## Trivia and Lore

*   Looker Bee was one of the earliest designed characters in the game, initially featuring signature eyes, a green color, and two small legs.
*   It was the first bee to have a Royal Jelly variant that could be obtained solely by turning a bee into it using the expired code 'ThoseEyes'.
*   The Looker Guard (available in the Pro Shop) features a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Looker Bee.
*   Before the nerf on December 26, 2021, Looker Bee shared the same gifted hive bonus as Tabby Bee.
*   Looker Bee was among the first bees introduced to the game alongside Commander Bee, Music Bee, and Photon Bee.
*   Along with Stubborn Bee, Looker Bee is one of the only Rare bees added after the game's initial launch.
*   Its skin design matches that of Commander Bee.
*   Looker Bee is unique among all bees for actively disliking the Sunflower Field.
*   The bee's preferences (liking Mountain Top Field and Clover Field) are theorized to relate to its observational nature—seeking good views or looking for four-leaf clovers. The dislike of the Sunflower Field may be due to blinding sunlight.
