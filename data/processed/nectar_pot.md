# Nectar Pot

The **Nectar Pot** is an item located in the 30 Bee Zone. It serves as a storage unit for various types of [Nectar](/wiki/Nectar "Nectar") buffs, allowing players to save them for later use at the cost of 5 [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").

*Note: Do not confuse this with the [Nectar Condenser](/wiki/Nectar_Condenser "Nectar Condenser"), which is located in the 35 Bee Zone and converts Nectar into a [Nectar Vial](/wiki/Nectar_Vial "Nectar Vial").*

## Mechanics and Storage

The Nectar Pot can store five specific types of nectar: Satisfying Nectar, Motivating Nectar, Refreshing Nectar, Comforting Nectar, and Invigorating Nectar.

*   **Storage Limit:** The player can only store one type of nectar at a time.
*   **Switching Types:** To switch from the currently stored nectar to a new type, the player must first claim (empty) the existing nectar, and then pay an additional 5 tickets to deposit the new type.
*   **Visual Feedback:** When nectar is stored, the pot's color changes to match the stored nectar, and the icon updates accordingly. The amount of nectar visually fills the pot as more is stored.
*   **Claiming Nectar:** When the player claims the nectar, emptying the pot, it will drain significantly, leaving only a small visual residue. Importantly, the icon and color of the pot will not change upon claiming.

## Tips for Use

*   **Boosting Preparation:** The Nectar Pot is highly useful for preparing boosts. Store your desired nectar beforehand and then claim/take it out immediately before initiating a boost.
*   **Efficiency:** It is most efficient to store large quantities of nectar at once. Storing small amounts and repeatedly claiming them results in higher ticket loss over time.
*   **Color Strategy (Boosting):**
    *   If you primarily use red bees, store Invigorating or Refreshing Nectar before boosting.
    *   If you primarily use blue bees, store Motivating or Comforting Nectar before boosting.
    *   If your bees are mostly colorless, Satisfying Nectar is the best choice.
    *   For mixed-color bee populations, Satisfying Nectar is generally recommended, though field type may influence the optimal choice.
*   **Log Off Strategy:** If you have a surplus of nectar and plan to log off, storing it in the pot ensures it remains available for use upon your next login.
*   **Maximizing Harvest:** You can instruct your bees to harvest a planter for several hours to achieve maximum nectar yield, which can then be stored in the Nectar Pot for later strategic usage.

## Trivia

*   **Historical Location:** The Nectar Pot is situated in the location previously occupied by the [Boost Market](/wiki/Boost_Market "Boost Market").
*   **Offline Behavior:** If a player logs out and rejoins without any nectar stored, the pot's color and icon will revert to the last type of nectar they had stored. If the player has never used the Nectar Pot, it will display a random nectar type upon login.
