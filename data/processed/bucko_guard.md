# Bucko Guard

![Bucko Guard](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/22/Bucko_Guard.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403020330)

The **Bucko Guard** is a piece of armor forged by the leader of Blue Bees and is purchasable in the Blue HQ. It is equipped on the right shoulder.

> "A piece of armor forged by the leader of Blue Bees!"

## Stats
*   +150,000 Capacity
*   +35% Blue Pollen
*   +10% Defense
*   +75% Critical Power
*   +2 Blue Bee Attack
*   +10% Blue Bomb Pollen

## Acquisition Details

**Cost:**
*   30,000,000 Honey
*   10 Blue Extracts
*   100 Blueberries
*   5 Glue
*   75 Moon Charms

**Crafting Ingredients:**
*   30,000,000 Honey
*   10 Blue Extracts
*   100 Blueberries
*   5 Glue
*   75 Moon Charms

## Trivia
*   This guard represents the Bucko Bee.
*   It is one of three guards available in the Blue HQ; the others are the Blue Guard and the Elite Blue Guard.
*   The Bucko Guard’s red counterpart is the Riley Guard.
*   **Historical Note:** Before the 2018-11-25 update, this guard cost 60,000,000 honey and required no crafting materials.
*   This is considered the highest tiered guard available in the Blue HQ.
*   If a player were to craft the necessary ingredients (excluding the base item), it would require 30,000,000 honey, 2,025 blueberries, 225 royal jellies, 1,650 pineapples, and 1,425 strawberries.
