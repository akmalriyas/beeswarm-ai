# Star Treat

[![Star Treat](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/Star_Treat.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041522)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/Star_Treat.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041522)

_"Turns a bee into a Gifted bee!"_

A **Star Treat** is a special type of [treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that, when fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), raises its [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") by 1,000 and transforms the bee into its [gifted form](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee"). It is unique in that it is guaranteed to turn any fed bee gifted.

It is also one of the few ways to obtain specific [Event bees](/wiki/Bees/Event "Bees/Event"), such as ![Gingerbread Bear](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ae/Gingerbread_Bear.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404041818)[Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") and ![Aged Gingerbread Bear](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Aged Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear").

## Ways to Obtain
*   **Purchase:** Buy from [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog") for 10,000 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake").
*   **Ticket Tent:** Purchase inside the [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent") for 1,000 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
*   **Memory Match:** Obtain through playing [Night Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Night_Memory_Match "Memory Match"), [Extreme Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Extreme_Memory_Match "Memory Match"), or [Winter Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Winter_Memory_Match "Memory Match") (exceptionally rare).
*   **Wind Shrine:** Given by the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") if the player already owns a [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee") after purchasing the [Windy Bee Pack](/wiki/Robux_Shop "Robux Shop").

### Drops
*   An exceptionally rare drop from [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick"), [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab"), and [Fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies").
*   Capturing [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick") for the 100th time guarantees a Star Treat.
*   An exceptionally low chance of dropping from [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower").
*   An extremely rare drop from [Festive Planters](/wiki/Festive_Planter "Festive Planter").

### Quests
*   Completing all quests for [Onett](/wiki/Onett "Onett"), [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear "Mother Bear"), and [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear "Panda Bear").
*   Completing 750 [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear") quests rewards a Star Treat. Additionally, every 500 quests after the 750th Brown Bear quest also grants one, and his 1000th quest awards five Star Treats.

### Outdated Ways
*   Buying the Gingerbread Cub Bundle in [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog") (1 if no cub buddy owned, 2 if one was already owned).
*   An extremely low chance of obtaining it from [Winter Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match#Winter_Memory_Match "Memory Match").
*   Completing the 15th [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") quest rewards a Star Treat if the player already owns a cub buddy.
*   An exceptionally rare drop from [Festive Nymphs](/wiki/Festive_Nymph "Festive Nymph").
*   An extremely rare drop from the [Stockings](/wiki/Stockings "Stockings").
*   Opening certain [Gift Boxes](/wiki/Gift_Boxes "Gift Boxes"): The [Mondo Gift Box](/wiki/Gift_Boxes "Gift Boxes") provided 1 Star Treat during Beesmas in 2020, 2021, and 2022.
*   Buying it from Bee Bear's Catalog for 10,000 Snowflakes (Note: This was a duplicate entry of the current method).

## Trivia
*   Along with [Atomic Treat](/wiki/Atomic_Treat "Atomic Treat"), Star Treat provides the highest bond increase per treat (1,000 bond). This is followed by [Neonberry](/wiki/Neonberry "Neonberry") and [Aged Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear "Aged Gingerbread Bear") (500 bond), then [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") and [Moon Charms](/wiki/Moon_Charm "Moon Charm") (250 bond).
*   Star Treat, along with Aged Gingerbread Bear, is one of the only treats that cannot be obtained from sprouts.
*   Historically, Star Treat used to grant 100 bond instead of the current 1,000.
*   Feeding a Star Treat to a gifted [Bear Bee](/wiki/Bear_Bee "Bear Bee") has a 1/3 chance of spawning a Bear Bee Offer [sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker").
