## Overview
The **Werewolf's Cave** is a location situated beyond the [Honey Bee Gate], positioned between the [Cactus Field] and the [Pumpkin Patch]. It serves as the lair for a werewolf and two spider-like creatures known as [Cave Monsters], which actively attempt to kill players inside. At the end of the cave, a valuable ![Gold Egg](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/50/Gold_Egg.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404043612) token can be found.

## Strategy and Mechanics
**Acquiring the Gold Egg:**
To retrieve the gold egg efficiently, players should prepare gear that grants haste, such as [Boots], [Guards], [Oil], [Bear Morph], and stacks of [Haste] or Haste+. The recommended strategy is to run the entire length of the cave by jumping continuously to avoid engaging the monsters. While extra boosts are not mandatory, successful jumps may allow players to obtain the egg even without preparation.

**Alternative Methods:**
*   **Glitch Exploitation:** Occasionally, a game glitch occurs where the Cave Monsters fail to spawn or lag significantly, making it easy to collect the gold egg without any preparation.
*   **Haste Boosts:** Haste+ can be obtained using the [Royal Jelly Dispenser] in the [Star Hall], through specific [codes], or by defeating the cave monsters (though this latter method is challenging).

**Exiting Safely:**
If a player has [Pollen] in their bag and wishes to preserve it, they must exit the Werewolf's Cave using a [Whirligig].

## Environmental Details
The Werewolf's Cave provides unique views of the surrounding environment:
*   A sprout located in the [Mountain Top Field] will have its light beam visible inside the cave.
*   Weather conditions from the Mountain Top Field, such as clouds or rain, may also be visible within the cavern.
*   Special weather events like Honeystorms, snow storms, and mythic meteor showers are viewable while inside the cave.
