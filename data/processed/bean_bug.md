# Bean Bug

The **Bean Bug** is a passive mob that can randomly appear in any field (except the Ant Field). It is often found spawning in groups of three or more, though they later split up.

## Mechanics and Spawning

### Location
All fields except the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field "Ant Field").

### Respawns & Summoning
Bean Bugs respawn approximately every 30 minutes, or can be manually spawned via the Wind Shrine by donating 50–100 basic eggs or a Honey Bee egg. They can also be manually spawned by Onett (Developer).

### Abilities and Tokens
When they spawn, Bean Bugs spit 1–3 [Jelly Beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans") tokens in volleys over the field. These tokens are temporary, lasting much less time than those from regular Jelly Beans, and can be picked up by any player. Rarely, a Bean Bug may also spawn a Standing Bean Bug Sticker.

After 7–15 volleys of Jelly Beans, the bugs hop to another location on the field to continue spitting beans. This process repeats until they despawn.

Bean Bugs aid players in collecting [Pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") and [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), as their tokens can boost a player’s [Critical Chance](/wiki/System_Page#Critical_Chance "System Page"), [Instant Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Instant_Conversion "System Page"), and other stats.

### Passive Nature
Bean Bugs are passive mobs; they cannot be damaged, nor can they attack players.

## History and Lore

The beans were utilized in several limited-time questlines:
*   **Sun Bear:** Used to receive 250 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
*   **Bee Bear (2019):** Part of a limited event questline.
*   **Spirit Bear:** Also featured in the Spirit Bear's questline.

## Trivia

*   **Passive Mobs:** Along with [Fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies"), [Frogs](/wiki/Frog "Frog"), and various chicks, Bean Bugs are one of the few passive mobs that cannot deal damage to players.
*   **Introduction:** They were first introduced in the 2019-09-28 Update when Sun Bear accidentally released them into the game world.
*   **Movement:** Bean Bugs are among the earliest mobs to utilize jumping for movement, with frogs being the second. (Chicks technically jump only when entering their egg).
*   **Getting Stuck:** They can get stuck in various areas (such as the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), or solid objects while jumping) and remain immobile until they despawn.
*   **Token Producers:** Bean Bugs and fireflies are among the only passive mobs capable of producing [Ability Tokens](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens").
*   **Debuffs:** While not a primary debuffer, Bean Bugs contribute to the group of mobs (along with Stick Bug, Snowbear, and Mechsquito) that can spawn negative effects. They are known for spawning spoiled Jelly Beans.
*   **Intelligence:** As confirmed by Bee Bear, Bean Bugs possess limited intelligence and do not understand concepts like "naughty" or "nice."
*   **Events:** They were featured in special events, such as being spawned as "The Bean Bug Brigade" at the end of Beesmas 2020, only to return later that year "Awoke from Hibernation."
