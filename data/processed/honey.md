# Honey

[![The honey icon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c6/Honey.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410071605)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c6/Honey.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410071605)

**Honey** is the primary currency in Bee Swarm Simulator.

## Overview

### Usage
Honey serves as a versatile resource used to purchase various items and participate in game mechanics:

*   **Items:** Tools, Bags, Tickets, Basic Eggs, Royal Jellies, Treats, Hive Slots.
*   **Accessories & Gear:** Guards and Gliding Tools (listed under Accessories).
*   **Game Modes:** Playing Memory Match.
*   **Boost Market:** Honey, when used alongside Tickets, could be spent on Market Boosts from the Boost Market (this feature has since been removed).

Note that the price of many consumables increases with each purchase, such as Basic Eggs.

### Obtaining Honey
Honey can be acquired through several methods:

**Conversion & Collection:**
*   The primary method is converting **Pollen** into Honey at a Hive or using an Instant Converter/Micro-Converter.
*   Honey tokens appear when collecting pollen in fields and can also be obtained from Honeystorms.
*   The Mythic Meteor Shower awards Honey tokens upon catching a meteor.

**Passive & Active Sources:**
*   Completing Quests, redeeming Codes, defeating Mobs, or collecting Sparkles and Goo from Fields.
*   Certain bees generate honey:
    *   Vicious Bee's Impale ability spawns a honey token when hitting a Mob.
    *   Festive Bee's festive gift ability always spawns Honey tokens.
    *   Other Bees may provide a Honey Gift or a Token Link.

**Instant Conversion:**
Certain items and actions allow for instant conversion of Pollen to Honey:
*   Specific Hats, Guards, and Amulets can instantly convert a percentage of pollen into honey.
*   Popping bubbles, spawning flames, or utilizing field conversions (like Field Winds) can result in instant conversion when collecting a Bomb token produced by a bee.

**Special Sources:**
*   The player can earn Honey for participating in the Ant Challenge.
*   The Honey Dispenser is available to members of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club.
*   Catching falling Coconut or Beesmas Light (during Beesmas) grants honey.

## Conversion Rate Mechanics

Bees convert Pollen into Honey at a set rate. For instance, a Basic Bee produces 80 honey in 4 seconds, resulting in a base conversion rate of 20 honey per second.

**Rate Modifiers:**
The production rate can be increased by Badges, Accessories, Gamepasses (from the Robux Shop), Enzymes, and Science Enhancement. These bonuses stack additively. There is currently no convert rate cap in the game.
