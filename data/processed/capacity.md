# Capacity

**Capacity** is a core mechanic that determines how much pollen the player can store at one time. Capacity can be increased through various methods, including buffs, items, stickers, and amulets. Increases can be applied as static amounts (addition) or as multipliers (multiplication or percentage).

The player's current capacity is displayed in the top-middle section of the screen, labeled "Pollen," and also on their bag. The overall Capacity Multiplier stat can be viewed on the System Page.

## Downgrades (Debuffs)

### Robo Bear Challenge Debuff
The Robo Bear Challenge applies a capacity debuff that reduces the player's capacity. This multiplier starts at x0.1 in Round 1 and increases by 0.015 per round, reaching a total of x0.46 Capacity Multiplier by the final rounds.

### Downgrade Upgrades
Certain upgrades purchased with Cogs during the Robo Bear Challenge can reduce capacity.

**Common Upgrades:**
*   **Overfit (Red):** Starts at x0.9 Capacity, maxes at 10 upgrades with x0.5 Capacity.
*   **Sharpen:** Starts at x0.95 Capacity, maxes at 10 upgrades with x0.75 Capacity.
**Rare Upgrades:**
*   **Crypto:** Starts at x0.85 Capacity, maxes at 3 upgrades with x0.55 Capacity.
**Epic Upgrades:**
*   **Malware:** Starts at x0.75 Capacity, maxes at 3 upgrades with x0.25 Capacity.
**Legendary Upgrades:**
*   **Overclock:** Grants x0.75 Capacity Multiplier.

## Trivia

*   **Maximum Static Capacity:** The highest possible static capacity (excluding multipliers) is 4,807,000. This is achieved by combining several high-capacity items and upgrades: Mondo B.B.M. Mask (+250K), Coconut Canister (+1M), Crimson Guard (+250K), Cobalt Guard (+250K), Coconut Belt (+350K), Moon Amulet (+250K), Stick Bug Amulet (max +300K), Cog Amulet (max +250K), two Berets (+27K), three Single Mittens (+45K), three Beesmas Tree Hats (+105K), Festive Wreath (+110K), Festive Nymph Blessing (max +250K), Robo Party Blessing (max +100K), RAM upgrade maxed (+750K), Grandmaster Ability Badge (+250K), and all Sticker Stack Effects (max +270K).
*   **Maximum Potential Capacity:** If all percentage multipliers were applied to the maximum static capacity, the player could achieve a total of 15,704,500 Capacity. With all available multipliers active, the theoretical maximum is over 771 billion Capacity. This extreme capstone requires replacing certain items (e.g., Mondo B.B.M. Mask with Diamond Mask for x3), utilizing Supreme Star Amulets (x2.5), and stacking Gifted Hive Bonuses (x1.1 & x1.2).
*   **Minimum Possible Capacity:** The lowest possible capacity is 0.7734357, though this value cannot be displayed in-game. This occurs by equipping the Pouch (+200) while simultaneously applying every available Robo Bear Challenge upgrade that reduces capacity and having no other capacity increases or multipliers active.
*   **Code Buffs:** The Capacity Buff, Field Code Buff, Field Capacity Buff, and the x2 Pollen Boost are unique buffs exclusive to certain codes.
