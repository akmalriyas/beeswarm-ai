# Blue Teleporter

The **Blue Teleporter** is a portal located inside the first floor of the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ").

## Mechanics and Usage

When activated, the Blue Teleporter instantly teleports the player behind the Red Cannon and next to the Ticket Tent. This transportation is strictly one-way; therefore, the destination teleporter cannot be used to travel back into the Blue HQ.

To activate the portal:
*   **PC/Laptop:** The player must walk onto the white teleport pad and press the "E" key.
*   **Mobile:** The player must walk onto the pad and tap the button that appears at the top of the screen.

## Trivia

*   Similar to its Red Counterpart, the Blue Teleporter previously featured an animation where rings would revolve in circles. However, this animation can sometimes become bugged, causing the rings to slow down until they completely stop.
*   There is a known bug where tapping the teleporter multiple times within a very short timeframe increases the number of times the player passes through the portal, even if they only intended to use it once.
