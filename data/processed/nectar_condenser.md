# Nectar Condenser

The Nectar Condenser is a machine introduced in the January 12, 2024 update. It allows players to convert raw nectar into usable nectar vials.

## Description and Usage

This device is located past the Windy Bee Gate near the Wind Shrine. The player uses it to condense 12 hours of any type of nectar into a single Nectar Vial, which holds 4 hours of that specific nectar type. This vial can be used at any time in-game and grants the corresponding duration of nectar benefit.

**Note:** The Nectar Condenser cannot be used to create Nectar Shower Vials. It is also not to be confused with the Nectar Pot (located in the 30 Bee Zone), which is used only for storing one type of nectar at a time.

## Mechanics and Stats

| Statistic | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Requirement** | 35 Bees |
| **Cooldown** | 5 seconds |
| **Input** | Raw Nectar (12 hours) |
| **Output** | Nectar Vial (4 hours) |
| **Location Access** | Windy Bee Gate area |

### Sticker Drop Chance
When condensing nectar, there is a $\frac{1}{25}$ (4%) chance to drop a corresponding sticker. The location where the sticker drops depends on the type of nectar condensed:

*   **Refreshing:** Coconut Field
*   **Satisfying:** Sunflower Field
*   **Motivating:** Rose Field
*   **Comforting:** Dandelion Field
*   **Invigorating:** Pepper Patch

### Vial Limit
If a player already possesses 10 or more vials of the specific nectar type they are attempting to condense, a notification will appear stating: "You already have 10 or more [nectar type] Vials."

## Trivia

*   By default, the Nectar Condenser model is purple and displays a Motivating Nectar decal. After its first use, it will always display the last nectar type condensed by the player.
*   The hitbox of the machine differs from its visual shape. Players can stand slightly off-center without falling, likely due to shared placeholder models during testing.
*   While players cannot use the Condenser without 35 bees, they can reach it by jumping off the roof of the Red HQ and gliding down.
