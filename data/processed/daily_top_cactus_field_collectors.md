# Daily Top Cactus Field Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Cactus Field Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator, viewable through the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page. This leaderboard tracks how much pollen players have collected specifically in the Cactus Field on a given day.

### Mechanics and Rules
*   **Scope:** The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, but users can scroll to view up to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

### Rewards
Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 receive a **Cactus Field Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 receive 25 **Tickets**.
