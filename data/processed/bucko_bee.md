# Bucko Bee

Bucko Bee is a worker bee, distinct from the NPC Gifted Bucko Bee. It is known as the leader of the Blue bees and has historically been a rival to Riley Bee. Its tenacity is considered its greatest strength.

**Classification & Appearance**
*   **Rarity:** Epic
*   **Color:** Blue
*   **Base Stats:** Energy: 30 | Speed: 15.4 | Attack: 5
*   **Color Scheme:** Skin #1b2a35, #3b8ed1, #1b2a35

Bucko Bee is categorized as a [Blue](Category:Blue) [Epic bee](Bees/Epic).

**Preferences**
*   **Favorite Treat:** Blueberries
*   **Likes:** Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest
*   **Dislikes:** Strawberry Field, Rose Field

*   +10% Movespeed

*   +4 Attack
*   +7 Gather Amount

**Gifted Hive Bonus:** +20% Blue Field Capacity.

## Trivia
*   As per the description, Bucko Bee is the rival and counterpart of Riley Bee. Both bees are leaders of their respective colors.
*   Bucko Bee shares the same skin colors as Bumble Bee.
*   A giant model of this bee is featured on top of the entrance of the Blue HQ.
*   The gifted version of Bucko Bee appears as a Quest Bee in the top floor of the Blue HQ.
*   Bucko Bee is considered the third strongest blue bee, ranking behind Vicious Bee and Cobalt Bee.
*   The Bucko Guard unit is based on Bucko Bee.
*   Bucko Bee, Buoyant Bee, and Bumble Bee are the only Blue bees that like at least three fields. Furthermore, Bucko Bee is the only Blue bee to dislike two fields.
