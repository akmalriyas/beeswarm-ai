# Bee Attack Mechanic

[![ Attacking a Mantis](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/88/MantisGettingAttacked.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200627040242)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/88/MantisGettingAttacked.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20200627040242) [](/wiki/File:MantisGettingAttacked.jpg)

The **Bee Attack** mechanic causes bees to attack mobs within a radius. When attacking, bees deploy a small red laser (presumably a stinger) to deal damage based on their Bee Attack Power.

### Core Mechanics and Stats

Bee attack boosts are categorized into five types:
1. Regular Bee Attack
2. Colorless Bee Attack
3. Red Bee Attack
4. Blue Bee Attack
5. Bee Attack Multiplier

**Base Bee Attack:** This refers to the total attack of a single bee before any multipliers, but after all additive bonuses have been applied.

**Calculation Order:** For an increased stat, additives (+ bee attack and + colored/blue/red bee attack) are summed first, and then the Bee Attack Multiplier is applied to that sum.

*Example:* A Lion Bee with 10 base attack gains +3 regular bee attack and +1 colorless bee attack from items. Its Base Bee Attack is $14$.
*Example (Multipliers):* A Brave Bee has a base attack of 5. It receives a +2 bonus from its Gifted Hive Bonus and Porcelain Port-O-Hive, bringing the total to 7. With a $\times 1.5$ bee attack multiplier (Gifted buff), its final displayed attack stat is $10.5$.

**Starting Attack:**
*   The starting Bee Attack for all bees ranges from 0 to 10.
*   Lion Bees have the highest starting attack (10).
*   Baby Bees have the lowest starting attack (0); however, even if their attack stat is raised, they remain unable to attack mobs.

## Improving Bee Attack

Bee attack can be improved through various methods, with specific bonuses available for each of the five categories (Regular, Colorless, Red, Blue, and Multiplier).

### Regular Bee Attack
*   **Ability Tokens:** Collecting a Rage ability token grants +1 bee attack, stacking up to 4 times (maximum +4).
*   **Brave Bees:** Gifted Brave Bees grant a bonus of +1 bee attack.
*   **Amulets:** King Beetle Amulet, Ant Amulet, and Stick Bug Amulets have a chance to grant +1 bee attack.
*   **Boots:** Hiking Boots and Beekeeper's Boots both grant a bonus of +1 bee attack.
*   **Clogs/Boots:** Coconut Clogs grant +2 bee attack, while Gummy Boots grant +3 bee attack.

### Colorless Bee Attack
*   **Belts:** Mondo Belt Bag, Honeycomb Belt, and Petal Belt all grant +1 colorless bee attack.
*   **Guards:** Brave Guard grants a bonus of +1 colorless bee attack.
*   **Amulets:** Cog Amulet (Gold+) has a chance to grant +1 or +2 colorless bee attack.

### Red Bee Attack
*   **Guards:**
    *   Red Guard and Elite Red Guard: Grant +1 red bee attack each.
    *   Riley Guard: Grants +2 red bee attack.
    *   Crimson Guard: Grants +3 red bee attack.
*   **Amulets:**
    *   King Beetle Amulet: Has a chance to grant +1 red bee attack.
    *   Stick Bug Amulet: Has a chance to grant +1 or +2 red bee attack.
    *   Cog Amulet (Gold+): Has a chance to grant +1 or +2 red bee attack.

### Blue Bee Attack
*   **Guards:**
    *   Blue Guard and Elite Blue Guard: Grant +1 blue bee attack each.
    *   Bucko Guard: Grants +2 blue bee attack.
    *   Cobalt Guard: Grants +3 blue bee attack.
*   **Amulets:**
    *   King Beetle Amulet: Has a chance to grant +1 blue bee attack.
    *   Stick Bug Amulet: Has a chance to grant +1 or +2 blue bee attack.
    *   Cog Amulet (Gold+): Has a chance to grant +1 or +2 blue bee attack.
*   **Sticker Stack:** Adding the Cop and Robber grants +0.5 Blue Bee Attack.

### Bee Attack Multiplier
*   **Rage Bees:** Gifted Rage Bees grant +10% bee attack multiplier.
*   **Items/Buffs:**
    *   Using a Stinger grants $\times 1.5$ bee attack multiplier for 30 seconds.
    *   Flame Heat grants a bonus of +5–25% depending on the stack level.
    *   Fire Mask grants +15% bee attack multiplier.
    *   Demon Mask grants +25% bee attack multiplier.
    *   Emergency Coconut Shield (Passive Ability) grants $\times 1.25$ bee attack multiplier for 10 seconds.
    *   Coconut Combo buff provides up to +50% bee attack at 100 stacks.
    *   Rose Field Market Boost grants +10% bee attack multiplier for 30 minutes (unobtainable).
    *   Invigorating Nectar grants a bonus of +1% to +10% bee attack multiplier.
*   **Amulets:**
    *   Stick Bug Amulet: Has a chance to grant +1–10% bee attack, depending on the amulet's tier.
    *   Cog Amulet: Has a chance to grant +3–15% bee attack, depending on the amulet's tier.
*   **Sticker Stack:**
    *   Foward Facing Aphid: Grants +1% Bee Attack.
    *   Walking Stick Nymph: Grants +1% Bee Attack.
    *   Theatrical Intruder: Grants +1% Bee Attack.
    *   Tough Potato: Grants +1% Bee Attack.
    *   Festive Pea: Grants +1% Gifted Bee Attack.
    *   Cyan Hilted Sword: Grants +2% Bee Attack.
    *   Stick Cub Skin: Grants +1% Bee Attack.
    *   Festive Nymph Blessing: Provides up to +5% bee attack.

### Robo Bear Challenge Upgrades
The Robo Bear Challenge provides buffs that either add or multiply Bee Attack, depending on the purchased upgrade. These buffs are only active during a round of the challenge.

**Common Upgrades:**
*   Sharpen: Starts at $\times 1.2$ Bee Attack and can max out at 10 upgrades for $\times 3$ Bee Attack.

**Rare Upgrades:**
*   Blue Screen: Grants $\times 1.03$ Bee Attack and Blue Bee Attack for every blue boost stack, maxing out at $\times 1.3$ Bee Attack and Blue Bee Attack.
*   Equalize: Grants +1 Bee Attack and $\times 1.25$ Ungifted Bee Attack.
*   Saturate: Starts with $\times 1.25$ Blue and Colorless Bee Attack, but $\times 0.8$ Red Bee Attack. Maxes at 3 upgrades for $\times 1.75$ Blue and Colorless Bee Attack and $\times 0.4$ Red Bee Attack.
*   SSD: White: Lowers Bee Attack, starting at $\times 0.8$ Bee Attack and maxing out at 3 upgrades for $\times 0.6$ Bee Attack.

**Epic Upgrades:**
*   Fission: Grants +1 Bee Attack.
*   Furnace: Does not improve Bee Attack but grants $\times 1.5$ Flame Damage.
*   Inject: Grants +2 Blue Bee Attack, +1 Colorless Bee Attack, and $\times 1.25$ Impale Damage.
*   Malware: Starts at +3 Bee Attack and can max out at 3 upgrades for +9 Bee Attack.
*   NFT: Lowers Bee Attack by granting -3 Bee Attack.
*   Pop-Up: Grants +2 Blue Bee Attack.

**Legendary Upgrades:**
*   Bluetooth: Gives $\times 2$ Blue Bee Attack.
*   Codec: Lowers Bee Attack, granting $\times 0.8$ Bee Attack.

### Other Damage Modifiers
These methods improve specific bee attacks that do not contribute to the standard Regular, Colorless, Red, Blue, or Multiplier stats.

*   **Critical Hits:** Increase damage outcome by the amount of Critical Power (minimum of 100% more damage). Super-crits increase critical hit damage by Super-Crit Power, though they are only 33% as powerful as a pollen super-crit.
*   **Gifted Bees:** Every gifted bee gains $\times 1.5$ bee attack for themselves.
*   **Mutations:** Certain mutations increase an individual bee's attack by +1 or a percentage multiplier (%).
*   **Gummy Morph:** Activating this passive ability grants +300 bonus bee attack specifically to Gummy Bees, along with other buffs.

#### Sticker Stack Specific Bonuses
Certain sticker additions grant specific, non-standard boosts:
*   Flying Brave Bee: Grants +2 Rare Bee Attack.
*   Coiled Snake: Grants +1 Epic Bee Attack.
*   Evil Pig: Grants +1 Epic Bee Attack.
*   Left Facing Ant: Grants +1 Legendary Bee Attack.
