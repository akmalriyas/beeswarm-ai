# All-Time Top Ant Exterminators

The **Global Top Ant Exterminators Leaderboard** is one of the game's leaderboards, tracking players' top scores in the [Ant Challenge]. This leaderboard displays the top 10 positions at any time, though players can scroll down to view scores all the way to the 100th place.

If a player steps on the white pad in front of the board, a message displaying "Your Highscore: X" will appear (where X is the player's current high score).

The structure conceals a secret passage that grants access to an [Ant Pass] and the [Gummy Bee Egg Claim].

## Location
The leaderboard is situated in front of the Ant Challenge room, near the [Ant Pass Dispenser] and the [Free Ant Pass Dispenser].

## Achievements
*   On August 1, 2018, SDMittens was the first user to reach 200 Ant Exterminations.
*   On December 26, 2018, SDMittens was the first user to reach 300 Ant Exterminations.
*   On December 14, 2019, Lipsisas was the first user to reach 400 Ant Exterminations.
*   On January 10, 2020, Zlib was the first user to reach 500 Ant Exterminations.

## Trivia
*   All scores on this leaderboard were reset following the [2018-11-25 update].
*   The lowest score currently recorded on the leaderboard is 530 points, achieved by players ranked from 67th to 100th place.
*   On January 12, 2024, many players on the leaderboard, including Natmab, lost their Top Ant Exterminator high scores. The reason for this data wipe remains unknown.
