# Magic Bean

A **Magic Bean** is an inventory item introduced in the 2018-11-25 update. It can be used to summon a random sprout in the field where the player is standing. When successfully planted, a server-wide message appears stating: 🌱{Username} has planted a (Sprout Rarity) Sprout...🌱

**Cooldown:** 5 seconds
> *Did you perhaps mean [Sprout] or [Festive Bean]?*

## Acquisition Methods

Magic Beans can be obtained through various methods, including purchasing, rewards, drops, and completing quests.

### Purchasing & Trading
*   Purchase for 20 Tickets each in the Magic Bean Shop located on the right side of Stump Field.
*   Purchase via the Robux Shop: 30 Robux for 1 Magic Bean, or 200 Robux for 10 Magic Beans.

### Rewards and Drops
Magic Beans are frequently obtained as rewards from challenges, donations, and game mechanics:
*   As a reward from the Stick Bug Challenge.
*   As a reward from donating to the Wind Shrine.
*   As a reward from completing Memory Match games.
*   **Sticker Stack Rewards:** Adding certain stickers grants 1 Magic Bean each: Forward Facing Aphid, Round Green Critter, Flying Magenta Critter, Blue Triangle Critter, Purple Pointed Critter, Orange Leg Critter, Sprout, Critter in a Stocking, and Festive Pea.
*   Redeeming valid codes (e.g., ClubBean grants 1 Magic Bean if the player is a member of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club).

### Drops
Magic Beans can drop from several sources:
*   Unknown chance when collecting Pollen in fields.
*   An unknown chance from Leaves.
*   As a drop from all Aphids and the Coconut Crab.
*   **Rare Drops:** Rhino Beetle, Ladybug, Spider, Stick Nymphs, Stick Bug, King Beetle, Wild Windy Bee, Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, Mondo Chick, Festive Gift (Ability Tokens), and Festive Mark (Ability Tokens).
*   A chance of dropping from any Sprout, excluding Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
*   As a drop from certain Planters; the rarity depends on the planter type and location.
*   From Stockings.

## Quest Rewards

Various NPCs and quests reward Magic Beans:

**Black Bear:**
*   Rewards 1 Magic Bean for completing: Pumpkins, Please!, Rocky Red Mountain, Rare Red Clover, Okey-Pokey, Capsaicin Collector, Mountain Mix, Variety Fetcher 2, Boss Battles (3 beans), and Weed Wacker 3 (3 beans).
*   Has a chance to reward beans on some repeatable quests.
*   Rewards 1 Magic Bean upon completing the 55th, 120th, 190th, 220th, and 280th quests.

**Brown Bear:**
*   (Outdated) Former repeatable quests occasionally rewarded Magic Beans.

**Science Bear:**
*   Subsidized Agriculture: Yields 1 Magic Bean upon receiving the quest.
*   Mark Mechanics: Rewards 1 Magic Bean and other items.
*   Testing Teamwork: Rewards 3 Magic Beans and other items.

**Polar Bear:**
*   Occasionally gives Magic Beans upon completing one of his quests.

**Spirit Bear:**
*   Out-Questing Questions: Rewards 1 Magic Bean and other items.
*   Memories of Memories: Rewards 3 Magic Beans and other items.
*   Beans Becoming: Grants a Magic Bean when starting the quest.
*   Do You Bee-lieve In Magic?: Rewards 5 Magic Beans and other items.
*   Beeternity: Rewards 5 Magic Beans and other items.
*   A Breath Of Blue: Rewards 10 Magic Beans and other items.
*   Rhubarb That Could Have Been: Rewards 5 Magic Beans and other items.
*   Dreams Of Being A Bee: Rewards 5 Magic Beans and other items.
*   The Sky Over The Stump: Rewards 5 Magic Beans and other items.

**Other Quest Rewards:**

## Outdated Information (Historical Notes)

*   **Science Bear's "Epistemological Endeavor"**: Before its nerf in the 2019-04-05 update, completing this quest rewarded 10 Magic Beans and 100 Tickets.
*   **Festive Nymph**: Was a rare drop from this creature.
*   **Beesmas Events**: Historically, various NPCs (like Onett and Gifted Bucko Bee) gave out Magic Beans during different Beesmas events (2018-2022).
*   **Robux Packs**: Several Robux shop packs historically included Magic Beans (e.g., The Sparkly Starter Pack, Shining Star Pack).
*   **Expired Codes**: Certain expired codes (BloxyCelebration, Breeze, NoobashaBonus, etc.) previously granted 1 Magic Bean.
