This leaderboard tracks players who have collected the most battle points in the game. It displays the top 10 positions at one time, though players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on the leaderboards for extended periods; hackers and exploiters are removed. Battle points are earned by defeating specific mobs within the game.

## Mechanics and Location
The Global Top Battlers leaderboard is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator. It tracks how many battle points players have collected over time. To check your own score, stepping on the white pad in front of the display will show a message stating, "You have x Battle Points."

**Location:**
The leaderboard is situated on the yellow platform, located next to the Panda Bear, in front of the Bamboo Field, and behind the Blue Flower Field.

## Trivia
*   This leaderboard is one of only two leaderboards that existed in the game's initial release; the other being the Global Top Honeymakers Leaderboard.
*   Sugarsmacks12 held the number 1 spot on the global top battlers for three years before being surpassed. This marks the longest sustained tenure at the number one position across all leaderboards in the game.
*   The Bubble Bee Man used to be positioned behind this leaderboard but was later moved to the Bear Gate during the Beesmas 2019 update.
