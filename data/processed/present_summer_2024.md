# Present Event (Summer 2024)

This event brought back the tradition of Presents during Beesmas Summer 2024. The primary function of these presents was to be given to NPCs, allowing them to add [Ornaments](/wiki/Ornaments "Ornaments") to the [Beesmas Tree](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree "Beesmas Tree").

## Acquisition Methods
Presents could be obtained through various activities during the event:

*   **Bee Bear Quests:** Multiple quests from Bee Bear awarded a total of 12 [Presents](/wiki/Present "Present"), along with other rewards.
*   **Bee Bear’s Catalog:** The catalog sold 1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present") for 250 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") and 10 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear").
*   **Onett Hive Claim:** Onett gave 1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present") (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event period.
*   **Galentine Shrine Donation:** Donating Strawberries to the [Galentine Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine#Galentine_Shrine "Wind Shrine") had a chance to give 1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present"), along with other rewards.
*   **Robo Party Rank:** Completing [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party")'s rank 25 for the first time gave 1 [Present](/wiki/Present "Present"), along with other rewards.
