# Cactus Field

[![Cactus Field](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ac/Cactus_Field.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/275?cb=20200407140702)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ac/Cactus_Field.png/revision/latest?cb=20200407140702)

The Cactus Field is a field located past the Honey Bee Gate and beside the Pumpkin Patch. It is protected by the Werewolf.

## Field Details and Resources
The Cactus Field can spawn the following tokens when collecting pollen: honey, treat, strawberry, blueberry, ticket, magic bean, bitterberry, and stinger.

When a Planter is planted in this field, it grants Invigorating Nectar.

A Level 4-7 Rogue Vicious Bee has a chance of spawning in this field. The flowers found here are white, red, and blue, available in single, double, or triple sizes.

## Obtaining Boosts
Various methods can boost pollen collection in the Cactus Field for 15 minutes:

*   **Field Booster:** Using a Field Booster may grant a Cactus Field Boost of +100% pollen.
*   **Glitter:** Glitter adds 100% Cactus Field pollen onto the current boost, provided it is not already maxed out.
*   **Dice:** Field Dice, Smooth Dice, and Loaded Dice can add between +100% and +300% Cactus Field pollen if the current boost isn't maxed out, depending on the dice type used.
*   **Field Winds:** Cactus Field Winds (obtained via Wind Shrine or codes) provide a base of +20% instant conversion and grant an additional +5% instant conversion and +15% pollen per stack. These stacks can accumulate up to 13 times.
*   **Beequip/Amulets:** Icicles Beequip can grant up to +10% Cactus Field pollen, which can be further boosted by the Shell Amulet (granting +5% to +27% depending on rarity).

### Historical Boost Methods (No Longer Possible)
*   **Gifts:** Giving a present to Onett provided Cactus Field Boost x1. Giving a present to Bubble Bee Man provided Cactus Field Boost x4.
*   **Market Purchase:** Purchasing a Cactus Field Market Boost from the Boost Market granted x1.25 Cactus Field Pollen, along with x1.5 Field Capacity, x1.5 Buzz Bomb Pollen, and +50 Convert Amount.
*   **Expired Codes:** Several codes previously provided various levels of boosts (e.g., Arizona gave Cactus Field Boost x4; FourYearFiesta gave Cactus Field Boost x4 and Winds x15).

## Trivia
*   This is the only field where three types of treats can be found naturally in the same place.
*   It is the fourth-smallest field in the game, following the Pumpkin Patch, Strawberry Field, and Stump Field.
*   Players can hide behind the cactus closest to the Pine Tree Forest to avoid attacks from the Werewolf.
*   The Cactus Field is one of six fields where fireflies travel (others include Bamboo Field, Pineapple Patch, Rose Field, Spider Field, and Strawberry Field).
*   It is one of three fields protected by the werewolf (the others being the Pumpkin Patch and Pine Tree Forest).
