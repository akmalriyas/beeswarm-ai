# Mushroom Field

The Mushroom Field is one of the five starting fields available to players in Bee Swarm Simulator. It is the first red field accessible upon starting the game and is protected by a Level 1 Ladybug.

## Mechanics and Boosts

This field provides Motivating Nectar when a Planter is planted here. Players can enhance pollen collection using various methods:

**Boost Methods:**
*   **Red Field Booster:** Grants +300% Mushroom Field pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Adds 100% Mushroom Field pollen to the current boost (if not maxed).
*   **Dice:** Using Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice can add +100% to +300% Mushroom Field pollen for 15 minutes (if not maxed), depending on the dice type.
*   **Field Winds:** Obtained via Wind Shrine or codes. They provide a base of +20% instant conversion and grant +5% instant conversion and +25% pollen per stack, stacking up to 15 times.
*   **King Beetle Amulet:** If equipped with a Mushroom Field Boost, it can increase the field boost by +1% to +100%.

**Historical/Obsolete Boost Methods (No longer possible):**
*   Gifting certain NPCs: Gifted Riley Bee (x3), Onett (x1), Bubble Bee Man (x4).
*   Completing specific quests: Gifted Riley Bee's Egg Hunt 2019 Quest (x7, capped at x5).
*   Purchasing Market Boosts from the Boost Market: Provides x1.5 Field Capacity, x1.25 Mushroom Field Pollen, x1.1 Red Pollen, and x1.25 Convert Rate At Hive.
*   Redeeming specific expired codes (examples include MischiefMaker for x6, RoyalRobzi for x5, etc.).

## Trivia

*   The Mushroom Field contains a small parkour course that can be used to obtain a royal jelly.
*   Occasionally, the Bubble Wand model on Blue HQ may grow a face and blow sparkles onto this field (along with the Blue Flower Field, Clover Field, and Dandelion Field).
*   Stick Bug is found on the tallest mushroom, where players can start the Stick Bug Challenge if they have given Stick Bug a translator.
*   A visual glitch occurred after the 2018-11-25 update where Stick Bug sometimes fell off the tallest mushroom into or near the Dandelion Field; this was patched shortly after.
*   This field is one of only two fields in the Starter Zone that features a Ladybug (the other being Clover Field).
*   The Yellow Walking Wiggly Person sticker cannot spawn in this field.
