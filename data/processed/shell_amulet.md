# Shell Amulet

![Digital Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/27/Digital_Bee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20230415203844) | **Disclaimer:** This content is based on datamined information, and details may be inaccurate or outdated.
*Datamined information regarding the Shell Amulet's possible stats and mechanics.*
*Date of data extraction: December 19th, 2024*

The **Shell Amulet** is an amulet obtained by defeating a [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail") in the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"). The Shell Amulet has multiple tiers, and higher tiers are unlocked by achieving specific kill counts of Stump Snails.

Amulets grant buffs to the owner and always increase both [Goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo") and [Stump Field Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Stump_Field_Pollen "System Page") as they ascend in tier. Higher quality amulets provide more potent and diverse buffs.

The amulet's quality increases linearly with the number of Stump Snail kills until it reaches its maximum possible quality.

## Trivia

*   The Shell Amulet is the second amulet to be dropped by a mob, following the [King Beetle Amulet](/wiki/King_Beetle_Amulet "King Beetle Amulet"), which is dropped by [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle").
*   It is unique among amulets for boosting both [Goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo") and [Goo Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Goo_Conversion "System Page"), as well as providing a direct boost to [Defense](/wiki/System_Page#Defense "System Page").
