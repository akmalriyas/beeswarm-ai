# Ticket Planter

![Ticket Planter](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5e/Ticket_Planter.png/revision/latest?cb=20230406145659)

A single-use planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. It is guaranteed to spawn a Puffshroom upon harvest and grants bonus Tickets.

> "Grows in about 2 hours of playtime. Grants Tickets and spawns a Puffshroom!"

## Mechanics and Drops

A player can store up to 100 Ticket Planters, but only one can be active at a time.

When harvested, the planter is guaranteed to spawn a Puffshroom between levels 1–2, which is always a Common Puffshroom.

Claiming the planter yields up to 25 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, one token is guaranteed to be a Ticket (10-150). The remaining tokens are picked randomly from the following non-exhaustive list:

## Acquisition Methods

**Direct Rewards & Purchases:**
*   Receiving 3 Ticket Planters from [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear "Dapper Bear") upon first interaction.
*   As a reward for completing certain [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") quests, specifically receiving one per Beequip slot if the player completed Dapper Bear’s quests previously.
*   Receiving 1 Ticket Planter from Bee Bear's "Searching For Snowflakes" quest.
*   As a rare reward from the [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge").
*   Buying the Tempting Ticket Pack from [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog") for 55,555 Snowflakes (rewards 5 planters).

**Drops & Stack Rewards:**
*   As a rare drop from [Sticker Sprouts](/wiki/Sprout#Variants "Sprout").
*   As a Stack Reward when adding the following stickers to the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack"):
    *   [Dapper From Above Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") (rewards 1 planter).
    *   [Ticket Voucher](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") (rewards 1 planter).
*   As a drop from Dapper Bear’s [Samovar](/wiki/Samovar "Samovar") after every fifth use (the 25th use drops Turpentine instead).

*   Completing certain 2021 and 2022 quests from [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear").

*   Using specific expired [Codes](/wiki/Codes "Codes") (e.g., "2Billion").

*   As a drop from [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party") mobs or upon completing the event.

## Trivia

*   The Ticket Planter is one of only two planters guaranteed to spawn a Puffshroom; the other is the [Sticker Planter](/wiki/Sticker_Planter "Sticker Planter").
*   It shares the same base model and decal outline with the [Paper Planter](/wiki/Paper_Planter "Paper Planter").
*   Along with the Sticker Planter, it is one of two planters whose name includes an in-game item.
*   It provides a 2x Nectar multiplier, which is the second-best available in the game (behind the Festive Planter's 3x).
*   The Ticket Planter, Paper Planter, Festive Planter, and Sticker Planter are four single-use planters; among these, the Sticker Planter and Paper Planter are the only ones that can be obtained infinitely.
