# System Page Stats

This page details stats that apply to the player, collecting pollen, and the hive. For stats specific to individual bees, please refer to the [Stats] page.

The **System Page** is accessible by clicking the cog icon in the menu bar (located between the Badges page and the Robux Shop). Here, players can redeem codes, send trade requests, view the Global Leaderboard, use the Cub Console, check player stats, toggle music/pollen display options, manage trading permissions, hide/show other players' bees, and toggle VFX/Auto-Jelly.

Note: Turning off in-game music only silences the background track; sound effects (such as those from Ability Tokens or obtaining Legendary, Mythic, Gifted, or Event Bees) will remain active. To mute all sounds, use the Roblox menu settings.

## Character Stats

The System Page lists many of the player's active stat values. The following stats are detailed below:

### Convert Amount
Boosts the base conversion rate of your bees before multipliers are applied.
*Example:* A Basic Bee with 20 Convert Amount will convert 100 Honey in 4 seconds instead of 80 Honey in 4 seconds.
*Sources:* Can be boosted by amulets, the B.B.M. Mask, the Honey Mask, and certain belts. (Formerly named 'Conversion Amount' until the Beesmas 2019 update).

### Convert Rate
Boosts your bees' conversion output by a percentage.
*Example:* A Basic Bee with 200% Convert Rate will convert 160 Honey in 4 seconds instead of 80 Honey in 4 seconds.
*Sources:* Can be boosted by amulets, Enzymes, Marshmallow Bees, Super Smoothies, Science Enhancement (Buffs & Debuffs), the Conversion Buff, the Honeyday Event buff, the Honey Badge, Gifted Diamond Bee, Yellow Jellybeans, Jellybean Sharing Bonus, certain Stickers in the Sticker Stack, some Beequips, and most Bags. (Formerly named 'Conversion Rate' until the Beesmas 2019 update).

### Convert Rate At Hive
Boosts your bees’ conversion output by a percentage while converting at the hive. This stat stacks with Convert Rate.
*Example:* A level 1 Basic Bee with 200% Convert Rate and 200% Hive Convert Rate will convert 320 Honey in 4 seconds while converting at the hive, but only 160 Honey outside of the hive.
*Sources:* Can be increased with the Pumpkin Badge, Bubble Bloat, and certain items. (Previously "Hive Convert Rate" until the 2021-12-26 update).

### Bonus Convert Amount
Increases the base conversion value of your player's bees.
*Sources:* Can be increased by certain amulets and items.

### Loot Luck
A passive attribute that increases the chances of receiving rewards from defeated mobs. (Formerly named 'Luck' until the 2019-04-05 update).
*Sources:* Can be increased with Baby Love, the Clover Badge, Baby Bee’s Gifted Hive Bonus, and certain items.

### Critical Chance
The chance of your bees hitting stronger or collecting more pollen (more accurately, by how large your Critical Power is).
*Example:* A Basic Bee with 50% Critical Chance will hit with increased damage half of the time.
*Sources:* Can be boosted with amulets, Tropical Drinks, Focus, the Green Jellybean, Gifted Commander Bee, and certain items. The highest critical chance a player can currently have is 88%.

### Critical Power
This stat multiplies how strong a Critical Hit will be. It applies to both fighting and pollen collecting.
*Example:* A Basic Bee with 300% Critical Power (on a critical hit) will hit with the power of 3 Basic Bees (3 damage).
*Sources:* Can be boosted with the Ant Amulet, Melody, the Battle Badge, Gifted Tabby Bee, Gifted Looker Bee, and certain items. The calculated maximum Critical Power is 695%.

### Super-Crit Chance
The possibility for a critical hit to become a Super-Crit. Super-Crits deal 33% more damage and collect twice as much pollen as a regular critical hit, and are instantly converted. A Super-Crit attack will be indicated by a purple color and the **!** sign next to it.
*Sources:* Can be boosted by having a Gifted Precise Bee, Tabby Blessing, Precision, a precise mark, the Bruteforce upgrade in the Robo Bear Challenge, purchasing Bloxiade in the Retro Swarm Challenge, or using a Super Smoothie. The maximum possible Super-Crit Chance is 53% (assuming participation in the Robo Bear Challenge).

### Super-Crit Power
The amount pollen is multiplied by when performing Super-Crits (after applying Critical Power). Super-crit attacks are only increased by 33% as much as Pollen Super-crits.
*Sources:* Can only be boosted by the Whistle and Kazoo Beequips, a Cog Amulet, or temporarily boosted by the Dark Scythe.

### Bee Ability Rate
Increases the speed at which your bees create Ability Tokens.
*Example:* If it takes a bee about 20 seconds to produce a token, with 20% Bee Ability Rate, the bee is likely to take approximately 16 seconds to produce the token.
*Sources:* Can be boosted by Star Amulets, Motivating Nectar, certain masks, items, bee mutations, and Beequips.

### Pollen
Increases pollen collected from all sources (from both bees and tools).
*Example:* With 100% Pollen, the player can collect 1,000 pollen; with 110% Pollen, they can collect 1,100 pollen from the same amount of time and flowers.
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets, Bear Morph, Inspire, Baby Love, Pollen Marks, the Honeyday Event buff, Clouds, Gifted Basic Bee, Gifted Bear Bee, the Periwinkle Jellybean, or certain items.

### White Pollen
Boosts your white pollen collection.
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets, Tropical Drinks, Marshmallow Bees, White Boost, the Spider Badge, the Coconut Badge, Gifted Shocked Bee, the White Jellybean, Wind Boosts, or certain items.

### Red Pollen
Boosts your red pollen collection.
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets, Red Extracts, Red Boost, Flame Heat, the Strawberry Badge, Gifted Rad Bee, or certain items.

### Blue Pollen
Boosts your blue pollen collection.
*Sources:* Can be increased with Amulets, Blue Extracts, Blue Boost, the Bamboo Badge, Gifted Cool Bee, or certain items.

### Bomb Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection from all bombs.
*Example:* If a player collects 5,000 pollen with a Buzz Bomb token, 200% Bomb Pollen will result in collecting 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets, Bomb Combos, Gifted Bomber Bee, Festive Mark, Black Jelly Bean, or certain items.

### Buzz Bomb Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection specifically from Buzz Bombs and Fuzz Bombs.
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets, Gifted Demo Bee, or certain items.

### Red Bomb Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection from Red Bombs.
*Sources:* Can be increased with Amulets, Gifted Rascal Bee, Festive Mark, or certain items.

### Blue Bomb Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection from Blue Bombs.
*Sources:* Can be increased with Amulets, Gifted Frosty Bee, or certain items.

### Bubble Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection from bubbles.
*Example:* If a player collects 5,000 pollen with a bubble, 200% Bubble Pollen will result in collecting 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.
*Sources:* Can be increased with Gifted Bubble Bee, Cog Amulet, Bubble Mask, or Diamond Mask.

### Flame Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection from flames created by bees, ability tokens, and passive abilities.
*Example:* If flames collect 1,000 over time, 200% Flame Pollen will result in collecting 2,000 pollen from the same flowers.
*Sources:* Can be increased with Gifted Fire Bee, Cog Amulet, or the Demon Mask.

### Bee Gather Pollen
Boosts pollen collection for all bees.
*Example:* With 200% Bee Gather Pollen, a Basic Bee will collect 20 pollen in 4 seconds.
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets, Glues, Gifted Music Bee, the x2 Pollen from Bees Gamepass, or certain items.

### Bee Ability Pollen
Boosts pollen collection specifically from all bees' abilities and passives.
*Sources:* Can be increased with Gifted Shy Bee Hive Bonus, certain Beequips, or Sticker Stack.

### Tool Pollen
Boosts your pollen collection when using your tool.
*Example:* With 100% (base) Tool Pollen, the player collects 1,000 pollen at once. With 125% Tool Pollen, the player collects 1,250 pollen at once in the same location.
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets, Glues, Gifted Carpenter Bee, or the Stump Badge, or certain items.

### Instant Conversion
Instantly converts your collected pollen while you are collecting on a field.
*Example:* A Basic Bee with 50% Instant Conversion that collects 10 pollen will instantly convert half of it into Honey (5 Honey and 5 remaining pollen), allowing for continued collection.
*Sources:* Can be boosted with amulets, Enzymes, the Dandelion Badge, the Cactus Badge, Gifted Photon Bee, Festive Blessing buff, or certain items.

### Instant White Conversion
Instantly converts white pollen you collect, in addition to standard Instant Conversion.
*Sources:* Can be boosted with amulets, the Pineapple Badge, Gifted Windy Bee, or the Coconut Canister.

### Instant Red Conversion
Instantly converts red pollen you collect, in addition to standard Instant Conversion.
*Sources:* Can be boosted with amulets, the Mushroom Badge, Gifted Crimson Bee, or certain items.

### Instant Blue Conversion
Instantly converts blue pollen you collect, in addition to standard Instant Conversion.
*Sources:* Can be boosted with amulets, the Blue Flower Badge, Gifted Cobalt Bee, Diamond Mask, or certain items.

### Instant Bomb Conversion
Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's bomb.
*Sources:* Can be temporarily boosted by standing in a festive mark, or permanently increased by owning a Gifted Demon Bee.

### Instant Flame Conversion
Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's summoned flames.
*Sources:* Can be boosted by having the Demon Mask equipped, or you can temporarily gain 50% Instant Flame Conversion by triggering the Scorching Star Passive.

### Instant Bubble Conversion
Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's popped bubbles. (Currently, there are no known ways to obtain this boost).

### Honey Per Pollen
Allows you to generate more Honey from the same amount of pollen, affecting conversion, Instant Converters, and Micro-Converters.
*Example:* With 200% Honey Per Pollen, a player will make 2,000 Honey from 1,000 Pollen.
*Sources:* Can be increased with the Moon Amulet, the Wealth Clock, the Goo Badge, Gifted Gummy Bee, or Nectar.

### Honey At Hive
Increases the amount of Honey you convert when converting at the hive.
*Sources:* Can be boosted with Satisfying Nectar and Balloon Blessing. Diamond Bee's Shimmering Honey directly multiplies this stat.

### Movespeed
The movement speed of your character, measured in studs.
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets, Oil, Haste or Haste+, Bear Morph, Coconut Haste, Gifted Hasty Bee, or boots.

### Movement Collection
Allows the player to collect pollen while walking on flowers. The higher this stat, the greater amount of pollen collected from walking on flowers. This boost also collects Goo if the player walks over goo patches.
*Sources:* Can be obtained by wearing boots or having a Moon Amulet.

### Jump Power
The jump power of your character, measured in studs. (Minimum: 55, Maximum: 97).
*Sources:* Can be increased with Bear Morph, all boots (except Basic Boots), or the Propeller Hat.

### Defense
Reduces the damage you take.
*Example:* If a mob deals 50 damage, 50% Defense will reduce it to 25 damage.
*Sources:* Can be increased with the Shell Amulet, hats, or guards. Having 100% defense means taking no damage and can be achieved by activating the Emergency Coconut Shield passive. The maximum defense without the emergency coconut shield is 60%, achievable via the Demon Mask or Diamond Mask (+35%), both Crimson and Cobalt Guards (+10% each), and a Shell Amulet (+5%). (Originally named Damage Reduction).

### Bond From Treats
Gives additional Bond for your bees when you feed them treats.
*Example:* If you feed 100 normal treats (1,000 total bond) to your bee, 125% Bond From Treats will grant 1,250 bond instead.
*Sources:* Can be increased with the Moon Amulet, Gifted Puppy Bee, or as a Hive Bonus from the Reindeer Antlers beequip.

### Bond From Battle
Gives additional bond for your bees when you are defeating a mob.
*Example:* Normally, scorpions give 15 bond per bee upon defeat. With 150% Bond From Battle, your bees will receive 22.5 Bond instead.
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets.

### Honey From Tokens
Boosts the amount of Honey received from all tokens (Honey Gift, Honey Gift+, Impale, mob loot, Coin Scatter, Honeystorm, etc.).
*Example:* A level 2 Diamond Bee gives 2,000 Honey with its Honey Gift+ token. With 200% Honey From Tokens, it will give 4,000 Honey instead.
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets, Gifted Honey Bee, or certain items.

### Bee Attack
Makes all your bees (regardless of color) deal more damage per hit. It adds one bee attack (Damage) for every point in the stat.
*Example:* With +4 Bee Attack, a Basic Bee attacks with the power of 5 Basic Bees (Base Attack + Bee Attack multiplier).
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets, Rage, Gifted Brave Bee, or certain items/buffs/beequips.

### Red Attack
Same as Bee Attack, but applies only to Red bees.
*Sources:* Can be boosted with amulets or certain items.

### Blue Attack
Same as Bee Attack, but applies only to Blue bees.
*Sources:* Can be boosted with amulets or certain items.

### Colorless Attack
Same as Bee Attack, but applies only to Colorless bees.
*Sources:* Can be boosted with amulets or certain items.

### Attack Multiplier
Multiplies the attack of all bees by a percentage.
*Sources:* Can be increased with Amulets, Stingers, Flame Heat, Emergency Coconut Shield (Passive Abilities), Gifted Rage Bee, or the Demon Mask.

### Goo
Multiplies all collected goo.
*Sources:* Can be increased with the Shell Amulet, Gummy Morph, Gummy Mask, Gummy Boots, Gummy Star, or Gummyballer.

### Goo Conversion
Instantly converts pollen equal to the goo gathered. With a high enough Goo Conversion % and Goo multiplier, this can convert all collected pollen (effectively serving as 100% Instant Conversion).
*Sources:* Can be increased by amulets, Gummy Mask, Gummy Morph, or Gummy Star.

### Capacity Multiplier
Gives additional pollen storage capacity based on the percentage value.
*Example:* If your backpack holds 200,000 pollen, a 200% Capacity Multiplier allows it to hold up to 400,000 instead.
*Sources:* Can be increased with amulets, Sunflower Badge, Quest Badge, Bumble Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, Buoyant Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, and certain items. (Note: If the multiplier is a decimal percentage, it rounds but does not affect capacity).

### White Field Capacity
Gives additional pollen storage based on the percentage of white flowers in a field.
*Example:* With 15,000,000 base Capacity and 180% White Field Capacity (in a Spider Field), the total capacity is 27,000,000.
*Sources:* Can be increased with Gummy Mask, Exhausted Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, Polar Bear's Ornament, and other inventory items. (Note: If the multiplier is a decimal percentage, it rounds but does not affect capacity).

### Red Field Capacity
Gives additional pollen storage based on the percentage of red flowers in a field.
*Example:* With 15,000,000 base Capacity and 150% Red Field Capacity (on an all-red flower field), the total capacity is 22,500,000.
*Sources:* Can be increased with Demon Mask, Riley Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, Gifted Riley Bee (NPC) Ornament, the Rose Badge, and other inventory items. (Note: If the multiplier is a decimal percentage, it rounds but does not affect capacity).

### Blue Field Capacity
Gives additional pollen storage based on the percentage of blue flowers in a field.
*Example:* With 15,000,000 base Capacity and 200% Blue Field Capacity (on an all-blue flower field), the total capacity is 30,000,000.
*Sources:* Can be increased with buffs like Bucko Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus, Gifted Bucko Bee (NPC) Ornament, the Pine Tree Badge, and other inventory items. (Note: If the multiplier is a decimal percentage, it rounds but does not affect capacity).

### Convert Total
The total conversion amount of all your bees combined (after applying conversion modifiers).

### Gather Total
The total gathering amount of all your bees combined (before pollen modifiers).

### Ticket Chance
A stat boost that increases the chances of obtaining Tickets.
*Sources:* Can be obtained through the Moon Amulet, Festive Wreath, stacking Ticket Vouchers, and the now unobtainable Ticket Chance gamepass.

### Attack Total
The total attack power of all your bees combined (after applying attack modifiers).

## Auto-Jelly Settings

The **Auto-Jelly Settings** are found at the bottom of the System Page. They allow players to use Royal Jellies or Star Jellies to automatically roll for specific bee requirements until they are fulfilled, helping players obtain desired bees quickly.

There are options defining the target requirement:
*   **Until Legendary:** Jellies auto-roll until you obtain a Legendary or Mythic Bee.
*   **Until Mythic:** Jellies auto-roll until you obtain a Mythic Bee.
*   **Require Gifted:** Jellies auto-roll until you obtain a Gifted or Mythic Bee.
*   **Require Mutation:** Jellies auto-roll until you obtain a Mutated or Mythic Bee.

Multiple options can be active simultaneously, and the jellies will stop when the combined requirement is met (e.g., Legendary + Gifted = Gifted Legendary Bee).

Specific combination rules apply:
*   If "Until Legendary" or both "Until Legendary" and "Until Mythic" are on, it stops at a Legendary or Mythic Bee.
*   If only "Require Gifted" is on, it stops at a Gifted or Mythic Bee.
*   If only "Require Mutation" is on, it stops at a Gifted or Mutated Bee.
*   If both "Require Gifted" and "Require Mutation" are on, it stops at a Gifted Mutated Bee or Mythic Bee.

**Usage Notes:**
1.  If the Mutation option is enabled but the selected bee is not radioactive, a pop-up will prevent auto-rolling for mutation; the jelly must be used one at a time.
2.  When using a Royal Jelly with an auto-jelly setting active, a reminder message appears confirming the auto-jelly status.
3.  **Warning Pop-ups:** Extra warnings appear when selecting extremely rare options: Mythic and Gifted; Mythic and Mutation; Mythic, Gifted, and Mutation; or Legendary, Gifted, and Mutation.
4.  When using a Star Jelly on a bee with an auto-jelly setting, another pop-up reminds the player that the jelly will be used as many times as necessary to fulfill the requirement (even if only "Until Gifted" is selected). If "Require Gifted" is active with a Star Jelly, the warning appears but no action is taken.
