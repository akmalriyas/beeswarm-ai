# Game Update Log

## 2026 Updates

### April 23, 2026
* Extended Beesmas to the end of May.
* Added the last 5 Bee Bear quests, rewarding the new Petal Cub skin.
* Nerfed the requirements of Bee Bear's 14th quest.

### January 30, 2026
* Bug fixes:
    * Fixed issue with frozen Frogs.
    * Fixed issue resulting in kicking players.
* Added badge requirements to the Sticker Printer.

### January 29, 2026
* Sticker Printer accessibility restrictions implemented: The user's Roblox account must be at least 7 days old and be a member of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club to use it. This is intended to combat bot-farming for Star Signs.

### January 16, 2026
* Added 5 more Bee Bear quests, including the 16th Dapper Bear quest, and several petal-related quests to the repeatable quest pools.
* Petal Shurikens now cause blooms they hit to spawn a petal and deal damage to the bloom.
* Added star stickers required in Onett's quest to Bee Bear's Catalog for Snowflake 10,000.
* Mark Duration hive bonuses are now applied to Precise Marks.
* Fixed a bug where pollen from tools multiplier was being applied twice in some cases (this did not affect unique tool effects like Porcelain Dipper's beam, Gummyball, Tide Popper waves, or Dark Scythe).
* Changed Carpenter Bee's gifted hive bonus to be multiplicative (now x1.25 Pollen From Tools, previously +25%).
* Fixed an issue where receiving Galentine's Blessing during the previous Beesmas event prevented obtaining the buff again. Any Strawberries donated prior during that event were refunded upon login.

### December 27, 2025
* Bee Bear's 2025 quests added.
* x2 Honeyday Event! Join to claim 48 hours of x2 Pollen and Convert Rate.

### December 25, 2025 (Beesmas 2025)
**Beesmas is here! Complete festive decorations around the map, with special perks and abilities! Collect Gingerbread Bears and Snowflakes to purchase limited-time offers.**
* **Soft Launch:** Returning Beesmas quests were introduced. Completing these quests allows players to complete a special Beesmas decoration for use in the game, which are required to open Gift Boxes.
* **Returning Items/Shop:** Snowflake, Gingerbread Bears, Ornaments, and Presents returned. Presents can be given to NPCs to receive an ornament viewable on the Beesmas Tree. Bee Bear's Catalog shop is back.
* **New Content:** A new passive mob, Blooms, was added (Blooms can be popped to spawn petals and earn buffs). The Bloom Shaker item allows players to spawn blooms in their field. New Vouchers were introduced: Offline Voucher (allows planters/blender to run while offline for up to 24 hours) and Ticket Voucher (redeemable once every 24 hours for 100 Tickets).
* **QoL:** Discovered gifted bees now show a faint star in the bee menu; undiscovered ones do not. Removed the Computer item.

## 2023 Updates

### May 7, 2023 (Robo Bear Update)
**Featured in Egg Hunt 2020: Agents of E.G.G.**
* **Egg Hunt 2019: Scrambled in Time Content Removed:** All content related to this event (Flight of the Bumble Egg, quests, Plastic Eggs) was removed. Plastic Egg tokens were replaced with Ticket tokens.
* **New Quests/Items (Egg Hunt 2019):** New quests added for Black Bear, Mother Bear, Brown Bear, Panda Bear, Science Bear, Polar Bear, Gifted Bucko Bee, Gifted Riley Bee, and Stick Bug. New items included Plastic Egg (used in Brown Bear's quest), Jelly Beans, and Marshmallow Bee (turn in 3 to get the event egg).
* **Map Alterations:** The Blue Maze was added; areas were adjusted for Plastic Egg tokens.
* **Balance Changes:** Sunflower badge bonus changed to capacity at x1.1/x1.2/x1.3/x1.4/x1.5. Pumpkin badge bonus changed to conversion amount at +20/+40/+60/+80/+100. Riley Bee and Carpenter Bee conversion amounts increased (110->120, 100->120). Lion Bee movespeed decreased (20.3->19.6), conversion amount increased (140->160). Shy Bee conversion amount increased (80->140). Cobalt and Crimson Bee conversion times reduced (4->3). Gummy Bee conversion amount increased (640->700). Tabby Bee movespeed decreased (17.5->16.1), conversion amount/time increased (80->160, 4->3). Gummy Mask passive cost reduced (25->20); Gummy Bear Morph goo increased (100%->x2), Gummy Bee attack increased (100->300). System Page gained a button to disable the Micro-Converter hotkey and now lists capacity multiplier.

### April 17, 2023
**Featured in Egg Hunt 2020: Agents of E.G.G.**
* **Egg Hunt 2020 Quests:** Sun Bear returned with new quests to obtain the ROBLOX item Swarming Egg of the Hive. Egg Hunt 2020 quests were added to Black Bear, Mother Bear, and Brown Bear. Black Bear received a Mythic Egg questline. Brown Bear's quests were reworked (Brown Cub skin for 300 quests).
* **New Items:** Purple Potion (grants x1.25 Capacity and improved boosts of Red/Blue Extracts and Glue for 15 minutes).
* **Leaderboard:** Top Brown Bear Helpers leaderboard added.
* **Mobs:** Chicks were introduced.
* **Bee:** Fuzzy Bee was added.
* **Balance Changes:** Gummy Mask goo decreased (+100%->+50%), x1.25 White Pollen added, x1.25 Capacity increased; Diamond Mask conversion/capacity adjusted; Demon Mask conversions reduced; Honey Mask capacity added; Bubble Mask defense/conversion adjusted; Fire Mask capacity/conversion adjusted.
* **Mechanics:** Crafting can now be sped up using tickets. Notification for bees running out of energy was added. Sound effects for quest completion were added. Marshmallow Bee cap set to 25.

### April 5, 2023 (Robo Bear Update)
**The game is updating to add Stickers and Trading.**
* **Stickers & Trading:** A new collectible type, Stickers, was introduced. They can decorate the hive or be stacked in the Sticker Stack for a boost. The base of the player's claimed hive was reworked to allow customization with Hive Skins (found in sticker inventory). Cub Buddy skins are now part of the sticker inventory and must be displayed on the hive. Trading is limited to stickers and Beequips (Beequip trading became official later in 2024-01-16).
* **Hive Hub:** A new area in a separate server, accessible via the Ant Gate. It features a central field where a Sticker Sprout spawns every 3 hours. Includes a Public Sticker Board and the Hub Field Shop (sells the Sticker-Seeker tool).
* **New Machines/Items:** Sticker Stack, Sticker Printer, Nectar Condenser, and Sticker Planter were added.
* **Badges:** New badges introduced: Sticker Stack Badge, Mountain Top Badge, Hive Hub Badge. Numerous new Leaderboards were added.
* **Map Changes:** Mythic Meteor Shower moved to the roof of the Badge Bearer's Guild. Path to Lava Obby behind Honey Dispenser made easier.
* **Balance Changes (Bees):** Rascal Bee, Demo Bee, and Frosty Bee hive bonuses changed from a specific bomb pollen percentage to x1.25.

### February 1, 2023 (Beesmas 2021)
**Beesmas 2021:**
* **Returning Content:** Bee Bear returned with 15 quests, rewarding a Cub Buddy (or Star Treat if owned). Returning Beesmas Quests (from 2020) were available. Returning items included Snowflake, Gingerbread Bears, Ornaments, and Presents via the Beesmas Tree. The Bee Bear's Catalog shop is back.
* **New Content:** New bees: Precise Bee (Red Mythic) and Buoyant Bee (Blue Mythic). New bear: Dapper Bear (located past Brave Bee Gate), whose quests involve Beequips and a new inventory item type, Planters.
* **New Mechanics/Items:** Planters were introduced (harvested for rewards/buffs). Nectar was added (Refreshing, Comforting, Satisfying, Motivating, Invigorating) determined by the field. Puffshroom (passive entity) was added (spawns on harvested planters; grants buffs and drops rewards). Balloon mechanic allows storing extra pollen.
* **New Items:** Paper Planter, Ticket Planter, Festive Planter, Soft Wax, Hard Wax, Caustic Wax, Swirled Wax (inventory limit removed), Turpentine (erases wax effects), various Balloons (Pink, Red, White, Black), Dice (Smooth/Loaded), Whirligig, Honeysuckle, Aged Gingerbread Bear.
* **New Tools:** Dark Scythe, Tide Popper, Gummyballer.
* **Balance Changes:** Jelly Bean buffs reworked. Field Winds duration increased to 30 minutes. Dice buffs cannot stack directly. Star Amulet passives adjusted (Guiding Star/Star Shower grant x1.25 Capacity). New bee passives: Diamond Bee's Shimmering Honey; Shy Bee's Nectar Lover.
* **Fixes:** Vector Bee's Triangulate now multiplies white pollen by x2.

## 2022 Updates

### December 26, 2022 (Beesmas 2022)
**Beesmas 2022 is here! Players joining for the first time receive 2 days of Honeyday Event buff.**
* **Returning Content:** Bee Bear returned with 15 quests, rewarding a Cub Buddy (or Star Treat if owned). Returning Beesmas Quests (from 2020) were available. Returning items included Snowflake and Gingerbread Bears. The Bee Bear's Catalog shop is back.
* **New Content:** New bee: Digital Bee ("virtual bee with malfunctioning AI"). Unlocked in Robo Bear's Shop for Honey, drives, and a Diamond Cog Amulet. New Beequips (Thimble, Camo Bandana, Whistle, Lei). New bear: Robo Bear.
* **Robo Bear Challenge:** A rogue-like minigame was introduced. Players complete timed rounds, fight enemies, unlock bees, and purchase upgrades using Cogs. Rewards include Cog Amulets and Drives.
    * **RBC Items/Currency:** Robo Pass (from Dispensers), Drives (boost pollen/capacity/attack), Cog (currency for rerolls/purchases).
    * **New Mobs:** Mechsquito, Mega Mechsquito, Cogmower, Golden Cogmower, Cogturret.
    * New amulet: Cog Amulet. New leaderboard: Highest Robo Bear Challenge Scores.
* **Dapper Bear Update:** Dapper Bear received 10 new quests focusing on planters, nectar, and Puffshrooms, rewarding Beequip Case slots (or Ticket Planters). Beequips can now be purchased in his shop; storage can be expanded using Tickets.
* **New Planters/Items:** Hydroponic Planter (Blue HQ), Heat-Treated Planter (Red HQ). Nectar Vials were added.
* **Balance Changes (Bees):** Carpenter Bee attack increased (+3->+4). Ninja Bee gained +10% critical chance. Buoyant Bee attack increased (+3->+4); spawns Blue Bomb instead of Blue Bomb+; Inflate Balloons pollen multiplier reduced (0.02%->0.01%). Spicy Bee's Ignite now has a chance to spawn Gifted Fire Bees. Vicious Bee's Impale gained +1 level of accuracy.
* **Tools:** Tide Popper maximum increased (x1.15->x1.2); Honey tokens from waves hitting balloons multiplier increased (x2->x2.5).

## 2019 Updates

### December 23, 2019 (Beesmas 2019)
**Beesmas 2019:**
* **Event:** Beesmas returned. Bee Bear returned with a limited questline rewarding Presents and a Festive Bee Egg. Science Bear received 5 new quests.
* **New Mechanics/Items:** Cub Buddies were introduced (cute cubs that activate Ability Tokens and drop gifts). The Beesmas Tree was added near the Ant Gate. New items: Ornaments, Ornament Presents.
* **Rarity:** Mythic rarity tier was added above Legendary bees.
* **New Bees:** Vector Bee, Tadpole Bee, and Spicy Bee were introduced.
* **Mechanics:** Radiation and Mutation mechanics were added (upgrading bees with mutations for stat bonuses and hive color changes). Frogs (passive mob) were introduced (spawn bubbles and collect ability tokens).
* **New Items/Tools:** Box-O-Frogs, Neonberry (raises bond by 500 and makes bee radioactive), Bitterberry, Atomic Treat (guarantees a mutation when fed to a bee).
* **QoL:** Auto-Jelly was added.
* **Stats:** New stats added: Hive Convert Rate, Pollen from Bubbles, Pollen from Flames.
* **Map Alterations:** Map reverted to Christmas colors of Beesmas 2018. Several obbies were added.

### September 28, 2019 (Ready Player Two)
**Featured in Ready Player Two Roblox event.**
* New NPC Bee: Digital Bee (NPC). Strange Goggles allow players to see into The Computer and complete Digital Bee's puzzles.
* **New Items:** 7-Pronged Cog (obtained from secret codes, used for Strange Goggles), Strange Goggles.
* **Amulets:** Supreme Star Amulet rarity was added; amulets now require discovered gifted bee types instead of those in the player's hive.
* **Items/Machines:** Super Smoothie (grants x1.5 Capacity and improved crafting boosts for 20 minutes). Mythic Meteor Shower machine allows triggering a pollen collection event by discovering 3 Mythic bees.
* **Leaderboards:** 6 new leaderboards added (All-Time and Daily color collection leaderboards in Red/Blue HQ and Petal Shop).
* **Removals:** Spotted Chicks and Hostage Chicks were removed. Honey Bee (NPC) moved to Pine Tree Forest rocks.
* **Stats:** Convert Total, Gather Total, and Attack Total stats added to the System Page. Field Winds modified and stacks up to 12.
* **Balance Changes:** Bubble Mask, Gummy Mask, Demon Mask, Diamond Mask received modifications to their passives. Bee reworks: Frosty Bee's Blue Bomb changed to Blue Bomb+; Bubble Bee's Blue Bomb+ changed to Blue Bomb. Ability Token changes (Red & Blue Pulse, Scratch, Flame Fuel, Triangulate, Mark Surge).
