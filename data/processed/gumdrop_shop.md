# Gumdrop Shop

[![The design of the Gumdrop Shop.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/22/Gumdrop_Shop.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20180604142523)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/22/Gumdrop_Shop.png/revision/latest?cb=20180604142523) [](/wiki/File:Gumdrop_Shop.png)

*The design of the Gumdrop Shop.*

The **Gumdrop Shop** is a shop introduced in the 2018-05-26 update. It is located between the Spider Field and the Bamboo Field. The shop allows players to purchase [gumdrops] using [tickets] in increments of 3, 30, or 300.

The shop's appearance features a teal gumdrop on top, an image of the gumdrops item on the sides with a pink background, a teal base, and gray rims.

**Shop Description:**
"Use while standing in a [field] to cover flowers in [goo]. Goo grants bonus [honey]."

## Prices

| Gumdrops | Tickets Cost |
| :---: | :---: |
| 3 | 1 |
| 30 | 10 |
| 300 | 100 |

## Trivia

*   The Gumdrop Shop was originally placed next to the [Gummy Bear] in the traveling bear section. It was later moved next to the [Panda Bear] during the 2018-07-11 update, where it remains today.
