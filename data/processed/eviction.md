# Eviction (Removed Item)

![Hivesticker eviction](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e3/Hivesticker_eviction.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20240120131955) | **Archival Content:** This item has been removed from the game, but this page serves as an archive of its mechanics and history.

---

## Overview

The Eviction was a consumable inventory item used to remove a bee from a hive slot.

### Details
*   **Description:** Removes a bee from the hive.
*   **Cost:** 50,000 Honey

The Eviction was previously purchasable at the Mountain Top Shop for 50,000 Honey. It was noted as the cheapest item available in that shop before its removal.

### Mechanics
Evictions were primarily used by players who could not afford a new Hive Slot to replace an existing bee with an egg. When an Eviction was used:
1.  The bee was removed from the hive slot.
2.  The entire hive slot was reset, meaning the previous bee's bond level and gifted status were cleared.
3.  If a new egg was subsequently hatched into that slot, the resulting bee would start at Level 1, unaffected by the previous occupant's type or gifted status.

**Note:** All Evictions previously held in player inventories have been removed from the game.

## Acquisition History (No Longer Possible)

The Eviction item is currently unobtainable in the game. The following methods of obtaining it have been removed:
*   Purchasing the Eviction at the Mountain Top Shop for 50,000 Honey.
*   Receiving 800 Evictions from Bubble Bee Man's Beesmas 2018 quest ('B.B.M. Mission').

## Removal Reason

The item was removed following the **2019-09-28 update**. This update introduced a mechanic allowing players to use eggs on existing bees, eliminating the necessity of evicting them and enabling new bees to retain their level progression. Consequently, Onett removed the Eviction from both the shop and all player inventories.
