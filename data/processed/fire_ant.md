# Fire Ant

[![FireAnt](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a2/FireAnt.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20191208201637)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a2/FireAnt.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201637 "FireAnt")

A Fire Ant is one of five ant types exclusively found within the Ant Challenge, located past the Ant Gate. It serves as the final mob encounter in this challenge.

## Characteristics and Stats

| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Ant Field | Only appears while the Ant Challenge is active. |
| **Base Health** | 25 | Increases based on the current wave level (1-16). |
| **Damage (Flame Trail)** | 30 | Damage dealt by its lingering flame trail. |
| **Damage (Contact)** | 40 | Damage dealt upon direct contact. |
| **Battle Points** | None | N/A |
| **Honey** | None | N/A |
| **Level Range** | 1–16 | Varies depending on the wave of the Ant Challenge. |

Fire Ants are colored red and behave similarly to Regular Ants, but they possess a unique mechanic: they leave a damaging flame trail behind them as they move. These flames linger for a short period even after the Fire Ant is defeated. They are considered one of the most threatening ant types because their trails can stack up on the field, making navigation dangerous.

## Strategies and Combat Tips

*   **Evasion:** Fire Ants move in predictable, straight lines and are relatively slow, allowing players to easily dodge their attacks. Players can also walk through the gap between the Ant Challenge window and the field to avoid stepping on fire trails.
*   **Damage Focus:** Standing close to a Fire Ant allows bees to quickly target it for efficient damage application. It is highly recommended to prioritize eliminating Fire Ants first, as their flame trails significantly impede movement and cause continuous damage across the field.
*   **Crowd Control:** Since Fire Ants often spawn in groups, they can overwhelm players who are focused on pollen collection. Bees with crowd control abilities—such as **Vicious Bee** (Impale) or **Windy Bee** (Tornado)—are excellent counters. **Digital Bee** can also stun multiple ants simultaneously, allowing for increased damage via Mind Hack.

## Trivia

*   This is the only ant type that has a second method of attack (the damaging flame trail).
*   They share the same health progression per level as Regular Ants.
*   While some sources noted that fire damage was unaffected by defense, having 100% defense (e.g., via Emergency Coconut Shield) will nullify all incoming damage.
