# Scorpion

The **Scorpion** is a hostile mob found in the Rose Field. It is known for its high damage output and valuable rewards, including Battle Points and Honey.

## Stats & Mechanics

| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Rose Field | Found in specific corners of the field. |
| **Respawn Time** | 20 minutes | Reduces to 17 minutes when a Gifted Vicious Bee is present. |
| **Base Health (Lvl 5)** | 170 | Increases to 200 at Level 6. |
| **Damage** | 25 | Damage dealt before accounting for player Defense. |
| **Battle Points Reward** | 5 | Guaranteed reward upon defeat. |
| **Honey Reward** | ~1000 | Guaranteed base amount. |

### Location Details

Scorpions spawn in the Rose Field, with two distinct locations:
*   **2 Scorpions:** Located in the corner nearest to the Red HQ (Level 5).
*   **6 Scorpions:** Located in the corner farthest from the Red HQ (Level 6).

## Drops

Defeating a Scorpion guarantees 5 Battle Points and approximately 1000 Honey. Beyond these guaranteed rewards, there is a chance for various other items:

### Guaranteed Drops
*   5 Battle Points
*   ~1000 Honey

### Possible Drops
The probability of receiving certain drops increases with the player's Loot Luck.

**Common/Rare Items:**
*   Tickets (2/15 chance)
*   Gumdrops (Increments: 1, 5, 10, or 25)
*   Royal Jellies
*   Treats (Increments: 1, 5, 10, 25, or 50)
*   Strawberries (Loot Luck increases drop rate; Increments: 1, 3, 5, 10, 25, 50, or 100)
*   Pineapples (Loot Luck increases drop rate; Increments: 1, 3, 5, 10, 25, 50, or 100)
*   Stingers (Increments: 1, 3, or 5)
*   Red Extracts (Rare; Increments: 1, 3, 5, or 10)
*   Field Dice (Increments: 1 or 5)
*   Bitterberries (Increments: 1 or 5)

**Special/High-Tier Drops:**
