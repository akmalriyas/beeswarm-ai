# Bears

In *Bee Swarm Simulator*, bears are in-game characters found throughout the map that players can interact with. There are currently 18 unique bear appearances available in the game. While some bears have no function, many serve as quest givers or provide special multipliers.

Most bears offer quests for various rewards, including Royal Jelly, Tickets, Treats, and Eggs. Other specialized bears grant players passive bonuses, such as Science Bear's *Science Enhancement* or Polar Bear's *Polar Power*. Quest bears are a significant element of the game, providing tips, challenges, and sometimes secret codes redeemable for valuable items.

## Quest Bears
Quest bears are permanent characters located across the map. Unlike traveling bears, these bears remain in the game unless removed by Onett. They all interact with players, provide dialogue, and offer quests for rewards. There are currently nine quest bears in the game.

Note: While Onett, Gifted Bucko Bee, Gifted Riley Bee, Honey Bee (NPC), and Digital Bee (NPC) also function as quest givers, they are not classified as bears. Digital Bee is unique among these NPCs as it does not engage in dialogue or utilize a quest meter.

**Quest Bears List:**
*   Black Bear
*   Mother Bear
*   Brown Bear
*   Panda Bear
*   Robo Bear
*   Science Bear
*   Dapper Bear
*   Polar Bear
*   Spirit Bear

## Traveling Bears
These bears interact and offer quests, similar to Quest Bears, but they appear for a limited time. Their final quest reward is often a unique item that was previously unavailable in the game.

Currently, there are three traveling bears:
*   **Sun Bear:** Appeared numerous times between 2018 and 2023, most recently during the Games event at Sunflower Field.
*   **Bee Bear:** Featured in the current 2026 Beesmas Event at Dandelion Field, and has appeared in all previous Beesmas events.
*   **Gummy Bear:** Gave quests from May 26 to June 24, 2018, awarding a Gummy Bee Egg. Although he returned to his lair, he currently only gives quests during the Beesmas event.

**Traveling Bears List:**
*   Sun Bear
*   Bee Bear
*   Gummy Bear

## Shop Bears
These bears are located inside their eponymous shops. Unlike Quest or Traveling Bears, they do not engage in dialogue or provide quests; instead, they idle in place. All shop bears utilize the Noob Skin color and display the "Smile" Roblox face.

**Shop Bears List:**
*   Noob Bear
*   Pro Bear
*   Top Bear

## Other Bears (Mobs)
There are three bears that do not currently provide benefits to the player. Instead, they either attack the player or have limited functions.
*   **Tunnel Bear and Shadow Bear:** These bears are hidden in brushed-off locations. Finding Shadow Bear under the platform past the Bear Gate allows players to attempt collecting a Diamond Egg nearby.
*   **Snowbear:** This bear requires the completion of Panda Bear's Beesmas quest before it can be summoned. During Beesmas, defeating Snowbear may reward snowflakes.

**Other Bears List:**
*   Tunnel Bear
*   Shadow Bear
*   Snowbear

## Trivia
*   Mother Bear, Spirit Bear, Dapper Bear, and Robo Bear are the only permanent quest bears added in a game update; all other permanent bears were present at the initial release of the game.
*   Mother Bear and Spirit Bear are the only female bears in the game.
*   Top Bear and Dapper Bear are the only shop-keeping bears introduced via updates.
*   It was previously possible to kill Top Bear if a Rogue Vicious Bee spawned in Mountain Top Field, but this mechanic was patched by giving Top Bear infinite HP.
*   Black Bear, Brown Bear, Panda Bear, Polar Bear, Gummy Bear, Sun Bear, and Spirit Bear are the only bears based on specific real-world counterparts.
