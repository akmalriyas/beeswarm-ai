# Red Cannon

The **Red Cannon** is a transportation device available once the player has discovered 25 different bee types. It is typically located near the Hive, adjacent to the Black Bear and close to the Ticket Tent and Blue Teleporter exit. The cannon operates with a 5-second cooldown period.

### Mechanics and Usage
The Red Cannon offers access to various areas of the map that might otherwise be tedious or impossible to reach. Its large range makes it highly effective when combined with gliding tools like the Parachute or Glider, allowing players to navigate efficiently. These combinations grant access to locations such as Star Hall, outside the map boundaries, and allows collection of most Royal Jelly tokens (e.g., those found on top of the Mountain Top Shop or the Hive).

**Landing Behavior:**
*   **Without Buffs/Gliding Tools:** If used without any buffs or gliding tools, the player usually lands in the middle of the Mountain Top Field. However, unpredictable outcomes can occur:
    *   It may undershoot (landing on the ramp to Lion Bee Gate, into Mushroom Field, or rarely, onto the Sunflower Field border).
    *   It may overshoot (shooting the player outside the map boundaries).
    *   If the Scythe is equipped, it may only launch the player into the Spider Field due to unknown reasons. This specific outcome can also occur without the Scythe. If this happens, jumping and pressing E simultaneously allows the cannon to fire normally.
*   **With Parachute/Glider:** When used with a Parachute or Glider, the player does not land in the Mountain Top Field. Instead, they lose momentum immediately upon firing and continue gliding until ground contact is made.

### Trivia

*   The Red Cannon is unique among the cannons as it has a distinct model—featuring a barrel with a hole at the top and a crosshair at the end.
*   It is one of four transportational cannons in the game, alongside the Yellow Cannon, Blue Cannon, and Gummy Cannon. The Yellow and Blue Cannons are its primary counterparts.
*   The Red Cannon possesses the largest range of all available cannons.
*   It requires the highest number of discovered bee types to unlock compared to other transportation methods.
*   While the cooldown is 5 seconds (shared by all transportational cannons), this duration is usually unnoticeable during normal gameplay due to the great distance traveled. The cooldown can be observed using a Glider/Parachute glitch to halt momentum immediately after exiting the cannon.
*   Historically, the requirement for use was lower: it could initially be used with 20 bee types upon release, then updated to 22 in the 2018-04-27 update, and finally set at 25 during the 2018-11-25 update.
*   Unlike other cannons, the Red Cannon does not launch players into the Starter Zone; it launches them directly toward the Mountain Top Field because of its starting location within the zone.
*   Early game versions allowed players to use the cannon to reach the Mountain Top Field even without 25 bees, but this has since been patched.
*   When combined with a Glider, the Red Cannon is widely regarded as the most effective method for traversing the entire map.
*   The cannon's launch trajectory was previously higher than it is now, which allowed players to glide outside the map and stand on top of the Bear Gate. This has since been adjusted to shoot at a lower angle.
