# Roblox: The Classic Event

[![ClassicLogo](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8c/ClassicLogo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/300?cb=20240527215749)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8c/ClassicLogo.png/revision/latest?cb=20240527215749)

**Overview**
*   **Duration:** May 23, 2024 – May 28, 2024
*   **Special Quest Givers:** None
*   **Related Challenge:** [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge")

The Classic was a Roblox event that spanned across multiple games within Bee Swarm Simulator. To participate and contribute to overarching prizes in the main event hub, players needed to earn 15 specific badges. These badges were earned through two primary methods:
*   **Tix Collection:** 10 of the 15 badges were awarded for collecting Tix (which were typically hidden around the map).
*   **Quest Completion:** The remaining 5 badges were awarded Tokens for completing quests or achieving certain milestones.

## Retro Swarm Challenge Details

The main article related to this event is the [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge"). This challenge is a round-based minigame where the primary objective is defending hives from hostile mobs, specifically [Slimes](/wiki/Slime "Slime") and [Zombies](/wiki/Zombie "Zombie").

To succeed in the challenge, players must accumulate Bricks to purchase necessary bees and weapons. The challenge subplace features four exclusive fields: Red, White, Blue, and Mixed Brick Fields.
