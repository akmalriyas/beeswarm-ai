# Tutorial Guide

The **Tutorial** provides essential information about the game and its mechanics. It automatically opens for new players upon loading. Established players can disable it by closing the window, and the game will remember this preference for future sessions. The tutorial can be reopened using the red circle icon with a question mark in the top right corner of the screen.

## Core Mechanics Overview

The goal of Bee Swarm Simulator is to collect pollen from flowers and transform it into honey. You lead your own personal swarm of bees to achieve this.

### 1. Claiming & Hatching Bees
*   **Claiming a Hive:** To begin, walk up to an unclaimed honeycomb and press "Claim Hive."
*   **Hatching Eggs:** Open the Egg menu. Click and drag an egg onto one of the available honeycomb cells to hatch a bee.

### 2. Collecting Pollen & Making Honey
*   **Pollen Collection:** Walk into a flower field. You can use your **Scooper** to collect pollen manually, or simply let your bees automatically gather pollen from nearby flowers.
*   **Bee Sleep Cycle:** After working for a while, your bees will become tired and return to the hive to sleep. They will rejoin you once they wake up.
*   **Honey Production:** As you collect pollen, the meter on your backpack fills up. When this meter is full, return to your hive and press "Make Honey." Your bees will then convert the collected pollen into honey.

### 3. Progression & Upgrades
*   **Shop Purchases:** Use the honey produced to purchase upgrades in the shop, such as a faster **Scooper**, a bigger backpack capacity, or more bee eggs.
*   **Hive Growth:** Growing your hive allows you to progress further into the map.

## Bee Types and Abilities

As you hatch more eggs, you will discover new types of bees. These different types possess unique strengths and abilities.

*   **Bee Transformation:** You can transform existing bees using **Royal Jelly**.
*   **Abilities:** Bees have various abilities that are activated by collecting tokens they spawn in the field. Combining these tokens allows for combo abilities, boosting their power.
*   **Stats Check:** To view a bee's specific stats and abilities, click on its face within the Bee menu.

### Special Abilities & Traits
Some bees possess special traits:
*   **Boostability:** Certain bees boost the amount of pollen received from flowers of specific colors.
*   **Areaability:** Some bees can collect pollen from multiple flowers simultaneously.

## Combat and Exploration

The world contains various elements that require interaction and caution.

### Bugs and Monsters
*   **Hazards:** Be aware that some flower fields contain hostile bugs and monsters.
*   **Risk:** If these creatures defeat you, you will lose all the pollen currently in your backpack.
*   **Defense:** You cannot attack monsters directly, but your bees will automatically defend you against them.

### Quests (Bears)
*   **Quest Givers:** Bears are scattered across the map and offer quests. They can be identified by a "!" symbol displayed in front of them.
*   **Interaction:** To interact with a bear, walk onto the platform in front of it and press "Talk to Bear."
*   **Tracking Progress:** Quests given by bears will appear in the Quest menu, allowing you to track tasks and progress toward completion. Completing quests is an excellent way to earn honey, eggs, and other resources.

## Map Gates and Discovery
Progression through the map is gated by specific achievements:
*   **Egg Gates:** Platforms marked with numbers require you to hatch a specified number of eggs before they can be passed.
*   **Discovery Gates:** Platforms featuring magnifying glass icons require you to discover a certain number of unique bee types to activate them. You can track your discovered bees and current count in the Bee menu.
