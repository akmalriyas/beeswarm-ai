# Moon Charm

[![Moon Charm](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b6/Moon_Charm.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041630)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b6/Moon_Charm.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041630)

> "Increases your bond with a bee by 250."

## Description and Mechanics

A **Moon Charm** is an item that, when fed to a Bee, raises its Bond by 250. Moon Charms are versatile crafting components used in generating [Moon Amulets], as well as in the creation of [Glitter] and various [Helmets].

If fed to a [Radiation] bee, there is a small chance (1/2,500 or 0.04%) that it will grant the bee a [Mutation].

*Note: Moon Charm tokens have a limited lifespan of 10 seconds before disappearing.*

The charm comes in two types:
1.  **Non-glowing:** Dropped by mobs or Mythic Meteors.
2.  **Glowing:** Dropped during nighttime from Fireflies, Sparkles, and Moon Sprouts.

## Acquisition Methods

### Crafting
Moon Charms can be crafted using the [Blender] with the following ingredients:
*   5 Pineapples
*   5 Gumdrops
*   1 Royal Jelly

### Rewards and Drops
**From Activities/Sources:**
*   **Memory Matches:** Awarded as a reward (excluding Extreme Memory Match and Winter Memory Match). The amount varies by match type:
    *   Potential: Increment of 5.
    *   Mega Memory Match Pair: Increment of 10.
    *   Night Memory Match Pairs: Two pairs with increments of 25 each.
*   **Wind Shrine:** Awarded as a reward from the Wind Shrine.
*   **Sticker Stack Rewards:** Accumulated by adding specific stickers to the Sticker Stack:
    *   Waxing Crescent Moon Sticker rewards 10 Moon Charms.
    *   Petal Wand Sticker rewards 10 Moon Charms.

**From Drops:**
*   **Mobs:**
    *   [King Beetle] and [Tunnel Bear] always drop Moon Charms upon defeat.
    *   Chasing all 11 [Fireflies] provides a chance for drops (Increments of 1, 5, and 10).
    *   Rarely dropped by [Mantis] and [Stump Snail].
*   **Environmental:**
    *   Common drop from [Mythic Meteors].
    *   Common drop from harvesting [Sparkles].
    *   Guaranteed drop from a [Moon Sprout].
    *   Harvesting planters in the [Pineapple Patch] also yields Moon Charms.

## Trivia

*   Moon Charm is one of eight items that can possess a glowing token. The others include Festive Gift, Festive Blessing Tokens, Beesmas Cheer Tokens, Map Corruption, Glitter, Night Bells, Star Jellies, and Star Treats (which are exceptionally rare drops from Fireflies).
*   The raw ingredients required to craft Moon Charms are: 15 Strawberries, 15 Blueberries, 20 Pineapples, and either 1 Royal Jelly or 1 Million Honey.
