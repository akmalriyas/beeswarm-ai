# Supreme Sprout

A **Sprout** is a plant that spawns in the center of a field. Sprouts emit a bright yellow beacon and have a counter at their base representing an amount of pollen. When players collect pollen, or when a cloud is present on the field (requiring at least one player to be in the field for growth), the pollen count decreases, and the sprout gradually grows taller. Once the pollen counter reaches zero, it sends out a shockwave and scatters various tokens throughout the field. If no interaction occurs with a sprout for more than five minutes, it will despawn.

## Spawning Mechanics

Sprouts can spawn randomly across the map or be planted using Magic Beans. They can also be summoned by using the Special Sprout Summoner near the Red HQ, provided the player has discovered all eight Legendary Bee types. This summoning ability is available once every sixteen hours.

When a sprout spawns naturally, it broadcasts a server-wide message:
🌱A (Rarity) Sprout has appeared...🌱

If a player plants a sprout, the message reads:
🌱 {Username} has planted a (Rarity) Sprout...🌱

Specific spawn messages include:
*   **Sticker Sprouts in Hive Hub:** 🌱 Sticker Sprouts are about to spawn in the Hive Hub...🌱
*   **Sticker Sprout Spawned:** 🌱 A Sticker Sprout has spawned in the Hive Hub...🌱

Sprouts cannot spawn or be planted in the Ant Field.

## Variants

There are ten different variants of sprouts, each with unique characteristics:

*   **Sprout (Green):** Common
*   **Rare Sprout (Silver):** Rare
*   **Epic Sprout (Gold):** Very Rare
*   **Legendary Sprout (Teal):** Extremely Rare
*   **Supreme Sprout (Luminescent green):** Unfathomably Rare
*   **Moon Sprout (Luminescent blue):** Only available during nighttime.
*   **Gummy Sprout (Translucent pink):** Very Rare
*   **Sticker Sprout (Rainbow, colors shifting):** Spawns every 3 hours in the Hub Field, or has a very rare chance when planting a magic bean there.
*   **Festive Sprout (White and Red stripes):** Planted using Festive Beans or Onett's developer ability.
*   **Debug Sprout (Black):** Can only be planted by Onett.

The amount of pollen required to harvest a sprout depends on its rarity and the field it is located in.

## Drops

### Location Specific Drops

The drops generated when harvesting a sprout can vary depending on the field where it was planted, affecting both quantity and item type:

*   **Red Fields:** Yield four times more Strawberries than any other field. No Blueberries will spawn here.
*   **Strawberry Field:** Yields twice as many Strawberries compared to sprouts in other red fields.
*   **Blue Fields:** Yield six times more Blueberries than any other field. No Strawberries will spawn here.
*   **Sunflower Field:** Yields seven times more Sunflower Seeds than any other field.
*   **Pineapple Patch:** Yields seven times more Pineapples than any other field.
*   **35 Bee Zone (Windy Bee Gate):** Yields 20% more rewards than sprouts in other areas, but requires significantly more pollen to pop.
*   **Coconut Field:** Yields Coconuts and Tropical Drinks in addition to regular treats.
*   **Other Fields:** May produce more regular Treats depending on the specific field type.

*   **Crafting Materials:** Includes Red Extracts, Blue Extracts, Oils, Enzymes, and Glitter.

## Tips for Token Collection

Several in-game elements can boost player efficiency when collecting tokens before sprouts despawn:

*   **Movespeed Boosts:**
    *   Using Oil grants a 1.2x movespeed bonus.
    *   Equipping the Hasty Guard grants a 1.1x movespeed bonus.
    *   Having "Haste" (up to 2x) is achieved primarily through Haste-producing bees and Dispensers:
        *   Honey, Treat, Strawberry, and Blueberry Dispensers provide 5 stacks of Haste.
        *   The Royal Jelly Dispenser provides 10 stacks of Haste.
    *   Equipping Coconut Clogs or Gummy Boots allows the player to trigger a short burst of Coconut Haste Surge from falling coconuts.
    *   Bear Bee's Bear Morph ability can significantly increase movespeed.
    *   The Free Royal Jelly Dispenser grants one stack of Haste+.

*   **Token Collection Tools:**
    *   Tadpole Bees and Boxes-O-Frogs can summon frogs that collect sprout tokens.
    *   The Star Saw passive ability collects massive amounts of tokens.
    *   Windy Bee's Tornado ability also collects large quantities of tokens.
    *   Cub Buddies can assist in collecting sprout tokens.

*   **Sticker Sprout Strategy:** For Sticker Sprouts, focus on staying near one location to collect loot that spawns close by, as distant loot is likely to be claimed by other players first.

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Token Collection Rules:** If the player who spawned a sprout leaves before it pops, the sprout will not drop any items.
*   **Sprout Naming History:** Sprouts were originally called "Seedlings" upon release; this name was changed in the 2019-12-23 update. The original message was: "🌱A Seedling has sprouted...🌱". Supreme Sprouts were formerly known as "Mythical Sprouts."
*   **Pollen Changes:** The Supreme Sprout previously required half the pollen of a Legendary Sprout; it now requires 50% more.
*   **Onett's Abilities:** Since the 2019-12-23 update, Onett has been able to plant sprouts server-wide under unique names (e.g., Festive, Debug, Gummy).
*   **Notification Colors:** Most sprout notifications are gold, but specific variants have unique colors:
    *   Gummy Sprouts: Light purple notification.
    *   Supreme, Moon, and Debug Sprouts: Gray notifications.
    *   Festive Sprouts: Red notification.
    *   Sticker Sprouts: Rainbow notification.
*   **Special Sprout Summoner:** This tool was formerly called "Sprout Summoner" and could plant any random sprout type except for Debug, Festive, or Sticker Sprouts.
*   **Debug Sprout Uniqueness:** The Debug Sprout is the only type that does not drop honey tokens, and it cannot be planted by a player (excluding Onett).
*   **Sticker Sprout Growth:** Unlike other sprouts, Sticker Sprouts grow on their own without needing clouds. They lose approximately 1 million pollen per second even if no one is collecting in the Hub Field.
*   **Gummy Sprouts Appearance:** Gummy Sprouts are the only translucent sprout type.
*   **Supreme/Moon Glow:** Supreme and Moon Sprouts are the only sprouts that glow.

### Sticker Sprout Rewards
Popping a Sticker Sprout can yield various stickers, including: Happy Fish, Green Plus Sign, Giraffe, Yellow Umbrella, Window (Rare), Simple Skyscraper (Rare), Uplooking Bear (Very Rare), Shining Halo (Extremely Rare), and Abstract Color Painting (Unfathomably Rare).
