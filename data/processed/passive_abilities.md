# Passive Abilities Guide

Passive abilities are effects found on certain accessories, amulets, or bees. These abilities can be active at all times or activate only when specific actions are performed as an additional effect of those actions.

## General Mechanics

*   **Display:** Passives granted by an accessory or amulet are displayed near the hotbar.
*   **Triggering:** Some passives require the player to perform the same action multiple times, indicated by a number on the passive's icon. When activated, this counter resets to 0.
*   **Cooldowns:** Passive abilities may have cooldowns. While cooling down, the remaining time is displayed in pale red on the icon. No trigger will activate the ability, nor will the trigger counter increase during cooldown. Items granting these passives also have an initial cooldown upon equipping them.

## Accessory Passives

### Haste Pulser
*   **Source:** Cobalt Guard
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th Haste token collected.
*   **Effect:** Fires a blue pulse that hops to each blue bee, identical to the effect of a Cobalt Bee.
*   **Condition:** Requires at least one blue bee to be present; otherwise, nothing happens.

### Focus Pulser
*   **Source:** Crimson Guard
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th Focus token collected.
*   **Effect:** Fires a red pulse that hops to each red bee, identical to the effect of a Crimson Bee.
*   **Condition:** Requires at least one red bee to be present; otherwise, nothing happens.

### Bubble Bombs
*   **Source:** Bubble Mask or Diamond Mask (if owning Bubble Mask)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 25th Bomb token collected, with a 2-minute cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Spawns 25 bubbles randomly in the current field.

### Diamond Drain
*   **Source:** Diamond Mask
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th Blue ability token collected, with a 45-second cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Summons a giant diamond that instantly converts 5 million pollen plus the player's total conversion amount.
    *   The conversion amount increases by 10% for every blue bee owned.
    *   It increases by 50% for every Gifted Blue bee type owned.
    *   It also pollinates 10 (+3 per Blue bee) blue flowers within the field.

### Ignite
*   **Source:** Fire Mask or Demon Mask (if owning Fire Mask)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 15th Red ability token collected.
*   **Effect:** Creates 5 flames in a plus shape where the 15th red token is collected.

### X-Flame
*   **Source:** Demon Mask
*   **Activation:** Activates every 25th Battle ability token collected, with a 20-second cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Creates 29 flames in an X shape around the player.

### Coin Scatter
*   **Source:** Honey Mask or Gummy Mask (if owning Honey Mask)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 20th Mark token collected, with a 2-minute cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Converts pollen equal to 300% of the player's total conversion amount and scatters it into 24 honey tokens around the field the player was last in.
    *   Honey tokens last for 1 minute.
    *   Each token gives a minimum of 1,000 honey, increasing with the converted pollen amount and the player's Honey From Tokens stat.
    *   These tokens can be collected by Triangulate but not Token Link.

### Gummy Morph
*   **Source:** Gummy Mask
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30 Gumdrops used or 10 Gummy Bee ability tokens collected (Gummy Bee tokens count as 3 gumdrops).
*   **Effect (Player):** The player transforms into a Gummy Bear, the Gummy Bee glows, and the field is covered in goo. As Gummy Bear:
    *   Grants +75% Goo.
    *   Grants +100% Instant Goo Conversion.
    *   Grants +24 Movespeed.
    *   Grants +60 Jump Power.
*   **Effect (Gummy Bee):** While glowing, the Gummy Bee gains:
    *   +1000% Gather Amount for 10 seconds.
    *   +300 Attack for 10 seconds.
    *   +200% Movespeed for 10 seconds.

### Coconut Haste
*   **Source:** Coconut Clogs or Gummy Boots
*   **Activation:** Activates whenever the player is hit by a falling Coconut.
*   **Effect:** The player gains one stack of Haste and 2 seconds of Coconut Surge, providing x1.5 Bee Movespeed and +10 Player Movespeed.

### Goo Trail
*   **Source:** Gummy Boots
*   **Activation:** Always active while equipped.
*   **Effect:** Passively covers 5 surrounding flowers with goo wherever the player walks on a field. Flowers directly behind the player receive more goo than those surrounding them.

### Emergency Coconut Shield
*   **Source:** Coconut Canister
*   **Activation:** Activates when the player takes damage directly from a mob, with a 5-minute cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Summons 5 Coconuts in the field the player was last in, grants 100% Defense, and increases Bee Attack by x1.25 for 10 seconds.
*   **Limitations:** Does not protect against instant kill sources like Tunnel Bear or Cave Monsters; however, it can be used preemptively to mitigate these risks.

### Inspire Coconuts
*   **Source:** Coconut Canister
*   **Activation:** Activates every 5th Inspire token (from Star Shower's shooting stars) or Beesmas Light collected.
*   **Effect:** Summons 5 falling Coconuts in the field the player was last in.

### Petal Storm
*   **Source:** Petal Belt or Coconut Belt (if owning Petal Belt)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th Boost token collected, with a 30-second cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Fires 30 Petal Shurikens around the player in a spiral pattern. When a shuriken passes through a bee, that bee instantly converts 10,000 pollen, increased by 7% of its conversion amount.

### Combo Coconuts
*   **Source:** Coconut Belt
*   **Activation:** Activates every 40th Coconut the player drops onto a field (via items or abilities). This coconut becomes a Combo Coconut.
*   **Mechanics:** When caught by any player, the Combo Coconut is kicked and falls onto either the summoner's field (if caught within the blue circle on the first drop) or another player's field. Every kick speeds up the coconut and grants stacks of Coconut Combo to the kicker.
    *   **Stack Bonuses:**
        *   Default: 1 stack.
        *   Bonus for different fields: +1 bonus stack if caught in a field different from where it was last caught.
        *   Color Classification Bonus: Between +1 to +4 bonus stacks depending on how long it has been since the coconut was caught in a field with the same color classification (Red/Blue/White/Mixed) as the current field.
        *   New Catcher Bonus: +10 bonus stacks if the catcher is another player who has not yet caught this coconut, in a field with a color classification that the coconut has not yet been caught in.
    *   **Buff Effects:** The buff grants between 25% to 75% Unique Instant Conversion, between x1.2 to x2 Pollen multiplier, and between +1% to +50% Red/Blue/White Pollen, Bee Attack, and Honey From Tokens.
*   **Limitations:** Combo Coconuts cannot be passed into the Ant Field (they always miss there). There is a limit of 50 kicks before premature termination.

## Amulet Passives (Star Amulets)

### Guiding Star
*   **Source:** Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet with this passive.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 250th Boost token collected, with a 5-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants 1.25x capacity.
*   **Effect:** Summons a Guiding Star over one of the five fields the summoner has collected the least from for 10 minutes. The chance of selection is based on relative pollen collection (the $n$-th least collected field has a $(6-n)/15$ chance).
    *   **Summoner Buff:** Grants x2.5 Pollen, Convert Rate, and Capacity to the summoner.
    *   **Other Player Buff:** Grants x1.25 Pollen, Convert Rate, and Capacity to other players in that field.
    *   **Pollination:** While active, it pollinates 15 (+1 per Gifted Bee Type) flowers every 15 seconds.
*   **Alternative Summoning:** Can also be summoned by using Onett's Lid Art during the Beesmas event.

### Star Shower
*   **Source:** Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet with this passive.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 40th Boost or Mark token collected, with a 30-second cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants 1.25x capacity.
*   **Effect:** 10 shooting stars fall onto the field where the player is standing. These collect and instantly convert 30 pollen (+50% per Gifted Bee Type) from 5 flowers.
    *   **Catching a Star:** Grants one stack of Inspire and instantly converts 1,000,000 pollen, plus an additional amount equal to 200% of the player's total conversion amount.
*   **Note:** This passive is similar to the falling Beesmas Lights activated during the Beesmas event via Science Bear's quest.

### Pop Star
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet with this passive.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th Boost token collected, with a 1-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants x1.25 Blue Field Capacity.
*   **Effect (Star):** Summons a Pop Star that grants 10% Instant Blue Conversion and x2 Blue Pollen for 45 seconds.
    *   **Bubble Bloat:** Automatically applies 2 minutes of Bubble Bloat upon activation. While active, every bubble popped increases the bonus Blue Pollen by 1% (up to x4.5 from x2) and grants 4 seconds of Bubble Bloat (6 seconds if gold; 1 second otherwise).
    *   **Bubble Bloat Effect:** Increases Blue Field Capacity and Convert Rate at Hive by up to x6 for up to 2.5 hours.
    *   **End State:** When the Pop Star ends, it spawns several bubbles over the field that increase based on the number of Bubbles popped.

### Gummy Star
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet with this passive.
*   **Activation:** Activates at a 2% chance when using Gumdrops, or at the 75th use after the cooldown ends, with a 1-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants x1.25 White Field Capacity.
*   **Effect (Star):** Summons a Gummy Star that grants 10% Instant White and Goo Conversion for 45 seconds. Collecting goo during this time makes the star grow, granting bonus White Pollen and Goo (up to x2).
    *   **End State:** After 45 seconds, the Gummy Star pops, scattering honey tokens and Gumdrops tokens on the last field. The total value of the honey tokens is a percentage of the goo collected during the duration, equal to 1.5% per gifted colorless bee type. The number of Gumdrops tokens increases with the amount of goo collected.

### Scorching Star
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet with this passive.
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th Boost token collected, with a 1-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants x1.25 Red Field Capacity.
*   **Effect (Star):** Summons a Scorching Star for 45 seconds that grants 50% Instant Flame Conversion, +5 Conversion Links, and x2 Red Pollen and Convert Rate.
    *   **Growth Mechanic:** Standing near flames causes the star to grow, increasing bonus Red Pollen and Convert Rate (up to x5 from x2) and the number of Conversion Links (up to 30 from 5).
    *   **End State:** When Scorching Star ends, it converts all of the player's pollen in their bag into Honey.

### Star Saw
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet with this passive.
*   **Activation:** Activates after using 3 Stingers. The third stinger used is refunded, and a Star Saw is summoned for 30 seconds.
*   **Effect:** The saw circles around the player, performing several functions:
    *   Damages hostile mobs (15% of the player's Total Attack). Accuracy equals the average bee level of the hive (excluding summons).
    *   Collects tokens.
    *   Pollinates 5 flowers 10 times a second, instantly converting 3 pollen (+0.5 for every 100 Total Attack) from each flower. The saw also instantly converts additional gathered pollen.

## Bee Passives (Passive Abilities Exclusive to Specific Bees)

### Gathering Bubbles
*   **Bee:** Bubble Bee, Tadpole Bee, and Frogs
*   **Mechanic:** These bees have a percentage chance to spawn bubbles when gathering.
    *   Bubble Bees: 35% chance (50% if gifted).
    *   Tadpole Bees: 65% chance (85% if gifted).

### Gathering Flames
*   **Bee:** Fire Bee, Demon Bee, Spicy Bee's Inferno, and bees with the Electric Candle Beequip.
*   **Mechanic:** These bees have a percentage chance to spawn flames when gathering.
    *   Fire Bees: 35% chance (50% if gifted).
    *   Demon Bees: 55% chance (75% if gifted).

### Steam Engine
*   **Bee:** Spicy Bee
*   **Effect:** Boosts any Spicy Bee's speed by up to 100% while active, granting Flame Heat. While active, the Spicy Bee increases steam particle output proportionally to the amount of flame heat.

### Fuzzy Coat
*   **Bee:** Fuzzy Bee
*   **Effect:** Has a chance to pollinate up to 5 surrounding flowers (9 if gifted) when gathering. Lower-tier flowers have a higher chance of being pollinated than higher-tier flowers.

### Nectar Lover
*   **Bee:** Shy Bee
*   **Effect:** Makes Shy Bee twice as likely to sip from a planter compared to any other bee. When sipping, it collects twice as much nectar and contributes twice as much to the planter's growth.

### Shimmering Honey
*   **Bee:** Diamond Bee
*   **Effect:** Whenever this bee converts at the hive, it grants a 25% bonus honey (+2.5% per level). This bonus is doubled if the bee is Gifted.

### Balloon Enthusiast
*   **Bee:** Buoyant Bee
*   **Effect (Balloons):** When converting from Balloons, this bee gains x3 its normal convert amount. Additionally, its base attack scales up to x3 with Balloon Blessing.

### Sniper
*   **Bee:** Precise Bee
*   **Effect (Battle):** Uses slow, long-range, more accurate shots dealing x2 damage. Accuracy is calculated as if the bee were 1 level higher (2 if gifted), and it cannot be below 5%. Critical Hits halve reload time; Super-Crits reduce it by 75%.

### Drive Expansion
*   **Bee:** Digital Bee
*   **Mechanics:** Using a Drive while this bee is active in Robo Bear's Challenge corrupts the field based on matching flower colors (Glitched Drives corrupt all fields equally). This permanently enhances the bee, granting stat bonuses:
    *   Red Drive: +0.03 Attack
    *   Blue Drive: +2 Convert Amount
    *   White Drive: +0.25 Gather Amount
    *   Glitched Drive: +0.05% Ability Rate
*   **Enhancement Bonus:** Every 10 Glitched Drives Enhancements grants a Hive Bonus of 0.03% Ability Duplication Chance.
*   **Maxing Out:** Digital Bee can be enhanced with up to 500 of each type of Drive. Upon maxing out all 2000 Drives, the bee gains +10 Movespeed.

## Other Passives

### Free Gumdrops (Unlimited Gumdrops Buff)
*   **Source:** Unlimited Gumdrops buff (obtained via codes and Glue Dispenser).
*   **Effect:** Allows the player to use Gumdrops without losing any from their inventory. This does not increase the total amount of Gumdrops in the player's inventory.

## Trivia & Notes
*   There is a visual glitch where, if the player has the Fire, Honey, Bubble, Demon, Gummy, or Diamond Mask equipped, looking at it through the Coconut Shield will cause the front of the mask to disappear due to glass textures overriding particles.
*   Bubble Bombs causes each bubble popped to increase in pitch; this effect is unique to that passive ability.
