# Werewolf

The **Werewolf** is a level 7 mob found exclusively behind the Honey Bee Gate. It is unique among mobs because it defends three fields instead of just one: the Cactus Field, Pine Tree Forest, and Pumpkin Patch. It spawns at the entrance of the Werewolf's Cave, situated between the Cactus Field and the Pumpkin Patch.

*   Standard: 60 minutes (1 hour)

**Location Details:**
The Werewolf defends the entire level containing the Cactus Field, Pumpkin Patch, and Pine Tree Forest.

Upon defeat, the Werewolf guarantees the following items:
*   50 Battle Points
*   50,000 Honey (Amount increases with Honey From Tokens)
*   750 Bond

## Strategies for Defeat

Several strategies can be used to defeat the Werewolf:

1.  **Obstruction:** Place large objects, such as a bigger cactus or smaller pumpkin, between the player and the werewolf. The werewolf will bounce off these items when attacking, making it easier to kill.
2.  **Run-and-Jump:** Similar to other mobs, the werewolf gets confused while the player is airborne. It will lunge at the point where the player was when they jumped, not their current location.
3.  **Glitch Exploitation:** The Frozen Field Defenders Glitch is an effective method against this mob.
4.  **Evasion Maneuver:** Players can pass through the Honey Bee Gate, quickly run behind the nearest Pine Tree to the Badge Bearer's Guild, and then repeatedly walk beside the werewolf as it returns to its cave entrance before running back behind the tree until defeated.

## Trivia

*   The Werewolf and the Spider are the only regularly-respawning mobs capable of attacking players outside of their immediate spawn field.
*   It is the only normal mob that defends three fields (Cactus Field, Pumpkin Patch, and Pine Tree Forest).
*   This was the first non-bug/arachnid mob added to the game.
*   The Werewolf uses the "White Fang Package" on the Roblox Catalog.
*   It is the second strongest normal mob in the game, surpassed only by the estimated health of Cave Monsters.
*   Its respawn timer (1 hour or more) makes it one of the first mobs to have such a long cooldown, alongside Stump Snail and Snowbear, and all bosses.
*   If players venture into the Werewolf's Cave, they may find a Gold Egg by crossing a tunnel, though this area is guarded by hostile Cave Monsters.
*   Defeating the werewolf can yield multiple eggs; for example, it has been known to drop five Silver Eggs at once.
