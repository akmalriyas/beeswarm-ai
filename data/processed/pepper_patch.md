# Pepper Patch

The **Pepper Patch** is a field introduced in the September 28, 2019 update. It is located past the Windy Bee Gate, to the right of the Extreme Memory Match, and to the left of the Wind Shrine. This field contains no protective mobs.

## Field Statistics
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Field Color** | Red |
| **Dimensions** | 27 × 21 flowers |
| **Total Flowers** | 567 |
| **Bee Prerequisite** | 35 bees |
| **Nectar Type** | Invigorating Nectar |

### Flower Distribution (Natural State)
The field contains red and white flowers, with the only natural size being triple.

| Size | White | Red | Blue | Total | Percentage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Single | 0 | 0% | 0 | 0 | 0% |
| Double | 0 | 0% | 0 | 0 | 0% |
| Triple | 85 | 15% | 482 | 85% | 100% |
| **Total** | **85** | **15%** | **482** | **85%** | **100%** |

*Note: Flower counts may vary due to rounding, and in-game generation is random.*

## Mechanics and Preferences
When gathering pollen here, the Pepper Patch can spawn tokens including Strawberry, Bitterberry, Royal Jelly, Red Extract, Ticket, and Magic Bean.

### Bee Preferences
*   **Likes:** Spicy Bee
*   **Dislikes:** Looker Bee, Frosty Bee, Baby Bee, Fuzzy Bee

This field grants Invigorating Nectar when a Planter is planted within it. A level 9-12 Rogue Vicious Bee has a chance to spawn here.

## Boosts and Enhancements
Several methods can enhance pollen collection on the Pepper Patch:

*   **Red Field Booster:** Using this booster (requires 35+ bees) may grant a Pepper Patch Boost, providing +100% Pepper Patch pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Applying Glitter adds 100% Pepper Patch pollen to the current boost for 15 minutes, provided the boost is not already maximized.
*   **Dice:** Using Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice may add +100% to +300% Pepper Patch pollen for 15 minutes, if the current boost limit has not been reached.
*   **Winds:** Pepper Patch Winds can be obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes. They offer a base of +20% instant Pepper Patch conversion and grant an additional +5% instant conversion and +10% pollen per stack. These winds can stack up to 15 times.

### Obsolete Boost Methods
The following methods are no longer possible:
*   Redeeming certain expired codes (e.g., Sriracha, DemiDecade).
*   Purchasing a Pepper Patch Market Boost from the Boost Market or specific codes, which previously offered x1.25 Pepper Patch Pollen alongside boosts to Capacity and Convert Rate.

## Trivia
*   The Pepper Patch is one of the few fields past a bee gate that has no protective mobs.
*   It is the only red field that yields Invigorating Nectar.
*   This field, along with Coconut Field, is one of the only locations where sprouts cannot appear naturally or be planted using the Special Sprout Summoner.
*   The Pepper Patch Badge, Coconut Badge, Mountain Top Badge, and Hive Hub Badge all require more pollen collection than other field badges.
