# Exhausted Bee

**Exhausted Bee** is a Colorless, Epic bee known for its unique endurance and slow, persistent work ethic. Its description reads: *"This bee suffers from insomnia. It moves slowly, but it never has to sleep."*

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Epic |
| **Color** | Colorless |
| **Energy** | Unlimited |
| **Speed** | 10.5 |
| **Attack** | 2 |

### Habitat Preferences
Exhausted Bee favors the following fields:
*   Stump Field
*   Sunflower Field
*   Dandelion Field

It dislikes the Cactus Field.

### Stats and Mechanics
The Exhausted Bee possesses several unique mechanical traits:

*   **Pollen Collection:** Collects 10 Pollen in 4.6 seconds (base rate).
*   **Honey Production:** Makes 240 Honey in 4 seconds (base rate).
*   **Movement/Gathering Penalty:** Suffers a -25% penalty to Move Speed and gather speed.
*   **Conversion Bonus:** Gains +160 Convert Amount.
*   **Energy Mechanic:** Does not require Sleep, thanks to its unlimited energy status.

**Gifted Hive Bonus:** When gifted, the Exhausted Bee provides a +20% White Field Capacity bonus.

### Abilities
Exhausted Bee has two unique abilities:

*   **Buzz Bomb:** Collects 7 pollen from 13 surrounding Flowers (+10% pollen per Level). This ability can be combined with other bombs to increase power.
*   **Token Link:** Collects all other ability tokens, granting 25 Honey (+10 per Level) for each token collected.

### Gallery and Trivia
*   **Visuals:** The bee has several variations, including its original hive slot design, a gifted version, and various facial models (standard, beta).
*   **Energy Setting:** Exhausted Bee's energy is technically set to an extremely high number (999,999,999,999), though due to coding limitations, it can still sleep if its value drops below a large finite amount.
*   **Unique Status:** It was one of the first bees implemented with unlimited energy, sharing this status only with the Photon Bee. It is the third bee dedicated a Royal Jelly variant.
*   **Developer Reference:** The developer Onett often refers to himself as an Exhausted Bee due to his dedication to the game; the expired code "OnettJelly" references this.
*   **Rare Finds:** When gathering in the Stump Field, Sunflower Field, or Dandelion Field, it has a rare chance to find a Moai Sticker.
*   **Stat Discrepancy:** While its in-game stat menu might list its movespeed as 15% less than normal (14), the actual penalty applied is 25%.
