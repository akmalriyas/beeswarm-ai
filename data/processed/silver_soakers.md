# Silver Soakers

![Silver Soakers](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f4/Silver_Soakers.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20180911213312)

The Silver Soakers are the second lowest tier of sprinkler available for purchase in the Badge Bearer's Guild. This item is comprised of two individual sprinklers that fire faster than basic models, providing enhanced coverage and efficiency.

## Acquisition Details
*   **Requirement:** 10 Hotshot Badges
*   **Cost:** 22,222,222 Honey (~22.2 million)

## Sprinkler Statistics
| Statistic | Value (Per Unit) | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Radius** | 7 tiles | Coverage area per sprinkler. |
| **Power** | 7 | Water intensity/effectiveness. |
| **Fire Rate** | 4.5 seconds | Time between water bursts. |
| **Setup Time** | 5 seconds | Time required to deploy the unit. |
