# Fire Mask

![Fire Mask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/70/Fire_Mask.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403020251)

**Description:**
"Ignite your mind to enhance your Red Bees."

The Fire Mask is a mask that can be purchased at the Red HQ. It is an Epic Bee item.

## Stats and Effects

| Stat | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Red Pollen** | x1.5 | Multiplier |
| **Instant Red Conversion** | +15% | Percentage increase |
| **Capacity** | x1.5 | Multiplier |
| **Instant Flame Conversion** | +50% | Percentage increase |
| **Bee Attack** | +15% | Percentage increase |
| **Defense** | +35% | Percentage increase |
| **Bee Ability Rate** | +15% | Percentage increase |

**Passive: Ignite**
Every 15th Red Ability token collected triggers the Ignite ability. This creates 5 spires of flames in a "+" formation, collecting 6 Red, 3 White, or 1 Blue pollen from 9 surrounding flowers. The collection yield is increased by 2% for every Red Bee and 4% for each Gifted Red Bee.
*   **Combat:** If touched by a mob, the flame spires deal 15 damage per second for 4 seconds.
*   **Cooldown:** The passive ability has no cooldown.

## Trivia and History

*   The Fire Mask is the red counterpart to the Bubble Mask.
*   It belongs to a group of Epic Bee items that includes the Bubble Mask, Riley Guard, Bucko Guard, and Honey Mask.
*   **Passive Inheritance:** If a player owns the Fire Mask, its passive ability (Ignite) is inherited by the Demon Mask.
*   **Historical Stats Changes:**
    *   **Before 2019-09-28 Update:** Red Pollen was x1.7; included +2 Red Bee Attack Power and +35% Defense.
    *   **Before 2019-04-05 Update:** Included +25% Bee Gather Pollen, +25% Instant Conversion, +115% Loot Luck, and +2 Red Bee Attack Power.
    *   **Pre-Passive Era:** Before the 2019-09-28 update, the mask did not have a passive ability. The passive previously had a 5-second cooldown before the 2020-06-06 Update.
