The Top Brown Bear Helpers leaderboard is one of the game's 64 leaderboards. It tracks the top 100 players who have completed the most quests for the Brown Bear entity. The displayed view shows 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view all 100 entries. Notably, this is currently the only leaderboard that does not feature a progress pad for players to track their current standing.

## Location
The Brown Bear quest area is located to the right of the main Brown Bear entity.

## Achievements and Milestones
*   **Brown Cub Skin:** On June 7, 2020, Lipsisas was the first user to reach 300 quests and obtain the [Brown Cub](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9d/Brown_Cub.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404050339) skin.
*   **1,000 Quests:** Sometime in mid-April 2023, 7Elevenss became the first user to complete 1,000 Brown Bear quests.

## Trivia and Mechanics
*   **Progress Tracking:** This leaderboard is unique because it does not display a progress pad showing how much of the current objective a player has completed. Instead, the Brown Bear entity informs the player about their quest progress every time they successfully complete a quest.
    *   Historically, there were three leaderboards without this feature. The [Most Commando Captures](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Most_Commando_Captures) leaderboard was removed from the game, and the [Highest Damage to a Single Puffshroom](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Highest_Damage_to_a_Single_Puffshroom) leaderboard later gained a progress pad.
*   **Quest Counting:** This is the only leaderboard that tracks the total number of quests completed for any specific bear type.
*   **Cross-Leaderboard Ranking:** 7Elevenss, who currently holds the top spot on this leaderboard, also ranks at the top of the [Top Riley Bee Helpers](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Top_Riley_Bee_Helpers) leaderboard.
