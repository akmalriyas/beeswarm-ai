# Pumpkin Patch Field

The Pumpkin Patch is a specialized field located past the Honey Bee Gate and adjacent to the Cactus Field. It is protected by the Werewolf mob.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Field Color** | Assorted |
| **Bee Prerequisite** | 15 bees |
| **Nectar Type** | Satisfying Nectar |
| **Dimensions** | 33 × 17 flowers |

## Field Mechanics and Resources

The Pumpkin Patch can yield various tokens when collecting pollen, including Honey, Treat, Ticket, Magic Bean, and Glue. This field grants Satisfying Nectar and increases the chance of dropping waxes if a Planter is planted here.

**Flower Distribution:**
The field contains 561 flowers in white, red, and blue varieties, consisting of single, double, and triple sizes.

### Special Features
*   A level 1–20 Wild Windy Bee has a chance of spawning here.
*   The largest pumpkin located in the corner of the field occasionally generates Sparkles before displaying a face.

## Obtaining Boosts

Several methods can enhance pollen collection in the Pumpkin Patch:

*   **Field Booster:** Using a Field Booster may grant a Pumpkin Patch Boost, providing +200% Pumpkin Patch pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Applying Glitter to the field adds 100% Pumpkin Patch pollen to the current boost (if not already maxed).
*   **Dice:** Using Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice may add +100% to +300% Pumpkin Patch pollen for 15 minutes (if not already maxed).
*   **Pumpkin Patch Winds:** These winds can be acquired via the Wind Shrine or codes. They offer a base of +20% instant Pumpkin Patch conversion, plus +5% instant conversion and +20% pollen per stack. Normally stacking up to 13 times, they can be boosted by up to 15x using codes.
*   **Shell Amulet:** If the Shell Amulet possesses a Pumpkin Patch Boost, it can further boost the field by +5% to +30%, depending on its rarity.
*   **Polar Bear Gift:** Giving Polar Bear a Present grants the player Pumpkin Patch Boost x3.

### Deprecated Methods (No Longer Possible)
The following methods for obtaining boosts are obsolete:

*   Giving Presents to certain NPCs:
    *   Polar Bear previously offered Pumpkin Patch Boost x4 and other items/boosts.
    *   Onett provided Pumpkin Patch Boost x1 and other items/boosts.
    *   During Beesmas 2021, Polar Bear gave Pumpkin Patch Boost x3.
*   Redeeming specific expired codes (Examples include: Tabby (x1), ThnxCyasToyBox (x3 + capacity bonus), Xanthan (x4), PumpkinOfJustice (x7), TrickOrTreat (x6), Boo swarm (4x boost + 15x winds)).
*   Purchasing a Pumpkin Patch Market Boost from the Boost Market, which previously offered x1.5 Capacity, x1.25 Pollen, x1.25 System Capacity, and +50 Convert Amount.

## Trivia
*   This field's badge and the Sunflower Badge are used three times for chances in Memory Match, making it one of the most frequently featured field badges.
*   A Royal Jelly token can be found behind the two pumpkins located in the corner of the field.
*   The Pumpkin Patch is one of only three fields where objects come to life; the others are Pineapple Patch and Sunflower Field.
*   Prior to the 2018-04-27 update, its flower mix was identical to that of the Cactus Field.
*   Historically, this field was the smallest until the Stump Field was added. Due to the expansion of the Stump Field, it has since become the smallest again (excluding the Ant Field).
*   It is one of three fields named with "Patch" instead of "Field," alongside Pineapple Patch and Pepper Patch.
*   Before the 2022-12-26 update, pumpkins near the Werewolf's Cave had collision detection; this feature has since been removed.
