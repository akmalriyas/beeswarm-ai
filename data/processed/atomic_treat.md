# Atomic Treat

[![Atomic Treat](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Atomic_Treat.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041728)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/45/Atomic_Treat.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041728)

*"Always causes a random Mutation in a bee! Mutations grant bonus stats."*

An **Atomic Treat** is an item that, when fed to a [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees"), raises its [bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") by 1000 and grants the bee a random [mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation"). Possible mutations include: [Attack](/wiki/Bee_Attack "Bee Attack"), [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page"), [Gather Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Gather_Total "System Page"), [Energy](/wiki/Energy "Energy"), and [Bee Ability Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Ability_Rate "System Page").

## Ways to Obtain

### Drops
*   As a very rare drop from defeating the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab").
*   As a rare reward from the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge").
*   As a rare drop from [Debug Sprouts](/wiki/Sprout#Debug_Sprout "Sprout").
*   As a very rare drop from a [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower").

### Quests
*   **Brown Bear:** Rewards Atomic Treats upon completion of specific quests:
    *   125th quest: 1 **Atomic Treat**.
    *   225th quest: 1 **Atomic Treat**.
    *   375th quest: 1 **Atomic Treat**.
*   **Science Bear:** Completing the "Repairing the White Drive" quest rewards 1 **Atomic Treat**.

### Beesmas & Events
*   **Bee Bear Quests:**
    *   Completing “Searching For Snowflakes” rewards 1 **Atomic Treat** and other items.
    *   Purchasing the Radioactive Bundle in [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog") (for 150 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") and 150 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear")) grants 5 **Atomic Treats** and other items.
*   **Outdated Event Rewards:**
    *   Purchasing the Tempting Treat Pack during Beesmas 2024 (for 10 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear")) granted 3 **Atomic Treats**.
    *   Purchasing it for 1,000 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake "Snowflake") during Beesmas 2021.
    *   Purchasing it for 10 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear "Gingerbread Bear") during Beesmas 2020.
    *   Completing Bee Bear's "Retro Ho Ho" quest (Winter 2024) rewarded 1 **Atomic Treat** and other items.
    *   Completing Bee Bear's "Peppermint Preparation (4/5)" quest (Beesmas 2022) rewarded 3 **Atomic Treats** and other items.
    *   Completing Bee Bear's 'Snow Cub Reformation (5/5)' quest (Beesmas 2021) rewarded 3 **Atomic Treats** and other items.
    *   Completing Bee Bear's "A Beesmas Miracle" quest (Beesmas 2021) rewarded 1 **Atomic Treat** and other items.
    *   Completing Bee Bear's "Bee Cub Workshop 5" quest (Beesmas 2019) rewarded 1 **Atomic Treat** and other items.
    *   Brown Bear’s Beesmas 2022 quest rewarded 1 **Atomic Treat**.
