# Pro Shop

[ ![Shop2New](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b9/Shop2New.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20240120122904) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b9/Shop2New.png/revision/latest?cb=20240120122904 "Shop2New")

## Location and Overview

The Pro Shop is a shop located past the Brave Bee Gate, next to the Pineapple Patch.

Historically, the Mythic Meteor Shower Summoner was positioned on top of this shop before being relocated to the Badge Bearer's Guild during the Trading Update.

Like the Noob Shop, the game does not explicitly name this vendor location. However, since the bear who runs the shop is named "Pro Bear," the community has adopted this name for the establishment.

The Pro Shop sells a variety of items including Bags, Tools, Guards, Hats, Belts, Boots, Paper Planters, and the Parachute.

## Trivia

*   The Slingshot sometimes undershoots the player near Panda Bear or in the middle of the Bamboo Field. If it does not undershoot, it will regularly shoot the player in front of the shop's door.
*   There are two Royal Jelly tokens: one on top of the shop at the end of a dark passage, and one hidden inside the shop (in the Mazes) behind Pro Bear. The player can obtain the indoor token by navigating to the end, turning right, going to the end again, turning right, and following the first corridor on the right.
*   Since the 2018-07-11 update, a Sunflower Seed token has been placed inside the maze that grants 10 Sunflower Seeds. This is found by navigating right twice (like the Royal Jelly), but then taking the second right, going to the end of the hall, and following through the last corridor to the right.
*   A giant pineapple near the Pro Shop holds three Tickets on top. These can be collected by using the Red Cannon or Parachute/Glider to glide down onto the top.
*   If a player stands on the piece of blue wall behind the backpack models and jumps high enough, they can enter the space between the roof and the ceiling.
*   Spotted chicks were previously able to spawn in this hidden space before being removed.
*   With 18 purchasable items, the Pro Shop has the most items available among all shops. It is also the only shop with a bear vendor that sells at least one type of guard.
