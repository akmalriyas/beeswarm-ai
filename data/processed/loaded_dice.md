# Loaded Dice

Loaded Dice is an inventory item introduced in the December 26, 2021 update. It functions as a powerful field boost utility with specific mechanics and acquisition methods.

**Stats:**
*   **Effect:** Boosts three random fields with 300% pollen for 15 minutes. The field the player is currently standing in has a biased chance of being selected.
*   **Cooldown:** 3 seconds
*   **Cap:** 25 (This item is unique in having this cap, unlike Field Dice which previously shared it.)

If a player uses Loaded Dice while another dice boost effect is active, the new boost will replace the previous one entirely, including its duration.

## Mechanics and Bias System

The core feature of Loaded Dice is its bias mechanism. To activate the bias, the player must be standing on a field when using the item.

*   **Initial Chance:** When the first Loaded Dice is used while standing in a field, there is a 25% chance that the first selected field will be the one the player is currently occupying.
*   **Increasing Bias:** With each subsequent use of Loaded Dice (while remaining on the same field), this bias chance increases by an additional 25%, up to a maximum of 100%.
*   **Reset Condition:** If the player leaves the field while the boost is active, the bias chance resets.

## Acquisition Methods

Loaded Dice can be obtained through various means:

### Crafting
It can be crafted using the Blender with the following materials:
*   3 Smooth Dice
*   3 Hard Waxes
*   3 Oils
*   1 Glue

### Rewards and Drops
*   **Robo Bear Challenge:** Can be received as a rare reward (up to 3 per challenge).
*   **Sticker Stack Rewards:** Received as a stack reward for adding specific stickers:
    *   Bomber Bee Bear rewards 1 Loaded Dice.
    *   Triple Exclamation rewards 1 Loaded Dice.
*   **Drops from Mobs/Planters:**
    *   Very rare drop from Werewolf and Tunnel Bear.
    *   Rare drop from Epic, Legendary, and Mythic Puffshrooms.
    *   Bonus drops from certain planters:
        *   Festive Planter, Tacky Planter, and Petal Planter (standard chance).
        *   Tacky Planter planted in the Mountain Top Field (36-day cooldown).
        *   Heat-Treated Planter planted in the Mushroom Field (~1 day cooldown).
        *   Hydroponic Planter planted in the Blue Flower Field (~1 day cooldown).

### Quests and Tokens
*   **Dapper Bear's Quests:** Completing Dapper Bear's 10th quest rewards 1 Loaded Dice. Completing the 15th quest rewards 3 Loaded Dice.
*   **Token Location:** A token located near the Red HQ roof grants 1 Loaded Dice.

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Real-World Analogy:** In reality, loaded dice are rigged to favor one side appearing more often, which mirrors the in-game bias towards the field the player is standing on.
*   **Dice Types:** The fields displayed on the dice include Sunflower Field, Clover Field, Coconut Field, Pumpkin Patch, Pine Tree Forest, and Blue Flower Field. An 'X' icon on the front of the die is presumed to indicate the "biased" slot for the player's current field.
*   **Rolling Conflict:** If a player attempts to roll another dice while one is already being rolled, a notification will appear: "You are already rolling a dice."
