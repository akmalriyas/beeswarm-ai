# Day/Night Cycle

The **Day/Night Cycle** was introduced in the 2018-09-10 update, replacing a previous state where the game was perpetually daytime.

*Note: As of the latest updates (e.g., 12/?/24), the cycle timing has been reported as irregular.*

## Daytime Mechanics

Daytime is the default setting for the game.
*   **Duration:** Historically, daytime lasted between 10 and 30 minutes (averaging 20 minutes). Recently, this duration has shortened to 2–10 minutes.
*   **Audio:** All standard music plays normally during the day.

## Nighttime Mechanics

When the day ends, the sun sets quickly, and the sky shifts to black, indicating nighttime.
*   **Duration:** The night cycle lasts for 2 minutes and 35 seconds (the duration of the "mountaincall" theme).
*   **Audio:** All outdoor music is replaced by the **Mountaincall** track. However, specific areas or events can override this:
    *   Music from vendors, crawlers, gbtune1, hibernation1, starhall, Ant Challenges, Robo Bear Challenges, and Stick Bug Challenges will override the night music.

### Nighttime Events & Features

During nighttime, several unique mechanics activate:

**Fireflies:**
*   **Appearance:** Fireflies appear in different fields (Rose Field, Cactus Field, Pineapple Patch, Spider Field, Strawberry Field, Bamboo Field).
*   **Interaction:** Walking near a firefly causes it to fly into the air and leave a 1x1 patch of **Sparkles**.
*   **Behavior:** A firefly can only stop in the same field three times. After being removed from a field three times, it will move to a different field.
*   **Token Drops:** Once all fireflies have been removed from a player's formation area, one of the following tokens may appear:
    *   **Moon Charms:** (Increments of 1, 5, or 10)
    *   **Glitter:** (Very rare)
    *   **Star Jelly:** (Extremely rare)
    *   **Night Bell:** (Extremely rare)
    *   **Star Treat:** (Exceptionally rare)
    *   **Waxing Crescent Moon Sticker:** (Rare)
    *   **Glowing Smile Sticker:** (Extremely Rare)
    *   **Star Signs Stickers:** (Extremely Rare)
    *   **Cyan Star Sticker:** (Exceptionally Rare)
    *   **Shining Star Sticker:** (Exceptionally Rare)

**Environmental Changes & Spawns:**
*   The background of the server and walls turn pitch black.
*   A **Rogue Vicious Bee** has an increased chance of spawning every night.
*   A moon sprout has a 60% chance of spawning every night.
*   **Sprout Mechanics:** When sprouts are planted at night, there is a 60% chance for it to be a Moon Sprout. This alters the odds of other sprout types (Supreme Sprouts become more common while others become rarer).

**Platform Changes (Moon Amulet):**
*   Since the 2018-11-25 and 2018-12-19 updates, moon-shaped platforms leading to the **Diamond Mask**, **Bubble Bee Man**, and **Night Memory Match** are solid yellow only during nighttime if the player possesses a **Moon Amulet**.
*   *Exception:* If the player owns the Diamond Mask, those specific platforms will be blue and remain permanently solid.

## Trivia

*   If the Stick Bug Challenge concludes at night, the normal daytime music will play instead of the night music.
*   If the time cycles between day and night while a **Honeystorm** or **Mythic Meteor Shower** is active, the fog will dissipate, but the storm/shower event will continue uninterrupted.
*   In some instances, the nighttime OST does not necessarily conclude; it may simply fade into daytime.
*   The Night Bell and Spirit Bear can manually summon nighttime. Onett (Developer) can also globally summon nighttime, accompanied by a message (e.g., "🌙 One of Mother Bear's cubs has summoned nighttime... 🌙").
