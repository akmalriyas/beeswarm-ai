## Overview

The Highest Robo Party Cake Rank is one of the game's 64 leaderboards. It was introduced in the April 1, 2023 update. This leaderboard is located near the Robo Party Cake in the Rose Field.

**Mechanics:**
To summon a Robo Party, a player must complete the Beesmas quest given by the Robo Bear. Each time a player successfully beats their Robo Party, their Robo Party Cake Rank increases by one. The leaderboard features 100 total ranks, with each subsequent rank requiring more party points to achieve than the previous one. The highest-ranked player is displayed at the top of the list.

## Trivia

*   This leaderboard is the third to be removed; it followed the Most Commando Captures and Highest Snowbear Level leaderboards.
*   It was one of two leaderboards that were temporarily removed, alongside the Highest Snowbear Level leaderboard.
