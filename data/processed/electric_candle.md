# Electric Candle

The **Electric Candle** is a Level 6 Beesmas Beequip, characterized by its red color requirement and an equip limit of three. It is described as "A battery powered candle that produces an acceptable flicker."

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Ways to Obtain
*   As a reward from Brown Bear's Stockings.
*   Completing Gifted Riley Bee's 2025 Beesmas quest grants one Electric Candle.

### Outdated/Historical Ways
The following methods have been retired or updated:
*   Buying the "Waxy Bundle" from Bee Bear’s Catalog for 1,500 Snowflakes and 15 Gingerbread Bears.
*   Completing Bee Bear's Snow Cub Reformation (1/5).
*   Redeeming the code "WintersEnd."
*   Buying the "Candlelight Bundle" from Bee Bear's Catalog for 1,500 Snowflakes and 50 Gingerbread Bears.
*   Upon completing Gifted Riley Bee's 2024 Beesmas Quest.
*   Buying the "Candlewax Bundle" from Bee Bear's Catalog for 333 Snowflakes and 3 Gingerbread Bears.
