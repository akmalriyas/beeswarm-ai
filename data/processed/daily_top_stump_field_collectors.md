# Daily Top Stump Field Collectors

The **Daily Top Stump Field Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. This leaderboard tracks how much pollen players have collected specifically within the Stump Field on a given day.

## Mechanics and Viewing

*   **Access:** The leaderboard is only viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu found on the System Page.
*   **Display:** It shows 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view all entries up to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

Players are awarded rewards based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 receive a **Stump Field Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 receive 25 **Tickets**.
