# The Supreme Saturator

[![TheSupremeSaturatorOriginal](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a4/The_Supreme_Saturator.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20230910221943)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a4/The_Supreme_Saturator.png/revision/latest?cb=20230910221943 "The Supreme Saturator Original")

## Overview
The Supreme Saturator is the highest tier sprinkler available for purchase in the Badge Bearer's Guild. It is described as, "The ultimate sprinkler. Nobody knows how it works or where it came from."

### Acquisition Requirements
*   **Cost:** 55,555,555,555 Honey
*   **Unlock Requirement:** Obtain 10 different Grandmaster Badges

### Statistics
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Radius** | 16 tiles |
| **Power** | 10 |
| **Rate** | 1 second |
| **Total Pollen Output** | 8,042 pollen per second |

The Supreme Saturator is a single sprinkler. Its high output of 8,042 pollen per second significantly outperforms four Diamond Drenchers (which produce 1,809 pollen per second each), making it over 340% more effective (4.44x).

## Trivia & Lore
*   The shape of The Supreme Saturator resembles the head of a bear.
*   It is currently the most expensive sprinkler in the game.
*   Prior to the 2021-12-26 update, it was the second most expensive item (excluding Hive Slots) in terms of Honey, only behind the Gummy Boots.

### Historical Milestones
*   SDMittens was the first player to purchase The Supreme Saturator on September 19, 2018. An in-game message congratulating her appeared across all servers: "Wow! SDMittens has unleashed THE SUPREME SATURATOR on the mountain!"
*   It is one of the few sprinklers capable of completely covering an entire field, alongside The Stump Field and various Brick Fields (Mixed, Blue, Red, and White). It could theoretically also cover the Hub Field.
