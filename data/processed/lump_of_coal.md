# Lump Of Coal

A **Lump of Coal** is a Level 13 Beequip that can be equipped by bees. It has an equip limit of three and requires no specific color requirement.

> "Naughty bees are forced to carry this burdensome load."
> — Beesmas

## Stats & Mechanics

The Lump of Coal provides various stat modifiers, including negative effects on Energy% and Movespeed%, alongside positive boosts to several types of Pollen.

**General Characteristics:**
*   **Level:** 13
*   **Equip Limit:** 3
*   **Color Requirement:** Any

## Acquisition

### Way(s) to Obtain
*   By dying from the Naughty List's falling coal during the 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025 events.

### Trivia
*   The Lump of Coal is one of eight Beequips designed to provide a negative stat. The other seven are Kazoo, Whistle, Beesmas Top, Toy Horn, Demon Talisman, Pink Eraser, and Toy Drum.
*   It is the only beequip that provides two negative stats (-Energy% and -Movespeed%).
*   Tied with the Charm Bracelet, it has the most possible hive bonuses (eight) among all Beequips.
