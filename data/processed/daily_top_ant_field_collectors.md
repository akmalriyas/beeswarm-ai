# Daily Top Ant Field Collectors (Archived)

The **Daily Top Ant Field Collectors** leaderboard was one of the 66 leaderboards in the game at the time. Unlike other leaderboards, it did not appear on the System Page or anywhere else in-game. This leaderboard tracked how much pollen players collected in the Ant Field during a single day. It reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

If a player reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they were awarded the **Ant Field Stamp Sticker**.

![Hivesticker ant field stamp](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/49/Hivesticker_ant_field_stamp.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20240204142302)

## Trivia

*   This leaderboard, along with the Brick Field Leaderboards, was one of the few leaderboards that did not display anywhere in the game.
*   It was the fourth leaderboard to be removed, following Most Commando Captures, Highest Snowbear Level, and Highest Robo Party Cake Rank.
*   This leaderboard shared a reward with the Top Ant Exterminators leaderboard; these two are the only leaderboards known to have shared rewards.
