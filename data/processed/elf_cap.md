# Elf Cap

![Elf Cap](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/74/Elf_Cap.png/revision/latest?cb=20230409130552) | Level: 3
---
**Description:** "A darling cap fitted with a bell to keep the laborer alert."

An **Elf Cap** is a level 3 Beesmas Beequip. It has an equip limit of two and no color requirement.

## Acquisition Methods

The Elf Cap can be obtained through several methods:
*   From the Ruby Gift Box.
*   As a drop from a Ladybug.
*   As a drop from Stockings.

**Outdated Method:**
*   Reward for completing Bee Bear's second quest during Beesmas 2020.
