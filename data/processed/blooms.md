# Blooms

Blooms are passive, field-based entities that can spawn naturally or be summoned using a [Bloom Shaker](/wiki/Bloom_Shaker). They first appeared in the [2025-12-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2025-12-25 "Updates"). These large flowers are adorned with colored petals.

## Mechanics and Behavior

Blooms scale their maximum petal count based on their level.

**Collection Rewards:**
Collecting petals from a bloom grants the respective petal's buff and honey equal to half of the bloom's original HP. Even if the bloom's health is below 100 pollen, collecting petals guarantees at least 50 honey.

**Growth Cycle:**
When created, a bloom has a 20-second timer and an initial "base" petal color, which typically makes up the majority of its petals. Once the required pollen is met, the bloom pops, scattering each petal in a circular pattern. After popping, a higher-level "child" bloom of the identical base color will spawn. Child blooms cease spawning one minute after the original "parent" bloom spawned.

**Mutation:**
Blooms have a small chance to be "mutated," meaning one or more petals are a different color than the bloom's base color. The chance of mutation increases as the bloom's level rises. A parent bloom being mutated does not affect the probability of its child blooms also mutating.

**Interaction (Petal Shuriken):**
Hitting blooms with a Petal Shuriken (from [Petal Wand](/wiki/Petal_Wand) or [Petal Belt](/wiki/Petal_Belt)) causes them to spawn bonus petals. This attack deals 20% of the bloom's remaining HP and 5% of its maximum HP.

## Levels

Blooms possess multiple levels, similar to how [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom) function; they respawn at a higher level upon defeat.

### Petals per Level
The number of petals a bloom can have is capped at 11 for level 20 and above. For any level below 20, the petal count is determined by the formula:

$$\lfloor (level \times 2) \div 5 \rfloor + 3$$

| Level Range | Petals |
| :---: | :---: |
| 1–2 | 3 |
| 3–4 | 4 |
| 5–7 | 5 |
| 8–9 | 6 |
| 10-12 | 7 |
| 13-14 | 8 |
| 15-17 | 9 |
| 18-19 | 10 |
| 20+ | 11 |

## Petals and Buffs

When a bloom explodes, it releases petals that grant stacks of petal buffs and honey. While the petals are randomized, their chances vary by field. However, petals in the [Hub Field](/wiki/Hub_Field) have nearly equal probabilities.

Each collected petal provides a temporary boost corresponding to its color, similar to [Jelly Bean](/wiki/Jelly_Beans) boosts. These stacks can accumulate up to 100 times, with each stack lasting 10 seconds. Every single stack grants at least +40% [Unique Instant Conversion](/wiki/Instant_Conversion#Unique_Instant_Conversion).
