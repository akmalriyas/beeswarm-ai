# Clouds

[ ![Screenshot 2019-09-28 at 12.06.56 PM](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4e/Screenshot_2019-09-28_at_12.06.56_PM.png/revision/latest?cb=20190928191520 "Cloud Screenshot") ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4e/Screenshot_2019-09-28_at_12.06.56_PM.png/revision/latest?cb=20190928191520 "Cloud Screenshot")

A Cloud is an entity that regrows flowers and grants short pollen buffs in the field. Clouds are small, white rain clouds that can be summoned through various methods (see below). They move aimlessly over a period of 1 to 5 minutes, depending on their source. A cloud rains over 49 surrounding flowers and functions as a secondary sprinkler.

**Mechanics:**
*   **Sprout Growth:** Clouds can help grow a sprout if a player is present in the same field. The amount of pollen given to the sprout depends on the type of sprout and the field itself.
*   **Cloud Boost Buff:** If a player is standing under a cloud, they receive the Cloud Boost buff:
    *   Base Buff: +25% Pollen and +10% Unique Instant Conversion.
    *   Enhanced Buff (Gifted/Windy Bee): +50% Pollen and +20% Unique Instant Conversion.
    *   Duration: Up to 8 seconds.
*   **Planter Acceleration:** Clouds can accelerate a planter by up to 20% when six clouds are present, or about 8% with just one cloud. However, maintaining clouds for extended periods is costly and generally not viable for long-term planter buffing.

**Limitations:**
*   There can only be a maximum of 6 clouds on a field at any given time. If a player attempts to summon another cloud when the limit is reached, they will receive the message: "There are too many clouds in this field."
*   Clouds cannot be summoned in challenge fields (e.g., Ant Field). Attempting to do so results in the message: "You can't make clouds in a challenge field."

## Methods To Summon

Clouds can be generated using the following methods, each with a specific duration:

*   **Cloud Vial:** Using a cloud vial (3 minute duration).
*   **Wind Shrine:** Receiving field winds from the Wind Shrine (5 minute duration).
*   **Wild Windy Bee:** Activating or defeating a Wild Windy Bee (3 minute duration).
*   **Windy Bee:** Summoned by a player's own Windy Bee (1 minute duration + 10 seconds per level of the Windy Bee).

## Types Of Clouds

There are two distinct types of clouds: Rain Cloud and Windy Cloud.

**Rain Cloud:**
These beneficial clouds are summoned via donations to the Wind Shrine, using a cloud vial, or while fighting a Wild Windy Bee. They also spawn when tamed Windy Bees appear in the field. Rain Clouds provide the full benefits (cloud buff, sprout growth, flower regeneration).

**Windy Cloud:**
The Windy Cloud is exclusively seen when a Wild Windy Bee has appeared in the field. It floats over the area containing the Wild Windy Bee. When a player approaches and jumps into the cloud, it transforms into a Rain Cloud, and the Wild Windy Bee begins to attack. Like Rain Clouds, it provides the cloud buff, allows sprout growth, and regenerates flowers.

**Cloud Token Notification:**
Collecting a Cloud Ability Token will display the message: 🌧️ Windy Bee summoned a Cloud in {Field Name}. 🌧️ If another player's Windy Bee spawns a cloud in your field, an announcement will appear: 🌧️ {Username}'s Windy Bee summoned a Cloud in this field. 🌧️
