# Pink Shades

Pink Shades is a Level 10 Beequip with an equip limit of one. It has no color requirement and can only be equipped to Gifted Bees (Basic, Rad, Bomber, Demo, Honey, and Shy).

**Description:** Flamboyant glasses that protect your eyes while making you look like a rock star.
**Type:** Beesmas

### Acquisition Methods

Pink Shades is an exceptionally rare item and can be obtained through several methods:

*   As a reward from the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   As a reward from the Stick Bug Challenge.
*   As a drop from Tacky Planter (1 in 100,000 at full growth).
*   As a drop from The Planter Of Plenty.
*   As a reward for completing Honey Bee's Beesmas quest (2025 version).

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
Pink Shades was previously obtainable as a reward from the Honey Bee's Beesmas quests in 2022, 2024 (Summer), and 2024 (Winter).
