# Super Smoothie

![Super Smoothie](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5f/Super_Smoothie.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404023801)

> "Grants the improved boosts of many consumable items combined for 20 minutes!"

**Cooldown:** 20 minutes

The **Super Smoothie** is a craftable inventory item introduced in the 2020-06-06 update. When consumed, it grants the following buffs for 20 minutes:

*   **Capacity Multiplier:** x1.5
*   **Pollen Buffs:**
    *   Red Pollen: x1.6
    *   Blue Pollen: x1.6
    *   White Pollen: x1.6
    *   Bee Gather Pollen: x1.4
    *   Tool Pollen: x1.4
*   **Conversion Buffs:**
    *   Convert Rate: x1.5
    *   Hive Convert Rate: x1.5
    *   Instant Conversion: +20%
*   **Combat Stats:**
    *   Super-Crit Chance: +1%
    *   Critical Chance: +6%
*   **Movement Speed:**
    *   Bee Movespeed: x1.25
    *   Player Movespeed: x1.25

***

### Interaction Notes

The Super Smoothie cannot be active simultaneously with other consumable buffs such as Red Extracts, Blue Extracts, Glue, Oils, Tropical Drinks, Purple Potions, or Enzymes. If any of these items are active when a Super Smoothie is used, their effects are immediately removed and replaced by the Super Smoothie's buffs.

***

## Acquisition Methods

The Super Smoothie can be obtained through several methods:

### Crafting
It can be crafted using a Blender with the following ingredients:

*   3 Neonberries
*   3 Star Jellies
*   3 Purple Potions
*   6 Tropical Drinks

### Drops and Rewards
*   **Mob Drops:** Extremely rare drops from certain mobs, such as the Coconut Crab or Tunnel Bear.
*   **Mythic Meteor Showers:** Can drop as a reward during Mythic Meteor Shower events.
*   **Robo Bear Challenge:** Awarded upon completion of the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   **Planters:**
    *   Plastic Planters have a rare chance to drop 1 Super Smoothie in any field (36-day cooldown).
    *   The Planter Of Plenty drops 10 Super Smoothies when harvested from the Bamboo Field (36-day cooldown).
*   **Quests:** Awarded by various quests, including:
    *   Dapper Bear's "More Than Myth" (#15)
    *   Science Bear's "Corrupting The Glitched Drive"
*   **Other Sources:**
    *   As a reward from certain Gift Boxes (3 per box).
    *   Completing Bee Bear's 10th quest rewards 3 Super Smoothies.
    *   Sun Bear’s “⌛Waiting With Sun Bear (6/6): Still Stranded” grants 1 Super Smoothie.
    *   Bee Bear’s “Cheering Up Gloomy Cub (5/5): Anything???” grants 5 Super Smoothies.

### Marketplace and Codes
*   **Bee Bear's Catalog:** Can be purchased in bundles from Bee Bear's Catalog Shop for Gingerbread Bears.
*   **Robux Offers (Outdated):** Historically available through various Robux packs during seasonal events.
*   **Codes (Outdated):** Activated by redeeming certain codes, including 3YearParty, RebootFriday, FourYearFiesta, boo swarm, 1MLikes, and WalmartToys.
*   **Memory Match:** Can be obtained as a rare reward from Winter Memory Match.

## Trivia

*   The Super Smoothie is one of four consumable items that increase capacity, alongside Marshmallow Bees, Purple Potions, and Snowflakes.
*   It is the consumable item in the game that provides the highest number of different buffs.
*   **Raw Ingredient Costs:** The base raw ingredients cost to create a Super Smoothie are: 12 Neonberries, 60 Coconuts, 600 Sunflower Seeds, 5,175 Strawberries, 6,450 Pineapples, 5,175 Blueberries, 1,035 Royal Jellies (or 1.04B Honey), and 9 Magic Beans (or 180 Tickets).
*   **Downgrading Cost:** The raw ingredients cost when downgrading a Super Smoothie (excluding Gumdrops) is: 12 Neonberries, 60 Coconuts, 600 Sunflower Seeds, 450 Strawberries, 600 Pineapples, 450 Blueberries, 225 Moon Charms, 810 Royal Jellies (or 810,000,000 Honey), 9 Magic Beans (or 180 Tickets), and 450 Gumdrops (or 150 Tickets).
*   The Super Smoothie is one of the few items that cannot be donated to the Wind Shrine; it is also the only craftable item with this restriction.
*   Bee Bear referred to it as the "ultimate boost granting beverage" in his 2021 Beesmas quest, "Quality Cousin Time."
*   Historically, Super Smoothies used to offer +7% Critical Chance and no Super-Crit Chance.
