# Honey Bee Gate

The **Honey Bee Gate**, also known as the **15 Bee Gate**, is a gate that requires players to have 15 Bees to pass through. It is the third major gate accessible, following the Basic Bee Gate and Brave Bee Gate. The gate is named after the large Honey Bee located on top of it.

## Contents
### Mobs
The area behind this gate features several hostile mobs:
*   Werewolf
*   Cave Monster
*   Mantis
*   Scorpion

### Quest Givers
Players can interact with the following quest givers in this zone:
*   Polar Bear
*   Gifted Riley Bee
*   Robo Bear
*   Honey Bee (NPC)

### Shops
The Honey Bee Gate area contains several commercial locations:
*   Royal Jelly Shop
*   Badge Bearer's Guild
*   Red HQ

### Fields
There are four distinct fields accessible through the gate:
*   Cactus Field
*   Pumpkin Patch
*   Pine Tree Forest
*   Rose Field

### Extras and Features
This zone also contains numerous unique assets and features, including:
*   Red Teleporter
*   Lion Bee Gate
*   Red Field Booster
*   Strawberry Dispenser
*   Top Riley Bee Helpers
*   Daily Top Red Collectors
*   All-Time Top Red Collectors
*   Blue Cannon
*   Special Sprout Summoner
*   Werewolf's Cave
*   Cloud Obby (Obstacle Course)
*   Diamond Room (Obstacle Course)
*   Sticker Stack

## Trivia
*   This gate has the highest number of fields behind it in the game, totaling four.
*   While there were previous ways to bypass the 15-bee requirement, as of the 2019-09-28 update, attempting to bypass it will always teleport the player back to spawn.
*   It holds the second-most quest givers (4), surpassed only by the Starter Zone (5).
*   The ramp leading to the Lion Bee Gate (also known as the 25 Bee Gate) originates from this gate.
*   This area features the most assets among all gates, with a total of 19 distinct items and locations.
