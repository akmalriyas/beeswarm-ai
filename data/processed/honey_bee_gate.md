# Honey Bee Gate

![HoneyBeeGate](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fb/HoneyBeeGate.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20180421061939) The Honey Bee Gate.

The **Honey Bee Gate**, also known as the **15 Bee Gate**, is a gate that requires 15 bees to pass through. It is the third major gate players should access, following the Basic Bee Gate and preceding the Brave Bee Gate. It is named after the large Honey Bee located on top of it.

### Mobs
The Honey Bee Gate area contains the following mobs:
* Werewolf
* Cave Monster
* Mantis
* Scorpion

### Quest Givers
Players can interact with these quest givers near the gate:
* Polar Bear
* Gifted Riley Bee
* Robo Bear
* Honey Bee (NPC)

### Shops
The area features several shops, including:
* Royal Jelly Shop
* Badge Bearer's Guild
* Red HQ

### Fields
There are four distinct fields accessible through this gate:
* Cactus Field
* Pumpkin Patch
* Pine Tree Forest
* Rose Field

### Extras
This location also contains numerous other assets and features:
* Red Teleporter
* Lion Bee Gate (The ramp leading to it is located here)
* Red Field Booster
* Strawberry Dispenser
* Top Riley Bee Helpers
* Daily Top Red Collectors
* All-Time Top Red Collectors
* Blue Cannon
* Special Sprout Summoner
* Werewolf's Cave
* Cloud Obby (Obstacle Course)
* Diamond Room (Obstacle Course)
* Sticker Stack

### Trivia
* This gate has the highest number of fields behind it, with four.
* While there were previous ways to bypass the gate without 15 bees, as of the September 28, 2019 update, attempting to bypass it will always teleport the player back to spawn.
* It features the second-most quest givers (4), trailing only the Starter Zone with 5.
* This gate holds the ramp that leads to the Lion Bee Gate, also known as the 25 Bee Gate.
* The Honey Bee Gate has one of the most assets in its vicinity, totaling 19 items/features.
