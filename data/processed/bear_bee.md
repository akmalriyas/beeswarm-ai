# Bear Bee

**Bear Bee** is a Colorless Event bee known for its unique ability to periodically transform the player into a bear.

[![Bear Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a9/Bear_Bee.png/revision/latest?cb=20190402203940)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a9/Bear_Bee.png/revision/latest?cb=20190402203940) [![BearBee Gifted](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cb/BearBee_Gifted.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cb/BearBee_Gifted.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051)

> "A friendly bee who periodically transforms you into a bear!"

**Rarity:** Event
**Color:** Colorless

## Acquisition and Gift Status

The Bear Bee can be obtained in several ways:
*   **Vouchers/Stickers:** By redeeming a [Bear Bee Voucher](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/05/Bear_Bee_Voucher.png/revision/latest?cb=20240614201601).
*   **Robux Shop:** Purchasing the bee in the [Robux Shop](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/65/Bear_Bee_Promo_Full.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/185?cb=20260126183736) for 800 Robux.
*   **Drops:** As an incredibly rare drop from [Tunnel Bear](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/9/Tunnel_Bear.png/revision/latest?cb=20190402203940) and very rarely from the [Retro Swarm Challenge](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cb/Retro_Swarm_Challenge.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051).

Like all Event bees, it has no favorite treat. However, the Bear Bee can become **Gifted** if acquired during its release by feeding it a [Star Treat](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/9/Star_Treat.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051), [Gingerbread Bear](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cb/Gingerbread_Bear.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051) or [Aged Gingerbread Bear](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cb/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051).

**Preferences:**
*   **Likes:** [Pumpkin Patch](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/9/Pumpkin_Patch.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051) and [Pine Tree Forest](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cb/Pine_Tree_Forest.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051).
*   **Dislikes:** [Blue Flower Field](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cb/Blue_Flower_Field.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051).

## Base Stats
| Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Energy** | 35 |
| **Speed** | 14 |
| **Attack** | 5 |

### Gathering and Production Rates
*   **Pollen Collection:** Collects 15 Pollen in 2 seconds (7.5 per second). This can reach 30 Pollen with a Critical Hit, or 45 Pollen with Melody active.
    *   With the x2 Bee Pollen gamepass, collection is 30 Pollen in 2 seconds.
*   **Honey Production:** Makes 200 Honey in 2 seconds (100 per second). This can reach 200 Honey in 1 second with the x2 Honeymaking Speed gamepass.

### Stat Bonuses
The Bear Bee provides the following bonuses:
*   +75% Energy
*   +50% Gather and Convert Speed
*   +5 Gathering Amount
*   +120 Convert Amount
*   +4 Attack

**Gifted Hive Bonus:** +10% Pollen.

## Abilities

### Bear Morph
The Bear Bee grants the **Bear Morph** ability, which transforms the player into a random bear. This transformation provides:
*   ×2 Pollen collection boost.
*   Boosts to Speed and Jump Power.

If the Bear Bee is Gifted, there is a 20% chance that the player will transform into a rare bear with special boosts:
1.  **Mother Bear:** Grants x2.5 Pollen and an additional x1.5 Pollen from Bee Gathering.
2.  **Science Bear:** Adds +1 Conversion Link.

## Trivia and Lore

*   Bear Morph's increased jump power and speed can grant early access to late-game items like [Royal Jelly](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/9/Royal_Jelly.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051) and [Tickets](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cb/Ticket.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051).
*   The Bear Bee was the first Event bee added to the game and is the only one from the original release.
*   It holds a unique place as the only Event bee that can become gifted without requiring a Star Treat or Gingerbread Bear, as all First Edition Bear Bees are automatically gifted upon joining if not already so.
*   This bee is one of three ways to transform into a bear; the others are using the [Gummy Mask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/9/Gummy_Mask.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051) or redeeming specific codes.
*   A rare drop from a Gifted Bear Bee is the **Bear Bee Offer Sticker**, which can be obtained while gathering in the [Pineapple Patch](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/9/Pineapple_Patch.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115014051) or with a 1/3 chance when feeding it a Star Treat.
*   The Bear Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus has changed over time: initially +20% White Pollen, then to +40% White Pollen, and currently settled at +10% Pollen.
