# Samovar

[![The Samovar when completed.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/77/Completedsamovar.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/238?cb=20230128015918)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/77/Completedsamovar.png/revision/latest?cb=20230128015918) [](/wiki/File:Completedsamovar.png)

The **Samovar** is a machine introduced in the Beesmas 2021 event, and it has since returned in Beesmas 2022, 2024, and 2025. It can be unlocked by completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas quest. When activated, the Samovar rewards a random Nectar type and items near the cup.

**Mechanics:**
*   **Cooldown:** The Samovar has a fixed cooldown of 6 hours after use.
*   **Prerequisite Check:** If a player attempts to activate the Samovar without completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas quest, the message "This strange thing is looking a little rusty..." will appear.
*   **Usage Messages:** When used, the following messages are displayed (where `<Nth>` refers to the number of times it was previously used):
    *   "This is your <Nth> time using the Samovar."
    *   "Each time you use it, the brew gets stronger..."

## Drops and Progression

The Samovar's rewards are tied to its usage count. It is important to note that the displayed message indicates how many times the machine was used *previously*, not the current use number. For example, if the message states it is your 24th time using the Samovar, you are actually on the 25th use.

**Nectar and Honeysuckles:**
*   Each successful use increases the amount of Nectar received by 5 minutes, capped at a maximum of 4 hours (this increase is also affected by nectar multipliers).
*   The number of **Honeysuckles** increases by 1 with each use, stopping once it reaches a maximum of 30.

**Reward Cycle:**
The Samovar follows a fixed cycle of rewards:
Oil $\rightarrow$ Enzymes $\rightarrow$ Gumdrops $\rightarrow$ Glitter $\rightarrow$ Ticket Planter.

On the 25th use, **Turpentine** is rewarded instead of following the standard cycle.

## Trivia
*   The Samovar and the Robo Party Cake are currently the only Beesmas decorations that were not included in the original Beesmas 2020 event.
*   It requires 36 uses to achieve the maximum amount of nectar, resulting in a total cooldown time of 216 hours (or 9 days).
*   The Nectar collected from the Samovar contributes toward quests that require specific types of nectar.
