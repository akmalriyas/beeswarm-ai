# Bubble Mask

![Bubble Mask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/42/Bubble_Mask.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403020355)

## Overview
The Bubble Mask is a hat accessory that can be purchased at the Blue HQ. It enhances blue pollen gathering by harnessing the power of the sea.

> "Harness the power of the sea to enhance your Blue pollen gathering."

### Cost
*   100,000,000 Honey
*   500 Blueberries
*   50 Blue Extracts
*   25 Oil
*   15 Glitter

## Stats and Abilities
The Bubble Mask provides a variety of bonuses to the wearer's stats:

*   **Blue Pollen:** x1.5
*   **Capacity:** x1.5
*   **Blue Field Capacity:** x1.25
*   **Bubble Pollen:** x1.25
*   **Defense:** +30%
*   **Bee Ability Rate:** +15%
*   **Passive: Bubble Bombs**

### Passive Ability: Bubble Bombs
Every 25th bomb ability token spawns 25 Bubbles that last for 8 seconds. If a player touches these bubbles, they pop and collect pollen (4R/6W/8B). This collected amount is increased by 15% per Gifted blue Bee type in the player's Hive, originating from 29 nearby Flowers, causing them to grow. This passive has a 2-minute cooldown.

## Trivia
*   The Bubble Mask represents the Bubble Bee and possesses an ability similar to it.
*   It belongs to the category of epic bees, alongside the Fire Mask, Honey Mask, Riley Guard, and Bucko Guard.
*   **Historical Stats (Pre-2019-04-05 Update):** Before this update, its stats included +25% Bee Gather Pollen, +25% Instant Conversion, +30% Damage Reduction, +11% Bee Ability Rate, +115% Luck (Loot Luck), +25% Blue Bomb Pollen, and x1.15 Blue Pollen.
*   **Historical Stats (Pre-2019-09-28 Update):** Before this update, the Bubble Mask did not possess any passive ability. If a player owned it during that time, its passive was inherited by the Diamond Mask.
*   The Bubbles generated by the Bubble Bombs passive feature a unique sound effect when popped.
