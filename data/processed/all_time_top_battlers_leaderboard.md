## Overview

The Global Top Battlers leaderboard (also known as the Battle Points Leaderboard) is one of the many leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator. It tracks the total battle points players have collected over time, displaying the top 10 positions at any given moment. Players can scroll down to view rankings all the way to the 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on these leaderboards for extended periods; hackers and exploiters will be removed. Battle points are earned by defeating specific mobs in the game.

If a player steps on the designated white pad near the leaderboard, a message displaying their current battle point total ("You have x Battle Points") will appear.

## Location

The leaderboard is situated on the yellow platform, located next to the Panda Bear, in front of the Bamboo Field, and behind the Blue Flower Field.

## Trivia

*   This is one of the two leaderboards present since the game's release; the other being the Global Top Honeymakers Leaderboard.
*   Sugarsmacks12 held the number 1 spot in the global top battlers for three years before being dethroned, making it the longest-sustaining number one position across all game leaderboards.
*   The Bubble Bee Man used to be located behind this leaderboard but was later moved to the Bear Gate area during the Beesmas 2019 update.
