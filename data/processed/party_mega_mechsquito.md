# Party Mega Mechsquito

The **Party Mega Mechsquito** is a mob exclusive to the Robo Party event in Bee Swarm Simulator. It functions similarly to a regular Mega Mechsquito, but unlike its normal counterpart, it roams around fields rather than spawning near the player.

It first appears starting at Rank 15 and continues to spawn in subsequent ranks of the Robo Party. Like other Mega Mechsquitos, they will fly away if a player gets too close.

## Combat Strategies

Since the Party Mega Mechsquito shares mechanics with its normal counterpart, Mega Mechsquitos, standard strategies apply:

*   **Movement:** The mob targets where the player *is*, not where they are going. Moving in circles can often cause the mech to miss its attack.
*   **Engagement Range:** Standing close to the Party Mega Mechsquito allows your bees to lock on and deal damage more quickly.
*   **Crowd Control (CC):** [Vicious Bee] and [Windy Bee] are excellent for crowd control, especially since these mobs often appear in pairs or packs. [Digital Bee] can stun multiple targets simultaneously, allowing them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack].
*   **Field Advantage:** Unlike normal variants, Party Mega Mechsquitos remain within the field boundaries. Players can utilize this by positioning their bees just outside the field perimeter; your bees will attack the mob while the mech does not aim for you. Be cautious, as they may hover near other parts of the field and might not be easily targeted from a distance.

## Trivia

*   This is the final mob to appear during a Robo Party if the player begins at the lowest ranks.
*   The Party Mega Mechsquito, along with [Party Mechsquitos] and [Party Cogturret], is one of the few Robo Party variants that maintains the same health progression per level as its standard counterpart.
