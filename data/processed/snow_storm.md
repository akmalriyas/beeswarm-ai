# Snow Storm

[![An active snow storm.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cf/Snowstorm.png/revision/latest?cb=20201228210218 "An active Snow Storm.")](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cf/Snowstorm.png/revision/latest?cb=20201228210218 "An active Snow Storm.")

A **Snow Storm** is a server-wide event that can be summoned either by using the [Snow Machine](/wiki/Snow_Machine) or randomly within the game world. It is similar to a [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm), but instead of honey, it spawns ![Snowflake](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cf/Snowstorm.png/revision/latest?cb=20201228210218 "An active Snow Storm.")[Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake). A Snow Storm lasts for 20 seconds.

**Summoning Mechanics:**
*   **Via Snow Machine:** When a player uses the Snow Machine to summon a Snow Storm, a server-wide announcement states: `❄️ {Username} has activated the Snow Machine! ❄️`
*   **Randomly:** When a Snow Storm is summoned randomly, the message states: `❄️ A Snow Storm has begun... ❄️`

During an active Snow Storm, [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake) begin to fall rapidly from the sky across every field. Unlike a Honeystorm or a [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower), there is no initial audio cue when a Snow Storm begins.

**Specific Summoners and Events:**
Occasionally, specific entities or events can summon a Snow Storm:
*   [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\)) would spawn a Snow Storm, usually under an alias (similar to how he globally summons Mythic Meteor Showers, [nighttime](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle#Night), special [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout), and [mobs](/wiki/Mobs)).
*   Snowcub summoning a Snow Storm.
*   Texas Bear summoning a Snow Storm.
*   Festive Bee summoning a Snow Storm in an attempt to re-freeze the Bean Bug Brigade.
*   Gifted Gingerbread Bear summoning a Snow Storm during Beesmas 2020.
*   A Gifted Festive Nymph (Lvl 9) (Aspect of Snow Mutation) (Winter Cap Nymph-quip with 5 Caustic Waxes) (HP Saves) summoning a Snow Storm during Beesmas 2021.
*   Gloomy Cub's Cold Heart summoning a Snow Storm.
*   Gloomy Cub's Immobilizing Pessism summoning a Snow Storm.
*   The realization that Beesmas is actually over summoning a Snow Storm.
*   Gifted Gatito Bee summoning a Snow Storm.
*   Festive Nymph summoning a Snow Storm.

## Trivia

*   **Werewolf's Cave Visibility:** Like Honeystorms and Mythic Meteor Showers, Snow Storms can illuminate the [Werewolf's Cave](/wiki/Werewolf%27s_Cave) sufficiently to allow players to see the [Cave Monsters](/wiki/Cave_Monster) and the ![Gold Egg](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cf/Snowstorm.png/revision/latest?cb=20201228210218 "An active Snow Storm.")[Gold Egg](/wiki/Egg#Gold_Egg) at the cave's end. However, unlike Honeystorms, Snow Storms do not spawn snowflake tokens in the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Challenge).
*   **Beesmas Events:** During Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025, giving a [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear) a ![Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cf/Snowstorm.png/revision/latest?cb=20201228210218 "An active Snow Storm.")[Present](/wiki/Present) would trigger a Snow Storm after the Spirit Bear's dialogue finished and the [ornament](/wiki/Ornaments) was given. Finishing Spirit Bear’s Beesmas quest also summons a Snow Storm.
*   **Falling Snowflakes:** The [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake) continue to fall for several seconds after a Snow Storm ends, as it takes time for them to reach the ground.
*   **Nighttime Persistence:** If a Snow Storm is summoned during [nighttime](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle#Night), the white fog will persist even after the Snow Storm has ended until nighttime concludes.
