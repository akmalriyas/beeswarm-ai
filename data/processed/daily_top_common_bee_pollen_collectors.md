# Daily Top Common Bee Pollen Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Common Bee Pollen Collectors** is a specific leaderboard within the game's Global Leaderboards menu. This ranking tracks how much pollen players have collected using only **Common Bees** on a given day.

## Mechanics and Reset
*   **Visibility:** Viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu in the System Page.
*   **Display:** Shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time, with users able to scroll down to view up to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The leaderboard resets every day at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes
Players are awarded prizes based on their daily ranking:

*   **Top 5 Finishers:** Receive the **Round Basic Bee Sticker**.
*   **Top 100 Finishers:** Receive 50 **Tickets**.
