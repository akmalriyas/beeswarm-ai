# Sprinklers

[![Sprinkler Builder](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/09/Sprinkler_Builder.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404051548)

> "Use while standing in flowers to place a Sprinkler."

---

**Cooldown:** 1 second

Sprinklers are items players can place in [Fields] to enhance [Flower] regeneration. They are purchased from the [Badge Bearer's Guild], which requires obtaining 15 badges of any kind.

There are five tiers of sprinklers: Basic Sprinkler, Silver Soakers, Golden Gushers, Diamond Drenchers, and The Supreme Saturator. All sprinklers appear as a simple structure rising from the ground beneath the flower where they are placed; their material reflects their tier color (with the exception of The Supreme Saturator). Higher-tier sprinklers allow players to place more units and offer greater effectiveness. Sprinklers cannot be placed in the [Hub Field].

### Placement Mechanics
Players cannot place their own sprinklers within 8 flowers of each other, though they can place them adjacent to another player's sprinkler. If a player attempts to violate this proximity rule with their own unit, they receive the message: "Cannot place sprinklers this close to eachother."

**Note on Glitches:** The [Sprinkler Glitch] allows players to bypass this restriction by placing a sprinkler mid-air, enabling closer placement than normal.

### Sprinkler Builder
It is important to note that what players purchase is actually a permanent **Sprinkler Builder**, an item accessible via the eggs/items tab. Players activate it by placing the item on the bottom middle of their screen and clicking (or using corresponding hotkeys). If a player exceeds their capacity, the oldest placed sprinkler will be removed.

A sprinkler remains in place until the player who placed it removes it, or until that player dies or leaves the game.
