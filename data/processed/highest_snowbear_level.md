## Overview and Mechanics

The Highest Snowbear Level is one of the game's leaderboards. It was introduced in the December 25, 2020 update and reappears during every Beesmas event thereafter.

To summon a Snowbear, players must first complete the Panda Bear's Beesmas quest to gain access to the Snowbear Summoner. Once summoned, defeating the Snowbear increases its level by one. This leaderboard displays the top 100 highest Snowbear levels achieved by players. Note that this specific leaderboard does not feature a white pad for standing.

## Trivia

*   The Highest Robo Party Cake Rank and the Highest Snowbear Level are two leaderboards that have been temporarily removed, but they reset and reappear during each Beesmas event.
