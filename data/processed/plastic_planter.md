# Plastic Planter

[![Plastic Planter](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/60/Plastic_Planter.png/revision/latest?cb=20230406150630)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/60/Plastic_Planter.png/revision/latest?cb=20230406150630)

> "Grows in about 2 hours. Harvest to collect pollen, items, and Nectar boosts!"

The **Plastic Planter** is a permanent planter added in the December 26, 2021 update. It can be purchased from Dapper Bear's Shop.

## Stats & Cost

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reusability** | Yes (Permanent) |
| **Growth Time** | ~2 hours |
| **Pollen Capacity** | 250,000 Pollen |
| **Pollen Multiplier** | None |
| **Nectar Multiplier** | None |

### Acquisition Cost
*   750,000 Honey
*   3 Magic Beans
*   1 Soft Wax

## Harvesting Mechanics

Harvesting a fully grown Plastic Planter yields up to 12 tokens worth of items.

**Puffshroom Chance:**
The planter has a $\frac{1}{7}$ chance to spawn a Puffshroom upon harvest. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 3 and 4, with the following distribution:
*   ~80% chance of being a Common Puffshroom
*   ~20% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom

**Item Drops:**
The tokens are picked randomly from a non-exhaustive list that includes general drops and special drops. Notable items that can drop include:
*   Treats, Tickets, Sunflower Seeds, Pineapples, Blueberries, Strawberries, Bitterberries, Neonberries, Enzymes, Gumdrops, Field Dice, Whirligigs, Soft Waxes, Honeysuckles, Hard Waxes, Blue Extracts, Red Extracts, Stingers, Micro-Converters, Royal Jellies, Star Jellies, Oils, Glitter, Coconuts (only in 35 Bee Zone Fields), Moon Charms (exclusive to Pineapple Patch or during night), Purple Potions (Rare), Swirled Wax (Very Rare), Caustic Wax (Very Rare), Bottle Caps (Very Rare), Turpentine (Exceptionally Rare), Gold Eggs (Exceptionally Rare), and Whistles (Exceptionally Rare).

**Special Drops:**
*   1 Soft Wax: When harvested from a Coconut Field, this item has a 1-month cooldown.
*   1 Caustic Wax: When harvested from a Rose Field, this item has a 1-month cooldown.
*   1 Super Smoothie: Uncommon drop with a 1-month cooldown.

## Trivia

*   The Plastic Planter and the Ticket Planter tie for the second fastest growing planters in the game. The Paper Planter is the fastest, taking only 1 hour of playtime.
*   It is the cheapest planter available in Dapper Bear's Shop, making it the second cheapest purchasable planter overall (behind the Paper Planter).
