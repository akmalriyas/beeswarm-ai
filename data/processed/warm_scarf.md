# Warm Scarf

The **Warm Scarf** is a Level 5 Beesmas Beequip. It has an equip limit of three and no color requirement. This winter scarf keeps bees warm in the field.

It can be equipped by Basic, Rad, Exhausted, Frosty, and Shy Bees.
