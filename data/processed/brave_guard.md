# Brave Guard

![Brave Guard](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a1/Brave_Guard.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403022436 "Brave Guard")

## Overview
The Brave Guard is a right shoulder pad crafted by a Brave Bee, purchasable in the Pro Shop. It can be equipped on the right shoulder slot.

> *"A right shoulder pad crafted by a Brave Bee."*

### Stats
| Stat | Bonus |
| :--- | :--- |
| Capacity | +15,000 |
| White Pollen | +5% |
| Colorless Bee Attack | +1 |

### Crafting Ingredients
To craft the Brave Guard, you will need:
*   300,000 Honey
*   3 Stingers

## Trivia
*   The Brave Guard is one of four guards available in the Pro Shop. The other options are the Looker Guard, Hasty Guard, and Bomber Guard.
*   It is the only guard that provides a boost to Colorless Bee Attack.
