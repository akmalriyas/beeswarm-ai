Robo Bear is a Non-Player Character (NPC) introduced in the Beesmas 2022 update. He is located in the 15 Bee Zone, near the Red HQ and at the far end of the Rose Field. His primary function is initiating the Robo Bear Challenge.

Before accessing Robo Bear, players must complete a quest to assemble his missing parts: the Red Drive, White Drive, Blue Drive, and Glitched Drive. These drives are acquired through various quests:
*   **Science Bear:** Repairing the White Drive.
*   **Dapper Bear:** Bartering for the Blue Drive.
*   **Panda Bear:** Ready For the Red Drive?
*   **Science Bear:** Corrupting the Glitched Drive.

Once all requirements are met, players can talk to Robo Bear and start the Challenge by spending 1 Robo Pass (obtainable from dispensers in the 30 Bee Zone).

***Note on Beesmas 2022 Completion:*** *During this event, drives could also be acquired via specific quests or purchases:*
*   *White Drive: Purchased in Bee Bear's Catalog.*
*   *Red Drive: Completed Science Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest.*
*   *Blue Drive: Completed Dapper Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest.*
*   *Glitched Drive: Completed Bee Bear's "Decorating For Drives" quest.*

**Robo Bear's Challenge Rules:**
The challenge is a minigame consisting of multiple rounds, each with a 5-minute time limit to complete a random quest. Players are rewarded Cogs after each round; bonus Cogs are earned for faster completion. Players can use Cogs to purchase upgrades that last for the duration of the challenge.

**Challenge Mechanics:**
*   At the start, all but 3 of the player's bees are deactivated. Deactivated bees do not assist the player, and their Gifted Hive and Beequip hive bonuses are removed. Tool Pollen and Capacity are greatly reduced initially but increase each round.
*   Each round, the player may choose 2 bees to re-activate from a random selection.
*   The player may use Cogs to re-roll bee and upgrade options.
*   Robotic enemies will attack as rounds progress.
*   If the player fails any round or leaves the game mid-round, the challenge ends.
*   Certain boosts (promocodes, Honeyday Events) are disabled during rounds.
*   Rewards are granted based on completed rounds.

**Cog Amulet Rewards:**
*   Bronze Cog Amulet: 5 rounds
*   Silver Cog Amulet: 10 rounds
*   Gold Cog Amulet: 15 rounds
*   Diamond Cog Amulet: 20 rounds
*   Supreme Cog Amulet: 25 rounds

After round 25, the player is VICTORIOUS.

## Trivia
*   Robo Bear is the second NPC to initiate a challenge, following Stick Bug.
*   He utilizes the Robot Animation Pack from Roblox.
*   Robo Bear is one of the few quest givers who own a shop; others include Mother Bear, Dapper Bear, Gummy Bear, and Bee Bear.
*   Robo Bear's original face decal was created on 2019-02-24, eventually releasing in Beesmas 2022.
*   He is one of the few bears whose design has been publicly changed since its initial release.
*   The "Repairing Robo Bear" quest can be instantly completed once redeemed if the player already possesses all necessary Drives.
