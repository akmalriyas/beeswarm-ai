# Red Field Booster

[![The Red Field Booster inside of the Red HQ.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/57/RedBooster.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20180502214407)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/57/RedBooster.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20180502214407)

The Red Field Booster is a machine located in the Red HQ that provides a Field Boost to a random red field for 15 minutes.

**Usage Requirements:**
*   Player must have discovered 5 different red bee types.
*   Cooldown: 45 minutes.

***

## Boost Effects

When activated, the booster applies one of the following boosts to a randomly selected red field:

*   **Mushroom Field:** +300% Pollen
*   **Strawberry Field:** +100% Pollen
*   **Rose Field:** +100% Pollen
*   **Pepper Patch:** +100% Pollen (Requires the player to have 35+ Bees)

***

## Trivia and History

*   The Red Field Booster is one of three field boosters in the game, alongside the standard Field Booster and the Blue Field Booster.
    *   It shares a similar design with the other two boosters; the primary difference is color.
*   Unlike the Blue Field Booster (which is located on the second floor of the Blue HQ), the Red Field Booster is situated near the entrance of the Red HQ.
*   **Nerf History:** In an update, the Strawberry Field boost was reduced from 200% to 100%.
*   Prior to the May 23, 2024 update, this booster could not apply a boost to the Pepper Patch.
