# Mythic Meteor Shower

[![The Mythic Meteor Shower summoner.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3f/MythicMeteorSummoner.png/revision/latest?cb=20200620022558)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/3f/MythicMeteorSummoner.png/revision/latest?cb=20200620022558)

A **Mythic Meteor Shower** is a server-wide event introduced in the 2020-06-06 update. It was originally located on top of the Pro Shop but was moved to the Badge Bearer's Guild in the 2024-01-12 update.

## Summoning Mechanics
To summon a Mythic Meteor Shower, a player must have discovered at least three different types of [Mythic Bee] types. The event has a cooldown period of 22 hours.

**Player Summon:**
When summoned by a player, the distorted voice of IcedTeaLatte announces "Mythic Meteors," and a server-wide announcement displays: `[Username] has summoned a Mythic Meteor Shower! ☄️`

**Developer/Global Summon (Onett):**
Rarely, Onett will trigger a global meteor shower. Most often, he uses a custom alias, resulting in the announcement: `☄️ A Mythic Meteor Shower has been summoned! ☄️`. Sometimes, this occurs before [Beesmas] ends.

## Event Details
When active, a light purple fog appears, and meteors begin raining down on three randomly selected [fields]. These fields display white boxes falling from the sky (similar to the pollen haze box), indicating where meteors are landing. The visual appearance of these boxes is vibrant from a distance but dims upon player entry.

**Duration and Movement:**
The event lasts for 3 minutes. During this time, the three chosen fields will swap locations a total of three times, with swaps occurring approximately once every minute.

**Rewards and Collection (Player Summon):**
If activated by a player, the meteors collect and instantly convert pollen for the summoner. The amount collected is determined by the number of Mythic and [Gifted] Mythic bees in their hive. If a player catches a meteor from a player-summoned shower, they collect twice as much pollen from the field, which is then given to the original summoner.

**Rewards and Collection (Developer Summon):**
The prizes scale with the size of the meteor drop zone: smaller meteors offer a higher chance for more valuable rewards. Tokens produced by the meteors can be collected using [token links] or other collection methods. The drops are not affected by a player's loot luck.

**Interaction:**
*   If a player is on cooldown, the pad will prompt: "Summon a Mythic Meteor Shower ([time remaining])."
*   If an active meteor shower is running and a player steps on the pad (regardless of their cooldown status), it prompts: "A Meteor Shower is in progress."

## Drops
When a player stands on the drop zone of a meteor (indicated by a purple circle), reward tokens are distributed in a line across the field, often leading to another drop zone. The quantity of tokens per meteor is determined by the number of Mythic bees in the summoner's hive, while the rarity of the token changes based on the size of the meteor drop zone (rarer items tend to be more common with smaller meteors).

The following audios play when a meteor shower is summoned: `Meteorshowervoice.ogg`, `Meteorshowerbackground.ogg`, and `MeteorFall.ogg`.

## Trivia
*   Similar to [Honeystorm] and [Snowstorm], the purple fog will disappear if the time cycle changes from day to night or vice versa. If a Honeystorm is summoned during a meteor shower, its yellow fog replaces the purple fog until the Honeystorm ends.
*   Player-summoned meteors are boosted by consumables that affect fields (such as [Glitter] and [Field Dice]) and field boosters, allowing the summoner to collect significantly more [Honey].
*   The quality of rewards is affected by the law of diminishing marginal utility, similar to [Wild Windy Bee]; repeated participation in a single day leads to gradually worse rewards.
*   This event is one of three summoners that require bees (along with the [Sprout Summoner] and [Honeystorm]). It is the only machine that requires players to discover Mythic bee types to be used.
*   Both this event and Honeystorm, when summoned by a player, play IcedTeaLatte's distorted voice announcing the event's name.
