# Strawberry

A **Strawberry** is a type of Treat that can be fed to a Bee to raise its bond by 25 points.

## Mechanics and Bonding Effects

*   **Bond Multiplier:** When fed to specific bee types—Rascal, Rad, Rage, Riley, Baby, Stubborn, Shy, or Spicy Bees—the amount of bond gained is doubled.
*   **Gifting Chance:** Strawberries have a small chance of causing a bee to become gifted upon feeding:
    *   Rare Bees: 0.0125% (1 in 8,000)
    *   Epic Bees: 0.01% (1 in 10,000)
    *   Common/Legendary Bees: 0.0083% (1 in 12,000)
    *   Mythic Bees: 0.00417% (1 in 24,000)
*   **Mutation Risk:** Feeding a Radioactive Bee a strawberry carries a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) chance to mutate the bee.

## Acquisition Methods

Strawberries can be obtained through various methods:

*   **Hidden Token:** Found behind the largest strawberry in the corner of Strawberry Field.
*   **Strawberry Dispenser:** Obtained from using the dispenser near Red HQ (yields one strawberry per red bee in your hive).
*   **Challenges & Rewards:** As a reward from the Ant Challenge, or as a reward from Memory Match.
*   **Codes:** Redeeming certain valid codes (e.g., 15MMembers grants 15 strawberries plus other items).
*   **Sticker Stack:** Awarded when adding specific stickers to the Sticker Stack:
    *   Pink Cupcake Sticker rewards 50 strawberries.
    *   Pale Heart Sticker rewards 50 strawberries.
*   **Beesmas Feast:** Digging in Beesmas Feast can yield strawberry tokens.

## Drops and Farming

Strawberries drop from several sources, including farming fields, mobs, and planters:

*   **Farming Fields:** They are harvested or dropped in the Mushroom Field, Strawberry Field, Rose Field, Cactus Field, and Pepper Patch. Harvesting Leaves in these fields may also yield strawberries.
    *   **Sprout Growth Multipliers:** When growing a sprout (excluding Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts):
        *   Mushroom Field, Rose Field, or Pepper Patch yields 4x more strawberries than other fields.
        *   Strawberry Field yields 8x more strawberries than other fields.
*   **Mobs:** Dropped by Ladybugs, Scorpions, and King Beetles.
*   **Festive Items:** Drops from a Festive Gift or Festive Mark.
*   **Cub Buddy Gifts:** Can be received as a gift from a Cub Buddy.
*   **Harvesting Sparkles:** Higher chances of dropping strawberries when harvesting Sparkles in red fields.
*   **Planters:** Dropped by various planters, most likely in red fields:
    *   The Red Clay Planter always drops strawberries upon harvest at full growth.

## Historical and Outdated Information

*   **Chicks Drops (Outdated):** Previously dropped from Hostage Chicks and Spotted Chicks.
*   **Expired Code Rewards:** Certain expired codes provided strawberry rewards, including 10mMembers (10 strawberries), 5mMembers (5 strawberries), FourYearFiesta (4 strawberries), and 1MLikes (1 strawberry).
