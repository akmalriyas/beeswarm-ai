# Mondo Bubble Bee Man Mask

![7-Pronged Cog](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2c/7-Pronged_Cog.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20230422153327) | **This content is currently unobtainable or inaccessible.**

## Overview
The Mondo B.B.M. Mask is a larger, enhanced version of the standard B.B.M. Mask. It was originally obtained by completing "B.B.M. Mission," a quest given out by Bubble Bee Man during the Beesmas 2018 Event in the 30 Bee Zone (Bear Gate).

Upon completion of this quest—which required players to finish Bee Bear’s entire now-unavailable Beesmas 2018 questline—the mask would automatically replace any hat the player was currently wearing. Players can reequip this mask by interacting with BBM in the 30 Bee Zone today.

## Stats
*   **Capacity:** +500,000
*   **Pollen:** +10%
*   **Bee Gather Pollen:** +10%
*   **Conversion Rate:** +50%
*   **Honey From Tokens:** +50%
*   **Bee Attack:** +1

## Trivia
*   This mask, along with the standard B.B.M. Mask, is one of the few masks that cannot be purchased and lacks a descriptive text entry in the game.
*   It belongs to a small group of masks (alongside the Fire Mask and Demon Mask) that provide Bee Attack buffs.
*   The Mondo B.B.M. Mask is part of a set, alongside the Mondo Belt Bag, consisting of "mondo" versions of standard accessories.
