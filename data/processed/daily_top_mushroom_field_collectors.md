# Daily Top Mushroom Field Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Mushroom Field Collectors** is one of the game's leaderboards, viewable within the Global Leaderboards menu. This leaderboard tracks how much pollen players have collected specifically in the **Mushroom Field** on a given day.

## Mechanics and Reset
*   **Ranking:** The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll to view rankings down to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM MST.

## Prizes
Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25 Finishers:** Receive a **Mushroom Field Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100 Finishers:** Receive 25 **Tickets**.
