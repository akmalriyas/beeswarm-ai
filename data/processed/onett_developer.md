# Onett (Developer)

[![Onett’s Roblox avatar.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8c/Onett2023.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410231928)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8c/Onett2023.png/revision/latest?cb=20230410231928)

This page details the developer of Bee Swarm Simulator. For information regarding the quest-giving NPC, please see [Onett](/wiki/Onett).

**Piercen Harbut**, better known as **[Onett](https://www.roblox.com/users/1912490/profile)** on Roblox, is the owner and programmer of [Bee Swarm Simulator](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator "Bee Swarm Simulator"). Bee Swarm Simulator is his first and primary game on Roblox. He also owns the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Club "Bee Swarm Simulator Club") group and [Onett's Testing Group](/wiki/Onett%27s_Testing_Group "Onett's Testing Group").

### Developer Accounts
Onett utilizes several alternate accounts to manage community interactions, bug reports, and testing:
*   **[BeeSwarmBugReport](https://www.roblox.com/users/556387844/profile)** handles player bug reports.
*   **[BeeSwarmSupport](https://www.roblox.com/users/556383852/profile)** manages purchase and save file reports.
*   He maintains a secret alternate account, **[ltpb2020](https://www.roblox.com/users/1953773238/profile/)**, primarily used for uploading decals during the [Ready Player Two](/wiki/Ready_Player_Two "Ready Player Two") event.
*   Another account, **[HiddenBeePotential](https://www.roblox.com/users/5098329817/profile)**, appears to be used solely for testing purposes.

## Trivia

*   **Inspiration:** Onett's real-life cat, Sam, was the inspiration for the [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"). Sam passed away in late 2018.
*   **Favorites:** His favorite bees are [Basic Bee](/wiki/Basic_Bee "Basic Bee"), [Rascal Bee](/wiki/Rascal_Bee "Rascal Bee"), [Tabby Bee](/wiki/Tabby_Bee "Tabby Bee"), and [Brave Bee](/wiki/Brave_Bee "Brave Bee").
*   **Mechanics:** His favorite ability is Triangulate, which he finds satisfying.
*   **Username Origin:** Onett's username references a city from the Nintendo game *EarthBound*.
*   **Items & Mobs:** He owns a special [tool](/wiki/Tools "Tools") called the Honey Hammer and his favorite mob is the [Rhino Beetle](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle").
*   **Music:** His preferred in-game music track is bpatrol.
*   **Events:** Onett occasionally summons mini events, using various aliases that replace his username (e.g., "[Alias] has summoned (a) [mini event]").
*   **Personal Details:** Onett's birthday is December 24, 1989. He was interviewed by Thundermaker300 in November 2018 as part of the Roblox Wiki's Developer Connections project.
