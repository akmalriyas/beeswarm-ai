# Onett (Developer)

Piercen Harbut, better known as **Onett** on Roblox, is the owner and programmer of *Bee Swarm Simulator*. This is his first and main game on Roblox. He manages the official community groups, including the Bee Swarm Simulator Club and Onett's Testing Group.

### Developer Accounts & Contact
Onett utilizes several alternate accounts to manage communication regarding the game:
*   **BeeSwarmBugReport:** Handles bug reports.
*   **BeeSwarmSupport:** Manages purchase and save file reports.
*   **ltpb2020:** A secret account primarily used for uploading decals during the Ready Player Two event.
*   **HiddenBeePotential:** An alternate account currently used mainly for testing purposes.

Onett's timezone is CST, as confirmed on Discord.

### Trivia and Facts
*   **Inspiration:** Onett's real-life cat, Sam (who passed away in 2018), was the inspiration for the Tabby Bee.
*   **Favorite Bees:** His favorite bees include Basic Bee, Rascal Bee, Tabby Bee, and Brave Bee.
*   **Favorite Ability:** He finds Triangulate satisfying.
*   **Username Origin:** Onett's username is a reference to a city in the Nintendo game *EarthBound*.
*   **Tools & Mobs:** He owns a special tool called the Honey Hammer, and his favorite mob is the Rhino Beetle.
*   **Music:** His favorite in-game music track is bpatrol.
*   **Events:** Onett occasionally summons mini events using various aliases (e.g., "[Alias] has summoned (a) [mini event]").
*   **Personal Details:** Onett's birthday is December 24, 1989. He was interviewed by Thundermaker300 in November 2018 as part of the Roblox Wiki's Developer Connections project.
