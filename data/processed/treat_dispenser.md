# Treat Dispenser

![Treat Dispenser](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0f/Treat_Dispenser.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20260218173753) The Treat Dispenser

The **Treat Dispenser** is a dispenser located between the Pineapple Patch and the entrance of the Pro Shop. It allows Club members to collect rewards on a set cooldown.

## Information

### Usage
Using the Treat Dispenser grants:
*   Honey
*   Treats
*   Pineapples (1)
*   Haste Tokens (x5)

### Requirements & Mechanics
*   **Requirement:** Membership in the Bee Swarm Simulator Club group. Players who do not meet this requirement will receive a prompt instructing them to join the club.
*   **Cooldown:** 1 hour.

## Reward Amounts

The amount of Honey and Treats received is calculated based on the number of bees ($\text{cnt}$) the player has in their hive.

### Formulas
*   **Honey:** $\left\lfloor {cnt}^{1.5}+0.5\right\rfloor \times 50$. The minimum reward is 500 Honey.
*   **Treats:** $\left\lfloor {\frac {{cnt}^{2.5}}{100}}+0.5\right\rfloor$. The minimum reward is 10 Treats.

## Trivia

*   The Treat Dispenser is one of three dispensers that grant Treats in the game; the others are the Blueberry Dispenser and the Strawberry Dispenser.
*   It is unique among these dispensers for providing two different collectible items: Treats and Pineapples.
*   This dispenser, along with the Honey Dispenser, Blueberry Dispenser, and Strawberry Dispenser, requires players to be part of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club.
