# White Tunnel

The **White Tunnel** is a hidden location accessible through the Brave Bee Gate area. It can be entered via the Instant Converter, located near the Pineapple Patch and Stump Field, and exited by standing on a platform in the middle of the tunnel. The tunnel is home to the hostile mob, [Tunnel Bear].

While it does not contain any collectible items, the end of the tunnel features a picture of a winking Basic Bee face. This image hints at the valid game code "Wink." As players proceed deeper into the tunnel, the passage narrows significantly. This narrowing mechanic makes exploiting Tunnel Bear more difficult, as players can only turn around at the beginning of the tunnel, thereby elongating combat encounters.

## Entrances and Exits

There are several methods to enter the White Tunnel:

**Instant Converter Methods:**
1.  By jumping into the chute at the top of the Instant Converter (after hopping up onto one of its shorter sections).
2.  By hopping directly onto the right side of the converter.
3.  Alternatively, players can jump down from the Yellow Cannon or hop onto the converter from the ledge beside it.

**Alternate Methods:**
1.  Players can glide toward the walls of the map (in the direction of Stump Field) and then hop over to the ground gap to enter the tunnel.

The tunnel features an exit platform located approximately one-third of the way down, which teleports players onto the top of the Instant Converter near the Sunflower Field.

## Audio Environment

When inside the White Tunnel, a specific audio track plays, known as "Crawlers." This music is also heard within the King Beetle's Lair and the Commando Chick's Hideout.

## Trivia

*   The winking bee face at the end of the tunnel is identical to a picture used in previous Bee Swarm Simulator thumbnails.
*   **Historical Note (Pre-2018 Update):** Before the 2018-09-10 update, an external entrance existed where players could find the spot on top of the tunnel where Tunnel Bear enters. This entrance was subsequently buried under green grass (or white snow during Beesmas) to make it less obvious, though the covering remains non-collidable and passable.
*   **Historical Note (Pre-2018 Update):** Prior to the 2018-11-25 update, the tunnel maintained a consistent width throughout its entire length, unlike its current narrowing design.
*   To utilize the alternate entrance, players must jump over the wall behind Science Bear. The recommended method is to use the Wind Shrine to reach the wall and then traverse across it.
