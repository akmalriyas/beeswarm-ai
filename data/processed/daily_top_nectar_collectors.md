# Daily Top Nectar Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Nectar Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator, viewable through the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page. This leaderboard tracks the total amount of Nectar players have collected within a single day.

## Mechanics and Rewards
*   **Display:** The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view all entries up to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The ranking resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

### Prizes
Players who achieve specific rankings by the end of the day are awarded rewards:

*   **Top 5 Finishers:** Awarded the **Dapper From Above Sticker**.
*   **Top 100 Finishers:** Awarded 25 **Tickets**.
