# Vicious Bee Egg Claim

The **Vicious Bee Egg Claim** is a platform located between the Spider Field and Strawberry Field. This interactive pad allows players to exchange 250 Stingers for a Vicious Bee egg. The platform is visually marked with a large Vicious Bee decoration and a "⚠️ WANTED ⚠️" sign, featuring the number "250" printed on its black surface.

### Mechanics and Exchange
This exchange can only be performed once per player.

*   **Cost:** 250 Stingers
*   **Reward:** Vicious Bee Egg

When a player steps onto the platform:
1.  If they do not have 250 or more stingers, a message appears at the top of the screen stating: "Turn in 250 Stingers to Claim a Vicious Bee (Number of stingers/250)".
2.  If the player has already claimed a Vicious Bee egg, attempting to claim it again will prompt the message: "You've Already Claimed a Vicious Bee".

Upon successfully purchasing the Vicious Bee egg, the Rogue Vicious Bee victory jingle plays, and a server-wide announcement is broadcast: 🎉 {Username} has tamed a Vicious Bee 🎉.
