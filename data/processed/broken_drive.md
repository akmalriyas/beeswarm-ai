# Broken Drive

[![Broken Drive](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f3/Broken_Drive.png/revision/latest?cb=20230507111545)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f3/Broken_Drive.png/revision/latest?cb=20230507111545)

> "Looks like a poorly preserved computer part. Maybe someone can fix it..."

**Cooldown:** None

The **Broken Drive** is an inventory item introduced in the 2023-05-07 update. It is used to activate Science Bear's quest, which involves repairing Robo Bear. Upon successful completion of this quest, the Broken Drive is replaced with a White Drive.

### Acquisition
A Broken Drive can only be obtained by finding it behind the Robo Pass Dispensers located in the 30 Bee Zone.

### Trivia
*   The Broken Drive is the only type of drive that cannot be used during the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   It is unique because it can only be obtained once, unlike other drives which can be repeatedly earned through the Robo Bear Challenge or purchased with Cogs. Consequently, it is also the only drive type that cannot be used to craft a Glitched Drive or a Digital Bee.
*   Players do not need to fix the Broken Drive to unlock the Robo Bear Challenge if Robo Bear was previously repaired during Beesmas 2022.
*   This item and the Plastic Egg are among the few items whose sole purpose is to complete a quest.
