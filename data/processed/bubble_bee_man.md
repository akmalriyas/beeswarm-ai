# Bubble Bee Man (BBM)

Bubble Bee Man is a temporary, eccentric quest giver associated with the annual "Beesmas" event in Bee Swarm Simulator. He is known for his chaotic personality and deliberate grammatical errors in dialogue.

## Overview and Lore
*   **Species:** Roblox Catalog Hat
*   **Location:** Inside the 30 Bee Zone (Currently) / Behind the Global Top Battlers Leaderboard (Beesmas 2018-2019).
*   **Bee Prerequisites:** 30 (Current); 5 (Beesmas 2018-2019).
*   **Access Conditions:** He is located on the inner top of the Bear Gate, accessible only during nighttime and requiring a Moon Amulet.
*   **Role:** BBM is one of four temporary quest givers during Beesmas, alongside Sun Bear, Gummy Bear, and Bee Bear. His quests often revolve around judging players via his "Naughty List."

## Dialogue and Personality Notes
BBM's dialogue is characterized by extreme energy, rambling, paranoia, and intentional grammatical errors/misspellings ("nauty," "Beesmas"). He acts as a quasi-divine judge of the player community.

**Key Dialogue Themes:**
*   **The Naughty List:** His primary function is to compile this list, judging whether players are good or bad based on their actions over time.
*   **Paranoia/Omniscience:** He claims to see everything ("I can see it all," "I'm the spirit of surveillance itself").
*   **Beesmas Philosophy:** He views Beesmas not just as a holiday, but as a spiritual test of faith and community cheer.

**Dialogue Snippets (Illustrative):**
*   *"You better be on your best behaviopr, You better not no w i'm telling you why, .. I'm telling you ..., (cough), Look all I'm saying is this: I'm writing the list."* (Accepting Naughty List)
*   *"I don't need gifts, like - What's the point? What? Have you given a present to everyone else yet?? Huh? Huh???"* (If player cannot give him a gift)
*   *"The horror of corporeal embodiment—that i am only what i see, From a multitude of angles."* (Upon receiving a gift)

## Trivia and Mechanics
*   **Origin:** BBM originated from Mah_Bucket's fan-event called "Beesmas."
*   **Uniqueness:** He is the only NPC to have deliberate grammatical errors in his dialogue.
*   **Rewards:** He is one of two NPCs (along with Sun Bear) who reward an Accessory for completing quests.
*   **Quest Giver Status:** BBM is one of six non-bear Quest Givers, and one of three quest givers that require the player to defeat a Stump Snail.
*   **Hats/Masks:** He gives out two different types of masks: the B.B.M. Mask and the Mondo B.B.M. Mask.
*   **Honey Rewards:** BBM is the NPC who rewards both the least (14 Honey) and most (40,023,627,477,283 Honey) amount of Honey upon quest completion.
