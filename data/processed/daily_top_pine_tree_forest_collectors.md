# Daily Top Pine Tree Forest Collectors

The **Daily Top Pine Tree Forest Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator, viewable through the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page. This leaderboard tracks the total amount of pollen players have collected specifically within the Pine Tree Forest on a given day. It displays 10 positions at a time, and users can scroll to view up to the 100th place. The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Awarded with the Pine Tree Forest Stamp Sticker.
*   **Top 100:** Awarded with 25 Tickets.
