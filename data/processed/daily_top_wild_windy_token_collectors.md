# Daily Top Wild Windy Token Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Wild Windy Token Collectors** is one of the game's 64 leaderboards. It can only be viewed through the Global Leaderboards menu in the System Page. This leaderboard tracks the total number of tokens collected daily by players who utilize the Wild Windy Bee.

The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, and users can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. The rankings reset every day at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 10:** Awarded a Tornado Sticker.
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 25 Tickets.
