# Camo Bandana

The Camo Bandana is a Level 8 Beequip with an equip limit of three and no color requirement. It can only be equipped to Bees that possess the "Bomb" ability token.

**Description:** "For any bee that wants to be seen looking like it wants to be unseen."
**Seasonal Status:** Available during Beesmas (🎄Beesmas).

## Acquisition Methods

The Camo Bandana can be obtained through the following methods:

*   Purchasing it in Dapper Bear's Shop.
*   Receiving it as a reward from the Stick Bug Challenge.

**Outdated Method:**
*   Completing Gummy Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest.
