# Marshmallow Bee

The **Marshmallow Bee** is an inventory item introduced during the Egg Hunt 2019 event in Bee Swarm Simulator. It has a base capacity limit of 25, which can only be exceeded through codes or quest rewards.

### Stats and Buffs

When used, the Marshmallow Bee grants the following buffs for 30 minutes:
*   +50% White Pollen efficiency.
*   +50% Capacity multiplier.
*   +250% Conversion Rate.

**Item Details:**
*   **Cooldown:** 1 second
*   **Capacity:** 25

### Acquisition Methods

#### Current Methods (Obtainable)
*   **Codes:** Redeeming specific valid codes, such as `15MMembers`, `ThreeBeeVee`, and `FOURtunate`.
*   **Gift Boxes:** The Shiny Gift Box rewards 1 Marshmallow Bee, while the Confectionary Gift Box rewards 3.
*   **Quests:** Completing Honey Bee's Beesmas quest rewards 9 Marshmallow Bees.

#### Outdated Methods (No longer possible)
The following methods were previously available but are now obsolete:
*   Defeating Mondo Chick (This method was removed in the 2020-12-25 update).
*   Completing various Egg Hunt 2019 quests from NPCs, including Panda Bear (2 Bees), Science Bear/Polar Bear (3 Bees each), and Gifted Riley Bee/Gifted Bucko Bee/Stick Bug (5 Bees each).
*   Completing Sun Bear's Egg Hunt 2020 quest.
*   Specific NPC quest completions: Bee Bear's "Snow Cub Reformation (2/5)" (3 Bees) and Honey Bee's 2024/2025 Beesmas quests (9 Bees).
*   Opening certain seasonal gift boxes, such as the Paper Gift Box from Beesmas 2020 (2 Bees), Minty Gift Box (Summer 2024 - 2 Bees), and Cherry Gift Box (Winter 2024 - 1 Bee).
*   Completing the Retro Swarm Challenge (chance reward).
*   Redeeming various expired codes, including `FestiveFinale`, `2Years`, `PineappleParty`, `3YearParty`, `PlushFriday`, and many others.

#### Old Token Locations
Marshmallow Bees could previously be found in specific map locations:
*   Behind Gifted Riley Bee.
*   Between two peppers in the Pepper Patch.
*   On top of the Pro Shop.
*   Behind Science Bear, next to the Red Teleporter.
*   Next to the Nectar Pot.

### Trivia
*   The Marshmallow Bee is unique among consumables because its pop-up description uses the word "eat" instead of "use."
*   It is one of four consumable items that increase capacity (along with Purple Potion, Super Smoothie, and Snowflakes).
*   The item features sugar crystals, possibly referencing the Peeps brand.
*   Before the April 6th, 2020 update, there was no inventory limit on how many Marshmallow Bees could be stored.
*   It is one of the few consumable buffs that will not be overridden by a Super Smoothie buff (along with Snowflakes, Stingers, Jelly Beans, and Glitter).
*   Marshmallow Bee is the only bee type that cannot be kept in the player's hive.
