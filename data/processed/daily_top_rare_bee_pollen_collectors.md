# Daily Top Rare Bee Pollen Collectors

The **Daily Top Rare Bee Pollen Collectors** is one of the game's 64 leaderboards, viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu in the System Page. This leaderboard tracks how much Pollen players have collected specifically using only Rare Bees on a given day.

It displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll through to see up to the 100th place. The ranking resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 by the end of the day are awarded a Gifted Mythic Egg.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 by the end of the day are awarded 25 Tickets.
