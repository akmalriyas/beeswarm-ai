# Chick Mobs

Chicks were mobs introduced as part of the Egg Hunt 2020 event. They possess a unique ability to hide inside their eggshells, which increases their defense and the chance for bee attacks to be blocked. Chicks spawn in various locations depending on their type.

There are five distinct types: Normal Chick, Hostage Chick, Spotted Chick, Commando Chick, and Mondo Chick. Generally, chicks (Normal, Hostage, Spotted) cannot deal damage to players, while Commando and Mondo Chicks can. When any chick loses all its health points, it is captured, and the player receives a notification in chat.

*Note: Normal, Hostage, and Spotted Chicks were removed from the game at the end of Egg Hunt 2020.*

***

## Normal Chick

Normal Chicks are passive mobs that spawn randomly when players collect pollen in specific areas. They could only spawn if the player had the Black Bear's Sunflower Corral Quest active.

**Stats:**
*   **Location:** Sunflower Field
*   **Respawns Every:** 30 seconds
*   **Health:** Varies (Level 1-15)
*   **Damage:** 0
*   **Battle Points:** Bond
*   **Honey:** Level
*   **Damage Range:** 1–15

**Drops:**
*   Honey
*   Sunflower seeds
*   Treats

***

## Hostage Chick

Hostage Chicks are passive mobs that have a chance of spawning after defeating a hostile mob or a Boss. (Defeating Ants or other chicks would not spawn a hostage chick.) They could spawn with a level range of 1–15 and always despawn 30 seconds after spawning. Mobs that are more difficult to defeat, such as Tunnel Bear, were more likely to cause a hostage chick to spawn. Hostage chicks spawned from harder mobs have higher health, levels, and better rewards.

**Stats:**
*   **Location:** Mob drops (Category:Mobs)
*   **Respawns Every:** 30 seconds
*   **Health:** Varies
*   **Damage:** 0
*   **Battle Points:** Bond
*   **Honey:** Level
*   **Damage Range:** 1–15

**Drops:**

*   Treats
*   Strawberries
*   Blueberries
*   Sunflower seeds
*   Pineapples
*   Bitterberries
*   Gumdrops
*   Micro-converters

## Spotted Chick

Spotted Chicks are passive mobs that could appear inside the Noob Shop, Pro Shop, Ticket Tent, and Blue Maze. They could spawn with a level range of 3–9.

**Mechanics:**
Capturing a spotted chick triggers another to spawn in a different location:
*   If captured in the Noob Shop, one spawns in the Ticket Tent (and vice versa).
*   If captured in the Pro Shop, one spawns in the Blue Maze (and vice versa).

**Stats:**
*   **Location:** Shops (Noob Shop, Pro Shop, Ticket Tent, Blue Maze)
*   **Respawns Every:** 30 seconds
*   **Health:** Varies
*   **Damage:** 0
*   **Battle Points:** Bond
*   **Honey:** Level
*   **Damage Range:** 3–9

**Drops:**
*   Honey
*   Treats
*   Strawberries

## Commando Chick

The Commando Chick is a mini-boss that spawns in its hideout. It deals damage to players and increases in power with each capture.

**Stats & Mechanics:**
*   **Location:** Commando Chick's Hideout (Left of Brown Bear and Wealth Clock)
*   **Respawn Time:** 30:00 (25:30 with Gifted Vicious Bee)
*   **Base Health/Level:** Starts at 150 Health, Level 3. Increases health and level with each capture.
*   **Damage:** 20 damage when colliding with the player; 50 damage if near an exploding grenade.
*   **Grenade Behavior:** When hiding in its egg, it throws grenades: 1 (if >2/3 health), 2 (<2/3 health), 3 (<1/3 health), or 4 (<1/100 health).

## Mondo Chick

The Mondo Chick is a level 8 boss mob located in the Mountain Top Field. Its rewards are shareable between players.

**Stats & Mechanics:**
*   **Location:** Mountain Top Field
*   **Respawn Timer:** Every hour (at full hours, e.g., 1:00, 2:00). Unaffected by Monster Respawn Time stat.
*   **Health/Level:** Always spawns at 300,000 Health, Level 8.
*   **Damage Over Time (DOT):** Automatically takes 333 damage per second. If not defeated within 15 minutes of spawning, it despawns.
*   **Egg Hiding:** It may hide in its egg to block some attacks. The egg can be cracked early by dealing 7500 damage.
*   **Defeat Location:** If the Mondo Chick falls out of the Mountain Top Field and into another field, rewards spawn there. If it dies outside any field, drops form in a ring.

**Trivia:**
*   The chicks are considered invasive to the Mountain, and their eggs are exotic (according to Sun Bear).
*   Spotted Chicks dislike the sun (according to Mother Bear).
*   Black Bear notes that bees do not hurt chicks when attacking; they work together to calm them down and capture them as they are "frightened."
*   While other players' Commando Chicks cannot damage a player directly, their grenades can. A player's bees can also damage other players' Commando Chicks.
*   The Mondo Chick's noise is audible throughout the entire map. It is the only mob with a fixed spawn point in the Mountain Top Field.
*   If a bee lands a critical hit on a chick hiding inside its egg, it ignores the damage reduction and deals normal critical damage.
*   Chicks, Ants, Aphids, Stick Nymphs, Mechsquitos, Cogmowers, and Cogturrets are the only mobs that come in different varieties.
*   Commando Chick is the only chick with red eyes; all others have black eyes.
