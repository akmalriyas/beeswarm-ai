# Micro-Converter

[![Micro-Converter](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/71/Micro-Converter.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404030856)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/71/Micro-Converter.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404030856)

*"Instantly converts all Pollen in your bag to Honey."*

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 5 seconds
*   **Cap:** 15

The **Micro-Converter** is an inventory item introduced in the April 5, 2019 update. When used, it instantly converts all pollen held in the player's bag into honey. It functions as an Instant Converter, eliminating the need for tickets or a journey back to the hive. Furthermore, it features a significantly shorter cooldown and is portable.

**Mechanics:**
The amount of honey received from using a Micro-Converter is not affected by the player's Convert Rate, but it is dependent on the Honey Per Pollen ratio. Players are limited to holding a maximum of 15 Micro-Converters in their inventory at any time. Obtaining more than this limit requires purchasing them from the Robux Shop, completing specific Quests, or adding certain Stickers to the Sticker Stack.

The following audio plays when a micro-converter is used: <https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Mconv.ogg>

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Sources
*   **Mob Drops:** Rarely dropped by various mobs, including Rhino Beetle, Mantis, Werewolf, Tunnel Bear, and Aphid. It is guaranteed to drop from Coconut Crab and Mondo Chick.
*   **Quest Rewards (Black Bear):** Completing specific quests for Black Bear's Star Jelly (15 total) and Mythic Egg quests.
*   **Quest Rewards (Spirit Bear):** Completing certain Spirit Bear quests (30 total).
*   **Quest Rewards (Dapper Bear):** Completing the 4th, 9th, and 13th Dapper Bear quest.
*   **Quest Rewards (Brown Bear):** Completing specific Brown Bear quests: 10, 30, 70, 155, 170, 215, and 270 reward an additional 5 Micro-Converters; completing the 80th rewards an additional 10.
*   **Gifts/Drops:** Rare gift from Festive Gifts or a rare drop from Festive Mark (Ability Tokens).
*   **Donation Reward:** Awarded for donating to the Wind Shrine.
*   **Memory Matches:** Matching a pair of Micro-Converters in Memory Matches (available in increments of 1, 2, 6, or 5 depending on the match type).
*   **Cub Buddy Gift:** Rarely received through a Cub Buddy gift (in increments of 1 or 5).
*   **Location Drop:** Found as a token behind Black Bear, located between the Red HQ and the Windy Bee Gate.
*   **Planter Drop:** Dropped from a Blue Clay Planter; planting it in the Spider Field results in at least 10 Micro-Converters dropping.
*   **Challenge Rewards:** Awarded as a reward from Robo Bear Challenge or Retro Swarm Challenge.
*   **Sticker Stack Reward:** Added to the Sticker Stack when matching specific stickers: Alert Icon, Simple Skyscraper, Lightning, Grey Diamond Logo, Magnet, Spark Staff, and Cub Buddy Voucher (each rewards 3 Micro-Converters).

### Outdated Sources
The following methods for obtaining Micro-Converters are no longer active or have been removed:
*   Completing certain Egg Hunt quests.
*   Opening the Shady Present (gave 24 Micro-Converters).
*   Purchasing the Booster Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog (5 Micro-Converters).
*   Purchasing the Robotic Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog (25 Micro-Converters).
*   The Vibrant Gift Box and Charcoal Gift Box.
*   Giving a present to Gifted Bucko Bee (5 Micro-Converters).
*   Completing Honey Bee's Beesmas 2022 quest (9 Micro-Converters).
*   Completing some of Bee Bear's quests.
*   Redeeming certain expired codes (e.g., ClubConverters gave 10, BillionVisits gave 10).
*   Purchasing specific finished packs from the Robux Shop (Spikey Spring Basket: 15; Cyber Monday Deal: 10).
*   Drops during Beesmas events (Stockings in 2020/2021).

## Crafting Uses

Micro-Converters can be used as a component in crafting. The total requirement for all single-purchase items is 10 Micro-Converters.

| Quantity | Ingredients | Result |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 10 | 7,000,000 Honey, 1 Glue, 1 Oil, 5 Soft Waxes, 5 Neonberries | Sticker-Seeker |

## Trivia
*   If a player attempts to use a Micro-Converter without any pollen in their bag, a message will appear stating: "You need Pollen to use a Micro-Converter."
*   Since the September 28, 2019 update, the hotkey for this item (C), along with those of Stingers and Gumdrops, has been removed.
