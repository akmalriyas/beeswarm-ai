# Top Bear

**Top Bear** is a bear who operates the Mountain Top Shop. Like other Shop Bears, Top Bear's presence is not required to make purchases in the shop.

## Overview

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Species** | Shop Bear |
| **Location** | Mountain Top Shop |
| **Bee Prerequisites** | 25 bees |

### Appearance Details
*   **Fur:** `#b89924` (Shade: `#b89924`)
*   **Snout:** `#b89924`
*   **Torso:** `#ffffff`
*   **Arms:** `#9f851f`
*   **Legs:** `#c6d77a`

Top Bear is characterized by its yellow skin, white shirt, green pants, and a smiley face. It shares a similar design to Noob Bear and Pro Bear; the only difference between these bears is the color of their clothing.

## Trivia
*   Top Bear is the only NPC located in the 25 Bee Zone (Lion Bee Gate).
*   This bear belongs to a group of six bears that do not have function quests. The others are Noob Bear, Pro Bear, Shadow Bear, Tunnel Bear, and Snowbear.
    *   Note: Noob Bear, Pro Bear, and Shadow Bear are among the bears without any specific in-game functions.
*   Unlike other Shop Bears, Top Bear was introduced in the 2018-07-11 update, meaning the Mountain Top Shop was initially unstaffed.
*   Prior to the 2019-09-28 update, players could damage or kill Top Bear by standing near him and causing a Rogue Vicious Bee attack. This mechanic was patched in that update, granting the bear infinite health.
