# Party Cogmower

The **Party Cogmower** is a mob exclusive to the Robo Party event. It functions similarly to a Golden Cogmower, but unlike its rarer counterpart, it remains in one field until the party ends.

## Location and Stats

**Location:**
Any fields except Coconut Field, Spider Field, and Ant Field.

## Drops

The Party Cogmower can drop a variety of items:

*   **Guaranteed:** Honey
*   **Common/Unspecified Rarity:** Robo Party Blessing, Gumdrops, Pink Balloon, Honeysuckle, Royal Jelly, Soft Wax, Hard Wax, Jelly Beans, Whirligig, Ticket, Ticket Planter (Very Rare), Robo Pass, Red Balloon (Extremely Rare), White Balloon (Extremely Rare), Black Balloon (Exceptionally Rare).
*   **Rare:** Mythic Egg (Extremely Rare)

## Combat Strategies

*   Party Cogmowers do not actively aim for the player, allowing players to easily sidestep them.
*   To maximize damage, position your bees near the Party Cogmower so they can lock on and deal consistent damage.
*   **Recommended Bees:** Vicious Bee provides strong single-target damage. Digital Bee can stun the mob in place, enabling follow-up attacks using Mind Hack for increased effectiveness.

## Trivia

*   The Party Cogmower was one of the first mobs introduced during a Robo Party event when players were at lower ranks.
*   It is unique among Robo Party mobs because its health per level is exactly half that of its normal, non-party version.
*   When Bubble Bee Man's Winter Beesmas 2024 quest was added, the mob was originally referred to as "Festive Cogmowers."
