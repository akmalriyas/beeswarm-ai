# Field Booster

The **Field Booster** is a machine that provides a temporary boost to specific fields. It can be located beyond the Lion Bee Gate, situated between the Mountain Top Shop and the Ticket Shop.

## Mechanics

*   **Effect:** Provides a [field boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost") for 15 minutes.
*   **Target Fields:** The booster targets random fields that are either colorless or mixed (fields without a primary color).
*   **Usage Requirement:** Players must have discovered 20 bee types to use the Field Booster.
*   **Cooldown:** The machine has a cooldown period of 45 minutes after activation.

The following audio plays when the Field Booster is activated: [Fieldbooster sound clip](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Fieldbooster.ogg)

## Boost Details

| Field Type | Pollen Boost |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sunflower Field | +300% pollen |
| Dandelion Field | +300% pollen |
| Spider Field | +200% pollen |
| Clover Field | +200% pollen |
| Pineapple Patch | +200% pollen |
| Pumpkin Patch | +200% pollen |
| Cactus Field | +100% pollen |

## Trivia and History

*   The Field Booster is one of three field boosters in the game, alongside the Blue Field Booster and Red Field Booster. It shares a similar design with the others, differing only by color and signage.
*   Certain fields, such as the Coconut Field and Mountain Top Field, cannot be boosted using this machine, even if they are not specifically colored.
*   **Historical Note:** Prior to the 2019-09-28 update, players could access the booster without having discovered 25 bees. Since that date, attempting to bypass gates results in being teleported back to spawn.
*   Prior to the 2019-09-28 update, the cooldown period was two hours.
*   The Field Booster is designed to provide increased pollen for fields encountered early in the game, compensating for their smaller flower sizes.
*   It is currently the only field booster that grants a 200% boost (previously, the Red and Blue Field Boosters granted specific boosts like Strawberry or Bamboo).
