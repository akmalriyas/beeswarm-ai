# Turpentine

[![Turpentine](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e1/Turpentine.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042848)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e1/Turpentine.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042848)

> "A strong solvent that can remove all Waxes from a bee's Beequip."

**CAP:** 25

**Turpentine** is a craftable inventory item, introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. Its primary function is to remove all waxes from a player's Beequip.

The maximum capacity (cap) for Turpentine is 25. If a player obtains Turpentine while already holding more than 25 units, they will not be added to their inventory unless the item is received through quests, codes, or purchased via limited-time offers in the Robux Shop.

## Ways to Obtain

Turpentine can be acquired through several methods:

**Crafting:**
*   Blender (Requires):
    *   10 Super Smoothies
    *   10 Caustic Waxes
    *   100 Star Jellies
    *   1,000 Honeysuckles

**Drops & Rewards:**
*   As an extremely rare drop from Epic Puffshrooms.
*   As a very rare drop from Legendary Puffshrooms.
*   As an uncommon drop from Mythic Puffshrooms.
*   As a very rare drop from any Planter.
*   As a reward for reaching the Top 25 Daily Leaderboard in the Retro Swarm Challenge.
*   As a very rare reward from the Retro Swarm Challenge.
*   As an extremely rare reward from the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   As a reward for completing Stick Bug's Beesmas quest.

**Purchases & Events:**
*   The Purple-Turple Mega Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog (gives 1 Turpentine + other items).
*   Certain packs in the Robux Shop:
    *   Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul Bundle (4,500 Robux) gives 1 Turpentine + other items.
*   As a drop from certain Beesmas decorations:
    *   Dapper Bear's Samovar drops one Turpentine on its 25th use.
    *   Completing Robo Party on rank 45 and 50 for the first time grants the player who started it a Turpentine and other rewards.
