# Gummyballer

[![Gummyballer](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c4/Gummyballer.png/revision/latest?cb=20211227045658)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c4/Gummyballer.png/revision/latest?cb=20211227045658)

The Gummyballer is a tool that can be crafted in [Gummy Bear's Lair]. It specializes in collecting pollen and generating powerful "Gummyball" combos.

## Overview & Stats

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Shop** | Gummy Bear's Lair |
| **Range** | 29 Flowers |
| **Collecting Pattern** | Ball |
| **Collecting Time** | 1 second |
| **Base Collection** | 9 pollen per patch |

### Pollen Collection Details
The tool collects 9 pollen from 29 patches in 1 second, providing a base collection rate of 652.5 pollen per second (when boosted).

Pollen yield varies by flower type:
| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 22.5 | 45 | 67.5 | 90 | 112.5 |
| Red | 22.5 | 45 | 67.5 | 90 | 112.5 |
| Blue | 22.5 | 45 | 67.5 | 90 | 112.5 |

*Special Multiplier: The Gummyballer takes goo; if there is goo when collecting pollen, it increases the stack of your gummyball.*

The Gummyballer requires the following ingredients to craft:
*   **Honey:** 10T
*   **Glue:** 1,500
*   **Gumdrops:** 2,000
*   **Caustic Wax:** 50
*   **Super Smoothie:** 50
*   **Turpentine:** 5
*   **Satisfying Vial:** 3

## Mechanics: Gummyball and Combo

### Goo Absorption & Buffs
Every swing absorbs any collected goo, removing it from the flower. In return for this action, the player receives a 12-second "Gummyball" buff with a maximum stack limit of 1000. The amount of Gummyball stacks gained per collected goo patch is proportional to how fresh the goo is (faded goo grants fewer stacks).

As Gummyball stacks increase, the ball on the collector grows larger, and a stronger aura surrounds it, providing up to a 1.1x white pollen boost.

### Gummyball Launch
Upon reaching the maximum stack of Gummyball (1000), or when swinging with the collector while jumping inside a field, a Gummyball will launch forward in the direction the player is facing. This ball bounces around the border of the field, leaving a trail of goo and collecting pollen from flowers it hits (collection rate and goo spread depend on the ball's size). It also grabs any ability or honey tokens and pops bubbles along its path. The larger the initial Gummyball stacks upon launch, the larger and longer-lasting the launched Gummyball will be.

### Gummyball Combo
The Gummyball rewards the player with "Gummyball Combo," which has a maximum stack of 1000. This combo always provides a 1.1x white pollen boost and up to an 11x goo boost, depending on the current stack size. Stacks are gained by:
*   Hitting a wall: +2 stacks
*   Ability Tokens: +1 stack per token
*   Honey Tokens: +3 stacks per token
*   Marks: +8 stacks per mark
*   Precise Marks: +30 stacks for 8 seconds

## Strategies
*   **Field Alignment:** Choose a narrow field and align your character perpendicular to the field border you are facing (using "," and "." keys with shift lock, or straight vertical lines on mobile). Launch the Gummyball at an area containing active marks, including the Honey Mark (+), Pollen Mark (+), Festive Mark, and Precise Mark. This allows the ball to bounce back and forth, hitting targets without drifting off course.
*   **Timing:** Time the Gummyball launch before [Gummy Morph] activates (around 21/30 if gumdrops are being dropped) to ensure sufficient time for stacking up before the passive ability triggers.

## Trivia
*   The Gummyballer is the second most expensive item in the game by honey cost, only behind The Planter Of Plenty (which costs 100T Honey).
*   Its red counterpart is the Dark Scythe, and its blue counterpart is the Tide Popper. However, the Gummyballer is more expensive than these counterparts, likely because the Dark Scythe and Tide Popper require completing 250 quests from their respective shop owners, while the Gummyballer does not.
*   The Gummyballer shares the title of slowest tool in the game (in terms of collection cooldown) with the Pulsar and the Sticker-Seeker.
*   On December 28, 2021, the Gummyballer was obtainable through an exploit that granted it for free, along with the Tide Popper and Dark Scythe. Onett later patched this, resulting in data resets for users who exploited it.
*   The Gummyballer requires the most base resources to craft behind The Planter Of Plenty. Its raw ingredient costs include: 10T Honey, 412,500 Blueberries, 412,500 Strawberries, 135,500 Sunflower Seeds, 902,500 Pineapples, 16,500 Bitterberries, 3,700 Neonberries, 12,500 Honeysuckles, 6,000 Coconuts, and 812,700 Royal Jellies (or 813B Honey).
*   The Gummyballer was the only item added to Gummy Bear's Lair in an update after the lair itself was released.
*   This is the only tool that undergoes visual changes corresponding to its ability.
*   It is the only tool capable of taking goo patches from flowers.
*   The Gummyball on the tool moves similarly to amulets, dragging behind the collector's actual movement. High server or client ping can cause the ball to appear dislocated from the collector.
*   The Gummyballer, along with The Planter Of Plenty, Hydroponic Planter, Heat-Treated Planter, and Coconut Belt, are currently the only five items requiring Turpentine to craft (needing 5, 25, 1, 1, and 3 respectively).
*   The Gummyballer, alongside the Dark Scythe and Tide Popper, is one of the few items without an in-game description detailing its stats.
*   Previously, any item costing more than 1 billion Honey would display the full number instead of using abbreviations; the Gummyballer was subject to this rule.
*   The crafting recipe for the Gummyballer changed on May 23, 2024: it was reduced from 2500 Gumdrops to 2000 Gumdrops and 3 Satisfying Nectar Vials.
