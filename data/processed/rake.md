# Rake

[![Rake](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bd/Rake.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20190402214714)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bd/Rake.png/revision/latest?cb=20190402214714)

## Overview
The Rake is a tool that can be purchased at the Noob Shop for 800 Honey.

| Statistic | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Range** | 3 Flowers |
| **Collecting Pattern** | Line |
| **Collecting Time** | 0.7 seconds |
| **Base Collection** | 2 pollen per flower |

### Pollen Collection Breakdown
The Rake collects pollen from flowers in a line pattern. The base collection rate is calculated based on the following table:

| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Red | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Blue | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |

*Special Multiplier(s): None*

The Rake collects 2 pollen from 3 flowers in a line every 0.7 seconds, resulting in a base pollen collection rate of approximately 8.57 pollen per second.

## Crafting Ingredients
To craft the Rake, you require:
*   800 Honey

## Trivia
*   Due to the Scooper being an initial reward for joining the game, the Rake was one of the first tools available and is currently the cheapest tool in the game.
*   The Rake is considered an inferior version of the Golden Rake, which can be purchased at the Mountain Top Shop for 20,000,000 (20 million) honey.
*   Originally, the Rake cost 1,100 honey before its price was reduced to 800 Honey.
