# Badges

Badges are a core part of the achievement system in Bee Swarm Simulator. There are currently 155 total badges, including inactive ones, with 30 being unobtainable.

Most standard badges are earned by collecting specific amounts of Pollen from various fields. Obtaining a non-event badge grants both Tickets and a permanent boost. Note that boosts within the same category do not stack; earning a higher-ranked badge overwrites the previous boost. The number of Tickets awarded depends on the rank achieved.

## Badge Mechanics & Usage
*   **Access Requirements:** Players must obtain the Goo Hotshot badge to enter Gummy Bear's Lair, 15 badges of any tier to access the Badge Bearer's Guild, and one Ace badge for a special room within that guild.
*   **Badge Shivers:** Certain badges unlock the chance to receive special one-time rewards from the Wind Shrine called Badge Shivers. The more items donated, the higher the chance of obtaining a Badge Shiver.
*   **Resource Boosts:** Better Coconut Badges are required to obtain more Coconuts from the Coconut Dispenser, and similarly, better Goo Badges are needed for increased Glue from the Glue Dispenser.
*   **Event Badges:** Event badges are unique as they do not grant Tickets or permanent buffs, nor do they appear in the main badge menu. However, some event badges provide special rewards:
    *   The Beesmas badges allow players to obtain two different masks (B.B.M. Mask and Mondo B.B.M. Mask).
    *   Badges from Egg Hunt 2019, Egg Hunt 2020, and Ready Player Two provided items for the Roblox avatar.
    *   The fifteen badges from The Classic, eight from The Games, and two from Winter Spotlight did not provide avatar items but functioned as currency within their respective event hubs.

**Notification System:** Upon claiming any non-event badge, a public server announcement is made with a cheerful audio notification:

> 🥇 {Username} earned the {Badge Name} - {Badge Rank} 🥇

## Trivia
*   In the 2018-06-02 update, Ticket prizes for badges were increased. If a player had already claimed these badges, they received extra Tickets upon logging back in.
*   Although Grandmaster Badges were added in the 2018-09-10 update, their icons still display images of the map prior to the 2018-07-11 update.
*   When the first badges were released in the 2018-05-12 update, the Clover Badge was bugged and did not grant any loot luck; this was fixed in the 2019-09-28 update.
*   The Hive Hub, Coconut, Pepper, and Mountain Top Badges require significantly more pollen than other field badges (e.g., 10 million for Hive Hub Cadet vs. 250 thousand for others).
*   Badges can be used to obtain Badge Shivers from the Wind Shrine for items; however, each shiver can only be obtained once.
*   As of January 17, 2026, the obtainable badges with the fewest players are the Hive Hub Grandmaster and Mountain Top Grandmaster Badges (with 552 and 413 players, respectively).
*   The Sticker Stack Grandmaster Badge is practically impossible to obtain because the required 500 Stickers exceeds the maximum number of stickers available in the game (290).
