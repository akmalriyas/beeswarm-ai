The **Fastest Crab Slayers** leaderboard is one of the game's leaderboards. It tracks the least amount of time a player has taken to defeat the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab). The board displays the top 10 positions, though players can scroll down to view rankings up to the 100th place.

If a player has never defeated the Coconut Crab, the game interface will display the message: "You haven't defeated the Coconut Crab."

## Location

The challenge is located past the [Windy Bee Gate](/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate). Players must enter the [Coconut Cave](/wiki/Coconut_Cave) to access the crab and view their score.

## Trivia

*   This is the only leaderboard that ranks players based on time (the duration before defeating a boss). It joins the [Highest Snowbear Level](/wiki/Highest_Snowbear_Level) and [Top Stick Bug Fighters](/wiki/Top_Stick_Bug_Fighters) leaderboards as one of the three leaderboards dedicated to ranking "bosses."
*   It is the only leaderboard designed for tracking the shortest amount of time required to accomplish a goal.
*   The leaderboard has been updated and repolished multiple times.
*   There was an oversight where Coconut Crab's health was accidentally set to only 20 HP by Onett. During this period, many players achieved unusually fast times. The leaderboard was subsequently reset in June 2024.
