# Motivating Vial

[![Motivating Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6f/Motivating_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042328)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6f/Motivating_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042328)

> Grants 4 hours of Motivating Nectar. (Boosts: Blue Pollen, Convert Rate, Bee Ability Rate)

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 1 Second
*   **Cap:** 10

The **Motivating Vial** is a consumable nectar vial item introduced in the December 26, 2022 update. Upon use, it instantly grants the player 4 hours of the Motivating Nectar buff. It can also be used as an ingredient for crafting other accessories.

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Ways to Obtain
*   Using a Nectar Condenser to condense 12 hours of Motivating Nectar.
*   Adding the Motivating Nectar Icon to the Sticker Stack rewards 1 Motivating Vial.
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 8th quest rewards 1 Motivating Vial and other items.

### Outdated Ways (Historical)
The following methods were previously available but may no longer be active:
*   Purchasing the Nighttime Nectar Pack in the Robux Shop for 400 Robux, which granted 1 Motivating Vial and other items.
*   Purchasing the Violet Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog during Summer 2024 (costing 400 Snowflakes and 20 Gingerbread Bears), granting 1 Motivating Vial and other items.
*   Purchasing the Hefty Honeyday Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2022 (costing 25,000 Snowflakes and 50 Gingerbread Bears), granting 1 Motivating Vial and other items.
*   Opening a Mythic Gift Box rewarded 2 Motivating Vials and other items.
*   Completing Bee Bear's Peppermint Preparation (2/5) quest rewarded 1 Motivating Vial plus other items.
*   Completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest rewarded 1 Motivating Vial and other items.
