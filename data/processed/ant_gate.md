# Ant Gate

![NewAntGate](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d0/NewAntGate.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20240116145547) The Ant Gate

The **Ant Gate**, also known as the **20 Bee Gate**, is a gate that requires 20 bees to pass through. It is located near the Noob Shop, behind the traveling/event bear area (e.g., Sun Bear). The gate gets its name from the large Army Ant featured on it.

## Features and Components

### Mobs
The following mobs are associated with this location:
*   Regular Ant
*   Army Ant
*   Flying Ant
*   Giant Ant
*   Fire Ant

### Shops & Dispenser
This area contains several related shops and dispensers:
*   Ant Pass Dispenser
*   Stinger Shop
*   Free Ant Pass Dispenser

### Fields & Extras
The gate is connected to or near the following features:
*   Ant Field
*   Ant Challenge
*   Ant Challenge Info
*   Top Ant Exterminators (Leaderboard)
*   Gummy Bee Egg Claim
*   Hive Hub Portal

## Audio Themes

**Daytime:** During daytime, the audio track "Wax" plays at this location.
**Beesmas Event:** During the Beesmas event, the audio track "Drone" replaces "Wax" during the day.

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   The Ant Gate and the Bear Gate are unique among all gates as they do not have a bee character representing them.
*   This gate, along with the Windy Bee Gate, is one of only two gates that does not feature a Royal Jelly token in the area directly behind it. However, the Windy Bee Gate possesses a Star Jelly token, making the Ant Gate the only gate without any royal jelly tokens whatsoever.
*   The Ant Gate and the Windy Bee Gate are also unique because they are designed based on components of their respective zones (e.g., the Ant Gate is integrated with the Ant Challenge).
*   A Gummy Bee Egg Claim location is situated near this gate, specifically on the side closest to the Hive.
*   There is a Ticket token placed directly on top of the gate structure.
*   While the Army Ant depicted on the gate has glowing eyes, in the context of the Ant Challenge, all ants (including Army Ants) do not have glowing eyes.
*   As of the 2019-09-28 update, attempting to bypass this gate results in teleportation back to spawn.
*   A secret path exists behind the Global Top Ant Exterminators Leaderboard, which leads to both an Ant Pass and the Gummy Bee Egg Claim location.
*   The Ant Gate indirectly serves as a temporary quest giver for the Gummy Bear character via his "Gummy Bear's Goo Years Beacon" quest.
*   This gate, along with the Basic Bee Gate, is one of only two gates that leads to an area where Honey cannot be purchased.
*   It is the sole gate in the game that features a mob representing it.
