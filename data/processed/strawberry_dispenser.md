# Strawberry Dispenser

![Strawberry Dispenser](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f7/Strawberry_Dispenser.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20210910135641) The Strawberry Dispenser

The Strawberry Dispenser is a dispenser located in the Red HQ. It grants players various resources and tokens based on their hive composition.

## Usage and Mechanics

**Location:**
*   Red HQ (Accessible by climbing the ladder inside, or jumping on the fence near the Special Sprout Summoner).

**Requirements:**
*   Membership in the Bee Swarm Simulator Club group.

**Cooldown:**
*   4 hours.

**Rewards Granted:**
When used, the dispenser grants:
*   Honey (Amount varies based on Red Bees)
*   Haste Tokens x5
*   Red Boost Tokens x10
*   Strawberries (1 Strawberry for every Red Bee in the player's hive)

*Note: If a player does not have any red bees in their hive, no strawberries will be granted.*

## Reward Calculation Details

The amount of honey and strawberries received is determined by the number of Red Bees ($\text{cnt}$) present in the player's hive.

**Strawberry Reward:**
The player receives $\text{cnt}$ Strawberries.

**Honey Reward Formula:**
The base amount of honey is calculated using the formula:
$$\left\lfloor {cnt}^{1.5}+0.5\right\rfloor \times 200$$
However, if this result is less than 500, the player receives a minimum of 500 Honey.

## Trivia

*   The Strawberry Dispenser is required three times in the quest "The Power of Information" within Science Bear.
*   This dispenser yields the same amount of honey per bee as the Blueberry Dispenser.
*   It is one of three dispensers that grant Treats in the game, alongside the Treat Dispenser and the Blueberry Dispenser.
*   It is one of four dispensers that require membership in the Bee Swarm Simulator Club, along with the Honey Dispenser, the Treat Dispenser, and the Blueberry Dispenser.
