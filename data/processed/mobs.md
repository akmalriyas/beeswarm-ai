# Mobs in Bee Swarm Simulator

There are 34 different kinds of enemies, creatures, and monsters (colloquially called "mobs") in Bee Swarm Simulator.

**Mob Statistics & Mechanics:**

*   The most abundant mob by spawn points is the Rhino Beetle, which has five spawn points. Ladybugs are second with four spawn points.
*   The Stump Snail has the longest respawn time, taking 96 hours.
*   Eight entities can attack other mobs: Bees, Flames, Frogs, Rogue Vicious Bee, Wild Windy Bee, Ability Tokens, Passive Abilities, Coconuts, and coal summoned from the Naughty List during Beesmas.
*   A reliable method for defeating most field-defending mobs (excluding King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Cave Monsters, Stick Bug, Rogue Vicious Bee, Wild Windy Bee, Commando Chick, Coconut Crab, and Mondo Chick) is continuous jumping. This exploits a glitch where jumping causes mobs to freeze and stop attacking, leading the player's bees to treat them as non-threats. Players must ensure their bees begin attacking the mob *before* starting the jump sequence.
*   **Loot Changes:** Before the 2019-02-01 update, rewards dropped directly into the player's inventory upon defeat. After this update, mobs drop a ring of tokens around them (Battle Points do not spawn as tokens). Other players cannot collect tokens unless the mob was spawned by them, unless the drops are from public mobs, in which case anyone can collect them regardless of who attacked it. Tokens can be collected using a token link.
*   **Level Scaling:** Mobs have levels displayed next to their name. If a player's bees are lower level than the mob they attack, they have a higher chance of missing attacks. The lower the bee's level compared to the mob's level, the more likely the high-level mob will deflect or dodge the attack.
*   **Passive Mobs:** Some mobs are passive, meaning neither the player nor the mob initiates an attack. These include Bean Bugs, Frogs, and Fireflies (with exceptions for Chicks: Chick, Spotted Chick, and Hostage Chick).
*   **Boss Classifications:** Mobs are categorized as regular mobs, mini-bosses, or bosses based on their properties. Mini-bosses are distinct from regular mobs but do not qualify as full bosses. Examples of mini-bosses include Wild Windy Bee, Rogue Vicious Bee, Snowbear, Commando Chick, and Stump Snail.
*   **Respawn Timing:** Daylight Saving Time affects the respawn times for the Stump Snail, King Beetle, and Tunnel Bear.
*   **Loot Luck:** Loot Luck directly influences the quantity and quality of mob rewards but does not affect honey drops (which are affected by Honey from Tokens).
*   **Mondo/Commando Chick Ability:** When Mondo Chick and Commando Chick use their egg block ability, it blocks almost all bees' attacks, even if the bee is equal to or higher level than the chick.
*   **Stump Snail Mechanics:** Stump Snail has the highest health of all mobs and does not intentionally attack the player. It moves in a circle around the edge of the Stump Field. Damage occurs only if the player stands directly in front of the snail or within its head area; standing inside the shell is safe.
*   **Bugs:** A Roblox bug between 2022-07-20 and 2022-07-29 caused mobs to malfunction.

## Regular Mobs

Regular mobs remain in specific fields and will attack the player if they enter that field. Once defeated, they respawn after a set period.

The Monster Respawn Time can be reduced by owning a Gifted Bee Vicious Bee. For example, the Stump Snail normally takes 96 hours (4 days) to respawn, but with this bee, the time is reduced by 15% to 81 hours and 36 minutes (3.4 days).

| Mob | Level Range | Fields Defending | Respawn Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ladybug | 1-3 | Mushroom Field, Clover Field, Strawberry Field | 5 minutes |
| Rhino Beetle | 1-3, 5 | Blue Flower Field, Clover Field, Bamboo Field, Pineapple Patch | 5 minutes |
| Spider | 4 | Spider Field | 30 minutes |
| Mantis | 4-6 | Pineapple Patch, Pine Tree Forest | 20 minutes |
| Scorpion | 5-6 | Rose Field | 20 minutes |
| Werewolf | 7 | Cactus Field, Pumpkin Patch, Pine Tree Forest | 1 hour |
