# Passive Abilities Guide

Passive abilities (or passives) are effects found on certain [accessories](/wiki/Category:Accessories), [amulets](/wiki/Amulet), or [bees](/wiki/Bees). These abilities can be active constantly or only activate when specific actions are performed, providing an additional effect to those actions.

Passives granted by accessories or amulets are displayed near the hotbar. Some passives require repeated actions; the number on the icon indicates how close the ability is to activating. Once activated, this counter resets to 0.

Some passive abilities have a cooldown. When in cooldown, the remaining time is shown on the passive's icon in pale red. No trigger will activate the ability, nor will the trigger counter increase during cooldown. These passives also feature an initial cooldown when items are first equipped to prevent immediate bypassing.

## Accessory Passives

### Haste Pulser
**Source:** [Cobalt Guard](/wiki/Cobalt_Guard)
**Trigger:** Activates every 30th [Haste token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste) collected.
**Effect:** Fires a blue pulse that hops to each blue bee, identical to the effect of [Cobalt Bee](/wiki/Cobalt_Bee).
*Note: If no blue bees are present, nothing happens.*

### Focus Pulser
**Source:** [Crimson Guard](/wiki/Crimson_Guard)
**Trigger:** Activates every 30th [Focus token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Focus) collected.
**Effect:** Fires a red pulse that hops to each red bee, identical to the effect of [Crimson Bee](/wiki/Crimson_Bee).
*Note: If no red bees are present, nothing happens.*

### Bubble Bombs
**Source:** [Bubble Mask](/wiki/Bubble_Mask) or [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask) (if owning Bubble Mask).
**Trigger:** Activates every 25th [Bomb token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Bomb), with a 2-minute cooldown.
**Effect:** Spawns 25 [bubbles](/wiki/Bubble) randomly in the player's current [field](/wiki/Fields).

### Diamond Drain
**Source:** [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask)
**Trigger:** Activates every 30th [Blue ability token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens), with a 45-second cooldown.
**Effect:** Summons a giant diamond that instantly converts 5 million pollen plus the player's total conversion amount. The conversion amount is boosted by +10% for every Blue bee owned and increases by an additional 50% for every Gifted Blue bee type owned. It also pollinates 10 (+3 per Blue bee) blue flowers within the field.

### Ignite
**Source:** [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask) or [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask) (if owning Fire Mask).
**Trigger:** Activates every 15th [Red ability token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens) collected.
**Effect:** Creates 5 [flames](/wiki/Flame) in a plus shape at the location where the 15th red token was collected.

### X-Flame
**Source:** [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask)
**Trigger:** Activates every 25th Battle [ability token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens) collected (tokens generated during combat), with a 20-second cooldown.
**Effect:** Creates 29 [flames](/wiki/Flame) in an X shape around the player.

### Coin Scatter
**Source:** [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask) or [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask) (if owning Honey Mask).
**Trigger:** Activates every 20th [Mark token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens), with a 2-minute cooldown.
**Effect:** Converts pollen equal to 300% of the player's total conversion amount, scattering it into 24 honey tokens around the field the player was last in. These tokens last for 1 minute and provide a minimum of 1000 honey per token, increasing with converted pollen and the [Honey From Tokens](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_From_Tokens) stat.
*Note: These tokens cannot be collected by Token Link but can be collected by Triangulate.*

### Gummy Morph
**Source:** [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask)
**Trigger:** Activates every 30 [Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops) used or 10 [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee) ability tokens collected (Gummy Bee tokens count as 3 gumdrops).
**Effect:** The player transforms into a [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear), the Gummy Bee glows, and the field is covered in [goo](/wiki/Goo).
* **As Gummy Bear:** Grants +75% [Goo](/wiki/System_Page#Goo), +100% [Instant Goo Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Goo_Conversion), +24 [Movespeed](/wiki/System_Page#Movespeed), and +60 [Jump Power](/wiki/System_Page#Jump_Power).
* **As Glowing Gummy Bee:** Grants +1000% [Gather Amount](/wiki/Stats#Gather_Amount), +300 [Attack](/wiki/Stats#Attack), and +200% [Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed) for 10 seconds. The bee also leaves a trail while glowing.

### Coconut Haste
**Source:** [Coconut Clogs](/wiki/Coconut_Clogs) or [Gummy Boots](/wiki/Gummy_Boots).
**Trigger:** Activates whenever the player is hit by a falling [Coconut](/wiki/Coconut).
**Effect:** The player gains one stack of [Haste](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens) and 2 seconds of [Coconut Surge](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens), providing x1.5 [Bee Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed) and +10 [Player Movespeed](/wiki/System_Page#Movespeed).

### Goo Trail
**Source:** [Gummy Boots](/wiki/Gummy_Boots).
**Trigger:** Passive; always active while equipped.
**Effect:** Passively covers 5 surrounding flowers with [goo](/wiki/Goo) wherever the player walks on a field. Flowers directly behind the player receive more goo than those surrounding them.

### Emergency Coconut Shield
**Source:** [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister).
**Trigger:** Activates when the player takes damage directly from a [mob](/wiki/Mobs), with a 5-minute cooldown.
**Effect:** Summons 5 [Coconuts](/wiki/Coconut) in the field the player was last in, grants 100% Defense, and increases Bee Attack by x1.25 for 10 seconds.
*Note: This shield does not protect against instant-kill sources like kill bricks, [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear), or [Cave Monsters](/wiki/Cave_Monster).*

### Inspire Coconuts
**Source:** [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister).
**Trigger:** Activates every 5th [Inspire token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inspire) or Star Shower's shooting stars / [Beesmas Light](/wiki/Beesmas_Lights) collected.
**Effect:** Summons 5 falling [Coconuts](/wiki/Coconut) in the field the player was last in.

### Petal Storm
**Source:** [Petal Belt](/wiki/Petal_Belt) or [Coconut Belt](/wiki/Coconut_Belt) (if owning Petal Belt).
**Trigger:** Activates every 30th [boost token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost), with a 30-second cooldown.
**Effect:** Fires 30 Petal Shurikens in a spiral pattern around the player. When a shuriken passes through a bee, that bee instantly converts 10,000 pollen, increased by 7% of its conversion amount.

### Combo Coconuts
**Source:** [Coconut Belt](/wiki/Coconut_Belt)
**Trigger:** Activates when the 40th [Coconut](/wiki/Coconut) is dropped onto a field (via items or abilities). This coconut becomes a Combo Coconut.
**Effect:** When caught by any player, the Combo Coconut is kicked and falls onto either the summoner's field (if caught in the blue circle on the first drop) or another player's field. Each kick speeds up the coconut and grants the kicker stacks of [Coconut Combo](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Passives).
* **Stack Bonuses:**
    * Default: 1 stack per kick.
    * Bonus (Different Field): +1 bonus stack if caught in a field different from where it was last caught.
    * Bonus (Color Classification): Between +1 to +4 bonus stacks depending on the time since being caught in the same color classification (Red/Blue/White/Mixed).
    * Bonus (New Catcher, New Field): +10 bonus stacks if the catcher is a new player for this coconut and the field has not previously been caught by it.
* **Buff:** Grants between 25% to 75% Unique Instant Conversion, between x1.2 to x2 Pollen, and between +1% to +50% Red/Blue/White Pollen, Bee Attack, and Honey From Tokens.
*Note: Combo Coconuts cannot be passed into the [Ant Field](/wiki/Ant_Field) and despawn if not caught; upon despawning, the buff reapplies to all players who previously caught it.*

## Star Amulet Passives

### Guiding Star
**Source:** Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet with this passive. (Can also be summoned during [Beesmas](/wiki/Beesmas) event using [Onett's Lid Art](/wiki/Onett%27s_Lid_Art)).
**Trigger:** Activates every 250th [Boost token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost), with a 5-minute cooldown. Additionally, it permanently grants 1.25x capacity.
**Effect:** Summons a Guiding Star over one of the five fields the summoner has collected the least from for 10 minutes. The chance of selection is based on relative pollen collection: the $n$-th least collected field has a $\frac{6-n}{15}$ chance of being chosen.
* **Benefits:** Grants x2.5 [Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Pollen), Convert Rate, and [Capacity](/wiki/System_Page#Capacity_Multiplier) to the summoner, and x1.25 Pollen, Convert Rate, and Capacity to other players in that field.
* **Pollination:** While active, it pollinates 15 (+1 per Gifted Bee Type) flowers every 15 seconds. It disappears if the summoner leaves the server.

### Star Shower
**Source:** Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet with this passive.
**Trigger:** Activates every 40th [Boost](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost) or [Mark](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mark) token collected, with a 30-second cooldown. Additionally, it permanently grants 1.25x capacity.
**Effect:** 10 shooting stars fall onto the player's current field. Each star collects and instantly converts 30 pollen (+50% per Gifted Bee Type) from 5 flowers. Catching a star grants one stack of [Inspire](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens) and instantly converts 1,000,000 pollen, boosted by 200% of the player's total conversion amount.

### Pop Star
**Source:** Supreme Star Amulet with this passive.
**Trigger:** Activates every 30th [Boost token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost), with a 1-minute cooldown. Additionally, it permanently grants x1.25 Blue Field Capacity.
**Effect:** Summons a Pop Star for 45 seconds that grants 10% [Instant Blue Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Instant_Blue_Conversion) and x2 [Blue Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Blue_Pollen). It automatically applies 2 minutes of [Bubble Bloat](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Passives).
* **Bubble Interaction:** While active, every bubble popped increases the bonus Blue Pollen by 1% (up to x4.5 from x2) and grants 4 seconds of Bubble Bloat (6 seconds for gold bubbles, 1 second otherwise).
* **Buff Duration:** Bubble Bloat increases [Blue Field Capacity](/wiki/System_Page#Blue_Field_Capacity) and [Convert Rate at Hive](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate_At_Hive) by up to x6 for up to 2.5 hours.
* **End Effect:** When the Pop Star ends, it spawns several bubbles over the field proportional to the number of Bubbles popped.

### Gummy Star
**Source:** Supreme Star Amulet with this passive.
**Trigger:** Activates at a 2% chance when using [Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops), or on the 75th use after cooldown ends, with a 1-minute cooldown. Additionally, it permanently grants x1.25 White Field Capacity.
**Effect:** Summons a Gummy Star for 45 seconds that grants 10% Instant [White Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#White_Pollen) and Goo Conversion. Collecting goo during this time makes the star grow, granting bonus White Pollen and Goo (up to x2).
* **End Effect:** After 45 seconds, the Gummy Star pops, scattering honey tokens and [Gumdrops] on the field. The total value of these tokens is 1.5% per gifted colorless bee type on the player's hive based on goo collected during its duration. The number of Gumdrop tokens increases with the amount of goo collected.

### Scorching Star
**Source:** Supreme Star Amulet with this passive.
**Trigger:** Activates every 30th [Boost token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost), with a 1-minute cooldown. Additionally, it permanently grants x1.25 Red Field Capacity.
**Effect:** Summons a Scorching Star for 45 seconds that grants 50% [Instant Flame Conversion](/wiki/System_Page#Instant_Flame_Conversion), +5 Conversion Links, and x2 [Red Pollen](/wiki/System_Page#Red_Pollen) and Convert Rate.
* **Growth:** Standing near flames causes the star to grow, increasing bonus Red Pollen and Convert Rate (up to x5 from x2) and the number of Conversion Links (up to 30 from 5).
* **End Effect:** When Scorching Star ends, it converts all pollen in the player's bag into Honey.

### Star Saw
**Source:** Supreme Star Amulet with this passive.
**Trigger:** Activates after using 3 [Stingers](/wiki/Stinger). The third stinger used is refunded, and the Star Saw summons for 30 seconds.
**Effect:** The saw circles around the player, damaging hostile mobs (15% of Total Attack, with accuracy based on average hive bee level) and collecting tokens. It collects and instantly converts 3 pollen (+0.5 for every 100 Total Attack) from 5 flowers 10 times per second, converting all gathered pollen instantly.

## Bee Passives

### Gathering Bubbles
**Source:** [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee), [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee), and [Frog](/wiki/Frog).
**Trigger:** Passive chance while gathering.
**Effect:** Bees have a percentage chance to spawn [bubbles](/wiki/Bubble) when gathering.
* Bubble Bee: 35% chance (50% if gifted).
* Tadpole Bee: 65% chance (85% if gifted).

### Gathering Flames
**Source:** [Fire Bee](/wiki/Fire_Bee), [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee), [Spicy Bee's Inferno](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inferno), and bees with the [Electric Candle](/wiki/Electric_Candle) Beequip.
**Trigger:** Passive chance while gathering.
**Effect:** Bees have a percentage chance to spawn [flames](/wiki/Flame) when gathering.
* Fire Bee: 35% chance (50% if gifted).
* Demon Bee: 55% chance (75% if gifted).

### Steam Engine
**Source:** Exclusive to Spicy Bee.
**Effect:** Boosts any Spicy Bee's [speed](/wiki/Stats#Speed) by up to 100% with [Flame Heat](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Passives). While active, the Spicy Bee increases steam particle emission proportionally to the amount of flame heat.

### Fuzzy Coat
**Source:** Exclusive to [Fuzzy Bee](/wiki/Fuzzy_Bee).
**Effect:** Has a chance to pollinate up to 5 surrounding [flowers](/wiki/Flowers) (9 if gifted) when gathering. Lower-tier flowers have a higher probability of being pollinated than higher-tier flowers.

### Nectar Lover
**Source:** Exclusive to [Shy Bee](/wiki/Shy_Bee).
**Effect:** Makes Shy Bee twice as likely to sip from a [planter](/wiki/Planter) compared to any other bee. When sipping, it collects twice the amount of [nectar](/wiki/Nectar) and contributes double to the planter's growth.

### Shimmering Honey
**Source:** Exclusive to [Diamond Bee](/wiki/Diamond_Bee).
**Effect:** Whenever this bee converts at the hive, it grants a 25% bonus honey (+2.5% per level). This bonus is doubled if the bee is Gifted.

### Balloon Enthusiast
**Source:** Exclusive to [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee).
**Effect:** When converting from Balloons, this bee gains x3 its normal [convert amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount). Additionally, its base attack scales up to x3 with [Balloon Blessing](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens).

### Sniper
**Source:** Exclusive to [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee).
**Effect:** In battle, this bee uses slow, long-range, more accurate shots dealing x2 damage. Accuracy is calculated as if the bee were 1 level higher (or 2 if gifted) and cannot be below 5%. A Critical Hit halves reload time; a Super-Crit reduces it by 75%.

### Drive Expansion
**Source:** Exclusive to [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee).
**Effect:** Using a [Drive](/wiki/Drives) while this bee is active in [Robo Bear's Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge) corrupts the field based on matching flower color (Glitched Drives corrupt all fields equally). This passive permanently enhances the Digital Bee, increasing Corruption of its abilities and granting stats:
* Red Drive: +0.03 [Attack]
* Blue Drive: +2 [Convert Amount]
* White Drive: +0.25 [Gather Amount]
* Glitched Drive: +0.05% [Ability Rate]
Every 10 [Glitched Drives](/wiki/Drives#Glitched_Drive) grants a Hive Bonus of 0.03% Ability Duplication Chance. Digital Bee can be enhanced with up to 500 of each drive type; maxing out (2000 drives total) grants +10 [Movespeed].

## Other Passives

### Unlimited Gumdrops
**Source:** Obtained through the [Unlimited Gumdrops](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Areas) buff, activated via certain [codes](/wiki/Codes) and the [Glue Dispenser](/wiki/Glue_Dispenser).
**Effect:** Allows the player to use their [Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops) without losing them from inventory. This buff does not increase the total quantity of Gumdrops in the inventory.
