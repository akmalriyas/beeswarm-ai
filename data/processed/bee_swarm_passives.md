# Passive Abilities Guide

Passive abilities, or simply passives, are effects found on certain accessories, amulets, or bees. These abilities can be active at all times or only activate when specific actions are performed as an additional effect of those actions.

Passives granted by an accessory or amulet are displayed near the hotbar. Some passive abilities require the player to perform the same action multiple times; the number displayed on the passive's icon indicates how close it is to activating the ability, and this counter resets upon activation.

Some passives have a cooldown. When in cooldown, the remaining seconds are displayed on the passive's icon in pale red. No trigger will activate the ability, nor will the trigger counter be increased. These abilities also feature an initial cooldown when items with them are equipped to prevent bypassing the cooldown mechanic.

## Accessory Passives

### Haste Pulser
*   **Source:** Cobalt Guard
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th [[Haste token]] collected.
*   **Effect:** Fires a blue pulse that hops to each blue bee, identical to that of [[Cobalt Bee]]. If no blue bees are present, nothing happens.

### Focus Pulser
*   **Source:** Crimson Guard
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th [[Focus token]] collected.
*   **Effect:** Fires a red pulse that hops to each red bee, identical to that of [[Crimson Bee]]. If no red bees are present, nothing happens.

### Bubble Bombs
*   **Source:** Bubble Mask or Diamond Mask (if owning the Bubble Mask)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 25th [[Bomb token]] collected, with a 2-minute cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Spawns 25 [[bubbles]] randomly in the current field.

### Diamond Drain
*   **Source:** Diamond Mask
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th [[Blue ability token]] collected, with a 45-second cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Summons a giant diamond that instantly converts 5 million pollen plus the player's total conversion amount. The conversion amount increases by 10% for every Blue bee and by 50% for every Gifted Blue bee type owned. It also pollinates 10 (+3 per Blue bee) blue flowers within the field.

### Ignite
*   **Source:** Fire Mask or Demon Mask (if owning the Fire Mask)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 15th [[Red ability token]] collected.
*   **Effect:** Creates 5 [[flames]] in a plus shape where the 15th red token is collected.

### X-Flame
*   **Source:** Demon Mask
*   **Activation:** Activates every 25th Battle ability token collected (tokens generated in combat), with a 20-second cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Creates 29 [[flames]] in an X shape around the player.

### Coin Scatter
*   **Source:** Honey Mask or Gummy Mask (if owning the Honey Mask)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 20th [[Mark token]] collected, with a 2-minute cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Converts pollen equal to 300% of the player's total conversion amount, scattering it into 24 honey tokens around the last field the player was in. These tokens last for 1 minute. Each token gives a minimum of 1000 honey, increasing with converted pollen and the [[Honey From Tokens]] stat.
*   **Note:** Honey tokens cannot be collected by [[Token Link]], but can be collected by [[Triangulate]].

### Gummy Morph
*   **Source:** Gummy Mask
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30 [[Gumdrops]] used or 10 [[Gummy Bee ability tokens]] collected (Gummy Bee tokens count as 3 gumdrops).
*   **Effect:** The player transforms into a [[Gummy Bear]], the Gummy Bee glows, and the field is covered in [[goo]].
    *   **As Gummy Bear:** Grants +75% [[Goo]], +100% [[Instant Goo Conversion]], +24 [[Movespeed]], and +60 [[Jump Power]].
    *   **As Glowing Gummy Bee (for 10 seconds):** Gains +1000% [[Gather Amount]], +300 [[Attack]], and +200% [[Speed]]. The bee also leaves a trail while glowing.

### Coconut Haste
*   **Source:** Coconut Clogs or Gummy Boots
*   **Activation:** Activates whenever the player is hit by a falling [[Coconut]].
*   **Effect:** Grants one stack of [[Haste]] and 2 seconds of [[Coconut Surge]], providing x1.5 [[Bee Movespeed]] and +10 [[Player Movespeed]].

### Goo Trail
*   **Source:** Gummy Boots
*   **Activation:** Always active while equipped.
*   **Effect:** Passively covers 5 surrounding flowers with [[goo]] wherever the player walks on a field. Flowers directly behind the player receive more goo than those surrounding them.

### Emergency Coconut Shield
*   **Source:** Coconut Canister
*   **Activation:** Activates when the player takes damage directly from a [[Mob]], with a 5-minute cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Summons 5 [[Coconuts]] in the last field, grants 100% Defense, and increases Bee Attack by x1.25 for 10 seconds.
*   **Limitation:** Does not protect against instant-kill sources like kill bricks, [[Tunnel Bear]], or [[Cave Monster]], though it can be used preemptively.

### Inspire Coconuts
*   **Source:** Coconut Canister
*   **Activation:** Activates every 5th [[Inspire token]], Star Shower's shooting stars, or [[Beesmas Lights]] collected.
*   **Effect:** Summons 5 falling [[Coconuts]] in the last field the player was in.

### Petal Storm
*   **Source:** Petal Belt or Coconut Belt (if owning the Petal Belt)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th [[Boost token]] collected, with a 30-second cooldown.
*   **Effect:** Fires 30 Petal Shurikens around the player in a spiral pattern. When a shuriken passes through a bee, the bee instantly converts 10,000 pollen, increased by 7% of its conversion amount.

### Combo Coconuts
*   **Source:** Coconut Belt
*   **Activation:** Activates every 40th [[Coconut]] dropped onto a field (via items or abilities).
*   **Effect:** The 40th coconut becomes a Combo Coconut. When caught by any player, it is kicked and falls onto the summoner's field (if first drop was caught in the blue circle) or another player's field. Each kick speeds up the coconut and grants the kicker stacks of [[Coconut Combo]].
    *   **Stack Bonuses:** Default 1 stack; +1 bonus stack if caught in a different colored field than where it was last caught; +1 to +4 bonus stacks based on how long it has been since being caught in the same color classification (Red/Blue/White/Mixed); +10 bonus stacks if caught by another player who hasn't caught it yet, in a new color classification.
    *   **Buff:** Grants between 25% to 75% Unique Instant Conversion, between x1.2 to x2 Pollen, and between +1% to +50% Red/Blue/White Pollen, Bee Attack, and Honey From Tokens.
*   **Limitations:** Despawns if not caught; reapplies the buff to all players who caught it once it despawns. Cannot be passed in the [[Ant Field]]. Has a limit of 50 kicks before premature ending.

## Star Amulet Passives

### Guiding Star
*   **Source:** Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 250th [[Boost token]] collected, with a 5-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants 1.25x capacity.
*   **Effect:** Summons a Guiding Star over one of the five fields the summoner has collected the least from for 10 minutes. The chance it falls on the $n$-th least collected field is $\frac{6-n}{15}$.
    *   **Buffs:** Grants x2.5 [[Pollen]], Convert Rate, and [[Capacity]] to the summoner; grants x1.25 Pollen, Convert Rate, and Capacity to other players in that field.
    *   **Pollination:** While active, it pollinates 15 (+1 per Gifted Bee Type) flowers every 15 seconds.
*   **Alternative Summoning:** Can also be summoned by using [[Onett's Lid Art]] during the Beesmas event.

### Star Shower
*   **Source:** Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 40th [[Boost]] or [[Mark token]] collected, with a 30-second cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants 1.25x capacity.
*   **Effect:** 10 shooting stars fall onto the player's current field. Each star collects and instantly converts 30 pollen (+50% per Gifted Bee Type) from 5 flowers. Catching a shooting star grants an [[Inspire]] stack and instantly converts 1,000,000 pollen, added by 200% of the player's total conversion amount.
*   **Note:** Similar to falling Beesmas Lights (activated during Science Bear's quest in Beesmas events).

### Pop Star
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th [[Boost token]] collected, with a 1-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants x1.25 Blue Field Capacity.
*   **Effect:** Summons a Pop Star for 45 seconds, granting 10% [[Instant Blue Conversion]] and x2 [[Blue Pollen]].
    *   **Bubble Bloat:** Automatically applies 2 minutes of [[Bubble Bloat]]. While active, every bubble popped increases the bonus Blue Pollen by 1% (up to x4.5 from x2) and grants 4 seconds of Bubble Bloat (6 seconds if gold, 1 second otherwise).
    *   **Long-Term Buff:** Bubble Bloat increases [[Blue Field Capacity]] and [[Convert Rate at Hive]] by up to x6 for up to 2.5 hours.
    *   **End Effect:** When it ends, the Pop Star spawns several bubbles over the field that increase with the number of Bubbles popped.

### Gummy Star
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)
*   **Activation:** Activated at a 2% chance when using [[Gumdrops]], or on the 75th use after cooldown ends, with a 1-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants x1.25 White Field Capacity.
*   **Effect:** Summons a Gummy Star for 45 seconds, granting 10% Instant [[White Pollen]] and Goo Conversion. Collecting goo during this time makes the star grow, granting bonus White Pollen and Goo (up to x2).
    *   **End Effect:** The star pops, scattering honey tokens and [[Gumdrops]] tokens on the last field. Honey token value is a percentage of the goo collected (1.5% per gifted colorless bee type), and the number of gumdrop tokens increases with total goo collected.

### Scorching Star
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)
*   **Activation:** Activates every 30th [[Boost token]] collected, with a 1-minute cooldown.
*   **Permanent Effect:** Permanently grants x1.25 Red Field Capacity.
*   **Effect:** Summons a Scorching Star for 45 seconds, granting 50% [[Instant Flame Conversion]], +5 Conversion Links, and x2 [[Red Pollen]] and [[Convert Rate]].
    *   **Growth Mechanic:** Standing near flames causes the star to grow, increasing bonus Red Pollen/Convert Rate (up to x5 from x2) and Conversion Links (up to 30 from 5).
    *   **End Effect:** When Scorching Star ends, it converts all of the player's pollen in their bag into Honey.

### Star Saw
*   **Source:** Supreme Star Amulet (with this passive)
*   **Activation:** Activates after using 3 [[Stingers]]. The third stinger used is refunded, and the Star Saw summons for 30 seconds.
*   **Effect:** The saw circles around the player, damaging hostile mobs (15% of Total Attack, accuracy based on average hive bee level), collecting tokens, and converting pollen:
    *   It collects and instantly converts 3 pollen (+0.5 per every 100 Total Attack) from 5 flowers 10 times a second.
    *   The saw instantly converts additional gathered pollen.

## Bee Passives (Worker Bees)

### Gathering Bubbles
*   **Source:** [[Bubble Bee]], [[Tadpole Bee]], and [[Frog]].
*   **Effect:** These bees have a percentage chance to spawn bubbles while gathering.
    *   [[Bubble Bee]]: 35% chance (50% if gifted).
    *   [[Tadpole Bee]]: 65% chance (85% if gifted).

### Gathering Flames
*   **Source:** [[Fire Bee]], [[Demon Bee]], [[Spicy Bee]]'s [[Inferno]], and bees with the [[Electric Candle]] Beequip.
*   **Effect:** These bees have a percentage chance to spawn flames while gathering.
    *   [[Fire Bee]]: 35% chance (50% if gifted).
    *   [[Demon Bee]]: 55% chance (75% if gifted).

### Steam Engine
*   **Source:** Spicy Bee exclusive.
*   **Effect:** Boosts any Spicy Bee's [[Speed]] by up to 100% with [[Flame Heat]]. While active, the Spicy Bee increases steam particle count proportionally to the amount of flame heat.

### Fuzzy Coat
*   **Source:** Fuzzy Bee exclusive.
*   **Effect:** Has a chance to pollinate up to 5 surrounding flowers (9 if gifted) when gathering. Lower-tier flowers have a higher chance of being pollinated than higher-tier flowers.

### Nectar Lover
*   **Source:** Shy Bee exclusive.
*   **Effect:** Makes Shy Bee twice as likely to sip from a [[Planter]] compared to any other bee. When sipping, it collects twice as much nectar and contributes twice as much to the planter's growth.

### Shimmering Honey
*   **Source:** Diamond Bee exclusive.
*   **Effect:** Whenever this bee converts at the hive, it grants a 25% bonus honey (+2.5% per level). This bonus is doubled if the bee is Gifted.

### Balloon Enthusiast
*   **Source:** Buoyant Bee exclusive.
*   **Effect:** When converting from Balloons, this bee gains x3 its normal [[Convert Amount]]. Additionally, its base attack scales up to x3 with [[Balloon Blessing]].

### Sniper
*   **Source:** Precise Bee exclusive.
*   **Effect:** In battle, uses slow, long-range, more accurate shots dealing x2 damage. Accuracy is calculated as if the bee were 1 level higher (2 if gifted), and it cannot be below 5%. If a shot is a [[Critical Hit]], reload time is halved; if it's a [[Super-Crit]], it's reduced by 75%.

### Drive Expansion
*   **Source:** Digital Bee exclusive.
*   **Effect:** Using a [[Drive]] while this bee is active in [[Robo Bear Challenge]] corrupts the field based on matching flower color (Glitched Drives corrupt all fields equally). It permanently enhances the bee, granting stats:
    *   Red Drive: +0.03 Attack
    *   Blue Drive: +2 Convert Amount
    *   White Drive: +0.25 Gather Amount
    *   Glitched Drive: +0.05% Ability Rate
*   **Enhancement Bonus:** Every 10 [[Glitched Drives]] Enhancements grants a Hive Bonus of 0.03% Ability Duplication Chance.
*   **Maxing Out:** Upon maxing out with all 2000 Drives, Digital Bee gains +10 Movespeed.

## Other Passives

### Unlimited Gumdrops (Free Gumdrops)
*   **Source:** [[Unlimited Gumdrops]] buff (obtained via certain codes and the [[Glue Dispenser]]).
*   **Effect:** Allows the player to use their [[Gumdrops]] without losing any from inventory. This does not increase the amount of gumdrops in the player's inventory.

## Trivia

*   There is a visual glitch where, if the player has the Fire, Honey, Bubble, Demon, Gummy, or Diamond Mask equipped, looking at it through the coconut shield will make the front of the mask disappear due to glass textures overriding particles and transparent parts.
*   Bubble Bombs causes each bubble popped to increase in pitch; this effect does not occur anywhere else in the game.
