# Coconut Belt

![Coconut Belt](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/23/Coconut_Belt.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20240525131914)

The **Coconut Belt** is a specialized cummerbund that can be crafted in the [Coconut Cave](/wiki/Coconut_Cave "Coconut Cave"). It is known for creating Combo Coconuts, which are essential for massive boosts and unique conversions.

## Crafting Requirements

| Ingredient | Quantity |

| Coconuts | 500 |
| Tropical Drinks | 1,500 |
| Purple Potions | 200 |
| Hard Wax | 200 |
| Turpentine | 3 |
| Refreshing Vials | 3 |

## Bonus Stats and Effects

*   **Capacity:** +350,000 / +75% Capacity
*   **Convert Amount:** +120
*   **Loot Luck:** +102%
*   **Honey From Tokens:** +100%
*   **Coconut Field Pollen:** x1.1 multiplier
*   **Gifted Bee Pollen:** +10%
*   **Bee Attack:** +1

### Passive Abilities

**Petal Storm (Requires Petal Belt)**
When the player collects every 30th [Ability Token](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Boost "Ability Tokens"), it fires 30 Petal Shurikens around them. Every bee hit by these shurikens instantly converts 10,000 pollen into honey. This conversion amount is calculated as (Base Conversion + 6% of the player's [Convert Amount](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Amount "System Page") + 4% per [Field Boost](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost") stack). The ability has a 30-second cooldown.

**Combo Coconuts**
Every 40th Coconut dropped onto a field (via items or abilities) becomes a Combo Coconut. When caught by any player, the coconut is kicked and falls again. If it is caught on the first drop within the blue circle, it lands back on your own field; otherwise, it may land on another player's field. Each kick increases its speed and grants stacks of "Coconut Combo." More stacks are granted if caught by other players in fields with different colors.

Catching a Combo Coconut grants a buff that provides:
*   Unique Instant Conversion: 25% to 75%
*   Pollen Multiplier: x1.2 to x2 (for Red, Blue, White Pollen)
*   Bee Attack and Honey From Tokens buffs.

If the coconut fails to be caught, it despawns but reapplies its buff to all players who have previously caught it.

## Trivia and Mechanics Notes

*   **Cost Ranking:** The Coconut Belt is one of the most expensive items in the game, surpassing both the [Dark Scythe](/wiki/Dark_Scythe "Dark Scythe") and [Tide Popper](/wiki/Tide_Popper "Tide Popper"), after the [Gummyballer](/wiki/Gummyballer "Gummyballer") and [The Planter Of Plenty](/wiki/The_Planter_Of_Plenty "The Planter Of Plenty"). It is also the most expensive item available in the Coconut Cave.
*   **Buff Changes:** The buff originally provided between x1.1 to x1.5 Pollen and +1% to +50% Pollen, but this has been updated to only include between x1.2 to x2 Pollen. However, the passive description remains unchanged in-game.
*   **Buff Accuracy:** The Unique Instant Conversion buff actually starts at +25%, not +10% as stated in the game interface.
*   **Bug Report:** There is a known issue where the blue circle for Combo Coconut capture does not function correctly; even if caught within it, the coconut may fall onto another player's field instead of staying on its original field.
