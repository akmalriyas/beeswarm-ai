# Looker Guard

![Looker Guard](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/dd/Looker_Guard-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20200404011143 "Looker Guard")

## Overview
The **Looker Guard** is a shoulder pad crafted by a Looker Bee. It is an equipment item that can be purchased in the Pro Shop and is equipped on the left shoulder slot.

> "_A left shoulder pad crafted by a [Looker Bee](/wiki/Looker_Bee "Looker Bee")._"

## Stats
| Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Capacity | +15,000 |
| Critical Chance | +1% |
| Critical Power | +25% |

## Acquisition & Cost
The Looker Guard can be acquired in two ways:

**Purchase (Pro Shop):**
*   300,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey")
*   25 [Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed")

**Crafting Ingredients:**
*   300,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey")
*   25 [Sunflower Seeds](/wiki/Sunflower_Seed "Sunflower Seed")

## Trivia
*   The Looker Guard is one of four guards available in the Pro Shop. The other guards are the Bomber Guard, Hasty Guard, and Brave Guard.
*   It shares the exact same cost and crafting materials as the Bomber Guard, making them the only two items with identical acquisition costs.
