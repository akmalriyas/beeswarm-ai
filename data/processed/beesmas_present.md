# Presents

A **Present** is an exclusive inventory item tied to the Beesmas holiday event. It functions as a gift that players exchange with various NPCs in the game, typically in return for special rewards and permanent boosts applied to the Beesmas Tree.

*Note: This item has appeared in every Beesmas event since 2018, though its availability and recipient list have changed across different years.*

## Mechanics and Usage
When a player interacts with an NPC while possessing a Present, a dialogue exchange occurs where the gift is given. Each NPC can only receive one present during the event period. If the player does not possess a Present or has recently gifted that specific NPC, normal dialogue will display. Certain NPCs may have prerequisites before accepting a gift.

The primary function of giving a Present is to grant the recipient an Ornament, which provides various passive boosts when placed on the Beesmas Tree.

## Ways to Obtain
Presents can be acquired through multiple methods during the event:

*   **Quests:** Completing specific quests from NPCs like Bee Bear awards multiple Presents.
*   **Catalog Purchase:** Purchasing from NPC catalogs (e.g., Bee Bear’s Catalog) for a cost of Snowflakes and other items.
*   **Event Rewards:** Claiming a hive for the first time during the event period with certain NPCs (like Onett).
*   **Donations/Achievements:** Donating specific crops to shrines or completing high-level ranks in events (e.g., Robo Party rank 25) grants Presents.
*   **Codes:** Specific promotional codes may grant multiple Presents.
