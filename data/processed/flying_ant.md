# Flying Ant

**Location:** Found in the **Ant Field** while the **Ant Challenge** is active.

Flying Ants are one of five mobs belonging to the family of **Ants**. They are exclusively found within the **Ant Challenge**, past the **Ant Gate**. This is one of the first Ant Challenge mobs players encounter, capable of appearing on the very first wave, though they usually begin appearing around the second or third waves.

Flying Ants are winged ants that pursue the player. They possess the ability to pounce from a distance and are the fastest type among the five ant varieties. However, they have low health compared to other ant types. Their level can increase depending on the Ant Challenge wave, ranging from Level 1 up to Level 16.

## Strategies

*   **Evasion:** Due to their high speed, players must react quickly to dodge Flying Ants attempting to pounce.
*   **Mobility Boost:** Bees that produce **Haste tokens** can help the player gain the necessary speed to maneuver around these fast-moving ants.
*   **Crowd Control:** Since Flying Ants occasionally spawn in overwhelming waves, using crowd control abilities is highly effective. **Vicious Bee** and **Windy Bee** provide excellent area control. **Digital Bee** can stun multiple Flying Ants simultaneously, allowing them to take extra damage via **Mind Hack**.

## Trivia

*   The **Supreme Ant Amulet** resembles a Flying Ant.
*   Despite having "flying" in its name, the amulet does not actually fly; it simply jumps. In this context, "flying" likely refers to moving quickly, similar to how the Flying Ant moves rapidly.
