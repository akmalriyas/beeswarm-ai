# Bloom Shaker

![Bloom Shaker Icon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fd/Bloom_Shaker.png/revision/latest?cb=20251226063019)

> Grows Blooms in the field you're standing in. Catch their Petals for Honey and boosts.

---
### Stats
*   **Cooldown:** 3 minutes
*   **Cap:** 100

A **Bloom Shaker** is an inventory item introduced in the December 25, 2025 update. When used, it causes three blooms to grow in the field where it is activated. These summoned blooms last for one minute (the standard bloom duration).

The primary renewable sources for Bloom Shakers are the "Petals" quests given by Riley Bee and Bucko Bee.

## Acquisition Methods

*   **Bee Bear Quests:**

    *   13th Quest: 3 Bloom Shakers
    *   14th Quest: 1 Bloom Shaker

*   **Gummy Bear Beesmas Quest:** 10 Bloom Shakers
*   **Honey Bee Beesmas Quest:** 9 Bloom Shakers
*   **Robo Bear Beesmas Quest:** 2 Bloom Shakers
*   **Bucko Bee "Petals" Quest:** 1 Bloom Shaker
*   **Riley Bee "Petals" Quest:** 1 Bloom Shaker
*   **Dapper Bear's 16th Quest:** 1 Bloom Shaker

### Shop & Discovery
*   **Robux Shop:** Purchasing the Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul for 4,500 Robux grants 25 Bloom Shakers.
*   **Bee Bear’s Catalog:** Purchasing the Festive Floral Bundle for 400 Snowflakes grants 1 Bloom Shaker.
*   **In-Game Find:** A Bloom Shaker token can be found on a white platform above the Pineapple Patch.
