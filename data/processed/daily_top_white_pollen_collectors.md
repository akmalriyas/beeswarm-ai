# Daily Top White Pollen Collectors

The **Daily Top White Pollen Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. This leaderboard tracks how much white pollen players have collected on a given day, displaying the top 10 positions at a time. Players can scroll down to view rankings up to the 100th place.

The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST. To check your progress, players must step on the designated white pad in front of the board, which will display the message: "You've collected 'X' White Pollen today."

## Location
This leaderboard is located inside the **Petal Shop**, positioned to the right of the All-Time Top White Collectors Leaderboard.

## Prizes
Players are awarded prizes based on their daily performance:

*   **Top 10:** Players who reach the top 10 by the end of the day receive a **Basic White Hive Skin**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 by the end of the day are awarded 25 **Tickets**.
