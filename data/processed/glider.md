# Glider

[![The Glider](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/79/Glider.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403022840)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/79/Glider.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403022840)

> "_Floats much faster than the Parachute, allowing you to fly through the sky! Press jump while in the air to open._"

The **Glider** is a gliding tool that can be purchased at the Mountain Top Shop. It serves as an upgraded version of the Parachute.

| Crafting Ingredients | Statistics |
| :---: | :---: |
| 5,000,000 Honey | Speed: 70<br>Float: -24 |

The Glider allows the player to glide much quicker and change direction faster than the Parachute. To activate it, jump once, then jump again while in mid-air. If the player becomes airborne through another method (such as a cannon), they perform a double jump. To disengage the gliding feature, jump one more time or make contact with the ground.

The gliding function is hardcoded to the player; they will glide even during a lag spike, although the Glider may not appear immediately.

### Usage and Mechanics
The primary use of the Glider is accessing areas on the map that would otherwise be inaccessible. It can be used to collect Royal Jelly and Ticket tokens across the map and facilitate several shortcuts.

*   **Access Points:** Along with the Parachute, the Glider is one of the only ways to obtain the Diamond Egg token located below the platform in the Bear Gate area. It is also essential for reaching the Star Hall atop the Noob Shop.
*   **Combat/Evasion:** The Glider can be used to dodge mobs, as most enemies do not have a high detection range while airborne. Combined with cannons (especially the Red Cannon), it offers one of the most efficient methods of map traversal.

The Glider's gliding speed is unaffected by movement-enhancing boosts such as Haste, Haste+, Bear Morph, or Oil.

## Trivia
*   The Glider and the Parachute are the only gliding tools in the game, excluding the unobtainable Debug Glider.
*   Using the Glider allows it to be possible to glide outside of the map boundaries. However, exiting the map is also possible if a Wild Windy Bee blows at the player with a gust of wind.
*   If the player enables shift-lock, they can slightly strafe to one side while using any gliding tool.
*   The Glider is displayed as a decoration on top of the Mountain Top Shop, similar to how the Parachute model is located on top of the Pro Shop.
*   It was previously the third cheapest item in the Mountain Top Shop before the Eviction item was removed.
*   Both the Glider and the Parachute can be utilized for movement during a game of Memory Match.
