# Critical Hit Mechanics

![Carpenter Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/75/Carpenter_Bee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20200403235126) | **Note:** This content is actively being updated to match the latest game changes.

**Critical Hits** are moments of increased pollen collection and attack damage, doubling their base power. Their frequency is determined by [Critical Chance], and their strength is determined by [Critical Power]. A critical hit will feature a visual shake and color change in both pollen collection and attack animations.

[![The Critical Chance, Critical Power, Super-Crit Chance, and Super-Crit Power stats in the System Page.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b7/CriticalStats1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230702211804)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b7/CriticalStats1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230702211804) [](/wiki/File:CriticalStats1.png)

All critical hit statistics can be viewed in the [System Page].

## Critical Chance

**Critical Chance** is the probability that any given hit will result in a critical hit. This stat can be improved through various methods, including ability tokens, guards, and inventory items.

### Bees

#### Tokens
*   [Focus](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Focus "Ability Tokens"): Grants +3% Critical Chance per token, capping at +30% from 10 tokens.
*   [Precise Mark](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Target_Practice "Ability Tokens"): Provides +7% Critical Chance per token from the [Target Practice] ability, capping at +21% from three Marks.
*   The [Tabby Blessing](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens "Buffs & Debuffs") buff, granted by collecting [Tabby Love] while having maximum stacks, grants +1% Critical Chance.

#### Gifted Hive Bonuses
*   Gifted [Commander Bee](/wiki/Commander_Bee "Commander Bee"): Grants +3% Critical Chance.

#### Beequips
**Individual Bee:**
*   [Bottle Cap](/wiki/Bottle_Cap "Bottle Cap"): Provides from +1% to +2% Critical Chance. Can be increased up to +12% using [waxes].
*   [Charm Bracelet](/wiki/Charm_Bracelet "Charm Bracelet"): Provides from +1% to +14% Critical Chance.
*   [Bang Snap](/wiki/Bang_Snap "Bang Snap"): Provides from +1% to +3% Critical Chance. Can be increased up to +23% using waxes.
*   [Pink Shades](/wiki/Pink_Shades "Pink Shades"): Provides from +22% to +26% Critical Chance. Can be increased up to +50% using waxes.
*   [Beesmas Top](/wiki/Beesmas_Top "Beesmas Top"): Provides from +5% to +10% Critical Chance. Can be increased up to +20% using waxes.
*   [Beesmas Tree Hat](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree_Hat "Beesmas Tree Hat"): Provides from +2% to +5% Critical Chance. Can be increased up to +10% using waxes.
*   [Snow Tiara](/wiki/Snow_Tiara "Snow Tiara"): Grants +1% Critical Chance.
*   [Toy Drum](/wiki/Toy_Drum "Toy Drum"): Grants +1% Critical Chance.

### Guards
*   [Looker Guard](/wiki/Looker_Guard "Looker Guard"): Grants +1% Critical Chance.
*   [Elite Red Guard](/wiki/Elite_Red_Guard "Elite Red Guard"): Grants +5% Critical Chance.
*   [Riley Guard](/wiki/Riley_Guard "Riley Guard"): Grants +8% Critical Chance.
*   [Crimson Guard](/wiki/Crimson_Guard "Crimson Guard"): Grants +10% Critical Chance.

### Amulets
*   **Star Amulets:** The Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Diamond Star Amulet provide from +1% to +5% Critical Chance. The Supreme Star Amulet provides from +1% to +7% Critical Chance.
*   **Ant Amulets:** The Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, and Supreme Ant Amulet can grant up to +3% Critical Chance.

### Buffs & Debuffs
*   [Tropical Drink](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink"): Grants +5% Critical Chance.
*   [Super Smoothie](/wiki/Super_Smoothie "Super Smoothie"): Grants +6% Critical Chance. This buff can also be obtained from certain codes.
*   Collecting [Jelly Beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans") grants +3% Critical Chance per jelly bean, capping at +5% from 3 jelly beans.
*   Collecting green petals from blooms grants 1% Critical Chance.

### Robo Bear Challenge
Upgrades purchased with [Cogs] during the Robo Bear Challenge can modify Critical Chance:

**Rare Upgrades:**
*   Commit: Starts at +5% Critical Chance and maxes at 2 upgrades for +10% Critical Chance.
*   Virus: Starts at +1% Critical Chance and maxes at 3 upgrades for +3% Critical Chance.

**Epic Upgrades:**
*   RGB: Grants 1% Critical Chance.

**Legendary Upgrades:**
*   Pseudo-RNG: Grants 3% Critical Chance.

#### Debuffs
Upgrades purchased with cogs during the Robo Bear Challenge can downgrade Critical Chance:

**Rare Upgrades:**
*   SSD (Blue): Starts at -3% Critical Chance and maxes at 3 upgrades for -6% Critical Chance.

**Epic Upgrades:**
*   FOV: Grants -2% Critical Chance.
*   Normalize: Sets Critical Chance to x0.

#### Beequips
**Individual Bee:**
*   [Thumbtack](/wiki/Thumbtack "Thumbtack"): Provides from +5% to +10% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +40% using waxes.
*   [Camo Bandana](/wiki/Camo_Bandana "Camo Bandana"): Provides from +1% to +6% Critical Power.
*   [Bottle Cap](/wiki/Bottle_Cap "Bottle Cap"): Provides from +5% to +10% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +60% using waxes.
*   [Kazoo](/wiki/Kazoo "Kazoo"): Provides from +2% to +5% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +65% using waxes.
*   [Whistle](/wiki/Whistle "Whistle"): Provides from +15% to +25% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +85% using waxes.
*   [Pink Shades](/wiki/Pink_Shades "Pink Shades"): Provides from +29% to +49% Critical Power.

**Hive Bonus:**
*   [Bottle Cap](/wiki/Bottle_Cap "Bottle Cap"): Provides from +2% to +15% Critical Power when waxed.
*   [Kazoo](/wiki/Kazoo "Kazoo"): Provides from +2% to +5% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +10% using waxes.
*   [Beesmas Top](/wiki/Beesmas_Top "Beesmas Top"): Provides from +2% to +5% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +15% using waxes.

### Robo Bear Challenge
Upgrades purchased with [Cogs] during the Robo Bear Challenge can modify Critical Power:

**Rare Upgrades:**

**Epic Upgrades:**

**Common Upgrades:**
*   Defragment: Starts at x0.9 Critical Power and maxes at 10 upgrades for x0.5 Critical Power.

**Epic Upgrades:**
*   Client-Side: Grants x0.75 Critical Power.

## Super-Crit

When a critical hit occurs, there is a chance to trigger a **Super-Crit** (indicated by a purple visual effect). A Super-Crit doubles the pollen collection and instantly converts that pollen. Additionally, attack damage is increased by roughly 33% of the player's Super-Crit Power. The frequency of a Super-Crit is determined by [Super-Crit Chance], and its strength is determined by [Super-Crit Power].

### Super-Crit Chance

**Super-Crit Chance** determines how often a critical hit will escalate into a Super-Crit. This stat can be improved through several methods.

#### Bees
##### Tokens
*   The [Precision](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#Precision "Buffs & Debuffs") buff from the [Target Practice] ability grants +2% per token, capping at +20% from ten tokens.
*   The [Precise Mark](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#Precise_Mark "Buffs & Debuffs") buff from the Target Practice ability grants +7% Super-Crit Chance per token, capping at +21% from three tokens.
*   The [Tabby Blessing+](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens "Buffs & Debuffs") buff, granted by collecting [Tabby Love] from a Gifted Tabby Bee while having maximum stacks, grants +1% Super-Crit Chance.

##### Gifted Hive Bonuses
*   Gifted [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee"): Grants +3% Super-Crit Chance.

##### Beequips
**Individual Bee:**
*   [Pink Shades](/wiki/Pink_Shades "Pink Shades"): Provides from +1% to +15% Super-Crit Chance. Can be increased up to +24% using waxes.

**Hive Bonus:**
*   [Pink Shades](/wiki/Pink_Shades "Pink Shades"): Grants +1% Super-Crit Chance as a hive bonus.

### Buffs
*   [Super Smoothie](/wiki/Super_Smoothie "Super Smoothie"): Grants +1% Super-Crit Chance. This buff can also be obtained from certain codes.
*   Collecting Violet Petals from blooms grants 5% Super-Crit Chance.

### Robo Bear Challenge
Upgrades purchased with [Cogs] during the Robo Bear Challenge can modify Super-Crit Chance:

**Legendary Upgrades:**
*   Bruteforce: Grants +6% Super-Crit Chance.

### Super-Crit Power

**Super-Crit Power** determines how strong a Super-Crit will be. This stat can be improved through several methods.

#### Bees
##### Beequips
**Individual Bee:**
*   [Camo Bandana](/wiki/Camo_Bandana "Camo Bandana"): Provides up to +14% Super-Crit Power.

**Hive Bonus:**
*   [Whistle](/wiki/Whistle "Whistle"): Provides from +1% to +20% Super-Crit Power when waxed.
*   [Pink Shades](/wiki/Pink_Shades "Pink Shades"): Provides from +1% to +5% Super-Crit Power. Can be increased up to +9% using waxes.
*   [Demon Talisman](/wiki/Demon_Talisman "Demon Talisman"): Grants up to 10% Super-Crit Power as a hive bonus when waxed.

### Amulets
*   **Cog Amulets:** The Diamond Cog Amulet provides from +10% to +12% Super-Crit Power. The Supreme Cog Amulet provides from +15% to +25% Super-Crit Power.

### Buffs
*   Dark Heat, made from dark flames using the [Dark Scythe](/wiki/Dark_Scythe "Dark Scythe"), grants x1.05 Super-Crit Power initially, with each additional dark flame granting an extra x0.05 Super-Crit Power.

#### Stickers
*   The Precise Eye Sticker grants +1% Super-Crit Power.
*   The Squashed Head Bear Sticker grants +1% Super-Crit Power.
*   Purple Fleuron Sticker grants +1% Super-Crit Power.
*   The Royal Symbol Sticker grants +1% Super-Crit Power.
*   The Royal Bear Sticker grants +1% Super-Crit Power.
*   The Purple 4-Point Flower Sticker grants +1% Super-Crit Power.

### Robo Bear Challenge
Upgrades purchased with [Cogs] during the Robo Bear Challenge can modify Super-Crit Power:

**Legendary Upgrades:**
*   Pseudo-RNG: Grants x1.25 Super-Crit Power.
