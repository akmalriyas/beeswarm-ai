# Honey Dipper Pollen Collecter

[![Honey Dipper](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c7/Honey_Dipper.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20190403020052)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c7/Honey_Dipper.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403020052)

The **Honey Dipper** is a tool available in the Pro Shop, designed for pollen collection.

## Overview
| Statistic | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | 1,500,000 Honey |
| **Shop Location** | Pro Shop |
| **Range** | 49 Flowers |
| **Collecting Pattern** | Surround |
| **Collecting Time** | 0.9 seconds |
| **Base Collection** | 2 pollen per flower |

The tool collects 2 pollen from 49 surrounding patches in 0.9 seconds, resulting in a base pollen collection rate of approximately 108.89 pollen per second. Unlike the Porcelain Dipper, this tool does not collect more pollen from specific flower colors.

### Pollen Collection Multipliers
The Honey Dipper's base collection is consistent across all flower types:

| Flower Color | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Red | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Blue | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |

*Special Multiplier(s): None*

## Crafting Ingredients
The Honey Dipper requires 1,500,000 Honey to craft.

## Trivia
*   The Porcelain Dipper, sold in the Mountain Top Shop, is an enhanced variant of the Honey Dipper. Both tools share the same collecting range.
*   In the Pro Shop, the Honey Dipper is the most expensive tool. It ranks third overall, behind the Hiking Boots and the Propeller Hat.
*   The Onett statue on top of the 6th hive holds a model of the Honey Dipper.
*   This tool is based on real-life honey dippers.
*   It is one of the slower tools in the game, surpassed by the Pulsar, Gummyballer, and Sticker-Seeker.
