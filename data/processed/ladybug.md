# Ladybug

The **Ladybug** is a common mob found in several starting fields within Bee Swarm Simulator. It serves as one of the first mobs players encounter.

## Overview & Mechanics

A Ladybug defends three different fields: Mushroom Field, Clover Field, and Strawberry Field.

### Stats (Level 1 - Level 3)
| Stat | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Health (HP)** | 12 | 20 | 28 |
| **Damage** | 15 | 15 | 15 |
| **Battle Points (BP)** | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| **Bond** | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| **Honey Drop** | ~75 | ~75 | ~75 |

### Respawn Time
The standard respawn time for a Ladybug is 5 minutes. This can be reduced to 4 minutes and 15 seconds when defeated by a Gifted Bee or Vicious Bee.

## Locations & Leveling

Ladybugs spawn in different fields, with their maximum level varying based on the location:

## Combat Strategy

Ladybugs follow standard mob behavior: they pause to aim, then lunge. If the player stops movement while the Ladybug is aiming, it will attack the location where the player was standing previously, rather than their current position.

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Respawn Timer:** The base respawn timer for a Ladybug counts down from 5 minutes (it was previously 5 minutes and 15 seconds). However, like most mobs, there is usually a short delay (10-20 seconds) before they fully respawn.
*   **Detection Range:** In the Mushroom Field specifically, the Ladybug's detection range is larger than that of the standard Field mob, allowing it to react even when the player is outside the field boundaries.
*   **Patch History:** Previously, Ladybugs would completely ignore players, but this behavior was patched in the 2018-05-11 Update.
*   **Mob Rarity:** With a maximum level of Level 3, the Ladybug has one of the lowest possible maximum levels among all mobs in the game.
*   **Design Influence:** The King Beetle, a boss mob, shares a similar design to the Ladybug, being described as a hybrid between a ladybug and a rhino beetle.
