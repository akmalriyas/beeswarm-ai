# Gingerbread Bear

[![Gingerbread Bear](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ae/Gingerbread_Bear.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041818)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ae/Gingerbread_Bear.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041818)

> "A cookie shaped like a bear! Gives 250 bond to the fed bee and has a 1% chance to turn that bee Gifted."

*Note: Do not confuse this item with the Gingerbread Cub skin or Aged Gingerbread Bear.*

A **Gingerbread Bear** is a unique type of [Treat](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Treats) introduced in the 2020-12-25 update. It can be obtained through various methods, including:
*   Checking Mother Bear's Gingerbread House.
*   Defeating specific mobs and bosses.
*   Opening Gift Boxes.
*   Completing Bee Bear's quests.
*   Giving Presents to certain NPCs around the map.
*   Completing Beesmas quests.
*   Using Onett's Lid Art.
*   Purchasing certain Robux Packs.

## Overview

Gingerbread Bears serve two primary functions: they can be fed to [Bees](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Bees) or saved up and spent in the Bee Bear's Catalog during specific Beesmas events (2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025).

When fed to a bee, it grants 250 bond and has a 1% chance of turning the bee into a [Gifted Bee](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Gifted_Bee). These treats can also be saved until the next Beesmas event, at which point they transform into [Aged Gingerbread Bears](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Aged_Gingerbread_Bear).

The probability of a bee becoming gifted is calculated by the formula:
$$(1 - 0.99^x) \times 100\%$$
Where $x$ is the number of Gingerbread Bears fed to the bee.

## Acquisition Methods

### Direct Rewards and Locations
*   As a reward from Polar Bear's Beesmas 2025 quest.
*   Obtained from Mother Bear's Gingerbread House (1 per every 2 hours of playtime).
*   Prize from the Winter Memory Match.
*   Found on the obby in the Hive Hub.
*   Found behind the Sticker Stack Machine.
*   Located in the Beesmas Tree near the Ant Gate.

### Drops and Chance Items
*   As a drop from a Festive Sprout.
*   Chance reward from combos using Puppy Bee's Beequip ability, Reindeer Fetch (limit 1 per day).
*   Rare drop from a Festive Mark.
*   Drop from a Festive Planter.
*   Drop from Onett's Lid Art (usable every 8 hours).
*   Common drop from Aphids (excluding Diamond Aphid), Stump Snail, Coconut Crab, King Beetle, and Tunnel Bear.
*   Rare drop from Scorpions, Ladybugs, Werewolves, and Festive Nymphs.

### Outdated/Event Methods
The following methods are no longer active or were limited to specific past events:
*   Finding it next to the hives in the Retro Swarm Challenge.
*   Redeeming expired codes (RebootXmas, WintersEnd, FrogFix).
*   **2020 Beesmas:** Found in the Beesmas Tree token; received from various NPC quests and Gift Box openings.
*   **2021 Beesmas:** Received from specific NPC quests and Gift Box openings.
*   **2022 Beesmas:** Rewarded by certain Beesmas 2022-exclusive quests and Gift Box openings.
*   **Summer 2024 Beesmas:** Found in the Beesmas Tree token, on the Trading Hub top platform, or next to the spawn of Zombies/Slimes in the Retro Swarm Challenge. Received from specific Gift Boxes and Bee Bear's Summer 2024 quests.

## Trivia

*   Gingerbread Bears, along with Snowflakes, are the only currencies usable in Bee Bear's Catalog. Unlike Snowflakes, Gingerbread Bears remain in the player's inventory after Beesmas ends and are still obtainable and usable.
*   This item, alongside Star Treat and Aged Gingerbread Bears, is one of the few items capable of turning Event bees into Gifted bees.
*   This and Festive Bean are noted as event items that are infinitely renewable outside of their respective events.
*   Feeding 100 Gingerbread Bears to a bee does not guarantee it will become gifted; the approximate chance remains around 63%.
