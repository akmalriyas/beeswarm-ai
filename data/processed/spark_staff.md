# Spark Staff

The **Spark Staff** is a tool added in the 2019-04-05 update, available for purchase at the Mountain Top Shop.

## Stats & Mechanics

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | 60,000,000 Honey |
| **Shop** | Mountain Top Shop |
| **Range** | 81 Flowers |
| **Collecting Time** | 0.5 seconds |
| **Base Collection Rate** | 30 pollen/second (Single Flower) |

### Pollen Collection Details

The Spark Staff collects pollen from the three fullest nearby flowers in 0.5 seconds, increasing the collected amount by 20%.

Its maximum base collection rate varies significantly based on flower tier:
*   **White:** 36 pollen/single, up to 180 pollen/star
*   **Red:** 36 pollen/single, up to 180 pollen/star
*   **Blue:** 36 pollen/single, up to 180 pollen/star

| Flower Tier | Single Pollen Rate | Double Pollen Rate | Triple Pollen Rate | Large Pollen Rate | Star Pollen Rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 36 | 72 | 108 | 144 | 180 |
| Red | 36 | 72 | 108 | 144 | 180 |
| Blue | 36 | 72 | 108 | 144 | 180 |

*Note: The actual collection rate may be lower if the nearby flowers are not full.*

## Trivia & Lore

*   It is the only tool that makes a sound upon use and can collect pollen while in mid-air.
*   It is one of the most expensive tools without an inherent ability.
*   Along with the Scissors, it is one of the few tools capable of collecting all pollen from the flowers it hits.
*   This is the only tool that can collect pollen from more than one flower simultaneously.
*   The tool uses the same model as the official Roblox gear "Nova Wand."
*   It is required for Science Bear's Drive questline:
    *   Collecting 16,777,216 pollen with this tool repairs The White Drive.
    *   Collecting 33,554,432 pollen with this tool corrupts The Glitched Drive.
*   While often considered a low-tier tool by the community, it can be extremely powerful when gathering from high-tier flowers (e.g., those pollinated by other players' Fuzzy Bees).
*   The original version of the tool had a 15% pollen increase instead of the current 20%.
