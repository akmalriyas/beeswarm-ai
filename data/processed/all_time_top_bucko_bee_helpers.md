The **All-Time Top Bucko Bee Helpers** leaderboard tracks the top 100 players who have completed the most quests for Gifted Bucko Bee. The leaderboard can display up to 10 positions at a time. If a player stands on the designated white circle, they can view their personal count of completed Gifted Bucko Bee quests.
