The **Star Hall** is located on the roof of the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop "Noob Shop"). Players can access it by using a [Parachute](/wiki/Parachute "Parachute") or a [Glider](/wiki/Glider "Glider") after firing a [Red Cannon](/wiki/Red_Cannon "Red Cannon"). Alternatively, players can glide down from the [Lion Bee Gate](/wiki/Lion_Bee_Gate "Lion Bee Gate").

Access methods also include:
*   If the player has sufficient [Jump Power](/wiki/System_Page#Jump_Power "System Page"), they can glide from the rock above [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear "Brown Bear") to the Star Hall's edge.
*   Players can jump off the top of the [Royal Jelly Dispenser](/wiki/Royal_Jelly_Dispenser "Royal Jelly Dispenser") next to the [Clover Field](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field").
*   During Beesmas, players can use the stockings decoration to glide to the Star Hall's edge.

Outside the Star Hall entrance, a [Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") token is located to the right of the gate, and a Green Cog with a [Robo Pass](/wiki/Robo_Pass "Robo Pass") token is on the left. Inside, the hall contains five types of [Star Amulet](/wiki/Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") Generators, an [Ant Pass](/wiki/Ant_Pass "Ant Pass") token (located behind the Supreme Star Amulet Generator), and the Royal Jelly Dispenser.

### In-Hall Mechanics

*   **Royal Jelly Dispenser**: Allows players to claim 1 [Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") per [Gifted Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") in their [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"). Using this machine activates the [Haste+](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens") boost for exactly 60 seconds. Players must have at least one Gifted Bee to use it. It is free to use but has a 22-hour cooldown.
*   **Bronze Star Amulet Generator**: Requires the player to discover 5 unique Gifted Bee types (excluding those currently in the hive). Costs 25 million [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") to generate a Bronze Star Amulet, with a 1 in 100,000 chance of granting a [Black Star Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker").
*   **Silver Star Amulet Generator**: Requires the player to discover 10 unique Gifted Bee types. Costs 50 million honey to generate a Silver Star Amulet.
*   **Gold Star Amulet Generator**: Requires the player to discover 20 unique Gifted Bee types. Costs 250 million honey to generate a Gold Star Amulet, with a 1 in 100,000 chance of granting a [Shining Star Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker").
*   **Diamond Star Amulet Generator**: Requires the player to discover 30 unique Gifted Bee types. Costs 1 billion honey to generate a Diamond Star Amulet, with a 1 in 10,000 chance of granting a [Cyan Star Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker"). It also has a 1 in 1 million chance to grant a [Star Cub](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins "Cub Buddy") upon summoning.
*   **Supreme Star Amulet Generator**: Requires the player to discover 40 unique Gifted Bee types. Costs 10 billion honey to generate a Supreme Star Amulet. There is also an option to spend 500 billion honey (if the player possesses 500 billion concurrent honey) to guarantee two Supreme Star Amulets and [Passive Abilities](/wiki/Passive_Abilities "Passive Abilities").

## Trivia

*   The room gets its name from the [music](/wiki/Music "Music") playing inside it: "[starhall](https://www.roblox.com/library/2062483297/starhall)".
*   The cog found outside the Star Hall serves as a hint to the in-game code, "Cog."
*   This room contains 5 of the 6 Amulet Generators in the game; the last one is the [Moon Amulet Generator](/wiki/Moon_Amulet_Generator "Moon Amulet Generator").
*   Prior to the 2019-09-28 update, the Royal Jelly Dispenser gave out 1 [Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") per Gifted Bee *type*, rather than based on the number of gifted bees owned.
*   Before the 2020-04-06 update, the [Ant Pass](/wiki/Ant_Pass "Ant Pass") token was located behind the Diamond Star Amulet Generator (due to layout changes before the 2020-06-06 update).
*   The Supreme Star Amulet Generator is currently the only Amulet Generator in the Star Hall that was added after the hall's initial release, appearing in the 2020-06-06 update.
*   To use the Supreme Star Amulet Generator, a player must discover at least 1 Gifted [Mythic](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic") bee type (excluding Bear Bee), as there are only 39 non-mythic bee types (zero Mythic types if including Bear Bee).
*   The sound pitch when creating a Star Amulet decreases as the tier level increases.
*   An ant pass could be found at the back of the Star Hall before the 2020-06-06 update.
*   If a player jumps on top of the Supreme Star Amulet Generator, they can partially glitch outside the roof area.
