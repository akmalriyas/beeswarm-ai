# Flowers

Flowers are a key resource in Bee Swarm Simulator, found throughout all fields. They can be interacted with to obtain pollen, which is later converted into honey.

## Mechanics and Stats

There are five flower sizes: single, double, triple, large, and star. These sizes determine the base pollen multiplier per harvest:
*   **Single:** Multiplies pollen by 1 (Base Pollen: 30)
*   **Double:** Multiplies pollen by 2 (Base Pollen: 45)
*   **Triple:** Multiplies pollen by 3 (Base Pollen: 60)
*   **Large:** Multiplies pollen by 4 (Base Pollen: 75)
*   **Star:** Multiplies pollen by 5 (Base Pollen: 90)

The base total pollen amount is the maximum amount a flower holds before it must regrow. This multiplier can be increased through various upgrades, including better tools, badges, ability tokens, and field boosters. Larger flowers also grow at a higher rate than smaller ones.

Flowers come in three colors: white, red, and blue. The color of the pollen is important for quests and bee abilities. Additionally, if a red or blue bee harvests a flower matching its color, it receives a 50% bonus pollen yield.

**Regeneration:**
*   Sprinklers, Clouds, and Bubbles can regenerate flowers.
*   The Pollen Haze ability improves the overall flower growth rate.

**Special Interactions:**
Sometimes, a patch of flowers may emit green leaves. Collecting pollen from these patches will either grant an item, a sticker, or spawn an aphid.

Large and Star flowers cannot be naturally grown; they must be created through pollination.

## Trivia

*   White flowers are the most common, as nearly all fields contain them. The Mountain Top Field is the only field that does not have white flowers.
*   The Spider Field is the only field with exactly one color of flower (white).
*   Specific fields host only one size of flower: Ant Field has single flowers, while Stump Field, Mountain Top Field, Coconut Field, and Pepper Patch all feature triple flowers.
*   Star flowers glow during nighttime.
*   Flowers in the Ant Field hold 50 pollen initially, plus an additional 10 pollen per tier (up to a maximum of 90).
*   Before the 2020-04-06 update, flower designs were different, and sizes were limited to small, double, and large.
*   If viewed from a nearly flat angle, flowers appear to change into their corresponding colors.
*   Pollination is only possible using Fuzzy Bee's abilities or passive traits like Diamond Drain, Guiding Star, or specific bee equip such as Poinsettia or Lei.
*   In terms of taste: Blue flowers are described by the Black Bear as minty, red flowers as spicy, and white flowers as neutral.
