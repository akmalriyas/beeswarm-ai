# Starter Zone

The **Starter Zone** is a collection of areas accessible to all players without requiring any specific bee prerequisites. Players can access this zone regardless of their current progression in the game, and it serves as the primary location for player hives.

## Trivia

* This is the only area that can obtain **Sparkles** in all of its fields from non-Firefly sources.
* The Starter Zone contains the highest number of Fields, with five unique types available.
