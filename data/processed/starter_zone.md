# Starter Zone

The **Starter Zone** is a collection of areas that do not require any specific bee prerequisites to access. Any player can enter the Starter Zone regardless of their current progress in the game. This zone also serves as the primary location for players' hives.

## Trivia

*   The Starter Zone is the only area where all fields can receive [sparkles](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles") from non-[firefly](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies") sources.
*   The Starter Zone contains the highest concentration of fields, featuring five distinct field types.
