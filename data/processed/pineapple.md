# Pineapple

A **Pineapple** is a type of treat that can be fed to a bee to raise its bond by 25.

## Mechanics and Effects

*   **Bond Boost:** Increases the recipient's bond by 25 points.
*   **Bonus Bond Gain:** When fed to specific bees—including Hasty, Brave, Honey, Exhausted, Shocked, Fire, Demon, Lion, or Fuzzy Bees—the amount of bond gained is doubled.
*   **Gifting Chance:** Feeding a Pineapple gives the bee a small chance of becoming gifted. The probability varies by rarity:
    *   Mythic Bees: 1/24,000 chance
    *   Legendary Bees: 1/12,000 chance
    *   Epic Bees: 1/10,000 chance
    *   Rare Bees: 1/8,000 chance
*   **Mutation Risk:** Feeding a Pineapple to a Radioactive bee carries a small risk of mutation (0.004%, or 1 in 25,000).

## Acquisition Methods

### General Sources
*   **Treat Dispenser:** One Pineapple is given to the player every time they use the Treat Dispenser.
*   **Pineapple Patch:** Five Pineapples can be found behind the pineapple near the Treat Dispenser.
*   **Challenges & Events:** Received as a reward from the Ant Challenge, or from Memory Matches (excluding Night and Winter Memory Matches).
*   **Sticker Rewards:** Certain Sticker Stacks yield Pineapples:
    *   Shocked Hive Slot Sticker rewards 50 Pineapples.
    *   Rhubarb Sticker rewards 50 Pineapples.

### Drops
Pineapples can be acquired as drops from various sources:
*   **Farming:** Harvesting leaves in the Pineapple Patch may also yield Pineapples. Planting a sprout in the Pineapple Patch yields 7x more Pineapples than other fields.
*   **Mobs:** Dropped by Mantises, Scorpions, Spiders, Werewolves, Commando Chick, Stick Nymphs, Stick Bugs, King Beetles, and Tunnel Bears.
*   **Gifts & Rewards:** Received as a drop from Festive Gifts or Festive Marks, or gifted by Cub Buddies.
*   **Planters:** Dropped from various planters, including Ticket Planters, Festive Planters, Plastic Planters, Candy Planters, Blue Clay Planters, Red Clay Planters, Pesticide Planters, and The Planter Of Plenty.
*   **Other Sources:** Digging into the Beesmas Feast sometimes grants Pineapples, and they can be harvested alongside sparkles in the Pineapple Patch.

### Quest Rewards
*   **Onett:** Star Journey 1 rewards 25 Pineapples.
*   **Spirit Bear:** Various quests reward large amounts:
    *   Witness Grandeur: 100 Pineapples
    *   Nature's Lessons: 250 Pineapples
    *   Rhubarb That Could Have Been: 250 Pineapples
    *   Do You Bee-Lleve in Magic?: 300 Pineapples
    *   Beans Becoming: 500 Pineapples
    *   Echoing Call: 500 Pineapples

## Outdated Methods
*   **Codes:** Redeeming certain expired codes (e.g., FourYearFiesta, 5MMembers) provided a small amount of Pineapples.
*   **Drops:** Previously dropped from the Hostage Chick.
