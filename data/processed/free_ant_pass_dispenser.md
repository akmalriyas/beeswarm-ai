# Free Ant Pass Dispenser

![Free Ant Pass Dispenser](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/62/Free_Ant_Pass_Dispenser.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20180716061518) The Free Ant Pass Dispenser

## Information

### Usage
Gives 1 [Ant Pass](/wiki/Ant_Pass "Ant Pass").

### Requirements
Access to the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate").

### Cooldown
2 hours.

### Location
[Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate").

> *Note: This dispenser should not be confused with the [Ant Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Ant_Pass_Dispenser "Ant Pass Dispenser"), which requires [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") to use.*

The **Free Ant Pass Dispenser** is a [dispenser](/wiki/Dispenser "Dispenser") located past the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate "Ant Gate") and to the left of the standard [Ant Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Ant_Pass_Dispenser "Ant Pass Dispenser"). Players can use this dispenser to obtain a free [Ant Pass](/wiki/Ant_Pass "Ant Pass") every 2 hours. This pass is required to participate in the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge").

**Usage Limit:** Players cannot use the Free Ant Pass Dispenser if they already possess 10 or more Ant Passes in their inventory.

## Trivia
* The Free Ant Pass Dispenser and the [Free Robo Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Free_Robo_Pass_Dispenser "Free Robo Pass Dispenser") are unique free dispensers that do not grant any passive [buffs](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs "Buffs & Debuffs") when used.
* Among all dispensers, only the following five do not grant [Haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens") upon use: The Ant Pass Dispenser, Free Robo Pass Dispenser, Robo Pass Dispenser, Coconut Dispenser, and Glue Dispenser.
* Similarly, only three specific dispensers—the Ant Pass Dispenser, Free Robo Pass Dispenser, and Robo Pass Dispenser—lack visible faucets; instead, they feature a small compartment resembling an ATM or vending machine.
