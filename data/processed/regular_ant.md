# Regular Ant

[ ![Ant-0](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b1/Ant-0.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20191208201559) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b1/Ant-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201559 "Ant-0")

## Overview
The Regular Ant is the most basic mob within the Ants family, exclusively found in the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge). It serves as the introductory enemy encountered by players on the first wave of this challenge.

### Stats and Location
| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Ant Field | Only appears while the Ant Challenge is active. |
| **Base Health** | 25 | Increases with level. |
| **Damage** | 40 | Standard damage output. |
| **Battle Points / Bond** | None | N/A |
| **Honey / Level** | None / 1-16 | Varies based on the current wave. |

*Note: The Regular Ant is distinct from the general category of [Ants](/wiki/Ants), which refers to the entire family of ant mobs.*

## Mechanics and Behavior
Regular Ants are characterized by their simple, predictable movement patterns. They move in fixed straight lines and are generally slow, making them highly susceptible to player avoidance tactics. As the challenge progresses, an Ant's level can increase from 1 up to 16, significantly boosting its health. Due to their basic nature, they are considered the least threatening of the five ant types and are often easily avoidable.

## Combat Strategies
*   **Dodging:** Because Ants move in predetermined straight lines and are slow, they can usually be easily dodged.
*   **Targeting:** Standing near the Ant allows bees to quickly target it for efficient damage application.
*   **Crowd Control:** Ants often spawn in groups, which can overwhelm players who are rapidly advancing through waves. Specialized bees provide effective crowd control:
    *   [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee): Its [Impale] ability is useful against large swarms.
    *   [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee): Its [Tornado] ability helps manage dense groups of ants.
    *   [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee): Can stun multiple ants simultaneously, allowing them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mind_Hack).

## Trivia
*   The [Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Diamond Ant Amulets](/wiki/Ant_Amulet) represent the Regular Ant mob.
*   This is unique as it is the only mob that shares its name with a larger group of mobs (the Ants family).
*   It possesses the same health progression per level as the [Fire Ant](/wiki/Fire_Ant).
*   The Regular Ant was Onett's initial design for the challenge. It was later replaced by [Ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug) because Onett wanted the mobs to function more cohesively as a team.
