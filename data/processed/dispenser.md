# Dispenser

A **Dispenser** is a machine used to dispense various items or boosts to the player, typically under a cooldown period.

To use a dispenser, players must stand on the pad in front of it. Some dispensers have specific requirements for access. These can be direct (such as possessing enough [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") or being part of the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Club "Bee Swarm Simulator Club")) or indirect.

Indirect requirements include:
*   The [Glue Dispenser](/wiki/Glue_Dispenser "Glue Dispenser") requires a specific [badge](/wiki/Badges "Badges").
*   Access to the Gummy Bear's Lair (which contains the Glue Dispenser) requires the player to have the [Goo Hotshot](/wiki/Badges#Goo_Badge "Badges") badge or higher.
*   The [Coconut Dispenser](/wiki/Coconut_Dispenser "Coconut Dispenser") requires defeating the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab"), similar to other content in the [Coconut Cave](/wiki/Coconut_Cave "Coconut Cave").
*   The [Robo Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Free_Robo_Pass_Dispenser "Free Robo Pass Dispenser") requires the player to have 30 bees.

## Dispenser Types

There are currently 12 types of dispensers, categorized by their usage requirements:

### Club Dispensers
These require the player to be a member of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club to use them.
*   [Blueberry Dispenser](/wiki/Blueberry_Dispenser)
*   [Honey Dispenser](/wiki/Honey_Dispenser)
*   [Strawberry Dispenser](/wiki/Strawberry_Dispenser)
*   [Treat Dispenser](/wiki/Treat_Dispenser)

### Free Dispensers
These can be used without joining the Bee Swarm Simulator Club or spending tickets.
*   [Coconut Dispenser](/wiki/Coconut_Dispenser)
*   [Free Ant Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Free_Ant_Pass_Dispenser)
*   [Free Robo Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Free_Robo_Pass_Dispenser)
*   [Free Royal Jelly Dispenser](/wiki/Free_Royal_Jelly_Dispenser)
*   [Glue Dispenser](/wiki/Glue_Dispenser)

### Ticket Dispensers
These require the player to spend tickets to use them.
*   [Ant Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Ant_Pass_Dispenser)
*   [Robo Pass Dispenser](/wiki/Robo_Pass_Dispenser)
*   [Royal Jelly Dispenser](/wiki/Royal_Jelly_Dispenser)

## Trivia & Mechanics

*   Dispensers are the most varied machine type in the game, with a total of 12 different variants.
*   Several dispensers are required for specific [quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests"):
    *   "Honey Bee's Honey Wreath?" requires the [Honey Dispenser](/wiki/Honey_Dispenser).
    *   "BBM's Naughty List" requires the Royal Jelly Ticket Dispenser.
    *   "The Power of Information" requires the Strawberry, Blueberry, and Glue Dispensers.
*   Three dispensers grant [treats](/wiki/Treats "Treats"): the Treat Dispenser, the Blueberry Dispenser, and the Strawberry Dispenser.
*   Six specific dispensers do not grant [haste](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Haste "Ability Tokens"): The Glue Dispenser, Free Ant Pass Dispenser, Ant Pass Dispenser, Free Robo Pass Dispenser, Robo Pass Dispenser, and Coconut Dispenser.
*   The longest cooldown in the game (22 hours) applies to three dispensers: The Glue Dispenser, Free Robo Pass Dispenser, and Free Royal Jelly Dispenser.
*   Four specific pass/ticket dispensers lack visible faucets, instead having a small compartment similar to a vending machine: Free Ant Pass Dispenser, Ant Pass Dispenser, Robo Pass Dispenser, and Free Robo Pass Dispenser.
*   The Glue Dispenser and the Coconut Dispenser are unique in that they provide increased items when the corresponding badge is upgraded.
*   The Coconut Dispenser is the only dispenser capable of granting a field boost.
