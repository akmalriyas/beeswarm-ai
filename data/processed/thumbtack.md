# Thumbtack

The **Thumbtack** is a Level 6 Beequip with an equip limit of two and requires Red color. It is described as "A small pin used to fasten stuff down or as a makeshift stinger."

## Acquisition Methods

*   As a rare drop from the [Red Clay Planter](/wiki/Red_Clay_Planter) and [Heat-Treated Planter](/wiki/Heat-Treated_Planter).
*   Available in [Dapper Bear's Beequip Shop](/wiki/Dapper_Bear%27s_Shop).
*   Awarded as a reward from the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge).
*   Drops from Level 7 [Snowbear](/wiki/Snowbear).

**Outdated Methods:**
*   Was previously a drop from a Level 7 Snowbear during Beesmas 2022, Summer 2024, and Winter 2024.
