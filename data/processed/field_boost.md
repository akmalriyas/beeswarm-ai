# Field Boost

A **Field Boost** is a stacking buff that provides a bonus to pollen gained from all sources within a specific field. Each stack increases the pollen gain from its respective field by 100%, up to a maximum limit of 400% (x4). Unlike Field Wind, which lasts for 30 minutes, Field Boosts last for 15 minutes, regardless of their source.

## Obtaining Field Boosts

Field boosts can be obtained through several methods:

*   **Boosters:** Using the Field Booster, Red Field Booster, or Blue Field Booster.
*   **Coconut Dispenser:** This item specifically boosts the Coconut Field.
*   **Items/Events:**
    *   Glitter
    *   Presents given to NPCs during specific Beesmas events (2018, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025).
    *   Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice.
    *   Redeeming certain codes.

### Stacking Mechanics

Multiple sources of field boosts can be used to achieve higher stacks. With each application, the multipliers add up, and the duration resets back to 15 minutes. This allows players to chain boosts—for example, using a Field Booster until the boost is nearly expired, then activating an item (like codes, glitter, or field dice) for another full 15-minute cycle at a higher multiplier. The maximum stack limit remains four times (x4).

A field that has been boosted by a Field Booster or Field Dice will display white sparkles. If the player leaves the game and returns while a boost is active, the pollen bonus remains, but the visual sparkling effect will disappear.

## Trivia and History

*   Sometimes, the field may glitch after the boost expires, continuing to sparkle. This is purely a visual bug and does not indicate that the boost is active.
*   Historically, Field Boosts used to stack up to x10. Following the 2019-04-05 update, this was nerfed to x5. Later, in the 2019-09-28 update, it was reduced again to x3, but subsequently buffed to x4 in the 2021-12-26 update to compensate for the Jelly Beans nerf.
    *   Note: If a player redeems codes that grant a field boost of x5 or higher, the game will cap the resulting multiplier at x4.
*   The original icon for field boosts was simply an image of grass placed on a background color corresponding to the boosted field.
*   The Ant Field cannot be boosted using glitter, field dice, or field boosters. Attempting to boost this challenge field results in the message: "You can't boost a challenge field." Based on unused assets, it is likely that boosting was intended before the feature was scrapped.
*   Field buffs granted via codes provide both the standard field boost and increased Field Capacity.
*   The Hub Field boost icon is unique among all field icons because it does not have a gray outline. It uses the same image as the Hivesticker hub field stamp sticker.
*   Smooth Dice and Loaded Dice are the only items capable of producing more than one field boost simultaneously.
