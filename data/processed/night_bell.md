# Night Bell

A **Night Bell** is an inventory item added in the 2018-12-25 update. It has a cooldown of 1 second.

When activated, the Night Bell summons nighttime, along with a Moon Sprout and a Rogue Vicious Bee. Activating it also plays a chiming sound and broadcasts a server-wide message:
> 🌙 {Username} has summoned nighttime... 🌙

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Ways to Obtain
*   **Mob Drops:** It can be obtained as a rare drop from several mobs, including Werewolf, King Beetle, Wild Windy Bee, Fireflies, Coconut Crab, and Tunnel Bear.
*   **Memory Match Rewards:** It is a rare reward from both Night Memory Match and Winter Memory Match.
*   **Robux Shop:** Players can purchase it for 80 Robux each.
*   **Badges/Quests:**
    *   It serves as a Playtime Master Badge reward when donating Enzymes to the Wind Shrine.
    *   Completing Spirit Bear's "Do You Bee-lieve In Magic?" quest rewards one Night Bell.
*   **Planter Drops:** It can be obtained as a rare drop from Festive Planters and Stockings.
*   **Festive Nymph Drop:** It is also a rare drop from Festive Nymphs.

### Outdated Ways to Obtain
The following methods for obtaining the Night Bell are no longer active:
*   Completing Bee Bear's "Bee Cub Workshop 5" quest.
*   Talking to Bubble Bee Man after completing his "B.B.M. Mission" during Beesmas 2018.
*   Opening various gift boxes (Brass Gift Box, Celestial Gift Box, Austere Gift Box) during Beesmas 2020.
*   Purchasing Night Bundles in Bee Bear’s Catalog or specific packs in the Robux Shop (e.g., Happy Honeyday Pack, Stocking Stuffer Pack).

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   **Usage Restriction:** If a player attempts to use a Night Bell when it is already nighttime, they will receive the message: "You can only use Night Bells during the day."
*   **Night Cycle Effect:** If you summon nighttime immediately after night ends, a new set of fireflies will spawn while the previous ones fly away. The sky may also flash if the bell is used extremely quickly as the cycle transitions from night to day.
*   **Consumable Mobs:** Night Bells and Box-O-Frogs are noted as the only two consumable items that summon mobs. The Night Bell specifically summons Fireflies and Rogue Vicious Bees.
