# Box-O-Frogs

[![Box-O-Frogs](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0d/Box-O-Frogs.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042538)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0d/Box-O-Frogs.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042538)

*"Summons 3 Frogs that last for 3 minutes."*

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** None
*   **Cap:** 15

**Description**
Box-O-Frogs is an inventory item introduced in the Beesmas 2019 update. When used, it summons three gifted frogs into any field where the player is currently standing. These summoned frogs last for three minutes and possess several abilities: they can collect Ability Tokens, produce bubbles around the field (if spawned in a field), and attack hostile mobs.

If Box-O-Frogs are used outside of a field, the summoned frogs will hop aimlessly. They will not perform any actions unless one of them is within a designated field. The frogs generated by this item have infinite HP and cannot die.

*   Adding the BBM From Below Sticker to the Sticker Stack rewards 1 Box-O-Frogs.

*   A low chance drop from Brown Bear's Stockings.
