# Blue HQ

![BlueHQBeesmas2021](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b8/BlueHQBeesmas2021.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20211230153809) Front view of the Blue HQ.

## Location and Access
The **Blue HQ** is a two-story base featuring numerous interactive elements. It is located behind the Blue Flower Field in the Starter Zone. The entrance features a Bucko Bee model above it. To gain entry, players must have discovered four different blue bee types. The Blue HQ serves as the blue counterpart to the Red HQ.

On the roof, an enlarged Bubble Wand occasionally grows a face and emits sparkles into nearby fields, including the Clover Field, Mushroom Field, Blue Flower Field, and Dandelion Field.

## Interior Features

### First Floor
The first floor contains four main objects:
*   **Blue Teleporter:** Teleports the player near the Ticket Tent and Red Cannon.
*   **Storefront:** A set of blue items available for purchase (see Store section).
*   **Blueberry Dispenser:** Provides a varying amount of Blueberries, determined by the number of * blue bees the player has in their Hive every four hours.
*   **All-Time Top Blue Collectors Leaderboard:** Displays the top 100 players who have collected the most blue pollen.

### Second Floor
The second floor features several specialized elements:
*   **Royal Jelly Token:** A token is located near the front window.
*   **Blue Field Booster:** This item provides a 15-minute field boost for specific fields, with a 45-minute cooldown. It can be used on:
    *   Stump Field (1 stack, only when Stump Snail is on cooldown)
    *   Pine Tree Forest (1 stack)
    *   Bamboo Field (1 stack)
    *   Blue Flower Field (3 stacks)
*   **Gifted Bucko Bee:** This bee requires a Translator to hand out quests.
*   **Top Bucko Bee Helpers Leaderboard:** Located to the left of Gifted Bucko Bee, this board tracks the top 100 players who have completed the most Bucko Bee quests.
*   **Daily Top Blue Collectors Leaderboard:** Located to the right of Gifted Bucko Bee, this board displays the top 100 players who collected the most blue pollen in a single day.

## Store
The store sells nine types of items (Tools, Bags, Accessories, Planters). To open it, press E when near the store's items and not standing on the Blue Teleporter, Blueberry Dispenser, Leaderboards, or the second floor.

## Audio
When inside the shop, the audio track "Vendor" plays. This sound is also heard in several other locations, including the Noob Shop, Pro Shop, Mountain Top Shop, Badge Bearer's Guild, Red HQ, Petal Shop, Hive Hub, and Blue Maze.

## Trivia
*   The Tide Popper is currently the most expensive item available for purchase in the Blue HQ store.
*   If a player stands near the corner of the ramp inside the Blue HQ closest to the Clover Field, they can hear the Crawlers music.
*   Unlike the Riley Bee model on the Red HQ, the wings of the Bucko Bee model do not have collision detection.
*   On January 1, 2022, a glitch occurred where attempting to view the Tide Popper could freeze the game shop until the player rejoined.
*   The Blue HQ is also referred to as the "Blue Clubhouse." If a player has not discovered four blue bee types, their screen will display: "Discover 4 Blue Bee types to enter the **Blue Clubhouse**."
