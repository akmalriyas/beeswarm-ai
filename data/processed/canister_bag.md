# Canister Bag

![Canister](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/5f/Canister.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190402214838)

The **Canister** is a Bag that can be purchased in the Noob Shop. It is described as "A high-tech container that improves conversion speed."

## Stats and Mechanics

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Purchase Cost | 22,000 Honey |
| Capacity Bonus | +10,000 Pollen |
| Conversion Rate Bonus | +30% Convert Rate |

## Acquisition

**Purchase:** Canister can be bought directly from the Noob Shop.

**Crafting Ingredients:**
*   22,000 Honey

## Trivia

*   The Canister is the biggest and most expensive Bag available in the Noob Shop.
*   It is one of the few Bags in the Noob Shop that provides a buff other than increased Capacity, and it is the cheapest such option.
*   In terms of honey cost at the Noob Shop, it is the second most expensive item, only surpassed by the Helmet (which costs 30,000 Honey).
