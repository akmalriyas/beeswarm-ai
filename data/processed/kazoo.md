The **Kazoo** is a Level 9 Beequip with an equip limit of one. It has no color requirement and can be equipped to Brave, Bumble, Bubble, Riley, Shocked, and Buoyant bees.

## Ways to Obtain

The Kazoo can be obtained in several ways:

*   It is available for purchase in Dapper Bear's Shop.
*   It can be a possible reward from the Stick Bug Challenge.
*   It may drop rarely from the Petal Planter.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Buying the Kazoo Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2022.

## Trivia

*   The Kazoo was released in the 2022-04-01 update.
*   It is one of the first non-Beesmas Beequips added to the game.
*   This is one of three instrumental Beequips, alongside the Toy Drum and Toy Horn.
*   The Kazoo is one of eight Beequips that provide a negative stat (specifically -Energy%).
