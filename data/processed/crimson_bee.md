# Crimson Bee

**Crimson Bee** is a Red Event Bee, known as "A Superhero and Defender of all things Red! Together with Cobalt Bee it works to unite bees of all colors."

## Overview & Acquisition
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Event |
| **Color** | Red |
| **Energy** | 35 |
| **Speed** | 18.2 |
| **Attack** | 7 |

Crimson Bee can be obtained by purchasing a Crimson Bee Egg from the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.

Like all Event Bees, it has no favorite treat. To make it Gifted, you must feed it a Star Treat, Gingerbread Bear, or Aged Gingerbread Bear.

### Preferences
*   **Likes:** Clover Field, Rose Field
*   **Dislikes:** Pineapple Patch

Its abilities are enhanced when Cobalt Bee is also present in the hive.

## Abilities

Crimson Bee possesses two unique abilities:

1.  **Red Pulse:** Fires a pulse that hops between your red bees, collecting pollen around them (+10% per level). The power of the pulse increases with each hop. If Cobalt Bee is also owned, it fires a Blue Pulse as well.
    *   *Gifted Effect:* The pulse causes bees to instantly convert pollen equal to 25% of the bee's Convert Amount (+25% per Field Boost stack).

2.  **Red Bomb Sync:** Grants 10% Red Pollen and Instant Red Conversion, allowing Red Bombs to collect from white Flowers for 30 seconds. If Blue Bomb Sync is active in the hive, this effect also applies to blue flowers.

## Gallery & Trivia

*   **Gifted Slot:** A Gifted Crimson Bee hive slot is available.
*   **Icons:** The Red Pulse and Red Bomb Sync icons are associated with this bee's abilities.

### Game Lore and Mechanics (Trivia)
*   Crimson Bee shares a similar ability structure with Cobalt Bee; they are among the only two bees whose Abilities can trigger another bee's corresponding ability.
*   This is one of four Event Bees that feature different color faces on the hive rather than on the bee itself (alongside Cobalt Bee, Festive Bee, and Vicious Bee). Crimson Bee was the first red Event Bee; Festive Bee was the second.
*   When both Crimson Bee and Cobalt Bee are in the same hive:
    *   Red Pulse additionally fires a Blue Pulse to collect from blue bees.
    *   If Red Bomb Sync and Blue Bomb Sync are active, red bombs can collect from both blue and white flowers.
*   Crimson Bee and Cobalt Bee are currently among the only Event Bees that cost 250 tickets. (Historically, Tabby Bee and Puppy Bee could also be purchased for this price).
*   The Crimson Guard, a purchasable item in the Badge Bearer's Guild, features a passive ability similar to Crimson Bee's Red Pulse.
