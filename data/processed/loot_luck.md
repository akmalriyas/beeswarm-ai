# Loot Luck

**Loot Luck** is a passive attribute that increases the chances of receiving certain rewards from defeated [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs").

### Mechanics

The probability formula for obtaining an item from a mob when the player defeats it is calculated as: $1 - ((1 - \text{base chance})^{\text{luck}})$. A general rule of thumb suggests that every 100% Loot Luck grants the player an extra roll opportunity for each potential drop.

Loot Luck also influences token spawns in [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields"). When a [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") token appears, it rolls through a "drop table." Each item has a chance to "pass," and Loot Luck boosts this probability. If an item passes, it spawns; otherwise, the roll moves to the next item. If all items fail, the honey token spawns as is.

**Note:** Loot Luck does not affect [amulet](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet") tiers, stats granted by amulets, drops from [chicks](/wiki/Chicks "Chicks"), or hatching and transforming [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees").

## Ways to Increase Loot Luck

Loot Luck can be increased through various sources, including specific belts, buffs, and badges.

### Belts
Each belt provides a fixed amount of Loot Luck:

*   **Belt Pocket:** +25%
*   **Belt Bag:** +50%
*   **Mondo Belt Bag:** +75%
*   **Honeycomb Belt:** +100%
*   **Petal Belt:** +101%
*   **Coconut Belt:** +102%

### Gifted Hive Bonuses
*   Gifted [Baby Bee](/wiki/Baby_Bee "Baby Bee"): +25% Loot Luck.

### Buffs
*   [Baby Love](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Baby_Love "Ability Tokens"): +50% Loot Luck.
*   [Cool Breeze](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Snowglobe_Shake "Ability Tokens"): Provides between 1% and 10% Loot Luck, depending on the current Cool Breeze level (up to 10% at 100% Cool Breeze).

### Badges
The [Clover Badge](/wiki/Badges#Clover_Badges "Badges") provides increasing amounts of Loot Luck based on its rank:

*   **Cadet Clover Badge:** +10% Loot Luck.
*   **Hotshot Clover Badge:** +20% Loot Luck.
*   **Ace Clover Badge:** +30% Loot Luck.
*   **Master Clover Badge:** +40% Loot Luck.
*   **Grandmaster Clover Badge:** +50% Loot Luck.

## Trivia

*   Prior to the [2019-04-05 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates"), this attribute was simply named Luck.
*   Loot Luck once influenced how honey tokens functioned, but this feature was removed because the existing [Honey From Tokens](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_From_Tokens "System Page") stat already covered that mechanic.
*   Before the [2019-09-28 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-09-28 "Updates"), the Clover Badge did not grant any Loot Luck, an error that was corrected after the update.
*   The [B.B.M. Mask](/wiki/B.B.M._Mask "B.B.M. Mask") previously offered a +50% Loot Luck bonus.
