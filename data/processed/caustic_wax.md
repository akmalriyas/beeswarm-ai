# Caustic Wax

[![Caustic Wax](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bc/Caustic_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042819)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bc/Caustic_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042819)

_"A glowing hunk of beeswax. Has a 25% chance to GREATLY improve a Beequip - but a 75% chance to DESTROY it!"_

Caustic Wax is an inventory item and a type of wax introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. It can be used on a Beequip to boost its stats, though this process carries significant risk. Upon usage, there is a 25% chance of greatly improving the Beequip's stats; however, there is a 75% chance that the Beequip will be permanently destroyed, and the Caustic Wax itself will be wasted.

Caustic Wax has an Upgrade Value of 4 for Beequips. It can also be used as a crafting ingredient to create various items, such as planters and tools.

### Usage Mechanics
*   **Success:** If successfully applied, a message appears: "The Wax improved the Beequip!"
*   **Failure:** If the upgrade fails, a message appears: "The Wax destroyed the Beequip..."
*   **Permanent Beequips:** Prior to the removal of Permanent Beequips, Caustic Wax could not be used on items like Festive Wreaths or Reindeer Antlers, resulting in the message: "Cannot use this wax on a Permanent Beequip."

Caustic Wax can be acquired through several methods:

*   By using a Blender with:

**Sticker Rewards:**
*   Obtained as a stack reward for adding specific stickers to the Sticker Stack:

**Bee Bear's Catalog Purchases:**

**Other Sources:**
*   As a reward from Gifted Riley Bee's Honeyday Candles (0.81% chance).

### Trivia
*   **Raw Ingredients Cost:** The raw ingredients required to craft one unit of Caustic Wax are:
    *   75 Honeysuckles
    *   750 Sunflower Seeds
    *   1,750 Pineapples
    *   165 Bitterberries
    *   25 Neonberries
    *   6,067 Royal Jellies or 6.06 Billion Honey.
*   **Upgrade Probability:** The probability of Caustic Wax improving a Beequip five times in a row is 1/1024 (approximately 0.098%). Due to its low success rate and destructive potential, it is generally advised not to use multiple Caustic Waxes consecutively unless prepared for failure; players often substitute Swirled Wax after a successful roll to maximize upgrade chances on desired stats.
*   **Unique Property:** Caustic Wax is the only wax in the game that can destroy Beequips.
*   **Historical Iconography:** Prior to the 2024-12-25 update, this item shared its icon with Debug Wax, although both items perform similar functions (Debug Wax having a guaranteed 100% success rate).
