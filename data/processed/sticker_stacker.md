# Sticker Stacker

The **Sticker Stack** is a machine located in the [15 Bee Zone](/wiki/Honey_Bee_Gate "Honey Bee Gate"), adjacent to the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest") and the [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)").

This machine allows players to donate various items—including stickers, hive skins, cub skins, or vouchers—to activate a buff. The resulting boost lasts for 15 minutes, with an additional 10 seconds granted for every sticker donated (up to a maximum of one hour). Each specific type of sticker can only be used once in the stack.

## Usage and Mechanics

To use the Sticker Stack, players must step into the podium in front of it and open the menu. Activation can occur in two ways:
1. **Ticket Payment:** Pay 25 tickets to activate the machine.
2. **Sticker Donation:** Use an unused sticker that has not yet been stacked.

Both methods grant a buff based on the stickers previously stacked, adhering to the base duration (15 minutes) plus 10 seconds per sticker, capped at one hour.

**Stacking an Unused Sticker:** When using an unused sticker for activation, two things happen:
*   A new buff corresponding to that specific sticker is granted.
*   The buff's duration is permanently extended by 10 seconds for all future activations.
*   The stacked sticker is consumed and cannot be used again.

In addition to the temporary buff, players receive rewards based on the item they stack.

## Trivia

*   The Sticker Stack was introduced in the January 12, 2024 update, alongside [stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") and the [Hive Hub](/wiki/Hub_Field "Hub Field").
*   The Sticker Stack buff ends a few seconds before the machine can be used again.
*   If a reward from the sticker stack has a capacity limit (e.g., Micro-Converter), that limit is ignored, and the reward will still be granted even if the player already possesses the maximum amount.
*   While the machine is used for 5 sticker stack badges, its Grandmaster variant is currently impossible to achieve as it requires stacking 500 different stickers, but only 290 stickers exist in the game.
*   Certain items require extra confirmation before they can be stacked, including all vouchers, cub skins, and hive skins.
