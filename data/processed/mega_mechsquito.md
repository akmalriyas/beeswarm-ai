# Mega Mechsquito

*Note: This mob is distinct from the smaller counterparts, [Mechsquitos](wiki/Mechsquito), or the group variant, [Party Mega Mechsquitos](wiki/Party_Mega_Mechsquito).*

## Overview

A **Mega Mechsquito** is a unique mobile enemy that appears during the Robo Bear Challenge. It is the fourth and final unique mob type encountered in this challenge, first appearing at Round 15.

### Location
Any field while the [Robo Bear Challenge](wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge) is active.

### Combat Stats
| Stat | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Health** | Increases with level and round. |
| **Damage (Laser)** | 4 |
| **Damage (Contact)** | 30 |
| **Level Range** | 13–22 |

### Behavior and Mechanics
Mega Mechsquitos share similarities with standard Mechsquitos, but exhibit distinct combat behaviors:
*   They fly away from the player when not actively attacking.
*   While attacking, they maintain a distance from the player.
*   Unlike regular Mechsquitos, Mega Mechsquitos attempt to retreat farther than their smaller counterparts.
*   They consecutively shoot 6 small exploding projectiles in a short time span.
*   Hitting the player inflicts the [Mechsquito Toxin](wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#Debuffs) debuff.

**Robo Bear Challenge Requirement:** In Round 15, players must defeat 10 Mega Mechsquitos and collect 2,000,000,000 pollen of a specific color to complete the challenge.

## Strategies and Tactics
*   **Movement:** Mega Mechsquitos aim for the player's current position, not their predicted future location. Therefore, moving in circles can often cause them to miss attacks.
*   **Engagement:** Standing close to a Mega Mechsquito allows your bees to lock onto it and deal damage more quickly.
*   **Support Bees:** [Vicious Bee](wiki/Vicious_Bee) and [Windy Bee](wiki/Windy_Bee) are excellent for crowd control, especially when facing packs of these mobs. Additionally, [Digital Bee](wiki/Digital_Bee) can stun multiple Mega Mechsquitos simultaneously, allowing them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mind_Hack).
