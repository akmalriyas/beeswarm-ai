# Onett's Lid Art

Onett's Lid Art, also known as Yard Art, is a Beesmas decoration unlocked after completing the [Onett's] Yard Art quest. It is located past the Bear Gate next to Onett and can be used every 8 hours.

When activated, the player receives:
*   3 temporary bees (Bumble Bee Level 20, Baby Bee Level 1).
*   An assortment of items.
*   A Guiding Star is activated on one of the five fields with the least pollen collected.

If a player attempts to use the Lid Art before completing Onett's quest, it displays the message: "This art is missing something... It's not very impactful."

## Appearance and Lore

The decoration depicts several characters arranged from left to right: Spirit Bear on King Beetle, Black Bear, Mother Bear, Bumble Bee, Stick Bug, and a Basic Bee fence gate.

Confirmed names for these figures include Queen Spirit Bear (Spirit Bear), King Stick Bug (Stick Bug), and King Bumble Bee (Bumble Bee).

After completing the Onett's Yard Art quest, Baby Bee, wearing a Santa hat, appears in between Black Bear and Mother Bear in the cradle. The art is also noted as being a reference to the birth of Jesus, celebrated during Christmas.

## Mechanics and Drops

### Item Drops
When activated, the Lid Art guarantees the following items:
*   5 Pineapples
*   5 Strawberries
*   5 Blueberries
*   5 Sunflower Seeds
*   1 Gingerbread Bear

Additionally, 2 random items are drawn from the following pool (probabilities listed):
*   Ticket (~49.38%)
*   Moon Charm (~29.63%)
*   Glitter (~9.88%)
*   Glue (~9.88%)
*   Star Jelly (~0.99%)
*   Toy Horn (~0.1%)
*   Paper Angel (~0.1%)
*   Box-O-Frogs (~0.04%)
*   Festive Bean (~0.01%)

### Temporary Bees
The Lid Art guarantees 3 temporary bees:
*   Bumble Bee (Level 20)
*   Baby Bee (Level 1)

One additional bee is drawn from the following pool (probabilities listed):
*   Lion Bee (Level 8, ~25%)
*   Music Bee (Level 8, ~25%)
*   Cobalt Bee (Level 8, ~18.75%)
*   Crimson Bee (Level 8, ~18.75%)
*   Festive Bee (Level 8, ~6.25%)
*   Tabby Bee (Level 8, ~6.25%)

**Notes on Summoned Bees:**
Bees summoned by Onett's Lid Art are able to attack the mobs that the player's actual bees are attacking, but they cannot convert at the hive.

### Guiding Star Activation
A passive ability known as a Guiding Star is summoned when the art is used. It appears on one of the five fields where the player has collected the least pollen. This decoration is one of only four machines (along with Honeyday Candles, Gummy Beacon, and Honey Wreath) that grant temporary bees.

## Trivia
*   Onett's Lid Art is the only Beesmas decoration to activate a passive ability (the Guiding Star).
*   The longest cooldown for Beesmas decorations is 8 hours; Onett's Lid Art shares this with the Gummy Beacon and the Naughty List.
*   This, along with the Gummy Beacon, was one of the only ways to have multiple Event bees of the same kind (though they are temporary).
*   Having a Cobalt Bee from the Lid Art alongside a Crimson Bee in your hive activates both a red and blue pulse.
*   Onett's Lid Art is one of two methods to summon a Guiding Star without using a Supreme or Diamond Star Amulet with the respective passive.
