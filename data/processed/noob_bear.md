Noob Bear is the bear who operates the Noob Shop. Like all other Shop Bears, his presence is not required to purchase items from the shop, and players cannot interact with him directly.

## Trivia

*   He is the first Shop Bear to be accessible in the game.
*   Like other bears, Noob Bear can occasionally suffer from glitches such as falling over or falling apart. This issue has been mostly patched and now occurs very rarely.
*   Noob Bear, Pro Bear, Top Bear, and Shadow Bear are among the only bears that do not serve a significant mechanical purpose in the game.
*   Noob Bear utilizes the Cartoony Animation Package.
*   He is represented by one of the twelve Cub Buddy skins: the Noob Cub. This skin was obtainable by purchasing the Cub Buddy Launch Pack.
