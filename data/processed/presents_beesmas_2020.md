# Presents in Beesmas 2020

This content relates to the Presents event held during Beesmas 2020. These presents functioned similarly to those in Beesmas 2018, but with additional NPCs available to receive them. Presents were the exclusive way to obtain [Ornaments] from certain NPCs and were also required for specific quests given by [Bee Bear].

## Acquisition Methods

Presents could be obtained through several means:

*   **Quests:** Multiple quests from Bee Bear awarded a total of 10 Presents, along with other rewards.
*   **Catalog Purchase:** Bee Bear’s Catalog sold 1 Present for 250 [Snowflakes] and 10 [Gingerbread Bears].
*   **Hive Claiming:** Onett gave 1 Present (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event period.
*   **Donation Chance:** Donating Strawberries to the Galentine Shrine had a chance to give 1 Present, along with other rewards.
