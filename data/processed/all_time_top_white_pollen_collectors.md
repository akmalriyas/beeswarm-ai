The All-Time Top White Pollen Collectors leaderboard is one of the game's leaderboards, tracking the total amount of white pollen players have collected over time. It displays the top 10 positions at a time, with players able to scroll down to view up to the 100th place. Only legitimate players are maintained on the leaderboards; hackers and exploiters will be removed.

**Mechanics:**
When a player steps on the white pad associated with this leaderboard, a message appears displaying the total amount of white pollen they have collected (e.g., "You've collected 'x' White Pollen").

## Location

This feature is located inside the 35 Bee zone, situated on the left wall of the Petal Shop, near the entrance.
