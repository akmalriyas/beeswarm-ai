# Dispensers

A **Dispenser** is a machine designed to dispense various items or boosts to the player, typically under a cooldown period.

To use a dispenser, players must stand on the designated pad in front of it. While some dispensers have no requirements, others require specific conditions for access. These requirements can be direct (such as possessing tickets) or indirect (such as meeting badge requirements or defeating certain mobs).

For example:
*   The **Glue Dispenser** requires a specific player badge to access.
*   Access to the Gummy Bear's Lair, which contains dispensers, requires the player to have the Goo Hotshot badge or higher.
*   The **Coconut Dispenser** requires players to defeat the Coconut Crab before gaining access, similar to other content in the Coconut Cave.
*   The **Robo Pass Dispenser** requires the player to possess 30 bees.

## Dispenser Types

There are three main categories of dispensers: Club Dispensers, Free Dispensers, and Ticket Dispensers.

### Club Dispensers
These dispensers require the player to be a member of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club to use them. They include:
*   [Blueberry Dispenser]
*   [Honey Dispenser]
*   [Strawberry Dispenser]
*   [Treat Dispenser]

### Free Dispensers
Players can use these dispensers without joining the Bee Swarm Simulator Club or spending tickets. They are:
*   [Coconut Dispenser]
*   [Free Ant Pass Dispenser]
*   [Free Robo Pass Dispenser]
*   [Free Royal Jelly Dispenser]
*   [Glue Dispenser]

### Ticket Dispensers
These dispensers require the player to spend tickets to use them. They are:
*   [Ant Pass Dispenser]
*   [Robo Pass Dispenser]
*   [Royal Jelly Dispenser]

## Trivia

*   Dispensers are the most varied machine type in the game, with 12 different variants.
*   Several dispensers are required for specific quests:
    *   "Honey Bee's Honey Wreath?" (from Honey Bee) requires the **Honey Dispenser**.
    *   "BBM's Naughty List" (from Bubble Bee Man) requires the **Royal Jelly Ticket Dispenser**.
    *   "The Power of Information" (from Science Bear) requires the **Strawberry Dispenser**, **Blueberry Dispenser**, and **Glue Dispenser**.
*   Three dispensers grant treats: the **Treat Dispenser**, the Blueberry Dispenser, and the Strawberry Dispenser.
*   Six specific dispensers do not grant Haste ability tokens: the Glue Dispenser, Free Ant Pass Dispenser, Ant Pass Dispenser, Free Robo Pass Dispenser, Robo Pass Dispenser, and Coconut Dispenser.
*   The Glue Dispenser, Free Robo Pass Dispenser, and Free Royal Jelly Dispenser share the longest cooldown in the game (22 hours).
*   Four dispensers—Free Ant Pass Dispenser, Ant Pass Dispenser, Robo Pass Dispenser, and Free Robo Pass Dispenser—do not have visible faucets; instead, they feature a small compartment resembling a vending machine.
*   The Glue Dispenser and Coconut Dispenser are the only dispensers that provide increased items when their corresponding badge is upgraded.
*   The Coconut Dispenser is the only dispenser capable of granting a field boost.
