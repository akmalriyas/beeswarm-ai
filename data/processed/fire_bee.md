# Fire Bee

**Fire Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Epic bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic "Bees/Epic"). It was accidentally left in the trunk of a car during a summer heatwave for over three days, resulting in its unique origin.

*   **Rarity:** Epic
*   **Color:** Red
*   **Energy:** 25
*   **Speed:** 11.2
*   **Attack:** 4

Fire Bee's favorite treat is [Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple "Pineapple"). It thrives in the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field") and [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field"), but dislikes the [Pine Tree Forest](/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest "Pine Tree Forest").

## Abilities and Mechanics

### Red Bomb+ (Ability Token)
Fire Bee utilizes the **Red Bomb+** ability token to collect 10 pollen from 29 surrounding red [Flowers](/wiki/Flowers "Flowers"). This value increases by +10% per bee level, and combining this ability with other bombs can increase its overall power.

### Passive: Gathering Flames (Flame)
Fire Bee has a **35% chance** (50% if Gifted) to spawn a [Flame](/wiki/Flame "Flame") when gathering resources. These flames persist for 4 seconds.

*   **Damage:** Each second, the flame deals 10 damage (+25% of the bee's base attack) to nearby enemies.
*   **Pollen Collection:** The flame collects [5 Red / 3 White / 2 Blue] pollen from 9 flowers, with collection increasing by +10% per bee level.
*   **Flame Heat Buff:** Standing near a flame grants Flame Heat, which increases both **Red Pollen** (between x1.2 and x2) and **Bee Attack** (between +5% to +25%).

## Trivia

*   The [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask "Fire Mask"), available in the [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ"), features a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Fire Bee.
*   Fire Bee is unique as it can be summoned by [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee") using its [Inferno](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inferno "Ability Tokens") ability, making it the only bee summonable via ability tokens. It can also be summoned using [Honeyday Candles](/wiki/Honeyday_Candles "Honeyday Candles").
*   Fire Bee serves as the red counterpart to the [Bubble Bee](/wiki/Bubble_Bee "Bubble Bee").
*   Its jelly was included in the Cozy Bundle and is listed in the [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog").
