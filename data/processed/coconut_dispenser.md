# Coconut Dispenser

## Overview

The **Coconut Dispenser** is a dispenser located within the Coconut Cave. It allows players to obtain coconuts and receive field boosts based on their current Badge Tier.

### Usage & Requirements

*   **Usage:** Obtain Coconuts.
*   **Requirement(s):** Access to the Coconut Cave.
*   **Cooldown:** 4 hours.
*   **Location:** Inside the Coconut Cave, located on the right side. Players must defeat the Coconut Crab to gain access to this area.

### Mechanics

When used, the dispenser grants a specific number of coconuts and provides a static x1 **Coconut Field Boost**. The field boost multiplier remains constant regardless of the player's Badge Tier.

| Badge Tier | Coconuts Granted |
| :--- | :---: |
| Junior | 3 |
| Cadet | 5 |
| Hotshot | 7 |
| Ace | 10 |
| Master | 12 |
| Grandmaster | 15 |

### Secret Tunnel

A secret entrance exists by jumping on top of the dispenser and into a small portion of the wall. Inside this tunnel, players can find three tokens each, which grant:
*   5 Tickets
*   5 Jelly Beans
*   1 Enzyme

## Trivia

*   This dispenser, along with the Free Ant Pass Dispenser, Ant Pass Dispenser, Glue Dispenser, Free Robo Pass Dispenser, and Robo Pass Dispenser, is one of a select group that does not grant Ability Tokens (Haste).
*   These same dispensers (Free Ant Pass Dispenser, Ant Pass Dispenser, Free Robo Pass Dispenser, and Robo Pass Dispenser) are the only ones that provide items with a capacity limit.
*   The Coconut Dispenser is unique in granting a Field Boost. This is the second mechanic to guarantee a specific field boost, with Glitter being the first.
*   It is the only dispenser that displays the message: "Earn coconut badges to receive more coconuts!" after use.
*   This dispenser and the Glue Dispenser are the only dispensers that provide items based on the rank of a badge.
