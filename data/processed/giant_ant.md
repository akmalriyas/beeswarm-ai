# Giant Ant

[![Giant Ant](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8f/Giant_Ant.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20191208201615)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8f/Giant_Ant.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201615 "Giant Ant")

A **Giant Ant** is a formidable mob belonging to the family of [ants](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Ants). They are exclusively found within the [Ant Challenge](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Ant_Challenge) area, specifically past the [Ant Gate](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Ant_Gate), and in the [Ant Field](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Ant_Field).

## Description

Giant Ants are enormous versions of the normal ant. They relentlessly pursue the player and are one of the most threatening mobs in the Ant Challenge. While they possess a relatively slow movement speed, this is compensated for by their massive health pool—more than any other type of ant—and high attack power.

A Giant Ant's level increases based on the current wave of the Ant Challenge, ranging from Level 1 up to Level 16. They appear every fifth wave and are considered one of the final mobs encountered in this challenge.

## Strategies

*   **Tanking and Damage:** Due to their high health, Giant Ants can effectively 'tank' hits from [bees](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Bees), allowing smaller, faster ants to attack the player with a lower chance of being defeated. Because of their size, they are difficult for the player to avoid. To defeat them, players should utilize bees with high attack power, such as [Lion Bee](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Lion_Bee).
*   **Utility:** The [Stinger](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Stinger) can be used to deal extra damage against the Giant Ant.
*   **Wave Pattern:** They always appear every fifth wave on a predictable side of the map, allowing players time to prepare for their arrival.
*   **Specialized Bee Tactics:**
    *   [Vicious Bee's](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Vicious_Bee) [Impale](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Ability_Tokens?so=search#Impale) can deliver high single-target damage, helping to rapidly deplete their massive health pool.
    *   [Windy Bee's](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Windy_Bee) [Tornado](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Ability_Tokens?so=search#Tornado) can chip away at their health and also clear out other ants hiding behind them.
    *   [Digital Bee](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Digital_Bee) can stun Giant Ants, making them vulnerable to increased damage via [Mind Hack](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mind_Hack).

## Trivia

*   Giant Ants are unique in that they follow a spawn pattern of appearing every five waves (5th, 10th, 15th, etc.). Additionally, two Giant Ants will spawn instead of one on the 40th wave.
*   They are the only ant type to possess health figures reaching into the thousands.
