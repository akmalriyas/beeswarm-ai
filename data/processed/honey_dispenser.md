# Honey Dispenser

![Honey Dispenser](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/91/Honey_Dispenser.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20201005134940 "Honey Dispenser")

The Honey Dispenser is a dispenser that grants both **Honey** and Ability Tokens (Haste) upon use. It has a 1-hour cooldown and requires the player to be a member of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club to operate.

## Information

### Usage
Players must give Honey into the dispenser in proportion to the number of bees they possess in their hive.

### Requirements
*   Membership in the Bee Swarm Simulator Club group.

### Cooldown
1 hour

### Location
The dispenser is located near the Ant Gate, in front of the Lava Obby obstacle course.

## Mechanics and Rewards

The Honey Dispenser grants x5 Haste tokens every time it is used. The amount of Honey granted varies based on the number of bees (`cnt`) in the player's hive.

**Historical Note:**
Initially, the dispenser gave 5x Haste and 500 Honey. Following the 2018-04-27 update, this changed to x5 Haste and 100 Honey per bee in the hive. The amount of Honey has been adjusted since that time.

**Honey Calculation Formula:**
The amount of honey received is calculated using the following formula:
$$\text{Honey} = \left\lfloor {cnt}^{1.7}+0.5\right\rfloor \times 100$$
*If the result is less than 500, the player receives 500 Honey.*

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Quest Requirement:** The Honey Dispenser is one of four specific club dispensers (along with the Treat, Strawberry, and Blueberry Dispensers) that must be used to complete certain quests.
    *   "Honey Bee's Honey Wreath?" (2020/2021): Required use.
    *   "The Power of Information": Requires use alongside the other three dispensers.
*   **Gummy Invasion:** During the Gummy Invasion event, the dispenser granted an additional Gumdrops per use.
*   **Usage Notification:** When using this dispenser, there is no notification in the chat log regarding the Honey received.
*   **Related Locations:** The Lava Obby obstacle course, found near the dispenser, leads to key areas such as the Royal Jelly token at the back of the Noob Shop and access to the Demon Mask shop.
