# Red Brick Field

[![Red Brick Field](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d2/Red_Brick_Field.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/275?cb=20250724191203)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d2/Red_Brick_Field.png/revision/latest?cb=20250724191203)

## Field Statistics
*   **Color:** Red
*   **Bee Prerequisite:** 10 bees
*   **Dimensions:** 19 × 19 flowers
*   **Total Flower Count:** 361
*   **Bee Preferences:** None (Likes/Dislikes)

The **Red Brick Field** is one of the four fields located within the Retro Swarm Challenge. This 19x19 field contains 361 flowers, which are a mix of white and red in single, double, and triple sizes. The field produces brick tokens while collecting pollen. There are no specific bee preferences towards or against this location.

Since planters cannot be used in the Retro Swarm Challenge, there is no confirmed nectar type for this field.

## Mechanics and Boosts
### Obtaining Pollen Boosts
Using [Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") in the Red Brick Field grants a 100% Red Brick Field pollen boost for 15 minutes.

### Trivia
*   The Red Brick Field, along with three other fields in the Retro Swarm Challenge, is one of the only fields without an associated field stamp.
*   Despite lacking its own unique stamp, the field utilizes the same boost icon as the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field").
