# Ant

[ ![Ant-0](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b1/Ant-0.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20191208201559) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b1/Ant-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201559 "Ant-0")

## Overview
An Ant is one of five mobs belonging to the Ants family, exclusively found within the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge). This mob is encountered past the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate).

As the first ant challenge mob players encounter, it appears on the very first wave. Ants are highly generic and common, moving in a fixed straight line. They are generally considered the easiest of the five ant types to deal with due to their basic nature and often being easily avoidable.

**Note:** Do not confuse this mob with [Ants](/wiki/Ants), which refers to the entire family of mobs that includes Ants and other ant variations.

## Combat Strategies
Ants move in predetermined, slow straight lines, making them relatively easy to dodge. However, they often appear in groups and can overwhelm players if the bees are focused on rapid pollen collection needed for advancing waves.

**Effective Tactics:**
*   **Targeting:** Positioning your bees near the ant allows them to quickly target and deal damage efficiently.
*   **Crowd Control (CC):** High-level bees with crowd control abilities are highly effective against large groups of Ants.
    *   [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee) and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee) can use their **Impale** or **Tornado** abilities to manage swarms.
    *   [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee) can stun multiple ants simultaneously using **Mind Hack**, allowing other bees to deal concentrated damage.

## Trivia
*   The [Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Diamond Ant Amulets](/wiki/Ant_Amulet) represent the Ant mob.
*   This is unique as it is the only mob that shares its name with a broader group of mobs (the Ants family).
*   It possesses the same health progression per level as [Fire Ants](/wiki/Fire_Ant).
*   The ant was Onett's initial design for the game. It was originally intended to be a regular spawning mob, but Onett replaced it with [Ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug) because he wanted the ants to function more "ant-like" as a cohesive team.
