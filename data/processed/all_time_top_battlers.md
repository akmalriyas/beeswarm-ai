## Overview and Mechanics

The **Global Top Battlers** leaderboard (also known as the Battle Points Leaderboard) is one of the game's many leaderboards. It tracks the total number of battle points players have collected over time. The board displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. Only legitimate players are maintained on the leaderboard; hackers and exploiters will be removed.

Battle points are earned by defeating specific mobs within the game. To check your current score, step onto the white pad in front of the leaderboard, which will display a message stating: "You have x Battle Points," where 'x' is your total score.

## Location

The leaderboard is situated on the yellow platform. It can be found in front of the Bamboo Field and behind the Blue Flower Field, next to the Panda Bear.

## Trivia

*   This leaderboard is one of two leaderboards present since the game's release; the other is the Global Top Honeymakers Leaderboard.
*   Sugarsmacks12 held the number 1 spot in the global top battlers for three years before being dethroned, making it the longest-sustaining number one position across all game leaderboards.
*   The Bubble Bee Man used to be located behind this leaderboard but was later moved to the 30 Bee Zone during the Beesmas 2019 update.
