# Paperclip

A **Paperclip** is a Level 7 Beequip with an equip limit of two. It has no color requirement and can only be equipped to Rare or Epic Bees.

"A helpful trinket that keeps stacks of paper together without staples, tape, or glue."

## Acquisition Methods

Paperclips can be obtained through several methods:
*   As a very rare drop from the Petal Planter.
*   Purchasing it from Dapper Bear's Shop.
*   As a drop from the Blue Clay Planter.
