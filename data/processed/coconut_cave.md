# Coconut Cave

The **Coconut Cave** is a cave located next to the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field").

## Access Mechanics
To access the interior of the cave, players must first defeat the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab") when it respawns outside. If a player attempts to enter without defeating the Coconut Crab (and is not already in the field), they will be teleported back to their spawn point. An invisible barrier prevents entry until the Coconut Crab has been defeated, preventing players from bypassing this requirement and causing lag.

The interior of the cave contains several features:
*   The [Fastest Crab Slayers Leaderboard](/wiki/Fastest_Crab_Slayers_Leaderboard "Fastest Crab Slayers Leaderboard")
*   A [Coconut Dispenser](/wiki/Coconut_Dispenser "Coconut Dispenser")
*   A shop where players can purchase various items.

## Trivia
*   If a player is inside the Coconut Cave when the Coconut Crab respawns, they will be teleported out (similar to bypassing a gate without meeting requirements). This teleportation area includes the cave roof. This can be avoided by entering the hole above the Coconut Dispenser.
*   The hole above the Coconut Dispenser leads to a tunnel containing 4 tokens: one [Enzyme](/wiki/Enzymes "Enzymes"), 5 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket"), 5 [Jelly Beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans "Jelly Beans"), and a [Robo Pass](/wiki/Robo_Pass "Robo Pass").
*   This is the only shop that is guarded by a [Mob](/wiki/Mob "Mob").
*   The shops in this cave—[Robo Bear’s Shop](/wiki/Robo_Bear%27s_Shop "Robo Bear's Shop"), [Ticket Tent](/wiki/Ticket_Tent "Ticket Tent"), and [Dapper Bear’s Shop](/wiki/Dapper_Bear%E2%80%99s_Shop "Dapper Bear’s Shop")—are the only shops that do not play the vendor soundtrack.
*   Players can view the cave interior from outside using the [Porcelain Dipper](/wiki/Porcelain_Dipper "Porcelain Dipper") or the [Hydroponic Planter](/wiki/Hydroponic_Planter "Hydroponic Planter").
