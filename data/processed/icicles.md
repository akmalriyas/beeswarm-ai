# Icicles

**Icicles** are a Level 10 Beequip with a blue color requirement and an equip limit of two. They can only be equipped to bees that possess the Blue Bomb ability token.

> "Incredibly cold and incredibly sharp."

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Sources
*   As a drop from the Rogue Vicious Bee during Beesmas (higher level bees have a higher chance of dropping Icicles).
*   As a drop from the Intimidating Gift Box.
*   As a drop from a Level 15 Snowbear.

### Outdated/Historical Sources
*   Buying the Beequip Bundle in Bee Bear’s Catalog.
*   Drops during Beesmas 2021 (Level 15 Snowbear).
*   Reward for completing Gummy Bear's Beesmas 2021 quest.
*   Purchasing the Jolly Jumbo Pack in the Robux Shop.
*   Drops during Beesmas 2022 and 2024 (Summer & Winter) from Level 15 Snowbears.

## Trivia
*   Icicles were initially made unobtainable after Beesmas 2020 ended, except for those dropped by Rogue Vicious Bee, which was an accidental oversight that was later corrected. This makes Icicles the first Beequip obtainable outside of Beesmas, followed by Poinsettia.
*   The maximum equip cap for this item was previously 3 but was changed to 2 in an unknown update; however, players who already had three equipped retained them.
