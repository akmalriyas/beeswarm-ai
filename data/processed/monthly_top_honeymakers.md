# Monthly Top Honeymakers Leaderboard

The **Monthly Top Honeymakers** leaderboard is one of 64 available leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator, accessible only through the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page.

This leaderboard tracks and displays the total amount of honey players have collected over a single month. It initially shows 10 positions, but users can scroll down to view results up to the 100th place. The leaderboards strictly feature legitimate players; accounts found using hacks or exploits will be removed.

**Reset Schedule:**
The leaderboard resets precisely on the 1st day of every month at 12:00 AM CST.

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   This is the only leaderboard in the game that resets monthly, distinguishing it from daily leaderboards (which reset every day) and all-time leaderboards (which never reset).
