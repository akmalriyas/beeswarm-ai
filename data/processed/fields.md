# Fields

Fields, also referred to as "patches" or "[Pine Tree Forest](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Pine_Tree_Forest)", are square or rectangular areas scattered across the map where players collect pollen. This pollen can then be converted into honey at a player's hive or through other methods.

There are 23 different fields in the game, including those found within the Ant Challenge, Hive Hub, and Retro Swarm Challenge. Each field is composed of flowers that come in three colors: blue, red, and white. The specific flower types present in any given field vary upon server generation.

### Field Mechanics
The density of flowers within a square directly impacts its yield; more flowers result in greater pollen output and faster regrowth. Several elements can enhance or modify fields:

*   **Bee Equipment:** Certain bees or equipped items can pollinate the flowers, increasing their count. This includes the Fuzzy Bee, or any bee wearing a Poinsettia, Rose Headband, or Lei.
*   **Passive Abilities:** The Guiding Star passive ability will automatically pollinate flowers when hovering over a field. Diamond Drain also specifically pollinates blue flowers.
*   **Tokens:** Fields can occasionally spawn Honey and Treats in the form of tokens.
