# Naughty List

The **Naughty List** is a recurring Beesmas machine unlocked after completing Bubble Bee Man's quest. As of now, this quest is unavailable, preventing the use of the machine.

### Location and Activation
The machine is located on top of the Bear Gate, situated between Night Memory Match and Bubble Bee Man. When activated, it triggers several server-wide events: a voiceline (presumably from Bubble Bee Man) saying "Naughty Naughty Naughty" plays, and a lump of coal falls onto every player in the server.

### Mechanics
The falling coal deals lethal damage to players if they do not move out of the way. This mechanism is similar to Coconut Crab's Coconut Attack.

If a player is killed by the falling coal:
*   They receive a Lump Of Coal item.
*   They gain the Beesmas Repentance buff for 2 hours.

Players can use the Naughty List every 8 hours. If a player attempts to activate it without completing Bubble Bee Man's quest, a pop-up message will appear stating: "There aren't any names on the Naughty List. That can't be right..."

## Trivia and Advanced Mechanics

*   **Voice Activation:** The Honey Wreath and Onett's Lid Art are the only Beesmas decorations that have voices upon activation, and the Naughty List is the only one to play an audio clip containing words.
*   **Mob Interaction:** If a player stands on a hostile mob while the coal is falling, the resulting impact can kill the player and deal 100 damage to the mob.
*   **List Names:** The names visible on the list include Aardvark Joe, Apple BEe, and Applebee's Cofounder 1.
*   **Cooldown Comparison:** The Naughty List is tied for having one of the longest cooldowns among Beesmas machines, alongside Onett's Lid Art and Gummy Beacon.
*   **Damage Capability:** It is unique among these machines in that it can damage both players and hostile mobs.
*   **Buff Stacking:** The Beesmas Repentance Buff does not stack, but its duration does stack if the player dies multiple times within the buff window.
*   **Cooldown Discrepancy:** While dialogue suggests a 22-hour cooldown after quest completion, the actual usable cooldown is 8 hours.
*   **Simultaneous Use:** If two or more players activate the Naughty List at the same time, they may receive multiple Lumps of Coal but will only die once.
