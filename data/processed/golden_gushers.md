# Golden Gushers

The **Golden Gushers** are a 3rd tier sprinkler available for purchase in the Badge Bearer's Guild. They consist of three individual sprinklers designed with enormous range.

## Acquisition and Cost
*   **Requirement:** Obtain 10 different Ace Badges.
*   **Cost:** 333,333,333 Honey (~333.3 million).

## Sprinkler Statistics
| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Number of Units** | 3 | Comprised of three individual sprinklers. |
| **Radius** | 8 tiles | The range of the sprinkler's coverage. |
| **Power** | 8 | The intensity of the watering effect. |
| **Rate** | 4.5 seconds | Time between water cycles. |
| **Setup Time** | 5 seconds | Time required to place the unit in a field. |
