# Balloons

**Balloons** are items used by players to collect [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") and transport it back to the [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") for conversion into [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"). There are six types of balloons: pink, red, white, black, blue, and gold.

*   **Pink**, **red**, **white**, and **black** balloons are summoned using standard [items](/wiki/Items "Items").
*   **Blue** and **gold** balloons are summoned via the abilities of the [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee").

Balloons return to the hive when they reach full capacity or hit their time limit. Both capacity and time limits vary by balloon type.

## In Fields
Balloons float around the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields"), similar to [Clouds](/wiki/Clouds "Clouds"). They cast a 5-tile radius shadow on flowers underneath them, absorbing pollen from those flowers. If multiple balloons cover the same flower, the pollen is absorbed by the balloon containing the lowest amount of pollen. Balloons cannot absorb pollen collected by other players.

The pollen within balloons can be increased using Buoyant Bee's [Inflate Balloons](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inflate_Balloons "Ability Tokens") ability, though this action has a maximum limit of 10 uses per balloon.

Standing underneath any player's balloon grants the **Balloon Aura** buff. Each balloon within the player's radius provides one stack of Balloon Aura, capping at x10. Each stack increases pollen yield by 1.02 and boosts honey from tokens by +2%, up to a maximum of x1.2 pollen and +20% honey from tokens.

Other mechanics can also convert pollen in the player's balloon into honey tokens:
*   Gifted [Frogs](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Summon_Frog "Ability Tokens") summoned by [Gifted Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee").
*   Waves from the [Tide Popper](/wiki/Tide_Popper "Tide Popper").
*   [Tornado](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Tornado "Ability Tokens") ability from the [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee").

If a balloon is returning to the hive, it can still be converted and will drop its tokens around its last location on the field. When a [surprise party](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Surprise_Party "Ability Tokens") activates, all balloons (regardless of color) generate random ability tokens for a short duration. These tokens include white and blue boost tokens, buzz and blue bombs, marks, Haste, Token Link, and Melody tokens. The type of boost tokens spawned appears to be based on the primary color of the field.

## Hive Balloon
When a balloon reaches the player's hive, it becomes a "Hive Balloon" and floats in place until conversion begins. Once conversion starts, the player's [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") will first convert any items in their [bag](/wiki/Items#Bags "Items") before processing the Hive Balloon.

A fully converted Hive Balloon grants **Balloon Blessing**, which provides increased [Capacity](/wiki/System_Page#Capacity "System Page") and [Honey at Hive](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_At_Hive "System Page"). The buffs increase as more pollen is stored in the balloon. It requires 10 trillion pollen to reach the maximum stack (x100) of Balloon Blessing, granting x4 Capacity and x2.5 Honey at Hive. When granted, a notification appears: "🎈The hive balloon granted you x{stacks} of Balloon Blessing!".

If the blessing provided by the Hive Balloon is lower than the player's current blessing, converting it will instead refresh the Balloon Blessing timer, displaying the message: "🎈The hive balloon refreshed your Balloon Blessing!".

Pollen in the Hive Balloon depletes over time based on its size relative to the player's capacity. The slowest depletion rate is 20% per hour. The fastest rate is 99.99% in 10 minutes (if it holds more than 100x the player's capacity). This reduction occurs gradually until the pollen reaches a sustainable level.

## Gold Balloon
Gold balloons possess the ability to buff [bubbles](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gathering_Bubbles "Passive Abilities") they pass over, transforming them into **gold bubbles**. These gold bubbles:
*   Collect 1.5x more pollen.
*   Contribute 50% more time to [Bubble Bloat](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Passives "Buffs & Debuffs").
*   Have a 25% chance to create a honey token equal to the amount of pollen collected by the bubble.

## Trivia
*   If a balloon of any color is used and the player leaves the game, the balloon will disappear.
*   Balloons are unique in that they are non-limited/event items not found in [crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") recipes, nor can they be donated.
*   Regardless of its initial color, any balloon arriving at the hive will always turn blue.
*   Currently, balloons are only obtainable through quests and mobs.
*   **Glitching Balloon Blessing:** It is possible to exploit a glitch during conversion by waiting for the balloon to disappear after starting the process but leaving the hive before conversion finishes. This can result in the game returning the balloon with its original Balloon Blessing while simultaneously granting the buff, effectively duplicating it.
*   Multiple "🎈The hive balloon refreshed your Balloon Blessing!" messages can be received during a single hive conversion if new balloons arrive at the correct time.
