# Editing Guidelines

Before making any edits, please read these guidelines to help prevent edit wars and maintain a positive community environment. For specific formatting rules and content standards, always refer to the [Manual of Style](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Wiki:Manual_of_Style).

## Community Conduct & Etiquette

*   **Good Faith Assumption:** Always assume that other editors are contributing in good faith. If you encounter an error or formatting issue, unless it is blatantly malicious, assume the user meant well with their edit.
*   **Vandalism and Trolling:** Vandalism and troll edits are strictly prohibited and will not be tolerated.
*   **Respecting Profiles:** Do not edit another user's profile without explicit permission. Unauthorized changes can be considered vandalism.
*   **Attribution/Signatures:** Do not sign main pages with your personal name. Your username is automatically recorded in the edit history, and manual signatures do not contribute positively to the page.

## Conflict Resolution & Editing Practices

### Edit Wars
If you find yourself reverting an edit only to have it reverted again by another user:
1.  **Check History:** Review the page history thoroughly. There is likely a valid reason why your original edit was reverted.
2.  **Direct Communication:** If you and another editor cannot reach an agreement, contact them directly via their message wall.
3.  **Staff Intervention:** If direct communication fails, contact a staff member for mediation. Continuing the conflict on the article will result in disciplinary action.

### Editing Workflow
*   **Edit Farming:** Avoid editing the same page multiple times in rapid succession. Use the preview function to review your changes before saving. If you cannot achieve the desired result within two edits, stop and request assistance. Repeated successive edits may be interpreted as edit farming.
*   **Summary Notes:** When making an edit, it is best practice to provide a short summary of what you changed. This helps prevent miscommunication among editors.
*   **New Pages:** Unless there has been a recent update or gap in coverage, do not create new pages. If you cannot locate existing information, assume the fault lies with your search unless proven otherwise. You may post on the [Discussions](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/f) if necessary to request content creation.

### Content Integrity
*   **Generative AI:** The use of Generative AI is not permitted for creating article content. This policy remains in place due to the high volume of existing wiki information and the need for human oversight.
*   **Unnecessary Pages:** If you locate a page that should not exist (e.g., it is unrelated, a duplicate, or fanon), please inform a [staff](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Wiki:Staff) member, or edit the page and place the special code `{{Delete}}` at the top of the content block.
