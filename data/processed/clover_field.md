## Field Mechanics
The Clover Field is one of the five starting fields available in the game. It is protected by a Ladybug and a Rhino Beetle.

### Bee Preferences
**Likes:** Basic Bee, Brave Bee, Looker Bee, Stubborn Bee, Music Bee, Cobalt Bee, Crimson Bee, Puppy Bee, Tabby Bee.
**Dislikes:** Lion Bee, Photon Bee.

### Item Drops
When collecting pollen in this field, players can spawn various tokens and items, including: Treat, red boost (Ability Token), blue boost, Ticket, Magic Bean, Royal Jelly, gold or diamond Egg, and Star Jelly.

### Field Events
*   A Puffshroom may naturally spawn between the 15th minute (xx:15) and 45th minute (xx:45) of the hour.
*   Level 1-3 Rogue Vicious Bees may spawn here.
*   An active Wild Windy Bee may move to this field.
*   During a Stick Bug Challenge, if a Stick Bug is in Sunflower Field or Bamboo Field, he will move to this field.

## Obtaining Boosts
Players can enhance pollen collection using several methods:

*   **Field Booster:** Using the Field Booster grants a Clover Field Field Boost (+200% Clover Field pollen for 15 minutes).
*   **Glitter:** Applying Glitter adds +100% Clover Field pollen to the current boost (up to maximum capacity) for 15 minutes.
*   **Dice:** Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice can add +100% to +300% Clover Field pollen for 15 minutes, provided the boost is not maxed out.
*   **Field Winds:** Clover Field Winds are obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes. They provide a base of +20% instant Clover Field conversion and grant +5% instant Clover Field conversion and +20% Clover Field pollen per stack. All Field Winds can stack up to 13 times.
*   **Amulets:**
    *   The King Beetle Amulet: Boosts the field by +1% to +100% if it has a Clover Field Boost active.
    *   The Shell Amulet: Boosts the field by +5% to +30%, depending on its rarity, if a Clover Field Boost is active.

### Outdated Methods (Historical)
Historically, boosts could be obtained through NPC gifts or expired codes:
*   **NPC Gifts:** In 2020, Brown Bear gave Clover Field Boost x3 and a star jelly. Onett gave Clover Field Boost x1 and other items. (Brown Bear also gave the boost in 2021).
*   **Market Purchases:** Purchasing a Clover Field Market Boost from the Boost Market provided 1.25x Clover Field Pollen, x1.5 Clover Field Capacity, x1.25 Bee Gather Pollen, and +15% Loot Luck.
*   **Expired Codes:** Various codes (e.g., 4MilMembers, JellyHill, Reboots) historically granted significant boosts of Clover Field Boosts and/or Clover Field Winds.

## Trivia
*   **Field Booster History:** The Clover Field booster has been decreased three times. It was reduced from x5 to x4 in the 2018-07-11 update, then to x3 in the 2019-04-05 update, and finally to x2 in the 2019-09-28 update.
*   **Size History:** Previously, before the 2018-07-11 update, Clover Field had 39 rows (1131 flowers) and was the largest field in the game. It is no longer the largest; it currently has only five fewer flowers than the Pineapple Patch.
*   **Sparkles:** The Bubble Wand on top of the Blue HQ occasionally blows sparkles onto this field, along with Mushroom Field, Blue Flower Field, and Dandelion Field.
*   **Location Changes:** The Honeystorm Summoner used to occupy the space where the Wealth Clock is currently located. When the Wealth Clock was added, the Honeystorm Pad was moved next to the bush in Clover Field where the Rhino Beetle spawns.
*   **Proximity:** The King Beetle Lair is situated directly beneath this field.
*   **Defense:** This is the first field a player encounters that is defended by two mobs (Ladybug and Rhino Beetle).
*   **Lair Visibility:** When a Rogue Vicious Bee spawns here, its spike can be seen in King Beetle's Lair, which remains activatable. If a sprout is present in Clover Field, its stem and light are also visible in the Lair.
*   **Jelly Chance:** Clover Field has a higher chance than any other field of dropping royal jelly when a sprout pops, and it also has a low chance of spawning a star jelly.
*   **Pollen Collection Mechanic:** Players with the Spark Staff can collect pollen from Clover Field by being in King Beetle's Lair and jumping.
*   **Uniqueness:** This is the only field that gives Eggs. It is also the only starting field with roughly equal amounts of Single and Double flowers; the other four starter fields are predominantly 90% Single or 90% Double.
