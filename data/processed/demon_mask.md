# Demon Mask

![Demon Mask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/96/Demon_Mask.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20210919013026)

## Description
"Embrace hate to take on the form of a Demon Bee. Become both extremely unpleasant and powerful."

The **Demon Mask** is the red counterpart of the Diamond Mask. It can be acquired by navigating the Lava Obby (located behind the Honey Dispenser or in the top passage of the Noob Shop) and following the course to the far back wall.

## Acquisition & Cost
### Standard Crafting Ingredients
*   5,000,000,000 Honey
*   500 Stingers
*   250 Red Extracts
*   150 Enzymes
*   100 Glue
*   1 Invigorating Vial

### Alternative/Raw Ingredient Costs (Trivia)
The mask can also be crafted using raw materials:
*   5B Honey
*   500 Stingers OR 5,000 Tickets
*   12,500 Strawberries
*   7,500 Pineapples
*   5,000 Gumdrops OR 1,667 Tickets
*   5,000 Royal Jellies OR 5B Honey

## Bonus Stats & Abilities
The Demon Mask provides the following stat bonuses:

**General Bonuses:**
*   +25% Pollen
*   x1.5 Capacity
*   x1.5 Red Field Capacity
*   +20% Instant Red Conversion
*   +50% Instant Flame Conversion
*   x1.25 Flame Pollen
*   +25% Bee Attack
*   +35% Defense
*   +20% Bee Ability Rate

**Passive Abilities:**
*   **Ignite** (Requires Fire Mask to use): Every 15th red ability token creates a sign of 5 spires of flames. These flames collect 4R/2W/1B from 9 surrounding flowers, increased by 2% per every Red bee and 4% for each Gifted Red Bee. The flame deals 15 damage per second if touched by a mob and lasts 4 seconds (no cooldown).
*   **X-Flame**: Every 25th battle ability token spawns 29 flames in an X shape around the player. Flames last 3s, collecting 4R/2W/1B from 9 surrounding flowers (+2% per Red bee, 4% if Gifted) and dealing 15 damage to nearby enemies each second. These flames also grant Flame Heat if the player stands near them, boosting red pollen and bee attack. (Cooldown: 20 seconds).

## Trivia & Lore
*   The Demon Mask is a Legendary Bee item, sharing its face decal with the Demon Bee.
*   When the X-Flame passive ability activates, it plays a distorted version of the music "bpatrol."
*   **X-Flame Triggers:** The X-Flame passive ability triggers upon collecting specific abilities, including Flame Fuel, Inferno, Melody, Focus, Target Practice, Token Link, Baby Love, Summon Frog, Haste, Impale, Tabby Love, Honey gift, Rage, Glitch, Mind Hack, and the ☺︎ emote.
*   **Historical Stats:** The Demon Mask's stats have changed significantly over time:
    *   **Original Stats:** +50% Bee Gather Pollen, +26% Instant Conversion, +35% Defense, +12% Bee Ability Rate, +125% Luck, +25% Bee Attack Power, +30% Bomb Pollen, x1.2 Red Pollen, and Passive: X-Flame.
    *   **2019-04-05 Update Stats:** x2 Red Pollen, +50% Bomb Pollen, +25% Bee Attack, +35% Defense, +12% Bee Ability Rate, and Passive: X-Flame.
