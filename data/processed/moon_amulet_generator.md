# Moon Amulet Generator

[![The Moon Amulet Generator.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6f/Moon_Amulet_Generator.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/214?cb=20180911122856)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6f/Moon_Amulet_Generator.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20180911122856) [](/wiki/File:Moon_Amulet_Generator.jpg)

The **Moon Amulet Generator** is a machine located between the Spider Field and Bamboo Field that allows players to generate or replace a Moon Amulet. Using the generator costs 100 Moon Charms.

### Accessing the Generator
Players can reach the Moon Amulet Generator in several ways:
*   By traversing the obstacle course connecting the Spider Field and Bamboo Field.
*   By dropping in from above using a gliding tool (e.g., cannon drop).
*   By falling off the ramp leading to the Lion Bee Gate onto the platform.

### Mechanics and Buffs
Every time the generator is used, the player's capacity buff increases. This buff starts at 25k and increments by 25k with each use, capping at a maximum of 250k capacity. To reach the highest potential for the Moon Amulet, a player must use the generator 25 times, which is when all other potential stat boosts have maxed out.

**In-Game Messages:**
*   When using the generator (and not at max buff): "🌙 The moon seems to grow brighter... 🌙"
*   When the maximum capacity buff (+250k) has been reached: "🌙 The moon seems happy! 🌙"

## Trivia
*   The generator glows during the night cycle, though this change is purely cosmetic and has no mechanical effect.
*   It is one of six available amulet generators; the other five are located in the Star Hall (Star Amulet Generators).
*   This is the only amulet generator that requires an item other than Honey to activate.
*   It is the only Generator that increases the Capacity Buff and has a defined maximum limit.
