# Top Sticker-Seekers

The **Top Sticker-Seekers** leaderboard tracks the total number of Sticker-Seeker Quests completed by players in Bee Swarm Simulator. It is one of 64 available leaderboards in the game and was introduced during the January 13, 2024 update.

## Mechanics and Location

This leaderboard displays the top 10 players at a time, though users can scroll down to view positions all the way to the 100th place.

**Location:**
The Sticker-Seekers leaderboard is located inside the **Hive Hub**, situated next to the **Hub Field Shop**.

## Trivia

*   The Top Sticker-Seekers leaderboard, along with the All-Time Tallest Sticker Stack, is one of only two leaderboards in the game that has a defined maximum score (currently 589).
*   This limit is based on the current number of stickers available in the game. With only 290 different stickers existing, reaching Rank 590 would require stacking 295 unique stickers, making it mathematically impossible to achieve under current conditions.
*   The Sticker-Seekers leaderboard is the only leaderboard located within the Hive Hub.
