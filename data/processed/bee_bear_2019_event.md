# Bee Bear / 2019 Event Archive

### Dialogue Archive

#### Beautify The Beesmas Tree
"Ho ho ho ho ho! It's beginning to look a lot like Beesmas! That's right, it's the most wonderful time of the year! And I'm back! Can you believe it's been a whole year since we met? It's my duty each year to flood the world with festive holiday cheer! It's a huge responsibility and a lot of work, but thankfully I don't work alone. My Festive Bees do most of the strenuous stuff, oh yes! But even they're not able to keep up with the global demand for Beesmas festivity. That's why this year I've brought along some extra special little helpers... The Cub Buddies! These adorable cubs are actually stuffed toys brought to life by pure Beesmas magic! And I've brought them here to help make this year's Beesmas truly special. If you work to spread Beesmas cheer, you may be able to bring a Cub Buddy of your own to life! Behind you I've set up a Beesmas Tree. But it's not complete yet - oh no. It needs Ornaments! Work with the bears around the map to make Ornaments to decorate the tree. Once you've put enough Ornaments on the tree, you'll be able to open presents! I've hidden presents just for you all over the mountain! But they can only be opened through the power of a well-dressed Beesmas Tree. Those presents are worth it though! Ho ho ho! I've put a Festive Bee Egg in one of them, can you guess which? And there's even the possibility of a Mythic Egg in one of the more difficult presents... Mythic Eggs always hatch into Mythic Bees - the newest, rarest, and most ostentatious bees yet! I won't spoil all the surprises! But here's something you can know for sure: If you help decorate the Beesmas Tree and complete 10 of my quests... I'll help you bring a Black Bear Cub Buddy of your own! That little munchkin will help keep the wonder of Beesmas alive in your heart all year long! It will also help you collect Tokens! And occasionally give you gifts! But we need to make haste, Beesmas day is approaching fast! To begin, here's what you'll need to do: Collect 1,000 Red Pollen and put 1 Ornament on the Beesmas Tree!"
