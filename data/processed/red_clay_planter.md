# Red Clay Planter

The **Red Clay Planter** is a reusable planter introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. It is available for purchase in the Red HQ.

> "Alone, grows in about 6 hours. Stores around 3 million Pollen. Grants bonus Red Extract and Waxes."

## Stats & Mechanics

| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reusable** | Yes | Permanent planter type. |
| **Growth Time** | ~6 hours | Can be sped up in red flower fields. |
| **Pollen Capacity** | 3,000,000 Pollen | Maximum storage capacity. |
| **Red Pollen Multiplier** | +50% | Increases the yield of Red Pollen. |
| **Blue Pollen Multiplier** | -50% | Decreases the yield of Blue Pollen. |
| **Nectar Multipliers** | +20% Invigorating, +20% Satisfying | Grants bonus amounts of these nectar types. |

### Growth Bonuses
*   The planter grows 25% faster when placed in fields containing red flowers.
*   Red bees are 25% more likely to sip nectar from this planter, while Blue bees avoid it entirely.

## Acquisition & Crafting

**Cost:**
*   10,000,000 Honey
*   5 Magic Beans
*   15 Red Extracts
*   20 Soft Waxes

### Drops (Claimed)
When claimed, the planter yields up to 16 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, three guaranteed tokens are provided:
*   1 Red Extract
*   2 Soft Waxes
*   1 Ticket

The remaining tokens are picked randomly from a non-exhaustive list of rewards (which does not include rewards based on the field type).

### Puffshroom Chance
Harvesting a fully grown Red Clay Planter has a 1/6 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 4 and 5, with the following rarity probabilities:
*   Common Puffshroom: ~76.28%
*   Rare Puffshroom: ~19.07%
*   Epic Puffshroom: ~4.58%
*   Legendary Puffshroom: ~0.08%

## Trivia

*   Its counterpart is the Blue Clay Planter.
*   The Red Clay Planter, along with the Heat-Treated Planter, are the only planters sold from the Red HQ.
*   Historically, the in-game description incorrectly stated a 4-hour growth time; it was corrected to 6 hours upon its introduction.
