## Field Description and Resources

The Pine Tree Forest is a field located past the Honey Bee Gate and near the Badge Bearer's Guild. It is protected by two Mantises and one Werewolf, making it the field with the most guarding mobs (3).

This field gathers various tokens while bees collect pollen:
*   Honey
*   Blueberry
*   Ticket
*   Blue Extract
*   Magic Bean
*   Neonberry

Planters placed in this field grant extra Whirligigs and Blue Extracts. When a planter is used, the field grants Comforting Nectar.

The flowers in this field are white and blue, available in single, double, and triple sizes. The total flower count is 713.

## Obtaining Boosts

Several methods can enhance pollen collection efficiency in the Pine Tree Forest:

*   **Blue Field Booster:** Using this booster may grant a Pine Tree Forest Boost (+100% pollen) for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Adds +100% Pine Tree Forest pollen to an existing boost for 15 minutes, provided the current boost is not maxed out.
*   **Dice:** Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice may add between +100% and +300% Pine Tree Forest pollen for 15 minutes, if the boost limit has not been reached.
*   **Field Winds:** Pine Tree Forest Winds can be obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes. They start with a base of 20% instant conversion and provide +5% instant conversion and +15% Pine Tree Forest pollen per stack, stacking up to 13 times total.
*   **Beequip:** The Pinecone Beequip can grant up to +15% Field Capacity and +10% Pine Tree Forest Pollen.
*   **Events:** During the Beesmas 2025 event, giving a Honey Bee an ornament present grants a x3 Pine Tree Forest boost.

### Outdated Methods (Historical Boosts)
*   Giving a Honey Bee a present during the Beesmas 2020 and 2024 events provided a x3 Pine Tree Forest boost.
*   Certain expired codes offered boosts, including: `300mVisit`, `WordFactory`, `PlushFriday`, `DemiDecade` (x4 boost + Winds), `ByeBeeBear`, `MinhMaMaMoons`, `12hourgeneral`, `YTFMMoons`, `RoyalRobzi`, `GummyBoost`, `Afternoon`, `LateNightGumdrops`, `WintersEnd`, `5mMembers` (+ Capacity x3), `RebootFriday`, `2Billion` (Winds x15 + Code buff), and `Discord100k`.
*   Purchasing a Pine Tree Forest Market Boost provides x1.5 Field Capacity, x1.25 Pine Tree Forest Pollen, x1.25 Blue Bomb Pollen, and x1.25 Convert Rate At Hive.

## Trivia

*   The Festive Bee likes the Pine Tree Forest, likely because most Christmas trees are coniferous (like pine). It is also the only red bee to like a field of the opposite color; Vicious Bee shares this trait liking the Rose Field.
*   This is the only field named "Forest" rather than "Field" or "Patch."
*   The field is guarded by one Werewolf and two Mantises, giving it the highest number of guarding mobs (3). Standing near the Sticker Stack area can prevent the werewolf from detecting players.
*   A royal jelly token rests on top of the tallest pine tree. It can be accessed via the Cloud Obby starting near Polar Bear using a Parachute/Glider, or more easily by using the Red Cannon and Glider.
*   As of the 2020-06-06 update, Honey Bees can be found on the gray platform behind the Pine Tree Forest.
*   The field has shrunk over time. It originally extended to the Badge Bearer's Guild but was later reduced in size and moved farther from the guild's door. One original pine tree remains near the Honey Bee Gate.
