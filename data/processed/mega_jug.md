# Mega-Jug

![Mega-Jug](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d6/Mega-Jug.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/267?cb=20190403021540)

## Overview
The **Mega-Jug** is a large container bag available for purchase in the Pro Shop. It boasts impressive capacity and conversion bonuses.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cost** | 50,000 Honey |
| **Capacity** | 25,000 Pollen |

### Stats & Bonuses
*   **Base Capacity Bonus:** +25,000 capacity.
*   **Convert Rate Bonus:** +40% Convert Rate.

## Crafting Information
The Mega-Jug requires the following ingredient:
*   50,000 Honey

## Trivia
*   This is the cheapest Bag available in the Pro Shop. It ranks second overall in price within the shop, only behind the Super-Scooper (which costs 44,000 Honey).
*   A smaller version of this item exists in the Noob Shop, called the Jar. The Jar has a base capacity of 750 Pollen and costs 650 Honey.
