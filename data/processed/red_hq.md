# Red HQ

![RedHQBeesmas2021](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/eb/RedHQBeesmas2021.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/267?cb=20211229004724) Front view of the Red HQ.

## Overview and Location

The **Red HQ** is a large, red building featuring a portal, a shop, and various interactive elements. It is situated next to the Rose Field and near the Special Sprout Summoner. A Riley Bee model stands above its entrance.

To gain access to the Red HQ, players must have discovered four different types of red bees. The Red HQ serves as the red counterpart to the Blue HQ.

On the roof, there is an enlarged Scythe; however, unlike the actual in-game item, this version does not emit flame particles.

## Interior Features and Mechanics

The player can access other sections of the HQ by climbing two ladders.

**First Floor:**
Five interactive elements are available on the first floor:
*   **Red Teleporter:** Allows players to teleport to the rock located behind Science Bear.
*   **Red Field Booster:** Provides a 15-minute boost for one of four fields: Mushroom Field (3x), Strawberry Field (1x), Rose Field (1x), or Pepper Patch (1x).
*   **Leaderboards:** Features two leaderboards: the All-Time Top Red Collectors and the Daily Top Red Collectors, both tracking the top 100 players based on red pollen collection.
*   **Store:** A set of red items is available for purchase (details in the Store section).

A hidden ticket token can be found near the back of the ceiling, visible from the ladder. There are three ways to reach it:
1.  Climb the Red Teleporter and jump or glide onto the token.
2.  From outside the HQ, stand on top of the All-Time Top Red Collectors leaderboard and then jump to the token (gliding may be necessary).

Players may require boots and/or a parachute/glider for certain navigation tasks.

**Upper Levels:**
The first ladder leads to the Strawberry Dispenser and a Royal Jelly token on the roof near the base. The second ladder leads to the top level, where players can find:
*   The Top Riley Bee Helpers Leaderboard.
*   Gifted Riley Bee, who requires a Translator (obtained from Science Bear) for communication. Once translated, players can complete quests for this bee.

Additionally, the code "ROOF" is visible on the back side of the white facade, near the large Scythe.

## Store Inventory

The Red HQ features a store selling nine types of items. To open the shop, press E when not standing on the Red Teleporter, Red Field Booster, or Leaderboards. The available categories include Tools, Bags, Accessories, and Planters.

## Music
When inside the shop, the audio track "Vendor" plays. This music is also heard in several other locations, including the Noob Shop, Pro Shop, Mountain Top Shop, Badge Bearer's Guild, Blue HQ, Petal Shop, Hive Hub, and the Blue Maze.

## Trivia
*   The Rogue Vicious Bee can target players if they are close enough to the wall next to the Rose Field.
*   Currently, the Dark Scythe is the most expensive item available for purchase in the Red HQ.
*   Historically, a glass roof was added after the 2019-04-05 update, preventing jumps into the Rose Field. This roof was later removed during the 2019-09-28 update due to new mechanics that teleported players back to spawn if they entered restricted areas.
*   A glitch known as the Shop Trap occurs if a player enters the Red Field Booster just as the shop is opened; this can only be reversed by disconnecting and rejoining the game.
*   Using Shift-Lock allows players to look through the white wall behind the Dark Scythe, revealing a pad marked with the number 250.
*   A Loaded Dice token is located on the far end of the roof near Robo Bear. It can be obtained by jumping onto the Top Riley Bee Helpers leaderboard, then onto the roof, and finally walking or gliding to the token. (Previously, this token was accessible via Shift-Lock from a rock next to the roof before Beesmas 2022).
*   The Red HQ is also commonly referred to as the "Red Clubhouse." If a player has not discovered four red bee types, their screen will display the message: "Discover 4 Red Bee types to enter the **Red Clubhouse**."
