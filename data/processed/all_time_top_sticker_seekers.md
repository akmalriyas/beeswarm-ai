## Overview

The Top Sticker-Seekers leaderboard was introduced in the January 13, 2024 update. It is one of the game's 64 leaderboards and tracks the total number of Sticker-Seeker Quests completed by players. The displayed view shows the top 10 positions, but players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place.

## Location

The leaderboard is located inside the Hive Hub, next to the Hub Field Shop.

## Trivia

*   The Top Sticker-Seekers leaderboard, along with the All-Time Tallest Sticker Stack, is one of only two leaderboards in the game that has a theoretical maximum limit (currently 589). This ceiling exists because there are only 290 different stickers in the game, and Rank 590 would require stacking 295 unique stickers, which is impossible.
*   This leaderboard is the only one located within the Hive Hub.
