# Ninja Bee

**Ninja Bee** is a Blue Legendary bee known for its exceptional speed. Its description states: *"This bee trained vigorously for years to become the swiftest bee that has ever lived."*

**Rarity:** Legendary
**Color:** Blue
**Base Stats:**
*   Energy: 20
*   Speed: 21
*   Attack: 4

Ninja Bee's favorite treat is Sunflower Seeds. It likes the Blue Flower Field and Bamboo Field, but dislikes the Mushroom Field.

## Statistics & Bonuses

### Base Collection Rates
*   **Pollen:** Collects 10 Pollen in 2 seconds (5 Pollen per second).
*   **Honey:** Makes 80 Honey in 2 seconds (40 Honey per second).

### Stat Boosts
Ninja Bee provides the following bonuses:
*   +50% Movespeed, Gather Speed, and Convert Speed.
*   +3 Attack.
*   +10% Critical Chance.

**Gifted Hive Bonus:** +5% Bee Movespeed.

## Abilities

*   **Haste:** Grants +10% Player Movespeed for 20 seconds. This ability stacks up to 10 times.
*   **Blue Bomb+:** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding Blue Flowers (+10% pollen per Level). Can be comboed with other bombs to increase power.

## Trivia & Lore

*   **Speed Ranking:** Ninja Bee is tied for the fastest bee in the game, alongside Photon Bee (despite its description calling it "the swiftest").
*   **Haste History:** In early versions of the game, Ninja Bee had an ability called "Haste+" which doubled player Movespeed for 20 seconds. This has since been removed; haste is now primarily obtained through Star Hall's Royal Jelly Dispenser or codes.
*   **Color Distinction:** Ninja Bee is one of only two non-Event blue bees that do not like Blueberries (the other being Cobalt Bee).
*   **Sunflower Preference:** Along with Precise Bee, Ninja Bee is one of the only non-Colorless bees to like Sunflower Seeds.
*   **Blue Counterpart:** Ninja Bee serves as the blue counterpart to Shy Bee.
*   **Trail Leaving Bees:** This bee does not leave a trail; those that do include Photon Bee, Cobalt Bee, Crimson Bee, Tadpole Bee, and Windy Bee.
*   **Historical Changes:** Before the 2018-11-25 Update, Ninja Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus was +20% Player Movespeed, rather than its current +5% Bee Movespeed.
*   **Original Treat:** Originally, Ninja Bee's favorite treat was Pineapples.
*   **Lore Connection:** Black Bear has stated that Ninja Bee is his choice of leader for the Blue HQ.
