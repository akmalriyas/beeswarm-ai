The Tallest Sticker Stack is one of the game's leaderboards. This leaderboard tracks the total number of stickers players have donated to the central Sticker Stack. It displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place.

## Location
The sticker stack is located past the Honey Bee Gate and behind the Pine Tree Forest.

## Trivia
The Tallest Sticker Stack and the All-Time Top Sticker-Seekers are unique leaderboards in the game because they have a fixed maximum amount (currently 290). This means that achieving the highest ranks on these boards will eventually become impossible.
