# Pesticide Planter

The **Pesticide Planter** is a reusable planter added in the December 26, 2021 update. It can be purchased from Dapper Bear's Shop and grows in approximately 10 in-game hours, storing up to 200 million Pollen. This planter grants bonuses for Bitterberries and Neonberries.

## Acquisition and Cost

The Pesticide Planter can be purchased using the following ingredients:

*   750,000,000 Honey
*   25 Magic Beans
*   25 Neonberries
*   15 Glues
*   3 Caustic Waxes
*   10 Hard Waxes

## Harvest Drops & Probabilities

Harvesting a fully grown Pesticide Planter has a 1/5 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 5 and 6, with the following rarity probabilities:

*   **Common Puffshroom:** ~72.59%
*   **Rare Puffshroom:** ~21.78%
*   **Epic Puffshroom:** ~5.44%
*   **Legendary Puffshroom:** ~0.18%
*   **Mythic Puffshroom:** ~0.01%

### Claimed Rewards (Tokens)

When claimed, the planter yields up to 28 tokens worth of items. If fully grown when claimed, two tokens are guaranteed:

*   1 Neonberry
*   2 Tickets

The remaining tokens are picked randomly from a non-exhaustive list of rewards, categorized as Bonus, Other, or Special (note that field bonuses do not apply to these random drops).

**Notable Drop Examples:**

*   **Bitterberries:** 100 Bitterberries (Requires harvesting in Strawberry Field; 1 month cooldown)
*   **Neonberries:** 25+ Neonberries (Requires specific sequence of fields: Stump $\rightarrow$ Mountain Top $\rightarrow$ Coconut; 1 week cooldown)
*   **Swirled Wax:** 1 Swirled Wax (Requires harvesting in Strawberry Field; 1 month cooldown)
*   **Caustic Wax:** 1 Caustic Wax (Has a higher chance when harvested from any field within the 5 Bee Zone).
