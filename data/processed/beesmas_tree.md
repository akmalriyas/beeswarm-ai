# Beesmas Tree

*Note: This is not to be confused with the [Beesmas Tree Hat](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree_Hat).*

[![The Beesmas Tree.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ad/Beesmastree.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20191222234856)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ad/Beesmastree.png/revision/latest?cb=20191222234856) [](/wiki/File:Beesmastree.png)

The **Beesmas Tree** is a seasonal feature that appeared during the Beesmas events of 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025. The tree allows players to view their collected [ornaments](/wiki/Ornaments), identify which NPC they came from, and see the boosts those ornaments provide. It is typically located in front of the [Ant Gate](/wiki/Ant_Gate), near the [Noob Shop](/wiki/Noob_Shop), and adjacent to the hives.

## Beesmas 2025
The Beesmas Tree returned in 2025, functioning identically to Winter Beesmas 2024. Players could earn ornaments by conducting a gift exchange with an NPC, which granted special boosts displayed on the tree.

**Ornament Placement (Top to Bottom):**
*   **Row 1:** [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man)
*   **Row 2:** Five ornaments: [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear), [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear), [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear), [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear), and [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear).
*   **Row 3:** Five ornaments: [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear), [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear), [Onett](/wiki/Onett), [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear), and [Robo Bear](/wiki/Robo_Bear).
*   **Row 4:** Four ornaments: [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee), [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee), [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug), and [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear). *(Note: The original wiki listed only three in the third row for 2025, but the ornament list provided here seems to combine data from multiple years. Based on the text structure, I have retained the specific listing of five ornaments in Row 2 and four in Row 3 as presented in the source.)*
    *   *Correction based on strict reading:* The third row holds four ornaments: [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee), [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee), [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug), and one unnamed ornament (the source text is ambiguous here, listing only three in the third row but then mentioning four ornaments on the 4th row).
*   **Row 5:** Two ornaments: Honey Bee and Gummy Bear.

**Tokens & Decor:**
Hidden at the bottom of the tree are a [Whirligig](/wiki/Whirligig) and five [Bitterberries](/wiki/Bitterberry). A [Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear) token is placed on top of the trunk.

## Beesmas 2020
The Beesmas Tree returned in 2020, functioning identically to previous years. To place ornaments on the tree, players had to collect and give [presents](/wiki/Present) to the corresponding NPC; they would then receive their ornament along with special boosts.

**Ornament Placement:**
*   **Row 1 (Bottom):** Four ornaments: [Black Bear](/wiki/Black_Bear), [Mother Bear](/wiki/Mother_Bear), [Brown Bear](/wiki/Brown_Bear), and [Panda Bear](/wiki/Panda_Bear).
*   **Row 2:** Four ornaments: [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear), [Polar Bear](/wiki/Polar_Bear), [Onett](/wiki/Onett), and [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear).
*   **Row 3:** Three ornaments: [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee), [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee), and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug).
*   **Additional Ornaments:** Three additional ornaments could be added to the tree, listed from left to top: [Honey Bee (NPC)](/wiki/Honey_Bee_\(NPC\)), [Gummy Bear](/wiki/Gummy_Bear), and [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man).

**Tokens & Decor:**
A [Gingerbread Bear](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear) token is located at the bottom, and a [Neonberry](/wiki/Neonberry) token is placed on top of the trunk.
