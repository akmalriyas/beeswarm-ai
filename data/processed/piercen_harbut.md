# Piercen Harbut (Onett)

Piercen Harbut, better known as Onett on Roblox, is the owner and programmer of *Bee Swarm Simulator*. This game is his first and primary project on the platform. He manages several associated groups, including the Bee Swarm Simulator Club and Onett's Testing Group.

### Developer Accounts and Support
Onett utilizes various alternate accounts to manage community interactions and technical support for *Bee Swarm Simulator*:
*   **BeeSwarmBugReport:** Handles bug reports submitted by players.
*   **BeeSwarmSupport:** Manages inquiries regarding purchases and save file issues.
*   **ltpb2020:** A secret alternate account primarily used to upload decals for the Ready Player Two event.
*   **HiddenBeePotential:** An account noted as being used mainly for testing purposes.

## Trivia and Interests

*   **Inspiration:** The Tabby Bee was inspired by a real-life cat named Sam, who passed away in 2018.
*   **Favorite Bees:** His preferred bees include Basic Bee, Rascal Bee, Tabby Bee, and Brave Bee.
*   **Favorite Ability:** He finds the Triangulate ability satisfying to use.
*   **Username Origin:** Onett's username is a reference to a city from the Nintendo game *EarthBound*.
*   **Tools & Mobs:** He owns a special tool called the Honey Hammer and his favorite mob is the Rhino Beetle.
*   **Music:** His preferred in-game music track is bpatrol.
*   **Events:** Onett occasionally summons mini events, often using aliases that replace his username (e.g., "[Alias] has summoned (a) [mini event]").
*   **Personal Details:** Onett's birthday is December 24, 1989. He was interviewed by Thundermaker300 in November 2018 as part of the Roblox Wiki's Developer Connections project.
