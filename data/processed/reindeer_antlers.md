# Reindeer Antlers

The **Reindeer Antlers** is a Level 8 Beequip available in the game. It has an equip limit of one and can only be equipped to Puppy Bee. There are no color requirements for this item.

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Method
*   Purchasing the Reindeer Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog for 250 Gingerbread Bears grants 1 Reindeer Antlers and other items.

### Outdated/Historical Methods
The item was previously obtainable through various limited-time events:
*   Buying the Reindeer Puppy Pack in the Robux Shop during Beesmas 2021 (800 Robux).
*   Purchasing it from Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2022 for 7,500 Snowflakes and 150 Gingerbread Bears.
*   Buying it from Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2024 (Summer) for 150 Gingerbread Bears.
*   Purchasing the Reindeer Games Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog for 200 Gingerbread Bears and 2,000 Snowflakes.

## Trivia

*   **Permanent Status:** The Reindeer Antlers shares several historical similarities with the Festive Wreath. Before the removal of permanent Beequips in the 2024-01-12 update, they were the only two permanent Beequips in the game.
    *   They were the first permanent Beequip added to the game (followed by the Festive Wreath).
    *   They were unique in having a set potential: Reindeer Antlers always had max potential, and the Festive Wreath always had 3 stars (6 points).
    *   They could not be discarded.
    *   Initially, Caustic Wax could not be used on these two Beequips, though this restriction has since been lifted.
*   **In-Game Model:** A model of a Puppy Bee wearing the Reindeer Antlers can be found outside the map, next to a Festive Bee equipped with the Festive Wreath.
*   **Cultural Reference:** This Beequip is likely inspired by Dr. Seuss’ children's book, *How the Grinch Stole Christmas*, referencing the Grinch dressing his dog as a reindeer.
*   **Unique Ability:** It is one of only three Beequips to grant a unique ability (along with the Snowglobe and Festive Wreath).
