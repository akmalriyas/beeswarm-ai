*Special Multiplier(s): None*

The **Scooper** is a [tool](Category:Tools) that new players automatically receive at the beginning of the game, alongside the [Pouch](Pouch). It collects 2 [pollen](Pollen) from two adjacent patches in front of it with a speed of 0.8 seconds.

It can be equipped and purchased within the [Noob Shop](Noob_Shop).

## Appearance
The scooper resembles a shovel, featuring a grey blade and a circular handle attached to a brown pole, presumably made of wood.

## Trivia
*   This tool is currently one of the least effective tools for collecting pollen due to its low base statistics compared to other available equipment in the game.
*   Its upgraded version, the [Super-Scooper](Super-Scooper), can be purchased from the [Pro Shop](Pro_Shop).
*   The Scooper is unique as it does not require any [honey](Honey) to purchase or use.
*   It is one of the slowest tools in the game, surpassed by the [Honey Dipper](Honey_Dipper) and the [Pulsar](/Gummyballer).
*   Along with the [Pouch](Pouch) and a [Basic Egg](Egg#Basic_Egg), the Scooper is one of the initial items players start with when joining the game.
