# Gummy Baller

![Gummyballer](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/c4/Gummyballer.png/revision/latest?cb=20211227045658)

The **Gummy Baller** is a specialized tool that can be crafted in [Gummy Bear's Lair](/wiki/Gummy_Bear%27s_Lair). It specializes in collecting pollen and generating powerful "Gummyball" effects.

| Attribute | Value |

### Pollen Collection Details
The Gummyballer collects a base of 9 pollen from 29 patches in 1 second, with an average collection rate of 652.5 pollen per second. The collection yields vary based on flower type:

| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 22.5 | 45 | 67.5 | 90 | 112.5 |
| Red | 22.5 | 45 | 67.5 | 90 | 112.5 |
| Blue | 22.5 | 45 | 67.5 | 90 | 112.5 |

**Special Multiplier:** The Gummyballer gains a special multiplier if it collects pollen while goo is present, increasing the stack count of the gummyball.

## Mechanics: Goo and Gummyball

Every swing with the Gummy Baller absorbs any collected [goo](/wiki/Goo). This action removes the goo from the flower and grants 12 seconds of a "Gummyball" buff, up to a maximum of 1000 stacks. The amount of Gummyball stacks gained per patch is proportional to the freshness of the goo (faded goo provides fewer stacks).

As the Gummyball stack count increases, the ball on the collector grows larger, and its aura strengthens, providing up to a 1.1x boost to white pollen collection.

### Gummyball Launch
Upon reaching the maximum stack (1000) or when swung while jumping inside a field, a Gummyball launches forward in the player's facing direction. This ball bounces around the field borders, leaving a trail of goo and collecting pollen from flowers it hits (collection rate and goo spread depend on the ball's size). It also collects any ability tokens and honey tokens, popping bubbles along its path. The initial stack count determines the starting size and duration of the Gummyball.

### Gummyball Combo
The Gummyball rewards the player with "Gummyball Combo," which provides stacking bonuses:
*   Hitting a wall: +2 stacks
*   Ability Token: +1 stack per token
*   Honey Token: +3 stacks per token
*   Mark (General): +8 stacks per mark
*   Precise Mark: +30 stacks for 8 seconds

Gummyball Combo has a maximum stack of 1000 and always provides a 1.1x white pollen boost, along with up to an 11x goo boost depending on the current combo stack.

## Crafting Ingredients

To craft the Gummy Baller, you will need:
*   10T Honey
*   1,500 Glue
*   2,000 Gumdrops
*   50 Caustic Waxes
*   50 Super Smoothies
*   5 Turpentines
*   3 Satisfying Vials

## Trivia and Lore

*   The Gummyballer is the second most expensive item in the game based on honey cost, only behind The Planter Of Plenty (100T Honey).
*   It has red and blue counterparts: the Dark Scythe and Tide Popper. However, the Gummy Baller is more expensive than these versions, likely because the Dark Scythe and Tide Popper require completing 250 quests from their respective shop owners, while the Gummyballer does not.
*   The Gummyballer shares the title of slowest tool in the game (in terms of collection cooldown) with the Pulsar and Sticker-Seeker.
*   On December 28, 2021, the Gummyballer was obtainable through an exploit that also granted the Tide Popper and Dark Scythe for free. Onett later patched this, resulting in data resets for users who exploited it.
*   The Gummyballer requires the most base resources to craft among all tools, behind The Planter Of Plenty.
*   It is one of the few items that undergoes visual changes corresponding to its active ability (the growing Gummyball).
*   Unlike other tools, the Gummy Baller is capable of taking goo patches from flowers.
*   The Gummyball on the tool moves similarly to amulets; it trails behind the collector's movement. High server or client ping can sometimes cause the ball to appear dislocated from the collector.
*   Historically, the Gummyballer cost 2500 gumdrops, but this was changed on May 23, 2024, to 2000 gumdrops and 3 Satisfying Nectar Vials.
