# Robo Pass Dispenser

![Robo Pass Dispenser](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Robo_Pass_Dispenser.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20221227131036) The Robo Pass Dispenser is a dispenser located near the entrance of the 30 Bee Zone (Bear Gate).

## Mechanics and Usage

The Robo Pass Dispenser allows players to acquire **Robo Passes**, which are required to participate in the Robo Bear Challenge.

*   **Output:** Grants 1 Robo Pass.
*   **Cost:** Requires 50 Tickets.
*   **Location:** Near the entrance of the 30 Bee Zone (Bear Gate).
*   **Cooldown:** Unlike the Free Robo Pass Dispenser, this dispenser does not have a cooldown timer.
*   **Limit:** Players cannot use this dispenser if they already possess 10 or more Robo Passes.

***

## Trivia

*   Previously, there was a free Robo Pass token available behind the dispenser; this has since been replaced with a Broken Drive.
*   This dispenser, along with the Ant Pass Dispenser, Free Ant Pass Dispenser, and Royal Jelly Dispenser, is one of the few dispensers that requires Tickets for use.
*   It belongs to a group of vending-style dispensers (including the Free Robo Pass Dispenser) that do not have visible faucets but instead feature a small compartment.
*   This dispenser, along with the Coconut Dispenser and Glue Dispenser, does not grant Ability Tokens (Haste) upon use.
