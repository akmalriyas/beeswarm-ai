The **Global Top Battlers** (also known as the **Battle Points Leaderboard**) is one of the game's leaderboards. It tracks the total battle points players have collected over time, displaying the top 10 positions at any given moment. Players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on these leaderboards for extended periods; hackers and exploiters will be removed. Battle points are earned by defeating specific mobs within the game.

To check a player's current battle point total, one must step on the white pad located in front of the leaderboard, which displays the message: "You have x Battle Points."

## Location

The Global Top Battlers Leaderboard is situated on the yellow platform. It is located next to the Panda Bear, positioned in front of the Bamboo Field and behind the Blue Flower Field.

## Trivia

*   This leaderboard is one of two leaderboards present since the game's release; the other is the Global Top Honeymakers Leaderboard.
*   Sugarsmacks12 held the number 1 spot in the global top battlers for three years before being dethroned, making it the longest-sustaining number one position across all game leaderboards.
*   The Bubble Bee Man used to be located behind this leaderboard but was later moved to the Bear Gate during the Beesmas 2019 update.
