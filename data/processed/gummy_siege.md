# Gummy Siege

The **Gummy Beacon** is a Beesmas machine introduced in the 2020+ updates. It can be unlocked after completing the "Gummy Bear's" Beesmas quest.

## Location and Activation

The Gummy Beacon is located on top of the Noob Shop, specifically on the corner closest to the Royal Jelly Dispenser, next to the Clover Field, and in front of the Star Hall.

*   **Pre-Quest State:** If a player attempts to activate the beacon before completing "Gummy Bear's" quest, it displays the message: *"The satellite dish isn't working, but it still emits bad vibes..."*
*   **Post-Quest Activation:** Completing the quest allows the player to activate the Gummy Siege.

## Mechanics of Gummy Siege

Upon activation, a platform appears featuring Gummy Bear and a Gummy Bee in front of the Gummy Beacon. The "Gummy Morph" tune begins playing.

The siege proceeds as follows:
1.  **Summoning:** Three random leveled Gummy Bees (level 8–20) are summoned for a duration of five minutes.
2.  **Attack Phase:** Gummy Bear begins shooting goo puddles at random fields, similar to the "Gummy Invasion" event. Each targeted field spawns Gumdrops tokens.
3.  **Targeting Pattern:** Gummy Bear selects five random fields (excluding the Ant Field and Hub Field). He shoots goo at each of these selected fields for one minute per field, resulting in a total siege duration of five minutes.

The cooldown period for the Gummy Siege is 8 hours.

### Server Messages
When the Gummy Siege starts, two server-wide messages appear:
*   Gummy Bear: "Artillery has arrived, Gummy Soldier {Username}."
*   Gummy Bear: "Beesmas ends now. Commence GOO YEAR celebrations!"

As Gummy Bear targets fields, additional server-wide messages display the target location:
*   Gummy Bear: "Sight set on {Field Name}!"

## Trivia and Mechanics Details

*   **Gummy Bear Appearance:** This is the second way (the first being via the Gummy Cannon) for Gummy Bear to appear in the mountain area. While visible during the siege, he can also be seen in his lair.
*   **Gummy Cannon Link:** This event is also the only way that the Gummy Cannon machine can appear.
*   **Temporary Bees:** The Gummy Beacon is one of four machines that summon temporary bees; the others are Honeyday Candles, Onett's Lid Art, and Honey Bee's Honey Wreath.
*   **Bee Count:** This is the only way to have more than one Gummy Bee simultaneously, though this effect is limited in duration.
*   **Passive Contribution:** The Gumdrops tokens spawned by Gummy Bees contribute towards activating the "Gummy Morph" passive ability for the player.
*   **Event Bees:** Along with Onett's Lid Art, the Gummy Beacon is one of only two methods to generate multiple Event Bees, though these are also limited in duration.
*   **Cooldown Status:** This machine, along with Onett's Lid Art and the Naughty List, possesses the longest cooldown (8 hours) among all Beesmas machines.
*   **Gummy Morph Interaction:** The Gummy Bees summoned during the siege are not affected by the "Gummy Morph" passive from the Gummy Mask; however, their ability tokens still contribute to activating the passive.
