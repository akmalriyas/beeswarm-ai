Frosty Bee is a Blue Epic bee.

**Preferences:**
*   **Favorite Treat:** [Blueberries]
*   **Likes:** [Blue Flower Field], [Mountain Top Field]
*   **Dislikes:** [Mushroom Field], [Pepper Patch]

## Stats

*   Collects 10 Pollen in 4 seconds.
*   Makes 80 Honey in 4 seconds.
*   Provides +25% Energy and -20% Movespeed.
*   🌟 **Gifted Hive Bonus:** Grants x1.25 Blue Bomb Pollen.

### Abilities

*   **Blue Boost:** Grants x1.2 pollen from blue Flowers for 15s. This ability stacks up to 10 times.
*   **Blue Bomb+:** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding blue flowers (+10% pollen per Level). Can be combined with other bombs to increase power.

Frosty Bee has a base pollen collection of **10 Pollen** in **4 seconds**, which is equivalent to 2.5 Pollen per second. This increases to **20 Pollen** in **4 seconds** when utilizing the x2 Bee Pollen gamepass.

## Trivia

*   Frosty Bee, [Commander Bee], and [Buoyant Bee] are the only bees that wear hats.
*   Frosty Bee is likely a reference to Frosty the Snowman.
*   The red counterpart of Frosty Bee is [Rage Bee].
*   A Frosty Bee Jelly could have been obtained from one of the bundles/packs in [Bee Bear's Catalog].
*   This bee's name may be a placeholder for any bee in a hive slot that has not loaded yet.
*   The bee may like [Mountain Top Field] because it is cold on high mountains.
