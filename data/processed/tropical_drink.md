# Tropical Drink

[![Tropical Drink](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0c/Tropical_Drink.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404023952)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0c/Tropical_Drink.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404023952)

*"Grants x1.25 White Pollen, +10% Instant Conversion and +5% Critical Chance for 10 minutes."*

**Cooldown:** 1 second

A Tropical Drink is a craftable inventory item introduced in the September 28, 2019 update. When consumed, it grants the following buffs for 10 minutes:
*   x1.25 White Pollen production
*   +5% Critical Chance
*   +10% Instant Conversion

**Note:** Using a Super Smoothie will override the buff granted by Tropical Drink.

## Acquisition Methods

Tropical Drinks can be obtained through several methods:

### Crafting
They can be crafted using a Blender with the following ingredients:
*   10 Coconuts
*   2 Enzymes
*   2 Oils

### Quests
Specific quests reward Tropical Drinks:
*   The Brown Bear's 85th quest rewards 1 Tropical Drink.
*   The Black Bear's "Boss Battles" questline (specifically after completing the "Black Bear: Triple Treck" quest) has a chance to reward 3 Tropical Drinks.

### Drops & Collection
Tropical Drinks can also be acquired as drops from various sources:
*   **Sprouts:** They drop from sprouts in the Coconut Field, with odds increasing based on sprout rarity.
*   **Mobs:** They drop from Wild Windy Bees, Armored Aphids, and Coconut Crabs (the amount depends on the time taken to kill the crab).
*   **Planters/Leaves:** They can be found as drops from fully-grown planters harvested in the Coconut Field or as a very rare drop from leaves in that field.
*   **Cub Buddy Gift:** They are occasionally given as a very rare Cub Buddy gift.
