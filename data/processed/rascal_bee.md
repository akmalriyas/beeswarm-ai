# Rascal Bee

**Rascal Bee** is a Rare Red bee known for its mischievous nature, quick movements, and strong attacks.

> "A mischievous bee who moves quick and hits hard. Keep an eye out on this one!"

## Statistics & Traits
*   **Rarity:** Rare
*   **Color:** Red
*   **Energy:** 20
*   **Speed:** 16.1
*   **Attack:** 3

### Preferences
*   **Favorite Treats:** Strawberries
*   **Likes:** Mushroom Field, Rose Field
*   **Dislikes:** Pine Tree Forest

### Collection & Production Rates
Rascal Bee has a base pollen collection of **10** in **4 seconds**, which is equivalent to 2.5 per second. With the x2 Bee Pollen gamepass, this increases to **20** in **4 seconds**.

*   **Pollen Collection:** Collects 10 pollen in 4 seconds (Base).
*   **Honey Production:** Makes 80 honey in 4 seconds (Base), equivalent to 20 per second. With the x2 Honeymaking Speed gamepass, this increases to **80** in **2 seconds**.

### Bonuses & Abilities
*   **Stat Boosts:** +15% Movespeed, +2 Attack.
*   **Gifted Hive Bonus:** Grants x1.25 Red Bomb Pollen collection rate.
*   **Ability: Red Bomb:** Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding red flowers (+10% pollen per level). Combining this ability with other bombs increases its power.

### Trivia
*   Its blue counterpart is Bumble Bee.
*   Rascal Bee, Riley Bee, and Demon Bee share the same skin colors.
*   Rascal Bee is one of Onett's favorite bee types because he likes its facial expression.
*   In Onett's inventory, it was revealed that Rascal Bee originally had a different face.
*   The description contains a common misspelling: "mischevious" instead of "mischievous."
*   Defeated ladybugs sometimes drop Rascal Bee eggs.
*   Rascal Bee has the second highest attack among all Rare bees, only behind Brave Bee.
*   A special jelly that could change a bee into a Rascal Bee was obtainable from the expired code 'MischiefMaker'.
