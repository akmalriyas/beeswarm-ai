# Party Cogturret

The **Party Cogturret** is a mob exclusive to the [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party). It functions similarly to a standard [Cogturret](/wiki/Cogturret), but instead of following the player, it spawns randomly on a field. They first appear starting at Cake Rank 5 and continue spawning in subsequent ranks.

> **Note:** Do not confuse this with the normal [Cogturrets](/wiki/Cogturret).

## Strategies

*   Party Cogturrets target the player's current location, not their predicted future position. Moving to the side immediately after they fire a cog often causes them to miss their attack.
*   They always shoot directly at the direction they are facing; therefore, you can avoid attacks simply by staying out of their line of sight.
*   Positioning near Party Cogturrets allows your bees to lock on quickly and deal damage efficiently.
*   [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee) and [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee) are excellent for crowd control, especially since these turrets often appear in packs. [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee) can also stun multiple Party Cogturrets at once, allowing them to take extra damage via [Mind Hack](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Mind_Hack).

## Trivia

*   The Party Cogturret is the only Party Robot that does not wear a Party Hat.
*   It shares the same health per level as its normal counterparts with [Party Mechsquitos](/wiki/Party_Mechsquito) and [Party Mega Mechsquitos](/wiki/Party_Mega_Mechsquito).
