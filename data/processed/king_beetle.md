# King Beetle

The **King Beetle** is a level 7 boss mob that resides in its lair, located between the Blue Flower Field and the Clover Field (above the blue flower closest to the Blue HQ via a transparent wall).

## Stats & Mechanics

| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Health** | 2,500 | |
| **Damage** | 40 | Damage dealt per hit if the player has no Defense. |
| **Battle Points (Initial)** | 150 | Guaranteed drop upon defeat. |
| **Bond (Initial)** | 500 | Guaranteed drop upon defeat. |
| **Honey (Initial)** | 5,000 | Base amount; multiplied by Honey From Tokens/2 if no Amulet is rewarded. |
| **Level** | 7 | Required bee level for effective attacks. |

**Respawn Time:**
*   Standard: 1 day (24 hours)
*   With a Gifted Vicious Bee: 20 hours and 24 minutes

**Loot Mechanics:**
The King Beetle drops its loot in a token circle around the center of the lair, unlike most other mobs. If the player receives the King Beetle Amulet, all rewards go directly into the inventory.

*   There is a one-in-seven (approximately 14%) chance that he will drop a **King Beetle Amulet**.
*   Having more Loot Luck increases the probabilities of other drops but does not improve the probability of the King Beetle Amulet dropping.

## Strategies for Defeat

Defeating the King Beetle is challenging. It is recommended that players do not fight it with a full bag.

**Movement & Dodging:**
*   **Predictable Pattern:** The beetle moves toward the player, stops to "aim" (about 3 seconds), and then lunges. Players can exploit this by running along the lair's edge: stop long enough for him to lock on, then quickly jump/run away before he lunges at the old position.
*   **Circling:** Keeping a good distance while circling the beetle allows bees to get close and defeat it faster.
*   **Positioning:** If too far from the King Beetle, player bees will follow the player instead of attacking. If too close, touching its aura can cause damage.
*   **Dodging:** Remember that the King Beetle does not follow directly behind the player; it can sidestep to block movement.

**Combat Tactics:**
*   Run around the lair and occasionally check his health. Rage Bees are particularly helpful for grabbing tokens while moving.
*   Building up maximum Haste allows for faster evasion, though this resource may run out.
*   Using a Parachute or Glider can help increase speed and escape attack paths.
*   If your bees are guaranteed to defeat him before they die, you can simply stand in front of the beetle and let them finish the fight.
*   A Rogue Vicious Bee active in the Clover Field can be tricked into damaging the King Beetle.

**Tips for Success:**
*   **Bee Quality:** Use high-attack power bees such as Lion Bee, Vicious Bee, Crimson Bee, Cobalt Bee, Riley Bee, Bucko Bee, Rage Bee, Brave Bee, and Precise Bee.
*   **Leveling:** High-level bees are crucial; attacks will likely miss if the player's bees are below Level 7. A minimum of 20–25 bees at Level 7 is usually sufficient.
*   **Equipment:** Wear Accessories that boost Bee Attack, Move Speed, Jump Power, or Defense (e.g., Guards).
*   **Ability Tokens:** Collecting Rage, Focus, and Melody tokens helps when having fewer bees. Haste and Bear Morph tokens can aid in dodging attacks.
*   **Controls:** WASD is suggested over arrow keys as it allows the player to maintain line of sight while moving.
*   **Stinger Use:** Using a Stinger can be useful when employing low-level bees.

## Trivia & Lore

*   King Beetle was the first boss added to the game.
*   It and Stump Snail are the only mobs that can drop an amulet (the King Beetle Amulet and Shell Amulet, respectively).
*   King Beetle has the fifth-largest health pool among all mobs in the game.
*   The lair contains a hint written on the floor ("Song Name") which refers to the music playing there (titled "Crawlers" in Onett's inventory).
*   Onett stated that the King Beetle emits toxins, causing hallucinations, which explains its and its lair's strange coloration.
*   King Beetle is unique among bosses because it does not have a time limit to kill it, and it drops loot in a circle.
*   Before the 2018-07-11 Update, King Beetle took two days to respawn, granted more battle points, and despawned after about five minutes.
*   King Beetle is considered a hybrid of a Rhino Beetle and a Ladybug, according to Onett.
