# Daily Top Sprout Token Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Sprout Token Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator, viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page. This leaderboard tracks how many tokens players have collected from Sprouts on a given day.

It displays 10 positions at a time, and players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Awarded the Basic Green Hive Skin.
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 50 Tickets.
