# Medals

Medals are a prestigious feature of the Bee Swarm Simulator Wiki, designed to recognize significant contributions and dedication within the community.

## Distinction from Badges
While badges track general activity and achievement points, Medals represent something unique and superior. Each medal signifies a specific story or major commitment, requiring substantial dedication to earn. They are intended as high-level recognition of effort, rather than simple metrics of participation.

## Acquisition Policy
The acquisition of medals is strictly managed by wiki staff. Unlike badges, which can sometimes be farmed through repetitive edits, Medals cannot be manipulated or earned through automated means. Staff actively monitor contributions and determine eligibility based on genuine commitment and merit.
