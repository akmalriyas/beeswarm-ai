# Field Wind

A **Field Wind** is a stacking buff that grants bonuses to pollen gain and instant conversion for a specific field. These buffs are obtained by donating certain items to the Wind Shrine or using various codes.

## Mechanics and Properties

*   **Duration:** Field Winds last for 30 minutes per application.
*   **Stacking:** With each application, the stacks add up, and the duration resets to 30 minutes. The maximum stack limit is x15.
*   **Stacking Limit Requirements:** To achieve the maximum x15 stack, the item donated must be rare enough, and the player must have sufficient Wind Shrine Favor.
*   **Conversion Bonus:** Each stack of a field wind increases the base instant conversion rate (starting at 25%) by an additional 3%.
*   **Replenishment:** Field winds can also be replenished via codes; if both the Wind Shrine and a code grant field winds for the same field, it restarts the 30-minute timer.
*   **Visual Distinction:** Unlike its counterpart, Field Boosts, fields boosted with Winds do not emit sparkles. However, their identifying icons are the same as Field Boosts, distinguished by having a white background.
