## Game Mechanics & Location

The Highest Damage to a Single Puffshroom leaderboard is one of the game's many leaderboards. This specific challenge can be found inside Dapper Bear’s Shop, located on the left side of Dapper Bear in the game world.

## Achievements and Milestones

*   **1 Billion Damage:** One billion damage was dealt to a single Puffshroom for the first time sometime in late December 2021.
*   **10 Billion Damage:** Ten billion damage was achieved for the first time between February and March 2022.
*   **100 Billion Damage:** HavingFun2228 was the first player to deal 100 billion damage to a single puffshroom.
*   **1 Trillion Damage:** SupereamXD was the first player to deal 1 trillion damage to a single puffshroom.

## Trivia

This leaderboard, along with the Highest Robo Bear Challenge Scores leaderboard, is one of only two leaderboards that features a background frame color other than brown.
