# Golden Cogmower

A Golden Cogmower is a rare mob that appears during the Robo Bear Challenge. It is distinguished by its unique golden and bronze accents, setting it apart from the more common Cogmower or Party Cogmower variants.

![GoldenCogmower](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bd/GoldenCogmower.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/267?cb=20230201003224)

## Mechanics and Appearance

**Location:**
Golden Cogmowers can only spawn in four specific fields while the Robo Bear Challenge is active: Clover Field, Pineapple Patch, Pumpkin Patch, or Mountain Top Field.

**Spawn Frequency:**
They are one of the rarest mob types in the challenge, spawning a total of three times:
*   Once on either Round 3, 4, or 6.
*   Once again on either Round 12, 14, or 16.
*   Once more on either Round 21 or 22.

**Behavior:**
Golden Cogmowers do not despawn until the round ends. Unlike regular Cogmowers, they remain in the field where they spawned and will not follow the player if the player moves to a different field. They rise from the ground before slowly moving around their designated area, draining flowers as they pass over them. The higher the mob's level, the more pollen it drains.

**Combat Attributes:**
*   They bounce off the edges of the field.
*   Golden Cogmowers only take half as much damage from Critical Hits.

## Loot Drops

Golden Cogmowers are the only mobs in the Robo Bear Challenge to drop loot.

*   **Cogs:** Guaranteed drops of Cogs (Quantity: 14–20).
*   **Sticker:** Unbelievably Rare chance to drop a Wavy Yellow Hive Skin Sticker.

## Strategies and Tactics

*   **Evasion:** Since Golden Cogmowers do not actively target the player, players can easily sidestep them while maintaining damage output.
*   **Targeting:** Positioning your bees near the mob allows them to lock on quickly and deal sustained damage.
*   **Bee Synergy:**
    *   The Vicious Bee provides strong single-target damage.
    *   The Digital Bee can stun the Golden Cogmower, allowing it to take extra damage via Mind Hack (though this does not negate its reduced critical hit damage).
*   **Preparation:** At the start of a round, use the Red Cannon across all fields to locate any Golden Cogmowers. The Cogs they drop can be used to afford crucial upgrades during the challenge.

## Trivia

*   The Golden Cogmower has a common counterpart known simply as the Cogmower.
*   While it does not have a Robo Party variant, the Party Cogmower behaves similarly to the Golden Cogmower in that it will remain on its spawned field.
*   Golden Cogmowers are unique among mobs in the Robo Bear Challenge for dropping loot.
