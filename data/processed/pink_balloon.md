# Pink Balloon

[![Pink Balloon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d2/Pink_Balloon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031404)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d2/Pink_Balloon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031404)

*"Floats on the field for 3 minutes, storing and doubling pollen you collect beneath it. Then it floats to your hive!"*

***

### Stats
*   **Cooldown:** 5 minutes
*   **Inventory Cap:** 25 items
*   **Field Limit:** 1 per player

The **Pink Balloon** is a consumable item introduced in the December 26, 2021 update. When deployed on a field, it creates a pink balloon that floats over the area. This balloon collects pollen gathered underneath it and doubles the amount collected. It holds up to double the player's current capacity. After floating for three minutes, the balloon automatically travels back to the hive.

***

## Acquisition Methods
