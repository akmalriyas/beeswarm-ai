# Daily Top Hub Field Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Hub Field Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator. It can only be viewed through the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page.

This leaderboard tracks the total amount of pollen collected by players specifically within the Hub Field during a single day. The board displays 10 positions at a time, but users can scroll down to view rankings all the way to the 100th place. The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes and Rewards

Players who achieve specific ranks by the end of the day are awarded rewards:

*   **Top 25:** Players reaching the top 25 receive a **Hub Field Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players reaching the top 100 receive 25 **Tickets**.
