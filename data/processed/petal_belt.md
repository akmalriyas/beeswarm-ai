# Petal Belt

[ ![Petal Belt](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0d/Petal_Belt.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20210402170808) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0d/Petal_Belt.png/revision/latest?cb=20210402170808 "Petal Belt")

The **Petal Belt** is a belt accessory found in the Petal Shop. It allows players to harness flower power by draping petals around their waist.

> *"Drape these petals about your waist to harness unlimited flower power."*

## Stats and Abilities

| Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Capacity | +250,000 |
| Capacity Bonus | +50% |
| Convert Amount | +100 |
| Loot Luck | +101% |
| Honey From Tokens | +75% |
| Buzz Bomb Pollen | +30% |
| Colorless Bee Attack | +1 |

### Passive: Petal Storm
Every 30th Ability Token collected activates the Petal Storm. This ability fires 30 Petal Shurikens around the player, which have a 30-second cooldown before reuse. Every bee hit by these shurikens instantly converts 10,000 pollen into honey. The conversion amount is modified by:
*   +6% of the bee's Convert Amount
*   +4% per Field Boost stack

## Crafting Information

**Cost:**
*   15,000,000,000 Honey
*   1 Spirit Petal
*   25 Royal Jelly (Star Jelly)
*   50 Glitter
*   100 Glue

**Crafting Ingredients (Alternative):**

*   1 Spirit Petal
*   61,875 Strawberries
*   61,875 Blueberries
*   77,500 Pineapples
*   125 Magic Beans
*   6,625 Royal Jellies

## Trivia and Lore

*   The Petal Belt is the 9th most expensive item in terms of honey (excluding Hive Slots).
*   This accessory emits a yellow particle effect in the center of the belt, similar to the Petal Wand.
*   It is one of three belt accessories that cannot be obtained from the Sun Bear.
*   The Petal Belt is one of only two belts with a passive ability (the other being the Coconut Belt).
*   The Petal Shurikens can pop bubbles and fuzz bombs, and they also spawn a petal when hitting blooms.
*   This belt is unique in that it does not have a hitbox on display.
*   A notable change log entry from the 2024-05-23 update confirmed that the passive ability conversion rate was adjusted to its current state (10,000 pollen +7% of the bee's convert amount).
