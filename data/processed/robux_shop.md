# Robux Shop

The **Robux Shop** is an in-game store that requires Robux to purchase various items. It can be accessed through the Shop tab on the menu bar, located next to the System Tab.

This shop sells a wide variety of goods, including gamepasses, Eggs, Royal Jelly, Honey pouches, Night Bells, Sticker Planters, Magic Beans, Tickets, and limited-edition packs (such as Festive Beans).

## Trivia
*   Those who purchased the x2 Ticket Chance Pass before its removal retained the pass.
*   All items in the shop can be obtained without spending Robux through trading or other methods (such as vouchers).
*   The Robux Shop and Bee Bear's Catalog are the only shops located within a menu area.
*   The Robux Shop contains more items than any other shop in-game.
    *   The Festive Bee Pack, Fuzzy Bee Pack, Buoyant Pack, Precise Pack, Digital Bee Pack, Carpenter's Crafting Pack, and Starry Night Special are unique packs that provided a specific Bee Egg (Bear Bee is not a pack).
*   Honey offers were previously higher; the Honey Vault used to offer 200,000 honey.
*   Royal Jelly historically cost 50 Robux each, and the pack of 10 once cost 400 Robux.
*   Bear Bee originally cost 1,000 Robux before the 2019-09-28 update; it now costs 800 Robux. Star Eggs were also lowered from 1,000 to 800 Robux.
