# Red Balloon

[![Red Balloon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/28/Red_Balloon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031416)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/28/Red_Balloon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031416)

> "Floats over the field for 3 minutes. Stores up to 5x your capacity and works best with Red flowers."

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 5 min
*   **Capacity (Inventory Cap):** 25

The **Red Balloon** is a consumable item added in the December 26, 2021 update. When used, it creates a red balloon over the player's current field. This balloon floats and stores pollen collected underneath it.

**Mechanics:**
*   Pollen collected while under the Red Balloon increases by 10%.
*   Red pollen collected increases by an additional 20% (total 30%).
*   The balloon holds up to five times the player's current capacity.
*   It floats for 3 minutes before migrating to the hive balloon.

**Acquisition Methods:**

### Current Ways to Obtain
*   As an exceptionally rare drop from a Scorpion.
*   As a rare reward from the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 6th quest grants one Red Balloon and other items.
*   A Red Balloon token can be found on top of the Hive Hub after completing the parkour.
*   Redeeming certain valid codes (e.g., `15MMembers`).

### Outdated Ways to Obtain
*   Purchasing the Balloon Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog for 333 Snowflakes.
*   Completing Onett's Beesmas 2022 quest.
*   Completing Bee Bear's Beesmas 2022 quests.
*   Giving a present to Gifted Riley Bee.
*   Receiving it as a reward from certain quests given by Bee Bear.
*   Buying the Balloon Bundle during Beesmas 2021.
*   Buying the Buoyant Pack from the Robux shop.
*   As a rare drop from Party Mega Mechsquitoes and Party Cogturrets.
*   Opening specific Gift Boxes across the map (Beige or Gilded).
*   Redeeming certain expired codes (e.g., `5MFavorites`, `DarzethDoodads`).
