# Daily Top Sunflower Field Collectors

The **Daily Top Sunflower Field Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator. This leaderboard tracks how much pollen players have collected specifically within the Sunflower Field on a given day. It can be accessed via the Global Leaderboards menu found on the System Page.

The leaderboard displays the top 10 positions at any time, but players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. The ranking resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes and Rewards

Players who achieve specific rankings by the end of the day are awarded rewards:

*   **Top 25:** Players ranked in the top 25 receive a **Sunflower Field Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players ranked in the top 100 receive 25 **Tickets**.
