# Pouch

[ ![Pouch](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/79/Pouch.png/revision/latest?cb=20190402214811) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/79/Pouch.png/revision/latest?cb=20190402214811 "Pouch")

### Description
"A small bag."

| Cost | Capacity |
| :--- | :--- |
| Free | 200 Pollen |

The **Pouch** is the starter bag that new players automatically receive at the beginning of the game, alongside the Scooper and the starter tool. It can be equipped in the Noob Shop. Since it is the first bag given to the player, it does not have a purchase price.

### Stats
*   +200 Capacity.

### Trivia
*   In early versions of the game, the Pouch stored 150 pollen. This capacity was later buffed to 250 and then subsequently nerfed back to 200.
*   Along with the Jar, the Pouch is one of the few bags whose base capacity is less than its cost (though the Pouch itself costs nothing).
*   The Pouch, the Scooper, and a Basic Egg are the only items that players start with when joining the game for the very first time.
