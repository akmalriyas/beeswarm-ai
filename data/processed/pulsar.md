# Pulsar

[![Pulsar](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/49/Pulsar.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20190403015905)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/49/Pulsar.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403015905)

The **Pulsar** is a tool available for purchase in the Pro Shop. It functions as an advanced pollen collector.

## Stats
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | 125,000 Honey |
| **Shop** | Pro Shop |
| **Range** | 29 Flowers |
| **Collecting Pattern** | Surround |
| **Collecting Time** | 1 second |
| **Base Collection Rate** | 58 pollen per second (2 pollen collected from 29 surrounding patches in 1 second) |

### Pollen Collection Breakdown
The Pulsar's base collection rate is calculated as follows:

| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Red | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Blue | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |

*Special Multiplier(s): None*

## Description and Appearance
The Pulsar is visually represented as a black orb emitting green smoke, surrounded by three black rings. Two of these rings have small black orbs connected to them—one positioned above the middle orb, and one to the right.

## Trivia
*   **Comparison:** The Pulsar has a similar collection range to the Honey Dipper and Porcelain Dipper, but both of those tools possess a slightly larger collection range. Additionally, the Porcelain Dipper summons a large pillar of light every 10th swing while collecting pollen.
*   **Speed:** The Pulsar is tied for being one of the slowest tools in the game (along with Gummyballer and Sticker-Seeker), based on its collection cooldown duration.
*   **Origin Theory:** It is theorized that the Pulsar may be an upgraded version of the Vacuum, given that pulsars are astronomical objects known for high gravity and vacuum-like suction properties.
