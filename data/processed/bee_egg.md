# Bee Egg

Eggs are inventory items in *Bee Swarm Simulator* that players drag into their hive to hatch a bee. They can also be donated to the Wind Shrine or used to roll on the Sticker Printer. Eggs serve as an entry point for new players and come in many types, some offering chances at different rarities of bees, while others hatch a specific type of bee.

Currently, there are 6 primary egg types (Basic, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Mythic, Star), plus various Gifted variants, specific non-event eggs, and specific event eggs. Note that Plastic Eggs, the Flight of the Bumble Egg, and the Swarming Egg of the Hive do not hatch into a bee.

## General Mechanics

*   **Hatching:** Players can hatch an egg on top of an existing bee in their hive without needing a vacant slot. The level of the host bee remains unchanged, acting like Royal Jelly.
*   **Restrictions:** Due to regulations (UK, Netherlands, Belgium, Australia), certain eggs available in the Robux Shop may not be purchasable by consumers in those regions.

## Egg Types and Probabilities

### Basic Egg
The most common egg type. It primarily hatches into a Basic Bee (87% chance). Rare outcomes include:
*   Rare Bee (10% chance)
*   Epic Bee (2.5% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (0.5% chance)
*   Gifted Bee (0.4% chance)
*   Gifted Basic Bee (0.348% chance, or 1 in 287.35632184)

**Restrictions:** Cannot hatch Mythic Bees or Event Bees. Cannot be used on a Basic Bee.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Free upon starting the game.
*   Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop (price scales up with each purchase).
*   Receiving 1 egg for adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack.

### Silver Egg
Most likely hatches into a Rare bee (64.9% chance), but also has chances to hatch:
*   Epic Bee (30% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (5% chance)
*   Mythic Bee (0.1% chance)

**Restrictions:** Cannot hatch Basic Bees or Event Bees.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 50 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 100 Robux.
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Silver Egg questlines.
*   Reward from Brown Bear's 25th quest.
*   Completing the 25th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine reward.
*   Drops: Mantis (1 or 5 increments), King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Rare Sprouts, Leaves (when collecting pollen in Clover Field), Festive Planter, Stockings (rare).

### Gifted Silver Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Silver Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, though chances decrease on subsequent defeats.
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Completing Black Bear's "Getting Stumped" quest.
*   1 reward from completing the 100th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Werewolf (exceptionally rare), Stump Snail (rare), Scorpion (exceptionally rare), Sticker Sprout.

### Gold Egg
Most likely hatches into an Epic bee (79% chance). Also has chances to hatch:
*   Legendary Bee (20% chance)
*   Mythic Bee (1% chance)

**Restrictions:** Cannot hatch Basic Bees, Rare bees, or Event bees.
*(Note: 5 Gold Eggs are required to craft the Honey Mask.)*

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 100 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 200 Robux each.
*   Rewards: Robo Bear Challenge, Extreme Memory Match.
*   In token form in Werewolf's Cave (guarded by two cave monsters).
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Gold Egg questlines.
*   Reward from Brown Bear's 50th, 340th, and 390th quests.
*   1 reward from completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 50th quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick 10 times.
*   Drops: King Beetle (low chance), Scorpion (extremely low chance), Epic sprouts (rare), Leaves (when collecting pollen in Clover Field, extremely rare), various Planters (Ticket, Candy, Red Clay, Festive, Pesticide, Petal).
*   Rare drop from Stockings.
*   Rare reward from Winter Memory Match.

### Gifted Gold Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Gold Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant. **Restriction:** Cannot be used to craft the Honey Mask.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, though chances decrease on subsequent defeats.
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Playtime Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 50 Gifted Bucko Bee quests or 50 Gifted Riley Bee quests (1 egg each).
*   Completing 275 Brown Bear quests (1 egg).
*   1 reward from completing the 150th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Stump Snail (very rare), Coconut Crab (very rare), Mantis (exceptionally rare).

### Diamond Egg
Most likely hatches into a Legendary bee (95% chance). Also has a rare chance to hatch into a Mythic bee (5% chance).
*(Note: 5 Diamond Eggs are required to craft the Diamond Mask.)*

**Restrictions:** Cannot hatch Basic Bees, Rare bees, Epic bees, or Event bees.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Token location: Past Bear Gate, under the platform where Onett stands (requires Glider/Parachute near Shadow Bear).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 400 Robux each.
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 400 Tickets each.
*   Rewards: Robo Bear Challenge, Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Black Bear's Diamond Egg questline.
*   Panda Bear's fifth Star Treat/Ants quest ("Colossal Combat").
*   Dapper Bear's tenth quest ("The Long Haul").
*   Brown Bear's 75th and 525th quests.
*   75th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Stump Snail, Diamond Aphids, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Coconut Crab (rare chance). Spiders, Mantises, Werewolves (very rare chance). Legendary sprouts (rare chance), Supreme sprouts (guaranteed drop). Leaves (when collecting pollen in Clover Field, extremely rare). Mythic Meteor Shower (very rare reward). Festive Planter (rare drop). Winter Memory Match (rare reward).

### Gifted Diamond Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Diamond Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant. **Restriction:** Cannot be used to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, though chances decrease on subsequent defeats.
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers.
*   Quest Grandmaster badge reward from donating Tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 100, 250, 500, 750, and 1,000 Gifted Bucko Bee or Gifted Riley Bee quests (1 egg each).
*   425 Brown Bear quests (1 egg).
*   1 reward from completing the 200th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops: Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, Coconut Crab (extremely rare chance).

### Mythic Egg
Always hatches into a Mythic bee. The player can obtain a Gifted Mythic Bee variant, though it is very rare.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Memory Matchers.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 1,200 Robux each (limited to 3 eggs per player).
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 2,500 Tickets each.
*   Rewards: Robo Bear Challenge (very rare), Extreme Memory Match (extremely rare).
*   Completing Black Bear's 75th quest ("Myth in The Making").
*   Dapper Bear's 15th quest ("More Than Myth").
*   Brown Bear's 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
*   1 reward from completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 300th quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick for the 50th time (guaranteed Mythic Egg).
*   Drops: Stump Snail, Mondo Chick, Coconut Crab (extremely rare chance). Mythic Meteor Shower (extremely rare chance). Festive Planter (very rare drop).
*   Purchasing from Bee Bear's Catalog for 500 Snowflakes and 100 Gingerbread Bears.
*   Completing Gummy Bear’s Goo Year’s Beacon quest during Beesmas 2025.

### Gifted Mythic Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Mythic Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Rare Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   1 reward from completing 500 Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quests.
*   Nearly impossible drop from Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Purchasing from Bee Bear's Catalog for 10,000 Snowflakes and 150 Gingerbread Bears.

### Choose-A-Mythic Egg
Allows the player to select any Mythic Bee upon hatching.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing the Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul from the Robux Shop for 4,500 Robux.

### Star Egg
Hatches into any type of gifted bee not already in the hive (excluding gifted Event bees). If all non-event gifted bees are owned, it hatches a random gifted bee. It will only hatch a Gifted Mythic Bee once all other non-event gifted bees are collected.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, including Star Egg, though chances decrease on subsequent defeats.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 eggs per player).
*   Drops: Mythic Meteor Shower (exceptionally rare chance), King Beetle (exceptionally rare chance), Festive Planter (exceptionally rare drop), Party Mechsquito (exceptionally rare drop).

## Specific Bee Eggs

These eggs always hatch into a specific, designated bee type. All share the appearance of a Basic Egg.

### Non-Event Specific Bee Eggs
*   **Bumble Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Bumble Bee (100%). Drop from Rhino Beetle (Exceptionally Rare).
*   **Rascal Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Rascal Bee (100%). Drop from Ladybug (Exceptionally Rare).
*   **Baby Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Baby Bee (100%). Drop from Tunnel Bear (Extremely Rare).
*   **Honey Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Honey Bee (100%). Drop from Cave Monster (Uncommon).
*   **Fuzzy Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Fuzzy Bee (100%). Purchasable via the Fuzzy Pack in the Robux Shop (currently unobtainable).
*   **Tadpole Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Tadpole Bee (100%). Reward for giving Bubble Bee Man a Present during Beesmas 2020.
*   **Bubble Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Bubble Bee (100%). Reward when completing BBM's Naughty List quest (currently unobtainable).
*   **Commander Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Commander Bee (100%). Achieved by capturing the Commando Chick for the 200th time.
*   **Buoyant Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Buoyant Bee (100%). Purchasable via the Buoyant Pack in the Robux Shop (currently unobtainable).
*   **Precise Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Precise Bee (100%). Purchasable via the Precise Pack in the Robux Shop (currently unobtainable).
*   **Music Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Music Bee (100%). Purchased via the Musical Mega-Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog for 123 Gingerbread Bears.

### Gifted Non-Event Specific Bee Eggs
*   **Gifted Bubble Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Gifted Bubble Bee (100%). Reward from completing Bubble Bee Man's Winter Beesmas 2024 quest.
*   **Gifted Carpenter Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Gifted Carpenter Bee (100%). Purchasable via the Carpenter's Crafting Pack in the Robux Shop during Winter Beesmas 2024 (expired March 31, 2025).
*   **Gifted Diamond Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Gifted Diamond Bee (100%). Purchasable via the Starry Night Special in the Robux Shop during Winter Beesmas 2024 (expired March 31, 2025), or by purchasing the Snow Queen Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   **Gifted Exhausted Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Gifted Exhausted Bee (100%). Purchasable via the Night-Before Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog for 10 Gingerbread Bears.
*   **Gifted Frosty Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Gifted Frosty Bee (100%). Purchasable via the Super Snowman Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog for 800 Snowflakes.

### Event Specific Bee Eggs
These eggs always hatch into a specific type of Event bee, granting an exclusive permanent royal jelly variant if successful. All share the appearance of a Basic Egg.

*   **Bear Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Bear Bee (100%). Purchasable in the Robux Shop for 800 Robux (previously cost more). Can also be obtained by redeeming a Bear Bee Voucher from players who bought the Basic Egg.
*   **Cobalt Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Cobalt Bee (100%). Available at the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.
*   **Crimson Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Crimson Bee (100%). Available at the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.
*   **Digital Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Digital Bee (100%). Available at Robo Bear's Shop after obtaining a Diamond Cog Amulet, requiring 7,777,777 Honey and 5 of all Drives.
*   **Festive Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Festive Bee (100%). Available at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets, or by completing specific quests/opening presents during Beesmas events.
*   **Gummy Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Gummy Bee (100%). Obtainable for 2,500 Gumdrops at the Gummy Bee Egg Claim.
*   **Photon Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Photon Bee (100%). Available at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.
*   **Puppy Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Puppy Bee (100%). Available at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets (previously 250 Tickets or 800 Robux during Beesmas 2020).
*   **Tabby Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Tabby Bee (100%). Available at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.
*   **Vicious Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Vicious Bee (100%). Obtainable from the Vicious Bee Egg Claim for 250 Stingers.
*   **Windy Bee Egg:** Always hatches into a Windy Bee (100%). Available at the Wind Shrine by donating a Spirit Petal and Cloud Vial, or via the Windy Bee Pack in the Robux Shop.

## Trivia

*   The Digital, Vicious, Windy, Bear Bee, and Gummy Bee Eggs are among the few Event bee eggs that can be obtained without spending Robux or Tickets (Bear Bee Egg can also use a Voucher).
*   Star Eggs and Basic Eggs are the only two egg types capable of hatching into a Basic Bee (or its Gifted variant via Star Egg).
*   The Mythic Egg is unique as it always hatches into a specific rarity, matching its name. It is also the only non-specific egg with a letter on it.
*   Basic Eggs are the only type of eggs that can be obtained directly using Honey.
*   Specific bee eggs and Event bee eggs all share the same basic icon in the inventory.
*   The player cannot hatch a specific bee egg onto a bee of that corresponding species (e.g., a Bumble Bee Egg cannot be hatched on a Bumble Bee).
