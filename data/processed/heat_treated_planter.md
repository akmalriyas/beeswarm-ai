# Heat-Treated Planter

The Heat-Treated Planter is a reusable planter introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. It is the red counterpart to the Hydroponic Planter, and its white counterpart is the Petal Planter. This planter can be purchased in the Red HQ after completing 150 Gifted Riley Bee quests.

## Stats & Mechanics
| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reusable** | Yes | Permanent item. |
| **Growth Time** | 12 hours | Can grow up to 50% faster in fields with red flowers. |
| **Pollen Capacity** | 25,000,000,000 Pollen | Stores approximately 25 billion pollen when fully grown. |
| **Red Pollen Multiplier** | +50% | Increases the yield of Red Pollen. |
| **Blue Pollen Multiplier** | -50% | Decreases the yield of Blue Pollen. |
| **Nectar Multipliers** | +40% Invigorating, +40% Motivating | Grants increased amounts of these nectar types upon harvest. |

## Acquisition & Cost
The Heat-Treated Planter can be crafted using the following ingredients:

*   750B Honey
*   75 Magic Beans
*   750 Red Extracts
*   150 Hard Waxes
*   25 Swirled Waxes
*   1 Turpentine

## Harvesting & Drops
When harvested, the planter grants bonus Red Extracts and Enzymes.

**Pollen Generation:**
The planter's pollen generation is influenced by its location:
*   Red bees are 33.3% more likely to sip nectar from it.
*   Blue bees are 50% less likely to sip nectar from it.

**Puffshroom Chance:**
Harvesting a fully-grown Heat-Treated Planter has a $\frac{2}{9}$ chance to spawn a Puffshroom (levels 6-7). The rarity distribution is as follows:
*   Common Puffshroom: ~72.59%
*   Rare Puffshroom: ~21.78%
*   Epic Puffshroom: ~5.44%
*   Legendary Puffshroom: ~0.18%
*   Mythic Puffshroom: ~0.01%

**Token Drops:**
When claimed, the planter gives up to 35 tokens worth of items. If fully grown when claimed, 3 guaranteed tokens include:
*   Red Extract (x1)
*   Enzymes (x1)
*   Ticket (x3)

Other drops are picked randomly from a non-exhaustive list including Bonus, Other, and Special rewards.

## Field Specific Drops & Ingredients
The planter is associated with several field-specific items:

**Red Extract:**
*   Can be harvested from Mushroom Field, Strawberry Field, Rose Field, and Pepper Patch (in stated order), with a 1 month cooldown.
*   Also available as a Rare drop from any field.

**Enzymes:**
*   Available upon harvesting at the Pineapple Patch three times in a row, with a 1 month cooldown.
*   Also available as a Rare drop from any field.

## Trivia
*   This planter and the Hydroponic Planter had models introduced during Beesmas 2021 but were not obtainable until Beesmas 2022.
*   The Heat-Treated Planter is one of five reusable planters that can be placed outside of Dapper Bear's Shop (the others being Red Clay, Blue Clay, Petal, and Hydroponic).
*   It and the Red Clay Planter are the only planters sold from the Red HQ.
