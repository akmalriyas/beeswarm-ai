# Slime

The **Slime** is a mob exclusive to the Retro Swarm Challenge. Its level varies depending on the round of the challenge.

## Behavior and Mechanics
The Slime moves constantly toward the hive, depleting flowers under its path. Stuns are ineffective at stopping the mob's forward movement; only the Trowel's walls can halt a Slime. As they level up, Slimes increase in size.

## Strategic Tips
*   **Crowd Control:** Slimes often appear in hordes alongside Zombies. It is highly recommended to use crowd-controlling bees such as the Digital Bee and Vicious Bee. Mind Hack and Impale are also effective, especially in later rounds when Slime health increases drastically.
*   **Damage Warning:** Slimes deal damage simply by making contact with you. Recklessness can lead to quick death from a horde attack.
*   **Weaponry:** The Rocket Launcher is an excellent weapon for dealing simultaneous damage to multiple slimes. Upgrading your sword to the Firebrand early provides a significant damage boost, allowing easy kills on low-to-mid level Slimes before they can inflict substantial damage. While the Illumina offers greater help, its utility may decrease around rounds 20 or 21.
*   **Sword Limitations:** Swords become less effective starting around round 20/21 due to increased health and spawn rates. At this point, investing in bees matching the Slime's level is advised, though this can be challenging for early-to-midgame players.
*   **Hitbox Vulnerability:** A Slime's hitbox is centered (the darker green sphere). Larger slimes may become completely immune to sword attacks if the swing cannot connect with this central point.
*   **Defense:** The Trowel can be used to temporarily block Slimes from approaching hives, which is crucial when clearing a horde is impossible.
*   **Passive Abilities:** Using abilities like the Star Saw (via the Passive Abilities system) can help defeat large groups of Slimes.
