# Leaves

*Note: Do not confuse Leaves with [Flowers] or [Pollen].*

**Leaves** are entities that appear in most [Fields]. They function similarly to [Sparkles], emitting leaf particles. To gather a leaf, the player must collect [Pollen] on the tile it occupies. Upon collection, a rustling sound plays, multiple leaf particles fly out, and a token appears. Rewards vary based on the field they are found in but will always be awarded in quantities of 1.

### General Mechanics
*   **Standard Reward:** Leaves often spawn specific items related to their environment (e.g., blueberries in blue fields).
*   **Aphid Chance:** Leaves have a rare chance of spawning an [Aphid]. This probability increases if the field has been inactive for some time.
*   **Stickers:** They can also summon various leaf [Stickers] in most fields.

### Field Variations and Special Rules

**Mountain Top Field**
Leaves here are significantly rarer than in any other field. They will always spawn either an [Aphid] or a [Lyrate Leaf Sticker]. On even rarer occasions, they may drop tokens such as [Bitterberry], [Neonberry], [Magic Bean], [Blowing Leaf Sticker], or [Super Smoothie].

**Stump Field & Ant Field**
Leaves in these two fields cannot spawn aphids. However, leaves found within the [Retro Swarm Challenge] can still spawn them.
