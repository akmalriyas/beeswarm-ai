# Nighttime Cycle

The Day/Night Cycle was introduced in the [2018-09-10 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-09-10 "Updates"). Prior to this update, the game remained perpetually daytime. Currently, due to issues following the 12/?/24 update, the time duration before night begins is irregular.

## Daytime
[![The game during daytime.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0a/RobloxPlayerBeta_2018-09-15_16-34-53.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20180924234406)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0a/RobloxPlayerBeta_2018-09-15_16-34-53.png/revision/latest?cb=20180924234406)

During the day, music plays normally and this is the default game setting. Previously, daytime lasted between 10 to 30 minutes (averaging 20 minutes), but recently it has shortened to 2–10 minutes.

## Nighttime
[![The game during nighttime.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/33/RobloxPlayerBeta_2018-09-24_18-02-11.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20180925001259)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/33/RobloxPlayerBeta_2018-09-24_18-02-11.png/revision/latest?cb=20180925001259)

When the day ends, the sun sets quickly, and the sky shifts to black, indicating nighttime. During this cycle, all outdoor music is replaced by [mountaincall](/wiki/Music#mountaincall "Music").

**Overrides:**
Certain areas or challenges will override the night music:
*   [vendor](/wiki/Music#vendor "Music")
*   [crawlers](/wiki/Music#crawlers "Music")
*   [gbtune1](/wiki/Music#gbtune1 "Music")
*   [hibernation1](/wiki/Music#hibernation1 "Music")
*   [starhall](/wiki/Music#starhall "Music")
*   Any music playing during an [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge"), [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"), or [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge").

**Night Mechanics:**
*   [Fireflies](/wiki/Fireflies "Fireflies") will appear, stopping in various [fields](/wiki/Fields "Fields").
*   The server background and walls turn pitch black.
*   A [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee") has an increased chance of spawning every night.
*   A moon sprout also has a 60% chance of spawning nightly.

**Firefly Interaction:**
Walking near a firefly makes it fly into the air, leaving a 1x1 patch of [sparkles](/wiki/Sparkles "Sparkles"). Fireflies can only stop in the same field three times; after this, they move to a different field. They are restricted to stopping in: [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), [Strawberry Field](/wiki/Strawberry_Field "Strawberry Field"), and [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field").

Once all fireflies are removed from a field, one of the following tokens will appear in the center of the player's formation:
*   [Moon Charms](/wiki/Moon_Charm "Moon Charm") (Increments of 1, 5, or 10).
*   [Glitter](/wiki/Glitter "Glitter") (Very rare).
*   [Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly") (Extremely rare).
*   [Night Bell](/wiki/Night_Bell "Night Bell") (Extremely rare).
*   [Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat") (Exceptionally rare).
*   [Waxing Crescent Moon Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") (Rare).
*   [Glowing Smile Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") (Extremely Rare).
*   [Star Signs](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") (Extremely Rare).
*   [Cyan Star Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") (Exceptionally Rare).
*   [Shining Star Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") (Exceptionally Rare).

**Night Duration and Mechanics:**
The night lasts 2 minutes and 35 seconds, corresponding to the duration of the 'mountaincall' theme. Since the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates") and [2018-12-19 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-12-19 "Updates"), moon-shaped platforms leading to the [Diamond Mask](/wiki/Diamond_Mask "Diamond Mask"), as well as platforms leading to [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") and the [Night Memory Match](/wiki/Memory_Match "Memory Match"), turn solid yellow only during nighttime if the player possesses a [Moon Amulet](/wiki/Moon_Amulet "Moon Amulet"). (Note: If the player owns the Diamond Mask, these platforms remain permanently blue.)

When planting [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") at night, there is a 60% chance they will be moon sprouts. This alters the odds of all other sprout types (Supreme Sprouts become more common, while others become rarer).

## Trivia
*   If the Stick Bug Challenge concludes during the night, normal music will play instead of the nighttime theme. If the time cycles from night to day or vice versa while a [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm") or [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower") is active, the fog will disappear, but the event itself will continue.
*   Occasionally, the nighttime OST simply fades out rather than ending to transition into daytime.
*   [Night Bell](/wiki/Night_Bell "Night Bell") and [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") can manually summon nighttime. [Onett (Developer)](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)") can also trigger this globally, accompanied by a message (e.g., "🌙 One of Mother Bear's cubs has summoned nighttime... 🌙"). A full gallery of these messages is available on the Night Bell page.
