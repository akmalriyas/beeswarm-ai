# Festive Wreath

The **Festive Wreath** is a Level 10 [Beesmas] [Beequip]. It has an equip limit of one and requires a Red color requirement. This item can only be equipped to [Festive Bee].

"This ornate wreath was specially made to suit Festive Bee's tastes."

## Acquisition Methods

**Current Method:**
*   Opening the [Festive Gift Box] during [Beesmas], located behind [Onett's Lid Art].

**Outdated Methods:**
*   Opening the Festive Gift Box during Beesmas 2021, 2022, and 2024. (Prior to Beesmas Summer 2024, players received 250 Tickets and a Festive Planter instead).
*   Completing Bee Bear's 25th Beesmas 2020 quest.

## Trivia

*   The **Festive Wreath** shares several similarities with the [Reindeer Antlers]: they are the only two Beequips to have visible models on the equipped bee, and before the 2024-01-12 update removed permanent Beequips, they were the only permanent ones.
*   This item is one of three Beequips that grant a unique ability; the others are [Snowglobe] and [Reindeer Antlers].
*   The model used for the Festive Wreath on the Festive Bee is based on the Deluxe Christmas Wreath Roblox accessory.
