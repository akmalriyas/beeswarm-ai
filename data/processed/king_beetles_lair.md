# King Beetle's Lair

**King Beetle's Lair** is a specific location where the King Beetle spawns. It can be accessed via a light green door located between Blue HQ and Clover Field, or next to the far end of the Blue Flower Field. To exit the lair, players must climb a ladder adjacent to the entrance. The interior space is completely empty, save for the ladder leading out.

The doors are one-way; standing too close to either entrance will push the player through, even if they are stationary. A notable feature of the lair is that its entrance cycles in color, making it stand out from the surrounding environment.

The King Beetle specifically spawns on the back wall adjacent to the Noob Shop and Lava Obby.

Near the back right corner of the lair, there is text on the floor reading "Song Name." This serves as a hint for the code "Crawlers," which refers to the music that plays inside the lair.

## Music
When inside King Beetle's Lair, the audio track "Crawlers" plays. This same piece of music can also be heard in Commando Chick's Hideout and White Tunnel.

## Trivia
*   **Early Game Bug:** In early versions, players who stood too close to the side of the lair nearest the Noob Shop could die due to lava blocks clipping into the wall from the Lava Obby; this issue was later patched.
*   The bottom of the pole belonging to the Honeystorm Summoner sign pokes slightly into the top of the lair structure.
*   If a sprout is present in the Clover Field, its light column and remaining pollen count can be observed within the lair. This visual effect is similar to when Rogue Vicious Bee's spikes and clouds spawn in the Clover Field.
*   A known glitch allows the King Beetle to fail its lunge and get stuck when positioned near the 'Song Name' corner.
*   During the Beesmas 2018 Event, the red roof of the lair was visible on the sides of flower blocks in the Clover Field. Even after the event, this red glow remains visible near the Noob Shop wall, regardless of whether it is snowy.
*   Any tool can collect pollen from the Clover Field inside the lair if the player continuously jumps and possesses sufficient Jump Power. However, the Spark Staff does not require excessive Jump Power for this action.
*   With enough Jump Power and a high jump, players can trigger the Rhino Beetle and Ladybug in the Clover Field. Additionally, the player's bees may mistakenly perceive they are in the Clover Field and begin collecting pollen.
*   The entrance to the lair is lighter than the surrounding ground color and features subtle changes in hue. During Beesmas, this contrast is much more pronounced as the surrounding walls turn white.
*   The cycling colors of the entrance match the cycling patterns found on any supreme-tier amulet.
