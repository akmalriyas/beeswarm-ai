# Fireflies

Fireflies are passive mobs that appear exclusively during nighttime in the game. Individually, they produce sparkles on the flowers they land upon. When grouped together, they generate various items for the player.

## Mechanics and Spawning

As soon as night begins, a group of eight fireflies will spawn above the Star Hall. These fireflies then fly to a random field. They can target one of the following fields:
*   Spider Field
*   Bamboo Field
*   Strawberry Field
*   Pineapple Patch
*   Cactus Field
*   Rose Field

Upon reaching their designated field, they land randomly in a circle, facing towards the center. While resting, fireflies continue to spawn sparkles on the flower they are occupying.

**Interacting with Fireflies:**
A player can force a firefly to fly up by walking close to it (within a three-flower radius). If a firefly is nudged away from its position, the flower it was sitting on will generate sparkles. When all eight fireflies are successfully nudged and fly up, potential drops may appear in the middle of their formation.

**Cycle Behavior:**
The fireflies repeat this cycle (flying to a field, being gathered) three times in one location before targeting a new field. This process continues until daytime arrives. When dawn breaks, they return above the Star Hall and eventually despawn.

## Trivia

*   The face model of a firefly is identical to that of the Gummy Bee, but its color is black rather than cyan.
*   Fireflies are one of only five passive mobs in the game (along with frogs, chicks, and bean bugs). They were the first passive mob introduced and are the only passive mobs that spawn exclusively at night.
*   If a player uses a Night Bell while a group of fireflies is leaving, another group will replace them; however, an unknown update caused the original group to return instead of a new one.
*   Despite consisting of eight individuals, the Glowing Smile Sticker pattern only displays seven "fireflies."
