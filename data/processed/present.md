# Present

A **Present** is a Beesmas-exclusive inventory item, introduced in 2018 and returning annually for the Beesmas event. It is distinct from [Ornament Presents](/wiki/Ornament_Presents) and [Gift Boxes](/wiki/Gift_Boxes).

> "A festively wrapped gift. Exchange it with an NPC for prizes!"

## Mechanics
Presents are given to most NPCs during the Beesmas event in exchange for various rewards, primarily specialized Ornaments that can be placed on the [Beesmas Tree](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree) to provide buffs. Each NPC can only receive one gift from a player.

To give a present, the player must interact with an eligible NPC. If the player does not have a Present in their inventory or has recently gifted that NPC, normal dialogue will display. Some NPCs may require specific prerequisites before accepting a gift.

## Acquisition
Presents can be obtained through several methods during the Beesmas event:

*   **Bee Bear Quests:** Multiple quests from [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear) award a total of 10 Presents, along with other rewards. (Note: This count has varied across different years.)
*   **Bee Bear’s Catalog:** The catalog sells 1 Present for 250 [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake) and 10 [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear).
*   **Onett's Hive Claim:** [Onett](/wiki/Onett) gives 1 Present upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event period.
*   **Galentine Shrine Donation:** Donating [Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry) to the [Galentine Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine#Galentine_Shrine) has a chance to yield 1 Present, along with other rewards.
*   **Robo Party Rank:** Completing [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party)'s rank 25 for the first time awards 1 Present, along with other rewards.
*   **Event Codes (Historical):** Specific codes have historically granted Presents (e.g., "LatePresents" gave 1 Present; "ByeBeeBear" gave 1 Present in 2018).

## Evolution of Presents
The mechanics and recipients of the Present item have changed over time:

*   **Beesmas 2018:** Presents were introduced solely for quest completion. They did not grant Ornaments, as they had not yet been added to the game.
*   **Beesmas 2020:** The list of NPCs who could receive presents expanded.
*   **Beesmas 2021:** [Dapper Bear](/wiki/Dapper_Bear) was added as a recipient.
*   **Beesmas 2022:** Presents could now be given to the new NPC, [Robo Bear](/wiki/Robo_Bear).
*   **Beesmas 2024 (Winter):** The Present could now be given to [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear). Event codes were also introduced for acquisition.
*   **Beesmas 2025:** Presents returned, maintaining their function of adding Ornaments to the Beesmas Tree.

## Trivia
*   **2018 Event:** There were 11 Present tokens in Beesmas 2018, but only 10 could be used. The token in the 30 Bee Zone was replaced by an [Enzymes](/wiki/Enzymes) token when the event ended.
*   **2021 Bug:** During Beesmas 2021, players who had gifted a Present to Honey Bee before January 6, 2022, were refunded their present due to a bug associated with the Bag of Honey Ornament's boost.
