# Dark Scythe

The **Dark Scythe** is a specialized pollen collector that can be crafted in the Red HQ. It requires players to have completed at least 250 Gifted Riley Bee quests to unlock and craft it.

The Dark Scythe is not a multiplier collector for Red Pollen, similar to the Tide Popper.

## Crafting Ingredients

To craft the Dark Scythe, players require the following materials:

*   2.5T Honey
*   1,500 Red Extracts
*   150 Stingers
*   100 Hard Waxes
*   50 Caustic Waxes
*   50 Super Smoothies
*   3 Invigorating Vials

## Mechanics and Effects

**Dark Flame Interaction:**
If the Dark Scythe collects pollen from a flame, it converts that flame into a **Dark Flame**. This process also reapplies flame fuel (if active) and resets the flame's lifespan.

**Dark Heat Buff:**
A Dark Flame grants a stack of **Dark Heat**, which provides significant buffs:
*   Initial Stack: +10% Instant Red Ability Conversion and x1.05 Super-Crit Power.
*   Stacking Effect: Each additional Dark Flame increases the buff by an extra x0.05 Super-Crit Power and +1% Instant Red Ability Conversion.
*   Cap: The buffs cap at x6 Super-Crit Power and 109% Instant Red Ability Conversion.

**Dark Flame Benefits:**
*   An active Dark Flame always grants a stack of Dark Heat, regardless of whether the player is touching it.
*   Touching a Dark Flame increases growth for Scorching Stars by 33%.
*   Dark Flames deal 50% more damage than regular flames.

## Trivia and History

*   The Dark Scythe's counterparts include the Tide Popper (blue) and Gummyballer (white).
*   Its scooping trail has the ability to pop bubbles.
*   This item is tied with the Tide Popper for being the fifth most expensive collectible in the game, both costing 2.5T Honey.
*   Historically, there were exploits that allowed players to obtain this tool without meeting the 250 Gifted Riley Bee quest requirement; however, these glitches were patched.
*   The Dark Scythe is one of the few items lacking a detailed in-game stat description.
