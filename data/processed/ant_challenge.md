The **Ant Challenge** is a challenge found past the Ant Gate, where the objective is to defeat as many ants as possible.

## Accessing and Participating

To participate in the Ant Challenge, the player must have 20 Bees. An Ant Pass is also required for each attempt.

Players can obtain a free Ant Pass every two hours by using the Free Ant Pass Dispenser located behind the Ant Gate. If players wish to acquire more passes, they can purchase one at the Ant Pass Dispenser for 10 Tickets. Additionally, Panda Bear sometimes grants ant passes through his quests.

## Gameplay Mechanics

Upon entering the challenge area, any remaining pollen in the player's bag is instantly converted. The player has a time limit of 5 minutes to defeat as many ants as possible. A timer on the anthill displays the remaining time.

Ants spawn in waves, starting with Level 1 and 2 ants, and increasing in level with each subsequent wave. Each wave features an assortment of Regular Ants, Fire Ants, Army Ants, and/or Flying Ants. Every fifth wave always contains a Giant Ant, except for every 40th wave, which contains two Giant Ants. Defeating any ant grants 1 point.

To progress to the next wave, players must fill the meter on the anthill by gathering pollen. The initial requirement is 4,000 pollen, and this requirement increases by at least 2,000 more than the previous wave for each subsequent wave.

[![A lawnmower during the Ant Challenge.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1f/Lawn_Mower.png/revision/latest?cb=20230421142803)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1f/Lawn_Mower.png/revision/latest?cb=20230421142803) [](/wiki/File:Lawn_Mower.png)

**Danger Zones:**
Starting at Wave 8, the white doors on the sides of the arena will begin flashing red. This indicates that a lawnmower is about to pass through the lane directed by that door. Players must move away from targeted lanes to avoid damage. Being hit by a lawnmower deals 50 base damage.

Lawnmowers become faster and appear more frequently as the challenge progresses, with multiple lawnmowers potentially running simultaneously near the end of the challenge.

**Challenge End:**
The challenge concludes when the player dies or when the 5-minute timer expires, whichever comes first. A common glitch allows players with the Haste+ buff to leave the challenge by walking up the ant hill.

## Tips and Strategy

*   **Preparation:** Collect as many Ability Tokens (such as Haste, Baby Love, Bear Morph) as possible before starting.
*   **Offense:** Rage tokens can be used to defeat ants faster by increasing Bee Attack. Mutations that boost attack are also highly beneficial.
*   **Bee Stats:** Higher Bond level bees make later waves easier. Bees with higher attack and/or speed are generally more effective than those with lower stats.
*   **Key Abilities:** A high-level Vicious Bee is vital, as its Impale ability deals significant damage to multiple ants. Wait until many ants are present before activating it for maximum effect.
*   **Sustained Combat:** Gifted Stubborn Bees increase the lifespan of ability tokens, giving more time to spawn ants before using abilities.
*   **Area Damage:** Windy Bee's Tornado can be extremely effective, dealing massive area damage (which cannot miss), collecting pollen, and gathering tokens. Haste stacks and powerful sprinklers are needed to maximize its potential.
*   **Pollen Collection:** Honey tokens can fill the progress meter. Consider using Honey Bees or Diamond Bees to spawn Honey Gift tokens, or use a Festive Bee to collect honey from Festive Gifts.
*   **Buffs & Gear:** Amulets that boost bee attack damage, combined with Gifted Brave Bees and Gifted Rage Bees, aid in ant defeat. The Fire Mask and Demon Mask boost attack and add Flame-generating passives; well-placed Flames grant Flame Heat, collect pollen, and deal damage.
*   **Damage & Utility:** Stingers provide a short-term attack boost for clearing ants and dealing with high-level threats. Red Extracts, Blue Extracts, Oils, Glues, Tropical Drinks, and Gumdrops help with honey production and bee capabilities (though not required for a good score). Coconuts are unpredictable but powerful; they deal incredible damage to high-level ants and collect decent pollen. If the player has Coconut Clogs, catching coconuts also grants Coconut Haste, temporarily increasing speed—useful for escaping danger.
*   **Timing:** To maximize scores, focus on defeating as many ants as possible in the first three minutes, as later waves are more difficult and lawnmowers appear faster and more frequently.
*   **Tool Usage:** Tools are excellent for filling the honey meter since bees cannot collect pollen while attacking. However, if there are too many ants and your bees cannot handle them, do not use a Tool, as it will fill the honey meter (if in the field) and summon more ants.
    *   *Note:* Since the Ant Challenge provides 100% Instant Conversion, attempting to collect tokens from Coin Scatter or coconuts is generally useless as they have nothing to convert from.
*   **Field Management:** Sprinklers can help regenerate flowers, making pollen collection easier when ants and lawnmowers restrict access. During Beesmas, playing with the Honeyday Buff increases how fast the meter fills.
*   **Movement & Safety:** Using a Glider helps players glide over ants and fire trails to escape situations. Zooming out allows players to track ant locations and potential lawnmower paths. When new ants spawn, move as far away from the anthill as possible to avoid being hit by an incoming ant.
*   **Passive Abilities:** Star Saw (a Supreme Star Amulet passive) is very effective for defeating ants and collecting pollen. Standing near the side of the field where lawnmowers emerge can aid in avoidance. Players can use the small gap between the field and the wall to avoid fire trails, though damage from lawnmowers and ants remains possible.
*   **Honeystorm:** If a Honeystorm is summoned, prioritize collecting honey tokens in earlier waves or when ant density is low, as these contribute significantly to the honey meter.
*   **Coconut Canister Shield:** While the Coconut Canister's Emergency Coconut Shield is active, being hit by lawnmowers will heal the player instead of damaging them. Intentionally activating this shield 2–4 minutes before starting can prevent wasting it in early levels.
*   **Ant Prediction:** Although ant spawns are random, patterns exist: Flying Ants are generally more common than Army Ants during the first 15 rounds; after 15 rounds, Army Ants become much more prevalent. Giant Ants spawn every 5 waves and always appear with either Fire or Regular Ants. This knowledge is useful for quests requiring specific ant types.
*   **Nectar:** Invigorating Nectar can help buff bee attack.

## Trivia

*   **Whirligig Glitch:** Previously, using a Whirligig during the Ant Challenge would teleport the player to the starting platform after entering the hive area, ending the challenge and granting an amulet based on the current score. This behavior has changed across updates: it was fixed to teleport the player to the center of the field, but later updates caused players to be teleported back to their hive, which then returned them to the challenge platform, also ending the challenge.
*   **Lawnmower Interaction:** Despite being lawnmowers, they do not affect any flowers when passing through.
*   **Historical Limits:** Prior to the 2018-11-25 update, the Ant Challenge could only hold up to 20 ants at once.
*   **Jelly Beans Reward:** Since the Egg Hunt 2019 update, Jelly Beans are available as a reward from the Ant Challenge. If players use jelly beans while inside or immediately outside the challenge area, no jelly bean tokens will appear.
*   **Instant Conversion Buff:** Upon entering the arena, the player's bag instantly converts, granting a +100% Instant Conversion boost (represented by the Ant Pass icon). There was a past visual glitch where this buff persisted after leaving the challenge; however, it was confirmed not to be an active mechanical boost. Before the 2018-11-25 update, an expired Haste token represented this boost.
*   **Hidden Token:** An ant pass token is hidden behind the Ant Challenge area. It can be found by going behind the All-Time Top Ant Exterminators leaderboard and following the hallway around the corner, which also leads to the Gummy Bee Egg Claim.
*   **Death State Glitch:** If a player equipped with a tool dies right as the challenge is about to end, they will respawn without their pollen collector equipped, although their character's arm will appear positioned as if holding it.
*   **Pollen Meter Bug Fix:** A bug where the pollen meter displayed the cumulative total instead of the required amount for the next wave was fixed in the 2018-09-10 update.
*   **Glitched Entry:** Players can glitch into the Ant Field without triggering the challenge by using a slow internet connection, Bear Morph, or Haste+. If a player who glitched inside is present when someone starts the challenge, they will be kicked out.
*   **Sprinkler Persistence:** Sprinklers left in the field remain there if the challenge ends without the player dying.
*   **Glitched Exit:** If a player manages to glitch out of the Ant Challenge, they are teleported back to the center of the field. Repeatedly doing this will eventually cause the challenge to end with the message: "Ant Challenge ended because you left."
*   **Giant Ant Bond:** Giant Ants grant 3 bond to the player's bees, which is considered the least efficient method for leveling up bees.
*   **Wave Limit:** The challenge can only reach 100 waves due to the time limit (5 minutes or 300 seconds) and the approximate 3-second progression time per wave.
