# Eggs in Bee Swarm Simulator

Eggs are inventory items that players drag into their hive to hatch a bee. They can also be donated to the Wind Shrine or used to roll on the Sticker Printer. Eggs are fundamental to starting gameplay and come in various types, some offering chances at different bee rarities while others guarantee a specific outcome.

Currently, there are 6 main egg types (or 11 if counting gifted variants), not including Plastic Eggs, specific bee eggs, the Flight of the Bumble Egg, or the Swarming Egg of the Hive.

## General Mechanics

*   **Hatching:** Players can hatch an egg on top of an existing bee in their hive. The level of the host bee remains unchanged, acting like a Royal Jelly variant.
*   **Availability:** Eggs are available for purchase in the Robux Shop and through various in-game vendors (like the Ticket Tent).
*   **Regional Restrictions:** Due to regulations in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands, Belgium, and Australia, many eggs purchased from the Robux Shop are unavailable to consumers in those regions.

## Egg Types by Rarity

### Basic Egg
The most common egg type. It has a high chance of hatching into a Basic Bee (87%), but can also hatch into Rare (10%), Epic (2.5%), Legendary (0.5%), or Gifted Bee (0.4%). There is a 0.348% chance of getting a Gifted Basic Bee from the egg. Mythic and Event bees cannot be hatched from this egg. This egg also cannot be used on a Basic Bee.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Free upon starting the game.
*   Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop near the Sunflower Field (Price scales up with each purchase).
*   Rewards 1 Basic Egg for adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack.

### Silver Egg
Most likely hatches into a Rare bee (64.9%), but has chances of hatching into Epic (30%), Legendary (5%), or Mythic (0.1%). Cannot hatch Basic or Event bees.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 50 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 100 Robux.
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Silver Egg questline.
*   Reward from Brown Bear's 25th quest.
*   Completing the 25th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine rewards 1 Silver Egg.
*   Low chance drop from Mantis, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Rare Sprouts, and leaves (when collecting pollen in Clover Field).
*   Drop from Festive Planter or rare reward from Stockings/Winter Memory Match.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Expired code "PreUpdate."
*   Beesmas 2020 rewards (Bubble Bee Man's Naughty List, Bee Bear's Catalog purchases for various events).

### Gifted Silver Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Silver Egg, but always hatches the bee into its gifted variant.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances on subsequent defeats).
*   Top 25 in Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Completing Black Bear's "Getting Stumped" quest.
*   100th quest completion of Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
*   Rare drops from Werewolf, Stump Snail, Scorpion, and Sticker Sprout.

### Gold Egg
Most likely hatches into an Epic bee (79%), with chances for Legendary (20%) or Mythic (1%). Cannot hatch Basic, Rare, or Event bees. Five Gold Eggs are required to craft the Honey Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 100 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 200 Robux each.
*   Rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge or Extreme Memory Match.
*   Token form in Werewolf's Cave (guarded by two Cave Monsters).
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Gold Egg questline.
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 50th, 340th, and 390th quests.
*   50th quest completion of Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
*   Capturing Commando Chick 10 times.
*   Low chance drop from King Beetle or extremely low chance from Scorpion.
*   Rare drops from Epic sprouts, leaves (in Clover Field), various Planters, Stockings, Festive Nymph, and Winter Memory Match.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Ornament Presents/Black Friday Bundle purchases; Wind Shrine donations during specific Beesmas events; Bee Bear's Catalog purchases for various events; Gilded Gift Box opening.

### Gifted Gold Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Gold Egg, but always hatches the bee into its gifted variant. Cannot be used to craft the Honey Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances on subsequent defeats).
*   Top 25 in Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Playtime Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 50 Gifted Bucko Bee or 50 Gifted Riley Bee quests.
*   275 Brown Bear quests completed.
*   150th quest completion of Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
*   Very rare drops from Stump Snail, Coconut Crab, and Mantis.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Mondo Present purchases during Beesmas 2019.

### Diamond Egg
Most likely hatches into a Legendary bee (95%), with a rare chance for Mythic (5%). Cannot hatch Basic, Rare, Epic, or Event bees. Five Diamond Eggs are required to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Token located past Bear Gate (requires Glider/Parachute near Shadow Bear).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 400 Robux each.
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 400 Tickets each.
*   Rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge or Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Black Bear's Diamond Egg questline.
*   Panda Bear's "Colossal Combat" quest (5th star treat/ant quest).
*   Dapper Bear's 10th quest, "The Long Haul."
*   Brown Bear's 75th and 525th quests.
*   75th quest completion of Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
*   Rare drops from Stump Snail, Diamond Aphids, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Coconut Crab.
*   Very rare drops from Spiders, Mantises, and Werewolves.
*   Guaranteed drop from Supreme sprouts; Rare chance from Legendary sprouts or leaves (in Clover Field).
*   Rare reward from Winter Memory Match or catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Various Robux Shop offers (Memorial Day Deal, Summer Treat Pack, Diamond Moon Pack, Sparkly Starter Pack, Stocking Stuffer Pack, Dual-Diamond Basket, Beginner's Bean Bundle, etc.).

### Gifted Diamond Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Diamond Egg, but always hatches the bee into its gifted variant. Cannot be used to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances on subsequent defeats).
*   Top 25 in Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers.
*   Quest Grandmaster badge reward from donating Tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing specific quests: 100, 250, 500, 750, and 1,000 Gifted Bucko Bee or Gifted Riley Bee quests; 425 Brown Bear quests completed; 200th quest completion of Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
*   Extremely rare drops from Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, or Coconut Crab.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Mondo Present purchases during Beesmas 2019.

### Mythic Egg
Always hatches into a Mythic bee (100%). The player can obtain a Gifted Mythic bee variant, but this is very rare.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Top 25 in Daily Top Memory Matchers.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 1,200 Robux each (limited to 3 per player).
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 2,500 Tickets each.
*   Very rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge or Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Black Bear's 75th quest ("Myth in The Making").
*   Dapper Bear's 15th quest ("More Than Myth").
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
*   300th quest completion of Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
*   Capturing Commando Chick for the 50th time guarantees a Mythic Egg.
*   Extremely rare drops from Stump Snail, Mondo Chick (higher chance when defeated faster), and Coconut Crab.
*   Catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Rare drop from Festive Planter or Winter Memory Match.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 500 Snowflakes and 100 Gingerbread Bears.
*   Gummy Bear’s Goo Year’s Beacon quest during Beesmas 2025.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Various expired purchases (Bee Bear's Catalog, Robux Shop deals); Opening Mondo/Mythic Gift Boxes; Party Mechsquito/Party Cogmower drops.

### Gifted Mythic Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Mythic Egg, but always hatches the bee into its gifted variant.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Top 25 in Daily Top Rare Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   500 Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quests completed.
*   Nearly impossible drop from Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 10,000 Snowflakes and 150 Gingerbread Bears.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Various expired purchases (Hefty Honeyday Bundle, Merry Mondo Bundle, Winter Beesmas 2024 catalog purchase); Mondo Gift Box drops; specific Robux Shop bundles.

### Choose-A-Mythic Egg
Allows the player to select any Mythic Bee upon hatching.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing the Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul from the Robux Shop for 4,500 Robux.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Expired limited-time offers in the Robux Shop.

### Star Egg
Hatches into any type of gifted bee not already present in the player's hive (excluding Gifted Event bees). If all non-event gifted bees are owned, it hatches a random gifted bee. It will only hatch a Gifted Mythic Bee after all other non-event gifted bees are collected.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances on subsequent defeats).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 per player).
*   Extremely rare chance drop from Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Completing Mother Bear's first ten quests ("Seven To Seven").
*   Panda Bear's "Royal Rumble" quest (first ten star treat/ant quests).
*   Exceptionally rare drops from King Beetle, Festive Planter, and Party Mechsquito.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Various limited-time Robux Shop offers.

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   **Iconography:** Any specific bee egg or event bee egg shares the same basic egg icon in inventory.
*   **Rarity Naming:** The Mythic Egg is unique as it matches the rarity of the bee it hatches into, and it is the only non-specific/non-event egg with a letter on it.
*   **Hatching Limitations:** Players cannot hatch a specific bee egg onto that same type of bee (e.g., Bumble Bee Egg cannot be hatched on a Bumble Bee). Basic Eggs also cannot be used on Basic Bees, even though they don't guarantee a Basic Bee outcome.
*   **Basic Egg Utility:** Basic Eggs are the only standard eggs obtainable directly with Honey.
*   **Gifted Variants:** The Gifted Silver Egg and Star Egg are unique gifted eggs that can be obtained by completing main questlines from NPCs (Black Bear, Mother Bear, Panda Bear).
*   **Uniqueness:** The Choose-A-Mythic Egg is the only egg type that allows the player to select which bee hatches.
