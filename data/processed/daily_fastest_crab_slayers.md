# Daily Fastest Crab Slayers

The **Daily Fastest Crab Slayers** is one of 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator, viewable only through the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page. This leaderboard tracks a player's fastest time to defeat the Coconut Crab on any given day.

It displays 10 positions at a time, and players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

**Top 5 Finishers:**
Players who reach the top 5 by the end of the day are awarded the **Cyan Hilted Sword Sticker**.

**Top 100 Finishers:**
Players who reach the top 100 by the end of the day are awarded 25 **Tickets**.

## Trivia

*   The leaderboard contains a known glitch where it inputs the player's best recorded time for slaying the crab, even if their current kill on the Coconut Crab is slower than that previous best.
