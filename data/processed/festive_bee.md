# Festive Bee

**Festive Bee** is a Red Event bee, known for its jolly nature and love of giving gifts. It can be purchased from the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.

## Acquisition and Habitat

Like all Event bees, the Festive Bee does not have a favorite treat. It can be made Gifted by feeding it a Star Treat, Gingerbread Bear, or Aged Gingerbread Bear.

**Preferred Fields:**
*   Mountain Top Field
*   Mushroom Field
*   Pine Tree Forest

**Disliked Fields:**
*   Blue Flower Field

## Abilities

The Festive Bee possesses three unique ability tokens:

*   **Festive Gift:** Grants random gifts (including Treats, honey, and the "Festive Blessing") to all players on the server. Gifts are improved with Level. A message displays: "🎁 Festive Gifts from [Player's Display Name]! 🎁". If gifted, this token can grant rarer items and "Beesmas Cheer."
*   **Honey Mark:** Marks a random area on the field for 7 seconds (+0.2s per level). While standing in the marked area, players gain 2 Conversion Links and x1.25 Convert Rate. This effect stacks up to 3 times.
*   **Red Bomb+:** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding red Flowers (+10% pollen per level). This ability can be combined with other bomb abilities to increase power.

## Trivia

*   When the Festive Bee's egg was moved to the Ticket Tent, it replaced Gummy Bee's spot.
*   This was the third Event bee to have ability tokens that can be summoned by non-Event bees (Honey Mark and Red Bomb+). The first were Photon Bee (Haste) and Vicious Bee (Blue Bomb+).
*   The Festive Bee likes the Pine Tree Forest, which is a reference to most Christmas trees being pine trees. This also makes it the only red bee to like this field.
*   Festive Bee, Buoyant Bee, and Digital Bee are the only bees that can apply buffs to the entire server (specifically Balloons, Festive Gifts, and Field Corruption).
*   Festive Bee, Rage Bee, and Spicy Bee are the only red bees to produce tokens that do not primarily benefit only red bees. (Festive Bee yields Honey Mark tokens; Rage Bee and Spicy Bee yield Rage tokens.)
*   This was the second bee available for purchase with Robux, following Bear Bee. It was also the first bee obtainable through both Quests and Robux; and the second bee overall to be obtained through quests (after Gummy Bee).
*   When Festive Gift activates, it plays the opening notes of "Deck the Halls." When collecting the Festive Blessing and Beesmas Cheer tokens, players can hear the next few notes.
*   A First Edition Festive Bee's flag is offset and placed behind where it normally sits to accommodate its bow.
*   Due to a bug, Festive Bees obtained from the Ticket Tent before the 2019-09-28 Update might have received a First Edition Flag in that update even if they were not true First Edition Bees.
*   It was the only Event bee that could be obtained in two different ways (excluding Robux packs) at the same time: via The Festive Present next to Bee Bear and in the Ticket Tent for 500 tickets during Beesmas 2019.
*   Festive Bee and Windy Bee are the only bees with tinted wings when they are not gifted.
*   Bee Bear's Festive Bee is female.
*   The bee was obtainable during the first three Beesmas events via means besides tickets:
    *   **Beesmas 2018:** By completing the Bee Bear questline or purchasing the Festive Bee Pack for 1,600 Robux in the Robux Shop.
    *   **Beesmas 2019:** Through The Festive Present event. If the player already owned Festive Bee, they would receive 500 Tickets instead.
    *   **Beesmas 2020:** After finishing Bee Bear’s first questline. If the player already owned a Festive Bee, they were awarded 500 Tickets instead.
*   Puppy Bee and Demon Bee are the only Bees that have an exclusive beequip for themselves.
*   Festive Bee is the only non-colorless bee capable of making Mark tokens.
*   After Beesmas 2020, the models of Festive and Puppy Bee (wearing their respective exclusive beequips) were moved outside of the main map, located behind Commando Chick's Hideout.
*   Festive Bee and Riley Bee are the only red bees to like three fields.
*   Festive Bee is the only bee with a Beesmas-exclusive sticker.
