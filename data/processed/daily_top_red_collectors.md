# Daily Top Red Pollen Collectors

The **Daily Top Red Pollen Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. This leaderboard tracks how much red pollen players have collected during the current day.

## Mechanics and Viewing

*   **Display:** The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.
*   **In-Game Notification:** When a player steps on the designated white pad in front of the leaderboard, a message will appear stating: "You've collected 'X' Red Pollen today (Resets in 'Y')."

## Location

The leaderboard is located inside the Red HQ, situated between the ladder and the Red Field Booster.

## Prizes

Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 receive either the **Left Mythic Gem Fleuron Sticker** or the **Right Mythic Gem Fleuron Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 receive 25 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
