# Bomber Guard

![Bomber Guard](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7b/Bomber_Guard.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403022713 "Bomber Guard")

## Overview
The **Bomber Guard** is a piece of equipment worn on the left shoulder, crafted by a Bomber Bee. It is purchasable in the Pro Shop.

> *"A left shoulder pad crafted by a Bomber Bee."*

### Stats & Bonuses
| Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Capacity | +15,000 |
| White Pollen | +5% |
| Bomb Pollen | +15% |

### Crafting Details
**Cost:**
*   300,000 Honey
*   25 Sunflower Seeds

## Trivia
*   The Bomber Guard is one of four guards available in the Pro Shop. The others are the Looker Guard, Hasty Guard, and Brave Guard.
*   Both the Bomber Guard and the Looker Guard require the exact same amount of honey and crafting materials to produce.
