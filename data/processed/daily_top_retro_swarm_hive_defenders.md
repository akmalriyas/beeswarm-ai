# Daily Top Retro Swarm Hive Defenders

The **Daily Top Retro Swarm Hive Defenders** is one of the game's leaderboards, viewable exclusively through the Global Leaderboards menu in the System Page. This leaderboard tracks and displays the highest scores players achieve on a given day during the Retro Swarm Challenge.

## Mechanics and Ranking
*   **Visibility:** The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time; players can scroll to view up to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Rewards
Players receive rewards based on their ranking by the end of the day:
*   **Top 25:** Awarded a [Turpentine](/wiki/Turpentine "Turpentine").
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 50 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
