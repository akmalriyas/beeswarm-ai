# Boost Market (Archived Content)

**Note:** This content has been removed from the game and is provided for archival purposes only.

The Boost Market was a shop that could be found in the 30 Bee Zone, located past the Bear Gate on the right side of the map. It was active until the Beesmas 2021 update, at which point it was replaced by the Nectar Pot.

### Mechanics
Every 30 minutes, the Boost Market allowed players to purchase a market boost. The price of these boosts fluctuated based on demand, similar to a real stock market. To purchase a boost, players needed both Tickets and Honey. All purchased boosts lasted for 30 minutes.

Market boosts could also be obtained through specific codes (such as `RedMarket`) or during events like the Ready Player Two event. When a player successfully purchased a market boost, a unique audio clip would play.

### Trivia
*   The Boost Market was removed because Onett deemed it a "Failed Experiment," and the prices became excessively high due to exploitative purchasing.
*   Along with the Boost Market, Evictions and the Most Commando Captures Leaderboard are among the few pieces of content that were removed from the game but were not exclusive to specific events.
