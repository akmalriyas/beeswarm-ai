# Panda Bear

Panda Bear is a permanent quest giver and one of the eight available bears in Bee Swarm Simulator. He is located on a small hill between the Bamboo Field and the Blue Flower Field, right next to the Gumdrop Shop and the Global Top Battlers Leaderboard.

Panda Bear was a quest giver for the Egg Hunt 2019 and gave the Boxing Glove ornament during Beesmas (2019 and beyond).

### Accessing Panda Bear

Panda Bear can be reached in several ways:
*   By climbing the ladder on the side of the platform closest to the Blue HQ.
*   By jumping onto the platform if the player has sufficient Jump Power.
*   Alternatively, by jumping on the Gumdrop Shop and then up to Panda Bear.

## Dialogue & Lore

Panda Bear provides unique information about the game mechanics and lore during his quests.

### Main Quest Dialogues (Summary)
*   **Lesson on Ladybugs:** Introduces basic combat training. Notes that enemies lose 1 energy when attacked, and players can check bee energy in the hive.
*   **Rhino Rumble:** Reinforces that Rhino Beetles are weak but aggressive. Explains that bees use 1 energy per attack and must return to the hive to sleep.
*   **Beetle Battle:** Introduces the concept of bee leveling. If a bee is lower level than the enemy, its attacks will miss.
*   **Spider Slayer:** Discusses the utility of [Royal Jelly] for transforming bees (e.g., Brave Bee, Rage Bee, Lion Bee). Notes that the Spider is fast and deadly.
*   **Ladybug Bonker:** Explains that Silver Eggs always hatch into Rare, Epic, Legendary, or Mythic bees.
*   **The REAL Final Showdown:** Confirms the reward of a Gold Egg upon completion.
*   **Ant Arrival:** Introduces the Ant Challenge mechanic (gathering pollen/honey to fill a meter) and how scoring works.
*   **Colossal Combat:** Reveals that Giant Ants were once mountain-sized. Explains that Diamond Eggs always hatch into Legendary Bees, and Star Eggs yield Gifted Bees with increased stats.
*   **Eager Exterminator:** Warns players about the LAWNMOWER during the Ant Challenge—a fast, deadly foe immune to attacks.
*   **Outrageous Onslaught:** Provides advanced tips: obtaining Gifted Brave and Gifted Rage bees (which permanently increase ALL bee attack by 1), and hunting amulets like Diamond Ant Amulets and King Beetle Amulets for attack boosts.
*   **Royal Rumble:** The quest is a reference to professional wrestling. Explains that Star Eggs yield unique Gifted Bees, which are necessary to generate [Star Amulets] in the Star Hall.
*   **Ultimate Ant Annihilation 5:** Concludes his training arc, stating he taught the player how to fight. He emphasizes the importance of defending the mountain from all threats, including the Tunnel Bear.

### Exclusive Beesmas Dialogue
Panda Bear has special interactions during Beesmas events:
*   **Beesmas (2019):** Acknowledges a notification gift (automatic monster respawn timer).
*   **Beesmas 2020:** Appreciates receiving a bamboo pull-up bar and rewards the player with the [Boxing Glove Ornament], which grants +25% Convert Rate, +25% Capacity in the Bamboo Field, and +5% Bee Attack Power.

## Trivia & Mechanics

*   **Quest Focus:** Panda Bear is unique among quest givers for having quests that primarily focus on defeating mobs (though some limited-time quests involve collection).
*   **Tunnel Bear Requirement:** He is the second quest giver to require the defeat of a [Tunnel Bear] in his quests.
*   **Pollen Collection:** Excluding limited-time events, Panda Bear and Mother Bear are the only bears who do not require collecting pollen from fields for their main quests.
*   **Total Rewards:** Completing all his standard quests grants a total of 120 Tickets.
*   **Mob Totals:** Across all his mobs (excluding seasonal/limited-time), he requires players to defeat 5,173 mobs in total.
*   **Dialogue Errors:** Some dialogue contains minor typos or inaccuracies regarding current game mechanics (e.g., the stacking limit of Rage Bee).
*   **Robo Bear Sentiment:** In his Beesmas quest, Panda Bear is revealed to dislike [Robo Bear].
