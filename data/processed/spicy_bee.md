# Spicy Bee

**Spicy Bee** is a [Red](/wiki/Category:Red "Category:Red") [Mythic bee](/wiki/Bees/Mythic "Bees/Mythic"). It is available in both Original and Gifted forms.

*"Some like it hot - this bee likes it scorching. Even the honey it makes is spicy."*

**Rarity:** Mythic
**Color:** Red
**Stats:**
*   Energy: 20
*   Speed: 14
*   Attack: 5

**Habitat Preferences:**
*   Likes: [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch")
*   Dislikes: [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field")

## Abilities and Stats

Spicy Bee has a base pollen collection rate of **14 Pollen** in **4 seconds**, which is equivalent to 3.5 Pollen per second. With the x2 Bee Pollen gamepass, this increases to 28 Pollen in 4 seconds.

### Core Statistics
*   **Pollen Collection:** Collects 14 Pollen in 4 seconds (Base).
*   **Honey Production:** Makes 300 Honey in 2 seconds (Base), equivalent to 150 Honey per second.
*   **Conversion Boost:** +220 Convert Amount, +50% Convert Speed, +4 Gather Amount, and +4 Attack.
*   **Gifted Hive Bonus:** Grants a +25% Flame Duration bonus when gifted.

### Abilities
*   **Inferno:** Summons a ring of 6 Flames, then summons 2 Fire Bees that last for 15 seconds (+1s per Level). These summoned bees have levels equal to two less than Spicy Bee's level. If the bee is Gifted, each Fire Bee has a 10% (+1% per Level) chance of also being Gifted. *Note: Gifted Fire Bees from Inferno do not grant their own gifted hive bonus.*
*   **Rage:** Grants all bees +1 Attack for 45 seconds. This ability stacks up to 4 times.
*   **[🌟Gifted Ability: Flame Fuel]:** For 15 seconds, Spicy Bee tosses oil into any flames that spawn, causing them to last 50% longer and instantly converting 50k pollen plus (10 + 5 per Field Boost stack)% of the Hive's Convert Amount. This ability only affects non-dark flames currently on the field.
*   **[Passive: Steam Engine]:** Spicy Bee's Movespeed and gather speed drastically increase up to 100% when affected by Flame Heat.

## Trivia and Lore

*   Spicy Bee, Tadpole Bee, Fuzzy Bee, Buoyant Bee, and Digital Bee are the only bees with four different Abilities.
*   This bee and Rage Bee are the only bees that possess the Rage Ability.
*   The Gifted color scheme of Spicy Bee resembles both Rage Bee's gifted appearance and Rage Aphids' color scheme.
*   Spicy Bee and Tadpole Bee are among the few Bees capable of summoning other entities; Spicy Bee is the only one that can summon other bees.
*   The Steam Engine passive ability was originally intended to theme the bee after a train, including train sounds for its tokens and exhaust pipes on its body.
*   When the Steam Engine is active, Spicy Bee emits a continuous trail of steam.
*   Like Vector Bee, Buoyant Bee, and Precise Bee, Spicy Bee emits light when gifted.
*   Unlike other gifted bees, the star on top of a gifted Spicy Bee has a different color than the stars on its sides (green on top, two black ones on the side).
*   Spicy Bee is the only Mythic bee to have completely whole-numbered base stats.
*   The Fire Bees summoned by Inferno cannot convert pollen in the player's hive.
*   Early balancing issues included a typo where flames converted 1% of the player's capacity instead of 1% of their total conversion; this was quickly noticed and corrected to its current stats.
*   When gifted, Spicy Bee's exhaust pipes emit more steam.
*   Spicy Bee is unique in that it only likes one specific field (Pepper Patch).
*   If Spicy Bee is level 1 or 2, the Fire Bees summoned by Inferno will be level 1 instead of two levels below, as no bee level can go below 1.
