# Soft Wax

[![Soft Wax](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/77/Soft_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042757)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/77/Soft_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042757)

_"A squishy hunk of beeswax. Has a 100% chance to slightly improve a bee’s Beequip."_

**Soft Wax** is an inventory item and a type of wax, introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. It can be used on a Beequip to boost its stats. Upon usage, it has a guaranteed 100% chance to slightly improve the Beequip's stats. Soft Wax has an upgrade value of 1 for Beequips.

Soft wax is also utilized in crafting various items and other types of wax, such as Hard Wax, Swirled Wax, and Caustic Wax. These waxes can all be produced using a Blender.

When soft wax is applied to a bee's Beequip, the following message appears:
> The Wax has Improved the Beequip!

## Acquisition Methods

### Crafting
Soft Wax can be crafted in the Blender using the following recipes:
*   5 Honeysuckles + 1 Oil + 1 Enzymes + 10 Royal Jellies

### Rewards and Drops
**As a Stack Reward (Sticker Stacks):**
Soft Wax is rewarded when adding certain Stickers to the Sticker Stack, granting 3 soft waxes per reward:
*   Window
*   TNT
*   Jack-O-Lantern
*   Barcode
*   Spore Covered Puffshroom
*   White Button Mushroom
*   Fly Agaric Mushroom
*   Porcini Mushroom
*   Oiler Mushroom
*   Morel Mushroom
*   Chanterelle Mushroom
*   Porcelain Dipper

**Other Rewards:**
*   Retro Swarm Challenge completion.

**Drops (Mobs & Planters):**
Soft Wax can be obtained as a drop from:
*   Aphids
*   Puffshrooms
*   Planters

Specific planters and environmental factors increase the chance of receiving soft wax:
*   Red Clay Planter and The Planter Of Plenty grant soft waxes as bonus drops.
*   Festive Planter can also drop soft waxes.
*   Planters located in the Pumpkin Patch grant extra waxes, including soft wax.
*   A fully grown Red Clay Planter in a Pumpkin Patch guarantees 2 soft waxes.

**Quest Rewards:**
Soft Wax is awarded by specific quests:
*   Dapper Bear's Liquid Motivation (#2) rewards 3 soft waxes and other items.
*   Dapper Bear's The Trick To Tickets (#11) rewards 10 soft waxes and other items.

## Trivia

*   **Raw Ingredients Cost:** The raw ingredients required to craft Soft Wax include 5 Honeysuckles, 50 Sunflower Seeds, 50 Pineapples, and 30 Royal Jellies (plus 30,000,000 Honey).
*   **Guaranteed Improvement:** Soft Wax, along with Swirled Wax and Debug Wax, are the only waxes guaranteed to change a Beequip's stats.
