# Stick Nymph

A Stick Nymph is a mob summoned by the Stick Bug during the Stick Bug Challenge. It should not be confused with Festive Nymph, a variant that only appears in Beesmas.

## Description
Stick Nymphs follow the player closest to them and deal damage upon contact. Although they have relatively low individual stats, they are often found in large groups, which can easily overpower weaker players.

## Trivia
*   Stick Nymphs can follow the player outside of Fields. They may pass through some objects, be obstructed by others, or enter specific locations like the King Beetle's Lair, which does not always align with player movement restrictions. This allows players to potentially deal damage from a distance.
*   Like Mondo Chick and Festive Nymphs, Stick Nymphs lose health every second. While Mondo Chick loses 333 health per second, Stick Nymphs and Festive Nymphs drain faster as their level increases. They can be defeated naturally; unlike Mondo Chick, which only has 300 health remaining after 15 minutes, Stick Nymphs will still drop rewards if they drain to zero without a player present.
*   Stick Nymphs can jump, but if the player is on a platform high enough that the nymph cannot reach them and is sufficiently far away, the nymph cannot jump onto the player.
*   Stick Nymphs and Festive Nymph are unique in being two of only 18 mobs that can be summoned by another mob. The other non-naturally spawning mobs include the five types of Ants, Snowbear, Stick Bug, the five Robo Bear Challenge Mobs, and the four Robo Party mobs.
*   Stick Nymphs were the first mob to drop tokens upon defeat. This distinction excludes the Stick Bug, as knocking it down to 0 health only increases its level by one point and resets its health to full.
*   A known glitch allows Stick Nymphs to climb up ramps when they spawn in a Strawberry Field.
*   If a player dies with large amounts of Stick Nymphs on their body, the nymphs may take away some health upon respawning.
*   There is no grace period for taking hits from a Stick Nymph; therefore, a cluster of them can be highly lethal.
