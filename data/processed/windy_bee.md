# Windy Bee

**Windy Bee** is a Colorless Event bee, described as "An ethereal bee as powerful and unpredictable as the weather." It hatches from a Windy Bee Egg, which can be obtained by donating Cloud Vials after previously donating a Spirit Petal to the Wind Shrine.

*Note: This page details the tamed version of Windy Bee. For the hostile variant, see [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee).*

## Traits and Preferences
**Rarity:** Event
**Color:** Colorless

**Likes:**
*   Dandelion Field
*   Coconut Field

**Dislikes:**
*   Bamboo Field
*   Strawberry Field

Like all other Event bees, Windy Bee does not have a favorite Treat. It can be made Gifted by feeding it a Star Treat, Gingerbread Bear, or Aged Gingerbread Bear.

*   +100 Convert Amount
*   +3 Attack

**Gifted Hive Bonus:**
*   +15% Instant White Conversion
*   x2 Boosts from Clouds

## Abilities
Windy Bee possesses three unique ability tokens:

*   **White Boost:** Grants x1.2 pollen from White Flowers for 15 seconds. This token stacks up to 10 times.
*   **Rain Cloud:** Summons a Cloud in a different field that lasts for 60 seconds (+10s per level). Clouds cause flowers to grow and grant nearby players +25% Pollen and 10% Unique Instant Conversion (or 50% Pollen and 20% Unique IC if the player has a gifted Windy Bee). If another player's Windy Bee spawns a cloud in your field, an announcement will appear: "🌧️ [Username]'s Windy Bee summoned a Cloud in this field. 🌧️"
*   **Tornado:** Consumes Haste stacks to summon a Tornado that damages enemies and collects Tokens and Pollen as it roams the field. The lifespan, speed, and damage of the tornado are increased by the number of Haste stacks spent and the bee's level.

## Trivia and Lore
*   **Tornado Path:** Windy Bee's tornado does not travel randomly across the field; it loosely follows a grid, which is more noticeable when Haste tokens are spent or the tornado is faster.
*   **Robux History:** It was formerly available for purchase with 800 Robux until October 27, 2019. This made it the third bee to be purchasable via Robux (after Festive Bee and Bear Bee).
*   **Gate:** There is a dedicated gate for Windy Bee, known as the Windy Bee Gate or the 35 bee gate.
*   **Hostile Variant:** Along with Vicious Bee, Windy Bee is one of only two bees to have a hostile variant.
*   **Ability Tokens:** Windy Bee, Music Bee, Vector Bee, Tadpole Bee, Spicy Bee, Fuzzy Bee, Buoyant Bee, Festive Bee, and Digital Bee are the only bees with three different ability tokens (the most of any bee).
*   **Single Skin Color:** Along with Ninja Bee, Shy Bee, and Fuzzy Bee, Windy Bee has only one skin color. It is also the only bee to maintain the same skin color when gifted.
*   **Gifted Bonuses:** This bee is unique among Event bees for having two specific gifted bonuses (excluding other Event bees that enhance their abilities).
*   **Field Dislikes:** This is the only Event Bee to dislike two fields.
*   **Token Production:** Along with Buoyant Bee, Windy Bee can produce a White Boost token. However, Buoyant Bee produces it exclusively through Surprise Parties.
*   **In-Game Hint:** The bee was hinted at during the Black Bear quest "Black Bear, Why?" when the character mentioned seeing a strange bee destroy a field with powerful gusts of wind.
*   **Origin Theory:** It is speculated that Windy Bee likes the Dandelion Field because dandelion seeds travel wherever the wind takes them.
