# Puppy Bee

**Puppy Bee** is a Colorless Event bee known for its playful nature, often associated with tennis balls and the player.

[![Puppy Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/01/Puppy_Bee.png/revision/latest?cb=20200404000127)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/01/Puppy_Bee.png/revision/latest) [![Puppy Bee Gifted](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f1/PuppyBee_Gifted.png/revision/latest?cb=20181115015225)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f1/PuppyBee_Gifted.png/revision/latest)

> "A playful bee who only cares about two things, its tennis ball and you!"

**Rarity:** Event
**Color:** Colorless

## Acquisition & Gift Status

Puppy Bee hatches from the **Puppy Bee Egg**, which can be purchased in the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.

Like all other Event bees, Puppy Bee does not have a favorite treat. To make it gifted, players must feed it one of the following treats:
*   Star Treat
*   Gingerbread Bear
*   Aged Gingerbread Bear

Puppy Bee likes the Clover Field and the Pumpkin Patch, but dislikes the Rose Field.

**Gifted Bee Bonus:** +20% Bond from Treats

## Abilities

Puppy Bee possesses two unique abilities:

*   **Fetch:** Creates a ball that collects tokens. This ability increases pollen collection by 100% (+15% per Level) as the player and Puppy Bee kick the ball back and forth. Collection increases with each successful kick, and combo hits are required to earn Treat Tokens.
*   **Puppy Love:** Increases your bond with up to 10 nearby bees by 60 (+20 per level). It also grants +10% Bee Movespeed and +50% Bee Gather Amount for 10 seconds (+1 second per level).

## Trivia & Lore

*   **First Token Creator:** Puppy Bee was the first bee capable of creating Treat Tokens, followed by Festive Bee.
*   **Treat Mechanics:** To drop a treat token using Fetch, it requires 3 hits from the owner and 3 hits from Puppy Bee (a total of 6 volleys). The reward doubles with each consecutive combo hit, capping at 256 treats per token. It takes a total of 54 hits to reach this maximum amount.
*   **Unique Features:** Puppy Bee is the only bee that can raise the bond of other bees using its Puppy Love Token. It was also one of the first bees with a 3D model on its face (its tongue).
*   **Exclusivity:** This bee, along with Bear Bee and Festive Bee, are among the few Event bees capable of producing Gingerbread Bears.
*   **Limited Availability:** When it debuted in the 2018-09-10 Update, Puppy Bee was available for a limited time (until September 22, 2018). It is one of the few bees that can be purchased twice via special bundles (e.g., during Beesmas 2020).
*   **Design Inspiration:** Puppy Bee was likely designed as the canine counterpart to Tabby Bee.
*   **Real-World Basis:** Along with Tadpole Bee, Lion Bee, and Bear Bee, Puppy Bee is one of the few bees based on real animals.
