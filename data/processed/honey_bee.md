# Honey Bee

The Honey Bee is a worker bee, and its NPC counterpart can be found on the [Honey Bee Gate](/wiki/Honey_Bee_Gate).

## Overview

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Epic |
| **Color** | Colorless |
| **Energy** | 20 |
| **Speed** | 14 |
| **Attack** | 2 |

*"A satisfied bee always full with the finest honey. If you're lucky it will share some."*

Honey Bee is a [Colorless](/wiki/Category:Colorless) and [Epic Bee](/wiki/Bees/Epic). It favors [Pineapples](/wiki/Pineapple), and enjoys visiting the [Pumpkin Patch](/wiki/Pumpkin_Patch) and the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field). Conversely, it dislikes the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field).

## Stats & Production

### Collection Rates
*   **Pollen:** Collects 10 Pollen in 4 seconds. This equates to 2.5 Pollen per second (or 20 Pollen in 4 seconds with the x2 Bee Pollen gamepass).
*   **Honey:** Produces 360 Honey in 2 seconds, which is a rate of 180 Honey per second (or 360 Honey in 1 second with the x2 Honeymaking Speed gamepass).

### Gifted Hive Bonus
*   **Gifted Bee Bonus:** Grants x1.5 Honey From Tokens.

## Abilities

*   **Honey Gift:** Occasionally spawns a honey token. The amount of honey generated is equal to 100 times the bee's current level (x).
*   **Honey Mark:** Marks a random area on the field for 7 seconds (+0.2 seconds per Level). While standing in this marked area, you gain 2 Conversion Links and receive x1.25 Convert Rate. This effect can stack up to 3 times.

### Trivia
*   There is a dedicated [Honey Bee Gate](/wiki/Honey_Bee_Gate) (also known as the 15 Bee Zone) for this bee.
*   The only way to obtain a [Honey Bee Egg](/wiki/Egg#Honey_Bee_Egg) is by defeating cave monsters. This may be why Honey Bee dislikes the Spider Field, due to perceived similarities between spiders and cave monsters.
*   The [Honey Mask](/wiki/Honey_Mask), purchasable in the Badge Bearer's Guild, resembles Honey Bee's face decal and color scheme.
*   Prior to the 2018-11-25 Update, the Gifted Hive Bonus for Honey Bee was +250% Convert Rate; this was later updated to x1.5 Honey From Tokens.
*   Honey Bee is one of only two bees that generate honey gifts (the other being Diamond Bee).
*   The Honey Mark ability was introduced after the 2019-04-05 update.
*   Both Honey Bee and Bubble Bee share a similar bubble effect, despite having different colors.
*   Like Bumble Bee and Carpenter Bee, Honey Bee is based on real-life bees, though its in-game appearance does not closely resemble them.
*   During the Beesmas 2020 and 2021 events, players could receive a Honey Bee Jelly by giving a present to the NPC version of the bee.
