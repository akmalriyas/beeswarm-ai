# Field Dice

[![Field Dice](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/93/Field_Dice.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404030928)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/93/Field_Dice.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404030928)

> "Boosts a random field, granting +100% pollen for 15 minutes."

**Cooldown:** 1 second

***

## Description and Mechanics

**Field Dice** is an inventory item introduced in the 2019-04-05 update. When used, it boosts a randomly selected field the player has access to, granting a **+100% Pollen Field Boost** for 15 minutes.

If the player uses another Field Dice or any other type of dice while an existing Field Boost from a dice is active, the new boost will override the old one; Field Boosts do not stack.

As of the 2021-12-26 Update, there are three types of dice in the game:
*   Field Dice
*   Smooth Dice
*   Loaded Dice

The following audio plays when a field dice is used: [Fdice.ogg](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Fdice.ogg)

### Field Boost Details (Trivia)

*   **Boosted Fields:** The icons on the dice represent the Pine Tree Forest, Strawberry Field, and Blue Flower Field.
*   **Advanced Boosting:** It is possible to obtain a Mountain Top Field Boost or Coconut Field Boost from field dice. Unlike these, Field Boosters cannot grant boosts to those specific fields.

### Dice Mechanics (Trivia)

*   **Stacking/Refreshing:** Normally, rolling the same field twice with Field Dice simply refreshes the boost. However, if you roll a field, leave the game, and then return to roll until you get that field again, the boost will stack (though it does not exceed any established limits).
*   **Triple Sticker Chance:** Rolling the same field three times in a row grants the Triple Exclamation Sticker, which spawns in the rolled field. The chance of this occurring is 1/289 ($\approx 0.34\%$). This also applies to rolling the same four or more times consecutively.
*   **Rework Notes:** Previously, Field Dice could stack up to grant 300% boosts; this mechanic was reworked in the 2021-12-26 update with the introduction of new dice types and system changes.

## Acquisition Methods

### Crafting

Field Dice can be crafted using a Blender with the following ingredients:
*   1 Soft Wax
*   1 Whirligig
*   1 Red Extract
*   1 Blue Extract

### Drops

Field Dice can be obtained through various sources:

**Mobs:**
*   Aphid
*   Ladybug
*   Scorpion
*   Spider
*   Werewolf
*   Wild Windy Bee
*   Mondo Chick

**Special Sources:**
*   A rare drop from Festive Gift (if a Festive Bee is gifted).
*   A rare drop from Festive Mark.
*   As a reward for donating to the Wind Shrine.
*   As a reward from the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   As a rare drop from a Cub Buddy gift.
*   From specific planters: Tacky Planters and Petal Planters (as a bonus drop).
*   From Mythic Meteor Shower events.

**Gameplay:**
*   Matching a pair of Field Dice in Memory Match.
*   Adding the Wishbone Sticker to the Sticker Stack rewards 5 Field Dice.
