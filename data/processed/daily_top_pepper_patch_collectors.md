# Daily Top Pepper Patch Collectors

The **Daily Top Pepper Patch Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator. It can only be viewed through the Global Leaderboards menu found on the System Page.

This leaderboard tracks the amount of pollen players have collected specifically within the Pepper Patch during a single day. The board displays 10 positions at a time, but users can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes and Rewards

Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 receive a **Pepper Patch Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 receive 25 **Tickets**.
