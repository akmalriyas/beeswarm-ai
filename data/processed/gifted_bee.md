# Gifted Bee

A bee is identified as "Gifted" if a yellow hexagon appears around its hive slot.

## Acquisition Methods

**1. Royal Jelly or Egg Hatching:**
Every time the player uses Royal Jelly or hatches an egg, there is a 0.4% (1 in 250) chance to obtain a gifted bee.

**2. Feeding Treats:**
Feeding a bee its favorite treat offers various chances of transformation based on the bee's rarity:
*   Rare Bees: 0.0125% (1 in 8,000)
*   Epic Bees: 0.01% (1 in 10,000)
*   Common/Legendary Bees: 0.0083% (1 in 12,000)
*   Mythic Bees: 0.00417% (1 in 24,000)

**Specific Treat Chances:**
*   Feeding a Gingerbread Bear has a 1% (1/100) chance of gifting the bee.
*   Feeding an Aged Gingerbread Bear has a 1.1% (1/90.909091) chance of gifting the bee.

**Guaranteed Transformation:**
*   **Star Treats** will always transform the chosen bee into its gifted version. This is the only method that can gift Event bees, such as the Bear Bee and Tabby Bee (excluding gingerbread bears). If a Star Treat is fed to an already gifted bee, it only grants bond, not extra benefits.
*   **Star Eggs** always hatch into a non-event gifted bee. Unless the player possesses all non-Event bees in their gifted form, they will hatch as a new type; otherwise, they hatch as a random non-Event gifted bee.
*   **Star Jelly** always transforms the chosen bee into a random non-Event gifted bee, using the same odds as Royal Jelly (with the exception of Basic Bees).

## General Effects and Mechanics

Gifted bees are special types of bees with improved stats and receive a "Gifted Hive Bonus," enhancing player performance similar to badges.

**Visual & UI Changes:**
*   The bee is equipped with a unique color scheme, stained wings, and a star on its back and sides.
*   A glowing gold hexagon appears around the hive slot.
*   Upon obtaining a gifted bee, the UI cycles through colors with the text: "⭐ Your [type] Bee Transformed Into A Gifted [type] Bee!⭐" and plays a specific sound file.

**Stat Improvements:**
All gifted bees receive base stat improvements:
*   x1.5 Gather Amount (Pollen)
*   x1.5 Convert Rate
*   x1.5 Attack
*   Improved certain passive abilities.

**New Abilities & Bonuses:**
*   **Inspire Token:** Gifted bees gain a new ability token, **[Inspire]**, which doubles pollen gained for 5 seconds and stacks up to 50 times (up to x51 bonus).
*   They are able to generate ⭐ Gifted Ability Tokens if they possess the base ability.
*   **Royal Jelly Dispenser:** Each gifted bee in the hive allows the player to claim an additional royal jelly from the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser in the Star Hall.

## Trivia

*   Since the Bear Bee is now purchasable from the Robux Shop, players who purchased a Bear Bee during the game launch event had their bee converted into a First Edition gifted Bear Bee. This makes the Bear Bee the only Event bee that can be gifted without using a Star Treat or Gingerbread Bears.
*   Multiple gifted bees of the same type do not stack their hive bonus multiple times, nor do they count towards the number of unique gifted bee types in the hive. However, they retain all improved base stats over their normal version.
*   Using Royal Jelly or a non-gifted egg on a gifted bee will almost certainly cause the bee to lose its gifted status. The chance for the resulting new bee to be gifted is 1/250.
*   Windy Bee is unique in that it provides two different gifted hive bonuses.
*   Tadpole Bee previously provided two different gifted hive bonuses before the May 23, 2024 update.
