# Stinger

[![Stinger](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b6/Stinger.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042929)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b6/Stinger.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042929)

*"Grants your bees x1.5 attack for 30 seconds."*

**Cooldown:** 1 second

**Stingers** are an inventory item introduced in the 2018-09-10 update. When used, it grants a temporary buff, increasing Bee Attack by x1.5 for 30 seconds. Stingers can also be spent to obtain Vicious Bees (cost: 250 Stingers) and are utilized in numerous crafting recipes.

## Acquisition Methods

Stingers can be obtained through various means:

*   **Rogue Vicious Bee Drops:** Defeating a Rogue Vicious Bee always grants stingers. The amount dropped depends on the bee's level and whether it was gifted or not.
*   **Rare Drops:** They are a rare drop from certain enemies, including Rage Aphids, Armored Aphids, Scorpions, and Spiders.
*   **Sticker Stack Rewards:** As a stack reward for adding specific Stickers to the Sticker Stack:
    *   Flying Brave Bee: 3 Stingers
    *   Walking Stick Nymph: 3 Stingers
    *   Coiled Snake: 3 Stingers
    *   Dark Flame: 5 Stingers
    *   Cyan Hilted Sword: 10 Stingers
    *   Red Wailing Cry: 3 Stingers
    *   Tough Potato: 3 Stingers
*   **Commando Chick Captures:** Rewards scale with the number of Commando Chicks captured:
    *   3 captures: 1 Stinger + other items
    *   12 captures: 3 Stingers + other items
    *   30 captures: 10 Stingers + other items
    *   40 captures: 10 Stingers + other items
*   **Purchases:** Available for purchase in the Stinger Shop (10 tickets).
*   **Field Drops:** A rare drop from leaves found in the Cactus Field.
*   **Wind Shrine Donations:** Shivering certain Badges in the Wind Shrine by donating specific items (Battle, Clover, Spider, Cactus, and Rose badge rewards Stingers plus other materials).
*   **Quest Completion:** Completing certain quests, such as those involving Black Bear, Gifted Riley Bee, or Spirit Bear.
*   **Memory Match Wins:** Winning the Mega, Night, or Extreme Memory Matches grants Stingers.
*   **Cub Buddy Gifts:** Very rarely received as a Cub Buddy gift.
*   **Bundle Purchases (Bee Bear's Catalog):** Purchasing the Super Snowman Bundle yields 25 Stingers + other items.
*   **Robo Party Rewards:** Guaranteed reward from defeating all party mobs of your Robo Party.
*   **Planter Harvesting:** Drops from planters harvested in the Cactus Field or Rose Field.
*   **Consecutive Planter Growth:**
    *   Fully growing a Red Clay Planter consecutively in the Clover, Spider, and Cactus Fields rewards 10 Stingers (2 weeks cooldown).
    *   Fully growing a Heat-Treated Planter in the Cactus Field rewards 10 Stingers (unknown cooldown).
    *   Fully growing The Planter Of Plenty in the Cactus Field rewards 10–15 Stingers (unknown cooldown).

### Looting from Rogue Vicious Bee

Rogue Vicious Bees can be summoned by touching or being close to a spike in any field. They are more likely to spawn at night and are guaranteed to spawn when using a Night Bell. If the bee is gifted and it is the first defeat of the day, it may drop 1–20 stingers.

**Regular Rogue Vicious Bee Drops:**
| Level | Stingers |
| :---: | :---: |
| 1-3 | 1 |
| 4-6 | 2 |
| 7-9 | 3 |
| 10-12 | 4 |

**Gifted Rogue Vicious Bee Drops:**
| Level | Stingers |
| :---: | :---: |
| 1-3 | 6 |
| 4-6 | 9 |
| 7-9 | 12 |
| 10-12 | 15 |

**Daily Bonus:** An additional bonus of 5 Stingers is given for the first Vicious Bee defeated each day. This applies separately to both regular and gifted Vicious Bees, with a cooldown of 22 hours after receiving the daily reward.

Rogue Vicious Bees can spawn in six fields:
*   Clover Field
*   Spider Field
*   Cactus Field
*   Rose Field
*   Mountain Top Field
*   Pepper Patch

## Trivia

*   The old hotkey for stingers, 'U', was removed shortly after the 2018-09-10 update due to frequent accidental use.
*   3 Stingers are required to activate the Star Amulet passive ability, "Star Saw." This passive also refunds 1 Stinger upon activation.
