# Snowflakes

[![Snowflake](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/27/Snowflake.png/revision/latest?cb=20230406132508)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/27/Snowflake.png/revision/latest?cb=20230406132508)

*Sends a cool, soothing breeze to all players on the server (Melts after Beesmas!).*

**Cooldown:** 3 seconds

A **Snowflake** is a limited-time inventory item introduced in the 2020-12-25 update. It is only available during the Beesmas event and becomes unusable outside of this period.

### Mechanics and Buffs

When used, a Snowflake grants 9 seconds of the **Cool Breeze** buff. This buff increases various stats, with the effect scaling based on duration:
*   Minimum effect starts at 1% (at less than 1 second duration).
*   Maximum effect reaches 100% (at 15 minutes duration).

The specific stat boosts provided by Cool Breeze include:
*   +10% to +25% Convert Rate At Hive.
*   +5% to +25% Pollen from Tools.
*   +5% to +25% Bee Gather Pollen.
*   +1% to +5% Honey Per Pollen.
*   +1% to +10% Loot Luck.
*   x1.25 Capacity multiplier (Note: The capacity value itself remains unchanged).

Snowflakes can also be used, along with Gingerbread Bears, to purchase various items from Bee Bear’s Catalog.

### Ways to Obtain Snowflakes

**From Gift Boxes:**
*   Shiny Gift Box: 50 snowflakes (3)
*   Ruby Gift Box: 40 snowflakes (5)
*   Stellar Gift Box: 50 snowflakes (6)
*   Blinking Gift Box: 50 snowflakes (8)
*   Ticket Gift Box: 50 snowflakes (10)
*   Crafty Gift Box: 25 snowflakes (11)

**From Drops & Environmental Sources:**
*   As a drop from a Festive Sprout.
*   Breaking Brick Blooms in the Retro Swarm Challenge.
*   Catching fallen snowflakes on fields. During a Snow Storm, snowflakes fall much more often, averaging 20 per field. (Snow Storms can be summoned using the Snow Machine after completing Gifted Bucko Bee’s Beesmas quest).
*   Snowbear drops snowflakes every 25% of its health; the amount increases with each level and is highest when killed.
*   Drops from various mobs: Rhino Beetle, Mantis, Spider, Aphid (Armored and Diamond Exclusive), Wild Windy Bee, Festive Nymph, and Mondo Chick.

**From Quests & Rewards:**
*   Brown Bear's Stockings: 25 snowflakes.
*   Science Bear's Beesmas Lights: 25 snowflakes.
*   Dapper Bear's Samovar: 25 snowflakes.
*   Spirit Bear's Galentine Shrine: 100 snowflakes.
*   Gummy Bear's Goo Years Beacon: 500 snowflakes.
*   Rewards for giving presents to specific Quest Givers:
    *   Black Bear, Panda Bear, Science Bear, Polar Bear, Robo Bear, Spirit Bear: 25 snowflakes each.
    *   Gifted Bucko Bee: 50 snowflakes.

**From Purchases (Robux Shop):**
*   Stocking-Stuffer Stuff: 500 snowflakes for 400 Robux.
*   Seasonal Slumber Special: 1,000 snowflakes for 1,700 Robux.

### Historical and Removed Methods

The following methods are no longer possible or have changed mechanics:
*   Purchasing the Snowey Starter Pack (400 Robux).
*   Purchasing the Reindeer Puppy Pack (800 Robux after receiving Reindeer Antlers from Bee Bear's Catalog; previously granted a refund of 7,500 Snowflakes).
*   Purchasing the Nighttime Nectar Pack in the Robux Shop.

**Note:** Snowflakes collected from Festive Sprouts outside the Beesmas event will not appear in the player's inventory.

### Trivia and Lore

*   Snowflakes are one of four consumables that can be used to increase capacity, alongside Marshmallow Bee, Purple Potion, and Super Smoothie.
*   Along with Nectar Shower Vial, Snowflakes are the only consumables that **directly** grant buffs to all players on the server.
*   The buff granted by Snowflake is named "Cool Breeze," rather than being named after the item itself. This may be because it can also be obtained from the Snowglobe Shake ability token (generated by bees equipped with a Snowglobe).
*   Historically, using snowflakes alone required at least 129 to achieve the 100% Cool Breeze buff due to the decreasing nature of the buff while being used; however, multiple players could speed up this process.
*   **Pre-Beesmas 2024 Changes:** Before Beesmas 2024, snowflakes remained in inventory after the event but were unusable. After Summer 2024, all collected snowflakes were set to zero, though they might still appear in the hotbar. At the end of Winter 2024, they remained in inventory but without regular use.
*   **Pre-Beesmas 2021 Buff Differences:** Before March 1, 2021, Cool Breeze offered different stats: +5% to +50% Convert Rate At Hive, +1% to +25% Tool Pollen, and increased White Pollen/Blue Pollen instead of Bee Gather Pollen/Honey Per Pollen. The minimum value for Convert Rate At Hive was half the current amount, and the maximum was double.
