# BBM Mask

![B.B.M. Mask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fd/BBMThumb.png/revision/latest?cb=20251210193027 "B.B.M. Mask")

The **B.B.M. Mask**, also known as the **Bubble Bee Man Mask**, is a mask accessory that was originally obtainable by completing Bubble Bee Man's quest, "Helping B.B.M.," during Beesmas 2018. Upon completion, this mask automatically replaced any other hat the player was wearing.

Currently, players can reequip this mask by talking to BBM in the 30 Bee Zone, provided they do not already own the Mondo B.B.M. Mask.

## Stats
The B.B.M. Mask provides the following bonuses:
*   +75,000 Capacity
*   +25 Convert Amount
*   +50% Honey From Tokens
*   +1 Blue Bee Attack

## Trivia
*   The B.B.M. Mask and the Mondo B.B.M. Mask were the first masks to be equipped by talking to an NPC.
*   In the Roblox version of the game, this item is classified as a hat accessory.
*   These two items are unique in that they cannot be purchased and do not have a description.
*   Following the 2019-04-05 Update, the mask became unobtainable because the NPC who provided it (Bubble Bee Man) was removed from its original location behind the Global Top Battlers leaderboard. It is one of only two unobtainable accessories in the game.
*   The stats associated with the B.B.M. Mask have been modified three times over time.
*   Wearing this mask does not grant access to Dapper Bear's Shop.
