# Egg Types in Bee Swarm Simulator

Eggs are inventory items that players drag into their hive to hatch a bee. They can also be donated to the Wind Shrine or used to roll the Sticker Printer. Eggs are fundamental to starting play in the game, and they come in various types, some offering chances at different bee rarities, while others guarantee a specific outcome.

Currently, there are several distinct egg categories: Basic, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Mythic, Star, and their corresponding Gifted variants, alongside numerous Specific Bee Eggs and Event Bee Eggs.

### General Egg Mechanics

*   **Hatching:** Players no longer require a vacant hive slot to hatch an egg; they can hatch it on top of an existing bee. The level of the resulting bee remains the same as the host bee, acting similarly to Royal Jelly.
*   **Restrictions:** Due to regulations in certain regions (United Kingdom, Netherlands, Belgium, and Australia), some eggs available in the Robux Shop may not be purchasable by consumers in those areas.

## General Egg Rarities

### Basic Egg
The most common egg type found in the game. It typically hatches into a **Basic Bee** (87% chance). However, it can rarely hatch into:
*   Rare Bee (10% chance)
*   Epic Bee (2.5% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (0.5% chance)
*   Gifted Bee (0.4% chance), with a specific 0.348% chance of obtaining a Gifted Basic Bee.

**Note:** Mythic Bees and Event Bees cannot be hatched from this egg. This egg also cannot be used on a Basic Bee.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Free upon starting the game (first-time players).
*   Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop near the Sunflower Field: 1,000 Honey (price scales up with each purchase, capping at 10,000,000 Honey).
*   Receiving a reward for adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack.

### Silver Egg
This egg most likely hatches into a **Rare bee** (64.9% chance), but has chances of hatching into:
*   Epic bee (30% chance)
*   Legendary bee (5% chance)
*   Mythic bee (0.1% chance)

**Note:** A Basic Bee or an Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent: 50 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop: 100 Robux.
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Silver Egg questline.
*   As a reward from Brown Bear's 25th quest.
*   Completing the 25th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine rewards you 1 Silver Egg.
*   Low chance drop from King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Rare Sprouts, and leaves when collecting pollen in Clover Field.
*   Drop from Festive Planter or a rare reward from Stockings/Winter Memory Match.

### Gifted Silver Egg
This egg shares the same probabilities as a standard Silver Egg, but it will always hatch the bee into its gifted variant.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (though chances decrease upon subsequent defeats).
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors poll.
*   Completing Black Bear's "Getting Stumped" quest.
*   1 Gifted Silver Egg reward from completing the 100th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Rare drops from werewolves, Stump Snail, Scorpion, and Sticker Sprout.

### Gold Egg
This egg is most likely to hatch into an **Epic bee** (79% chance), with chances for:
*   Legendary bee (20% chance)
*   Mythic bee (1% chance)

**Note:** A Basic Bee, Rare bee, or Event bee cannot hatch from this egg. Five Gold Eggs are required to craft the Honey Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent: 100 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop: 200 Robux each.
*   Rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge or Extreme Memory Match.
*   Token form in Werewolf's Cave (guarded by two cave monsters).
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Gold Egg questline.
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 50th, 340th, and 390th quests.
*   1 Gold Egg reward from completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 50th quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick 10 times.
*   Low chance drop from King Beetle; extremely low chance drop from Scorpion.
*   Rare drops from Epic sprouts, leaves in Clover Field, or various planters (Ticket, Candy, Red Clay, Festive, Pesticide, Petal).
*   Rare reward from Stockings or Winter Memory Match.

### Gifted Gold Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a standard Gold Egg, but always hatches the bee into its gifted variant. This type cannot be used to craft the Honey Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (chances decrease upon subsequent defeats).
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors poll.
*   Playtime Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 50 Gifted Bucko Bee quests or 50 Gifted Riley Bee quests (1 egg each).
*   Completing 275 Brown Bear quests (1 egg).
*   1 Gifted Gold Egg reward from completing the 150th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Very rare drops from Stump Snail and Coconut Crab; exceptionally rare drop from Mantis.

### Diamond Egg
This egg is most likely to hatch into a **Legendary bee** (95% chance), with a rare chance for a Mythic bee (5% chance).

**Note:** A Basic Bee, Rare bee, Epic bee, or Event bee cannot hatch from this egg. Five Diamond Eggs are required to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Token located past Bear Gate under Onett's platform (accessible via Glider/Parachute near Shadow Bear).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop: 400 Robux each.
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent: 400 Tickets each.
*   Rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge or Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Black Bear's Diamond Egg questline.
*   Completing Panda Bear's fifth Star Treat/Ant quest ("Colossal Combat").
*   Completing Dapper Bear's tenth quest, "The Long Haul".
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 75th and 525th quests.
*   75th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest reward.
*   Rare drops from Stump Snail, Diamond Aphids, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, and Coconut Crab.
*   Very rare drop from Spiders, Mantises, and Werewolves.
*   Rare chance from Legendary sprouts; guaranteed drop from Supreme sprouts.
*   Extremely rare chance from leaves while collecting pollen in Clover Field.
*   Very rare reward from catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Rare drops from Festive Planter or Winter Memory Match.

### Gifted Diamond Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a standard Diamond Egg, but always hatches the bee into its gifted variant. This type cannot be used to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (chances decrease upon subsequent defeats).
*   Reward for being in the top 25 in the Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers poll.
*   Quest Grandmaster badge reward from donating Tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Rewards from completing specific quests: 100, 250, 500, 750, and 1,000 Gifted Bucko Bee or Gifted Riley Bee quests; 425 Brown Bear quests; 200th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Extremely rare drops from Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, or Coconut Crab.

### Mythic Egg
This egg always hatches into a **Mythic bee**. The player cannot obtain any other type of bee besides a Mythic bee (though obtaining a Gifted Mythic bee is very rare).

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Memory Matchers poll.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop: 1,200 Robux each (limited to 3 eggs per player).
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent: 2,500 Tickets each.
*   Very rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge or Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Black Bear's 75th quest ("Myth in The Making").
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 15th quest ("More Than Myth").
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
*   1 Mythic Egg reward from completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 300th quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick for the 50th time guarantees a Mythic Egg.
*   Extremely rare drops from Stump Snail, Mondo Chick, and Coconut Crab (Mondo Chick has a higher chance when defeated faster).
*   Extremely rare drop when catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Rare drop from Festive Planter or Winter Memory Match.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog: 500 Snowflakes and 100 Gingerbread Bears.

### Gifted Mythic Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a standard Mythic Egg, but always hatches the bee into its gifted variant.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Rare Bee Pollen Collectors poll.
*   1 Gifted Mythic Egg reward from completing 500 Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quests.
*   Nearly impossible drop from Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog: 10,000 Snowflakes and 150 Gingerbread Bears.

### Choose-A-Mythic Egg
This unique egg allows the player to select any Mythic Bee they desire upon hatching.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop: 4,500 Robux (Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul).

### Star Egg
The Star Egg hatches into any type of **gifted bee** that is not already in the player's hive, excluding gifted Event bees. If the player owns all non-event gifted bees, it will hatch a random gifted bee. It will only hatch a Gifted Mythic Bee once all other non-event gifted bees are present.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (chances decrease upon subsequent defeats).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop: 800 Robux each (limited to 5 eggs per player).
*   Exceptionally rare chance drop from Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Completing Mother Bear's first ten quests ("Seven To Seven").
*   Completing Panda Bear's first ten star treat/ant quests ("Royal Rumble").
*   Exceptionally rare chance drop from King Beetle, Festive Planter, or Party Mechsquito.
