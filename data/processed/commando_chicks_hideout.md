# Commando Chick's Hideout

[![The location of the Commando Chick’s hideout.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2d/CommandoChickHideout.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200406200050)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2d/CommandoChickHideout.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200406200050)

The **Commando Chick’s Hideout** is a location within the [Starter Zone] that can be accessed by completing the obstacle course between the [Blue HQ] and the [Wealth Clock]. To begin the parkour, players must first cut the vines using either the [Clippers], [Scissors], or the [Scythe]. This area is home to the [Commando Chick].

## Strategy

When standing on the last stair near the hideout, the Commando Chick typically flees. However, a specific strategy allows players to prevent this: by positioning themselves on the edge of that stair (as close as possible to the hideout so one of your body parts is inside), the Commando Chick will not flee. This allows the player's bees to defeat the chick without taking damage.

*Note: This method does not guarantee success, as there is a slight chance the Commando Chick may still flee. If this occurs, the player can move into the hideout and then step back onto the stair to reset the situation.*

## Music

While inside the tunnel leading to the hideout, the audio track "Crawlers" plays.

## Trivia

*   This location previously housed the [Most Commando Captures leaderboard] before it was removed from the game.
*   During Beesmas 2019, this area contained the [Ornament Presents]. The obstacle course remained after the presents were removed; however, it was repurposed into the hideout following the 2020-04-06 update.
