# Neonberry

[![Neonberry](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bb/Neonberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041653)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/bb/Neonberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041653)

> "Increases bond with a bee by 500 and makes it Radioactive for a while."

A **Neonberry** is a type of Treat that, when fed to a Bee, raises its Bond by 500 and causes it to become Radioactive for 11 minutes. This treat guarantees the chance of making a bee radioactive, unlike other treats. Note that this is not a favorite treat for any bee.

## Acquisition Methods

Neonberries can be obtained through various means:

*   **Challenges:**
    *   As a reward from the Ant Challenge.
    *   As a common reward from the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   **Sticker Stack Rewards:** Adding specific stickers to the Sticker Stack rewards Neonberries:
    *   Wobbly Looker Bee Sticker: 3 Neonberries
    *   Traffic Light Sticker: 3 Neonberries
    *   Red Beesmas Light Sticker: 3 Neonberries
    *   Green Beesmas Light Sticker: 3 Neonberries
    *   Yellow Beesmas Light Sticker: 3 Neonberries
    *   Blue Beesmas Light Sticker: 3 Neonberries
    *   Atom Symbol Sticker: 5 Neonberries

### Drops and Farming

Neonberries can also be found as drops or through farming activities:

*   **Mob Drops:** Has a low chance of dropping from Rhino Beetles, Ladybugs, Mantises, King Beetles, Stump Snails, Mondo Chicks, and Coconut Crabs.
*   **Harvesting:**
    *   Has a low chance of appearing when harvesting Sparkles.
    *   Farming in the Blue Flower Field, Pineapple Patch, Mountain Top Field, and Pine Tree Forest has a rare chance to drop Neonberries (harvesting Leaves in these fields may also yield them).
*   **Rare Drops:**
    *   As a rare drop from an Ability Token: Festive Mark.
    *   As a gift from a Cub Buddy.
    *   As a rare drop from Mythic Meteors.
    *   As an uncommon drop from Puffshrooms.
    *   As a drop from Planters.

**Planter Mechanics:**
*   The Pesticide Planter drops extra Neonberries in the Stump Field, Mountain Top Field, and Coconut Field.
*   Planting the Pesticide Planter sequentially (Stump Field $\rightarrow$ Mountain Top Field $\rightarrow$ Coconut Field) results in at least 25 Neonberries (1 week cooldown).

## Quests

*   Black Bear's Triple Treck grants 1 Neonberry.
