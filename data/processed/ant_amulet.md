# Ant Amulet

All tiers are assumed to have consistent quality scaling regardless of obtainability.

The **Ant Amulet** is an [amulet](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet") obtained exclusively by participating in the [Ant Challenge](/wiki/Ant_Challenge "Ant Challenge").

This amulet provides various buffs and always boosts the player's [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page"). There are five tiers of Ant Amulets, each awarded for reaching a specific point milestone within the Ant Challenge.

The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the player’s score until it reaches its maximum quality at 400 points.[1]

## Trivia

*   The Supreme Ant Amulet is the only tier of Ant Amulets that resembles a [Flying Ant](/wiki/Flying_Ant "Flying Ant") rather than a [Regular Ant](/wiki/Regular_Ant "Regular Ant").
*   As the tiers increase, more sparkles are emitted from the Ant Amulet.
