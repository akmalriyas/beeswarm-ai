# Leaderboards

This page details the in-game leaderboards feature. For information regarding the Achievement Leaderboard, please see the [Achievement] page.

**Leaderboards Overview**
Leaderboards are a core game feature that tracks player performance. Each leaderboard displays the ranks of the top 100 players, showing ten ranks at a time. While they are not strictly real-time, leaderboards update very frequently. When standing on the designated white pad in front of a leaderboard, the system will display the player's current score for that specific ranking.

Out of a total of 64 available leaderboards, 18 can be viewed at various locations around the map. The remaining leaderboards are accessible via the [System Page]. Six leaderboards have been removed from the game entirely.

**Rewards and Mechanics**
Players gain rewards by achieving high rankings on daily and monthly leaderboards. These rewards can take the form of [tickets], consumable [items], or [stickers].

## Types of Leaderboards

Leaderboards are categorized based on their function:

*   Collection (Tracking resources like Honey, Pollen, Goo, Nectar, and Tokens)
*   Quests (Tracking NPC assistance/completion)
*   Battling and Challenges (Tracking combat performance)
*   Other (Various specific objectives)
*   Removed (Leaderboards that have been taken out of the game)

## Trivia

*   All leaderboards that reset on a daily basis reset at 12:00 AM GMT-5.
*   The [Highest Snowbear Level leaderboard] was introduced during the Beesmas update on Christmas Day 2020, but it was removed one week after the Beesmas 2020 event concluded.
*   The [Top Brown Bear Helpers] leaderboard is unique in that it does not display the player's current amount of the tracked objective directly on the board. Players can still view their score by checking the leaderboard menu in the System Page or by speaking to the [Brown Bear] when claiming or turning in a quest.
*   The [Most Commando Captures Leaderboard] previously shared this non-display property but has since been removed from the game entirely.
*   On March 17, 2022, Onett abbreviated the amount of honey displayed on both the Daily Top Honeymakers and All-Time Top Honeymakers leaderboards to accommodate players who accumulated massive amounts of honey.
*   During Winter Beesmas 2024, the [Highest Snowbear Level leaderboard] and [Highest Robo Party Cake Rank] did not receive a standard reset.
