# Basic Boots

![Basic Boots](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/7c/Basic_Boots.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190402214940)

**Description:**
"Collect pollen as you walk through flowers!"

The Basic Boots are a pair of boots introduced in the 2018-09-10 update. They can be purchased from the Noob Shop.

**Stats & Bonuses:**
*   +4 Player Movespeed
*   +2 Movement Collection (Increases pollen collection while walking through flowers)

**Cost:**
To acquire Basic Boots, players must spend:
*   4,400 Honey
*   3 Sunflower Seeds
*   3 Blueberries

**Trivia & Mechanics:**
*   These boots were originally obtainable from the Sun Bear by completing his first five quests before he departed on 2018-10-08.
*   They are unique among boots in that they do not grant Jump Power.
*   Prior to the 2018-11-25 update, this item, along with Hiking Boots and Beekeeper's Boots, did not require crafting materials.
*   Unlike Gummy Boots and Coconut Clogs, Basic Boots (along with Hiking Boots and Beekeeper's Boots) were available through quests.
*   These boots are used to obtain the Yellow Wiggly Walking Person Sticker by walking over a randomly chosen flower in Starter, 5, or 10 bee zones.
