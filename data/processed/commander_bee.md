# Commander Bee

**Commander Bee** is a Colorless Epic bee. It is described as "A strong, no-nonsense bee who stays level headed when things get rough."

[![Commander Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0e/Commander_Bee.png/revision/latest?cb=20200403235837)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/0e/Commander_Bee.png/revision/latest?cb=20200403235837) [![CommanderBee Gifted](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/CommanderBee_Gifted.png/revision/latest?cb=20200403235843)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d7/CommanderBee_Gifted.png/revision/latest?cb=20200403235843)

**Rarity:** Epic
**Color:** Colorless
**Base Stats:**
*   Energy: 30
*   Speed: 14
*   Attack: 4

Commander Bee's favorite treat is Sunflower Seeds. It likes the Spider Field and the Cactus Field, but dislikes the Dandelion Field.

## Statistics

### Collection Rates
*   **Pollen:** Collects 15 Pollen in 4 seconds (3.75 per second).
    *   With x2 Bee Pollen gamepass: 30 Pollen in 4 seconds (7.5 per second).
*   **Honey:** Makes 80 Honey in 4 seconds (20 per second).
    *   With x2 Honeymaking Speed gamepass: 80 Honey in 2 seconds (40 per second).

### Bonuses
*   +50% Energy
*   +5 Gather Amount
*   +5% Critical Chance
*   +3 Attack
*   🌟 **Gifted Hive Bonus:** +3% Critical Chance.

## Abilities

*   **Focus:** Grants +3% Critical Chance for 20 seconds. This ability stacks up to 10 times. When critical hits occur, pollen and damage increase by 100%.
*   **Buzz Bomb:** Collects 7 pollen from 13 surrounding Flowers (+10% pollen per Bond level). Can be combined with other bombs to increase power.

### Trivia
*   Commander Bee shares its skin with Looker Bee.
*   Along with Buoyant Bee and Frosty Bee, this is one of the only three bees in the game that wear hats by default. Commander Bee is the only colorless bee among them to have a hat.
    *   Festive Bee and Puppy Bee can also acquire accessories using the Festive Wreath and Reindeer Antlers, respectively.
*   Commander Bee is one of three bees (along with Looker Bee and Music Bee) that possess the Focus ability by default.
*   Commander Bee was the only epic bee added in a dedicated update.
*   A Commander Bee Egg can be obtained by capturing Commando Chick 200 times.
*   The bee is likely inspired by the video game protagonist, Commander Keen. This inspiration is supported by an expired code ("Keen4") that yielded a Commander Bee Jelly.
