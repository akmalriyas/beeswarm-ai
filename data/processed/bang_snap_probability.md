These probabilities show the likelihood of a stat falling within specific ranges, or being upgraded to a certain range by spending one wax point. Only stats that possess both base and upgrade ranges are included in these charts.

*Note: Due to the extensive size of these data tables, they have been presented here for reference. Results are rounded to three to five decimal places; precise percentages may vary slightly due to floating-point arithmetic.*
