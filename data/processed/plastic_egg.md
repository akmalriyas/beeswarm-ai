# Plastic Egg

[![Plastic Egg](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d1/Plastic_Egg.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404043959)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d1/Plastic_Egg.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404043959)

The Plastic Egg was a blue-colored egg that appeared during Brown Bear's 2019 Egg Hunt quest. Players needed to find all three eggs to complete the quest, which rewarded them with a Marshmallow Bee. The item had no special abilities and would be removed from the player's inventory upon turning in the quest.

After the Egg Hunt 2019 concluded (following the 2019-05-13 update), the locations of the Plastic Eggs were replaced by Ticket tokens.

## Locations
The Plastic Egg could be found in several areas:

*   **Blue Maze:** Located next to the Ticket Tent. To find it, turn left first and then follow the right wall.
*   **Basic Bee Gate Ramp:** Found opposite the Blue Flower Field and behind the original location of the Honey Bee NPC.
*   **Lava Parkour (Obstacle Course):** The token is visible through a small window outside the lava parkour area. Upon entering, immediately turn right and climb up the rocks to find it.

## Trivia
*   The Plastic Egg was the first and only egg item that could be obtained in inventory but could not hatch into a bee.
*   During the Egg Hunt 2019 lobby, the portal incorrectly stated players needed "Find 5 Plastic Eggs," when only three were required to obtain the Marshmallow Bee. This quest was not the sole requirement for completing the event.
*   The Plastic Egg and the Broken Drive are among the few items whose sole purpose is to complete a specific quest.
