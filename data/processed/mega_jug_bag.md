# Mega-Jug Bag

## Overview

![Mega-Jug](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d6/Mega-Jug.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/267?cb=20190403021540)

The **Mega-Jug** is a large bag that can be purchased in the Pro Shop. It boasts a humongous capacity and conversion rate bonus.

### Stats & Properties
| Property | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cost** | 50,000 Honey |
| **Capacity** | 25,000 Pollen |
| **Bonus Stat** | +40% Convert Rate |

## Acquisition and Crafting

### Purchase Information
The Mega-Jug is available for purchase in the Pro Shop. It is noted as the cheapest bag in the Pro Shop.

### Crafting Ingredients
*   50,000 Honey

## Trivia & Lore

*   **Pro Shop Ranking:** The Mega-Jug is the second cheapest item overall in the Pro Shop, only surpassed by the Super-Scooper (which costs 44,000 Honey).
*   **Smaller Variant:** A smaller version of the Mega-Jug exists and can be found in the Noob Shop. This variant is called the Jar, which has a base Capacity of 750 Pollen for a price of 650 Honey.
