# Basic Bee Gate

[![The Basic Bee on top of the gate with a Santa hat on during Beesmas.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d9/BasicGateXmas.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211231135637)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d9/BasicGateXmas.png/revision/latest?cb=20211231135637) The Basic Bee Gate.

The **Basic Bee Gate**, also known as the **5 Bee Gate**, is the first gate a player must unlock in the game. To pass through, players must own 5 or more bees. It is named after the large Basic Bee featured on top of the structure. When passing through during the daytime, the audio track "[bpatrol](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Music)" plays.

### Contents
*   **Mobs:**
    *   1 Spider
    *   2 Rhino Beetles
    *   2 Ladybugs
*   **Quest Givers:**
    *   Panda Bear
*   **Shops:**
    *   Gumdrop Shop
*   **Fields:**
    *   Spider Field
    *   Bamboo Field
    *   Strawberry Field

### Trivia and Mechanics

*   The Basic Bee Gate is the only gate that has more than one other gate directly leading from it: the Brave Bee Gate and the Honey Bee Gate.
*   It requires the minimum number of bees to pass through any gate in the game.
*   Along with the Ant Gate, this is one of the few zones where nothing can be purchased using Honey.
*   Historically, Bubble Bee Man was located behind the Global Top Battlers leaderboard but was removed after Beesmas 2018.
*   Prior to the 2020-06-06 update, a Honey Bee NPC was positioned next to the ramp.
*   Underneath the ramp, on the side facing the Blue HQ, there is a ticket token (previously a Plastic Egg).
*   A glitch once allowed the Onett statue near the hives to be seen from this gate; this has since been patched.
*   This gate is unique because it prevents Wild Windy Bee spawns in its fields (unless assisted by Onett, during Beesmas, or accessed from another field), excluding the Ant Gate and Bear Gate.
*   Unlike some other gates, if a player does not have the required number of bees, this gate will not teleport them back to the spawn point.
*   During the Beesmas event, the Basic Bee model wears a festive Santa hat, making it the only gate whose bee changes appearance during an event.
