The Global Top Ant Exterminators Leaderboard is one of the game's leaderboards, tracking players' highest scores in the Ant Challenge. While it displays 10 positions at a time, players can scroll down to view scores all the way to the 100th place.

**Mechanics:**
*   Stepping on the white pad in front of the leaderboard displays the message: "Your Highscore: X" (where X is your personal high score).
*   The structure conceals a secret passage that grants access to both an Ant Pass and the Gummy Bee Egg Claim.

## Location

This leaderboard is located in front of the Ant Challenge room, near the Ant Pass Dispenser and the Free Ant Pass Dispensers.

## Achievements

Milestones tracking players who reached specific extermination counts:
*   **2018-08-01:** SDMittens was the first user to reach 200 Ant Exterminations.
*   **2018-12-26:** SDMittens was the first user to reach 300 Ant Exterminations.
*   **2019-12-14:** Lipsisas was the first user to reach 400 Ant Exterminations.
*   **2020-01-10:** Zlib was the first user to reach 500 Ant Exterminations.

## Trivia

*   The scores on this leaderboard were reset during the 2018-11-25 update.
*   The lowest recorded score on the leaderboard is 530 points, achieved by players ranked from 67th to 100th place.
*   On 2024-01-12, many players' high scores were lost due to an unexplained data wipe.
