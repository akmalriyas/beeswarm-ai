# How to Program BASIC

The **How to Program BASIC** is a cosmetic Roblox hat that was available during the [Ready Player Two] event, starting on November 23, 2020. It could be obtained by successfully solving all of the puzzles found in [The Computer]. As a Roblox accessory, players can wear this item on their avatar.

When a player completes the puzzle sequence inside The Computer, the hat will appear within the computer structure. However, if the player leaves and subsequently rejoins the game, the hat will not reappear.
