# Blue Guard

![Blue Guard](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/94/Blue_Guard.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403020304 "Blue Guard")

## Description
A durable pad worn on the right shoulder of Blue Beekeepers.

The **Blue Guard** is a guard purchasable in the Blue HQ and is equipped on the right shoulder. It is one of three guards available in the Blue HQ, alongside the Elite Blue Guard and Bucko Guard.

> "A durable pad worn on the right shoulder of Blue Beekeepers."

## Stats
*   **Capacity:** +25,000
*   **Blue Pollen:** +15%
*   **Defense:** +5%
*   **Blue Bee Attack:** +1

## Cost & Crafting Ingredients
| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :--- | :--- |
| Honey | 1,000,000 |
| Blueberries | 50 |
| Royal Jelly | 1 |
| Moon Charms | 3 |

## Trivia
*   Before the 2018-11-25 update, the Blue Guard cost only 1,000,000 (1 million) honey and required no crafting materials.
*   Its counterpart in the Red HQ is the Red Guard.
*   It is the cheapest guard available in the Blue HQ and one of the cheapest items in the shop.
