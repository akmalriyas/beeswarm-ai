# Ticket Tent

![TicketTent2021](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/44/TicketTent2021.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20260203055053)

## Overview

The Ticket Tent is a shop located behind the Red Cannon and near the Hives. It sells six types of Event Bees, as well as Star Treats. The shop's name comes from the fact that all items must be purchased using Tickets. A Royal Jelly token is also found on top of the tent.

## Items for Sale

All items are priced in Tickets.

| Item | Price (Tickets) | Description & Hatching Odds |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |

| **Gold Egg** | 100 (Originally 50) | Usually hatches into an Epic Bee (79% chance). May hatch as a Legendary (20%) or Mythic (1%). |
| **Diamond Egg** | 400 | Usually hatches into a Legendary Bee (95% chance). Has a 5% chance to be Mythic. |
| **Mythic Egg** | 2,500 | Always hatches into a Mythic Bee! |

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   **Festive Bee:** The Festive Bee was one of the only bees in this shop that could be obtained without tickets (for a limited time).
*   **Gummy Bee Availability:** Gummy Bees used to also have limited availability but are now permanently available for 2,500 Gumdrops.
*   **Royal Jelly Token:** The Royal Jelly token found on the tent was mentioned in dialogue by Sun Bear.
*   **Star Treats Acquisition:** Buying a Star Treat from this shop is one of the few ways to obtain them without using Robux.
*   **Historical Pricing:** When first implemented, the most expensive item was the Star Treat (1,000 tickets). After the Beesmas 2019 event ended, it became the Cub Buddy (2,000 tickets). As of the 2024-05-23 update, Mythic Eggs are the most expensive at 2,500 tickets.
*   **Gummy Bear's Lair:** Before the 2019-04-05 update, players with the Goo Hotshot Badge could use gumdrops on the Gummy Bee to enter Gummy Bear's Lair; this mechanic was later moved to the Gummy Bee Egg Claim.
*   **Model Details:** The wings on the Gifted Photon Bee model are not transparent, unlike the giant Festive Bee and Puppy Bees' wings. Conversely, the Festive Bee's wings are not solid, while the Puppy Bee and Gifted Photon Bee models have solid wings. The Photon Bee is also the only model available in gifted form.
*   **Spotted Chicks:** Like other specialized shops (e.g., Noob Shop or Pro Shop), Spotted chicks could spawn here during the Egg Hunt 2020 event.
*   **Location Access:** It is possible to view the back of the Ticket Tent by going out of bounds behind the Coconut Field.
