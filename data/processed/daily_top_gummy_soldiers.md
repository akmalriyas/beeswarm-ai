# Daily Top Gummy Soldiers Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Gummy Soldiers** is one of 64 leaderboards available in the game, viewable only through the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page. This leaderboard tracks the total amount of [Goo](/wiki/Goo "Goo") collected by players on a given day. It displays the top 10 positions at any time, but users can scroll down to view all the way to the 100th place. The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

**Top Rank (Position #1):**
Players who reach the top rank by the end of the day are awarded a [Glowering Gummy Bear Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker").

**High Rank (Positions 1-100):**
Players who place within the top 100 by the end of the day are awarded 50 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
