# Mantis

The **Mantis** is a mob that defends the Pineapple Patch and the Pine Tree Forest in Bee Swarm Simulator. It is characterized by its green body and red eyes, with arms extended like a praying mantis.

## Strategy Guide

The Mantis possesses a long attack range. Players should avoid engaging until they are close or the Mantis initiates an attack. A recommended tactic is to position near the edge of its jump range, then walk in a wide circle around it when it prepares to strike. This often causes the Mantis to undershoot and land closer to the player, allowing Bees to begin attacking even after the Mantis retreats.

Additionally, players can defeat the Mantis using the Frozen Field Defenders Glitch, similar to other mobs. If a Mantis becomes stuck in an object (such as the wall surrounding the Pineapple Patch field or smaller pineapples), it is also easier to defeat.

## Trivia

*   **Naming Conventions:** Early on, there were inconsistencies with the plural form of "Mantis" when referenced in quests (e.g., "4 Mantiss" instead of "4 Mantises"). This error was later corrected.
*   **Quest Dialogue:** In Bee Bear's quest 'BEE-liz Honey-dad', he initially requested 10 Mantises, but this detail was not mentioned upon subsequent conversations.
*   **Mob Behavior:** The Mantis has the longest attack jump length of any mob, rivaled only by the Werewolf. This allows it to jump outside its defending field into adjacent fields.
*   **Respawn Time Consistency:** Mantises share the same respawn time as the Scorpion.
*   **Historical Design:** According to a former moderator, the Mantis was originally intended to be a cricket before being changed into its current form; the reason for this change is unknown.
