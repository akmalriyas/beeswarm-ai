# Easter Eggs

The map contains numerous hidden **Easter Eggs**. Some of these have been removed from the game, while others remain active.

## Codes and Hints

Hints for codes are scattered across the map, typically written in white text and placed in unusual locations. The known codes and their hiding spots include:

*   **Cog:** Found at the top of the Noob Shop. (Note: This location was moved when Star Hall was added in the 2018-07-11 update.)
*   **38217:** Located on the backpack of the Onett statue, which is situated on top of the rightmost hive. (The interactive NPC Onett has a different number on his backpack; this specific number is the code.)
*   **Connoisseur:** A hint hidden behind the Brave Bee Gate Instant Converter.
*   **Crawlers:** A hint found within the King Beetle's Lair.
*   **Roof:** Found on top of the Red HQ.
*   **Wink:** A hint located in the White Tunnel.
*   **BOPMASTER:** This code is revealed through Panda Bear's last dialogue after completing his quest.

## Tutorial Decals (Removed)

The original help textures, which served as tutorial decals outside the map, were found underneath the map, by the side. These are no longer active elements in the game. Players can access a [Tutorial] section by pressing the small red '?' icon on the far right side of the screen.

## Seasonal and Decorative Easter Eggs (Level 2)

*   **Puppy Bee with Reindeer Antlers & Festive Bee with Festive Wreath:** These two decorative statues are located behind the wall of the Blue HQ, outside the map area.
    *   The Puppy Bee is named this due to its nose, not because it possesses actual [Reindeer Antlers].
    *   The Festive Bee was added during Part 2 of Beesmas 2020 and was placed next to the Puppy Bee statue.
    *   **Access:** These can be reached by either using a Red Cannon combined with the parachute/glider to glide towards them, or by jumping from a great distance.

## Onett Statue

This easily discoverable Easter Egg is located on top of the rightmost hive (the 6th hive). Behind the statue, there is a [Royal Jelly] token. The number visible on its backpack, **38217**, functions as a code that grants 5 tickets.

**Access Methods:**
1.  Use a Yellow Cannon, Red Cannon, or Blue Cannon (using only the glider) combined with the parachute/glider to glide onto the top of the hive.
2.  On rare occasions, the statue may fall to the ground and be found inside the honeycombs within the rightmost hive. It can also sometimes be found behind the 5-Bee Gate.
3.  Jump from the tail of the Puppy Bee on the Ticket Tent or the Gifted Photon Bee on the ticket tent, aiming for the top of the hive (requires sufficient jump power/speed).
4.  Jump into the Gummy Bee in the Gummy Bee Egg Claim area and use the Glider to reach the hives.
5.  Use a combination of jumping and gliding from the coconut field onto the hive tops.

*Note: Unlike the Onett NPC found in the Bear Gate, this statue cannot be interacted with.*

## Dancing Bear / Shadow Bear

[Shadow Bear] is located beneath the platform where the [Onett (NPC)] stands. It resembles the Tunnel Bear but is significantly larger and does not attack upon contact. In front of the bear, there is a collectible [Diamond Egg] token. Collecting this token is challenging because if the player remains on the ground for too long, they will die. Players with high speed can run across the floor without dying. Furthermore, players with good ping may be able to hold jump, as the death timer increases with ping.

## Hallway Behind Badge Bearer's Guild

A hidden hallway exists behind the [Ace Shop] within the Badge Bearer's Guild. This area is inaccessible through normal gameplay but can be viewed using ROBLOX's Shift Lock feature. It leads to an entrance into the Honey Void area below the game map. While this path was open in the test realm, it is currently blocked by a transparent wall in the main game, though the hole remains visible via shift lock.

## Honey Void

This expansive, out-of-bounds area is located past the Red HQ, towards a large green wedge outside the map. Upon entering the hole, players find flat land with its own unique music track, "Hibernation1."

### Bee Hibernation
A notable feature of this area is that bees appear to freeze or enter hibernation upon first entering the Honey Void, matching the theme of the music. While this effect was not present throughout the entire history of the area's appearance, bees can occasionally unfreeze when a player approaches a corner and then freeze again as they reach the player.

### Strange Drawing
Located roughly in the middle of the Honey Void is a two-dimensional drawing depicting various game elements: a [Bear], [Windy Bee], [Vicious Bee], [Coconut Crab], [Onett] (equipped with a Petal Wand and Petal Belt and Beekeeper's Mask), [Basic Bee], [King Beetle], and [Stick Bug].

### Vector Bee and Tadpole Bee Statues (Level 2)
In the same area as the strange drawing, there are two non-interactive bee models: one of a Tadpole Bee and one of a Vector Bee.

## Windy Bee and Vicious Bee Statues (Level 2)

If players go out of bounds to the right side of the map, they can find statues of a Level 2 [Vicious Bee] and [Windy Bee]. These statues are non-interactive.

## Removed Easter Eggs

The following items were previously in the game but have since been removed:

*   **Tabby Bee Block:** During the Tabby Bee Update (2018-05-12), a block featuring a [Tabby Bee] image was found outside the map, along with the bee itself. This hinted at the expired code "Meow," which granted 5 Tickets.
*   **Invisible Platform:** An invisible platform once existed in the air above and just in front of the middle hive. It could be accessed using a cannon and glider near the middle hive's white platform, but this is no longer possible due to the current configuration of six hives.
*   **Mysterious Figure:** Also known as Eggwick from the Egg Hunt 2019 event, this figure was located in the Blue Maze to the right of the Festive Bee on the Ticket Tent. After the Egg Hunt 2019 event concluded, the statue and its associated items (one Plastic Egg token and one Jelly Beans) were removed. It has since been replaced by a [Star Jelly] token.
*   **Red HQ Room:** A hidden room behind a wall in the Red HQ. This area cannot be accessed without glitches. The room underwent minor changes during the 2021-12-26 and 2022-12-26 updates, though it originally featured an unusable Red Extract dispenser.
*   **Red HQ Attic:** By flying out of bounds through a hole on the back wall of the Red HQ area, players can enter the attic space inside the Red HQ structure.
