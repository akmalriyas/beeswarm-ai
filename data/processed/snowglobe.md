### Description and Mechanics

A **Snowglobe** is a Level 7 Beesmas Beequip. It has an equip limit of two and requires no specific color. It can be equipped to Basic, Bumble, Cool, Bucko, Frosty, or Windy bees.

The Snowglobe grants the following base bonuses:
*   +Bomb Pollen%
*   +Ability Rate%
*   **Ability:** Snowglobe Shake

### Stat Generation and Potential

Beequip stats are generated using old RNG functions. The potential of a Beequip is displayed in-game rounded to the nearest 1 ★ (or 0.5 ★ in Dapper Bear's Shop), but the internal values are not rounded.

### Acquisition Methods

#### Current Ways to Obtain
*   As a rare drop from the Mondo Chick.
*   By purchasing the Super Snowman Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.

#### Outdated/Historical Acquisition Methods
*   Purchasing the Beequip bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog (cost: 75 snowflakes and 15 Gingerbread Bears + other items).
*   Dropped upon defeating a level 10 Snowbear.
*   Purchasing the Snowglobe bundle for 2000 snowflakes during Beesmas 2020.
*   Buying the Snowey Starter Pack (400 Robux) from the Robux Shop.
*   By opening the Lapis Gift Box.
*   As a reward for completing Gifted Bucko Bee's Beesmas 2024 Quest.
*   As a drop from the Glass Gift Box.

### Trivia

*   The Snowglobe is one of three unique ability-granting Beequips, alongside Reindeer Antlers and Festive Wreath.
*   Its unique ability is also the only way to obtain the Cool Breeze buff outside of Beesmas events.
