# Diamond Mask

[ ![Diamond Mask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/75/Diamond_Mask.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403022722) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/75/Diamond_Mask.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403022722 "Diamond Mask")

### Description
"Proudly show your extreme wealth to the world. Shine so brightly that others will complain."

The Diamond Mask is a mask and the blue counterpart of the Demon Mask. It can be acquired by finding the doorway near the end of the Cloud Obby, followed by completing an additional obstacle course made up of moon platforms. Players can bypass the Cloud Obby using a Parachute or Glider with sufficient height to steer toward the entrance of the Diamond Room. If the player owns the Diamond Mask, the moon platforms in the Diamond Room become permanently solid and are shaded deep blue.

### Cost
*   5,000,000,000 Honey
*   250 Blue Extracts
*   150 Oil
*   100 Glitter
*   5 Diamond Eggs
*   1 Comforting Vial

***

### Stats and Bonuses
The Diamond Mask provides the following bonuses:

*   **Blue Pollen:** x1.5
*   **Pollen:** +25%
*   **Bubble Pollen:** x1.5
*   **Capacity:** x3
*   **Defense:** +35%
*   **Bee Ability Rate:** +20%
*   **Instant Blue Conversion:** +15%

#### Passive Abilities
The mask's passive abilities activate after collecting the 30th blue ability token.

**+Passive: Bubble Bombs (Requires Bubble Mask)**
Every 25th Bomb Ability Token spawns 25 Bubbles that last for 8 seconds. If a player touches these bubbles, they pop, collecting 4R/6W/8B pollen. This collection amount is increased by 15% per Gifted blue Bee type from nearby Flowers (up to 29 flowers). The bubbles also cause the surrounding flowers to grow.
*   *Cooldown:* 2 minutes

**+Passive: Diamond Drain**
Every 30th blue Ability Token converts 5 million Pollen plus a percentage of the total Convert Amount of all your bees combined. This conversion amount is increased by 10% per blue bee and +50% per gifted blue bee type in your Hive. It also pollinates 10 (+3 per blue bee) blue flowers within the field.
*   *Cooldown:* 45 seconds

### Trivia
*   Prior to the 2019-09-28 Update, players could not jump on moon platforms during the day, even if they owned the Diamond Mask.
*   The mask and the Honey Mask are the only items that require eggs to craft.
*   This mask and the Demon Mask are the only masks in the game that resemble Legendary bees. The Diamond Mask is based on the Diamond Bee and uses its face decal.
*   When receiving the quest 'Breath of Blue', Spirit Bear mentioned that Diamond Drain was activated every 25th blue Boost token, though this mechanic was later changed to every 30th blue ability token collected.
*   The Diamond Mask Shop, the Hub Field Shop, and the Demon Mask Shop are the only shops in the game that sell a single item.
*   This mask, the Demon Mask, and the Gummy Mask are the only masks with decorative accessories (the Diamond Mask has a tiara).
*   Unlike the Demon Mask and Gummy Mask, the sound effect of its passive is not based on music in the game.
