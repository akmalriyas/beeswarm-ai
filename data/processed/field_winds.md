# Field Winds

[![Field Wind visual representation](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/56/Screenshot_2022-03-26_120621.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20220326050702)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/56/Screenshot_2022-03-26_120621.png/revision/latest?cb=20220326050702) [](/wiki/File:Screenshot_2022-03-26_120621.png)

A **Field Wind** is a stacking buff applied to specific fields, granting bonuses to pollen gain and instant conversion rates. Each stack increases the amount of pollen gained from its associated field by a set amount and adds 3% instant conversion, starting from a base rate of 25%. Field Winds last for 30 minutes.

Field Winds are obtained by donating certain items to the Wind Shrine or by using specific codes. Note that field wind tokens received directly from the Wind Shrine have a lifespan of only 30 seconds.

### Mechanics and Stacking
When applied, Field Wind stacks accumulate, and the duration is reset to 30 minutes with each application. If another code grants a boost for the same field, it can also be used to extend or reset the timer for another 30 minutes. The maximum stack limit for Field Winds is x15. This cap can be reached if the donated item is rare enough and the player has sufficient Wind Shrine favor.

Field Winds can also be replenished using codes; for instance, if both the Wind Shrine and a code grant field winds, applying either will restart the 30-minute timer.

**Note:** Unlike Field Boosts, fields enhanced by Field Winds do not generate sparkles. However, their identifying icons are identical to those of Field Boosts, distinguished only by having a white background.

Lower level fields generally yield more field pollen than higher level fields.

### Wind Shrine Donations
