# Mixed Brick Field

## Field Statistics
*   **Assorted Color:** Mixed Brick
*   **Bee Prerequisite:** 10 bees
*   **Dimensions:** 19 × 19 flowers
*   **Total Flower Count:** 361
*   **Bee Preferences:** None (Likes/Dislikes)

## Field Description and Mechanics
The Mixed Brick Field is one of the four fields located in the Retro Swarm Challenge. It is a large, 19x19 field containing 361 flowers of white, blue, and red colors, available in single, double, or triple sizes. This field produces brick tokens while collecting pollen.

Since planters cannot be used in the Retro Swarm Challenge, there is no confirmed nectar type for this field.

### Flower Distribution
| Size | White Flowers | Red Flowers | Blue Flowers | Total by Size | Percentage of Field |

*Note: Flower counts within the table may vary slightly due to rounding. In-game, all flowers are randomly generated and may not precisely align with these percentages.*

## Obtaining Boosts
Using Glitter in the Mixed Brick Field grants a 100% Mixed Brick Field pollen boost for 15 minutes.

## Trivia
*   The Mixed Brick Field and three other fields present in the Retro Swarm Challenge are the only fields without an associated field stamp.
*   All of these unique challenge fields borrow the Sunflower Field's boost icon.
