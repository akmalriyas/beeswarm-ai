# Supreme Star Amulet

The **Star Amulets** are a series of amulets that can be purchased in five different tiers from machines located in the [Star Hall]. These amulets provide buffs to the owner and always increase the user's capacity as they ascend through the tiers. Higher-tier amulets grant more powerful benefits, while Diamond and Supreme Star Amulets possess additional unique Passive Abilities.

**Note:** This content contains information obtained through datamining. Details may be inaccurate or outdated.

## Mechanics and Generation

Each Star Amulet requires the user to have a specific number of [Gifted Bee](/wiki/Gifted_bee "Gifted bee") types discovered. After successfully generating an amulet, there is a 1-second cooldown before another can be generated. The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the number of discovered gifted bee types until it reaches its maximum tier:

*   **Non-Supreme Star Amulets:** Maximum quality requires 45 types.
*   **Supreme Star Amulet:** Maximum quality requires 50 types (Note: Currently, only 46 total bee types exist in the game).

When an amulet is generated, a specific audio clip plays, with the sound pitch decreasing as the tier increases.

## Stat Selection and Buffs

All amulets grant a guaranteed increase to Capacity, and then randomly select additional stats based on the tier. The probability tables below detail how many stats are selected per amulet type:

### Bronze Star Amulet
*   **Guaranteed:** Capacity (x1.25) - 100%
*   **Selects 2 Stats:** Convert Rate, Pollen, White Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, Bee Ability Rate, Critical Chance, Bee Gather Pollen, Instant Conversion.

### Silver Star Amulet
*   **Guaranteed:** Capacity (x1.5) - 100%
*   **Selects 3 Stats:** Convert Rate, Pollen, White Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, Bee Ability Rate, Critical Chance, Bee Gather Pollen, Instant Conversion.

### Gold Star Amulet
*   **Guaranteed:** Capacity (x1.75) - 100%
*   **Selects 4 Stats:** Convert Rate, Pollen, White Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, Bee Ability Rate, Critical Chance, Bee Gather Pollen, Instant Conversion.

### Diamond Star Amulet
*   **Guaranteed:** Capacity (x2) - 100%
*   **Selects 5 Stats:** Convert Rate, Pollen, White Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, Bee Ability Rate, Critical Chance, Bee Gather Pollen, Instant Conversion.
*   **Passive Selection:** Has a small chance to grant passive abilities (Guiding Star or Star Shower).

### Supreme Star Amulet
*   **Guaranteed:** Capacity (x2.5) - 100%
*   **Selects 5 Stats:** Convert Rate, Pollen, White Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, Bee Ability Rate, Critical Chance, Bee Gather Pollen, Instant Conversion.
*   **Passive Selection:** This is the primary feature of this amulet. There are six possible passive abilities (Guiding Star, Pop Star, Star Shower, Gummy Star, Scorching Star, Star Saw). The base chance for any specific passive is 1/6. There is also a 1/50 chance to receive two passive abilities.
    *   **Guarantee:** If the player has 500B honey or more when generating an amulet, they can spend this amount to guarantee a double passive ability.

***Note on Stat Probabilities:*** *Detailed tables showing the percentage of specific stat strengths at various quality milestones are available on designated subpages for each amulet type (e.g., Star Amulet/Probability#Bronze_Star_Amulet).*

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Development History:** The Supreme Star Amulet was introduced in the 2020-06-06 update, succeeding the Diamond Star Amulet Generator locationally. The original Star Amulets were released earlier (2018-07-11).
*   **Passive Abilities:** Only the Diamond and Supreme Star Amulets can possess passive abilities.
    *   The Diamond Star Amulet was buffed in the 2020-06-06 update, gaining a 1/100 chance of receiving a passive upon generation.
    *   The Diamond Star Amulet is limited to granting only two specific passives: Guiding Star and Star Shower.
*   **Supreme Passives:** The Supreme Star Amulet allows for double passive abilities. If the player has between 490 billion and 500 billion honey, they can purchase a guaranteed double passive for 490B.
*   **Passive Visuals/Audio:**
    *   The summoned Gummy Star, Pop Star, and Scorching Star rotate counterclockwise around the player.
    *   The background audio for these passives plays differently: 3 times for Gummy and Scorching star, 1.5 times for Pop star, and 7 times for Star Saw.
    *   If a player dies while one of these stars is active, the star will continue to hover at the death location, maintaining its buffs and playing its tune (though without the model following the player).
*   **Specific Passives:** The Star Shower passive functions similarly to [Science Bear's Beesmas Lights].
*   **Rare Drops:** The Diamond Star Amulet has a 1/1,000,000 chance of giving a Star Cub Skin.
