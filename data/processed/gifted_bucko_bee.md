# Gifted Bucko Bee NPC

> **Note:** This page contains information documented late and may have missing content (e.g., "Bucko Bee's Snow Machine" quest dialogue for 2024 Winter is partially missing).

This page details the quest-giving version of Bucko Bee. For information on the worker bee variant, see the [Bucko Bee] page.

## Overview
| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Species** | Bee NPC |
| **Location** | Blue HQ (Second Floor) |
| **Prerequisites** | Discovered 4 blue types of bees |

Gifted Bucko Bee is one of three permanent Quest Bees, alongside Gifted Riley Bee and Honey Bee. He is located on the second floor of the Blue HQ, situated between the Top Bucko Bee Helpers and the Daily Top Blue Collectors Leaderboards.

To begin receiving or completing his quests, the player must first obtain a [Translator] from Science Bear (three times).

**Quest Progression Milestones:**
*   Completing 150 quests grants access to crafting the [Hydroponic Planter].
*   Completing 250 quests grants access to crafting the [Tide Popper].

## Rewards
Rewards vary based on quest completion count. Every reward includes one [Blue Extract] and [Honey].

**Special Quest Rewards:**
*   The "Petals" quest rewards a [Bloom Shaker].

### Honey Amount Scaling
The amount of honey received is determined by the highest value in this table, where $\text{Q}$ is the number of quests completed plus 1.

| Quest Milestone | Honey Amount |
| :--- | :--- |
| Every 100th quest | $25,000,000 \times \text{Q}$ |
| Every 5th quest | $2,500,000 \times \lfloor \text{Q}^{0.95} \rfloor$ |
| All other quests | $1,000,000 \times \lfloor \text{Q}^{0.9} \rfloor$ |

## Trivia
*   Since the quests are all chosen at random, including the first, the formula for the initial requirements of any quest is "X divided by (number of Gifted Bucko Bee quests completed + 1)".
*   Gifted Bucko Bee is one of three NPCs based on bees; the others are Gifted Riley Bee and Honey Bee (NPC). This bee is unique among the trio for residing indoors, although Gifted Riley Bee previously did as well.
*   In the dialogue for the 2020 Beesmas Snow Machine quest, Gifted Bucko Bee mentions defeating ten spiders, but the actual quest requirement only asks the player to defeat five spiders.
*   Gifted Bucko Bee enjoys playing with marbles, which is evidenced by their "Present" dialogue where they remark: “Can't wait to crack this open and roll those bad boys around.”
