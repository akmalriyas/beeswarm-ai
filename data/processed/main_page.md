# Bee Swarm Simulator Wiki

Welcome to the official community wiki for **Bee Swarm Simulator**! This collaborative resource covers everything about the popular bee hive-themed ROBLOX game, created by [Onett](/wiki/Onett_\(Developer\) "Onett \(Developer\)").

This wiki is open to all contributors. We currently host **701 articles** and feature **27,010 images**, supported by over **446,808 edits**.

***

## 📰 Recent News & Updates

**April 23, 2026: Beesmas Extended (Minor Update)**
Beesmas has been extended until May 31st! [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") has received five final quests, rewarding players with the exclusive [Petal Cub](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins "Cub Buddy") skin.

**December 27, 2025: x2 Event (Minor Update)**
The Honeyday Event is live! Join to gain x2 Pollen and x2 Convert Rate for 48 hours. Additionally, [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") quests have been released.

**December 25, 2025: Beesmas 2025 (Minor Update)**
*   **Bloom Mechanic:** A new mechanic allowing players to find or grow Blooms in fields and catch petals for various buffs.
*   **Vouchers:** Introduction of the Offline Voucher and Ticket Voucher.
*   **Traveling Bear:** [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") is helping prepare for Beesmas by delivering Presents.
*   **Decorations & Tree:** Complete honeyday decorations to unlock special map machines, and exchange gifts at the [Beesmas Tree](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree "Beesmas Tree") for boost-granting [Ornaments](/wiki/Ornaments "Ornaments").
*   **Catalog:** The [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog") is available to purchase special items using Gingerbread Bears and Snowflakes.
*   **Stickers:** Limited-time festive stickers are available during Beesmas!

**December 25th, 2024: Christmas Day (Minor Update)**
The Honeyday Event offers x2 Pollen and x2 Convert Rate for 48 hours. A new Robux Pack, "Royal Winter Wonder Haul," was released, introducing the [Nectar Shower Vial](/wiki/Nectar_Shower_Vial "Nectar Shower Vial") and the [Icy Crowned Hive Skin Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker").

**December 17th, 2024: Second Beesmas! (Minor Update)**
*   **Winter Spotlight Partnership:** Partnered with the Roblox Winter Spotlight Event. Players could earn currency by completing tasks in Bee Swarm; rewards continued post-event via Gift Boxes.
*   **Traveling Bear:** Complete quests to receive a Standard Token for the event, and continue to obtain Presents and a [Festive Planter](/wiki/Festive_Planter "Festive Planter").
*   **Elite Tokens:** Open the [Gift Boxes](/wiki/Gift_Boxes#2024_\(Winter\) "Gift Boxes") to receive Elite Tokens.
*   Decorations, Tree exchanges, and Catalog items were available during this event.

**August 1st, 2024: The Games Roblox Event (Minor Update)**
[Traveling Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") returned with limited-time quests for the Roblox event. Completing three quests earned points toward progression.
*   **Golf Mechanic:** A new mechanic was introduced, allowing players to hit Golf Balls into holes and activate targets for rewards.
