Dapper Bear is one of the eight permanent quest Bears in Bee Swarm Simulator. He resides inside his shop, located next to Stump Field.

**Shop & Rewards**
Inside Dapper Bear's shop, he sells various items, including planters (ranging from 750K to 100T Honey) and different types of Beequips. Some Beequip purchases are locked behind completing specific quests.

Upon speaking with him for the first time, Dapper Bear rewards the player with **3 Ticket Planters** and a **Beequip Case** (if the player does not already possess one).

***

## Requirements & Access
To enter Dapper Bear's shop, players must equip the following accessories:
*   Hiking Boots
*   Propeller Hat

Players may also use gear that is superior to these listed items.

***

## Quests

Dapper Bear currently offers 16 main quests. Most focus on collecting nectar, gathering tokens from planters, and popping Puffshrooms. The 16th quest (added in the 2026-01-19 Update) focuses mainly on stickers instead of the typical activities.

The quest for obtaining the **Blue Drive** to repair Robo Bear still requires players to collect Puffshrooms, nectar, and tokens from planters.

### Quest Rewards Mechanics
*   Quests #2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, and 16 reward an additional **Beequip Case Slot**.
*   If a player previously received Beequip Case Slots from Bee Bear, they will instead be rewarded with Ticket Planters until they catch up to the slot count of a new player.
*   If a player already has the Beequip Case Slot that a quest was supposed to award, they receive a Ticket Planter instead.

## Dialogue & Lore

**Initial Interaction:**
Dapper Bear is highly concerned with aesthetics and presentation. He explains that Planters grow by storing Pollen, Treats, Waxes, and Nectar—a substance that grants temporary boosts to honeymaking when bees sip it near the planter. He notes that some planters (like Ticket Planters) can only be harvested once, while those in his shop are reusable.

**Key Dialogue Insights:**
*   Dapper Bear views Beequips as accessories that help bees overcome self-doubt and stand out.
*   He provides tips on Planter usage: placing them on flower fields causes automatic growth; leaving the game pauses growth but maintains progress.
*   He discusses wax quality, noting that Soft Wax gives a minor buff to any Beequip, while Hard Wax works 60% of the time for double the buff, and Caustic Wax offers the best bonuses but only works 25% of the time (and destroys the Beequip if it fails).
*   He emphasizes the importance of variety in his shop, mirroring his own eclectic tastes.

**Beesmas Dialogue:**
Dapper Bear's seasonal dialogue focuses on warming up during winter and polishing his Samovar—a traditional Russian device used to warm Nectar and reconstitute Honeysuckles. He views this as a way to bring "spice" to the cold season.

## Trivia & Mechanics

*   **Size:** Dapper Bear is shorter than every other bear in the game, a detail referenced in his Tacky Planter quest chain.
*   **Indoor Bears:** Along with Gummy Bear, Tunnel Bear, Noob Bear, Pro Bear, Top Bear, and Shadow Bear, he is one of the few bears that resides indoors. He is the only permanent quest bear to reside indoors.
*   **Shop Ownership:** Dapper Bear is one of a select group (alongside Mother Bear and Robo Bear) who own shops. He is also the only permanent quest giver whose first quest was seasonal.
*   **Appearance:** Like Mother Bear and Gummy Bee, his face emits light, which is most noticeable at night.
*   **Samovar Mechanics:** The Samovar allows players to use it once every 6 hours for random Nectar (starting at 1 hour) and Honeysuckles. Each subsequent use during the Winter event increases the Nectar duration by 5 minutes (up to 4 hours).
