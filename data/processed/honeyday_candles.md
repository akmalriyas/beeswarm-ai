# Honeyday Candles

![Digital Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/27/Digital_Bee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20230415203844) | *Note: Some statistical data presented here is based on datamining and may be inaccurate or outdated.*

![The Honeyday Candles after completing Beesmas Quest.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1a/Honeyday_Candles.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210201015119)

The Honeyday Candles are a Beesmas-exclusive machine unlocked after completing Gifted Riley Bee's Honeyday Candles quest. They have a cooldown of 4 hours and are located next to the Red HQ's interior ladder.

### Mechanics
When activated, the machine summons three random Level 10 red bees for a duration of 30 minutes, and spawns three waxes of a random type.

#### Wax Drop Chances
The chances for each wax type are as follows:

| Wax | Chance |
| :--- | :--- |
| Soft Wax | ~81.3% |
| Hard Wax | ~16.26% |
| Swirled Wax | ~1.63% |
| Caustic Wax | ~0.81% |

#### Bee Summon Chances
The chances for each summoned bee are as follows:

| Bee | Chance |
| :--- | :--- |
| Fire Bee | ~70.59% |
| Demon Bee | ~23.53% |
| Spicy Bee | ~5.88% |

**Note:** If a player stands on the activation pad before completing the quest, a red text box will appear at the top of the screen reading: "The red bees are having trouble lighting the candles..."

### Trivia and Details

*   A Hard Wax token used to be found hidden behind the candles.
*   These candles are one of four machines that grant players temporary bees; the others are Onett's Lid Art, Gummy Beacon, and Honey Wreath (after completing Honey Bee's Beesmas quest).
*   There was a glitch where generated bees would remain in place until they despawned even after the player left the server. If the player rejoined the same server, the bees would not despawn.
*   The machine was introduced during the Beesmas 2020 event and has been included in all subsequent Beesmas updates.
*   During Beesmas 2020, Honeyday Candles were one of only two ways to obtain Swirled Waxes; the other method was giving Gifted Riley Bee a Present during that same event. For every Beesmas event succeeding Beesmas 2020, the candles no longer guarantee a Swirled Wax drop.
*   This is the only machine capable of summoning bees that produce flames (Passive Ability).
*   Loot Luck does not affect Honeyday Candles drops.
*   Summoned bees do not need to rest, even if the player dies.
