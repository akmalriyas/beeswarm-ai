# Public Sticker Board

The **Public Sticker Board** is located in the [Hive Hub](/wiki/Hive_Hub). It allows players to add or take stickers from it, serving as a way to obtain new stickers or remove spare ones.

### Mechanics and Cooldowns
*   **Taking Stickers:** There is a 22-hour cooldown before a player can take another sticker from the board.
*   **Adding Stickers:** There is a 10-minute cooldown before a player can add a sticker to the board.
*   **Quest Interaction:** The [Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine](/wiki/Sticker-Seeker_Quest_Machine) may occasionally require players to donate stickers to the Public Sticker Board.

## Trivia
*   Play Button and Game Chat Icon stickers generally cannot be donated to the Public Sticker Board; however, this restriction does not apply to players who are authorized to give out these specific stickers.
*   During the Beesmas of Summer 2024 event, a [Fluorescent Gift Box](/wiki/Gift_Boxes#2024_\(Summer\)) was placed behind the Public Sticker Board.
