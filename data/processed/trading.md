# Trading

**Trading** is a core mechanic introduced in the January 12, 2024 update that allows players to exchange various items, including Beequips, Cub Buddy Skins, and Stickers.

## Mechanics and Usage

The trade request menu can be initiated in two ways: by accessing the settings menu or by walking over another player's hive and accepting a prompt. Players manage their inventory within the trade window by interacting with the item buttons located in the top left corner.

To complete a trade, both players must select the "Accept" button. The trade then enters a waiting period of 5 to 20 seconds, depending on the total value of the items being exchanged. Once this time expires, the menu closes, and both participants receive their respective items, followed by a display of the item's stats.

**Key Rules:**
*   Trading can be performed an unlimited number of times with an unlimited number of players.
*   A player cannot trade with multiple individuals simultaneously.
*   After accepting or declining a trade request from a specific player, that player must initiate a new request before trading again.

## Trade Request Management

Players have the option to control whether they can receive trade requests by adjusting the "Allow trade requests" setting in the game settings. A player may be ineligible to receive requests for two reasons:
1.  They have not met the prerequisites for unlocking trading.
2.  They have manually disabled receiving trade requests.

If a player is unable to receive trades, they will see one of two messages:
*   **Unqualified:** "[{Player} has not unlocked trading (Requires Playtime Cadet, Ability Cadet, and Battle Hotshot Badges)]"
*   **Disabled:** "[{Player} has disabled trading]"

### Trading Requirements

To unlock the ability to trade, players must meet all of the following criteria:
*   20 Bees
*   Playtime Cadet Badge (8 hours of playtime)
*   Ability Cadet Badge (2500 Ability Tokens)
*   Battle Hotshot Badge (1000 Battle Points)

### Trade Cancellation and Safety

If a player decides to cancel a trade, both participants retain their original items. The trade menu closes immediately without displaying a cancellation popup.

**Safety Tips:**
*   Be cautious of scams involving "trust trades" or cross-trades. Always verify that the items being exchanged are of similar value before accepting.
*   Always aim for fair exchanges. If you initiate an unfair trade, or if your partner does, it is likely the trade will be automatically cancelled, and you should cancel it yourself.
*   Utilize the item inspector to ensure the quality and stats of Beequips meet your expectations, especially when collecting.

## Trivia

*   The item inspector within the trading menu is the sole official method for viewing the methods of obtainment for Play Button and Gamer Chat Icon stickers, as these items are not listed in the Sticker Index.
*   Trading was temporarily disabled on September 26, 2025, for an unknown reason. It was re-enabled on December 17, 2025, but initially restricted to the Hive Hub and disallowed trading of Cub Skins. Trading was fully re-enabled across all servers a week later, on December 28, 2025.
