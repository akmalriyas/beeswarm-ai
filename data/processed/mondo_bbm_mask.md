## Overview
[![MONDOB.B.M.M](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/00/Mondo_Bubble_Bee_Man_Mask.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20181225185556)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/00/Mondo_Bubble_Bee_Man_Mask.png/revision/latest?cb=20181225185556 "MONDOB.B.M.M")

The **Mondo B.B.M. Mask** is a mask that serves as the larger version of the standard [B.B.M. Mask](/wiki/B.B.M._Mask).

### Acquisition
This item was originally obtained by completing the "B.B.M. Mission" quest, which was part of Bubble Bee Man's questline during the Beesmas 2018 Event in the [30 Bee Zone](/wiki/Bear_Gate). To start this quest, players needed to complete Bee Bear’s entire (now unavailable) Beesmas 2018 questline.

Upon completion, the mask would automatically replace the player's current hat. Players can currently reequip this mask by speaking with BBM in the [30 Bee Zone](/wiki/Bear_Gate).

## Trivia
*   The Mondo B.B.M. Mask and the standard B.B.M. Mask are the only masks that cannot be purchased and have no description.
*   This mask, the B.B.M. Mask, the [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask), and the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask) are the only masks to provide a [Bee Attack](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Attack) buff.
*   It is one of only two items (the other being the [Mondo Belt Bag](/wiki/Mondo_Belt_Bag)) that is a "mondo version" of an accessory.
