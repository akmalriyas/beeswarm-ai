# Belt Pocket

![[Belt Pocket-0](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f2/Belt_Pocket.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190402215230)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f2/Belt_Pocket.png/revision/latest?cb=20190402215230 "Belt Pocket-0")

The **Belt Pocket** is a belt accessory found in the Noob Shop. It attaches to your waist, expanding the size of your container.

### Stats
| Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Capacity | +5,000 |
| Convert Amount | +10 |
| Loot Luck | +25% |

### Cost
*   **Honey:** 14,000
*   **Sunflower Seeds:** 10

### Trivia
*   The Belt Pocket was added after the Sun Bear left and is not given out by them.
*   It is the cheapest belt accessory in the game.
*   It is the only item in the Noob Shop that provides Loot Luck.
