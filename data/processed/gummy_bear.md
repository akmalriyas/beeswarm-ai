# Gummy Bear

**Gummy Bear** is a Non-Player Character (NPC) residing in **Gummy Bear's Lair**.

**Gummy Bear** is an NPC found in his lair who will attack players who approach him without possessing any of the gear sold in the shop. To interact with him, a player must own one of the following items: **Gummy Mask**, **Gummy Boots**, or **Gummyballer**. These items do not need to be worn.

To access Gummy Bear's Lair, players must have the Goo Hotshot Badge (or higher) and 20 bees. The entrance features the Gummy Bee Egg Claim at the Ant Gate.

### Mechanics
*   **Interaction:** Players can talk to him (if they own the required gear), claim his Beesmas quest, or give him a present during Beesmas events. He can also teleport players back to their Hive.
*   **Safety Protocol:** If a player without Gummy gear wishes to see Gummy Bear without losing pollen, they must empty their bag before entering the lair. Inside the lair, players can use the **Micro-Converter** or the **Whirligig** to empty their bags or teleport back to their hive, respectively.
*   **Warning:** The Whirligig *must* be used before the dialogue begins; otherwise, the player will still die when Gummy Bear's dialogue concludes.

### History and Appearances
Gummy Bear first appeared on the mountain during the **Gummy Invasion** event on May 26, 2018. During this event, he gave out 15 quests that ultimately rewarded players with a **Gummy Bee Egg**. He used his Gummy Cannon to shoot Gumdrops at fields and cover flowers in Goo, targeting every field except the Mountain Top Field, Stump Field, Coconut Field, and Pepper Patch (as these were not yet in the game).

He left on June 24, 2018, but returned as a permanent NPC in the November 25, 2018 update, accessible via the Gummy Bee model previously located at the Ticket Tent. Starting with Beesmas 2020, he offered the "Gummy Bear's Goo Years Beacon" quest during subsequent Beesmas events.

*   "They're not seeing clearly?"

## Trivia
*   Gummy Bear was originally the traveling bear for the Gummy Invasion event, running from May 26 to June 24, 2018 (30 days). He gave 15 quests culminating in a **Gummy Bee Egg**. He returned permanently on November 25, 2018.
*   Onett added an extra week to Gummy Bear's initial timer due to player requests.
*   Gummy Bear uses the Levitation Animation Pack and is one of two bears (along with Shadow Bear) that float rather than stand.
*   Like other permanent bears, he has a Bear Morph, which can be activated by the **Gummy Mask's Gummy Morph Passive Ability**. He is the only traveling bear to possess his own morph.
*   During his initial visit, Gummy Bear introduced several items: the **Gummy Bee**, the **Gummy Cannon** (a cannon unusable for transportation), and the **Gumdrop Shop**.
*   He is translucent, reflecting the candy he is named after, though his pink and teal colors do not match classic gummy bear hues. He is the first and currently only bear to have unnatural colors.
*   Gummy Bear gave a "Tapioca" code in one of his quests ("Peace Through Goo-nity").
*   Spirit Bear has stated that Gummy Bear was made out of gumdrops, and both he and his Gummy Bee are made of the same goo.
*   He is notable for being the first and only quest-giver capable of killing the player (alongside Tunnel Bear and Snowbear).
*   Gummy Bear is one of four NPCs to have moved locations permanently: Honey Bee, Bubble Bee Man, Sun Bear, and Gummy Bear.
