## Overview

The **Bang Snap** is a Level 10 Beequip. It has an equip limit of one, requires no specific color, and can be equipped to Common, Rare, or Epic Bees.

## Acquisition Methods

Bang Snap can be obtained through the following methods:

*   As a very rare drop from the **Pesticide Planter**.
*   Purchasing it in **Dapper Bear's Shop**.
*   Receiving it as a reward from the **Robo Bear Challenge**.

### Outdated Acquisition Methods
In the past, certain Gift Boxes dropped Bang Snap:
*   The Charcoal Gift Box.
*   The Gooey Gift Box.
