## Overview

Onett's Testing Group grants members access to the **Test Realm**, a dedicated Roblox place where Onett (Developer) tests updates before they are released in the main Bee Swarm Simulator game.

Joining this group is not automatic; users must submit a join request and await acceptance from Onett or an authorized group admin (such as zlib). The group is known for having a highly selective admission process, with acceptances occurring randomly at unannounced times, typically when the Test Realm is active.

## Purpose and Access Limitations

The primary function of this group is to limit access to temporary testing environments. Onett uses these places to test bug fixes and measure statistics on new content.

*   **Admission Policy:** The group rarely accepts new members. Due to how Roblox handles applications, the most recent requests are processed first. For the best chance of acceptance, applicants are advised to cancel and resend their request.
*   **Disclaimer:** Access to this group may be limited at any time. All games owned by this group are subject to going private or having their data reset.
