# Translator

[![Translator](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/92/Translator.png/revision/latest?cb=20200403235359)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/92/Translator.png/revision/latest?cb=20200403235359)

_"A device used to decipher languages you don't understand."_

**Cooldown:** N/A

Translators are inventory items that allow the player to communicate with specific NPCs: [Gifted Riley Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Riley_Bee), [Gifted Bucko Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bucko_Bee), and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stickbug).

### Usage Mechanics
To use a Translator, the player must initiate dialogue with the target NPC. The NPC will speak to the player in its native language, and then prompt the player with an option: either give the translator or leave. Giving the item allows the player to talk and receive quests from that NPC.

Translators are single-use items; once given to an NPC, they are removed from the player's inventory, but the ability to speak with that NPC is permanent. If a player accidentally uses a Translator on an NPC, they can leave the game before the item is consumed by finishing the conversation.

### Acquisition
All three Translators must be obtained from [Science Bear](/wiki/Science_Bear). He rewards players with a Translator after completing specific quests: "Limits of Language" (Quest 21), "Beesperanto" (Quest 26), and "Epistemological Endeavor" (Quest 31).

## Trivia
*   The player can acquire a total of three Translators, allowing communication with all three required NPCs. Without them, it is impossible to take quests, receive rewards, talk to, or give presents to Gifted Bucko Bee, Stick Bug, and Gifted Riley Bee.
*   Translators were originally implemented in the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25). Initially, only two translators were available because the third was tied to "Epistemological Endeavor," Science Bear's final quest. His last five quests were added on [2018-12-19](/wiki/Updates#2018-12-19), making all three available.
*   There is a consistent 5-quest difference between the acquisition of each Translator: Limits of Language (Q21) $\rightarrow$ Beesperanto (Q26) $\rightarrow$ Epistemological Endeavor (Q31).
*   The Translator is one of only two unique items obtainable while completing a bear's questline; the other is [Spirit Petals](/wiki/Spirit_Petal) from Spirit Bear.
*   It is one of the few items that can be given to an NPC, alongside [Presents](/wiki/Present).
*   If a player attempts to give Gifted Riley, Gifted Bucko, or Stick Bug a present without first giving them a Translator, only the regular dialogue will appear.
