# Mazes

Mazes are secret areas within the game that contain valuable items. To obtain these items, players must navigate through the mazes and locate them. Currently, there are two distinct mazes in the game, each located in a specific area.

## Blue Maze

The **Blue Maze** is situated next to the Ticket Tent (or behind the Beige Gift Box during Beesmas 2021). It was introduced in the Egg Hunt 2019 Update. Since it is located in the Starter Zone, no bee prerequisites are required to enter.

Inside the maze, players can obtain five tickets by taking a left turn followed by a right turn, or a jelly bean token by following the path on the right. During the 2019 Egg Hunt, the tickets were housed within a plastic egg; this has since been replaced by the ticket token. A star jelly token is also present on the right side, though it is initially blocked by a glass wall. This token can be accessed by going through a passage located behind the Petal Shop.

When inside the maze, the "Vendor" audio track plays.

*Note: Spotted Chicks could spawn in this maze previously but have since been removed.*

## Pro Shop Maze

The **Pro Shop Maze** is located behind the Pro Bear within the Pro Shop. Accessing it requires a minimum of 10 bees to pass through the Brave Bee Gate, which is adjacent to the Pro Shop.

Players can obtain a royal jelly token by proceeding to the end of the maze and turning right twice, then following the first available opening. This path leads to the royal jelly token. Additionally, there is a sunflower seed token inside that awards 10 sunflower seeds. To find this token, follow the same initial path as the royal jelly, but instead of making the final right turn, continue to the end of the hall and proceed through the last corridor.

If players approach closely enough, they can grab both tokens simultaneously by passing through the walls, which makes collection easier.

## Trivia

Before the Egg Hunt 2019 Event concluded on May 13, 2019, a plastic egg token was located where the ticket token is now found in the Blue Maze. Furthermore, the Mysterious Figure occupied the space reserved for the star jelly token. Both of these elements were removed during a subsequent minor update, along with other Egg Hunt content. The physical room they occupied remains present.
