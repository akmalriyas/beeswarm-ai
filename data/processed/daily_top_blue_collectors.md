# Daily Top Blue Collectors

The **Daily Top Blue Pollen Collectors** leaderboard is one of 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. This board tracks how much blue pollen players have collected during a single day.

## Mechanics and Details
*   **Location:** The leaderboard is found inside the Blue HQ, positioned to the right of the All-Time Top Blue Collectors Leaderboard.
*   **Display:** It displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view all 100 entries.
*   **Reset Time:** The leaderboard resets every day at 12:00 AM CST.
*   **Interaction:** When a player steps on the white pad in front of the board, a message appears displaying their current daily collection amount and the time remaining until the reset (e.g., "You've collected 'X' Blue Pollen today (Resets in 'Y')").

## Location
The leaderboard is located on the second floor of the Blue HQ, situated to the right of the Gifted Bucko Bee.

## Prizes
Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 receive either the **Left Shining Diamond Fleuron Sticker** or the **Right Shining Diamond Fleuron Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 receive 25 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
