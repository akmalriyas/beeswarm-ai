# Gift Boxes

Gift Boxes are special presents that appear during each Beesmas update, scattered across the map. To open them, players must help a specific number of NPCs complete their seasonal decorations and quests.

*Note: Do not confuse these with Presents or Ornament Presents.*

The game has featured various gift boxes over time, including annual themed versions (e.g., Shiny Gift Box 2025) and permanent types (e.g., Festive Gift Box).

## Trivia
*   Originally, the Mondo Gift Box required 12 NPCs. It later changed to 14 NPCs during a second update, then increased to 15 NPCs when Dapper Bear was added. Subsequently, all gift boxes requiring between 6 and 11 NPCs were also raised by one requirement level, and the Festive Gift Box (requiring 13 NPCs) was introduced after the Midnight Gift Box.
*   The Midnight Gift Box and the Chestnut Gift Box were unique in being added mid-Beesmas event. The Midnight Gift Box was the first to do so.
*   The Mondo Gift Box was one of the last gift boxes removed from the game during Beesmas 2020 due to an extension of Bubble Bee Man's quest.
*   The Stellar Gift Box (Beesmas 2025) is currently the only Gift Box with a unique, non-striped shape.
*   The Blinking Gift Box (Beesmas 2025) is the only gift box to feature an animation.
*   Pristine, Restful, and Glass Gift Boxes did not require 35 bees to access; players could reach them by jumping onto ledges from the Red HQ.
*   The number of available gift boxes has varied: there were 10 in Beesmas 2020, 12 in 2021 and 2022, and currently 13. There has never been a gift box requiring only one NPC to help.
*   Prior to the 2024-12-25 update, there was no gift box that required exactly two NPCs until the Elite Gift Box was modified from 3 NPCs down to 2 NPCs. The Elite Gift Box is notable for being the most frequently modified gift box in the game's history.
