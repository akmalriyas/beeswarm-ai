# Energy

**Energy** represents a bee's capacity to perform actions before needing to rest. Each action—such as gathering [pollen], sipping [nectar], or attacking [mobs]—costs one unit of energy. This process occurs before the bee returns to the hive. Spawning [Ability Tokens] does not consume energy.

The total energy pool for all bees in a hive can be increased through several methods:
*   Stacks of [Polar Power] obtained from [Polar Bear]'s quests.
*   Specific [mutations].
*   Certain [Beequips].
*   [Leveling up] the bee's bond.

## Energy Recovery Mechanics

By default, a bee takes 30 seconds to rest and fully recover its energy within the hive. However, there are exceptions:

*   **Shocked Bee:** Recovers in less time than the standard duration.
*   **Exhausted Bee & Photon Bee:** These bees do not sleep at all (they maintain near-infinite energy).

**Player Reset Exception:** If the player dies or resets the hive, all bees will return to the hive to sleep regardless of any buffs. In this scenario:
*   Standard bees sleep for 30 seconds.
*   Shocked Bees sleep for only 15 seconds.

When a bee runs out of energy, it enters a resting state and displays the following message: "\[Type] Bee is out of energy! It's going to sleep."

## Trivia & Glitches

*   The bees with the highest finite maximum energy are the [Lion Bee] and [Buoyant Bee].
*   **Energy Glitch:** There was a known glitch where applying [Royal Jelly] to an Exhausted or Photon Bee could cause their energy to reach absurd amounts.
*   **Dying/Reset Glitch (Patched):** An earlier bug allowed players who died twice in quick succession to trigger the hive recovery process, even if the standard 30-second cooldown after death had not elapsed. This affected Exhausted and Photon Bees because they did not technically possess "infinite" energy.
    *   Exhausted Bee's base energy is set to 999,999,999,999.
    *   Photon Bee's base energy is set to 999,999,999,999,999.
