# Gift Boxes (Winter 2024)

This guide details the various seasonal gift boxes available during the Winter 2024 event, including their locations and rewards.

---

## Elite Gift Box
*   **Location:** Next to the Blue Flower Field
*   **NPCs Helped:** 2
*   **Rewards:**
    *   2 Red Extracts
    *   3 Magic Beans
    *   200 Blueberries
    *   20 Snowflakes

## Ecru Gift Box
*   **Location:** Next to the Noob Shop
*   **NPCs Helped:** 4
*   **Rewards:**
    *   1 Star Jelly (Royal Jelly)
    *   5 Whirligigs
    *   100 Sunflower Seeds
    *   2 Oils
    *   2 Gingerbread Bears

## Silky Gift Box
*   **Location:** On Top of Blue HQ
*   **NPCs Helped:** 5
*   **Rewards:**
    *   4 Gingerbread Bears
    *   8 Smooth Dice
    *   1 Satisfying Vial
    *   4 Tropical Drinks
    *   1 Warm Scarf

## Sticker Gift Box
*   **Location:** Between the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine & Donation Board
*   **NPCs Helped:** 6
*   **Rewards:**
    *   3 Glues
    *   3 Enzymes
    *   50 Snowflakes
    *   3 Sticker Planters
    *   1 random sticker from the following pool: Random Critter Sticker, Pink Cupcake Sticker, Rhubarb Sticker, Coiled Snake Sticker

## Confetti Gift Box
*   **Location:** Above Pineapple Patch
*   **NPCs Helped:** 7
*   **Rewards:**
    *   2 Red Balloons
    *   10 Glitter
    *   3 Pink Balloons
    *   1 White Balloon
    *   50 Snowflakes

## Cherry Gift Box
