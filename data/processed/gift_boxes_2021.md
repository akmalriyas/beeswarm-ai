# Gift Boxes (2021)

Gift boxes are collectible items found throughout the game world that provide various resources, materials, and rare rewards upon opening. The contents of each box vary significantly based on its rarity and location.

---

*   **Location:** Near the [Basic Bee Gate](/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate) and the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field).
