# Polar Bear

**Polar Bear** is a permanent quest giver and one of the eight recurring bears in Bee Swarm Simulator. He is known for his love of cooking, which grants players the unique buff, *Polar Power*.

### Accessing Polar Bear
To reach Polar Bear, players must travel past the Honey Bee Gate and ascend the ramp next to the Pumpkin Patch. Turn right and follow the path between the snow/ice blocks until reaching him before turning left toward the Lion Bee Gate. Alternatively, shortcuts include using the Red Cannon with a Parachute or Glider, or jumping from the tallest cactus in the Cactus Field if the player has sufficient Jump Power.

## Polar Power Mechanic
*Polar Power* stacks additively increase the maximum energy of your bees by 5% (or 0.05x) per stack. The multiplier is calculated using the formula: $\frac{1}{20}n + 1$, where $n$ is the quantity of stacks. All *Polar Power* earned is permanent and cumulative. This buff is indicated by a fork in a blue square, with the number representing completed quests.

## Quests
Polar Bear offers 20 different repeatable quests that can be completed indefinitely. These quests require players to defeat mobs and collect specific pollen/items. In return, rewards include Honey, Treats, *Polar Power*, Crafting Materials, and occasionally Tickets. There is also a chance (1 in 100) for the Chef Hat Polar Bear sticker and (1 in 500) for the Fork And Knife sticker.

## Dialogue Snippets
Polar Bear's dialogue centers around his passion for cooking and the benefits of *Polar Power*.

**Initial Talk:** "Whoa! Haven't seen you before. I'm Polar Bear... I love to cook. But it's hard for me to collect ingredients... If you get the ingredients, I'll share my dishes with you and your bees. My food's so good it gives you Polar Power!"

**Quest Completion:** "That's all the ingredients I need! Ok, let me whip something up... ...(chop)...(chop)... ...(sizzle)... A dash of honey... ...(stir)... All done! Enjoy! Well, I'm always ready to eat. I'll be waiting when you're ready for more cooking!"

## Trivia
*   Polar Bear is one of the only three bears with endless repeating quests (alongside Black Bear and Brown Bear).
*   He is one of two bears who grant a unique ability (*Polar Power*), the other being Science Bear.
*   His quest "Thick Smoothie" is the only mention of a smoothie in the game outside of the Super Smoothie.
*   If the player gives Polar Bear a Present, he rewards them with 3 Oils, 2,500 Treats, an x4 Pumpkin Patch Boost, and a +10 Polar Power buff.
