# Pinecone

![Pinecone](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a7/Pinecone.png/revision/latest?cb=20230409131349)

**Level:** 9
**Color:** Any
**Limit:** 2
**Description:** A seed-producing organ produced by Conifer trees.

A **Pinecone** is a Level 9 Beesmas Beequip that can be equipped to Bumble, Stubborn, Bucko, Frosty, Carpenter, or Bear bees. It has an equip limit of two and no color requirement.

## Stats & Generation Mechanics

The Pinecone's stats are randomized upon generation. The following tables detail the potential base and upgraded values for both Non-Caustic and Caustic versions.

### Non-Caustic Pinecone Stats

| Stat | Base Chance | Upgrades Chance | Range (Base) | Range (Upgrades) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Convert Amount (%)** | 100% | Random (+20% to +30%) | N/A | Intervals of +1% |
| **Ability Rate (%)** | Random (10% to 25%) | Random (+1% to +2%) | Bias scales linearly | Intervals of +1% |
| **Pine Tree Forest Capacity (%)** | 100% | Random (+8% to +15%) | Exact value (0.05 to 0.2) | Intervals of +1% |
| **Pine Tree Forest Pollen (%)** | 100% | Random (+5% to +10%) | Exact value (0.03 to 0.1) | Intervals of +1% |

*Note: The values shown may differ greatly from the real in-game potential, as players can only see a rounded representation.*

## Acquisition Methods

Pinecone can be obtained through several methods:

**Current Sources:**
*   As a rare drop from Werewolf.
*   As a reward for participating in the Stick Bug Challenge.
*   As a rare drop from a Festive Nymph.
*   Purchasing the Frozen Forest Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog.

**Outdated/Removed Sources:**
*   Reward from completing Stick Bug's Beesmas 2020, 2021, and Winter 2024 quests.
*   Opening the Earthen Gift Box (2021) or Chestnut Gift Box (2022).
*   Buying the Merry Mythic Pack in the Robux Shop.
*   Purchasing from Bee Bear's Catalog in 2020.
