## 📰 Recent News & Updates

**April 23, 2026: Beesmas Extended (Minor Update)**
Beesmas has been extended until May 31st! [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") was given five final quests, rewarding players with the exclusive ![Petal Cub](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Petal Cub](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins "Cub Buddy")!

**December 27, 2025: x2 Event (Minor Update)**
The Honeyday Event is live! Join the game for 48 hours to gain x2 Pollen and x2 Convert Rate. Additionally, [Bee Bear's](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") quests have been released!

**December 25, 2025: Beesmas 2025**
*   🌼 **Bloom Mechanic:** Find Blooms in fields or grow them using the [Bloom Shaker](/wiki/Bloom_Shaker "Bloom Shaker"). Catching their petals grants various buffs.
*   🎟️ **New Vouchers:** The Offline Voucher and Ticket Voucher have been added.
*   🎅 **Traveling Bear:** [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear") is back! Help him prepare for Beesmas by delivering Presents.
*   🕯️ **Decorations:** Complete honeyday decorations to unlock special machines around the map.
*   🎄 **Beesmas Tree:** Exchange gifts with Mountain inhabitants to obtain boost-granting [Ornaments](/wiki/Ornaments "Ornaments").
*   📰 **Bee Bear's Catalog:** Collect Gingerbread Bears and Snowflakes to purchase limited-time items.
*   🖨️ Limited time festive stickers are available!

**December 25th, 2024: Christmas Day (Minor Update)**
The Honeyday Event is active for 48 hours, offering x2 Pollen and x2 Convert Rate. A new Robux Pack, "Royal Winter Wonder Haul," was introduced, featuring the ![Nectar Shower Vial](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Nectar Shower Vial](/wiki/Nectar_Shower_Vial "Nectar Shower Vial") and the ![Hive model icy crowned](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)[Icy Crowned Hive Skin](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker").

**December 17th, 2024: Second Beesmas!**
The game partnered with the Winter Spotlight Roblox Event. Players could earn currency in Bee Swarm to participate. Key features included:
*   🎅 **Traveling Bear:** Complete quests for Standard Tokens and Presents/Festive Planters.
*   🎁 **Elite Token:** Obtainable by opening the [Elite Gift Box](/wiki/Gift_Boxes#2024_\(Winter\) "Gift Boxes").
*   🕯️ Decorations and Beesmas Tree exchanges were available to unlock special items.

**August 1st, 2024: The Games Roblox Event**
😎 **Traveling Bear:** [Sun Bear](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") returned with limited-time quests for the Roblox event.
⛳ **New Mechanic: Golf!** Hit golf balls into holes and activate targets to earn rewards.

## 📜 Wiki Guidelines & Policies
To maintain a high-quality, safe environment, all users must adhere to the following guidelines. Violations of Fandom's [Terms of Use](https://www.fandom.com/terms-of-use) are strictly prohibited.

**Prohibited Content:**
*   Posting or linking to illegal content.
*   Posting or linking to explicit and/or sexual content.
*   Impersonating another person (wiki user or otherwise).
*   Spamming.
*   Exploiting wiki features to farm badges.

**Other Rules & Conduct:**
*   Advertising any social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Twitter) or other wikis is forbidden and will result in punishment.
*   Vandalism results in a warning and/or block.
*   Adding fake content or making false statements (including codes) is not allowed.
*   Editing another user's profile without permission requires a warning/reminder.
*   Evading a block leads to longer punishments.
*   Creating or encouraging drama will result in a block. Discussions suggesting you are leaving the wiki may also be grounds for a block, depending on context.
*   Cursing is not tolerated; bypasses (censoring words) are also forbidden.

**Warning Policy:**
If you receive a warning for breaking any rule, **do not delete it**. Deleting warnings will result in heavier consequences. If you believe your block was unjust or the punishment too severe, contact a [staff member](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Wiki:Staff "Bee Swarm Simulator Wiki:Staff").
