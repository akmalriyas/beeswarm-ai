# Bee Egg Guide

Eggs are inventory items in *Bee Swarm Simulator* that players drag into their hive to hatch a bee, donate to the Wind Shrine, or use in the Sticker Printer. They are also essential for starting gameplay. Eggs come in various types; some offer chances at different bee rarities, while others guarantee a specific bee type.

Currently, there are 6 primary egg types (plus Gifted variants), excluding specialized eggs like Plastic Eggs, Flight of the Bumble Egg, or Swarming Egg of the Hive.

### General Mechanics
*   **Hatching:** Since the 2019-09-28 update, players do not require a vacant hive slot to hatch an egg; they can replace an existing bee. The level of the replaced bee remains unchanged (acting like Royal Jelly).
*   **Restrictions:** Due to international regulations (UK, Netherlands, Belgium, Australia), most eggs available in the Robux Shop are restricted from purchase by consumers in those regions.

## Rarity Egg Types

### Basic Egg
The most common egg type. It primarily hatches into a Basic Bee (87% chance). Rare outcomes include:
*   Rare Bee (10% chance)
*   Epic Bee (2.5% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (0.5% chance)
*   Gifted Bee (0.4% chance), including Gifted Basic Bee (0.348% chance).

**Cannot hatch:** Mythic Bees or Event Bees. Cannot be used on a Basic Bee.

**How to Obtain:**
*   Free upon starting the game.
*   Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop near the Sunflower Field (Price scales up with each purchase, starting at 1,000 Honey).
*   Receiving 1 egg for adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack.

### Silver Egg
Most likely hatches into a Rare bee (64.9% chance). Other chances include:
*   Epic Bee (30% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (5% chance)
*   Mythic Bee (0.1% chance)

**Cannot hatch:** Basic Bees or Event Bees.

**How to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 50 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 100 Robux.
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Silver Egg questlines.
*   Reward from Brown Bear's 25th quest.
*   Completing the 25th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine reward.
*   Low chance drop from Mantis, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Rare Sprouts, leaves (when collecting pollen in Clover Field), Festive Planter, Stockings, and Memory Match (Winter).

### Gifted Silver Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Silver Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant.

**How to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, though subsequent chances are lower.
*   Reward for Top 25 in the Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Completing Black Bear's "Getting Stumped" quest.
*   1 reward from completing the 100th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Rare drops from Werewolf, Stump Snail, Scorpion, and Sticker Sprout.

### Gold Egg
Most likely hatches into an Epic bee (79% chance). Other chances include:
*   Legendary Bee (20% chance)
*   Mythic Bee (1% chance)

**Cannot hatch:** Basic Bees, Rare bees, or Event bees. Requires 5 eggs to craft the Honey Mask.

**How to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 100 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 200 Robux each.
*   Rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge or Extreme Memory Match.
*   Token in Werewolf's Cave (guarded by two Cave Monsters).
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Gold Egg questlines.
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 50th, 340th, and 390th quests.
*   1 reward from completing the 50th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick 10 times.
*   Low chance drop from King Beetle, Scorpion, Epic Sprouts, leaves (when collecting pollen in Clover Field), Ticket Planter, Candy Planter, Red Clay Planter, Festive Planter, Pesticide Planter, Petal Planter, Stockings, and Memory Match (Winter).

### Gifted Gold Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Gold Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant. Cannot be used to craft the Honey Mask.

**How to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, though subsequent chances are lower.
*   Reward for Top 25 in the Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Playtime Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 50 Gifted Bucko Bee quests or 50 Gifted Riley Bee quests (1 egg each).
*   Completing Brown Bear's 275th quest (1 egg).
*   1 reward from completing the 150th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Very rare drops from Stump Snail and Coconut Crab.

### Diamond Egg
Most likely hatches into a Legendary bee (95% chance). Rare outcomes include:
*   Mythic Bee (5% chance)

**Cannot hatch:** Basic Bees, Rare bees, Epic bees, or Event bees. Requires 5 eggs to craft the Diamond Mask.

**How to Obtain:**
*   Token located past Bear Gate under Onett (accessible via Glider/Parachute near Shadow Bear).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 400 Robux each.
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 400 Tickets each.
*   Rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge or Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Black Bear's Diamond Egg questline.
*   Completing Panda Bear's fifth Star Treat/Ants quest ("Colossal Combat").
*   Completing Dapper Bear's tenth quest ("The Long Haul").
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 75th and 525th quests.
*   1 reward from completing the 75th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Drops from Stump Snail, Diamond Aphids, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Coconut Crab (rare chance).
*   Drops from Spider, Mantis, Werewolf (very rare chance).
*   Rare chance from Legendary sprouts, guaranteed drop from Supreme sprouts.
*   Extremely rare chance from leaves (when collecting pollen in Clover Field).
*   Very rare reward from catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Rare drops from Festive Planter and Memory Match (Winter).

### Gifted Diamond Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Diamond Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant. Cannot be used to craft the Diamond Mask.

**How to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, though subsequent chances are lower.
*   Reward for being in the top 25 in the Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers.
*   Quest Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Rewards from completing specific quests (100, 250, 500, 750, and 1,000 Gifted Bucko Bee or Gifted Riley Bee quests).
*   Completing Brown Bear's 425th quest.
*   1 reward from completing the 200th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Extremely rare drops from Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, and Coconut Crab.

### Mythic Egg
Always hatches into a Mythic bee (with a very rare chance of hatching a Gifted Mythic bee).

**How to Obtain:**
*   Reward for Top 25 in the Daily Top Memory Matchers.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 1,200 Robux each (limited to 3 per player).
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 2,500 Tickets each.
*   Very rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge or Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Black Bear's 75th quest ("Myth in The Making").
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 15th quest ("More Than Myth").
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
*   1 reward from completing the 300th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick for the 50th time guarantees a Mythic Egg.
*   Extremely rare drops from Stump Snail, Mondo Chick (higher chance if defeated faster), and Coconut Crab.
*   Extremely rare drop when catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Rare drop from Festive Planter and Memory Match (Winter).
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 500 Snowflakes and 100 Gingerbread Bears.

### Gifted Mythic Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Mythic Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant.

**How to Obtain:**
*   Reward for Top 25 in the Daily Top Rare Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Completing 500 Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quests.
*   Nearly impossible drop from Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 10,000 Snowflakes and 150 Gingerbread Bears.

### Choose-A-Mythic Egg
Allows the player to select any Mythic Bee upon hatching.

**How to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing the Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul from the Robux Shop for 4,500 Robux.

### Star Egg
Hatches into any type of gifted bee not already in the player's hive (excluding Gifted Event bees). If all non-event gifted bees are owned, it hatches a random gifted bee. It will only hatch a Gifted Mythic Bee once all other eligible bees are collected.

**How to Obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, though subsequent chances are lower.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 per player).
*   Exceptionally rare drop from a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Completing Mother Bear's first ten quests ("Seven To Seven").
*   Completing Panda Bear's first ten star treat/ant quests ("Royal Rumble").
*   Exceptionally rare chance drop from King Beetle, Festive Planter, and Party Mechsquito.

## Trivia and Game Mechanics

*   **Iconography:** All specific bee eggs and event bee eggs share the same basic egg icon in inventory.
*   **Rarity Naming:** The Mythic Egg is unique as it shares the name of the rarity it hatches into. It is also the only non-specific egg that guarantees a certain rarity (100%), aside from specific and event bee eggs.
*   **Basic Bee Hatching:** Only Basic Eggs and Star Eggs can hatch into a Basic Bee (or its Gifted variant).
*   **Egg Usage Restriction:** Players cannot hatch a specific bee egg on that same type of bee (e.g., a Bumble Bee Egg cannot be hatched on a Bumble Bee). Similarly, Basic Eggs cannot be used on Basic Bees.
*   **Obtainment Methods:** Basic Eggs are the only eggs obtainable directly using Honey.
