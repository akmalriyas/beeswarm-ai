# Balloon

**Balloons** are items used by players to transport [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") back to the [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive"). Once at the hive, this pollen can be converted into [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"). There are six types of balloons: pink, red, white, black, blue, and gold.

Pink, red, white, and black balloons are summoned using [items](/wiki/Items "Items"). Blue and gold balloons are summoned via the abilities of the [Buoyant Bee](/wiki/Buoyant_Bee "Buoyant Bee"). Balloons automatically return to the hive when they reach full capacity or their time limit. Both capacity and time limits vary depending on the balloon type.

## In Fields

Balloons float around the [field](/wiki/Fields "Fields"), similar to [Clouds](/wiki/Clouds "Clouds"). They cast a 5-tile radius shadow over flowers underneath them, causing those flowers to absorb pollen into the balloons above. If multiple balloons cover the same flower, the pollen is absorbed by the balloon containing the lowest amount of pollen. Balloons cannot collect pollen from other players' fields.

The pollen held in balloons can be increased using Buoyant Bee's [Inflate Balloons](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inflate_Balloons "Ability Tokens") ability, though this action has a maximum limit of 10 times per balloon.

Standing within the radius of any player's balloon grants the [Balloon Aura](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens "Buffs & Debuffs") buff. Each balloon a player is near grants one stack, capping at x10 Balloon Aura. Each stack increases pollen collection by 1.02x and provides +2% honey from tokens, up to a maximum of 1.2x pollen and +20% honey from tokens.

Additionally, certain abilities can convert some pollen in the player's balloon into honey tokens:
*   [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee") [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee") frogs.
*   The waves from the [Tide Popper](/wiki/Tide_Popper "Tide Popper").
*   [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee")'s [Tornado](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Tornado "Ability Tokens").

If a balloon is returning to the hive, it can still be converted from these abilities, and it will drop its resulting tokens around its last location on the field.

When a [surprise party](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Surprise_Party "Ability Tokens") activates, all balloons (regardless of color) generate random ability tokens for a short duration. These tokens include white and blue boost tokens, buzz and blue bombs, marks, Haste, Token Link, and Melody tokens. The type of boost tokens spawned appears to be based on the primary color of the field.

## Hive Balloon

When a balloon reaches the player's hive, it transforms into a "Hive Balloon." This balloon floats stationary until conversion begins. When converting, the player's [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees") will first convert any items in their [bag](/wiki/Items#Bags "Items") before starting on the Hive Balloon.

A fully converted Hive Balloon grants the [Balloon Blessing](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens "Buffs & Debuffs"), which provides increased [Capacity](/wiki/System_Page#Capacity "System Page") and [Honey at Hive](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_At_Hive "System Page"). The buffs increase as the pollen needed for subsequent stacks increases. It requires 10 trillion pollen stored in the Hive Balloon to reach the maximum stack (x100) of Balloon Blessing, granting x4 Capacity and x2.5 Honey at Hive. When granted, a message displays: "🎈The hive balloon granted you x{stacks} of Balloon Blessing!"

If the blessing provided by the Hive Balloon is lower than the player's current level, fully converting it will instead refresh their Balloon Blessing timer, displaying: "🎈The hive balloon refreshed your Balloon Blessing!".

Pollen within the Hive Balloon gradually depletes based on its size relative to the player's capacity. The slowest rate of shrinkage is 20% per hour, while the fastest possible depletion is 99.99% in 10 minutes (if it holds over 100x the player's capacity). This depletion occurs gradually over time until the pollen reaches a sustainable level.

## Gold Balloon

Gold balloons possess the ability to buff [bubbles](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gathering_Bubbles "Passive Abilities") they pass over, converting them into [gold bubbles](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gathering_bubbles "Passive Abilities"). These gold bubbles:
*   Collect 1.5x more pollen.
*   Contribute 50% more time to [Bubble Bloat](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Passives "Buffs & Debuffs").
*   Have a 25% chance to create a honey token equal to the amount of pollen the bubble collected.

## Trivia

*   If a balloon of any color is used and the player leaves the game, the balloon will disappear.
*   Balloons are unique in that they are non-limited/event items not found in [crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") recipes, nor can they be donated.
*   Regardless of its initial color, any balloon arriving at the hive will always turn blue.
*   Currently, balloons are only obtainable through quests and mobs.
*   A glitch exists during conversion: by waiting for the balloon to disappear after starting conversion but leaving the hive before it finishes, players can duplicate the Balloon Blessing buff while retaining the original balloon in the hive.
*   It is possible to receive multiple "🎈The hive balloon refreshed your Balloon Blessing!" messages within a single hive conversion if new balloons arrive at the correct timing.
