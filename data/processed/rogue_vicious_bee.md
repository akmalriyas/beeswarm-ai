# Rogue Vicious Bee

The Rogue Vicious Bee is a Mini-Boss that can spawn in several fields, including Clover Field, Spider Field, Cactus Field, Rose Field, Mountain Top Field, and Pepper Patch. It has a high chance of spawning at night or can be manually summoned using a Night Bell.

## Variants
*   **Normal:** The standard Rogue Vicious Bee variant.
*   **Gifted:** A rarer variant (approximately 1 in 30-40 chance, or 2.5–3.3%). Gifted variants possess increased health, higher attack rate, and greater damage, rewarding significantly more Stingers and Honey upon defeat.

## Stats and Mechanics

### Spawning
*   **Respawns Every:** 10–30 minutes.
*   **Summoning:** The bee is hidden in the field, indicated by a small gray spike (golden if Gifted) sticking out of the ground. Players must walk close to or onto this spike to summon it; doing so causes damage. In King Beetle's Lair, a large spike can be seen under the Clover Field.
*   **Server Notification:** When attacking a player, a server-wide message is sent: `⚠ [Gifted] Vicious Bee is attacking {Username} in the {Field Name}! ⚠`
*   **Defeat Announcement:** Upon defeat, a message announces: `🎉 [Gifted] Vicious Bee has been defeated! 🎉`

### Attack Pattern
The Rogue Vicious Bee attacks by summoning spikes from the ground, utilizing two patterns:
1.  **Tracking:** It faces the nearest player and sends out 3 (Normal) or 4 (Gifted) targeting spikes.
2.  **Random:** After tracking, it spins in a circle, sending out random spikes while moving to a new spot on the field after each volley.

*   **Spike Duration:** Spikes despawn after five minutes of appearance.
*   **Movement Tips:** When tracking mode is active, continue moving around the field. When attacking randomly (spinning), stop moving unless a warning circle is directly under your character. The spike hitboxes are small, allowing players to jump over them without taking damage.

## Rewards and Levels

### Stingers & Honey
*   **Bond Gain:** Bees gain 50 Bond upon defeat, plus an additional 50 for every level above Level 2.
*   **Stinger Reward:** Awarded as long as the player deals any amount of damage to the bee, even if they leave or are killed before it is defeated. The higher the level, the more stingers are yielded. A daily bonus of 5 Stingers is given for the first Rogue Vicious Bee defeated that day (22-hour cooldown).
*   **Honey Reward:** Based on the total damage dealt to the bee.

## Special Notes and Trivia

*   **Icicles Drop:** During Beesmas events, the Rogue Vicious Bee has a small chance to drop the Icicles beequip. This chance increases with the bee's level. (Note: This did not apply during Beesmas 2021.)
*   **Despawn:** If the bee is in attack mode but no players are within range, it will eventually despawn and announce its departure.
*   **Mob Classification:** Along with Windy Bee, the Rogue Vicious Bee is one of the few mini-bosses that is also a Bee mob. It was the third boss added to the game.
*   **Unique Abilities:** Unlike most hostile mobs, it does not attack by collision; its spikes constitute a "special ability."
*   **Environmental Interaction:** The bee shines light directly under itself at night (unlike its tamed counterpart). Players can angle their camera underneath the field location of a spike to glimpse the hidden Rogue Vicious Bee.
*   **Damage Potential:** Its spikes damage other mobs without a damage cap, which can be exploited for fast captures (e.g., Mondo Chick running into them).
*   **Collision Damage:** Colliding with the bee by jumping into its body will cause damage. The initial spike sticking out of the ground also deals damage but does not trigger the Emergency Coconut Shield.
