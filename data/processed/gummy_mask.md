# Gummy Mask

[ ![Gummy Mask](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/50/Gummy_Mask.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20210919012419) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/50/Gummy_Mask.png/revision/latest?cb=20210919012419 "Gummy Mask")

The **Gummy Mask** is a mask available for purchase in Gummy Bear's Lair. It can be obtained by touching the Gummy Bee model on the Gummy Bee Egg Claim and using gumdrops, provided the player has acquired the Goo Hotshot badge.

> "_The official mask of a Gummy Soldier._ "

## Cost
*   5,000,000,000 Honey
*   250 Glue
*   100 Enzymes
*   100 Oil
*   100 Glitter
*   1 Satisfying Vial

## Stats and Abilities

| Stat | Bonus |
| :--- | :--- |
| Goo | +75% |
| Goo Conversion | +10% |
| White Pollen | x1.25 |
| Pollen | +25% |
| Capacity | x1.5 |
| White Field Capacity | x1.5 |
| Defense | +30% |
| Bee Ability Rate | +20% |

**Passive Abilities:**

*   **Coin Scatter (Requires Honey Mask):** Activated every 20 Mark Tokens. Converts 300% of the player's total Hive Convert Amount into honey tokens, which cannot be collected via a Token Link (visible only to the player). These honey tokens last for one minute. The passive has a two-minute cooldown before reuse.
*   **Gummy Morph:** Triggers when 30 regular gumdrops are used OR 10 Gummy Bee ability tokens are collected (+1 stack per gumdrop used, +3 stacks every Gummy Bee ability). When triggered, the player transforms into a Gummy Bear, and their Gummy Bee glows and leaves a trail. The field is fully covered in goo.
    *   **As Gummy Bear:** Gains +75% Goo, instantly converting all of it, along with increased jump power and player speed.
    *   **Gummy Bee Effect (while glowing):** Gathers 1000% more pollen, gains +300 Attack, and moves 200% faster for 10 seconds. This passive has no cooldown time.

## Appearance Details
The Gummy Mask features a teal gumdrop on top that emits tiny bubbles. Below this is a pink dome with a neon teal disc, which rests over a pink disc. A Gummy Bee face is placed on a pink barrier surrounding the player's face.

## Trivia and History
*   This mask represents an Event bee (Gummy Bee), unlike other masks based on Epic or Legendary Bees.
*   The Gummy Mask was initially nerfed in the 2019-04-05 Update due to its power, but was later slightly buffed following community complaints.
*   Before the 2019-09-28 Update, the passive ability would not activate from gumdrops if the Unlimited Gumdrops buff was active, as inventory gumdrops were not consumed under that buff. This issue was resolved in the update.
*   The music played during Gummy Bear Morph is a rendition of Gummy Bear's theme found in Gummy Bear's Lair.
*   Along with the Honey Mask, this is one of only two masks requiring a badge to access and purchase.
*   This mask (or Gummy Boots) was required for the Beesmas quest.
*   The Gummy Morph icon shares the same design as the Goo Badge icon.
