**Radiation** is a mechanic that grants a temporary "radioactive" status to a bee, lasting for 11 minutes. During this period, the bee remains radioactive regardless of any changes made to its hive slot.

### Effects and Mechanics
When a bee is radioactive, several effects occur:

*   **Mutation Chance:** The bee has an increased chance of gaining a mutation when fed specific items, including Bitterberries, Royal Jellies, Moon Charms, or Star Jellies. It also increases the chance of mutations from feeding special treats.
*   **Sticker Discovery:** Certain radioactive bees have a higher chance of finding stickers in some fields.
*   **Visual Indicators:** Radioactive bees glow green. The brightness of this glow indicates how much longer the radioactivity will last; brighter means more time remaining. A bee's hive slot also emits visible green particles while it is irradiated.

### Sources of Radiation
Bees can become radioactive through several methods:

*   **Neonberry Feeding:** Feeding a bee a Neonberry will immediately make it radioactive.
*   **Mutation Cascade:** If a bee is already mutated, it occasionally becomes radioactive. This event has the potential to cause surrounding bees to also become radioactive.
*   **Global Events (Onett):** Global radioactivity events can be initiated by Onett (the Developer). For example, during Beesmas 2020, he triggered a game-wide message and caused many bees to become radioactive.
*   **Random Occurrence:** Occasionally, a bee will randomly become radioactive without meeting any of the above criteria.
