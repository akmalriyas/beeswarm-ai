# Free Royal Jelly Dispenser

![Free Royal Jelly Dispenser](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/ef/Free_Royal_Jelly_Dispenser.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20180716172144) The Free Royal Jelly Dispenser is a dispenser located in the Star Hall.

## Mechanics and Usage

The Free Royal Jelly Dispenser grants players **Royal Jellies** and **Haste+ Ability Tokens**.

*   **Requirement:** 1 Gifted Bee
*   **Cooldown:** 22 hours
*   **Location:** Star Hall

### Output Details
The amount of Royal Jelly received is directly based on the total number of Gifted Bees currently present in the player's hive. For example, if a player has 15 gifted bees (even if some are duplicates), they will receive 15 Royal Jellies.

Currently, players can obtain a maximum of 50 Royal Jellies per use, provided they have 50 Gifted Bees in their hive.

***
*Note: This dispenser should not be confused with the standard [Royal Jelly Dispenser], which requires tickets or must be purchased at the [Royal Jelly Shop].*

## Trivia

*   While the standard Royal Jelly Dispenser grants a stack of 10x Haste, the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser provides **Haste+** and is free to use.
*   This dispenser, along with certain game codes, is one of only two ways to acquire the Haste+ Buff.
*   Unlike the standard Royal Jelly Dispenser (which limits output to one Royal Jelly per use), the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser can give up to 50 Royal Jellies.
*   The Free Royal Jelly Dispenser is not located directly next to its ticket counterpart, the Royal Jelly Dispenser, unlike other free dispensers such as the Free Ant Pass Dispenser or Free Robo Pass Dispenser.
*   This dispenser, along with the Glue Dispenser and the Free Robo Pass Dispenser, shares the longest cooldown in the game: 22 hours.
