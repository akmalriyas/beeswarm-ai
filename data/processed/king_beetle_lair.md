# King Beetle Lair

The King Beetle Lair is a specific location where the King Beetle spawns. It can be accessed via a light green door located between Blue HQ and Clover Field, or next to the far end of the Blue Flower Field.

## Location Mechanics
*   **Access:** The lair features two one-way doors. If a player stands too close to either entrance without moving, they will be pushed through.
*   **Interior:** The interior is generally empty, save for a ladder used as the exit point.
*   **Entrance Appearance:** The lair's entrance cycles in color, making it visually distinct from its surrounding environment.

The King Beetle specifically spawns on the back wall of the lair, adjacent to the Noob Shop and the Lava Obby.

## Lair Features & Codes
Near the back right corner of the lair floor, there is text that reads "Song Name." This serves as a hint for the game code "Crawlers," which corresponds to the music played inside the lair.

### Music: Crawlers
The audio track playing within the King Beetle Lair is titled "Crawlers." This same piece of music can also be heard in Commando Chick's Hideout and the White Tunnel.

## Trivia & Lore
*   **Historical Patch:** In early game versions, players who stood too close to the side of the lair near the Noob Shop could suffer damage due to lava blocks clipping into the wall from the Lava Obby; this issue has since been patched.
*   **Environmental Details:** The bottom of the Honeystorm Summoner sign pole pokes into the top of the lair structure.
*   **Sprout Visibility:** If a sprout is present in Clover Field, its light column and remaining pollen count can be observed from inside the lair. This phenomenon is similar to when Rogue Vicious Bee's spikes or Clouds spawn in Clover Field.
*   **Glitches:** A known glitch exists where the King Beetle may fail to lunge and become stuck when positioned near the "Song Name" text corner.
*   **Beesmas Event:** During the Beesmas 2018 Event, the red roof of the lair was visible on the sides of flower blocks in Clover Field; however, its glow near the Noob Shop wall remained visible even outside of snowy conditions.
*   **Pollen Collection:** Any tool can collect pollen from the Clover Field inside the lair if the player continuously jumps and possesses sufficient Jump Power. The Spark Staff is noted as requiring less Jump Power for this action.
*   **Triggering Mobs:** With enough Jump Power, players can trigger the Rhino Beetle and Ladybug in the Clover Field by jumping high enough. Furthermore, a player's bees may mistake the area for Clover Field and begin collecting pollen.
*   **Entrance Coloration:** The lair entrance is slightly lighter than the surrounding ground color and exhibits subtle color changes. During Beesmas events, this coloration becomes much more noticeable as the surrounding walls turn white.
*   **Amulet Match:** The cycling colors of the lair's entrance match the cycling patterns found on any supreme-tier amulet.
