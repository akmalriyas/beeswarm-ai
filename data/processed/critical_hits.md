# Critical Hits

**Critical Hits** are moments of increased pollen collection and attack damage, doubling at their base power. Their frequency is determined by [Critical Chance], and their strength is determined by [Critical Power]. A critical hit will feature a visual shake and color change, similar to the animations below.

## Critical Chance

**Critical Chance** is the probability of a successful attack being a critical hit. It can be improved through various methods, including ability tokens, guards, and inventory items.

### Bees
#### Tokens
*   **Focus:** Grants +3% Critical Chance per token, capping at +30% from 10 tokens.
*   **Precise Mark:** From the [Target Practice] ability, grants +7% Critical Chance per mark, capping at +21% from three marks.
*   **Tabby Blessing:** Granted by collecting [Tabby Love] while having maximum stacks, this buff provides +1% Critical Chance.

#### Gifted Hive Bonuses
*   **Commander Bee:** Grants +3% Critical Chance.

#### Beequips
*   **Individual Bee:**
    *   **Bottle Cap:** Provides from +1% to +2% Critical Chance. Can be increased up to +12% using [Waxes].
    *   **Charm Bracelet:** Provides from +1% to +14% Critical Chance.
    *   **Bang Snap:** Provides from +1% to +3% Critical Chance. Can be increased up to +23% using waxes.
    *   **Pink Shades:** Provides from +22% to +26% Critical Chance. Can be increased up to +50% using waxes.
    *   **Beesmas Top:** Provides from +5% to +10% Critical Chance. Can be increased up to +20% using waxes.
    *   **Beesmas Tree Hat:** Provides from +2% to +5% Critical Chance. Can be increased up to +10% using waxes.
    *   **Snow Tiara:** Grants +1% Critical Chance.
    *   **Toy Drum:** Grants +1% Critical Chance.

### Guards
*   **Looker Guard:** Grants +1% Critical Chance.
*   **Elite Red Guard:** Grants +5% Critical Chance.
*   **Riley Guard:** Grants +8% Critical Chance.
*   **Crimson Guard:** Grants +10% Critical Chance.

### Amulets
*   **Star Amulets (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond):** Provide from +1% to +5% Critical Chance.
*   **Supreme Star Amulet:** Provides from +1% to +7% Critical Chance.
*   **Ant Amulets (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Supreme):** Can provide up to +3% Critical Chance.

### Buffs & Debuffs
*   **Tropical Drink:** Grants +5% Critical Chance.
*   **Super Smoothie:** Grants +6% Critical Chance. This buff can also be obtained from certain codes.
*   **Jelly Beans:** Collecting [Green Jelly Beans] grants +3% Critical Chance per bean, capping at +5% from 3 jelly beans.
*   **Petals:** Collecting green petals from blooms grants 1% Critical Chance.

### Robo Bear Challenge
Upgrades bought with [Cogs] during the Robo Bear Challenge can affect Critical Chance:
*   **Rare Upgrades:**
    *   **Commit:** Starts at +5% Critical Chance and maxes at two upgrades for +10% Critical Chance.
    *   **Virus:** Starts at +1% Critical Chance and maxes at three upgrades for +3% Critical Chance.
*   **Epic Upgrades:**
    *   **RGB:** Grants 1% Critical Chance.
*   **Legendary Upgrades:**
    *   **Pseudo-RNG:** Grants 3% Critical Chance.

#### Debuffs
Upgrades bought with cogs during the Robo Bear Challenge can downgrade Critical Chance:
*   **Rare Upgrades:**
    *   **SSD (Blue):** Starts at -3% Critical Chance and maxes at three upgrades for -6% Critical Chance.
*   **Epic Upgrades:**
    *   **FOV:** Grants -2% Critical Chance.
    *   **Normalize:** Sets Critical Chance to x0.

## Critical Power

**Critical Power** determines the strength of a critical hit. It can be improved through ability tokens, guards, and beequips.

### Bees
#### Tokens
*   **Melody:** Grants +100% Critical Power.
*   **Tabby Blessing:** Granted by collecting [Tabby Love] while having maximum stacks, this buff provides +25% Critical Power.

#### Gifted Hive Bonuses
*   **Looker Bee:** Grants +25% Critical Power.
*   **Tabby Bee:** Grants +50% Critical Power.

#### Beequips
*   **Individual Bee:**
    *   **Thumbtack:** Provides from +5% to +10% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +40% using waxes.
    *   **Camo Bandana:** Provides from +1% to +6% Critical Power.
    *   **Bottle Cap:** Provides from +5% to +10% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +60% using waxes.
    *   **Kazoo:** Provides from +2% to +5% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +65% using waxes.
    *   **Whistle:** Provides from +15% to +25% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +85% using waxes.
    *   **Pink Shades:** Provides from +29% to +49% Critical Power.
*   **Hive Bonus:**
    *   **Bottle Cap:** Provides from +2% to +15% Critical Power when waxed.
    *   **Kazoo:** Provides from +2% to +5% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +10% using waxes.
    *   **Beesmas Top:** Provides from +2% to +5% Critical Power. Can be increased up to +15% using waxes.

### Guards
*   **Looker Guard:** Grants +25% Critical Power.
*   **Elite Blue Guard:** Grants +50% Critical Power.
*   **Bucko Guard:** Grants +75% Critical Power.
*   **Cobalt Guard:** Grants +100% Critical Power.

### Amulets
*   **Ant Amulets (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Supreme):** Can provide up to +50% Critical Power.

### Badges
*   **Battle Badge:** Provides critical power based on rank:
    *   Cadet Battle Badge: +10% Critical Power.
    *   Hotshot Battle Badge: +20% Critical Power.
    *   Ace Battle Badge: +40% Critical Power.
    *   Master Battle Badge: +70% Critical Power.
    *   Grandmaster Battle Badge: +100% Critical Power.

### Buffs
#### Stickers
*   **Wobbly Looker Bee Sticker:** Grants +1% Critical Power.
*   **Tabby From Behind Sticker:** Grants +1% Critical Power.
*   **Uplooking Bear Sticker:** Grants +1% Critical Power.
*   **Wishbone Sticker:** Grants +1% Critical Power.
*   **Triple Exclamation Sticker:** Grants +1% Critical Power.
*   **Eighth Note Sticker:** Grants +1% Critical Power.
*   **Green Circle Sticker:** Grants +1% Critical Power.
*   **Interrobang Block Sticker:** Grants +1% Critical Power.
*   **Taunting Doodle Person Sticker:** Grants +1% Critical Power.
*   **Blue Square Sticker:** Grants +2% Critical Power.
*   **Black Diamond Sticker:** Grants +2% Critical Power.

### Robo Bear Challenge
Upgrades bought with [Cogs] during the Robo Bear Challenge can affect Critical Power:
*   **Rare Upgrades:**
    *   **Nullify:** Starts at x1.5 Critical Power and maxes at two upgrades for x2 Critical Power.
*   **Epic Upgrades:**
    *   **Demarcate:** Makes Mark Tokens grant x1.03 Critical Power for 15 seconds, stacking up to 10 times for x1.3 Critical Power.

#### Debuffs
Upgrades bought with cogs during the Robo Bear Challenge can downgrade Critical Power:
*   **Common Upgrades:**
    *   **Defragment:** Starts at x0.9 Critical Power and maxes at 10 upgrades for x0.5 Critical Power.
*   **Epic Upgrades:**
    *   **Client-Side:** Grants x0.75 Critical Power.

## Super-Crit

When a critical hit occurs, there is a chance to perform a **Super-Crit** (indicated by a purple animation). A Super-Crit doubles pollen collection and instantly converts the collected pollen. Attack damage is increased by roughly 33% of the player's Super-Crit Power. The frequency of a Super-Crit is determined by [Super-Crit Chance], and its strength is determined by [Super-Crit Power].

### Super-Crit Chance

**Super-Crit Chance** dictates how often a critical hit will escalate into a Super-Crit.

#### Bees
##### Tokens
*   **Precision:** The buff granted from the [Target Practice] ability provides +2% per token, capping at +20% from ten tokens.
*   **Precise Mark:** The buff from the Target Practice ability grants +7% Super-Crit Chance per token, capping at +21% from three tokens.
*   **Tabby Blessing+:** Granted by collecting [Tabby Love] from a Gifted Tabby Bee while having maximum stacks, this buff grants +1% Super-Crit Chance.

##### Gifted Hive Bonuses
*   **Precise Bee:** Grants +3% Super-Crit Chance.

##### Beequips
*   **Individual Bee:**
    *   **Pink Shades:** Provides from +1% to +15% Super-Crit Chance. Can be increased up to +24% using waxes.
*   **Hive Bonus:**
    *   **Pink Shades:** Grants +1% Super-Crit Chance as a hive bonus.

### Buffs
*   **Super Smoothie:** Grants +1% Super-Crit Chance. This buff can also be obtained from certain codes.
*   **Petals:** Collecting Violet Petals from blooms grants 5% Super-Crit Chance.

### Robo Bear Challenge
Upgrades bought with [Cogs] during the Robo Bear Challenge can affect Super-Crit Chance:
*   **Legendary Upgrades:**
    *   **Bruteforce:** Grants +6% Super-Crit Chance.

### Super-Crit Power

**Super-Crit Power** determines how strong a Super-Crit will be.

#### Bees
##### Beequips
*   **Individual Bee:**
    *   **Camo Bandana:** Provides up to +14% Super-Crit Power.
*   **Hive Bonus:**
    *   **Whistle:** Provides from +1% to +20% Super-Crit Power when waxed.
    *   **Pink Shades:** Provides from +1% to +5% Super-Crit Power. Can be increased up to +9% using waxes.
    *   **Demon Talisman:** Provides up to 10% Super-Crit Power as a hive bonus when waxed.

### Amulets
*   **Cog Amulets:**
    *   **Diamond Cog Amulet:** Provides from +10% to +12% Super-Crit Power.
    *   **Supreme Cog Amulet:** Provides from +15% to +25% Super-Crit Power.

### Buffs
*   **Dark Heat:** Created using [Dark Scythe] and dark flames, this grants x1.05 Super-Crit Power initially, with each additional dark flame granting an extra x0.05 Super-Crit Power.

#### Stickers
*   **Precise Eye Sticker:** Grants +1% Super-Crit Power.
*   **Squashed Head Bear Sticker:** Grants +1% Super-Crit Power.
*   **Purple Fleuron Sticker:** Grants +1% Super-Crit Power.
*   **Royal Symbol Sticker:** Grants +1% Super-Crit Power.
*   **Royal Bear Sticker:** Grants +1% Super-Crit Power.
*   **Purple 4-Point Flower Sticker:** Grants +1% Super-Crit Power.

### Robo Bear Challenge
Upgrades bought with [Cogs] during the Robo Bear Challenge can affect Super-Crit Power:
*   **Legendary Upgrades:**
    *   **Pseudo-RNG:** Grants x1.25 Super-Crit Power.
