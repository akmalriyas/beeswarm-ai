# Daily Top Buzz Bomb Pollen Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Buzz Bomb Pollen Collectors** leaderboard is one of the 64 available leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator, accessible via the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page.

This daily challenge tracks the total amount of pollen collected by players using *only* Buzz Bombs. The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, and players can scroll down to view results up to the 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

Players are awarded rewards based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 10:** Awarded a Basic Black Hive Skin.
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 25 Tickets.
