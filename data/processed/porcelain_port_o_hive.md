# Porcelain Port-O-Hive

![Porcelain O Hive](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/59/Porcelain_O_Hive.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20180718033113)

## Description
A rare and precious Port-O-Hive that boosts white pollen.

**Stats:**
*   **Capacity:** 600,000 Pollen
*   **Cost:** 250,000,000 Honey, 3 Glitter, 3 Soft Wax, 10 Moon Charms

**Bonus Stats:**
*   +250% Convert Rate
*   x1.25 Convert Rate at Hive
*   +10% Instant Conversion
*   +50% White Pollen

The **Porcelain Port-O-Hive** is a bag that can be purchased in the Mountain Top Shop, which was added in the 2018-07-11 update. It holds the second-highest capacity and has the second-highest price among all containers.

## Crafting Ingredients
*   250,000,000 Honey
*   3 Glitter
*   3 Soft Wax
*   10 Moon Charms

## Trivia
*   Out of the four variants of Port-O-Hive, the Porcelain Port-O-Hive was the second one added to the game, following the regular Port-O-Hive.
*   The bag's stats have been changed multiple times since its introduction:
    *   After the 2019-04-05 update, its base capacity briefly increased from 500,000 pollen to 1,000,000 pollen, but this was reverted back to 500,000 after the 2019-05-13 Update.
    *   Following the 2024-05-23 update: its base capacity increased from 500,000 pollen to 600,000 pollen. It also lost bonuses for +10% Blue Pollen, +10% Red Pollen, and +1 Bee Attack.
*   Before the 2019-04-05 update, its description was "The rarest and most precious Port-O-Hive in the world. Can store up to 500,000 pollen."
*   This container is worn by Onett in the Bear Gate, alongside items such as Porcelain Dipper, Beekeeper's Boots, Mondo Belt Bag, Beekeeper's Mask, Riley Guard, and Bucko Guards.
*   It is the only container that emits particles.
