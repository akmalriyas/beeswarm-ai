## Location and Overview

The Petal Shop is a shop located in the cave near the Windy Bee Gate, situated under the Wind Shrine. It can be accessed past the Windy Bee Gate.

The shop is named for the giant petals draped across its back wall. Here, players can craft several unique items: the Petal Wand, the Petal Belt, and the Petal Planter. The location also hosts the All-Time Top White Collectors Leaderboard and the Daily Top White Collectors Leaderboard.

Most of the crafted items require a Spirit Petal to create (the Petal Planter is an exception).

## Trivia

*   The wall behind the Petal Shop is semi-transparent and can be walked through. Behind it, there is a corridor leading to a Star Jelly token found in the Blue Maze (where the jellybeans token is also located). This token awards one star jelly. Previously, a Mysterious Figure occupied this spot.
*   Occasionally, rain from Clouds in the Pepper Patch may be visible inside the Petal Shop. A beam of a Sprout can also be seen behind the semi-transparent wall.
