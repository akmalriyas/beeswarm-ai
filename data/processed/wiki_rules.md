# Wiki Rules and Conduct Policy

The rules listed here, along with the [Editing Guidelines](/wiki/Editing_Guidelines) and the [Discussions Guidelines](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/d/g), form the general basis for acceptable conduct on this wiki. Punishments are applied on a case-by-case basis; therefore, they may not always strictly match what is stated here. Repeated or more severe violations will result in harsher punishments.

If you were punished for an action not listed here, it does not mean you did nothing wrong. If you are unsure why you received a punishment or believe it was too severe, please contact a [staff member](/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator_Wiki:Staff). **Do not create a new account to appeal a block; this will be treated as ban evasion.** Such matters should be addressed via Community Central.

***

## Verbal Reminder
*This is typically given for very minor, first-time violations. Sometimes, it may be replaced by direct replies or messages.*

*   **Language:** Only English must be used to prevent communication barriers.
*   **Staff Status:** Do not ask to become a staff member; this status must be earned.

## Reminder
*This is typically given for first-time minor violations.*

*   **Moderation:** Do not minimod or act as staff if you are not one. Report situations needing attention to an actual staff member.
*   **User Profiles:** Do not edit user profiles belonging to others without permission.
*   **Spamming:** Do not spam, as it can obscure content and cause disruption. Minor, unnecessary spam will result in a reminder.
*   **Profanity:** Profanity is prohibited. Bypassing rules (including censoring or using acronyms) is still forbidden and will result in a reminder, regardless of how much is censored. The only exception is the word "lmao."

## Warning
*This is typically given for more severe violations.*

*   **Spamming:** Severe spam, particularly content that floods a page and makes it difficult to scroll through, will result in a warning.
*   **Profanity:** Profanity is prohibited in any form, including bypassing, partial censorship, or acronyms.
*   **Staff Punishments:** Do not delete a staff member's reminder or warning. This negates the purpose of the punishment by hiding it from their view unless restored by staff. Exceptions are:
    *   If a staff member explicitly states the warning is nullified.
    *   If the punishment was a two-day necroposting penalty issued on October 17th, 2021, or earlier.
*   **Advertising:** Do not advertise anything (including other wikis or social media) anywhere except your user profile. Posting videos is acceptable as long as you do not instruct viewers to subscribe or view related content.
*   **Feature Abuse:** Do not abuse wiki features for editing or badge farming. Minor cases, specifically short edits that do not ruin a page, will result in a warning.
*   **Edit Wars:** Do not engage in edit wars, as they cause disruption. Consult a staff member regarding specific edits to confirm if they are permitted. Short edit wars will result in a warning.
*   **Toxicity/Drama:** Do not start or encourage toxicity, drama, harassment, or bullying. Minor cases will result in a warning. This includes e-dating.

## Block
*This is typically given for severe violations or repeated minor offenses that warrant temporary removal from the wiki.*

*   **Edit Wars:** Prolonged edit wars, especially after clear explanations have been provided, will result in a ban. (Block Time: One Week — One Year.)
*   **Gore/Violence:** Posting gory scenes warrants a temporary block unless the content is excessively graphic. (Block Time: One Week — One Year.)
*   **Toxicity/Harassment:** Major cases of starting or encouraging toxicity, harassment, bullying, or drama will result in a ban. (Block Time: Two Weeks — One Year.) Posts about leaving the wiki that leverage previous drama may also be grounds for a block.
*   **Feature Abuse:** Major cases of abusing wiki features to edit or badge farm—especially those that significantly damage a page or are continuous—will result in a ban. (Punishment: One Month — One Year.)
*   **Fake Content:** Adding or displaying fake content as if it were real is prohibited. (Block Time: One Month — One Year.)
*   **Vandalism:** Minor cases of vandalism will result in a block. (Block Time: One Month — One Year.)
*   **Age Restriction:** For online safety, users must be 13 years or older to have an account on the wiki. (Block Time: Until the user turns 13.)
*   **Impersonation/Profile Abuse:** Do not use inappropriate usernames, profile pictures, or mastheads. Usernames indicating impersonation fall under this category. You will be blocked until the requested changes are made. (Block Time: Until appropriate changes have been made.)
*   **Ban Evasion:** Attempting to evade a block by creating a new account is strictly forbidden. If caught, all found alternate accounts will receive a permanent ban, and your main account will receive an extension of twice the original block duration.

## Indefinite Block
*Aside from global blocks, this is the most severe punishment, reserved for actions so egregious that the user should no longer be allowed on the wiki.*

*   **NSFW Content:** Posting or linking to NSFW content (including gory, illegal, explicit, or sexual material) is prohibited.
*   **Feature Abuse:** Cases of abusing wiki features to edit or badge farm that severely damage the wiki will result in an indefinite block.
*   **Toxicity/Drama:** Very severe cases of starting or encouraging toxicity, harassment, bullying, or drama will result in an indefinite block.
*   **Fake Content:** Adding or displaying fake content as if it were real is prohibited. Deliberately repeating false information to prove its authenticity will result in an indefinite block.
*   **Vandalism:** Vandalism will result in an indefinite block if:
    *   It is intentional or severe.
    *   It occurs during one of your first edits, and will be treated as trolling regardless of the type of vandalism.
*   **Trolling:** Trolling on this wiki is prohibited.
*   **Impersonation:** Impersonating any individual (wiki user or otherwise) is not allowed. Note: This does not apply to cases relating solely to usernames.
*   **Raid Activity:** Do not raid wikis, whether on this site or another. If caught attempting to promote a raid, you will be blocked and reported to the related or target wikis.
*   **Ban Evasion (Severe):** If caught evading a block, and your original ban reasons/durations were malicious enough, or if you have evaded a block twice, all found accounts will receive an indefinite block.
