# Blue Clay Planter

[![Blue Clay Planter](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ab/Blue_Clay_Planter.png/revision/latest?cb=20230407232645)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ab/Blue_Clay_Planter.png/revision/latest?cb=20230407232645)

> "Grows in about 6hrs, but faster near Blue flowers. Grants bonus Blue Extract, Micro-Converters, and x1.2 Refreshing and Comforting Nectar."

The **Blue Clay Planter** is a reusable planter introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. It can be purchased at the [Blue HQ](/wiki/Blue_HQ "Blue HQ").

## Stats & Mechanics

| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reusable** | Yes | Permanent planter. |
| **Growth Time (Base)** | ~6 hours | |
| **Cost** | 10,000,000 Honey, 5 Magic Beans, 15 Blue Extracts, 20 Soft Waxes | Crafting/Purchase Ingredients. |
| **Pollen Capacity** | 3,000,000 Pollen | Maximum capacity. |

### Growth Modifiers
*   **Growth Time Bonus:** +25% faster growth in fields with blue flowers.
*   **Pollen Multiplier:**
    *   +25% Blue Pollen
    *   -25% Red Pollen
*   **Nectar Multiplier:**
    *   +20% Refreshing Nectar
    *   +20% Comforting Nectar

### Harvesting & Drops
When harvested, the planter grants bonus items: [Blue Extract](/wiki/Blue_Extract "Blue Extract"), [Micro-Converter](/wiki/Micro-Converter "Micro-Converter"), and [Honeysuckle](/wiki/Honeysuckle "Honeysuckle").

**Puffshroom Chance:**
Harvesting a fully grown Blue Clay Planter has a 1/6 chance to spawn a [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom"). The spawned puffshroom is between levels 4-5 and has the following probabilities:

*   Common Puffshroom: ~76.28%
*   Rare Puffshroom: ~19.07%
*   Epic Puffshroom: ~4.58%
*   Legendary Puffshroom: ~0.08%

**Token Rewards (Fully Grown):**
When claimed, the planter gives up to 16 tokens worth of items. If fully grown, 4 tokens are guaranteed:

*   1 [Blue Extract](/wiki/Blue_Extract "Blue Extract")
*   1 [Micro-Converter](/wiki/Micro-Converter "Micro-Converter")
*   3 [Honeysuckles](/wiki/Honeysuckle "Honeysuckle")
*   1 [Ticket](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket")

The remaining tokens are picked randomly from a non-exhaustive list of Bonus, Other, and Special items.

## Trivia

*   Its red counterpart is the [Red Clay Planter](/wiki/Red_Clay_Planter "Red Clay Planter").
*   This planter is unique in that it drops [Honeysuckles](/wiki/Honeysuckle "Honeysuckle") as a bonus item.
*   It is one of five reusable planters available outside of Dapper Bear's Shop, alongside the Red Clay Planter, Petal Planter, Hydroponic Planter, and Heat-Treated Planter.
*   Along with the [Hydroponic Planter](/wiki/Hydroponic_Planter "Hydroponic Planter"), it is one of only two planters sold from the Blue HQ.
