# Leaf

*Note: Do not confuse Leaves with [Flowers] or [Pollen].*

A leaf is an entity found in most [Fields]. Similar to [Sparkles], leaves emit leaf particles. To gather a leaf, the player must collect [Pollen] on the tile it occupies. Upon gathering, a rustling sound plays, multiple leaf particles fly out, and a token appears. Rewards vary by field but are always granted in quantities of one.

**General Mechanics:**
*   Leaves often spawn specific items based on their location (e.g., blueberries in blue fields).
*   They have a rare chance to spawn an [Aphid]. This chance increases if the field has been inactive for a period.
*   Most fields also allow leaves to summon various leaf [Stickers].

**Field-Specific Mechanics:**
*   **Mountain Top Field:** Leaves are significantly rarer here. They will always spawn either an [Aphid] or a [Lyrate Leaf Sticker], and occasionally yield rare tokens such as [Bitterberry], [Neonberry], [Magic Bean], [Blowing Leaf Sticker], or [Super Smoothie].
*   **Stump Field & Ant Field:** Leaves in these fields cannot spawn aphids.
*   **Retro Swarm Challenge:** Leaves within this challenge can spawn aphids, unlike the Stump and Ant Fields.
