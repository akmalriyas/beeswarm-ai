# Swirled Wax

[![Swirled Wax](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/91/Swirled_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042825)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/91/Swirled_Wax.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042825)

> "A spiraled hunk of beeswax. Used to redetermine the stats of a Bee's Beequip."

**Swirled Wax** is an inventory item and a type of wax used on a **Beequip** to reroll its statistics. Beequips with higher potential have a greater chance of receiving better stats when rerolled. Swirled Wax has an Upgrade Value of 0.

When applied to a Beequip, it changes the item's stats. If the Beequip already has waxes applied, the enhancements will also be altered.

Upon application, a message pops up stating:
> The Wax altered the Beequip's stats

Note that sometimes the stats may not show any change after using Swirled Wax. In most cases, this issue can be resolved by rejoining the game.

## Acquisition Methods

### Crafting
Swirled Wax can be crafted in a **Blender** using the following ingredients:
*   3 Hard Waxes
*   9 Soft Waxes
*   6 Purple Potions
*   3,333 Royal Jellies

### Rewards and Drops
Swirled Wax can be obtained through various methods:

**From Stickers:**
*   Auryn rewards 1 Swirled Wax for completing certain sticker stacks.
*   Pearl Girl rewards 1 Swirled Wax for completing certain sticker stacks.

**Event/Bundle Rewards:**
*   As a reward from the **Winter Memory Match**.
*   Opening the 11th Crafty Gift Box grants 5 Swirled Waxes alongside other rewards.
*   Purchasing the "Frozen Forest Pack" and "Snow Queen Bundle" in Bee Bear's Catalog gives 5 Swirled Waxes amongst other items.
*   Purchasing the "Purple-Turple Mega Pack" bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog grants 10 Swirled Waxes and other rewards.
*   Giving a Present to Honey Bee (NPC) rewards 3 Swirled Waxes alongside other items.
*   Purchasing the "Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul" bundle in the Shop rewards 25 Swirled Waxes with other rewards.

**Drops:**
*   As a reward from Gifted Riley Bee's **Honeyday Candles** (1.62% chance).
*   As a drop from a level 13 Snowbear.
*   From Puffshrooms: Uncommon drop from an Epic Puffshroom, common drop from a Mythic Puffshroom.
*   As a very rare drop from Diamond Aphids.
*   **From Planters:**
    *   Plastic Planter in the Coconut Field guarantees one every month.
    *   Candy Planter in the Cactus Field guarantees one every month.
    *   Blue Clay Planter in the Spider Field guarantees one every month.
    *   Red Clay Planter: Has a rare chance of dropping one in any field. Planting it in any blue field increases this chance, and planting it in the Sunflower Field or Pine Tree Forest guarantees one monthly/weekly, respectively.
    *   Tacky Planter in the Pumpkin Patch guarantees one every month.
    *   Pesticide Planter in the Strawberry Field guarantees one every month.
    *   Heat-Treated Planter in the Blue Flower Field guarantees one every month.
    *   Hydroponic Planter in the Mushroom Field guarantees one every month.
    *   Petal Planter in the Strawberry Field guarantees one every month.
    *   The Planter Of Plenty has a high chance of dropping one Swirled Wax in any field, and may drop 5 with one every month.

### Quests
Swirled Wax can be rewarded by completing quests for various NPCs:
*   **Gifted Riley Bee:** Rewards 5 Swirled Waxes upon completion of "Riley Bee's Honeyday Candles."
*   **Stick Bug:** Rewards 5 Swirled Waxes upon completion of "Stick Bug's Nymph Hats."
*   **Dapper Bear:** Rewards 1 Swirled Wax for completing multiple quests, including: "Means of Mutation" (#5), "The Long Haul" (#10), "The Trick To Tickets" (#11), "Solitarily Steadfast" (#14), "More Than Myth" (#15), "Sticker Style" (#16), "Barter For The Blue Drive," and "Dapper Bear's Samovar."

## Outdated Acquisition Methods
*   **Beesmas 2020:** Riley Bee's Honeyday Candles always gave 1 Swirled Wax when used.
*   **Beesmas 2021 & 2022:** Using Riley Bee's Honeyday Candles had a 1.63% chance to give 3 Swirled Waxes.
*   **Beesmas Rewards (Various):** Several NPCs and bundles provided Swirled Wax during the Beesmas events of 2021 and 2022, including specific quest completions for Bee Bear, Dapper Bear, Stick Bug, and Spirit Bear.
*   **Bundle Purchases:** Various Robux and catalog bundles historically included large amounts of Swirled Waxes (e.g., Festive Frenzy Haul, Mondo Robo Bundle).

## Trivia and Lore

*   Swirled Wax was the first wax added to the game, introduced during Beesmas 2020 (while other waxes were added in Beesmas 2021).
*   Along with Soft Wax and Debug Wax, Swirled Wax is one of the few waxes guaranteed to change a Beequip's stats.
*   Before the 2021-12-26 update, this item had no favor when donated to the Wind Shrine.
*   The limit on how many Swirled Waxes could be used was removed in the 2021-12-26 update (it previously had a limit of 5).
*   Before the 2021-12-26 update, its icon design did not include a wick.
*   It is the only wax that was not included in the Waxy Bundle.
*   **Raw Ingredient Cost:**
    *   90 Honeysuckles
    *   900 Sunflower Seeds
    *   4,050 Pineapples
    *   3,600 Strawberries
    *   3,600 Blueberries
    *   99 Bitterberries
    *   18 Neonberries
    *   4,602 Royal Jellies or 4.60B Honey
