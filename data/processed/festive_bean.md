# Festive Bean

A **Festive Bean** is a special variant of the Magic Bean that always plants a Festive Sprout in the field where it is used.

## Mechanics and Cooldown
The Festive Bean has a cooldown period of 2 hours (120 minutes) between planting Festive Sprouts. Only the player who planted the sprout can receive the associated buffs, specifically the Festive Blessing Token and Beesmas Cheer Token, to prevent overuse. When a player successfully plants a Festive Sprout, the following message appears: "🌱 {Username} has planted a Festive Sprout 🌱"

## Festive Sprout Rewards
When a Festive Sprout is harvested, it grants various rewards, including:
*   Treats
*   Tickets
*   Gumdrops
*   Festive Blessing Token (Provides 30 seconds of buff; visible only to the planter)
*   Beesmas Cheer Token (Provides 30 seconds of buff; visible only to the planter)
*   Snowflakes
*   Gingerbread Bears
*   Glues
*   Glitter
*   Enzymes
*   Red Extracts
*   Blue Extracts
*   Magic Beans
*   Oils
*   Flying Bee Bear Sticker (Rare)

## Trivia and Lore
*   The Festive Bean is the only variant of Magic Bean in the game.
*   It is the item with the longest cooldown in the game (2 hours).
*   This bean, along with the Marshmallow Bee, are the only event items that can be donated to the Wind Shrine.
*   Festive Beans are one of the few event items that are infinitely renewable outside of an event.
*   The Festive Bean cannot be used during the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Festive Beans are the only way to obtain Snowflakes outside of Beesmas, although they will not appear in the player's inventory even if collected via tokens; they must already be in the player's hotbar to be visible.
*   When summoned, the server-wide sound matches that of the Festive Bee's Festive Gift.
