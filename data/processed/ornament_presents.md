# Ornament Presents

**Note:** This content has been removed from the game. While this page may be kept for archival purposes, feel free to edit it if necessary.

Ornament Presents were special holiday presents featured during the Beesmas 2019 event. They have since been discontinued and replaced by visually identical Gift Boxes in subsequent Beesmas events. These are not to be confused with standard [Present] or [Gift Boxes].

<https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Present1.ogg> [](/wiki/File:Present1.ogg)
*(Sound of opening a gift)*

There were 12 different types of Ornament Presents available during the event. To open them, players needed to obtain a specific number of [Ornaments] from various [NPCs]. When opened, each present dropped various items (in token form) around the box. Although the rewards were initially tokens, these items could stack past their limits if applicable. Each ornament present could only be opened once.

## Trivia

*   A token that gave 15 [Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") was located behind the Golden Present.
*   Tokens that gave 1 [Royal Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") and 3 [Micro-Converters](/wiki/Micro-Converter "Micro-Converter") were each next to the Mythic Present.
*   If some tokens weren't collected from an ornament present, the items from those tokens would immediately go to the player's inventory upon despawning.
*   The room housing the Golden Present has been replaced and reused as [Commando Chick's Hideout](/wiki/Commando_Chick%27s_Hideout "Commando Chick's Hideout").
*   The Gooey Present's location behind the [Gummy Bee Egg Claim](/wiki/Gummy_Bee_Egg_Claim "Gummy Bee Egg Claim") remains accessible. The Mythic Present's room, previously located to the left of the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"), was removed some time ago.
