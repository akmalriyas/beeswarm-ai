# Stick Nymph

The **Stick Nymph** is a mob summoned by the [Stick Bug] during the [Stick Bug Challenge]. It is not to be confused with [Festive Nymphs], a variant that only appears in Beesmas.

## Description and Mechanics

Stick Nymphs follow the player closest to them and deal damage upon contact. Although they possess low individual stats, they are frequently encountered in large groups, which can easily overpower weaker players.

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Mob Behavior:** Stick Nymphs can follow players outside of [Fields]. Their movement through objects is inconsistent; they may pass through some, be obstructed by others, or enter specific locations like the [King Beetle's Lair], allowing for ranged damage exploitation.
*   **Health Drain:** Like [Mondo Chick] and [Festive Nymphs], Stick Nymphs lose health every second. Their health drain rate increases with their level. They can be defeated naturally, unlike Mondo Chick, which has a fixed remaining health pool after 15 minutes. If they drain to zero without player intervention, they still drop rewards.
*   **Movement:** While Stick Nymphs can jump, they cannot jump onto players who are positioned high enough or far enough away from them.
*   **Summoning:** Stick Nymphs and Festive Nymphs are unique in that they are the only mobs capable of being summoned by another mob. They are part of a small group (18 total) of mobs that cannot spawn naturally in fields, alongside several types of [Ants], [Snowbear], Stick Bugs, and various Robo Challenge/Party Mobs.
*   **Token Drops:** Stick Nymphs were the first mob to drop tokens upon defeat. This differs from the [Stick Bug], which is impossible to permanently defeat; knocking it down only raises its level by one point while resetting its health.
*   **Glitches and Hazards:** A glitch allows Stick Nymphs to climb ramps when spawning in a [Strawberry Field]. Furthermore, if players are hit by a cluster of them, the lack of a grace period can be lethal. They may also inflict damage upon respawning after player death if large numbers are present on the body.
