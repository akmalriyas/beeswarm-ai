# Precise Bee

**Precise Bee** is a Red Mythic bee, known for its sharpshooting abilities and precision. Its motto states: *"This sharpshooting bee is always on point and expects the same of you."*

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Mythic |
| **Color** | Red |
| **Energy** | 40 |
| **Speed** | 11.2 |
| **Attack** | 8 |

Precise Bee's favorite treat is Sunflower Seeds. It favors the Mountain Top Field and Rose Field, but dislikes the Bamboo Field and Pine Tree Forest.

## Stats & Bonuses

*   **Pollen Collection:** Collects 20 Pollen in 4 seconds (equivalent to 5 per second).
*   **Honey Production:** Makes 80 Honey in 4 seconds (equivalent to 20 per second).
*   **Base Stat Boosts:** +7 Attack, -20% Movespeed, +10 Gather Amount, +100% Energy, +5% Critical Chance, +3% Super-Crit Chance.
*   **Gifted Hive Bonus:** +3% Super-Crit Chance.

## Abilities

### Target Practice (Ability Tokens)
This ability causes the bee to fly into the air and project 3 targets on the field for a few seconds. Running on a target activates it.

*   **Precision Buff:** When all 3 targets are activated, this collects all ability tokens and grants a stack of **Precision**, which provides +2% Super-Crit Chance for 60s and stacks up to 10 times. Super-Crits only occur during Critical Hits and gain x2 power and 100% Instant Conversion.
*   **Focus Token:** After a few seconds, any activated target generates a **Focus** token and is shot, collecting pollen equal to double this bee's attack (+10% per Level) from Flowers. With Flame Heat active, the shots gain up to x4 Pollen and up to 50% instant conversion.
*   **Red Boost Token:** Any unactivated target spawns a **Red Boost** token instead.
*   **Flame Heat Conversion:** If the player is shot while standing on a target, this collects all ability tokens and consumes Flame Heat to instantly convert Pollen in the bag. Half of the converted amount turns into Honey Tokens. The conversion amount equals 50% of your Convert Total + 10x this bee's convert amount, scaling up to x10 with consumed Flame Heat and up to x3 with current field boost stacks.
*   **Gifted Bonus:** If Gifted, activating only the farthest target summons a **Precise Mark**, granting +7% Critical Chance and Super-Crit Chance, stacking up to 3 times.
*   **Off-Field Token Collection:** If the player collects a Target Practice token outside the field, they are granted either the Precision (20% chance) or Focus buff (80% chance).

### Passive: Sniper
When fighting mobs, this bee uses slow, long-ranged shots that are more accurate and deal x2 damage. The accuracy is calculated as if the bee was 1 level higher (or 2 if gifted), and it cannot be below 5%. If the shot is a Critical Hit, reload time is reduced by 50%. If it's a Super-Crit, it's reduced by 75%.

## Trivia

*   Excluding Event bees, this bee, Demon Bee and Fire Bee, are the only red bees currently in the game whose favorite treat is not strawberries. This is the only red Mythic Bee who doesn’t like strawberries.
*   The Precise Bee is the only Mythic Bee that does not have a Gifted ability.
*   Precise Bee's attack is based on a long-ranged weapon with a sharp scope, such as a sniper. It is the only bee that can attack mobs from outside the standard bee range and likewise the only bee that shoots projectiles towards hostile mobs to deal damage.
*   This bee's attack changes appearance when landing a Critical Hit or Super-Crit. It fires a bigger projectile for a Critical Hit, and a purple projectile for a Super-Crit. This makes Precise Bee the only bee to have a standard attack that changes depending on whether a Critical hit (or Super-Crit) is rolled.
*   Precise Bee, Buoyant Bee, and Fuzzy Bee are among the newer Mythic bees added since the original three (Vector Bee, Tadpole Bee, and Spicy Bee). Precise Bee and Buoyant Bee were both added in the 2021-12-26 update.
*   This bee is the only bee that has a part of its body that glows even if it is not gifted.
*   Along with Tabby Bee, this bee has an ability that grants Super-Critical chance.
*   Its passive guarantees it never misses against mobs one level higher (two if gifted). It is the only bee to always hit a level 26 mob (27 if gifted) when it is level 25.
*   Unlike Fuzz Bombs and Bubbles, players cannot activate each other's targets; other player's targets are seen as gray to prevent confusion. If "Hide Other Bees" is activated, they become invisible.
*   This bee has the highest attack damage, only surpassed by Gummy Bee when the Gummy Bear Morph ability is active.
*   Although spawning Precise Marks is considered a mark ability, it is not affected by Mark Ability Pollen multipliers.
*   Target Practice does not receive the 10% pollen buff per level.
