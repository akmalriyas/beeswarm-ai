# White Field Booster

The **White Field Booster** is a machine that provides a field boost to a random colorless or mixed-color field for 15 minutes.

## Mechanics and Usage
*   **Effect:** Boosts a random colorless or mixed-color field (e.g., Sunflower, Dandelion) by the specified percentage.
*   **Duration:** 15 minutes.
*   **Cooldown:** 45 minutes.
*   **Requirement:** The player must have discovered at least 20 bee types to use the booster.
*   **Location:** It is located beyond the Lion Bee Gate, situated between the Mountain Top Shop and the Ticket Shop.

## Boost Details
The White Field Booster provides the following boosts:

| Field | Pollen Boost |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sunflower Field | +300% |
| Dandelion Field | +300% |
| Spider Field | +200% |
| Clover Field | +200% |
| Pineapple Patch | +200% |
| Pumpkin Patch | +200% |
| Cactus Field | +100% |

## Trivia and History
*   The White Field Booster is one of three field boosters in the game, alongside the Blue Field Booster and Red Field Booster. They share a similar design, differing only by color and signage.
*   Certain fields, such as Coconut Field and Mountain Top Field, cannot be boosted using this machine, even if they are not specifically colored.
*   **Historical Note:** Prior to the 2019-09-28 update, players could access the booster with fewer bee discoveries (previously needing 25 bees for the Red Cannon). Since that update, attempting to bypass gates results in being teleported back to spawn.
*   Prior to the 2019-09-28 update, the cooldown was two hours.
*   The White Field Booster provides increased pollen for fields located early in the game to compensate for their smaller flower sizes.
*   It is notable that this booster is currently one of the only field boosters capable of granting a 200% boost (historically, the Red and Blue Boosters granted specific boosts to Strawberry and Bamboo Fields).
