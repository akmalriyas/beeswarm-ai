**Ability tokens** are collectible tokens generated by bees, which activate special abilities and buffs for the player. Almost all bees spawn ability tokens, with exceptions being ungifted Basic Bees and Brave Bees without Beequips. If a token is not collected within a certain time, it fades away. Tokens are client-sided; players cannot collect tokens generated by other players' bees, though this visibility restriction changed prior to the May 7th, 2018 update.

Some tokens originate from sources other than player bees (e.g., honey tokens from flowers). Player-generated bee tokens give off gold sparkles, distinguishing them from non-sparkling field tokens. All visible tokens are collectible, although a bug sometimes prevents collection during Honeystorms or Snowstorms.

## Mechanics and Stats

### Token Spawning Categories
Ability tokens fall into three categories based on the activity of the spawning bee:
*   **Gather:** Tokens generated while bees are collecting pollen from flowers. Most Gather tokens boost pollen collection.
*   **Battle:** Tokens generated while bees are targeting or attacking mobs. These often inflict damage or debuffs and enhance combat processes.
*   **Idle:** Tokens generated when bees are not actively gathering, attacking, or converting.

Some ability tokens can fall into multiple categories.

### Token Spawning Attempts
When a bee pauses after movement (gathering, attacking, or turning), it attempts to spawn its ability tokens. Each token is governed by three variables:
*   **Use Cooldown:** The time required after the token has been spawned before the bee attempts to summon that specific token again.
*   **Attempt Cooldown:** The minimum waiting period between spawning attempts for a given token type.
*   **Attempt Success Rate:** The probability that an attempt to spawn the token succeeds. If successful, the token is added to the bee's pool.

The average time required for an ability token to spawn can be calculated using the formula:
$$time = useCooldown + attemptCooldown \times {\frac {1}{attemptSuccessRate}}$$

### Token Characteristics
*   **Bee Ability Rate:** This stat modifies the tokens' spawn rate by dividing the use cooldown and attempt cooldown, and multiplying the attempt success rate. A 200% Bee Ability Rate reduces a token's effective cooldowns to 50% of their original value and doubles its success rate.
*   **Ability Token Lifespan:** Extends how long uncollected tokens remain on the field before despawning. This can be boosted by Gifted Bees, Stubborn Bees, Cog Amulets, Paper Angel/Paperclip Beequips, or the Drooping Stubborn Bee Sticker. The maximum possible lifespan is 145%.
*   **Ability Duplication Chance:** Tokens have a chance to spawn duplicated variants randomly on the field. Duped tokens last for double the original token's lifespan, which is further increased by 5% per Digital Bee level. This chance can be boosted by Field Corruption (from Digital Bee's Glitch and Map Corruption) or Gifted status on Digital Bees. It can also be temporarily increased via the 'Corrupt' upgrade from the Robo Bear Challenge or through Science Bear's questing in specific fields.
*   **Bee Movespeed:** While not directly related to token generation, higher bee movespeed allows bees to make more attempts at creating Ability Tokens, thereby increasing the overall average spawn rate of tokens.

## Specific Ability Tokens

### Bomb (Gather)
These tokens collect pollen from flowers and grant the Bomb Combo buff. They are categorized by flower type and range:
*   **Buzz Bombs:** Collect 7 pollen from all types of flowers.
*   **Red Bombs:** Collect 10 pollen only from Red flowers.
*   **Blue Bombs:** Collect 10 pollen only from Blue flowers.

Range categories include **Bomb** (collects from 13 surrounding flowers) and **Bomb+** (collects from 29 surrounding flowers). Pollen collected increases by 10% per bee level.

**Other Sources:** Balloons during a Surprise Party (Blue/Buzz), Beequips (Bang Snaps: any color; Smiley Stickers: Blue Bomb; Bottle Caps: Buzz Bomb).

*   *Pollen Haze:* When Pollen Haze is active, all Bomb tokens are automatically collected and may pollinate flowers.

### Festive Mark (Gather)
Exclusive to Festive Bees with the Festive Wreath beequip. Deploys a Festive Mark for 12 seconds (+0.5s per level) with a ~22-tile radius. Grants the Festive Mark buff, providing extra Honey Per Pollen, Bomb Pollen, Red Bomb Pollen, Instant Bomb Conversion, and Conversion Links. Unlike other marks, it grants boosts to other players and its range is visible to others; it can also link with other players' marks.
*   **Gifts:** While active, it summons random gifts within its range (including Red Boost Tokens every 4th token).

### Reindeer Fetch (Gather)
Enhances Puppy Bee's Fetch ability when the Reindeer Antlers beequip is equipped. The ball glows red and emits white sparkles; every combo grants the player the Reindeer Guidance buff, which increases capacity. High combos can spawn a Gingerbread Bear token (max 1 per day), allowing for up to 512 treats instead of the normal 256.
