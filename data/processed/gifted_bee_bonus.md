# Gifted Bee Bonus

A bee is identified as "gifted" when a yellow hexagon appears around its hive slot.

## Becoming Gifted

Gifted bees are special variants of standard bees, possessing improved stats and the unique "Gifted Hive Bonus." They function similarly to Badges, enhancing player performance.

**Methods for Gaining Gifted Status:**

1. **Royal Jelly or Egg Hatching:**
    * Using a Royal Jelly or hatching an egg grants a 0.4% (1 in 250) chance of obtaining a gifted bee.
2. **Feeding Favorite Treats:**
    * Feeding a bee its favorite treat has varying chances depending on the bee's rarity:
        * Rare Bees: 0.0125% (1 in 8,000)
        * Epic Bees: 0.01% (1 in 10,000)
        * Common/Legendary Bees: 0.0083% (1 in 12,000)
        * Mythic Bees: 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) per favorite treat.
    * **Gingerbread Bear:** Has a 1% (1/100) chance of gifting the bee.
    * **Aged Gingerbread Bear:** Has a 1.1% (1/90.909091) chance of gifting the bee.

**Guaranteed Gifted Status Methods:**

*   **Star Treat:** Always transforms the chosen bee into its gifted version. This is the only method that can gift Event Bees (like Bear Bee and Tabby Bee), excluding gingerbread bears.
    * *Note:* Feeding a Star Treat to an already gifted bee will only grant bond, not extra benefits.
*   **Star Egg:** Will always hatch into a non-event gifted bee unless the player already possesses all non-Event bees in their gifted forms; in that case, it hatches into a random non-Event gifted bee.
*   **Star Jelly:** Always transforms the chosen bee into a random non-Event gifted bee, using the same odds as a regular Royal Jelly (with the exception of Basic Bees).

## Gifted Bee Effects

Upon obtaining its gifted status, a bee's UI will cycle through colors, display the text "⭐ Your [type] Bee Transformed Into A Gifted [type] Bee!⭐", and play a unique sound file.

**General Improvements:**
*   The bee gains a new color scheme, stained wings, and a star on its back and sides. The hive icon does not change color.
*   A glowing gold hexagon appears around the bee's hive slot.
*   **Stats Multipliers (Base):**
    * Gather Amount: x1.5
    * Convert Rate: x1.5
    * Attack: x1.5
*   Improved certain passive abilities.
*   New Ability Token: **Inspire**. This doubles pollen gained for 5 seconds and stacks up to 50 times, providing a bonus up to x51.
*   Ability to generate ⭐ Gifted Ability Tokens if the bee possesses one.
*   **Star Hall Bonus:** Each gifted bee allows the player to claim an additional Royal Jelly from the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser in the Star Hall.

## Trivia

*   Since Bear Bee is now purchasable from the Robux Shop, players who purchased a Bear Bee during the game launch event had their bee automatically converted into a First Edition gifted Bear Bee. This makes Bear Bee the only Event bee that can be gifted without using a Star Treat or Gingerbread Bears.
*   Multiple gifted bees of the same type do not stack their hive bonus multiple times, nor do they count towards the number of unique gifted bee types in the hive. However, they will still retain improved base stats over their normal version.
*   Using a regular Royal Jelly or a non-gifted egg on a gifted bee will almost certainly cause the bee to lose its gifted status. The chance for the resulting new bee to be gifted is 1/250.
*   Windy Bee is unique in that it provides two different gifted hive bonuses.
*   Tadpole Bee previously offered two different gifted hive bonuses before the May 23, 2024 update.
