# Vicious Bee

**Vicious Bee** is a Blue Event bee, known for its aggressive nature ("This cold-blooded bee takes great pleasure in inflicting pain.").

> This page details the tamed version of Vicious Bee. For the hostile variant, see [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee).

## Acquisition and Traits
Vicious Bee can be claimed for 250 Stingers at the Vicious Bee Egg Claim. As an Event bee, it does not have a favorite treat; however, it can be gifted by feeding it a Star Treat, Gingerbread Bear, or Aged Gingerbread Bear.

**Habitat Preferences:**
*   **Likes:** Cactus Field, Rose Field
*   **Dislikes:** Dandelion Field

Vicious Bee's base production rates are:

*   Honey Making: 80 Honey in 4 seconds (20 per second).

### Gifted Hive Bonuses
When gifted, Vicious Bee receives the following bonuses:
*   **Impale Accuracy:** +1 level of accuracy for its Impale ability.
*   **Mob Respawn Time Reduction:** -15% Mob Respawn Time.

### Ability Details

*   **Impale:** Summons spikes (one per level) to damage enemies equal to 5% of their current health. Damage is increased by Vicious Bee's attack stat. Each enemy hit generates a honey token (worth 50 base honey + 50 per bee level). Multiple spikes on the same target deal reduced damage. Impale can damage mobs that target other players, but it will not affect Tunnel Bear, King Beetle, Stump Snail, or Coconut Crab.
*   **Blue Bomb+:** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding blue Flowers (+10% pollen per Level). This ability can be combined with other bombs to increase power.

## Trivia and Lore
*   Vicious Bee's base attack was previously 7, before being increased to 8 in the 2021-12-26 update.
*   Gifted Vicious Bees have golden-colored spikes on their Impale ability instead of the normal gray ones. The gifted status also provides a +50% attack bonus, which indirectly increases Impale damage.
*   Vicious Bee is unique among bees in that it can summon an Ability Token that directly attacks enemies. Alongside Precise Bee (Sniper), it is one of only two bees with direct offensive abilities.
*   It is one of three bees to have an official plushie on the Official Bee Swarm Simulator Website, along with Basic Bee and Tabby Bee.
*   Vicious Bee is the only blue bee that likes the Rose Field (possibly due to both roses and cacti having thorns). The Rogue Version can also spawn in these fields.
*   Vicious Bee and Windy Bee are among the few bees that possess a hostile variant.
*   Acquiring Vicious Bee by purchasing all 250 stingers from the Stinger Shop would cost 2,500 Tickets, making it one of the most expensive event bees to obtain via tickets.
*   Vicious Bee, Ninja Bee, and Cobalt Bee are among the blue bees that do not like Blueberries.
*   Vicious Bee is one of several Event bees (along with Bear Bee, Gummy Bee, Windy Bee, and Digital Bee) that cannot be purchased in the Ticket Tent.
