# Music Soundtrack

**Music** is a component within *Bee Swarm Simulator*. It plays in the background during gameplay, and different areas of the map feature distinct songs. The audio can be toggled on or off via the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page).

There are 15 principal songs used in the game, all originally found in Onett's inventory.

## Trivia
* A majority of the songs were created using music programs called "Nanostudio" and "Nanostudio 2".
* icedtealatte composed the StarHall song, making it the only known in-game track not created by Onett (Developer).
