# Petal Planter

The **Petal Planter** is a reusable planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. It can be purchased at the Petal Shop.

## Overview and Stats

| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reusable?** | Yes | Permanent item. |
| **Growth Time** | ~14 hours | Alone. |
| **Pollen Capacity** | 100,000,000,000 Pollen | Stores up to 100 billion Pollen. |
| **Cost (Purchase)** | 5T Honey, 100 Magic Beans, 100 Glitters, 250 Soft Waxes, 50 Swirled Waxes, 25 Super Smoothies | Cost at the Petal Shop. |

### Growth Mechanics and Bonuses

*   **Field Bonus:** Grows 50% faster in fields with white flowers.
*   **Pollen Multiplier (White):** Grants +50% bonus White Pollen on harvest.
*   **Nectar Multipliers:**
    *   Colorless bees are 33% more likely to sip nectar from this planter.
    *   Grants +50% Comforting Nectar and +50% Satisfying Nectar.

## Harvesting and Drops

When harvested, the Petal Planter grants bonus Jelly Beans, Field Dice, and Glitter.

### Puffshroom Spawn Chance
Harvesting a fully grown Petal Planter has a 1/4 chance to spawn a Puffshroom (levels 7-8). The rarity probabilities are:

*   **Rare:** ~89.28%
*   **Epic:** ~8.93%
*   **Legendary:** ~1.79%
*   **Mythic:** ~0.01%

### Token Drops
When claimed, the planter gives up to 45 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed:

*   **Guaranteed Items (2 Tokens):** 1 Glitter and 5 Tickets.
*   The remaining tokens are picked randomly from a non-exhaustive list including Bonus, Other, and Special rewards.

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Rarity/Cost Comparison:** The Petal Planter is the fourth most expensive item in the game based on honey cost, ranking behind the Coconut Belt (7.5T), Gummyballer (10T), and The Planter of Plenty (100T).
*   **Exclusivity:** It is currently one of only five reusable planters not sold in Dapper Bear's Shop. Furthermore, it is the only item in the Petal Shop added after the shop itself was introduced.
*   **Crafting Ingredients Note:** While the in-game description suggests +33% white pollen and 50% more likelihood for colorless bees to sip nectar, these values are swapped in reality (it grants +50% White Pollen and colorless bees are 33% more likely).
