# Red Extract

[![Red Extract](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b9/Red_Extract.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404020836)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b9/Red_Extract.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404020836)

> Grants x1.25 Red Pollen for 10 minutes.

**Cooldown:** 1 second

A **Red Extract** is an inventory item introduced in the 2018-11-25 update. It serves two primary purposes: as a material for crafting accessories, or to activate a temporary buff that grants x1.25 Red Pollen for 10 minutes.

The associated buff cannot stack; using another **Red Extract** while one is active will simply reset the timer of the current buff. Note that using a Purple Potion or Super Smoothie will override this buff entirely.

## Ways to Obtain

### Crafting
*   Crafted via Blender using:
    *   50 Strawberries and 10 Royal Jellies.

### Memory Match Rewards
*   Matching a pair of Red Extracts in Mega Memory Match yields 1 Red Extract.
*   Matching a pair of Red Extracts in Extreme Memory Match yields 3 Red Extracts.

### Environmental & Event Sources
*   As a reward from the Wind Shrine.
*   As a Stack Reward when adding specific Stickers to the Sticker Stack:
    *   Flying Rad Bee Sticker (1)
    *   Round Rascal Bee Sticker (1)
    *   Flying Festive Bee Sticker (1)
    *   Little Scorpion Sticker (1)
    *   Red X Sticker (1)
    *   AFK Sticker (1)
    *   Small Flame Sticker (1)
    *   Shrugging Heart Sticker (1)
    *   Red Doodle Person Sticker (1)
    *   Scythe Sticker (1)
    *   Dark Scythe Sticker (1)
*   As a reward from the Retro Swarm Challenge.

### Drops and Harvesting
**From Mobs:**
*   Ladybug (Rare)
*   Scorpion (Rare)
*   King Beetle
*   Rage Aphid

**From Fields/Plants:**
*   Can rarely drop in the Mushroom Field, Strawberry Field, Rose Field, or Pepper Patch.
    *   A rare drop from leaves in the Mushroom Field, Strawberry Field, or Rose Field.
*   As a rare drop from Sprouts (odds increase based on sprout rarity and location in red fields).

**From Gifts & Planters:**
*   As a drop from a Festive Gift (if gifted to a Festive Bee) or a Festive Mark.
*   As a rare gift from a Cub Buddy.
*   Guaranteed drops from certain planters:
    *   Red Clay Planter
    *   Heat-Treated Planter (Drops extra Red Extracts in red fields.)

## Quests
*   Completing any of Gifted Riley Bee's quests always rewards one Red Extract.
*   **Black Bear:**
    *   Get Red-y: 1 Red Extract + other items.
    *   Rocky Red Mountain: 1 Red Extract + other items.
    *   Capsaicin Collector: 1 Red Extract + other items.
    *   Repeatable quests occasionally grant Red Extracts.
*   **Science Bear:**
    *   Meticulously Crafted: 1 Red Extract + other items.
    *   Mushroom Measurement Monotony: 3 Red Extracts + other items.

*   Opening Ornament Presents:
    *   Colorful Present (1 Red Extract + other items).
    *   Festive Present (4 Red Extracts + other items).
    *   Mythic Present (3 Red Extracts + other items).

*   Redeeming expired codes: "Crafty" (1 Red Extract), "RedCatBee" (1 Red Extract), "ExoExtract" (1 Red Extract), and "3YearParty" (1 Red Extract).
