# Diamond Drenchers

![DiamondDrenchers](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fc/DiamondDrenchers.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20191208202233)

The **Diamond Drenchers** are a 4th tier sprinkler available for purchase in the Badge Bearer's Guild. They are comprised of four enhanced sprinklers made of durable artificial diamond.

### Stats
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Count | 4 |
| Radius | 8 tiles |
| Power | 9 |
| Rate (Flower Regeneration) | 4 seconds |
| Setup Time | 4 seconds |

### Acquisition Details
*   **Requirement:** 10 Master Badges
*   **Cost:** 4,444,444,444 Honey
