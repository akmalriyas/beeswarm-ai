# Magic Bean Shop

[![The Magic Bean Shop.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b3/Magic_Bean_Shop.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20260204020602)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b3/Magic_Bean_Shop.png/revision/latest?cb=20260204020602) [](/wiki/File:Magic_Bean_Shop.png)

The **Magic Bean Shop** is a shop added in the 2018-11-25 update, located next to the Stump Field. Players use this shop to purchase Magic Beans with Tickets, available in increments of 1 and 10. Each single magic bean costs 20 tickets.

The shop is decorated with a small normal sprout on top, along with the magic bean icon displayed on all of its sides.

## Prices

| Quantity | Cost (Tickets) |
| :---: | :---: |
| 1 | 20 |
| 10 | 200 |

## Trivia

*   In terms of Robux value, the Magic Bean Shop is overpriced. In the Robux Shop, a ticket costs 10 robux, while a magic bean costs 30 robux. Since players need 20 tickets for one magic bean, they are effectively spending 200 robux to acquire an item that can be purchased directly in the Robux Shop for only 30 robux (a situation similar to the Royal Jelly Ticket Dispenser).
*   For comparison, 200 robux can also be used to purchase a bundle of 10 magic beans in the Robux Shop. If a player buys 20 tickets from the standard shop to spend on Magic Beans, they miss out on 9 additional magic beans compared to buying the bundle directly with Robux.
