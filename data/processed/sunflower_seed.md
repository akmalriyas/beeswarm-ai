# Sunflower Seeds

![Sunflower Seed](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/66/Sunflower_Seed.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041336)

> "Increases your bond with a bee by 25."

A **Sunflower Seed** is a type of treat that can be fed to a Bee to raise its bond by 25.

### Bond Mechanics
*   **Standard Bees:** When fed to Basic, Bomber, Looker, Demo, Commander, Carpenter, Ninja, Vector, or Precise Bees, the bond gain is doubled, raising their bond by 50.
*   **Gifted Chance:** Feeding Sunflower Seeds gives a small chance for a bee to become Gifted:
    *   Rare bees: 0.0125% (1 in 8,000)
    *   Epic bees: 0.01% (1 in 10,000)
    *   Common and Legendary bees: 0.0083% (1 in 12,000)
    *   Mythic bees: 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) per seed.
*   **Mutation:** Feeding a Radioactive bee a sunflower seed has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) chance to mutate the bee.

### Acquisition Methods

**Obtaining Sunflower Seeds:**
*   Ten seeds can be found inside the Mazes in the Pro Shop.
*   As a reward from the Ant Challenge.
*   Redeeming certain valid codes (e.g., 15MMembers).
*   The Spirit Bear grants 500 Sunflower Seeds upon receiving the Echoing Call quest.
*   As a reward from Memory Matches.
*   Adding the Tiny House Sticker to the Sticker Stack rewards 50 Sunflower Seeds.

**Drops:**
Seeds can be obtained as drops from various Mobs and Planters:

**Mobs:**
*   Werewolf
*   Spider
*   Stick Bug
*   Stick Nymph
*   Wild Windy Bee
*   King Beetle
*   Commando Chick
*   Tunnel Bear
*   Puffshroom

**Planters:**
Seeds drop from the following planters:
*   Paper Planter (1, 3, 5, or 10)
*   Ticket Planter
*   Sticker Planter
*   Festive Planter
*   Plastic Planter
*   Candy Planter
*   Blue Clay Planter
*   Red Clay Planter
*   Tacky Planter
*   Pesticide Planter
*   Heat-Treated Planter
*   Petal Planter

**Other Sources:**
*   As a gift from Cub Buddy.
*   Harvesting Leaves in the Sunflower Field.
*   Sparkles can drop Sunflower Seeds when harvested (with higher chances in the Sunflower Field).
*   Sprouts dropped in the Sunflower Field yield 7x more seeds than other fields (excluding Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts).
*   As a drop from a Festive Gift or Festive Mark.

**Outdated/Removed Methods:**
*   Digging in to the Beesmas Feast.
*   Redeeming expired codes (5mMembers, FourYearFiesta, 1MLikes).
*   Defeating chicks found in Sunflower Field (requires Black Bear's Sunflower Corral quest).
