# Daily Top Blue Flower Field Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Blue Flower Field Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. It is accessible via the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page.

This leaderboard tracks the total amount of Pollen players have collected specifically within the Blue Flower Field on a given day. The board displays 10 positions at a time, but users can scroll to view all 100 ranked spots. The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes and Rewards

Players who achieve specific rankings by the end of the day are awarded rewards:

*   **Top 25:** Players in the top 25 receive a **Blue Flower Field Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players in the top 100 receive 25 **Tickets**.
