# Bee Eggs

Eggs are inventory items in the game that players drag into their hive to hatch a bee. They can also be donated to the Wind Shrine or used to roll on the Sticker Printer. Eggs serve as an entry point for new players and come in various types, some offering chances at different rarities of bees, while others guarantee a specific outcome.

Currently, there are 6 main egg types (plus gifted variants), excluding specialized eggs like Plastic Eggs, Flight of the Bumble Egg, or Swarming Egg of the Hive.

**Hatching Mechanics:**
Since the 2019-09-28 update, players do not require a vacant hive slot to hatch an egg; they can hatch it on top of an existing bee. The level of the host bee remains unchanged, acting similarly to Royal Jelly.

***

## General Egg Types

*   Basic Egg
*   Silver Egg
*   Gold Egg
*   Diamond Egg
*   Mythic Egg
*   Star Egg

### Basic Egg
The most common egg type in the game. It has a high chance of hatching into a Basic Bee (87%), but can rarely hatch into:
*   Rare Bee (10%)
*   Epic Bee (2.5%)
*   Legendary Bee (0.5%)
*   Gifted Bee (0.4%)

There is also a 0.348% chance of hatching a Gifted Basic Bee from the egg. Mythic and Event bees cannot hatch from this egg, nor can it be used on a Basic Bee.

**Ways to obtain:**
*   Free upon starting the game.
*   Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop near the Sunflower Field (Base price: 1,000 Honey; caps at 10,000,000 Honey). The price scales up with each purchase.
*   Receiving a reward for adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack.

### Silver Egg
This egg most likely hatches into a Rare bee (64.9%), but also has chances of hatching into:
*   Epic bee (30%)
*   Legendary bee (5%)
*   Mythic bee (0.1%)

A Basic Bee or an Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg.

**Ways to obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 50 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 100 Robux.
*   Completing Silver Egg questlines for Black Bear and Panda Bear.
*   Receiving a reward from Brown Bear's 25th quest.
*   Completing the 25th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quest.
*   Low chance drop from Mantis, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Rare Sprouts, leaves (while collecting pollen in Clover Field), Festive Planter, Stockings, and Memory Match (Winter).

### Gifted Silver Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Silver Egg, but always hatches the bee into its gifted variant.

**Ways to obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, though subsequent defeats have lower chances.
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Completing Black Bear's "Getting Stumped" quest.
*   Receiving a reward from the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine upon completing its 100th quest.
*   Rare drops from Werewolf, Stump Snail, Scorpion, and Sticker Sprout.

### Gold Egg
Most likely hatches into an Epic bee (79%), with chances for:
*   Legendary bee (20%)
*   Mythic bee (1%)

A Basic Bee, Rare bee, or Event bee cannot hatch from this egg. Five Gold Eggs are required to craft the Honey Mask.

**Ways to obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 100 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 200 Robux each.
*   Rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge and Extreme Memory Match.
*   Found as a token in Werewolf's Cave (guarded by two cave monsters).
*   Completing Gold Egg questlines for Black Bear and Panda Bear.
*   Receiving rewards from Brown Bear's 50th, 340th, and 390th quests.
*   Receiving a reward from the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine upon completing its 50th quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick 10 times.
*   Low chance drop from King Beetle, Scorpion, Epic sprouts, leaves (while collecting pollen in Clover Field), and various planters (Ticket, Candy, Red Clay, Festive, Pesticide, Petal).
*   Rare drops from Stockings and Memory Match (Winter).

### Gifted Gold Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Gold Egg, but always hatches the bee into its gifted variant. This egg cannot be used to craft the Honey Mask.

**Ways to obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, though subsequent defeats have lower chances.
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Playtime Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 50 Gifted Bucko Bee quests or 50 Gifted Riley Bee quests.
*   Completing Brown Bear's 275th quest.
*   Receiving a reward from the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine upon completing its 150th quest.
*   Very rare drops from Stump Snail, Coconut Crab, and Mantis.

### Diamond Egg
Most likely hatches into a Legendary bee (95%), with a rare chance to hatch into a Mythic bee (5%). A Basic Bee, Rare bee, Epic bee, or Event bee cannot hatch from this egg. Five Diamond Eggs are required to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Ways to obtain:**
*   Found as a token past Bear Gate, under Onett's platform (requires Glider/Parachute near Shadow Bear).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 400 Robux each.
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 400 Tickets each.
*   Rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge and Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Diamond Egg questlines for Black Bear, Panda Bear (Colossal Combat), and Dapper Bear (The Long Haul).
*   Receiving rewards from Brown Bear's 75th and 525th quests.
*   Receiving a reward from the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine upon completing its 75th quest.
*   Rare drops from Stump Snail, Diamond Aphids, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Coconut Crab.
*   Very rare drops from Spiders, Mantises, and Werewolves.
*   Rare chance drop from Legendary sprouts.
*   Guaranteed drop from Supreme sprouts.
*   Extremely rare chance drop from leaves (while collecting pollen in Clover Field).
*   Very rare reward from catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Rare drops from Festive Planter and Memory Match (Winter).

### Gifted Diamond Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Diamond Egg, but always hatches the bee into its gifted variant. This egg cannot be used to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Ways to obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, though subsequent defeats have lower chances.
*   Reward for being in the top 25 in the Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers.
*   Quest Grandmaster badge reward from donating Tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 100, 250, 500, 750, and 1,000 Gifted Bucko Bee or Gifted Riley Bee quests.
*   Completing Brown Bear's 425th quest.
*   Receiving a reward from the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine upon completing its 200th quest.
*   Extremely rare drops from Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, and Coconut Crab.

### Mythic Egg
This egg always hatches into a Mythic bee. The player can obtain a Gifted Mythic bee, but this is very rare.

**Ways to obtain:**
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Memory Matchers.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 1,200 Robux each (limited to 3 eggs per player).
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 2,500 Tickets each.
*   Very rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge and Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Myth in The Making (Black Bear's 75th quest) or More Than Myth (Dapper Bear's 15th quest).
*   Receiving rewards from Brown Bear's 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
*   Receiving a reward from the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine upon completing its 300th quest.
*   Capturing Commando Chick for the 50th time guarantees a Mythic Egg.
*   Extremely rare drops from Stump Snail, Mondo Chick (higher chance if defeated faster), and Coconut Crab.
*   Extremely rare drop when catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Rare drop from Festive Planter and Memory Match (Winter).
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 500 Snowflakes and 100 Gingerbread Bears.

### Gifted Mythic Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Mythic Egg, but always hatches the bee into its gifted variant.

**Ways to obtain:**
*   Reward for achieving Top 25 in the Daily Top Rare Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Completing 500 Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quests.
*   Nearly impossible drop from Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 10,000 Snowflakes and 150 Gingerbread Bears.

### Choose-A-Mythic Egg
Allows the player to select any Mythic Bee upon hatching.

**Ways to obtain:**
*   Purchasing the Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul from the Robux Shop for 4,500 Robux.

### Star Egg
This egg hatches into any type of gifted bee not already in the player's hive (excluding gifted Event bees). If all non-event gifted bees are owned, it hatches a random gifted bee. It will only hatch a gifted mythic bee after all other gifted non-event bees are collected.

**Ways to obtain:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, including Star Egg, though subsequent defeats have lower chances.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 eggs per player).
*   Exceptionally rare chance drop from Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Completing Mother Bear's first ten quests ("Seven To Seven").
*   Completing Panda Bear's first ten star treat/ant quests ("Royal Rumble").
*   Exceptionally rare chance drop from King Beetle, Festive Planter, and Party Mechsquito.

## Specific Event Bee Eggs
These eggs always hatch into their respective specific event bee type, granting an exclusive permanent royal jelly variant. All share the appearance of a basic egg.

### Bear Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Bear Bee (100%).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux. (Previously cost 1,000 Robux or was discounted).
*   Obtained via a Bear Bee Voucher from players who bought the Basic Egg.

### Cobalt Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Cobalt Bee (100%).
*   Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.

### Crimson Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Crimson Bee (100%).
*   Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.

### Digital Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Digital Bee (100%).
*   Obtained from Robo Bear's Shop after acquiring a Diamond Cog Amulet, 7,777,777 Honey, and 5 of all Drives. (Previously available in the Digital Bee Pack for 2,500 Robux).

### Festive Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Festive Bee (100%).
*   Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.
*   Completing the 15th quest of Bee Bear during Beesmas 2020 (if the player doesn't have Festive Bee).
*   Opening the Festive Present during Beesmas 2019.

### Gummy Bee Egg

### Puppy Bee Egg

### Tabby Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Tabby Bee (100%).

### Vicious Bee Egg
