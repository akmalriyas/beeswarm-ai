# Bee Egg Types

Eggs are inventory items in Bee Swarm Simulator that players drag into their hive to hatch a bee, donate to the Wind Shrine, or use in the Sticker Printer. They are also essential for starting gameplay. Eggs vary widely; some offer chances at different bee rarities, while others guarantee a specific bee type.

Currently, there are several main egg types (Basic, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Mythic, Star), along with numerous specific and event-themed eggs.

**Hatching Mechanics:**
*   Since the 2019-09-28 update, players do not require a vacant hive slot to hatch an egg. Eggs can be hatched on top of an existing bee; the level of that bee remains unchanged (acting like Royal Jelly).
*   Specific and Event Bee Eggs always hatch into their respective designated bee type.

## General Egg Types

### Basic Egg
The most common egg type in the game. It primarily hatches into a Basic Bee (87% chance), but can rarely yield: Rare Bee (10%), Epic Bee (2.5%), Legendary Bee (0.5%), or Gifted Bee (0.4%). There is also a 0.348% chance of hatching a Gifted Basic Bee directly from the egg. Mythic and Event bees cannot be hatched from this egg, nor can it be used on a Basic Bee.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Free for first-time players.
*   Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop (price scales up with each purchase).
*   Receiving 1 Basic Egg reward by adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack.

### Silver Egg
This egg most likely hatches into a Rare bee (64.9%), but has chances of hatching an Epic bee (30%), Legendary bee (5%), or Mythic bee (0.1%). A Basic Bee or Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 50 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 100 Robux.
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Silver Egg questlines.
*   As a reward from Brown Bear's 25th quest.
*   Completing the 25th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine rewards you 1 Silver Egg.
*   Drops: Mantis (low chance), King Beetle/Tunnel Bear (low chance), Rare Sprouts (low chance).
*   Rare drops from Leaves when collecting Pollen in the Clover Field, Festive Planters, Stockings, and Winter Memory Match.

### Gold Egg
This egg is most likely to hatch into an Epic bee (79%), with chances of hatching a Legendary bee (20%) or Mythic bee (1%). A Basic Bee, Rare bee, or Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg. Five Gold Eggs are required to craft the Honey Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 100 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 200 Robux each.
*   Rewards: Robo Bear Challenge, Extreme Memory Match.
*   In token form in Werewolf's Cave (guarded by two Cave Monsters).
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Gold Egg questlines.
*   As a reward from Brown Bear's 50th, 340th, and 390th quests.
*   Completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 50th quest rewards 1 Gold Egg.
*   Capturing Commando Chick 10 times.
*   Drops: King Beetle (low chance), Scorpion (extremely low chance), Epic sprouts (rare chance).
*   Rare drops from Festive Planters, Stockings, and Winter Memory Match.

### Diamond Egg
This egg most likely hatches into a Legendary bee (95%), with a rare chance to hatch a Mythic bee (5%). A Basic Bee, Rare bee, Epic bee, or Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg. Five Diamond Eggs are required to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Token location: Past Bear Gate, under the platform where Onett stands (accessible via Glider/Parachute near Shadow Bear).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 400 Robux each.
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 400 Tickets each.
*   Rewards: Robo Bear Challenge, Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Black Bear's Diamond Egg questline.
*   Completing Panda Bear's "Colossal Combat" quest.
*   Completing Dapper Bear's tenth quest, "The Long Haul".
*   As a reward from Brown Bear's 75th and 525th quests.
*   Completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine 75th quest.
*   Drops: Stump Snail/Diamond Aphids/King Beetle/Tunnel Bear/Coconut Crab (rare chance); Spiders/Mantises/Werewolves (very rare chance).
*   Rare drops from Legendary sprouts, guaranteed drop from Supreme sprouts, Festive Planters, and Winter Memory Match.

### Mythic Egg
This egg always hatches into a Mythic bee. The player cannot obtain any other type of bee besides a Mythic bee (though a Gifted Mythic bee is very rare).

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Rewards: Daily Top Memory Matchers (Top 25), Robo Bear Challenge (very rare chance).
*   Drops: Extreme Memory Match (extremely rare chance); Stump Snail/Mondo Chick/Coconut Crab (extremely rare chance).
*   Quest Rewards: Black Bear's 75th quest, Dapper Bear's 15th quest, Brown Bear's 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
*   Completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine 300th quest rewards one Mythic Egg.
*   Capturing Commando Chick for the 50th time guarantees a Mythic Egg.
*   Rare drops from Festive Planters, Winter Memory Match, and Stockings.
*   Purchasing from Bee Bear's Catalog (e.g., 500 Snowflakes + 100 Gingerbread Bears).

### Star Egg
This egg hatches into any type of Gifted bee not already in the player's hive, excluding gifted Event bees. If all non-event gifted bees are owned, it hatches a random gifted bee. It will only hatch a Gifted Mythic bee once all other non-event gifted bees are collected.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Token location: (Available via specific methods).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 eggs per player).
*   Drops: Mythic Meteor Shower (exceptionally rare chance); King Beetle (exceptionally rare chance); Festive Planter (exceptionally rare chance); Party Mechsquito (exceptionally rare drop).
*   Quest Rewards: Mother Bear's first ten quests, Panda Bear's first ten star treat/ant quests.

### Gifted Specific Eggs
These eggs share the probabilities of their base type but always hatch the gifted variant.

*   **Gifted Bubble Bee Egg:** Always hatches a Gifted Bubble Bee (100%). Obtained by completing Bubble Bee Man's Winter Beesmas 2024 quest.
*   **Gifted Carpenter Bee Egg:** Always hatches a Gifted Carpenter Bee (100%). Purchased via the Carpenter's Crafting Pack in the Robux Shop during Winter Beesmas 2024 (Expired).
*   **Gifted Diamond Bee Egg:** Always hatches a Gifted Diamond Bee (100%). Purchased via the Starry Night Special in the Robux Shop during Winter Beesmas 2024 (Expired), or from Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   **Gifted Exhausted Bee Egg:** Always hatches a Gifted Exhausted Bee (100%). Purchased via the Night-Before Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   **Gifted Frosty Bee Egg:** Always hatches a Gifted Frosty Bee (100%). Purchased via the Super Snowman Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog.

## Trivia & Mechanics

*   **Egg Appearance:** Any specific bee egg or event bee egg will have the same icon as a Basic Egg in the inventory.
*   **Rarity Naming:** The Mythic Egg is unique because its name matches the rarity of the bee it hatches into (Mythic). It is also the only non-specific/non-event egg with a letter on it.
*   **Basic Bee Hatching:** Basic Eggs and Star Eggs are the only two types that can hatch into a Basic Bee (or Gifted variant, in the case of Star Egg).
*   **Non-Bee Eggs:** Plastic Eggs, Flight of the Bumble Egg, and Swarming Egg of the Hive do not hatch into bees.
*   **Hatching Restrictions:** Players cannot hatch a specific bee egg on that same type of bee (e.g., a Bumble Bee Egg cannot be hatched on a Bumble Bee). Basic Eggs also cannot be used on Basic Bees.
*   **Obtainable Event Eggs:** The Digital, Vicious, Windy, Bear, and Gummy Bee Eggs are among the few event eggs obtainable without spending Robux or Tickets.
