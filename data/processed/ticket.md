# Ticket

![Ticket](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Ticket.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404015820)

_"Used to activate and purchase special things!"_

**Tickets** are a form of currency added in the 2018-04-27 update. They can be used to purchase various items within the game.

## Ways to Obtain

*   **Badges (Ticket Shop):** Players can obtain Tickets as rewards for acquiring badges through the Ticket Shop:
    *   Cadet Badges: 5 Tickets
    *   Hotshot Badges: 10 Tickets
    *   Ace Badges: 15 Tickets
    *   Master Badges: 25 Tickets
    *   Grandmaster Badges: 40 Tickets
    *(Total potential tickets from badges is 2,375. This excludes the currently impossible Sticker Stack Grandmaster Badge.)*

*   **Ticket Shop (Honey Purchase):** Players can buy Tickets from the Ticket Shop using Honey, located outside the Mountain Top Shop. The price increases with every Ticket purchased.
*   **Quests:** Certain bears grant Tickets as rewards for completing specific quests.
*   **Codes:** Redeeming valid codes grants Tickets:
    *   38217, Bopmaster, Crawlers, Roof, Connoisseur: 5 Tickets each.
    *   Wax: 5 Tickets + other items.
*   **Robux Shop Purchases:** Tickets can be bought using Robux in several bundles:
    *   1 Ticket for 10 Robux (10 Robux per Ticket).
    *   100 Tickets for 400 Robux (4 Robux per Ticket).
    *   510 Tickets for 1,700 Robux (~3.33 Robux per Ticket).
    *   1,800 Tickets for 4,500 Robux (~2.5 Robux per Ticket).
*   **Wealth Clock:** Interacting with the Wealth Clock grants Tickets every hour. The reward increases incrementally each hour, capping at 5 tickets (1st interaction = 1 ticket; 5th interaction and onwards = 5 tickets).
*   **Memory Matches:** Tickets can spawn as a potential match from Normal, Mega, Night, or Extreme Memory Matches.
*   **Stickers:** Clicking on any hidden Sticker grants 1 Ticket each (excluding seeker-stickers). Adding certain Stickers to the Sticker Stack also grants Tickets.
*   **Leaderboards:** Being in the top 100 of any daily leaderboard (ranging from 25, 50, or 100) rewards a varying amount of Tickets depending on the specific leaderboard.
*   **Ticket Voucher Redemption:** Redeeming a Ticket Voucher grants 100 Tickets.
*   **Digital Bee Bonus:** Receiving 1,000 Tickets if you already own a Digital Bee after purchasing the Robo Bear's Shop Digital Bee deal.

### Drops and Rewards

Tickets can be obtained from various in-game drops and rewards:

*   **Planters:** The number of Tickets dropped depends on the planter type. Ticket Planters always drop 10+ Tickets when harvested at full growth.
*   **Mythic Meteors:** Common drop from Mythic Meteor Showers.
*   **Stockings:** Drop from Stockings.
*   **Mobs:** There is a chance of getting a Ticket as a reward for defeating mobs; the probability depends on the mob type and player Loot Luck.
    *   Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, King Beetle: Guaranteed to grant Tickets upon defeat.
    *   Wild Windy Bee: Yields a limited amount of Tickets per day.
    *   Commando Chick: Always drops 1 Ticket upon capture. Capturing it for the 25th time rewards 100 Tickets.
    *   Snowbear: Drops an amount of Tickets equal to its level upon defeat.
*   **Robo Party Cake:** Completing a Robo Party rewards an amount of Tickets equal to the party's rank.
*   **Cub Buddy Gift:** Received through a Cub Buddy gift.
*   **Puffshrooms:** Drop Tickets (rarer Puffshrooms yield more).
*   **Honeystorms:** Guaranteed to spawn Ticket tokens.
*   **Retro Swarm Challenge:** Awarded as a reward for completing this challenge.
*   **Festive Gifts:** Rare drop from Festive Gifts.
*   **Sprout Rewards:** As a Sprout reward (besides Moon and Debug variants), the rarer the sprout, the more Tickets it yields; rare sprouts guarantee Tickets.
*   **Sparkles:** May yield Tickets.
*   **Flowers:** Very rarely may generate 1 Ticket token (base probability is around 1/1000 or 0.1%, increasing with Loot Luck and Ticket Chance).

### Token Locations

Tickets can be found in specific locations:

*   On top of the biggest pineapple in the Pineapple Patch: Grants 3 Tickets.
*   At the back of the cave where the spider comes from: Grants 1 Ticket.
*   In the back of Red HQ, near the ceiling: Requires enhanced movement abilities (e.g., certain hats/boots or Bear Morph) to reach.
*   Next to the Glider inside the Mountain Top Shop.
*   On a platform in the Cloud Obby, high above the Pine Tree Forest and Pumpkin Patch: Grants 5 Tickets.
*   On a small ledge between the Lion Bee Gate and the Brave Bee Gate: Grants 5 Tickets (requires specific movement/slingshot usage).
*   Behind the red shoulder guard of the Bear Gate (inside the 30 Bee Area): Grants 10 Tickets.
*   On the awning over Mother Bear's area (accessible from the Honey Bee Gate ramp): Grants 1 Ticket.
*   On top of the Ant Gate: Grants 5 Tickets.
*   Behind the Honey Dispenser in the Lava Obby: Grants 3 Tickets.
*   Underneath the Basic Bee Gate ramp (where Honey Bee used to be): Grants 3 Tickets.
*   In the Blue Maze, turn left and then take the first right: Grants 5 Tickets.
*   Inside the tunnel behind the Coconut Dispenser in the Coconut Cave: Grants 5 Tickets.

## Uses

Tickets are utilized across various game mechanics:

**Purchases:**
*   Royal Jelly Dispenser: Buying Royal Jelly costs 6 Tickets (3-second cooldown).
*   Gumdrop Shop: Buying Gumdrops costs 1 Ticket per 3 Gumdrops.
*   Stinger Shop: Buying Stingers costs 10 Tickets per Stinger.
*   Magic Bean Shop: Buying Magic Beans costs 20 Tickets per Magic Bean.

**Mechanic Costs:**
*   Instant Converter: Requires 1 Ticket to use.
*   Ant Pass Dispenser: Requires 10 Tickets to use.
*   Stick Bug Challenge: Requires 50 Tickets to start a new challenge when the free one is used.
*   Blender: Tickets can be used to speed up materials currently being made in the Blender.
*   Nectar Pot: Requires 5 Tickets to store Nectar.
*   Robo Pass Dispenser: Requires 50 Tickets to use.

**Ticket Tent Items:**
The Ticket Tent offers various items for purchase:
*   Silver Egg: 50 Tickets
*   Gold Egg: 100 Tickets
*   Diamond Egg: 400 Tickets
*   Mythic Egg: 2,500 Tickets
*   Cobalt Bee Egg / Crimson Bee Egg (Basic Eggs): 250 Tickets each (one-time purchase).
*   Event Bees: 500 Tickets each (one-time purchase).
*   Star Treat: 1,000 Tickets.

**Storage Upgrades:**
*   Beequip Storage: Varying amounts of Tickets can be used to increase storage capacity. A total of 123,500 Tickets is required to max out Beequips.
*   Sticker Storage: Varying amounts of Tickets (starting at 20 and capping at 1,000 per upgrade) can be used to increase sticker storage capacity. A total of 42,420 Tickets is required to max out Stickers.

**Dapper Bear's Shop:**
Tickets, along with Honey, are used to purchase Beequips in Dapper Bear's Shop.

## Trivia

*   The Ticket bears the face of a Basic Bee.
*   Currently, the most expensive single purchase using Tickets is the final slot upgrade for Beequip Storage, costing 20,000 Tickets (increasing storage from 95 to 100).
*   Tickets and Honey were used to purchase Market Boosts from the Boost Market.
*   A player needs a total of 179,420 Tickets to max out Sticker and Beequip storages, buy all event bees from the Ticket Tent, and gift them with Star Treats.
