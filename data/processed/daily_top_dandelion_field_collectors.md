# Daily Top Dandelion Field Collectors

The **Daily Top Dandelion Field Collectors** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in the game, accessible via the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page. This leaderboard tracks the amount of pollen players collect specifically within the Dandelion Field on a given day.

## Mechanics and Reset
*   **Display:** The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view results all the way to the 100th place.
*   **Reset Time:** The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes
Players are awarded items based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Awarded a [Dandelion Field Stamp Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker").
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 25 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
