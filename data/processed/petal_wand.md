# Petal Wand

The **Petal Wand** is a tool added in the 2019-09-28 update that can be crafted at the Petal Shop. It emits a yellow particle effect at the top of the tool, similar to the Petal Belt.

## Overview & Stats
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | 1 Spirit Petal, 10 Star Jellies, 25 Glitter, 75 Enzymes, 1,500,000,000 Honey |
| **Shop** | Petal Shop |
| **Range** | 37 Flowers |
| **Collecting Pattern** | Flower |
| **Collecting Time** | 0.7 seconds |
| **Base Collection** | 6 (per swing) |

**Collection Breakdown:**

The wand collects pollen from various flower types:

| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 12 | 24 | 36 | 48 | 60 |
| Red | 12 | 24 | 36 | 48 | 60 |
| Blue | 12 | 24 | 36 | 48 | 60 |

*Special Multiplier: 100% Pollen Multiplier.*

The Petal Wand has an average base pollen collection rate of 634.29 pollen per second.

## Mechanics
**Pollen Collection:** The wand collects 6 pollen from 37 patches in 0.7 seconds, boosting the collected amount by 100%.

**Petal Shuriken:** Every third swing, the Petal Wand fires a Petal Shuriken. This shuriken causes bees to instantly convert pollen if it touches their hitbox.
*   **Conversion Effect:** The shuriken instantly converts $10,000 + 6\%$ of the bee's conversion amount into honey.
*   **Field Boost Bonus:** An additional $6\%$ is added for every Field Boost stack in the current field.
*   **Secondary Effects:** Shurikens can also pop bubbles and fuzz bombs.

**Update Changes (2026-01-16):** In this update, shurikens were modified to cause blooms that were hit to spawn a petal. These affected blooms take damage ($20\%$ remaining HP + $5\%$ max HP) and spawn a random petal from the impact point.

## Crafting Ingredients
To craft the Petal Wand, you will need:
*   1 Spirit Petal
*   10 Star Jellies
*   25 Glitter
*   75 Enzymes
*   1,500,000,000 Honey

## Trivia
*   **Alternative Crafting:** If the ingredients were to be crafted using the Blender (excluding Spirit Petals), it would require 1,750 Royal Jellies, 1,375 Moon Charms, 55 Magic Beans, and 3,750 Pineapples. Buying the required royal jelly for this alternative recipe would cost 1.75 billion honey.
*   **Usage:** The Petal Wand can launch its shurikens even when the player is not in a field, allowing players to spend less time at the hive converting pollen.
*   **Exclusivity:** It is the only tool that can be held by a Spirit Bear (though Onett also holds a tool, he is not a bear). The version held by the Spirit Bear is significantly larger than a player's.
*   **Sound Effect:** After the shuriken contacts one of the player's bees, the associated sound effect changes and increases in pitch with every consecutive bee hit.
