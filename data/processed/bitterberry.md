**Bitterberries** are a type of [Treat](/wiki/Treats "Treats") that provide two main effects when fed to a [Bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees"):
1. Increases the bee's [Bond](/wiki/Bond "Bond") by 100.
2. Has a base chance of 1/1000 to cause a [Mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation").

This mutation chance is boosted significantly to 1/100 if the bee is [Radioactive](/wiki/Radiation "Radiation"). Note that Bitterberries are not considered a favorite treat by any bee.

## Acquisition Methods

Bitterberries can be obtained through various means:

**Codes & Rewards:**
*   Redeeming valid codes, such as `BeesBuzz123` (rewards 5 **Bitterberries**) or `15MMembers` (rewards 15 **Bitterberries**).
*   As a stack reward for adding [Squashed Head Bear Stickers](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker") to the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack "Sticker Stack"), which rewards 25 **Bitterberries**.

**World Drops & Locations:**
*   Finding 10 **Bitterberries** under the [Beesmas Tree](/wiki/Beesmas_Tree "Beesmas Tree").
*   Harvesting [Leaves](/wiki/Leaves "Leaves") in several fields, including the [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"), [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field "Spider Field"), [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"), [Cactus Field](/wiki/Cactus_Field "Cactus Field"), [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field "Rose Field"), [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field"), and [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch "Pepper Patch").
*   As a guaranteed drop from the following creatures: [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick"), [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick"), and the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab").
*   As drops from various mobs, including [Ladybugs](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug"), [Spiders](/wiki/Spider "Spider"), [Scorpions](/wiki/Scorpion "Scorpion"), [Rage Aphids](/wiki/Aphid#Rage_Aphid "Aphid"), [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), and [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle").
*   As a gift from a [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy").
*   Commonly dropped by [Mythic Meteors](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower").
*   Dropped by the Debug [Sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout").
*   Uncommonly dropped by [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom").

**Planter Drops:**
Bitterberries can be obtained from the [Pesticide Planter](/wiki/Pesticide_Planter "Pesticide Planter") and [The Planter Of Plenty](/wiki/The_Planter_Of_Plenty "The Planter Of Plenty"). The Pesticide Planter has a chance to drop a bonus 100 **Bitterberries** when harvested at full growth in the Spider Field, Mountain Top Field, or Pepper Patch.

## Outdated and Legacy Methods

These methods are no longer active or relevant:
*   Completing [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear")'s "A Time for Sharing" and "Bee Cub Workshop 1" quests.
*   As a drop from [Hostage Chick](/wiki/Chicks#Hostage_Chick "Chicks").
*   Redeeming certain expired codes (e.g., `5mMembers`, `FourYearFiesta`, `1MLikes`, `Cubly`).
*   Purchasing from [Bee Bear's Catalog](/wiki/Bee_Bear%27s_Catalog "Bee Bear's Catalog") via the Tempting Treat Pack (rewards 100 bitterberries).
*   Digging into the [Beesmas Feast](/wiki/Beesmas_Feast "Beesmas Feast").
