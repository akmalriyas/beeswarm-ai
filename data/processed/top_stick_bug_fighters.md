The **Global Top Stick Bug Fighters** leaderboard is one of the 64 available leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. It tracks the top 100 scores achieved by fighting [Stick Bugs] in the [Stick Bug Challenge]. While it displays only 10 positions at a time, players can scroll down to view all entries up to the 100th place.

When a player stands on the white pad located in front of the leaderboard, they can see their personal score displayed as "Your highscore: [ScoreVariable]", where `[ScoreVariable]` is the score obtained from the Stick Bug Challenge.

### History and Resets
The Top Stick Bug Fighters leaderboard has undergone several resets due to game glitches or exploits:
*   It was initially reset on 2019-09-27 after fixes were implemented for fight glitches.
*   It was later reset again on 2022-12-26.
*   Most recently, it was reset on 2024-01-12 due to the exploitation of attack buffs in Robo Bear's Challenge to drastically increase damage output.

### Location
The leaderboard is situated right behind the Stick Bug area, positioned on top of the largest mushroom in the [Mushroom Field].

**Methods to Access:**
*   Using a [Red], [Blue], or [Yellow Cannon] paired with either a [Parachute] or [Glider].
*   Parachuting or gliding from a tall location, such as near the [Lion Bee Gate].
*   Completing the [Mushroom Field Obby] and jumping/gliding to the tallest mushroom.

### Achievements
*   **First 100 Million:** The player `zlib` was the first to reach a score of 100 million or more.
*   **First 1 Billion:** The player `Rara0w0x3` was the first to reach a score of 1 billion or more.

### Trivia
*   This leaderboard, along with the [Highest Snowbear Level] and [Fastest Crab Slayers] leaderboards, is one of only three leaderboards that ranks specific "[bosses]".
*   Following the reset on 2024-01-12, the score displayed when stepping on the podium reflects your score *before* the reset, but this historical score does not appear in the leaderboard rankings.
