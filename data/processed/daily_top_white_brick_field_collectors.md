# Daily Top White Brick Field Collectors

The **Daily Top White Brick Field Collectors** was one of the game's former leaderboards. This leaderboard tracked how much pollen players collected in the White Brick Field during a single day. It reset every day at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

If a player reached the top 100 on this leaderboard by the end of the day, they were awarded 25 Tickets.

## Trivia

*   This leaderboard, along with other Brick Field collector leaderboards and the Daily Top Ant Field Collectors, was one of the few leaderboards that did not appear anywhere in the game interface.
*   It was tied for the fourth leaderboard to be removed from the game, alongside Most Commando Captures, Highest Snowbear Level, and Highest Robo Party Cake Rank.
