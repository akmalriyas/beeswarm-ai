# Hive

The **Hive** serves as the player's resting place when their energy runs out, allowing them to convert collected pollen into [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), or it is where bees return upon player death.

## Claiming and Functionality

Upon joining a server, players must first claim a hive. The claiming process varies by platform:
*   **PC/Laptop:** Follow the red arrow and press "E".
*   **Xbox:** Press "X".
*   **Playstation:** Press "Square".
*   **Mobile (Tablet, iOS, Android):** Tap the "claim hive" button.

Players can hatch [Eggs](/wiki/Egg "Egg") and utilize [Royal Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly "Royal Jelly") by placing them into available hive slots.

A server contains a maximum of six hives. A basic hive provides 25 bee slots. Players can expand their capacity by purchasing additional [Hive Slots](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot") at the [Mountain Top Shop](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Shop "Mountain Top Shop"). While the theoretical maximum is 50 bees, achieving this requires a significant investment in hive slots.

## Aesthetics and Customization

Each bee slot features the icon of the corresponding bee, colored according to its species. The background color of the slot indicates the bee's rarity:
*   **Bronze:** Common
*   **White:** Rare
*   **Bright Yellow:** Epic
*   **Aqua Blue:** Legendary
*   **Light Purple:** Mythic
*   **Green:** Event

Additional visual indicators include:
*   A thin yellow glowing line traces the border of a slot if the bee is [Gifted](/wiki/Gifted_Bee "Gifted Bee").
*   If a bee has undergone a [Mutation](/wiki/Mutation "Mutation"), its [Bond level](/wiki/Bond "Bond") will change color to reflect the mutation type.

Players can customize their hive by applying various [Stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker"). They can also change the overall appearance of the hive using Hive Skin Stickers, and further personalize it by placing Cub Buddy Skin Stickers on the [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy#Skins "Cub Buddy").

## Notable Features

On top of the sixth hive is a statue of Onett, the game's creator. This statue has been known to rarely fall off and land in the rightmost hive due to glitches.

## Trivia

*   **Death Mechanics:** When a player dies, all their bees return to the hive to rest but do not recover energy. This mechanic prevents players from abusing resets to maximize bee energy. (A previous bug allowing double-reset recovery has been patched.)
*   **Onett's Pollen:** The statue of Onett on the top of the sixth hive contains 38,217 pollen in his [Port-O-Hive](/wiki/Port-O-Hive "Port-O-Hive"), which references a specific game code that rewards five [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
*   **Historical Updates:** Prior to the [2018-11-25 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-11-25 "Updates"), there were only five hives available in the game.
*   **Visual Changes:** In earlier versions of Bee Swarm Simulator, bee faces on hive slots were larger and would sparkle based on rarity (excluding Basic Bees).
*   **Glitches:** The [2018-07-11 update](/wiki/Updates#2018-07-11 "Updates") introduced a glitch where viewing another hive could display incorrect bee rarities. This has been mostly patched.
*   **Wall Glitch (2019):** Following the [2019-04-05 update](/wiki/Updates#2019-04-05 "Updates"), a blue wall next to the leftmost hive was not solid, allowing players to bypass gates without meeting requirements. This was fixed in a subsequent update and was noted by Onett as an oversight during development testing.
*   **Interaction:** Players can click on their bee's hive slot from any distance, provided they click directly on the bee face.
*   **Skin Recognition:** A user's name will display in a unique font on a hive if that player has equipped the Wavy Festive Hive Skin, Wavy Doodle Hive Skin, or Icy Crowned Hive Skin.
