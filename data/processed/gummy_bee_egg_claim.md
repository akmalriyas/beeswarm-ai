# Gummy Bee Egg Claim

The **Gummy Bee Egg Claim** is a platform located beyond the Ant Gate, situated on a ledge near the Hive and the Ant Challenge area. It can be accessed by passing through the gap behind the Top Ant Exterminators Leaderboard, walking across the top of the Ant Gate, or traversing the hives to reach the claim.

## Mechanics

The player must combine 2,500 Gumdrops at this location to receive a Gummy Bee Egg (a Basic Egg), which can only be claimed once per player.

### Claiming the Egg
When standing on the white platform:
*   If the player has not yet claimed an egg, a prompt appears stating: "Combine 2500 Gumdrops into a Gummy Bee."
*   If the player already owns one, the message displayed is: "You Already Own a Gummy Bee."

Upon successfully forming a Gummy Bee Egg, two things occur:
1.  A slight remix of the music track `gbtune1` (the theme played around the claim and in Gummy Bear's Lair) plays throughout the map.
2.  A server-wide announcement is made: 🎉 {Username} has formed a Gummy Bee 🎉

### Teleporting to Gummy Bear's Lair
The Gummy Bee model also serves as a portal to Gummy Bear's Lair, but access depends on player badges:

*   **Without Goo Hotshot Badge:** Standing on the model prompts the message: "Only Goo Hotshots can hear Gummy Bee..." Using gumdrops while this message is displayed will not teleport the player.
*   **With Goo Hotshot Badge:** Standing on the model prompts: "The Gummy Bee wants Gumdrops." Using Gumdrops while standing on the model with the Goo Hotshot badge teleports the player to Gummy Bear's Lair. A Gummy Bee is not required for this teleportation.

## Trivia
*   This claim is one of two egg claims in the map; the other is the Vicious Bee Egg Claim for the Vicious Bee.
*   During the Beesmas events following the first one in 2018, several gumdrop-themed presents were placed beyond an obstacle course next to this claim.
*   At night time, both the Gummy Bee model's face and the teal outline of the pad glow.
*   An alternative route to reach the platform involves jumping onto an Ant Pass Dispenser, hopping onto the rock by the Ant Gate, crossing the Ant Gate, and finally landing on the pad.
*   This is the only bee claim that features a soundtrack different from the surrounding area's `gbtune1`.
*   When Gummy Bee was available in the Ticket Tent, the giant model used here was located atop the Ticket Tent (to the right of Gifted and Photon Bee), where the Festive Bee is now. It still functioned as a teleportation point to Gummy Bear's Lair if gumdrops were used on it.
*   The total cost to acquire all necessary Gumdrops for one Gummy Bee Egg is 834 Tickets.
