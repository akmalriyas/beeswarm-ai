# Beesmas Lights

Beesmas Lights are a collectible item that appears in fields after completing Science Bear's Beesmas quest. They function as falling objects, similar to Star Shower passive abilities.

## Mechanics and Functionality

*   **Appearance:** Beesmas Lights fall from the sky over any field, including the Ant Field and areas within the Retro Swarm Challenge.
*   **Falling Time:** They take 2 seconds to descend.
*   **Spawning Condition:** Beesmas Lights spawn after a player collects 25 Ability Tokens (subject to a 30-second cooldown), dropping five lights into the field.
*   **Collection Effect:** When caught, a Beesmas Light grants the Inspire buff and converts based on the player's Convert Total. If the light is not collected before it lands, it simply collects pollen instead.

### Incomplete Activation Dialogue
If a player approaches the switch for the Beesmas Lights while Science Bear's quest has not been completed, a text box will display: "This \_sic\_ Beesmas Lights aren't powering on..."

## Sticker Rewards
Catching a falling Beesmas Light has a 1 in 10,000 chance to reward a colored sticker (Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow), depending on the light's color.

*   Green Beesmas Light Sticker
*   Blue Beesmas Light Sticker
*   Red Beesmas Light Sticker
*   Yellow Beesmas Light Sticker

## Trivia

*   **Comparison to Star Shower:** Beesmas Lights share a similar function with the Star Shower passive ability, but they are more focused in their area of effect. This makes them an exclusive source for obtaining the Inspire buff without relying on Gifted Bees or redeeming specific codes.
*   **Historical Context:** Although introduced during the Beesmas 2020 event, the physical Beesmas Light decorations along the mountain were present during prior Beesmas events.
*   **Lingering Effects:** Following the removal of several Beesmas events, Beesmas Lights would occasionally continue to fall into fields for a few days after the official event conclusion.
*   **Developer Oversight:** The text displayed when standing under the light switch contains a grammatical error, reading "This Beesmas Lights" instead of "The Beesmas Lights." This is believed to be an oversight by Onett (Developer).
