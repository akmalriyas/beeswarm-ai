# Star Amulets

The **Star Amulet** is a type of amulet that can be purchased in five different tiers from machines located in the [Star Hall](/wiki/Star_Hall). These amulets grant buffs to the owner and always increase [capacity](/wiki/Capacity) as they advance through the tiers. Higher tier amulets provide more substantial and superior buffs. The Diamond and Supreme Star Amulets also possess additional passive abilities.

Each Star Amulet requires the user to have a specific number of discovered [gifted bee](/wiki/Gifted_bee) types. After generating an amulet, there is a 1-second cooldown before another can be generated.

The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the number of discovered gifted bee types until it reaches its maximum:
*   For non-Supreme Star Amulets, the player needs 45 types to max out quality.
*   For Supreme Star Amulet, the player needs 50 types. (Note: This is currently unattainable as there are only 46 total bee types in the game.)

When a Star Amulet is generated, a specific audio plays, with the sound pitch decreasing as the amulet tier increases.

## Requirements and Tiers

| Requirement(s) | Amulet Tier | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 [Gifted Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bee) types discovered | Bronze Star Amulet | 25M [honey](/wiki/Honey) |
| 10 [Gifted Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bee) types discovered | Silver Star Amulet | 50M [honey](/wiki/Honey) |
| 20 [Gifted Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bee) types discovered | Gold Star Amulet | 250M [honey](/wiki/Honey) |
| 30 [Gifted Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bee) types discovered | Diamond Star Amulet | 1B [honey](/wiki/Honey) |
| 40 [Gifted Bee](/wiki/Gifted_Bee) types discovered | Supreme Star Amulet | 10B [honey](/wiki/Honey) + Option to spend 500B honey for guaranteed two [Passive Abilities](/wiki/Passive_Abilities) |

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   The Supreme Star Amulet Generator replaced the original Diamond Star Amulet Generator.
*   Diamond and Supreme Star Amulets are the only amulets capable of having or always possessing passive abilities.
*   The Diamond Star Amulet can only grant two types of passive abilities: Guiding Star and Star Shower.
*   The Supreme Star Amulet is newer than the original Star Amulets, introduced in the 2020-06-06 update (while others were released in the 2018-07-11 update). The requirement for these amulets changed from requiring gifted bee types in the player's hive to simply discovering them.
*   The Supreme Star Amulet is unique as it can grant double passive abilities.
*   In a buff, if the player has between 490 billion and 500 billion honey, they can still buy the guaranteed double passive offer for only 490 billion.
*   The Diamond Star Amulet has a 1/1,000,000 chance of giving a Star Cub Skin.
*   **Passive Ability Details:**
    *   The summoned Gummy Star/Pop Star/Scorching Star rotates counterclockwise around the player.
    *   The background audio for these passives plays three times for Gummy and Scorching star, 1.5 times for Pop star, and seven times for Star Saw.
    *   If a player dies while Scorching Star, Pop Star, or Gummy Star is active, the star will continue to hover at the death location, maintaining its buffs even if it no longer follows the player model.
    *   When the Star Shower Passive is activated, the large indicator star has a slightly stretched top.
