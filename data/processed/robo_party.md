# Robo Party Cake

[![The Robo Party Cake from Beesmas 2022.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/05/Cake.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20230401193148)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/05/Cake.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20230401193148)

The **Robo Party Cake** is a Beesmas-exclusive machine introduced in the second part of Beesmas 2022, and it has been included in every subsequent Beesmas event. It becomes accessible after completing the Robo Bear's Party Cake quest and can initiate a special challenge every three hours.

## Appearance

Before completing Robo Bear's quest, the party cake appears as only three metal trusses stacked into a pyramid, topped with an unlit olive green candle. The number on this candle changes annually to reflect how many years the game has been active.

After the quest is finished, the "number" candle lights up with neon lights and features a blue outline. The cake layers are olive green but feature different colored frames: the bottom layer is blue, the middle layer is yellow, and the top layer is purple. Candles and sparklers are placed on the corners, and neon sprinkles decorate the front of each layer.

## Robo Party Event Mechanics

The **Robo Party** is a server-wide event where all participants must defeat the scattered Party Robots found across the fields. The party begins immediately after a player interacts with the cake and has a strict time limit of three minutes.

*   **Success:** If all Party Robots are defeated, all participants receive the Robo Party Blessing buff. The player who initiated the Robo Party is rewarded with items and gains the ability to summon a higher-ranked Robo Party in the future.
*   **Failure:** If not all party mobs are killed within the time limit, the initiating player's rank will be lowered.

Robo Parties can also be manually summoned by Onett (Developer).

If successful, the player who started the event receives an amount of Robo Party Blessing equal to the rank that was completed (e.g., completing Rank 5 rewards the player with 5 stacks of Robo Party Blessing).

### Mobs

The Robo Party summons party variants of the Robo Bear Challenge robots. Unlike standard Robo Bear Challenges, every enemy spawns in a field and remains there for the duration of the event. The rank determines the level, health, and quantity of the summoned robots. There are four types of robots:
*   Party Mechsquitoes
*   Party Cogmowers
*   Party Cogturrets (Spawns on Rank 5)
*   Party Mega Mechsquitoes (Spawns on Rank 15)

## Strategies for Robo Parties

*   **Stingers:** Using Stingers grants a x1.5 attack boost to your bees for 30 seconds, which is highly effective against high-level mobs like Party Cogturrets or Party Mega Mechsquitoes.
*   **Star Saw:** If the mob count is overwhelming, use Star Saw (if available). It circles around you for 30 seconds. While hitting flying targets (Party Mechsquitoes and Party Mega Mechsquitoes) can be difficult, it allows easy targeting of ground mobs like Party Cogmowers and Party Cogturrets while your bees are distracted by the flyers.
*   **Crowd Control:** Vicious Bee and Windy Bee offer excellent crowd control, especially since most mobs spawn in pairs or packs (excluding Mega Mechsquitoes). Digital Bee can stun multiple mobs simultaneously, allowing them to take extra damage via Mind Hack.
*   **Targeting Behavior:**
    *   Party Cogturrets always shoot straight at the direction they are facing, allowing players to avoid attacks by positioning themselves off-center.
    *   Party Cogmowers do not aim for the player, making them easy to sidestep.
    *   Party Mechsquitoes and Party Mega Mechsquitoes target the player's current location, not where the player is moving. Moving in circles can often cause these flying enemies to miss their attacks.
*   **Combat Positioning:** Standing near the Party Mobs allows your bees to lock on quickly and deal damage efficiently.
*   **Safety:** Always monitor your health during a Robo Party. It is easy to become overly focused on eliminating the robots and neglect incoming damage. If you die, look for fields containing Party Robots upon respawning until your bees are active again.

## Trivia

*   The number displayed on the Party Cake is determined by the game's nearest anniversary relative to its release date.
*   Party Cogturret is the only type of Party Robot that does not wear a party hat.
*   Only one Robo Party can be active in the server at any given time.
*   The Robo Party Blessing Icon bears a resemblance to Gifted Buoyant Bee's Surprise Party ability token, and the spawned Party Hat resembles the hats worn by most Party Mobs.
*   All Party Mobs behave similarly to Golden Cogmowers: they spawn in random or specific fields rather than swarming the player or spawning where the player is currently located.
