# Hydroponic Planter

The **Hydroponic Planter** is a reusable planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. It is purchasable in the Blue HQ and requires players to complete 150 Gifted Bucko Bee quests to unlock.

***
*Note: Some information regarding loot generation and probabilities has been derived from datamining, which may contain inaccuracies or assumptions.*
***

## Stats & Mechanics

| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reusable** | Yes | Permanent planter. |
| **Growth Time** | 12 hours | Base growth time. |
| **Pollen Capacity** | 10,000,000,000 Pollen | Maximum storage capacity. |
| **Pollens Multiplier** | +50% Blue Pollen / -50% Red Pollen | Modifies pollen yield upon harvest. |
| **Nectar Multiplier** | +40% Refreshing Nectar / +40% Comforting Nectar | Increases nectar quality when harvested. |
| **Growth Bonus** | Up to +50% | Achieved when planted in fields with blue flowers. |

## Crafting & Acquisition

The Hydroponic Planter can be crafted using the following ingredients:

*   750,000,000,000 Honey
*   75 Magic Beans
*   750 Blue Extracts
*   500 Soft Waxes
*   25 Caustic Waxes
*   1 Turpentine

## Harvest & Drops

When harvested, the planter grants bonus Blue Extract and Oil. Additionally, there is a chance of spawning a Puffshroom.

### Pollen Yield Modifiers (In Field)
*   **Blue Bees:** 33.3% more likely to sip Nectar.
*   **Red Bees:** 50% less likely to sip Nectar.

### Puffshroom Spawn Chance
A fully grown Hydroponic Planter has a **2/9 chance** of spawning a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 6-7, with the following probability distribution:

*   Common Puffshroom: ~72.59%
*   Rare Puffshroom: ~21.78%
*   Epic Puffshroom: ~5.44%
*   Legendary Puffshroom: ~0.18%
*   Mythic Puffshroom: ~0.01%

### Token Drops (When Claimed)
A fully grown planter yields up to 35 tokens worth of items. Guaranteed drops include:
*   1 Blue Extract
*   1 Oil
*   3 Tickets

Other tokens are picked randomly from a non-exhaustive list, including Bonus, Other, and Special rewards.

**Specific Drop Notes:**
*   Blue Extracts drop extra when harvested in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, or Stump Field.
*   Oil drops extra when harvested at the Sunflower Field three times in a row (1 month cooldown).

## Trivia & Lore

*   The Hydroponic Planter is the blue counterpart to the Heat-Treated Planter; its white version is the Petal Planter.
*   It is one of five reusable planters available outside of Dapper Bear's Shop, alongside the Red Clay Planter, Blue Clay Planter, Petal Planter, and Heat-Treated Planter.
*   The Hydroponic Planter does not require soil as its ground texture.
