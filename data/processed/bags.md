# Bags

Bags are accessories that can be purchased in various shops across the map. Their primary function is to store pollen gathered by the player until the bag is full. Once full, the player must visit their hive to convert the pollen into honey using methods such as a Coconut, an Instant Converter, a Micro-Converter, or a Honeysuckle (which only converts some). Alternatively, they can admire Black Bear's Honey Wreath during Beesmas events.

When a bag is full, the following message appears: "Pollen container full. Go to your hive to make honey." Players can use an Instant Converter to instantly gain all their honey (costing 1 ticket) or utilize a micro-converter.

Certain bags offer bonuses beyond simple capacity increases. These boosts include Instant Conversion, improved Convert Rate, Bee Attack power, and various Passive Abilities (currently only available from the Coconut Canister). Note that other accessories, such as belt bags, also increase overall capacity. The Pouch bag is automatically provided to all new players.

There are currently 12 distinct bags in the game.
