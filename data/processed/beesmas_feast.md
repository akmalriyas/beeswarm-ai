# Beesmas Feast

![Beesmas Feast2](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/14/Beesmas_Feast2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20260131043310) Beesmas Feast

The Beesmas Feast is a special machine available after completing Polar Bear's Beesmas quest. It provides valuable rewards to the player and their bees during the holiday event.

## Information

### Usage
To use the Beesmas Feast, players must possess Bond, Bitterberries, and various Treats.

### Requirements
*   Completion of Polar Bear's Beesmas quest.

### Cooldown
90 minutes.

### Location
The machine is located behind Polar Bear.

### Mechanics
The **Beesmas Feast** is a Beesmas-exclusive machine unlocked upon completing the required quest from Polar Bear. It can be used every 90 minutes. When utilized, it grants Bond for all of the player's bees and a variety of Treats. The amount of bond and rewards received are directly affected by the player's current level of Polar Power (a buff).

Upon interacting with the feast, two notifications appear:
*   🍴 Serving up some Beesmas Dinner 🍴
*   🍴 More Polar Power = Larger Servings! 🍴

If a player attempts to access the feast without completing the prerequisite quest, they will receive the message: "An empty plate... where’s the Beesmas Feast, Polar Bear?"

## Rewards

| Reward | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Bond (per Bee) | $100 + \lfloor \frac{\text{PolarPower}^{1.5}}{10} \rfloor \cdot 10$ | The bond amount increases with Polar Power. |
| Treats (total) | $2\cdot \left\lfloor \frac {25+\left\lfloor {\text{PolarPower}}^{1.5}+0.5\right\rfloor }{2}}+0.5\right\rfloor$ | Total treats received. |
| Bitterberries (total) | $2\cdot \left\lfloor \frac {10+\left\lfloor {\text{PolarPower}}^{0.4}+0.5\right\rfloor }{2}}+0.5\right\rfloor$ | Total bitterberries received. |
| Specific Treat | $2\cdot \left\lfloor 15+{\frac {\left\lfloor {\text{PolarPower}}^{0.6}+0.5\right\rfloor }{2}}+0.5\right\rfloor$ | One of the following treats is granted: Strawberries, Pineapples, Blueberries, or Sunflower Seeds. |

## Trivia
*   The feast displays a variety of foods, including Bitterberries, Treats, a floret of broccoli, a carrot, and a stew containing chives, onions, corn, peas, and meat.
*   Polar Bear's feast design is reminiscent of "Big Ol' Turkey Legs," a running gag within the ThnxCya community. Polar Bear referenced this in his dialogue after the Beesmas 2020 quest completion with the phrase, "_A big ol' turkey and treat..._".
*   Onett stated on Discord that the Beesmas Feast was modeled using the Hostage Chicks from the Egg Hunt 2020 event.[1]
*   The eating sound effect is a cut-out section of the song "Nom Nom Nom Nom Nom Nom Nom," created by Parry Grip.
*   Initially, the Beesmas Feast dropped all four types of treats, but this was changed in Beesmas 2022.
*   It was first introduced during the Beesmas 2020 update and has been included in every subsequent Beesmas event.
