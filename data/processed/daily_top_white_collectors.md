# Daily Top White Pollen Collectors

The **Daily Top White Pollen Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. This leaderboard tracks the amount of white pollen players have collected on a given day.

## Mechanics and Viewing
*   The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to 100th place.
*   It resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.
*   To check your progress, players must step on the white pad located in front of the board, which will display the message: "You've collected 'X' White Pollen today."

## Location
This leaderboard is found inside the **Petal Shop**, situated to the right of the All-Time Top White Collectors Leaderboard.

## Prizes
Players are awarded rewards based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 10:** Players who reach the top 10 receive a **Basic White Hive Skin**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 receive 25 [Tickets].
