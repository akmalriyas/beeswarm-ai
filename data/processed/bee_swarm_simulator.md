# Bee Swarm Simulator

Bee Swarm Simulator is an online multiplayer game created in Roblox by Onett. The core objective of the game is to hatch bees to form a swarm, collect pollen, and convert that pollen into honey.

The game was initially in development for approximately three months before being released to the public on March 23, 2018.

> **Note:** This wiki page serves as an overview of the game; please consult other pages for detailed information on specific mechanics or items.

## Gameplay Mechanics

New players begin with a Tutorial—an optional 36-page slideshow accessible via the "?" button in the top right corner of the screen. Players must then claim one of six available hives by pressing 'E' or clicking the large blue button near their hive.

Upon starting, new players automatically receive a Basic Egg, a Pouch, and a Scooper. Using the Scooper allows players to collect pollen from various Fields. This collected pollen is stored in the player's bag, where the bees can convert it into honey while the player remains at their hive.

The resulting honey can be used to purchase new Tools, Accessories, Eggs, and other Items necessary for progression.

Players can also engage with NPCs to receive Quests. These quests primarily focus on collecting pollen or defeating Mobs, but may include more advanced objectives such as gathering Ability Tokens or Goo.

## Private Servers

Private servers cost 150 Robux per month. Owners of private servers have access to the following administrative commands:

*   `/kick PlayerName`: Kicks the specified player. *Note: `PlayerName` must be their true Roblox username, not their Display Name.*
*   `/ban PlayerName`: Kicks the named player and prevents them from rejoining until the ban is lifted or the server is closed.
*   `/unban PlayerName`: Removes the named player from the ban list.

If a private server becomes inactive due to non-payment, banned players will be automatically unbanned when the server reactivates.

## Game Milestones and Statistics

### Game Statistics
Bee Swarm Simulator currently boasts over 4 Billion visits, more than 6.35 Million favorites, over 2.8 Million likes, and a 95% rating. It is notable for being the nineteenth game on Roblox to reach 1 billion visits and ranks as the third most visited simulator on the platform.
