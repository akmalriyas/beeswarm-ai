# Autumn Sunhat

The **Autumn Sunhat** is a Level 16 Beequip with an equip limit of one and no color requirement. It can be equipped by Hasty, Bumble, Looker, Exhausted, Diamond, and Puppy Bees.

*Description:* "A lovely straw hat made from 100% fall-sourced materials."
*Affiliation:* Beesmas

## Ways to Obtain

The Autumn Sunhat can be obtained through several methods:

*   Purchasing from Dapper Bear's Beequip Shop.
*   As a reward from the Stick Bug Challenge.
*   As a very rare drop from Petal Planter or Tacky Planter.
