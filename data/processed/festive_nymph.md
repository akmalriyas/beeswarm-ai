# Festive Nymph

*Note: This mob is distinct from the standard Stick Nymph.*

The **Festive Nymph** is a variant of the [Stick Nymph](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Stick_Nymph) that can be summoned by the [Stick Bug](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Stick_Bug) during the [Stick Bug Challenge](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge).

Festive Nymphs are only available after completing Stick Bug's Beesmas quest (specifically, "Stick Bug's Nymph Hats") during the 2020 and subsequent Beesmas events. While other players can attack and collect rewards from them, the player who initiates the challenge must complete the prerequisite quest first for Festive Nymphs to appear.

Like their regular counterparts, Festive Nymphs follow the nearest player and deal damage upon contact. Despite having relatively low base stats, they are typically summoned in large groups alongside Stick Nymphs, often outnumbering players.

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   **Movement:** Like Stick Nymphs, Festive Nymphs can follow players out of the field they were in. They can pass through or be obstructed by certain objects, allowing for potential exploitation to deal damage from afar (e.g., entering the King Beetle's Lair).
*   **Health Depletion:** Similar to Mondo Chick and Stick Nymphs, Festive Nymphs lose health every second. Their health depletion rate increases as their level rises. Unlike Mondo Chick, they can be defeated naturally, and they will drop rewards regardless of whether they were attacked or not.
*   **Summoning Status:** Festive Nymphs and Stick Nymphs are unique among mobs because they are the only two that can be summoned by another mob (the Stick Bug). They cannot spawn naturally in a field.
*   **Combat Limitations:** Festive Nymphs are unable to jump, meaning players positioned above their hitbox cannot damage them.
*   **Location:** The Stick Bug Challenge featuring Festive Nymphs has also been held in Onett.
