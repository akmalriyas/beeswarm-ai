# Presents (Beesmas 2021)

*Note: This event content has been removed from the game.*

Presents were distributed during Beesmas 2021 and functioned similarly to previous years' events. They could be given to new NPCs, such as Dapper Bear. Presents were the primary method for obtaining Ornaments from these NPCs and were necessary to complete quests for Bee Bear.

## Acquisition Methods

*   **Bee Bear Quests:** Multiple quests from Bee Bear awarded a total of 12 Presents, along with other rewards.
*   **Bee Bear’s Catalog:** The catalog sold 1 Present for 250 Snowflakes and 10 Gingerbread Bears.
*   **Onett Hive Claim:** Onett gave 1 Present (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event period.
*   **Galentine Shrine Donation:** Donating Strawberries to the Galentine Shrine had a chance to give 1 Present, along with other rewards.
