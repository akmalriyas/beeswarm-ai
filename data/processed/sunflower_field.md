# Sunflower Field

The Sunflower Field is one of the five starting fields available to players when beginning the game. It contains no protective mobs.

## Field Statistics

| Statistic | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Field Color** | White | |
| **Nectar Type** | Satisfying Nectar | Grants satisfying nectar when a planter is used here. |
| **Dimensions** | 20 × 33 flowers | Total area size. |
| **Total Flower Count** | 660 | |

## Bee Preferences

The Sunflower Field attracts specific types of bees:

*   **Likes:** Basic Bee, Baby Bee, Hasty Bee, Exhausted Bee.
*   **Dislikes:** Looker Bee.

## Field Mechanics and Boosts

### Event Triggers
*   **Sparkles:** Randomly, the large sunflower behind the Daily Top Honeymakers may display a face and spit sparkles around the field.
*   **Stick Bug Challenge:** The Stick Bug first spawns in this field when starting a challenge, and may jump into it during a round if he is not already present.

### Boosting Pollen Yield
The pollen yield of the Sunflower Field can be significantly increased using various methods:

*   **Field Booster:** Using a Field Booster grants +300% Sunflower Field pollen for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Adds 100% Sunflower Field pollen onto the current boost (if not maxed).
*   **Dice:** Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice can add +100% Sunflower Field pollen (if not maxed).
*   **Field Winds:** Obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes. Each stack provides +5% instant conversion and +25% pollen for a base of 20% instant conversion. Stacks are limited to 15 times.
*   **Shell Amulet:** If equipped with a Sunflower Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +27%, depending on amulet rarity.
*   **Beequip Bonuses:**
    *   Active Autumn Sunhat: Provides a Sunflower Field Pollen Hive Bonus (+18% to +24% base, up to +44% with Waxing).
    *   Active Lei: Provides a Sunflower Field Pollen Hive Bonus (+3% to +5% base, not guaranteed, up to +25% with Waxing).

### Outdated Boost Methods (Historical Notes)
Historically, players could obtain boosts through specific methods that are now outdated or less common:

*   **NPC Gifts:** Black Bear previously granted x5 and later x3 Sunflower Field Boosts during Beesmas events. Onett also offered a x1 boost.
*   **Market Purchases:** Purchasing a Sunflower Field Market Boost from the Boost Market provided x1.5 Field Capacity, x1.25 Pollen, x1.05 general pollen, and x1.25 Convert Rate at Hive.
*   **Expired Codes:** Various codes (e.g., `100mVisits`, `Reboot`, `FuzzyFarewell`) historically granted specific Sunflower Field Boosts or Winds.

## Trivia

*   This is the only field where sunflower seeds spawn naturally without requiring sprouts.
*   It was the second-largest field in the game, after Clover Field. Following updates (2018-07-11), its size was reduced to accommodate Mother Bear and the Treat Shop.
*   Along with Pineapple Patch and Pumpkin Patch, this is one of only three fields where an object can come to life and shoot sparkles.
*   The fence bordering the Sunflower Field and the Hive was temporarily removed during Beesmas Winter 2024 but was restored in a subsequent update (2025-06-30).
