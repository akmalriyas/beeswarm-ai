**Color:** Blue
**Limit:** 2
"Only the blue bees are cool enough to don this comfy cap."

## Acquisition Methods
The Beret can be obtained in several ways:
*   A small chance of dropping from the Blue Clay Planter and The Planter Of Plenty.
*   Purchasing it from Dapper Bear's Shop.
*   Completing Gifted Bucko Bee's 2025 Beesmas quest.
*   Giving Dapper Bear a Present during Beesmas 2025.
