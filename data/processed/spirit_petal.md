# Spirit Petal

[![Spirit Petal](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ab/Spirit_Petal.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404045242)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ab/Spirit_Petal.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404045242)

_"A delicate flower petal used in rituals."_

**Cooldown:** N/A

*Note: This item is not to be confused with petals from Blooms.*

The **Spirit Petal** is a special crafting material. It can be used as an ingredient to craft the [Petal Wand](/wiki/Petal_Wand "Petal Wand") and the [Petal Belt](/wiki/Petal_Belt "Petal Belt").

### Wind Shrine Donation
Players can donate one Spirit Petal along with a [Cloud Vial](/wiki/Cloud_Vial "Cloud Vial") to the [Wind Shrine](/wiki/Wind_Shrine "Wind Shrine") for a chance to obtain a [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee "Windy Bee"). If the player already possesses a Windy Bee, they have a chance of obtaining a [Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat").

**Important:** Only one Spirit Petal can be donated per attempt. If a player attempts to donate two or more petals, only one will be accepted; the rest will be returned to the player.

### Acquisition
Three Spirit Petals are awarded every 10th [Spirit Bear quest](/wiki/Spirit_Bear#Quests_\(30\) "Spirit Bear"). These quests include Sway Away, Tickle The Wind, and Spring Out Of The Mountain.

## Trivia
* This is one of only two unique items obtainable from completing a [bear](/wiki/Category:Bears "Category:Bears")’s quests; the other being the [Translator](/wiki/Translator "Translator") from Science Bear.
* If a player attempts to donate more than one Spirit Petal to the Wind Shrine after already donating one, the Spirit Bear will shout, "Only one, dear!" upon rejection.
