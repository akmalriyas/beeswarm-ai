The **All-Time Top Bucko Bee Helpers** leaderboard is one of the game's extensive leaderboards. It tracks the top players who have completed the most quests for Gifted Bucko Bee. The leaderboard can display up to 100 positions, though only 10 are shown at a time. Players standing on the designated white circle can view their personal completion count for Gifted Bucko Bee quests.

## Location
The leaderboard is located on the second floor of the Blue HQ, situated next to Gifted Bucko Bee.
