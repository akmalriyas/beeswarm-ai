## Description and Appearance
The Super-Scooper is an advanced version of the standard Scooper. It features a noticeably larger, more rectangular blade compared to its predecessor. Its color scheme consists of blue and yellow accents. The handle retains the grey color of the basic Scooper but includes dark red detailing in the middle. The pole itself is dark red and adorned with six yellow and blue attachments. When held by the player, the Super-Scooper appears to be pointing upwards.
