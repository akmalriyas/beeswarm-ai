# Robo Bear

**Robo Bear** is a Robot NPC introduced in the Beesmas 2022 update. It is located between the Red HQ and the Badge Bearer's Guild, behind the Rose Field.

## Overview

| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Species** | Robot |
| **Location** | Between the Red HQ and the Badge Bearer's Guild, behind the Rose Field |
| **Bee Prerequisites** | 15+ (to initiate dialogue) / 30+ (to obtain Broken Drive in 30 Bee Zone) |

**Color Scheme:**
*   Fur: #364854
*   Fur Shades: #003200, #0D571C
*   Snout: #0D441A
*   Torso: #242B34
*   Arms/Legs: #364854

Robo Bear's primary function is initiating the **Robo Bear Challenge**. Before a player can access this challenge, they must first complete a quest to assemble Robo Bear by obtaining four specific drives: Red Drive, White Drive, Blue Drive, and Glitched Drive.

These drives are obtained through various quests involving other bears:
*   **Science Bear:** Repairing the White Drive and Corrupting the Glitched Drive.
*   **Dapper Bear:** Bartering for the Blue Drive.
*   **Panda Bear:** Obtaining the Red Drive.

Once all requirements are met, players can talk to Robo Bear and initiate the Challenge by spending 1 **Robo Pass**, which is obtainable from dispensers in the 30 Bee Zone.

### Beesmas Update Notes (2022)
During the Beesmas 2022 event, alternative methods were available:
*   White Drive could be purchased in Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   Red and Blue Drives could be acquired by completing specific Beesmas 2022 quests for Science Bear and Dapper Bear, respectively.
*   Glitched Drive could be obtained through Bee Bear’s "Decorating For Drives" quest.

## The Robo Bear Challenge Mechanics

### Initial Dialogue (Pre-Challenge)
Robo Bear initially informs the player that the unit lacks necessary Drives for normal operation and enters hibernation mode. Once all drives are installed, he activates:

> "SUCCESS. Welcome User. I am Robo Bear, the world's first Challenge generating automaton. I have been programmed specifically to push beekeepers to their limits. I am capable of generating over 6,459,542,340,244,001 unique combinations of Quests. I can simultaneously control up to 340 Robotic monsters using cog-based technologies. I can present any bee with obstacles they cannot overcome. And I am here to challenge you and your bees today. Using a Robo Pass, you may participate in Robo Bear's Challenge."

### Full Rules Explanation (Initial Talk)
The challenge is a minigame consisting of multiple rounds. Each round has a 5-minute time limit to complete a random quest.

**Core Mechanics:**
*   **Rewards:** Players are rewarded with **Cogs** after each successful round, with bonus Cogs awarded for faster completion.
*   **Upgrades:** Cogs can be used to purchase Upgrades that persist throughout the challenge. Unaffordable upgrades can be locked for later purchase.
*   **Bee Selection (Deactivation):** At the start, all but 3 of the player's bees are deactivated. Deactivated bees do not assist and their Gifted Hive bonuses are removed. Each round, the player may choose 2 bees to re-activate from a random selection.
*   **Resource Scaling:** Tool Pollen and Capacity are greatly reduced at the start but increase each round.
*   **Robotic Enemies:** Mechanical minions attack during quests, designed to push the player to their limits.
*   **Failure Condition:** If the player fails any quest or leaves the game while a round is in progress, the challenge ends.
*   **Progression & Victory:** Rounds increase in difficulty until elimination or completion of Round 25 (Victory).

### Challenge Progression Details
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Drives Usage** | Drives can only be activated during the Challenge. Activating a Drive grants a Pollen and Capacity multiplier, plus +1 attack for that round. They can be purchased between rounds using Cogs at Robo Bear's Shop. |
| **Amulets (Rewards)** | Cog Amulets are granted upon challenge completion based on the number of rounds completed: Bronze (5), Silver (10), Gold (15), Diamond (20), Supreme (25). |
| **Seasonal Boosts** | Certain boosts (like those from promocodes or Honeyday Events) are disabled during the Challenge to ensure fair comparison. |

### In-Round Interaction
If a player attempts to talk to Robo Bear while a round is in progress, they receive the message: "ROUND IS IN PROGRESS. Dear User: Time is the essence. Don't delay!"

### Trivia
*   Robo Bear is the second NPC in the game capable of initiating a challenge; the first was Stick Bug.
*   It utilizes the Robot Animation Pack from Roblox.
*   Robo Bear is one of the few quest givers who owns a shop (alongside Mother Bear, Dapper Bear, Gummy Bear, and Bee Bear).
*   Its original face decal was created in 2019 but released for Beesmas on December 14, 2022.
*   Robo Bear is the only bear whose design has been publicly changed from its pre-release state.
*   The "Repairing Robo Bear" quest can be instantly completed if the player already possesses all the necessary Drives.
