# Magnet

[![Magnet](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8c/Magnet.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20200403234529)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8c/Magnet.png/revision/latest?cb=20200403234529)

## Overview
The Magnet is a tool that can be purchased at the Noob Shop for 5,500 Honey.

**Pollen Collection Stats:**
*   **Range:** 9 Flowers
*   **Collecting Pattern:** Surround (Square)
*   **Collecting Time:** 0.8 seconds per collection cycle
*   **Base Collection Rate:** 2 pollen per flower collected per cycle
*   **Average Pollen Collection Rate:** 22.5 pollen per second

| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| White | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Red | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Blue | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |

*Special Multiplier(s): None*

## Description
The Magnet is a tool designed for collecting pollen. It collects 2 pollen from 9 surrounding patches every 0.8 seconds. Its base collection rate averages out to 22.5 pollen per second, making it the second-best pollen collector available in the Noob Shop.

### Appearance
The magnet consists of a red colored magnet with white tips. The pole is a shade of grey.

### Sticker
The Magnet has an associated sticker.

## Trivia
*   According to Slaries, an administrator on the official Bee Swarm Simulator Discord, the Magnet was the first tool Onett ever developed when creating the game.
*   It is a weaker version of the Electro-Magnet, which can be purchased in the Pro Shop for 300,000 honey. The Electro-Magnet differs visually (yellow body instead of red) and mechanically (it has four poles with electricity between them). Both tools are unique because they collect pollen in a square pattern.
*   The Magnet's associated sticker portrays a different gray magnet with colored ends; this is the only tool sticker in the game that does not match the original tool's appearance.
*   The design of the Magnet is likely based on a real-life horseshoe magnet.
