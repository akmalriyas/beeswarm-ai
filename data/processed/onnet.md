# Onett

[![OnettNPC](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d0/OnettNPC.png/revision/latest?cb=20240413003313)

## Overview
| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Species** | Robloxian |
| **Location** | Behind the [Bear Gate](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate"). |
| **Bee Prerequisites** | 30 bees |

Onett is a quest giver located past the Bear Gate. He provides a series of five challenging Star Journey quests, culminating in a [Star Treat](/wiki/Star_Treat "Star Treat") reward. Across all his Star Journey quests, players must collect a total of 3,673,750,000 Pollen, 136,000,000 Goo, 153,000 Ability Tokens, and defeat 940 Mobs.

## Quests

Onett currently offers several quest lines: Star Journey, Ornament Quest, Beesmas Quest, and various limited event quests.

## Dialogue Snippets
**Star Journey 1 Completion:** "Bee Swarm Simulator is my first ROBLOX game. I use this as my excuse every time I run into problems! If I was a little more experienced, I bet this area would be done by now. You're lucky I'm not! You get to do these quests!"

**Star Journey 2 Completion:** "Simulator games seem all about the rush of a reward after pushing through monotonous tasks... The promise of a reward gives meaning to our work... at least that's how we feel."

**Star Journey 3 Completion:** "Oh, nice you're done! By the way... Did you know the King Beetle has a 1 in 7 chance of dropping a [King Beetle Amulet]? Also, every time you defeat a King Beetle, the quality of amulets you get from it tends to go up. This caps out at 100 King Beetle kills."

**Star Journey 4 Completion:** "Wow! You really like making honey! Oh - how was the goo collecting? Thought you were safe with Gummy Bear gone didn't you? Hah! I spent a long time making the goo so you guys are going to use it whether you like it or not."

**Star Journey 5 Completion:** "Holy moly! You and your bees did it all! You must be in like the top 0.01% of ALL players! You must really want this Star Treat. It's all yours!"

**Ornament Quest (Beesmas) Dialogue Summary:** Onett discusses the competitive nature of ROBLOX, his struggles with bugs and updates, and reveals that he is creating a [Candy Cane Reindeer Ornament](/wiki/Ornaments "Ornaments") for the holiday season. He also explains new mechanics like bee mutations using Royal Jellies or Star Jellies on radioactive bees.

**Beesmas Yard Art Dialogue Summary:** Onett expresses his artistic block, stating that he needs help to complete a nativity scene mural on his lid. The completion of the quest allows players to view the art and summon temporary bees and Guiding Stars.

## Trivia
*   Onett is the first and only Robloxian-species Quest Giver. He and the Mysterious Figure are the only NPCs of this species.
*   He is unique among quest givers because his appearance changes during every event (e.g., wreath, robot mask, Krampus costume).
*   The total required amount of Pollen for all his quests is 3,673,750,000, and the Goo requirement is 136,000,000.
*   Onett's backpack contains pollen (32318), which represents the date Bee Swarm Simulator was first released (March 23, 2018).
*   He and Panda Bear were the first quest givers to require defeating a Tunnel Bear, followed by Science Bear.
*   Onett is one of only two NPCs whose interaction has been required to complete a quest (the other being Polar Bear).
