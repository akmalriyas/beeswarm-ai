# The Planter Of Plenty

The Planter Of Plenty is a reusable planter introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. It can be purchased at Dapper Bear's Shop.

> "Alone, grows in about 16 hours. Stores around 1 Trillion Pollen. Grants many bonus items, including Neonberries, Waxes, and Turpentine."

## Mechanics & Stats

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reusable** | Yes |
| **Growth Time (Alone)** | 16 hours |
| **Pollen Capacity** | 1 Trillion Pollen |
| **Pollen Multiplier (Mountain Top Field)** | +33% |
| **Nectar Multipliers** | +50% Refreshing, Comforting, Satisfying, Motivating, Invigorating |

### Growth Bonuses
The Planter Of Plenty grows 50% faster in the following fields:
*   Pepper Patch
*   Stump Field
*   Coconut Field
*   Mountain Top Field

It also grants 50% more pollen when placed in the Mountain Top Field. Gifted Bees are 50% more likely to sip nectar from this planter, and it provides a +50% boost to all Nectar Types.

### Crafting Ingredients (Cost)
To craft The Planter Of Plenty, you require:
*   100T Honey
*   500 Magic Beans
*   100 Super Smoothies
*   100 Swirled Waxes
*   100 Caustic Waxes
*   25 Turpentine

## Harvesting & Drops

Harvesting a fully grown Planter Of Plenty has a 1/3 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 9-10 and has the following probability distribution:

*   Rare Puffshroom: ~72.94%
*   Epic Puffshroom: ~24.07%
*   Legendary Puffshroom: ~2.92%
*   Mythic Puffshroom: ~0.07%

When claimed, the planter yields up to 60 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when harvested, two specific guaranteed rewards are provided:
*   5 Neonberries
*   10 Tickets

The remaining tokens are picked randomly from a non-exhaustive list of drops (rewards do not vary based on the field the planter is in):

**Guaranteed Drops:**
*   Neonberries
*   Tickets

**Random Drop Pool:**
*   Soft Waxes (~50% Chance)
*   Caustic Waxes (~33% Chance)
*   Turpentine (Rare, ~10% chance)
*   Micro-Converters
*   Star Jellies
*   Bonus Items
*   Other Items
*   Special Items

## Trivia & Notes

*   **Cost Comparison:** The Planter Of Plenty is one of the most expensive items in the game regarding honey and requires a high number of ingredients to craft.
*   **Pollen Discrepancy:** While the in-game description suggests it grants +50% pollen from boosted fields, datamined information indicates it only provides a +33% increase on Mountain Top Field.
*   **UI Detail:** The UI prompt when harvesting this planter reads: "Harvest the The Planter of Plenty" (including two instances of "the").
