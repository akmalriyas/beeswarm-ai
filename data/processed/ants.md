# Ants

[![A statue of an Army Ant on the Ant Gate.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ad/AntGateStatue.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/224?cb=20180712043853)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ad/AntGateStatue.png/revision/latest?cb=20180712043853) [](/wiki/File:AntGateStatue.png)

A statue of an Army Ant on the Ant Gate.

**Ants** are mobs exclusively found in the Ant Field during the Ant Challenge, past the Ant Gate. In the Ant Challenge, players fight waves of ants before a timer runs out. The level and health of the ants gradually increase as the player completes more rounds.

## Types

There are five types of ants. Giant Ants, Army Ants, and Flying Ants are the only types that grant Bond upon defeat.
*   **Giant Ants:** Grant 3 bond each.
*   **Army Ants & Flying Ants:** Grant 1 bond to each of the player's bees (this does not account for Bond From Battle, though it is usually too small a boost to significantly change the amount).

All ants deal 40 damage on contact, except Giant Ants, which deal 60 damage.

### Ant

[![An Ant.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b1/Ant-0.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20191208201559)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b1/Ant-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201559) [](/wiki/File:Ant-0.png)

An Ant.

**Ants** move in a predetermined straight line and are the most generic ant type. They have approximately 25 health at level one and are the least threatening of the five types, as they are often easily avoidable. They are the most common ant and always appear in the first few waves.

### Army Ant
_Main article: [Army Ant]_

[![An Army Ant.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/09/ArmyAnt.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20191208201511)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/09/ArmyAnt.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201511) [](/wiki/File:ArmyAnt.png)

An Army Ant.

**Army Ants** wear army helmets and actively pursue the player. They possess a pouncing ability, higher damage output, and slightly more health than normal or fire ants (about 35 at level one). Due to their defense, they deal less damage overall. Army Ants can spawn as early as the first wave, but typically begin appearing around the second or third waves. If they appear, they may occasionally spawn in waves, though this is not guaranteed.

### Flying Ant
_Main article: [Flying Ant]_

[![A Flying Ant.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/af/FlyingAnt.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201535)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/af/FlyingAnt.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201535) [](/wiki/File:FlyingAnt.png)

A Flying Ant.

**Flying Ants** are winged ants that track the player's movement. They are the fastest of the five ant types and can pounce from a considerably greater distance than Army Ants. Despite their speed, they have low health, similar to or sometimes less than a normal ant. Flying Ants can spawn as early as the first wave, but usually start appearing around the second or third wave. They deal higher damage than a standard Ant.

### Giant Ant
_Main article: [Giant Ant]_

[![A Giant Ant.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8f/Giant_Ant.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201615)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/8f/Giant_Ant.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201615) [](/wiki/File:Giant_Ant.png)

A Giant Ant.

**Giant Ants** are enormous versions of the normal ant that relentlessly pursue the player. They are relatively slow, which allows players to escape if they constantly move away. However, their high health (about 10 times that of an ordinary ant) and powerful attack compensate for their speed. Their size makes them difficult to avoid. Giant Ants spawn every five waves (5th, 10th, 15th, etc.).

### Fire Ant
_Main article: [Fire Ant]_

[![A Fire Ant.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a2/FireAnt.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201637)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a2/FireAnt.png/revision/latest?cb=20191208201637) [](/wiki/File:FireAnt.png)

A Fire Ant.

**Fire Ants** are colored red and behave like regular ants, but they leave a damaging fire trail as they move. Their flames deal 30 damage upon contact. These flames linger even after the Fire Ant that left them is defeated, disappearing after a short time. Fire Ants typically spawn in later waves, such as after the first Giant Ant. While noted by Panda Bear that their fire is not affected by Defense, this appears to be untrue; however, 100% defense (such as from Emergency Coconut Shield) will nullify the damage.

## Use in Quests

*   **Panda Bear's Gold Egg Questline:** After completing Panda Bear's questline, he offers 15 Ant Quests. Each requires defeating several ants and may include other requirements. These quests grant at least one Ticket and one Ant Pass, and most also provide Royal Jelly. The main prizes are a Diamond Egg (after five ant quests), a Star Egg (after ten), and a Star Treat (after all 15).
*   **Onett's Quests:** Each of Onett's five Star Journey quests requires the player to defeat ants along with several other requirements.
*   **Sun Bear's Visits:** During Sun Bear's second visit, he offers quests requiring the defeat of normal Ants and Giant Ants.
*   **Science Bear's Quest:** Science Bear's "Limits of Language" quest requires defeating ants alongside other specific requirements.
*   **Gifted Bucko Bee's Quests:** The "Bombard" and "Picnic" quests require the player to defeat Ants and Army Ants.
*   **Gifted Riley Bee's Quests:** The "Rampage" and "Picnic" quests specifically require the defeat of Fire Ants.
*   **Gummy Bear's Quest:** Gummy Bear's Beesmas 2020 quest, "Gummy Bear's Goo Years Beacon," required defeating 75 ants (this quest is no longer obtainable).

## Tips for Combat

*   Jumping usually helps evade Army Ants, Flying Ants, and Giant Ants. Using a Parachute or Glider can enhance this strategy.
*   Bees focus on one ant at a time. To ensure a specific ant is targeted, the player must get closer to it than any other threat. Prioritizing Fire Ants is advisable due to their highly disruptive flames.
*   Using higher-level bees ensures that attacks land effectively.
*   Bees with high attack power (such as Lion Bee, Cobalt Bee, Crimson Bee, Brave Bee) are very effective at dealing damage.
*   Gifted Bees provide extra damage and bonuses:
    *   Brave Bee grants +1 Attack.
    *   Looker Bee grants +25% Critical Power.
    *   Commander Bee grants +25% Critical Chance.
*   Vicious Bee and Windy Bee offer excellent crowd control, especially against packs of ants. Digital Bee can stun multiple ants simultaneously, allowing them to take extra damage via Mind Hack.
*   When a wave begins, the player should remain near the glass wall opposite the anthill to prevent being pounced on or landed upon by spawning ants.

## Trivia

*   Normal and Fire Ants, along with Wild Windy Bee, Rogue Vicious Bee, Mondo Chick, Stick Bug, and stick nymphs, are mobs that do not grant Battle Points when defeated.
*   All ants, along with the Coconut Crab, Cave Monsters, Robo Bear Challenge mobs, and Snowbear, do not drop Honey upon defeat.
*   Ants and Stick Bugs do not have a respawn timer or time range; they can only spawn by starting a Challenge.
*   The quantity and types of ants vary for each round, meaning scores are variable.
*   Ants, aphids, chicks, Stick Nymphs (including Festive Nymphs), Mechsquitos, Cogmowers, Cogturrets, and Mega Mechsquitos are the only mobs that come in different varieties.
*   All types of ants, Mechsquitos, Cogmowers, Cogturrets, and Mega Mechsquitos are unique in that they do not drop any loot upon defeat.
*   The Supreme Ant Amulet is the sole Ant Amulet that resembles a Flying Ant; all others resemble normal Ants.
