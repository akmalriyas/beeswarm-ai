# Scythe

[![Scythe](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cd/Scythe.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20190403020018)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/c/cd/Scythe.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403020018)

The **Scythe** is a tool that can be purchased in the Red HQ. It is known for its high efficiency and unique combat capabilities.

## Overview
| Statistic | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | 3,500,000 Honey |
| **Shop Location** | [Red HQ](/wiki/Red_HQ "Red HQ") |
| **Range** | 7 Flowers |
| **Collecting Pattern** | Line |
| **Collecting Time** | 0.47 seconds |
| **Base Collection** | 7 Pollen |

### Pollen Collection Details
The Scythe collects pollen from a line of up to 7 flowers in 0.47 seconds. It features a special multiplier for red pollen, collecting double the amount.

| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 7 | 14 | 21 | 28 | 35 |
| Red | 14 | 28 | 42 | 56 | 70 |
| Blue | 7 | 14 | 21 | 28 | 35 |

*Special Multiplier: x2 Red Pollen Multiplier.*

The Scythe's average base pollen collection rate is approximately 139.01 pollen per second (208.51 from red flowers, and 104.26 from white and blue flowers). It emits fire particles at the blade; every 20th swing spawns a flame.

***Note: Do not confuse this item with the [Dark Scythe](/wiki/Dark_Scythe "Dark Scythe").***

## Crafting
The Scythe requires 3,500,000 Honey to craft or purchase.

## Trivia and Mechanics
*   **Parkour Utility:** The Scythe is one of three items (along with the Scissors and Clippers) capable of cutting vines in front of the parkour leading to the Commando Chick's Hideout.
*   **In-Game Model:** A model of the Scythe can be found on top of the Red HQ. Its reflective surface causes it to appear dark during nighttime.
*   **Counterpart:** Its blue counterpart is the [Bubble Wand](/wiki/Bubble_Wand "Bubble Wand").
*   **Speed and Cost:** It is the fastest tool in the game regarding collection cooldowns, and it is the second cheapest item available at Red HQ (behind the Red Guard).
*   **Combat Capability:** The Scythe is unique among tools because the flame it spawns has the ability to damage mobs.
