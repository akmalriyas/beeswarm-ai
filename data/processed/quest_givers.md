# Quest Givers

Quest Givers are interactive Non-Player Character (NPC) entities who provide quests to the player for completion, offering various rewards upon successful turn-in. There are 13 permanent quest givers in the game, excluding traveling or temporary NPCs.

**Mechanics Overview:**

*   **Receiving a Quest:** When a quest is accepted from a quest giver, the audio file `Queststart.ogg` plays.
*   **Main Quest Completion:** Upon completing a main questline task, a notification appears: "\[Name of quest] is ready for \[Quest Giver]!"
*   **Repeatable Quest Completion:** When completing a repeatable quest, a message appears stating: "\[Name of quest] is ready to turn in!" This is accompanied by the sound effect `Ring.ogg` to remind the player to claim rewards. This notification will reappear if the player rejoins the game without turning in the quest.
*   **Quest Turn-In:** When the player hands in a completed quest, a message appears: "\[Name of quest] Complete!" followed by the audio file `Questend.ogg`.
