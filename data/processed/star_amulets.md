# Star Amulets

![Digital Bee](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/27/Digital_Bee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20230415203844) | **Disclaimer:** This content is based on datamined information and may contain inaccuracies or outdated details.

The **Star Amulet** is a collectible amulet that can be purchased in five distinct tiers from machines located in the [Star Hall](/wiki/Star_Hall). These amulets grant buffs to their owner and always increase overall [Capacity](/wiki/Capacity). Higher tier amulets provide more significant and superior buffs. The Diamond and Supreme Star Amulets also possess unique [Passive Abilities](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Star_Amulet_Passives).

### General Mechanics
*   **Acquisition:** Each Star Amulet requires the user to have discovered a minimum number of specific [Gifted Bee types](/wiki/Gifted_bee). After generating an amulet, there is a 1-second cooldown before another can be generated.
*   **Quality Scaling:** The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the total number of discovered gifted bee types up to a maximum threshold:
    *   Non-Supreme Star Amulets max out at 45 types.
    *   The Supreme Star Amulet requires 50 types (though currently, only 46 bee types exist in the game).
*   **Audio:** When an amulet is generated, a specific audio clip plays, with the sound pitch decreasing as the tier increases.

## Possible Buffs and Passives

### Bronze Star Amulet
*   **Guaranteed Stat:** Capacity x1.25 (100% chance).
*   **Selectable Stats (2):** Convert Rate, Pollen, White Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, Bee Ability Rate, Critical Chance, Bee Gather Pollen, Instant Conversion.

### Silver Star Amulet
*   **Guaranteed Stat:** Capacity x1.5 (100% chance).
*   **Selectable Stats (3):** Convert Rate, Pollen, White Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, Bee Ability Rate, Critical Chance, Bee Gather Pollen, Instant Conversion.
*   *Detailed probability tables for stat ranges based on quality milestones are available on the designated subpage.*

### Gold Star Amulet
*   **Guaranteed Stat:** Capacity x1.75 (100% chance).
*   **Selectable Stats (4):** Convert Rate, Pollen, White Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, Bee Ability Rate, Critical Chance, Bee Gather Pollen, Instant Conversion.
*   *Detailed probability tables for stat ranges based on quality milestones are available on the designated subpage.*

### Diamond Star Amulet
*   **Guaranteed Stat:** Capacity x2 (100% chance).
*   **Selectable Stats (5):** Convert Rate, Pollen, White Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, Bee Ability Rate, Critical Chance, Bee Gather Pollen, Instant Conversion.
*   **Passive Abilities (1):** Guiding Star (0.5% chance) or Star Shower (0.5% chance).
*   *Detailed probability tables for stat ranges based on quality milestones are available on the designated subpage.*

### Supreme Star Amulet
The Supreme Star Amulets feature unique passive abilities, making them the pinnacle of amulet collection.
*   **Guaranteed Stat:** Capacity x2.5 (100% chance).
*   **Selectable Stats (5):** Convert Rate, Pollen, White Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, Bee Ability Rate, Critical Chance, Bee Gather Pollen, Instant Conversion.
*   **Passive Abilities (1 or 2):** The player can obtain one of six specific passives (Guiding Star, Pop Star, Star Shower, Gummy Star, Scorching Star, Star Saw) with a 1/6 chance each. There is also a 1/50 chance to receive two passive abilities.
*   **Guarantee:** If the player has 500B honey or more when generating an amulet, they can spend that amount to guarantee a double passive.
*   *Detailed probability tables for stat ranges based on quality milestones are available on the designated subpage.*

## Trivia and Lore

*   The Supreme Star Amulet Generator replaced the original Diamond Star Amulet Generator in its location within the Star Hall.
*   Only the Diamond and Supreme Star Amulets can possess or guarantee passive abilities.
*   **Diamond Star Specifics:** The Diamond Star Amulet is unique as it was buffed in the 2020-06-06 update, gaining a 1/100 chance of receiving a passive upon generation. It is also the only amulet to have received such an upgrade after its initial release.
*   **Supreme Star Specifics:** The Supreme Star Amulet was introduced in the 2020-06-06 update, while the others were released earlier (2018-07-11). It is the only Star Amulet capable of granting double passive abilities.
*   **Passive Effects:** The summoned Gummy Star/Pop Star/Scorching Star rotates counterclockwise around the player. When a player dies while one of these passives is active, the star continues to hover at the death location, maintaining its buffs and sound effect.
*   The Star Shower passive functions similarly to [Science Bear's](/wiki/Science_Bear "Science Bear") [Beesmas Lights](/wiki/Beesmas_Lights "Beesmas Lights").
*   The audio used for obtaining a Star Amulet was originally the same as the sound from the Ready Player Two ROBLOX event.
