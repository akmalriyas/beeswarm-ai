# Compressor

![Compressor](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/ac/Compressor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403021556)

The **Compressor** is a storage bag that can be purchased from the Pro Shop. It functions by packing pollen to significantly increase overall storage capacity and conversion efficiency.

## Stats & Mechanics
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cost** | 160,000 Honey |
| **Capacity Bonus** | +50,000 Pollen |
| **Convert Rate Bonus** | +55% |

## Acquisition & Crafting
*   **Acquisition:** Purchased from the Pro Shop.
*   **Crafting Ingredients:** 160,000 Honey.

## Trivia
The Compressor holds a specific position in the Pro Shop's pricing structure:
*   It is the third most expensive bag, falling behind the Elite Barrel and Port-O-Hive.
*   It ranks as the fifth cheapest item in the Pro Shop, following the Pulsar, Mega-Jug, Super-Scooper, and Paper Planter.
