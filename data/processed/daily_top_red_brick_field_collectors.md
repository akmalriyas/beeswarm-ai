# Daily Top Red Brick Field Collectors (Archived)

This leaderboard was one of 66 leaderboards present in the game at the time, tracking how much pollen players collected specifically within the Red Brick Field during a single day. It reset daily at 12:00 AM CST. Unlike most other leaderboards, this one did not appear on the System Page or anywhere else in-game.

## Prizes
If a player reached the top 100 ranking by the end of the day, they were awarded 25 Tickets.

## Trivia
* This leaderboard, along with other Brick Field collector leaderboards and the Daily Top Ant Field Collectors, was one of the few leaderboards that did not display anywhere in the game interface.
* It was tied as the fourth leaderboard to be removed from the game, alongside the aforementioned field collectors' leaderboards, Most Commando Captures, Highest Snowbear Level, and Highest Robo Party Cake Rank.
