## Trivia and Mechanics

*   This leaderboard, along with the Top Brown Bear Helpers, is one of only two leaderboards where players cannot see the exact objective progress achieved (e.g., percentage complete).
*   It was among a group of unique wall-mounted leaderboards, alongside Fastest Crab Slayers, Most Damage Done to a Single Puffshroom, and Top Brown Bear Helpers.
*   The leaderboard was removed shortly after its introduction due to a glitch where Commando Chicks would instantly respawn. Instead of resetting player progress, the feature was simply hidden from the game.
    *   This makes it one of the shortest-lived features in the game. It shares this removal status with the Highest Snowbear Level leaderboard, making them the only two leaderboards removed from the game.
