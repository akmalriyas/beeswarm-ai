# Daily Top Ant Exterminators

The **Daily Top Ant Exterminators** is one of the 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator, accessible via the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page. This leaderboard tracks and displays the highest scores players achieve in the Ant Challenge during a single day. It shows 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

Players are awarded rewards based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Awarded an Ant Field Stamp Sticker.
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 50 Tickets.
