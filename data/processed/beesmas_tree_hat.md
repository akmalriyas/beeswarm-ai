# Beesmas Tree Hat

| ![Beesmas Tree Hat](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/14/Beesmas_Tree_Hat.png/revision/latest?cb=20230409131942) |
| :---: | Level: 10 |

**Description:** A party hat that's been painted to look like a Beesmas Tree. 🎄Beesmas

*   **Color Requirement:** Any
*   **Equip Limit:** 3
*   **Usage:** Can only be equipped to bees with a mutation.

A **Beesmas Tree Hat** is a Level 10 Beequip exclusive to the Beesmas event. It has an equip limit of three and no color requirement.

***

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Ways to Obtain
*   Purchasing the Radiocative Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
*   As a drop from Tunnel Bear.
*   As a drop from Stockings.

### Outdated/Historical Ways
*   Buying the Festive Bundle from Bee Bear’s Catalog (2021 and Summer 2024).
*   By completing Bee Bear's Snow Cub Reformation (5/5).
*   From the Honeyday Mega-Haul pack in the Robux Shop.
*   Opening the Merry Gift Box.
*   Completing the 20th Bee Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest (gives 1 Beesmas Tree Hat).
*   Buying the Brilliant Beesmas Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas Winter 2024.
