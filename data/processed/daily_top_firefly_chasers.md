# Daily Top Firefly Chasers Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Firefly Chasers** is one of the 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator. It can only be viewed through the Global Leaderboards menu within the System Page.

This leaderboard tracks how many fireflies a player bumps into during a single day. The board displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. The leaderboard resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes and Rewards

Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Players who reach the top 25 receive the **Glowing Smile Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Players who reach the top 100 receive 50 **Tickets**.
