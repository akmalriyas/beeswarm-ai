# Honey Bee (NPC)

*This page details the quest-giving NPC version of the Honey Bee. For information on the standard worker bee, see [Honey Bee](/wiki/Honey_Bee).*

[![Honey Bee NPC](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/49/Honey_Bee_NPC.png/revision/latest?cb=20210907181947)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/49/Honey_Bee_NPC.png/revision/latest)

## Overview
| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Species** | Bee |
| **Location** | Next to the Pine Tree Forest and to the right of the [Sticker Stack](/wiki/Sticker_Stack). Previously located next to the Basic Bee Gate ramp. |
| **Bee Prerequisites** | 15 bees |

The Honey Bee is a permanent Quest Giver, one of three permanent quest-giving Bees (along with the Gifted Riley Bee and Gifted Bucko Bee). It was introduced in the [2019-02-01 Update], initially as temporary content but later becoming permanent.

Unlike other quest-giving bees, Honey Bee does not require a Translator to interact with it.

## General Quest: Honey Hunt
Honey Bee offers one single, repeatable quest focused on collecting Honey Tokens.

| Requirements | Rewards |
| :--- | :--- |
| Collect $500 \times n$ Honey Tokens (where *n* is the quest number) | $1,000 \times n^2 + 10,000$ [Honey](/wiki/Honey) |
| | Conversion Boost (30 minutes) |

**Additional Rewards:**
An extra reward is chosen based on the following probabilities:

| Item | Chance |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ticket | ~24.39% |
| Treats | ~24.39% |
| Royal Jelly | ~24.39% |
| Sunflower Seeds | ~24.39% |
| Magic Bean | ~2.439% |

*Note: For every 10th quest completed between the 10th and 100th quest, one [Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly) is rewarded instead of a random item.*

## Tips for Completion
To complete Honey Bee's quests efficiently, consider the following strategies:

1.  **Token Farming:** Fighting [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug), [Stick Nymphs](/wiki/Stick_Nymph), and [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick) is highly effective for spawning honey tokens. Defeating normal mobs also contributes.
2.  **Wild Bees & Sprouts:** Defeating many [Wild Windy Bees](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee) or farming [sprouts](/wiki/Sprout) are viable ways to gather required tokens.
3.  **Passive Abilities:** The [Coin Scatter](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Coin_Scatter) passive is effective, especially when triggered by multiple Honey Bees, Carpenter Bees, Stubborn Bees, and Vector Bees in open fields (like the Mountain Top Field). The [Gummy Star](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Star) passive also spawns many honey tokens.
4.  **Resource Gathering:** Catching [coconuts](/wiki/Coconut) and [meteors](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower) produces large amounts of honey tokens, as does summoning a [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm).
5.  **Field Level:** Lower-level fields (e.g., Sunflower Field) generally spawn more honey tokens than higher-level fields.
6.  **Quest Claiming:** Once the quest is finished and you are about to boost, claim it immediately to utilize the 2x conversion buff.

## Trivia
*   Honey Bee is the third bee-based NPC in the game and the first non-gifted bee-based NPC.
*   It is the only Bee Quest giver that does not require a translator.
*   Along with Science Bear and Polar Bear, Honey Bee is one of the few quest givers whose rewards include a buff.
*   Honey Bee, along with Gifted Bucko Bee, Gifted Riley Bee, Onett, Stick Bug, and Bubble Bee Man, are among the only non-bear quest givers.
*   In the Egg Hunt 2019 update, a [Plastic Egg](/wiki/Plastic_Egg) token was replaced by a ticket token granting 3 Tickets. Honey Bee did not participate in this event.
*   Honey Bee is unique as the only NPC whose Beesmas quest affected another NPC's machine (Black Bear's Honey Wreath).
*   Along with Spirit Bear, Honey Bee can summon a [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm).
*   It was one of three NPCs that moved location in the game.
*   Honey Bee is one of only two NPCs (the other being Science Bear) who require joining the [Bee Swarm Simulator Club] to access specific dispensers and complete certain quests.
