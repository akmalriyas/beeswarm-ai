# Golf Mechanic

The Golf system was introduced in Bee Swarm Simulator with the 2024-08-01 update as part of "[The Games]" event, and it was subsequently removed in the 2025-04-30 update. This mechanic was utilized throughout Sun Bear's 2024 questline, requiring players to hit golf balls into holes and activate Targets.

Golf elements are exclusively found within the Dandelion Field, Blue Flower Field, and Sunflower Field.

## Golf Ball

A golf ball is a collectible item with a pollen meter, similar to [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom") and [Blooms](/wiki/Bloom "Bloom").

**Mechanics:**
1. The player fills the ball's pollen meter by collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") near it.
2. Once full, the player can putt the ball. The distance from the player determines how far and high the ball travels.
3. A moving golf ball collects [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") from the flowers underneath it.
4. Golf balls interact with the environment and Pink Fences but do not affect [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") or [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"). They can also hit Targets, granting rewards.

**Note:** Unlike other pollen-collecting items, golf balls only have a single appearance and do not level up through repeated use; there are no variations of the ball.

**Spawning:**
Golf Balls can only spawn in the Dandelion Field, Blue Flower Field, and Sunflower Field. A maximum of two Golf Balls can exist in a single field at any time. A new Golf Ball spawns after it is putted into a hole or sinks into mud.

## Hole

Holes are located in the Dandelion Field, Blue Flower Field, and Sunflower Field.

**Effects:**
Putting a golf ball into a hole rewards 7 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") tokens around the hole. This reward scales based on the points achieved and the level of the golf ball. After a successful putt, a new golf ball with a different level spawns.

## Target

Targets can be found in and around the Dandelion Field, Blue Flower Field, and Sunflower Field.

**Mechanics:**
Hitting a Target with a Golf Ball grants the player rewards. Targets are single-use; once activated, they turn green and cannot be hit for further rewards.

## Mud

Mud is found on flowers in the Dandelion Field, Blue Flower Field, and Sunflower Field. It tints all underlying flowers brown.

**Effects:**
If a golf ball comes to a complete stop over mud, it sinks into the ground, causing a new golf ball to spawn. This mechanic is similar to the mud pattern encountered during the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge").

## Pink Fences

Pink Fences are transparent barriers found in the Blue Flower Field and Sunflower Field.

**Effects:**
These fences block moving Golf Balls shot at them. They do not affect players, [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs"), or any other game mechanics.
