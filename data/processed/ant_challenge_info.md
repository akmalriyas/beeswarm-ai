# Ant Challenge Info

The Ant Challenge Info is a location found behind the Ant Gate and to the right of the main Ant Challenge area. It provides basic information about the challenge. When players step on the white pad, they are prompted with the message: "Read Ant Challenge Info."

## Mechanics and Rewards

To participate in the Ant Challenge, players must spend 1 Ant Pass to enter the Ant Field. The objective is to collect pollen and honey to fill the meter located on the ant hill. Once the meter is full, ants will emerge.

*   **Scoring:** Each defeated ant grants 1 point.
*   **Goal:** Players have 5 minutes to defeat as many ants as possible to increase their reward potential.
*   **Rewards:** Successful completion yields Royal Jellies, special treats, Honey, and Ant Amulets.

### Ant Amulet Tiers

Ant Amulets are special items that provide various boosts. The quality of the amulet is directly tied to the player's score:

*   **0–24 Points:** Bronze Ant Amulet
*   **25–49 Points:** Silver Ant Amulet
*   **50–99 Points:** Gold Ant Amulet
*   **100–150 Points:** Diamond Ant Amulet
*   **150+ Points:** Supreme Ant Amulet

While each amulet has randomly generated values, they tend to improve proportionally with the player's score.
