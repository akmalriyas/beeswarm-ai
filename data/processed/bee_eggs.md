# Bee Eggs

Eggs are inventory items in *Bee Swarm Simulator* that players drag into their hive to hatch a bee. They can also be donated to the Wind Shrine or used to roll on the Sticker Printer. These eggs are fundamental to starting gameplay and come in various types, some offering chances at different bee rarities, while others hatch a specific bee type.

Currently, there are 6 main egg types (Basic, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Mythic, Star), plus numerous gifted variants and specialized non-event/event bee eggs. Plastic Eggs, the Flight of the Bumble Egg, and the Swarming Egg of the Hive are notable exceptions that do not hatch into a standard bee.

**Hatching Mechanics:**
Since the 2019-09-28 update, players no longer require a vacant hive slot to hatch an egg. An egg can be hatched on top of an existing bee; the level of the host bee remains unchanged, acting similarly to Royal Jelly.

*Note: Due to regulations in certain regions (UK, Netherlands, Belgium, Australia), many eggs available in the Robux Shop may not be purchasable by consumers in those areas.*

## General Egg Types

### Basic Egg
The most common egg type, the Basic Egg, will almost always hatch into a Basic Bee (87% chance). However, it can rarely hatch into:
*   Rare Bee (10% chance)
*   Epic Bee (2.5% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (0.5% chance)
*   Gifted Bee (0.4% chance), with a 0.348% chance of specifically hatching a Gifted Basic Bee.

Mythic and Event bees cannot be hatched from this egg, nor can it be used on a Basic Bee.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Free starting item for first-time players.
*   Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop near the Sunflower Field (Price scales up with each purchase: 1,000 Honey to 10,000,000 Honey).
*   Receiving 1 Basic Egg reward by adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack.

### Silver Egg
This egg most likely hatches into a Rare bee (64.9% chance), but has chances of hatching into:
*   Epic bee (30% chance)
*   Legendary bee (5% chance)
*   Mythic bee (0.1% chance)

A Basic Bee or an Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 50 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 100 Robux.
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Silver Egg questlines.
*   Receiving a reward from Brown Bear's 25th quest.
*   Completing the 25th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine rewards you 1 Silver Egg.
*   Low chance drop from King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Rare Sprouts, and leaves while collecting pollen in Clover Field.
*   Extremely rare chance drop from Mantis (in increments of 1 or 5).
*   Drop from Festive Planter, Stockings, and as a reward from the Winter Memory Match.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Expired code "PreUpdate" reward.
*   Beesmas 2020 Bubble Bee Man quest reward.
*   Purchases in Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2020, 2021, and Summer/Winter Beesmas 2024.

### Gifted Silver Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Silver Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances on subsequent defeats).
*   Top 25 reward in the Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Completing Black Bear's "Getting Stumped" quest.
*   100th quest completion of the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
*   Rare drops from werewolves, Stump Snail, Scorpion, and Sticker Sprout.

### Gold Egg
Most likely to hatch into an Epic bee (79% chance), but has chances of hatching into:
*   Legendary bee (20% chance)
*   Mythic bee (1% chance)

A Basic Bee, Rare bee, or Event bee cannot hatch from this egg. Five Gold Eggs are required to craft the Honey Mask.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 100 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 200 Robux.
*   Rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge and Extreme Memory Match.
*   Token form in Werewolf's Cave (guarded by two cave monsters).
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Gold Egg questlines.
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 50th, 340th, and 390th quests.
*   50th quest completion of the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine rewards 1 Gold Egg.
*   Capturing Commando Chick 10 times.
*   Low chance drop from King Beetle; extremely low chance from Scorpion.
*   Rare chance drop from Epic sprouts, leaves in Clover Field, and various planters (Ticket, Candy, Red Clay, Festive, Pesticide, Petal).
*   Rare drops from Stockings and a Festive Nymph.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Opening the Golden Present or specific Black Friday Bundles in the Robux Shop.
*   Wind Shrine donation rewards during Beesmas 2020/2021 (Spirit Bear quest required).
*   Purchases in Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2020, 2022, and Summer Beesmas 2024.
*   Opening the Gilded Gift Box.

### Gifted Gold Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Gold Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant. This egg cannot be used to craft the Honey Mask.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances on subsequent defeats).
*   Top 25 reward in the Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   Playtime Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 50 Gifted Bucko Bee or 50 Gifted Riley Bee quests awards 1 Gifted Gold Egg.
*   Completing 275 Brown Bear quests awards 1 Gifted Gold Egg.
*   150th quest completion of the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine rewards 1 Gifted Gold Egg.
*   Very rare drops from Stump Snail, Coconut Crab, and Mantis.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Opening the Mondo Present during Beesmas 2019.

### Diamond Egg
Most likely to hatch into a Legendary bee (95% chance), but has a rare chance to hatch into a Mythic bee (5% chance). A Basic, Rare, Epic, or Event bee cannot hatch from this egg. Five Diamond Eggs are required to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Token located past Bear Gate near Shadow Bear (requires Glider/Parachute).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 400 Robux each.
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 400 Tickets each.
*   Rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge and Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Black Bear's Diamond Egg questline.
*   Panda Bear's fifth star treat/ant quest, "Colossal Combat".
*   Dapper Bear's tenth quest, "The Long Haul".
*   Brown Bear's 75th and 525th quests.
*   75th quest completion of the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
*   Rare drops from Stump Snail, Diamond Aphids, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, and Coconut Crab.
*   Very rare chance drop from Spiders, Mantises, and Werewolves.
*   Rare chance drop from Legendary sprouts.
*   Guaranteed drop from Supreme sprouts.
*   Extremely rare chance drop from leaves in Clover Field while collecting pollen.
*   Very rare reward from catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Rare drops from Festive Planter, Stockings, and Winter Memory Match.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing specific offers from the Robux Shop (e.g., Memorial Day Deal, Summer Treat Pack).
*   Purchases in Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022, and Beesmas 2024.

### Gifted Diamond Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Diamond Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant. This egg cannot be used to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances on subsequent defeats).
*   Top 25 reward in the Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers.
*   Quest Grandmaster badge reward from donating Tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing specific quests: 100, 250, 500, 750, and 1,000 Gifted Bucko Bee or Gifted Riley Bee quests awards one gifted diamond egg each.
*   425 Brown Bear quests completion awards one Gifted Diamond Egg.
*   200th quest completion of the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine awards one Gifted Diamond Egg.
*   Extremely rare drops from Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, or Coconut Crab.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Opening the Mondo Present during Beesmas 2019.

### Mythic Egg
This egg always hatches into a Mythic bee. The player can obtain a Gifted Mythic bee, though this is very rare.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Top 25 reward in the Daily Top Memory Matchers.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 1,200 Robux each (limited to 3 eggs per player).
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 2,500 Tickets each.
*   Very rare reward from Robo Bear Challenge and Extreme Memory Match.
*   Completing Black Bear's 75th quest ("Myth in The Making").
*   Dapper Bear's 15th quest, "More Than Myth".
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
*   300th quest completion of the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine awards one Mythic Egg.
*   Capturing Commando Chick for the 50th time guarantees a Mythic Egg.
*   Extremely rare drops from Stump Snail, Mondo Chick (higher chance when defeated faster), and Coconut Crab.
*   Extremely rare drop when catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Rare drop from Festive Planter.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 500 Snowflakes and 100 Gingerbread Bears.
*   Gummy Bear’s Goo Year’s Beacon quest during Beesmas 2025 rewards you 1 Mythic Egg.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Expired purchases in Bee Bear's Catalog (Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024).
*   Opening the Mondo Gift Box or Mythic Gift Box during Beesmas events.
*   Purchasing certain expired packs in the Robux Shop.
*   Opening the Mythic Present or Mondo Present.

### Gifted Mythic Egg
Shares the same probabilities as a Mythic Egg, but always hatches into its gifted variant.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Top 25 reward in the Daily Top Rare Bee Pollen Collectors.
*   500 Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quests completion.
*   Nearly impossible drop from Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 10,000 Snowflakes and 150 Gingerbread Bears.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Expired purchases in Bee Bear's Catalog (Hefty Honeyday Bundle, Merry Mondo Bundle).
*   Opening the Mondo Gift Box from Beesmas events.
*   Purchasing certain limited-time offers in the Robux Shop.

### Choose-A-Mythic Egg
This egg allows the player to select any Mythic Bee they desire upon hatching.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing the Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul from the Robux Shop for 4,500 Robux.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Expired purchases in the Robux Shop during Beesmas events.

### Star Egg
The Star Egg hatches into any type of gifted bee that is not already present in the player's hive (excluding gifted Event bees). If all non-event gifted bees are owned, it hatches into a random gifted bee. It will only hatch a Gifted Mythic Bee once all other non-event gifted bees are collected.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances on subsequent defeats).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 eggs per player).
*   Exceptionally rare chance drop from Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Completing Mother Bear's first ten quests ("Seven To Seven").
*   Panda Bear's first ten star treat/ant quests ("Royal Rumble").
*   Exceptionally rare chance drop from King Beetle, Festive Planter, and Party Mechsquito.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing certain limited-time offers in the Robux Shop (Summer Star Pack, Happy Honeyday Pack, Silent Night Special, Jolly Jumbo Pack, Nighttime Nectar Pack).

### Music Bee Egg

## Specific Event Bee Eggs

Event bee eggs always hatch into their respective named event bee, granting a permanent royal jelly variant of that specific bee (unless the player already possesses it). All these eggs share the appearance of a Basic Egg.

### Bear Bee Egg
*   **Acquisition:** Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux. Also obtainable via a Bear Bee Voucher received from players who bought the Basic Egg.

### Cobalt Bee Egg
*   **Acquisition:** Obtaining at the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.

### Crimson Bee Egg
*   **Acquisition:** Obtaining at the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.

### Digital Bee Egg
*   **Acquisition:** Purchasing from Robo Bear's Shop after obtaining a Diamond Cog Amulet, 7,777,777 Honey, and 5 of all Drives. (Previously available in the Digital Bee Pack for 2,500 Robux).

### Festive Bee Egg
*   **Acquisition:** Obtaining at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets. Also obtainable from completing the 15th quest of Bee Bear during Beesmas 2020 (if the player doesn't have Festive Bee yet), or opening the Festive Present during Beesmas 2019.

### Gummy Bee Egg
*   **Acquisition:** Obtaining at the Gummy Bee Egg Claim for 2,500 Gumdrops. (Previously available from completing all of Gummy Bear's quests or in the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets).

### Photon Bee Egg
*   **Acquisition:** Obtaining at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.

### Puppy Bee Egg
*   **Acquisition:** Obtaining at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets. (Previously available for 250 Tickets or 800 Robux during Beesmas 2020).

### Tabby Bee Egg
*   **Acquisition:** Obtaining at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.

### Vicious Bee Egg
*   **Acquisition:** Obtaining at the Vicious Bee Egg Claim for 250 Stingers.

### Windy Bee Egg
*   **Acquisition:** Obtained at the Wind Shrine by donating a Spirit Petal and subsequently donating Cloud Vials. (Previously available in the Windy Bee Pack for 800 Robux).
