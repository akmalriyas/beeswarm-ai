# Beekeeper's Boots

![Beekeeper's Boots](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/89/Beekeeper%27s_Boots.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403024614)

**Description:**
Practical and stylish boots that aid in the beekeeping process.

The Beekeeper's Boots are a pair of boots added in the 2018-09-10 update. They can be purchased at the Mountain Top Shop for 15,000,000 Honey.

**Stats:**
*   +25% Bee Gather Pollen
*   +6 Player Movespeed
*   +20 Jump Power
*   +10 Movement Collection
*   +1 Bee Attack

**Cost & Crafting:**
The boots can be acquired in two ways:

**Purchase Cost (Mountain Top Shop):**
*   15,000,000 Honey
*   5 Oil
*   3 Red Extracts
*   3 Blue Extracts

**Crafting Ingredients (Blender):**
*   15,000,000 Honey
*   110 Royal Jelly
*   150 Strawberry
*   150 Blueberry
*   250 Sunflower Seed

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Acquisition:** They were originally obtainable from the Sun Bear by completing his last quest before he left on 2018-10-08. The version worn by Sun Bear is larger to fit his model.
*   **Price Change:** After Sun Bear departed, the boots were transferred to the Mountain Top Shop with an initial price of 33,000,000 Honey. This was later reduced to the current price (15,000,000 Honey) in the 2018-11-25 update due to changes in crafting requirements.
*   **Historical Context:** Prior to the 2018-11-25 update, this item, along with Basic Boots and Hiking Boots, did not require any items for crafting.
*   **Gear Evolution:** Onett wears a pair of Beekeeper's Boots. He does not wear Gummy Boots or Coconut Clogs because those items were introduced in later updates (2018-11-25 and 2019-09-28, respectively).
*   **Stat Replacement:** Following the nerf to hats, these boots replaced some bonuses previously held by the Beekeeper's Mask. The movespeed bonus was 6, Jump Power was boosted, and Bee Gather Pollen was 25%. Now, wearing both the mask and the boots results in a combined 30% boost for Bee Gather Pollen.
*   **Tier Ranking:** Initially, these were the highest-tiered boots until the introduction of Gummy Boots in the 2018-11-25 update. In the 2019-09-28 update, Coconut Clogs surpassed them, making Beekeeper's Boots currently the third highest-tiered boot option (behind Gummy Boots and Coconut Clogs).
