# Cobalt Bee

**Cobalt Bee** is a Blue Event bee, known as "A superhero and defender of all things Blue! Together with Crimson Bee, it works to unite bees of all colors."

## Acquisition & Basics

*   **Rarity:** Event
*   **Color:** Blue
*   **Energy:** 35
*   **Speed:** 18.2
*   **Attack:** 7

Cobalt Bee can be obtained by purchasing a Cobalt Bee Egg from the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.

Like all other Event bees, it does not have a favorite treat. It can be made Gifted by feeding it a Star Treat, Gingerbread Bear, or Aged Gingerbread Bear.

**Preferences:**
*   **Likes:** Clover Field, Pine Tree Forest
*   **Dislikes:** Pineapple Patch

## Abilities

### Blue Pulse
Fires a pulse that hops between your blue bees, collecting pollen around them (+10% per Level). The power increases with each hop. If you own a Crimson Bee, this ability also fires a Red Pulse.
*   **Gifted Effect:** The pulse causes bees to instantly convert pollen equal to 25% of the bee's Convert Amount (+25% per Field Boost stack).

### Blue Bomb Sync
Grants 10% Blue Pollen and Instant Blue Conversion, allowing Blue Bombs to collect from white Flowers for 30 seconds. If Red Bomb Sync is also active, this effect applies to red flowers as well.

## Trivia

*   Cobalt Bee and Crimson Bee share the same base stats. The only difference in their field preferences is that Cobalt Bee likes the Pine Tree Forest, while Crimson Bee prefers the Rose Field.
*   They are the first colored Event bees, followed by Vicious Bee and Festive Bee.
*   Cobalt Bee and Crimson Bee are unique among all bees because they can activate another bee's ability (Red Pulse/Blue Bomb Sync) in addition to their own.
*   When both Cobalt and Crimson Bees are in the same hive, Blue Pulse additionally fires a Red Pulse, allowing collection from red flowers. If both Bomb Sync abilities are active simultaneously, blue bombs can collect from red and white flowers.
*   Cobalt Bee is one of the few Event bees whose hive slot design differs from the bee itself (along with Crimson Bee, Festive Bee, and Vicious Bee).
*   It was the first Blue Event bee introduced to the game.
*   Currently, Cobalt Bee and Crimson Bee are the only event bees that cost 250 tickets.
