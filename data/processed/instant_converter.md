# Instant Converter

[![An Instant Converter.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/35/Converter.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/300?cb=20180427214459)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/35/Converter.png/revision/latest?cb=20180427214459 "An Instant Converter.")

The **Instant Converter** is a machine used to immediately convert pollen into honey, functioning similarly to a hive but without requiring bees and providing instant conversion.

## Locations

There are three types of Instant Converters available in the game:

1.  **Basic Access:** Located behind the Basic Egg Shop, next to the Sunflower Field, and in front of the Mushroom Field. This converter is available to all players.
2.  **Brave Bee Gate Zone:** Found just around the corner from the Brave Bee Gate. Players must have 10 bees to access this unit.
3.  **Mountain Top Field Zone:** Located next to the Mountain Top Field. Players must have 25 bees to access this unit.

## Mechanics and Costs

*   **Usage Cost:** Each machine costs one Ticket to use.
*   **Cooldown:** All Instant Converters have a cooldown period of 15 minutes after use.
*   **Conversion Rate:** The player's Honey Per Pollen rate applies to the Instant Converter, just as it would at a hive.

## Tips and Strategy

*   **Field Boost Optimization:** Players can utilize an Instant Converter to squeeze in an extra trip to a boosted field. This is particularly useful when the remaining time on a boost is less than the time required for bees to convert pollen back at the hive.
*   **Efficiency:** Using the converter is most cost-effective when the player's bag is full, allowing them to maximize output per use.
*   **Cooldown Management:** The Instant Converter's 15-minute cooldown matches the duration of a field boost. Therefore, the same converter cannot be used twice during a single field boost unless the boost duration is extended.

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Tunnel Bear Access:** In the Brave Bee Gate zone, players can jump into the chute of the converter. This leads to the White Tunnel where Tunnel Bear spawns. A pad in the middle of the tunnel teleports the player onto the converter located behind the Basic Egg Shop.
*   **Historical Changes (Pre-2018-07-11 Update):**
    *   Before this update, the Instant Converter in the 10 Bee Zone was the only one available in the entire game and had a shorter cooldown of only 10 minutes.
    *   Prior to the update, the conveyor belt mechanism would push the player out of the converter.
*   **Black Bear Hint:** Behind the converter in the Brave Bee Area, there is a question asking for "Black Bear's Title?". This serves as a hint for the code 'Connoisseur', referencing Black Bear's dialogue about being a Honey Connoisseur during his first quest.
*   **Future Plans:** According to Spirit Bear, it was originally planned for an Instant Converter to be built in the location now occupied by the Wind Shrine.
