# Mountain Top Shop

[![The Mountain Top Shop at an angle.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/38/GliderShop.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/294?cb=20180421050057)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/38/GliderShop.png/revision/latest?cb=20180421050057 "the Mountain Top Shop at an angle.")The **Mountain Top Shop**, also known as the **Top Shop**, is a shop located past the Lion Bee Gate. Before the name of the Mountain Top Field was established, this location was referred to as the 25 Bee Shop.

This store sells various high-end items and is run by Top Bear.

### Shop Features
The shop contains several interactive tokens:
*   A Royal Jelly token on the roof.
*   A Ticket token in a corner inside the shop.
*   The respawn timer for the Mondo Chick can be found on the side facing the Mountain Top Field.

## Trivia and History

*   The Mondo Belt Bag and Beekeeper's Boots could have also been obtained by completing Sun Bear's quests before he left during his first visit.
*   This shop is the only place in the game where the player can purchase additional Hive Slots.
*   Top Bear was introduced in the 2018-07-11 update, meaning this shop was not run by any bear before that date.
*   The Eviction item used to be for sale until the 2019-09-28 update, when it was removed.
*   When the game was first released, the only item in this shop was the Glider until the 2018-04-10 update added the Beekeeper's Mask and Hive Slots.
