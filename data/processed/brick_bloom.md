# Brick Bloom

Brick Blooms are passive entities that spawn naturally within any of the four fields of the Retro Swarm Challenge. They are retro-styled flowers characterized by a green stem, purple petals, and a yellow center. (For a similar mob found in standard fields outside of the Retro Swarm Challenge, see [Bloom](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Bloom).)

## Mechanics
Brick Blooms accumulate pollen within their radius. Once enough pollen is collected, they burst, spawning tokens. These tokens can be either local or global, meaning players receive them regardless of whether they contributed to the destruction of the Brick Bloom.

**Spawning:**
*   Brick Blooms spawn on rounds 1, 3, 7, 12, and 18, and may continue spawning in higher waves.
*   The first Brick Bloom always spawns in the Mixed Brick Field.
*   In later waves, multiple Brick Blooms can be active simultaneously.

**Levels:**
There are multiple levels of Brick Blooms that can spawn. Generally, the later a round is, the higher level the Brick Bloom will be upon spawning. Similar to Puffshrooms, a higher-level Brick Bloom appears larger. When a Brick Bloom is defeated, another one spawns in a random field at the next consecutive level.

## Drops
Defeating Brick Blooms rewards players with Bricks. Higher-level Brick Blooms yield more bricks. The tokens that spawn are:

*   **Bricks:** (Standard token)
*   **Snowflakes:** (Exclusive to Beesmas)

## Strategy Guide
*   **Teamwork:** Having multiple players significantly speeds up the collection of required pollen. One player can be designated as the primary Brick Bloom manager.
*   **Bee Selection:** Using bees with extra pollen multipliers, such as Gifted Bees, is highly effective.
*   **Field Awareness:** Pay attention to which field the Brick Bloom spawned on and coordinate actions accordingly. For example, a player managing Blooms with a predominantly red hive may struggle against high-level Blooms in the Blue Brick Field. Communication about field handling improves efficiency.
*   **Ranged Combat:** Using ranged weapons allows players to fend off hostile mobs while their bees collect pollen for a Brick Bloom, preventing distraction.
*   **Consumables:** Certain consumables can be used to apply buffs that increase overall pollen collection rates.
*   **Cycle Tracking:** When spawning, Brick Blooms follow a randomly chosen cycle through the four Retro fields (e.g., Mixed $\rightarrow$ White $\rightarrow$ Red $\rightarrow$ Blue $\rightarrow$ Mixed). The cycle of each individual Brick Bloom is independent of the others.

## Trivia
Despite having a level system, there is no way to "miss" a Brick Bloom. This is because Brick Blooms are passive entities, similar to Puffshrooms and Blooms.
