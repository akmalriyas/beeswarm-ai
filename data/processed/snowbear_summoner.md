# Snow Bear Summoner

[![Snowbear Summoner2](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/72/Snowbear_Summoner2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20260217215402)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/7/72/Snowbear_Summoner2.png/revision/latest?cb=20260217215402) [](/wiki/File:Snowbear_Summoner2.png)

The **Snow Bear Summoner** is a Beesmas-exclusive summoner machine. It is located near the [Basic Bee Gate](/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate), the [Highest Snowbear Level](/wiki/Highest_Snowbear_Level) leaderboard, and the [Spider Field](/wiki/Spider_Field).

### Prerequisites and Cooldown
The Summoner can only be used after completing Panda Bear's Snowbear quest. It has a cooldown of 90 minutes to prevent instant farming or overcrowding of [Snowbears](/wiki/Snowbear) in the Spider Field.

If a player attempts to use the summoner without meeting this prerequisite, they will receive the message: *'Last year's Snowbear is already complete! But it's starting to melt...'*.

## Appearance
The Snow Bear Summoner is designed in the shape of a snowman.

*   **Incomplete State:** Before completing Panda Bear's quest, it appears as three stacked snowballs with two small snowballs for ears and two sticks for hands, lacking any decorations.
*   **Complete State:** After completing the quest, the summoner gains decorative elements: a top hat, three black buttons on the torso, two black buttons for eyes, and one large black button for the nose.

## Usage
The Snow Bear Summoner spawns a snowbear onto the Spider Field. The first time it is used, it generates a Level 1 snowbear.

When a player steps on the summoner pad, they will see the message: 'Summon a level <current level> Snowbear.' If a player has defeated a Level 4 snowbear, the next summoned bear will be Level 5. The snowbear's level increases by one after each defeat until it reaches its maximum potential.

## Trivia
*   This is the first machine that allows a player to manually summon a [bear](/wiki/Category:Bears). However, it was the second feature mechanic overall; the first being the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge).
*   It is the first mechanic in the game to allow summoning a [mob](/wiki/Mobs) with a defined level directly into a specific field (the Spider Field).
*   The Snow Bear Summoner's cooldown is not affected by owning a [Gifted Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee), because the player must actively summon it, unlike regular mobs which respawn automatically.
