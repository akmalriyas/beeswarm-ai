# Retro Swarm Challenge

***Disclaimer:** This content is based on datamined information and may be inaccurate or outdated.*

**Datamined Information Focus:** Pollen collection of sword items.
**Date of Datamining:** March 18th, 2026

The **Retro Swarm Challenge** is a special challenge accessible in the 10 Bee Zone, located near Dapper Bear's Shop. Players access it via a portal and are transported to a dedicated lobby featuring four waiting rooms, each accommodating up to four players. The core objective of this challenge is defensive: players must protect their hives from endless attacks by Slimes and Zombies. The game ends when 10 monsters successfully reach the hives.

## Accessing the Challenge

The portal for the Retro Swarm Challenge is located in the 10 Bee Zone, adjacent to Dapper Bear's Shop. Upon entering this portal, players are teleported into a separate experience titled: "Retro Swarm Challenge ⚔️ [DEFENSE THE HIVES!!!!] v1.2(fixed)".

Players spawn on a classic-styled island surrounded by smaller islands containing historic models. The main island features five entry cubes, an informational billboard explaining the minigame rules, and a portal leading back to Bee Swarm Simulator.

**Lobby Mechanics:**
*   Teams must form groups of four.
*   A twenty-five-second countdown begins upon entering a cube.
*   As a cube fills up, the timer rapidly decreases. Once time expires, players are teleported into the minigame.

## Gameplay Mechanics

The challenge takes place in a long, rectangular arena. Players spawn in front of four claimable hives. The map is divided into four distinct fields (Mixed, Blue, Red, and White), includes a central Shop on the left, and features a large cave at one end.

**In-Game HUD:**
Below the Honey and Pollen bar, players can track their Bricks, the current round number, remaining time in the round, and the HP of the hives. A server leaderboard displays each player's Brick, Honey, and Damage totals on the right side of the screen.

**Starting Conditions & Objectives:**
*   Players begin with fifty Bricks, and the hives start with ten HP.
*   The primary objective is to survive as many rounds as possible while defending the hives.
*   To fight monsters, players must unlock bees or purchase weapons at the Shop. Killing enemies drops Brick Tokens, which are used for purchases.

**Progression:**
Players retain their gear and bees from the main game, providing an advantage if they have made significant progress. Players without active bees can rent and level up rental bees using Bricks. The challenge concludes when hive HP reaches zero, at which point players receive rewards based on their final scores.

**Round Structure:**
*   A new wave of monsters spawns every 2 minutes.
*   OG Tokens are awarded for completing rounds 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10.
*   Players start with **no honey**, and all their bees are initially locked away.

## Enemies

There are two types of enemies: Slimes and Zombies. Both enemy types gradually level up. Similar to mobs, if a player's bee is lower level than the enemy, their accuracy decreases by 50% per level.

**Slimes:**
*   Move slowly in a straight line toward the hives.
*   They destroy flowers as they pass through the fields, similar to a Cogmower.
*   As they level up, they increase in size, hitbox, and health.

**Zombies:**
*   Also move towards the hives.
*   Unlike Slimes, if a Zombie spots a nearby player, it targets them instead of the hive.
*   They are not fast enough to catch players. Critical hits are highly effective against Zombies.

## Currency and Resources (Bricks)

**Bricks** are the main currency of the Retro Swarm Challenge. They can be acquired by:
1.  Killing enemies.
2.  Collecting Leaves on the fields.
3.  Destroying Brick Blooms.

All Bricks are collected via Brick Tokens.

### Brick Blooms
Brick Blooms are retro-styled flowers that spawn on any of the four fields. Their behavior is similar to Puffshrooms: after a certain amount of pollen is gathered in their radius, they burst into Brick Tokens. These tokens are both local and global, ensuring players receive them regardless of whether they contributed to the destruction.

*   **Spawning Rounds:** 1, 3, 7, 12, 18, and potentially higher rounds.
*   The first Bloom always spawns in the Mixed Brick Field.
*   When a Bloom is defeated, another one spawns in a random field, one level higher. Higher-level Blooms reward more bricks. Multiple Blooms may be active simultaneously in later waves.

**Currency Conversion:**
*   At the standard Shop, players can convert Honey into 10 Bricks at an exponentially increasing price.
*   A hidden shop located to the left of the cave allows for a large conversion: Honey into 100 Bricks at once.

## Rewards System

Upon the challenge's conclusion, a panel displays total damage dealt, bricks collected, rounds completed, and final score. The scoring system is comparable to the Stick Bug Challenge:

**Score Calculation:**
$$(100,000 \times \text{Rounds Completed}) + (10 \times \text{Bricks Collected}) + (\text{Random number between } 0.0001 \text{ and } 0.0002 \times \text{Attack})$$
*(Note: The random attack multiplier only applies if the player dealt 100% of the server's total damage.)*

Rewards are based on performance, with higher scores yielding better drops. Rewards may include three or four items from the following list, some locked behind a higher score threshold:

**Possible Reward Drops:**
Tickets, Ticket Planters, Gumdrops, Honeysuckles, Whirligigs, Micro-Converters, Jelly Beans, Oils, Enzymes, Red Extracts, Blue Extracts, Field Dice, Smooth Dice, Loaded Dice, Soft Waxes, Hard Waxes, Pink Balloons, Star Jellies (Royal Jelly), Turpentine (Very Rare), Bear Bee Voucher (Nearly Impossible), x2 Bee Gather Voucher (Nearly Impossible), x2 Convert Speed Voucher (Nearly Impossible).

## Strategies and Tips

*   **Brick Bloom Farming:** It is highly recommended that at least one player focuses on destroying Brick Blooms. This allows the team to acquire bricks for stronger weapons, buffs, and more bees to aid defense.
*   **Snowflake Farming (Beesmas):** During Beesmas events, 2-4 players should focus on farming Brick Blooms to maximize Snowflake drops, as higher levels can drop 15+ snowflakes.
*   **Rocket Launcher Tactics:** The Rocket Launcher is a powerful area-of-effect weapon, beneficial when Zombies are grouped near Slimes. Critical hits can one-shot low-level Zombies and deal heavy damage to Slimes.
    *   **Caution:** Be careful not to hit yourself or teammates, as the fling effect can be severe. Aiming at the bottom or between mobs maximizes impact.
    *   **Advanced Movement:** A difficult technique involves using a rocket shot below oneself while jumping to launch across the map rapidly.
    *   **Vision Advantage:** Launching upward with the Rocket Launcher allows players to glide and land on invisible walls/roofs in the cave, providing wide vision and maximizing damage by landing directly on mobs.
*   **Illumina Synergy:** After acquiring the Rocket Launcher, it is recommended that one or more players use Illumina. In later stages (Stage 15+), certain Slimes become significantly higher level than others, making the Rocket Launcher ineffective against them. Illumina provides exceptional single-target damage for these tougher enemies.

## Trivia and Lore

*   **Location History:** The Retro Swarm Challenge was initially accessible at spawn in front of the Noob Shop but was later relocated to the 10 Bee Zone during the 2024-07-17 Beesmas update.
*   **Roblox Inspiration:** The challenge heavily references classic Roblox content:
    *   The lobby uses free models common in older games, including elements from "Roblox World Headquarters," "Sword Fights on the Heights," and "Crossroads."
    *   The event is inspired by classic zombie survival games like "Build To Survive The Zombies." The zombies share sound effects, models, and behavior with those summoned by Zombie Staff gear.
    *   Wave announcements mimic old announcement scripts.
    *   Slimes are a nod to the game "ATTACK OF THE SLIMES: BUILD TO SURVIVE!!!"
    *   All available gears in the challenge are among Roblox's oldest items.
*   **Terminology:** Enemies within the Retro Swarm Challenge are consistently referred to as "monsters" rather than "mobs."
*   **Consumables & Restrictions:** Consumables such as Purple Potions, Stingers, Glitter, and Cloud Vials can be used during the event. However, Field Dice and Balloons are prohibited.
*   **Pollen Carryover:** Any pollen remaining in a player's bag before entering the challenge will disappear. Conversely, any pollen obtained *during* the challenge carries over to the main game after losing (provided the player’s bag was empty upon entry).
*   **Map Location:** The Retro Swarm Challenge map is located on the main map, behind the Badge Bearer's Guild, beyond the baseplate and teleportation wall. The Hive Hub shares this location.
*   **Digital Bee Interaction:** When a gifted Digital Bee's Map Corruption ability activates, the server-wide announcement message remains visible even if the affected field is inaccessible.
*   **Tool Usage:** Weapons can be unequipped by selecting them in the inventory and accepting the prompt. Alternatively, placing weapons on the hotbar allows for quicker switching. Rocket Launcher projectiles can collide with Amulets, potentially freezing or flinging players.
*   **Gingerbread Bear Token:** The Gingerbread Bear token used to be placed next to the hives but was replaced by the Aureolin Gift Box during the 2024 Winter Beesmas event.
*   **Trowel Restriction:** Attempting to use the Trowel without Bricks results in a message: "You’re out of bricks!".
