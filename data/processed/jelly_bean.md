# Jelly Bean

Jelly Beans are an inventory item introduced in the Egg Hunt 2019 update. When used, they scatter various colored beans in the direction the player is facing. Each color represents a different buff. These scattered beans turn into collectible tokens when they land on flowers.

**Mechanics:**
*   **Cooldown:** 30 seconds
*   **Cap:** 100 items
*   Each buff lasts for one minute.
*   Each Jelly Bean item spawns between 11 and 13 jelly bean tokens.

**Sharing Bonus:**
If multiple players are nearby, when a different player picks up the scattered tokens, both the owner (who used the original Jelly Bean) and the collector receive the token's buffs, plus the **Jelly Bean Sharing Bonus**. This bonus provides:
*   +100% Convert Rate
*   +10% White Pollen
*   +10% Red Pollen
*   +10% Blue Pollen

The Jelly Bean Sharing Bonus lasts for up to 30 minutes, depending on the amount of tokens picked up by other players.

**Usage Notes:**
*   Jelly Beans can be activated anywhere, but it is recommended to use them in a field to ensure tokens spawn correctly.
*   If multiple stacks are used (like Field Winds), further Jelly Bean stacks do not simply add their percentages; instead, they gain their own independent stack of percentage buffs.

## Acquisition Methods

### Direct Purchase & Rewards
*   **Quests:** Jelly Beans can be rewarded by various NPCs:
    *   **Black Bear:** Can reward 3 or 5 jelly beans depending on the specific quest completed (e.g., "Can't Without Ants," "You Blue It"). The Brown Bear rewards them after every third quest completion.
    *   **Spirit Bear:** Rewards vary, ranging from 3 to 10 jelly beans across multiple quests (e.g., "Out-Questing Questions," "Rhubarb That Could Have Been").
*   **Challenges/Events:** They are a reward from the Retro Swarm Challenge, Ant Challenge, Memory Match, and Robo Bear Challenge.
*   **Robux Shop:** Available in various packs, such as The Beginner's Bean Bundle (400 Robux for 25 Jelly Beans) or The Spikey Spring Basket (2,700 Robux for 50 Jelly Beans).

### Drops & Collection
*   **Planters:** They can drop as a bonus from specific planters: Candy Planter, Petal Planter, and The Planter Of Plenty.
*   **Wind Shrine:** Donating certain items to the Wind Shrine yields jelly beans.
*   **Cub Buddy Gifts:** Rarely received through Cub Buddy gifts (in increments of 1 or 5).
*   **Tokens:** Jelly Bean tokens can be found in specific locations:
    *   In the Blue Maze, next to the Ticket Tent (taking the path to the right).
    *   Inside a hole in the Coconut Cave, located on top of the Coconut Dispenser.

### Sticker Stack Rewards
Jelly Beans are granted as stack rewards for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
*   Standing Bean Bug Sticker: Grants 5 Jelly Beans.
*   Baseball Swing Sticker: Grants 5 Jelly Beans.
*   Colorful Buttons Sticker: Grants 5 Jelly Beans.

## Trivia and Notes
*   **Misspelling:** The Merigold Jelly Bean is often misspelled in the game; the correct spelling is 'Marigold'.
*   **Token Collection Sound:** When a jelly bean token is collected, one of five specific audios will play.
*   **Passive Mobs:** Bean Bugs are passive mobs that spawn randomly and scatter jelly bean tokens around their field.
*   **Past Mechanics:** Prior to the 2019-09-28 update, Jelly Beans did not have a maximum capacity, allowing players to obtain them in unlimited amounts.
