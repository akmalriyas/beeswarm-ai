# Stinger Shop

The **Stinger Shop** is a specialized shop located behind the Ant Gate. It sells Stingers, which are essential items in Bee Swarm Simulator.

## Function and Appearance
This shop dispenses Stingers for Tickets. The item can be purchased in three increments: 1, 10, or 100.

Visually, the Stinger Shop features pictures of stingers on its sides, a red circular base with a white top, and lacks the decorative element found on other similar shops. Its vending mechanism consists of a black semicircle with a grey separator, suggesting it operates as a rotary dispenser.

## Pricing
The cost structure for Stingers is based on Tickets:

| Quantity | Cost (Tickets) |
| :---: | :---: |
| 1 | 1 |
| 10 | 10 |
| 100 | 1,000 |
