# Stingers

[![Stinger](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b6/Stinger.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042929)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/b6/Stinger.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404042929)

*"Grants your bees x1.5 attack for 30 seconds."*

**Cooldown:** 1 second

**Stingers** are an inventory item introduced in the September 10, 2018 update. When used, they grant a temporary buff to your bees, increasing their Attack by 1.5x for 30 seconds. Stingers can also be spent to obtain Vicious Bees (cost: 250 Stingers) and are utilized in numerous crafting recipes.

## Acquisition Methods

Stingers can be obtained through various means:

**Drops & Rewards:**
*   **Rogue Vicious Bee:** Defeating a Rogue Vicious Bee always grants stingers. The amount dropped depends on the bee's level and whether it is gifted or not (see Looting section below).
*   **Rare Drops:** They are a rare drop from certain hostile mobs, including Rage Aphids, Armored Aphids, Scorpions, and Spiders.
*   **Planter Harvests:** Stingers can be dropped when harvesting planters in the Cactus Field or Rose Field.
*   **Robo Party:** Guaranteed reward for defeating all party mobs of your Robo Party.

**Collection & Events:**
*   **Sticker Stack Rewards:** Adding specific stickers to a Sticker Stack grants stingers:
    *   Flying Brave Bee: 3 Stingers
    *   Walking Stick Nymph: 3 Stingers
    *   Coiled Snake: 3 Stingers
    *   Dark Flame: 5 Stingers
    *   Cyan Hilted Sword: 10 Stingers
    *   Red Wailing Cry: 3 Stingers
    *   Tough Potato: 3 Stingers
*   **Commando Chick Captures:**
    *   3 captures: 1 Stinger + other items.
    *   12 captures: 3 Stingers + other items.
    *   30 captures: 10 Stingers + other items.
    *   40 captures: 10 Stingers + other items.
*   **Wind Shrine Donations:** Shivering certain Badges in the Wind Shrine (e.g., Battle, Clover, Spider, Cactus, and Rose) rewards stingers plus other items.
*   **Quests:** Completing specific quests grants them (e.g., Black Bear, Gifted Riley Bee, Spirit Bear).
*   **Memory Matches:** Winning Mega, Night, or Extreme Memory Matches awards Stingers.
*   **Cub Buddy Gifts:** Very rarely received as a Cub Buddy gift.

**Purchases & Trading:**
*   **Stinger Shop:** Available for purchase at 10 tickets per stinger.
*   **Bee Bear's Catalog:** Purchasing the Super Snowman Bundle grants 25 Stingers plus other items.
*   **Planter Growth Rewards (Consecutive):**
    *   Fully growing a Red Clay Planter consecutively in Clover, Spider, and Cactus Fields rewards 10 Stingers (2-week cooldown).
    *   Fully growing a Heat-Treated Planter in the Cactus Field rewards 10 Stingers (unknown cooldown).
    *   Fully growing The Planter Of Plenty in the Cactus Field rewards 10–15 Stingers (unknown cooldown).

## Looting from Rogue Vicious Bee

The Rogue Vicious Bee can be summoned by touching or being close to a spike in a field. It has a higher chance of spawning during nighttime and is guaranteed when using a Night Bell.

**Daily Bonus:** For the first Vicious Bee defeated each day, a daily bonus of 5 Stingers is awarded (with a 22-hour cooldown). This applies separately to Gifted Vicious Bees.

The Rogue Vicious Bee can spawn in six fields: Clover Field, Spider Field, Cactus Field, Rose Field, Mountain Top Field, and Pepper Patch.
