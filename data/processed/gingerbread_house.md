# Gingerbread House

[![The Gingerbread House after being assembled by completing 's Beesmas quest.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1d/Gingerbread_House.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20241021092327)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1d/Gingerbread_House.png/revision/latest?cb=20241021092327) [](/wiki/File:Gingerbread_House.png)

The **Gingerbread House** is a Beesmas-exclusive machine that can be assembled after completing Mother Bear's Beesmas quest. It is located between Mother Bear and the Treat Shop.

When activated, the house generates one Gingerbread Bear for every two playtime hours, which builds up over time. If a player attempts to use the Gingerbread House before it is completed, they will see the message: "This Gingerbread House is far from complete..."

## Overview

The structure features gumdrops as corner decorations, a Gingerbread Bear in the middle, and a candy cane acting as a possible chimney.

Its sole function is to produce a Gingerbread Bear every two **in-game** hours. If the player is offline, it does not generate bears unless they have redeemed an Offline Voucher (Sticker), which keeps the house active for up to 24 hours after logging out. Upon completing the quest, the Gingerbread House can be used immediately for one Gingerbread Bear.

## Trivia

*   The Gingerbread House can store multiple gingerbread bears simultaneously, allowing players to collect several in one go after four or more hours of accumulation.
*   **Generation Timer Mechanics:** The cooldown for collecting a bear resets upon collection, but the underlying generation timer does not. For example, if a player checks the house after 3 hours, they receive one bear and the collection cooldown resets to 2 hours. However, the remaining hour of accumulated time is saved, meaning the next bear will be ready in only 5 more hours (totaling 6 hours from the start), rather than the full 2 hours plus the initial 1 hour used. *(Note: This mechanic has since been updated so that the timer fully saves.)*
*   The Gingerbread House was introduced during Beesmas 2020 and has returned in subsequent Beesmas events.
*   Quest givers, such as Bee Bear, require players to interact with the Gingerbread House a varying number of times.
