# Stump Snail

The **Stump Snail** is a level 6 mini-boss that occupies the [Stump Field](/wiki/Stump_Field "Stump Field"). It idly moves along the circumference of the field, posing no direct threat until it makes contact, at which point it deals 30 damage.

Like the [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick"), the Stump Snail's health is saved across multiple sessions, allowing players to defeat it over time.

## Stats and Mechanics

| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Health | 30,000,000 (30 million) | Saved across sessions. |
| Damage | 30 | Damage dealt upon contact. |
| Battle Points | 500 | Reward upon defeat. |
| Bond | 2,000 | Reward upon defeat. |
| Honey | 30,000,000 | Reward upon defeat. |
| Level | 6 | Mob level. |

**Respawn Timer:**
*   Standard: 96 hours (4 days).
*   With Gifted Vicious Bee: Reduced to 81 hours and 36 minutes (3.4 days).

## Tips and Strategies

*   **Bee Composition:** Bees oriented toward fighting, such as [Rage Bee](/wiki/Rage_Bee "Rage Bee"), [Demon Bee](/wiki/Demon_Bee "Demon Bee"), [Lion Bee](/wiki/Lion_Bee "Lion Bee"), [Vicious Bee](/wiki/Vicious_Bee "Vicious Bee"), [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee"), and [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee"), will help defeat the Stump Snail faster.
*   **Damage Amplification:** Two additional [Fire Bees](/wiki/Fire_Bee "Fire Bee") from [Spicy Bee](/wiki/Spicy_Bee "Spicy Bee")'s [Inferno](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Inferno "Ability Tokens") and [Frogs](/wiki/Frog "Frog") from [Tadpole Bee](/wiki/Tadpole_Bee "Tadpole Bee")'s [Summon Frog](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Summon_Frog "Ability Tokens") may be summoned to deal further damage.
*   **Precision:** Using a [Precise Bee](/wiki/Precise_Bee "Precise Bee") increases overall bee damage and provides Critical and Super-Crit chance bonuses.
*   **Equipment Buffs:** Players should wear equipment that boosts bee attack, such as the [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask "Fire Mask") or [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask").
    *   The [Gummy Mask](/wiki/Gummy_Mask "Gummy Mask") allows players to intentionally trigger [Gummy Morph](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Gummy_Morph "Passive Abilities"), boosting [Gummy Bee](/wiki/Gummy_Bee "Gummy Bee")'s attack for 10 seconds.
    *   The [Coconut Canister](/wiki/Coconut_Canister "Coconut Canister") allows players to trigger the [Emergency Coconut Shield](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Emergency_Coconut_Shield "Passive Abilities"), granting 10 seconds of ×1.25 Bee Attack.
*   **Utility:** When AFK, a [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy") is recommended for collecting tokens the player might miss.
*   **Stat Boosts:** [Stingers](/wiki/Stinger "Stinger") greatly boost attack, [Oil](/wiki/Oil "Oil") increases [Bee Movespeed](/wiki/Stats#Speed "Stats"), and [Tropical Drinks](/wiki/Tropical_Drink "Tropical Drink") increase critical chance by 5%.
*   **Event Usage:** During specific events (Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022, summer 2024, and 2025), summoned bees from [Onett's Lid Art](/wiki/Onett%27s_Lid_Art "Onett's Lid Art"), [Honeyday Candles](/wiki/Honeyday_Candles "Honeyday Candles"), and [Gummy Siege](/wiki/Gummy_Siege "Gummy Siege") can be used to attack the Stump Snail.

## Trivia

*   The Stump Snail is one of only two mobs (the other being [Commando Chick](/wiki/Chicks#Commando_Chick "Chicks")) where damage dealt across multiple sessions is saved.
*   Stump Snail, [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle"), and [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") are the only mobs that can drop amulets; however, Stump Snail is the only one guaranteed to drop an amulet.
*   It is the only mob that guarantees a drop of [Glue](/wiki/Glue "Glue").
*   As of the 2019-01-02 update, while Stump Snail does not drop tokens upon defeat (like Rogue Vicious Bee or Ants), the total amount of honey rewarded is still increased by [Honey From Tokens](/wiki/System_Page#Honey_From_Tokens "System Page").
*   [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") refers to the Stump Snail as "Ol' Stumpy," possibly referencing Youtuber ThnxCya.
*   It is possible to damage other players' snails using [Flames](/wiki/Flame "Flame").
*   The initial health of the Stump Snail was 25,000,000 before the 2019-04-05 update; this increase was due to general bee attack buffs in that patch.
*   Stump Snail possesses both the highest health and the longest respawn time among all mobs.
*   This snail is one of three hostile mobs that require multiple days to respawn, alongside [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear") and [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab").
*   When a player leaves the Stump Field, the snail returns to its cave without a specific target. In this state, it can damage any player that comes into contact with it. Only the head of the snail deals damage.
*   If the player is in the middle of a [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"), bees will not target the Stump Snail, allowing players to collect pollen without distraction (though the snail can still spawn and deal damage).
