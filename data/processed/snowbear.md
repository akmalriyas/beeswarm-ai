# Snow Bear

## Overview

Snowbear is a mini-boss that can be summoned every 90 minutes after completing Panda Bear's Beesmas quest, using the Snowbear Summoner. It spawns in the Spider Field and players have 60 seconds (1 minute) to defeat it before it despawns (melts). All players' bees can attack another player's Snowbear, but the summoning player receives increased rewards.

## Attacks and Mechanics

Snowbear possesses two primary attack types:

1.  **Contact:** If a player makes physical contact with Snowbear, it deals 36 base damage. Snowbear does not track players; instead, it moves randomly through the field, hitting any players along its path.
2.  **Snowball Hurl:** Snowbear hurls a snowball at players in or around the Spider Field. This attack deals 60 base damage and applies the **Frozen Debuff** for approximately 2.5 seconds. The Frozen debuff sets player movespeed, jump power, and bee movespeed to ×0 (or ×0.2 for bees).

## Drops and Rewards

Snowbear drops several types of items:

*   **Bond:** (100 – ???) Increased by Bond from Battle and level.
*   **Tickets:** Equal to the Snowbear's current level.
*   **Snowflakes:** These drop every time Snowbear loses a quarter of its health. Only the player who summoned it can pick up these specific drops, though additional random snowflakes spawn in the field that are available to all players.

### Snowflake Drop Types (Exclusive to Summoner)
1.  **Damaged Rings:** Dropped upon damaging the Snowbear by a certain portion of its health. These rings are initially worth an unknown amount but their total value matches the Snowbear's level at higher levels. Up to four Damage Rings can be collected before the Defeat Ring drops.
2.  **Defeat Ring:** Dropped only upon the Snowbear's defeat, valued at approximately 20 times the Snowbear's level.
3.  **Scattered Snowflakes:** These drop scale with the Snowbear's level and share the same conditions/damage requirements as the Damage Rings, but can be collected by all players.

## Strategies and Tips

### Combat Strategy
*   **Damage Output:** Since Snowbear's health increases significantly with level, high-level bees capable of dealing substantial damage are highly recommended.
*   **Dodging Attacks:** The snowball attack has a short reload time. If you are frozen, repeated hits will deal damage while the debuff is active, leading to death if hit multiple times. Players using a Glider or Parachute can dodge snowballs. When thawing from the Frozen effect, immediately jump away and use a glider to prevent re-freezing.
*   **Positioning:** Try to stay behind Snowbear, as it takes time for the mob to turn around.
*   **Teamwork:** Having multiple players attack high-level Snowbears is an effective strategy.

### Bee Optimization Tips
*   **Attack Buffs:** Use Oils to increase Bee Movespeed and Tropical Drinks/Super Smoothies (if budget allows) to boost Critical Chance.
*   **Immunity/Damage Scaling:** Certain items, such as Demon Mask or Fire Mask's passive abilities, allow bees to ignore enemy levels, ensuring consistent damage regardless of Snowbear's level.
*   **High Attack Bees:** Utilize multiple high-attack bees like Lion Bee or Vicious Bee.
*   **Stingers:** Use Stingers to multiply a bee's attack by ×1.5 for 30 seconds. The Star Saw passive further enhances this damage.
*   **Token Generation:** Generating battle tokens is crucial:
    *   **Rage/Impale:** Multiple Rage Bees or Spicy Bees generate Rage Tokens, while Vicious Bee deals massive damage with Impale.
    *   **Stun/Damage:** Digital Bee's Mind Hack can stun and increase Snowbear's vulnerability.
    *   **Focus/Precision:** Looker Bees, Commander Bees, Music Bees, and Puppy Bee (with Reindeer Antlers) generate Focus tokens. Precise Bees generate Target Practice tokens, granting Precision and increasing Super-Crit Chance.
    *   **Critical Power:** Music Bee generates Melody, which increases critical power. This can also be generated by equipping Toy Horn or Kazoo.
*   **Gifted Bees:** Gifted Bees provide 50% more attack. Recommended options include Gifted Looker Bee, Gifted Brave Bee, Gifted Rage Bee, Gifted Commander Bee, Gifted Ninja Bee, and Gifted Precise Bee.
*   **Movement Tactics:** Snowball attacks register before the visual snowball appears. To prevent freezing, keep moving while staying in range. If a snowball is incoming, do not run directly away from it; instead, move left or right of its trajectory.
*   **Coconut Shield:** The Coconut Canister allows players to activate Emergency Coconut Shield for 10 seconds, granting a ×1.25 bee attack boost and damage immunity (note: this does not prevent freezing).

## Trivia

*   **Animation:** Snowbear uses the Cartoony Animation Pack.
*   **Debuff Icon:** The Frozen debuff icon is based on an asset from *Battle for Dream Island*.
*   **Summoning Mobs:** Snowbear and Stick Bug are among the only hostile mobs that can be directly summoned by players, and their cooldowns are unaffected by Gifted Vicious Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus.
*   **Game History:** Snowbear was one of four mobs removed from the game (along with Chick, Spotted Chick, and Hostage Chick), but it is the only mob to return after removal.
*   **Leveling Mechanic:** Its leveling mechanic functions similarly to Stick Bug and Wild Windy Bee. It is also unique in having a changing size that increases with level, potentially making it the largest mob in the game.
*   **Unique Ability:** Snowbear is the first and only mob capable of freezing a player.
*   **Bear Mobs:** Along with Tunnel Bear, Snowbear is one of the two hostile mobs that are bears.
*   **Appearance:** Similar to Tunnel Bear and Shadow Bear, Snowbear has no face and uses a single texture.
*   **Token Drop Exception:** It is one of four mobs that does not drop Honey Tokens (the others being Coconut Crab, Ants, and Cave Monster).
*   **Beesmas Content:** Along with the Naughty List, Festive Nymphs, and Snowflakes, it was part of the extended Beesmas 2020 content.
*   **Naming Convention:** While generally referred to as "Snowbear," the failure message uses the phrase "**Snow Bear**".
*   **Faceless Bears:** Along with Tunnel Bear and Shadow Bear, Snowbear is a faceless bear.
*   **Glitches:** There are known glitches where Snowbear becomes idle (not moving or attacking) or spawns inside the Mushroom Field.
*   **Cub Buddy Skin:** Snowbear has a dedicated Cub Buddy skin, the Snow Cub, which was a reward for Bee Bear's 2021 Beesmas quest. Unlike its counterpart, this skin features a face and carrot nose and uses the Ninja Animation Pack.
*   **Field Collision:** Even when spawned in an empty field, Snowbear maintains collision detection.
*   **Design Detail:** Snowbear's top hat is a retextured version of the Psychedelic Top Hat.
