# Port-O-Hive Bag

![Port-O-Hive](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/4a/Port-O-Hive.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20190403021635)

The **Port-O-Hive** is a specialized bag that can be purchased in the [Pro Shop](/wiki/Pro_Shop). It functions as a miniature, wearable hive designed to instantly convert pollen into honey.

### Stats & Costs
| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purchase Cost** | 1,250,000 Honey | Purchased from the Pro Shop. |
| **Capacity** | +250,000 Pollen | Additional capacity for pollen storage. |
| **Convert Rate** | +100% | Increases the rate at which pollen converts to honey. |
| **Instant Conversion** | +5% | Boosts the chance of instant conversion upon collection. |

### Crafting Ingredients
*   1,250,000 [Honey](/wiki/Honey)

### Trivia & Lore
*   The Port-O-Hive is the most expensive bag available in the Pro Shop.
*   It ranks as the fourth most expensive item in the Pro Shop, following the [Honey Dipper](/wiki/Honey_Dipper), [Hiking Boots](/wiki/Hiking_Boots), and the [Propeller Hat](/wiki/Propeller_Hat).
*   This specific variant was the first of four versions added to the game. The other variants include the [Red Port-O-Hive](/wiki/Red_Port-O-Hive), the [Blue Port-O-Hive](/wiki/Blue_Port-O-Hive), and the [Porcelain Port-O-Hive](/wiki/Porcelain_Port-O-Hive).
*   The [Onett statue](/wiki/Easter_Eggs#Statue_of_Onett) features a Port-O-Hive on top of its sixth hive, bearing the code "38217."
