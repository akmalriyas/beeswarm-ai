## Trivia and Mechanics

*   Common rarity and Event rarity are the only bee rarities that cannot be obtained using a normal Royal Jelly or Star Jelly.
*   Excluding Gifted Bee forms, Common Bees and Rare bees are the only types of bees that do not spawn Ability Tokens. (The specific rare bee that cannot spawn ability tokens is the Brave Bee.)
*   In the game, the Hive Slot color assigned to a Common Bee is brown, which often represents bronze.
*   Common rarity has the fewest bee types in the game, consisting of only one type (the Basic Bee).
*   There are three ways to obtain a bee of Common rarity: via Basic Eggs, Star Eggs, and Basic Bee Jelly variants.
