# Daily Top Damage to A Single Puffshroom

The **Daily Top Damage to A Single Puffshroom** is a daily leaderboard tracking the highest damage players have dealt to a single [Puffshroom](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom").

This leaderboard is accessible via the Global Leaderboards menu on the [System Page](/wiki/System_Page "System Page") and displays 10 positions at a time, allowing players to scroll through up to 100 entries. The ranking resets daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Rewards

Players are awarded prizes based on their placement by the end of the day:

*   **Top 10:** Awarded the **Black Truffle Mushroom Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 50 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
