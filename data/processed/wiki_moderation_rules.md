# Wiki Moderation Rules

These rules, along with the [Editing Guidelines] and [Discussions Guidelines], form the basis of acceptable conduct on this wiki. All punishments are applied on a case-by-case basis; therefore, they may not strictly match the guidelines listed here. Repeated or more severe violations will result in harsher penalties.

If you believe you have been punished unfairly or if you are unsure about a penalty, contact a staff member. Do not attempt to appeal a block by creating a new account, as this will be treated as ban evasion. Such matters should be addressed through Community Central.

## **Verbal Reminder**
*A minor, first-time violation.* These may sometimes be issued instead of standard reminders or replies.

*   Do not use languages other than English to prevent language barriers.
*   Do not request a staff position; these must be earned.

## **Reminder**
*Issued for minor, first-time violations.*

*   Do not "minimod" or act as staff when you are not authorized. Report situations needing attention to an actual staff member.
*   Do not edit user profiles belonging to others without explicit permission.
*   Do not spam; this disrupts the wiki and can obscure important information. Minor, unnecessary spam will result in a reminder.
*   Do not use profanity. Bypassing or censoring language (including acronyms) is prohibited and will result in a reminder, regardless of how it is masked. The only exception is "lmao."

## **Warning**
*Issued for more severe violations.*

*   Do not spam severely; flooding a page to the point where scrolling is difficult constitutes severe spam and warrants a warning.
*   Do not use profanity in any form, including bypassing or partial censorship.
*   Do not delete a staff member's reminder or warning. This negates the purpose of the penalty unless:
    *   A staff member explicitly states the warning is nullified.
    *   The punishment is a two-day necroposting ban issued on October 17th, 2021, or earlier.
*   Do not advertise anything (including other wikis or social media) anywhere except your user profile. Posting videos is acceptable provided you do not ask viewers to subscribe or view other content.
*   Do not abuse wiki features for editing or badge farming. Minor cases that do not significantly damage a page will result in a warning.
*   Do not engage in edit wars, as this causes disruption. Consult a staff member regarding an edit if unsure of its permissibility. Short edit wars will receive a warning.
*   Do not initiate or encourage toxicity, drama, harassment, or bullying. Minor cases will result in a warning.

## **Block**
*Issued for severe violations or repeated minor offenses that warrant temporary removal from the wiki.*

*   Prolonged edit wars (especially after clear explanations are provided) will result in a ban. Consult staff before making contentious edits. **[Duration: One Week – One Year.]**
*   Do not post gory scenes. Unless the content is excessively graphic, this warrants a temporary block. **[Duration: One Week – One Year.]**
*   Do not start or encourage toxicity, harassment, bullying, or drama. Major cases will result in a ban. **[Duration: Two Weeks – One Year.]** Posts about leaving the wiki that exploit previous drama may also be grounds for a block.
*   Do not abuse wiki features for editing or badge farming. Major cases—especially those that significantly damage a page or are continuous—will result in a ban. **[Punishment: One Month – One Year.]**
*   Do not add or display fake content as if it were real. **[Duration: One Month – One Year]**
*   Vandalism is prohibited. Minor cases will result in a block. **[Duration: One Month – One Year.]**
*   For online safety, users must be at least 13 years old to have an account on the wiki. **[Duration: Until the user turns 13.]**
*   Do not use inappropriate usernames, profile pictures, or mastheads, including those that indicate impersonation. You will be blocked until these items are changed as requested. **[Duration: Until appropriate changes are made.]**
*   Do not attempt to evade a block by creating new accounts. If caught, all found alternate accounts will receive a permanent ban, and your main account will receive an extension of its original block duration. **[Duration: Twice the original block duration.]**

## **Indefinite Block**
*The most severe punishment, reserved for actions so egregious that the user should no longer be allowed on the wiki.*

*   Do not post or link to NSFW content, including gory, illegal, explicit, or sexual material.
*   Do not abuse wiki features for editing or badge farming in a manner that severely damages the wiki.
*   Do not start or encourage toxicity, harassment, bullying, or drama in very severe cases.
*   Do not add or display fake content as if it were real. Deliberately repeating false information to prove its legitimacy will result in an indefinite block.
*   Vandalism will result in an indefinite block if:
    *   It is intentional or severe.
    *   It occurs during your first edit, and will be treated as trolling regardless of the type of vandalism.
*   Trolling on this wiki is prohibited.
*   Impersonating any individual (wiki user or otherwise) is not allowed. This does not apply to cases concerning usernames.
*   Do not raid wikis, whether internal or external. If caught promoting a raid, you will be blocked and reported to the related or target wikis.
*   Do not attempt to evade a block by creating new accounts. If caught, and if your original ban reasons/durations were malicious enough or you have evaded twice, all found accounts will receive an indefinite block.
