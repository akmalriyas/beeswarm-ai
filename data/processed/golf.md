# Golf Mechanic

The Golf mechanic was introduced with the [2024-08-01 update](/wiki/Updates#2024-08-01 "Updates") as part of Bee Swarm Simulator's "[The Games](/wiki/The_Games "The Games")" event and was later removed in the [2025-04-30 update](/wiki/Updates#2025-04-30 "Updates").

This mechanic was primarily used throughout [Sun Bear's](/wiki/Sun_Bear "Sun Bear") 2024 questline, requiring players to hit golf balls into holes and activate targets. The quests were exclusive to Sun Bear during this period.

Golf elements are found exclusively in the following fields:
*   [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field")
*   [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field")
*   [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field")

***

## Golf Ball

A golf ball is an interactive object used in the golfing mechanic. It possesses a pollen meter, similar to [Puffshrooms](/wiki/Puffshroom "Puffshroom") and [Blooms](/wiki/Bloom "Bloom"). When this meter is full (by collecting [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") near the ball), the player can putt it.

The distance and height of the shot are determined by how far away the player stands from the ball. A moving golf ball collects pollen from flowers underneath it, but it does not interact with [mobs](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs") or [bees](/wiki/Bees "Bees"). It does have collision with the environment and Pink Fences.

Unlike Puffshrooms and Blooms, a golf ball only has one appearance and does not level up based on usage; there are no variations of golf balls.

### Spawning Rules
Golf Balls can only spawn in the Dandelion Field, Blue Flower Field, and Sunflower Field. A maximum of two golf balls can exist in any single field at one time. A new Golf Ball spawns after it is putted into a hole or sinks into mud.

***

## Hole

Holes are locations within the three designated fields where golf balls can be successfully putt.

### Effects
Putting a golf ball into a hole rewards 7 [Honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey") tokens initially, which scales based on the points and level of the golf ball used. After a successful putt, a new golf ball with a different level spawns in the area.

### Source
Holes are located in the Dandelion Field, Blue Flower Field, and Sunflower Field.

***

## Targets

Targets are interactive objects found in and around the three fields. Hitting them with a golf ball grants rewards.

### Mechanics
*   Targets can only be activated once; after being hit, they turn green and cannot provide further rewards.
*   They appear in and around the Dandelion Field, Blue Flower Field, and Sunflower Field.

## Mud

Mud is a terrain feature found on flowers in the Dandelion Field, Blue Flower Field, and Sunflower Field. It tints all underlying flowers brown.

### Effects
If a golf ball comes to a complete stop over mud, it sinks into the mud, causing a new golf ball to spawn at that location. This type of mud is identical to the pattern used during the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge").

***

## Pink Fences

Pink Fences are transparent pink barriers found in the Blue Flower Field and Sunflower Field.

### Effects
These fences block moving golf balls shot at them. They do not affect players, mobs, or any other mechanics.
