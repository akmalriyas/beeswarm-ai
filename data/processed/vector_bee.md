# Vector Bee

**Vector Bee** is a Colorless Mythic bee. It was described poetically as "A bee brought to life by an extremely complex trigonometric equation."

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Mythic |
| **Color** | Colorless |
| **Energy** | 45.6 |
| **Speed** | 16.24 |
| **Attack** | 5 |

Vector Bee's favorite treats are Sunflower Seeds, and it likes the Spider Field and Coconut Field. It dislikes the Pineapple Patch.

## Stats

*   Collects 18 Pollen in 4 seconds (equivalent to 4.5 per second).
*   Makes 144 Honey in 2.72 seconds.
*   **Bonuses:** +4 Attack, +8 Gather Amount, +16% Movespeed, +32% Convert Speed, +64 Convert Amount, +128% Energy.
*   **Gifted Hive Bonus:** +15% Mark Duration.

## Abilities

Vector Bee possesses three unique abilities: Pollen Mark+, Triangulate, and Mark Surge (when gifted).

### 🌟 Pollen Mark+
Marks a large random area on the Field for 8 seconds (+0.25s per Level). While standing in the marked area, all pollen collection increases by 50%. This mark can stack up to 3 times.

### Triangulate
Draws a triangle connecting the player, the bee, and an ability token. After 3 seconds, it collects Ability tokens and gains 10 Pollen (+2 per Level) from all Flowers inside the triangle. All active Marks within the triangle increase pollen by 50%.
*   If the triangle contains a Pollen Mark, it gains x2 White pollen.
*   If the triangle contains a Honey Mark or Festive Mark, it grants 50% instant conversion.
*   If the triangle contains a Precise Mark, it always deals critical hits.

### 🌟 Gifted Ability: Mark Surge
Causes all of the player's active Marks to collect 7 Pollen (+10% per Level) from all flowers in their radius. It also extends the duration of these Marks by 1 second (+0.1s per Level). Each mark can perform up to 5 surges.
*   When a Honey Mark or Festive Mark surges, the collected Pollen is Instantly Converted.
*   When a Precise Mark surges, it always deals Critical Hits.

### Trivia
*   Vector Bee's stats follow a Geometric Progression pattern, where each number is double the amount of the previous number.
*   It is the only Mythic bee that does not share its abilities with other bees and is also the only Mythic Bee without a Passive Ability.
*   Vector Bee is unique in having a different skin pattern on each side.
*   Its design is heavily inspired by mathematics:
    *   Trigonometry is implied by its description and triangular motifs on its head.
    *   Vectors reference Euclidean vectors (objects with magnitude and direction), as a triangle can be composed of three vectors.
    *   Triangulation is a method used to determine the location of a point through forming triangles from known points.
*   Vector Bee, Spicy Bee, Precise Bee, and Buoyant Bee all emit light when gifted.
*   It shares similar color schemes with Brave Bee.
*   The Triangulate ability is noted as being Onett's favorite due to its satisfying nature.
*   **Token Classification Note:** The Mark Surge token is classified by the game as a "mark" token, even though it does not create a physical mark like other tokens do. This means collecting it counts toward quests and passive abilities requiring "mark" tokens. Triangulate, however, is not considered a "mark" ability token. (For comparison, Fuzz Bombs are not classified as "bomb" tokens, and Honey Mark is not classified as a "honey" token.)
