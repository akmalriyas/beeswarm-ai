# Blueberry

[![Blueberry](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9e/Blueberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041412)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9e/Blueberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041412)

*"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."*

A **Blueberry** is a type of Treat that can be fed to a Bee to raise its bond by 25.

### Effects and Mechanics
When fed to certain specialized bees, the bond gain is doubled:
*   Bumble Bee
*   Cool Bee
*   Bubble Bee
*   Frosty Bee
*   Bucko Bee
*   Diamond Bee
*   Music Bee
*   Tadpole Bee
*   Buoyant Bee

**Gift Chance:**
Blueberries offer a small chance of gifting the bee, with probabilities based on rarity:
*   Rare bees: 0.0125% (1 in 8,000)
*   Epic bees: 0.01% (1 in 10,000)
*   Common and Legendary bees: 0.0083% (1 in 12,000)
*   Mythic bees: 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) per blueberry.

**Mutation:**
Feeding a Radioactive bee a Blueberry has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) chance to mutate the bee.

## Acquisition Methods

### Direct Sources
*   Using the **Blueberry Dispenser** inside the Blue HQ. The player receives one blueberry per blue bee in their hive per use.
*   As a reward from the **Ant Challenge**.
*   Redeeming specific valid codes:
    *   `1MLikes` (Gives 1 blueberry + other items).
    *   `15MMembers` (Gives 15 blueberries + other items).
*   As a reward from **Memory Match**.

### Drops and Farming
Blueberries can be obtained through various drops and farming activities:
*   **Farming:** Harvesting leaves in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Cactus Field, or Pine Tree Forest may yield blueberries.
*   **Beast Drops:** Dropped by Rhino Beetles, Mantises, and King Beetles.
*   **Tokens/Gifts:** As a drop from a Festive Gift or Festive Mark (Ability Tokens).
*   **Cub Buddy:** Received as a gift from your Cub Buddy.
*   **Sprout Growth:** As a reward for completely growing any sprout, excluding Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts. Planting a sprout in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, or Stump Field yields 6x more blueberries than other fields.
*   **Sparkles:** Harvesting sparkles (higher chances in blue fields).
*   **Puffshrooms:** Dropped by Puffshrooms.
*   **Planters:** Dropped from planters in blue fields. Non-blue fields containing a significant portion of blue flowers can also drop them (e.g., Clover Field, Cactus Field, Mountain Top Field). When used in blue fields, the Paper Planter drops multiple blueberry tokens in increments of 1, 3, 5, and 10.

## Quest Rewards
*   Gifted Bucko Bee may reward blueberries as a special reward.
*   Spirit Bear provides blueberries on the "Out-Questing Questions" quest (1 blueberry).

## Outdated Acquisition Methods
The following methods are no longer active:
*   Redeeming expired codes (`10mMembers`, `5mMembers`, `FourYearFiesta`).
*   As a drop from Hostage Chick.
*   Digging into the Beesmas Feast.
