# Candy Planter

The **Candy Planter** is a reusable planter introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. It can be purchased at Dapper Bear's Shop.

> "Alone, grows in about 4 hours and stores around 1 million Pollen. Grants bonus Gumdrops and Jelly Beans."

## Planter Details

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reusable** | Yes |
| **Growth Time (Base)** | ~4 hours |
| **Pollen Capacity** | 1,000,000 Pollen |
| **Pollen Multiplier** | +10% White Pollen |
| **Nectar Multiplier** | +20% Motivating Nectar |
| **Bonus Items** | Gumdrops, Jelly Beans |

### Growth Modifiers
*   The planter grows 25% faster when placed in the Strawberry Field, Pineapple Patch, or Coconut Field.
*   When harvested from these specific fields, it grants bonus Gumdrops and Jelly Beans.

## Harvesting Mechanics & Drops

### Puffshroom Chance
Harvesting a fully grown Candy Planter has a 1/7 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom will be between levels 4 and 5, with the following probability distribution:
*   Common Puffshroom: ~80% chance
*   Rare Puffshroom: ~16% chance
*   Epic Puffshroom: ~4% chance

### Token Drops (When Claimed)
A fully grown Candy Planter yields up to 14 tokens worth of items. Three specific tokens are guaranteed upon harvest:
*   Gumdrops: 5
*   Jelly Beans: 1
*   Ticket: 1

The remaining tokens are picked randomly from a non-exhaustive list, which includes (but is not limited to): Bonus, Other, and Special rewards.

### Field-Specific Rewards
Harvesting the Candy Planter in certain fields grants unique items:
*   **Stump Field:** If planted fully grown three times in a row, there is a guaranteed chance of receiving 10 Glues (1 week cooldown).
*   **Cactus Field:** Grants 1 Swirled Wax (1 month cooldown).
*   **Mountain Top Field:** Grants 1 Caustic Wax (1 month cooldown).
*   **Strawberry Field:** Has a very rare chance to drop 1 Pink Cupcake Sticker.
