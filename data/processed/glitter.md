# Glitter

[![Glitter](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/63/Glitter.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404021017)

> "Boosts the field you're standing in, granting +100% pollen for 15 minutes."

**Cooldown:** 15 minutes and 5 seconds

Glitter is a craftable inventory item introduced in the 2018-11-25 update. Upon activation, it grants a +100% pollen boost in the field where the player is standing for 15 minutes. If used in a field containing a Planter, that planter also receives a 25% growth rate boost until harvest.

Glitter has a cooldown of 15 minutes and 5 seconds between uses to prevent stacking buffs in a single field. It can be used in all fields except Challenge Fields (like the Ant Field), which results in the message: "You can't boost a challenge field."

## Acquisition Methods

### Crafting
Glitter can be crafted using the Blender with the following ingredients:
*   25 Moon Charms and 1 Magic Bean

### Gameplay & Rewards
*   Completing any type of Memory Match (yields increments of 1 or 5).
*   As a reward from donating to the Wind Shrine.
*   Obtained as a Festive Gift or Festive Mark token.
*   Purchasing the White Beesmas Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog for 100 Snowflakes and 3 Gingerbread Bears grants 3 Glitter, among other items.

### Sticker Stack Rewards
Glitter is obtained as a stack reward for adding specific Stickers to the Sticker Stack:
*   Sideways Spirit Bear rewards 1 glitter.
*   Shining Halo rewards 1 glitter.
*   Glowing Smile rewards 1 glitter.
*   Every Star Sign sticker rewards 1 glitter.

## Drops and Generation

### Mob Drops
Glitter can drop from various mobs, including:
*   Tunnel Bear
*   Coconut Crab
*   Stump Snail
*   Scorpion (Rare)
*   Mantis (Rare)
*   Diamond Aphid (Rare)
*   King Beetle (Uncommon)
*   Wild Windy Bee (Rare)

### Field & Environmental Drops
*   Rarely drops when farming in the Dandelion Field, Pineapple Patch, and Spider Field. Harvesting leaves in these fields may also yield glitter.
*   Can be generated by Fireflies during nighttime.
*   Obtained as a rare gift from a Cub Buddy.
*   A rare drop from Mythic Meteor Showers.

### Sprout & Plant Drops
*   All Sprouts (excluding the Debug Sprout) can generate a glitter token, with Moon Sprouts usually yielding it.
*   A rare drop from harvesting Sparkles or Puffshrooms.
*   Drops from certain Planters: Candy Planter, Tacky Planter, Festive Planter, Pesticide Planter.
    *   The Petal Planter may yield a bonus 10 Glitter (with a 1-week cooldown) when planted in any white field.

## Trivia and Mechanics

*   Glitter is one of four consumable items that grant a Field Boost (the others being Field Dice, Smooth Dice, and Loaded Dice).
*   It is the only consumable item among these four that displays glitter particles on the field when the boost is active.
*   This is the only consumable that allows the player to choose the specific type of Field Boost they wish to apply.
*   Spirit Bear mentions in her quest dialogue, "Spirit's Starter," that Glitter was once so rare that a single pouch cost 5 million honey.
*   The raw ingredients for crafting glitter are: 375 Strawberries, 375 Blueberries, 500 Pineapples, 25 Royal Jellies (or 25M Honey), and 1 Magic Bean (or 20 Tickets).
