# All-Time Top Red Pollen Collectors

## Mechanics

To check your personal red pollen total, players can step on the white pad located near the leaderboard. A message will appear displaying "You've collected 'x' Red Pollen," where 'x' is your lifetime total.

## Location

The All-Time Top Red Pollen Collectors board is found inside **Red HQ**, situated between the ladder and the Dark Scythe.
