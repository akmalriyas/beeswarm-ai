# First Edition Bee Flag

[![The decal for the First Edition Flag.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f0/1stflag.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404052534)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f0/1stflag.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404052534)

The First Edition Bee Flag is a cosmetic designation given to certain bees that were acquired or purchased during their initial release. This status is represented by a white and yellow "1st" flag displayed on top of the bee, which emits a sparkling effect. The flag has no impact on gameplay mechanics whatsoever.

This designation is currently available for: Bear Bee, Tabby Bee, Gummy Bee, Puppy Bee, Festive Bee, Digital Bee, and Windy Bee.

**Note:** If a player removes a First Edition Bee from their hive, the bee will retain its first edition status when it is added back to the hive later.

## Acquisition Details

*   **Bear Bee:** These were available during the first month after the game's launch. All First Edition Bear Bees automatically received an additional bonus of being gifted upon acquisition.
*   **Tabby Bee & Puppy Bee:** Purchased during their respective initial release sales at half price.
*   **Gummy Bee:** Obtained by completing all quests related to Gummy Bear during the Gummy Invasion Event.
*   **Festive Bee:** Can be obtained after completing all of Bee Bear's quests during the Beesmas 2018 Event, or purchased from the Festive Bee Pack for 1,600 Robux in the Robux Shop.
*   **Windy Bee:** Originally acquired as a special prize from the Wind Shrine, or purchased in the Robux Shop for 800 Robux before October 27, 2019.
*   **Digital Bee:** Obtained from Robo Bear's Shop behind Robo Bear, or by purchasing the Digital Bee Pack for 2,500 Robux during Beesmas 2022.

## Trivia

*   Onett (Developer) has stated that a First Edition Vicious Bee does not exist because there is insufficient space for the "1st" flag on its back due to its spikes.
*   The earliest bee to receive this designation was the Bear Bee, coinciding with the game's initial release.
*   Although First Edition Bear Bees are automatically gifted upon acquisition, their gifted status can still be removed if a player subsequently removes and re-adds them to the hive.
