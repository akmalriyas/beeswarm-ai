# Black Bear

**Black Bear** is a permanent Quest Giver located between the Red Cannon and Special Sprout Summoner. He is one of eight permanent Quest Bears in the game, alongside Brown Bear, Mother Bear, Panda Bear, Science Bear, Dapper Bear, Polar Bear, and Spirit Bear.

## Overview
| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Species** | Quest Bear |
| **Location** | Between Red Cannon and Special Sprout Summoner |
| **Bee Prerequisites** | None |
| **Color Scheme** | Fur: #212325 / Shade: #2d3235, Snout: #212324 #7b6941, Torso: #262a2c, Arms: #171d22, Legs: #191d21 |

Black Bear is often one of the first quest givers accessed. He provides quests focused primarily on **Pollen** collection to help players progress and achieve specific goals. After completing his main questlines, he offers repetitive quests hourly, similar to Brown Bear.

### Quest Focus
*   **Primary Resource:** Pollen (various colors: Red, Blue, White)
*   **Main Rewards:** Honey, Royal Jelly, various Eggs (Silver, Gold, Diamond, Mythic), Tickets, and specialized items.
*   **Total Reward Value (All Quests):** 2,352,532,300 Honey

### Quest Mechanics Notes
*   Diamond Egg quests reward 1 Royal Jelly every 4 quests (total of 5) plus the Diamond Egg.
*   Star Jelly quests reward 5 Tickets and 1 Royal Jelly per quest completed, except for "Solo On The Stump," which rewards 10 Tickets instead of 5.

## Dialogue & Lore Summary

Black Bear is a self-aware character who frequently references external media, the nature of the Roblox platform, and other bears. He introduces players to advanced beekeeping concepts like color specialization (Red/Blue Boosts), field bonuses, and the importance of diverse pollen sources for flavor.

**Key Lore Points:**
*   He proudly calls himself a "Honey Connoisseur."
*   He is acrophobic (afraid of heights) and arachnophobic (afraid of spiders).
*   He often discusses how different fields yield unique flavors, contrasting his refined palate with other bears.
*   His dialogue frequently references the limitations or peculiarities of the Roblox environment ("But this is ROBLOX! Anything goes!").

**Notable Quest Dialogue Insights:**
*   **Pollen Fetcher (Silver Egg):** Introduces the concept of bee classes (Basic, Rare, Epic, Legendary) and how egg types guarantee minimum rarity.
*   **Red Request (Gold Egg):** Explains that Red bees collect twice as much pollen from red flowers, and Blue bees collect 50% more from blue flowers.
*   **Quest Of Legends:** This quest is presented as the ultimate test of a beekeeper's mastery, requiring massive amounts of all three colored pollens to earn the Diamond Egg (which hatches into a Legendary Bee).

## Beesmas Event Quests

Black Bear participates in several seasonal events, notably providing quests for his Honey Wreath decoration.

### Black Bear's Honey Wreath
This quest involves decorating the map with a wreath that instantly converts all pollen in the player's bag into Honey Tokens when viewed every 30 minutes. Requirements vary by season:

*   **2025:** Collect 5,000 Pollen, Convert 5,000 Pollen at hive, Collect 500 Honey from Bloom Petals, Collect 50 Honey Tokens, Collect 5 Leaves tokens (Dandelion Field & Blue Flower Field).
*   **Winter 2024:** Collect 10,000 Pollen at Hive, Collect 2,500 Pollen from Dandelion Field and Sunflower Field, Collect 25 Honey Tokens, Collect 5 Leaves tokens.
*   **Winter 2023:** Collect 5,000 White Pollen, Collect 25 Honey Tokens, Collect 10 Leaves tokens, Collect 1 Sticker.
*   **Winter 2022:** Collect 1,000 White Pollen, Make 1,000 Honey, Collect 10 Honey Tokens, Collect 3 Sunflower Seed Tokens, Collect 1 Leaf token, Use 3 Snowflakes.
*   **Winter 2021:** Collect 750 Red Pollen and 750 White Pollen, Collect 10 Honey Tokens, Collect 3 Snowflake tokens.
*   **Winter 2020:** Collect 500 Pollen from Mushroom Field and 500 Pollen from Blue Flower Field, Collect 25 Honey Tokens.

## Trivia

*   Black Bear is the third bear to have a list of endless repeating quests.
*   He was the first quest giver to offer a Diamond Egg (followed by Panda Bear and Brown Bear).
*   He references external media in his dialogue, including "Breakin’ 2: Electric Boogaloo" and The Beatles' "Strawberry Fields Forever."
*   His total main questline requires over 10 billion pollen.
