# Legendary Bees

A collection of unique and powerful bees, each possessing distinct characteristics and abilities within the hive.

| Bee | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Baby Bee** | This little bee may not be skilled at all tasks yet, but it is guaranteed to bring joy (and luck) to the colony. |
| **Carpenter Bee** | A bee with a natural knack for construction; it built its own body out of wood. |
| **Demon Bee** | A powerful bee fueled by magical powers derived from pure hatred. |
| **Diamond Bee** | An extremely wealthy and pompous bee who possesses the money and talent to justify its attitude. |
| **Lion Bee** | Half lion, half bee. This magnificent creature is considered the king of both the jungle and the hive. |
| **Music Bee** | This bee's buzz is so beautiful it can bring anyone to tears. It uses this gift to motivate others in the colony. |
| **Ninja Bee** | This bee trained vigorously for years, becoming the swiftest bee that has ever lived. |
| **Shy Bee** | A talented but introverted bee who prefers working quietly and being left alone rather than socializing. |
