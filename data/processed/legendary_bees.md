# Legendary Bees Guide

Legendary bees are rare and powerful variants found within Bee Swarm Simulator. They possess unique characteristics, abilities, or lore that distinguish them from common hive members.

## Legendary Bee Roster

| Bee Name | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Baby Bee** | This little bee may not be proficient at all tasks yet, but it is guaranteed to bring joy and luck to the swarm. |
| **Carpenter Bee** | A skilled builder with a knack for construction; this bee built its own body out of wood. |
| **Demon Bee** | A powerful entity fueled by magical powers derived from pure hatred. |
| **Diamond Bee** | An extremely wealthy and pompous bee who possesses the money and talent to justify its attitude. |
| **Lion Bee** | Half lion, half bee. This creature reigns as the king of both the jungle and the beehive. |
| **Music Bee** | This bee's buzz is so beautiful it can bring anyone to tears. It uses this gift to motivate others. |
| **Ninja Bee** | This bee trained vigorously for years to become the swiftest insect that has ever lived. |
| **Shy Bee** | A talented but reserved bee who prefers working quietly and being left alone rather than socializing. |

***

## Legendary Probability Mechanics

The chance of obtaining a specific legendary bee depends on the item used (Egg or Royal Jelly). The probabilities listed below represent the likelihood of acquiring *any* legendary bee from that source, followed by the probability of getting a particular one.

| Item | Base Drop Chance | Legendary Bee Specific Probability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Egg** | 0.5% | 0.0625% |
| **Silver Egg** | 5% | 0.625% |
| **Gold Egg** | 20% | 2.5% |
| **Diamond Egg** | 95% | 11.875% |
| **Mythic Egg** | 0% | 0% |
| **Royal Jelly** | 3% | 0.375% |
