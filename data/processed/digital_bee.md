# Digital Bee (NPC)

[![Digital Bee's appearance.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f9/DigitalBee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20201123221445)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/f9/DigitalBee.png/revision/latest?cb=20201123221445) [](/wiki/File:DigitalBee.png)

Digital Bee's appearance.

> _Note: Do not confuse this NPC with the standard worker bee counterpart, [Digital Bee](/wiki/Digital_Bee "Digital Bee")._

**Digital Bee** (also known as **?NULL**, **0x4C4F4C**, and **> > ☺**) is a Non-Player Character found inside the [Computer](/wiki/Computer "Computer"). Digital Bee was central to the [Ready Player Two](/wiki/Ready_Player_Two "Ready Player Two") event, functioning as a quest NPC that provides three puzzles for the player to solve.

To interact with Digital Bee, players must first purchase and equip the [Strange Goggles](/wiki/Strange_Goggles "Strange Goggles").

Upon successfully completing Puzzle 3, the player can walk into the screen where Digital Bee was located and claim a ![Star Jelly](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e7/Star_Jelly.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/25?cb=20230404020706)[Star Jelly](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Star_Jelly "Royal Jelly"). The “[How to Program BASIC](/wiki/How_to_Program_BASIC "How to Program BASIC")” book also occupies Digital Bee's spot until the player exits the game.

Its visual appearance is a computer entity featuring a pixelated smiley face (or the symbols it displays), complete with an antenna, body, and wings similar to a regular bee.

## Puzzles
This information is also available on the [Computer](/wiki/Computer "Computer") page, but this guide serves as a reference for the mechanics. Note that every player has a unique code sequence.

| Puzzle | Guide | Example Code Sequence | Reward(s) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Puzzle 1** | Upon interacting with The Computer, Digital Bee flashes four symbols acting as a code. Go to the [Dandelion Field](/wiki/Dandelion_Field "Dandelion Field") and use your collector to reveal and change the appropriate symbols from left to right. The first symbol must be closest to the [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field "Sunflower Field"). Success triggers a sound, and the symbols turn into happy faces. | Digital Bee flashes `***?~ &`. The correct sequence for the Dandelion Field is `*****`, `?`, `~`, and `&`. | 1 Royal Jelly, 512 Honey |
| **Puzzle 2** | After Puzzle 1 is complete, Digital Bee disappears from The Computer and begins teleporting between the Sunflower, [Clover](/wiki/Clover_Field "Clover Field"), and [Mushroom Field](/wiki/Mushroom_Field "Mushroom Field"). At each field, it flashes three symbols: `& ~ ***`. Players must uncover these hidden symbols with their collector and change them into the correct sequence. The universal combination is: Sunflower = `&`, Clover = `*****`, Mushroom = `~`. Success triggers a sound and symbol change. | The code sequence is constant for all players, as detailed in the guide above. | 1 Royal Jelly, 8 Gumdrops |
| **Puzzle 3** | After Puzzle 2 is complete, Digital Bee flashes four letters: **S**unflower, **D**andelion, **M**ushroom, and **C**lover. In each of these four fields, a symbol can be revealed using the collector. Players must reveal the symbols in the order flashed by Digital Bee, moving left to right in the [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field "Blue Flower Field"). The first symbol should be closest to the [Basic Bee Gate](/wiki/Basic_Bee_Gate "Basic Bee Gate"). Success triggers a sound and symbol change. | Digital Bee flashes **DSMC**. Dandelion shows `*****`, Sunflower shows `&`, Mushroom shows `?`, Clover shows `~`. Therefore, the correct combination for this example is `*** &?~`. | 1 Atomic Treat, 8 Tickets |

## Trivia
*   Digital Bee is unique among NPCs in that it communicates exclusively through symbols rather than spoken dialogue.
*   Some of the sounds Digital Bee makes are modified versions of Windows XP sounds, including a slightly altered startup sound upon its initial appearance.
*   Prior to the [2022-12-26 update](/wiki/Updates#2022-12-26 "Updates"), Digital Bee and [Marshmallow Bee](/wiki/Marshmallow_Bee "Marshmallow Bee") were the only bees players could not obtain for their hive. Since Digital Bee is now obtainable, this restriction applies only to Marshmallow Bee.
*   Digital Bee is the sole NPC bee that provides puzzles rather than standard quests.
*   It is the only NPC capable of directly modifying the flowers in various fields.
