# Ant Field

The **Ant Field** is a specialized field located past the Ant Gate and near the Top Ant Exterminators leaderboard. Accessing this field requires starting the Ant Challenge, which summons waves of ants.

## Field Statistics

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Field Color** | Assorted |
| **Bee Prerequisites** | 20 bees |
| **Nectar Type** | Invigorating Nectar |
| **Dimensions** | 32 × 12 flowers |
| **Total Flower Count** | 384 |

### Bee Preferences
*   **Likes:** Lion Bee
*   **Dislikes:** None

## Field Details and Mechanics

The Ant Field can produce honey and ticket tokens while collecting pollen. The only bee that likes this field is the Lion Bee; all other bees are neutral to it.

**Note on Planting:** Planters cannot be planted in the Ant Field, although developers have confirmed that the field contains Invigorating Nectar.

### Flower Composition
The field contains 384 flowers of three types: white, red, and blue. All flowers are of a single size.

| Size | White Flowers | Red Flowers | Blue Flowers | Total |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Single** | 77 | 154 | 154 | 384 |
| **Double** | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **Triple** | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

*Note: Flower counts within the table may vary slightly due to rounding. In-game, all flowers are randomly generated and may not precisely align with these percentages.*

## Boosts and Pollination

*   **Ant Pass:** Using an Ant Pass to start the Ant Challenge grants 100% Instant Ant Field Conversion.
*   **Pollen Boosting:** There is no way to boost pollen collection in the Ant Field. Flowers can still be pollinated normally.

## Trivia

*   **Pollen Collection Outside Challenge:** Pollen can be collected from the Ant Field even outside of the challenge by using a Sticker-Seeker or spawning flames with a Scythe.
*   **Unique Characteristics:** The Ant Field is unique among fields:
    *   It does not have its own dedicated badge.
    *   It is the only field that exclusively contains single flowers.
    *   The flowers on this field contain 50 pollen per flower. Instead of increasing by 15 pollen per tier, they increase by 10 per pollination tier up to a maximum of 90 (the pollen multiplier remains standard).
    *   It cannot be boosted with Glitter.
    *   A Magic Bean cannot be planted here.
    *   Clouds cannot be summoned.
    *   It did not receive a Market Boost from the Boost Market.
    *   It cannot gain Field Wind from the Wind Shrine, nor can planters be placed in it.
*   **Size Comparison:** Despite having 384 flowers, the Ant Field is one of the smallest fields in the game (smaller than Pumpkin Patch with 561 flowers and Stump Field with 563 flowers).
*   **Challenge Fields:** It is one of five fields exclusively used for a challenge. The others are the Mixed Brick Field, White Brick Field, Blue Brick Field, and Red Brick Field.
*   **Glitching:** Players can glitch into the Ant Field without triggering the actual challenge by using slow internet connections or abilities like Bear Morph and/or Haste+.
*   **Design Note:** Despite being impossible to boost, the field possesses an unused Field Boost icon.
