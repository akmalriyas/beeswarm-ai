# Strawberries

[![Strawberry](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6d/Strawberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041355)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/6d/Strawberry.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404041355)

_"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."_

A **Strawberry** is a type of treat used to raise a bee's bond. When fed to a standard bee, it increases the bond by 25 points.

### Bee Mechanics
When strawberries are fed to specific types of bees—including Rascal, Rad, Rage, Riley, Baby, Stubborn, Shy, or Spicy Bees—the amount of bond gained is doubled.

Strawberries also carry small chances of gifting a bee:
*   **Rare Bees:** 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance per strawberry.
*   **Epic Bees:** 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance per strawberry.
*   **Common & Legendary Bees:** 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance per strawberry.
*   **Mythic Bees:** 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance per strawberry.

Additionally, feeding a radioactive bee a strawberry carries a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) risk of causing a mutation.

## Ways to Obtain
Strawberries can be acquired through various methods:

**Field & Dispenser Sources:**
*   Finding a hidden strawberry token behind the largest strawberry in Strawberry Field.
*   Using the Strawberry Dispenser near Red HQ (yields one strawberry per red bee in the hive).
*   Farming in Mushroom Field, Strawberry Field, Rose Field, Cactus Field, and Pepper Patch. Harvesting Leaves in these fields may also yield strawberries.

**Rewards & Drops:**
*   As a reward from the Ant Challenge or Memory Match.
*   As drops from Ladybugs, Scorpions, and King Beetles.
*   As a gift from a Cub Buddy.
*   From harvesting Sparkles (higher chances in red fields).
*   As a drop from Puffshrooms in red fields or fields with high amounts of red flowers (Clover Field, Cactus Field, Mountain Top Field).

**Sprout Farming Multipliers:**
When planting sprouts in specific fields, the strawberry yield is multiplied:
*   Mushroom Field, Rose Field, and Pepper Patch: 4x multiplier.
*   Strawberry Field: 8x multiplier.

**Planters & Gifts:**
*   From certain planters (most likely in red fields). The Red Clay Planter always drops strawberries upon harvest at full growth.
*   As a drop from Festive Gifts or Festive Marks.

**Codes & Stickers:**
*   Redeeming specific valid codes (e.g., `15MMembers` grants 15 strawberries plus other items).
*   Receiving stack rewards for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
    *   Pink Cupcake Sticker: Rewards 50 strawberries.
    *   Pale Heart Sticker: Rewards 50 strawberries.
