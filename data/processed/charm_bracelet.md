# Charm Bracelet

The **Charm Bracelet** is a Level 12 Beequip with an equip limit of one. It has no color requirement and can only be equipped to Legendary bees.

"Bedazzles your bees with a shimmering assortment of symbols that are lucky somehow."
🎄Beesmas

## Stats

### General Information
*   **Color:** Any
*   **Limit:** 1
*   **Ability:** Melody (Requires Caustic Points Only)
*   **Potential:** The true potential of a Beequip cannot be viewed in-game; players only see the potential rounded to the nearest 1 ★ (or 0.5 ★ in Dapper Bear's Shop).

## Acquisition Methods

*   As a rare drop from the Petal Planter.
*   Available as a product in Dapper Bear's Beequip Shop.

### Outdated Ways
*   Buying the Charming Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.

## Trivia
The Charm Bracelet, tied with the Lump Of Coal, has the most possible hive bonuses out of any Beequip, at eight.
