The Global Top Stick Bug Fighters leaderboard is one of the game's leaderboards. It tracks the top 100 scores achieved by fighting Stick Bugs in the Stick Bug Challenge. While it displays only 10 positions at a time, players can scroll down to view all entries up to the 100th place.

When a player stands on the white pad in front of the leaderboard, they can see their personal high score for the Stick Bug Challenge displayed as "Your highscore: [ScoreVariable]".

### History and Resets
The Top Stick Bug Fighters leaderboard has undergone several resets due to game glitches or exploit usage:
*   **2019-09-27:** The initial glitch issues with the fight were fixed, leading to a reset.
*   **2022-12-26:** A subsequent reset occurred.
*   **2024-01-12:** The leaderboard was recently reset again due to exploits involving using attack buffs in Robo Bear's Challenge to drastically increase damage.

### Location
The leaderboard is situated right behind the Stick Bug area, on top of the largest mushroom in the Mushroom Field.

There are several ways to reach this location:
*   **Cannon/Glider:** Use a Red, Blue, or Yellow Cannon paired with either a Parachute or Glider.
*   **Aerial Descent:** Parachuting or gliding from a tall area, such as near the Lion Bee Gate.
*   **Obby Route:** Completing the Mushroom Field Obby and jumping/gliding to the tallest mushroom is also a viable method.

### Achievements
*   zlib was the first player to achieve a score of 100 million or more.
*   Rara0w0x3 was the first player to reach a score of 1 billion or more.

### Trivia
*   This leaderboard, along with the Highest Snowbear Level and Fastest Crab Slayers leaderboards, is one of only three leaderboards that ranks "bosses."
*   Following the reset on 2024-01-12, the score displayed when stepping on the podium reflects your score *before* the reset, but this score does not appear in the leaderboard rankings.
