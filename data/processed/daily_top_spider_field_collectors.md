# Daily Top Spider Field Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Spider Field Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards available in Bee Swarm Simulator, accessible via the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page. This leaderboard tracks how much pollen players have collected specifically within the Spider Field during a single day.

## Mechanics and Viewing
*   The leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time. Players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place.
*   The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes
Prizes are awarded when the leaderboard resets, based on the player's ranking:

*   **Top 25:** Awarded a [Spider Field Stamp Sticker](/wiki/Sticker#Sticker_Index "Sticker").
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 25 [Tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket").
