# Present (Beesmas 2025)

Presents have returned for Beesmas 2025. Their primary function remains adding [Ornaments] to the [Beesmas Tree] when given to NPCs. However, players cannot give a present to the [Sun Bear], as it is not present during this event.

## Acquisition Methods

*   **Bee Bear Quests:** Multiple quests from the [Bee Bear] award 10 [Presents], along with other rewards.
*   **Bee Bear’s Catalog:** The [Bee Bear’s Catalog] sells 1 [Present] for 250 [Snowflakes] and 10 [Gingerbread Bears].
*   **Onett Hive Claim:** [Onett] gives 1 [Present] (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event period.
*   **Galentine Shrine Donation:** Donating [Strawberries] to the [Wind Shrine] has a chance to give 1 [Present], along with other rewards.
*   **Robo Party Rank:** Completing [Robo Party]'s rank 25 for the first time gives 1 [Present], along with other rewards.
