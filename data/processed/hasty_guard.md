# Hasty Guard

![Hasty Guard](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/10/Hasty_Guard.png/revision/latest?cb=20190403022730 "Hasty Guard")

## Description
A right shoulder pad crafted by a Hasty Bee.

The **Hasty Guard** is a guard purchasable in the Pro Shop and equipped on the right shoulder.

> "_A right shoulder pad crafted by a Hasty Bee._"

## Stats & Cost
| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cost** | 300,000 Honey and 5 Moon Charms |
| Capacity Bonus | +15,000 |
| Bee Movespeed | +1% |
| Player Movespeed | x1.1 |

## Crafting Ingredients
*   300,000 Honey
*   5 Moon Charms

## Trivia
*   This is the only guard that provides a Player Movespeed boost.
*   It is one of four guards available in the Pro Shop; the others are the Looker Guard, Bomber Guard, and Brave Guard.
