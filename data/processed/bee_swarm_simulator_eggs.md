# Egg Types Overview

Eggs are inventory items in Bee Swarm Simulator that players drag into their hive to hatch a bee, donate to the Wind Shrine, or use with the Sticker Printer. They are also essential for starting gameplay.

Eggs come in various types, some offering chances at different bee rarities, while others guarantee a specific bee type. Currently, there are 6 main egg types (or 11 if counting Gifted variants), excluding specialized eggs like Plastic Eggs, Flight of the Bumble Egg, or Swarming Egg of the Hive.

**Hatching Mechanics:**
Since the 2019-09-28 update, players do not require a vacant hive slot to hatch an egg. An egg can be hatched on top of an existing bee; the level of that bee remains unchanged, acting like Royal Jelly.

## General Egg Types

*   Basic Egg
*   Silver Egg
*   Gold Egg
*   Diamond Egg
*   Mythic Egg
*   Star Egg

### Basic Egg

The most common egg type in the game. It primarily hatches into a Basic Bee (87% chance). Rare outcomes include:
*   Rare Bee (10% chance)
*   Epic Bee (2.5% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (0.5% chance)
*   Gifted Bee (0.4% chance), with a 0.348% chance of hatching a Gifted Basic Bee.

**Restrictions:** Mythic Bees and Event Bees cannot be hatched from this egg. This egg also cannot be used on a Basic Bee.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   First-time players receive one free in their inventory.
*   Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop near the Sunflower Field, priced at 1,000 Honey (up to 10,000,000 Honey). The price scales up with each purchase.
*   Receiving 1 Basic Egg for adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack.

### Silver Egg

This egg most likely hatches into a Rare bee (64.9% chance), but can also hatch into:
*   Epic Bee (30% chance)
*   Legendary Bee (5% chance)
*   Mythic Bee (0.1% chance)

**Restrictions:** Basic Bees or Event Bees cannot be hatched from this egg.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 50 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 100 Robux.
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Silver Egg questlines.
*   Receiving a reward from Brown Bear's 25th quest.
*   Completing the 25th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine rewards you 1 Silver Egg.
*   Drops: Mantis (low chance, increments of 1 or 5), King Beetle and Tunnel Bear (low chance), Rare Sprouts (low chance).
*   Rare drops from Leaves while collecting pollen in the Clover Field.
*   Drop from Festive Planter.
*   Rare drop from Stockings.
*   Reward from Winter Memory Match.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Expired code "PreUpdate" reward.
*   Bubble Bee Man's Beesmas 2020 quest reward.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog during various Beesmas events (2020, 2021, 2022, 2024).

### Gifted Silver Egg

This egg shares the same probabilities as a standard Silver Egg, but it *always* hatches the bee into its gifted variant.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances upon subsequent defeats).
*   Reward for ranking in the Daily Top Legendary Bee Pollen Collectors (Top 25).
*   Completing Black Bear's "Getting Stumped" quest.
*   Completing the 100th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine rewards you 1 Gifted Silver Egg.
*   Drops: Werewolf (exceptionally rare), Stump Snail (rare chance), Scorpion (exceptionally rare), Sticker Sprout.

### Gold Egg

This egg most likely hatches into an Epic bee (79% chance), but can also hatch into:
*   Legendary Bee (20% chance)
*   Mythic Bee (1% chance)

**Restrictions:** Basic Bees, Rare bees, or Event bees cannot be hatched from this egg.
*(Note: 5 Gold Eggs are required to craft the Honey Mask.)*

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 100 Tickets each.
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 200 Robux each.
*   Reward from Robo Bear Challenge (rare).
*   Reward from Extreme Memory Match (rare).
*   Token form in Werewolf's Cave, guarded by two Cave Monsters.
*   Completing Black Bear's and Panda Bear's Gold Egg questlines.
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 50th, 340th, and 390th quests.
*   Completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 50th quest rewards 1 Gold Egg.
*   Capturing Commando Chick 10 times.
*   Drops: King Beetle (low chance), Scorpion (extremely low chance), Epic Sprouts (rare chance).
*   Rare drops from Leaves while collecting pollen in the Clover Field.
*   Rare drop from Ticket Planter, Candy Planter, Red Clay Planter, Festive Planter, Pesticide Planter, and Petal Planter.
*   Rare drop from Stockings.
*   Low chance to drop from Festive Nymph.
*   Reward from Winter Memory Match.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Opening the Golden Present gave 1 gold egg.
*   Purchasing the Black Friday Bundle for 800 Robux.
*   Donation reward from Wind Shrine (Spirit Bear's Beesmas 2020/2021).
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog during various Beesmas events (2020, 2021, 2024).
*   Opening the Gilded Gift Box gave 1 Gold Egg.

### Gifted Gold Egg

This egg shares the same probabilities as a standard Gold Egg, but it *always* hatches the bee into its gifted variant.

**Restrictions:** This type of egg cannot be used to craft the Honey Mask.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances upon subsequent defeats).
*   Reward for ranking in the Daily Top Epic Bee Pollen Collectors (Top 25).
*   Playtime Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 50 Gifted Bucko Bee quests or 50 Gifted Riley Bee quests awards 1 Gifted Gold Egg.
*   Completing 275 Brown Bear quests awards 1 Gifted Gold Egg.
*   Completing the 150th Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine rewards 1 Gifted Gold Egg.
*   Drops: Stump Snail (very rare chance), Coconut Crab (very rare chance), Mantis (exceptionally rare chance).

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Opening the Mondo Present during Beesmas 2019 gave 2 gifted gold eggs.

### Diamond Egg

This egg most likely hatches into a Legendary bee (95% chance), but has a rare chance to hatch into a Mythic bee (5% chance).

**Restrictions:** Basic Bee, Rare bee, Epic bee, or Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg.
*(Note: 5 Diamond Eggs are required to craft the Diamond Mask.)*

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Token location: Past the Bear Gate, under the platform where Onett stands (requires Glider or Parachute).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 400 Robux each.
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 400 Tickets each.
*   Reward from Robo Bear Challenge (rare).
*   Reward from Extreme Memory Match (rare).
*   Completing Black Bear's Diamond Egg questline.
*   Completing Panda Bear's fifth Star Treat/Ant quest, "Colossal Combat".
*   Completing Dapper Bear's tenth quest, "The Long Haul".
*   Completing Brown Bear's 75th and 525th quests.
*   Completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 75th quest.
*   Drops: Stump Snail, Diamond Aphids, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, Coconut Crab (rare chance).
*   Spiders, Mantises, Werewolves (very rare chance).
*   Rare drop from Legendary sprouts.
*   Guaranteed drop from Supreme sprouts.
*   Extremely rare chance of dropping from Leaves while collecting pollen in the Clover Field.
*   Very rare reward from catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Rare drop from Festive Planter.
*   Reward from Winter Memory Match.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing certain offers from the Robux Shop (e.g., Memorial Day Deal, Summer Treat Pack, Diamond Moon Pack).
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog during various Beesmas events (2020, 2021, 2022, 2024).

### Gifted Diamond Egg

This egg shares the same probabilities as a standard Diamond Egg, but it *always* hatches the bee into its gifted variant.

**Restrictions:** This type of egg cannot be used to craft the Diamond Mask.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances upon subsequent defeats).
*   Reward for ranking in the Daily Top Honey Gift Receivers (Top 25).
*   Quest Grandmaster badge reward from donating tickets to the Wind Shrine.
*   Completing 100, 250, 500, 750, and 1,000 Gifted Bucko Bee or Gifted Riley Bee quests awards one gifted diamond egg each.
*   Completing 425 Brown Bear quests awards one Gifted Diamond Egg.
*   Completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 200th quest awards one Gifted Diamond Egg.
*   Drops: Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, Coconut Crab (extremely rare chance).

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Opening the Mondo Present during Beesmas 2019 gave 2 gifted diamond eggs.

### Mythic Egg

This egg *always* hatches into a Mythic bee. The player cannot obtain any other type of bee from this egg, though a Gifted Mythic bee is possible (very rare).

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Reward for ranking in the Daily Top Memory Matchers (Top 25).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 1,200 Robux each (limited to 3 eggs per player).
*   Purchasing from the Ticket Tent for 2,500 Tickets each.
*   Reward from Robo Bear Challenge (very rare).
*   Drops: Extreme Memory Match (extremely rare chance).
*   Completing Black Bear's 75th quest, "Myth in The Making".
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 15th quest, "More Than Myth".
*   Rewards from Brown Bear's 100th, 200th, and 400th quests.
*   Completing the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's 300th quest awards one Mythic Egg.
*   Capturing Commando Chick for the 50th time guarantees a Mythic Egg.
*   Drops: Stump Snail, Mondo Chick, Coconut Crab (extremely rare chance). *Mondo Chick has a higher drop chance when defeated faster.*
*   Extremely rare chance of dropping when catching a Mythic Meteor Shower.
*   Rare drop from Festive Planter.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 500 Snowflakes and 100 Gingerbread Bears.
*   Completing Gummy Bear’s Goo Year’s Beacon quest during Beesmas 2025 rewards you 1 Mythic Egg.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog during various Beesmas events (2020, 2021, 2022, 2024).
*   Opening the Mondo Gift Box awarded 5 Mythic Eggs.
*   Opening the Mythic Gift Box awarded 1 Mythic Egg.
*   Purchasing certain expired packs in the Robux Shop (e.g., Mythic Pack, Jumbo June Pack, Black Friday Bundle).
*   Opening the Mythic Present or Mondo Present.
*   Opening specific seasonal Gift Boxes during Beesmas events.

### Gifted Mythic Egg

This egg shares the same probabilities as a standard Mythic Egg, but it *always* hatches the bee into its gifted variant.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Reward for ranking in the Daily Top Rare Bee Pollen Collectors (Top 25).
*   Completing 500 Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine quests.
*   Nearly impossible drop from Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 10,000 Snowflakes and 150 Gingerbread Bears.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing the Hefty Honeyday Bundle or Merry Mondo Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas events.
*   Opening the Mondo Gift Box from various Beesmas seasons awarded 1 Gifted Mythic Egg.
*   Purchasing certain limited-time offers in the Robux Shop (e.g., Mondo Robo Bundle, Festive Frenzy Haul).

### Choose-A-Mythic Egg

This egg allows the player to select any specific Mythic Bee they want upon hatching.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing the Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul from the Robux Shop for 4,500 Robux.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Expired purchases during Winter Beesmas 2024 or Cyber Monday Mythic Pack.

### Star Egg

This egg hatches into any type of gifted bee that is not already in the player's hive (excluding gifted Event bees). If all non-event gifted bees are owned, it hatches a random gifted bee. It will only hatch a Gifted Mythic Bee once all other eligible gifted bees are present.

**Acquisition Methods:**
*   Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg (lower chances upon subsequent defeats).
*   Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 eggs per player).
*   Drops: Mythic Meteor Shower (exceptionally rare chance), King Beetle (exceptionally rare chance).
*   Completing Mother Bear's first ten quests ("Seven To Seven").
*   Completing Panda Bear's first ten star treat/ant quests ("Royal Rumble").
*   Rare drop from Festive Planter.
*   Exceptionally rare drop from Party Mechsquito.

**Outdated Acquisition Methods:**
*   Purchasing certain limited-time offers in the Robux Shop (e.g., Summer Star Pack, Happy Honeyday Pack).

Always hatches into a Bear Bee (100%).
*   **Acquisition:** Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux. *(Historically cost 1,000 Robux, reduced to 650/600 Robux during early events.)*
*   Obtained via Bear Bee Voucher from players who bought a Basic Egg.

### Cobalt Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Cobalt Bee (100%).
*   **Acquisition:** Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.

### Crimson Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Crimson Bee (100%).
*   **Acquisition:** Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 250 Tickets.

### Digital Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Digital Bee (100%).
*   **Acquisition:** Purchased from Robo Bear's Shop after obtaining a Diamond Cog Amulet, 7,777,777 Honey, and 5 of all Drives. *(Historically available in the Digital Bee Pack for 2,500 Robux.)*

### Festive Bee Egg
Always hatches into a Festive Bee (100%).
*   **Acquisition:** Purchasing at the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.
*   Completing the 15th quest of Bee Bear ("Festive Fight Finale") during Beesmas 2020. *(If player lacks the bee, this grants 500 Tickets; otherwise, it grants 500 Tickets.)*

*   *(Historically available in the Festive Bee Pack for 800 Robux.)*
