## Overview
[ ![MONDOB.B.M.M](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/00/Mondo_Bubble_Bee_Man_Mask.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20181225185556) ](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/0/00/Mondo_Bubble_Bee_Man_Mask.png/revision/latest?cb=20181225185556 "MONDOB.B.M.M")

The **Mondo B.B.M. Mask** is a [mask](/wiki/Category:Hats "Category:Hats") that serves as the larger version of the standard [B.B.M. Mask](/wiki/B.B.M._Mask "B.B.M. Mask").

### Acquisition
This mask was obtained by completing the "B.B.M. Mission" quest, which was given out by [Bubble Bee Man](/wiki/Bubble_Bee_Man "Bubble Bee Man") during the [Beesmas 2018 Event](/wiki/Category:Beesmas "Category:Beesmas"). This event took place in the [30 Bee Zone](/wiki/Bear_Gate "Bear Gate").

To obtain this quest, players were required to complete the entire (now unavailable) Beesmas 2018 questline associated with [Bee Bear](/wiki/Bee_Bear "Bee Bear"). If the prerequisite quests were not completed, Bubble Bee Man would respond with a confused phrase. Upon successful completion of the mission, the mask automatically replaced the player's current hat. Players can currently reequip this mask by speaking to BBM in the 30 Bee Zone.

## Trivia
*   This mask, along with the standard B.B.M. Mask, is one of the only masks that cannot be purchased and lacks a description.
*   The Mondo B.B.M. Mask, the [Fire Mask](/wiki/Fire_Mask "Fire Mask"), and the [Demon Mask](/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") are among the few masks that provide [Bee Attack](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Attack "System Page") buffs.
*   This item, the B.B.M. Mask, and the [Mondo Belt Bag](/wiki/Mondo_Belt_Bag "Mondo Belt Bag") are unique mondo versions of an accessory.
