# Cogs

Cogs are inventory items specifically introduced for use during the Robo Bear Challenge. They should not be confused with 7-Pronged Cog.

> "Used for purchases during Robo Bear's Challenge. Removed when the challenge ends."

**Cooldown:** N/A

## Overview
Cogs were added to the game in the December 26, 2022 update. They function as a temporary currency used exclusively within the Robo Bear Challenge. Players use Cogs to purchase upgrades, acquire Drives, and reroll bees or upgrades during the challenge period. Once the Robo Bear Challenge concludes, all collected Cogs are removed from the player's inventory.

Additionally, players can accumulate 250,000 total Cogs to unlock the Robo Cub skin for their Cub Buddy.

## Acquisition Methods
### Current Methods
*   **Robo Bear Quests:** Completing quests within the Robo Bear Challenge is the primary way to earn Cogs.
    *   Starting a challenge grants 10 Cogs immediately.
    *   The difficulty of the quest determines the amount of Cogs awarded.
    *   Bonus Cogs may be received for completing a quest within the first two minutes, or through specific upgrades purchased in the challenge.
*   **Golden Cogmower:** Defeating a Golden Cogmower guarantees at least 14 Cogs, with the potential to scale up to 18 Cogs per kill.

### Outdated Methods
*   Cogs were previously awarded as a reward for completing Bubble Bee Man's Naughty List during Beesmas 2024.

## Trivia
*   Cogs were initially the first item added that was exclusively rewarded in a challenge while also serving as a temporary form of currency. This status changed when Cogs became obtainable outside of the Robo Bear Challenge via Bubble Bee Man.
*   The visual design of the Cog item bears a slight resemblance to the cog found on top of the Noob Shop.
