# Peppermint Antennas

Peppermint Antennas are a Level 7 Beesmas Beequip, granting the wearer a soothing cooling sensation. They have an equip limit of three and no color requirement.

## Stats Overview

The base stats for Peppermint Antennas are:
*   **Convert Amount:** 100%
*   **Gather Amount:** 100%
*   **Movespeed:** +1%
*   **Ability Pollen:** Random value between 20% - 60%
*   **Ability Rate:** Random value between 20% - 60%
*   **Bee Ability Rate:** Variable (See potential tiers)

***Note:*** *The values shown in the detailed tables below represent various statistical iterations of this Beequip, including base stats and upgraded/potential ranges. The exact values can vary significantly due to game generation quirks.*

## Ways to Obtain

Peppermint Antennas can be obtained in the following ways:

*   As a drop from the King Beetle.

### Outdated/Historical Methods
*   From the Gummy Gift Box.
*   From the Peppermint Gift Box.
*   Giving Onett a Present.
*   As a reward for completing Bee Bear's "Festive Feast With the Whole Family" Beesmas 2020 quest.
