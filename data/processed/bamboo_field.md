## Field Description and Mechanics
The Bamboo Field is a blue field located past the Basic Bee Gate, to the right of the Spider Field, and two fields away from the Strawberry Field. It is protected by two Rhino Beetles (one level 2, one level 3).

This field contains 702 flowers in single, double, and triple sizes. When a planter is placed here, it grants Comforting Nectar. Collecting pollen in this field can spawn tokens such as Honey, Blueberry, Ticket, Blue Boost, Magic Bean, and Blue Extract.

## Obtaining Boosts
The Bamboo Field can be enhanced using various in-game items and mechanics:

*   **Blue Field Booster:** Grants a Bamboo Field Boost (+100% pollen) for 15 minutes.
*   **Glitter:** Adds +100% Bamboo Field pollen to the current boost (if not maxed out) for 15 minutes.
*   **Dice:** Using Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice can add +100% to 300% of Bamboo Field pollen for 15 minutes, depending on the dice type and existing stack.
*   **Field Winds:** Obtained via the Wind Shrine or codes. They provide a base of +20% instant conversion and grant +5% instant conversion and +15% pollen per stack, stacking up to 13 times.
*   **King Beetle Amulet:** If equipped with a Bamboo Field Boost, this amulet can further boost the field by +1% to +100%.

### Historical Boost Methods (No Longer Available)
The following methods for obtaining boosts are no longer possible:
*   Receiving presents from specific NPCs (e.g., Panda Bear, Gifted Bucko Bee, Onett).
*   Redeeming certain expired codes (e.g., Teespring, WeekExtension, FestiveFinale).
*   Purchasing a Bamboo Field Market Boost from the Boost Market, which provided x1.5 Field Capacity, x1.25 Pollen, x1.1 Blue Pollen, and x1.25 Convert Rate at Hive.

## Trivia
*   Bamboo Field is one of six fields accessible to Fireflies, alongside Cactus Field, Pineapple Patch, Rose Field, Spider Field, and Strawberry Field.
*   Two Royal Jellies can be found near the field: one under the ramp to the Brave Bee Gate, and another at the end of an Obstacle Course starting between this field and the Spider Field.
*   The bamboo stems feature claw marks, presumably left by Panda Bear.
*   If a player stands on the corner closest to the Spider Field while a Snowbear is summoned, the Snowbear can throw snowballs and damage the player.
*   The three bees that dislike this field (Riley Bee, Precise Bee, Windy Bee) each have another disliked field: Riley/Precise dislike Pine Tree Forest; Windy dislikes Strawberry Field.
