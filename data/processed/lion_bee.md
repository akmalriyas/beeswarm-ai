# Lion Bee

**Lion Bee** is a Colorless Legendary bee, described as "Half lion, half bee. This is the king of both the jungle and bee hive."

## General Information
*   **Rarity:** Legendary
*   **Color:** Colorless
*   **Favorite Treat:** Pineapples
*   **Likes:** Ant Field, Pineapple Patch
*   **Dislikes:** Clover Field

## Base Stats
| Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Energy | 60 |
| Speed | 19.6 |
| Attack | 10 |

### Collection & Production Rates (Base)
*   **Pollen Collection:** Collects 20 Pollen in 4 seconds.
*   **Honey Production:** Makes 160 Honey in 2 seconds.

## Stats and Bonuses
Lion Bee provides the following bonuses:
*   +200% Energy
*   +40% Movespeed
*   +50% Convert Speed
*   +100 Convert Amount
*   +10 Gather Amount
*   +9 Attack

**Gifted Hive Bonus:** +5% Gifted Bee Pollen

## Abilities
Lion Bee possesses the **Buzz Bomb+** ability. This allows it to collect 7 pollen from 29 surrounding Flowers, with a bonus of +10% pollen per level. Combining this ability with other bombs increases its power.

### Collection Rates Breakdown
*   **Pollen:** Base collection is 20 Pollen in 4 seconds (equivalent to 5 per second). With the x2 Bee Pollen gamepass, this increases to 40 Pollen in 4 seconds.
*   **Honey:** Base production is 180 Honey in 2 seconds (equivalent to 90 per second). With the x2 Honeymaking Speed gamepass, this increases to 180 Honey in 1 second.

## Trivia and Lore
*   Lion Bee has the highest base attack stat in the game.
*   It is confirmed to be a male bee, referred to as "he" by Panda Bear, and possesses a mane, earning it the title of king. It is the only confirmed male bee in the game.
*   The Lion Bee is tied for the third-highest base Energy stat in the game, alongside Buoyant Bee, and is surpassed only by infinite stamina bees (e.g., Photon Bee or Exhausted Bee).
*   A dedicated gate, the Lion Bee Gate, leads into the 25 Bee Zone and is located near Polar Bear.
*   Lion Bee belongs to a group of five bees (Tadpole Bee, Tabby Bee, Puppy Bee, Bear Bee) that are based on real-life animals not classified as bee species. Specifically, Lion Bee and Tabby Bee are felines.
*   It is the only Legendary bee derived from a real-life non-bee animal.
*   Lion Bee is unique in liking the Ant Field; this may be an allusion to antlions or its high base attack stat relating to fighting ants in the Ant Challenge.
