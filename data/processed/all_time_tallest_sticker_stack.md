# All-Time Tallest Sticker Stack

The **Tallest Sticker Stack** is one of 64 leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator, tracking the total number of stickers players have donated to the central Sticker Stack. This leaderboard displays 10 positions at a time, and players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place.

## Trivia

The Tallest Sticker Stack and the All-Time Top Sticker-Seekers are unique leaderboards in the game because they have a defined maximum amount (currently 290). This means that achieving the highest ranks on these boards is eventually impossible.
