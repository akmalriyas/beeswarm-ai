# Drives

Drives are inventory items introduced in the 2022-12-26 update. They serve multiple functions within the game, including repairing Robo Bear, purchasing Digital Bees and upgrading them, and granting powerful boosts during a round of the Robo Bear Challenge.

All Drives (excluding Broken Drives) can be purchased from Robo Bear's Shop for a base cost of 50 Cogs and an increasing amount of Honey with each purchase. The pricing structure varies by drive type:
*   **White, Red, Blue Drives:** The nth drive costs $P(n) = 250,000 \cdot n^2$ Honey (in addition to the base Cog cost).
*   **Glitched Drive:** The nth drive costs $P(n) = 1,000,000 \cdot n^2$ Honey.

---

## White Drive

The White Drive grants the following boosts during a single round of Robo Bear's Challenge:
*   x1.25 White Pollen
*   x1.25 White Field Capacity
*   +1 Colorless Bee Attack

### Acquisition Methods

**Current:**
*   Purchasing from Robo Bear's Shop for 50 Cogs and a base of 250,000 Honey.
*   Chance to receive during the Robo Bear Challenge.
*   Completing Science Bear's "Repairing The White Drive" quest grants one White Drive, replacing a Broken Drive.
*   As a reward for finishing your own Robo Party.

**Outdated:**
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 222 Snowflakes.
*   Obtained from the Digital Bee pack (2,500 Robux) which granted 10 White Drives plus other items.

## Glitched Drive

The Glitched Drive grants the following boosts during a single round of Robo Bear's Challenge:
*   x1.25 Pollen
*   x1.25 Field Capacity
*   +1 Bee Attack

### Acquisition Methods

**Current:**
*   Purchasing from Robo Bear's Shop for 100 Cogs, 5 Red Drives, 5 Blue Drives, 5 White Drives, and a base of 1,000,000 Honey. (Requires a Gold Cog Amulet to unlock purchase ability).
*   **Challenge Rewards:** Completing 10 or more rounds of the Robo Bear Challenge gives a chance for a Glitched Drive; the chance increases with higher completion rounds.
    *   One Glitched Drive is guaranteed after completing Round 20.
    *   One Glitched Drive is guaranteed upon completing Round 25, with a 1/7 chance for a second drive.
*   Completing Science Bear's "Corrupting The Glitched Drive" quest grants one Glitched Drive (Requires completion of White Drive, Blue Drive, and Red Drive quests).
*   As a reward for finishing your own Robo Party.

**Outdated:**
*   Completed Bee Bear's 5th 2022 Beesmas quest.
*   Obtained from the Digital Bee pack (2,500 Robux) which granted 10 Glitched Drives plus other items.
*   Purchasing the Mondo Robo-Bundle (4,500 Robux) grants 10 Glitched Drives plus other items.
*   Purchasing the Glitching Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog for 111 Gingerbread Bears.
*   Opening the Mondo Gift Box rewarded 10 Glitched Drives.

## Broken Drive

The Broken Drive is a unique drive added in the 2023-05-07 update that can only be collected once. It initiates Science Bear's drive questline. Once collected, it will eventually be replaced with a White Drive upon completing Science Bear's associated quest.

### Acquisition Methods
*   Found behind the Robo Pass Dispensers in the 30 Bee Zone (Bear Gate).

## Trivia

*   Excluding the Broken Drive, these drives are the only items that can permanently enhance a bee.
*   Excluding the Broken Drive, these drives are exclusively used during challenges.
*   The existence of a Yellow Drive is hinted at through Robo Bear’s 2022 present dialogue, possibly referencing unused yellow flowers in Onett's decal inventory.
