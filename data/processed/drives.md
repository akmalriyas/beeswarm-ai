# Drives

**Drives** are inventory items introduced in the 2022-12-26 update. They serve several purposes: repairing Robo Bears, purchasing Digital Bees and upgrading them, and granting boosts during a round of the Robo Bear Challenge.

All Drives (except Broken Drives) can be purchased from Robo Bear's Shop for 50 Cogs or 100 Cogs for Glitched drives, plus an increasing amount of Honey with each purchase.

## Drive Types

### White Drive
The White Drive grants the following boosts during a single round of Robo Bear's Challenge:
*   x1.25 White Pollen
*   x1.25 White Field Capacity
*   +1 Colorless Bee Attack

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   **Robo Bear's Shop:** Purchased for 50 Cogs and a base of 250,000 Honey. The cost for the $n^{th}$ drive is calculated by the formula: $P(n) = 250,000 \cdot n^2$.
*   **Robo Bear Challenge:** Chance to receive as a reward.
*   **Science Bear Quest:** Completing "Repairing The White Drive" replaces a Broken Drive with one White Drive.
*   **Robo Party:** Awarded upon finishing your own Robo Party.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Purchasing in Bee Bear's Catalog for 222 Snowflakes.
*   Obtained from the Digital Bee pack (2,500 Robux grants 10 White Drives + other items).

### Red Drive
The Red Drive grants the following boosts during a single round of Robo Bear's Challenge:
*   x1.25 Red Pollen
*   x1.25 Red Field Capacity
*   +1 Red Bee Attack

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   **Robo Bear's Shop:** Purchased for 50 Cogs and a base of 250,000 Honey. The cost for the $n^{th}$ drive is calculated by the formula: $P(n) = 250,000 \cdot n^2$.
*   **Robo Bear Challenge:** Chance to receive as a reward.
*   **Panda Bear Quest:** Completing "Ready For The Red Drive?" gives 1 Red Drive (requires completing Science Bear's White Drive quest first).
*   **Robo Party:** Awarded upon finishing your own Robo Party.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Completing Science Bear's 2022 Beesmas quest.
*   Obtained from the Digital Bee pack (2,500 Robux grants 10 Red Drives + other items).
*   Opening the Festive Gift Box rewards 5 Red Drives.

### Glitched Drive
The Glitched Drive grants the following boosts during a single round of Robo Bear's Challenge:
*   x1.25 Pollen
*   x1.25 Field Capacity
*   +1 Bee Attack (Regular)

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   **Robo Bear's Shop:** Purchased for 100 Cogs, 5 Red Drives, 5 Blue Drives, 5 White Drives, and a base of 1,000,000 Honey. The cost for the $n^{th}$ drive is calculated by the formula: $P(n) = 1,000,000 \cdot n^2$. Requires a Gold Cog Amulet to purchase.
*   **Robo Bear Challenge:** Chance to receive as a reward.
    *   A Glitched Drive is guaranteed after completing round 20.
    *   A Glitched Drive is guaranteed upon completing round 25, with a 1/7 chance for a second one.
*   **Science Bear Quest:** Completing "Corrupting The Glitched Drive" gives 1 Glitched Drive (requires completing White, Blue, and Red Drive quests).
*   **Robo Party:** Awarded upon finishing your own Robo Party.

**Outdated Ways:**
*   Completing Bee Bear's 5th 2022 Beesmas quest.
*   Obtained from the Digital Bee pack (2,500 Robux grants 10 Glitched Drives + other items).
*   Purchasing the Mondo Robo-Bundle for 4,500 Robux grants 10 Glitched Drives + other items.
*   Purchasing the Glitching Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog for 111 Gingerbread Bears.
*   Opening the Mondo Gift Box rewards with 10 Glitched Drives.

### Broken Drive
The Broken Drive is a unique drive added in the 2023-05-07 update that can be collected only once from an area behind the Robo Pass Dispenser and Free Robo Pass Dispenser. Collecting it initiates Science Bear's drive questline, after which it will be replaced with a White Drive.

**Ways to Obtain:**
*   Found behind the Robo Pass Dispensers in the 30 Bee Zone (Bear Gate).

## Trivia
*   Excluding the Broken Drive, these are the only items that can permanently enhance a bee.
*   Excluding the Broken Drive, these drives can only be used during a challenge round.
*   The existence of a Yellow Drive is hinted at through Robo Bear’s 2022 present dialogue, possibly referencing unused yellow flowers found in Onett's decal inventory.
