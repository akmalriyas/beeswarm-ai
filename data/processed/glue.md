# Glue

[![Glue](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d4/Glue.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404021042)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/d/d4/Glue.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404021042)

> "Grants x1.25 Bee Gather Pollen and Tools for 10 minutes."

**Cooldown:** 1 second

Glue is a craftable inventory item, introduced in the 2018-11-25 update. It serves two primary functions: as a crafting material for accessories, or as a consumable buff that grants x1.25 Bee Gather Pollen and Tools when used directly.

The buff cannot stack; using another Glue will reset the timer of an active buff. This effect can be overridden by consuming a Purple Potion or Super Smoothie.

## Ways to Obtain

### Crafting
*   Crafted in the Blender using 50 Gumdrops and 10 Royal Jellies.

### Quests & NPCs
*   **Black Bear:** Rewards Glue on specific quests, including "All-Whitey" (gives 1 glue) and occasionally through repeatable quests.
*   **Brown Bear:** Rewards Glue upon completing certain milestones:
    *   140th quest: 1 glue
    *   235th quest: 1 glue
    *   310th quest: 3 glues
    *   395th quest: 5 glues
*   **Science Bear:** Rewards Glue through quests:
    *   Chemical Analysis: 1 glue
    *   Epistemological Endeavor: 4 glues (Note: Previously gave 5 glues).
*   **Glue Dispenser:** Located inside Gummy Bear's Lair, this dispenser allows players to claim 2-5 Glues based on their Goo Badge level (Hotshot: 2, Ace: 3, Master: 4, Grandmaster: 5).
*   **Gummy Sprouts:** Drops Glue.

### Drops & Environmental Sources
Glue can be obtained through various drops and environmental interactions:

*   **Mobs:** Rare drop from mobs such as the Spider, Werewolf, Puffshroom, King Beetle, and Coconut Crab.
*   **Stump Snail:** Guaranteed drop from the Stump Snail.
*   **Robo Bear Challenge:** Drops Glue (chance affected by challenge round).
*   **Aphids:** Drop from Armored aphids.
*   **Fields:** Small chance of dropping in place of a honey token from the Dandelion Field, Pumpkin Patch, or Stump Field.
*   **Leaves:** Rare drop from leaves found in the Pineapple Patch, Dandelion Field, Pumpkin Patch, Stump Field, or Spider Field.
*   **Pollen Collection:** Rare drop when collecting pollen in the Pineapple Patch, Spider Field, or Pumpkin Patch.
*   **Goo Flowers:** Rarely appears as a token when collecting goo from flowers.
*   **Planters:**
    *   Candy Planter: Planting this planter three times in a row in the Stump Field guarantees at least 10 Glues (can give up to 20 glues, with a one-week cooldown).
    *   Rare drop from Candy Planter, Blue Clay Planter, Pesticide Planter, and Festive Planter.
*   **Gummy Sprouts:** Drops Glue.

### Special & Miscellaneous Sources
*   **Festive Gifts/Mark:** Rare drop from Festive Gifts (if the Bee is Gifted) or Festive Mark.
*   **Wind Shrine:** Badge reward for donating to the Wind Shrine.
*   **Memory Match:** Matching a pair of glue tokens in Memory Match.
*   **Commando Chick:** Capturing Commando Chick for the 5th time drops 1 Glue.
*   **Sticker Stack Rewards:**
    *   Right Facing Stump Snail: 1 glue
    *   Yellow Sticky Hand: 1 glue
    *   Glowering Gummy Bear: 1 glue

## Outdated Methods (Nerfed/Removed)
The following methods for obtaining Glue are no longer active or have been significantly changed due to nerfs:

*   **Purchases:** Buying the Buoyant Pack (800 Robux), Loaded Bundle, Booster Bundle, and Nighttime Nectar Pack.
*   **NPC Gifts:** Giving presents to certain NPCs (Mother Bear, Science Bear, Onett).
*   **Gift Boxes/Ornaments:** Opening Gooey Present, Glowing Gift Box, or Gummy Gift Box.
*   **Bee Bear Quests:** Various quests from Bee Bear (e.g., "All I want For Beesmas Is Goo" gave 3 glues).
*   **Codes:** Redeeming specific expired codes ("2YearsAfterParty," "Willgoold," etc.) to gain Glue or activate the buff.
*   **Robo Bear Challenge:** As a common drop (Note: This method is now listed as current, but was previously noted here).
