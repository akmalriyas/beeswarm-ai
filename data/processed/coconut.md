# Coconut

[![Coconut](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2f/Coconut.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404022348)

> Drops a huge Coconut into the field. Catch it to convert Pollen to Honey Tokens.

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 10 seconds
*   **Capacity Cap:** 500

A **Coconut** is an inventory item introduced in the 2019-09-28 update. When used on a field, it spawns a large green circle. If caught by the player, it converts Pollen into a ring of 10 Honey tokens. Coconuts can also collect pollen for the summoning player, deal damage to mobs, and are utilized in various crafting recipes.

Pollen yield from coconuts can be increased using Coconut Clogs, Pollination, and the Coconut Field Market Boost (note: the Boost Market has since been removed). The maximum capacity limit for this item is 500 units.

If a coconut is placed in the player's hotbar, an On/Off switch allows it to be used automatically when activated. When a coconut hits a field, the following audio plays:
<https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Cocothud.ogg>

## Conversion Mechanics

When a coconut lands on a player, Pollen is converted in the Backpack. The maximum amount of pollen that can be converted is capped at 100% of the player's total Hive Convert Amount (e.g., if a player has 500,000 total hive convert amount, up to 500,000 pollen will be converted).

The final amount of honey received from the coconut is further multiplied by the Honey From Tokens multiplier.

## Acquisition Methods

### Current Sources
*   Defeating the Coconut Crab (yield depends on defeat time).
*   Growing sprouts in the Coconut Field.
*   Farming in the Coconut Field: Harvesting Leaves here may also yield Coconuts.
*   Finding a token worth 10 coconuts under the leaves of the palm tree closest to Spirit Bear.
*   Using the Coconut Dispenser (yield increases with each Coconut Badge tier, up to 15 Coconuts).
*   Redeeming certain valid codes (e.g., `1MLikes` grants 1 Coconut + other items).
*   Winning them from Mega Memory Match (3 Coconuts) and Extreme Memory Match (5 Coconuts).
*   Receiving a rare gift from Cub Buddy (1 Coconut).
*   As a drop from an Armored Aphid.
*   Harvesting planters in the Coconut Field, Pine Tree Forest, or Pepper Patch.
*   As a drop from puffshrooms within the 35 bee zone.
*   Purchasing the Reindeer Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog (250 Gingerbread Bears grants 100 Coconuts + other items).
*   The Palm Tree Sticker rewards 10 Coconuts when added to the Sticker Stack.

### Outdated Sources
*   Purchasing certain packs from the Robux Shop:
    *   Gooey Goodies Pack (100 Coconuts + other items).
    *   Stocking Stuffer Pack (25 Coconuts + other items).
*   Opening Funky Presents (20 Coconuts + other items).
*   Purchasing certain bundles from Bee Bear's Catalog:
    *   Percussive Bundle (Summer 2024) (25 Coconuts + other items).
    *   Tropical Bundle (Summer 2024) (250 Coconuts + other items).
    *   Island Bundle (Winter 2024) (250 Coconuts + other items).
*   Completing Panda Bear's Beesmas 2020 Quest (5 Coconuts + other items).
*   Redeeming certain expired codes (e.g., `Mocito100T` gave 10 Coconuts + other items).

## Trivia

*   Prior to the 2021-12-26 update, the maximum number of Coconuts a player could hold was 150; this limit has since been increased to 500.
*   Originally, being hit by a coconut converted 75% of the player's hive convert amount into honey.
*   This is the only inventory item that can directly damage mobs.
*   If a player has no Pollen in their bag, catching a coconut will still spawn honey tokens, granting 1 Honey each (before the Honey From Tokens multiplier). This occurs because a honey token must yield at least one unit of honey.
*   When used in the Hive Hub, Coconuts grant a total of 1,500 Honey, meaning each resulting honey token grants 150 honey.
