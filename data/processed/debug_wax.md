**Debug Wax** is an inventory item and a type of wax introduced in the 2024-07-17 update. It can be used on a **Beequip** to boost its stats, guaranteeing a great improvement with a 100% chance upon usage. Debug Wax has an Upgrade Value of 4.

When applying Debug Wax to a bee's Beequip, the following message appears:
> The Wax improved the Beequip!

## Acquisition Methods

Currently, there are no ways to obtain **Debug Wax**.

### Outdated Methods

In the past, Debug Wax could be obtained as a reward for completing specific quests during seasonal events:

*   As a reward for completing Bubble Bee Man's quest "B.B.M's Naughty List" during the Winter Beesmas 2024 event.
*   As a reward for completing Bubble Bee Man's quest "B.B.M's Naughty List" during the Summer Beesmas 2024 event.

## Trivia

*   Debug Wax is the only wax that cannot be crafted.
*   It functions almost identically to **Caustic Wax**, with the sole difference being that Debug Wax always succeeds in improving the Beequip. (This item originally shared its icon with Caustic Wax before the 2024-12-25 update.)
*   The item originated from Onett's Testing Group, serving as a risk-free method to upgrade Beequips before being officially added to the game.
*   Debug Wax, along with Soft Wax and Swirled Wax, is one of the few waxes guaranteed to change a beequip's stats.
