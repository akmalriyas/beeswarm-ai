# Radioactive Status

A bee becomes "radioactive" when an orange and white ☢️ symbol appears next to its slot, accompanied by green particles.

**Radiation** is a mechanic that grants a temporary "radioactive" status to a bee, lasting for 11 minutes. During this period, the bee remains radioactive regardless of changes made to its hive slots.

### Effects of Radioactivity
When a bee is radioactive:
*   It has an increased chance of gaining a mutation when fed specific treats, including Bitterberries, Royal Jellies, Moon Charms, or Star Jellies.
*   The bee's glow intensity indicates the remaining duration of the effect; brighter bees have more time left until the radioactivity ends.
*   The bee's hive slot emits green particles while active.
*   Certain radioactive bees may find stickers in specific fields.

### How to Obtain Radioactivity
Bees can become radioactive through several methods:

*   **Neonberries:** Feeding a bee a Neonberry will instantly make it radioactive.
*   **Mutation:** Mutated bees occasionally become radioactive, and this event has a chance to spread the radioactivity to surrounding bees.
*   **Global Events:** Global radioactivity events have been initiated by Onett (Developer). For example, during Beesmas 2020, he triggered a game-wide message announcing "☢️ Testing global radioactivity... ☢️" which caused many bees to become radioactive.
*   **Random Chance:** Occasionally, a bee will randomly gain the radioactive status without meeting any of the above criteria.

### Audio Cue
The sound effect played when a bee turns radioactive is available here: [Radioactivity.ogg](/wiki/File:Radioactivity.ogg). Note that the speed and pitch of this sound effect are randomized for each mutation.
