# Frogs

Frogs are passive mobs introduced in the 2019-12-23 Update. They can be summoned by collecting a Summon Frog ability token generated by a Tadpole Bee, or by using a Box-O-Frogs item.

There are two types of frogs: Normal and Gifted.

### Despawn Timers
*   **Box-O-Frogs Summon:** Frogs despawn after a set period of time (3 minutes).
*   **Tadpole Bee Summon:** Frogs despawn after 20 seconds (+2s per level).

### Frog Types
*   **Normal Frogs:** Can be summoned via Tadpole Bee tokens or Box-O-Frogs.
*   **Gifted Frogs:** Can be summoned via Box-O-Frogs, or by a Gifted Tadpole Bee with a 10% (+2% per level) chance when collecting its Summon Frog ability token.

### Abilities and Interactions
*   **Passive Behavior:** Neither players nor frogs can inflict harm on one another, nor can they be harmed by hostile entities.
*   **Token Collection:** Frogs use their tongues to collect Ability Tokens, Honey Tokens, and other tokens (such as rewards from Mobs) for the player, simultaneously creating Bubbles during this process.
*   **Hopping:** Upon summoning, frogs hop around the field until they despawn, generating Passive Abilities (Bubbles) on their 2nd, 3rd, and 4th hop.
*   **Combat:** Frogs can attack Hostile Mobs when summoned from a Box-O-Frogs. This attack ignores accuracy checks. The damage dealt is either x5 the damage of the Tadpole Bee that summoned them or a fixed 7 damage.

### Gifted Frog Enhancements
Gifted frogs possess several advantages over normal frogs:
*   Longer tongue range and faster token collection/attack speed.
*   50% increased attack power.
*   A chance to summon Gold Bubbles while hopping.
*   The ability to hit balloons with their tongue, converting pollen into honey tokens.

## Trivia and Lore
*   **Unique Mob Status:** Frogs are the only mob in the game that can be summoned by an ability token. They are also the only passive mob that is passive to players but hostile toward harmful mobs.
*   **Token Collectors:** Along with Cub Buddies and Windy Bee (with its Tornado Ability), frogs are one of the few entities capable of collecting tokens from flowers, leaves, and sprouts for the player.
*   **Damage Dealers:** Frogs, Rogue Vicious Bees, and Wild Windy Bees are among the few mobs that can deal damage to other hostile mobs with attacks. However, frogs are unique in deliberately attacking other mobs, unlike the others which only attack if their path intersects with a target.
*   **Bubble Spawners:** Frogs are one of the only mobs capable of spawning bubbles.
*   **Item Spawning:** The frog and Rogue Vicious Bee are the only mobs that can be spawned using an item (Box-O-Frogs for frogs, Night Bell for Rogue Vicious Bees).
*   **Field Behavior:** If a frog is at high elevation, it may fall outside of its field. In this state, it hops uselessly until it despawns.
*   **Historical Variants:** During special events, Onett spawned specialized frog variants: the "Honeyday-Cleanup Frog Crew" (end of Beesmas 2020) and the "Frog Foundation" (end of Beesmas 2021).
*   **Gifted Variant Holders:** Frogs and Rogue Vicious Bees are the only mobs that possess a gifted variant.
*   **Facing Direction:** Frogs will only spawn facing towards the 30-Bee Zone or away from the Hive. They are also one of the few passive mobs capable of being summoned by a Bee.
