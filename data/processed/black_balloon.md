# Black Balloon

[![Black Balloon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e1/Black_Balloon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031434)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/e/e1/Black_Balloon.png/revision/latest?cb=20230404031434)

> "Floats over the field for 5m. Stores up to x25 your capacity and increases pollen inside 4x."

**Stats:**
*   **Cooldown:** 15 minutes
*   **Capacity Cap:** 25 (times player's base capacity)

The **Black Balloon** is a consumable item introduced in the December 26, 2021 update. When used, it summons a black balloon over the player's current field. The balloon floats for five minutes before moving to the hive, where it stores collected pollen. Pollen gathered beneath the balloon is multiplied by four, and the balloon can hold up to 25 times the player's capacity. Only one Black Balloon can be active in a single field per player.

The item has an inventory cap of 25. If the player already possesses 25 Black Balloons, obtaining more will not increase their count unless acquired through Robux packs, quests, or codes.

## Ways to Obtain

*   As a very rare drop from Tunnel Bear.
*   As an exceptionally rare drop from Spider.
*   Completing Dapper Bear's 12th quest rewards one Black Balloon and other items.
*   Sticking the Black Star Sticker onto the Sticker Stacker grants one Black Balloon.
*   As a drop from Robo Party mobs.
*   Purchasing Saint Puff's Pack in Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2025 provides one Black Balloon and other items.
