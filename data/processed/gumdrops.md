# Gumdrops

Gumdrops are an inventory item introduced during the Gummy Invasion Event. They function as a goo-splattering agent when used in a field, granting bonus honey to flowers they hit.

**Usage and Mechanics:**
*   **Effect:** When dropped onto a flower, gumdrop blobs splatter Goo. This Goo grants an additional 40% bonus Honey; larger puddles provide even greater bonuses.
*   **Cooldown:** The item has a 2-second cooldown when used automatically.
*   **Automation:** Placing Gumdrops in a hotbar slot enables an ON/OFF switch, allowing the player to use them automatically every two seconds. This automatic usage can be disabled by manually using the item.
*   **Crafting Use:** Gumdrops are also required for crafting other gear, such as the Gummyballer.

## Ways to Obtain

Gumdrops can be acquired through various methods:

### Crafting
*   Using a Blender with 3 Blueberries, 3 Strawberries, and 3 Pineapples.

### Purchasing and Rewards
*   **Gumdrop Shop:** Spending Tickets at the Gumdrop Shop yields 3 Gumdrops per ticket.
*   **Quests:** As rewards for completing certain Black Bear and Brown Bear quests.
*   **Science Bear Quest:** Receiving Science Bear's "Chemical Analysis" quest grants 10 Gumdrops.
*   **Cub Buddy Gift:** Received as a Cub Buddy gift in increments of 1 or 5.
*   **Stick Bug Challenge:** Possible reward for participating; the amount increases with score.
*   **Supreme Star Amulet:** Collected by gathering tokens from the "Gummy Star" passive.
*   **Tokens:** Finding specific gumdrop tokens (e.g., a token worth 5 Gumdrops near the Ant Gate, likely left over from the Gummy Invasion event).
*   **Codes:** Redeeming various valid codes grants Gumdrops and other items (examples include BeesBuzz123 for 10 Gumdrops, 15MMembers for 15 Gumdrops, and GumdropsForScience for 15 Gumdrops).
*   **Sticker Stack Rewards:** Adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack yields rewards:
    *   Silly Tongue: 25 Gumdrops.
    *   Yellow Walking Wiggly Person: 25 Gumdrops.
    *   Gummyballer: 25 Gumdrops.
*   **Gift Boxes:** Opening the Confectionery Gift Box rewards 500 Gumdrops and other items.

### Drops
Gumdrops can drop from several sources:
*   Any Planter.
*   Festive Gifts (Ability Tokens).
*   Puffshrooms.
*   Mythic Meteor Showers.
*   Stockings.
*   Certain Mobs (0 to 2,500 Gumdrops per mob, depending on Loot Luck and mob type).
*   Leaves in the Stump Field.
*   Gummy Sprout or Festive Sprout.
*   Using Dapper Bear's Samovar: Drops 25 Gumdrops after every 5th use, except the 25th.

## Outdated Acquisition Methods (Historical)

The following methods for obtaining Gumdrops are no longer active or relevant:

*   **Honey Dispenser:** Using it during the Gummy Invasion granted 1 Gumdrop per use.
*   **Gummy Bear Quests:** Before Gummy Bear left, completing his quests gave 1 Gumdrop each (except the last quest). A token near where he used to be granted 5 Gumdrops.
*   **Event Tokens:** Tokens were found during the Egg Hunt 2019 Event and under the Beesmas Tree.
*   **Ornament Presents:** A token worth 15 Gumdrops was behind the Golden Present, and opening the Gooey Present rewarded 100 Gumdrops.
*   **Gift Boxes (Seasonal):** Various seasonal gift boxes provided Gumdrops:
    *   Kitsch Gift Box (Beesmas 2021): 10 Gumdrops.
    *   Gummy Gift Box (Beesmas 2021): 50 Gumdrops.
    *   Gummy Gift Box (Beesmas 2022): 25 Gumdrops.
    *   Gooey Gift Box (Beesmas 2022): 100 Gumdrops.
    *   Sweet-n-sour Gift Box (Summer 2024): 400 Gumdrops.
    *   Honeydew Gift Box (Winter 2024): 500 Gumdrops.
*   **Gummy Siege:** Fields targeted by Gummy Bear during the siege would spawn Gumdrops.
*   **Tokens/Codes:** Various expired codes provided small amounts of Gumdrops.

## Trivia

*   Gumdrops are the only item that changes the overlay on Leaves.
*   They are among the cheapest items in the game to purchase with tickets (1/3 ticket cost) and are also cheap to speed up in the Blender, requiring 1 ticket for every 10 Gumdrops.
*   A known typo ("Gumdropss") appears when crafting Glue or donating Gumdrops to the Wind Shrine.
*   The "Unlimited Gumdrops" buff allows players to use gumdrops without consuming them from inventory for three minutes (obtainable via specific codes and the Glue Dispenser). This does not increase total stock.
*   To enter Gummy Bear's Lair, a player must use a gumdrop on top of the Gummy Bee at the Egg Claim near the Ant Gate after earning the Goo Hotshot badge.
*   Gumdrops are currently the only way to obtain Gummy Bee following the 2019-04-05 update.
*   The hotkeys for Gumdrops (G), Stingers, and Micro-Converters were removed after the 2019-09-28 update.
*   Before the 2019-04-05 update, specific cooldown messages appeared in the chat box when attempting to use them too soon or when inventory was empty.
