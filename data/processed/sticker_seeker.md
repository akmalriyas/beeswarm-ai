# Sticker Seeker

The **Sticker-Seeker** is a tool released in the 2024-01-12 update that allows players to find Stickers and unlock the Sticker-Seeker quests. It can be crafted at the Hub Field Shop.

## Overview & Stats

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Collection Range** | 13 Flowers |
| **Collecting Pattern** | Diamond |
| **Base Collection Rate** | 10 pollen per second (from 13 patches) |
| **Total Collecting Time** | 1 second |

### Pollen Collection Multipliers

The Sticker-Seeker's base collection rate is modified by the type of flower:

| Flower Type | Single | Double | Triple | Large | Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| White | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
| Red | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
| Blue | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |

**Special Multiplier:** Grants the ability to use the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine, dig up Stickers in fields, and find Seeker-Stickers.

## Crafting Information

The Sticker-Seeker can be crafted using the following ingredients:

*   7,000,000 Honey
*   1 Glue
*   1 Oil
*   5 Soft Wax
*   5 Neonberries
*   10 Micro-Converters

## Mechanics and Stickers

When equipped, the Sticker-Seeker has a chance to find stickers in the field.

**Seeking Seeker-Stickers:**
The tool allows players to locate **Seeker-Stickers**. When clicking on one of these tokens, a message appears in the bottom-right corner: "🔎You found a Seeker-Sticker! ({amount} to go)".

**Digging Up Stickers:**
The Sticker-Seeker also has a chance to dig up stickers in the field. Upon finding one, the notification reads: "Oh! Your **Sticker-Seeker** dug something weird up". The sticker appears as a rainbow token on a random flower in the current field.

## Trivia

*   The Sticker-Seeker is the only tool used to spot and obtain Seeker-Stickers as a quest requirement for the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
*   Excluding the stickers found when equipping this tool, it is the only tool in the game without an associated tool sticker—an irony given its function.
*   The tool bears a striking resemblance to a magnifying glass.
*   It is tied with the Pulsar and Gummyballer for being one of the slowest tools in the game (in terms of cooldown duration).
