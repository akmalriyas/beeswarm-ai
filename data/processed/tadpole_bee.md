# Tadpole Bee

**Tadpole Bee** is a Blue Mythic bee, known for its amphibious nature and desire to become a frog upon maturity. It can be acquired in Original or Gifted forms.

> "A tiny amphibious bee who wants to become a frog when it grows up."

### Collection Rates
*   **Pollen:** Collects 10 Pollen in 6 seconds (equivalent to 1.67 per second).
    *   *With x2 Bee Pollen gamepass:* Collects 20 Pollen in 6 seconds.
*   **Honey:** Makes 120 Honey in 4 seconds (equivalent to 30 per second).
    *   *With x2 Honeymaking Speed:* Makes 120 Honey in 2 seconds.

### Modifiers & Bonuses
*   **Base Stats:** +40 Convert Amount, -20% Movespeed, -50% Gather Speed, and -50% Energy.
*   **Gifted Hive Bonus:** Grants a +25% Bubble Duration bonus.

## Abilities

Tadpole Bee possesses several unique abilities:

### Active Abilities
*   **Blue Boost:** Grants x1.2 pollen collection from Blue Flowers for 15 seconds. This ability can stack up to 10 times.
*   **Summon Frog:** Summons a frog that lasts for 20 seconds (+2s per Level). Frogs can generate bubbles after hopping or by collecting tokens with their tongue, and they attack nearby enemies with an attack power equal to 5x the bee's base attack.
    *   **Gifted Variant:** If Gifted, there is a 10% chance (+2% per Level) to summon a Gifted Frog. This variant features increased tongue range, improved token gathering and attack speed, 50% more attack power, a chance to summon Golden Bubbles, and the ability to hit balloons with its tongue to convert pollen into Honey Tokens.
*   **🌟Gifted Ability: Baby Love:** Grants x2 pollen collection and +50% Loot Luck for 30 seconds. Loot Luck increases the chance of obtaining prizes when defeating enemies.

### Passive Abilities
*   **Gathering Bubbles+:** Has a 65% chance (85% if Gifted) to spawn a Bubble while gathering. If any player touches this bubble, it pops, collecting 4 Red / 6 White / 8 Blue pollen from 29 nearby flowers and causing them to replenish.

## Trivia

*   **Realistic Representation:** The Tadpole Bee is one of only five bees (along with Lion Bee, Tabby Bee, Bear Bee, and Puppy Bee) to represent realistic animals. It is the only Mythic bee that represents a realistic animal.
*   **Baby Love Ability:** Along with Baby Bee, Tadpole Bee is one of only two bees possessing the Baby Love ability. Both also appear smaller than the normal bee size.
*   **Gifted Bonuses:** Tadpole Bee and Windy Bee are among the few bees (excluding Event Bees) to have two gifted bonuses.
*   **Exclusive Abilities:** Tadpole Bee, Vector Bee, Fuzzy Bee, Spicy Bee, Buoyant Bee, and Digital Bee are unique in having Gifted exclusive abilities.
*   **Summoning:** This bee, along with Spicy Bee, is one of only two bees capable of summoning other entities. It is the only bee that can summon a Mob.
*   **Passive Ability:** Tadpole Bee shares the Gathering Bubbles passive ability exclusively with Bubble Bee.
*   **Ability Count:** Along with Fuzzy Bee, Spicy Bee, Buoyant Bee, and Digital Bee, Tadpole Bee has four different abilities—the most for any bee in the game.
*   **Attack History:** Tadpole Bee was previously the only bee in the game to have a decimal base attack (0.5).
*   **Shared Ability Token:** It is one of the few Mythic bees whose Gifted-exclusive ability token is shared with another bee.
*   **Introduction History:** Along with Vector Bee and Spicy Bee, Tadpole Bee was among the first Mythic Bees introduced to the game.
*   **Egg Acquisition:** A Tadpole Bee Egg could be obtained during the Beesmas 2020 event by giving Bubble Bee Man a Present. It is the second mythic bee to have its own egg (after Fuzzy Bee).
*   **Design Notes:** Tadpole Bee is one of eight bees with a unique trail and serves as the blue counterpart to Spicy Bee. Along with Fuzzy Bee, it is one of only two Mythics that do not feature any glowing parts when gifted.
