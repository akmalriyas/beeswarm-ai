# Egg Hunt 2019 Event Information

The **Egg Hunt Info** was an event location in *Bee Swarm Simulator* (Roblox) tied to the [Egg Hunt 2019] event. This quest giver was situated near the Ant Gate. During this event, players could collect Marshmallow Bees from various NPCs and turn them in for rewards, including the **Flight of the Bumble Egg** and a special badge.

### Event Mechanics

*   **Objective:** Collect three (3) Marshmallow Bees.
*   **Quest Givers:** Nearly all NPCs in the game at the time offered quests for marshmallow bees, with the exception of the Honey Bee and Onett.
*   **Rewards (Standard Completion):**
    *   2,500 Honey
    *   10 Tickets
    *   5 Jelly Beans
    *   15 Gumdrops
    *   1 Royal Jelly
    *   Flight of the Bumble Egg

**Note:** Players who completed the quests before they were modified received an additional reward package: two Field Dice, two marshmallow bees, and five Jelly Beans.

### Dialogue and Quest Progression

#### Initial Quest (Egg Hunt 2019)

**Dialogue:** "Welcome Egg Hunters! Look at this one - egg-ceptionally bee-utiful. Meticulously molded out of the finest beeswax. And one just like it can be yours! Here's how: Each bear around the map has an Egg Hunt Quest for you. Every time you complete one, you'll be rewarded with a Marshmallow Bee. (It's just a cute springtime treat - not a real bee) Return here with 3 Marshmallow Bees to trade them for the Egg Hunt Egg! If you're new here, you should talk to Black Bear and Mother Bear first. They'll keep things simple. You can find them near the Sunflower Field. Let's get to it! Happy Hunting!"

**Initial Quest Givers:**
*   Black Bear (Right of hives near Red Cannon)
*   Mother Bear (Near Sunflower Field)
*   Brown Bear (Near Clover Field)

#### During Event Progression

The bears offer quests for Marshmallow Bees. Players are encouraged to work their way up the mountain and speak with all available NPCs during the event period.

#### Completion Dialogue

**Dialogue:** "1... 2... 3 Marshmallow Bees! You've earned our Egg Hunt Egg, good work! Its proper name is 'Flight Of The Bumble Egg'. Wear this bad boy on your head to truly embody the concept of bee swarm simulation. Hopefully that didn't take too long - we do things a little slow around here. You're free to buzz off now! But there are many more Marshmallow Bees to earn during this Egg Hunt event! Work your way up the mountain and introduce yourself to all the bears. When you're out of bears, well, try talking to whoever else you run into. We've got a lot to see here if you've got the time and a taste for the grind. For now, you ought to collect the other Egg Hunt eggs. You're welcome back anytime!"

**Conclusion:** Upon completion, players receive the Flight of the Bumble Egg, which can be equipped in the avatar tab on the Roblox website.
