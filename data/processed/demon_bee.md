# Demon Bee

**Demon Bee** is a Red, Legendary bee known for its powerful magical abilities fueled by pure hatred.

| Attribute | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rarity** | Legendary |
| **Color** | Red |
| **Energy** | 20 |
| **Speed** | 10.5 |
| **Attack** | 8 |

Demon Bee's favorite treat is Pineapples. It likes the Mushroom Field and Spider Field, but dislikes the Mountain Top Field.

## Stats & Mechanics

### Core Statistics
*   **Pollen Collection:** Collects 35 Pollen in 4 seconds (equivalent to 8.75 per second).
    *   With x2 Bee Pollen gamepass: 70 Pollen in 4 seconds.
*   **Honey Production:** Makes 80 Honey in 4 seconds (equivalent to 20 per second).
    *   With x2 Honeymaking Speed: 80 Honey in 2 seconds.

### Stat Modifiers
Demon Bee possesses the following inherent modifiers:
*   -25% Movespeed
*   +25 Gather Amount
*   -20 Convert Amount
*   +10% Instant Conversion (Increases to +20% if Gifted)
*   +7 Attack

**Gifted Hive Bonus:** When housed in a Gifted Hive, Demon Bee gains an additional +20% Instant Bomb Conversion.

## Abilities

### Red Bomb & Red Bomb+
Demon Bee has two bomb abilities:
1.  **Red Bomb:** Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding red flowers (+10% pollen per level). This ability can be combined with other bombs to increase power.
2.  **Red Bomb+:** Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding red flowers (+10% pollen per level). This ability can also be combined with other bombs to increase power.

### Passive: Gathering Flames
Demon Bee has a passive ability, Gathering Flames:
*   It has a 65% chance (85% if Gifted) to spawn a Flame when gathering.
*   Flames last for 4 seconds. Each second, they deal 10 damage (+25% of the bee's base attack) to nearby enemies and collect [5 Red / 3 White / 2 Blue] pollen (+10% per bee level) from 9 flowers.
*   Standing near a flame grants Flame Heat, which increases Red Pollen (between x1.2 and x2) and Bee Attack (between +5% to +25%).

## Trivia

*   Demon Bee is one of the slowest bees in the game, but it ranks among the most powerful due to its high attack and gather amount.
*   It is the only bee in the game that possesses two bomb abilities (Red Bomb and Red Bomb+).
*   Demon Bee shares skin colors with Riley Bee and Rascal Bee.
*   Both Demon Bee and Fire Bee share the same flame effect.
*   The Demon Mask, purchasable in the Lava Obby, has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Demon Bee.
*   Onett (Developer) once mentioned that early versions were intended to summon Fire Bees, a role now taken by Spicy Bee.
*   Prior to the 2020-04-06 Update, Demon Bee held the highest base gather amount, surpassed only by Tabby Bee with massive stacks of Tabby Love. Fuzzy Bee has since taken this position.
*   Demon Bee is considered the red counterpart to Diamond Bee.
*   One theory suggests Demon Bee dislikes the Mountain Top Field because it is geographically closer to heaven (the sky) than any other field.
*   This bee can be summoned using Honeyday Candles.
*   Along with Fire Bee, Precise Bee, Festive Bee, and Crimson Bee, Demon Bee is one of the few red bees that does not dislike Strawberries.
*   Note: While the in-game stat menu lists its attack as 6 more than normal (1), it actually has +7 Attack.
