# Bee Bear's Catalog

**Bee Bear's Catalog** is a limited-time in-game shop accessible via a side tab on the right side of the screen. It was introduced during Beesmas 2020 and has reappeared in subsequent events, including 2021, 2022, Summer 2024, Winter 2024, and 2025.

The shop's description is: "Beesmas event shop! Purchase items for Gingerbread Bears and Snowflakes." A countdown timer below the icon tracks the time remaining until the Beesmas event ends.

**Mechanics:**
*   The Catalog sells various items and bundles.
*   Players can only purchase any single item or bundle once.
*   Purchasing items is sometimes required to progress in NPC quests, such as those given by Bee Bear.
*   All purchases must be made using either **Snowflakes** or **Gingerbread Bears**.

**Purchase Feedback:**
*   Successful Purchase: `Purchased {Item/Bundle}!`
*   Insufficient Funds: `Can't afford {Item/Bundle}`

## Trivia & Mechanics

*   **One-Time Purchase:** Items from the Bee Bear's Catalog are one-time purchases; players can only buy them once.
*   **Purchase Limits:** While items in general have purchase limits, items bought from this specific catalog may exceed those caps.
*   **Catalog Evolution:** The shop has changed over time. For example, the description of certain bundles was updated (e.g., "Winter" to "Beesmas") during Beesmas 2024 due to its release timing.
*   **Gingerbread Bear Aging:** Gingerbread Bears obtained in previous events age into Aged Gingerbread Bears when a new Beesmas event starts. These aged bears cannot be used for purchases in the Catalog; only fresh ones can.
*   **Gifted Mythic Egg Acquisition:** The Gifted Mythic Egg is obtainable through non-Robux methods, including the Mondo Gift Box, Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine, and Leaderboards.
*   **Catalog History Notes:**
    *   The catalog was the very first limited-time shop in the game.
    *   It was the only non-robux shop to feature bundles.
    *   During Beesmas 2021, the timer was extended with the release of Beesmas Part 2 to allow players more time for new quests.
    *   A scripting error existed in the 2020 Pinecone Bundle, which incorrectly granted Glue instead of Enzymes.
*   **Item Naming Convention:** When an item was purchased from the catalog, the system initially displayed `+[Item Name] (From [Item Name])`. This format changed to simply `+[Item Name]` starting in Beesmas 2022.
*   **808 Reference:** The "Percussive Bundle" price of 808 Snowflakes is a reference to the Roland TR-808 drum machine, and the 8 Gingerbread Bears likely refer to the Roland TR-08 Rhythm Composer or TR-8S.
*   **Timer Format:** The countdown timer uses the format DD:HH:SS (Days:Hours:Seconds).
