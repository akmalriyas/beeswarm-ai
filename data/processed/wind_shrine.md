# Wind Shrine

The **Wind Shrine** is a monument located beyond the [Windy Bee Gate](/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate), next to the [Pepper Patch](/wiki/Pepper_Patch). It functions as an offering point, allowing players to donate items in exchange for rewards such as [Field Winds](/wiki/Field_Wind) and [Honey](/wiki/Honey).

The shrine has a cooldown of 1 hour between donations. Donating a [Cloud Vial](/wiki/Cloud_Vial) after donating a [Spirit Petal](/wiki/Spirit_Petal) is currently the only way to obtain a [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee).

## Functionality and Mechanics

### General Usage
Upon interacting with the shrine, players are prompted: "Would you like to make an offering to the Wind Shrine?" A pop-up window appears, allowing donation of any amount of almost any item in the inventory. After a successful donation, the chimes move swiftly. If the player cancels, the message reads: "You canceled the donation."

If the shrine is on cooldown, it displays: "You may only donate to the Shrine once per hour. Come back in x: y." (x = minutes, y = seconds).

Players can hold down the "+" button to rapidly increase the amount of a specific item being donated.

### Favor Mechanic
Favor is a [mechanic](/wiki/Mechanics) that directly affects the rewards received from the Wind Shrine. Favor increases with every donation.

**Note:** A Spirit Petal is *not* required to start increasing favor. All donations grant favor (5,000 + the favor value of all donated items).

## Windy Bee Acquisition
To obtain a Windy Bee from the Shrine, players must first donate a Spirit Petal. Subsequently, any Cloud Vial donated grants a chance to receive a Windy Bee Egg. The probability of obtaining this egg is influenced by the player's favor level.

*   **Success:** If successful, the shrine displays: "And it calls back to you!". The Windy Bee victory jingle plays, and a server-wide message appears: "🎉 [Player] has tamed a Windy Bee! 🎉".
*   **Failure:** If unsuccessful, the message reads: "But it doesn't respond...".

If a player purchased a Windy Bee Egg from the [Robux Shop](/wiki/Robux_Shop), they receive a Star Treat instead of a Windy Bee Egg.

## Wind Field Buffs
Donating certain items gives a chance to summon random Field Winds. The likelihood of obtaining high stacks is increased by the quantity donated and the player's favor level.

*   **Color Correlation:** Generally, the color of the item donated correlates with the color of the field generated.
*   **Purple Potion:** Biased toward generating large stacks in red or blue fields; recommended for boosting.
*   **Eggs:** Often yield large stacks across most fields; rarer eggs provide better and more winds.
*   **Swirled Wax:** Tends to generate 7-8x white field winds.
*   **Gifted Gold Egg:** Often yields 7-9x winds.
*   **Gifted Diamond Egg:** Often yields 10-11x winds.

## Donation Outcomes and Responses

All donations grant Honey and Favor, but other effects vary by item type.

### Spirit Petal Donations (Special)
Donating a Spirit Petal is crucial for Windy Bee acquisition. The rewards are:
*   Summoning clouds on fields and granting field winds.
*   Summoning a [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee).
*   Summoning [Bean Bugs](/wiki/Bean_Bug) and granting a [Jelly Bean](/wiki/Jelly_Beans).
*   Granting badge rewards.
*   Enabling the player to receive a [Windy Bee] (and other specific rewards): 500 [Treats], 1 [Star Jelly], 5 [Enzymes], 5 [Oil], 5 [Red Extracts], 5 [Blue Extracts], and 5 [Tropical Drinks].
*   Failing to obtain a Windy Bee grants only [Treats](/wiki/Treat).

**Note:** The rewards are somewhat random. While offering the same donation twice in the same amount may yield different results, donating more items generally leads to better outcomes.

## Galentine Shrine (Event)
The Galentine Shrine is a special version of the Wind Shrine obtained after completing specific [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear) Beesmas quests (2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025). It features hearts, red bows, and pink-painted pillars.

Donating [Strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry) offers a chance to receive rewards and the **Galentine's Blessing buff**, which lasts for 2 days. The odds of success start at 1% with one Strawberry and scale linearly up to 50,000 (guaranteed).

The probability $P(x)$ of receiving the blessing when donating $x$ strawberries is:
$$ P(x) = \left(\frac{0.99}{49999}(x-1)+0.01\right) \cdot 100\% $$

### Galentine's Blessing Buffs & Rewards (Type 1)
*   **Buffs:** +50% Capacity, +15% Instant Bee Ability conversion, +50% [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field) pollen, +50% [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field) pollen, +50% [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field) pollen, +5% Ticket Chance, +5% Loot Luck, +1% Super-Crit Chance.
*   **Rewards:** 1 [Present](/wiki/Present), 10 [Jelly Beans](/wiki/Jelly_Beans), 5 [Purple Potions](/wiki/Purple_Potion), 25 [Honeysuckles](/wiki/Honeysuckle), 50 [Gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops), 1 [Ticket Planter](/wiki/Ticket_Planter).

### Galentine's Blessing Buffs & Rewards (Type 2)
*   **Buffs:** +50% Capacity, +20% Instant Bee Ability conversion, +10% Instant Bubble Conversion, +50% [Rose Field](/wiki/Rose_Field) pollen, +50% [Blue Flower Field](/wiki/Blue_Flower_Field) pollen, +50% [Sunflower Field](/wiki/Sunflower_Field) pollen, +5% Ticket Chance, +5% Loot Luck.

## Trivia & Notes
*   The Wind Shrine is based on wind chimes, a type of percussion instrument.
*   According to [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear), the Wind Shrine is one of the oldest standing monuments in the game and predates her existence. She also notes that its responses vary based on player favor.
*   The shrine's chimes are collidable and not anchored; running into them will cause movement.
*   Using the Wind Shrine was the primary way to obtain a [Windy Bee](/wiki/Windy_Bee) upon initial release, aside from purchasing it in the Robux Shop.
*   If a player disconnects after donating but before rewards fade out, the next donation grants the last set of rewards and sets the 1-hour cooldown (unless at least one reward was collected).
*   **Donation Restrictions:** Items that cannot be donated include [Super Smoothies](/wiki/Super_Smoothie), [Translators](/wiki/Translator), [Sprinklers](/wiki/Sprinklers), certain [Specific Bee Eggs](/wiki/Egg#Specific_Bee_Egg) (excluding Honey Bee Egg), specific [Specific Bee Jellies](/wiki/Royal_Jelly#Specific_Bee_Jelly_Variants), [Spirit Petals] (if already donated), [Gingerbread Bears](/wiki/Gingerbread_Bear), [Snowflakes](/wiki/Snowflake), [Balloons](/wiki/Balloon), [Planters](/wiki/Planter), [Robo Passes](/wiki/Robo_Pass), [Beequip Case](/wiki/Beequip), and [7-Pronged Cogs](/wiki/7-Pronged_Cog).
*   Attempting to donate zero items results in an error message.
