# Wind Shrine

The Wind Shrine is a monument located beyond the Windy Bee Gate, next to the Pepper Patch. It allows players to donate items in exchange for rewards such as Field Winds and Honey. The shrine has a donation cooldown of 1 hour.

## Mechanics and Usage

### Donation Process
Upon interacting with the shrine, the player is prompted: "Would you like to make an offering to the Wind Shrine?" A pop-up window allows players to donate any amount of almost any item from their inventory. After donating, the chimes move around swiftly. If the donation is canceled, a message reads, "You canceled the donation."

If the player attempts to donate during cooldown, they receive the message: "You may only donate to the Shrine once per hour. Come back in x: y." (x = minutes, y = seconds).

Players can hold down the "+" button to increase the quantity of items being donated at a faster rate.

### Favor Mechanic
Favor is a core mechanic that affects the rewards received from the Wind Shrine. It increases with every donation. **A Spirit Petal is not required to be donated to start increasing favor.** All donations grant favor (5,000 + the favor value of all items donated).

## Rewards and Outcomes

### Field Winds
Donating certain items grants a chance to summon random Field Winds. The probability of getting a high stack or specific color winds is influenced by the amount donated and the player's favor level.

*   **Color Correlation:** Generally, the color of the item donated correlates with the color of the field received.
*   **Purple Potion:** Biased toward large stacks in red or blue fields; recommended for boosting.
*   **Eggs:** Often provide a large stack in most fields; rarer eggs yield better winds.
*   **Swirled Wax:** Tends to give 7–8x white field winds.
*   **Gifted Gold Egg:** Often gives 7–9x winds.
*   **Gifted Diamond Egg:** Often gives 10–11x winds.

### Windy Bee Acquisition
To obtain a Windy Bee from the Shrine, players must first donate a Spirit Petal. After this initial donation, any subsequent Cloud Vial donated grants a chance to obtain a Windy Bee Egg. The success rate is dependent on the player's favor level.

*   **Success:** If successful, the shrine displays: "And it calls back to you!". The Windy Bee victory jingle plays, and a server-wide message reads: "🎉 [Player] has tamed a Windy Bee! 🎉".
*   **Failure:** If unsuccessful, the message reads: "But it doesn't respond...".

If a player purchased a Windy Bee Egg from the Robux Shop, they receive a Star Treat instead of a Windy Bee Egg.

## Galentine Shrine (Special Event)

The Galentine Shrine is a special version of the Wind Shrine available after completing specific Spirit Bear Beesmas quests. It is decorated for Galentine’s Day and offers the **Galentine's Blessing** buff upon successful donation.

### Probability
Donating Strawberries grants a chance to receive rewards and the Galentine's Blessing buff, which lasts for 2 days. The odds start at 1% with one Strawberry and scale linearly up to 50,000 (guaranteed).

The probability $P(x)$ of receiving the blessing when donating $x$ Strawberries is:
$$P(x) = \left(\frac{0.99}{49999}(x-1)+0.01\right) \cdot 100\%$$

## Trivia and Notes

*   The Wind Shrine is based on a percussion instrument: the wind chimes.
*   Spirit Bear mentions that the Wind Shrine is one of the oldest standing monuments in the game, predating her existence.
*   Using the Wind Shrine is currently the only way to obtain a Windy Bee, outside of purchasing it in the Robux Shop (at its initial release).
*   If a player disconnects before rewards fade out after a donation, the next donation will grant the last set of rewards and reset the 1-hour cooldown, provided at least one reward was collected.
*   The chimes are collidable and not anchored; running into them causes movement.

## Error Handling

Several errors can occur when interacting with the Wind Shrine:

*   **Insufficient Bees:** If a player attempts to donate without having 35 bees, an error message appears: "Error: Player does not have 35 bees. Please show Onett."
*   **Zero Donation:** Attempting to donate zero items results in errors like "Error: Cannot donate 0 items." or "Error: Player does not have selected quantity of items."
*   **Non-Donatable Items:** Certain inventory items (e.g., Super Smoothies, Translators, specific Bee Eggs/Jellies, Spirit Petals after the first donation) cannot be donated to the shrine.
