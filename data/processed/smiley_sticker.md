# Smiley Sticker

The **Smiley Sticker** is a Level 7 Beequip with an equip limit of two. It has no color requirement and can only be equipped to Gifted Bees. The sticker features the quote, "This happy seal of approval is only for the brightest of bees," and includes the festive tag "🎄Beesmas."

## Stats and Mechanics

The Smiley Sticker provides several bonuses:
*   +Energy%
*   +Bomb Pollen%
*   +Mark Duration%
*   Ability: Blue Bomb
*   Ability: Honey Mark
*   +Max Bee Energy%

## Acquisition Methods

The Smiley Sticker can be obtained through several methods:
*   As a rare drop from the Tacky Planter or Petal Planter.
*   Purchasing it from Dapper Bear's Beequip shop.
*   Receiving it as a reward from the Stick Bug Challenge.
