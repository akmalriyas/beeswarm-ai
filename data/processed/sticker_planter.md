# Sticker Planter

[![Sticker Planter](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/43/Sticker_Planter.png/revision/latest?cb=20231227175040)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/4/43/Sticker_Planter.png/revision/latest?cb=20231227175040)

A single-use planter added in the 2024-01-12 update, the Sticker Planter grows in approximately 3 hours of playtime. It is unique among its single-use counterparts for having a cap of 25.

> "Grows in about 3 hours of playtime. Always grants some Stickers and spawns a Puffshroom (Rare+)."

## Stats & Mechanics

| Attribute | Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Type** | Single-Use Planter | Reusable: No |
| **Max Capacity (Cap)** | 25 | Maximum number of planters a player can store. Only one can be active at a time. |
| **Cost** | 100 Robux | Purchase price in the Robux Shop. |
| **Growth Time** | ~3 hours | Playtime required for full growth. |
| **Pollen Capacity** | 200,000 Pollen | Maximum pollen storage. |
| **Nectar Multiplier** | x2 | Provides a general Nectar multiplier of x2. |

### Puffshroom Drop Rates (Upon Full Harvest)

Harvesting a fully grown Sticker Planter is guaranteed to spawn a Puffshroom between levels 5-7, with the following probability distribution:

*   Rare Puffshroom: ~89.28%
*   Epic Puffshroom: ~17.86%
*   Legendary Puffshroom: ~0.89%
*   Mythic Puffshroom: ~0.01%

## Ways to Obtain

Sticker Planters can be obtained through several methods:

*   **Sprout Drop:** As a drop from Sticker Sprouts.
*   **Robux Shop Purchase:** For 100 Robux (requires having 20 bees and has a limit of 10 per player).
*   **Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine:** Completing quests offers chances to award the planter:
    *   No Sticker-Seeker quests completed: 2% chance.
    *   500+ Sticker-Seeker quests completed: 5% chance.
    *   Specific Quests Guarantee: Quests 11, 33, 55, 66, 77, 99, 111, 133, 144, 155, 166, 199, 244, 255, 266, 277, and 411 each guarantee 1 Sticker Planter.
    *   Higher Guarantee Quests: Quest 222 (2 planters), Quest 333 (3 planters), Quest 444 (4 planters), and Quest 555 (5 planters).
*   **Bee Bear's Catalog:**
    *   Completing the "Stocking Up On Stickers" quest rewards a Sticker Planter.
    *   Purchasing Saint Puff's Pack (50 Gingerbread Bears) grants a Sticker Planter and other items.

## Trivia

*   The planter continuously alternates between different colors, which can be adjusted in Settings.
*   It is one of four single-use planters (alongside the Festive Planter, Ticket Planter, and Paper Planter).
*   The Sticker Planter and the Ticket Planter share the same base models and decal outlines as the Paper Planter.
*   It shares the second-best Nectar multiplier in the game (x2), behind the Festive Planter (x3).
*   This planter, along with the Ticket Planter, is one of only two planters that will always spawn a Puffshroom upon full growth, and it is the only single-use planter guaranteed to spawn at least a Rare Puffshroom.
