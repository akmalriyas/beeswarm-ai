# Glitches

![Hivesticker 4-pronged vector bee](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) | **"When the Mythic Egg gets the math wrong."** This information can often change and may become inaccurate. You can help the Bee Swarm Simulator Wiki by updating or improving existing text.

**Glitches**, or **bugs**, are unintended situations that can occur in-game, which are likely to be patched in future Updates. Some of these glitches can be detrimental to gameplay, while others are mostly visual and do not affect players. Glitches caused purely or partially by internet connection issues are generally not listed here.

## Mob Glitches

### Mobs Unable to Move
[![Coconut Crab stuck in the during 2022-07-20. Image taken from a video on Reddit.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Cococrab.png/revision/latest?cb=20220720073246) [![Coconut Crab stuck in the during 2022-07-20. Image taken from a video on Reddit.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Cococrab.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/356?cb=20220720073246)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9c/Cococrab.png/revision/latest?cb=20220720073246) [](/wiki/File:Cococrab.png)

Coconut Crab stuck in the [Coconut Cave](/wiki/Coconut_Cave "Coconut Cave") during 2022-07-20. Image taken from a video on [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/BeeSwarmSimulator/comments/w3fqwx/what_the_heck_is_up_with_my_coconut_crab_ive/).

Occasionally, a [mob](/wiki/Mob "Mob") will get trapped in the collision box of certain objects around it. This usually renders them unable to move or escape, which makes killing them easier. The mob can still be attacked and will drop rewards normally. This glitch seems to happen primarily to [Mantises](/wiki/Mantis "Mantis") when they leap into objects, such as a wall. Sometimes, the [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle") gets stuck in the corner that says "Song Name" near it, making it easy to kill him. This can also happen to [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") in certain places.

In mid-2022 (specifically July 19–20, 22-31, and August 2–3), all mobs (excluding the [Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf"), Stick Bug, [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"), [Wild Windy Bee](/wiki/Wild_Windy_Bee "Wild Windy Bee"), [Rogue Vicious Bee](/wiki/Rogue_Vicious_Bee "Rogue Vicious Bee"), and [Aphids](/wiki/Aphid "Aphid")) would be frozen in place upon entering their spawn area. Affected bosses included the [Coconut Crab](/wiki/Coconut_Crab "Coconut Crab"), [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle"), and [Stump Snail](/wiki/Stump_Snail "Stump Snail"). This made killing them easier, as they could not voluntarily attack the player. On 2022-07-21, Onett temporarily fixed this glitch by adding the code "FrozenBugReboot." The issue persisted and was finally fixed permanently in servers of version 448+ on August 3, 2022.

### Collapsing Mobs
[![An example of a Collapsed Ladybug](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/67/Collapsed_ladybug.png/revision/latest?cb=20210515021802) [![An example of a Collapsed Ladybug](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/67/Collapsed_ladybug.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210515021802)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/6/67/Collapsed_ladybug.png/revision/latest?cb=20210515021802) [](/wiki/File:Collapsed_ladybug.png)

A collapsed [Ladybug](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug").

This glitch is purely visual, but it can be triggered when a mob, such as a [Ladybug](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug") or [Rhino Beetle](/wiki/Rhino_Beetle "Rhino Beetle"), is defeated while attempting to move. The mob's head or other parts may move inward. This glitch seems more common with the [Ladybug](/wiki/Ladybug "Ladybug") and [Spider](/wiki/Spider "Spider").

### Floating Stick Nymph
This bug occurs when a [Stick Nymph](/wiki/Stick_Nymph "Stick Nymph") is left floating above a field, even after the challenge has ended.

### Stick Bug in King Beetle's Lair
Occasionally, a [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") can phase through Clover Field and drop down into the King Beetle's Lair, as shown in this video. The cause of this glitch is currently unknown.

### Cub Buddy Stuck in Bag
When a [Cub Buddy](/wiki/Cub_Buddy "Cub Buddy") enters a bag in the Pro Shop or [Mountain Top Shop](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Shop "Mountain Top Shop"), it cannot exit. While inside the bag, they cannot be interacted with.

### Enormous Mob HP/Timer Bars
This visual glitch occurs when a mob first spawns. Mobs are coded to have a very large default HP/timer number (usually 1,300,400,000) before quickly switching to the normal amount. If the player suffers from bad internet or the server is experiencing issues, it might take longer for this number to switch over.

### Snowbear Leaving the Spider Field
Sometimes, if the [Snowbear](/wiki/Snowbear "Snowbear") receives [Mind Hacked](/wiki/Ability_Tokens "Ability Tokens"), it will wander out of its field, sometimes getting stuck. It can even potentially wander out of the 5 Bee Zone.

### Confused Commando Chick
If a player stands on the entrance to [Commando Chick's lair](/wiki/Commando_Chick%27s_Hideout "Commando Chick's Hideout"), placing them on the elevated floor and inside the spawn area at the same time, Commando Chick will not be able to damage the player by touching them. Commando Chick can still damage the player with grenades, and this glitch also occurs when it goes onto the ledge while in egg form.

### Speedy Commando Chick Glitch
This glitch occurs randomly when [Commando Chick](/wiki/Commando_Chick "Commando Chick") spawns. The chick's feet become stuck in the ground, causing it to move around extremely quickly until it hides in its egg. This bug can occur in conjunction with the Moving Commando in Egg glitch.

### Grenade Rain
This glitch can be performed by repeatedly exiting and entering [Commando Chick's hideout](/wiki/Commando_Chick%27s_Hideout "Commando Chick's Hideout"). This causes the Commando Chick to spawn and despawn while summoning two grenades at a time. These grenades can kill players inside the hideout, which is annoying for those trying to defeat their Commando Chicks. Very rarely, if performed enough times, it will knock the fleeing Commando Chick into the ground. It will remain there as the new Commando Chick performs its normal behavior, despawning after about 5 seconds.

### Fallen Mondo Chick
Occasionally, [Mondo Chick](/wiki/Mondo_Chick "Mondo Chick") may fall off of the [Mountain Top Field](/wiki/Mountain_Top_Field "Mountain Top Field") into the [Bamboo Field](/wiki/Bamboo_Field "Bamboo Field") or the [Pineapple Patch](/wiki/Pineapple_Patch "Pineapple Patch"). Loot dropped from Mondo Chick's defeat will spawn inside the field it was defeated in. If it fails to reach a field before this happens, no loot spawns at all. This glitch results from Mondo entering its egg just close enough to the edge of the Mountain Top that upon leaving, if moving in the correct direction, it falls out of the field.

### Crashing Mondo
This sometimes occurs when letting Mondo Chick despawn. During its leaving jump animation, it can crash into the Instant Converter or the Mountain Top Shop, spin back, and lay for a few seconds before finally despawning.

### Other Players' Mobs Attacking You
If a mob has no target (either because the target died or left the attack zone), the mob becomes capable of hitting anyone within its hitbox. This is noticeable when collecting pollen in the [Coconut Field](/wiki/Coconut_Field "Coconut Field") after your Coconut Crab has been defeated. This glitch can be replicated while fighting a Coconut Crab if you are inside the player’s Coconut Crab’s hitbox. If the player dies or leaves, the Coconut Crab can damage you while traveling back to its spawn location. This can occur with any mob that has a set spawn location and occasionally with [Tunnel Bear](/wiki/Tunnel_Bear "Tunnel Bear"). The cause of this glitch is unknown.

### Invisible Coconut Crab
This glitch can occur if the player has bad internet or has set their graphics at 1. The coconut crab will become invisible and unable to attack using its abilities. However, players can still take damage from the coconut crab's body because the hitbox remains active even when it is visually invisible.

## Player Glitches

### Talking to Spirit Bear with less than 35 bees
If a player has laggy internet or high ping, they may be able to talk to [Spirit Bear](/wiki/Spirit_Bear "Spirit Bear") by gliding from the top of the Windy Bee Gate while having fewer than 35 bees.

### Shop Trap
This glitch can occur in any shop. When players reset with a shop open, they are unable to close the interface because no closing button is visible. Players must exit the shop by resetting their character and spamming the E key, though this may not always work, requiring multiple resets or rejoining. This mostly happens in [Gummy Bear's Lair](/wiki/Gummy_Bear%27s_Lair "Gummy Bear's Lair").

A secondary version occurs if a player activates the red field booster and quickly opens the shop; the button for activating the red field booster will appear instead of the "Close Shop" button, trapping the player. The same glitch happens if a player has a high-speed boost and runs toward the blender at the correct angle before quickly opening the sprinkler shop.

### Invisible Splinter Trap
If a player respawns immediately after [Stick Bug](/wiki/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug") uses his [Splinter Trap](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge#Splinter_Trap "Stick Bug Challenge"), they will still receive the debuff. However, players will not see the pollen requirement needed to remove it and will continuously take damage until death unless they fulfill this unknown pollen requirement.

### Sticker Quests Buggy Progress Bar
Sticker requirements for a quest may reset to zero before moving back to the player's current progress.

### Buff Stacking Glitch
[![6 Bear Morphs active at once.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fb/HexaBearMorph.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20250413103113) [![6 Bear Morphs active at once.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fb/HexaBearMorph.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250413103113)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/f/fb/HexaBearMorph.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20250413103113) [](/wiki/File:HexaBearMorph.jpg)

6 Bear Morphs active at once.

On mobile, when exiting the app, players are usually kicked out of the game after a short time. However, sometimes they remain in-game. This can potentially stack up all tokens and events simultaneously, allowing for more than two [Bear Morphs](/wiki/Bear_Morphs "Bear Morphs") at once. While this may be purely visual, it could also potentially stack effects like Inspire x50.

### Broken Arm Glitch
Normally, because the player is constantly holding a tool, their arm remains permanently extended away from their body. However, a glitch can cause the player's arm to rest instead of being held up, making the tool stick out awkwardly facing the wrong direction. Typically, when using a tool, the arm moves down and then back up to collect pollen; under this bug, the arm cycles up, down, up again, then back down. Visually, this makes it appear as though the tool collected pollen twice, but it is merely a visual complication. This issue can be resolved by rejoining the game.

## Bee Glitches

### Stuck Vector Bee Glitch
During the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge"), if a player collects a duplicate token of a [triangulate ability](/wiki/Ability_Tokens#Triangulate "Ability Tokens") token from a [Vector Bee](/wiki/Vector_Bee "Vector Bee") that is currently in the [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") and was not chosen by the player during the challenge, the bee will get stuck in the air for a while and fail to move even after the round ends.

### Summoned Bee Glitch
If a player enters the [Robo Bear Challenge](/wiki/Robo_Bear_Challenge "Robo Bear Challenge") while there is a [summoned bee](/wiki/Bees#Summoned_Bees "Bees") in their [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive")—such as those spawned by [Onett's Lid Art](/wiki/Onett%27s_Lid_Art "Onett's Lid Art"), [Honeyday Candles](/wiki/Honeyday_Candles "Honeyday Candles"), or the [Gummy Beacon](/wiki/Gummy_Beacon "Gummy Beacon")—the bees will be teleported to the white circle on the player's hive and remain stuck there until their despawn timer finishes, even if the player completes the challenge beforehand.

### Negative Sprout Numbers Glitch
[![An example of the sprout glitch.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/83/RobloxScreenShot20210107_143451471_%283%29.png/revision/latest?cb=20210107194226) [![An example of the sprout glitch.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/83/RobloxScreenShot20210107_143451471_%283%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210107194226)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/8/83/RobloxScreenShot20210107_143451471_%283%29.png/revision/latest?cb=20210107194226) [](/wiki/File:RobloxScreenShot20210107_143451471_\(3\).png)

An example of the sprout glitch.

When a [sprout](/wiki/Sprout "Sprout") is popped too quickly, or if a player has high ping, the numbers showing the required amount of [pollen](/wiki/Pollen "Pollen") will display an excess amount with a negative sign in front of it. If a sprout is popped normally, it shows small negative numbers for a split second before disappearing. These numbers may go below the field and turn red; if the collected pollen quantity is high enough, they will turn purple.

### Phantom Token
This glitch can occur when a player joins the game while a [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm") or [Snowstorm](/wiki/Snowstorm "Snowstorm") is active, after a sprout has been popped, or through other means if the token appears almost exactly when the player joins. Images of tokens are visible, but the actual token itself is not. Players cannot collect phantom tokens; they remain until the player leaves. This can also happen on PC if the game is left minimized for too long, causing lag to prevent visual token despawning. A similar effect can be observed with [flames](/wiki/Flame "Flame").

### Honeystorm/Mythic Meteor Shower Fog
[![The no fog glitch (Honeystorm).](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a2/No_fog.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20221211103355) [![The no fog glitch (Honeystorm).](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a2/No_fog.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20221211103355)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/a/a2/No_fog.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20221211103355) [](/wiki/File:No_fog.jpg)

The no fog glitch (Honeystorm).

This glitch occurs when a [Honeystorm](/wiki/Honeystorm "Honeystorm"), [Snowstorm](/wiki/Snowstorm "Snowstorm") (from Beesmas 2020, 2021, and 2022), or [Mythic Meteor Shower](/wiki/Mythic_Meteor_Shower "Mythic Meteor Shower") is active, and a switch between [day and night](/wiki/Day/Night_Cycle "Day/Night Cycle") occurs: the fog disappears. This does not end these events; the fog simply becomes invisible. Similarly, if a Honeystorm or Snow Storm is summoned during a Mythic Meteor Shower (or vice versa), the fog will override to match the newer spawn, but then it will disappear afterward.

### Red Hive Slot Glitch
[![Red Hive Slots.](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/ba/RobloxScreenShot20191113_201729946.png/revision/latest?cb=20191114013608) [![Red Hive Slots.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/ba/RobloxScreenShot20191113_201729946.png/revision/latest?cb=20191114013608)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/b/ba/RobloxScreenShot20191113_201729946.png/revision/latest?cb=20191114013608) [](/wiki/File:RobloxScreenShot20191113_201729946.png)

Red Hive Slots.

This glitch causes a [hive slot](/wiki/Hive_Slot "Hive Slot") to appear red. To activate it, click and hold a random consumable from the inventory that does not provide bond (such as [gumdrops](/wiki/Gumdrops "Gumdrops") or [magic beans](/wiki/Magic_Bean "Magic Bean")) and use it as if feeding a bee. To fix it, players can either rejoin the game or click another random item (though [tickets](/wiki/Ticket "Ticket") and [Spirit Petals](/wiki/Spirit_Petal "Spirit Petal") do not work). This also occurs on empty hive slots; instead of turning red, they turn white. Using consumables that can be used outside of fields, like Red and Blue Extracts, can cause multiple bees to have red Hive Slots.

### Green Hotbar Glitch
[![green screen hotbar glitch](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2d/Green_Screen_Hotbar_Glitch.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200501185821) [![green screen hotbar glitch](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2d/Green_Screen_Hotbar_Glitch.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200501185821)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/2d/Green_Screen_Hotbar_Glitch.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200501185821) [](/wiki/File:Green_Screen_Hotbar_Glitch.jpeg)

The green hotbar glitch.

This glitch causes the player's hotbar to turn green. To trigger it, a player must move an item from their items tab to their hotbar and then close their item tab while still holding the item. Upon releasing the item, the hotbar should remain green until the player drags something else into the hotbar and lets go without closing the inventory tab.

### Stick Bug Music Glitch
This glitch occurs if the [Stick Bug Challenge](/wiki/Stick_Bug_Challenge "Stick Bug Challenge") is started and ends at night. Instead of playing the normal nighttime music, it plays the music corresponding to the player's current area. This is because the Stick Bug Challenge does not account for whether or not it is day or nighttime when changing the background music back.

### Translation Glitch
This glitch occurs when a player is running Bee Swarm Simulator in a language other than English. The cooldown timer on interactive objects will display static numbers, preventing the player from knowing the remaining time. Pollen counts on quests also glitch, and some mob names translate to their literal translation. Symbols (like plus `+`) may be swapped for others (like slash `/`), and various other errors can occur.

Invisible [Party Cogturret](/wiki/Party_Cogturret "Party Cogturret").

If a player joins while a [Robo Party](/wiki/Robo_Party "Robo Party") is in progress, all the Party Robots will appear invisible. The player can still attack them and collect their loot, and the robots can still harm the player.

### Uncounted Quest Pollen
Occasionally, the progress bar for a quest task appears as if it is not progressing, even while the player is actively completing the task. This issue is fixed by closing and reopening the quest tab.

## Item Glitches

### Sprinkler Glitch
When a player jumps and uses a sprinkler multiple times, they can stack sprinklers on top of each other or place them very close together, which may have certain usages.

### Combo Coconut Blue Circle Not Working
Even if the player catches the first [Combo Coconut](/wiki/Passive_Abilities#Combo_Coconuts "Passive Abilities") within the blue circle, it will travel to another player's field instead of remaining on the original field as intended.

### Rocket Launcher Glitch
In the [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge"), shooting yourself or another player with the [Rocket Launcher](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge#Shop "Retro Swarm Challenge") tool can sometimes cause the player to be sent flying erratically, similar to many other ROBLOX games.

## Other Glitches

### Honey/Field Grandmaster Badge Glitch
[![Broken honey badge](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9d/Broken_honey_badge.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231228024422) [![Broken honey badge](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9d/Broken_honey_badge.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231228024422)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/9/9d/Broken_honey_badge.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231228024422) [](/wiki/File:Broken_honey_badge.jpg)

If the requirement for a [badge](/wiki/Badges "Badges") is 10,000,000,000 or more of a specific resource, and the player completes the requirement, the badge will only display "_n_ / _n_" instead of the expected message: "_n_ / _n_ Complete! Press to collect."

### Double Passive Supreme Star Amulet for 490 Billion Honey
[![Ssaorsomething](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/38/Ssaorsomething.png/revision/latest?cb=20240304002803) [![Ssaorsomething](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/38/Ssaorsomething.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240304002803)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/3/38/Ssaorsomething.png/revision/latest?cb=20240304002803) [](/wiki/File:Ssaorsomething.png)

If a player possesses between 490 billion and 500 billion [honey](/wiki/Honey "Honey"), they will still receive the prompt to generate a double passive [Supreme Star Amulet](/wiki/Star_Amulet#Supreme_Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") for 500 billion honey when using the [Supreme Star Amulet](/wiki/Star_Amulet#Supreme_Star_Amulet "Star Amulet") Generator. If they select yes, they will receive a double passive SSA for only 490 billion honey.

### Ghost Sticker Glitch
If a player places a [sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") on the [Public Sticker Board](/wiki/Public_Sticker_Board "Public Sticker Board") and another player claims it very quickly, a ghost sticker may remain on the board. This sticker cannot be seen by other players nor can it be claimed, but it remains interactable.

### Retro Swarm Challenge Map Shift
[ ![NEW_RETRO_SWARM_CHALLENGE_GLITCH!!_*GAME_BREAKING_GLITCH*](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D) ](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:NEW_RETRO_SWARM_CHALLENGE_GLITCH!!_*GAME_BREAKING_GLITCH*) A video showcasing the fractured map.

For an unknown reason, the map of [Retro Swarm Challenge](/wiki/Retro_Swarm_Challenge "Retro Swarm Challenge") will shift, creating two large gaps—one in front of the hives and one in front of the spawner. The map then pushes players and brick walls toward a hole near the hive. Players and mobs can fall through these holes; however, players will teleport back to their hives when they exit the map's range. Mobs that fall off the map are considered killed, allowing the player to advance to the next round.

### Buff Rejoin Glitch
[Puffshroom Blessing](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Other "Buffs & Debuffs"), [Festive Nymph Blessing](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Other "Buffs & Debuffs"), and [Robo Party Blessing](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs#From_Other "Buffs & Debuffs") stop applying after rejoining a server, even if they continue to display. A player must refresh the desired buff after a rejoin to keep it active.

### Unpeelable Sticker Glitch
Sometimes, certain [stickers](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") will not peel off of your hive when using the 'Peel All' button. The sticker remains visible on your [hive](/wiki/Hive "Hive") and appears stuck in the [sticker](/wiki/Sticker "Sticker") console but cannot be interacted with at all.
