# Galentine Shrine

The Wind Shrine is a monument located beyond the Windy Bee Gate, next to the Pepper Patch. It allows players to donate items in exchange for rewards such as Field Winds and honey. The shrine has a cooldown of 1 hour between donations.

## Mechanics Overview

### Donation Process
Upon interacting with the shrine, the player is prompted: "Would you like to make an offering to the Wind Shrine?" Donating any amount of almost any item from the inventory triggers a message, followed by the chimes moving swiftly. If the donation is canceled, the message reads, "You canceled the donation."

*   **Cooldown:** Interacting with the shrine during cooldown results in the message: "You may only donate to the Shrine once per hour. Come back in x: y."
*   **Bulk Donation:** Players can hold down the "+" button to increase the quantity of donated items at an accelerating rate.

### Favor Mechanic
Favor is a mechanic that directly affects the rewards received from the Wind Shrine, including the chance and quality of Field Winds. Favor increases with every donation (5,000 + the favor value of all donated items). Starting a donation does not require a Spirit Petal to begin increasing favor.

## Outcomes and Effects

### Field Winds
Donating certain items gives a chance to summon random Field Winds. The likelihood of receiving high stacks increases with the quantity donated and player Favor.
*   **Color Bias:** Generally, the color of the item corresponds to the color of the field generated. Purple Potions are recommended for boosting red or blue fields. Eggs often yield large stacks across most fields; rarer eggs provide better winds. Swirled Wax tends to grant 7–8x white field winds. Gifted Gold Egg yields 7–9x winds, and Gifted Diamond Egg yields 10–11x winds.

### Windy Bee Summoning
To obtain a Windy Bee from the Shrine, players must first donate a Spirit Petal. Subsequently, donating Cloud Vials gives a chance to receive a Windy Bee Egg. The probability of success is tied to the player's Favor level.

*   **Success:** If successful, the shrine displays: "And it calls back to you!". The Windy Bee victory jingle plays, and a server-wide message appears: "🎉 [Player] has tamed a Windy Bee! 🎉".
*   **Failure:** If unsuccessful, the message reads: "But it doesn't respond...".

*(Note: Players who purchase a Windy Bee Egg from the Robux Shop receive a Star Treat instead of a Windy Bee Egg.)*

## Galentine Shrine (Event)

The Galentine Shrine is a special version of the Wind Shrine, obtained after completing Spirit Bear's Beesmas quests in various years. It features hearts and pink paint.

### Galentine's Blessing
Donating Strawberries offers a chance to receive rewards and the **Galentine's Blessing buff**, which lasts for 2 days. The probability starts at 1% with one Strawberry and scales linearly up to 50,000, where it is guaranteed.

The probability $P(x)$ of receiving Galentine's Blessing when donating $x$ Strawberries is:
$$P(x) = \left(\frac{0.99}{49999}(x-1)+0.01\right) \cdot 100\%$$

## Trivia and Notes

*   The Wind Shrine is based on wind chimes.
*   Spirit Bear mentions that the Wind Shrine is one of the oldest monuments in the game and provides different responses based on player Favor.
*   Using the shrine is currently the primary way to obtain a Windy Bee, aside from purchasing it via Robux Shop (historically).
*   If a player disconnects after donating but before rewards fade out, the next donation will grant the last set of rewards and reset the 1-hour cooldown, provided at least one reward was collected.
*   The chimes are collidable and not anchored; running into them causes movement.
*   **Donation Restrictions:** Items such as Super Smoothies, Translators, Sprinkler Builders, specific Bee Eggs/Jellies (unless already donated), Spirit Petals (if already donated), Gingerbread Bears, Snowflakes, Balloons, Planters, Robo Passes, the Beequip Case, and 7-Pronged Cogs cannot be donated.
*   Donating zero items results in an error message: "Error: Cannot donate 0 items."
