# Daily Top Mountain Top Field Collectors Leaderboard

The **Daily Top Mountain Top Field Collectors** leaderboard is one of 64 available leaderboards in Bee Swarm Simulator. It can only be viewed through the Global Leaderboards menu on the System Page.

This leaderboard tracks the amount of pollen players have collected specifically within the **Mountain Top Field** during that day. The board displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll down to view up to the 100th place. The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes and Rewards

Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Awarded a **Mountain Top Field Stamp Sticker**.
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 25 **Tickets**.
