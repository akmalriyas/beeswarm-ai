# Glue Dispenser

![Glue Dispenser](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/5/56/Glue_Dispenser.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20260203222530) The Glue Dispenser

## Overview
The Glue Dispenser is a dispenser located in Gummy Bear's Lair, situated behind the player teleportation point. It dispenses Glue and grants the Unlimited Gumdrops buff for 3 minutes.

### Mechanics
*   **Cooldown:** 22 hours
*   **Location:** Gummy Bear's Lair
*   **Usage:** Grants Glue based on the player's Goo Badge Rank, along with the Unlimited Gumdrops buff (for 3 minutes). The type of goo badge does not affect the duration of the buff.

### Requirements
Access to Gummy Bear's Lair is required, which necessitates having at least a Goo Hotshot badge.

## Glue Dispensed by Rank
| Goo Badge Rank | Glue Granted |
| :---: | :---: |
| **Hotshot** (5 Million Goo) | 2 |
| **Ace** (50 Million Goo) | 3 |
| **Master** (500 Million Goo) | 4 |
| **Grandmaster** (Goo) | 5 |

## Trivia & Notes
*   The Glue Dispenser is one of only two ways to obtain the Unlimited Gumdrops Buff; the other method involves specific in-game codes.
*   It is unique among dispensers because it requires a specific badge rank for access, as Gummy Bear's Lair itself requires Goo Hotshot or higher.
*   This dispenser, along with the Free Ant Pass Dispenser, Ant Pass Dispenser, Free Robo Pass Dispenser, Robo Pass Dispenser, and Coconut Dispenser, is one of the few that does not grant Haste.
*   It is also unique in being the only dispenser capable of granting a passive ability.
*   The Glue Dispenser shares the 22-hour cooldown with the Free Robo Pass Dispenser and the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser—the longest cooldown in the game.
*   Along with the Coconut Dispenser, this is one of the few dispensers that grants more items based on the player's badge rank.
*   Players can go underneath the white platform, but they cannot collide with the neon green decoration surrounding it.
