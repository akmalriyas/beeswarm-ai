# Commando Chick Drops

This article details the rewards obtained from capturing a Commando Chick.

## Milestone Rewards

The following items are awarded upon reaching specific capture milestones:

*   **5th Capture:** 1 Glue
*   **10th Capture:** 1 Gold Egg
*   **15th Capture:** 1 Star Jelly
*   **20th Capture:** 3 Blue Extracts
*   **25th Capture:** 100 Tickets
*   **30th Capture:** 10 Stingers
*   **40th Capture:** 10 Ant Passes and 10 Stingers
*   **50th Capture:** 1 Mythic Egg
*   **100th Capture:** 1 Star Treat
*   **200th Capture:** 1 Commander Bee Egg
