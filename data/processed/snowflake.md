# Snowflake

[![Snowflake](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/27/Snowflake.png/revision/latest?cb=20230406132508)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/2/27/Snowflake.png/revision/latest?cb=20230406132508)

_"Sends a cool, soothing breeze to all the players on the server (Melts after Beesmas!)."_

**Cooldown:** 3 seconds

A **Snowflake** is a limited-time inventory item introduced in the 2020-12-25 update. It is only available during the Beesmas event and becomes unusable outside of that period.

## Mechanics and Buffs

When used, Snowflake grants 9 seconds of the **Cool Breeze** buff, which increases various stats across the server. The buff effect scales with duration: it has a minimum bonus of '1%' at less than 1 second duration and a maximum bonus of '100%' at 15 minutes duration.

The specific stat bonuses granted by Cool Breeze include:
*   **Convert Rate At Hive:** +10% to +25%
*   **Pollen From Tools:** +5% to +25%
*   **Bee Gather Pollen:** +5% to +25%
*   **Honey Per Pollen:** +1% to +5%
*   **Loot Luck:** +1% to +10%
*   **Capacity Multiplier:** x1.25 (Note: The total capacity remains unchanged).

Snowflakes, along with Gingerbread Bears, can also be used to purchase various items from Bee Bear’s Catalog.

## Ways to Obtain

### During Beesmas Event
*   **Gift Boxes:**
    *   Shiny Gift Box: 50 snowflakes (3)
    *   Ruby Gift Box: 40 snowflakes (5)
    *   Stellar Gift Box: 50 snowflakes (6)
    *   Blinking Gift Box: 50 snowflakes (8)
    *   Ticket Gift Box: 50 snowflakes (10)
    *   Crafty Gift Box: 25 snowflakes (11)
*   **Environmental Drops:**
    *   As a drop from a Festive Sprout.
    *   Breaking Brick Blooms in the Retro Swarm Challenge.
    *   Catching fallen snowflakes on fields. During a Snow Storm, snowflakes fall much more often, averaging 20 per field. Players can summon a Snow Storm using the Snow Machine after completing Gifted Bucko Bee’s Beesmas quest.
*   **Quest Rewards:**
    *   Brown Bear's Stockings: 25 snowflakes
    *   Science Bear's Beesmas Lights: 25 snowflakes
    *   Dapper Bear's Samovar: 25 snowflakes
    *   Spirit Bear's Galentine Shrine: 100 snowflakes
    *   Gummy Bear's Goo Years Beacon: 500 snowflakes
*   **Present Rewards:**
    *   Black Bear, Panda Bear, Science Bear, Polar Bear, Robo Bear, and Spirit Bear give 25 snowflakes each when given a present.
    *   Gifted Bucko Bee gives 50 snowflakes.

### General & Permanent Sources
*   **Mobs:**
    *   Snowbear: Drops snowflakes every 25% of its health; the amount increases with level, dropping the most upon death.
    *   Rhino Beetle, Mantis, Spider, Aphid (Armored and Diamond Exclusive), Wild Windy Bee, Festive Nymph, Mondo Chick.
*   **Robux Shop:**
    *   Stocking-Stuffer Stuff: 500 snowflakes for 400 Robux.
    *   Seasonal Slumber Special: 1000 snowflakes for 1700 Robux.

## Historical Notes and Mechanics Changes (Trivia)

*   **Consumable Role:** Snowflake is one of four consumables that can increase capacity, alongside Marshmallow Bee, Purple Potion, and Super Smoothie. It, along with Nectar Shower Vial, are the only consumables that directly grant buffs to all players on the server.
*   **Buff Name:** Unlike many item buffs, Cool Breeze does not contain its own name; it is named after the buff itself, possibly because Snowflake is not the sole source of the effect (it can also be generated by the Snowglobe ability token).
*   **Pre-2021 Buffs:** Before March 1, 2021, Cool Breeze offered different bonuses: +5% to +50% Convert Rate At Hive, +1% to +25% Tool Pollen, +1% to +25% White Pollen, +1% to +25% Blue Pollen, and +1% to +10% Loot Luck, plus x1.25 Capacity.
*   **Usage Requirements:** Using only snowflakes required at least 129 of them to achieve the full 100% Cool Breeze buff, as the buff decreases while being used (though multiple players using it could reduce this minimum).
*   **Post-Beesmas Behavior:** Attempting to use a Snowflake after Beesmas prompts the message: "The Snowflake melts immediately...", rendering them useless outside the event.
    *   Historically, before 2024, keeping snowflakes in the hotbar allowed players to see their count even if they were hidden from inventory. After Summer 2024, all collected snowflakes were set to zero, though they might still appear in the hotbar but cannot be used. At the end of Winter 2024, remaining snowflakes stayed in inventories but could not be used normally.
    *   Even after Beesmas ends, Festive Sprouts may still drop them, but they will not register in the player's inventory upon collection.
