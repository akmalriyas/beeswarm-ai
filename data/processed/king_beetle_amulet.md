The **King Beetle Amulet** is an [amulet](/wiki/Amulet "Amulet") that has a 1/7 chance of dropping upon defeating the [King Beetle](/wiki/King_Beetle "King Beetle").[1]

This amulet grants a random selection of [buffs](/wiki/Buffs_%26_Debuffs "Buffs & Debuffs"). These buffs can include field-specific [pollen boosts](/wiki/Field_Boost "Field Boost"), [Convert Rate](/wiki/System_Page#Convert_Rate "System Page") bonuses, and/or [Bee Attack](/wiki/System_Page#Bee_Attack "System Page") bonuses.

The amulet's quality increases linearly with the number of King Beetle defeats, reaching a maximum quality when 100 kills are achieved.[1]

**Visual Placement:**
*   If the player possesses three or more types of amulets, the King Beetle Amulet appears above their right shoulder.
*   If the player possesses two types, it appears above their left shoulder.
*   If it is the only amulet type, it appears above the player's head.

## Trivia

*   The King Beetle Amulet was previously a guaranteed drop from the King Beetle, but this mechanic has since changed.
*   Visually, the amulet resembles the head of a King Beetle, tilted at a 45-degree angle.
*   It is the first amulet in the game to be dropped by a [mob](/wiki/Mobs "Mobs"). The second such item is the [Shell Amulet](/wiki/Shell_Amulet "Shell Amulet"), which drops from the Stump Snail.
*   This is the only amulet in the game that does not have a guaranteed drop rate from its source mob.
*   All rewards received from the King Beetle automatically appear in the player's inventory if the King Beetle Amulet is acquired.
