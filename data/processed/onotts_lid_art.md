# Onett's Lid Art

***Disclaimer:** This content contains information obtained through datamining. Due to the nature of this data, details regarding probabilities and item availability may be inaccurate or outdated.*
*Datamine Date: December 19th, 2024*

**Onett's Lid Art**, also known as **Yard Art**, is a special Beesmas decoration unlocked after completing the [Onett] Yard Art quest. It is located past the Bear Gate next to Onett and has an activation cooldown of 8 hours.

When activated, the player receives three temporary bees, an assortment of items, and activates a Guiding Star passive ability. If used before completing the required quest, it displays the message: "This art is missing something... It's not very impactful."

## Appearance
The Lid Art decoration features several distinct elements arranged from left to right: [Spirit Bear] on [King Beetle], [Black Bear], [Mother Bear], [Bumble Bee], [Stick Bug], and a Basic Bee fence gate.

Lore notes confirmed by Onett include the names: Queen Spirit Bear (Spirit Bear), King Stick Bug (Stick Bug), and King Bumble Bee (Bumble Bee).

After completing the Onett's Yard Art quest, a Baby Bee wearing a Santa hat appears in the cradle between Black Bear and Mother Bear.

## Mechanics & Drops

### Item Drops
When activated, the Lid Art guarantees certain items and provides two additional items drawn from a specific pool.

**Guaranteed Items:**
*   5 Pineapples
*   5 Strawberries
*   5 Blueberries
*   5 Sunflower Seeds
*   1 Gingerbread Bear

**Pool Drop (2 items selected):**
The player receives 2 random items from the following list, based on their respective probabilities:
*   Ticket (~49.38%)
*   Moon Charm (~29.63%)
*   Glitter (~9.88%)
*   Glue (~9.88%)
*   Star Jelly (Royal Jelly) (~0.99%)
*   Toy Horn (~0.1%)
*   Paper Angel (~0.1%)
*   Box-O-Frogs (~0.04%)
*   Festive Bean (~0.01%)

### Temporary Bees
The Lid Art guarantees two specific bees and provides one additional bee drawn from a pool of Level 8 variants (excluding the guaranteed ones).

**Guaranteed Bees:**
*   Bumble Bee (Level 20)
*   Baby Bee (Level 1)

**Pool Drop (1 bee selected):**
The player receives 1 random bee from the following list:
*   Lion Bee (Level 8) (~25%)
*   Music Bee (Level 8) (~25%)
*   Cobalt Bee (Level 8) (~18.75%)
*   Crimson Bee (Level 8) (~18.75%)
*   Festive Bee (Level 8) (~6.25%)
*   Tabby Bee (Level 8) (~6.25%)

### Guiding Star Activation
The Lid Art summons a [Guiding Star] passive ability on one of the five fields that the player has collected the least pollen from.

## Trivia
*   Onett's Lid Art is unique among Beesmas decorations for being the only one to activate a passive ability (the Guiding Star).
*   The longest cooldown period for any Beesmas decoration is 8 hours, shared with the Gummy Beacon and the Naughty List.
*   This art was historically one of two ways (alongside the Gummy Beacon) to obtain multiple temporary Event bees of the same kind.
*   The design references the birth of Jesus, a central event in Christian faith celebrated during Christmas.
*   It is one of four machines that grant players temporary bees; the others are the Honeyday Candles, the Gummy Beacon, and the Honey Wreath.
*   Bees summoned by Onett's Lid Art can attack mobs targeted by the player's actual hive bees, but they cannot convert into permanent hive members.
*   The only way to summon a Guiding Star without possessing a Supreme or Diamond Star Amulet is through this decoration.
