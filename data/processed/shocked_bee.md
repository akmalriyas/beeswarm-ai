# Shocked Bee

**Shocked Bee** is a Colorless Epic bee.

> "This bee is startled by everything it comes across. It has learned special talents to cope."

### General Information

Shocked Bee is a Colorless Epic bee. Its favorite treat is Pineapples, and it enjoys the Spider Field and Pineapple Patch. It dislikes the Mushroom Field.

### Stats & Collection Rates

*   Collects 10 Pollen in 4 seconds (Base).
*   Makes 80 Honey in 2 seconds (Base).
*   **Bonuses:** +40% Movespeed, +50% Convert Speed, +1 Attack, -50% Sleep Time.
*   🌟 **Gifted Hive Bonus:** x1.1 White Pollen.

**Trivia:**
*   Shocked Bee, Hasty Bee, Shy Bee, and Rascal Bee are the only bees in the game that have a spelling or grammar mistake in their description.
*   Shocked Bee requires less sleep than other bees; it is the only bee with this trait.
*   Shocked Bee and Music Bee share the same skin pattern.
*   Shocked Bee is the fastest bee to wake up from sleeping.
*   It previously had the Buzz Bomb ability, but this was replaced by Token Link.
*   This is the only gifted bee that does not have tinted wings.
