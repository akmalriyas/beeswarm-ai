# Bubble Light

![Bubble Light](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bee-swarm-simulator/images/1/1e/Bubble_Light.png/revision/latest?cb=20230409132141)

**Level:** 8
**Limit:** 3
**Color:** Any
**Description:** "The incandescent light bulb in this ornament brings methylene chloride to a magical boil."

A **Bubble Light** is a Level 8 Beesmas Beequip. It has an equip limit of three and no color requirement. It can only be equipped to bees that possess an Energy Mutation.
