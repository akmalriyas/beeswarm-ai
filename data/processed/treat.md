# Treat

A **Treat** is a consumable item that can be fed to a bee to raise its bond by 10 points. Treats are considered the easiest type of treat to obtain, although they are not the favorite food for any specific bee species. They also serve as a crafting material, notably required in large quantities for the Honey Mask.

***

## Acquisition Methods

Treats can be obtained through various means:

**Purchasing & Dispensing:**
*   **Treat Dispenser:** Treats can be acquired from Treat Dispensers located near the Pro Shop/Pineapple Patch. Access to these dispensers requires membership in the BSS Club.
*   **Treat Shop:** They can be purchased at the Treat Shop, which is located beneath Mother Bear's awning. The cost is 100 Honey per treat, with a maximum purchase limit of 10,000 treats.

**Rewards & Drops:**
*   As a reward from Memory Match gameplay.
*   As a stack reward for adding specific stickers to the Sticker Stack (e.g., the Fork And Knife sticker rewards 500 treats).
*   Farming in various fields: Dandelion, Mushroom, Clover, Spider, Cactus, Pumpkin, Coconut, or Stump Fields. Treats may also drop from leaves within these fields.
*   Drops from specific creatures, including Rhino Beetles, Ladybugs, Spiders, Mantises, Scorpions, Werewolves, Aphids, Stick Bugs, Stick Nymphs, Tunnel Bears, King Beetles, and Stump Snails.
*   A guaranteed drop when obtaining Mondo Chicks.
*   The 2nd capture of a Commando Chick rewards 2 treats; the 3rd capture rewards 5 treats.
*   "Playing" Fetch with a Puppy Bee.
*   As a drop from Festive Gifts or Festive Marks (Ability Tokens).
*   As a gift received from a Cub Buddy.
*   For completely growing any sprout, excluding Gummy and Debug sprouts.
*   Harvesting Sparkles.
*   Drops from Puffshrooms.
*   From various Planters: Plastic Planters, Candy Planters, Red Clay Planters, and Tacky Planters.
*   Activating certain Targets grants a small amount of treats.
*   Using Beesmas Feast during the Beesmas event provides treats and other items.

## Quest Rewards

Treats are frequently awarded as rewards for completing various quests:

**Black Bear Quests:**
*   Get Red-y: 100 treats
*   One Stop On The Tip Top: 200 treats
*   Blue Mushrooms 2: 300 treats
*   Pretty Pumpkins: 400 treats
*   Black Bear, Why?: 500 treats
*   Bee A Star: 1,000 treats
*   The Mythic Egg questline provides a total of 195,500 treats across all quests.
*   Repeatable Black Bear quests also reward treats.

**Science Bear Quests:**
*   Mark Mechanics: 3,000 treats
*   Epistemological Endeavor: 10,000 treats

**Other Quest Rewards:**
*   All of Mother Bear's quests total 4,020 treats.
*   All of Polar Bear's quests reward 10 treats each.
*   All of Onett's quests total 25,000 treats.
*   Every fifth completion of Honey Bee's quest may grant treats.

| Treats Required | Other Ingredients | Result Item |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |

## Outdated/Historical Information

*   **Obsolete Drops:** Treats were previously a drop from Chick, Hostage Chick, and Spotted Chick.
*   **Expired Codes:** Various expired promotional codes provided treats (e.g., TrickOrtreat gave 50 treats; MegaMittens gave 10 treats).
