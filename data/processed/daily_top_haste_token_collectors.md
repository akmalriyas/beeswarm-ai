# Daily Top Haste Token Collectors

The **Daily Top Haste Token Collectors** is one of the game's 64 leaderboards, viewable through the Global Leaderboards menu in the System Page. This leaderboard tracks how many Haste Tokens players have collected on a given day. It displays 10 positions at a time, but players can scroll to see up to the 100th place. The rankings reset daily at 12:00 AM CST.

## Prizes

Players are awarded prizes based on their ranking by the end of the day:

*   **Top 25:** Awarded the Basic Pink Hive Skin (Sticker).
*   **Top 100:** Awarded 50 Tickets.
