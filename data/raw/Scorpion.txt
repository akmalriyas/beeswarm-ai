The Scorpion is a mob that spawns in the Rose Field.
It takes 20 minutes for a scorpion to respawn after being defeated (17 minutes with Gifted Vicious Bee). Defeating it guarantees 5 Battle Points and 1000 Honey. Various other rewards are possible with their probability increasing with Loot Luck. It deals 25 damage to the player (before accounting Defense).
