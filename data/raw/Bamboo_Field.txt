The Bamboo Field is a field located past the Basic Bee Gate, to the right of the Spider Field, and 2 fields away from the Strawberry Field. It is protected by two Rhino Beetles, one being level 2 and the other level 3.
This is an 18×39 field, containing 702 flowers. The flowers are blue and white, in sizes of single, double, and triple.
This field grants Comforting Nectar when a planter is planted here.
This field can spawn honey, blueberry, ticket, blue boost, magic bean, and blue extract tokens when collecting pollen.
Bees that like the Bamboo Field are Cool Bee, Bucko Bee, Buoyant Bee, Carpenter Bee, and Ninja Bee. Bees that dislike the Bamboo Field are Riley Bee, Precise Bee, & Windy Bee.

Obtaining Boosts[]
The Bamboo Field Boost icon.
* Using the Blue Field Booster may give a Bamboo Field Boost. When it does, it will give +100% Bamboo Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using Glitter will add 100% Bamboo Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the boost isn't already maxed out.
* Using Field dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice may add +100% to 300% of Bamboo Field pollen for 15 minutes based on what type of dice is used, and also based on the current stack before the boost is added.
* Bamboo Field Winds can be obtained through the Wind Shrine or codes. They have a base of +20% instant Bamboo Field conversion and give +5% instant Bamboo Field conversion and +15% Bamboo Field pollen for each stack. Bamboo Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
* It can be boosted by the King Beetle Amulet. If it has a Bamboo Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +100%.

Trivia[]
* This is one of six fields that fireflies can travel to, with the others being Cactus Field, Pineapple Patch, Rose Field, Spider Field, and Strawberry Field.
* The player can find 2 royal jellies near the Bamboo Field, one in a nook under the ramp to the Brave Bee Gate, and the other at the end of an obstacle course, starting in between this field and the Spider Field.
* The bamboo stems have claw marks on them, presumably from Panda Bear.
* If a player stands on the corner of the Bamboo Field closest to the Spider Field while a Snowbear is summoned, the Snowbear can throw snowballs and damage the player.
* The three bees that dislike the Bamboo Field also have another disliked field (Riley Bee and Precise Bee; Pine Tree Forest, Windy Bee; Strawberry Field)
