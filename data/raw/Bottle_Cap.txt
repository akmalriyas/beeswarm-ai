Bottle Cap
"The discarded top from a bottle of pop."
Fact: Bottle Cap statistic: +Convert Amount.

The Bottle Cap is a level 4 Beequip. It has an equip limit of two. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to common or rare bees. It was released in the 2022-04-01 update.
