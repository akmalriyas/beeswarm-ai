Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Field Capacity

Fact: Field Capacity statistic: # Field Capacity.
Fact: Field Capacity statistic: The Pepper Patch Capacity Buff Icon..
Fact: Field Capacity statistic: **Field Capacity** is a mechanic that increases the player's Capacity Multiplier depending on what kind of field they are in. There are 2 types of field capacity: colored field capacity (shown as a stat) and field capacity for a specific field (shown as a buff)..
Ways to Obtain
* Donating certain stickers to the sticker stack can grant field capacity.
Fact: Ways to Obtain statistic: * Red Field Capacity can be obtained from Riley Bee's gifted hive bonus, the Demon Mask and Scorching Star..
Fact: Ways to Obtain statistic: * Blue Field Capacity can be obtained from Bucko Bee's gifted hive bonus and Bubble Bloat (Bubble Bloat can only be obtained if the player has Pop Star passive on Supreme Star Amulet)..
Fact: Ways to Obtain statistic: * White Field Capacity can be obtained from Exhausted Bee's gifted hive bonus, the Gummy Mask and Gummy Star..
Fact: Ways to Obtain statistic: * Coconut Field Capacity can be obtained from the Coconut Canister (x1.25)..
* Redeeming certain valid codes:
Fact: Ways to Obtain statistic: * ThreeBeeVee (gives Mountain Top Field Code Buff, granting +100% Mountain Top Field Capacity)..
Fact: Ways to Obtain statistic: * Using certain Beequips that include a Field Capacity boost stat..
Fact: Ways to Obtain statistic: * The Warm Scarf grants +1% to +3% Red and White Field Capacity..
Fact: Ways to Obtain statistic: * The Pinecone grants +8% to +15% Pine Tree Forest Capacity..
Fact: Ways to Obtain statistic: * The Snow Tiara grants +2% to +5% Blue Field Capacity and +3% to +5% White Field Capacity..
Fact: Ways to Obtain statistic: * The Rose Headband grants 14% to +40% Rose Field Capacity..

Fact: Outdated Methods statistic: ## Colored Field Capacity Formula.
The following formula shows how much capacity multiplier a player gets in a field:
Let x equal the colored capacity multiplier.
Let y equal the percentage of that pollen type in the field.
Fact: Outdated Methods statistic: Capacity multiplier in the field =.
{{ x − 1 } × { y 100 } + 1 ~\times ~\\{{\frac {y}{100}}\\}~+~1}} }
* The Spider Field is the only field where the player is guaranteed to get 100% of a colored capacity bonus, specifically white field capacity.
* This is achievable with Exhausted Bee's gifted hive bonus, by using the Gummy Mask, activating Gummy Star, the Warm Scarf's hive bonus, or the Snow Tiara's hive bonus.
* The only opposite version of this is in the Mountain Top Field; it generates no white flowers and therefore doesn't give white field capacity.
* It is also possible to get 100% of a colored capacity bonus on any other field if they generate entirely with one color of flowers, but the odds of a field generating like that is exceptionally rare.
