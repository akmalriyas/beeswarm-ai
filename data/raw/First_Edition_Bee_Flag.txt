Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - First Edition Bee Flag

First Edition Bee Flag
The decal for the First Edition Flag.
A first edition bee is a designation given to certain Event bees bought or earned during their first release. This designation is represented by a white and yellow "1st" flag appearing on top of the bee, and the flag emits a sparkling effect. The flag is purely cosmetic and does not change or affect gameplay at all.
This designation is currently only available for Bear Bee, Tabby Bee, Gummy Bee, Puppy Bee, Festive Bee, Digital Bee, and Windy Bee.
If the player removes a first edition bee, the bee will retain its first edition status when added back to thehive.
First Edition Bees[]
* Bear Bee: Bought during the first month after the game's launch (all First Edition Bear Bees got an additional bonus of being automatically gifted).
* Tabby Bee and Puppy Bee: Purchased during the their respective release sales (half price).
* Gummy Bee: Obtained after completing all of Gummy Bear's quests during the Gummy Invasion Event.
* Festive Bee: Obtained after completing all of Bee Bear's quests during the Beesmas 2018 Event or obtaining it from the Festive Bee Pack for 1,600 Robux in the Robux Shop.
* Windy Bee: Obtained as a special prize from the Wind Shrine or purchased in the Robux Shop for 800 Robux before October 27, 2019.
* Digital Bee: Obtained from Robo Bear's Shop behind Robo Bear or by purchasing the Digital Bee Pack for 2,500 Robux in the Robux Shop during Beesmas 2022.

Trivia[]
* Onett "Onett \(Developer\)") has stated that the reason a First Edition Vicious Bee does not exist is because there is no room for a 1st Flag on its back with the spikes.
* The earliest 1st Edition Bee was Bear Bee, coming in at the game's release.
* Even though First Edition Bear Bees are automatically gifted, the gifted status can still be removed if a player adds it to the hive again.
