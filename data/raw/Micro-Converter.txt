Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Micro-Converter

Micro-Converter
Micro-Converter
Fact: Micro-Converter statistic: _"Instantly converts all Pollen in your bag to Honey."_.
The Micro-Converter is an inventory item added in the 2019-04-05 update. When used, it instantly converts all pollen in the player's bag into honey. It acts as an Instant Converter without the need for tickets and the journey to the hive, a significantly shorter cooldown, and is portable.
Fact: Micro-Converter statistic: The amount of honey the player receives from a micro-converter is not affected by their Convert Rate, although it is affected by Honey Per Pollen..
The player can have a maximum of 15 micro-converters in their inventory at a time. Obtaining more than 15 will not add any micro-converters to their inventory unless purchased from the Robux Shop, obtained from Quests, or adding certain stickers to your Sticker Stack.
The following audio plays when a micro-converter is used: <https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Mconv.ogg>
