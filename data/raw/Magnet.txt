The Magnet is a tool that can be purchased at the Noob Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 5,500 Honey.
It collects 2 pollen from 9 surrounding patches every 0.8 seconds. It has a base pollen collection rate of 22.5 pollen per second on average, making it the second best tool in the Noob Shop.
Appearance[]
The magnet is consisted of a magnet colored red with white tips. The pole is a shade of grey.

The Magnet's associated sticker.
Trivia[]
* According to Slaries, an administrator on the official Bee Swarm Simulator Discord, the Magnet was the first tool Onett "Onett \(Developer\)") ever made when developing the game.
* The Magnet is a weaker version of the Electro-Magnet, a tool that can be purchased in the Pro Shop for 300,000 honey.
* In addition, the Electro-Magnet bears a different appearance to the Magnet; instead of the body color being red, it is yellow. In addition, it has four poles instead of two, and there is electricity in between the poles, unlike the Magnet.
* The two are the only tools to collect pollen in a square pattern. They also have the same exact collecting pattern.
* Interestingly, the Magnet's associated sticker portrays a different gray magnet with colored ends.
* This is the only tool sticker in the entire game that is completely different from the original tool's appearance.
* The Magnet’s design is most likely based upon a real-life horseshoe magnet.
