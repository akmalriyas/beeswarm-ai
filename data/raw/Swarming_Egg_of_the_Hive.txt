Swarming Egg of the Hive
The Swarming Egg of the Hive.
The Swarming Egg of the Hive is a cosmetic hat accessory that could have been obtained from completing the quest from Sun Bear during the Roblox Egg Hunt 2020 event. As it is a Roblox hat, players can wear it on their avatar for display. The Onett statue on top of the hives and the quest giver Onett wore the Swarming Egg of the Hive during the event. It could have been obtained from April 7 to April 28, 2020, during the three-week duration of the event.
Like the Flight of the Bumble Egg and How To Program BASIC, the Swarming Egg of the Hive does not give any special boosts and is just a Roblox hat that was only obtainable for a limited time.
