"A party hat that's been painted to look like a Beesmas Tree."

_Not to be confused withBeesmas Tree, a Beesmas-exclusive location of a similar name._
A Beesmas Tree Hat is a level 10 Beesmas Beequip. It has an equip limit of three. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to bees with a mutation.
