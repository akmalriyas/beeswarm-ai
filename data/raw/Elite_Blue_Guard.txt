> "_A Blue Guard reserved for the most dedicated Blue Beekeepers._ "
The Elite Blue Guard is a guard purchasable in the Blue HQ. It is equipped on the right shoulder.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 5,000,000 Honey.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 3 Blue Extracts.
50 Blueberries
5 Royal Jellies
Fact: 5 Royal Jellies requirement: 15 Moon Charms.
This guard is one of 3 guards that can be purchased in the Blue HQ, the others being the Blue Guard, and the Bucko Guard.

* Before the second part to the 2020-12-25 update, the player could glitch their Cub Buddy into this guard.
* This guard’s red counterpart is the Elite Red Guard.
