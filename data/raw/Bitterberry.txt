Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Bitterberry

Bitterberry
Bitterberry
_"Increases bond with a bee by 100. Small chance to cause Mutations."_
The text that appears in the chat when a bee gains a mutation, fed with bitterberry.
A Bitterberry is a type of treat that, when fed to a bee, raises its bond by 100 and has a 1/1000 chance to make it mutated. The chance is boosted to 1/100 if the bee is radioactive. These are not the favorite treat of any bee.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Redeeming certain valid codes:
* BeesBuzz123 (rewards 5 Bitterberries \+ other stuff).
* 15MMembers (rewards 15 Bitterberries \+ other stuff).
* As a stack reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* Squashed Head Bear Stickers rewards 25 Bitterberries.
* Finding the 10 Bitterberries under the Beesmas Tree.
Drops[]
* Farming in the Mushroom Field, Spider Field, Pineapple Patch, Cactus Field, Rose Field, Mountain Top Field, and Pepper Patch.
* Harvesting leaves in these fields may also yield bitterberries.
* As a guaranteed drop from Commando Chick, Mondo Chick, and the Coconut Crab.
* As a drop from ladybugs, spiders, scorpions, Rage Aphids, Tunnel Bear, and King Beetle.
* As a gift from a cub buddy.
* As a common drop from Mythic Meteors.
* As a drop from a Debug Sprout.
* As an uncommon drop from Puffshrooms.
* As a drop from the Pesticide Planter and The Planter Of Plenty.
* The Pesticide Planter has a chance of dropping a bonus 100 Bitterberries when harvested at full growth in the Spider Field, Mountain Top Field, or Pepper Patch.
