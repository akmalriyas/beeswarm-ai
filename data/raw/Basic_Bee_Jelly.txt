Royal Jelly[]
Royal Jelly is an inventory item that can be found as a token around the map, received as certain quest rewards, redeemed in valid codes, purchased from various shops or dispensers, or various other different ways. When used on a bee, it turns it into either a Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, a gifted variant of any of those bees, or a mutated variant of those bees.
The probabilities of Royal Jelly transformations are following:
Fact: The Variant in Basic Bee Jelly has/costs Chance.
Fact: The Common in Basic Bee Jelly has/costs 0%.
Fact: The Rare in Basic Bee Jelly has/costs ~70%.
Fact: The Epic in Basic Bee Jelly has/costs 27%.
Fact: The Legendary in Basic Bee Jelly has/costs 3%.
Fact: The Mythic in Basic Bee Jelly has/costs 0.004% (1/25,000).
Fact: The Event in Basic Bee Jelly has/costs 0%.
Fact: The Gifted in Basic Bee Jelly has/costs 0.4% (1/250).
Fact: The Mutated (Not radioactive) in Basic Bee Jelly has/costs 0.02% (1/5,000).
Fact: The Mutated (Radioactive) in Basic Bee Jelly has/costs 1% (1/100).
Royal Jelly does NOT necessarily turn a bee into a better rarity. For example, if a player used it on a Legendary bee, they have the same 70% chance of getting a Rare bee as if they had used it on a Basic Bee. This rule also applies to gifted and mutated bees.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Completing Brown Bear's quests rewards one or more Royal Jelly and other items. Other quest givers give Royal Jelly less frequently.
* Completing Black Bear's Diamond Egg, Star Jelly, and Mythic Egg quests rewards one or more royal jellies.
* Completing Spirit Bear's quests often rewards multiple Royal Jellies. The amount generally increases with each further quest taken.
* The Royal Jelly Dispenser near the Slingshot, Clover Field, and Dandelion Field dispenses them for 6 tickets each while also giving 10x Haste.
* Royal Jelly can be purchased individually or in packs of 10 from the Robux Shop. It costs 45R$ for one Royal Jelly, and 300R$ for 10 royal jellies.
* Royal Jelly can be found in different hidden and hard-to-reach locations. These jellies can be obtained only once for every player (In other words, they don't respawn).
* Royal Jelly can be claimed using the dispenser in the Star Hall every 22 hours. The number of Royal Jelly the player obtain depends on the number of Gifted bees in their hive.
* Finishing the Ant Challenge gives at least 2 Royal Jelly with the quantity increasing as the score increases by 50 points.
* From the sparkles made by the faces that can appear on certain objects or by fireflies.
* Finishing the Stick Bug Challenge or drops from Stick Nymphs during the challenge.
* Royal Jelly can also naturally spawn in fields, but the probability is extremely low.
* Growing a sprout may drop Royal Jelly tokens depending on the type of sprout.
* The Royal Jelly Shop near the Red HQ, where players can buy a Royal Jelly for 250,000 honey initially, with the price increasing by 10,000 honey with each purchase until capping at 1,000,000 honey.
* As a drop from specific Planters (Ticket, Plastic, Blue Clay, Tacky, Petal).
* A sprout in Clover Field drops more Royal Jelly than any other field.
* As a reward from placing certain Stickers on the Sticker Stack.
* Defeating mobs may drop Royal Jelly.
* As a reward from the Memory Match.
* As a drop from Festive Gifts.
* As a drop from Festive Mark.
* As a reward from the Wind Shrine.
* As a gift from a Cub Buddy.

All of these jellies share the same appearance of a royal jelly. These are primarily obtained from limited-time codes, Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas, and Bubble Bee Man.

Trivia[]
* Event bee jellies have infinite uses; as long as the hive does not already have that bee, the player can use the jelly.
* Royal Jelly is based off the real-life counterpart. The eponymous substance is used as nutrition for honey bee larvae and queens.
* Sun Bear gave a hint about the Royal Jelly on top of the Ticket Tent in his dialogue.
* When the player is transforming any bee with any type of royal jelly, the number of exclamation marks after the bee's name is the same as that bee's tier.
* Common/Rare = 1 exclamation mark (!), Epic = 2 exclamation marks (!!), Legendary/Event = 3 exclamation marks (!!!) and Mythic = 4 exclamation marks (!!!!).
* Using a specific jelly on said bee (eg. using a Bubble Bee Jelly on a Bubble Bee) does not use the jelly, and no transformation pop-up appears.
* Event Bee jellies cannot make the bee gifted even if it was gifted before.
* The plural for royal jelly is incorrectly spelled as 'Royal Jellys', rather than 'Royal Jellies'. This is a mistake that has happened with other items with a plural form that exchanges a 'y' for 'ies'.
* Using a normal jelly on a gifted/mutated bee is almost CERTAIN to cause the bee to lose its gifted/mutated status. It is a 1/250 chance to get a gifted bee back and 1/5000 chance to get a mutated bee back. However, the bee will most likely have a different mutation.
* If you attempt to use a royal jelly on a bee inbetween Robo Bear Challenge rounds, it will prompt you with the message: "⚠ Hatching or transforming a bee will end your Robo Bear Challenge. Continue?⚠". Choosing "Yes" will end your Robo Bear Challenge, and "No" will cancel it.
* The rarest possible bee obtained from Royal Jelly is a specific mythic bee that is gifted and has the mutation bee ability rate with a chance of 1/104T.
* The description of Royal Jelly does not actually say that you can get a Mythic bee.
* Star Jelly token behind Blue Maze used to give 3 Star Jellies, instead of 1.
* An effective method of obtaining large amounts of Star Jelly is fighting Stick Bug with its Beesmas quests completed, as the player can obtain a few star jellies when the challenge ends and some from Festive Nymphs or Stick Bug himself.
* Star Jelly’s raw ingredients cost:
* 1125 Strawberries
* 1125 Blueberries
* 1500 Pineapples
* 175 Royal Jellies or 175M Honey
* 3 Magic Beans or 60 Tickets
