Trivia[]
* Rage Bee is the first bee to produce an ability token that has their face on it, the second being Tabby Bee, and the third being Puppy Bee.
* This bee is also the only non-Event bee to do so.
* Rage Bee and Spicy Bee are the only bees that can produce rage tokens.
* Rage Bee has three different skin colors, similar to Baby Bee. When gifted, it only has two different skin colors, while gifted Baby Bee maintains this trait.
* A Special Jelly that changes a bee into a Rage Bee could be obtained by giving Panda Bear a Present during the Beesmas 2018 event (no longer obtainable).
* The Rage Aphid and gifted Spicy Bee has a similar color scheme to Rage Bee's gifted form.
* This is one of the only bees that can have an extra ability from Beequips (specifically Melody from the Toy Horn Beequip).
* The ability could previously stack up to only 3 times.
* The blue counterpart for this bee is Frosty Bee.
