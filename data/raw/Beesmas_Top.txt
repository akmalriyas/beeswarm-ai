Beesmas Top
Beesmas Top
"Younger bees enjoy giving this traditional Beesmas toy a spin."

Fact: Beesmas Top statistic: +Bee Attack.

A Beesmas Top is a level 4 Beesmas Beequip. It has an equip limit of three. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to common or rare bees.
