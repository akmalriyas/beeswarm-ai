Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Shell Amulet

Shell Amulet
Datamined information: Shell Amulet's possible stats and relevant information.
Date of datamined file: December 19th, 2024
The Shell Amulet is an amulet given by defeating the Stump Snail in the Stump Field. The Shell Amulet's tier can vary, but the player needs to have a certain amount of Stump Snail defeats to unlock higher Shell Amulet tiers.
The amulets grant buffs to the owner and always increase goo and Stump Field pollen as they go up a tier. Higher tier amulets grant more and better buffs.
The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the number of Stump Snail kills up to an unknown number, where its quality is maxed.
