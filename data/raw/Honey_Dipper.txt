The Honey Dipper is a tool available to be bought in the Pro Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 1,500,000 Honey.
It collects 2 pollen from 49 surrounding patches in 0.9 seconds, which comes to a base pollen collection rate of 108.89 pollen per second. This tool does not collect more pollen from a certain color of flower, unlike the Porcelain Dipper.

Trivia[]
* The Porcelain Dipper, sold in the Mountain Top Shop, is the enhanced variant of the Honey Dipper.
* The Honey Dipper and the Porcelain Dipper have the same collecting range.
* The Honey Dipper is the most expensive tool in the Pro Shop.
* The Honey Dipper is the third most expensive item in the Pro Shop, behind the Hiking Boots and the Propeller Hat respectively.
* The Onett statue on top of the 6th hive is holding the Honey Dipper.
* This tool is based on real life honey dippers.
* This is the second slowest tool in the game, surpassed by the Pulsar, Gummyballer, and Sticker-Seeker.
