The Silver Soakers are the 2nd lowest tier of sprinkler available for purchase in the Badge Bearer's Guild. To unlock it, the player must obtain 10 different Hotshot badges. Once the player unlocks it, they can purchase it for 22,222,222 (~22.2 million) honey. Its description reads, "Two sprinklers which fire faster!"
The Silver Soakers are comprised of 2 individual sprinklers.
Its radius extends to 7 flower tiles, its rate is 4.5 seconds, and its power is 7.
