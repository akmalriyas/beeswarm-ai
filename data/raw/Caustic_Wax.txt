Caustic Wax
Caustic Wax
_"A glowing hunk of beeswax. Has a 25% chance to GREATLY improve a Beequip - but a 75% chance to DESTROY it!"_
Caustic Wax is an inventory item and a type of wax added in the 2021-12-26 update that can be used on a Beequip to boost its stats. Upon usage, it has a 25% chance of greatly improving the Beequip's stats; however, it has a 75% chance of permanently destroying the Beequip instead, destroying the Beequip and also wasting the Caustic Wax. Caustic Wax has Upgrade Value of 4.
It also can be used to craft a variety of different items, such as planters and tools.
When successfully applying Caustic Wax to a bee's Beequip, a message pops up saying:
The Wax improved the Beequip!
If the wax fails to upgrade the Beequip, a message will pop up saying:
The Wax destroyed the Beequip...
Prior to the removal of Permanent Beequips, using a Caustic Wax on a Festive Wreath or Reindeer Antlers resulted in the following message:
Cannot use this wax on a Permanent Beequip

Trivia[]
* Caustic Wax's raw ingredients cost:
* 75 Honeysuckles
* 750 Sunflower Seeds
* 1,750 Pineapples
* 165 Bitterberries
* 25 Neonberries
* 6,067 Royal Jellies or 6.06B Honey
* The probability of Caustic Wax improving a Beequip 5 times in a row is 1/1024 or ~0.098%. Because of its low chances and its ability to destroy the Beequip, it is not recommended to try and use 5 Caustic Waxes on a Beequip when you are not prepared yet, instead people usually use Swirled Wax right after getting 1 successful caustic, to get upgrade rolls in desired stats.
* For some time, this wax could not be used on permanent Beequips.
* This is the only wax that can destroy Beequips.
* Bubble Bee Man's Beesmas 2022 quest required players to destroy 31 Beequips with Caustic Wax.
* Prior to the 2024-12-25 update, this item used to share the same icon with the Debug Wax.
* Although the Debug Wax texture has been changed, both waxes do the same thing, just that Debug Waxes have a 100% chance of working.
