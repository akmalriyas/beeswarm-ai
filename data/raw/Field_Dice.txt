Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Field Dice

Field Dice
_"Boosts a random field, granting +100% pollen for 15 minutes."_
Field Dice is an inventory item added in the 2019-04-05 update. When used, It boosts a random field the player has access to, granting a +100% pollen Field Boost for that field for 15 minutes. If the player uses another field dice, or any other type of dice while a Field Boost from a dice is still active, the new field boost will override the old one, preventing the Field Boost from stacking up.
As of the 2021-12-26 Update, there are currently 3 types of dice in the game, those being Field Dice, Smooth Dice and Loaded Dice.
The following audio plays when a field dice is used:<https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Fdice.ogg>

Trivia[]
* If another dice is rolled while a dice is already being rolled, this notification would pop up:
You are already rolling a dice
* Field Dice's raw ingredients cost:
* 1 Whirligig
* 5 Honeysuckles
* 50 Sunflower Seeds
* 50 Pineapples
* 50 Blueberries
* 50 Strawberries
* 50 Royal Jellies or 50,000,000 Honey
* The field icons shown on the dice are the Pine Tree Forest, the Strawberry Field, and the Blue Flower Field.
* This item is similar to Glitter, because they both give a 100% pollen boost on a field. However, it can only be used every 15 minutes, and the player can choose which field to boost.
* Field Dice is actually a plural name. The singular version of this item would be Field Die.
* It is possible to get a Mountain Top Field Boost and Coconut Field Boost from field dice.
* Unlike this, Field Boosters can’t give a boost to said fields.
* Donating 3 field dice to the Wind Shrine is almost guaranteed to summon Wild Windy Bee.
* There used to be a limit of 25 field dice, but it was removed in the 2021-12-26 update.
* The Field Dice used to be able to stack field boost allowing player to get up to 300% field boosts from it, it was later reworked in 2021-12-26 update, where dice system was reworked and with the introduction of new dice types.
* Rolling the same field three times in a row grants the Triple Exclamation Sticker, spawning it in the field rolled.
* The chance of this happening is 1/289 (≈0.34%). This is because the first field can be any field and the others must be the same.
* In addition, rolling the same four or more times in a row will continue to grant the sticker.
* The message for this is "Oh! That Field Dice had something on it."
* Normally, rolling the same field twice with field dice would just refresh the boost, but if you roll a field, leave, and then roll until you get the field again, it would stack (doesn’t go beyond the limit though).
