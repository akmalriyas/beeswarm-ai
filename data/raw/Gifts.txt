Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Gifts

_Not to be confused withPresents and Ornament Presents._
Gift Boxes are presents that appear during each Beesmas update, which are located around the map. To open them, the player needs to help a certain number of NPCs complete their Beesmas decorations by completing their quests. There are 13 gift boxes this year.

Trivia[]
* Originally, the Mondo Gift Box required 12 NPCs.
* When the second update had arrived, it changed to 14 NPCs.
* In Beesmas 2021, the Mondo Gift Box required 14 NPCs, then due to the occurrence of Dapper Bear being added, it changed to 15 NPCs. Everything that required 6-11 NPCs were made to require 1 more than before, and the Festive Gift Box was added, requiring 13 NPCs. This happened after the Midnight Gift Box was added.
* In Beesmas 2022, the requirements of every gift box that required above 7 NPCs had their requirement increase by 1. This happened after Chestnut Gift Box was added in part 2 of Beesmas, where Spirit Bear, Gummy Bear, Bubble Bee Man, Robo Bear, and Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") got new Beesmas quests.
* The Midnight Gift Box and the Chestnut Gift Box were the only gift boxes to be added in the middle of Beesmas.
* The Midnight Gift Box was the first to accomplish this.
* The Mondo Gift Box was the last gift box to get removed from the game in Beesmas 2020, because B.B.M's quest was extended by 1 week.
* The Stellar Gift Box (Beesmas 2025, 6th present) is currently the only Gift Box to have a unique shape outside of stripes.
* The Blinking Gift Box (Beesmas 2025, 8th present) is the only one to have an animation.
* It was not necessary to have 35 bees to access the Pristine, Restful, and Glass Gift Boxes. Instead, the player could go to the roof of the Red HQ, where they could jump onto the ledge with the box. However, taking just a few steps beyond that would bring them back to the spawn point.
* There were 10 gift boxes in total during Beesmas 2020, 12 gift boxes in 2021 and 2022, and there are 13 gift boxes this year. There has never been a gift box for helping one NPC..
* Up to the 2024-12-25 update, there was never a gift box for helping two NPCs, until the Elite Gift Box was changed from 3 NPCs to 2 NPCs.
* Additionally, the Elite Gift Box was originally by the ladder to Panda Bear's platform, which makes the Elite Gift Box the most modified gift box.
