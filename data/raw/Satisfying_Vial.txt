Satisfying Vial
Satisfying Vial
Fact: Satisfying Vial statistic: _"Grants 4 hours of Satisfying Nectar. (Boosts: White Pollen, Red Convert Rate, Honey at Hive)"_.
The Satisfying Vial is a consumable nectar vial item that was added in the 2022-12-26 update. Once used, it grants the player 4 hours of the Satisfying Nectar buff instantly. It can also be used to craft other accessories and tools.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Completing Dapper Bear's 5th, 11th and 15th quests each reward 1 Satisfying Vial each and other items.
* Using the Nectar Condenser to condense 12 hours of Satisfying Nectar.
* Adding the Satisfying Nectar Icon to the Sticker Stack rewards 1 Satisfying Vial.
Outdated Ways[]
* Purchasing the Hefty Honeyday Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog granted 5 Satisfying Vials and other items.
* Purchasing the Nighttime Nectar Pack in the Robux Shop granted 1 Satisfying Vial and other items.
* Completing Polar Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest rewarded 1 Satisfying Vial and other items.
* Opening the Gooey Gift Box rewarded 1 Satisfying Vial and other items.
* Opening the Silky Gift Box "Gift Boxes/2024 \(Winter\)") rewarded 1 Satisfying Vial and other items.
* Purchasing the Angelic Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog granted 1 Satisfying Vial and other items.
* Completing Bee Bear's Peppermint Preparation (5/5) rewarded 5 Satisfying Vials and other items.
