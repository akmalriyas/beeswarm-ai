> "_A durable pad worn on the left shoulder of Red Beekeepers._ "
The Red Guard is a guard purchasable in the Red HQ. It is equipped on the left shoulder.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 750,000 Honey.
50 Strawberries
Fact: 50 Strawberries requirement: 1 Royal Jelly.
Fact: 50 Strawberries requirement: 1 Stinger.
This guard is one of 3 guards that can be purchased in the Red HQ, the others being the Elite Red Guard, and the Riley Guard.
