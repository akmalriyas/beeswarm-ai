Trivia[]
* This is the second belt accessory awarded by Sun Bear on his first visit, the first being the Belt Bag.
* In the 2018-09-10 update, Sun Bear returned for a second visit wearing the Mondo Belt Bag and the Beekeeper's Boots. The Mondo Belt Bag worn by Sun Bear is much bigger than the player's.
* Before the 2018-11-25 update, the Mondo Belt Bag didn't require stingers, pineapples, or sunflower seeds to be purchased.
* This is the 4th strongest belt accessory in the game, behind the Honeycomb Belt, Petal Belt and the Coconut Belt.
* This is one of the few items that went from being a quest reward to being purchasable in a shop.
* Other items with this quality are the Belt Bag and the original three pairs of boots.
