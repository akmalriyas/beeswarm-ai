Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - OST

Music is a component in Bee Swarm Simulator. It is heard in the background during gameplay. Certain areas of the map have different songs. Music can be turned on or off in the System Page.
There are 15 songs principally used, all found in Onett's inventory.

Trivia[]

* icedtealatte made the StarHall song.
* This makes StarHall the only known song in game to be made by someone other than Onett "Onett \(Developer\)").
