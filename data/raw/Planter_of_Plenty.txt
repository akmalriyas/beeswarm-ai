BONUS ITEMS
Neonberries
The Planter Of Plenty is a reusable planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in about 16 hours and stores 1 trillion pollen. When harvested, it grants many bonus items, including Neonberries, Waxes and Turpentine. It can be purchased at the Dapper Bear's Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 100T Honey.
500 Magic Beans
Fact: 500 Magic Beans requirement: 100 Super Smoothies.
Fact: 500 Magic Beans requirement: 100 Swirled Waxes.
Fact: 500 Magic Beans requirement: 100 Caustic Waxes.
Fact: 500 Magic Beans requirement: 25 Turpentines.
Fact: 500 Magic Beans requirement: It grows 50% faster in the Pepper Patch, Stump Field, Coconut Field, and the Mountain Top Field, and grants 50% more pollen in the Mountain Top Field. Gifted Bees are 50% more likely to sip nectar from this planter, and it grants +50% of all Nectar Types..
Harvesting a fully grown Planter Of Plenty has a 1/3 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 9-10, and has a:
* ~72.94% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
* ~24.07% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
* ~2.92% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom,
* ~0.07% chance of being a Mythic Puffshroom.
When claimed, the planter gives up to 60 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, 2 of the tokens are guaranteed to be 5 Neonberries and 10 Tickets. Trivia[]
* This is the most expensive item in the game in terms of honey, surpassing the Gummyballer. It also requires the most ingredients to be crafted in the game.
* The Planter Of Plenty's raw ingredients cost:
* 100T Honey
* 60,250 Honeysuckles
* 562,500 Sunflower Seeds
* 7,025,000 Pineapples
* 4,983,750 Strawberries
* 4,983,750 Blueberries
* 21,000 Coconuts
* 67,650 Bitterberries
* 14,750 Neonberries
* 11,150 Magic Beans or 223,000 Tickets
* 3,383,400 Royal Jellies or 3.38T Honey
* While the in-game description states that it gives +50% pollen from the planter's boosted fields, it only gives +33% more pollen, and only on Mountain Top Field.
* The UI when harvesting The Planter of Plenty says "Harvest the The Planter of Plenty". Including two "the" because of the planters' name.
