The Pepper Patch is a field that was added in the September 28, 2019 update, located past the Windy Bee Gate, to the right of the Extreme Memory Match and to the left of the Wind Shrine. There are no mobs that protect this field.
This is a 21x27 field, containing 567 flowers. The flowers are red and white, and the only natural size is triple.

_Note:_ flower counts within the table may vary due to rounding. Additionally, in game, all flowers are randomly generated and may not precisely align with the percentages.
This field can spawn strawberry, bitterberry, royal jelly, red extract, ticket and magic bean tokens when gathering pollen. The only bee that likes the Pepper Patch is Spicy Bee. Bees that dislike the Pepper Patch are Looker Bee, Frosty Bee, Baby Bee and Fuzzy Bee.
This field grants Invigorating Nectar when a planter is planted here.
A level 9-12 Rogue Vicious Bee has a chance to spawn here.
Obtaining Boosts[]
* Using the Red Field Booster may give a Pepper Patch Boost (only if the player has 35+ bees). When it does, it will give +100% Pepper Patch pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using glitter will add 100% Pepper Patch pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
The Pepper Patch boost icon.
* Using field dice, smooth dice, or loaded dice may add +100% - +300% Pepper Patch pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
* Pepper Patch Winds can be obtained through the Wind Shrine or codes. They have a base of +20% instant Pepper Patch conversion and give +5% instant Pepper Patch conversion and +10% Pepper Patch pollen for each stack. Pepper Patch Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.

Trivia[]
* This, the Coconut Field, the Stump Field, the Ant Field and the Hub Field are currently the only fields released in updates.
* This, the Mountain Top Field, Stump Field, and Coconut Field are the only fields to solely have natural triple flowers.
* The Pepper Patch is the only field past a bee gate with nothing guarding it.
* This and the Coconut Field are the only fields where sprouts cannot appear naturally or be planted with the Special Sprout Summoner.
* The Pepper Badge, Coconut Badge, Mountain Top Badge and the Hive Hub Badge require more pollen collected than the other field badges.
* The Pepper Patch used to cover the entire platform it is placed on. Now, the field has shrunk a little and the area was amplified. As a result, it does not cover the edges of the platform anymore.
* The peppers used as decoration were also reorganized.
* This is the only red field that gives Invigorating Nectar.
* There used to be a Marshmallow Bee token next to this field.
