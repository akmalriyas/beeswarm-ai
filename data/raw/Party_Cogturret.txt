The Party Cogturret is one of the mobs exclusive to the Robo Party. It behaves similarly to a normal Cogturret but spawns on a random field instead of following the player on the field they are standing on. They first appear on Cake Rank 5 and continue to spawn in the following ranks.

Strategies[]
* Party Cogturrets aim for where the player is, not where the player will be. Because of this, moving to the side after they fire a cog will often result in Party Cogturrets missing their attack.
* Party Cogturrets will always shoot straight at where they are facing so you can avoid their attacks simply by not being in front of them.
* Stand near the Party Cogturrets, this will allow your bees to lock on to the Party Cogturrets and quickly deal damage.
* Vicious Bee and Windy Bee provide excellent crowd control, especially since Party Cogturrets often come in pairs or packs. Digital Bee can also stun many Party Cogturrets at once and allow them to take extra damage via Mind Hack.
