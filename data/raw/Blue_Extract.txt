Blue extract
Blue Extract
Fact: Blue Extract statistic: _"Grants x1.25 Blue Pollen for 10 minutes."_.
Fact: Blue Extract statistic: The **Blue Extract** is an inventory item added in the 2018-11-25 update. The purpose of it is to use it as material to craft equipment and accessories, or for a 10-minute boost that grants x1.25 Blue Pollen. The buff cannot stack, and will only reset its timer if the player used a **Blue Extract** when the effect is already active. Using a Purple Potion or Super Smoothie will override the buff..
A blue extract token.

* Crafting it via Blender for:
* 50 Blueberries
* 10 Royal Jellies
* Matching a pair of blue extracts in Mega Memory Match (worth 1 blue extract) or Extreme Memory Match (worth 3 blue extracts).
* As a reward from the Wind Shrine.
* Purchasing bundles from Bee Bear's Catalog:

Drops[]
* As a rare drop from rhino beetles and mantises.
* As a common drop from King Beetle and Diamond Aphids.
* Farming in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, or the Stump Field can rarely drop blue extracts.
* A rare drop from leaves in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, or the Stump Field.
* As a rare drop from sprouts (the odds increase depending on the rarity of the sprout and if the sprout is located on the blue fields).
* As a drop from a Festive Gift (if Festive Bee is gifted) or Festive Mark.
* As a rare gift from a cub buddy.
* As a drop from the certain Planters:
* The Blue Clay Planter, and Hydroponic Planter are guaranteed to drop blue extracts when harvested at 100% growth.
* The Hydroponic Planter drops extra blue extracts in blue fields (the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, and the Stump Field).
* Planting the Hydroponic Planter in the Blue Flower Field, then the Bamboo Field, then the Pine Tree Forest, then the Stump Field results in an additional 100 Blue Extracts (36-day cooldown).
* Planters harvested in blue fields may drop blue extracts.
* As a drop from a Wind Shrine badge shiver.
