The Electro-Magnet is a tool added in the 2018-06-02 update. It can be purchased in the Pro Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 300,000 Honey.
It collects 4 pollen from 9 surrounding flowers in 0.5 seconds, which comes to a base pollen collection rate of 72 pollen per second.

Trivia[]
* This tool is the upgraded version of the Magnet. Their appearances are similar and they are the only tools to collect pollen in a square pattern. However, the two have their differences. The Electro-Magnet is yellow and white in color, and it has electricity running on its tips, while the Magnet is red and gray, without electricity. It collects faster and also has four tips, whereas the Magnet only has two.
* The Electro-Magnet, Clippers, Golden Rake, Spark Staff, Petal Wand, Dark Scythe, Tide Popper and the Gummyballer are the only tools added in an update.
* The Electro-Magnet's associated sticker is missing its handle.
