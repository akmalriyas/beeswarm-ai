Comforting Vial
Comforting Vial
Fact: Comforting Vial statistic: _"Grants 4 hours of Comforting Nectar. (Boosts: Blue Pollen, Convert Rate, Convert Rate at hive.)"_.
The Comforting Vial is a consumable nectar vial item that was added in the 2022-12-26 update. Once used, it grants the player 4 hours of the Comforting Nectar buff instantly. It can also be used to craft other accessories and tools.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Completing Dapper Bear's 6th, 10th, and 13th quests each reward 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
* Completing Dapper Bear's Blue Drive quest rewards 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
* Using the Nectar Condenser to condense 12 hours of Comforting Nectar.
* Adding the Comforting Nectar Icon to the Sticker Stack rewards 1 Comforting Vial.
* Buying the Frozen Forest Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog for 1,200 Snowflakes and 60 Gingerbread Bears grants 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
* Giving a Present to Gifted Bucko Bee rewards 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
Outdated Ways[]
* Giving a Present to Gifted Bucko Bee during Beesmas 2022 rewarded 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
* Buying the Hefty Honeyday Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog granted 5 Comforting Vials and other items.
* Purchasing the Nighttime Nectar Pack in the Robux Shop rewarded 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
* Opening Certain Gift Boxes:
* Opening the Soothing Gift Box rewarded 2 Comforting Vials and other items.
* Completing certain quests:
* Completing Bucko Bee's Snow Machine during Beesmas 2022 rewarded 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
* Completing Peppermint Preparation 2 (Bee Bear) rewarded 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
* Completing Bubble Bee Man's Naughty List in Beesmas 2022 rewarded 8 Comforting Vials and other items.
