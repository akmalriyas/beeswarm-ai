Fact: Daily Top Red Pollen Collectors statistic: # Daily Top Red Pollen Collectors.
Fact: Daily Top Red Pollen Collectors statistic: The **Daily Top Red Pollen Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards in the game. This leaderboard shows how much red pollen players have collected on that day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM, CST..
Fact: Daily Top Red Pollen Collectors statistic: If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message "You've collected "X" Red Pollen today (Resets in "Y")" will appear..
Location[]
It is located inside the Red HQ, between the ladder and the Red Field Booster.
Prizes[]
If the player has reached the top 25 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with either the Left Mythic Gem FleuronSticker or the Right Mythic Gem Fleuron Sticker.
If the player has reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with 25 Tickets.
