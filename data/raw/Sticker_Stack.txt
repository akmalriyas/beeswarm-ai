Sticker Stack
The Sticker Stack while activated
The icon located on top of the Sticker Stack.
Fact: The Sticker Stack while activated requirement: The **Sticker Stack** is located in the 15 Bee Zone next to the Pine Tree Forest and the Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)"). It allows players to donate a sticker, hive skin, cub skin or voucher and receive a boost and item depending on the sticker you donated. The boost lasts for 15 minutes (+10 seconds for every sticker donated, maximum 1 hour). Each type of sticker can only be donated once..
To use the Sticker Stack, step into the podium in front of it and open the menu. The player can then choose to either pay 25 tickets or use a sticker that hasn't been stacked yet to activate the stack. Both ways lead to activating the sticker stack, granting the sticker stack buff with the duration and buffs depending on the past stacked stickers (15-minute base duration with an extra 10 seconds for each sticker stacked, 1-hour maximum duration). Stacking an unused sticker for activating the machine will grant a new buff depending on the stacked sticker and an extra 10 seconds duration to the buff permanently for future activations and the sticker that was stacked cannot be used to stack again. Upon stacking a sticker, the player will be granted rewards also depending on the sticker stacked.

Trivia[]
* The sticker stack was added in the January 12, 2024 update, along with stickers and the Hive Hub.
* The Sticker Stack buff ends a few seconds before you can use the Sticker Stack again
* If you get a reward from the sticker stack that contains a limit (like the Micro-Converter), the capacity limit is ignored and the reward is still given even if the player has the maximum amount.
* The machine is used for the 5 sticker stack badges, but its Grandmaster variant is impossible because it requires 500 different stickers stacked, whereas only 290 stickers currently exist in the game.
* Stacking certain stickers requires extra confirmation to stack them, which includes all vouchers, cub skins, and hive skins.
