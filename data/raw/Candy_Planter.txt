The Candy Planter is a reusable planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in about 4 hours and stores 1 million pollen. When harvested, it grants bonus Gumdrops and Jelly Beans. It can be purchased at the Dapper Bear's Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 5,000,000 Honey.
5 Magic Beans
Fact: 5 Magic Beans requirement: 30 Gumdrops.
3 Jelly Beans
Fact: 3 Jelly Beans requirement: 5 Soft Waxes.
It grows 25% faster in the Strawberry Field, Pineapple Patch, and Coconut Field, and gives 10% bonus white pollen from harvests. It also grants 20% more Motivating Nectar.
Harvesting a fully grown Candy Planter has a 1/7 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 4-5, and has a:
* ~80% chance of being a Common Puffshroom,
* ~16% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
* ~4% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
When claimed, the planter gives up to 14 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, 3 of the tokens are guaranteed to be 5 Gumdrops, 1 Jelly Beans and 1 Ticket.
