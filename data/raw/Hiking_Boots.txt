The Hiking Boots are a pair of boots that were added in the 2018-09-10 update. They can be bought from the Pro Shop.

Trivia[]
* These are the second most expensive item in the Pro Shop in terms of honey, only behind the Propeller Hat.
* These along with the Propeller Hat are required in order to access Dapper Bear's Shop.
* These could have been obtained from Sun Bear after completing his tenth quest before he left on 2018-10-08.
* Before the 2018-11-25 update, this item, Basic Boots, and the Beekeeper's Boots didn't require any crafting materials.
* Unlike the Gummy Boots and Coconut Clogs, these, along with the Basic Boots and Beekeeper's Boots, were obtainable by quests.
