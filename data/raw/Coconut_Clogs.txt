Trivia[]
* This is the second boot accessory that could not have been received from Sun Bear, mainly because it did not exist back then. It was preceded by the Gummy Boots.
* It is the only pair of boots whose name doesn't end with "Boots".
* This is the ninth most expensive item in the game in terms of honey (excluding hive slots).
* This is the second boot accessory to have a passive ability; the first one being the Gummy Boots.
* This, the Coconut Canister and the Coconut Belt are currently the only items that require a mob (Coconut Crab) to be defeated to access the item.
* This and the Coconut Belt are the only items to boost pollen from a field.
