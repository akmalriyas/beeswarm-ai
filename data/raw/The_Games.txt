The Games was a Roblox event that spanned across multiple games. In each participating game, players could earn 8 badges to contribute to overarching prizes in the main event hub.
In Bee Swarm Simulator, 5 of the 8 badges were awarded for collecting Shines, and 3 of the badges were awarded Silver for completing quests.
Overview[]
_Main article:Sun Bear_
Sun Bear arrived at the mountain and brought ⛳ golfing to the mountain. He had 3 quests required for the event, each one rewarding a Silver, which contributes to prizes in The Games event hub.
The 5 Shines were located in the following locations:
* On top of Sun Bear's RV
* Inside the Ticket Tent.
* Inside the Noob Shop.
* Next to the flower in the Blue Flower Field.
* To the right of the Beesmas Tree.
