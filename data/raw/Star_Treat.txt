Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Star Treat

Star Treat
_"Turns a bee into a Gifted bee!"_
The text that appears in the chat when a bee turns gifted via a treat.
A Star Treat is a special type of treat that, when fed to a bee, raises its bond by 1,000 and transforms the bee into its gifted form. It is the only treat in the game which is guaranteed to turn a bee gifted. It is also one of the only ways to get gifted Event bees, including Gingerbread Bears and Aged Gingerbread Bears.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Buying it from Bee Bear's Catalog for 10,000 Snowflakes.
* Buying it for 1,000 Tickets inside the Ticket Tent.
* Playing Night Memory Match/Extreme Memory Match/Winter Memory Match (exceptionally rare).
* Given by the Wind Shrine if you already have a Windy Bee after buying the Windy Bee Pack.
Drops[]
* An exceptionally rare drop from the Mondo Chick, Coconut Crab, and fireflies.
* Capturing Commando Chick for the 100th time guarantees a star treat.
* An exceptionally low chance of dropping from Mythic Meteors.
* An extremely rare drop from Festive Planters.
Quests[]
* Completing all of Onett's quests, Mother Bear's quests, and Panda Bear's quests.
* Completing 750 Brown Bear quests rewards a star treat. Every 500 quests after the 750th Brown Bear quest also rewards a star treat. Additionally, his 1000th quest grants the player 5 Star Treats.
Outdated Ways[]
* Buying the Gingerbread Cub Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog (1 if the player didn't have a cub buddy, 2 if they already had one.)
* An extremely low chance of getting it from Winter Memory Match.
* Completing the 15th Bee Bear quest rewards a star treat if the player already owns a cub buddy.
* An exceptionally rare drop from Festive Nymphs.
* An extremely rare drop from the Stockings.
* By opening certain Gift Boxes:
* The Mondo Gift Box gave 1 star treat + other items during Beesmas 2020, 2021 and 2022.
* Buying it from Bee Bear's Catalog for 10,000 Snowflakes.
Trivia[]
* This and the Atomic Treat give the most bond (1,000 bond) out of any treat, followed by a single Neonberry and Aged Gingerbread Bears (500 bond), then Gingerbread Bears and Moon Charms (250 bond).
* The star treat, along with Aged Gingerbread Bears are the only treats that cannot be obtained from sprouts.
* The star treat formerly gave 100 bond instead of 1,000.
* Feeding a Star Treat to a gifted Bear Bee has a 1/3 chance to spawn a Bear Bee Offer sticker.
