Trivia[]
* It closely resembles an American football helmet, but it’s completely yellow and it doesn't have a faceguard.
* This, the Propeller Hat, the Beekeeper's Mask, and the Strange Goggles are the only masks not to be based on a bee.
* This is the most expensive item in the Noob Shop.
* This is the cheapest obtainable mask in the game in terms of honey.
