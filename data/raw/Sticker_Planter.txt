BONUS ITEMS
The Sticker Planter is a single-use planter added in the 2024-01-12 update. Alone, it grows in about 3 hours of playtime and stores 200,000 pollen. A player can store up to 25 Sticker Planters at a time, but only 1 can be active.
It does not have any growth nor pollen multipliers, and grants x2 nectar.
Harvesting a fully grown Sticker Planter is guaranteed to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 5-7, and has a:
* ~89.28% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
* ~17.86% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
* ~0.89% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom,
* ~0.01% chance of being a Mythic Puffshroom.

Ways To Obtain[]
* As a drop from Sticker Sprouts.
* By purchasing it in the Robux Shop for 100 Robux (requires having 20 bees to purchase, and has a limit of 10 per player.)
*   * Completing Sticker-Seeker quests has between a 2% and 5% chance of awarding a Sticker Planter based on if you haven't completed any Sticker-Seeker quests or if you have completed 500 or more respectively.
* Completing Quests 11, 33, 55, 66, 77, 99, 111, 133, 144, 155, 166, 199, 244, 255, 266, 277, or 411 each guarantee a Sticker Planter.
* Completing Quest 222 and 422 guarantees 2 Sticker Planters.
* Completing Quest 333 guarantees 3 Sticker Planters.
* Completing Quest 444 guarantees 4 Sticker Planters.
* Completing Quest 555 guarantees 5 Sticker Planters.
* Completing Bee Bear's "Stocking Up On Stickers" quest rewards a Sticker Planter and other items.
* Purchasing the Saint Puff's Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog for 50 Gingerbread Bears gives a Sticker Planter and other items.
Outdated Ways[]
* Being in the top 100 of the Tallest Sticker Stack leaderboard in the Public Test Realm on 2024-01-13 gave 10 Sticker Planters. This reward was also accidentally given to the top 100 in the main game too.
* Completing Bee Bear's fifth quest during Beesmas 2024 rewarded a Sticker Planter and other items.
* By purchasing certain bundles from Bee Bear's Catalog:
* The Planter Sampler Pack that costed 400 Snowflakes and 8 Gingerbread Bears gave 1 Sticker Planter and other items.
* The Sticker Sticker Bundle that costed 50 Gingerbread Bears gave 5 Sticker Planters and other items.

Trivia[]
* The Sticker Planter continuously alternates between different colors, which can be tuned down in the Settings.
* Out of the four single-use planters, the Sticker Planter is the only one of them to have a cap of 25.
* This, the Ticket Planter and thePaper Planter share the same base models.
* They also share the same outline for their decals.
* It is tied with the Ticket Planter for the second-best Nectar multiplier in the game, behind the Festive Planter at x3.
* It is one of four single-use planters in the game, the others being the Festive Planter, the Ticket Planter and the Paper Planter.
* This and the Ticket Planter are the only planters that will always spawn a Puffshroom at full growth.
* Additionally, this is the only planter to always spawn at least a Rare Puffshroom.
