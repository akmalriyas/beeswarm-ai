Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Glitter

_"Boosts the field you're standing in, granting +100% pollen for 15 minutes."_
15 minutes 5 seconds
Fact: 15 minutes 5 seconds requirement: **Glitter** is a craftable inventory item that was added in the 2018-11-25 update. It grants a +100% pollen boost in the field the player is standing in for 15 minutes upon activation. If used in a field with a planter, the planter gains a 25% growth rate boost until it is harvested. **Glitter** has a 15 minute and 5 second cooldown between uses. This is to prevent the user from stacking the item's buff for one field..
It can be used in all fields except the Ant Field. Attempting to do so will result in the following message:  You can't boost a challenge field

* Purchasing the White Beesmas Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog for 100 Snowflakes and 3 Gingerbread Bears grants 3 Glitter and other items.
* As a stack reward for adding certain Stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* Sideways Spirit Bear rewards 1 glitter.
* Shining Halo rewards 1 glitter.
* Glowing Smile rewards 1 glitter.
* Every Star Sign sticker rewards 1 glitter.

Trivia[]
* This is one of four consumable items to grant a field boost, the others being Field Dice, Smooth Dice, and Loaded Dice
* It also is the one of the four consumables that shows glitter particles on the field when the said field is boosted.
* This is the only consumable to grant a Field Boost of the player's choice.
* Spirit Bear mentions in one of her quest dialogues, Spirit's Starter, that glitter was once so rare that 1 pouch of it costed 5 million honey.
* Glitter’s raw ingredients cost:
* 375 Strawberries
* 375 Blueberries
* 500 Pineapples
* 25 Royal Jellies or 25M Honey
* 1 Magic Bean or 20 Tickets
