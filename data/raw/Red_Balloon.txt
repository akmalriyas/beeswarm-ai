Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Red Balloon

Red Balloon
Red Balloon
_"Floats over the field for 3 minutes. Stores up to 5x your capacity and works best with Red flowers."_
The Red Balloon is a consumable item that was added in the 2021-12-26 update. Once used, it creates a red balloon over the field the player is in. The balloon floats over the field, storing pollen collected underneath it. The pollen collected increases by 10%, with red pollen increasing by 20%. It holds up to 5x the player's capacity, and floats over the field for 3 minutes before floating to the hive balloon.
Red balloons have a cap of 25 in the player's inventory, and 1 per player in the field.
Ways to Obtain[]
* As a exceptionally rare drop from a scorpion.
* As a rare reward from the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
* Completing Dapper Bear's 6th quest gives 1 Red Balloon and other items.
* A Red Balloon token can be found on top of the Hive Hub after completing the parkour.
* Redeeming certain valid codes:
* 15MMembers (gives 1 Red Balloon and other stuff).
Outdated Ways[]
* Purchasing the Balloon Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog for 333 Snowflakes, granting 3 Red Balloons and other items.
* By completing Onett's Beesmas 2022 quest, which rewards 5 Red Balloons.
* By completing Bee Bear's Beesmas 2022 quests.
* Giving a present to Gifted Riley Bee gives 1 Red Balloon and other items.
* As a reward from completing certain quests from Bee Bear.
* Buying the Balloon Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2021, granting 3 Red Balloons and other items.
* Buying the Buoyant Pack from the Robux shop gave 5 Red Balloons and other items.
* As a rare drop from Party Mega Mechsquitoes and Party Cogturrets.
* Opening certain Gift Boxes across the map:
* Beige Gift Box gave 1 Red Balloon and other items.
* Gilded Gift Box gave 1 Red Balloon and other items.
* Redeeming certain expired codes:
* 5MFavorites (gave 1 Red Balloon and the Honeyday Event Boost).
* DarzethDoodads (gave 1 Red Balloon and other stuff).
