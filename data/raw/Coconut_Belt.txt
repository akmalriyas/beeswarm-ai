Fact: 3 Refreshing Vials requirement: * Every 30th boost token collected fires 30 Petal Shurikens around you. Every bee hit will instantly convert 10,000 (+6% of their Convert Amount, +4% per Field Boost stack) pollen into honey. It has a 30 second cooldown before another usage..
Fact: 3 Refreshing Vials requirement: * +Passive: Combo Coconuts..
Fact: 3 Refreshing Vials statistic: * Every 40th Coconut you drop onto a field (through items or abilities) becomes a Combo Coconut. When caught by any player. Combo Coconuts are kicked and fall again, onto your own field if caught on the first drop within the blue circle, or onto another players field. Every time they are kicked, they speed up, and gain stacks of Coconut Combo. Many more stacks are granted if caught by other players in fields with different colors. Catching the Coconut grants a buff that grants between 25% to 75% Unique Instant Conversion, between x1.2 to x2 Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, White Pollen, Bee Attack, and Honey From Tokens. When the Coconut fails to be caught it despawns and reapplies the buff to all players who caught it at least once..

Trivia[]
* This is the third most expensive item in the game, surpassing both the Dark Scythe and Tide Popper, after the Gummyballer and The Planter Of Plenty.
* This is the most expensive item in the Coconut Cave.
* Coconut Belt's raw ingredients cost:
* 7.5T Honey
* 17,300 Coconuts
* 996,000 Pineapples
* 220,500 Sunflower Seeds
* 612,750 Blueberries
* 612,750 Strawberries
* 11,550 Bitterberries
* 8,250 Honeysuckles
* 1,710 Neonberries
* 374,160 Royal Jellies or 374B Honey
* 1,170 Magic Beans or 23,400 Tickets
Fact: * 1,170 Magic Beans or 23,400 Tickets statistic: * The buff used to give between x1.1 to x1.5 Pollen and +1% to +50% Pollen, but it has been changed to only include between x1.2 to x2 Pollen. However, the passive description in-game remains unchanged..
* Unique Instant Conversion buff actually starts at +25%, not +10% like it states in-game.
* The blue circle doesn't work properly and even if the player catches within it, the coconut will fall onto another player's field instead of staying.
