Trivia[]
* This is the only available Hat in the Pro Shop.
* This is the most expensive Item available in the Pro Shop.
* This along with the Hiking Boots are required in order to access Dapper Bear's Shop.
* This, the Beekeeper's Mask, the Helmet, and the Strange Goggles are the only masks not to be based off bees and NPCs.
* This hat can be used along with the Hiking Boots to access the Rose Field from the Sunflower Field and enable access to various Royal Jelly tokens around the map.
* After the 2018-09-10 Update, its stats were nerfed. +15 Jump Power was reduced to +10, and +4 Movespeed was removed.
* Before the 2018-11-25 Update, it cost 3,000,000 honey and no Crafting Materials.
* After the 2018-09-10 update, this became the only hat to grant Jump Power.
