Trivia[]
* Based on its description, this bee is the rival and counterpart of Bucko Bee.
* Both of them are the leaders of their respective colors.
* Riley Bee, Rascal Bee, and Demon Bee all have the same skin colors.
* A giant model of a Riley Bee is on the front of the Red HQ.
* Gifted Riley Bee is a quest giver represents the gifted version of it in the game.
* The Riley Guard is based on this bee.
* Riley Bee and Festive Bee are the only red bees to like three fields.
* Riley Bee is also the only red bee to dislike two fields.
