Trivia[]
* The Honey Mask, the Bubble Mask, Fire Mask, Bucko Guard and Riley Guard all represent epic bees.
* The Honey Mask and the Diamond Mask are the only items that require eggs to be crafted.
* After the 2019-04-05 update, the Instant Conversion buff was removed from the mask, along with all of the other masks.
* After the 2019-09-28 update, the Coin Scatter passive ability was added to the mask.
* If the player owns the Honey Mask, its passive is inherited by the Gummy Mask.
* If the ingredients to craft the Honey Mask item were to be crafted, the Honey Mask mask costs 100,000,000 Honey, 9,999 Treats, 2,500 Sunflower Seeds, 1,250 Pineapples, 750 Royal Jellies, and 5 Gold Eggs. It also takes approximately 375 minutes (6 hours and 15 minutes) to craft all of the required items.
* The Honey & Gummy Masks are the only masks in the game that require a specific Badge to access and purchase.
* During Beesmas 2020 and Beesmas 2021, in order to give Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") a Present, the player had to equip the Honey Mask before doing so.
* Token links can't collect honey tokens from Coin Scatter. However, Vector Bee's Triangulate can.
