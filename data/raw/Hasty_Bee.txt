Hasty Bee is a Colorless Rare bee.
Hasty Bee's favorite treat is Pineapples
Hasty Bee likes the Sunflower Field and Cactus Field. It dislikes the Pumpkin Patch and Stump Field.
* Collects 10 pollen in 3 seconds.
* Makes 80 honey in 3 seconds.
* +40% movespeed, +25% gather and convert speed.
* 🌟 Gifted Hive Bonus: +15% Player Movespeed.

Trivia[]
* The Hasty Guard, which can be bought in the Pro Shop, has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Hasty Bee.
* This is the only bee to have a gifted bonus that affects Player Movespeed.
* However, Ninja Bee also originally gave the same gifted bonus before it was changed to +5% Bee Movespeed in the 2018-11-25 update.
* This bee, Shocked Bee, Shy Bee, and Rascal Bee are the only bees that have either a spelling or grammar mistake in their description.
* A special jelly that changes a bee into a Hasty Bee can be obtained by using the code, 'HasteHelper' (expired).
* This bee, Shocked Bee, Ninja Bee, and Photon Bee, are the only bees that grant the Haste token without any certain Beequips.
* Hasty Bee most likely dislikes the Stump Field because it is home to the Stump Snail, who moves very slowly.
* Hasty Bee is the fastest Rare bee.
