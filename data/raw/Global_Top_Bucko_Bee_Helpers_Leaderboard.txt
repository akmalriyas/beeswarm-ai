The All-Time Top Bucko Bee Helpers is one of the 64 leaderboards in the game. It displays the top 100 people who've completed the most quests for Gifted Bucko Bee. It can display 10 leaderboard positions at a time. If the player stands on the white circle, they can see how many Gifted Bucko Bee quests that they have completed.
Location[]
It is located on the second floor of the Blue HQ next to Gifted Bucko Bee.
