Moon Amulet Generator
The Moon Amulet Generator.
The Moon Amulet Generator is a machine located between Spider Field and Bamboo Field that allows the player to generate and/or replace a Moon Amulet. It costs 100 Moon Charms to use.
It can be reached by traversing the obstacle course between the Spider Field and Bamboo Field, by dropping in from above by cannon and using a gliding tool or by falling off the ramp leading to the Lion Bee Gate and onto the platform.
Every time the player uses the generator, the capacity buff increases, starting at 25k and increasing by 25k each. This buff stops at the maximum of 250k capacity. However, to obtain the Moon Amulet at its highest potential, the player would have to use the generator 25 times, being when the other (potential) stat boosts max out.
When using the generator, it will say:  🌙 The moon seems to grow brighter... 🌙 If the player has reached the maximum capacity buff (+250k), the chat instead states:  🌙 The moon seems happy! 🌙

Trivia[]
* The Moon Amulet Generator glows at night, although nothing changes mechanically.
* The Moon Amulet Generator is one of the 6 amulet generators, the other 5 being the star amulet generators in the Star Hall.
* This is the only amulet generator that requires something other than honey to activate.
