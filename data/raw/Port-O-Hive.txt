Trivia[]
* This is the most expensive bag in the Pro Shop.
* This is the fourth most expensive item in the Pro Shop, behind the Honey Dipper, Hiking Boots, and the Propeller Hat respectively.
* Out of the 4 variants of the Port-O-Hive, this was the first one added into the game. The other variants are the Red Port-O-Hive, the Blue Port-O-Hive, and the Porcelain Port-O-Hive.
* The Onett statue on top of the 6th hive wears a Port-O-Hive saying "38217", which is a code.
