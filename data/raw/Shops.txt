Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Shops

Shops contain various items that can be purchased with honey, tickets or other currencies and, for some (usually found in mid to late-game shops), crafting materials as well. They may have items such as bags, guards, tools, eggs, and other miscellaneous items that can give more capacity and other buffs. Some shops sell only one type of item and may have a price that increases every time the item is purchased, such as tickets.
