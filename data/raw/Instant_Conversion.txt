Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Instant Conversion

Instant Conversion
Fact: Instant Conversion statistic: **Instant Conversion** is a mechanic that instantly converts some of the player's collected pollen into honey. The amount of converted pollen depends on how much instant conversion (%) the player has, with support from the respective type of instant conversion (white, red, and blue instant conversion, etc.). This effectively makes the player's bag bigger, in the sense that the player can collect more pollen than the bag would normally hold. The player's Honey Per Pollen stat boosts the amount of honey received from instant conversion. There are also color variants for each flower color (blue, red, white) that only instantly convert pollen of their respective colors..
Instant Conversion Variants[]
Instant Conversion has 8 variants :
* Flame Conversion
* Field Conversion (e.g. field winds)
* Colored Conversion
* Goo Conversion
* Bomb Conversion
* Unique Instant Conversion
* Instant Tool Conversion
* Bee Gather Conversion
Goo Conversion[]
_Goo Conversion_ converts a flat amount of pollen equal to a percentage of the Goo collected from a flower.
Unique Instant Conversion[]
_Unique Instant Conversion_ is applied independently of all other Instant Conversion types.
For example, if you have two different sources of 50% Unique Instant Conversion , each source is applied separately. This results in an effective 75% Instant Conversion , not 100%.
Conversion Order[]
Instant Conversion is applied in the following order:[1]
1. Colored Conversion
2. Field Conversion
3. Regular Instant Conversion
4. Goo Conversion
It is unknown where Unique Instant Conversion and Ability-specific Instant Conversion is placed in this order.
For example, if a player has 50% of four different conversion variants and collects 10,000 red pollen, 50% of the pollen is converted, then 50% of the remaining amount, and so on for each variant.

Effects[]
* When instant conversion is in effect, and pollen text is turned on, the player will see yellow numbers in addition to the blue/red/white pollen numbers while they're working in the fields. Those yellow numbers are the amount of pollen that instantly became honey; Collected honey amounts also show up in yellow, but those are also listed in the bottom right corner of the player's screen.
* The formula for the effective increase in capacity given by instant conversion is: (x/(100-x)), where x is the player's total instant conversion.
