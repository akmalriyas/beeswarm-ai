It grows 30% faster (7 Hours) and grants 50% more pollen on harvest in the 5 Bee Zone (Strawberry, Spider and Bamboo Fields). Bees with mutations are 25% more likely to sip Nectar from this planter, and it grants 30% more Satisfying and Motivating Nectar.
Harvesting a fully grown Pesticide Planter has a 1/5 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 5-6, and has a:
* ~72.59% chance of being a Common Puffshroom,
* ~21.78% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
* ~5.44% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
* ~0.18% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom,
* ~0.01% chance of being a Mythic Puffshroom.
When claimed, the planter gives up to 28 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, 2 of the tokens are guaranteed to be 1 Neonberry and 2 Tickets.
