The Pine Tree Forest is a field located past the Honey Bee Gate and near the Badge Bearer's Guild. It is protected by two mantises and the Werewolf.
This field can spawn honey, blueberry, ticket, blue extract, magic bean, and neonberry tokens while gathering pollen. Planters in this field grant extra whirligigs and blue extracts.
Bees that like the Pine Tree Forest are Bear Bee, Cool Bee, Bucko Bee, Bubble Bee, Carpenter Bee, Cobalt Bee, Festive Bee, Fuzzy Bee, and Tadpole Bee. Bees that dislike the Pine Tree Forest are Rascal Bee, Rad Bee, Riley Bee, Fire Bee, Shy Bee, Precise Bee, Digital Bee and Baby Bee.
This field grants Comforting Nectar when a planter is planted here.
This is a 23x31 field, containing 713 flowers. The flowers are white and blue, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

Obtaining Boosts[]
The Pine Tree Forest boost icon.
* Using the Blue Field Booster may give a Pine Tree Forest Boost. When it does, it will give +100% Pine Tree Forest pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using glitter will add 100% Pine Tree Forest pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
* Using field dice, smooth dice, or loaded dice may add +100% - +300% Pine Tree Forest pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
* Pine Tree Forest Winds can be obtained through the Wind Shrine or codes. They have a base of 20% instant Pine Tree Forest conversion and give +5% instant Pine Tree Forest conversion and +15% Pine Tree Forest pollen for each stack. Pine Tree Forest Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
Fact: Obtaining Boosts[] statistic: * The Pinecone Beequip can grant up to +15% Pine Tree Forest Capacity and +10% Pine Tree Forest Pollen..
* Giving Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") a present during the Beesmas 2025 event gives you a x3 Pine Tree Forest boost.

Trivia[]
* Festive Bee likes the Pine Tree Forest likely since most Christmas trees are coniferous trees, like pine trees.
* It is also the only red bee to like a field of the opposite color. The only other bee that shares this trait is Vicious Bee liking the Rose Field.
* The Pine Tree Forest is the only field with "Forest" in its name instead of "Field" or "Patch".
* This field is guarded by the werewolf and 2 mantises. This makes the Pine Tree Forest the field with the most mobs guarding it, at 3.
* Standing in the area of the field closest to the Sticker Stack will prevent the werewolf from detecting the player.
* There is a royal jelly token on top of the tallest pine tree in this field. To get it, the player can do the Cloud Obby starting near Polar Bear using the Parachute to glide to the next step. However, it is much easier to use the Red Cannon and the Parachute/Glider.
* As of the 2020-06-06 update, Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") can be found on the gray platform behind the Pine Tree Forest.
* The Pine Tree Forest has shrunk since it was released. Before the Badge Bearer's Guild was added, the field stretched all the way to where the Badge Bearer's Guild currently is. When the Badge Bearer's Guild was added, the field was shrunken to end at the door of the guild. It has been shrunken again, now being farther from the guild's door.
* One of the pine trees still remains near the Honey Bee Gate from the field's original spot.
