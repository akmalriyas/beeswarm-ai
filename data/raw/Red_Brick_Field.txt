The Red Brick Field is one of the four fields located in the Retro Swarm Challenge.
This is a 19x19 field, containing 361 flowers. The flowers are white and red, in sizes of single, double, and triple.
This field can produce brick tokens while collecting pollen.
There are no bees with any preference towards or against this field.
Since planters cannot be used in the Retro Swarm Challenge, there is no confirmed nectar type for this field.

Obtaining Boosts[]
* Using Glitter in the Red Brick Field will grant a 100% Red Brick Field pollen boost for 15 minutes.
