Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Treat Shop

Treat Shop
The Treat Shop in-game.
The Treat Shop is a shop located to the left of Mother Bear. It sells treats at a base cost of 100 honey. The treat price increases by 100 honey for every treat bought after the first, capping at 10,000 honey for one treat.
A player can buy treats in increments of 1, 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000, 1,000,000, 10,000,000, 100,000,000, 1,000,000,000 and 10,000,000,000.
