Spirit Petal
Spirit Petal
_"A delicate flower petal used in rituals."_
_Not to be confused withpetals, spawned by blooms._
The Spirit Petal is a special crafting material that can be used as one of the ingredients to craft the Petal Wand and the Petal Belt. It can also be donated to the Wind Shrine to allow the player a chance of obtaining a Windy Bee (or a Star Treat, if player already had Windy Bee) when donating Cloud Vials. Note that players are only able to donate one Spirit Petal with Cloud Vials to the Wind Shrine to get a chance to obtain a Windy Bee, as the other two are strictly to be used for crafting the Petal Belt and Petal Wand.
There are 3 Spirit Petals that are given every 10th Spirit Bear quest "Spirit Bear"), first one being Sway Away, second being Tickle The Wind, and third being Spring Out Of The Mountain.

Trivia[]
* This is one of the two unique items that can only be obtained from completing a bear’s quests, the other being Translators from Science Bear.
* If a player attempts to donate another Spirit Petal to the Wind Shrine after already donating one, Spirit Bear will shout "Only one, dear!" upon rejection.
* If the player tries to donate 2 or more Spirit Petals , only one will be donated and the rest will be given back to the player.
