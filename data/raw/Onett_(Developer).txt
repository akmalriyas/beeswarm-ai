Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Onett (Developer)

Onett (Developer)
Onett’s Roblox avatar.
_This page is for the developer of the game. For the quest-giving NPC, seeOnett._
Piercen Harbut , better known as Onett on Roblox, is the owner and programmer of Bee Swarm Simulator. Bee Swarm Simulator is his first and main game on Roblox. He owns the Bee Swarm Simulator Club group and Onett's Testing Group.
Onett runs alternate accounts to handle messages about errors experienced while playing Bee Swarm Simulator. BeeSwarmBugReport handles bug reports and BeeSwarmSupport handles the purchase and saves file reports. Onett also has a secret alternate account named ltpb2020, which he mainly used to upload decals for the Ready Player Two event. Another account was found by the name HiddenBeePotential but seems to only be used for testing as of now.
Onett's Twitter account can be found by searching @OnettDev.
Onett also has an Instagram account for the game, a personal Instagram account, a Threads account, a YouTube account, a Twitch account, a Reddit account, and a Discord account (onett1).
Onett lives in the CST timezone, as confirmed on Discord.

Trivia[]
* Onett had a cat named Sam1] in real life, which was the inspiration for [Tabby Bee. It passed away later in 2018.[2]
* Onett's favorite bees are Basic Bee,3] [Rascal Bee,4] [Tabby Bee,4] and [Brave Bee.[5]
* Onett's favorite ability is Triangulate, since he thinks it's satisfying.
* Onett's username is a reference to a city in the Nintendo game EarthBound.[6]
* Onett owns a special tool called the Honey Hammer.
* Onett's favorite mob is the Rhino Beetle.[7]
* Onett's favorite in-game music is bpatrol.[8]
* Occasionally, Onett summons mini events. They have various aliases which replace the username, such as "[Alias] has summoned (a) [mini event]".
* Onett's birthday is on 1989-12-24, and he is currently 36 years old.
* Onett was interviewed by Thundermaker300 in November 2018 as part of the Roblox Wiki's Developer Connections project. See here to read the interview.
