The Mountain Top Field is a field located past the Lion Bee Gate. The Mondo Chick protects this field and respawns roughly every hour.
This field grants Invigorating Nectar when a planter is planted and harvested here.
This field can spawn Red Boost, Blue Boost, Honey, Magic Bean, Ticket, Bitterberry, and Neonberry Tokens while collecting pollen.
Bees that like the Mountain Top Field are Basic Bee, Looker Bee, Frosty Bee, Honey Bee, Precise Bee, Buoyant Bee, Digital Bee, Festive Bee and Gummy Bee. Bees that dislike the Mountain Top Field are Carpenter Bee and Demon Bee.
This is a 24×28 field, containing 672 flowers. The flowers are red and blue, and the natural size is triple.

Possible Events[]
* A level 6-8 Rogue Vicious Bee may spawn here either naturally, when the night starts, or if a player has used a Night Bell.
* A level 4-6 Wild Windy Bee may spawn here, if manually summoned by a player from the Wind Shrine, it is level 5-6 instead.
* There are special conditions where a Wild Windy Bee can spawn here.
* When claiming Spirit Bear’s “Tickle the Wind” quest, 3 level 4 Wild Windy Bee's will spawn here.
* Mondo Chick spawns here at the beginning of the hour.
* During a Stick Bug Challenge. If Stick Bug is in Pineapple Patch, Pumpkin Patch, and Cactus Field, he may jump to this field.
* An Aphid may spawn here upon gathering Leaves.
Obtaining Boosts[]
* Using Glitter will add 100% Mountain Top Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the boost isn't already maxed out.
* Using Field Dice, Smooth Dice, or Loaded Dice may add +100% Mountain Top Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't maxed out yet.
* Mountain Top Field Winds can be obtained through the Wind Shrine or codes. They have a base of +20% instant Mountain Top Field conversion and give +5% instant Mountain Top Field conversion and +10% Mountain Top Field pollen for each stack. Mountain Top Field can stack up to a total of 13 times.

Trivia[]
* This is the only field that doesn't produce treats from farming. However, harvesting sprouts in the Mountain Top Field will still drop normal rewards.
* Leaves in this field are much less frequent, and always either spawn bitterberries, aphids, or a sticker.
* This was the only field that wasn't targeted by the Gummy Cannon during the Gummy Invasion event. (The Stump Field, Coconut Field, Pepper Patch, and the Ant Field were not in the game at that time.)
* The Mountain Top Field used to not have a badge dedicated to it.
* This was changed in the 2024-01-12 Update, when the Mountain Top Field received a badge, thus making the Ant Field the only field without a badge, and, later, the Brick Fields as well.
* This is the first field that consists of only triple flowers, the second being the Stump Field, the third and fourth being the Pepper Patch and the Coconut Field.
* This is also the only field without any white flowers.
* Before the 2018-11-25 update, the Red Cannon required 22 discovered bee types, but now it requires 25 instead, so the player can't bypass the Lion Bee Gate without 25 bees or 25 bee types discovered.
* After the 2019-09-28 update, even by bypassing the Lion Bee Gate with 25 bee types discovered but not 25 bees, the player will be teleported back to spawn.
* The Mountain Top Field requires the most amount of pollen to unlock its Grandmaster badge, at a grand total of 1 quadrillion pollen.
* The Mountain Top Field is the only field that cannot be boosted without the help of items or codes.
