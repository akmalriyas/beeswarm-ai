Red Extract
The Red Clay Planter is a reusable planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in about 6 hours and stores 3 million pollen. It grants bonus Red Extracts and waxes. It can be purchased in the Red HQ.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 10,000,000 Honey.
5 Magic Beans
Fact: 5 Magic Beans requirement: 15 Red Extracts.
Fact: 5 Magic Beans requirement: 20 Soft Waxes.
The planter grows 25% faster in fields with red flowers and gives 50% bonus red pollen from harvests, but also 50% less blue pollen. Red bees are 25% more likely to sip nectar from it, but Blue bees avoid sipping nectar from it. It also grants 20% more Invigorating and Satisfying Nectar.
Harvesting a fully grown Red Clay Planter has a 1/6 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 4-5, and has a:
* ~76.28% chance of being a Common Puffshroom,
* ~19.07% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
* ~4.58% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
* ~0.08% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom.
When claimed, the planter gives up to 16 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, 3 of the tokens are guaranteed to be 1 Red Extract, 2 Soft Waxes and 1 Ticket. Trivia[]
* Its blue counterpart is the Blue Clay Planter.
* This is one of the five reusable planters to reside outside of Dapper Bear's Shop, the others being the Blue Clay Planter, Petal Planter, Hydroponic Planter, and Heat-Treated Planter.
* This and the Heat-Treated Planter are the only planters to be sold from the Red HQ.
* At some point the description in the inventory used to state that it grows in 4 hours, but it would actually grow in 6 hours ever since it was added.
