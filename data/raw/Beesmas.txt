Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Beesmas

This category houses all pages related to Beesmas. Beesmas is an annual event that occurs around late December through late March, and is based on Christmas. Beesmas was first introduced in the 2018-12-19 update and can be seen in the 2019-12-23, 2020-12-25, 2021-12-26, 2022-12-26, 2024-07-17, 2024-12-17, and 2025-12-25 updates.
Contents tagged with this category aren't always removed once the event ends, as in the case of Festive Beans, Gingerbread Bears, along with many other items.
