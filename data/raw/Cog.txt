Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Cog

_This article is about the items used in the Robo Bear Challenge. Not to be confused with7-Pronged Cog._
_"Used for purchases during Robo Bear's Challenge. Removed when the challenge ends."_
Cogs are inventory items added in the 2022-12-26 update. They are used to purchase upgrades, purchase drives, and reroll bees and upgrades in the Robo Bear Challenge. They are removed from the inventory when the Robo Bear Challenge ends. Additionally, you can reach 250,000 Cogs collected in total to unlock the Robo Cub.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Completing quests in the Robo Bear Challenge.
* Starting a challenge gives 10 Cogs.
* The harder the quest, the more Cogs it gives.
* You may get bonus cogs for completing a quest within the first two minutes.
* You may also get bonus cogs from certain upgrades you can purchase in the challenge.
* Defeating a Golden Cogmower guarantees at least 14 Cogs and scales up to 18.
Outdated Methods[]
* As a reward for completing BBM's Naughty List in Beesmas 2024.
Trivia[]
* This is the first item to be added that is not only exclusively rewarded in a challenge, but also used as a temporary form of currency.
* This is no longer the case, as you can now get a cog outside of the Robo Bear Challenge from Bubble Bee Man.
* The item slightly resembles the cog on top of the Noob Shop.
