Puppy Bee is a Colorless Event bee. It hatches out of a Puppy Bee Egg, which is available in the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.
Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, and the only way to make it gifted is by feeding it a Star Treat, Gingerbread Bears or Aged Gingerbread Bears.
Puppy Bee likes the Clover Field and the Pumpkin Patch. It dislikes the Rose Field.
Fact: Skin #ffe089 #453529 #ffe089 statistic: * Collects 25 Pollen in 4 seconds..
* Makes 280 Honey in 4 seconds.
* +100% Energy, +15% Movespeed, +15 Gather Amount, +200 Convert Amount, +1 Attack.
* 🌟 Gifted Hive Bonus: +20% Bond from Treats.
Abilities[]
* [Fetch] Creates a ball that collects tokens and increases pollen by 100% (+15% per Level) as you and Puppy Bee kick it to each other. Collection increases per kick. Combo hits to win Treats.
* [Puppy Love] Increases your bond with up to 10 nearby bees by 60 (+20 per lvl). Grants +10% Bee Movespeed and +50% Bee Gather Amount for 10 seconds (+1s per lvl).

Trivia[]
* When Puppy Bee came out on the 2018-09-10 Update, it was on sale in the Ticket Tent for 250 tickets until September 22, 2018.
* It takes 3 hits of the ball by the owner and 3 hits by the Puppy Bee (3 volleys) to drop a treat token. The more times the ball is hit in a row, the more treats are earned from the treat token (Doubled every time a combo is done, starting at 1, capping at 256).
* Once the treat token reaches the capped amount of treats, the ball will permanently stay light green and will not be able to produce any more treat tokens.
* It takes a total of 54 hits to reach the max amount of treats, and 60 to get 10x Reindeer Guidance.
* When a treat token of this kind is collected, even if more than 1 treat is earned from the treat token, it will always count as 1 treat token collected in quests.
* Puppy Bee was the first bee to be able to create treat tokens, the second being Festive Bee with its festive gift ability.
* Puppy Bee was likely made to be Tabby Bee's canine counterpart.
* Sometimes, the ball spawned by the fetch token can get stuck, allowing the player and Puppy Bee to easily pass it back and forth.
* Puppy Bee was the first bee that has a 3D model on its face (its tongue).
* Puppy Bee, Bear Bee, and Tabby Bee are currently the only bees that were sold at a discount for a limited amount of time.
* This bee, Bear Bee, Gummy Bee, Tabby Bee, Festive Bee, Digital Bee, and Windy Bee are the only Event bees that can be a First Edition Bee.
* First Edition Puppy Bee could have been obtained by buying it before September 22, 2018.
* Treat tokens produced by Puppy Bee's fetch can be collected with a Token Link, similar to Honey Gifts.
* Puppy Bee is the third bee to produce a token that has its face on it, the second being Tabby Bee, and the first being Rage Bee.
* This is the only bee that is able to raise the bond of other bees, through its puppy love token.
* This bee, Vicious Bee, Crimson Bee, Cobalt Bee, and Windy Bee are the only Event bees that don't have an 'enhanced' ability if gifted.
* The puppy love token glows when collected.
* Puppy Bee's wings clip through its ears.
* Bee Bear's Puppy Bee is male.
* This and Festive Bee are the only bees that can produce gingerbread bears.
* This bee, Festive Bee, and Demon Bee are the only bees that have an exclusive beequip for themselves.
* During the Beesmas 2020 Event, players could purchase a special bundle for 800 Robux in the Robux Shop that would grant them a Puppy Bee Egg, the special Reindeer Antlers Beequip specifically made for the Puppy Bee, and 50 Gingerbread Bears as a bonus.
* If the player already had a Puppy Bee when they bought the bundle, they would get 500 tickets instead.
* This makes Puppy Bee the only bee available to purchase in the Robux Shop twice.
* This bee, along with Tadpole Bee, Tabby Bee, Lion Bee, and Bear Bee are the only bees based on real animals.
