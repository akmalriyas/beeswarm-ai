The Vacuum is a tool that can be purchased in the Noob Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 14,000 Honey.
It collects 2 pollen from 13 surrounding flower patches every 0.8 seconds and has a base pollen collection rate of 32.5 pollen per second.
Appearance[]
The vacuum cleaner is easily the most complex of the tools in the noob shop. It looks like the standard vacuum, a brownish bag with a grey handle connecting to the suction colored grey, black, and 2 yellow squares at the front, resembling lights.
Gallery[]
The Vacuum's associated sticker.
Trivia[]
* This is the third most expensive item that can be purchased in the Noob Shop, behind the Canister and the Helmet.
* This tool has a similar collection range as the Honey Dipper and the Porcelain Dipper, the only difference being that both of them have a bigger collection range, and the Porcelain Dipper summons a pillar of light every 10th swing that collects pollen.
* This tool is based off the household appliance known as the _vacuum cleaner._
