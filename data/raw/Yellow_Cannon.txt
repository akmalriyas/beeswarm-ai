Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Yellow Cannon

Yellow Cannon
The Yellow Cannon.
The Yellow Cannon is available for use once the player has discovered 12 bee types. It is located on a hill accessible from the Brave Bee Gate next to Science Bear and the Red Teleporter exit. It has a 5 second cooldown period. It targets the spawn platform.
If the player has the Parachute or Glider, they can use it in combination with the Yellow Cannon to perform a few shortcuts around the map as well as collect Royal Jelly tokens formerly inaccessible, such as the one in the Dandelion Field, on top of the hives, on top of the Noob Shop near the entrance of the Star Hall and the one near Brown Bear.
Trivia[]
* This is one of the four cannons in the game, which the other three cannons are Blue Cannon, Red Cannon, and Gummy Cannon.
* This is one of the three transportational cannons in the game, which the other two cannons are Blue Cannon and Red Cannon.
* The Yellow Cannon has a cooldown of 5 seconds. While this is not normally possible to see during regular gameplay as the cannon launches the player too far to return in 5 seconds, the cooldown can be seen by using the Glider/Parachute glitch to stop all momentum as soon as the player exits the cannon.
* This cooldown is shared by all the transportational cannons.
