Abilities[]
* [Blue Boost] Grants x1.2 pollen from Blue Flowers for 15 seconds. Stacks up to 10 times.
* [Summon Frog] Summons a frog that lasts for 20s (+2s per Level). Frogs can make bubbles after every few hops, or after collecting a token with their tongue. They also attack nearby enemies with attack equal to 5x this bee's attack.
Fact: Abilities[] requirement: * If Gifted, this has a 10% (+2% per Level) chance to summon a Gifted Frog instead, which has increased tongue range, Token gathering and attack speed, 50% more attack, has a chance to summon golden bubbles, and can hit balloons with its tongue to convert pollen to Honey Tokens..
* [🌟Gifted Ability: Baby Love] Grants x2 pollen and +50% Loot Luck for 30 seconds. Loot Luck increases your chance of obtaining prizes from defeating enemies.
* [Passive: Gathering Bubbles+] 65% chance (85% if Gifted) to spawn a Bubble when gathering. If any player touches it, it pops, collecting 4 Red/6 White/8 Blue pollen (+15% per Gifted blue bee type, +5% per bee level) from 29 nearby flowers and causing them to replenish.

Trivia[]
* The Tadpole Bee, Lion Bee, Tabby Bee, Bear Bee, and Puppy Bee are the only bees to represent realistic animals.
* The Tadpole Bee is the only Mythic bee that represents a realistic animal.
* Tadpole Bee and Baby Bee are the only bees with the Baby Love ability.
* Tadpole Bee and Baby Bee are also the only bees that appear smaller than the normal bee size.
* Tadpole Bee and Windy Bee are the only bees with 2 gifted bonuses, excluding other Event Bees who enhance their ability.
* Tadpole Bee, Vector Bee, Fuzzy Bee, Spicy Bee, Buoyant Bee and Digital Bee are the only bees with gifted exclusive abilities.
* This bee and Spicy Bee are the only bees who can summon other entities.
* This is the only bee that can summon a Mob.
* Tadpole Bee and Bubble Bee are the only bees with the Gathering Bubbles passive ability.
* Tadpole Bee, Fuzzy Bee, Spicy Bee, Buoyant Bee, and Digital Bee all have four different Abilities, the most for any bee.
* Tadpole Bee used to be the only bee in the game that had a decimal base attack (0.5).
* Tadpole Bee is the only Mythic bee that has its Gifted-exclusive ability token shared with another bee.
* Along with Vector Bee and Spicy Bee, Tadpole Bee was one of the first Mythic Bees to be introduced to the game.
* A Tadpole Bee Egg could be obtained by giving Bubble Bee Man a Present during the Beesmas 2020 event.
* Tadpole Bee is the second mythic bee to have an egg of its own, the first being Fuzzy Bee.
* Tadpole Bee is one of the 8 other bees with a unique trail.
* Tadpole Bee is the blue counterpart of Spicy Bee.
* Tadpole Bee and Fuzzy Bee are the only Mythics, when gifted, to not have any glowing parts.
