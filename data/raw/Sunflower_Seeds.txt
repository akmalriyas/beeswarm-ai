Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Sunflower Seeds

Sunflower Seeds
Sunflower Seed
_"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."_
A Sunflower Seed token.
A Sunflower Seed is a type of treat that can be fed to a Bee to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to Basic, Bomber, Looker, Demo, Commander, Carpenter, Ninja, Vector, or Precise Bees, the amount of bond they gain is doubled, raising their bond by 50. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed Sunflower Seeds, though this only happens with a 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance for Rare bees, 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance for Epic bees, 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance for Common and Legendary bees, and 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance for Mythic bees per Sunflower Seed. Feeding a radioactive bee a sunflower seed has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) to mutate a bee. Sunflower Seeds can also be used to craft Oils, Paper Planters, Belt Pocket, Mondo Belt Bag, Bomber Guard, Looker Guard and Basic Boots.
