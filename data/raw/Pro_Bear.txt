Pro Bear is a bear who runs the Pro Shop. He is the second shop bear encountered by most players. His presence is not required to purchase items in the shop.
Pro Bear has a similar appearance to Noob Bear and Top Bear: yellow skin, red shirt, green pants, and a smiley face. The only difference between them is the colors of their clothing.
Trivia[]
* Pro Bear is one of the four bears in the 10 Bee Area, the others being Science Bear, Dapper Bear, and Tunnel Bear.
* This bear, Noob Bear, Top Bear, and Shadow Bear are the only bears without a function whatsoever.
* Behind Pro Bear, there is a maze that contains a royal jelly token, sunflower seed token, and has the ability to host hidden stickers.
