Trivia[]
* The Reindeer Antlers and the Festive Wreath share many similarities:
* The two are the only Beequips to have models visible on the bee that they are equipped to.
* Before the 2024-01-12 update's removal of permanent Beequips, the two were the only permanent Beequips in the game.
* This is the first permanent Beequip added to the game, followed by the Festive Wreath.
* These were the only Beequips to have a set potential, with this Beequip always having max potential and the Festive Wreath always having a potential of 3 stars (6 points).
* They were the only Beequips that cannot be discarded.
* Caustic Waxes were unable to be used on these two Beequips. However, this is no longer the case.
* A model of a Puppy Bee with Reindeer Antlers can be found outside the map, next to a model of a Festive Bee with the Festive Wreath.
* This Beequip is likely a reference to Dr. Seuss’ children's book “How the Grinch Stole Christmas”, where the Grinch dresses his dog as a reindeer.
* This is the one of three Beequips to give a unique ability.
* The other two are the Snowglobe and Festive Wreath.
