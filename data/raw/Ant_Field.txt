The Ant Field is a field located past the Ant Gate and near the Top Ant Exterminators leaderboard. It can only be accessed by starting the Ant Challenge which will summon waves of ants.
This field can produce honey and ticket tokens while collecting pollen.
The only bee that likes the Ant Field is Lion Bee. There are no bees that dislike the Ant Field.
Planters cannot be planted in this field, although Onett "Onett \(Developer\)") has stated that the Ant Field contains Invigorating Nectar.
This is a 12×32 field, containing 384 flowers. The flowers are white, red, and blue, and the only natural size in single.

Obtaining Boosts[]
* Using an Ant Pass to start the Ant Challenge will give 100% Instant Ant Field Conversion.
* There is no possible to way to boost the pollen for the Ant Field.
* The flowers can still be pollinated.

Trivia[]
* You can collect pollen from the Ant Field outside the challenge by using the Sticker-Seeker or spawning flames with the Scythe.
* The Ant Field is the only field that does not have its own badge.
* Lion Bee is the only bee to like the Ant Field; all other bees are neutral to this field.
* The Ant Field is the only field to have exclusively single flowers.
* The flowers on this field contain 50 pollen per flower, and instead of going up by 15 pollen per tier, go up 10 per pollination tier up to 90, although pollen multiplier remains the same as regular flowers.
* The Ant Field is the only field that can't be boosted with glitter, the only field where a magic bean cannot be planted, the only field where clouds cannot be summoned, the only field that had no Market Boost from the Boost Market, the only field that can't get winds from the Wind Shrine, and the only field where planters cannot be planted.
* The Ant Field is the smallest field in the game with 384 flowers, smaller than the Pumpkin Patch with 561 flowers and the Stump Field with 563 flowers.
* The Ant Field is one of the 5 fields that are exclusively used for a challenge. The other others include the Mixed Brick Field, White Brick Field, Blue Brick Field and Red Brick Field.
* Players are able to glitch into the Ant Field without triggering the actual challenge. This can only be achieved by having a slow internet connection or bear morph and/or haste+.
* Despite being impossible to boost, the Ant Field has an unused Field Boost icon.
