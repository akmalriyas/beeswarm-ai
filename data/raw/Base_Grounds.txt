Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Base Grounds

Base Grounds
The Starter Zone is the collection of places that do not need any bee prerequisites to access. Any player can access the Starter Zone, no matter how far they are into the game. This is also where the players' hives are.
Fact: The **Title** in Base Grounds has/costs **Audio**, **Information**.
Fact: The Wax in Base Grounds has/costs <https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Wax.ogg>, Plays during Daytime.
Fact: The Drone in Base Grounds has/costs <https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Drone.ogg>, Plays during Daytime (Beesmas Only).
Fact: The Mountaincall in Base Grounds has/costs <https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Mountaincall.ogg>, Plays during Nighttime.
