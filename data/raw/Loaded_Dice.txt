Loaded dice
Loaded Dice
_"Boosts 3 random fields with 300% pollen for 15 minutes. A bit biased towards the field you're in..."_
Loaded Dice is an inventory item added in the 2021-12-26 update. It boosts three random fields with 300% pollen for 15 minutes, and the field the player is in has a biased chance, meaning it has a higher chance to boost the field the player is standing in. If the player uses it when there is another dice boost active, it will replace the last dice boosts and duration. It has a cooldown of 3 seconds.
Bias Mechanism[]
To activate the bias, the player must be standing on a field, and when the first loaded dice is used, there will be a 25% chance for the first field picked to be the field you’re standing in, as more loaded dice is used, the chance will increase by 25% with a maximum chance of 100%. If the player leaves the field, the chance will reset.

Trivia[]
* If you some how roll another dice while a dice is already being rolled, this notification would pop up:
You are already rolling a dice
* This is the only dice to currently have a cap (25).
* Field Dice used to have a cap of 25 until the 2021-12-26 update.
* This and the Smooth Dice are the only items which can produce more than 1 field boost at a time, and produce more than x1 stack of field boost at a time.
* The fields shown on the dice are the Sunflower Field, the Clover Field, the Coconut Field, the Pumpkin Patch, the Pine Tree Forest and the Blue Flower Field.
* There also is an X icon on the front dice. It is presumed it's the "biased" slot for the field that the player is standing on.
* In real life, loaded dice are rigged dice designed to make one side show up more often than the rest, which correlates to the dice's in-game bias of the field your standing in.
