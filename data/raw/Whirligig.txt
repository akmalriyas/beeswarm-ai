Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Whirligig

Whirligig
_"Teleports you and your bees back to your hive and briefly increases Convert Rate."_
Whirligigs are inventory items added in the Beesmas 2021 update. Using one simply teleports the player and their bees back to their hive, as well as temporarily increasing Convert Rate.
Whirligigs have a limit of 100. Obtaining any while having more than 100 whirligigs will not add them to the player's inventory, unless they are obtained through quests or codes.
Attempting to use a Whirligig without claiming a hive will prompt the message:  You must claim a hive to use a Whirligig
Ways to Obtain[]
* A common drop from the Robo Bear Challenge.
* A reward from Retro Swarm Challenge.
* As a rare drop from mobs such as Rhino Beetles and Mantises.
* As a reward from finishing your own Robo Party.
* As a drop from Robo Party mobs.
* Buying the Frozen Forest Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog.
* Buying the Winter Winds Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog.
* As a drop from certain planters.
* Planters harvested in Pine Tree Forest have a higher chance to drop whirligigs than other fields. Petal Planter gives the most whirligigs, although Blue Clay Planter has the highest chance of dropping them.
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* The Launching Rocket rewards 10 whirligigs.
* The Simple Mountain rewards 10 whirligigs.
Outdated Ways[]
* As a reward from quests.
* Bee Bear's Bringing On Beesmas (#1) - x5 Whirligigs.
* Bee Bear's Very Merry Berries (#7) - x2 Whirligigs.
* Bee Bear's By Golly, Get Jolly (#9) - x10 Whirligigs.

* Completing Bee Bear's 8th, 10th, and 15th quest. (10, 10 and 25 for those accordingly)
* As a reward from the Whirling Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
* Buying the Starter Sampler Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog.
* Opening certain Gift Boxes across the map:
* Beige Gift Box gave 10 whirligigs and other items.
* Hitting the target on the right wall in the Blue Flower Field next to the King Beetle's Lair rewarded 3 whirligigs.

Trivia[]
* Using a whirligig during an Ant Challenge will teleport the player to the center of the Ant Field.
* Using a whirligig used to teleport the player away from the challenge, thus ending it.
* This is also one of the ways to exit the White Tunnel other than defeating or being killed by Tunnel Bear, leaving and rejoining the game, and using the gray plate at the middle of the tunnel.
* They are based on maple tree seeds (nicknamed whirligigs), which, when falling, spin, thus traveling further from the tree. This may have inspired the whirligig's mechanic of teleporting or rather "carrying" you back to your hive.
* Before the 2022-12-26 update, Whirligigs had a cooldown of 5 minutes.
* If the player has the Frozen debuff (from Snowbear), the whirligig will only teleport the player's bees.
* This may also occur if the player is lagging.
