Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Black Balloon

Black Balloon
Black Balloon
_"Floats over the field for 5m. Stores up to x25 your capacity and increases pollen inside 4x."_
The Black Balloon is a consumable item that was added in the 2021-12-26 update. Once used, it creates a black balloon over the field the player is in. The balloon floats over the field for 5 minutes before floating to the hive balloon, storing the pollen collected underneath it. The pollen collected below is multiplied by 4, and it holds up to 25 times the player's capacity. Only 1 Black Balloon can be summoned in a field per player.
Black balloons have a cap of 25 in the player's inventory. Obtaining one while already having 25 will not add a Black Balloon to the player's inventory, unless obtained from robux packs, quests, or codes.
Ways to Obtain[]
* As a very rare drop from Tunnel Bear.
* As an exceptionally rare drop from a Spider.
* Completing Dapper Bear's 12th quest rewards 1 black balloon and other items.
* Sticking the Black Star Sticker to the Sticker Stacker rewards one Black Balloon.
* As a drop from Robo Party mobs.
* Purchasing the Saint Puff's Pack in Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2025 gives you 1 Black Balloon and other items.
Outdated Ways[]
* Opening certain Gift Boxes:
* The Midnight Gift Box gave 1 black balloon + other items.
* Purchasing certain offers in the Robux Shop:
* Purchasing the Buoyant Pack for 800 robux gave 3 black balloons and other items.
* Purchasing the Cyber Monday Pack for 800 robux gave 1 black balloon and other items.
* Completing Onett's Beesmas 2021 Quest "Onett's Yard Art on The Lid" rewarded 1 Black Balloon and other items.
* Completing Bee Bear's 5th Snow Cub Reformation rewarded 3 Black Balloons and other items.
* As a reward from completing Panda Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest (Rewards 1 Black Balloon \+ other items).
* Purchasing certain offers in Bee Bear's Catalog.
* Purchasing the Balloon Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2022, granting 1 Black Balloon \+ other items.
* Purchasing the Dicey Bundle in Beesmas 2024 gave 1 black balloon and other items.
* Completing Sun Bear's Waiting With Sun Bear (6/6): Still Stranded rewards 1 Black Balloon and other items.
* As a reward from completing Bee Bear's 20th Beesmas 2022 quest (Rewards 5 Black Balloons + other items).
