Gumdrop Dispenser
_The design of the Gumdrop Shop._
The Gumdrop Shop is a shop introduced in the 2018-05-26 update, located between the Spider Field and the Bamboo Field. It can be used to purchase gumdrops with tickets, in increments of 3, 30, and 300.
It is decorated with a teal gumdrop on the top, a picture of the gumdrops item on the sides with a pink background, a teal base, and gray rims.
The description of the shop is: "Use while standing in a field to cover flowers in goo. Goo grants bonus honey."
