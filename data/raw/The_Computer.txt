Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - The Computer

The Computer
The Computer.
The Computer was a green screen located between the Windy Bee Gate and the Red HQ. It was the core part of the Ready Player Two event. It has an ellipsis (...) written on it and can only be interacted with using the Strange Goggles. However, with the removal of the Strange Goggles from the Noob Shop, the player must already have the item equipped to interact with it once more.
Interior[]
When interacted with when wearing the Strange Goggles, the player can see Digital Bee "Digital Bee \(NPC\)") appear and give the player certain puzzles to complete in order to get the How to Program BASIC cosmetic hat. Only after the player finished all puzzles given by Digital Bee, the player would then be able to enter the computer to receive a Star Jelly and Robo Pass (Pass only available during Beesmas 2022) as a reward with the How to Program BASIC cosmetic floating in Digital Bee's original spot. When inside, the music, overflowin4, that plays in the 30 Bee Zone and 35 Bee Zone is also played here.
