The Lion Bee Gate , also known as the Mountain Top Gate or the 25 Bee Gate , is a gate that requires 25 bees to pass through. It is located at the end of the slope beside the Pumpkin Patch.
It gets its name from the large statue of a Lion Bee located on top of it.
This area contains the Mountain Top Field, Mondo Chick, Sticker Printer, Field Booster, Instant Converter, Ticket Shop and the Mountain Top Shop.
Trivia[]
* As of the 2019-09-28 update, the player will be teleported back to spawn if they manage to bypass the gate, whether if it was by jumping around it or using the Red Cannon without 25 bees.
* This and the Ant Gate share a few similarities.
* They are the only gates that don't have a quest giver behind them.
* If one were to count the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine inside the Hive Hub Portal past the Ant Gate, or Gummy Bear, then this would be the only gate that doesn't have a quest giver behind it.
* They are the only gates that only contain 1 field.
* If one were to count the Hub Field inside the Hive Hub Portal past the Ant Gate, then this would be the only gate that contains 1 field.
* This used to be the final gate accessed in the game, until the Bear Gate was added in the 2018-7-11 update.
