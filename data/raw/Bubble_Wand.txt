The Bubble Wand is a tool that can be bought in the Blue HQ.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 3,500,000 Honey.
The Bubble Wand collects 6 pollen from 16 flower patches in 0.8 seconds and collects x2 pollen from blue flowers. Every 10th swing it spawns a bubble somewhere on the field. It has an average base pollen collection rate of 160 pollen per second: 240 from blue flowers and 120 from red and white flowers. It emits bubble particles.

Trivia[]
* A replica of the Bubble Wand rests on the roof of the Blue HQ.
* Its red counterpart is the Scythe.
* The Bubble Wand collects about one more pollen per second than the Scythe, though this is hardly noticeable. It is also offset by its collection pattern, sometimes making it harder to collect goo, sparkles, or leaves.
* This is the second cheapest item in the Blue HQ, behind the Blue Guard.
