Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Dispenser

Dispenser
A Dispenser is a machine whose main purpose is to dispense various items or boosts to the player typically under a cooldown.
To use a dispenser, simply stand on the pad in front of the dispenser to use it. Some of the dispensers have certain requirements in order to be used such as having enough tickets or being a part of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club. While there are some dispensers that don't directly have any requirements to be used, there are some that have indirect requirements such as the Glue Dispenser requiring the player have a certain badge to access, as the Gummy Bear's Lair requires the player to have Goo Hotshot or above to enter, or the Coconut Dispenser needing the player to defeat the Coconut Crab to gain access to it, similar to the rest of the content in the Coconut Cave or the Robo Pass Dispenser which requires the player to have 30 bees.
Dispenser Types[]
There are currently 12 types of dispensers listed below:
* Club Dispensers
* Free Dispensers
* Ticket Dispenser
Club Dispensers are dispensers that require the player to be part of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club in order to use them. They are the:
*  Blueberry Dispenser
*  Honey Dispenser
*  Strawberry Dispenser
*  Treat Dispenser
Free Dispensers are dispensers that the player can use without having to join the Bee Swarm Simulator Club or spend tickets. They are the:
*  Coconut Dispenser
*  Free Ant Pass Dispenser
*  Free Robo Pass Dispenser
*  Free Royal Jelly Dispenser
*  Glue Dispenser
Ticket Dispensers are dispensers that require the player to spend tickets to use them. They are the:
*  Ant Pass Dispenser
*  Robo Pass Dispenser
*  Royal Jelly Dispenser

Trivia[]
* Of all the machines in the game, dispensers have the most variants, with a total of 12 different types.
* The Honey Dispenser, the Strawberry Dispenser, the Blueberry Dispenser, the Royal Jelly Dispenser, and the Glue Dispenser are the only dispensers that are required to be used for a quest ("Honey Bee's Honey Wreath?" from Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") requires the player to use the Honey Dispenser, "BBM's Naughty List" from Bubble Bee Man requires the player to use the Royal Jelly Ticket Dispenser, and "The Power of Information" from Science Bear requires the player to use the Strawberry Dispenser, the Blueberry Dispenser, and the Glue Dispenser).
* There are three dispensers that give treats in the game being the Treat Dispenser, the Blueberry Dispenser, and the Strawberry Dispenser.
* The Glue Dispenser, the Free Ant Pass Dispenser, Ant Pass Dispenser, Free Robo Pass Dispenser, Robo Pass Dispenser, and the Coconut Dispenser are the only dispensers to not grant haste.
* The Glue Dispenser, the Free Robo Pass Dispenser, and the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser are the only dispensers with a 22-hour cooldown, the longest in the game.
* The Free Ant Pass Dispenser, the Ant Pass Dispenser, the Robo Pass Dispenser, and the Free Robo Pass Dispenser are the only dispensers that don't have any visible faucets, and instead have a small compartment, like a vending machine that gives out tickets.
* The Glue Dispenser and the Coconut Dispenser are the only dispensers that give more items when the player upgrades the badge that corresponds to it.
* The Coconut Dispenser is the only dispenser to give a field boost.
