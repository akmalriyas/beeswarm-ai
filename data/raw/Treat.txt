Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Treat

_"Increases your bond with a bee by 10."_
A Treat is a type of treat that can be fed to a bee to raise its bond by 10. They are the easiest treat to obtain but are not the favorite treat of any bee. They can also be used to craft the Honey Mask where 9,999 treats are needed.
A treat token.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Using the Treat Dispenser near the Pro Shop/Pineapple Patch (Like the Honey Dispenser, this requires being a member of the BSS Club).
* Buying from the Treat Shop next to under Mother Bear's awning for 100 Honey per treat and capping out at 10,000 Honey per treat.
* As a reward from memory matches.
* As a stack reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* The Fork And Knife rewards 500 treats.
Drops[]
* Farming in the Dandelion, Mushroom, Clover, Spider, Cactus, Pumpkin, Coconut, or Stump Fields.
* Drops from leaves in these fields may also yield treats.
* A relatively high chance of dropping from rhino beetles, ladybugs, spiders, mantises, scorpions, werewolves, aphids, Stick Bug, Stick Nymphs, Tunnel Bear, King Beetle, and stump snail.
* A guaranteed drop from Mondo Chicks.
* 2nd and 3rd capture of Commando Chick rewards 2 and 5 treats respectively.
* "Playing" Fetch with Puppy Bee.
* As a drop from a Festive Gift or Festive Mark.
* As a gift from a cub buddy.
* As a reward for completely growing any kind of sprout except for Gummy and Debug sprouts.
* Harvesting sparkles.
* As a drop from Puffshrooms.
* As a drop from Planters, which include:
* Plastic Planters
* Candy Planters
* Red Clay Planters
* Tacky Planters
* Activating certain targets grants a small amount of treats.
* Using Beesmas Feast during Beesmas Will give treats and other items.

* The Mythic Egg questline gives treats on all quests, at a sum of 195,500 treats.
* Black Bear's repeatable quests also reward treats.
* Science Bear gives treats on the following quests:
* Mark Mechanics gives 3,000 treats.
* Epistemological Endeavor gives 10,000 treats.
* All of Mother Bear's quests give treats, 4,020 treats in total.
* All of Polar Bear's quests give 10 treats.
* All of Onett's quests give treats, 25,000 treats in total.
* Every 5th completion of Honey Bee's quest may give treats.
