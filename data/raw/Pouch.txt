Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Pouch

Description
"A small bag."
Fact: The Free in Pouch has/costs 200 Pollen.
> "_A small bag._ "
The Pouch is the starter bag that new players automatically receive at the beginning of the game, along with the Scooper, the starter tool. You can equip it in the Noob Shop.
It doesn't have a price as it is the first bag given to the player for free.
Fact: > "_A small bag._ " statistic: * +200 Capacity..
Trivia[]
* In early versions of the game, the pouch stored 150 pollen.
* It was later buffed to 250 then later nerfed to 200.
* This and the Jar are the only bags that cost less Honey than their base capacity.
* This, the Scooper, and a Basic Egg are the only items that the player starts off with when joining the game for the very first time.
