Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Blueberries

Blueberries
_"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."_
A Blueberry is a type of treat that can be fed to a bee to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to a Bumble, Cool, Bubble, Frosty, Bucko, Diamond, Music, Tadpole, or Buoyant Bee, the amount of bond they gain is doubled. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed blueberries, though this only happens with a 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance for Rare bees, 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance for Epic bees, 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance for Common and Legendary bees, and 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance for Mythic bees per blueberry. Feeding a radioactive bee a blueberry has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) to mutate a bee.

Drops[]
* Farming in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Cactus Field, and the Pine Tree Forest.
* Harvesting leaves in these fields may also yield blueberries.
* As a drop from Rhino Beetles, Mantises, and King Beetle.
* As a drop from a Festive Gift or Festive Mark.
* As a gift from your Cub Buddy.
* As a reward for completely growing any sprout except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
* Planting a sprout in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, or Stump Field yields 6x more blueberries than other fields.
* Harvesting sparkles (Higher chances in blue fields).
* As a drop from Puffshrooms.
* As a drop from planters in blue fields.
* Non-blue fields that contain a significant portion of blue flowers can drop Blueberries (Clover Field, Cactus Field, Mountain Top Field).
* When in blue fields, Paper Planter drops multiple Blueberry tokens in increments of 1, 3, 5, and 10.
