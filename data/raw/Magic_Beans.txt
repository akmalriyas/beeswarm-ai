Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Magic Beans

Magic Beans
_"Plants a randomSprout in the field you're in"_
> _Were you looking forSprout or Festive Bean?_
A Magic Bean is an inventory item that was added in the 2018-11-25 update. It can be used to summon a random sprout in the field the player is standing in. When one is planted, a server-wide message will appear saying:  🌱{Username} has planted a (Sprout Rarity) Sprout...🌱
Ways to Obtain[]
* Purchasing for 20 Tickets each in the Magic Bean Shop on the right side of Stump Field.
* Purchasing for 30 Robux for 1 Magic bean or 200 Robux for 10 Magic beans in the Robux Shop.
* As a reward from the Stick Bug Challenge.
* As a reward from donating to the Wind Shrine.
* As a reward from Memory Match games.
* As a stack reward for adding certain Stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* Forward Facing Aphid rewards 1 magic bean.
* Round Green Critter rewards 1 magic bean.
* Flying Magenta Critter rewards 1 magic bean.
* Blue Triangle Critter rewards 1 magic bean.
* Purple Pointed Critter rewards 1 magic bean.
* Orange Leg Critter rewards 1 magic bean.
* Sprout rewards 1 magic bean.
* Critter in a Stocking rewards 1 magic bean.
* Festive Pea rewards 1 magic bean.
* Redeeming certain valid codes.
* ClubBean (Gives 1 magic bean and other stuff, requires being a member of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club)
Drops[]
* Unknown chance of dropping when collecting pollen in fields.
* As an unknown chance from Leaves.
* As a drop from all aphids and the Coconut Crab.
* As a rare drop from a rhino beetle, ladybug, spider, Stick Nymphs, Stick Bug, King Beetle, Wild Windy Bee, Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, and the Mondo Chick.
* As a rare drop from Festive Gift and Festive Mark.
* As a rare gift from a cub buddy.
* A chance of dropping from any sprout except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
* As a drop from certain planters, rarity depending on which one and where it was planted.
* As a drop from Stockings.
