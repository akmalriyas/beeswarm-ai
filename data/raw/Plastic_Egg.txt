Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Plastic Egg

Plastic Egg
Plastic Egg
_"Turn in 3 to Brown Bear for a prize!"_
The Plastic Egg was a blue-colored egg that was scattered around the map for Brown Bear’s 2019 Egg Hunt quest. The player had to find all three eggs to finish Brown Bear's Egg Hunt Quest, which awarded a Marshmallow Bee. They had no special abilities and were removed from the player's inventory when turning in the quest. The egg locations were replaced by Ticket tokens after the Egg Hunt 2019 ended and the 2019-05-13 update.
Locations[]
* In the Blue Maze, next to the Ticket Tent.
* Turn left first, and follow the right wall after that to find the token.
* In the ramp leading up to the Basic Bee Gate, opposite the Blue Flower Field and behind the Honey Bee's "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") original location.
* Inside the Lava Parkour. There will be a little window outside of the lava parkour that shows the token.
* When entering, immediately turn right, and climb up the rocks.

Trivia[]
* This is the first and only egg that can be obtained as an inventory item and yet can't hatch into a bee.
* In the Egg Hunt 2019 lobby, the portal to Bee Swarm Simulator said, "Find 5 Plastic Eggs", though the player only needed 3 to obtain a Marshmallow Bee.
* Compounding the error, this was also not the only quest needed to complete the Egg Hunt.
* This and the Broken Drive are the only items that only serve a purpose to complete a quest.
