Fact: Page last updated on 01/24/2026 statistic: The **All-Time Top Blue Pollen Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards in the game. This leaderboard shows how much blue pollen players have collected over time. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on the leaderboards for lengths of time (hackers/exploiters will be removed)..
Fact: Page last updated on 01/24/2026 statistic: If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message "You've collected "x" Blue Pollen" will appear, "x" being the amount of blue pollen the player has collected..
