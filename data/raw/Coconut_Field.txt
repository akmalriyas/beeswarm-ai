The Coconut Field is a field added in the September 28, 2019 update, located past the Windy Bee Gate and next to Spirit Bear. It is home to and protected by the Coconut Crab.
The field can spawn honey, treats, coconuts, tropical drinks, ticket and magic bean tokens when collecting pollen.
Bees that like the Coconut Field are Vector Bee, Buoyant Bee, Digital Bee, and Windy Bee. There are no bees that dislike the Coconut Field.
This field grants Refreshing Nectar when a planter is planted here.
A level 1-20 Wild Windy Bee has a chance to spawn in this field.
This is a 30x21 field, containing 630 flowers. The flowers are white, red, and blue, and the only natural size is triple.

Trivia[]
* This is the only field where sprouts and leaves can yield coconuts and tropical drinks.
* It is also the only field to have a regular boss defending it.
* This is one of four fields to solely have contain triple flowers without pollination, the others being the Mountain Top Field, Stump Field, and the Pepper Patch.
* Furthermore, this field is the only triple-flower field dominated by white flowers.
* Under the coconut tree opposite of the cave and closest to Spirit Bear, there is a coconut token that can be collected to give 10 coconuts.
* This and the Pepper Patch are the only fields where sprouts cannot naturally appear or be planted with the Special Sprout Summoner.
* Like the Pepper Badge, the Coconut Badge requires more pollen collected than the other field badges. For example, the cadet badge for the Coconut Field requires 50,000,000 (50 million) pollen, compared to the normal requirement of 250,000 (250 thousand) pollen.
* It is possible to gather pollen from this field without 35 bees from the ledge facing the Ticket Tent.
* Players can also collect from the field by going outside the map and finding the back of the Ticket Tent. Jumping on the Ticket Tent with enough jump height will reach the field, allowing bees to farm.
Fact: Trivia[] requirement: * The Coconut Dispenser used to boost this field differently based on the player's Coconut Badge, with the Junior badge giving no boost, Cadet giving a +100% pollen boost, Hotshot giving a +200% pollen boost, and anything Ace and above giving a +300% pollen boost. After the Beesmas 2019 update, the Coconut Dispenser will always provide a +100% pollen boost, regardless of the player's Coconut Badge..
* This field, the Ant Field and the Hub Field are the only fields without any bees to dislike them.
