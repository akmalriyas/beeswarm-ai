Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Egg Hunt Info

Egg Hunt Info
The Egg Hunt Info was a quest giver that was located in front of the Ant Gate. This was the quest location of the Egg Hunt 2019 event that occurred in Roblox for Bee Swarm Simulator. The player could collect 3 Marshmallow Bees and turn in it here for various in-game rewards, the Flight of the Bumble Egg, and a badge. If obtained before the event ended, the player could equip the Flight of the Bumble Egg in the avatar tab on the Roblox website.
Each of the NPCs in game at the time, except for the Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") and Onett, gave a quest for a marshmallow bee.
Note: If you completed the Egg Hunt quests before they were modified, you will be rewarded two Field Dice, two marshmallow bees, and five Jelly Beans.
