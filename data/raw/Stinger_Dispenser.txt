Stinger Dispenser
The Stinger Shop.
The Stinger Shop is a shop located behind the Ant Gate that sells Stingers for 10 Tickets each. Stingers are purchasable in increments of 1, 10, and 100.
It has pictures of stingers on the sides and a red circular base with a white top. Unlike other shops with a similar appearance, it does not have a decoration on the top. It also contains a black semicircle with a grey separator as the vending mechanism, which seems to make it a rotary dispenser.
