The Golden Rake is a tool that was added in the 2019-04-05 update. It can be purchased in the Mountain Top Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 20,000,000 Honey.
The Golden Rake collects 7 pollen from 4 lines of 4 patches in 0.75 seconds. It gets supercharged for every 5th scoop, collecting 7 pollen from 4 lines of 12 patches for every fifth scoop. It emits sparkles. Its average base pollen collection rate is 149.333 pollen per second, 238.933 pollen per second when including the supercharged collection.

Trivia[]
* If the supercharged Golden Rake is activated right when the player turns their body, the rake will collect sideways.
* Like all the other tools with an ability, it still activates when the player is not in a field.
* This is the superior version of the Rake, which can be bought in the Noob Shop for 800 Honey
