Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Ant Pass

_"Grants you 1 admission to the Ant Challenge."_
No cooldown
An Ant Pass is an inventory item that was added in the 2018-07-11 update. You must use one in order to participate in the Ant Challenge. Players can only have a maximum of 10 ant passes in their inventory at a time (unless they had more than 10 passes before the 2019-04-05 update, purchased them from Robux packs, or received them from quests).
An ant pass can be used by stepping on the pad in front of the Ant Challenge and pressing "E" on the player's keyboard or by clicking on the prompt.
Ways to Obtain[]
* There is a Free Ant Pass Dispenser past the Ant Gate which can be used every two hours.
* There is also an Ant Pass Dispenser to the right of the Free Ant Pass Dispenser which gives an ant pass for 10 tickets with no cooldown.
* If the player already has at least 10 ant passes, they cannot claim a free ant pass from the Free Ant Pass Dispenser or use the Ant Pass Dispenser.
* Completing Panda Bear's Ant Questline "Panda Bear") rewards an ant pass after every quest. (Excluding Ultimate Ant Annihilation 5)
* A possible reward from a Ladybug, Rage Aphid, King Beetle, and Tunnel Bear when defeated.
* Defeating the Commando Chick will reward ant passes if the player reaches certain milestones.
* Ant passes are sometimes available in the Robux Shop, generally combined with other things in sale packs.
* There are two ant pass tokens hidden in the game.
* Behind the Ant Challenge (go behind the Top Ant Exterminators Leaderboard, then go through the tunnel and exit out through the other side).
* At the very back of the Star Hall behind the Supreme Star Amulet Generator.
* Summoning and defeating a level 4 Snowbear rewards 3 Ant Passes.
* Purchasing the Poinsettia Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog for 250 Snowflakes and 25 Gingerbread Bears gives 5 Ant Passes and other items.
Outdated Methods[]
* Redeeming certain expired codes:
* Oodlesofnoodles (Granted 1 Ant Pass and other stuff).
* ExtraPass (Granted 1 Ant Pass).
* SecretProfilecode (Granted 1 Ant Pass and other stuff).
* Finishing Bubble Bee Man's B.B.M. Mission quest rewarded an ant pass.
