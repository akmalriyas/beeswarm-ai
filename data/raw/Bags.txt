Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Bags

Fact: Bags statistic: **Bags** are accessories that can be bought in the various shops around the map. The main purpose of bags is to store pollen gathered by the player until it is full, to which the player will need to go to their hive and convert it into honey, use a Coconut, use an Instant Converter, use a Micro-Converter, use a Honeysuckle (only converts some) or admire Black Bear's Honey Wreath (only during Beesmas). When the player's bag is full, the following message is displayed:  Pollen container full. Go to your hive to make honey. They may use an Instant Converter to instantly get all their honey (costs 1 ticket) or use a micro-converter. Certain bags are intended to give the player additional boosts other than capacity, such as Instant Conversion, Convert Rate, Bee Attack and even Passives (currently only from the Coconut Canister). There are other accessories (such as belt bags) that increase capacity. The Pouch bag is given automatically to new players. There are currently 12 bags in the game..
