Refreshing Vial
Refreshing Vial
Fact: Refreshing Vial statistic: _"Grants 4 hours of Refreshing Nectar. (Boosts: Red Pollen, Blue Convert Rate, Bee Ability Pollen)"_.
The Refreshing Vial is a consumable nectar vial item that was added in the 2022-12-26 update. Once used, it grants the player 4 hours of the Refreshing Nectar buff instantly. It can also be used to craft other accessories.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Completing Dapper Bear's 7th and 14th quests each reward 1 Refreshing Vial.
* Using the Nectar Condenser to condense 12 hours of Refreshing Nectar.
* Adding the Refreshing Nectar Icon to the Sticker Stack rewards 1 Refreshing Vial.
* Completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas quest rewards 1 Refreshing Vial and other items.
Outdated Ways[]
* From purchasing certain bundles from Bee Bear's Catalog.
* Purchasing the Tropical Bundle from Summer 2024 for 2,500 Snowflakes and 100 Gingerbread Bears granted 1 Refreshing Vial and other items.
* Purchasing the Refreshing Bundle from Beesmas 2022 for 50 Snowflakes and 5 Gingerbread Bears granted 1 Refreshing Vial and other items.
* Purchasing the Hefty Honeyday Bundle from Beesmas 2022 for 25,000 Snowflakes and 50 Gingerbread Bears granted 5 Refreshing Vials and other items.
* Purchasing the Nighttime Nectar Pack in the Robux Shop for 400 Robux granted 1 Refreshing Vial and other items.
* Opening the Tropical Gift Box granted 1 Refreshing Vial and other items.
* Completing Onett's Beesmas 2022 Quest rewarded 1 Refreshing Vial and other items.
