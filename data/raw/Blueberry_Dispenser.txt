The Blueberry Dispenser is a Dispenser located on the first floor of the Blue HQ, next to the Blue Teleporter. When used, it grants the player honey, haste x5, blue boost x10, and 1 blueberry for each Blue bee in the player's hive.
It has a cooldown of 4 hours.
If the player does not have any blue bees in their hive, it will not grant any blueberries.
The player must be in the Bee Swarm Simulator Club in order to use this.

Trivia[]
* Before the 2018-11-25 update, the Blueberry Dispenser was in the Gifted Bucko Bee's spot. However, it was moved after said update to make room for the NPC.
* The only time this dispenser appears in a quest is in Science Bear's _"The Power of Information"_.
* This dispenser gives the same honey per bee as the Strawberry Dispenser.
* This is one of three dispensers that give treats in the game with the other two being the Treat Dispenser and the Strawberry Dispenser.
* This is one of four dispensers requiring the player to be part of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club with the others being the Honey Dispenser, the Treat Dispenser, and the Strawberry Dispenser.
