Trivia[]
* Shocked Bee, Hasty Bee, Shy Bee, and Rascal Bee are the only bees in the game that has a spelling or grammar mistake in their description.
* Shocked Bee requires less sleep than other bees, the only bee to have this trait.
* Shocked Bee and Music Bee have the same skin pattern.
* Shocked Bee is the fastest bee to wake up from sleeping.
* Shocked Bee used to have the Buzz Bomb ability, but it was replaced by Token Link.
* This is the only gifted bee to not have tinted wings.
