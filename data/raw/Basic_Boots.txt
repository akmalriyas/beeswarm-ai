The Basic Boots are a pair of boots that were added in the 2018-09-10 update. They can be bought from the Noob Shop.

Trivia[]
* These could've been obtained from Sun Bear by completing his first 5 quests before he left on 2018-10-08.
* These are the only boots that don't grant Jump Power.
* Before the 2018-11-25 update, this item, the Hiking Boots, and the Beekeeper's Boots didn't require any crafting materials.
* Unlike the Gummy Boots and Coconut Clogs, these, along with the Hiking Boots and Beekeeper's Boots, were obtainable from quests.
* These boots are used to obtain the Yellow Wiggly Walking Person Sticker, by walking over a certain flower chosen at random in the Starter, 5, or 10 bee zones.
