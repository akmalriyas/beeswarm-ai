> "_Embrace hate to take on the form of aDemon Bee. Become both extremely unpleasant and powerful._"
The Demon Mask is the red counterpart of the Diamond Mask. It can be reached by going to the Lava Obby (located behind the Honey Dispenser or by going to the passage in the top of the stall in the Noob Shop) and following the course to the far back wall.

* Every 15th red ability token creates a + sign of 5 spires of flames that collect 4R/2W/1B from 9 surrounding flowers, increased by 2% per every red bee and 4% for each Gifted Red Bee. Does 15 damage per second if touched by a mob, and lasts 4 seconds, no cooldown.
* +Passive: X-Flame:
Fact: * +20% Bee Ability Rate statistic: * Every 25th battle ability token (any token that can spawn during battle) spawns 29 flames in an X shape around the player. Flames last 3s. Each second, they collect 4R/2W/1B Pollen (+2% per Red bee, 4% If Gifted) from 9 surrounding flowers and deal 15 dmg to nearby enemies. Flames also grant Flame Heat if the player stands near them, boosting red pollen and bee attack. (Cooldown: 20 seconds).

Trivia[]
* Demon Mask's raw ingredients cost (excluding gumdrops downgrading):
* 500 Stingers or 5,000 Tickets
* 12,500 Strawberries
* 7,500 Pineapples
* 5,000 Gumdrops or 1,667 Tickets
* 5,000 Royal Jellies or 5B Honey
* Crafting all the required materials takes a total of 2,500 minutes (41 hours and 40 minutes) of playtime.
* This mask and the Diamond Mask both represent Legendary bees.
* The Demon Mask is based on Demon Bee and uses the same face decal.
* The sound effect that plays when the X-Flame passive ability is activated is a distorted version of the music "bpatrol".
* The Demon Mask's stats have gone through multiple changes.
* Originally, the Demon Mask's stats were:
Fact: * 5,000 Royal Jellies or 5B Honey statistic: * +50% Bee Gather Pollen.
* +26% Instant Conversion
* +35% Defense
* +12% Bee Ability Rate
* +125% Luck (Loot Luck)
* +25% Bee Attack Power (Bee Attack)
Fact: * +25% Bee Attack Power (Bee Attack) statistic: * +30% Bomb Pollen.
Fact: * +25% Bee Attack Power (Bee Attack) statistic: * x1.2 Red Pollen.
* +Passive: X-Flame
* When the 2019-04-05 Update came out, its stats changed to:
Fact: * +25% Bee Attack Power (Bee Attack) statistic: * x2 Red Pollen.
Fact: * +25% Bee Attack Power (Bee Attack) statistic: * +50% Bomb Pollen.
* +25% Bee Attack
* +35% Defense
* +12% Bee Ability Rate
* +Passive: X-Flame
* With the Roblox Classic event, this, the Diamond Mask, the Gummy Mask, the Coconut Clogs, the Coconut Canister, the Dark Scythe, the Tide Popper, the Gummy Baller, and the Gummy Boots had at least 1 Nectar Vial added to their crafting recipes.
* The Diamond Mask Shop, the Demon Mask Shop, Basic Egg Shop, and the Sticker-Seeker Shop are the only shops in the game that have a total of 1 item.
* Abilities that trigger “X-Flame” include the following:
* Flame Fuel
* Target Practice
* Token Link
* Baby Love
* Summon Frog
* Tabby Love
* Honey gift
* Mind Hack
