> "_A rare and precious Port-O-Hive that boosts white pollen._ "
The Porcelain Port-O-Hive is a bag that can be purchased in the Mountain Top Shop which was added in the 2018-07-11 update. It has the second-highest capacity and second-highest price of all containers.

Trivia[]
* Out of the four variants of Port-O-Hive, the Porcelain Port-O-Hive was the second Port-O-Hive added into the game, after the regular Port-O-Hive.
* The bag's stats were changed multiple times since its addition to the game.
* After the 2019-04-05 update, its base capacity was increased from 500,000 pollen to 1,000,000 pollen. This was reverted back to 500,000 after the 2019-05-13 Update.
* After the 2024-05-23 update:
* Its base capacity was increased from 500,000 pollen to 600,000 pollen.
Fact: Trivia[] statistic: * It no longer gave +10% Blue Pollen, +10% Red Pollen and +1 Bee Attack..
* Before the 2019-04-05 update, its description was read as, "The rarest and most precious Port-O-Hive in the world. Can store up to 500,000 pollen."
* This container is worn by Onett in the Bear Gate, along with the Porcelain Dipper, Beekeeper's Boots, Mondo Belt Bag, Beekeeper's Mask, and the Riley and Bucko Guards.
* This is the only container that emits particles.
