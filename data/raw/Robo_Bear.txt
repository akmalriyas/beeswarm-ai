Robo Bear is an NPC released in Beesmas 2022 update. It is located in the 15 Bee Zone, found next to the Red HQ and at the other end of the Rose Field. The main feature of Robo Bear is initiating the Robo Bear Challenge. Before gaining access to Robo Bear, the player will be first given a quest to assemble the missing parts of Robo Bear being Red Drive, White Drive, Blue Drive, and Glitched Drive. The drives can be obtained in the following quests:
* Science Bear: Repairing the White Drive
* Dapper Bear: Bartering for the Blue Drive
* Panda Bear: Ready For the Red Drive?
* Science Bear: Corrupting the Glitched Drive
When all requirements are met, the player can then access talking to Robo Bear and initiate the Robo Bear Challenge at the cost of 1 Robo Pass, obtainable through the dispensers in the 30 bee zone.
During Beesmas 2022, players were able to complete the quest by purchasing a white drive in Bee Bear's Catalog, completing Science Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest for a red drive, completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest for a blue drive, and completing Bee Bear's “Decorating For Drives” quest for a glitched drive.

Robo Bear's Challenge is a minigame consisting of multiple rounds. During each round, the player has 5 minutes to complete a random quest. After each round, players are rewarded [Cogs]. Bonus [Cogs] are earned for faster completion. Players can use [Cogs] to purchase upgrades that last for the duration of the challenge. At the start, all but 3 of the player's bees are deactivated. Deactivated bees will not assist the player, and their Gifted Hive and Beequip hive bonuses are removed. Tool Pollen and Capacity is also greatly reduced, but increases each round. Each round, the player may choose 2 bees to re-activate from a random selection. The player may use [Cogs] to re-roll bee and upgrade options. Robotic enemies will attack the player as the rounds progress. If the player fails to complete any round, the challenge ends. If the player leaves the game while a round is in progress, the challenge ends. Certain boosts, including ones from promocodes and Honeyday Events, are disabled during the rounds. Players are rewarded according to the number of rounds they complete. [Cog Amulets] are granted after completing a number of rounds: Bronze Cog Amulet: 5 rounds, Silver Cog Amulet: 10 rounds, Gold Cog Amulet: 15 rounds, Diamond Cog Amulet: 20 rounds, Supreme Cog Amulet: 25 rounds. Trivia[]
* Robo Bear is the second NPC in the game that can initiate a challenge, the first being Stick Bug.
* Robo Bear uses the Robot Animation Pack.
* Robo Bear is one of the few quest givers who own a shop, the others being Mother Bear, Dapper Bear, Gummy Bear, and Bee Bear.
* Robo Bear's original face decal was made on 2019-02-24, eventually releasing on 2022-12-14 for Beesmas.
* Robo Bear is the only bear to publicly have its design changed from before release.
* Robo Bear's Quest, "Repairing Robo Bear" can be instantly completed once redeemed, that is if you already have the Drives.
