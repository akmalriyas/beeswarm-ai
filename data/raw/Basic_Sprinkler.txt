The Basic Sprinkler is the lowest tier sprinkler available for purchase in the Badge Bearer's Guild. To unlock it, the player must obtain 10 different Cadet badges. Once the player unlocks it, they can purchase it for ~1.1 million Honey. Its description reads, "When planted in the ground, causes _[sic]_ nearby flowers to regrow faster!"
The Basic Sprinkler is comprised of an individual sprinkler, meaning the player is able to place down only one sprinkler at any moment. Previous sprinklers are removed if a new one is placed.
Its radius extends to 7 flower tiles, its rate is 5 seconds, and its power is 7.
