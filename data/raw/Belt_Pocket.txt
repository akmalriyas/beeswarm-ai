Trivia[]
* Unlike the Belt Bag and the Mondo Belt Bag, the Belt Pocket was not given out by Sun Bear. It was added later after Sun Bear left.
* This is the cheapest belt accessory in the game.
* This is the only item in the Noob Shop that gives Loot Luck.
