> "_Proudly show your extreme wealth to the world. Shine so brightly that others will complain._ "
The Diamond Mask is a mask and the blue counterpart of the Demon Mask. It can be reached by finding the doorway near the end of the Cloud Obby, then completing an additional obstacle course made up of moon platforms. The player is able to skip the Cloud Obby by using a Parachute or Glider with enough height to steer themselves to the entrance to the Diamond Room. If the player owns the Diamond Mask, the moon platforms in the Diamond Room are permanently solid and are shaded a deep blue.

Trivia[]
* Prior to the 2019-09-28 Update, the player was unable to jump on the moon platforms during the day, similar to the moon platforms in the Bear Gate and behind Dapper Bear's Shop, even if they already own the Diamond Mask.
* If the ingredients to craft this item were to be crafted, a total of 5,000,000,000 honey, 4,000 Royal Jellies, 12,500 Blueberries, 7,500 Sunflower Seeds, 100 Magic Beans, 2,500 Moon Charms, and 5 Diamond Eggs are needed, or 2000 tickets if one decides to purchase all the diamond eggs from the ticket tent.
* This and the Honey Mask are the only items that require eggs to craft.
* This mask and the Demon Mask are the only masks in the game that resemble Legendary bees.
* The Diamond Mask is based on Diamond Bee and uses the same face decal as it.
* When receiving the quest 'Breath of Blue', Spirit Bear says that Diamond Drain is activated on every 25th Blue Boost token collected, though it was later changed to every 30th blue ability token collected.
* Spirit Bear also states that the amount of pollen converted by the ability decreases with each use, though this feature was eventually removed.
* The Diamond Mask Shop, the Hub Field Shop, and the Demon Mask Shop are the only shops in the game that have a single item.
* This, the Demon Mask, and the Gummy Mask are the only masks with decorative accessories (the Diamond Mask has a tiara).
* Unlike the Demon Mask and Gummy Mask, the sound effect of its passive is not based on music in the game.
* This mask, the Demon Mask, and the Mondo B.B.M. Mask are the only masks that require an obby to equip or reach the shop.
