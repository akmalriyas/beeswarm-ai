Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Bloom Shaker

Bloom Shaker
Bloom Shaker
_"Grows Blooms in the field you're standing in. Catch their Petals for Honey and boosts."_
A Bloom Shaker is an inventory item added in the 2025-12-25 update. When used, three blooms will grow in the field the item is used in. It has a 3 minute cooldown. The blooms that are summoned by the bloom shaker will last for a minute, the default for any blooms. The only current renewable source for Bloom Shakers are Riley Bee's and Bucko Bee's "Petals" quests.
Ways To Obtain[]
* Purchasing the Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul from the Robux Shop for 4,500 Robux grants 25 Bloom Shakers and other items.
* Purchasing the Festive Floral Bundle from Bee Bear’s Catalog for 400 Snowflakes grants 1 Bloom Shaker and other items.
* Giving a Present to Onett rewards 3 Bloom Shakers and other items.
* Giving a Present to Polar Bear rewards 1 Bloom Shaker and other items.
* Giving a Present to Gummy Bear rewards 5 Bloom Shakers and other items.
* As a reward for completing certain quests:
* Completing Bee Bear's 9th quest (Gives 1 Bloom Shaker \+ other stuff)
* Completing Bee Bear's 13th quest (Gives 3 Bloom Shakers \+ other stuff)
* Completing Bee Bear's 14th quest (Gives 1 Bloom Shaker \+ other stuff)
* Completing Bee Bear's 15th quest (Gives 3 Bloom Shakers \+ other stuff)
* Completing Gummy Bear's Beesmas quest (Gives 10 Bloom Shakers \+ other stuff)
