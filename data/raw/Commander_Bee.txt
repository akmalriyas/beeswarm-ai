Trivia[]
* Commander Bee's skin is the same as Looker Bee's.
* This bee, Buoyant Bee and Frosty Bee are the only 3 bees in the game that wear hats by default.
* Of the three, this one is the only colorless bee to have one.
* Festive Bee and Puppy Bee, however, can have an accessory by using the Festive Wreath and Reindeer Antlers respectively.
* This bee is one of the 3 bees that have the Focus ability by default, the other 2 are Looker Bee, and Music Bee.
* Commander Bee is the only epic bee added in an update.
* A Commander Bee Egg can be obtained by capturing Commando Chick 200 times.
* As the name and model suggest, this bee is likely inspired by the eponymous video game protagonist known as Commander Keen.
* This point is further supported by the expired code "Keen4", which yielded a Commander Bee Jelly.
