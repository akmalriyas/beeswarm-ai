The Sticker Printer is a machine past the Lion Bee Gate. It is located to the right of the Instant Converter next to the Mountain Top Field. The Sticker Printer costs eggs (excluding Bee Specific Eggs) to print stickers.
Mechanics[]
The printer can print stickers depending on the type of egg donated, and the quality of the type of stickers from the printer is also affected by the type of the egg inserted. The types of Hive Skin, Cub Skin or sticker chances can be checked when clicking on an egg before using it to print a sticker.
To use the Sticker Printer, the user's Roblox account must be at least 7 days old and be members of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club in order to use it.
The Printer will print one sticker upon use and has a cooldown of 1 hour before it can be used again.
