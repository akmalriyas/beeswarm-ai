The All-Time Top Honey Bee Helpers is one of the 64 leaderboards in the game, and one of the 20 leaderboards with an in-game model. This leaderboard shows how many Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") quests players have completed over time. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on the leaderboards for lengths of time (hackers/exploiters will be removed).
