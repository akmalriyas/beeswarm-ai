Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Poinsettia

Poinsettia
"A gorgeous red flower so vibrant it is reserved for a special time of the year."
Fact: Poinsettia statistic: +Red Gather Amount%.
Fact: Poinsettia statistic: +Gather Pollination Chance%.
Fact: Poinsettia statistic: +Bee Gather Pollen%.
Fact: Poinsettia statistic: +Red Pollen%.
A Poinsettia is a level 6 Beesmas Beequip. It has an equip limit of two. There is no color requirement. They can only be equipped to bees that know the Boost ability.

Ways To Obtain[]
* Purchasing Poinsettia Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
* As a rare reward from the Ant Challenge during the Beesmas event. A higher score has a higher chance to drop a Poinsettia.

* As a reward from Spirit Bear's Galentine's Shrine quest in Beesmas 2021 (Gave 1 Poinsettia \+ other items).
* Purchasing the Beequip Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog for 75 Snowflakes and 15 Gingerbread Bears (Gave 1 Poinsettia \+ other items).
* Purchasing the Poinsettia Pack in Bee Bear's Catalog for 500 Snowflakes and 15 Gingerbread Bears (Gave 1 Poinsettia \+ other items).

* This makes the Poinsettia the second beesmas beequip that still can be obtained after Beesmas, with the first being Icicles.
* This Beequip, along with the Lei and the Rose Headband are the only Beequips to have Gather Pollination Chance buff and makes it the only way for bees to pollinate flowers other than the Fuzzy Bee abilities.
