The Werewolf is a level 7 mob found only behind the Honey Bee Gate. Unlike other mobs, it defends three fields instead of one: the Cactus Field, Pine Tree Forest, and the Pumpkin Patch. It spawns at the entrance of the Werewolf's Cave, in between the Cactus Field and the Pumpkin Patch.
It takes 1 hour for the werewolf to respawn after being defeated (51 minutes with Gifted Vicious Bee).

Strategies[]
The main strategy players use is to have something in the way between them and the werewolf. The main things players use are the bigger cactus or the smaller pumpkin. The werewolf will bounce off the object when he tries to attack, making killing him easy.
The run-and-jump method also works: like most Mobs, the werewolf gets confused while the player is in the air and will lunge at the point the player was when they jumped, not where they’re at after they jumped.
The Frozen Field Defenders Glitch works well against the werewolf.
Players can also just go through the Honey Bee Gate, then quickly run behind the Pine Tree nearest to the Badge Bearer's Guild. Then, if he goes back to the cave, just walk beside him and then run back behind the tree. The player should keep doing this until they defeat him.
Trivia[]
* This mob and the Spider are the only regularly-respawning mobs that can attack players beyond the field they spawn in.
* This is the only normal mob to defend three fields (Cactus Field, Pumpkin Patch, and Pine Tree forest).
* The werewolf uses the "White Fang Package" on the Roblox Catalog.
* This was the first non-bug/arachnid mob added into the game.
* The player can earn more than one egg after defeating the werewolf; for example, he can drop 5 Silver Eggs at once.
* This is the second strongest normal mob in the game, only beaten by the estimated health of the cave monsters.
* This is the first mob in which the respawn timer is 1 hour or more, the other 7 being Stump Snail, Snowbear and all other bosses.
* If you run inside the Werewolf's Cave, you would be faced with a tunnel, which, if you cross, rewards you with a Gold Egg. However, the cave is guarded by Cave Monsters that you will have to rush past to reach the egg.
