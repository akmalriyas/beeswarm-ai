The Honey Dispenser is a dispenser that grants Honey and x5 haste every time it's used. It has a 1 hour cooldown and requires the player to be in the Bee Swarm Simulator Club in order to be used by the player. It initially gave 5x haste and 500 Honey, but after the 2018-04-27 update, the amount was changed to 100 Honey per bee in the player's hive and x5 haste. Since then, the amount of Honey was changed again.
It is one of the four club dispensers in the game. However, it is the first one to be accessed.
The Lava Obby can be found behind it and leads to a Royal Jelly token at the back of the Noob Shop and also to the Demon Mask shop.
If the player tries to use the Honey Dispenser without being in the group, they will get a prompt saying that they must join the Bee Swarm Group (or club).

Trivia[]
* During the Gummy Invasion, the dispenser granted an additional Gumdrops per use.
* This, the Strawberry Dispenser, the Blueberry Dispenser, the Royal Jelly Dispenser, and the Glue Dispenser are the only dispensers that are required to be used for a quest ("Honey Bee's Honey Wreath?" from Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") in 2020 and 2021 required the player to use the Honey Dispenser, "BBM's Naughty List" from Bubble Bee Man in Beesmas 2022 requires the player to use the Royal Jelly Dispenser, and "The Power of Information" from Science Bear requires the player to use the other three).
* This is one of four dispensers requiring the player to be part of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club with the others being the Treat Dispenser, the Strawberry Dispenser, and the Blueberry Dispenser.
* When using this dispenser, there is no notification in the chat log of the Honey given.
