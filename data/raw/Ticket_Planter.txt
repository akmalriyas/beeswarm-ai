BONUS ITEMS
A Ticket Planter is a single use planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in about 2 hours and stores 50,000 pollen. When harvested, it grants bonus Tickets. A player can store up to 100 Ticket Planters at a time, but only 1 can be active.
It does not have any growth nor pollen multipliers, and grants x2 nectar.
Harvesting a fully grown Ticket Planter is guaranteed to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 1-2, and is guaranteed to be a Common Puffshroom.
When claimed, the planter gives up to 25 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, 1 of the tokens is guaranteed to be 10 Tickets. The others are picked randomly from the following, non-exhaustive list (note that the list does not include rewards based on the field the planter is in):

Ways to obtain[]
* Dapper Bear gives 3 Ticket Planters if the player speaks to him for the first time.
* As a reward from certain quests:
* If the player completes Dapper Bear’s quests and they already got Beequip slots from Bee Bear in previous years, they get a Ticket Planter for each Beequip slot.
* Bee Bear's "Searching For Snowflakes" quest rewards 1 Ticket Planter and other items.
* As a rare reward from the Retro Swarm Challenge.
* As a rare drop from Sticker Sprouts.
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* Dapper From Above Sticker rewards 1 Ticket Planter.
* Ticket Voucher rewards 1 Ticket Planter
* Buying the Tempting Ticket Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog for 55,555 Snowflakes gives 5 Ticket Planters and other items.
* As a drop from Dapper Bear’s Samovar after every 5th use (except for the 25th, which drops a turpentine instead).

Trivia[]
* This is one of the two planters that is guaranteed to spawn a Puffshroom. The other is the Sticker Planter.
* This, the Sticker Planter and the Paper Planter share the same base model.
* They also share the same outline for their decals.
* It is one of two planters that mentions an in-game item in its name, the other planter is the Sticker Planter.
* It gives the second-best nectar multiplier in the game, 2x, behind the Festive Planter with 3x.
* It is one of 4 single-use planters, the others being the Paper Planter, the Festive Planter and the Sticker Planter.
* This, the Sticker Planter and the Paper Planter are the only one-use planters in the game that can be obtained infinitely.
