The Tallest Sticker Stack is one of 64 leaderboards in the game. This leaderboard shows the amount of stickers players have donated to the Sticker Stack. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place.
Location[]
It is located past the Honey Bee Gate and behind the Pine Tree Forest.

* On 2024-01-13, mateusz8992 was the first player to earn the Sticker Stack Hotshot badge (50 Stickers in Stack).
