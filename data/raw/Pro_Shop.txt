Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Pro Shop

Pro Shop
Location
Past the Brave Bee Gate.
The Pro Shop is a shop located past the Brave Bee Gate, next to the Pineapple Patch. The Mythic Meteor Shower Summoner used to be on top of the shop before being moved in the Trading Update to the Badge Bearer's Guild.
This shop sells four Bags, five Tools, four Guards, a Hat, a Belt, a pair of Boots, Paper Planters, and the Parachute.
As with the Noob Shop, the game does not explicitly name the shop. However, the bear who runs the shop is named "Pro Bear". Therefore, the community has named the shop as such.

Trivia[]
* The Slingshot will sometimes undershoot the player near Panda Bear or in the middle of the Bamboo Field. If the Slingshot does not undershoot, it will regularly shoot the player in front of the shop's door.
* There are two Royal Jelly tokens, one of them on top of the shop at the end of a dark passage and one within inside the shop, the latter being hidden in the Maze behind Pro Bear. The player can obtain it by going all the way to the end, turning right, going to the end, turning right, and going to the first corridor on the right and follow through it to find the token.
* Since the 2018-07-11 update, there is a Sunflower Seed token inside the maze that gives 10 Sunflower Seeds. The player can obtain it by going right two times, just like the Royal Jelly, but on the second right, go to the end of the hall and follow through the last corridor to the right.
* There is a giant pineapple near the Pro Shop, where there are 3 Tickets on top that can be collected by using the Red Cannon and the Parachute or Glider to glide down onto the top.
* If the player stands on the piece of blue wall behind the backpack models and jumps high enough, they can go into the space between the roof and the ceiling.
* Spotted chicks were able to spawn here before they were removed.
* Spotted chicks were also able to spawn in the space between the roof and the ceiling. When they spawned there, the feet of the chick would be visible.
* With 18 items, the Pro Shop has the most purchasable items out of all of the shops.
* Out of the three shops that have a shop bear, this is the only one that sells at least 1 type of guard.
