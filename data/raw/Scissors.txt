The Scissors are a tool that can be purchased at the Pro Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 850,000 Honey.
The Scissors collect all pollen from the patch in front of you in 0.5 seconds. It has a maximum base pollen collection rate of 60 pollen per second from single flowers, 90 pollen per second from double flowers, 120 pollen per second from triple flowers, 150 pollen per second from large flowers, and 180 pollen per second from star flowers, varying immensely due to digging the entire flower, with pollen held by flowers depending on the tier. However, the nearby flowers may not be full, so it doesn't always reach its complete potential.

Trivia[]
* The Scissors are an enhanced version of the Clippers. The main difference in appearance is that the Clippers are smaller and have yellow handles while the Scissors have red and blue handles.
* This is one of the four items in the game that can cut the vines in front of the parkour leading to Commando Chick's Hideout, the others being the Clippers, the Scythe, and the Dark Scythe.
* This and the Spark Staff are the only tools that collect all the pollen from the flowers that they hit.
* This and the Clippers are the only tools that collect pollen from a single flower.
* This tool can become very powerful compared to other tools in the same tier if collecting from higher tier flowers, such as by gathering in fields pollinated by other player's Fuzzy Bees.
