The Diamond Drenchers are the 4th tier sprinkler available for purchase in the Badge Bearer's Guild. To unlock it, the player must obtain 10 different Master badges. Once the player unlocks it, they can purchase it for ~4.4B Honey. Its description reads, “Four enhanced sprinklers made of durable artificial diamond!"
The Diamond Drenchers are comprised of 4 individual sprinklers.
Its radius extends to 8 flower tiles, its rate is 4 seconds, and its power is 9.
