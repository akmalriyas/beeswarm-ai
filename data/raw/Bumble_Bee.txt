Trivia[]
* Bumble Bee has the same skin color as the Bucko Bee.
* Bumble Bee is the blue counterpart of the Rascal Bee.
* Defeated Rhino Beetles will sometimes drop Bumble Bee Eggs.
* A special jelly that changes a bee into a Bumble Bee could have been obtained from Bubble Bee Man and the code, 'BeeDay2019' and 'Cubly' (expired).
* Bumble Bee, Carpenter Bee, and Honey Bee are named after their real-life counterparts.
* Bumble Bee, Bucko Bee, and Buoyant Bee are the only blue bees to like at least three fields.
* Bumble Bee is the slowest rare bee.
* Bumble bees gifted stat used to be +20% blue bomb pollen and has since been changed.
