The Brave Bee Gate , also called the 10 Bee Gate , is a gate that requires 10 bees to pass.
It gets its name from the large Brave Bee on top of it.
Trivia[]
* The player can reach the top of the Blue HQ if the gate is unlocked, by jumping off the ledge near Science Bear. This gives a shortcut to Brown Bear, the Honeystorm Summoner, the Wealth Clock, and Commando Chick's Hideout. This could also grant access to Bucko Bee's Snow Machine during Beesmas.
* Formerly, the Slingshot could bypass the gate even if the player didn't have 10 bees, since it launched them past it.
* This was fixed in the 2019-09-28 update, as the player gets teleported back to spawn if they don't meet the requirements to enter an area.
* This gate doesn't have any fields in which Rogue Vicious Bee can spawn.
