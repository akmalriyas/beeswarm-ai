Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - White Balloon

White Balloon
White Balloon
_"Floats over the field for 5m. Stores up to 10x your capacity and works best with White flowers."_
The White Balloon is a consumable item added in the 2021-12-26 update. Once used, it creates a white balloon over the field the player is in, storing pollen collected underneath it. The pollen collected below it increases by 10%, with white pollen increasing by 20%. It holds up to 10x the player's capacity and floats over the field for 5 minutes before floating to the hive balloon.
White balloons have a cap of 25 in the player's inventory, and 1 per player in the field.
A White Balloon floating above a field.
Ways to Obtain[]
* As a exceptionally rare drop from a spider.
* As a rare reward after finishing a Robo Bear Challenge.
* Completing certain Dapper Bear quests:
* Wandering Ambition rewards 1 white balloon and other items.
* The Long Haul rewards 1 white balloon and other items.
* Solitarily Steadfast rewards 3 white balloons and other items.
Outdated Ways[]
* Completing Bee Bear's Beesmas Bouquet and Snow Cub Reformation (4/5).
* Purchasing the Buoyant Pack in the Robux Shop gave 3 white balloons and other items.
* Purchasing the Balloon Bundle in Bee Bear’s Catalog during Beesmas 2021, granting 3 White Balloons.
* Opening certain Gift Boxes during the Beesmas 2021 event:
* The Gilded Gift Box gave 1 White Balloon and other items.
* Opening certain Gift Boxes during the Beesmas 2022 event:
* The Lapis Gift Box gave 2 White Balloons and other items.
* The Tropical Gift Box gave 3 White Balloons and other items.
* Redeeming certain expired codes
* FourYearFiesta (gave 1 White Balloon and other stuff).
* 6irthday (gave 1 White Balloon and other stuff).
* Giving a present to Science Bear in Beesmas 2022 gives 1 White Balloon and other items.
* Purchasing the Balloon Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog for 333 Snowflakes, granting 2 White Balloons and other items.
* As a reward from certain Robo Party ranks.
