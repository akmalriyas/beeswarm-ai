Trivia[]
* Just like Music Bee, this bee originated from Number1Riker's Bee Swarm showcase.
* Carpenter Bee and Music Bee are also the only Legendary bees added in an update.
* This bee uses the wood texture and has a hammer mesh in its body.
* This bee, Spicy Bee, and Vector Bee are the only non-Event bees that have special body parts (hammer mesh).
* This is one of two bees added in the 2018-11-25 Update, along with Stubborn Bee.
* One reason Carpenter Bee likes the Pine Tree Forest might be because pine trees are made of wood.
* One reason Carpenter Bee dislikes the Mountain Top Field might be because on the tops of tall mountains, there are no trees.
* Carpenter Bee, along with Honey Bee and Bumble Bee, are the only bees named after their real-life counterparts.
Fact: Trivia[] requirement: * Carpenter Bee is the only bee that can generate the Honey Mark+ ability token..
* This bee was one of the bees to have a specific jelly in the Bee Bear's Catalog. The jelly was given as part of the Pinecone Bundle.
* This bee is the only Legendary bee who can't find a sticker while gathering.
