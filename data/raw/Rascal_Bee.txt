Trivia[]
* Its blue counterpart is Bumble Bee.
* Rascal Bee, Riley Bee, and Demon Bee have the same skin colors.
* Rascal Bee is one of Onett's "Onett \(Developer\)") favorite bee types because he likes its facial expression.
* In Onett's inventory, it is revealed that Rascal Bee was originally going to have a different face.
* In Rascal Bee's description, "mischi evous" is written as "mischevi ous", a common misspelling of the word.
* Defeated ladybugs will sometimes drop Rascal Bee eggs.
* Rascal Bee has the second highest attack of all Rare bees, only behind Brave Bee.
* A special jelly that changes a bee into a Rascal Bee could have been obtained from the code 'MischiefMaker' (expired).
