Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Daily Top White Collectors

Daily Top White Collectors
Fact: Daily Top White Collectors statistic: The **Daily Top White Pollen Collectors** is one of 64 leaderboards in the game. This leaderboard shows how much white pollen players have collected on that day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM CST..
Fact: Daily Top White Collectors statistic: If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message "You've collected "X" White Pollen today " will appear..
Location[]
It is located inside of the Petal Shop, to the right of the All-Time Top White Collectors Leaderboard.
Prizes[]
If the player has reached the top 10 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with the Basic WhiteHive Skin.
If the player has reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with 25 Tickets.
