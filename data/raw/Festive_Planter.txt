The Festive Planter is a Beesmas-exclusive single-use planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in 4 hours of playtime and stores 250,000 pollen. A player can store up to 100 Festive Planters at a time, but only 1 can be active.
It does not have any growth nor pollen multipliers, and grants 3x more nectar.
Harvesting a fully grown Festive has a 1/10 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 1-2, and is guaranteed to be a Common Puffshroom.
When claimed, the planter gives up to 80 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, 1 of the tokens is guaranteed to be 15 Tickets. The others are picked randomly from the following, non-exhaustive list (note that the list does not include rewards based on the field the planter is in):

Ways to Obtain[]
* Purchasing the "Feelin' Festive Pack" from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux gives 5 Festive Planters and other items.
* Purchasing the "Festive Fun Pack" in Bee Bear's Catalog for 1,000 Snowflakes and 5 Gingerbread Bears gives 1 Festive Planter and other items.
* Purchasing the "Purple-Turple Mega Pack" in Bee Bear's Catalog for 25,000 Snowflakes and 250 Gingerbread Bears gives 10 Festive Planters and other items.
* Completing Bee Bear's "An Extra Merry Mission" rewards 1 Festive Planter , among other things.
* Completing Spirit Bear's Galentine Shrine quest rewards 1 Festive Planter and other items.
* Completing Onett's Lid Art quest rewards 1 Festive Planter and other items.
* As a reward for completing Rank 40 and 50 of your Robo Party.

Trivia[]
* This is the only planter that can drop magic beans.
* This planter has the highest nectar multiplier in the game.
* This is one of the four planters that aren't reusable, with the others being the Ticket Planter, Sticker Planter and the Paper Planter.
* Out of these four, the Festive Planter is the only one that is non-renewable.
