Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Tropical Drink

Tropical Drink
Tropical Drink
Fact: Tropical Drink statistic: _"Grants x1.25 White Pollen, +10% Instant Conversion and +5% Critical Chance for 10 minutes."_.
Tropical Drink is a craftable inventory item added in the 2019-09-28 update. When used, it grants following buffs for 10 minutes:
Fact: Tropical Drink statistic: * x1.25 White Pollen.
* +5% Critical Chance
* +10% Instant Conversion
It can also be used as a material to craft certain items. Using a Super Smoothie will override the buff.
Ways to Obtain[]
A Tropical Drink inside the player’s inventory.
* Crafting it via Blender for:
* 10 Coconuts
* 2 Enzymes
* Completing certain quests:
* Brown Bear's 85th quest rewards 1 Tropical Drink.
* Black Bear's "Boss Battles" quest rewards 3 Tropical Drinks.
* Black Bear has a chance to reward Tropical Drinks after the completion of the "Black Bear: Triple Treck" quest in his repeatable questline.
* As a drop from sprouts in the Coconut Field (the odds increase depending on the rarity of the sprout).
* Matching a pair of Tropical Drink in Extreme Memory Match or Mega Memory Match or Winter Memory Match.
Drops[]
* As a drop from from certain mobs:
* Wild Windy Bee
* Armored Aphid
* Coconut Crab (guaranteed, amounts depend on the time it took to kill the crab).
* As a very rare Cub Buddy gift.
* As a drop from fully-grown planters harvested on Coconut Field.
* As a very rare drop from leaves in the Coconut Field.
Outdated Ways[]
* By purchasing certain bundles from Bee Bear's Catalog:
* Kazoo Bundle gave 10 Tropical Drinks and other stuff.
* Tropical Bundle gave 25 Tropical Drinks and other stuff.
* Completing Honey Bee's (NPC) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") Beesmas 2022 quests gives 9 Tropical Drinks.
* By purchasing certain packs in the Robux Shop.
* Purchasing the Star Treat Special for 1700 robux gave 100 Tropical Drinks and other stuff.
* Purchasing the Merry Mutations Pack gave for 1700 robux gave 50 Tropical Drinks and other stuff.
* Purchasing the Wavy Doodle Hive Bundle for 1700 robux gave 50 Tropical Drinks and other stuff.
* Redeeming certain expired codes:
* Using the code "2YearsAfterParty" (granted the buff and other stuff).
* Using the code "PineappleParty" (gave 1 Tropical Drink and other stuff).
* Using the code "Strawbeary" (gave 1 Tropical Drink and other stuff).
