Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Broken Drive

Broken Drive
Broken Drive
_"Looks like a poorly preserved computer part. Maybe someone can fix it..."_
No cooldown
The Broken Drive is an inventory item that was introduced in the 2023-05-07 update. They can be used to activate Science Bear's quest to repair Robo Bear. After completing Science Bear's quest the Broken Drive will be replaced with a White Drive.
A Broken Drive can be found behind the Robo Pass Dispensers in the 30 Bee Zone, which is the only way to obtain one.

Trivia[]
* The Broken Drive is the only type of drive that can't be used during the Robo Bear Challenge.
* The Broken Drive is the only type of drive that can only be obtained once, as the others can be repeatedly earned through the Robo Bear Challenge and bought with Cogs.
* This also means the Broken Drive is the only type of drive that cannot be used to craft a Glitched Drive or a Digital Bee.
* Players do not need to fix the Broken Drive to unlock the Robo Bear Challenge if Robo Bear was previously repaired during Beesmas 2022.
* This item and the Plastic Egg are the only items whose sole purpose is to complete a quest.
