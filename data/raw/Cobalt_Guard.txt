The Cobalt Guard is a guard that can be crafted in the Badge Bearer's Guild. It is currently the highest tier blue guard. Its description reads, "A guard bestowed with the heroic power of Cobalt Bee \- Defender of the Blue Bees."
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 200,000,000 Honey.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 100 Blue Extracts.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 100 Stingers.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 50 Enzymes.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 25 Glitter.
It is equipped on the right shoulder. When equipped, it leaves a trail behind it, similar to Cobalt Bee's trail.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients statistic: * +250,000 Capacity..
Fact: Crafting Ingredients statistic: * +50% Blue Pollen..
Fact: Crafting Ingredients statistic: * +20% Blue Bomb Pollen..
* +3 Blue Bee Attack.
* +10% Defense.
* +100% Critical Power.
* +Passive Ability: Haste Pulser.
* Effects: Each 30th haste token releases a blue pulse that hops to each blue bee, collecting pollen around them. Power increases with each hop.
* Unlike the ability token, this effect cannot trigger a red pulse, even if the player owns a Crimson Bee or the Crimson Guard.

Trivia[]
* If the ingredients to craft this item were to be crafted, a total of 200,000,000 Honey, 15,000 Pineapples, 14,375 Blueberries, 9,375 Strawberries, 2,125 Royal Jellies, 100 Stingers, and 25 Magic Beans are needed.
* This is the only blue guard that isn't purchasable in the Blue HQ.
* This is one of the two guards that have a passive ability, the other being the Crimson Guard.
* Its red counterpart is the Crimson Guard.
* This and the Crimson Guard are the only guards to be based off an Event bee.
* The blue trail's _min length value_ is higher than the Crimson Guard's trail, making the blue trail to be less smooth than the red one. This could be to not show the trail when using a Petal Wand.
* This and the Crimson Guard are the only guards in the game to leave a trail behind when the player moves.
