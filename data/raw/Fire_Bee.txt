Trivia[]
* The Fire Mask, which can be purchased in the Red HQ, has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Fire Bee.
* Fire Bee can be summoned by Spicy Bee with its Inferno ability, making it the only bee summonable by ability tokens.
* This bee can also be summoned by using the Honeyday Candles.
* Fire Bee is the red counterpart of Bubble Bee.
* This bee was one of the bees to have a specific jelly in the Bee Bear's Catalog. The jelly was given as part of the Cozy Bundle.
