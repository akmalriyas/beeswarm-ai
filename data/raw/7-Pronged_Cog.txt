7-Pronged Cog
_Not to be confused withCog._
7-Pronged Cog
_"A component used in mysterious electronics."_
7-Pronged Cog is a special inventory item added in the 2020-11-23 update for the Ready Player Two event. It was used as one of the two ingredients to craft the Strange Goggles.
To obtain the 7-Pronged Cogs for the event, the player would need to redeem seven codes, discovered by various riddles scattered across the map. As 7-Pronged Cogs are unobtainable but not completely removed, any of them redeemed will remain in players' inventories.
NOTE : These codes are now expired. They are also listed here.
