Trivia[]
* Bubble Bee is the blue counterpart of Fire Bee.
* Bubble Bee emits a bubble effect, a trait also shared with Honey Bee and its gifted variant.
* Bubble Bee and Tadpole Bee are the only bees that have the Gathering Bubbles passive ability.
* The Bubble Mask, which can be purchased in the Blue HQ, has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Bubble Bee.
* While gathering in the Clover Field, it has an uncommon chance to find a Sitting Green Shirt Bear Sticker.
* In spite of this bee's name, Bubble Bee has nothing to do with Bubble Bee Man.
* However, Bubble Bee Man did give out a Bubble Bee Jelly in Beesmas 2018 and a Bubble Bee Egg in Beesmas 2020 and Beesmas 2021.
