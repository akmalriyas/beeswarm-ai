Trivia[]
* This guard represents Bucko Bee.
* Before the 2018-11-25 update, it used to cost 60,000,000 honey and no crafting materials.
* This is the highest tiered guard available in the Blue HQ.
* This is the fourth most expensive item in the Blue HQ, after the Bubble Mask, the Hydroponic Planter, and the Tide Popper.
* This guard’s red counterpart is the Riley Guard.
* If a player were to craft the ingredients needed to craft this item, it would cost 30,000,000 honey, 2,025 blueberries, 225 royal jellies, 1,650 pineapples and 1,425 strawberries.
