Commando Chick's Hideout
The location of the Commando Chick’s hideout.
The Commando Chick’s Hideout is a location in the Starter Zone that can be entered by completing the obby between the Blue HQ and the Wealth Clock. To enter the parkour, the player first needs to cut the vines with either the Clippers, Scissors, or the Scythe. It is home to the Commando Chick.
Strategy[]
Standing on the last stair, nearest to Commando Chick's hideout normally makes the chick flee. However, if the player stands on the edge of that stair (as close as possible to the hideout where one of your body parts is inside of the hideout), the Commando Chick does not flee; therefore, the player's bees can defeat the Commando Chick whilst they take no damage. This strategy does not 100% work as there could be a slight chance of the Commando Chick fleeing. The player could just move into the hideout and then back onto the stair again to fix this.
