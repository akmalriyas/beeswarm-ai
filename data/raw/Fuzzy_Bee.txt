Abilities[]
* [Fuzz Bombs] Spawns 2 (+1 every 5 bee lvls) fuzz bombs that wander the Field for 8 seconds. Catching them collects pollen and Pollinates nearby Flowers. The amount of pollen collected scales with buzz bomb multipliers and bee Level.
* [Buzz Bomb+] Collects 7 pollen from 29 surrounding flowers (+10% pollen per Level). This combined with other bombs will increase power.
Fact: Abilities[] statistic: * **[🌟Gifted Ability: Pollen Haze]** Summons a yellow haze over the field for 30s that pollinates up to 30 random flowers every second and improves flower growth rate. Bomb Tokens within the haze are automatically collected and pollinate flowers. If there has been a Pollen Haze over the field within the last 3 minutes, this summons fuzz bombs instead..
* [Passive: Fuzzy Coat] This bee may pollinate up to 5 nearby flowers (9 if gifted) every time it gathers. Pollinated flowers are temporarily upgraded to grant and store more pollen and grow faster. Lower tier flowers have a higher chance of being pollinated.

Trivia[]
* This bee, Buoyant Bee, and Precise Bee are the first Mythic bees to be added since the release of the original Mythic bees.
* It's also the only Mythic bee to be introduced on its own.
* Additionally, Fuzzy Bee is the only Mythic bee to be added outside of Beesmas.
* This bee is currently the only bee that can pollinate flowers without the help of a Beequip.
* This bee may like the Dandelion Field because dandelions in real life are also soft and fuzzy.
* This bee, Shy Bee, Ninja Bee, and Windy Bee are the only bees with one skin color.
* This bee, Spicy Bee, Tadpole Bee, Buoyant Bee and Digital Bee all have four different abilities, the most for any bee in the game.
* The Fuzzy Bee Egg was the fifth specific bee egg that could have been bought for Robux, after Bear Bee, Festive Bee, Windy Bee, and Puppy Bee and before Precise Bee and Buoyant Bee.
* Fuzzy Bee was the first non-Event bee that could have been purchased with Robux.
* Fuzz bombs can be popped by using petal Shurikens from the Petal Wand and Petal Storm, Windy Bee's tornado, and Puppy Bee's fetch ball.
* Any player can pop fuzz bombs, even if they were summoned by another player's Fuzzy Bee.
* Fuzzy Bee is the only hive bee that was introduced in 2020 (not counting Digital Bee, as it wasn't usable until 2022).
* Fuzzy Bee's Fuzz Bombs can pop bubbles when the fuzz bombs touch them.
* There was a visual glitch that causes Fuzzy Bee to not have it's fur on it despite the fact it has it when you obtain it through Eggs or Royal Jelly (You will only see Fuzzy Bee's Body and face)
