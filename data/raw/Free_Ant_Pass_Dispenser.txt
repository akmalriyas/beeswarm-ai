> _Not to be confused with theAnt Pass Dispenser, which requires tickets._
The Free Ant Pass Dispenser is a dispenser located past the Ant Gate and to the left of the Ant Pass Dispenser. Players can use the dispenser to get a free Ant Pass every 2 hours, which can be used to participate in the Ant Challenge. Players cannot use the dispenser if they already have 10 or more ant passes in their inventory.
Trivia[]
* This and the Free Robo Pass Dispenser are the only free dispensers in the game that do not grant any buffs when used.
* This, the Ant Pass Dispenser, the Free Robo Pass Dispenser, the Robo Pass Dispenser, the Coconut Dispenser, and the Glue Dispenser are the only dispensers that do not grant Haste when used.
* This, the Ant Pass Dispenser, the Free Robo Pass Dispenser, and the Robo Pass Dispenser are the only dispensers that don't have any visible faucets. Instead, they have a small compartment, similar to a vending machine or an ATM.
