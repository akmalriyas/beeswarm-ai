Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Tickets

_"Used to activate and purchase special things!"_
Tickets are a form of currency added in the 2018-04-27 update. They can be used to purchase certain items.
Ways to Obtain[]
The Ticket Shop.
* The player can obtain Tickets as a reward for acquiring badges. Collecting badges gives:
* 5 Tickets for earning Cadet badges.
* 10 Tickets for earning Hotshot badges.
* 15 Tickets for earning Ace badges.
* 25 Tickets for earning Master badges.
* 40 Tickets for earning Grandmaster badges.
* In total, 2,375 Tickets can be obtained from Badges. (2,335 Tickets if not counting the currently impossible Sticker Stack Grandmaster Badge.)
* The player can buy Tickets from the Ticket Shop with Honey, located outside the Mountain Top Shop. The price increases with every Ticket bought.
* Some bears give Tickets as rewards for completing certain quests.
* Redeeming certain valid codes:
* 38217 (Gives 5 Tickets).
* Bopmaster (Gives 5 Tickets).
* Crawlers (Gives 5 Tickets).
* Roof (Gives 5 Tickets).
* Connoisseur (Gives 5 Tickets).
Fact: Ways to Obtain[] requirement: * Wax (Gives 5 **Tickets** \+ other items)..
* Buying them from the Robux Shop for Robux:
* 1 Ticket for 10 Robux ( 10 Robux per Ticket).
* 100 Tickets for 400 Robux ( 4 Robux per Ticket).
* 510 Tickets for 1,700 Robux ( 3.33 Robux per Ticket).
* 1,800 Tickets for 4,500 Robux ( 2.5 Robux per Ticket).
* The Wealth Clock gives Tickets every hour after interacting with it, and increases incrementally every hour, stopping at the fifth (1 ticket first time, 2 second time, 3 third time, 4 fourth time, and 5 fifth time and onwards).
* They can spawn as a potential match from Normal, Mega, Night and Extreme Memory Matches.
* Clicking on any Hidden Sticker grants 1 Ticket each (excluding seeker-stickers).
* Adding certain Stickers to the Sticker Stack grants Tickets.
* As a Reward for being in the top 100 of any daily leaderboard, ranging from 25, 50 to 100 Tickets depending on the leaderboard.
* Redeeming a Ticket Voucher grants 100 Tickets.
* 1,000 Tickets will be given if you already have a Digital Bee after purchasing the Robo Bear's Shop's Digital Bee deal.
Drops[]
* As a drop from planters:
* The number of Tickets it can potentially drop depends on the type of planter.
Fact: Drops[] requirement: * Ticket Planters always drop 10+ **Tickets** when harvested at full growth..
* As a common drop from Mythic Meteors.
* As a drop from Stockings.
* There is a chance of getting a Ticket as a reward for defeating mobs. The probability depends on the type of mob as well as the player’s Loot Luck.
* Tunnel Bear, Stump Snail, and King Beetle are guaranteed to grant Tickets upon defeat.
* Wild Windy Bee will yield a limited amount of Tickets per day.
* The Commando Chick will always drop 1 Ticket upon being captured.
* Capturing it for the 25th time rewards 100 Tickets.
* Defeating Snowbear will drop an amount of Tickets equal its level upon defeat.
* Completing a Robo Party will reward an amount of Tickets equal to the party's rank.
* Through a Cub Buddy gift.
* Puffshrooms drop Tickets (the rarer the Puffshroom, the more it gives).
* Honeystorms are guaranteed to spawn Ticket tokens.
* As a Retro Swarm Challenge Reward.
* As a rare drop from Festive Gifts.
* As a Sprout reward, besides Moon and Debug variants (the rarer the sprout, the more Tickets it will yield; rare sprouts and above guarantee Tickets).
* Sparkles may yield Tickets.
* Very rarely, flowers may generate a Ticket token. The base probability is around 1/1000 (0.1%), however it does increase with Loot Luck and Ticket Chance.
Token Locations[]
* On top of the biggest pineapple in the Pineapple Patch. Grants 3 Tickets.
* At the back of the cave that the spider comes from. Grants 1 Ticket.
* In the back of Red HQ, near the ceiling (the best way to get it seems to be to get x5 Haste, climb on top of the teleporter, jump up and walk on the edge of the leaderboard, and then glide to the Ticket. The enhanced jump ability granted by certain hats, boots, and/or the bear morph will also help).
* Next to the Glider inside the Mountain Top Shop.
* On a platform in the Cloud Obby, fairly high above the Pine Tree Forest and Pumpkin Patch. Grants 5 Tickets. (Jump off the tip of the red shoulder guard of the 30 Bee Gate, then glide or parachute down to it).
* On a small ledge between the Lion Bee Gate and the Brave Bee Gate. Grants 5 Tickets. The player must use the slingshot and move to the left. Parachute at the right moment to land on the rock. Alternative: Jump down from in front of the Lion Bee Gate. A Parachute/Glider can be helpful, but not absolutely necessary.
* Behind the red shoulder guard of the Bear Gate (i.e. inside the 30 Bee Area). Grants 10 Tickets.
* On the awning over Mother Bear's area. (Easily reached from the ramp that leads to the Honey Bee Gate). Grants 1 Ticket.
* On top of the Ant Gate. Grants 5 Tickets. (Reachable by jumping on top of the Stinger Shop, then crossing to the ledge.
* Behind the Honey Dispenser in the Lava Obby. Upon entering, immediately turn right, and jump up onto the ledge. Grants 3 Tickets. This replaced one of the Plastic Egg locations.
* Underneath the Basic Bee Gate ramp, where Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") used to be. Grants 3 Tickets. This replaced one of the Plastic Egg locations, when they were removed.
* In the Blue Maze, turn left and then take the first right. Grants 5 Tickets. This replaced one of the Plastic Egg locations.
* Inside the tunnel behind the Coconut Dispenser in the Coconut Cave. Grants 5 Tickets.

The Ticket Tent.
* Buying a Royal Jelly From the Royal Jelly Dispenser for 6 Tickets with a cooldown of 3 seconds.
* Buying Gumdrops from the Gumdrop Shop at the cost of 1 Ticket per 3 Gumdrops.
* Buying Stingers from the Stinger Shop at the cost of 10 Tickets per Stinger.
* Buying Magic Beans from the Magic Bean Shop at the cost of 20 Tickets per Magic Bean.
* 1 Ticket is required to use an Instant Converter.
* 10 Tickets are required to use the Ant Pass Dispenser.
* The Ticket Tent has the following items available:
* Silver Egg (50 Tickets).
* Gold Egg (100 Tickets).
* Diamond Egg (400 Tickets).
* Mythic Egg (2,500 Tickets).
* Cobalt Bee Egg and Crimson Bee Egg (250 Tickets each, one time purchase).
* Miscellaneous Event Bees (500 Tickets each, one time purchase).
* Star Treat (1,000 Tickets).
* 50 Tickets are required to start another Stick Bug Challenge when the player’s free challenge is already used.
* Tickets can be used to speed up materials that are currently being made in the Blender.
* 5 Tickets are required to store nectar in the Nectar Pot.
* 50 Tickets are required to use the Robo Pass Dispenser.
* Varying amounts of Tickets can be used to increase the maximum amount of Beequips one can hold in their Beequip Storage, requiring 123,500 Tickets in total to max it out.
* Varying amounts of Tickets (Starting at 20 and capping at 1,000 per upgrade) can be used to increase the maximum amount of Stickers one can hold in their Sticker Storage, requiring 42,420 Tickets in total to max it out.
* Tickets , along with Honey, are used to purchase Beequips in Dapper Bear's Shop.
Trivia[]
* It bears the face of a Basic Bee.
* Currently, the most expensive purchase that can be made using Tickets is the final slot upgrade for the Beequip Storage, costing 20,000 Tickets to increase storage from a maximum of 95 stored Beequips to 100 stored Beequips.
* Tickets , along with Honey, were used to purchase Market Boosts from the Boost Market.
* You need a total of 179,420 Tickets in total to Max out Sticker and Beequip storages, buy all event bees from the Ticket Tent and gift all of the event bees with Star Treat.
