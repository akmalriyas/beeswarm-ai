Stubborn Bee is a Colorless Rare bee.
Stubborn Bee's favorite treat is Strawberries .
Stubborn Bee likes the Dandelion Field and the Pineapple Patch. It dislikes the Rose Field.
* Collects 10 pollen in 6 seconds.
* Makes 80 Honey in 4 seconds.
* -15% movespeed, +25% convert speed, +2 attack.
* 🌟 Gifted Hive Bonus: +15% Ability Token Lifespan.

Trivia[]
* Stubborn Bee was one of the two bees added in the 2018-11-25 update, the other being Carpenter Bee.
* Stubborn Bee and Looker Bee are the only Rare bees added after the game launch.
* The gifted hive bonus (+15% ability token lifespan) used to be broken. The player could have a Gifted Stubborn Bee in their hive, but the ability token lifespan would not apply. This was fixed on an unknown update in February 2021.
* Stubborn Bee is the only Colorless Rare Bee that does not have its own guard in the Pro Shop. This is because it was added in the 2018-11-25 update, after the guards were added.
