_Not to be confused with theHive Hub._
The Hub Field is a field that was added in the 2024-01-12 update and is located in the trading portal beyond the Ant Gate inside the Hive Hub.
This field contains 697 flowers. They are white, blue, and red, in single, double, and triple sizes.
When planting a sprout using a Magic Bean on the Hub Field, there is a rare chance of it spawning a Sticker Sprout. A Sticker Sprout is also guaranteed to spawn every 3 hours, granted there is no sprout occupying the field.
All pollen collected in this field will instantly convert to honey.
This field can spawn Blueberry, Honey, Pineapple, Strawberry, Sunflower Seed, Treat, and Magic Bean tokens.
Only through the use of glitches, this field grants Comforting Nectar when a planter is planted here.

Trivia[]
* This, the Ant Field, and the Stump Field are the only fields that the player can collect pollen from while not directly standing on the flowers.
* This and the Stump Field are the only non-quadrilateral fields in the game and the only fields that the player can collect pollen from while not directly standing on the flowers.
* This, the Ant Field, and the Stump Field are the only fields where Wild Windy Bee cannot spawn in nor fly to.
* This, the Coconut Field, the Pepper Patch, the Ant Field and the Stump Field are the only fields that were added after the game's first release.
* This, the Mountain Top Field, Ant Field, and the Coconut Field are the only fields that can't be given a field boost by field boosters.
* This field is one of three fields with no decorations in it, indicating what field it is. The other two fields to do this are the Ant Field and the Mountain Top Field.
* This field gets its name from the place it is located in, the Hive Hub.
* The Hub Field and the Brick Fields are the only field in the entire game without a truly unique field boost icon. Instead, the Hub Field’s icon is reused from its associated field stamp.
* You cannot use many types of consumable items while on this field.
* The player cannot use sprinklers, stingers, cloud vials, balloons, snowflakes, planters, whirligigs, night bells or any kind of dice while in this field.
* As such, this is the only field in the game where Sprinklers are completely disabled.
* However, you can use Glitter, Jelly Beans, and Box-O-Frogs.
* This is the only field that instantly converts your collected pollen into honey (the Ant Field does not instantly convert your honey by itself, and it happens instead because of the Ant Challenge Buff).
* The Hub Field was used to test the max amount of Combo Coconuts that could be caught from a single Combo Coconuts Ability, the idea behind it was that 34 players can cover all spots on the field where Combo Coconut could land, thus, if it could land infinitely, it would last forever, since in normal conditions Combo Coconut accelerates too fast for players to even reach 40th coconut. So, the final result showed that Combo Coconut can land a total of 50 Times, after which it will just finish as if players didn't catch it, but there won't be a message about the player missing it.
* 5 minutes before a natural Sticker Sprout spawns, the following message will be displayed: [🌱 A Sticker Sprout is about to spawn in Public Server Hive Hubs… 🌱 ]
* The Hub Field spawns the most colors of Bloom petals out of all fields. The only colors that the Hub Field can't spawn are yellow and merigold.
