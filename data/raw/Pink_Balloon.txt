Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Pink Balloon

Pink Balloon
Pink Balloon
_"Floats on the field for 3 minutes, storing and doubling pollen you collect beneath it. Then it floats to your hive!"_
The Pink Balloon is a consumable item that was added in the 2021-12-26 update. Once used, it creates a pink balloon over the field the player is in. The balloon floats over the field, storing pollen collected underneath it. The pollen collected below is doubled. It holds up to double the player's capacity. It floats over the field for 3 minutes before floating to the hive balloon.
Pink balloons have a cap of 25 in the player's inventory, and 1 per player in the field.
Ways to Obtain[]
* As an exceptionally rare drop from the Mantis and the Ladybug.
* As a drop from the Retro Swarm Challenge.
* As a drop from the Party Mega Mechsquito.
* As a drop from completing a Robo Party.
