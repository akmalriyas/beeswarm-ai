The Pineapple Patch is a field located past the Brave Bee Gate and to the right of the Pro Shop. It is protected by a level 4 mantis and a level 5 rhino beetle.
This is a 33x23 field, containing 759 flowers. The flowers are white, red, and blue, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

This field can spawn honey, pineapple, bitterberry, neonberry, enzyme, ticket, magic bean, glue and glitter tokens when gathering pollen.
This field grants Satisfying Nectar when a planter is planted here.
Bees that like the Pineapple Patch are Shocked Bee, Diamond Bee, Lion Bee, Gummy Bee, and Photon Bee. Bees that dislike the Pineapple Patch are Vector Bee, Cobalt Bee, and Crimson Bee.
A level 1-20 Wild Windy Bee has the chance of spawning here.

Trivia[]
* This is the the biggest field in the game.
* The mobs in this field (Rhino Beetle and Mantis) are associated with blue, despite the field being white.
* There is a pineapple token behind the pineapple closest to the shop. Collecting it grants the player five pineapples.
* There is a ticket token worth three tickets on top of the giant pineapple. It can be obtain by using the Red Cannon and the Parachute or Glider, or by jumping up from the side of the pineapple.
* There is a glue token behind the biggest pineapple between the Stump Field.
* This was the third biggest field in the game before the 2018-07-11 update.
* This field is one of the three fields to have the word "Patch" in their name instead of "Field," along with the Pumpkin Patch and the Pepper Patch.
* The Pine Tree Forest is another field without the word "Field" in it, having "Forest" in its name instead.
* This is one of six fields that fireflies can travel to, with the others being Bamboo Field, Cactus Field, Rose Field, Spider Field, and Strawberry Field.
* This is one of the fields that the Stick Bug can move to during the Stick Bug Challenge.
* This field, along with the Sunflower Field and the Pumpkin Patch, is the only field where an object may come to life and shoot sparkles.
* The biggest pineapple will occasionally grow a face, where it will blow bubbles at patches of flowers, producing sparkles (in diamond formation) upon contact. Collecting pollen on the affected flowers can produce tickets, pineapples, moon charm, royal jelly, enzymes, or honey tokens.
* While doing one of Spirit Bear's quests, Spirit Bear states that the Pineapple Patch was originally supposed to be a "Rhubarb Field". Instead, she and Stick Bug planted pineapples.
* This is the only field to be guarded by two mobs of different tiers (Rhino Beetle and Mantis), if you don't include the Werewolf guarding the Pine Tree Forest as it merely happens to be located in the Werewolf's attack territory.
