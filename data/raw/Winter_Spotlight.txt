The Winter Spotlight is a Roblox event that spanned across multiple games. In each game, players had to earn badges, named "Standard Tokens" and "Elite Tokens", to contribute to overarching prizes in the main event hub.
Overview[]
* Completing Bee Bear's first three quests awarded a Standard Token.
* Opening the Elite Gift Box "Gift Boxes") awarded an Elite Token.
