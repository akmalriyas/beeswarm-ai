The Strawberry Field is a field located past the Basic Bee Gate, to the left of the Spider Field, and beside the Vicious Bee Egg Claim. This field is protected by 2 ladybugs; one of which is level 2 and the other level 3.
This field can spawn honey, Strawberry, Red Boost, Red Extract, Bitterberry, Ticket and Royal Jelly tokens while collecting pollen.
Bees that like the Strawberry Field are Fire Bee, Riley Bee, and Shy Bee. Bees that dislike the Strawberry Field are Cool Bee, Bubble Bee, Bucko Bee, and Windy Bee.
This field grants Refreshing Nectar when a planter is placed here.
It is a 22x26 field, containing 572 flowers. The flowers are red and white, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

Trivia[]
* This is the third smallest field in the game, outranked by the Stump Field and Pumpkin Patch.
* There is a strawberry token behind the biggest strawberry in the top left of the field.
* This is one of six fields that fireflies can travel to during nighttime, with the others being Bamboo Field, Cactus Field, Pineapple Patch, Rose Field, and Spider Field.
* Mother Bear states that finding strawberry tokens are much more common in this field than in any other field.
* According to Spirit Bear, the Strawberry in the field were planted from seeds imported from Sweden. She also states that sprouts in this field spawn the most strawberry tokens, 8 times more than normal sprouts.
* If you run towards the strawberry on the corner fast enough, you may glitch inside of the strawberry. If this happens you will have to reset your character, rejoin the game, or use a Whirligig.
