The Strawberry Dispenser is a Dispenser located in the Red HQ. It can be accessed by climbing the ladder inside of the Red HQ, or by jumping on the fence near the Special Sprout Summoner. When used, it grants the player Honey, Haste x5, Red boost x10, and 1 Strawberry for every Red Bee in the player’s Hive.
It has a cooldown of 4 hours.
If the player doesn't have any red bees in their hive, it will not grant any strawberries.
The player must be in the Bee Swarm Simulator Club in order to use this.

Trivia[]
* The only time this dispenser appears in a quest is in Science Bear's _"The Power of Information"_ , in which it requires the player to use this machine 3 times.
* This dispenser gives the same honey per bee as the Blueberry Dispenser.
* This is one of three dispensers that give Treats in the game with the other two being the Treat Dispenser and the Blueberry Dispenser.
* This is one of four dispensers requiring the player to be part of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club with the others being the Honey Dispenser, the Treat Dispenser, and the Blueberry Dispenser.
