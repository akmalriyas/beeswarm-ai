A Robo Pass is an inventory item that was added in the 2022-12-26 update. The player must use one in order to participate in the Robo Bear Challenge. Players can only have a maximum of 10 Robo Passes in their inventory at a time unless purchased from Robux packs or received from quests.
A robo pass can be used by talking to Robo Bear and initiating Round 1 of the Robo Bear Challenge.
Methods of Obtaining[]
* There is a Free Robo Pass Dispenser past the Bear Gate which can be used every 22 hours.
* There is also a Robo Pass Dispenser to the right of the Free Robo Pass Dispenser which gives a Robo Pass for 50 Tickets with no cooldown.
* As a rare drop from Rhino Beetles, Ladybugs, Spiders, Scorpions, Coconut Crab, Tunnel Bear, King Beetle, or Armored Aphids.
* There is a Robo Pass token hidden behind a rock in the Coconut Cave.
* There is a Robo Pass token on the green cog near the Star Hall.
* As a possible reward from the Ant Challenge.
* As a possible drop from Puffshrooms.
* As a stack reward for adding a certain Sticker to the Sticker Stack
* Robot Head Sticker rewards 1 Robo Pass.
* Party Robo Bear Sticker rewards 1 Robo Pass.
Outdated Ways[]
* There was a Robo Pass token hidden behind the Robo Pass Dispensers. It was replaced by a Broken Drive token after Beesmas 2022 ended.
* There was a Robo Pass token hidden inside The Computer during Beesmas 2022.
* There was a Robo Pass token hidden inside the Beesmas Tree during Beesmas 2022.
* Defeating Level 4, 11, and 18 Snowbears.
* As a first time reward from beating specific Robo Party ranks.
* The Digital Bee pack gave 10 Robo Passes + other items.
* The Mondo Robo Bundle gave 25 Robo Passes + other items.
* The Robotic Bundle in Bee Bears Catalog gave 10 Robo Passes, along with other items.
* From certain quests:
* Onett's Beesmas 2022 quest gives 3 Robo Passes.
* Riley Bee's Beesmas 2022 quest gives 1 Robo Pass.
* Bucko Bee's Beesmas 2022 quest gives 1 Robo Pass.
* Stick Bug's Beesmas 2022 quest gives 1 Robo Pass.
* As a reward from certain Gift Boxes:
* The Charcoal Gift Box gave 1 Robo Pass.
* The Obnoxious Gift Box gave 1 Robo Pass.
* The Tropical Gift Box gave 1 Robo Pass.
* From giving Presents in the Beesmas 2022 Event:
* Giving Stick Bug a Present gave 1 Robo Pass.
* Giving Onett a Present gave 1 Robo Pass.
* Giving Robo Bear a Present gave 1 Robo Pass.
* Giving Gummy Bear a Present gave 1 Robo Pass.
* Purchasing the Reindeer Games Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog for 2,000 Snowflakes and 200 Gingerbread Bears gave 10 Robo Passes and other items.

Trivia[]
* The Robo Pass took the longest time to get implemented out of all the items in the game, taking over 3 years after it was initially leaked on April 4, 2019.
* Unlike Ant Passes, a Robo Pass cannot be donated to the Wind Shrine and will not appear when attempting to select an item to donate.
