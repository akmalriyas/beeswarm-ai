The Supreme Saturator is the highest tier sprinkler available for purchase in the Badge Bearer's Guild. To unlock it, the player must obtain 10 different Grandmaster badges. Once the player unlocks it, they can purchase it for ~55.5 billion Honey. Its description reads, “The ultimate sprinkler. Nobody knows how it works or where it came from."
The Supreme Saturator is a single sprinkler. Its radius extends to 16 flower tiles, its rate is 1 second, and its power is 10. This adds up to 8042 pollen per second, outperforming four Diamond Drenchers (1809 pollen per second) by over 340% (4.44x as effective).

Trivia[]
* The shape of The Supreme Saturator resembles the head of a bear.
* The Supreme Saturator is currently the most expensive sprinkler in the game.
* Before the 2021-12-26 update, The Supreme Saturator was the second most expensive item in terms of honey in the game (excluding hive slots), only behind the Gummy Boots.
SDMittens obtaining The Supreme Saturator.
* SDMittens was the first player to buy The Supreme Saturator. She purchased it on September 19, 2018, and an in-game message appeared in every server in order to congratulate her on the achievement. The message said: "Wow! SDMittens has unleashed THE SUPREME SATURATOR on the mountain!"
* This is the only sprinkler capable of completely covering a single field; those being:
* The Stump Field.
* The Mixed, Blue, Red and White Brick Fields.
* Theoretically, it could also cover the Hub Field as well.
