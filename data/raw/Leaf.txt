Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Leaf

Fact: Leaf statistic: _Not to be confused withFlowers or Pollen._.
Leaves are entities that appear in most fields. They work similarly to sparkles and emit leaf particles. To gather leaves, the player needs to collect pollen on the tile it is on, similar to sparkles. When the leaves are gathered, a rustling sound plays, and multiple leaf particles fly out before the token appears. Rewards vary depending on which field the leaves are in but will always be in quantities of 1. For example, leaves will often spawn blueberries in blue fields. Leaves also have a rare chance of spawning an aphid that varies based on field activity, with the chance increasing if a field has been inactive for a while. They can also summon leaf stickers in most fields.
Leaves in the Mountain Top Field are noticeably much rarer than in any other field and will always spawn an aphid or Lyrate Leaf Sticker, and on rarer occasions, a Bitterberry, Neonberry, Magic Bean, Blowing Leaf Sticker or Super Smoothie token. Leaves in the Stump Field and Ant Field cannot spawn aphids, however leaves in the Retro Swarm Challenge can.
