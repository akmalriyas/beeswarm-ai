Frogs are passive mobs added in the 2019-12-23 Update. They despawn after a set period of time (3 minutes if summoned by a Box-O-Frogs or 20 seconds (+2s per level) if summoned by a Tadpole Bee).
Fact: N/A requirement: There are two types of frogs: normal frogs and gifted frogs. Normal frogs can be summoned by collecting a Summon Frog ability token generated by Tadpole Bee or by using a Box-O-Frogs, and the gifted variant can be summoned by a Box-O-Frogs or a Gifted Tadpole Bee with a 10% (+2% each level) chance of summoning it when collecting its Summon Frog ability token. Gifted frogs have a longer tongue range, faster token collection and attack, 50% more attack, a chance to summon Gold Bubbles while hopping, and can hit balloons with their tongue to convert pollen into honey tokens..
Neither players nor frogs can inflict harm on one another, nor can they be harmed by hostile entities.
Abilities[]
* Upon summoning, frogs will hop around the field until they despawn, creating Bubbles on their 2nd, 3rd and 4th hop.
* Frogs can collect Ability Tokens, Honey Tokens and other Tokens (such as rewards from Mobs) for the Player by using their Tongues, which can also create Bubbles.
* Frogs can attack Hostile Mobs (dealing x5 the damage of the attack power of the Tadpole Bee that summoned them or dealing 7 damage) when summoned from a Box-O-Frogs. This attack ignores accuracy checks. [1]

Trivia[]
* Frogs are the only mob in the game that can be summoned by an ability token. It is also the only passive mob that is passive to players, but hostile to harmful mobs.
* Frogs, Cub Buddies, and Windy Bee (with its Tornado Ability) are the only entities that can collect tokens from flowers, leaves, and sprouts for the player.
* Frogs, Rogue Vicious Bee, and Wild Windy Bee are the only mobs that can deal damage to other hostile mobs with attacks.
* However, frogs are the only mob that will deliberately attack other mobs, as Rogue Vicious Bee and Wild Windy Bee will only "attack" other Mobs if they happen to be where they are going to Attack.
* Collecting bubbles from frogs summoned by other players will currently grant pollen to both players and would also add to both players' quest requirements.
* The frog and the Rogue Vicious Bee are the only mobs that can be spawned by using an item, with the items being the Box-O-Frogs and the Night Bell respectively.
* Frogs can sometimes fall outside of the field they are in if it is at a high enough elevation. If this happens, they will hop around uselessly until they despawn.
* At the end of Beesmas 2020, Onett spawned frogs under the name of the "Honeyday-Cleanup Frog Crew".
* At the end of Beesmas 2021, Onett spawned frogs under the name of the "Frog Foundation".
* Frogs are the only mobs capable of spawning bubbles.
* The frog and Rogue Vicious Bee are the only mobs to have a gifted variant.
* Frogs will only spawn facing towards the 30-Bee Zone, or away from the Hive.
* Frogs are the only passive mobs that can be summoned by a Bee.
