Nectar Shower Vial
Nectar Shower Vial
_"Grants 4 hours of all Nectar types to every player in the server!"_
The Nectar Shower Vial is a consumable nectar vial item that was added in the 2024-12-25 update. Once used, it grants 4 hours of all nectar buffs to every player in the server.
When one is used, a message pops up saying  (Username) has popped open a Nectar Shower Vial! for every player in the server.
Ways to Obtain[]
* As a stack reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* The Offline Voucher rewards 1 Nectar Shower Vial.
* Purchasing the Season's Sharing Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog for 600 Snowflakes and 60 Gingerbread Bears grants 1 Nectar Shower Vial and other items.
* Purchasing the Feelin' Festive Pack for 800 Robux grants 5 Nectar Shower Vials and other items.
* Completing Spirit Bear's Galentine Shrine quest in Beesmas 2025 rewards 1 Nectar Shower Vial and other items.
* Completing Dapper Bear's Sticker Style quest rewards 1 Nectar Shower Vial and other items.
Outdated Ways[]
* Purchasing the Royal Winter Wonder Haul (Expired April 30, 2025) for 4,500 Robux granted 10 Nectar Shower Vials and other items.
* Completing Bee Bear's "Cheering Up Gloomy Cub (4/5): Material Things?" rewarded 3 Nectar Shower Vials.
* Completing Bubble Bee Man's "BBM's Naughty List" during Winter Beesmas 2024 rewarded 7 Nectar Shower Vials.
* Purchasing the Cyber Monday Mythic Pack for 1,000 Robux granted 5 Nectar Shower Vials and other items.

Trivia[]
* This item and the Nectar Tester are the only items that grant more than one type of nectar buff.
* This and Snowflake are the only consumable items that directly grant buff(s) to all players in the server.
* This is the only Nectar Vial that grants other players nectar.
