Triangulate
Datamined information:
* How many tokens, majorly Impale, Rain Cloud and Tornado, works internally
* The use cooldown, trial cooldown and trial success rate of every ability
Some of the information were taken from the Ability Token Spreadsheet.
Date of datamined file: December 19th, 2024
Assumptions made: Much of the technical information provided is based on Onett's Discord messages, most of which are dated back to 2018. The assumption is that the information is still accurate to this day.
An array of ability tokens.
Ability tokens are tokens generated by bees, which can be collected to activate special abilities and buffs. Almost all bees spawn ability tokens, with the exceptions being ungifted Basic Bee and Brave Bee without Beequips. If the player doesn't collect a token within a certain amount of time, it fades away. Players can't collect the tokens generated by other players' bees and vice versa, as the tokens are client sided. Prior to the May 7th, 2018 update, other players' tokens were visible, but still couldn't be collected by others.
Some tokens aren't generated by the player's bees (such as honey tokens from flowers). The player can tell apart the two types of tokens by the fact that tokens dropped by bees give off gold sparkles, while tokens generated through other means do not sparkle. Also, tokens dropped by someone else's bees are not visible to the player, which means every visible token is collectible (though there is a bug where some loot tokens cannot be collected, usually at the end of a Honeystorm or Snowstorm).
Details[]
A Looker Bee generating a focus token.
Spawning categories[]
Ability tokens fall into 3 categories, based on when a bee can spawn that token:
* Gather: Ability tokens that can be generated when the bees are collecting pollen. Most ability tokens that can be generated under this situation can collect pollen and/or boost pollen collection.
* Battle: Ability tokens that can be generated when the bees are targeting/attacking mobs. Most ability tokens that can be generated under this situation can inflict damage or debuffs to enemies and boost bees' combat process.
* Idle: Ability tokens that can be generated when the bees are not doing any other activities (gathering, attacking, converting,...)
Some ability tokens fall into multiple or all of the categories above.
Spawning attempts[]
When a bee pauses after making movement (either to gather a flower, to attack, or to simply turn), it will attempt to spawn its ability tokens.
Each ability token has 3 variables that dictate how often the ability token can spawn:
* Use cooldown: The amount of time it takes after the ability token has been spawned for the bee to attempt to summon that ability token again.
* Attempt cooldown: The amount of time a bee has to wait in-between attempts before it can attempt to spawn the ability token again.
* Attempt success rate: The probability that the attempt succeeds. If the attempt succeeds, the ability token is added to a pool that the bee uses to spawn its ability tokens.
When a bee attempts to spawn its ability tokens, it goes through every token ability it can spawn and check if its use cooldown and attempt cooldown has expired. If it has, the bee attempts to spawn the token. If this attempt succeeds, the token is added to a pool. Once every ability has been checked, the bee looks in its pool and picks a random token to spawn, removing that token while keeping every other token in the pool for the next spawn attempt. The spawned token then starts its use cooldown again.
The average time an ability token takes to spawn can be found using the formula:  t i m e = u s e C o o l d o w n + a t t e m p t C o o l d o w n × 1 a t t e m p t S u c c e s s R a t e {attemptSuccessRate}}} .
These are stats which affect the characteristics of ability tokens.
Bee Ability Rate[]
Bee Ability Rate increases or decreases how often bees spawn their ability tokens, by dividing the use cooldown and attempt cooldown, as well as multiplying the attempt success rate. Note that because of this, Bee Ability Rate increases or decreases ability tokens' spawn rate by much more than its stated amount.
For example: a 200% bee ability rate will reduce an ability's use and attempt cooldown to 50% of its original value (100% ÷ 200%), and increase its success rate by 100% (or double it).
Ability Token Lifespan[]
Ability Token Lifespan extends the duration of uncollected ability tokens generated by bees before they despawn. It can be boosted by a Gifted Stubborn Bee and Cog Amulet and by the Paper Angel and Paperclip beequips, and also temporarily by adding the Drooping Stubborn Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack. The maximum amount currently possible is 145%.
Ability Duplication Chance[]
An ability token being duplicated and that token being collected
When there is a chance, ability tokens will spawn its duplicated/duped variants randomly on a field. Duped tokens hover over the field and last for double the tokens' lifespan, and that lifespan is further increased by 5% per Digital Bee's level. Duped tokens can be collected/activated by standing beneath it for 1 second.
Ability duplication chance can be increased by field corruption from Digital Bee's Glitch and Map Corruption ability tokens. It also can be increased from Gifted Digital Bee. Other than from Digital Bees, ability duplication chance can also be increased by the 'Corrupt' upgrade purchasable from the Robo Bear Challenge and only applies during that challenge. It can also be provided by Science Bear during his "Corrupting The Glitched Drive" quest, where he corrupts the Clover, Spider, Pineapple, Rose, Pine Tree and Mountain Top fields at random.
Bee Movespeed[]
Bee Movespeed affects how fast bees move and perform actions.
While not directly related to ability tokens, having more Bee Movespeed allows bees to make more attempts at creating Ability Tokens, and vice versa. This overall increases or decreases the average spawn rate of an ability token.
Bee Ability Tokens[]
The Red Bomb (left), Blue Bomb (middle), and Buzz Bomb(right) tokens.
Bomb collects pollen from flowers in an area.
Split by the types of flowers the ability collects, there are 3 categories:
* Buzz Bombs collect 7 pollen from all types of flowers.
* Red Bombs collect 10 pollen from only Red flowers.
* Blue Bombs collect 10 pollen from only Blue flowers.
The amount of pollen collected increases by 10% per level (e.g., 200% on a level 20 bee).
Split by the range of the ability, there are 2 categories:
* Bomb tokens collect from 13 surrounding flowers.
* Bomb+ tokens collect from 29 surrounding flowers.
Collecting Bomb tokens also grants the Bomb Combo buff, which grants a buff to System Page#Bomb Power.
Many bees have at least one of the bomb variants:
* Red Bomb is granted by Rascal, Demon, and Shy Bees.
* Red Bomb+ is granted by Fire, Demon, and Festive Bees.
* Blue Bomb is granted by Bumble, Bubble, and Buoyant Bees.
* Blue Bomb+ is granted by Frosty, Ninja, and Vicious Bees.
* Buzz Bomb is granted by Bomber, Commander, and Exhausted Bees.
* Buzz Bomb+ is granted by Demo, Lion, and Fuzzy Bees.
There are also other sources of Bomb tokens:
* Balloons during a Surprise Party have a chance to generate Blue and a Buzz Bomb tokens.
* Many Beequips can give the bee that equipped it Bomb abilities, if the beequip has them.
* Bang Snaps can give Bomb and Bomb+ of any color.
* Smiley Stickers can give Blue Bomb.
* Bottle Caps can give Buzz Bomb.
If Red Bomb Sync is on, all Red Bombs collect white pollen. If Blue Bomb Sync is on, all Blue Bombs collect white pollen. If both Syncs are active, all Bombs collect any color of pollen.
Fact: Bee Ability Tokens[] statistic: When Pollen Haze is active, all Bomb ability tokens are automatically collected, and they may also pollinate flower patches that they collect from..
Bomb falls into the Gather category, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers.
Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Bomb has a use cooldown of 8 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 4 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 25%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 20 seconds.
The token lasts for 4 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

The Red Boost (left) and Blue Boost (right) tokens.
The White Boost token.

* Many Beequips can give the bee that equipped it Boost abilities, if the beequip has them.

Token Link[]
The Token Link token.
A Token Link collects all other ability tokens (except for other Token Links) and grants 25 honey and an additional 10 honey per level for each other token collected this way (e.g. 175 honey per token collected on level 15 bee).
For stacking purposes, note that the ability tokens are collected based on how early they were spawned. If two or more link tokens are collected at the same time, they may link the same tokens. The other tokens activate once, but the player gets two sets of honey from link tokens.
A Token Link can also collect Honey Gift tokens generated by Honey Bee, Diamond Bee, honey tokens from Vicious Bee's Impale, treat tokens from Puppy Bee's Fetch ability, tokens from Festive Bee's Festive Gift ability, tokens from Cub Buddy gifts, and tokens from mobs. However, it does not collect honey tokens generated by Coin Scatter, Gummy Star or Target Practice.
Exhausted, Rage, Shocked, and Music bees have the ability. Other sources of Token Link tokens include:
* Balloons during a Surprise Party have a chance to generate Token Link tokens.
* Paper Angels, Paperclips and Bead Lizards can give the bee that equipped it the ability, if the beequip has it.
Token Link falls into the Gather and Battle categories, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers and while attacking mobs.
Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Token Link has a use cooldown of 5 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 5 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 33.33%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 15 seconds.
The token lasts for 4 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

Bear Morph[]
The Bear Morph tokens (first and third row); players with a Bear Morph active (Black Bear , Brown Bear , Panda Bear , Polar Bear , Science Bear , and Mother Bear).
Bear Morph is an ability exclusive to Bear Bee.
When collected, the player is granted the Bear Morph buff, which transforms the player into the bear on the token (either Black Bear, Brown Bear, Panda Bear or Polar Bear). This gives the player a buff in pollen gain, and sets the player's base movement speed and base jump power to a higher value than normal.

* Mother Bear Morph grants more pollen than the base buff, as well as extra bee gather pollen.

Impale[]
The Impale token.
Impale is an ability exclusive to Vicious Bee.
Vicious Bee generates an Impale token that summons a number of spikes equal to the bee's level, that damages enemies for 5% of their current health and increases with Vicious Bee's attack, but the damage is reduced past 1,000. Mobs that have been hit by a spike take less damage from other consecutive spikes.
Fact: Impale[] requirement: Each enemy hit by a spike generates a honey token worth 50 (+50 per level) honey. These honey tokens can be collected by Token Link and take 10 seconds to fade..
Stick Nymphs being attacked by a Gifted Vicious Bee's impale.
If Vicious Bee is Gifted, gold spikes spawn instead of normal ones. Impale will have +1 levels of accuracy added (meaning Vicious Bee's spikes can hit mobs that are one level higher than it without missing).
A more detailed explanation of the ability is provided below:
* Impale first targets every living enemy in a 75-stud radius of the player.
* If it finds a group of targets, it summons a spike at the nearest target's position, then waits up to 0.2 seconds before summoning another at the next target. This is repeated until every target in the group has been damaged once.
* It then prepares a number of spikes equal to the level of the Vicious Bee that activated the ability.
* If Impale has not run out of spikes yet, it will target every living enemy in a 75-stud radius of the player again. This is repeated until it runs out of spikes, or there are no more living targets in the radius.
* The number of times an enemy has been targetted is stored, henceforth referred to in formulas as _cnt_.
* The summoned spikes' accuracy work the same way as bee attacks, just with 1 extra level if Vicious Bee is gifted.
* The damage dealt is calculated in this order:
* Let _atk_ be Vicious Bee's attack at the time of activation. Then  b a s e D a m a g e = 2 ∗ a t k  .
* If the target has the Humanoid tag (as of the current update, every enemy has this tag), adds 5% of target's HP to _baseDamage_.
* If  b a s e D a m a g e > 1000  , excess damage is square rooted:  b a s e D a m a g e = 1000 + b a s e D a m a g e − 1000 }}
* The damage then is nerfed by the number of times the enemy has been targetted:  b a s e D a m a g e = b a s e D a m a g e ∗ 0.05 + b a s e D a m a g e c n t ∗ 0.95 {cnt}}*0.95} .
* Critical hits and Super-Crits are then applied, along with any Impale damage multiplier, resulting in the variable _finalDamage_.
* If a critical hit or Super-Crit occured, the damage is nerfed to 75%:  f i n a l D a m a g e = ⌊ f i n a l D a m a g e ∗ 0.75 + 0.5 ⌋ \right\rfloor }
* The enemy is dealt _finalDamage_ damage.
Impale falls into the Battle category, meaning it spawns while attacking mobs.
Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Impale has a use cooldown of 30 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 1 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 100%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 30 seconds.
The token lasts for 24 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

Tornado is an ability exclusive to Windy Bee.
When collected, all of the player's Haste stacks are taken to summon a tornado at the position of the token, assuming the token is on a field. The tornado moves around the field while dealing damage to mobs, collecting pollen and tokens, and popping bubbles and fuzz bombs, around a 5-tile-radius range (approximately 49 surrounding flower tiles) every second. It will also collect pollen out of balloons it passes through.

Glitch[]
The Glitch token.
Glitch is an ability exclusive to Digital Bee.
When collected, the field the player is currently in is corrupted for 20 seconds (+1s per level). A glitch effect will occasionally appear on the field (which can be disabled in the System Page), every ability token collected in that field has a chance to be duplicated or "Duped", and Digital Bee will be able to spawn ☺︎ tokens.
The corruption on the field can be increased to raise the ability duplication chance and the pollen collection and instant conversion of duped abilities. The corruption can be increased by enhancing Digital Bee with Drives. Colored Drives increase the corruption of field with matching flowers while Glitched Drives increase corruption of all fields.
Glitch falls into the Gather and Battle categories, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers and while attacking mobs.
Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Glitch has a use cooldown of 60 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 10 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 33.33%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 80 seconds.
The token lasts for 12 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

Map Corruption[]
The Map Corruption token.
Map Corruption is an ability exclusive to Gifted Digital Bee.
When collected, a random field is corrupted by a small amount for 3 minutes (+15s per level) for all players in the server.
Once the token is collected, a server-wide message announces:  ☺︎ {Username}'s Digital Bee corrupted the [Field Name] ☺︎
The amount of corruption from this can be increased by enhancing Digital Bee with Drives. There are also special effects based on the most recent drive used to enhance Digital Bee:
* If the most recent drive is a colored drive, Map Corruption is more likely to corrupt the field with more flowers matching the color of that drive
* If the most recent drive is Glitched Drive, the field it chooses will be completely random but gains 25% more corruption.
Map Corruption falls into the Gather category, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers.
Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Map Corruption has a use cooldown of 360 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 15 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 12.5%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 465 seconds.
The token lasts for 24 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

Mind Hack[]
The Mind Hack token.
Mind Hack is an ability exclusive to Digital Bee.
When collected, the minds of up to 3 nearby enemies (+1 per 3 lvls) are "hacked", stunning them for 3 seconds (+0.1s per lvl). While hacked, the enemies take 25% more damage (+0.1% per Glitched Drive Digital Bee has been enhanced with). Hacking is only half as effective against bosses.
This token also spawns a ☺︎ token if collected inside the field.
Mind Hack falls into the Battle category, meaning it spawns while attacking mobs.
Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Mind Hack has a use cooldown of 90 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 2 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 33.33%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 94 seconds.
The token lasts for 16 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.

The ☺ token.
☺ (otherwise known as Smile Tokens) is an ability exclusive to Digital Bee.
The token can only be generated as a duped token, while inside a corrupted field. When collected, they temporarily:
* increase field corruption and activate all duped tokens,
* hack a nearby non-boss enemy (with a similar effect as Mind Hack),
* and collect and double pollen equal to 50% of Digital Bee's gather amount (+50% pollen multiplier per duped token activated) in a "☺", "&", "*", "~" or "?" arrangement of flowers, with 50% of the pollen being instantly converted.
* The flowers collected by this are also stamped for 10 seconds, causing them to yield x2 pollen.
The frequency for this token to be generated increases with the number of Glitched Drives Digital Bee has been enhanced with. While exact stats for the token are unknown, some information can be inferred from update patch notes:
* The average base delay between Smile Tokens is 22 seconds.
* At maxed Glitched Drives, this average is 14 seconds.
* The first Smile Token will always be created after half the average of the base delay.
☺ tokens take roughly 4 seconds to fade away.
Beequip-Exclusive Ability Tokens[]
Snowglobe Shake[]
The Snowglobe Shake token.
Snowglobe Shake is an ability exclusive to bees that have the Snowglobe beequip equipped.
When collected, they give all players in the server the Cool Breeze for 20 seconds, granting a plethora of stats.
The same buff can also be obtained from using Snowflakes during the Beesmas event, which add 10 seconds of Cool Breeze per use.
Snowglobe Shake falls into the Gather, Battle, and Idle categories, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers, while attacking mobs, and while idling.
Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Snowglobe Shake has a use cooldown of 300 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 30 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 16.67%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 450 seconds.
The token lasts for 8 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.
Festive Mark[]
The Festive Mark token.
A Festive Mark on a field.
Festive Mark is an ability exclusive to Festive Bees that have the Festive Wreath beequip equipped.
Fact: Festive Mark[] requirement: When collected, a Festive Mark is deployed in a random area in the field that the player is standing in for 12 seconds (+0.5s per level), with a ~22-tile radius. Standing within its range grants the Festive Mark buff, which gives extra Honey Per Pollen, Bomb Pollen, Red Bomb Pollen, Instant Bomb Conversion, and Conversion Links..
Unlike other marks, Festive Mark also grants boosts to other players and its range can be seen by other players. It also can link with other players' marks. However, the Festive Mark buff cannot be stacked even if two festive marks overlap each other.
When Festive Mark is active, it summons random gifts within its range that can be collected by any player and flashes colors cycling through red, blue, green and yellow at the top of the pole. Rarer gifts appear more likely with bee level and certain gifts are only obtainable when Festive Bee is gifted. Every 4th token generated by the festive mark is always a Red Boost token.
The gifts include:
* Red Boost Tokens.
* Strawberries.
* Pineapples.
* Blueberries.
* Sunflower Seeds.
* Royal Jelly.
* Gumdrops.
* Star Jelly (Rare).
* Magic Bean (Rare).
* Blue Extract (Rare).
* Red Extract (Rare).
* Glue (Rare).
* Oil (Rare).
* Glitter (Rare).
* Enzymes (Rare).
* Neonberry (Rare).
* Field Dice (Rare).
* Micro-Converter (Rare).
* Gingerbread Bear (Very rare).
* Festive Bean (Exceptionally rare).
* Flying Festive Bee Sticker (Beesmas only, Unbelievably rare in red fields, odds increase with bee's level)
Festive Mark falls into the Gather category, meaning it spawns while gathering from flowers.
Without any Bee Ability Rate modifiers, Festive Mark has a use cooldown of 300 seconds, an attempt cooldown of 30 seconds, and an attempt success rate of 16.67%. This give an average spawn rate of 1 token every 450 seconds.
The token lasts for 16 seconds before despawning, without any Ability Token Lifespan modifiers.
Reindeer Fetch[]
The Reindeer Antlers beequip, when equipped to Puppy Bee, enhances its Fetch ability into Reindeer Fetch.
Puppy's ball glows bright red and emits white sparkles, and every combo grants the player the Reindeer Guidance buff, which grants extra capacity.
A Gingerbread Bear and 512 Treats combo.
Additionally, the ball has a small chance to spawn 1 Gingerbread Bear at high combos (max 1 per day). While 256 treats is normally the highest amount from a combo, it's possible to get to 512 treats if the gingerbread bear spawns in the middle of the combo progression.
