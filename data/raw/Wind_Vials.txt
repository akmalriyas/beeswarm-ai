Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Wind Vials

Wind Vials
_"Summons a Cloud in the field you're standing in. Lasts for 3 minutes."_
The Cloud Vial is a consumable inventory item that was added in the 2019-09-28 update. Once used, it will spawn a cloud in the field you are standing in.
The player can have a maximum of 10 in their inventory at a time. Obtaining more than 10 will not be added into the inventory unless obtained from a quest, Sticker Stack reward, or Ornament Presents.
The summoned cloud will move around the field. It grants a 25% (50% with Gifted Windy Bee) pollen boost if the player is under it, while causing flowers under the cloud and sprouts in the field to grow faster. If there is a sprout in the field, the clouds will help the sprout grow by connecting to the sprout with a straight blue line. The growing speed applied to the sprout increases with the pollen amount required for the sprout. Using it on a field with a planter causes the planter to grow 8% faster per cloud, capping at 20%. Each field has a limit of clouds, with bigger and higher-tier fields having a higher limit. If the limit for clouds has reached, using anymore clouds will result in the following message:  There are too many clouds in this field. Windy Bee's Rain Cloud ability will ignore this field and will not further summon anymore clouds into the field.
It can't be used in the Ant Field, attempting to do so will result in the following message:  You can't make clouds in a challenge field.
It also can't be used in the Hub Field, attempting to do so will result in the message:  You can't make clouds in the Hub Field.
Donating a Cloud Vial to the Wind Shrine after having donated a Spirit Petal will give the player a chance to obtain a Windy Bee Egg if they do not already own a Windy Bee.

Ways to Obtain[]
* A reward from some of Spirit Bear's and Bee Bear's quests.
* As a very common drop from Wild Windy Bee.
* A rare reward from Extreme Memory Match.
* Redeeming certain valid codes:
* "BeesBuzz123" (Gives 1 Cloud Vial \+ other items).
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* Yellow Umbrella rewards 1 Cloud Vial.
* Simple Cloud rewards 1 Cloud Vial.
* Grey Raining Cloud rewards 3 Cloud Vials.
* Tornado rewards 3 Cloud Vials.
* Blowing Leaf rewards 3 Cloud Vials.
* Purchasing the Winter Winds Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog gives you 3 Cloud Vials and other items.
The methods below are no longer possible.[]
* Completing Spirit Bear's Ornament Quest rewarded 10 Cloud Vials and other items.
* Opening the Pearly Present gave 30 Cloud Vials and other items.
* Purchasing packs in the Robux Shop:
* The Beginner's Bean Bundle gave 5 Cloud Vials and other items.
* The Star Treat Special gave 10 Cloud Vials and other items.
* Redeeming certain codes:
* Gumaden10T (Gave 1 Cloud Vial \+ other stuff).
* "1MLikes" (Gives 1 Cloud Vial \+ other stuff).
* ”ThnxCyasToyBox" (Gives 1 Cloud Vial \+ other stuff)
* As a reward from some of Sun Bear's quests.
* Purchasing the Refreshing Bundle in the Bee Bear's Catalog gives you 20 Cloud Vials and other items
* Completing some of Bee Bear's quests.
Token Locations[]
* In the Windy Bee Gate, to the right of the Wind Shrine.

Trivia[]
* Cloud Vials do not spawn a Wild Windy Bee. Instead, if a Spirit Petal isn't donated yet, it will summon clouds. To summon a Wild Windy Bee, the player would need to donate items to the Wind Shrine.
* Cloud Vials are the only items that can directly help grow sprouts.
* Clouds, sprinklers and bubbles are the only ways to regenerate Flowers without them naturally regenerating.
* With high enough jump power, collecting the Cloud Vial token past the Windy Bee Gate is possible without 35 bees. Jumping from the roof of the Red HQ to the area where the token is makes it possible.
