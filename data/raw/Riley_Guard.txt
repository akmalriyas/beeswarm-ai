Trivia[]
* Before the 2018-11-25 update, the Riley Guard costed 60,000,000 honey and no crafting materials.
* This is the highest-tiered guard that can be obtained from the Red HQ.
* This guard’s blue counterpart is the Bucko Guard.
* This guard represents Riley Bee.
* If a player were to craft the ingredients needed to craft this item, it would cost 30,000,000 honey, 1,350 strawberries, 750 pineapples, 750 blueberries, and 150 royal jellies.
