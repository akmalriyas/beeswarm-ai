The Treat Dispenser is a dispenser located between the Pineapple Patch and the entrance of the Pro Shop. Members of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club can use it to collect Honey, Treats, 1 Pineapple, and x5 Haste every hour. The number of Treats varies, depending on the number of bees the player has.
If a player tries to use the Treat Dispenser without being in the group, they will get a prompt saying they must join the Bee Swarm Simulator Club.
Honey and Treats reward amounts[]
The amount of honey and treats given is based on the number of bees the player has in their hive. More specifically, let _cnt_ be the number of bees in the player's hive:
Fact: Honey and Treats reward amounts[] requirement: * The amount of honey the player receives is equal to  ⌊ c n t 1.5 + 0.5 ⌋ × 50 ^{1.5}+0.5\right\rfloor \times 50} , or 500 if the result is less than 500..
* The amount of treats the player receives is equal to  ⌊ c n t 2.5 100 + 0.5 ⌋ ^{2.5}}{100}}+0.5\right\rfloor } , or 10 if the result is less than 10.

Trivia[]
* This is one of three dispensers that give Treats in the game with the other two being the Blueberry Dispenser and the Strawberry Dispenser.
* This is the only dispenser to give two different treats, being Treats and Pineapples.
* This is one of four dispensers requiring the player to be part of the Bee Swarm Simulator Club with the others being the Honey Dispenser, the Blueberry Dispenser, and the Strawberry Dispenser.
