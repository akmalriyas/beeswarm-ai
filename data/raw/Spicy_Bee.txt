Abilities[]
* [Inferno] Summons a ring of 6 flames, then summons 2 Fire Bees that last for 15s (+1s per Level) with levels equal to 2 less than this bee's level. If Gifted, each Fire Bee has a 10% (+1% per Level) chance of being Gifted as well. However, the gifted Fire Bees from inferno DO NOT grant their gifted hive bonus.
* [Rage] Grants your bees +1 attack for 45s. Stacks up to 4 times.
Fact: Abilities[] requirement: * **[🌟Gifted Ability: Flame Fuel]** For 15 seconds, Spicy Bee tosses oil into any flames that spawn, causing them to last 50% longer and instantly convert 50k pollen and (10 + 5 per Field Boost stack)% of your Hive's Convert Amount. Unless converted to a dark flame, Spicy Bee won't throw oil into flames currently on the field..
* [Passive: Steam Engine] This bee’s Movespeed and gather speed drastically increases up to 100% with Flame Heat.

Trivia[]
* Spicy Bee, Tadpole Bee, Fuzzy Bee, Buoyant Bee and Digital Bee all have four different Abilities, the most for any bee.
* This bee and Rage Bee are the only bees with the Rage Ability.
* Spicy Bee’s gifted color scheme resembles Rage Bee’s gifted and Rage Aphids' color scheme.
* This bee and Tadpole Bee are the only Bees who can summon other entities.
* This is the only bee that can summon other bees.
* This bee was originally going to be themed like a train, hence the Steam Engine passive ability, train sounds the tokens make and exhaust pipes on the bee's body.[1]
* While Steam Engine is active, Spicy Bee will emit a continuous trail of steam.
* This bee, along with Vector Bee, Buoyant Bee, and Precise bee emits light when gifted.
* Unlike other gifted bees, the star on top of a gifted Spicy Bee is a different color to the star on its side, with a green star on the top and 2 black ones on the side.
* This is the only Mythic bee to have completely whole numbered base stats.
* The Fire Bees summoned by its Inferno ability cannot convert pollen in the player's hive.
* At launch, this bee was improperly balanced because of a typo. It was supposed to have each flame converting 1% of the player’s total conversion, but instead it converted 1% of their capacity. X-Flame converted 29% of the player's capacity (Demon Mask was required). It was quickly noticed and nerfed to the regular stats it has.
* When gifted, Spicy Bee's exhaust pipes emit more steam.
* Spicy Bee is the only bee to like one field.
* If Spicy Bee is level 1 or 2, the Fire Bees will be level 1 instead of two levels below. This is because there are no levels that go below 1.
