The All-Time Top Riley Bee Helpers is one of the 64 leaderboards in the game. It displays the top 100 people who've completed the most quests from Gifted Riley Bee. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at one time. If the player stands on the white circle in front of it, they can see how many Gifted Riley Bee quests they've completed.
Location[]
It is located on the roof of the Red HQ next to Gifted Riley Bee.
