How to Program BASIC
The How to Program BASIC is a Roblox cosmetic hat that was able to be obtained by solving all the puzzles from The Computer. It was available starting from November 23, 2020 through the Ready Player Two event. As it is a Roblox hat, players can wear it on their avatar. When the player solves the puzzles, the hat will appear in the center inside the computer. However, if they leave the game, it won't appear when they rejoin.
The How to Program BASIC's texture.
Like the Flight of the Bumble Egg and Swarming Egg of the Hive accessories, the How to Program BASIC does not give any special boosts.
