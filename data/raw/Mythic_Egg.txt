Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Mythic Egg

Mythic Egg
Eggs available inside the Robux Shop.
Eggs are a type of inventory item where the player drags into their hive to hatch a bee, can be donated to the Wind Shrine or are used to roll the Sticker Printer. They are also the items that help players start playing in the game. Eggs come in many different types, some offering chances at different rarities of bees, and some hatching a specific bee. Currently, a total of 6 (or 11 if counting the gifted variants) different types of eggs exist in the game, not including Plastic Eggs, specific bee eggs, the Flight of the Bumble Egg, or the Swarming Egg of the Hive.
Ever since the 2019-09-28 update, players do not need a vacant hive slot to hatch an egg. Now, players can hatch an egg on top of an existing bee to replace it. The level of the bee remains the same, acting like a Royal Jelly.
Please also note that due to regulations in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands, Belgium, and Australia, any eggs in the Robux Shop won't be available to purchase for British, Dutch, Belgian, and Australian consumers. This restriction applies to most of the eggs in the shop.

Basic Egg
The Basic Egg is the most common type of egg found in the game. It will almost always hatch into a Basic Bee (87% chance), but in rare cases, it can hatch into a Rare Bee (10% chance), an Epic Bee (2.5% chance), a Legendary Bee (0.5% chance), or into a Gifted Bee (0.4% chance), with a 0.348% (1 out of 287.35632184) chance of getting a Gifted Basic Bee from the egg. Mythic bees and Event bees cannot be hatched from this egg. This egg cannot be used on a Basic Bee.
_Ways to obtain a Basic Egg:_
* First-time players receive a free Basic Egg in their inventory.
* Purchasing from the Basic Egg Shop located near the Sunflower Field for a base price of 1,000 Honey and capping at 10,000,000 Honey. Every time the player purchases a Basic Egg, the price of the next Basic Egg scales up.
* Adding the Round Basic Bee Sticker to the Sticker Stack rewards 1 Basic Egg.

Star Egg
The Star Egg is a type of egg that hatches into any type of gifted bee that is not already in the player's hive, aside from gifted Event bees. If the player already has all the different types of gifted bees in their hive besides gifted event bees, the egg will hatch into a random type of gifted bee instead. The egg will never hatch a gifted mythic bee until all other gifted non-event bees are in the hive.
_Ways to obtain a Star Egg:_
* Defeating Tunnel Bear for the first time guarantees any type of gifted egg, including a star egg, but with lower chances of obtaining upon subsequent defeats.
* Purchasing from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux each (limited to 5 eggs per player).

* Completing Panda Bear's first ten star treat/ant quests ("Royal Rumble" quest).
* An exceptionally rare chance to drop upon defeating the King Beetle.

Specific Event Bee Eggs[]

After an Event bee egg is hatched, the player will receive an exclusive type of permanent royal jelly for that specific Event bee. The Event bee royal jelly will turn any bee into that kind of Event bee. It cannot be applied if the player currently has that Event bee in their hive though. And instead of hatching into a random bee of a particular rarity, specific event bee eggs will always hatch into the egg's respective event bee. Trivia[]
* The Digital Bee, Vicious Bee, Windy Bee, Bear Bee and Gummy Bee Eggs are the only types of Event bee eggs that could or can be obtained without spending Robux or Tickets. (A Bear Bee Egg can be obtained by redeeming a Bear Bee Voucher.)
* Star Eggs and Basic Eggs are the only two eggs that can hatch into a Basic Bee(Gifted variant if Star Egg)
* Plastic Eggs, the Flight of the Bumble Egg (Egg Hunt 2019), and the Swarming Egg of the Hive (Egg Hunt 2020) are the only types of eggs that do not hatch into a bee.
* Any kind of specific bee egg and event bee egg will have the same icon as a Basic Egg in the inventory.
* The Bear Bee, Festive Bee, Puppy Bee, Windy Bee, and Digital Bee Eggs are the only type of Event bee eggs that can or could be obtained directly by spending robux. However, as of now, Bear Bee is the only event bee that can be obtained directly by Robux
* Basic Eggs are the only type of eggs that can be obtained directly with honey.
* The star that appears on the Star Egg is slightly larger than the stars on the other types of gifted eggs.
* The Mythic Egg is the only type of egg with the name of the bee rarity that it hatches into.
* The Mythic Egg is also the only egg that has a 100% of hatching into a certain bee rarity (except for Event bee eggs and specific bee eggs). However, before mythic bees were added, Diamond Eggs used to have a 100% chance of hatching into a Legendary bee.
* The Diamond and Basic Eggs are the only non-specific or event eggs that can hatch into a bee of their name (the Diamond and Basic Bees, respectively), though you can get other bees from these eggs.
* The Mythic Egg is also the only egg with a letter on it.
* The Choose-a-Mythic Egg is the only egg that allows the player to choose which bee hatches.
* The Fuzzy Bee, Buoyant Bee, and the Precise Bee Eggs are the only (non-event) specific bee eggs that could have been bought from the Robux Shop.
* The Gifted Silver Egg and the Star Egg are the only Gifted eggs that can be obtained by completing the main questlines from NPCs (Black Bear's "Getting Stumped", Mother Bear's "Seven To Seven", and Panda Bear's "Royal Rumble").
* The player cannot hatch a specific bee egg on that specific bee. For example, a Bumble Bee Egg cannot be hatched on a Bumble Bee.
* Even though Basic Eggs don't guarantee a Basic Bee, they still can't be used on Basic Bees.
* The Basic Egg and Choose-A-Mythic Egg are the only eggs without gifted variants of themselves, excluding specific bee eggs.
* Similarly, the Star Egg is the only gifted egg without a regular variant.
* The Bubble Bee Egg is the only non-Event specific bee egg that can be obtained from a quest (B.B.M's Naughty List).
* The Tadpole Bee Egg is the only egg that can be obtained by giving an NPC present (obtained specifically from Bubble Bee Man).
