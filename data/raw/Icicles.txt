Trivia[]
* Briefly after Beesmas 2020 ended, all Beesmas beequips were made unobtainable with the exception of Icicles from Rogue Vicious Bee, which were accidentally left behind. This was quickly fixed, however.
* This makes the Icicles the first Beequip obtainable outside of Beesmas with the Poinsettia being the second.
* Previously it used to have a max cap of 3, but this was changed in an unknown update to 2 only, however anyone who had 3 equipped already keeps them equipped.
