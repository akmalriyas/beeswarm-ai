The Petal Wand is a tool added in the 2019-09-28 update that can be crafted in the Petal Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 1.5B Honey.
10 Star Jellies
Fact: 10 Star Jellies requirement: 25 Glitter.
Fact: 10 Star Jellies requirement: 75 Enzymes.
1 Spirit Petal
It collects 6 pollen from 37 patches in 0.7 seconds, and boosts pollen collected by 100%. It has an average base pollen collection rate of 634.29 pollen per second. It emits a yellow particle effect at the top of the tool, similar to the Petal Belt.
Fact: 1 Spirit Petal requirement: Every 3rd swing, it fires a Petal Shuriken, which causes bees to instantly convert pollen if it touches their hitbox. The shuriken instantly converts 10,000 + 6% of the bee's convert amount in pollen to honey + an additional 6% per field boost stack of the field you're standing in. Shurikens can also pop bubbles and fuzz bombs..
In the 2026-01-16 update, shurikens were made to cause blooms that were hit to spawn a petal, dealing damage (20% remaining hp + 5% max hp) to the bloom as well as spawning a random petal from it.

Trivia[]
* Excluding Honey & Spirit Petals, if the ingredients were to be crafted in the Blender, then it would require 1,750 Royal Jellies, 1,375 Moon Charms, 55 Magic Beans and 3,750 Pineapples. If one were to buy the royal jelly required for it, that would be 1.75 billion honey.
* The Petal Wand can launch its shurikens even while the player isn't in a field.
* This can be used to spend less time at the hive converting pollen.
* The Petal Wand is the only tool to be held by a bear (Onett holds a tool but he's not a bear), specifically Spirit Bear.
* The version held by Spirit Bear is much bigger than a player's.
* After the shuriken comes in contact with one of the player's bees, the sound effect changes. This also increases in pitch with every consecutive bee.
