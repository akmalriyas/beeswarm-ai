Marshmallow Bee
Fact: Marshmallow Bee statistic: _"+50% White Pollen, +50% Capacity, and +250% Conversion Rate _[sic]_ for 30 minutes."_.
The Marshmallow Bee buff icon.
The Marshmallow Bee is an inventory item that was added during the Egg Hunt 2019 event. It has a capacity limit of 25, which can only be exceeded by code and quest rewards. Collecting 3 Marshmallow Bees and handing them in to the Egg Hunt Info gave the player the Flight of the Bumble Egg and a badge during the Egg Hunt 2019 Event.
When used, it grants the following buffs for 30 minutes:

Fact: Marshmallow Bee statistic: * +50% Capacity..
* +250% Convert Rate.

Trivia[]
* This is the only item to use the word "eat" instead of "use" in the pop-up when double clicked in the inventory.
* This is one of the four consumables that increase capacity, the others being the Purple Potion, Super Smoothie and Snowflakes.
* There are sugar crystals on the Marshmallow Bee, something most real life marshmallows don't have. This may have taken influence from/be a reference to the brand Peeps, as they are also animal-shaped marshmallows coated in sugar, and are popular during Easter.
* Before the April 6th, 2020 update, there was no capacity limit on how many Marshmallow Bees could be stored in the player's inventory. This was likely because the player could only obtain a limited amount of Marshmallow Bees prior to this update.
* Marshmallow bees are one of the few consumable buffs that won't be overridden by a Super Smoothie buff. The others being Snowflakes, Stingers, Jelly Beans and Glitter.
* Marshmallow Bee is the only bee that the player can't have in their hive.
