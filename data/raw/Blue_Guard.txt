> "_A durable pad worn on the right shoulder of Blue Beekeepers._ "
The Blue Guard is a guard purchasable in the Blue HQ. It is equipped on the right shoulder.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 1,000,000 Honey.
50 Blueberries
Fact: 50 Blueberries requirement: 1 Royal Jelly.
Fact: 50 Blueberries requirement: 3 Moon Charms.
This guard is one of 3 guards in the Blue HQ, the others being the Elite Blue Guard, and the Bucko Guard.

* This guard’s red counterpart is the Red Guard.
* It is the cheapest guard in the Blue HQ.
