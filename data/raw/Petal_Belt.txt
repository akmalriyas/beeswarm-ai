Trivia[]
* If the ingredients to craft this item were to be crafted, a total of 15,000,000,000 Honey, 1 Spirit Petal, 61,875 Strawberries, 61,875 Blueberries, 77,500 Pineapples, 125 Magic Beans and 6,625 Royal Jellies are needed.
* This is the 9th most expensive item in terms of honey (excluding hive slots).
* This item emits a yellow particle effect in the center of the belt, similarly to the Petal Wand.
* This is one of the three belt accessories that were not obtainable from Sun Bear.
* This is one of many accessories to be worn by an NPC, following the Mondo Belt Bag and the Beekeeper's Boots (both worn by Sun Bear and Onett), and the Porcelain Port-O-Hive, Riley and Bucko guards and also the Beekeeper's mask worn by Onett.
* The Petal Belt worn by Spirit Bear is much bigger than the one worn by a player.
* The Petal Shurikens can pop bubbles, fuzz bombs, and also spawn a petal when they hit blooms.
* This is the only belt on display which doesn't have a hitbox.
* This is one of two belts to have a passive, the other being the Coconut Belt.
* Although the Petal Belt's passive in-game states that a Petal Shuriken converts 10,000 pollen +7% of the bee's convert amount, Onett has stated in the change log during the 2024-05-23 update that it was changed to its current state.
