Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Snowglobe

Snowglobe
"Keep a little ball of winter wonder with you all year long."
Fact: Snowglobe statistic: +Bomb Pollen%.
Fact: Snowglobe statistic: +Ability Rate%.
Fact: Snowglobe statistic: +Ability: Snowglobe Shake.
Fact: Snowglobe statistic: +Bomb Pollen%.
A Snowglobe is a level 7 Beesmas Beequip. It has an equip limit of two. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to Basic, Bumble, Cool, Bucko, Frosty, or Windy bees.

Ways to Obtain[]
* As a drop from Mondo Chick (rare).
* By purchasing the Super Snowman Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
Outdated Ways[]
* Purchasing the Beequip bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog gives 1 Snowglobe for the cost of 75 snowflakes and 15 gingerbread bears \+ other items.
* Dropped upon defeating a level 10 Snowbear.
* Purchasing the Snowglobe bundle for 2000 snowflakes in Beesmas 2020.
* Buying the Snowey Starter Pack for 400 Robux gave 1 Snowglobe and other items.
* By opening the Lapis Gift Box.
* As a reward for completing Gifted Bucko Bee's Beesmas 2024 Quest.
* As a drop from the Glass Gift Box.

Trivia[]
* This is the one of three Beequips to give a unique ability.
* The other two are Reindeer Antlers and Festive Wreath.
* The Snowglobe's unique ability is also the only way to get the Cool Breeze buff outside of Beesmas.
