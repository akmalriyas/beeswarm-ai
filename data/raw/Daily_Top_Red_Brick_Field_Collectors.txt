Daily Top Red Brick Field Collectors
The Daily Top Red Brick Field Collectors was, at the time, one of the 66 leaderboards in the game. Unlike other leaderboards, it did not appear in the System Page, nor anywhere else in-game. This leaderboard measured how much pollen players had collected in the Red Brick Field that day. It reset every day at 12:00 AM CST.
Prizes[]
If the player had reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they would be awarded with the 25 Tickets.
Trivia[]
* This, along with the other Brick Field collector leaderboards, and the Daily Top Ant Field Collectors were the only leaderboards not to be displayed anywhere in the game.
* This was tied to be the fourth leaderboard to be removed, tied with the aforementioned leaderboards, and the rest being the Most Commando Captures, Highest Snowbear Level and Highest Robo Party Cake Rank leaderboard.
