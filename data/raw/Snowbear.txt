Snowbear is a mini-boss that can be summoned every 1 hour and 30 minutes at the Snowbear Summoner after completing Panda Bear's Beesmas quest. When summoned, it spawns in the Spider Field, and players have 60 seconds (1 minute) to defeat it. If 60 seconds has passed without enough damage done to it, it will despawn (melt). All players' bees can attack another player's Snowbear, but the player that summoned it would receive more rewards.
Attacks[]
Snowbear has two different attacks:
* Contact: If a player makes contact with Snowbear, it deals a base damage of 36. Snowbear does not track players; instead it moves randomly through the field hitting players along the way.
* Snowball Hurl: Snowbear hurls a snowball at a player who is in, or around, the Spider Field. This snowball deals 60 base damage and applies the "Frozen" Debuff to the player who gets hit by the snowball for approximately 2.5 seconds, applying ×0 player movespeed and Jump Power, as well as ×0.2 Bee Movespeed.
The Frozen effect icon.
Health[]
From level one to level ten, its health increases non-linearly so that at level 10 the Snowbear has 100,000 health.
From level 10, every time the Snowbear levels up it gets +5,000 health.

Strategies[]
Every time the player defeats the Snowbear, its health increases (e.g. 20,000 health at level 5), thus making it stronger and harder to defeat in a short amount of time. High-level bees that can do a good amount of damage are recommended.
Its reload time for using a snowball is short, so if it catches the player in ice, it may be able to keep them frozen by using the snowball attack on them repeatedly. The snowball will still do damage while the player is frozen, and the player will die if they get hit multiple times. If they have a glider or parachute, they can use it to dodge the snowballs. If they get frozen, immediately after thawing, they can jump away and use the glider to prevent being frozen a second time. It is also a good idea to try and stay behind the Snowbear, as it takes time for it to turn around.
It is also recommended to have another player have their bees attack a high-leveled Snowbear.
* Use Oils to increase the Bee Movespeed, and Tropical Drinks to increase Critical Chance. Super Smoothies are also an option, as they increase both stats. However, they are expensive, so it is not recommended to use them unless you are far into the game and/or the Snowbear is very high-level.
* Certain items like Demon Mask and Fire Mask's passive abilities, Windy Bee's tornado ability, frogs and Spicy Bee's Inferno ability ignore enemy levels meaning they will always deal damage regardless of the Snowbear's level.
* Have multiple bees with high attack such as Lion Bee or Vicious Bee.
* Use Stingers to multiply the bee's attack by ×1.5 for 30 seconds.
* If you have the Star Saw passive, you can use it to deal more damage.
* Certain bees that generate battle tokens may be a necessity.
* It is recommended to have multiple Rage Bees or Spicy Bees, in order to get more Rage Tokens.
* Vicious Bee is important, as it can deal massive damage with Impale.
* Digital Bee is useful, as it can stun and make it take more damage with its Mind Hack ability.
* Bees that can generate Focus tokens, such as Looker Bees, Commander Bees, Music Bees, and Puppy Bee (with Reindeer Antlers) can help greatly.
* Music Bee can generate Melody, which increases critical power. Melody can also be generated by bees that have the Toy Horn or the Kazoo equipped.
* Precise Bees generate Target Practice tokens, which grants Precision, increasing Super-Crit Chance.
* High-leveled bees are useful for landing hits on high-leveled Snowbears.
* Precise Bees can be very useful against high-leveled Snowbears, as their attacks never miss if the Snowbear is one level higher (two if Gifted).
* The Snowball attack will land sooner than the actual snowball, so to prevent being frozen by seemingly nothing, try to keep moving while remaining in range of the Snowbear.
* In addition, if a snowball comes your way, do not run away in the direction of the snowball, as you will likely be frozen. Instead, run towards the left or the right of it.
* Gifted Bees have 50% more attack than normal bees, making them useful for fighting Snowbears.
* Having other players in the server to help the player is also a viable strategy.
* Use Gifted Bees that increase bee attack, critical chance, critical power, super-crit chance and bee movespeed, such as Gifted Looker Bee, Gifted Brave Bee, Gifted Rage Bee, Gifted Commander Bee, Gifted Ninja Bee, Gifted Tabby Bee and Gifted Precise Bee.
* Use Jelly Beans to get the green Jelly Bean token, which boosts Critical Chance.
* Equip accessories that can boost the attack of the player's bees, primarily guards and masks, such as Fire Mask and Demon Mask.
* Use Onett's Lid Art, Honeyday Candles, Gummy Beacon, and the Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)")'s Honey Wreath as three of the Beesmas decorations summon 3 extra bees and the Honey Wreath will summon 1 extra bee for a certain amount of time.
* With the Coconut Canister, a player can intentionally activate the Emergency Coconut Shield to gain a ×1.25 bee attack boost for 10 seconds while also being immune to damage. Do note that having the Emergency Coconut Shield active does not make a player immune to freezing.
* Try to stack battle buffs such as focus and precision to their maximum before spawning the Snowbear.

Trivia[]
* Snowbear uses the Cartoony Animation Pack.
* The icon for the Frozen debuff is the asset for Ice Cube from the animated show Battle for Dream Island.
* Snowbear and Stick Bug are the only hostile mobs that can be directly summoned by players. They are also the only mobs whose cooldowns are not affected by Gifted Vicious Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus.
* Snowbear is the fourth mob to be removed from the game, after the Chick, Spotted Chick, and Hostage Chick.
* It's also the only mob to return after being removed.
* Snowbear's leveling up mechanic work similarly to Stick Bug and Wild Windy Bee.
* It's also the only mob that has a changing size, which also increases by leveling. This can make it the largest mob in the game.
* Snowbear is the first and only mob able to freeze a player.
* Snowbear and Tunnel Bear are the only hostile mobs that are bears.
* Similar to Tunnel Bear and Shadow Bear, Snowbear has no face and uses one texture.
* This is the fourth mob to not drop Honey Tokens, with the first three being the Coconut Crab, ants and the cave monsters.
* This, the Naughty List (plus the quest related with it), Festive Nymphs, and Snowflakes were the only Beesmas 2020 content that was extended.
* Although a majority of the game refers to this mob as Snowbear , the message the player gets when they fail to defeat Snowbear says "Snow Bear ".
* This, Tunnel Bear and Shadow Bear are the only faceless bears.
* There was a glitch which causes the Snowbear to go idle, and it does not move around or attack the player. The cause of this glitch is unknown.
* Snowbear has its own Cub Buddy skin, the Snow Cub, which was the final reward for Bee Bear's 2021 Beesmas quest.
* However, unlike its counterpart, the Snow Cub skin has a face and a carrot for a nose, and uses the Ninja Animation Pack.
* There is a glitch which causes Snowbear to spawn inside the Mushroom Field. The cause of this glitch is unknown.
* Digital bee´s mind hack ability can make it teleport to Mushroom Field, Bamboo Field or Strawberry Field.
* Although spawning in a field with no stuff, Snowbear has collision.
* Snowbear's top hat is a retextured version of the Psychedelic Top Hat.
