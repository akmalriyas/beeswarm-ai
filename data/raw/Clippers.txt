The Clippers are a tool that was added in the 2018-06-02 update.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 2,200 Honey.
The clippers collect 9 pollen from 1 flower tile in front of the player every 0.6 seconds. This gives it a base pollen collection rate of 13.85 pollen per second.

Trivia[]
* The Clippers are the smaller and weaker version of the Scissors with the color of the handles changed to yellow.
* The Clippers, Electro-Magnet, Golden Rake, Spark Staff, Porcelain Dipper, Petal Wand, Dark Scythe, Tide Popper and Gummyballer are the only tools added in an update.
* These are one of the three items in the game that can cut the vines in front of the parkour leading to Commando Chick's Hideout, the others being the Scissors and the Scythe.
* These and the Scissors are the only tools that target one flower.
