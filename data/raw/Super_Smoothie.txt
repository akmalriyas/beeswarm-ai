Super Smoothie
Super Smoothie
_"Grants the improved boosts of many consumable items combined for 20 minutes!"_
The Super Smoothie is a craftable inventory item added in the 2020-06-06 update. When used, it grants the following buffs for 20 minutes:
Fact: Super Smoothie statistic: * x1.5 Capacity;.
Fact: Super Smoothie statistic: * x1.6 Red Pollen;.
Fact: Super Smoothie statistic: * x1.6 Blue Pollen;.
Fact: Super Smoothie statistic: * x1.6 White Pollen;.
Fact: Super Smoothie statistic: * x1.4 Bee Gather Pollen;.
Fact: Super Smoothie statistic: * x1.4 Tool Pollen;.
* x1.5 Convert Rate;
* x1.5 Convert Rate At Hive;
* +20% Instant Conversion;
* +1% Super-Crit Chance;
* +6% Critical Chance;
* x1.25 Bee Movespeed; and
* x1.25 Player Movespeed.
Red Extracts, Blue Extracts, Glues, Oils, Tropical Drinks, Purple Potions, and Enzymes cannot be active simultaneously with a Super Smoothie. If any of these buffs are active when a Super Smoothie is used, they are removed and replaced by the Super Smoothie buff.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Crafting via Blender for:
* 3 Neonberries
* 3 Star Jellies
* 3 Purple Potions
* 6 Tropical Drinks
* As an extremely rare drop from defeating certain mobs, such as the Coconut Crab or Tunnel Bear.
* As an extremely rare drop from Mythic Meteor Showers.
* As a reward from the Robo Bear Challenge.
* As a very rare drop from planters. Some guaranteed sources are listed below:
* Plastic Planters have a rare chance of dropping 1 Super Smoothie in any field, 36 days cooldown.
* The Planter Of Plenty drops 10 Super Smoothies when harvested from the Bamboo Field, 36 days cooldown.
* As a reward from certain quests:
* Dapper Bear's "More Than Myth" (#15) gives 1 Super Smoothie and other items.
* Science Bear's "Corrupting The Glitched Drive" gives 1 Super Smoothie and other items.
* Buying 50 of them from Bee Bear Catalog Shop for 500 Gingerbread Bears

Trivia[]
* This is one of the four consumable items that increases capacity, with the others being Marshmallow Bees, Purple Potions and Snowflakes.
* Super Smoothies were once misspelled as "Super Smoothy."
* The Super Smoothie is the consumable that gives the highest number of different buffs in the game.
* Super Smoothies ' raw ingredients cost:
* 12 Neonberries
* 60 Coconuts
* 600 Sunflower Seeds
* 5,175 Strawberries
* 6,450 Pineapples
* 5,175 Blueberries
* 1,035 Royal Jellies or 1.04B Honey
* 9 Magic Beans or 180 Tickets
* Super Smoothies ' raw ingredients cost (excluding Gumdrops' and Moon Charm's downgrading):
* 12 Neonberries
* 60 Coconuts
* 600 Sunflower Seeds
* 450 Strawberries
* 600 Pineapples
* 450 Blueberries
* 225 Moon Charms
* 810 Royal Jellies or 810,000,000 Honey
* 9 Magic Beans or 180 Tickets
* 450 Gumdrops or 150 Tickets
* The Super Smoothie is one of the items that cannot be donated to the Wind Shrine.
* The Super Smoothie is also the only craftable item that cannot be donated to the Wind Shrine.
* Bee Bear calls this the "ultimate boost granting beverage" in his 2021 Beesmas quest, "Quality Cousin Time".
* The only other mention of a smoothie is by Polar Bear and his "Thick Smoothie" quest.
