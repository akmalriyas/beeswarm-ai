Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Eviction

Eviction
Description
"Removes a bee from the hive."
Fact: "Removes a bee from the hive." requirement: 50,000 Honey.
The Eviction was an inventory item that used to be purchasable in the Mountain Top Shop for 50,000 Honey. When used, it removed a bee from the hive. It was the cheapest item available at the Mountain Top Shop before being removed.
Evictions were mainly used to remove bees and replace them with eggs if the player couldn't afford a Hive Slot, taking note that eggs could not be placed on hive slots with a bee in the game version that had evictions.
The Eviction Shop in the Mountain Top Shop.
Evicting a bee would completely reset the hive slot, meaning that the bee's bond, level, and gifted status, would be reset, so if a new egg were hatched, the new bee would have started at level 1. The previous bee will not affect the bee type or gifted status of the new bee.
All evictions that anyone might have had will no longer be in their inventory.
Way(s) To Obtain[]
There are currently no methods to obtain the Eviction , as it has been removed from the game.
The methods below are no longer possible.
* Purchasing an Eviction in the Mountain Top Shop for 50,000 Honey.
* Finishing Bubble Bee Man's Beesmas 2018 quest 'B.B.M. Mission' rewarded 800 Evictions and other items.
Removal Reason[]
As of the 2019-09-28 update, the player can now use eggs on existing bees, removing the need to evict bees, while also allowing a bee to keep its level. Thus, Onett "Onett \(Developer\)") removed the Eviction from both the shop and every player's inventory.
