Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Moon Amulet

Moon Amulet
Datamined information: How many Moon Amulets needed to max out its quality.
Date of datamined file: December 19th, 2024
The Moon Amulet icon.
The Moon Amulet is an amulet created by spending 100 Moon Charms at the Moon Amulet Generator.
This amulet grants buffs to the owner and allows the player to walk on the moon-shaped platforms (located at the back of Dapper Bear’s Shop, in the Diamond Room, and at the 30 Bee Zone Obby) at night.
The quality of the amulet increases linearly with the number of Moon Amulets the player has generated, up to 40 amulets, where its quality is maxed.
When using the generator, a message will appear in the chat, saying:   The moon seems to grow brighter...
If the player has maxed out their capacity buff, the message will instead say:   The moon seems happy!
When the player generates a Moon Amulet, the following audio plays:
