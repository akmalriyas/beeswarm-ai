Hub Field Shop
The Hub Field Shop is a shop inside the Hive Hub, right next to the Hub Field.
This shop is a quest giver and it sells 1 item, being the Sticker Seeker.
Fact: The Sticker-Seeker in Hub Field Shop has/costs 7,000,000 Honey.
Fact: Sticker-Seeker requirement: 1 Glue.
Fact: Sticker-Seeker requirement: 1 Oil.
Fact: Sticker-Seeker requirement: 5 Soft Waxes.

Quests[]
_Main article:Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine_
Quests from this shop require the Sticker Seeker. The quests consists of multiple parts: collect pollen with the Sticker Seeker, donating stickers to the Public Sticker Board, collecting Rainbow Sticker Tokens, collecting stickers from sprouts or Puffshrooms, and finding hidden Seeker-Stickers at random bee zones, shops, or HQ's.
