The Heat-Treated Planter is a reusable planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in about 12 hours and stores around 25 billion pollen. When harvested, it grants bonus Red Extracts and Enzymes. It is purchasable in the Red HQ. The player also needs to complete 150 Gifted Riley Bee quests for it to be unlocked.

It grows up to 50% faster in fields with red flowers. Red bees are 33.3% more likely to sip nectar from it, but blue bees are 50% less likely to sip from it. When harvested, the planter grants 50% more red pollen, but also 50% less blue pollen. It also grants 40% more Invigorating and Motivating Nectar.
Harvesting a fully-grown Heat-Treated Planter has a 2/9 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 6-7, and has a:
* ~72.59% chance of being a Common Puffshroom,
* ~21.78% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
* ~5.44% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
* ~0.18% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom,
* ~0.01% chance of being a Mythic Puffshroom.
When claimed, the planter gives up to 35 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, 3 of the tokens are guaranteed to be 1 Red Extract, 1 Enzymes and 3 Tickets. Trivia[]
* This and the Hydroponic Planter had models introduced in Beesmas 2021, but they weren't added as an obtainable item until Beesmas 2022.
* This planter is the red counterpart of the Hydroponic Planter, and its white counterpart is the Petal Planter.
* The Heat-Treated Planter's raw ingredients cost:
* 750B Honey
* 254,250 Blueberries
* 291,750 Strawberries
* 51,000 Sunflower Seeds
* 363,250 Pineapples
* 7,425 Bitterberries
* 820 Neonberries
* 5,500 Honeysuckles
* 600 Coconuts
* 220,970 Royal Jellies or 221B Honey
* 465 Magic Beans or 9,300 Tickets
* This is one of the five reusable planters to reside outside of Dapper Bear's Shop, the others being the Red Clay Planter, Blue Clay Planter, Petal Planter, and Hydroponic Planter.
* This and the Red Clay Planter are the only planters to be sold from the Red HQ.
