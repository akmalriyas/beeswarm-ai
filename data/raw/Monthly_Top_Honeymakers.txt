Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Monthly Top Honeymakers

Monthly Top Honeymakers
The Monthly Top Honeymakers is one of the 64 leaderboards in the game and is only viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu in the System Page. This leaderboard shows how much honey players have collected over the span of 1 month. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on the leaderboards for lengths of time (hackers/exploiters will be removed). The leaderboard resets on the 1st day of every month at 12:00 AM, CST.
