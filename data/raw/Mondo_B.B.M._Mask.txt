The Mondo B.B.M. Mask is a mask that is the bigger version of the B.B.M. Mask. It was obtained by completing Bubble Bee Man's Quest, "B.B.M. Mission", which was given out in the 30 Bee Zone during the Beesmas 2018 Event.
To obtain the quest the player would have needed to complete Bee Bear’s entire now unavailable Beesmas 2018 questline. Otherwise, Bubble Bee Man would say, 'uhh... ..ummm....'. After the completion of this quest, this hat would automatically replace the hat the player was wearing. Currently, players can reequip this mask by talking to BBM in the 30 Bee Zone.

Trivia[]
* This and the B.B.M. Mask are the only masks that cannot be purchased, and the only ones with no description.
* This, the B.B.M. Mask, the Fire Mask, and the Demon Mask are the only masks to have Bee Attack buffs.
* This and the Mondo Belt Bag are the only items that are a mondo version of an accessory.
