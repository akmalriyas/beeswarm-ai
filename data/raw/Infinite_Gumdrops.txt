The Glue Dispenser is a dispenser located in Gummy Bear's Lair, behind the location where the player teleports to. It can be used every 22 hours and dispenses glue (number given depends on what goo badge the player has) along with the Unlimited Gumdrops buff for 3 minutes. The type of goo badge doesn't affect the duration of the buff.

Trivia[]
* Using this is one of the two only ways to get the Unlimited Gumdrops Buff, the other being certain codes.
A player under the bottom platform of the Glue Dispenser.
* It is the only dispenser that requires the player to have a certain badge to access, as the Gummy Bear's Lair requires the player to have Goo Hotshot or above to enter.
* This, the Free Ant Pass Dispenser, Ant Pass Dispenser, Free Robo Pass Dispenser, Robo Pass Dispenser, and the Coconut Dispenser are the only dispensers to not grant haste.
* This is the only dispenser that can give a passive ability.
* This, the Free Robo Pass Dispenser, and the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser are the only dispensers with a 22 hour cooldown, the longest in the game.
* This and the Coconut Dispenser are the only dispensers to give more items based on the rank of a badge.
* The player cannot collide with the neon green decoration around the platform, which means they can go underneath the white platform.
