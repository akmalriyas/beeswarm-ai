Daily Top Ant Field Collectors
The Daily Top Ant Field Collectors was, at the time, one of the 66 leaderboards in the game. Unlike other leaderboards, it did not appear in the System Page, nor anywhere else in-game. This leaderboard measured how much pollen players had collected in the Ant Field that day. It reset every day at 12:00 AM CST.
Prizes[]
If the player had reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they would be awarded with the Ant Field Stamp Sticker.
Top 100 Prize for Daily Top Ant Field Collectors.
Trivia[]
* This and the Brick Field Leaderboards were the only leaderboards not to be displayed anywhere in the game.
* This was the fourth leaderboard to be removed, the rest being the Most Commando Captures, Highest Snowbear Level and Highest Robo Party Cake Rank leaderboard.
* This leaderboard shares a reward with the Top Ant Exterminators leaderboard.
* These are the only leaderboards to have shared rewards.
