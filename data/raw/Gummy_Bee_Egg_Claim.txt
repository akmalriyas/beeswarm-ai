Gummy Bee Egg Claim
The Gummy Bee Egg Claim.
The Gummy Bee Egg Claim is a platform that can be found beyond the Ant Gate. It's located on a ledge near the Ant Challenge and the hives. It can be reached by going through the gap behind the Top Ant Exterminators Leaderboard, going on top of the Ant Gate, or by walking on top of the hives to the egg claim.
The player can combine 2,500 Gumdrops here in order to receive a Gummy Bee Egg, once.
Claiming the Egg[]
Standing on the white platform prompts players with a message saying, "Combine 2500 Gumdrops into a Gummy Bee" if they haven't claimed a Gummy Bee Egg. If the player already owns one, the text saying, "You Already Own a Gummy Bee" is displayed.
Whenever a player forms a Gummy Bee Egg, a slight remix of "gbtune1" (the music played around the Gummy Bee Egg Claim and in Gummy Bear's Lair) is played throughout the map and a server-wide announcement is made reading: 🎉 {Username} has formed a Gummy Bee 🎉
Teleporting to the Lair[]
Standing on the Gummy Bee model without the Goo Hotshot badge prompts the player with " Only Goo Hotshots can hear Gummy Bee...
Using gumdrops when this message is shown will not teleport the player to Gummy Bear's Lair. If the player has the Goo Hotshot badge, standing on the model prompts the player with:  The Gummy Bee wants Gumdrops
Using Gumdrops while standing on the model with Goo Hotshot teleports the player to Gummy Bear's Lair. A Gummy Bee is not required to enter the lair.

Trivia[]
* Over the course of the Beesmas events succeeding the first one in 2018, there were several presents placed beyond an obstacle course next to the claim with a theme similar to gumdrops.
* This is one of two egg claims in the map, the other being the Vicious Bee Egg Claim for Vicious Bee.
* At night time, the Gummy Bee model's face glows as well as the teal outline of the pad.
* An alternative way to reach this is by jumping on top of one of the Ant Pass Dispensers, jumping on to the rock by the Ant Gate, walking across the Ant Gate, and jumping onto the pad.
* This is the only bee claim to have a different soundtrack than the area around it playing gbtune1.
* While Gummy Bee was available in the Ticket Tent, the giant Gummy Bee used in the egg claim was located on the top of the Ticket Tent to the right of Gifted Photon Bee where the Festive Bee is now. It would still teleport players to Gummy Bear's Lair if a gumdrop was used on top of it.
* If the player were to buy all the Gumdrops needed to combine for Gummy Bee, it would cost 834 Tickets.
