Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Glue

Fact: Glue statistic: _"Grants x1.25 Bee Gather Pollen and Tools for 10 minutes."_.
Fact: Glue statistic: **Glue** is a craftable inventory item added in the 2018-11-25 update. Its purpose is as a creating material for accessories, or as a 10 minute boost that grants x1.25 Bee Gather Pollen and tools when used directly. The buff cannot stack and will only reset its timer when another glue is used when one is currently active. Using a Purple Potion or Super Smoothie will override the buff..
Ways to Obtain[]
* Crafting it in the Blender for 50 Gumdrops and 10 Royal Jellies.
* Black Bear gives glue on the following quests:
* All-Whitey Then gives 1 glue and other items.
* Black Bear's repeatable quests occasionally reward glue.
* Brown Bear will give glue on the completion of these quests:
* Brown Bear's 140th quest rewards 1 glue.
* Brown Bear's 235th quest rewards 1 glue.
* Brown Bear's 310th quest rewards 3 glues.
* Brown Bear's 395th quest rewards 5 glues.
* Science Bear gives glue on the following quests:
* Chemical Analysis rewards 1 glue and other items.
* Epistemological Endeavor gives 4 glues and other items.
* As a rare drop from mobs such as the Spider, Werewolf, Puffshroom, King Beetle, and Coconut Crab, or guaranteed from the Stump Snail.
* As a drop from Robo Bear Challenge (The chances are affected by the round the challenge ends at)
* A drop from Armored aphids.
* Small chance of dropping from the Dandelion Field, Pumpkin Patch, and the Stump Field in place of a honey token.
* A rare drop from leaves that appear in the Pineapple Patch, Dandelion Field, Pumpkin Patch, Stump Field, or Spider Field.
* A rare drop when collecting pollen in the Pineapple Patch, Spider Field, or the Pumpkin Patch.
* Rarely, a glue token can appear from collecting goo from flowers.
* You can claim 2-5 Glues from the Glue Dispenser depending on your Goo Badge. Hotshot rewards 2, Ace rewards 3, Master rewards 4, and Grandmaster rewards 5 Glues. The Glue Dispenser is located inside Gummy Bear's Lair.
* As a drop from Gummy Sprouts.
* As a rare drop from Festive Gifts (if Festive Bee is gifted).
* As a rare drop from Festive Mark.
* As a badge reward by donating to the Wind Shrine.
* Matching a pair of glue in Memory Match.
* Capturing Commando Chick the 5th time drops 1 Glue.
* As a rare drop from the Candy Planter, Blue Clay Planter, Pesticide Planter and Festive Planter.
* Planting the Candy Planter in the Stump Field 3 times in a row will guarantee at least 10 Glues (Can give 20 glues, 1 week cooldown)
* A glue token is hidden behind the big pineapple in between the Pineapple Patch and the Stump Field.
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* Right Facing Stump Snail rewards 1 glue.
* Yellow Sticky Hand rewards 1 glue.
* Glowering Gummy Bear Rewards 1 glue.
