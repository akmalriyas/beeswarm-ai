Trivia[]
* This, the Fire Mask, Honey Mask, Riley Guard and Bucko Guard all represent epic bees.
* The Bubble Mask represents the Bubble Bee, having an ability similar to it.
* Before the 2019-04-05 Update, the Bubble Mask's stats were:
Fact: Trivia[] statistic: * +25% Bee Gather Pollen.
* +25% Instant Conversion
* +30% Damage Reduction
* +11% Bee Ability Rate
* +115% Luck (Loot Luck)
Fact: * +115% Luck (Loot Luck) statistic: * +25% Blue Bomb Pollen.
Fact: * +115% Luck (Loot Luck) statistic: * x1.15 Blue Pollen..
* Before the 2019-09-28 Update, the Bubble Mask didn't have any Passive Ability. Other stats were also changed.
* If the player owns the Bubble Mask, its passive is inherited by the Diamond Mask.
* If a player were to craft the ingredients to craft the item, it would take 100,000,000 Honey, 7,500 Pineapples, 8,625 Blueberries, 5,625 Strawberries, 1,250 Sunflower Seeds, 15 Magic Beans, and 1,125 Royal Jellies.
* The Bubbles from its Bubble Bombs passive have a unique sound effect when popped.
