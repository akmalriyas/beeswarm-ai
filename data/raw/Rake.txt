The Rake is a tool that can be purchased at the Noob Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 800 Honey.
The Rake collects 2 pollen from 3 flowers in a line every 0.7 seconds, which gives it a base pollen collection rate of 8.57 pollen per second.

Trivia[]
* Due to the fact that the Scooper is earned by first joining the game, this is the first tool that can be bought, thus also making it the cheapest tool available for purchase in the game.
* This is an inferior version of the Golden Rake, which can be bought in the Mountain Top Shop for 20,000,000 (20 million) honey.
* The Rake originally costed 1,100 honey, however this was eventually lowered.
