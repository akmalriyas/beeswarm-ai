Trivia[]
* Baby Bee has the second-lowest base energy (lowest being Tadpole Bee), second-lowest base Convert Rate (lowest being Fuzzy Bee), and the lowest base gather amount in the game. Baby Bee also has the second-slowest base gather speed, 1 second faster than Tadpole Bee and Fuzzy Bee.
* Baby Bee and Tadpole Bee tie for the smallest bees in the game. They are also the only two bee types to have a different size compared to other bees.
* Baby Bee is the only bee that has 0 base attack. Because of that, it cannot attack any mobs, even if it has an attack mutation.
* Baby Bee used to be able to attack and could do damage if the player had any damage boosts. After the 2018-11-25 Update, this is no longer the case.
* According to Black Bear, the reason why they don't attack is because "They're too dumb to notice" the mobs.
* Even though it says, "0 attack", attack added to baby bee still counts towards total attack, which can buff Star Saw.
* Baby Bee, Bumble Bee, Demon Bee, and Exhausted Bee are tied as the slowest bees in the game.
* Baby Bee has the most field preferences in the game. It also dislikes the most fields.
* Baby Bee prefers the lower-tier fields (Dandelion, Blue Flower, Sunflower, etc.) and dislikes the higher-tier fields (Rose, Cactus, Pine Tree Forest, etc.).
* Baby Bee is the only bee that isn't red to like the Mushroom Field.
* Baby Bee and Stubborn Bee are the only Colorless bees that like strawberries.
* Baby Bee and Rage Bee are the only bees with three unique colors on their skins. Baby Bee is also the only gifted bee with three unique colors on its skin.
* There is a small chance that a Baby Bee egg will drop from Tunnel Bear.
* Before 2018-11-25 update, the Gifted Hive Bonus of Baby Bee was +5% Loot Luck, which was then changed to +10% in said update. This was then changed to +20% Loot Luck in the 2019-04-05 Update and once again to +25% Loot Luck in the Beesmas 2019 update.
* Onett paid $2 for the laughing baby sound that Baby Love uses. It can be bought Here.
* This is one of the only 4 bees that has a specific egg obtainable from mob drops, with the others being a Rascal Bee egg from ladybugs, a Bumble Bee egg from rhino beetles, or a Honey Bee egg from cave monsters.
* Baby Bee, Basic Bee, and Bumble Bee are the only bees shown on Onett's Lid Art.
* When gifted and in the Dandelion Field, Sunflower Field, Mushroom Field, or Blue Flower Field it has a very rare chance to find a Pale Heart Sticker.
* Baby Bee's in-game stat menu does not actually display that its convert speed and gather speed is decreased by 25%.
