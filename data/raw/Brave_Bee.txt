Trivia[]
* Brave Bee and Basic Bee are the only bees in the game that don't produce any ability tokens unless they are in their gifted form or have a beequip that grants an ability token.
* The Brave Bee Gate has a large model of a Brave Bee on it.
* Brave Bee is the strongest Rare bee in terms of attack, with a base attack power of 6.
* The Brave Guard, which is purchased in the Pro Shop, has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Brave Bee.
* Brave Bee's body color scheme is a darker version of the Vector Bee's colour scheme.
* Brave bee is one of the 16 bees to have a sticker based off of them.
* During Bee Bear's "Ability Token Tidings" quest Bee Bear refers to Brave Bee and Basic Bee as "Poor little merely mortals bees".
* Bee Bear also says they aren't magic because they can't create ability tokens.
* Although it is non-functional, Brave Bee is the only bee in the game to have a coded disliked treat, being blueberries.
