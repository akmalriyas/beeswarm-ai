Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Present 2024 (Winter)

Present/2024 (Winter)
Presents returned once again in Beesmas 2024 (Winter). They still had their main function of adding Ornaments to the Beesmas Tree when given to NPCs, but could now be given to Sun Bear.
Ways to obtain[]
* Multiple quests from Bee Bear awarded a total of 10 Presents, along with other rewards.
* Bee Bear’s Catalog sold 1 Present for 250 Snowflakes and 10 Gingerbread Bears.
* Onett gave 1 Present (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event's period.
* Donating Strawberries to the Galentine Shrine had a chance to give 1 Present, along with other rewards.
* Completing Robo Party's rank 25 for the first time gave 1 Present, along with other rewards.
* Using the code "LatePresents" gave 2 Presents.
* Using the code "LatePresent" gave 1 Present.
