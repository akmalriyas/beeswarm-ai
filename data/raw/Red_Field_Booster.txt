Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Red Field Booster

Red Field Booster
The Red Field Booster inside of the Red HQ.
The Red Field Booster is a machine that provides a Field Boost to a random red field for 15 minutes. The machine is located in the Red HQ. In order to use it, the player needs to have discovered 5 different red bee types. It has a cooldown of 45 minutes.
The following audio plays when the Red Field Booster has been activated:<https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Fieldbooster.ogg>

Trivia[]
* This is one of the three field boosters in the game, along with the Field Booster and the Blue Field Booster.
* The Red Field Booster has the same design as the Field Booster and the Blue Field Booster, the only difference being the colors.
* The Red Field Booster, unlike the Blue Field Booster, is located near the entrance of the Red HQ, while the Blue Field Booster is located on the second floor in the Blue HQ.
* In an update, the Strawberry Field boost was nerfed from 200% to 100%.
* Prior to the 2024-05-23 update, this booster was not able to boost the Pepper Patch.
