Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Enzymes

_"Grants +10% Instant Conversion and x1.25 Convert Rate for 10 minutes."_
Enzymes are an inventory item that was added in the 2018-11-25 update. When used, it grants +10% Instant Conversion and x1.25 Convert Rate for 10 minutes when activated. Using another Enzymes will not stack them, but will reset the timer. Enzymes can also be used for crafting other accessories or items. Using a Super Smoothie will override the buff.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Crafting it via Blender for:
* 50 Pineapples
* 10 Royal Jellies
* As a potential match from all Memory Matches.
* The Stick Bug Challenge includes other sources of Enzymes on top of rewarding Enzymes upon completion:
* Stick Bug can drop Enzymes.
* Defense Totems can drop Enzymes.
* Redeeming certain valid Codes.
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain Stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* Honey Bee Bear rewards 1 Enzymes.
* Panicked Science Bear rewards 1 Enzymes.
* Forward Facing Spider rewards 1 Enzymes.
* Standing Caterpillar rewards 1 Enzymes.
* Yellow Hi rewards 1 Enzymes.
* Taunting Doodle Person rewards 1 Enzymes.
* Orange Step Array rewards 1 Enzymes.
* Orange Green Tri Deco rewards 1 Enzymes.
Drops[]
* As a drop from the Armored Aphid, King Beetle, Tunnel Bear, and the Coconut Crab.
* As a rare drop from a scorpion and the spider.
* Farming in the Pineapple Patch and the Spider Field may rarely yield Enzymes.
* Harvesting leaves in those fields may also yield Enzymes
* As a rare drop from sprouts (the odds increase depending on the rarity of the sprout and if the sprout is in the Pineapple Patch).
* As a drop from Stick Bug, stick nymphs, or defense totems.
* As a drop from a Festive Gift (if Festive Bee is gifted) or Festive Mark.
* As a rare gift from a cub buddy.
* As a drop from planters.
* Planters in Pineapple Patch makes them drop Enzymes.
* The Heat-Treated Planter drops Enzymes as a bonus drop.
* Planting it in Pineapple Patch and Pepper Patch may make it drop 100 Enzymes (unknown cooldown).
* Planting it in Pineapple Patch three times in a row guarantees 100 Enzymes (unknown cooldown).
* As a drop from Stockings.

Trivia[]
* Enzymes used to look like pills. However, Roblox removed them because they appeared to have violated the Roblox Terms Of Service. They were later re-created, published under the name "Pineapple Candies" and with the appearance they have currently.
* If multiple Enzymes are used in a Blender's crafting recipe, the chat log displaying the lost materials would display "Enzymess" adding an extra 's' instead of the proper plural. This small bug applies to Gumdrops and various other items.
