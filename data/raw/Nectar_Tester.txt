Nectar Tester
Nectar Tester
_"This test item grants 24 hours of all Nectars."_
The Nectar Tester is a special item that acts like nectar vials, but instead of providing a single nectar for 4 hours, it provides every type of nectar for 24 hours. It was meant to be only available on Test Realm, but was added to the main game during 2024 Summer Beesmas as a reward for completing Bubble Bee Man's Naughty List.
Ways to Obtain[]
There are currently no way to obtain a Nectar Tester.
The methods below are no longer possible.[]
* As a reward for completing Bubble Bee Man's quest "B.B.M's Naughty List" during the Beesmas 2024 events.

Trivia[]
* This item originated from the test realm as a quicker way to get nectars without spending too much time.
* This item gives nectar equivalent to 30 nectar vials.
* This also means this item gives the most nectar of any item, at 120 hours overall.
* The Nectar Tester and the Debug Wax are the two inventory items exclusively obtainable from Bubble Bee Man's quests.
* This item shares the same icon as the Basic Egg, likely because items without an assigned decal typically use such.
