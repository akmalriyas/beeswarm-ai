Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Masks

This category houses all pages about hats (or in most cases, masks), which is a sub-category of accessories. This type of accessory is worn on the player's head. Each hat/mask can be crafted with honey and other various materials. Hats give out a variety of boosts that are unique to them.
Some hats have passive abilities. For example, the Honey Mask has Coin Scatter. Some hats can have 2 passive abilities if one is unlocked from another hat. For example, the player can have the Ignite passive on the Demon Mask if they own the Fire Mask.
