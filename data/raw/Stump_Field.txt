The Stump Field is a field that was added in the 2018-11-25 update and is located past the Brave Bee Gate next to the Pineapple Patch.
This field can spawn honey, red boost, blue boost, treat, gumdrop, glue, magic bean, ticket and blue extract tokens when collecting pollen.
Bees that like the Stump Field are Bumble Bee, Exhausted Bee, Tadpole Bee, and Gummy Bee. Bees that dislike the Stump Field are Hasty Bee, Baby Bee, and Spicy Bee.
This field grants Motivating Nectar when a planter is planted here.
This field contains 563 flowers. The flowers are white, red, and blue, and the only natural size is triple.

Possible Events[]
* Stump Snail protects the field by circling around the circumference of the tree stump but never attacks you directly. While the Stump Snail is still alive, your Bees will prioritise damaging it rather than collecting pollen/spawning tokens which collect pollen (bombs, boosts, etc.).
* When there is a Stick Bug Challenge active, if Stick Bug is in Mountain Top Field, Bamboo Field or Pineapple Patch, he may summon a defense totem.
Obtaining Boosts[]
The Stump Field Boost icon.
* Using the Blue Field Booster may give a Stump Field Boost, if the Stump Snail is on respawn cooldown. When it does, it will give +100% Stump Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using glitter will add 100% Stump Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
* Using field dice, smooth dice, or loaded dice may add +100% - +300% Stump Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out and is based on the type of dice used.
* Stump Field Winds can be obtained through the Wind Shrine or codes. They have a base of +20% instant Stump Field conversion and give +5% instant Stump Field conversion and +10% Stump Field pollen for each stack for 30 minutes. Stump Field Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.
* It can be boosted by the Shell Amulet, which boosts the field by +10% to +65% depending on the amulet's rarity. All Shell Amulets are guaranteed to give this buff.
* Activating the Guiding Star passive can summon a guiding star to this field if it is one of the player's five least collected fields.
* Using Onett's Lid Art could summon a guiding star to this field.
The methods below are no longer possible.[]

Trivia[]
* Before the 2019-09-28 update, the Stump Field was originally a more balanced colored field. It was updated to act as a blue-oriented counterpart to the Pepper Patch.
* This is the first field the player will encounter that only has triple flowers naturally, the second one being the Mountain Top Field. Third being the Coconut Field and fourth being Pepper Patch.
* This is the only field where gumdrops can be obtained from leaves.
* This and the Hub Field are the only non-quadrilateral fields in the game.
* Before the 2024-01-12 Update, the Stump Field was the only non-quadrilateral field in the game.
* This is the only blue field where blueberries cannot spawn naturally.
* This field is also the only blue field that has red flowers.
* This, the Ant Field and the Hub Field are the only fields where Wild Windy Bee cannot spawn in nor fly to.
* However, Onett "Onett \(Developer\)") can summon Wild Windy Bees in this field. If they are defeated, they will fly in place until their timer is up as there is no flight pattern programmed for Stump Field Windy Bees.
* This, the Coconut Field, the Pepper Patch, the Ant Field and the Hub Field are the only fields that were added after the game's first release.
* Stump Field, Coconut Field, and Spider Field's caves have the same appearance.
* No special rewards can be found in the Stump Snail's Cave, unlike the Werewolf's Cave and the Coconut Cave, making it closer to the Spider Field's cave.
* There is a glue token between the large pineapple and the base of the stump.
* According to Black Bear, there used to be a gigantic tree that blocked the sunlight and didn't let the plants and flowers grow. Then one day, a huge bear came and cut the tree down. A field then grew on the stump of the tree, which many assumed is what the Stump Field is. It is unknown which bear cut the tree down in the first place.
* Spirit Bear also climbed the tree before it was cut down and met Windy Bee. This is said when getting the quest, "The Sky Over The Stump".
* This field is one of three fields with no decorations in it, indicating what field it is. The other two fields to do this are the Ant Field, the Mountain Top Field and the Hub Field.
* This field gets its name from the stump it sits on top of instead.
* During Beesmas 2019 and 2020, there was an obby in a hole to the left of the Stump Field. Completing the obby would lead to the Purple Gift Box.
* This has been replaced by Dapper Bear's Shop.
* This is the only field that can be completely covered by a sprinkler, that being The Supreme Saturator.
* This Field is currently the third smallest field in the game, only being behind the Pumpkin Patch and the Ant Field.
* Because the field has a square hitbox, it is possible to place planters on the outer rim of the field.
* Harvesting a planter utilizing this glitch will cause the loot tokens spawn on the closest flowers in the field.
* This is the only field in the game with the flowers being arranged into a circle, while others being rectangles or squares.
