The Golden Gushers are the 3rd tier sprinkler available for purchase in the Badge Bearer's Guild. To unlock it, the player must obtain 10 different Ace badges. Once the player unlocks it, they can purchase it for 333,333,333 (~333.3 million) honey. Its description reads, "Three gorgeous sprinklers with enormous range."
The Golden Gushers are comprised of 3 individual sprinklers.
Its radius extends to 8 flower tiles, its rate is 4.5 seconds, and its power is 8.
