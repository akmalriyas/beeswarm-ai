The Crimson Guard is a guard that can be crafted in the Badge Bearer's Guild. It is currently the highest tier red guard. Its description reads, "A guard bestowed with the heroic power of Crimson Bee \- Defender of the Red Bees."
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 200,000,000 Honey.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 100 Red Extracts.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 100 Stingers.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 50 Oils.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 25 Glitter.
It is equipped on the left shoulder. When equipped, it leaves a trail behind it similar to Crimson Bee's trail.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients statistic: * +250,000 Capacity..
Fact: Crafting Ingredients statistic: * +50% Red Pollen..
Fact: Crafting Ingredients statistic: * +20% Red Bomb Pollen..
* +3 Red Bee Attack.
* +10% Defense.
* +10% Critical Chance.
* +Passive Ability: Focus Pulser.
* Effects: Every 30th focus token releases a red pulse, which hops to each red bee, collecting pollen around them. Power increases with each hop.
* Unlike the ability token, this effect does not trigger a blue pulse, even if the player owns a Cobalt Bee or the Cobalt Guard.

Trivia[]
* If the ingredients to craft this item were to be crafted, a total of 200,000,000 Honey, 14,375 Strawberries, 12,500 Pineapples, 9,375 Blueberries, 2,500 Sunflower Seeds, 2,125 Royal Jellies, 100 Stingers, and 25 Magic Beans are needed. The total crafting time is 875 minutes (or 14 hours and 35 minutes) of playtime.
* This is the only red guard that cannot be purchased in the Red HQ.
* This is one of the two guards that have a passive ability, the other being the Cobalt Guard.
* Its blue counterpart is the Cobalt Guard.
* This and the Cobalt Guard are the only guards in the game to leave a trail behind when the player moves.
