Abilities[]
* [Blue Bomb] Collects 10 pollen from 13 surrounding Blue Flowers (+10% pollen per Level). Combo with other bombs to increase power.
* [Inflate Balloons] Increases the pollen in Balloons on your field by 5% (+0.01% per Level), then spawns a Blue Balloon for 20s (+2s per level) with capacity that scales with bee level and your capacity.
Fact: Abilities[] statistic: * Pollen collected from flowers near (the shaded ones) the balloon is increased by 10% (20% if blue) and added to the balloon instead of your Container. Upon filling up or timing out, the balloon floats back to your Hive. Converting from a balloon at your hive grants bonus Capacity and Honey at Hive for 1 hour..
Fact: Abilities[] statistic: * Standing near a balloon grants a small bonus to Pollen, Blue Pollen, and Honey from Tokens which stacks up to 10 times..
Fact: Abilities[] statistic: * If Gifted, this has a 5% (+0.5% per level, doubled when the field is boosted) chance to summon a Gold Balloon instead, which has x2 Capacity and an additional x1.25 Pollen instead..
Fact: Abilities[] requirement: * **[🌟Gifted Ability: Surprise Party]** Summons a Gold Balloon (+1 every 5 levels) for all players, then causes all Balloons to spawn random ability tokens for a few seconds. Gold Balloons gain bonus pollen (increases with bee level) and transform nearby Bubbles into Gold Bubbles, which collect 50% more pollen and have a chance to spawn Honey Tokens. If you have triggered a Surprise Party within the last 8 minutes, performs "Inflate Balloons" instead (always spawns a Gold Balloon). When a Surprise Party is activated, a server wide message will say "🎉Surprise Balloons from [Player's Display Name].".
* [Passive: Balloon Enthusiast] When converting from Balloons, this bee has x3 convert amount. Additionally, this bee's base attack scales up to x3 with Balloon Blessing.

Trivia[]
* This bee, Digital Bee, Windy Bee, and Festive Bee are the only bees that can grant boosts to all the players on the server.
* This bee, Commander Bee and Frosty Bee are the only 3 bees in the game that wear hats.
* As such, the Buoyant Bee is the only hat-wearing bee that is not an Epic.
* This is the only bee to not dislike any field.
* Onett "Onett \(Developer\)") stated on Discord that the reason why Buoyant Bee has no disliked fields is that it's supposed to be a "happy" bee.
* The Buoyant Bee and the Lion Bee have the most amount of finite energy.
* The Buoyant Bee, Precise Bee, and Fuzzy Bee are the only Mythic bees to be added since the release of the original three Mythic bees.
* The Buoyant Bee and the Precise Bee are the only two bees to have their own Robux pack in the same update.
* The Buoyant Bee and Windy Bee are the only bees to be able to produce a White Boost token. Buoyant Bee, however, only produces it through Surprise Parties, meaning it has to be gifted in order to produce white boost tokens.
* If you have a lot of Balloon Blessing, the bee will have the same attack as Lion Bee because of the Balloon Enthusiast passive.
* If the player is not in a field when another player in the same server activates a Surprise Party, the subtitle for it will appear on the player's screen but won't summon any Golden Balloons.
* Gifted Buoyant Bee's hat glows.
* This bee’s gifted form is similar to Bumble Bee's gifted appearance.
* Additionally, both Buoyant Bee and Bumble Bee are the only bees to have gifted abilities that multiply a player’s base capacity.
* This bee used to be able to summon Blue Bomb+ tokens, but it was nerfed to Blue Bomb in the 2022-12-26 update.
