Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Honeysuckle

Honeysuckle
Honeysuckle
Fact: Honeysuckle statistic: _"Automatically makes bees instantly convert some Pollen into Honey when your bag becomes full. (Cooldown 30s)"_.
The Honeysuckle is a consumable inventory item that was introduced in the 2021-12-26 update. Unlike other consumable items with an auto setting, Honeysuckles are automatically used once a player's pollen container is full, and it is also the only item with an auto setting that cannot be put in the hotbar. When a player's pollen container is full, the Honeysuckle causes the player's bees to instantly convert some pollen in their bag into honey. It has a cooldown of 30 seconds before it can activate again, and can be toggled on or off by clicking the item in the inventory.
Unlike most consumables, this cannot be dragged to the hotbar. There is, however, an On/Off switch in the honeysuckle's inventory section. Turning it ON causes it to work automatically when the bag becomes full, and turning it OFF disables using honeysuckles.
Ways to Obtain[]
* As a rare drop from Ladybugs.
* As a drop from planters.
* Planters in Blue Flower Field, Rose Field and especially Sunflower Field give honeysuckles more often than other fields.
* The Blue Clay Planters drops honeysuckles as a bonus drop.
* As a reward from the Robo Bear Challenge.
* As a common reward from Retro Swarm Challenge.
* As a reward from the Memory Match, Mega Memory Match, Night Memory Match or Extreme Memory Match.
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* The Small Tickseed rewards 10 honeysuckles.
* The Small White Daisy rewards 10 honeysuckles.
* The Small Pink Tulip rewards 10 honeysuckles.
* The Small Dandelion rewards 10 honeysuckles.
* As a drop from Robo Party mobs.
* As a reward from the Robo Party.
* Using the Samovar grants 3-30 Honeysuckles (Starts from 3, increases by 1 each use and caps at 30).
Token Locations[]
* A Honeysuckle token is located on the roof of Dapper Bear's Shop which grants 25 Honeysuckles.
Outdated Ways[]
* Completing 6th and 18th Bee Bear's Beesmas 2022 quests granted 25 and 50 Honeysuckle respectively.
* Honeysuckle token under the Beesmas Tree which granted 5 Honeysuckles.
* Giving Onett a present granted the player 25 honeysuckles and other items.
* As a potential match from the 2021 and 2022 Winter Memory Match.
* Purchasing the Honeysuckle Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog rewarded 150 Honeysuckles and other items.
* From certain Beesmas related quests.
* Opening certain Gift Boxes:
* The Gilded Gift Box gave 20 Honeysuckles.
* Purchasing the Poinsettia Pack in Bee Bear's Catalog gives 50 Honeysuckles and other items during Beesmas Winter 2024.
