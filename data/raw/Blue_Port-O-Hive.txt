Trivia[]
* This is one of the three improved Port-O-Hives, alongside the Red Port-O-Hive and the Porcelain Port-O-Hive.
* Out of the 4 Port-O-Hive variants, this and the Red Port-O-Hive were the last ones to be added to the game.
* This is the only bag purchasable in the Blue HQ.
* Prior to the 2024-05-23 Update, this item had no material requirements.
* During Beesmas 2021, Bee Bear said Snow Cub Buddy hates “Blue Backpackers” (players with Blue Port-O-Hive equipped). This is a reference to a running stereotype that went on in the BSS community.
* The Cool Backpack Sticker is also a reference to the Blue Port-O-Hive. With it being the only backpack sticker currently in the game, it is likely another reference to the stereotype.
