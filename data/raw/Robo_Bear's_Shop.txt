Robo Bear's Shop is a shop run by Robo Bear located right of the Red HQ, next to the Rose Field. This shop sells all of the drives, and Digital Bee. It was added alongside the rest of Robo Bear's area in the 2022-12-26 update.
Robo Bear's Shop is not the official name of the shop; it is only called this (assumably) because the owner of the shop is Robo Bear.
The formula for the price of each colored drive in honey is
P ( n ) = 250,000 n 2 000n^{2}}
where _n_ represents the _n_ th drive of that type to purchase (1st is 250,000, 2nd is 1,000,000, 3rd is 2,250,000, etc). The price for Glitched Drives begins at _n + 1_.
