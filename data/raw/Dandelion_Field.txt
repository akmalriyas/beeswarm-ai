The Dandelion Field is one of the five starting fields any player is able to visit upon starting the game. There are no mobs that protect this field.
This field can produce honey, red boost, blue boost, ticket, glue, glitter, magic bean, and treat tokens when collecting pollen.
Item Drops list

Magic Beans (0.0059% Chance)

Bees that like the Dandelion Field are Baby Bee, Bomber Bee, Demo Bee, Exhausted Bee, Music Bee, Stubborn Bee, Windy Bee, Fuzzy Bee, and Digital Bee. Bees that dislike the Dandelion Field are Commander Bee, Brave Bee, and Vicious Bee.
This field gives Comforting Nectar when a planter is placed here.
A level 1-5 Wild Windy Bee can spawn here.
This is an 18x36 field, containing 648 flowers. The flowers are white, red, and blue, in sizes of single and double.

Obtaining Boosts[]
* Using the Field Booster may give a Dandelion Field Boost. When it does, it will give +300% Dandelion Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using glitter in the field will add 100% Dandelion Field pollen onto the current boost if it isn't already maxed out.
* Using field dice, smooth dice, or loaded dice may add +100%-300% Dandelion Field pollen for 15 minutes based on the dice used if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
* Dandelion Field Winds can be obtained through the Wind Shrine or codes. They have a base of 20% instant Dandelion Field conversion and give +5% instant Dandelion Field conversion and +25% Dandelion Field pollen for each stack. Dandelion Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
* Redeeming certain valid codes:
* Jumpstart (Activates Dandelion Field Code Buff + other stuff).
* PlushFriday (Gives Dandelion Field Code Buff + other stuff).
* It can be boosted by the Shell Amulet. If it has a Dandelion Field Boost, it can boost the field by +5% to +30% depending on the amulet rarity.

Trivia[]
* Before the 2019-09-28 update, the Dandelion Field was the only field that was mob-proof, meaning no mobs could spawn/attack there.
* There is a royal jelly token on top of the tallest dandelion which can be obtained by using the Parachute/Glider with cannons or from a high place.
* This is also the only dandelion in the field that has collision at its top.
* The Bubble Wand model on the Blue HQ can grow a face and blow sparkles onto this field, along with the Mushroom Field, Clover Field, and Blue Flower Field.
* The All-Time Top Honeymakers Leaderboard is adjacent to the Dandelion Field.
* There used to be a glitch where Stick Bug could fall from the tallest mushroom and land in the Dandelion Field. This has now been patched.
* During the Ready Player Two event, the field can be seen glitching after using the Computer.
* Onett threatens to remove every field but the Dandelion Field if the bees and bears don't stop complaining in his Star Journey 2 quest dialogue. He then follows this up stating he wouldn't really do this though.
