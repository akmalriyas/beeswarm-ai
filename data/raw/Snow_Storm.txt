Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Snow Storm

Snow Storm
An active Snow Storm.
A Snow Storm is a server-wide event that can be summoned by using the Snow Machine or randomly in the game. It is similar to a Honeystorm, but spawns Snowflakes instead. A Snow Storm lasts for 20 seconds.
When a player uses the Snow Machine to summon a Snow Storm, a server-wide announcement states:  ❄️ {Username} has activated the Snow Machine! ❄️
Snowflakes will start to fall rapidly from the sky on every field. There is no audio at the beginning of a Snow Storm, unlike a Honeystorm or Mythic Meteor Shower.
When a Snow Storm is randomly summoned, the message states:  ❄️ A Snow Storm has begun... ❄️

Trivia[]
* Like Honeystorms and Mythic Meteor Showers, Snow Storms can light up the Werewolf's Cave enough to see the Cave Monsters and the Gold Egg at the end of the cave.
* Unlike Honeystorms, Snow Storms don't spawn snowflake tokens in the Ant Field.
* During Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025, giving Spirit Bear a Present would trigger a Snow Storm after her dialogue finishes and the ornament was given.
* Finishing Spirit Bear’s Beesmas quest would also summon a Snow Storm.
* The Snowflakes keep falling for a few seconds after a Snow Storm ends because it takes time for the Snowflakes to reach the ground.
* If a Snow Storm is summoned during nighttime, the white fog will stay even after the Snow Storm has ended until nighttime ends.
