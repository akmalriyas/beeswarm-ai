The Scythe is a tool that can be purchased in the Red HQ.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 3,500,000 Honey.
The Scythe collects 7 pollen from 7 patches in 0.47 seconds and collects x2 pollen from red flowers. Every 20th swing spawns a flame. Its average base pollen collection rate is 139.01 pollen per second: 208.51 from red flowers and 104.26 from white and blue flowers. It emits fire particles at the blade.

Trivia[]
* This is one of the three items in the game that can cut the vines in front of the parkour leading to the Commando Chick's Hideout, the other two being the Scissors and the Clippers.
* There is a model of the Scythe on top of the Red HQ. The code "Roof" is next to it.
* The surface of the Scythe is reflective, which is why it becomes dark during nighttime.
* Its blue counterpart is the Bubble Wand.
* This is the fastest tool in the game (in terms of how long the cooldown of each collection lasts).
* Similarly to the Bubble Wand, it is the second cheapest item in Red HQ in terms of honey, behind the Red Guard.
* This is the only tool that has the ability to damage mobs by the flame it spawns.
