The Scooper is a tool that new players automatically receive at the beginning of the game, along with the Pouch.
It collects 2 pollen from 2 patches in front of it with a speed of 0.8 seconds, which equals a base pollen collection rate of 5 pollen per second.
It can be equipped in the Noob Shop.
Appearance[]
The scooper resembles a shovel with a grey blade and circular handle with a brown pole presumably made of wood.
Gallery[]
The Scooper's associated sticker.
Trivia[]
* This tool is currently the least effective tool at collecting pollen, because its base statistics are lower than any of every other tool in the game.
* The Super-Scooper is the upgraded version of this. It can be purchased in the Pro Shop.
* This is the only tool in the game that doesn’t cost any honey.
* This is the third slowest tool in the game, surpassed by the Honey Dipper and Pulsar/Gummyballer.
* This, the Pouch, and a Basic Egg are the only items that the player starts off with when joining the game for the very first time.
