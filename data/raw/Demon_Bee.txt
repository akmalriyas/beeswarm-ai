Trivia[]
* Demon Bee is one of the slowest bees in the game, but is also one of the most powerful in terms of attack and gather amount.
* Demon Bee is the only bee in the game to have 2 bomb abilities (Red Bomb and Red Bomb+).
* Demon Bee, Riley Bee, and Rascal Bee have the same skin colors.
* Both Demon Bee and Fire Bee have the same flame effect.
* The Demon Mask, which can be purchasable in the Lava Obby, has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Demon Bee.
* Onett "Onett \(Developer\)") has said on Discord that during early stages, it was supposed to summon Fire Bees. This role has now been taken over by Spicy Bee.
* Before the 2020-04-06 Update, Demon Bee had the highest base gather amount in the game with the exception of a Tabby Bee with a 500+ stack of Tabby Love. Since then, Fuzzy Bee has taken that position.
* Demon Bee is the red counterpart of Diamond Bee.
* One reason Demon Bee dislikes the Mountain Top Field might be because it is closer to heaven (the sky) than all other Fields.
* This bee can be summoned by the Honeyday Candles.
* This Bee, Fire Bee, Precise Bee, Festive Bee and Crimson Bee are the only red bees that do not like Strawberries.
* In the in-game stat menu for Demon Bee, its attack is stated to be 6 more than the normal amount (1). However, it is actually 7 more.
