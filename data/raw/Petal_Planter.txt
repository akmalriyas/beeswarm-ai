The Petal Planter is a reusable planter added in the 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in about 14 hours and stores 100 billion pollen. When harvested, it grants bonus Jelly Beans, Field Dice, and Glitter. It can be purchased at the Petal Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 5T Honey.
100 Magic Beans
Fact: 100 Magic Beans requirement: 100 Glitter.
Fact: 100 Magic Beans requirement: 250 Soft Waxes.
Fact: 100 Magic Beans requirement: 50 Swirled Waxes.
Fact: 100 Magic Beans requirement: 25 Super Smoothies.
The planter grows 50% faster in fields with white flowers, and grants 50% bonus white pollen on harvest. Colorless bees are 33% more likely to sip nectar from this planter, and it grants 50% more Comforting and Satisfying nectar.
Harvesting a fully grown Petal Planter has a 1/4 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 7-8, and has a:
* ~89.28% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
* ~8.93% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
* ~1.79% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom,
* ~0.01% chance of being a Mythic Puffshroom.
When claimed, the planter gives up to 45 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, 2 of the tokens are guaranteed to be 1 Glitter and 5 Tickets. Trivia[]
* This is the fourth most expensive item in the game in terms of honey, only behind the Coconut Belt, which costs 7.5 trillion honey, the Gummyballer, which costs 10 trillion honey, and The Planter Of Plenty, which costs 100 trillion honey.
* The Petal Planter is currently the only item in the Petal Shop not to require a Spirit Petal.
* It is the only item in the Petal Shop added after the shop was added.
* This is the most expensive item in the game in terms of honey that does not require Turpentine to craft.
* The Petal Planter's raw ingredients cost:
* 346,875 Blueberries
* 346,875 Strawberries
* 72,500 Sunflower Seeds
* 426,250 Pineapples
* 4,950 Bitterberries
* 1,200 Neonberries
* 5,650 Honeysuckles
* 1,500 Coconuts
* 97,990 Royal Jellies or 98B Honey
* 425 Magic Beans or 8,500 Tickets
* This is currently one of the five reusable planters to not be sold in Dapper Bear's Shop, the others being the Red Clay Planter, Blue Clay Planter, Hydroponic Planter, and the Heat-Treated Planter.
* Of those, this is the only planter to not be sold in a headquarters shop.
* While the in-game description states that it gives +33% white pollen and colorless bees are 50% more likely to sip from the planter, the 2 numbers are swapped in reality.
