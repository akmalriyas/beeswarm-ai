The Spark Staff is a tool that was added in the 2019-04-05 update. It can be bought in the Mountain Top Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 60,000,000 Honey.
The Spark Staff collects all pollen from the 3 fullest nearby flowers in 0.5 seconds and increases it by 20%. It has a maximum base pollen collection rate of 216 pollen per second from single flowers, 324 pollen per second from double flowers, 432 pollen per second from triple flowers, 540 pollen per second from large flowers, and 648 pollen per second from star flowers, varying immensely due to digging the entire flower, with pollen held by flowers depending on the tier. However, the nearby flowers may not be full, so it doesn't always reach its complete potential.

Trivia[]
* This is the only tool that makes a sound every use. It is also the only tool that can collect pollen while in mid-air and the most expensive tool without an ability.
* This is the only tool in the Mountain Top Shop that neither have an ability nor make a specific "shape" when collecting pollen.
* This and the Scissors are the only tools that collect all pollen from the flowers they hit.
* This is the only Tool that can collect all the pollen from more than 1 flower.
* This tool uses the same model as the "Nova Wand", which is an official Roblox gear that can be bought for 1,000 Robux.
* This tool is needed for Science Bear's Drive questline.
* Collecting 16,777,216 pollen with this tool is required for "Repairing The White Drive."
* Collecting 33,554,432 pollen with this tool is required for "Corrupting The Glitched Drive."
* This tool is often considered as one of the worst tools in the game by the community. However, this tool can become very powerful compared to other tools in the same tier if collecting from higher tier flowers, such as by gathering in fields pollinated by other player's Fuzzy Bees.
* The oldest version of the tool is known to not have the pollen increase of the current one. The previous version of the tool is known to have 15% pollen increase instead of the current 20%.
* This is one of the (if not the) smallest tool in the game, barely being visible in the players hand unless the camera is zoomed in.
