Trivia[]
Fact: Trivia[] requirement: * In the early versions of the game, Ninja Bee had an ability called "Haste+," which doubled the player's Movespeed for 20 seconds. This ability has now been stripped from the bee and is currently only obtainable through the Star Hall's Royal Jelly Dispenser and codes..
* Ninja Bee, Hasty Bee, Shocked Bee, and Photon Bee are the only bees that grant haste without any certain Beequips.
* Ninja Bee is the only colored bee to grant haste, with the exception of those with the Toy Drum Beequip.
* This bee, Shy Bee, Fuzzy Bee, and Windy Bee are currently the only bees that have a solitary colored skin.
* Ninja Bee is the fastest bee in the game, tied with Photon Bee.
* In Ninja Bee's description, it is supposedly the "swiftest bee". However, Photon Bee is just as fast.
* Before the 2018-11-25 Update, Ninja Bee's Gifted Hive Bonus was +20% Player Movespeed instead of +5% Bee Movespeed.
* Ninja Bee, Cobalt Bee, and Vicious Bee are the only blue bees that do not like Blueberries.
* It is the only non-Event blue bee to not like blueberries.
* This and Precise Bee are also the only non-Colorless bee to like Sunflower Seeds.
* This bee, Photon Bee, Cobalt Bee, Crimson Bee, Tadpole Bee, and Windy Bee are the only bees that leave a trail.
* Ninja Bee is the blue counterpart of Shy Bee.
* This bee is the placeholder image for any bee in a hive slot that hasn't loaded yet.
* Ninja Bee's favorite treat was originally Pineapples.
* Ninja Bee is stated by Black Bear to be his choice of leader for the Blue HQ.
