> "_A durable Plastic Jar. Holds over twice as much as the Pouch!_ "
The Jar is a Bag that can be purchased in the Noob Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 650 Honey.
Fact: Crafting Ingredients statistic: * +750 Capacity..
Trivia[]
* This is the cheapest Bag that can be purchased in the Noob Shop and overall, in the game.
* This is the third cheapest item in terms of Honey, only beaten by the Pouch, Scooper and Strange Goggles.
Fact: Trivia[] statistic: * This and the Pouch are the only Bags that cost less honey than their base Capacity..
* Its description reads that it holds over twice as much as the Pouch, but it holds over three times as much.
Fact: Trivia[] statistic: * There is a larger version of the Jar that can be purchased in the Pro Shop, called the Mega-Jug, which has a base Capacity of 25,000 Pollen for a price of 50,000 Honey..
