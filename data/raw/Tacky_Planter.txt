The Tacky Planter is a reusable planter added in 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in about 8 hours and stores around 15 million pollen. When harvested, it grants bonus Field Dice and Sunflower Seeds. It can be purchased in Dapper Bear's Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 50,000,000 Honey.
10 Magic Beans
Fact: 10 Magic Beans requirement: 1 Purple Potion.
Fact: 10 Magic Beans requirement: 5 Hard Waxes.
Fact: 10 Magic Beans requirement: 20 Soft Waxes.
It grows 25% faster and grants 100% more pollen on harvest in the Starter Zone (Sunflower Field, Dandelion Field, Mushroom Field, Blue Flower Field, Clover Field), and grants 25% more Satisfying and Comforting Nectar.
Harvesting a fully grown Tacky Planter has a 1/5 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is level 5, and has a:
* ~73.15% chance of being a Common Puffshroom,
* ~21.95% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
* ~4.75% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
* ~0.15% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom.
When claimed, the planter gives up to 22 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, 2 of the tokens are guaranteed to be 1 Field Dice and 1 Ticket.
