The Rose Field is one of the four fields located past the Honey Bee Gate. This field can be found near the Red HQ. It is protected by two scorpions, one level 5, and the other level 6.
This field can spawn honey, strawberry, red boost, red extract, bitterberry, and magic bean tokens when collecting pollen.
Bees that like the Rose Field are Rad Bee, Rascal Bee, Rage Bee, Riley Bee, Precise Bee, Crimson Bee, and Vicious Bee. Bees that dislike the Rose Field are Puppy Bee, Stubborn Bee, Bucko Bee, Demo Bee, Baby Bee, and Diamond Bee.
This field grants Motivating Nectar when a planter is planted here.
A level 5-7 Rogue Vicious Bee has a chance of spawning here.
This is a 20×31 field, containing 620 flowers. The flowers are red and white, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

Obtaining Boosts[]
The Rose Field Boost icon.
* Using the Red Field Booster may give a Rose Field Boost. When it does, it will give +100% Rose Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using glitter will add 100% Rose Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the boost isn't already maxed out.
* Using field dice, smooth dice, or loaded dice may add +100% - 300% Rose Field pollen for 15 minutes based on the type used and based on its current stack before getting another one.
* Rose Field Winds can be obtained through the Wind Shrine or codes. They have a base of +20% instant Rose Field conversion, and give +5% instant Rose Field conversion and +15% Rose Field pollen for each stack. Rose Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
The methods below are no longer possible.[]
* Giving a present to Onett gave Rose Field Boost x1 and other boosts and items and giving Gifted Riley Bee a present gave Rose Field Boost x3 and other boosts and items during Beesmas 2021.
* Redeeming certain expired codes.

Trivia[]
* This is one of six fields that fireflies travel to, with the others being Bamboo Field, Cactus Field, Pineapple Patch, Spider Field, and Strawberry Field.
* Vicious Bee likes this field most likely because roses commonly have thorns on their stems.
* It is also the only blue bee to like a field of the opposite color. The only other bee to share this trait is Festive Bee and Pine Tree Forest.
* This field was one of the fields which has a small section outside of the map.
* There is a royal jelly token next to this field in a small ledge, which can be accessed by walking a small path along the Badge Bearer's Guild wall.
* This is the only field past the Honey Bee Gate to not be defended by the Werewolf.
* After the 2019-09-28 update, treat tokens could naturally spawn in this field.
* This is the only field to be at a lower elevation than the gate leading to it.
* If a Rogue Vicious Bee spawns in this field, it can attack players inside the Red HQ with its spikes.
* Stepping in the row of flowers closest to Robo Bear does not spawn the Scorpions.
