The Plastic Planter is a planter added in 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in around 2 hours and stores 250,000 pollen. The planter can be bought from Dapper Bear's Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 750,000 Honey.
3 Magic Beans
Fact: 3 Magic Beans requirement: 1 Soft Wax.
The planter has no special multipliers.
Harvesting a fully grown Plastic Planter has a 1/7 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 3-4, and has a:
* ~80% chance of being a Common Puffshroom,
* ~20% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom.
When claimed, the planter gives up to 12 tokens worth of items. The tokens are picked randomly from the following, non-exhaustive list (note that the list does not include rewards based on the field the planter is in):

Trivia[]
* Plastic Planter and Ticket Planter tie as the 2nd fastest growing planters, the first being the Paper Planter with only 1 hour of in-game playtime.
* This is the cheapest planter in Dapper Bear's Shop.
* It's also the 2nd cheapest purchasable planter in-game behind the Paper Planter.
