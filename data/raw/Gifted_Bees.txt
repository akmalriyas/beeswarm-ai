Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Gifted Bees

Gifted Bees
A bee can be identified as gifted if there is a yellow hexagon around its hive slot.
A bee becoming gifted when fed its favorite treat, star treat, gingerbread bear, or an aged gingerbread bear.
Gifted bees are a special type of bee with improved stats (x1.5 more collection, convert rate, and attack) and a buff called a "Gifted Hive Bonus." It improves the performance of the player and/or their bees, similar to badges.
Every time the player uses a Royal Jelly or hatches an egg, they have a 0.4% (1 in 250) chance to get a gifted bee. Similarly, feeding a bee its favorite treat has a chance for it to turn gifted, though this only happens with a 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance for Rare bees, 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance for Epic bees, 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance for Common and Legendary bees, and 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance for Mythic bees per favorite treat. Feeding a bee a Gingerbread Bear also has a 1% (1/100) chance of turning it into its gifted version. Feeding a bee an Aged Gingerbread Bear has a 1.1% (1/90.909091) chance of turning it into its gifted version.
Star Treats will always turn the chosen bee into its gifted version. This is the only method that can turn Event bees like Bear Bee and Tabby Bee into a gifted bee (excluding gingerbread bears). If the player feeds a Star Treat to an already gifted bee, the bee will only gain the bond and no extra benefits. Star Egg will always hatch into a non-event gifted bee that is not already found in the hive unless the player already has all non-Event bees in their gifted forms, in which case it hatches into a random non-Event gifted bee instead. Star Jelly will always turn the chosen bee into a random non-Event gifted bee, using the same odds as a royal jelly (except for a Gifted Basic Bee).
A gifted bee's UI upon obtaining will cycle through colors, with text saying, "⭐ Your [type] Bee Transformed Into A Gifted [type] Bee!⭐" and play the following sound file:
<https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Giftsong.ogg>
On top of the Noob Shop, there is a chamber called the Star Hall. There, the player can generate Star Amulets and claim royal jellies depending on the number of gifted bees they currently have, as well as giving them Haste+ for 1 minute.
You can tell what gifted bees you have by looking at the "⭐" icon in the bee menu. There is no star if the gifted variant hasn't been discovered yet, a faded star if it's been discovered but isn't in your hive, and a black star if you have it currently in your hive. This can help track what bees are needed for Star Amulet progress.
Effects[]
* The bee is equipped with a new color scheme, unique to each type of bee, stained wings, and a star on its back and sides. The hive icon doesn't change color.
* x1.5 gather amount.
* x1.5 convert rate.
* x1.5 attack.
* Applies a Hive Bonus specific to that bee (see below list for details).
* Improved certain passive abilities.
* New ability token - [Inspire]: Doubles pollen gained for 5 seconds, Stacks up to 50 times, giving up to a x51 bonus.
* Able to generate [⭐ Gifted Ability] Tokens if the bee has one.
* A glowing gold hexagon around its hive slot (as seen in the picture above).
* Able to claim an additional royal jelly from the Royal Jelly Dispenser in the Star Hall per gifted bee in the hive.

Trivia[]
* Since Bear Bee is now purchasable from the Robux Shop, players who purchased a Bear Bee from the game launch event had their Bear Bee turned into a First Edition gifted Bear Bee.
* Therefore, this makes Bear Bee the only Event bee that can be gifted without the use of a Star Treat or Gingerbread Bears.
* Multiple gifted bees of the same type do _NOT_ stack their hive bonus multiple times, nor do they count towards the number of gifted bee types in the hive. However, they will still have improved stats over their normal version.
* Using a royal jelly or a non-gifted egg on a gifted bee will almost certainly make the bee LOSE ITS GIFTED STATUS. The chance of the new bee being gifted is 1/250.
* Windy Bee is the only bee to give two different gifted hive bonuses.
* Tadpole Bee used to give two different gifted hive bonuses before the 2024-05-23 update.
