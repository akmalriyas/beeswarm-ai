Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Public Sticker Board

Public Sticker Board
The Public Sticker Board is located in the Hive Hub, and allows players to add or take stickers from it. There is a 22-hour cooldown for taking a sticker from the board and a 10-minute cooldown for adding a sticker to the board. The Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine might occasionally require players to donate a sticker to the Public Sticker Board as well. It serves as a way of obtaining stickers or removing spare stickers.

Trivia[]
* Play Button and Game Chat Icon stickers cannot typically be donated to the Public Sticker Board.
* This restriction does not apply to people who can give out these stickers.
* During the Beesmas of Summer 2024, the Fluorescent Gift Box "Gift Boxes") was located behind the Public Sticker Board.
