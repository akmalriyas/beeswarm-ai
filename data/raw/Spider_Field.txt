The Spider Field is a field located past the Basic Bee Gate and lies between the Strawberry Field and Bamboo Field. The field is protected by the spider.
This field can spawn bitterberry, treat, honey, ticket, enzyme, and magic bean tokens when collecting pollen.
Bees that like the Spider Field are Brave Bee, Commander Bee, Rage Bee, Shocked Bee, Demon Bee, Vector Bee, and Tabby Bee. Bees that dislike the Spider Field are Basic Bee, Honey Bee, and Baby Bee.
This field grants Motivating Nectar when a planter is placed here.
A level 2-4 Rogue Vicious Bee has the chance of spawning here.
It is a 26x28 field, containing 728 flowers. The flowers are all white, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

Obtaining Boosts[]
* Using the Field Booster may give a Spider Field Boost. When it does, it will give +200% Spider Field pollen for 15 minutes.
The Spider Field Boost icon.
* Using glitter will add 100% Spider Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
* Using field dice, smooth dice, or loaded dice may add +100% Spider Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
* Spider Field Winds can be obtained through the Wind Shrine or codes. They have a base of +20% instant Spider Field conversion and give +5% instant Spider Field conversion and +20% Spider Field pollen for each stack. Spider Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
* It can be boosted by the Shell Amulet. If it has a Spider Field Boost, it can boost the field by +5% to +30% depending on the amulet rarity.
* Giving Dapper Bear a Present will give the field a x3 boost for Beesmas 2025.

Trivia[]
* The Spider Field is the only field where all of the flowers are white. This also makes it the only field where all of the flowers are the same color, as there are no fields that are entire blue or red.
* There is a 1 Ticket hidden in the middle of the spider cave.
* This is one of six fields where fireflies can land, with the others being Bamboo Field, Cactus Field, Pineapple Patch, Rose Field, and Strawberry Field.
* There is a glitch that occurs at the Spider Field where amulets and cub buddies are spotted without an owner. This happens when a player left the server at the Spider Field.
* This field is one of Black Bear's favorite fields, as mentioned in his dialogue.
* This field, the Stump Field, the Hub Field, and the Mountain Top Field are the only fields that are not named after a plant or a fruit.
* The Spider Cave, the Stump Field's Cave, and the Coconut Cave have the exact same cave decoration.
* This field, along with Clover Field, Cactus Field, Rose Field, Mountain Top Field, and Pepper Patch are the only fields in which a Rogue Vicious Bee can spawn.
* This is one of the only two fields named after the mob(s) that reside within the field. The other being the Ant Field, with the multiple types of ants.
* This field has a row of flowers inside the mountain wall that's normally not visible, but you can still collect pollen in that area, kind of like how the flowers in the Bamboo Field that go through the ramp aren't visible normally, but you can still collect those flowers.
