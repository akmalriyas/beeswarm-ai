The Sticker-Seeker is a tool that was released on the 2024-01-12 update and can be crafted in the Hub Field Shop.

The Sticker-Seeker collects 10 pollen from 13 patches in 1 second. It has a chance to find Stickers in fields and unlocks the Sticker-Seeker quest. When you equip the Sticker-Seeker, you have the ability to find Seeker-Stickers. Once you click on a Seeker-Sticker, there will be a message on the bottom-right hand corner saying:
🔎You found a Seeker-Sticker! ({amount} to go)
The Sticker-Seeker has a chance to dig up a sticker in the field you are in. Once you dig it up, like the Seeker-Sticker, it will say:  Oh! Your Sticker-Seeker dug something weird up
The sticker appears as a rainbow token on a random flower on the field you're in.

Trivia[]
* The Sticker-Seeker is the only tool you can use to spot and obtain Seeker-Stickers as a quest requirement in the Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine.
* Not counting the Seeker-Stickers that appear when equipping this tool, the Sticker-Seeker is the only tool in the game with no associated tool sticker. This is ironic considering that the secondary function of this tool revolves around locating stickers.
* This tool bears a striking resemblance to a magnifying glass.
* This is tied with the Pulsar and the Gummyballer for the slowest tool in the game (in terms of how long the cooldown lasts).
