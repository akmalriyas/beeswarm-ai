Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Neonberry

Neonberry
_"Increases bond with a bee by 500 and makes it Radioactive for a while."_
A Neonberry is a type of treat that, when fed to a bee, raises its bond by 500 and makes it radioactive for 11 minutes. This is the only treat to have guaranteed chance of making the bee radioactive. These are not the favorite treat of any bee.

* Has a low chance of dropping from rhino beetles, ladybugs, mantises, King Beetle, Stump Snail, Mondo Chick, and Coconut Crab.
* Has a low chance of appearing when harvesting Sparkles.
* Farming in the Blue Flower Field, Pineapple Patch, Mountain Top Field, and the Pine Tree Forest has a rare chance to drop neonberries.
* Harvesting Leaves in these fields may also yield neonberries.
* As a rare drop from a Festive Mark.
* As a gift from a cub buddy.
* As an uncommon drop from all sprouts.
* Debug sprouts are guaranteed to drop neonberries.
* As a rare drop from Mythic Meteors.
* As an uncommon drop from Puffshrooms.
* As a drop from planters.
* The Pesticide Planter drops extra neonberries in the Stump Field, Mountain Top Field, and the Coconut Field.
* Planting the Pesticide Planter in the Stump Field, then the Mountain Top Field, then the Coconut Field results in at least 25 neonberries (1 week cooldown).
