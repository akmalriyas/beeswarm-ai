Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Presents

_This article is about the gifts given to NPCs. Not to be confused withOrnament Presents and Gift Boxes._
_"A festively wrapped gift. Exchange it with an NPC for prizes!"_
A Present is a Beesmas-exclusive inventory item that has appeared in every Beesmas event since 2020 following its debut in 2018 and hiatus in 2019. Presents can be given to most NPCs in the game in exchange for various prizes from the NPC. Each NPC can only receive one gift.
To give a present, the player needs to talk to an NPC of their choice. When interacted with, special dialogue will appear, allowing the player to give the present to the chosen NPC. If the player does not have a present in their inventory or have already recently given said NPC a present, normal dialogue will display. Some NPCs may have other requirements for the player before it allows itself to receive a gift.
* 2024 (Winter)
* 2024 (Summer)
Presents returned once again in Beesmas 2025. They still have their main function of adding Ornaments to the Beesmas Tree when given to NPCs, though players can't give a present to Sun Bear as the bear is not present.
Ways to obtain
* Multiple quests from Bee Bear award a total of 10 Presents , along with other rewards.
* Bee Bear’s Catalog sold 1 Present for 250 Snowflakes and 10 Gingerbread Bears.
* Onett gives 1 Present (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event's period.
* Donating Strawberries to the Galentine Shrine has a chance to give 1 Present , along with other rewards.
* Completing Robo Party's rank 25 for the first time gives 1 Present , along with other rewards.

Presents returned in Beesmas 2021. They functioned the same as the years before that they have appeared in, but could now be given to the new NPC, Dapper Bear. Presents were the only way to obtain Ornaments from NPCs, and they were needed to be delivered to do quests from Bee Bear.
Ways to obtain
* Multiple quests from Bee Bear awarded a total of 12 Presents , along with other rewards.
* Bee Bear’s Catalog sold 1 Present for 250 Snowflakes and 10 Gingerbread Bears.
* Onett gave 1 Present (along with other items) upon claiming a hive for the first time during the event's period.
* Donating Strawberries to the Galentine Shrine had a chance to give 1 Present , along with other rewards.
