Trivia[]
* There is a gate dedicated to the bee, the Honey Bee Gate (or the 15 Bee Zone).
* The only way to obtain a Honey Bee Egg is to defeat cave monsters.
* This may be the reason why Honey Bee dislikes the Spider Field, due to the similarities between spiders and cave monsters.
* The Honey Mask, which can be purchased in the Ace Badge area of the Badge Bearer's Guild, resembles Honey Bee's face decal and color scheme.
Fact: Trivia[] requirement: * Before the 2018-11-25 Update, the Gifted Hive Bonus of Honey Bee was +250% Conversion Rate. This was later updated to x1.5 Honey From Tokens..
* Honey Bee is one of the two bees that generate honey gifts, the other being Diamond Bee.
* Honey Bee was given the honey mark ability after the 2019-04-05 update.
* Both Honey Bee and Bubble Bee emit the same bubble effect, although the colors of these 2 bees are different.
* Along with Bumble Bee and Carpenter Bee, Honey Bee are based on bees found in real life, though they don't show resemblance to the actual bee.
* During Beesmas 2020 and 2021, players were able to receive a Honey Bee Jelly if they gave a present to Honey Bee (NPC) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)").
* During Beesmas 2021, Honey Bee Jelly was also available by purchasing the Honeysuckle Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog.
