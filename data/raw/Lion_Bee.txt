Trivia[]
* The Lion Bee has the highest base attack stat in the game.
* Lion Bee is referred to by Panda Bear as "he" , has a mane, and is called a king in its description. This makes Lion Bee the only confirmed male bee in the game.
* The Lion Bee is tied for the third-highest amount of base Energy in the game; it is only beaten by infinite stamina bees such as Photon Bee or Exhausted Bee, and is tied with Buoyant Bee.
* There is a gate dedicated to it, known as the Lion Bee Gate, the entrance to the 25 Bee Zone. It is located near Polar Bear.
* Lion Bee, Tadpole Bee, Tabby Bee, Puppy Bee, and Bear Bee are the only bees that are based on real-life animals that are not Bee Species.
* Lion Bee and Tabby Bee are the only bees that are based on felines.
* Lion Bee is the only Legendary bee that is based on a real-life non-bee species of animal.
* Lion Bee is also the only bee in the game that likes the Ant Field.
* Lion Bee may like the Ant Field as a reference to antlions in real life.
* It might also like the Ant Field because it has the highest base attack stat in the game and the Ant Challenge is about fighting ants.
