Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Ornaments

Ornaments
Fact: Ornaments requirement: **Ornaments** are limited-time items that are obtainable only during Beesmas events. Ornaments can be obtained from all non limited-time quest givers upon player giving them a Present. When the player receives an ornament, it gets placed on the Beesmas Tree. The ornaments will grant additional buffs, always with one field capacity buff, with the exception of Gifted Riley Bee, Gifted Bucko Bee, and Stick Bug, who gives two. Honey Bee just gives extra capacity, no field capacity. Every NPC's ornament will also give +25% Convert Rate, except Gummy Bear, Robo Bear and Bubble Bee Man, who all give none..
