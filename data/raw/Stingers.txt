Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Stingers

_"Grants your bees x1.5 attack for 30 seconds."_
Stingers are an inventory item added in the 2018-09-10 update. When used, it will grant x1.5 Bee attack for 30 seconds. It can also be used to obtain Vicious Bee (costs 250 Stingers). Stingers are also used in many crafting recipes.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Defeating Rogue Vicious Bee always grants stingers. The number of stingers dropped depends on the Rogue Vicious Bee's level, and whether it is gifted or not.
* A rare drop from a Rage Aphid, Armored Aphid, scorpion, or spider.
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain Stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* Flying Brave Bee rewards 3 Stingers.
* Walking Stick Nymph rewards 3 Stingers.
* Coiled Snake rewards 3 Stingers.
* Dark Flame rewards 5 Stingers.
* Cyan Hilted Sword rewards 10 Stingers.
* Red Wailing Cry rewards 3 Stingers.
* Tough Potato rewards 3 Stingers.
* Capturing certain amounts of Commando Chicks:
* 3 captures give 1 Stinger and other items.
* 12 captures gives 3 Stingers and other items.
* 30 captures gives 10 Stingers and other items.
* 40 captures gives 10 Stingers and other items.
* Buying one for 10 tickets in the Stinger Shop.
* A rare drop from leaves in the Cactus Field.
Fact: Ways to Obtain[] requirement: * Shivering certain badges in the Wind Shrine by donating certain items (Battle, Clover, Spider, Cactus, and Rose badge rewards Stinger + other stuff)..
* Completing certain quests (Black Bear, Gifted Riley Bee, and Spirit Bear).
* Winning it from the Mega, Night or Extreme Memory Matches.
* Very rarely, as a Cub Buddy gift.
Fact: Ways to Obtain[] requirement: * Purchasing the Super Snowman Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog gives 25 **Stingers** \+ other items..
* As a guaranteed reward from defeating all party mobs of your Robo Party.
* As a drop from planters harvested in the Cactus Field or Rose Field.
* Fully growing a Red Clay Planter in the Clover Field, Spider Field and Cactus Field consecutively rewards 10 Stingers (2 weeks cooldown).
* Fully growing a Heat-Treated Planter in the Cactus Field rewards 10 Stingers (unknown cooldown).
* Fully growing a The Planter Of Plenty in the Cactus Field 10-15 Stingers (unknown cooldown).
Looting from Rogue Vicious Bee[]
The Rogue Vicious Bee can be summoned by touching or being close to a spike in a field. It has a higher chance of spawn during the nighttime, and is guaranteed to spawn when using a night bell. Depending on the level, if it’s gifted, and if it’s the first defeat of the day, a Rogue Vicious Bee can drop 1-20 stingers.

* Spider Field.
* Cactus Field.
