The Blue Clay Planter is a reusable planter that was added in the 2021-12-26 update. Alone, it grows in about 6 hours and stores around 3 million pollen. When harvested, it grants bonus Blue Extracts and Micro-Converters. It is purchasable in the Blue HQ.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 10,000,000 Honey.
5 Magic Beans
Fact: 5 Magic Beans requirement: 15 Blue Extracts.
Fact: 5 Magic Beans requirement: 20 Soft Waxes.
It grows 25% faster in fields with Blue flowers, and grants 25% more blue pollen, but 25% less red pollen. Blue bees are 25% more likely to sip Nectar from this planter, but Red bees avoid sipping nectar from it. It also grants 20% more Refreshing and Comforting Nectar.
Harvesting a fully grown Blue Clay Planter has a 1/6 chance to spawn a Puffshroom. The spawned puffshroom is between levels 4-5, and has a:
* ~76.28% chance of being a Common Puffshroom,
* ~19.07% chance of being a Rare Puffshroom,
* ~4.58% chance of being a Epic Puffshroom,
* ~0.08% chance of being a Legendary Puffshroom.
When claimed, the planter gives up to 16 tokens worth of items. If the planter was fully grown when claimed, 4 of the tokens are guaranteed to be 1 Blue Extract, 1 Micro-Converter, 3 Honeysuckles and 1 Ticket. Trivia[]
* Its red counterpart is the Red Clay Planter.
* This is the only planter that drops Honeysuckles as a bonus item.
* This is one of the five reusable planters to reside outside of Dapper Bear's Shop, the others being the Red Clay Planter, Petal Planter, Hydroponic Planter, and Heat-Treated Planter.
* This and the Hydroponic Planter are the only planters to be sold from the Blue HQ.
* In the inventory's description, it doesn't say that it drops Honeysuckles as a bonus drop.
