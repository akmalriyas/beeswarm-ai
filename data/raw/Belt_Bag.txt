Trivia[]
* This was given from Sun Bear on his first visit after completing the quest “Snag a Belt Bag” before he left. Therefore, it was the first Belt Bag he awarded. After Sun Bear left, the Belt Bag was put on sale at the Pro Shop.
* Before the 2018-11-25 update, this item didn't require pineapples, sunflower seeds, or stingers to be purchased, as before this update, these items didn't exist.
* This is one of the few items that went from being a quest reward to being purchasable in a shop.
* Other items with this quality are the Mondo Belt Bag, Basic Boots, Hiking Boots, and the Beekeeper's Boots.
