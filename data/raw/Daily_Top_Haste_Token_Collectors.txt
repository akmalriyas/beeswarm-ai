Daily Top Haste Token Collectors
The Daily Top Haste Token Collectors is one of the 64 leaderboards in the game, and is only viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu in the System Page. This leaderboard shows how many Haste Tokens players have collected on that day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM CST.
Prizes[]
If the player has reached the top 25 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with the Basic Pink Hive Skin.
If the player has reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with 50 Tickets.
