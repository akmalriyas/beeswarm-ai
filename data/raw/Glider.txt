Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Glider

The Glider.
> "_Floats much faster than theParachute, allowing you to fly through the sky! Press jump while in the air to open._"
The Glider is a gliding tool that can be purchased at the Mountain Top Shop, it is the upgraded version of the Parachute.
Fact: The Crafting Ingredients in Glider has/costs Statistics.
Fact: The 5,000,000 Honey in Glider has/costs Speed, 70.
Fact: The Float in Glider has/costs -24.
It allows the player to glide much quicker and change direction faster than the Parachute. To activate it, jump, then jump again while in mid-air. If the player goes airborne through some other method (a cannon, for example), double jump; to disengage it, jump once more or make contact with ground. The gliding feature is hardcoded to the player, and they will glide even during a lag spike; although, the Glider doesn't immediately appear.
The primary use of the Glider is to glide to places on the map that would otherwise be inaccessible. It can be used to collect some of the Royal Jelly and Ticket tokens around the map and can be used to perform several shortcuts. The Glider, along with the Parachute, are the only ways to obtain the Diamond Egg token below the platform in the 30 Bee Zone. It is also used to reach the Star Hall located on top of the Noob Shop. The Glider can also be used to dodge mobs, since most of them don't have a high detection range in the air. Combined with the cannons (especially with the Red Cannon), it is one of the most efficient ways to travel around the map.
Fact: Float requirement: The Glider's gliding speed is not affected by boosts that enhance the player’s movement speed, such as Haste, Haste+, Bear Morph or Oil..

Trivia[]
* The Glider and the Parachute are the only gliding tools in the game, excluding the unobtainable Debug Glider.
* With the Glider, it is possible to glide outside of the map.
* However, it is also possible to exit out of the map via a Wild Windy Bee blowing at the player with a gust of wind.
* If the player enables shift-lock, they can slightly strafe to one side while using the gliding tools.
* The Glider is located on top of the Mountain Top Shop as a decoration, similar to how a Parachute model is located on top of the Pro Shop.
* The Glider is the second cheapest item in the Mountain Top Shop, after the first hive slot.
* It was the third cheapest item in the shop before the Eviction was removed.
* The Glider along with the Parachute can be used to move during a game of Memory Match.
