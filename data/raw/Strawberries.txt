Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Strawberries

Strawberries
_"Increases your bond with a bee by 25."_
A Strawberry is a type of treat that can be fed to a bee to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to a Rascal, Rad, Rage, Riley, Baby, Stubborn, Shy, or Spicy Bee, the amount of bond they gain is doubled. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed strawberries, though this only happens with a 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance for Rare bees, 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance for Epic bees, 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance for Common and Legendary bees, and 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance for Mythic bees per strawberry. Feeding a radioactive bee a strawberry has a 0.004% (1 in 25,000) to mutate a bee.
Ways to Obtain[]
* There is a strawberry token hidden behind the largest strawberry in the corner of Strawberry Field.
* From using the Strawberry Dispenser near the Red HQ. The player will get one strawberry per red bee in their hive per use.
* As a reward from the Ant Challenge.
* Redeeming certain valid codes:
* 15MMembers (Gives 15 strawberries + other stuff).
* As a reward from memory matches.
* As a stack reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* Pink Cupcake Sticker rewards 50 strawberries.
* Pale Heart Sticker rewards 50 strawberries.
* Digging in to the Beesmas Feast.
Drops[]
* Farming in the Mushroom Field, Strawberry Field, Rose Field, Cactus Field, and the Pepper Patch.
* Harvesting Leaves in these fields may also yield strawberries.
* As a drop from Ladybugs, Scorpions, and King Beetle.
* As a drop from a Festive Gift or Festive Mark.
* As a gift from a cub buddy.
* As a reward for completely growing any kind of Sprout except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
* Planting a sprout in the Mushroom Field, Rose Field, and Pepper Patch yields 4x more strawberries than other fields. Planting a sprout in the Strawberry Field yields 8x more strawberries than other fields.
* Harvesting Sparkles (higher chances in red fields).
* As a drop from Puffshrooms in red fields or fields with high amounts of red flowers (Clover Field, Cactus Field, and Mountain Top Field).
* As a drop from planters (besides Tacky Planter, Blue Clay Planter and The Planter Of Plenty), most likely in red fields.
* The Red Clay Planter always drops strawberries upon harvest at full growth.
