Flight of the Bumble Bee Egg
The Flight of the Bumble Egg is a cosmetic hat that could have been obtained by completing the quest from Egg Hunt Info, from April 17, 2019, through May 13, 2019, which was during the Roblox Egg Hunt 2019 event. Since it is a Roblox hat, players can wear it on their avatar for display.
The quest required 3 Marshmallow Bees, which could be obtained by any NPC on the map (at the time) except Onett and the Honey Bee NPC "Honey Bee \(NPC\)"), and through the code, “Marshmallow” (now invalid). When the player had 3 marshmallow bees in their inventory, they could turn it in to the Egg Hunt Info for the Flight of the Bumble Egg. Receiving the Flight of the Bumble Egg also rewards an Event Badge exclusive for the Egg Hunt.

Trivia[]
* Like the Swarming Egg of the Hive and How to Program BASIC, the Flight of the Bumble Egg _does not give any special boosts._
* This item is used in Black Bear’s Honey Wreath.
* The name is a reference to the song "Flight of the Bumble Bee," by a Russian composer, Rimsky-Korsakov.
