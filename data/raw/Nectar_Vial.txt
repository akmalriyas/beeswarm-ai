Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Nectar Vial

Nectar Vial
Nectar Vials inside a player's inventory.
Nectar Vials are a type of inventory item that can be used to increase a player's current Nectar by 4 hours when used. Using any Nectar Vial grants 4 hours of their respective nectar type. You can hold a maximum of 10 Nectar Vials of each type at a time. However, this limit can be bypassed by obtaining them through quests or Robux Packs.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Using the Nectar Condenser to convert 12 hours of nectar into a Nectar Vial which stores 4 hours of nectar.
* As a reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack.
* Adding the Satisfying, Refreshing, Motivating, Invigorating, and Comforting Nectar Icon rewards the player with their respective nectar vial.
* Adding the Offline Voucher rewards 1 Nectar Shower Vial.
* Defeating a Level 10 Snowbear drops 1 Invigorating Vial.
* Buying the Feelin' Festive Pack for 800 Robux grants 5 Nectar Shower Vials and other items.
* From purchasing certain bundles from Bee Bear's Catalog.
* Purchasing the Frozen Forest Pack for 1,200 Snowflakes and 40 Gingerbread Bears grants 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
* Purchasing the Season's Sharing Bundle for 600 Snowflakes and 60 Gingerbread Bears grants 1 Nectar Shower Vial and other items.
* From giving Presents to certain NPCs.
* Giving Gifted Bucko Bee a Present rewards 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
* Giving Gifted Riley Bee a Present rewards 1 Invigorating Vial and other items.
* From opening certain gift boxes.
* Opening the Ruby Gift Box rewards 1 Invigorating Vial and other items.
* Opening the Restful Gift Box rewards 1 Nectar Shower Vial and other items.
* As a reward from certain quests.
* Completing several Dapper Bear quests reward the player with at least one of every nectar vial.

Variants[]
There is a total of six variants of Nectar Vials. Five of which; immediately grant 4 hours of a type of Nectar buff to the player. The other grants 4 hours of every Nectar buff to all players in the server. The type of Nectar that the player is granted depends on the type of Nectar Vial that they choose to use, with the exception of the Nectar Shower Vial.

Trivia[]
* While most of the Nectar Vials have a letter on them which represents the first letter of their name (C for Comforting, I for Invigorating, M for Motivating, S for Satisfying), the Refreshing Vial doesn't have an R on it to represent the first letter of its name. The Nectar Shower Vial has no icon at all, as it just seems to be a mix of all the previous Nectars.
* There is only a limited amount of Nectar Vials that can be obtained by the player without the use of the Nectar Condenser.
* For Comforting Vials, the total amount that can be obtained is 26.
* For Invigorating Vials, the total amount that can be obtained is 22.
* For Motivating Vials, the total amount that can be obtained is 13.
* For Refreshing Vials, the total amount that can be obtained is 14.
* For Satisfying Vials, the total amount that can be obtained is 19.
* For Nectar Shower Vials, the total amount that can be obtained is 34.
* The Nectar Shower Vial is the only vial that is not obtainable from the Nectar Condenser.
* Using Nectar Vials does count toward collecting nectar in quests.
* However, they do not contribute to the Top Nectar Collector Leaderboards.
