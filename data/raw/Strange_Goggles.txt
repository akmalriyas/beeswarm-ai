> "_This futuristic looking headset doesn't seem to do very much..._ "
The Strange Goggles is a hat accessory that was added in the 2020-11-23 Update. It could be bought from the Noob Shop until it was taken out from the shop in the 2024-07-17 Update for being bugged. It currently can be worn but it can't be re-equipped.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 77 Honey.
1 Micro-Converter
7 7-Pronged Cogs
This accessory was made for the Ready Player Two event in Bee Swarm Simulator. As such, it is very distinct from other accessories in this game. and it required 7 codes to be redeemed. The codes to obtain all 7 of the 7-Pronged Cogs are: Dysentery, Luther, Millie, CarmenSanDiego, Jumpstart, Troggles and WordFactory. Upon equipping this item, the player was able to interact with Digital Bee (NPC) "Digital Bee \(NPC\)") in The Computer.
Fact: 7 7-Pronged Cogs statistic: * +77 Capacity..
* +7% Defense.

Trivia[]
* This is the cheapest item in the game in terms of honey besides the Pouch and Scooper, which are automatically received for free.
* This is the only item that doesn't show up in the item/gear space.
* This is the first item that is required to enter a specific area, the second being the Propeller Hat and the Hiking Boots.
* While wearing these and doing the Digital Bee "Digital Bee \(NPC\)")'s quests, the objective field will occasionally "Glitch" with brightly colored textures appearing on the Field decorations. This is purely cosmetic and does not affect gameplay.
* This is the third hat that has been made unobtainable, the first and second being the B.B.M. Mask and the Mondo B.B.M. Mask. Unlike the other two, this hat was removed, meaning it cannot be re-equipped anymore.
