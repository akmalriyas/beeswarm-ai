The Cactus Field is a field located past the Honey Bee Gate and lies right beside the Pumpkin Patch. It is protected by the Werewolf.
This field can spawn honey, treat, strawberry, blueberry, ticket, magic bean, bitterberry, and stinger tokens when collecting pollen.
Bees that like the Cactus Field are Bomber Bee, Hasty Bee, Commander Bee, Demo Bee, and Vicious Bee. Bees that dislike the Cactus Field are Exhausted Bee, Baby Bee, Music Bee, Tadpole Bee, and Tabby Bee.
This field grants Invigorating Nectar when a planter is planted here.
A Level 4-7 Rogue Vicious Bee has a chance of spawning in this field. This is a 33×18 field, containing 594 flowers. The flowers are white, red, and blue, in sizes of double and triple.

Obtaining Boosts[]
* Using the Field Booster may give a Cactus Field Boost. When it does, it will give +100% Cactus Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using glitter will add 100% Cactus Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
The Cactus Field Boost icon.
Using field dice, smooth dice, and loaded dice may add +100% - 300% Cactus Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't maxed out and is based on the type of dice used.
* Cactus Field Winds can be obtained through the Wind Shrine or codes. They have a base of +20% instant Cactus Field conversion and give s+5% instant Cactus Field conversion and +15% Cactus Field pollen for each stack. Cactus Field Winds can stack up to a total of 13 times.
* The Icicles Beequip can grant up to +10% Cactus Field pollen.
* It can be boosted by the Shell Amulet. If it has a Cactus Field Boost, it can boost the field by +5% to +27% depending on the amulet rarity.

Trivia[]
* This is the only field where three types of treats can be found naturally in the same place.
* This is the fourth-smallest field in the game, the smaller ones being the Pumpkin Patch, Strawberry Field, and the Stump Field.
* It is possible to hide behind the cactus closest to the Pine Tree Forest so the werewolf cannot attack the player.
* This is one of six fields where fireflies travel to, with the others being Bamboo Field, Pineapple Patch, Rose Field, Spider Field, and Strawberry Field.
* This is one of three fields that is protected by the werewolf, the others being the Pumpkin Patch and the Pine Tree Forest.
