Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Coconut

Fact: Coconut statistic: _"Drops a huge Coconut into the field. Catch it to convert Pollen to Honey Tokens."_.
A Coconut is an inventory item that was added in the 2019-09-28 update. It will spawn a large, green circle on the field you used it on. If caught, it will convert pollen into a ring of 10 honey tokens. It can also collect pollen for the player that summoned it, damage mobs, and can be used in certain crafting recipes.
Fact: Coconut statistic: Pollen from coconuts can be increased with the Coconut Clogs, pollination, and the Coconut Field Market Boost (now impossible to obtain as the Boost Market has been removed)..
A maximum of 500 coconuts can be held at a time, as the item’s highest capacity limit, excluding items that don’t have a capacity limit.
There’s also an On/Off switch on the player’s hotbar (if the coconut Item is in the player’s hotbar). The coconut will be used automatically when this switch is turned on.

Conversion[]
Fact: Conversion[] statistic: When the coconut lands on a player, Pollen is converted in your Backpack and has a maximum amount of 100% of the player's total hive convert amount. For example, if a player has 500,000 total hive convert amount and a coconut lands on them, up to 500,000 pollen would be converted into honey tokens..
The amount of honey received from the coconut is multiplied by Honey From Tokens.

Trivia[]
* Before the 2021-12-26 update, the player could only hold a max of 150 Coconuts. It has been increased to 500 after said update.
* Getting hit by a coconut originally converted 75% of the player's hive convert amount into honey.
* This is the only inventory item that can directly damage mobs.
* If you have no pollen in your bag, catching a coconut will still spawn honey tokens that give 1 Honey each, (before Honey From Tokens is applied). This happens because a honey token needs to give you at least 1 honey.
* When used in the Hive Hub, a total of 1,500 Honey is given, meaning each honey token grants 150 honey.
