The Clover Field is one of the five starting fields any player is able to visit upon starting the game. It is protected by a Ladybug and a Rhino Beetle.
Events[]
* A Puffshroom may naturally spawn here between the 15th minute (xx:15) and 45th minute (xx:45) of the hour.
* A level 1-3 Rogue Vicious Bee may spawn here.
* An active Wild Windy Bee may move to this field.
* During a Stick Bug Challenge, if Stick Bug is in Sunflower Field or Bamboo Field, he will move to this field.
This field is a 26x29 field, containing 754 flowers. The flowers are white, red, and blue, in sizes of single and double.

This field can spawn treat, red boost, blue boost, ticket, magic bean, royal jelly, gold or diamond egg and star jelly tokens when collecting pollen.
Bees that like the Clover Field are Basic Bee, Cobalt Bee, Crimson Bee, Brave Bee, Looker Bee, Stubborn Bee, Music Bee, Puppy Bee, and Tabby Bee. Bees that dislike the Clover Field are Lion Bee and Photon Bee.
Planters planted here produce Invigorating Nectar.
Obtaining Boosts[]
* Using the Field Booster may give a Clover Field Boost. When it does, it will give +200% Clover Field pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using glitter will add 100% Clover Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes, if it isn't already maxed out.
* Using field dice, smooth dice, or loaded dice may add +100% (field dice) \- 300% (loaded dice) Clover Field pollen for 15 minutes if the boost isn't maxed out yet.
* Clover Field Winds can be obtained through the Wind Shrine or codes. They have a base of +20% instant Clover Field conversion and give +5% instant Clover Field conversion and +20% Clover Field pollen for each stack. All Field Winds (Including Clover Field Winds) can stack up to a total of 13 times.
* Redeeming certain valid codes:
* Troggles (Gives Clover Field Code Buff + other stuff).
* It can be boosted by the King Beetle Amulet. If it has a Clover Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% to +100%.
* It can be boosted by the Shell Amulet. If it has a Clover Field Boost, it can boost the field by +5% to +30% depending on the amulet rarity.

Trivia[]
* The field booster for the Clover Field has been decreased three times. In the 2018-07-11 update, it was decreased from x5 to x4, in the 2019-04-05 update it was decreased to x3, and in the 2019-09-28 update, it was decreased to x2.
* Before the 2018-07-11 update, the Clover Field had 39 rows instead of the current 26 so it had 1131 flowers, and it was the largest field in the game. It is no longer the largest field in the game, with only 5 less flowers than the new largest, the Pineapple Patch.
* The Bubble Wand on top of the Blue HQ will sometimes blow sparkles on to this field, along with the Mushroom Field, Blue Flower Field and Dandelion Field.
* The Honeystorm Summoner used to be where the Wealth Clock is currently, but when the Wealth Clock was added, the Honeystorm Pad was moved to right beside the bush in the Clover Field where the Rhino Beetle spawns.
* The King Beetle Lair is right under this field.
* This is the first field the player can encounter in the game that is defended by two mobs.
* When a Rogue Vicious Bee spawns in the Clover Field, its spike can be seen in King Beetle's Lair, which can still be activated.
* If a sprout is in the Clover Field, the stem and the light can be seen in King Beetle's Lair.
* Clover Field has a higher chance than any other field of dropping royal jelly when a sprout pops.
* This field also has a low chance of spawning a star jelly.
* If the player has the Spark Staff, is in the King Beetle's Lair, and jumps, they are able to collect pollen from the Clover Field.
* This is the only field that gives eggs.
* This is the only starting field with roughly the same amount of Single and Double flowers, while the other 4 fields in the Starter Zone all have around 90% Single flowers, and the rest being Double.
