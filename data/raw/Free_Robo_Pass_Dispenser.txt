> _Not to be confused with theRobo Pass Dispenser which requires tickets._
The Free Robo Pass Dispenser is a dispenser located in the 30 Bee Zone, directly opposite of the Nectar Pot. When used, it gives the player a free Robo Pass every 22 hours, which can be used to participate in the Robo Bear Challenge. Players cannot use the dispenser if they already have 10 or more Robo Passes in their inventory.
Trivia[]
* There used to be a free Robo Pass token behind the Free Robo Pass Dispenser, this has been replaced with the Broken Drive.
* This, the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser and the Glue Dispenser, have a cooldown of 22 hours, being tied with the longest cooldown of any dispenser in the game.
* This and the Free Ant Pass Dispenser are the only free dispensers in the game that do not give any buffs when used.
* This, Robo Pass Dispenser, the Free Ant Pass Dispenser, the Ant Pass Dispenser, the Coconut Dispenser, and the Glue Dispenser are the only dispensers that do not grant Haste when used.
* This, the Robo Pass Dispenser, the Ant Pass Dispenser, and the Free Ant Pass Dispenser are the only dispensers that don't have any visible faucets. Instead, they have a small compartment, similar to a vending machine or an ATM.
