Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Box O Frogs

Box O Frogs
Box-O-Frogs
_"Summons 3 Frogs that last for 3 minutes."_
Box-O-Frogs is an inventory item that was added in the Beesmas 2019 update. When used, three gifted frogs will be summoned in any field the player is currently standing in, which last for three minutes, that can collect tokens, produce bubbles around the field (if spawned in a field), and attack hostile mobs.
It can also be used outside of a field, but the frogs summoned will aimlessly hop, which then won’t do anything unless one of the frogs is in the field. The frogs have infinite HP and cannot die.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Completing 111 Brown Bear quests rewards 5 Boxes-O-Frogs.
* Completing 333 Brown Bear quests rewards 3 Boxes-O-Frogs.
* Completing Black Bear’s "Bubble Trouble" quest rewards 1 Box-O-Frogs and other items.
* Completing Black Bear's "Boss Battles" quest rewards 3 Boxes-O-Frogs and other items.
* Adding the BBM From Below Sticker to the Sticker Stack rewards 1 Box-O-Frogs.
* A low chance to drop from Onett's Lid Art.
* A low chance to drop from Brown Bear's Stockings.

Trivia[]
* Box-O-Frogs and Night Bells are the only two consumable items that summon mobs.
* Box-O-Frogs are one of the two ways to summon a frog, the other being Tadpole Bee's Summon Frog ability.
* Frogs summoned in the Gummy Bear's Lair fall through the floor.
