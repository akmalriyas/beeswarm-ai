Gifted Bucko Bee is a quest giver and one of three permanent Quest Bees, the others being  Gifted Riley Bee and  Honey Bee "Honey Bee \(NPC\)"). Gifted Bucko Bee is located on the second floor of the Blue HQ, in between the Top Bucko Bee Helpers and the Daily Top Blue Collectors Leaderboards. The player cannot start receiving or completing its quests without a Translator, which is given by Science Bear three times. Completing 150 quests from Gifted Bucko Bee is required to craft the Hydroponic Planter and completing 250 quests from Gifted Bucko Bee is required to craft the Tide Popper.

Trivia[]
* Since the quests are all chosen at random, including the first, the formula for the first requirements of any quest is "x divided by (number of Gifted Bucko Bee quests completed +1)".
* Gifted Bucko Bee is one of the three NPCs to be based on bees, the other two being Gifted Riley Bee and Honey Bee (NPC) "Honey Bee \(NPC\)").
* This Bee is the only quest bee to reside indoors, though Gifted Riley Bee resided indoors too before an update.
* In the Bucko Bee's Snow Machine for Beesmas 2020 dialogue, Gifted Bucko Bee says to defeat ten spiders. However, the quest requires the player to defeat five spiders.
* Gifted Bucko Bee enjoys playing with marbles, as evidenced by their Present dialogue in which they remark “Can't wait to crack this open and roll those bad boys around.”
