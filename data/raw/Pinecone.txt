Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Pinecone

"A seed-producing organ produced by Conifer trees."
Fact: Pinecone statistic: +Convert Amount%.
Fact: Pinecone statistic: +Ability Rate%.
Fact: Pinecone statistic: +Pine Tree Forest Capacity%.
Fact: Pinecone statistic: +Pine Tree Forest Pollen%.
A Pinecone is a level 9 Beesmas Beequip. It has an equip limit of two. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to Bumble, Stubborn, Bucko, Frosty, Carpenter, or Bear bees.
