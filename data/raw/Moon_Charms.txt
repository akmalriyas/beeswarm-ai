Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Moon Charms

Moon Charms
_"Increases your bond with a bee by 250."_
The two types of moon charm tokens: On the left, there is a token that is not glowing, dropped by mobs or mythic meteors. On the right, there is a glowing variant that is dropped during nighttime from fireflies, sparkles, and moon sprouts.
A Moon Charm , when fed to a bee, raises its bond by 250. Moon Charms are also used in generating Moon Amulets and they are used in crafting of glitter and the Helmet. If fed to a radioactive bee, it has a 1/2,500 (0.04%) chance to give that bee a mutation.
Moon charm tokens last for 10 seconds before disappearing.
