* [Red Boost] Grants x1.2 pollen from red Flowers for 15s. Stacks up to 10 times.

Trivia[]
* This bee, Ninja Bee, Fuzzy Bee, and Windy Bee are the only bees that have only one skin color.
* This bee used to have a spelling or grammar mistake in its description, along with Hasty Bee, Shocked Bee, and Rascal Bee, but it was fixed in an update.
* This bee is the only bee that has a smoke effect.
* This bee is the red counterpart of Ninja Bee.
* A Special Jelly that changes a bee into a Shy Bee could have been obtained from the code, 'NewMic' (expired).
* This bee, Diamond Bee and Demon Bee are the only legendary bees with a passive ability.
