Photon Bee is a Colorless Event bee that hatches out of a Photon Bee Egg, which is available in the Ticket Tent for 500 Tickets.
Like all other Event bees, this bee does not have a favorite treat, the only way to make Photon Bee gifted is either with a Star Treat, Gingerbread Bears or Aged Gingerbread Bears.
Photon Bee likes the Pineapple Patch and the Pumpkin Patch. It dislikes the Clover Field.

Trivia[]
* Photon Bee's energy is set to 999,999,999,999,999.
* It was the first Event bee to be added in the Ticket Tent and the second Event bee added to the game, with Bear Bee being the first.
* It was also the first Event bee that is purchased using tickets.
* This bee and the Ninja Bee are tied as the fastest Bees in the game, with a speed of 21.
* Photon and Exhausted Bee are the only bees that don't require sleep and have infinite energy. However, they still need to rest at the hive when you die.
* This bee, Festive Bee and Vicious Bee are the only Event bees that have Ability Tokens generated by non-Event bees.
* This bee, Ninja Bee, Cobalt Bee, Tadpole Bee, Windy Bee, Crimson Bee, and Precise Bee are the only bees that create a trail behind them (not counting particle trails).
* This is the first event bee with a trail.
* Gummy Bee also creates a trail, but it can only be seen while the Gummy Mask ability is active.
* This bee, Bear Bee, Festive Bee, Gummy Bee, Tadpole Bee and Tabby Bee are the only bees to have a Gifted Bonus that affects their signature ability.
* If Photon Bee is gifted, its Beamstorm is white instead of yellow and instantly converts its collected pollen.
* The sun model on Photon Bee's rear has 20 spikes.
* Because Photon Bee has infinite energy, there is a glitch if a Royal jelly is used on a Photon Bee, the transformed bee will have a ridiculous amount of energy.
* This glitch is also shared when transforming an Exhausted Bee, since that has endless energy as well.
* All versions of the Ticket Tent have had this bee on top, except for the initial release.
* Although Photon Bee's energy is infinite, it is still possible to get an energy mutation on Photon Bee. However, the mutation will have no effect, similar to an attack mutation on a Baby Bee.
* Photon Bee's Beamstorm ability is able to collect pollen from other fields. If the token was close enough to the other field, the range of the Beamstorm can possibly be able to reach it.
