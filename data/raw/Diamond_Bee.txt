Trivia[]
* The light colors around Diamond Bee make it nearly translucent.
* Diamond Bee has the highest base convert rate out of all bees, only beaten by Tabby Bee when its Tabby Love stack is at 626 or higher.
Fact: Trivia[] requirement: * Diamond Bee and Honey Bee are the only bees in the game which can generate Honey Gift or Honey Gift+ tokens..
* This, Bubble Bee, Fire Bee, Honey Bee, Demon Bee, and Gummy Bee are the only bees to have masks based on them, specifically the Diamond Mask for Diamond Bee.
* Diamond Bee is the blue counterpart of Demon Bee.
* Diamond Bee and Gummy Bee are the only bees to have a sparkle effect.
* Diamond Bee has a unique conversion link color (cyan/light blue). It is the only bee to have a conversion link that is not yellow.
* Diamond Bee is one of only two bees to have the same name as an egg they can hatch from; the other being Basic Bee, and those eggs being the Diamond Egg and Basic Egg respectively.
* Diamond bee may like the Pineapple Patch because pineapples have historically been symbols of wealth.
