Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Mazes

Mazes are secret areas in the game that contain items inside of them. To obtain these items, the player needs to navigate through the mazes and find them. There are currently two mazes that exist in the game, each located in their own respective location.
Blue Maze[]
The Blue Maze is located next to the Ticket Tent (or behind the Beige Gift Box in Beesmas 2021) . It was added in the Egg Hunt 2019 Update. This requires no bee requisites to enter since it's located in the Starter Zone.
Inside the maze, the player can obtain 5 tickets by taking a left turn first and then a right turn, and a jelly bean token by taking the path on the right. There used to be a plastic egg where the tickets were in the 2019 Egg Hunt. The player can also find a star jelly token on the right, but it is blocked by a glass wall. It can be obtained by going through a passage behind the Petal Shop.
When in the maze, the following audio "Vendor" plays:
<https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/File:Vendor.ogg>
Spotted Chicks could spawn in this maze before they were removed.
Pro Shop Maze[]
The Pro Shop Maze is located behind Pro Bear in the Pro Shop. It requires at least 10 bees to pass through the Brave Bee Gate, where the Pro Shop is located.
The player can obtain a royal jelly by going forward to the end and turning right two times, then going to the first opening available. Follow through it to find the royal jelly token.
There is also a sunflower seed token inside the maze that awards 10 sunflower seeds. The player can obtain it by taking same path as the royal jelly, but instead of turning on the last right, go to the end of the hall and follow through the last corridor.
If the player gets close enough, they can grab both tokens through the walls, making collecting them easier.
