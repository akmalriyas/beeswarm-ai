Swirled Wax
Swirled Wax
_"A spiraled hunk of beeswax. Used to redetermine the stats of a Bee's Beequip."_
Swirled Wax is an inventory item and a type of wax that can be used on a beequip to reroll its stats. Beequips with higher potential have a greater chance of having better stats when rerolled. Swirled wax has Upgrade Value of 0.
When used on a beequip, it will change its stats. If the Beequip has waxes used on it, it will alter the enhancements as well.
When a swirled wax is applied to a beequip, a message pops up saying:
The Wax altered the Beequip's stats
Sometimes, the stats won't show any changes when you use a swirled wax. Most of the time, the bug can be fixed by rejoining the game.

* As a reward from Gifted Riley Bee's Honeyday Candles. (1.62%)

* As a rare drop from a Epic Puffshroom. An uncommon drop from a Legendary Puffshroom, and a common drop from a Mythic Puffshroom.
* As a very rare drop from Diamond Aphids.
* As a drop from certain planters.
* Planting a Plastic Planter in the Coconut Field guarantees one every month.

* Planting a Pesticide Planter in the Strawberry Field guarantees one every month.
* Planting a Heat-Treated Planter in the Blue Flower Field guarantees one every month.
* Planting a Hydroponic Planter in the Mushroom Field guarantees one every month.
* Planting a Petal Planter in the Strawberry Field guarantees one every month.
* The Planter Of Plenty has a high chance of dropping one swirled wax in any field.
* The Planter Of Plenty may drop 5 swirled waxes with one every month.

Trivia[]
* This is the first wax to be added to the game. It was added in Beesmas 2020 while the rest were added in Beesmas 2021.
* This wax, Soft Wax and Debug Wax are the only waxes guaranteed to change a Beequip's stats.
* Before the 2021-12-26 update, this item had no favor when donated to the Wind Shrine.
* Swirled wax used to have a limit of 5, but the limit was removed in the 2021-12-26 update.
* Before the 2021-12-26 update, swirled wax did not have a wick in its icon design.
* This is the only wax that was not included in the Waxy Bundle.
