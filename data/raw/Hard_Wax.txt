Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Hard Wax

_"A firm hunk of beeswax. Has a 60% chance to improve a bee's Beequip."_
Hard Wax is an inventory item and a type of wax added in the 2021-12-26 update that can be used on a Beequip to improve its stats. Upon usage, it has a 60% chance of improving the Beequip's stats. The other 40% and the Hard Wax gives no improvement, simply taking up a space in a wax slot. Hard Wax has Upgrade Value of 2.
Hard Waxes can also be used to craft tools and items, such as planters.
If giving a Hard Wax to a bee's Beequip is successful, the message pops up saying:
The Wax has Improved the Beequip!
However, if it fails, the message pops up saying:
The Wax didn't seem to help...
