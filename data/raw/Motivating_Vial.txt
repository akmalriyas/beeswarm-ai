Motivating Vial
Motivating Vial
Fact: Motivating Vial statistic: _"Grants 4 hours of Motivating Nectar. (Boosts: Blue Pollen, Convert Rate, Bee Ability Rate)"_.
The Motivating Vial is a consumable nectar vial item that was added in the 2022-12-26 update. Once used, it grants the player 4 hours of the Motivating Nectar buff instantly. It can also be used to craft other accessories.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Using the Nectar Condenser to condense 12 hours of Motivating Nectar.
* Adding the Motivating Nectar Icon to the Sticker Stack rewards 1 Motivating Vial.
* Completing Dapper Bear's 8th quest rewards 1 Motivating Vial and other items.
Outdated Ways[]
* Purchasing the Nighttime Nectar Pack in the Robux Shop for 400 Robux granted 1 Motivating Vial and other items.
* Purchasing the Violet Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog during Summer 2024 for 400 Snowflakes and 20 Gingerbread Bears granted 1 Motivating Vial and other items.
* Purchasing the Hefty Honeyday Bundle from Bee Bear's Catalog during Beesmas 2022 for 25,000 Snowflakes and 50 Gingerbread Bears granted 1 Motivating Vial and other items.
* Opening the Mythic Gift Box rewarded 2 Motivating Vials and other items.
* Completing Bee Bear's Peppermint Preparation (2/5) rewarded 1 Motivating Vial + other items.
* Completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest rewarded 1 Motivating Vial and other items.
