Invigorating Vial
Invigorating Vial
Fact: Invigorating Vial statistic: _"Grants 4 hours of Invigorating Nectar. (Boosts: Red Pollen, Convert Rate, Bee Attack.)"_.
The Invigorating Vial is a consumable nectar vial item that was added in the 2022-12-26 update. Once used, it grants the player 4 hours of the Invigorating Nectar buff instantly. It can also be used to craft other accessories and tools.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Completing Dapper Bear's 9th and 12th quests rewards 1 Invigorating Vial and other items.
* Using the Nectar Condenser to condense 12 hours of Invigorating Nectar.
* Adding the Invigorating Nectar Icon to the Sticker Stack rewards 1 Invigorating Vial.
* Giving a Present to Gifted Riley Bee rewards 1 Invigorating Vial and other items.
* Defeating a Level 10 Snowbear drops 1 Invigorating Vial.
Outdated Ways[]
* Purchasing the Charming Bundle in Bee Bear's Catalog granted 1 Invigorating Vial and other items.
* Purchasing the Hefty Honeyday Bundle in Bee Bear’s Catalog granted 5 Invigorating Vials and other items.
* Giving Gifted Riley Bee a Present during Beesmas 2022 rewarded 1 Invigorating Vial and other items.
* Opening the Obnoxious Gift Box rewarded 1 Invigorating Vial and other items.
* Opening the Cherry Gift Box "Gift Boxes") rewarded 1 Invigorating Vial and other items.
* Completing Stick Bug's Beesmas 2022 quest's Beesmas 2022 quest rewarded 2 Invigorating Vials and other items.
* Defeating a Level 10 during Beesmas 2022, Summer 2024, or Winter 2024 dropped 1 Invigorating Vial.
* Defeating a Level 24 during Beesmas 2022, Summer 2024, or Winter 2024 dropped 1 Invigorating Vial.
* Purchasing the Nighttime Nectar Pack in the Robux Shop for 400 Robux granted 1 Invigorating Vial and other items.
