The Ticket Tent is a shop located behind the Red Cannon and near the Hives. It sells six types of Event Bees, Silver Eggs, Gold Eggs, Diamond Eggs, Mythic Eggs, and Star Treats. They can only be purchased with Tickets, hence its name. There is also a Royal Jelly token on top of the Ticket Tent.

Trivia[]
* The only bee in this shop that could be obtained without tickets (for a limited time) was Festive Bee.
* This used to also apply to Gummy Bee, which is now permanently available for 2500 gumdrops.
* The Royal Jelly token on top of the Ticket Tent was mentioned by Sun Bear in his dialogue.
* Buying a star treat from here is one of the only ways to obtain star treats without Robux.
* Before the 2019-04-05 update, if the player had the Goo Hotshot Badge, they could go up to the Gummy Bee on top of the tent and use gumdrops to enter Gummy Bear's Lair. However, this mechanic was moved to the Gummy Bee Egg Claim when it was added.
* The wings on the gifted Photon Bee's model are not transparent, while the giant Festive Bee and Puppy Bees' wings are.
* Additionally, the wings of the Festive Bee model aren't solid, while the wings of the Puppy Bee and Gifted Photon Bee models are.
* The Photon Bee is also the only model that is in gifted form.
* When the Ticket Tent was implemented, the most expensive item was the star treat, costing 1,000 tickets. However, after the Beesmas 2019 event ended, the most expensive item was the Cub Buddy, costing 2,000 tickets. After the 2024-05-23 update, Mythic Eggs now cost the most, at 2,500 tickets.
* Like the Noob Shop or Pro Shop, Spotted chicks could spawn here during the Egg Hunt 2020 event.
* Cub Buddies used to be sold in this shop, but after the 2024-05-23 update, this is no longer the case.
* It is possible to see the back of the Ticket Tent by going out of bounds behind the Coconut Field.
