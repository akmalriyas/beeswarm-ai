The Boost Market was a shop that could have been found in the 30 Bee Zone. Since the Beesmas 2021 update, it has been replaced by the Nectar Pot.
Every 30 minutes, the shop allowed the player to purchase a market boost at a certain price. The price depended on the number of people who have purchased that specific boost, much like in a real stock market. It cost both Tickets and Honey to purchase a boost. These boosts lasted for 30 minutes. Market boosts could also have been obtained from certain codes, like RedMarket, or the ones used in the Ready Player Two event.

Trivia[]
* This was removed because Onett said that it was a "Failed Experiment," [1] and the prices were also getting excessively high due to exploiters purchasing them.
* The Boost Market, Evictions, and the Most Commando Captures Leaderboard are the only removed content that weren't exclusive to events.
