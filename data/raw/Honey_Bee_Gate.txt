The Honey Bee Gate , also called the 15 Bee Gate , is a gate that requires 15 bees to be able to pass through. It is the third gate that the player should be able to access, ahead of the Brave Bee Gate and the Basic Bee Gate.
There are 4 different fields behind the Honey Bee Gate, being the Cactus Field, the Pumpkin Patch, the Pine Tree Forest, and the Rose Field.
It is given its name from the large Honey Bee located on top of it.
Trivia[]
* This gate has the highest number of fields behind it, which is four.
* There were numerous ways to bypass the gate without 15 bees, but as of the 2019-09-28 update, it will always teleport the player back to spawn.
* It has the second-most quest givers, at 4, with the Starter Zone beating it with 5.
* This gate holds the ramp that leads to the Lion Bee Gate, also known as the the 25 Bee Gate.
* This gate has the most assets in it, with 19 of them.
