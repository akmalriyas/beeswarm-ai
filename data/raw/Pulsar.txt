The Pulsar is a tool that can be purchased in the Pro Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 125,000 Honey.
It collects 2 pollen from 29 surrounding patches in 1 second, which comes out to a base pollen collection rate of 58 pollen per second.
It has what looks to be a black orb with green smoke emitting from it and is surrounded by three black rings. Two of these rings have a small black orb connected to them, with one above and one to the right of the middle orb.

Trivia[]
* The Pulsar has a similar collection range as the Honey Dipper and the Porcelain Dipper, only both of them have a slightly bigger collection range, and the Porcelain Dipper summons a large pillar of light every 10th swing that collects pollen.
* The Pulsar, Gummyballer, and Sticker-Seeker are tied for the slowest tool in the game (in terms of how long the cooldown of each collection lasts).
* The Pulsar may possibly be the upgraded version of the Vacuum.
* This is because pulsars are a type of neutron star (which have high gravity and suck stuff in, similar to a vacuum's function).
