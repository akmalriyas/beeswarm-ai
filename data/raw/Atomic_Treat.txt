Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Atomic Treat

Atomic Treat
Atomic Treat
_"Always causes a random Mutation in a bee! Mutations grant bonus stats."_
An atomic treat token.
An Atomic Treat , when fed to a bee, raises its bond by 1000 and gives the bee a random mutation. The mutations are Attack, Convert Amount, Gather Amount, Energy, and Bee Ability Rate. These are not the favorite treat of any bee.
Ways to Obtain[]
* As a very rare drop from defeating the Coconut Crab.
* As a rare reward from the Robo Bear Challenge.
* As a rare drop from Debug Sprouts.
* As a very rare drop from a Mythic Meteor.
