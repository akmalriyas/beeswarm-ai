Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Red Extract

Red Extract
Red Extract
Fact: Red Extract statistic: _"Grants x1.25 Red Pollen for 10 minutes."_.
Fact: Red Extract statistic: A **Red Extract** is an inventory item that was added in the 2018-11-25 update. The purpose of it is to use it as a material to craft accessories, or for a 10-minute boost that grants x1.25 Red Pollen. The buff cannot stack and will only reset its timer if you used a **Red Extract** when there is another one active. Using a Purple Potion or Super Smoothie will override the buff..
A red extract token.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Crafting it via Blender for:
* 50 Strawberries
* 10 Royal Jellies
* Matching a pair of red extracts in Mega Memory Match (worth 1 red extract) or Extreme Memory Match (worth 3 red extracts).
* As a reward from the Wind Shrine.
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain Stickers to the Sticker Stack:
* Flying Rad Bee Sticker rewards 1 red extract.
* Round Rascal Bee Sticker rewards 1 red extract.
* Flying Festive Bee Sticker rewards 1 red extract.
* Little Scorpion Sticker rewards 1 red extract.
* Red X Sticker rewards 1 red extract.
* AFK Sticker rewards 1 red extract.
* Small Flame Sticker rewards 1 red extract.
* Shrugging Heart Sticker rewards 1 red extract.
* Red Doodle Person Sticker rewards 1 red extract.
* Scythe Sticker rewards 1 red extract.
* Dark Scythe Sticker rewards 1 red extract.
* As a reward from the Retro Swarm Challenge.
Drops[]
* As a drop from certain mobs
* Ladybug (Rare)
* Scorpion (Rare)
* King Beetle
* Rage Aphid
* Farming in the Mushroom Field, Strawberry Field, Rose Field, or the Pepper Patch can rarely drop red extracts.
* A rare drop from leaves in the Mushroom Field, Strawberry Field, or the Rose Field.
* As a rare drop from sprouts (the odds increase depending on the rarity of the sprout and if the sprout is located in red fields).
* As a drop from a Festive Gift (if Festive Bee is gifted) or Festive Mark.
* As a rare gift from a cub buddy.
* As a guaranteed drop from the certain planters:
* Red Clay Planter
* Heat-Treated Planter (Drops extra red extracts in red fields.)
* Planting the Heat-Treated Planter in the Mushroom Field, then the Strawberry Field, then the Rose Field, then the Pepper Patch results in an additional 100 Red Extracts (unknown cooldown).
* Additionally, it has a small chance to drop 100 Red Extracts upon harvesting in any field.
* Planters harvested in red fields have a chance to drop red extracts.
