The Super-Scooper is a tool that can be purchased in the Pro Shop.
Crafting Ingredients
Fact: Crafting Ingredients requirement: 40,000 Honey.
It collects 4 pollen from 5 flowers in a line every 0.5 seconds, which comes out to a base pollen collection rate of 40 pollen per second.
Appearance[]
The Super Scooper is the advanced version of the Scooper. Its blade is noticeably bigger than the Scooper and has a more rectangular shape along with it's blue and yellow scheme. The handle is the same grey as the Scooper with dark red in the middle. The pole is dark red and has 6 yellow and blue attachments on it. The player holds the Super Scooper so that it appears to be pointing upwards.
