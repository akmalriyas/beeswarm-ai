Daily Fastest Crab Slayers
The Daily Fastest Crab Slayers is one of the 64 leaderboards in the game, and is only viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu in the System Page. This leaderboard shows players' fastest time to defeat the Coconut Crab on that day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM CST.
Prizes[]
If the player has reached the top 5 by the end of the day, they will be awarded the Cyan Hilted Sword Sticker.
If the player has reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with 25 Tickets.
Trivia[]
