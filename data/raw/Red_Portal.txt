Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Red Portal

Red Portal
The Red Teleporter pad in the Red HQ.
The destination of the Red Teleporter, behind Science Bear.
The Red Teleporter is a portal located inside the Red HQ. When activated, it will instantly teleport the player on top of the rock located directly behind Science Bear. The transportation is one-way; thus, the destination teleporter cannot be used to travel back into the Red HQ.
To activate the Red Teleporter, the player must walk onto the white teleport pad and press the "E" key on their keyboard if they are on PC or laptop. However, if they are on mobile, then they must walk onto the pad and tap on the button that appears at the top of the screen.
Trivia[]
* Similar to its blue counterpart, the Red Teleporter used to use an animation where each of the rings would revolve in circles. However, this can get buggy since the rings will start to spin, but then they will slow down until they reach the point of completely stopping.
* A Ticket token is located near the ceiling of the Red HQ. This can be collected by jumping up on top of the Red Teleporter, then jumping or parachuting to the token. Enhanced Jump Power, such as from bear morph, or boots, is required to jump high enough.
* The seven circles on the Red Portal near Science Bear are solid, and with enhanced Jump Power, it is possible to jump outside of the barriers of the map by jumping on the center part.
