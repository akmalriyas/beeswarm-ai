Noob Bear is the bear who runs the Noob Shop. Like other Shop Bears, his presence is not required to purchase from the shop, and players cannot interact with him.

Trivia[]
* He is the first shop bear to be accessed in the game.
* As with the other bears, Noob Bear can sometimes fall over or fall apart.
* This glitch has been mostly patched, so it occurs very rarely as of now.
* This bear, Pro Bear, Top Bear, and Shadow Bear are the only bears without any significant purpose.
* Noob Bear uses the Cartoony Animation Package.
* Noob Bear is represented by one of the twelve Cub Buddy skins, specifically the Noob Cub. This skin was obtainable by buying the Cub Buddy Launch Pack.
