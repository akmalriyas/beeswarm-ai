Petal Shop
Petal Shop
The interior of the Petal Shop.
Location
In the cave near the Windy Bee Gate, under the Wind Shrine.
The Petal Shop is a shop located past the Windy Bee Gate. Inside, the player can craft the Petal Wand, the Petal Belt and the Petal Planter. The All-Time Top White Collectors Leaderboard and Daily Top White Collectors Leaderboard can also be found here.
It is called the Petal Shop because of the giant petals draped across the back wall. In addition, each of its items require a spirit petal to craft (except for the Petal Planter).

Trivia[]
* The wall behind the Petal Shop is semi-transparent and can be walked through. Behind it, there's a corridor which leads to a star jelly token that can be seen in the Blue Maze, from where the jellybeans token is found. The token awards one star jelly.
* There used to be the Mysterious Figure in the place of the Star Jelly.
* Occasionally, rain from clouds in the Pepper Patch may be visible in the Petal Shop.
* The beam of a sprout can also be seen behind the semi-transparent wall.
