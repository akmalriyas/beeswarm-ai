Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Turpentine

Turpentine
_"A strong solvent that can remove all Waxes from a bee's Beequip."_
Turpentine is a craftable inventory item added in the 2021-12-26 update. It is used to remove all waxes from a Beequip.
Turpentine have a maximum cap of 25. Obtaining while having more than 25 turpentines will not add them to the player's inventory unless received through quests, redeeming codes, or purchasing them in the Robux Shop through limited-time offers.
Ways to Obtain[]
* Crafting via Blender for:
* 10 Super Smoothies
* 10 Caustic Waxes
* 100 Star Jellies
* 1,000 Honeysuckles
* As an extremely rare drop from an Epic Puffshroom, a very rare drop from a Legendary Puffshroom and an uncommon drop from a Mythic Puffshroom.
* As a very rare drop from any planter.
* As a reward for reaching the Top 25 Daily Leaderboard for Retro Swarm Challenge.
* As a very rare reward from the Retro Swarm Challenge.
* As an extremely rare reward from the Robo Bear Challenge.
* As a reward from completing Stick Bug's Beesmas quest.
Fact: * 1,000 Honeysuckles requirement: * Purchasing the Purple-Turple Mega Pack from Bee Bear's Catalog gives 1 Turpentine + other items..
* By purchasing certain packs from the Robux Shop.
Fact: * 1,000 Honeysuckles requirement: * Purchasing the Merry Mythic Mondo-Haul Bundle for 4,500 Robux gives 1 Turpentine + other items..
* As a drop from certain Beesmas decorations:
* Dapper Bear's Samovar drops a Turpentine on the 25th use.
* Completing Robo Party on rank 45 and 50 for the first time gives the player who started it a Turpentine and other rewards.
