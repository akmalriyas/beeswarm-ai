Trivia[]
* The total amount of crafting materials needed to craft this is 2,500 gumdrops, 1,250 royal jellies, 2,500 pineapples, and 1,250 sunflower seeds.
* Prior to the 2019-04-05 update, the Honeycomb Belt's description was just 'Honey'.
* This is one of the four belt accessories released after Sun Bear left, the others being the Petal Belt, the Coconut Belt and the Belt Pocket.
* It is the 3rd most expensive belt accessory in the game behind the Petal Belt and the Coconut Belt.
