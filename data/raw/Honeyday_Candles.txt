Context: Bee Swarm Simulator - Honeyday Candles

Honeyday Candles
Datamined information: The probability of getting every item and bee.
Date of datamined file: December 19th, 2024
The Honeyday Candles after completing Gifted Riley Bee's Beesmas Quest.
The Honeyday Candles are a Beesmas-exclusive machine which is unlocked after completing Gifted Riley Bee's Honeyday Candles quest. They have a cooldown of 4 hours, and are located next to the Red HQ's interior ladder.
When activated, 3 random level 10 red bees are summoned for 30 minutes, and 3 waxes of a random type are spawned.
The chances for each wax type are as follows:
Fact: The Wax in Honeyday Candles has/costs Chance.
Fact: The 3 Soft Waxes in Honeyday Candles has/costs ~81.3%.
Fact: The 3 Hard Waxes in Honeyday Candles has/costs ~16.26%.
Fact: The 3 Swirled Waxes in Honeyday Candles has/costs ~1.63%.
Fact: The 3 Caustic Waxes in Honeyday Candles has/costs ~0.81%.
The chances for each bee are as follows:
Fact: The Bee in Honeyday Candles has/costs Chance.
Fact: The Fire Bee in Honeyday Candles has/costs ~70.59%.
Fact: The Demon Bee in Honeyday Candles has/costs ~23.53%.
Fact: The Spicy Bee in Honeyday Candles has/costs ~5.88%.
When a player stands on top of the pad before completing the quest, a red text box appears on the top of the screen reading: The red bees are having trouble lighting the candles...
Trivia[]
* A Hard Wax token used to be found hidden behind the candles.
* These are one of the four machines that grants the player temporary bees, the others being Onett's Lid Art, Gummy Beacon, and the Honey Wreath (after completing Honey Bee's "Honey Bee \(NPC\)") Beesmas quest).
* There is a glitch when the player leaves, the bees generated would stay in place until they despawned.
* Even when the player joins the same server, the bees will not despawn.
* They were introduced in the Beesmas 2020 event and returned in all Beesmas updates since.
* During Beesmas 2020, using these candles were one of the two only ways of getting Swirled Waxes, the other way is by giving Gifted Riley Bee a Present during that same event.
* For every Beesmas event succeeding Beesmas 2020, the Honeyday Candles would no longer guarantee a Swirled Wax.
* This is the only machine that can summon bees who produce flames.
* Loot Luck does not affect Honeyday Candles drops.
* Summoned bees don't have to rest, even if you die.
