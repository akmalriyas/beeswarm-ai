Commando Chick/Drops
Milestone rewards include:
* Glue upon the 5th capture.
* Gold Egg upon the 10th capture.
* Star Jelly upon the 15th capture.
* 3 Blue Extracts upon the 20th capture.
* 100 Tickets upon the 25th capture.
* 10 Stingers upon the 30th capture.
* 10 Ant Passes and 10 Stingers upon the 40th capture.
* Mythic Egg upon the 50th capture.
* Star Treat upon the 100th capture.
* Commander Bee Egg upon the 200th capture.
